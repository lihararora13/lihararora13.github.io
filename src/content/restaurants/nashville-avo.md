AVO started life in a shipping container and grew up into one of Nashville's smartest dining rooms, now in a proper building in oneC1TY under the Element Hotel, but the idea never changed: take food people already crave and rebuild it entirely from plants until nobody is thinking about what is missing.

Kimchi spring rolls with cashew pate, kale Caesar with walnut dressing, lentil-walnut burgers and that frozen avocado margarita make this the easiest table in town for mixed groups. Most of the menu is gluten-free, lunch can be quick, and dinner happily stretches.
