Pura Vida Tacos is a vegan Mexican restaurant in the city centre, near the Stadsschouwburg theatre, with a large front terrace. The menu is tacos, burritos, burrito bowls and nachos, with vegan chorizo and chilli sin carne among the favourites, and the room is bright, loud and built for a fun dinner.

It is open for lunch and dinner most days, with evening-only service on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Order the vegan tacos and the chilli sin carne. Note: an older HappyCow update form mentions a Bocadero address at Rijnkaai; the Nieuwstad restaurant is the trading address on this page.
