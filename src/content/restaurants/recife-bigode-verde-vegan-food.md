Bigode Verde Vegan Food is a fully vegan brazilian spot in Boa Viagem. In business since 2014, it is the city's most established all-vegan address, a compact counter in the Galeria Centro Sul that regulars treat as a canteen.

Order the Hambúrguer vegano, Parmigiana vegana, Salads, and Muffins. Come for lunch when the burger and parmigiana are at their best, and save room for a smoothie.
