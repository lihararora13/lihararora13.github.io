Snackyee's mission is to serve Indian street food in a hygienic dining room, and it does it well: jhol momos, chilli momos, masala dosa and white-sauce pasta, all veg, all day, with breakfast, lunch and dinner from 10 in the morning to midnight.

The jhol momos are the dish to order, and the masala dosa is the safe second. Ask for no cheese and no paneer and the menu is largely plant-based as served.
