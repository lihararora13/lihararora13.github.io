Benedict is a breakfast restaurant at BIG Krayot on Histadrut Boulevard, one of a chain across Israel, serving Yo Egg vegan sunny-side up eggs, salads that can be made vegan with vegan cheese, shakshuka with a Yo Egg option, three vegan pancake options and a variety of full breakfast options.

It is open daily, with Friday hours until 3:00pm and Saturday from 8:00am. Order the Yo Egg shakshuka or the vegan pancakes. Note the pin is at neighbourhood level for the Krayot area.
