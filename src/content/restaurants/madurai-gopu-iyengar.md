Gopu Iyengar is the Iyengar tiffin room that Madurai locals name when asked for the pure-veg spots not to miss, alongside Murugan Idli and the Modern Restaurant. It is small, old and exact about its craft: idli, dosa and filter coffee, done the way the Iyengar kitchens have always done them.

Order the idli and a dosa, and drink the filter coffee the proper way, poured high from the davara. It is breakfast the way Madurai has eaten it for generations.
