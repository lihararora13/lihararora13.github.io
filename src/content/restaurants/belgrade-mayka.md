Mayka has been feeding Belgrade's vegetarians since 2016, and its mixed menu is the point: burgers, goulash, samosas and pizza, all in one room, with vegan options across the board. It is the easy group dinner when everyone wants something different.

Hours are reported to be unreliable, so check ahead. Order a samosa to start, a burger or a pizza for the main, and enjoy the most easygoing room on this list.
