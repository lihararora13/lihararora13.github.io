La Cave is the most unusual table in this batch: a fully plant-based French wine bar on Green Street in Old Pasadena, run by chef Romuald Guiot and connected to the vegan pastry shop ID Eclair next door. The menu is small plates, a cheese board made with cashew-based cheeses rather than store-bought alternatives, leek salad, a vegetable garden bouquet, hummus plate, Parisian sandwich and daily soup, with a wine list that reviewers call heavenly. After dinner the server brings a tray of vegan pastries from ID Eclair, eclairs, croissants and macarons.

Hours are Wednesday to Sunday 3pm to 9pm, closed Monday and Tuesday. It is a wine bar first, so come in the evening, order the cheese board and a glass, and finish with an eclair.
