Radost Fina Kuhinjica, little kitchen of joy, has been Belgrade's plant-based anchor for years, and it still draws the crowds. Hummus and dips, soups, ramen, tofu dishes, smoothies and vegan ice cream, in a hidden room you enter through the grey building at number 3.

Go in, turn right, look for the HappyCow sticker on the door. Order a falafel bowl, a ramen if it is on, and save room for the ice cream.
