La Cordobesita is Tulum's best vegan truck stand: a fully vegan food truck where you pull up a plastic stool and eat greasy street food the way it was intended. The Centro truck runs weekend nights only, roughly 6pm to 1am, while the second truck in La Veleta opens daily from around 12:30pm.

It is an evening and late-night stop, so do not go looking for it at lunchtime on a weekday. Bring cash, order the pastor, and expect the friendliest service in town.
