60 seconds to napoli is Dortmund's Neapolitan pizzeria on the Markt, a wood-fired-oven room with red pendant lights and a menu built for sharing. The vegan side is solid: pizzas with plant-based cheese, arugula and tomato toppings, and the signature pizza bowl, a crust formed into a bowl and filled with pasta, all of it open every day from half past eleven until midnight.

It is a mixed menu, so order the vegan-marked pizzas and ask for plant-based cheese explicitly. Come for a late dinner after the shops close, order the vegan margherita first, and split a pizza bowl for the table.
