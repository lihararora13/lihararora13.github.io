Evergreen Organics is the pioneer: established in 2016 and reported to be the first fully vegan restaurant in Qatar, with a focus on seasonal, local and organic food. The Pearl branch in Qanat Quartier does smoothie bowls, juices, salads, open-face sandwiches, burgers, pancakes, French toast and cakes, with a grab-and-go selection too.

Order a smoothie bowl for breakfast or a burger for lunch, and take a jar of nut butter home. This is the restaurant Doha's vegan scene is built on.
