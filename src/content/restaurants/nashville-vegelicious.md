Vegelicious tops HappyCow's Nashville list for a reason: this is vegan soul food done with real love, the kind of cooking that makes first-timers stop mid-bite. The Soul Food Plate is the headline act, but the hot fried chick'n, the Reuben with house seitan and the Saturday brunch pancakes all have their own devoted followings.

It is a small, warm, Black-owned cafe where the owner is often the person greeting you, and the cakes, pineapple, strawberry and coconut, are non-negotiable. Come hungry, order the ginger beer, and thank us later.
