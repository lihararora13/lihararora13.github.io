Exotiko is a Mediterranean kitchen on the Frauentorstraße that describes itself in five words, exotic, fresh, regional, sustainable and organic, and cooks to match: falafel sandwiches and wraps, schawarma plates, mixed plates and bowls, all of it vegetarian with a strong vegan range. It is one of the highest-rated tables in Augsburg, and the room has the busy, friendly feel of a place the whole old town eats at.

It is open Monday to Saturday from half past eleven to nine. Order the falafel sandwich or the Exotiko mix plate, and expect a generous, well-spiced lunch for a fair price.
