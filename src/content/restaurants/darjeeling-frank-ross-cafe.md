Frank Ross Cafe is the cosy one: a pure-vegetarian cafe with wooden parquet floors, wicker chairs, green marble tables and a large mountain mural, filmed by a visitor in July 2026 who calls it a must-visit for pure-veg travellers in Darjeeling. The fried momos, vegetable chow mein and Manchurian-style gravy are the dishes to order.

The star, by the visitor's account, is the hot chocolate, a large mug of rich chocolate topped with shavings. Ask for the momos and chow mein without dairy and you have a proper hill-station vegan lunch.
