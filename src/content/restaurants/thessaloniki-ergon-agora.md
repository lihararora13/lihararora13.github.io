Ergon Agora is the market restaurant on Tsimiski Street, part of the Ergon brand that celebrates Greek produce, with carefully crafted vegetarian options that adapt to vegan. The strength is ingredient quality: olive oils, seasonal vegetables and local grains, with vegan swaps to replace dairy with soy cheese and plant yogurts.

It is open daily from 8am to 11:30pm. Ask for the vegan swaps when you order, since the menu is not vegan by default.
