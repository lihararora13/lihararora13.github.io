Street Beet is Detroit's vegan fast-food paradise: a pop-up turned permanent Corktown restaurant, doing smashburgers, supreme crunchwraps, patty melts and coconut ube milkshakes, all of it a playful riff on the fast-food menus it grew up on. The smashburger is the signature, the crunchwrap is the cult item, and the milkshake is the finish.

It moved into its permanent home in 2025 and is now expanding with a soft-serve window and an oat milk coffee shop under the same roof. Order the smashburger, add the crunchwrap, and enjoy the nostalgia.
