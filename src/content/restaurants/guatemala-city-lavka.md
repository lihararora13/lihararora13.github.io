Lavka is a fully vegan restaurant inside the Mercadito 14 in Zona 14, listed on HappyCow as fully vegan since October 2020. Its address is disputed, with place data saying 4 Avenida 12-29 and HappyCow saying 22-29; the HappyCow address is used here.

Order the Falafel, Fettuccini con crema de anacardo, Stroganoff vegano, and Medovik. The cashew-cream fettuccini is the plate to try; the medovik is the dessert.
