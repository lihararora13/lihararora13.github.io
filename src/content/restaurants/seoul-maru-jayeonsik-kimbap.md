Maru is proof that the best seat in Insadong might be a plastic stool: a pocket-sized, all-vegan bunsik bar tucked inside the Insadong Maru complex, rolling kimbap and ladling tteokbokki for a few thousand won a plate. HappyCow calls it top-rated in Seoul, reviewers keep going back three, four, six times in a single trip, and the listing was still showing fresh hours days before this guide went live.

Get the fried tofu kimbap, the tteokbokki with its fish-free odeng, and a cup of sweet pumpkin sikhye to finish. Closed Mondays, cash-friendly, and worth the small hunt down the alley.
