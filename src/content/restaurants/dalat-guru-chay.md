Guru Chay is a vegetarian cafe on Hoang Dieu in Dalat, serving Asian dishes, tofu, spring rolls, some Western dishes and coffee, open 10am to 10pm. It is the latest-opening vegetarian kitchen in Dalat, as most others close by 8pm or 9pm, which makes it the best vegan-friendly dinner option in the city.

Order the tofu dishes and spring rolls, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is a comfortable, modern cafe, and it suits an evening meal.
