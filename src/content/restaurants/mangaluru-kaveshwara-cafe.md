Kaveshwara Cafe is the Kadri Park institution, a 100% pure-veg room that has been feeding the neighbourhood across breakfast, lunch and dinner for years. The sign lists South Indian, North Indian, Chinese, chaats and desserts, and the local food pages pitch it as the hassle-free family stop, with ample parking and 7 AM to 10 PM hours.

Order the masala dosa or a chaat plate and ask the kitchen to hold the dairy. It is the most complete pure-veg menu in Mangaluru, and the safest bet when the group wants everything.
