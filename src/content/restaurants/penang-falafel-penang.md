Falafel Penang is the change of pace every long trip needs: a fully vegan Middle Eastern spot on Lebuh Chulia serving crisp falafel wraps, hummus plates and fresh salads. After days of laksa and fried mee, a good falafel wrap tastes like a holiday in itself, and this one is done properly.

Come for a quick lunch, order a wrap and a hummus plate, and take the rest of the afternoon slow. This is the one for when you want something different.
