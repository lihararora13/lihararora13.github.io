Murugan Idli Shop is the Madurai tiffin room, the 1991 flagship on West Masi Street that the city measures all other idli by. The malligai idlis are soft as flowers and come with four chutneys, and the ghee podi idli is the signature, though vegans will want the podi without the ghee.

Order the malligai idli and a filter coffee, and go early, because the queue is part of the experience. This is the single most Madurai meal on the list.
