Trigo Verde has been Oaxaca's naturista institution for years, a vegetarian restaurant and health-food store in one, and it is the most established plant-based room in the city. Burgers, entomatadas, memelas, hummus and the house salad, with vegan options across the menu.

Order memelas and the house salad. It is the old-school, wholesome lunch on this list, and the one locals have been eating at for decades.
