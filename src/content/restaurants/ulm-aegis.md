Aegis Café describes itself as a café like no other, a place where good food, literature, music and art come together, and the room lives up to it: bagels, hummus, cakes and coffee, with vegan options, served in a space that hosts readings and exhibitions as readily as it serves lunch. It is open seven days a week, nine to ten on weekdays and ten to six on Sundays, which makes it the most reliable café table in Ulm.

Order a hummus bagel and an oat cappuccino, stay for a slice of vegan cake, and treat it as the afternoon to spend rather than the meal to rush.
