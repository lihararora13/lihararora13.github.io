Real Vegetal is a casual vegan restaurant and grocery store a short walk from Parque Poblado, and one of the most quietly loved tables in El Poblado. The menu is built for sharing: small dishes centred on cashew cheese fermented for 24 hours, from hasselback potatoes with black garlic cream cheese to crispy oyster mushroom tacos, birria tacos, four cheese pasta and vegetable lasagna, plus risotto, soups and baos.

The room is minimalist, concrete walls and a calm, unhurried feel, and the grocery shelf means you can take some of the pantry home. A July 2026 visit from a travelling vegan vlogger ended in dinner here followed by cocktails, which feels about right for a Poblado evening.
