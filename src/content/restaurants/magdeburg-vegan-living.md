Vegan Living is Magdeburg's fully vegan Asian restaurant, a health-focused kitchen by the Domfelsen serving dumplings, sushi, spring rolls, banh bao and udon, all of it plant-based. The house line is simple, everything is vegan and lovingly plated, and the menu covers the Japanese and Vietnamese classics with a light touch. It is open for lunch and dinner every day, which makes it the most reliable all-vegan table in the city.

Come for a spread of small plates rather than a single main: mix maki, banh bao and cha gio cover the range nicely. Note that it is cash only, and the afternoon break splits lunch and dinner service. This is the everyday all-vegan pick, the one to return to.
