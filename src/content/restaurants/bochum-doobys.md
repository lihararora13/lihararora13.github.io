Dooby's is Bochum's fully vegan street-food shop on Universitätsstraße in Ehrenfeld, a counter doing the comfort-food canon without a scrap of animal product: the dicker Dürüm, smashed cheeze burgers, falafel bowls and quesadillas, with private events on the side. The room is small, the portions are not, and the menu reads like a dare to anyone who thinks vegan street food is a salad.

Hours are split: lunch from twelve until two and evenings from five until eight on weekdays, with afternoons and evenings at weekends. Order the dicker Dürüm first, the smashed cheeze burger second, and come hungry.
