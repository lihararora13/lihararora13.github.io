Saborami is a small café a few blocks off Kokusai-dori on Ukishima-dori, run by one owner who makes everything by hand, and it is one of Naha's most loved vegan-friendly stops. The menu runs to vegan curry rice, tantan noodles and tomato ramen, toast, ice cream and chocolate tart, plus a soy-milk hot ginger latte, and the house apple bread is made without dairy or butter.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, the café also serves fish, so specify vegan when you order. It closes at 20:00 and is reliably closed on Sundays; a second closed day is reported inconsistently, so check ahead. Everything is made by the owner alone, so be patient and it will be worth the wait.
