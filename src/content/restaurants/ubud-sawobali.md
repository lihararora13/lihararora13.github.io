Sawobali is the best value vegan meal in Ubud, and one of the most loved. The little warung in Peliatan has served the same daily all-you-can-eat buffet since 2016: Indonesian fusion cooking with tempeh, tofu, salads, soup and desserts, all vegan, gluten-free and Sattvic, which means no onion or garlic. The owners happily explain every dish, the sambal is very spicy, and the whole spread costs about 75,000 rupiah.

It moved from its original Jalan Hanoman home to Jalan Sukma Kesuma, and the buffet runs daily from 3pm to 9pm, so plan it as an early dinner rather than lunch. Come hungry, take your time, and save room for the desserts.
