Amazing Thai Restaurant is a Thai restaurant on Fenton Street in Rotorua, serving vegan-friendly Thai dishes. It is a comfortable, modern room in the centre, and it suits a dinner.

Order the vegetable curries and a pad Thai, and ask for the vegan dishes without fish sauce. It is central and reliable.
