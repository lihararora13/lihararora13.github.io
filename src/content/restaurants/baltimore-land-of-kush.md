Land of Kush has been Baltimore's vegan soul food anchor since 2011, a Black-owned and woman-owned kitchen downtown that built its name on plates that convert skeptics. Everything on the menu is plant-based, from the crab cakes and BBQ ribs to the curry chicken and sweet potato cake, and the room is a funky, mural-lined counter with takeout as the main format.

Order the vegan crab cakes and the BBQ rib tips with collard greens and mac and cheese, and save room for the carrot cake. It is the safest first-night choice in the city and the clearest proof that Baltimore's vegan scene starts with soul food.
