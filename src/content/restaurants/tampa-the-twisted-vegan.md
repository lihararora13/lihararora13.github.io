The Twisted Vegan is an all-vegan comfort-food restaurant on West Busch Boulevard, doing burgers, loaded fries, and Southern plates. The loaded fries are the signature, and the room is casual, counter-service, and built for a hearty lunch.

Order the vegan burger and the loaded fries. Note the distance: the Busch Boulevard corridor is a drive from downtown.
