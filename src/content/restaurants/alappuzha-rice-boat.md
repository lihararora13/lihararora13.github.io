Rice Boat is the pure-veg restaurant inside Hotel Grand Alleppey on Mullakkal Road, and a May 2026 food post recommends it for the cheese masala dosa, the standard masala dosa and the neer roast, calling them super tasty with chutney and sambar. The room caters largely to tourists, and the signage carries the pure-vegetarian tagline proudly.

Order the masala dosa and the neer roast, and skip the cheese version. It is the hotel restaurant for the Mullakkal stay, and the dosa is the reason to go.
