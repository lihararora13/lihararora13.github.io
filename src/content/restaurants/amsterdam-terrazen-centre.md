Jamaican and Japanese vegan food under one roof sounds like a fever dream, but TerraZen Centre has been making it work since the 1970s. This funky, family-run spot near Nieuwmarkt serves communal-seated meals of plantains, curry, and rice alongside dragonroll sushi made with tempeh. Small, cozy, and full of character.

Prices are reasonable, portions are hearty, and the vibe is warm in every sense. It is the kind of place regulars guard jealously, so now you are in on the secret.
