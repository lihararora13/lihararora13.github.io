Biga is the national cafe chain's Nazareth branch, a reliable cafe with vegan-friendly dishes, salads and pastas. It is a chain room, so expectations should be set accordingly, but it is open long hours.

Order a vegan salad and a coffee with oat milk. It is the safe fallback when the Al-Bishara rooms are full.
