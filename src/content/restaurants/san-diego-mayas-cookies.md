Every city guide needs a sugar stop, and San Diego's is Maya's Cookies. Founder Maya Madsen bootstrapped the business from farmers' market tents a decade ago, and it has grown into America's number-one Black-owned gourmet vegan cookie company with stores in Grantville and San Marcos plus nationwide shipping. The cookies are artisanal and hand-scooped: crunchy outside, soft inside, and the kind of thing non-vegans steal off your plate.

The rotating weekly menu keeps regulars on their toes, but the Everything cookie, the birthday cake cookie, and the chocolate chip s'mores (a PETA Libby Award winner for favorite vegan baked good) are the legends. Buy the box. You will not share it.
