Jambo Cafe brings African and Caribbean cooking to Cerrillos Road, and its menu has always had a deep vegan-friendly bench: jerk-spiced tofu, curried vegetables, coconut rice, and plantains, served in a relaxed, family-run room. It is the most affordable sit-down dinner on this list.

Come hungry and share across the table; the curries and the plantains travel well together.
