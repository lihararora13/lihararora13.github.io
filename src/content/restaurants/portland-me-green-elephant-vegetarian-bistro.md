Green Elephant is the anchor of Portland's vegan scene, a Pan-Asian vegetarian bistro on Congress Street that has been cooking this way since 2007. Most of the menu is vegan or veganises easily, with dishes clearly labelled, and the Siamese Dream Curry Noodles and the vegetable dumplings are the ones reviewers name over and over.

It is walk-in only and fills up, especially at dinner, so come early or plan on takeout. Save room for the pumpkin tapioca pudding.
