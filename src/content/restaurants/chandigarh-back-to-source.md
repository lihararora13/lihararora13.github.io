Back to Source is the most thoughtful cafe in Chandigarh: an organic cafe, restaurant and bakery in Sector 17 where the sourdough is baked that morning, the cold coffee is sweetened with desi khaand instead of refined sugar, and the vegan Buddha bowl is genuinely plant-based. The room is calm and plant-filled, the kind of place where a two-hour lunch feels like no imposition at all.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, the kitchen also uses bilona desi ghee and milk products, so order the vegan dishes as named. The Buddha bowl and the falafel with hummus are the orders, and the vegan cakes, made on specific days, are worth asking about.
