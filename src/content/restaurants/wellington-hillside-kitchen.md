Hillside Kitchen is Wellington's fine dining answer to plant-based eating, a small villa restaurant on Tinakori Road that went fully vegetarian in 2018 and has been one of the city's most acclaimed tables ever since. There is no a la carte menu: the kitchen writes a set multi-course menu around what is in season, what it has grown in its own garden, and what it has foraged, and a full vegan menu is always available when you book. The shorter set menu runs to $92 and the ten-course tasting menu to $127, with a non-alcoholic pairing that reviewers rate above the wine list.

This is the special-occasion pick on the list, calm and Scandi-styled rather than stuffy, and the one to book when you want to show a sceptic what vegetables can do as the main event. Tell them about dietary requirements when you book, commit to the ten-course menu, and take home a loaf of the house sourdough, which is dependable enough to be a souvenir in its own right.
