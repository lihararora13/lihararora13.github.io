The Lazy Barn is the café that tries hardest for vegans in Jodhpur: a relaxed Ratanada room above Marwar Sweets where the reviews praise the vegan options, and where the owner once went out and bought vegan chocolate specially so a guest could have a vegan hot chocolate. That is the kind of place this is.

Order the vegan hot chocolate and a pasta, and settle into the slowest afternoon on this list. The pin is approximate to the Ratanada area, so navigate by the Marwar Sweets landmark.
