Mediterranea is an Italian and Mediterranean restaurant on Tirtodipuran, a short walk from Prawirotaman, with a relaxed garden setting and a menu of pasta, pizza and salads. It is the Italian pick on this list, and the vegan-friendly dishes are a proper part of the menu rather than an afterthought.

Order a vegan pasta and a salad. It is a meat-serving restaurant with vegan options, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it is the best evening table in the Prawirotaman area for a mixed group.
