Elvie is a small café on the Fuggerstraße, doing what small cafés do best: cinnamon buns, cakes, wraps and a catering service, with vegan options across the board. It keeps daytime hours, Monday to Friday until four and Saturday until two, which makes it a morning and lunch stop rather than an evening one. The Zimtschnecken are the thing regulars come back for.

Order a cinnamon bun and a coffee, add a wrap if it is lunchtime, and treat it as the sweet pause between the old town's bigger tables.
