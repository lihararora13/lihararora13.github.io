Sree Krishna Inn is the hidden one: a vegetarian restaurant in a two-storey ancestral house with a red-tiled roof on Warriam Road, two minutes off MG Road, established in 2000 and still trading daily from 8am to 10:30pm. It does no marketing and is barely signed, but locals have eaten here for decades and TripAdvisor tags it vegetarian-friendly with vegan options.

Come for breakfast, a large dosa with chutneys, or the Sunday vegetarian breakfast, and expect a peaceful, uncrowded room with wooden furniture and traditional decor. Ask for no ghee and no curd and the South Indian dishes are vegan.
