Spice Cove is a vegetarian Vietnamese bistro on Main Street with a 5.0 HappyCow rating and a kitchen that knows exactly which dishes are vegan and which need a tweak. The vegan pho and the fresh spring rolls are the dishes to ask for first, and the lemongrass tofu is the regulars' pick for something heartier.

Reservations are recommended, and the dining room is small, so book ahead for dinner. It is open Thursday and Friday from 8am and weekends from 10:30am.
