Terlingua brings Texas smokehouse energy to East Bayside, and its vegan-friendly bench is better than a barbecue joint has any right to offer: veggie tacos, smoked vegetables, and sides that hold their own next to the meat dishes. The room is loud, fun, and dog-friendly.

Go for the tacos and stay for a margarita. It is the best evening-energy stop on this list.
