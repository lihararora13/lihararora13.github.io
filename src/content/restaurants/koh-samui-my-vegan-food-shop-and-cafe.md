MY Vegan Food Shop & Cafe is a fully vegan cafe in Taling Ngam, on the quiet south-west side of the island, rated a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow. It was previously called Marga Yoga and was reported fully vegan in December 2023. The room is a traditional wooden building with seating on two floors, and it doubles as a shop supplying vegan cheese, vegan meat and other vegan products. The menu covers cooked and raw vegan dishes: pizza, burgers, wraps, salads, vegan eggs, vegan cheese and meat platters, plus water kefir, nut milks and ayurvedic drinks.

It is open Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 3:30pm, and it is cash only, so bring baht and do not plan a weekend visit. Its own Facebook page posted in October 2026 that it is on the Kohme Samui food delivery app, confirming it is still trading.
