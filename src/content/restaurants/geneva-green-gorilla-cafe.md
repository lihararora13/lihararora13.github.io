Green Gorilla is the easy, everyday stop on this list: a bright café on a shopping street where the avocado toast is genuinely famous and the smoothies, açaí bowls and vegan cookies do the rest. It is the kind of place you duck into between errands and end up staying for a second coffee.

It is not a white-tablecloth affair, and that is the point. Come for breakfast or a light lunch, order the avocado toast, and let it reset your expectations of what a Geneva café can do for vegans.
