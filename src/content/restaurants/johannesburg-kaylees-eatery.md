Kaylee's Eatery is the most fun room on this list: a fully vegan bistro inside the Comic Warehouse complex in Bedfordview, where you eat Belgian waffles and Beyond burgers surrounded by vintage comics and collectables. Founder Kaylee Gottschalk trained at Le Cordon Bleu in London and the Institute of Culinary Education in New York, and the menu shows it, from the showstopper Beyond cheddar burger to a monthly vegan high tea at R395 a head.

It is quirky, family-friendly and pet-friendly, with a vegan market attached for stocking up on supplies. Order the Beyond Vegan Cheddar Cheese Burger, the Belgian waffles with caramelised apple, wholewheat pancakes, and one of the gourmet vegan milkshakes, thick, creamy, and topped with vegan treats. The cafe now trades under the Comic Cafe theme, same kitchen, same address.
