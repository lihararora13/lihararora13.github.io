The Green Rooftop Café sits on the fourth floor at Ludwigsplatz in the Altstadt, and it is the rooftop on this page: açaí bowls, matcha lattes, avocado sandwiches and Biscoff iced lattes, with a view over the old town. It is the sunny-day pick, and the room is built for lingering.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so check the vegan dishes on the menu. The açaí bowl and the Biscoff iced latte are the orders.
