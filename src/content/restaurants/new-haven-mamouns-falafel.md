Mamoun's Falafel is a falafel restaurant on Howe Street in downtown New Haven, doing the namesake falafel in pita bread baked fresh daily. The falafel is vegan and gluten-free, and the room is a long-standing downtown institution.

Order the falafel in pita with hummus and tabbouleh. It is counter service, so eat and move on.
