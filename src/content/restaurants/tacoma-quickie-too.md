Quickie Too is Tacoma's vegan soul food kitchen and the only fully vegan restaurant in the city: a takeout-only spot on Martin Luther King Jr Way in the Hilltop, serving a Crazy Jamaican Burger, Mac & Yease, a Friday Night Fish Fry and hush puppies. It is cashless and open limited days, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday core, with some sources also listing Monday 3pm to 7:30pm, so check before you go. The sandwiches are also sold at Central Co-op, Stadium Thriftway and Tacoma Boys if the kitchen is closed.

Order the Crazy Jamaican Burger and the Mac & Yease. It is takeout only, so plan to eat elsewhere, and go on a Friday for the fish fry.
