Mornings is Batel's bright, easy cafe: brunch plates, avocado toast and good coffee, with vegan-friendly options across the breakfast and lunch menu. It is the kind of place where a slow morning stretches happily into the afternoon, and the Batel neighbourhood around it is lovely for a walk afterwards.

Come for brunch, order avocado toast and a coffee, and take your time. This is the one for a slow Curitiba morning.
