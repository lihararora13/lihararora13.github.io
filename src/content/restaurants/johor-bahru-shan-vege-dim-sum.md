Shan Vege Dim Sum is Johor Bahru's first fully vegetarian dim sum restaurant, out in Taman Ekoperniagaan near Senai airport, and it is worth the drive: an excellent selection of traditional Chinese dim sum, with staff who will help vegan guests work out which pieces are suitable. The chee cheong fun, steamed rice noodle rolls stuffed with mushrooms, is the dish reviewers name first, alongside xiaolongbao, fried enoki mushroom, carrot cake, cabbage rolls and siu mai. An October 2026 vegan food tour of the city called the soup-filled buns its favourite bite in Johor Bahru.

It is a breakfast and lunch room, open 7:30am to 2:30pm Monday to Saturday and till 2pm on Sunday, so go early. Some guides list Wednesday as closed while the restaurant's own listing shows Wednesday hours, so check ahead if Wednesday is your day.
