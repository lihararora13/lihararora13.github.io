Nutri Vida is a no-frills vegetarian lunch restaurant in the north wing, and its vegan feijoada and bobó de abóbora are the plates regulars come back for.

Order the Feijoada vegana, Bobó de abóbora, and Churrasco de glúten. The feijoada vegana is the signature; the churrasco de glúten is the second pick.
