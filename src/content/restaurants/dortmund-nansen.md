Nansen is a restaurant and learning gastronomy project in Dortmund's old harbour on Speicherstraße, a vegetarian kitchen where trainees learn the trade and the menu changes with the seasons. The vegan side of the menu includes farinata and dumplings alongside the house dishes, with matcha and a small drinks list, in a converted harbour building that feels a world away from the shopping streets.

It is an evening place, open Tuesday to Saturday from five, until ten on weeknights and eleven at the weekend. Come for a quiet harbour dinner, order the farinata, and treat it as Dortmund's most unusual vegan-friendly room.
