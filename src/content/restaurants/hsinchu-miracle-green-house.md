Miracle Green House is a vegan restaurant in the North District serving pasta, pizza and hot pots, with no meat, egg or dairy, according to the Taiwanese vegetarian guide, which gave it two stars in 2025. It is also listed on the Hsinchu City tourism site, and it is the best vegan Italian table in the city.

Order the vegan pasta and the vegan pizza, and try the hot pot if you want something warming. It is closed on Thursdays, so do not plan a Thursday visit.
