Men Impossible calls itself a ramen restaurant on the edge, and honestly, it earns the title. Every bowl is 100% plant-based with no MSG and no additives, served up in a narrow brick-lined space that smells like the best kind of decision. The broths are deep, the noodles are springy, and even the tofu skeptics leave converted.

The 3-course dinner deal at 34 EUR with tea and water included is one of the best-value vegan nights out in Amsterdam. Come hungry, slurp loudly, and leave plotting your return before you have reached the tram stop.
