Fala Fala Vege Food is a vegetarian kitchen in the arcades off Rua de Sao Francisco, and it is the old town's straightforward answer to a plant-based lunch: vegetable dishes, noodles and bowls at local prices, with vegan options across the menu.

Order the vegetable noodles, the daily veg bowl and whatever vegan special is chalked up. It is a lunch spot more than a dinner destination, so go midday.
