Bosque Bambu is Montevideo's other great Chinese buffet, and it works two ways: take away and pay by weight, or sit down for an all-you-can-eat spread. The ingredients are labelled on small signs, no garlic or onion is used, and the buffet runs from late morning to mid-afternoon with house-made grains, chop suey, pies, cakes and cookies.

Come for lunch, take the all-you-can-eat option, and save room for a cake. This is the one for a big, leisurely buffet lunch in Centro.
