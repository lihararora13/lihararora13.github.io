Pho Cambridge sits on Wheeler Street in the city centre, and it does what the chain does well: fragrant pho, curries, wok-fried noodles and salads, with over a third of the menu vegan and nearly all of it gluten free, accredited by Coeliac UK.

Order the three mushroom pho or a vegan curry, and finish with the gluten-free banana fritters. It is a meat-serving chain with a serious vegan section, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it is open daily from noon to late.
