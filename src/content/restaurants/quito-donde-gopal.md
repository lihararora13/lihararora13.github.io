Donde Gopal is a vegetarian restaurant in La Floresta with a good vegan bench: vegan plates, soup, dessert, salad and juices, plus a little shop selling vegan products to take home. It is the sister spot to Govinda Gopal, with the same wholesome cooking in a prettier neighbourhood.

Order a vegan plate and a soup. It is a calm, nourishing lunch, and a good stop after exploring La Floresta's galleries and cafes.
