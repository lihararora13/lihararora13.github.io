White Cub exists for the moment every vegan knows: dinner is sorted, and then dessert turns out to be a dairy minefield. This little dessert parlour in DLF Phase 1 fixes that with cakes, brownies and ice creams that are fully vegan and genuinely good.

It is a small, sweet stop rather than a full meal, so pair it with dinner elsewhere. The vegan chocolate cake is the order, and the dairy-free ice cream is the backup plan.
