BayLeaf is an Indian restaurant in Five Points South with a menu that vegans can work through comfortably. Curries, dals, and vegetable dishes adapt well, and the kitchen is used to dairy-free requests.

Order the chana masala and dal tadka with vegan roti, and ask the server to confirm which breads are dairy-free, since some are brushed with ghee.
