Vegano is a vegetarian South Indian and Sri Lankan restaurant on Vor dem Steintor, and it is the one vegan-friendly pick on Bremen's list: the kitchen is vegetarian, not fully vegan, but the vegan dishes are plentiful and clearly part of the cooking, not an afterthought. Dosa, curries and thalis come out of a small, warm dining room, and the vegan options are the ones regulars order.

It sits in the Ostertor, in the middle of the quarter's restaurant strip. Order a masala dosa, ask which curries are vegan that day, and say so clearly when you order. This is the table for South Indian food in Bremen, and a good reminder that a vegetarian kitchen can still cook a serious vegan meal.
