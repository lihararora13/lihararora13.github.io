ANs is the calm, grown-up vegetarian restaurant in downtown Da Nang: set lunches, seasonal vegetables, tofu claypots and a menu that leans Vietnamese with a gentle, modern touch. It is vegetarian with plenty of vegan plates, so ask the staff and they will steer you well. The room is quiet and comfortable, a nice contrast to the beachside buzz.

Come for lunch, order the set lunch, and enjoy the slow pace. This is the one for a proper, unhurried Vietnamese meal downtown.
