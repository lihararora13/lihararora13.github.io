PMV, Pure Minded Vegetarian Cafe, is the Bukit Indah branch of a Tzu Chi Buddhist vegetarian chain, and its menu is one of the biggest in Johor Bahru: lei cha in two styles, mushroom steak nasi lemak, vegetarian BBQ meat rice, claypot mushroom rice, dried chili lion mane mushroom, herbal bak kut teh soup, curries, pastas, burgers and a mushroom satay that regulars single out. There is also a retail corner selling frozen foods, dried foods and noodles, and a September 2026 post from the group shows it catering buffets for a hundred guests.

It is vegetarian with vegan options rather than fully vegan, and some dishes carry egg, the mushroom steak nasi lemak may have egg in the mayo, so ask when you order. Hours are around 9:30am to 9:30pm, closed Mondays. Note that PMV has several branches across Johor; this guide uses the Bukit Indah one at 18 Jalan Indah 8/15.
