Earth Bistro Cafe is the Edgewater bistro where everything can be made vegan: a Cuban-flavoured menu of sandwiches, wraps and salads, with the spicy fried chicken sandwich, the fried tofu sandwich and the pasta salad all available in vegan form. Regulars call it some of the best vegetarian food they have had, and the owner makes everyone feel welcome.

It is a meat-serving bistro with a vegan heart, so it lands as vegan-friendly, but the vegan versions are the ones reviewers rave about. Order the spicy chicken sandwich, add pasta salad, and enjoy the Sunday brunch if you can.
