Taste and See describes itself as 100 percent plant-based, vegan and healthy, and its 4.8-star regulars clearly agree. The menu is Brazilian comfort without the heaviness: feijoada, moqueca and snacks that travel well from breakfast through dinner.

One planning note that matters: it closes on Saturday and finishes early on Friday at 4pm. Go Sunday to Thursday for the full, relaxed version.
