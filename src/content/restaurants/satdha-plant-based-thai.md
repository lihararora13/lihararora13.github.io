Why we love it — Satdha proves Thai food barely misses a beat without fish
sauce or eggs. The kitchen is entirely plant-based and takes the classics
seriously: creamy coconut curries, properly charred noodle dishes, and a
mango sticky rice worth saving room for. It is the kind of neighborhood
standby that Santa Monica vegans plan whole evenings around.

It appears on every 2026 LA roundup we cross-checked as the go-to Thai pick
on the Westside. Order the khao soi, share a curry, and fight politely over
the mango sticky rice.
