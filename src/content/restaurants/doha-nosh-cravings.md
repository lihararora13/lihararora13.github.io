Nosh Cravings is Doha's vegan fast-food joint, launched in October 2020, doing American-style vegan food with enthusiasm: burgers, pizza, waffles, salads, breakfast burritos, donuts, brownies and milkshakes. There is also a food truck, and the restaurant is tucked down a small alley off Al Khaleej Street, so follow the signboard.

Order a burger and a milkshake, add a donut, and enjoy the fact that all of it is vegan. It is the fun pick on this list.
