Canela is Calgary's first fully vegan bakery and cafe, and it is the sweetest stop on this list. Cookies, cupcakes, pies, cheesecakes and savoury pastries line the counter, alongside breakfast sandwiches like the spinach and ricotta roll and the Korean-style sausage bread, all vegan.

It takes custom cake orders too, and the ham and cheese croissants fool everyone. Order a croissant and a slice of cheesecake, and take a box of cookies for the road.
