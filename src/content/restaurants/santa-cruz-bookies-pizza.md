Bookie's Pizza is Santa Cruz's Detroit-style answer to vegan pizza: a restaurant on Water Street serving deep-dish square pies, with a vegan pie made with vegan cheese and vegetables, plus mushroom hot wings and salad. The square slices were a feature of the 2026 Vegan Chef Challenge coverage, and the room is a casual, outdoor-seating spot that suits a group. It is the evening pizza pick in this guide, after Chocolat's deep-dish.

Hours are Monday to Thursday noon to 9pm, Friday to Saturday noon to 10pm and Sunday noon to 9pm. Order the vegan pie and the mushroom hot wings, and ask about the day's vegetable toppings.
