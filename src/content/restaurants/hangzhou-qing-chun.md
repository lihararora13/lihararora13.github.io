Qing Chun is the downtown vegan canteen of Hangzhou: a fully vegan restaurant on Qingchun East Road with a large menu in Chinese and English, near Qingling metro station Exit A1, where you scan the menu and pay with Alipay and order more dishes as you go. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, and a vegetarian meal photographed there in August 2026 shows tofu skin, steamed taro, sauteed mushrooms and morel millet porridge.

Order the dan dan noodles, which reviewers single out, plus wontons, a tofu dish and fried rice. It was previously named Su Qingchun, so older guides may use that name. Note that a different Qingchun venue at the Kerry Centre, Qingchunli, was reported closed in November 2026; this is the Qingchun East Road restaurant. Open daily 11am to 9pm.
