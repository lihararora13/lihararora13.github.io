Hevalo is a vegetarian Turkish and Arabic restaurant on Hans-Böckler-Straße, going since 2015 under its current name after starting life as Gül Veggiehaus. The kitchen serves Turkish and Arabic food with labelled vegan dishes: vegan döner, vegan köfte, çiğköfte, falafel wraps, hummus, lahmacun, manakish and fattoush, with outdoor seating in summer.

It is a vegetarian kitchen rather than a fully vegan one, so order the dishes labelled vegan, and bring cash: the restaurant is cash only. Open Monday to Saturday from eight until seven, it is the earliest vegan-friendly lunch in Bochum.
