Au Lac Dickson is the restaurant Canberra's vegans plan their week around, and it tops the city's plant-based lists for good reason. It is fully vegan, family-run, and the menu is a tour of Vietnamese and Asian-fusion comfort food: creamy laksa, pho piled with tofu and mock meats, soy duck wraps, vegan fish curry and claypots, all of it generous and cooked to order. The dining room is calm, airy and welcoming, and the gluten-free options are extensive.

It is the kind of place where regulars have a usual order and newcomers get friendly recommendations. Come hungry, order the laksa if you like it rich and spicy, and save room for the soy duck wraps. This is the one to take someone who thinks vegan food in Canberra means a sad salad.
