That'so Vegan is the dessert pick on this list and the city's second fully vegan kitchen: a non-dairy, plant-based frozen dessert shop in Prahlad Nagar, fully vegan and fully gluten-free, with no preservatives, colours or artificial additives, open daily from 11am to 11:30pm. It is listed as vegan on HappyCow and holds a 4.9 on Magicpin.

Order the Plant Based Choco Orange Ice Cream, the Raspberry Rose and the Rose Pistachio, or the Chocolatey Party Bucket if you are sharing. It is takeout and delivery focused, so plan it as a dessert stop rather than a sit-down meal, and use the Dev Atelier Commercial address on this page: an older GNFC Tower location is permanently closed.
