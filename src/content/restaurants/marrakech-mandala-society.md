Mandala Society is the medina's brunch spot: a meat-free kitchen, it does serve fish, with vegan bowls, good coffee with plant milk, and a second branch near the Koutoubia. It is the place for a slow morning after a late night in the souks.

Order a vegan bowl and an oat milk coffee, and ask about honey and butter on the breakfast dishes. The Bahia Palace branch is the prettier of the two.
