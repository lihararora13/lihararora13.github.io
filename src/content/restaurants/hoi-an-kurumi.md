Kurumi is a fully vegan cafe on Ly Thuong Kiet, bright, minimalist and strongly air-conditioned, and the vegan cafe people work from on hot days. The menu leans Western and brunchy, with a chickpea omelette, a smoked tofu sandwich, a homemade vegan brie that reviewers rave about, momos, bowls and cakes, many of them gluten-free. It is rated 4.9 from 2,456 Google reviews, and it is open 8am to 9pm daily.

Order the chickpea omelette and the vegan brie, and try the Happy Stomach bowl. It is the best vegan brunch in Hoi An, and it is busy at lunch, so come early.
