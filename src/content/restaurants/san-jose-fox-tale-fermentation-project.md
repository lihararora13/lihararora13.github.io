Fox Tale is the oddball of the list and all the better for it: a craft beer and kombucha bar downtown that ferments almost everything it serves, from sauerkraut and kimchi to cashew queso and pickles. Some drinks and dishes contain honey, so it lands as vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, but the vegan options are the point of the place.

It is the stop for the fermentation nerd in your group, and the cashew queso is the thing to order while you work through the tap list. Small, weird, and very San Jose.
