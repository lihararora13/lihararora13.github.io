Kanha Sweets is where Amritsar starts its day: poori-chhole with a khatti-meethi aloo sabzi, gur ka halwa, and a glass of buttery lassi for the dairy eaters in your group. It has 4,900 reviews and a 4.3 rating, and it earns every one of them.

The poori-chhole is the order, and the samosas are the backup. Skip the lassi and the halwa if you are keeping it fully vegan, and the savoury side will still make the morning.
