Every great vegan city has one sacred first-timer stop, and in Melbourne it's Vegie Bar. The Brunswick Street legend has been feeding hungry Fitzroy locals for decades with a sprawling menu of veggie classics, and the buzzing room never seems to slow down.

Come hungry and bring friends, because sharing is practically mandatory here. Big bowls, bigger flavours, and a dessert cabinet that has ended many good intentions.
