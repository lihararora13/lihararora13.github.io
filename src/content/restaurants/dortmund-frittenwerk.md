Frittenwerk brings Belgian fries and Canadian poutine to Dortmund's Westenhellweg, and the vegan side of the menu is the point for this list: fries with vegan cheese sauce, vegan mayo and loaded toppings, served fast and cheap on the city's main shopping street. It is counter food rather than a restaurant meal, and it knows it, which is exactly why it works as a stop between sights.

Order the poutine with vegan cheese sauce, add sweet potato fries if you are sharing, and eat them while they are hot. The chain's vegan options are consistent across its German branches, so what you get in Dortmund is what the menu promises.
