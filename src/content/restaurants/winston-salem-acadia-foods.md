Acadia Foods is a market and cafe on West Acadia Avenue, doing plates, sandwiches, and prepared foods with a strong vegan bench. The address here is 228 West Acadia Avenue; some listings say 1203, which is wrong.

Order the vegan plate and a prepared bowl. It is a daytime stop, so plan it for lunch rather than a late dinner.
