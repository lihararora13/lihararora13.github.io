BBQ4LIFE is a smokehouse on South Vista Avenue that built a vegan barbecue menu alongside its meat program, rather than treating it as an afterthought. Smoked jackfruit, vegan sides, and sauces give plant-based diners a real barbecue plate in a city where that is rare.

Order the vegan barbecue plate with smoked jackfruit and the vegan coleslaw. It is a drive from downtown, so plan it as a lunch stop with a car.
