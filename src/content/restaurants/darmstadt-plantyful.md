Plantyful is the fully vegan restaurant in Bessungen, south of the centre, serving plant-based bowls, burgers and cakes. It is the only listing in Darmstadt that HappyCow categorises as fully vegan, and it is the anchor of this list.

Order the vegan bowl, a burger and a cake, and walk the ten minutes south from the Luisenplatz, since it sits in Bessungen rather than the old town.
