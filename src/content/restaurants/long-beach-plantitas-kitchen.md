PlanTita's Kitchen is the Westside's neighbourhood vegan spot: a fully vegan kitchen on Willow Street that made HappyCow's best-of-Long-Beach list on the strength of its daily plates, tacos and burritos. It is not a destination restaurant, and that is the point.

It is the pick for the west side of town, where the vegan scene is thinner and the cooking is honest. Order the daily special, add tacos, and enjoy a meal made by people who feed their own neighbourhood.
