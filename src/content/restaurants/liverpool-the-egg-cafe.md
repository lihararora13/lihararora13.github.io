The Egg Cafe has been running since the 1970s and is the oldest vegetarian cafe in Liverpool, and it feels like it in the best way: no Instagram-optimised interior, no menu rebrands, just wholesome cooking done well for fifty years. The casual second-floor room near Central and Lime Street stations serves one or two vegan soups daily, daily specials with at least one vegan option, salads, quiche and a vegan spicy beanburger.

Sunday afternoons bring a special menu with a full vegan roast, which is the time to go if you can. Order the beanburger, ask what the vegan special is, and enjoy a piece of Liverpool history.
