Phuket I'm Vegan is a fully vegan Thai restaurant on W End Road, a short walk from the centre. The menu covers the Thai classics, all plant-based, with no fish sauce or egg anywhere.

Order the vegan green curry and pad thai. One of the few fully vegan Thai rooms on the south coast.
