Bowl Recipe is a bowl kitchen on Sangmujungang-ro in Seo-gu, with customisable bowls and salads that can be built vegan with tofu, avocado and vegetables. It is a fast-casual kitchen rather than a restaurant, and the vegan options are a real part of the menu rather than a single token bowl.

Build a vegan bowl with tofu and avocado, and ask about dressings, as some may contain dairy or egg. It is a useful lunch stop in the west of the city.
