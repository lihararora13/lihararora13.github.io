Falafel Kikar Paris is a small all-vegan falafel restaurant established in 2001 on Natanzon Street, serving fresh falafel with salads. It is rated 5.0 on HappyCow and open Monday to Thursday and Sundays from 9:30am to 5:30pm.

Order the falafel and eat it standing on the square, as the locals do.
