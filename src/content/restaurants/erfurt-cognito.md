Cognito is Erfurt's big all-day cafe, a bright room just off the old town's main streets with one of the longest vegan menus in the city: soups, salads, bowls, paninis, pastas, curries, burritos and burgers, plus a walnut French baguette for breakfast. The cake case is the draw for many visitors, with slices like poppy seed cake and rhubarb vanilla cake labelled vegan and gluten-free. It is open daily from eight in the morning, which makes it the easiest vegan breakfast in town.

A note on the status: Cognito is vegan-friendly, not fully vegan, it serves meat alongside its vegan dishes, so check the labels. Order a bowl for lunch, save room for a slice of cake, and linger over coffee. This is the comfortable, central pick for any time of day.
