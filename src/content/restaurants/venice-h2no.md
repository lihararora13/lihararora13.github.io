H2NO is an enobirroteca, a bar that takes both wine and beer seriously, and in practice it works as a bacaro: you stand at a counter lined with plates, point at what you want, and it arrives with a glass of something. By early evening it fills with people just finished work, nobody sits down, and that is the Venice most visitors never see.

Ask what is vegan on the day and they will put together a plate: grilled vegetables on polenta, artichoke hearts, bruschetta with tomato and basil, and saor onions without the sardines. Arrive around six, order two or three cicchetti and an ombra, and stay standing at the counter. Closed Sundays.
