Vöner Döner is a small stand at Barbarossaplatz, at the corner of Juliuspromenade and Kaiserstraße, and it serves meat alongside a proper vegan range: vegan döner kebab and falafel in flatbread, wrap or box, plus vegan hot dogs with cheese, chilli cheese or chilli sin carne, and extras like rice, fried potatoes, zucchini and aubergine. The vegan kebab is seitan, the sauces are good, and the price is fair.

Two practical notes: it is cash only, and there is no seating, not even a bench nearby. Order a vegan döner, take it to the nearby green, and treat it as Würzburg's cheapest proper vegan lunch.
