Hungry Vegan is Savannah's only fully vegan kitchen, a Black-owned, women-owned soul food counter on East Lathrop Avenue, and it is the table this whole guide is built around. The Big Vegan Soul plate with fried oyster mushrooms is the signature, the mac and cheese is the side, and the spaghetti is the sleeper.

It keeps short hours, Wednesday to Friday from 11:30am to 5:30pm, so plan around them. Order the soul plate and come hungry.
