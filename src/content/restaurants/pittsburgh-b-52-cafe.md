B-52 Cafe is Lawrenceville's longtime vegan favourite: a bright, casual cafe on Butler Street doing brunch, sandwiches and bowls, all of it plant-based and all of it made to be lingered over. It is the neighbourhood cafe that Pittsburgh vegans have been meeting at for years, and the brunch is the reason.

It is the morning stop on the list, and the one to hit before a walk down Butler Street. Order brunch, add a bowl, and enjoy the Lawrenceville buzz.
