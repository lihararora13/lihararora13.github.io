Why we love it — Greenr has been doing plant-forward food in Delhi since
2015, which in vegan years makes it practically ancient. The GK1 flagship
sits across from the N Block park with earthy tones, a calm room, and a
menu that reads like a greatest-hits of everything vegan food does well:
black rice sushi, truffle mushroom soup, raw cakes, kombucha on tap.

It is a vegetarian cafe with a deep vegan bench rather than all-vegan, so
vegans should scan for the vegan marks. But the consensus is unusually
loud here: HappyCow's top-rated spot in the city, Swiggy's 2026 headline
pick, and a thousand-plus reviews that keep saying the same thing. When a
city agrees this hard, you listen.
