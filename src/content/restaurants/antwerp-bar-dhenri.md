Bar d'Henri is a tapas bar and restaurant on Troonplaats in Antwerp Zuid, with local beers, French wines, cocktails and plant-based food, plus live music and art on the walls. The signature is Billy's Smoked BBQ Burger, alongside miso cauliflower croquettes, samosas and a pesto portobello burger.

Note the status change: under new ownership in March 2025 it is no longer fully vegan, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly. Order the plant-based burgers and tapas, and confirm the vegan dishes when you order.
