Kafe Botanika is a courtyard cafe in the centre, with a tofu curry that regulars recommend and vegan dishes across the menu. The courtyard is one of the prettiest settings in San Sebastián.

Order the tofu curry and a cake. Best on a dry day, when the courtyard is in use.
