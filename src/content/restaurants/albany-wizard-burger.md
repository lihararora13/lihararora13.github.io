Wizard Burger is an all-vegan burger restaurant on North Pearl Street in downtown Albany, doing smash burgers, loaded fries, and shakes with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the strongest pick in Albany, and the room is casual, counter-service, and built for a hearty lunch.

Order the smash burger and the loaded fries, and save room for a vegan shake. It is a good stop for a group, since the burgers are built to share.
