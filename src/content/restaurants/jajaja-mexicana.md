Jajaja does not do quiet. With outposts across Manhattan and Brooklyn, it is
the spot that convinced half of New York that vegan Mexican was never a
compromise. The "steak" birria tacos arrive glistening and dunkable, the
nachos are an event, and the enchiladas mole taste like someone actually
cares about mole, which, frankly, is rarer than it should be.

Every 2026 roundup we checked puts Jajaja at or near the top, and diners
keep saying the same thing: the food is delicious and the room is electric.
Go for tacos, stay for the margaritas, and absolutely save room for dessert.
