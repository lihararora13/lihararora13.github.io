Greener Jinwol is a branch of the Korean poke and salad chain on Seomun-daero in Nam-gu, open daily from 10am to 8:30pm, with customisable made-to-order poke bowls and salads. Vegan ingredients are clearly labelled and include lettuce, sweet corn, tofu, avocado, onion and noodles, which makes it one of the easiest vegan-friendly builds in the city.

Build a vegan poke bowl on brown rice or soba noodles with tofu and avocado, and check the sauces. It is inexpensive, fast and reliable.
