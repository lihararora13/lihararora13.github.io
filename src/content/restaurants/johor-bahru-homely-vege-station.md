Homely Vege Station is the pick Johor Bahru's vegans name first: a 100% vegan stall inside the food court on Jalan Dedap 13 in Taman Johor Jaya, where owner Mandy and her sister cook a daily-changing menu of home-style dishes. There is no need to worry about hidden egg or dairy, everything is vegan, and Vegan Food Quest calls it one of their favourites in the city and highly recommends it.

It is a food-court stall, not a restaurant, so come for lunch and come early: dishes sell out well before the 2pm close. It is open Wednesday to Sunday, 7am to 2pm, and closed Monday and Tuesday. Do not confuse it with the neighbouring Vege Station at 36 Jalan Dedap 19, a separate vegetarian restaurant known for its lok lok skewers.
