Mo' Brunch + Brews describes itself as "Coffee, Vegan and Vinyl," and the vibe does the rest. The shaded patio hums with live music on Friday nights, the staff remembers regulars, and the menu leans into Southern comfort: chik'n fried shrooms and grits, boudain balls, fried mushroom and waffles, and a gumbo that converts skeptics. Reviewers keep calling it the best vegan spot in Houston, and the photos of the strawberry cheesecake waffles do the persuading.

It is a brunch place first but the Friday night dinner crowd, saxophone and all, is a whole scene. Come hungry, leave with the vinyl collection noted for your next visit.
