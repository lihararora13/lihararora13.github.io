Momo Tibetan Dumplings is a small dumpling spot on the Schustergasse, serving Tibetan momos with vegan fillings alongside the meat ones. It is the dumpling pick on this list.

Order the vegan momos and a soup, and ask for the vegan fillings, since the menu also runs meat dumplings.
