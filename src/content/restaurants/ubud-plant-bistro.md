Plant Bistro is the most polished vegan table in Ubud, and HappyCow's community rates it the town's best. Chef Simona Paolicchi, from Livorno, runs a fully vegan Italian kitchen in the Blanco Museum area: bruschetta topped with plant-based carrot salmon, gnocchi di zucca with sage and walnut crumble, vitello tonnato built on chickpea beef, and a mushroom tagliata steak, alongside pizzas, risotto, nasi campur and mie goreng. The bakery case alone is worth the trip, with vegan croissants, cakes, sorbet and gelato made in house.

It opens daily from 9:30am to 10pm and reservations are recommended. Come for a long lunch or a proper dinner, and save room for the gelato.
