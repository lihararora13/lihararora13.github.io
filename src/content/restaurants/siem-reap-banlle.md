Banlle sits in a beautifully restored wooden house with garden seating, and it blends vegetarian and vegan dining with a farm-to-table feel: the restaurant uses produce from its own organic garden, and many dishes are clearly marked vegan or easily adaptable.

Order the lotus root ceviche and a pad Thai served on a banana leaf, and finish at the gelato place next door, which has plenty of vegan options beyond sorbet. It is a vegetarian restaurant with a strong vegan bench, so file it as vegan-friendly.
