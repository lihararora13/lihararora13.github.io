Empty Bowl is Santa Barbara's Thai noodle bar: a small room on West Victoria Street where all the noodle dishes can be made vegan if specified, along with the green papaya salad. It is the quick-lunch pick in this guide, open 11am to 9pm daily, with outdoor seating and a menu that is short enough to read in a minute. Specify vegan when ordering: the default noodle dishes are not vegan.

Order a noodle bowl with tofu and the green papaya salad, and say vegan clearly at the counter.
