Muse is the city's top-rated restaurant, a relaxed fine-dining room in Walmer that reinvents comfort food with fresh ingredients and careful presentation. It is not a vegan restaurant, but reviewers consistently report the kitchen moving heaven and earth to veganise dishes, and the vegan dark chocolate and Biscoff fondant is a genuine destination dessert.

Book ahead, ask for the veganised menu, and order the chickpea fries and the fondant. It is the one table in Gqeberha for a proper evening out.
