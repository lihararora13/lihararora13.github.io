Folk is the garden cafe Byron locals escape to when the main street gets too busy: a vegetarian cafe with a leafy garden, a small menu of salads, wraps, sweets and smoothies, and different plant milks for your coffee. It is a short drive out of town on Ewingsdale Road, and the slower pace suits it perfectly. The food is fresh, light and made for sunny mornings.

Come for breakfast or a late lunch, sit in the garden, and order the wrap of the day with a smoothie. This is the one for a quiet morning away from the crowds.
