Why we love it — Chickpea started as a food truck and grew into the brick-and-
mortar that Vancouver's vegans measure everything else against. The room is
bright and plant-filled, the menu is entirely vegan and gluten-free friendly,
and the namesake fries (crispy, golden, gone in seconds) have a fan club.

Order the falafel plate if it's your first visit, get the chickpea fries no
matter what, and come back for brunch. The breakfast menu with waffles and
a take on eggs benedict is a weekend sleeper hit.
