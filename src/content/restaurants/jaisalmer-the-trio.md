The Trio sits near Nathmal Ki Haveli in Mainpura, a comfortable multicuisine dining room that serves both veg and non-veg, with a veg section that runs deep. It is the kind of place that works for a mixed group, and for a traveller who wants one reliable dinner spot near the fort.

The North Indian mains are the move, with dal tadka and mixed veg sabzi leading, and the kitchen is used to dietary requests. Ask for the ghee and cream held and the meal turns plant-based.
