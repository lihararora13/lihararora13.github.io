Geetha Canteen is the old-school canteen of Ram Nagar, the kind of place where the meals arrive on banana leaves and the prices still look like they belong to another decade. The local guide calls it old-school nostalgia, and the regulars call it lunch.

Order the banana-leaf meal and ask for it without curd. It is the cheapest proper meal on this list, and the most Coimbatore.
