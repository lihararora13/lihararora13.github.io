Hum is the name that comes up first in almost every Saigon vegan guide, and the flagship District 3 garden restaurant shows why. Set back from the street in a calm, green space, it serves refined Vietnamese cooking made with fresh organic ingredients, from lotus salad and pumpkin curry to oyster mushrooms steamed in lotus leaf. It is a vegetarian restaurant with a deep bench of vegan dishes, so ask for the plant-based options and the staff will steer you happily.

Come here for the meal you dress up slightly for, or for a long lazy lunch when the city gets loud. The room is peaceful, the plating is lovely, and the menu is wide enough that a table of mixed eaters will all find something to love. Book ahead at weekends, and save room for the crystal salad.
