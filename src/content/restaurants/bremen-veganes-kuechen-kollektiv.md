The Veganes Küchen Kollektiv, VKK for short, is Bremen's vegan kitchen in the truest sense: a collective that cooks a fully plant-based menu from seasonal, regional ingredients, and changes it as the market changes. There is no fixed greatest-hits list here, which is the point. What you get is whatever is good right now, cooked by people who clearly love the work, in a room that feels more like a neighbourhood kitchen than a restaurant.

It sits in Findorff, a short walk from the city centre, and it opens in the late afternoon, so this is a dinner stop rather than a lunch one. Go with an open mind, order whatever the kitchen is proudest of that week, and save room for cake. This is the table that shows what Bremen's vegan scene is actually about.
