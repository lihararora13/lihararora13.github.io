Rasam is Canberra's South Indian vegetarian specialist, and it is a joy for vegans because so much of the menu is already plant-based or a small tweak away from it. Dosa, idli, vada, curries, breads and rice, all cooked the traditional way, with vegan options across the menu if you ask. It is a proper sit-down restaurant, so book ahead, especially on weekends.

Come hungry and order a dosa with sambar, then a vegetable curry to share. This is the one for a proper, unhurried South Indian meal in the middle of the city.
