Veganissimo is one of Yogyakarta's dedicated vegan restaurants, out in the Pringwulung area of Sleman, north-east of the centre. It is a smaller, quieter room than the central cafes, and it is worth the trip for a proper plant-based meal away from the tourist strip.

Order a vegan bowl and a tempeh dish. It is listed as a vegan restaurant, so file it as all-vegan, and note the pin is neighbourhood-level for the Pringwulung and Condongcatur area.
