Katzentempel is Nuremberg's fully vegan cat café, on Peter-Vischer-Straße in the Altstadt, and it is exactly what it sounds like: breakfast, a daily plate, cakes and an açaí bowl, all plant-based, with cats in residence. It is the most charming room on this page.

Come for breakfast or a slow afternoon coffee, and expect to share the room with the cats. The daily plate is the order, and the cakes are worth saving room for.
