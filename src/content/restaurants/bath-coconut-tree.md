The Coconut Tree brings Sri Lankan street food to Broad Street, and much of its menu is vegan by tradition rather than by adaptation. Hoppers, kotthu and dhal are the dishes to know.

Order hoppers with dhal and coconut sambol, then a vegetable kotthu to share. The vegan dishes are marked, and the kitchen is happy to confirm which curries are dairy-free.
