Løvetann Café & Bistro sits on Klosteret on Nordnes, a short walk from the harbour, and it is the café pick on this page. The vegan miso ramen is the signature, with a falafel salad and hummus toast alongside, in a calm room that suits a slow lunch.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so check the vegan dishes on the menu. The miso ramen is the order.
