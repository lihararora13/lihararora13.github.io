Relicioso is the treat-yourself stop: portions are famously generous, everything is completely vegan, and the menu runs from breakfast medialunas to a milanesa the size of the plate. Their own site promises a menu that is delicious, abundant and kind to your wallet, and reviewers agree.

Come hungry, order the milanesa a la pizza, and expect leftovers. Heavy in the best possible way, and open from 8am for the early crowd.
