The Kind Bowl does one cuisine, fully vegan, and does it with real heart: slow, fragrant broths, armfuls of fresh herbs, and a pho that regulars and the abillion community keep putting at the top of Singapore lists. The Killiney Road flagship sits a short stroll from Somerset MRT.

Start with the Kind Pho, add a crisp Saigon Delight banh mi with its mushroom-walnut pate, and grab summer rolls for the table. Portions are generous, prices are kind, and allium-free options make it an easy invite for almost anyone.
