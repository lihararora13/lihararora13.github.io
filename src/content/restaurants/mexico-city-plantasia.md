When you need a breather from tacos, Plantasia is the answer: a serene, jungle-draped room serving pan-Asian plates that are fully plant-based and properly refined. Reviewers call the sushi their new favourite in the city, which is not a sentence anyone expected.

Book ahead at weekends, sit in the garden, and work through the udon, the tataki and a botanical mocktail. It is the upscale end of CDMX vegan, and it earns it.
