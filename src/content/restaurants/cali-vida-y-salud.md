Vida y Salud is a 5.0-rated vegetarian restaurant in Prados del Norte whose daily menu is vegan throughout: a menu del dia with soup, rice, salad, juice and dessert, a tamal vegano as the house plate, and a vegan bandeja paisa as the special. Everything is fresh, affordable and cooked for lunch, with delivery to nearby neighbourhoods.

Order the tamal vegano, or the bandeja paisa vegana if you are very hungry, and note the menu changes daily, so the exact dishes rotate. It is the best-value vegan lunch in the north of the city.
