La Cabo Bistro is Kolhapur's newest pure-veg opening: a bistro inside Hotel K Tree in Shivaji Park, launched in November 2026 with a modern café menu. It is the most contemporary room on this list, and a sign of where the city's food scene is heading.

The pasta, salads and grilled vegetable sandwiches veganise easily with the dairy held. Come early in its life and you will be one of the first vegans through the door.
