Al Hayat is a Middle Eastern restaurant on Hafenstraße near the harbour, a straightforward neighbourhood kitchen with a solid vegan side: falafel plates, hummus and vegan mezze, with outdoor seating in summer and a takeaway trade alongside. The room is unfussy and the portions are generous, which makes it the practical dinner pick on this list.

It is a mixed menu, so order the vegan-marked dishes. Open Monday to Saturday from twelve until ten and Sundays from two until eight, it is a good harbour-side stop after a walk along the canal.
