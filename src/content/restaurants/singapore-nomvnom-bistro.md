Perched in Clarke Quay Central with a view of the river, nomVnom is the easy crowd-pleaser of the guide: fully vegan, fairly priced, and with a menu that happily wanders from burgers and pizza to pasta, udon and pho, so nobody at the table has to compromise.

The lion's mane truffle burger is the headline, the rendang burger brings the local flavour, and reviewers keep praising the consistency plate after plate. A great first vegan stop in Singapore, especially with non-vegan friends in tow.
