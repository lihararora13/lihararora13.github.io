Paladares Vegan is a fully vegan restaurant at the central bus station, the workhorse of Braga's vegan scene, serving a daily vegan plate, seitan dishes and homemade desserts in a simple canteen-style room. It is the first stop for anyone arriving in Braga by bus.

Order the daily vegan plate, a seitan dish and a homemade dessert, and come for lunch, when the menu is at its fullest.
