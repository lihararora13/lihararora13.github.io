The Wild Jordan Center sits near Rainbow Street with a seasonal menu, clearly labelled vegan options, and one of the best sunset views over downtown Amman you will get from any restaurant terrace. It is the pretty pick on this list, and the food is fresh, organic and local.

Come at sunset, order a vegan mezze and a fattoush, and watch the city light up. It is pricier than the downtown falafel counters, and worth it for the view alone.
