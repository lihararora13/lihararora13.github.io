1912 Gozleme ve Cay Evi is a gozleme house in Karsiyaka selling Turkish savoury crepes. The dough is confirmed vegan and staff understand what vegan means, with vegan filling options including spinach, potatoes, mushrooms and eggplant. It is open daily from 7:00am to 9:30pm and rated 3.5 on HappyCow.

Order a spinach gozleme and a potato gozleme with tea. It is the cheapest table on this list.
