Fermaggio is the only place on this list where you can eat lunch and then take the cheese home. The bistrot pairs stuffed focaccia pizzas and baguette sandwiches with a deli counter of house-made nut cheeses, vegan mozzarella, smoked scamorza, stracciatella, burrata and brie, all produced the traditional way with organic ingredients and sold by weight. It is one of the few vegan gastronomies in Italy doing this properly.

Order the vegan cheese board to taste several varieties at once, then a stuffed focaccia, and go early if you want pizza, the ovens switch off around 6:30pm. Closed Mondays, and a short walk from Ops! on the same street, so the Trieste quarter makes a fine vegan crawl.
