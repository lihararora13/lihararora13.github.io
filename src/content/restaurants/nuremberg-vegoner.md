Vegöner is a fully vegan döner counter on Poppenreuther Straße in Johannis, and it is the cheapest dinner on this page. The vegan döner is the signature, with dürüm, burgers and hot dogs alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it fast.

Use this address: there is a second branch, Vegöner Süd at Augustenstraße 37. The döner is the order, and the dürüm runs it close.
