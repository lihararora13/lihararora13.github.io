Hotel Shree Annapoorna at Kalavasal is the western Madurai pure-veg stop, a clean 100% vegetarian kitchen on Theni Main Road that the October 2026 guide verified dish by dish. The sambar mini idli and the South Indian mushroom biriyani are the picks, and the midday meals are the reason the buses stop here.

Order the sambar idli and the mushroom biriyani, and ask for the roast without ghee. It is the best pure-veg room on the western side of the city.
