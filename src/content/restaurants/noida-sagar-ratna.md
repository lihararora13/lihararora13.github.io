Sagar Ratna in Sector 18's Ansal Fortune Arcade is the reliable old hand: a long-running pure vegetarian South Indian restaurant with ample seating, courteous staff, and thalis that regulars call simply tasty.

The masala dosa combo and the uttapam are the vegan-friendly orders, and the dahi vada is there if your companions eat dairy. Ask for no ghee on the dosa and you are set.
