Matcha Mama is Tulum's smoothie-bowl icon, a plant-based café where the Papacito and Mamacita bowls come in coconut shells for about 200 pesos, with matcha drinks and kombucha alongside. The original sits on the beach road with swing chairs, and there is now a second branch in town in Aldea Zama, which is the address pinned on this page.

Listed hours are about 8am to 6pm daily. Recent reviews are more mixed than they used to be, but the smoothie bowls are still the stars, and it is the best breakfast stop before the beach.
