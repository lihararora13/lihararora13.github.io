Beelzebab is Brighton at its most fun: a vegan food vendor operating inside the Hope & Ruin pub since 2015, serving seitan doner, hot dogs, vegan wings and fries, ordered at the bar while the music plays and the 150-capacity venue upstairs does its thing. It is a music-led pub first and a restaurant second, and that is exactly the charm.

Kids are welcome until 8pm, and delivery runs via UberEats and Deliveroo if you would rather eat at home. Order the seitan doner, add fries, and stay for a set.
