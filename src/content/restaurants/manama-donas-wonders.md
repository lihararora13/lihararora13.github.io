Donas Wonders is a vegan cafe in Manama run by Dona Maria, who won a Startup Bahrain pitch award for it, serving gluten-free maamoul, vegan tacos filled with falafel and pomegranate, a classic Bounty chocolate coconut dessert and an iced latte. It is a red-walled, pink-pillared garden cafe.

Order the vegan tacos and the maamoul. It is a warm, personal room, and the World Vegan Day event there in November 2025 shows it is at the heart of Bahrain's vegan community.
