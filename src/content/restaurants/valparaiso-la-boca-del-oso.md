La Boca del Oso is the Cerro Alegre spot for Chilean favourites done vegan: completos, empanadas and hearty plates, in a hillside room that stays open late on Wednesdays through Saturdays. It is the place local guides name alongside Medialuna when they talk about eating vegan in Valparaiso.

Order a vegan completo, add an empanada, and ask what the plate of the day is. The kitchen is vegetarian and vegan rather than strictly vegan, so confirm the build when you order.
