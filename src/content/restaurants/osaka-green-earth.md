Green Earth is the pick every 2026 Osaka vegan guide leads with, and it has earned that spot over three decades. Open since 1991, the city's oldest vegetarian restaurant went fully vegan in 2019, and the kitchen still cooks the way it always has: a daily set plate that changes with the seasons, Japanese-style curry, rice bowls, and a pumpkin cake regulars plan their lunch around. A lunch set runs about ¥1,100, the curry about ¥950, and the staff speak English with an English menu on the table.

It is a small, unfussy room near Hommachi Station, which is exactly the point. Come for lunch, not dinner, it only opens for dinner on Fridays and closes on Sundays, and let the daily set do the deciding. If you only have time for one vegan meal in Osaka, this is the one.
