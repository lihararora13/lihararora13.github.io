Doko Demo V made the list for a reason. A vegan cafe and bar in Burleigh Waters with the largest selection of vegan food on the coast, spanning regions and cuisines. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Burleigh Waters, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Plant-based burger, Vegan cheese pizza, Plant-based roast duck if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
