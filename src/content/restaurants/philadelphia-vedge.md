Vedge is the reason Philadelphia takes vegan fine dining seriously. Since 2011, chefs Richard Landau and Kate Jacoby have been turning vegetables into an art form inside a charming Center City brownstone, with zero animal products anywhere in the kitchen. Michelin recommended it in 2025, and a string of glowing 2026 reviews confirms it is still at the top of its game.

Order the rutabaga fondue, the dish every local swears by: a warm soft pretzel dunker in rutabaga cheese sauce that sounds improbable and tastes inevitable. The dan dan noodles and tasting menu are for the adventurous; the sugar pea cocktail is for everyone.
