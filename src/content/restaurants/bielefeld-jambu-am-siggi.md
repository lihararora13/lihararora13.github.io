Jambu am Siggi is a mixed restaurant with clearly marked vegan choices at the Siegfriedplatz, documented by HappyCow, serving seitan doner, falafel, dips and wraps. It is the street-food pick on this list.

Order the seitan doner, the falafel and a wrap, and ask for the vegan dips, since the menu also runs meat and dairy.
