Quan Chay Ba Minh is a local vegetarian restaurant near the Imperial City, on Han Thuyen, serving simple, home-style dishes loved by generations of locals. It is inexpensive and authentic, the kind of place where the cooking is the point and the room is plain.

Order the home-style vegetable dishes and tofu with rice, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is a good lunch stop after the Imperial City.
