Madras Veg Tables calls itself Alleppey's first pure-veg restaurant with a live kitchen, and the reviews back it up: 4.4 across 47 reviews, with praise for hygiene, cleanliness and the welcome for families. The menu is sadya, masala dosa, ilayada, kozhukkatta and idli vada, and the kitchen is visible from the dining room.

Order the sadya without curd and a masala dosa, and ask about the ilayada. It is the most polished pure-veg room in Alleppey, with ample parking and seating.
