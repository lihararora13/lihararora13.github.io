Rainbow Restaurant is Fort Collins' vegetarian institution: a family-owned restaurant on West Laurel Street near Colorado State University since 1976, with extensive vegan options across breakfast, brunch and lunch. The tofu scramble and migas are the vegan breakfast core, the pumpkin bread French toast is the sweet pick, and Sarah's Sandwich is the lunch order. It is the longest-running vegetarian room in this batch, and the one local guides call a Fort Collins landmark.

Hours are Monday to Friday 7am to 2pm and Saturday to Sunday 8am to 2pm. It is a breakfast and lunch room, so come in the morning, and specify vegan when ordering.
