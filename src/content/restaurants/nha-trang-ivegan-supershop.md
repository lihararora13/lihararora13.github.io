iVegan Supershop is a fully vegan restaurant on Hung Vuong Street in central Nha Trang, where the whole menu is plant-based. People come for the falafel bowl and the koi pond beside the tables, and the menu covers bowls, salads and bagels. It is open 8:30am to 8:30pm, and it is the strict-vegan starting point in Nha Trang.

Order the falafel bowl and a salad, and sit by the koi pond. Skip it if you want traditional chay dishes, as the menu is bowls, salads and bagels rather than Vietnamese cooking.
