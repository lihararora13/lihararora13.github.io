Delivore is the cafe half of a vegan double act: it shares its Sodermalm home with Goodstore, the vegan grocery Stockholm vegans swear by, so lunch comes with a shopping trip built in. Visit Stockholm lists it among the city's fully vegan restaurants, and HappyCow reviewers call the baked goods perfect vegan decadence.

Order the Swedish classics done plant-based: Toast Skagen, lasagne, mac and cheese sandwiches and wraps, then finish with a Snickers milkshake and whatever cake is on the counter, semla included in season. Browse the shelves afterwards and try to leave with only one bag.
