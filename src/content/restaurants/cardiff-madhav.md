Madhav is a vegan-friendly Indian restaurant on Lower Cathedral Road in Riverside, and it is the third dosa pick on this page. The masala dosa is the signature, with a Madhav thali, samosa and dal kachori alongside, all of it hearty and all of it cheap.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so order the vegan dishes clearly. The Madhav thali is the order, and the dosa runs it close.
