ARP is Busan's fine-dining pick: short for Around Plants, the city's only plant-based restaurant in the Michelin Guide, serving high-end Asian fusion with fresh, locally sourced ingredients and creative presentation, overlooking the sea from Yeongdo.

It is a destination restaurant, so pair it with a Yeongdo day. Order the seasonal dishes and enjoy the room.
