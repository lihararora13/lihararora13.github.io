MIIT Coffee is a local favourite, an entirely vegetarian cafe serving hearty meals, top-quality coffee and healthier takes on popular treats. Vegans come for the weekend brunch in particular, which is one of the best in Riga, and the coffee is worth the visit on its own.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so confirm the vegan options when you order. Go at the weekend, order the brunch, and take your time over the coffee.
