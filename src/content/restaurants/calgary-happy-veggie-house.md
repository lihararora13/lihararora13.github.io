Happy Veggie House is Calgary's Chinatown vegan gem: a fully vegan Chinese kitchen with more than a hundred items on the menu, from wonton soup and black bean veggie chicken to chow mein and sweet-and-sour pork, all plant-based.

The lunch deal is a steal, with jasmine tea included, and the spring rolls get praised in nearly every review. Order the wonton soup, the chow mein and the spring rolls, and come hungry.
