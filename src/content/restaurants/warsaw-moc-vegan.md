Moc Vegan is a five-star vegan Vietnamese restaurant across the river in Saska Kepa, and it is worth the trip. Pho, pad thai, ramen, curries and fresh spring rolls fill a menu that reviewers rate among the best vegan food in Warsaw.

It is open daily until 10pm. Order the pho, add the pad thai and the spring rolls, and enjoy one of the calmest, prettiest neighbourhoods in the city.
