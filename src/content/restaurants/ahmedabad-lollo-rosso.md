Lollo Rosso is the bowl place of Bodakdev: a modern, green-booth dining room in One World Capital off Rajpath Rangoli Road, serving dozens of globally inspired bowls, and the green-dotted items on its menu are the ones available in vegan form. A food review filmed there in November 2026 shows sushi rolls, a Korean-inspired bowl, bibimbap and a Basque cheesecake finish.

Ask for the vegan bowls and the vegan sushi, and order a Korean-inspired bowl with vegetables and red sauce. It is one of the few Ahmedabad spots where the vegan options are marked on the menu itself, so ordering is straightforward.
