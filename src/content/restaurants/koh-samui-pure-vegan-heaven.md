Pure Vegan Heaven is the Lamai branch of the Thai vegan chain, open since 2018 and rated 4.5 from 156 HappyCow reviews. The menu is the chain's broad one: Thai dishes, Mexican plates, American comfort food, juice bar drinks and desserts, with rice paper salad rolls, banana pancakes, nachos, quesadilla, burrito, mushroom larb, burger and potato wedges, plus a build-your-own salad. It is the most Western-leaning vegan room in Lamai, which makes it the easy pick when a group wants burgers and burritos rather than a Thai buffet.

It is one of a few outlets in Thailand, and the Samui one is the Lamai Beach Road address. HappyCow confirmed it open in February 2023 and it remains on the current best-of list, so it is still trading.
