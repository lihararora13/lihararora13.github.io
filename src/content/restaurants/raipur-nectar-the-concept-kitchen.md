Nectar is Raipur's most interesting food idea: a concept kitchen inside Omaya Plaza that changes its specials by the day, misal pav on Tuesdays, Sri Lankan curry with Malabar paratha on Wednesdays, and an elaborate dal baati churma thali on Sundays, all by reservation. It is rated 4.5 stars and sits on the city's date-night lists, and its listings note vegan options on the menu.

Two honest notes: Nectar serves both veg and non-veg food, so confirm your dishes are the vegetarian ones, and the weekly specials need advance booking. Get those two things right and this is the most memorable meal on the list.
