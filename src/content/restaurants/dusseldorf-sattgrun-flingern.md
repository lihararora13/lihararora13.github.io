Sattgrün Flingern is the highest-rated of Düsseldorf's three Sattgrün buffets, and it has been all-vegan since 2013: a self-service spread of cold and hot dishes, from salads and antipasti to soups, curries, pasta and vegan cakes and pastries, priced by the size of your plate. Plant milks are available for coffee and tea, and the whole place is companion-animal friendly.

Come hungry, take a large plate, and work through the buffet like a local. It is the easiest great lunch in Düsseldorf, and the best introduction to the city's vegan scene.
