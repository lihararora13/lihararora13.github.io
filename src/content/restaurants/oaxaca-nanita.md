Nanita is one of Oaxaca's top-rated vegan restaurants, a small room in Centro where the cooking is creative, seasonal and confidently plant-based. It is the kind of place where the daily special is usually the right order.

Order the seasonal plate and a dessert. It is a quiet, thoughtful dinner, and a good pick for a proper night out in the Centro.
