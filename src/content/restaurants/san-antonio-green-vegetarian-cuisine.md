Green Vegetarian Cuisine is San Antonio's kosher vegetarian institution: a family-owned counter-service spot at the Quarry Market doing Southern American comfort food with daily specials, fresh fruit smoothies, organic teas and a gluten-free menu. It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but the vegan options are serious and clearly marked.

It is the reliable, old-school stop on the list, and the one to hit after shopping the Quarry. Order the daily special, add a smoothie, and save room for dessert.
