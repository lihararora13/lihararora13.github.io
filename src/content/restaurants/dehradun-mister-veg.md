Mister Veg is a vegetarian health-food restaurant and store on Rajpur Road, at Pacific Hills by the Mussoorie Diversion, open 11 AM to 11 PM. The menu leans healthy and plant-forward, and the store shelf carries the same philosophy home with you.

Order a vegan bowl and a cold-pressed juice. It is a practical, everyday table, and a good one for a working lunch.
