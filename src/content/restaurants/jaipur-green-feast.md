Green Feast made the list for a reason. A hydroponic, health-first C-Scheme kitchen cooking plant-protein paninis and kung pao tofu in olive oil, with vegan options throughout. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in C-Scheme, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Plant protein panini, Kung pao tofu, Buddha bowl if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
