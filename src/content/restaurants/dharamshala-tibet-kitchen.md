Tibet Kitchen is the definitive Tibetan food experience in McLeod Ganj, on Jogiwara Road near the main square. The steamed momos and the thentuk, hand-pulled noodle soup, are the dishes the guides keep naming, and the room is the classic market-side Tibetan kitchen.

Order the veg momos and the thentuk. It is cheap, filling, and the most Tibetan meal on this list.
