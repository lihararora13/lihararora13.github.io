The Diamond Plaza branch puts Chowpaty's South Indian vegetarian cooking right in the middle of Nairobi's Indian shopping heart. It is the same formula as Westlands, dosa, chaat, curries and juices, in a busier, more local setting. Come here if you are already shopping the plaza, and stay for lunch.

Vegan options are not labelled, so ask when you order. The dosa and the vegetable curries are the safest route, and the fresh juices are a good finish.
