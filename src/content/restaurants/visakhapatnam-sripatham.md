Sripatham is the Vizag tiffin house that a March 2026 family travel video put first on its list: South Indian breakfast, giant batura and puri, dosa and filter coffee, in a temple-inspired room with MS Subbulakshmi melodies playing. The ghee karam dosa and the peri peri punugulu are the dishes the reviewers keep naming.

Order the ghee karam dosa without ghee and the peri peri punugulu, and finish with the filter coffee. It is the best breakfast in Vizag, and the room is a small temple of its own.
