Mozambik is a Portuguese restaurant chain, and the Mbombela branch on Ferreira Street is a lively, family-friendly room built around peri-peri prawns, calamari and ribs. It lists vegetarian food, and salads and grilled vegetables are the vegan fallback.

Order grilled vegetables and a salad, and confirm no butter or cheese. This is a fallback rather than a destination for vegans, but it is central and open late.
