Tiny, cozy, and unapologetically traditional, Le Potager du Marais does
what almost nobody else in Paris attempts: full-throttle French classics
with zero animal products. French onion soup, hachi parmentier, escalope
de seitan, and a proper mousse au chocolat for dessert.

The room is small and the blackboard menu leans on the market's freshest
ingredients, so reservations are a must. Bring a curious omnivore and
watch them convert course by course.
