Distrito Vegano is Madrid vegan junk food done with style: bacon cheese nachos, mac and cheese, loaded burgers and hot dogs, then a proper sit-down surprise in the form of vegan bourguignon and chorizo cooked with cava. The room is warm and brick-walled, and the team have even published their own recipe book.

Save room for the cakes. The selection of vegan and gluten-free cheesecakes and brownies is the correct ending, and booking ahead is wise for dinner in the main room.
