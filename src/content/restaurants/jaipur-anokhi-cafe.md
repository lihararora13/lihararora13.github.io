Anokhi Café made the list for a reason. An organic farm-to-table cafe above the Anokhi textile store, cooking what its own farm picked that morning, with vegan options clearly labelled. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in C-Scheme, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Farm salad, Seasonal thali, Cold-pressed juice if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
