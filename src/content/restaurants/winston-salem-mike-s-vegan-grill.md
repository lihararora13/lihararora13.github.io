Mike's Vegan Grill is an all-vegan grill on West Third Street in downtown Winston-Salem, doing burgers, sandwiches, and Southern plates with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the strongest pick in Winston-Salem, and the same grill that anchors the Greensboro list.

Order the vegan burger and the Southern plate. It is a good stop for a group, since the plates are built to share.
