Vegan Garden is the pretty one: a bright, plant-filled dining room a short walk from Deák Ferenc tér, with a fully vegan menu that runs from modernised Hungarian classics like spiced cottage cheese and bean goulash to Mediterranean crowd-pleasers like cashew hummus and melon carpaccio. It is the spot every Budapest vegan guide agrees on, and the one visitors keep coming back to twice in a trip.

It is the easiest table in the city to bring a mixed group to, because nothing on the menu needs an apology and the setting feels like a proper night out rather than a health-food compromise. Book ahead at weekends, order the goulash, and save room for dessert.
