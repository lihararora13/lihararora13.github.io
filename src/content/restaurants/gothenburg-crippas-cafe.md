Crippas Cafe is the green and good heart of Majorna, a delightful vegan cafe that serves burgers, grilled sandwiches and salads at dinner and weekend lunch. It is the neighbourhood cafe Gothenburg's vegans call their own: relaxed, friendly and entirely plant-based, with a menu that changes with the seasons.

It sits on Kusttorget, a short walk from the harbour. Order a burger or a grilled sandwich, add a salad, and enjoy a slow Majorna afternoon.
