Cafe Mila is a little paradise of a vegan cafe on 3e Avenue in old Limoilou, a few doors from Kagai. It serves breakfast and lunch: croissants, bagels, sandwiches and pastries, all fully vegan, with coffee and a relaxed neighbourhood feel.

It is the morning pick on this list, open from 7:30am on weekdays. Order a croissant and a bagel sandwich, and take a pastry for the walk to the Old Town.
