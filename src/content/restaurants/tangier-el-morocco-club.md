El Morocco Club is a storied restaurant and hotel in the Kasbah, with a garden terrace and a long history. It serves Moroccan and international food, and the vegetable tagine and salads are the vegan picks.

Order the vegetable tagine and eat on the terrace. It is a grand, old-Tangier room, so book ahead and expect grand prices.
