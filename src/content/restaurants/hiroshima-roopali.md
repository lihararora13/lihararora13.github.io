Roopali is the Indian restaurant a few minutes from Hiroshima Station, and it is the budget pick in this guide: huge portions, a lunch set of two curries with turmeric rice, naan and salad, and spice levels you can tailor from mild to very hot. For vegans the pick is the spinach and potato curry, with roti, papad and salad without dressing also vegan; most other curries and naan are made with cream and butter, so ask the staff to make your curry vegan rather than assuming.

Note there are two Roopali branches in Hiroshima: this is the Wakakusacho one near the station, not the Hakushima Q-Garden branch. The staff speak excellent English, and the vegetarian thali is the move if you are hungry.
