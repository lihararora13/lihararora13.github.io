Prana Vegan Club is a large-menu vegan club on Ugarte with a terrace and city views, but HappyCow notes from 2023 reported dairy and honey in some of its options, so it is filed vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan.

Order the Chicharrón de berenjena, Milanesa de tofu, Lomo saltado vegano, and Arroz chaufa vegano. The eggplant chicharrón and the tofu milanesa are the plates to try; check the current menu when ordering.
