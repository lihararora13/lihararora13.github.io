Glacis No.28 is Dresden's green dining room, a vegetarian restaurant, bar and theatre rolled into one, with a modern dining room, a busy outdoor patio and a kitchen that takes its vegan dishes seriously. The vegan plates are clearly labelled: fried asparagus with mashed potatoes, tomato salsa and vegan hollandaise, mashed potatoes with beetroot, spinach, mustard foam and onion, and a matcha tiramisu with raspberry for dessert. Cocktails and a theatre programme round out the evening.

It is the smartest table in the Neustadt, so book ahead, especially for the evening service. Lunch runs on weekdays, dinner most evenings, and the room rewards an unhurried visit. This is the pick for a proper night out that happens to be plant-based.
