Vimala's Curryblossom Cafe is a vegetarian Indian restaurant on West Franklin Street, doing curries, dals, and breads with a strong vegan bench. It is the curry anchor of this list, and the chana masala is the reliable order.

Order the chana masala and dal tadka with vegan roti, and confirm which breads are dairy-free, since most naan is brushed with butter or ghee.
