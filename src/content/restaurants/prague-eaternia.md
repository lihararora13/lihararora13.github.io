Eaternia is the lovable oddball of the Prague vegan scene: a former malt-house canteen tucked between two railway bridges in Smichov, where punks, skateboarders and vegans share tables over diner classics. The menu runs from smoky seitan hot dogs and beetroot burgers to poutine, langos, gnocchi and Czech dishes, with a well-priced daily lunch menu and homemade kombucha.

Come for dumpling Fridays if you time it right, and do not skip dessert. The salted caramel puddings and kremrole rotate daily, and the whole place is proof that the best vegan meal in Prague might be a tram ride from the tourist trail.
