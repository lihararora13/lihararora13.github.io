Die Pie is Edmonton's vegan pizza destination, and it is fancier than the name suggests. Everything is 100 percent plant-based and gluten-free, from the baked truffle brie to the Big Mack pizza, the spicy Korean po boy and the Die Pie nachos, with a wine bar to match.

It is the date-night pick on this list. Order the Big Mack pizza and the nachos, add a glass of wine, and enjoy the fact that the whole menu is yours.
