Worry-Free Tree (无忧树素食) is Kunming's cheapest fully vegan buffet: an all-you-can-eat spread in the basement of South Asia Style City on Dianchi Road, for 22 yuan a person, with lots of vegetables and local Yunnan food such as rice noodles, bean curd noodles, eight-treasure rice, fried mushrooms and stinky tofu. It is rated 5.0 on HappyCow, and Ctrip lists the same address, phone number and dishes at 20 yuan a person.

Finding it takes a moment: take the downstairs escalator next to the Starbucks in the mall, then ask a shopkeeper for directions, showing the Chinese name. Takeout is available for an extra 22 yuan. Note that HappyCow also lists this exact venue under the name Wújíshù, same unit number and same phone, so it is one restaurant, not two. Open 11:30am to 2:00pm and 5:30pm to 8:00pm.
