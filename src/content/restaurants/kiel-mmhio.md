Mmhio is a fully organic vegetarian café on Knooper Weg, and it is the calmest stop on Kiel's list: breakfast, lunch plates and homemade cake, with plenty of vegan dishes, and everything 100% BIO. The cooking is simple and seasonal, the room is bright and quiet, and the cake counter is the reason regulars keep coming back.

It opens weekday mornings and Saturday until early afternoon, so this is a breakfast and lunch stop. Order the organic breakfast, add a slice of cake, and take your time over coffee. This is the table for a slow morning in Kiel, and the one to pick when you want organic food without the worthy atmosphere.
