Abracado is the wholesome one. A vegan comfort-food restaurant that makes everything in-house, from cashew cream cheese and cold-pressed olive-oil mayonnaise to carrot lox cured with seaweed and herbs, and refuses sugar, fake meats and deep-frying on principle. Bagels, Vietnamese rolls, pancakes, salads, spaghetti bolognese, cakes: the menu wanders the world and stays plant-based the whole way.

It is a small, sunny daytime spot, open until four, and the kind of place where a light lunch somehow leaves you full of energy. Come for a bagel and stay for dessert.
