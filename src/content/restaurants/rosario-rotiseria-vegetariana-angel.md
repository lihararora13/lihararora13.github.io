Rotiseria Vegetariana Angel is a vegetarian rotiseria on Sarmiento, rated 4.6, serving takeaway lunches on weekdays from 11:30 am to 3 pm. The kitchen describes itself as sharing love and a way of life alongside the food, and the regulars treat it as a neighbourhood institution.

Order a takeaway lunch plate and ask which dishes are vegan. It is lunch-only and closed on weekends, so do not plan a Saturday visit.
