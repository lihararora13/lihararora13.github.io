Jain Siddham Rasoi is a small, serious kitchen in Bhopal's old city, and it is a quiet gift to vegans: Jain cooking is already no onion and no garlic, and the thalis are built around dal, seasonal sabzi and roti rather than paneer and cream. Ask for no ghee and the meal is vegan with almost nothing lost.

It is cheap, open nearly around the clock, and gloriously unpretentious. Come for a Jain thali when you want the most straightforward vegan meal in the city, served without fuss near the old markets.
