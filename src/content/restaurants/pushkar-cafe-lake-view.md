Cafe Lake View is exactly what the name promises: a café at Badri Ghat with the lake spread out in front of it, and the best breakfast view in Pushkar. It is the slow-morning pick on this list, the place to sit with a coffee and watch the ghats wake up.

Order a vegan breakfast plate and a fruit bowl, ask for plant milk in the coffee, and take your time. The food is simple and the view does the rest.
