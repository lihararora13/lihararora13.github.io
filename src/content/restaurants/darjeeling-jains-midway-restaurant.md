Jain's Midway sits on N.C. Goenka Road, a short walk from Chowrasta, and it is the kind of central pure-veg restaurant that Darjeeling's vegetarian visitors have relied on for years. It is open 8 in the morning to 9 at night, serving thalis and North Indian staples at budget prices.

Order the veg thali, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you have a quick plant-based meal in the middle of town. It is a simple room, and that is exactly what a hill-station lunch should be.
