Fresh Theory, formerly Salad Republic, is Vadodara's clean-eating headquarters: two cafés, everything made to order, calories and protein printed on the menu, and no frozen shortcuts. The Diwalipura branch is the original, open daily from 8:30 in the morning.

The veg bowls are the move, quinoa, burrito or mushroom, with coconut milk available in the smoothies. Note the kitchen also serves chicken and egg, so order the veg versions and skip the paneer if you want it fully plant-based.
