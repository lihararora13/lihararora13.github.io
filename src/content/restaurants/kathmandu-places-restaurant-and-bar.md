Places Restaurant & Bar made the list for a reason. A long-running vegetarian restaurant and bar in Thamel with an extensive menu, live performances and plenty of vegan dishes. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Thamel, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan thali, Hummus plate, Veg momo if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
