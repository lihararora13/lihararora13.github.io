Cafe Bateel is a fresh organic cafe on Olaya Street with vegan and gluten-free choices across the day: foul with Arabic bread, muesli and granola with soy milk, quinoa tabbouleh, black lentil salad, vegetable couscous and a selection of vegetable and lentil soups. Pasta and risotto can be made vegan by omitting cheese, and coffees come with soy or almond milk.

Order the foul for breakfast or the quinoa tabbouleh and vegetable couscous later. Note the pin is at street level on Olaya Street.
