Santé Spa Cuisine tops the list for a reason. Pune's most ambitious plant-based menu: 200+ vegan dishes, organic sourcing and a serene garden setting in Koregaon Park. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Koregaon Park, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Ragi base pizza, Avocado dynamite sushi rolls, Crunchy chlorophyll dimsums if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
