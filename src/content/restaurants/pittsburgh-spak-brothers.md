Spak Brothers is Garfield's pizza joint, and it is the late-night vegan stop on the list: vegan pizza, hoagies and wings in a loud, fun room that stays open when everywhere else has closed. It is a meat-serving pizza place with a real vegan menu, so it lands as vegan-friendly, but the vegan pizza is the one reviewers order on purpose.

It is the pick for the post-show slice, the group order, and the friend who thinks vegan pizza is a sad compromise. Order a vegan pizza, add wings, and enjoy the noise.
