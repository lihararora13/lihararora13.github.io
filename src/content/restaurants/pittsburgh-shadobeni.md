ShadoBeni is the North Side's vegan Trinidadian restaurant, and reviewers rate it a perfect 5.0: doubles, roti and curry plates, all of it plant-based and all of it cooked with the kind of warmth that makes a small restaurant feel like a family kitchen. The doubles are the signature, and the curry plates are the reason to come back.

It is the most transportive meal on the list, and the one to take someone who thinks vegan food is all burgers and bowls. Order the doubles, add a curry plate, and do not rush.
