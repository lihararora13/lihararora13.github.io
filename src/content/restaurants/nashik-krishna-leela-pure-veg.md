Krishna Leela is Nashik's South Indian counter on Ahilyadevi Holkar Road, opposite Suyash Hospital: a bright, busy pure-veg room where the dosa griddle never really stops. It is the kind of place that works for breakfast, lunch or a quick evening snack.

The masala dosa and idli sambar are vegan by default, just skip the ghee roast and the curd. Cheap, fast and satisfying, it is the most South Indian meal on this guide.
