Quyu's Taproom made the list for a reason. The sister venue to The Vegan Room: kombucha on tap, vegan sushi and eggless desserts from an in-house bakery, all plant-forward. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in C-Scheme, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan sushi, Kombucha, Eggless dessert if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
