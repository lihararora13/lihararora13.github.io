Cafe Sunflower has been feeding Atlanta's vegans for over three decades, which in restaurant years is basically forever. The Buckhead menu roams the globe without ever leaving the kitchen: sesame soy chicken one minute, enchiladas the next, pad Thai right after.

This is the reliable pick for mixed groups, anniversaries, and anyone who wants proof that plant-based cooking doesn't mean compromise. Save room for the carrot cake; people have strong feelings about it, and they are correct.
