Avocateur is exactly what it sounds like: a restaurant built around the avocado, on Oeder Weg in the Nordend, serving brunch and dinner with smashed avocado, tacos, shakshuka and guacamole. It is not fully vegan, but the vegan options are plentiful and clearly the point of the place.

Come for brunch, order the smashed avocado, and add tacos. It is the most fun breakfast on this list, and the most Instagrammable too.
