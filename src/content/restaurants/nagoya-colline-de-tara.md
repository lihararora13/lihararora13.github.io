Colline de Tara is the quiet gem of the Nagoya list: a fully vegan lunch restaurant near Chikusa Station, keeping things simple with set lunches built from carefully sourced ingredients. The vege ball, made with okara and mushroom, is a favourite.

It is lunch only and closed Monday and Tuesday. Order the set lunch and take the afternoon slow.
