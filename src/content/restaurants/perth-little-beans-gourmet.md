Little Beans Gourmet is worth the drive south, and Perth knows it: the Canning Vale dining room is fully vegetarian and mostly vegan, spinning Malaysian-Chinese comfort food with mock meats so convincing your non-vegan friends will not notice. The Penang curry noodles and Assam laksa are the dishes people cross the city for, and the nasi lemak arrives with all the traditional sides made plant-based.

Weekends are the main event, with a vegan dim sum session of char siu bao, satay sticks and siu mai dumplings that fills tables fast. Book ahead, bring an appetite, and order the laksa.
