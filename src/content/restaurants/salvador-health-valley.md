Health Valley is Salvador's budget hero: a natural restaurant near Piedade serving feijoada vegetariana, strogonoff with cashew cream, yakisoba vegano and farofa with peanuts, all for a gentle price and all rated 4.9 by reviewers. It is small, friendly and gloriously unfussy, the kind of place where the food does all the talking.

Come for lunch, order the feijoada vegetariana, and wash it down with sugarcane juice. This is the one for a cheap, delicious Salvador lunch.
