Cande is the special-occasion pick in Cartagena: a polished dining room with a full vegan menu that reworks traditional favourites, including a flavourful ceviche, sancocho, and Cartagenian rice slowly cooked in plantain leaves. It is not a vegan restaurant, but the vegan menu is a real menu rather than a couple of adapted sides.

Book ahead, come for dinner and dancing, and order the vegan ceviche served in a coconut followed by the arroz vegano in a banana leaf. It is the priciest table on this list and the one most worth dressing up for.
