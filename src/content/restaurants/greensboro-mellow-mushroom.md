Mellow Mushroom is a pizza restaurant on South Elm Street in downtown Greensboro, with vegan pizza options on the menu. The pies are the draw, and the vegan cheese and marinara options give plant-based diners a real pizza.

Order the vegan pizza and a salad. Ask the server to confirm the vegan cheese, since the standard pie is dairy.
