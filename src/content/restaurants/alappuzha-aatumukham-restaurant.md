Aatumukham is the open-air restaurant at Kuppapuram that the backwaters boats stop at, and an October 2026 video shows exactly why: a traditional Kerala vegetarian meal on a banana leaf, white rice with sambar poured over it, papadum, banana, pineapple and several vegetable curries, in a busy dining hall under a high tiled roof among the palms.

Order the banana-leaf meal and ask for the curd to be left off. It is the backwaters lunch, simple and wholesome, and the setting is half the meal.
