VQV sits inside the Mercado del Patio, Rosario's food hall, and it is the most convenient vegan table in the city: open 8 am to 11 pm every day, with a vegetarian and vegan kitchen serving lunch plates, burgers and salads. The market setting means you can eat well and then shop the hall.

Order a lunch plate or a vegan burger, and use the long hours: this is the pick for a late lunch or an early dinner when the rotiserias have already closed.
