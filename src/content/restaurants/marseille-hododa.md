Hododa, which means sense in Armenian, brings the vegan flavours of Armenia to a sunlit corner of Le Panier, and it tops HappyCow's Marseille list with a perfect score. The menu rotates, the cooking is conscious and colourful, and the dishes range from falafel and batata harra to tofu marinated shish-taouk style and a pistachio Lebanese flan that regulars plan their week around.

It is open for breakfast and lunch, weekends included, and the room is bright and welcoming. Order the falafel, add the vegan labneh, and finish with the flan. The best table in Marseille's oldest quarter.
