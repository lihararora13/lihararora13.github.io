Lulu Green is the overachiever of Boston vegan food, and it wears the trophy well. Boston Magazine crowned it the city's Best Vegan Restaurant in 2024, 2025, and 2026, and one bite of the hot honey chick'n sandwich explains why. By day it is an organic plant-based cafe slinging juices and Middle Eastern-leaning eats; by night it pours craft cocktails next to a 25-seat dining room that fills up fast.

The menu is entirely vegan, entirely made from scratch, and endlessly surprising: spiced sesame lattes, birria mushroom tacos on Taco Tuesday, and a cacio e pepe that will make dairy forgettable. It is the rare spot that pleases picky carnivores, devoted vegans, and your friend who just wants a good cocktail.
