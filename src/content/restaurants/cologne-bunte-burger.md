Bunte Burger is Ehrenfeld's vegan burger joint, and it does exactly what the name promises: colourful, organic, plant-based burgers with fries and shakes, in a cheerful room that takes sustainability as seriously as it takes flavour. It is the fun, fast end of Cologne's vegan scene, and the perfect partner to a day in Ehrenfeld.

Order the signature burger, add loaded fries, and finish with a shake. Burgers, done the vegan way, done right.
