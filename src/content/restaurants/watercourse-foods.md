Watercourse Foods is the reason Denver has a vegan scene at all.
The scratch kitchen has been fully plant-based since 2012, and its
comfort food menu is the benchmark every newer spot gets measured
against. Buffalo cauliflower wings, smoked jackfruit BBQ, blackened
tofu Caesar, and a mac and cheese that regulars order on reflex.

Weekend brunch on the shaded patio is the full experience: biscuits
and gravy, a breakfast torta stacked with barley sausage, and the
souffle pancake that regulars never shut up about. It closes on
Mondays, so plan around it.
