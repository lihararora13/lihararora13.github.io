South Indian food is a vegan's oldest friend in India, and House of Tamil in Gomti Nagar plays it straight: crisp dosas, soft idlis, sambar with real depth, and filter coffee for the finish. Most of the menu is a small tweak away from fully plant-based.

Ask for the masala dosa without ghee and the idli sambar as it comes, and you have one of the best-value vegan-friendly meals in the city. It is simple, fast, and exactly what a hungry traveller wants.
