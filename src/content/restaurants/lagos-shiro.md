Shiro is Lagos' polished pan-Asian restaurant, and it is famous among vegetarians for a menu that takes plant-based eating seriously rather than treating it as an afterthought. Vegan sushi, vegetable tempura and vegan curries are all on the table, with service to match. It is the special-occasion pick on this list.

Come for dinner, tell the staff you are vegan when you book, and let them guide you through the sushi and small plates. It is the priciest meal here, and the most polished.
