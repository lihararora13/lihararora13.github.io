Happy Veggie is a vegetarian restaurant on Koh Lanta, at Baan Phra Ae, serving black sticky rice, yellow curry, green curry, veggie burgers and tofu, and rated 4.9 from 30 reviews. It is open daily from 8:30am to 10pm, and it is the plant-based anchor for Koh Lanta, a ferry ride from Ao Nang.

Order the yellow curry and the veggie burger, and try the black sticky rice. It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so ask which dishes are plant-based, and it suits a day trip to Koh Lanta rather than a walk from Ao Nang.
