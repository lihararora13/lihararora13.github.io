Subrosa is a café and pub collective in Gaarden, run by the neighbourhood for the neighbourhood, and it is the most Kiel stop on this list: pizza bianca, vegan mac and cheese, a Sunday brunch, and a room that feels like a community centre that happens to cook very well. The kitchen is vegetarian and vegan, the prices are low, and the evenings run late on Fridays and Saturdays.

It sits on Elisabethstraße, across the harbour from the city centre. Come on a Sunday for brunch, or on a Friday evening for pizza and a drink, and expect a crowd of locals rather than tourists. This is the table for the Kiel that lives across the water.
