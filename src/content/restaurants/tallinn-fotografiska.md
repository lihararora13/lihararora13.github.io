Fotografiska might be the most important restaurant in Tallinn for plant-based dining, even though it is not fully vegan. Chef Gerli Travkin has earned the Green Star four years running by combining near-zero-waste practices with flavours that make you forget you are eating vegetables, and the restaurant sits in the same Telliskivi complex as F-Hoone and Nihe.

It is the special-occasion table on this list. Book ahead, tell them you are vegan, and let one of the Baltic's best kitchens cook for you.
