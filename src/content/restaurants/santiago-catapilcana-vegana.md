Catapilcana is the late-night hero of this list: a fully vegan bar and restaurant two blocks from Baquedano station that stays open when most of Santiago has gone home. The menu runs from pizzas and sandwiches to sharing boards and Chilean completos, with draft beer and a relaxed, activist atmosphere that reviewers keep praising as much as the food.

Try the vegan completo first, the Chilean hot dog done properly plant-based, then a pizza or the yuba mechada sandwich if you are still hungry. Service can take a little longer than a fast food stop, so settle in, order a drink, and enjoy one of the friendliest rooms in the city.
