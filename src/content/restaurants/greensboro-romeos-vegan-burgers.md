Romeo's Vegan Burgers is an all-vegan burger restaurant on Tate Street in downtown Greensboro, doing smash burgers, loaded fries, and shakes with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the burger anchor of this list, and the room is casual and counter-service.

Order the smash burger and the loaded fries, and save room for a vegan shake.
