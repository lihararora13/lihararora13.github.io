Cafe NICE is the daytime counterpart to Yadokari: a bright, casual, 100 percent vegan cafe a few minutes from Yakuin-Odori Station, doing plant-based burgers and soft-serve nice cream with an English menu.

It is closed Mondays, so plan around that. Order a burger and a nice cream.
