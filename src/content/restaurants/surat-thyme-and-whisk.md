Thyme & Whisk is the café Surat's vegan crowd keeps pointing each other to, and it is easy to see why. The Piplod room is bright and leafy, the menu is genuinely plant-friendly rather than one token salad, and the kitchen knows what vegan means without needing a long negotiation at the table.

Order a vegan pasta, a smoothie bowl and whatever vegan dessert is on the board, and settle in. It is the kind of place that works for a slow brunch, a working lunch or a first date where nobody has to explain themselves to the waiter.
