Cardamom Pod made the list for a reason. One of the Gold Coast's first vegetarian and vegan institutions, doing acai bowls, pancakes and a blinged-out benny in a beautiful room. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Southport, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Acai bowl, Vegan pancakes, Blinged-out benny, vegan if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
