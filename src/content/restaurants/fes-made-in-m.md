Made In M is a cosy restaurant in the heart of the medina with a bright blue facade, serving breakfast, lunch and dinner. The video tour shows a red vegan smoothie being poured and mint tea over fresh mint leaves, and the room is warm and welcoming.

Order a vegan smoothie and a vegetable tagine. It is a good refuelling stop on a long medina walk, and the blue doorway is easy to find.
