Veg.Edible is a 100 percent vegan, gluten-free, and nut-free takeout eatery and bakery on North 61st Street in Benson, with soy-free options too. The menu runs from BBQ jackfruit fries and Beyond burgers to the Ruby Melt and the S'mores cake, and the open kitchen and incense give the small room a distinctive feel.

Order the BBQ jackfruit fries and the Bertha burger, and save room for the S'mores cake. Note the hours: closed Monday and Tuesday, open Wednesday through Saturday 11am to 7pm and Sunday 11am to 3pm.
