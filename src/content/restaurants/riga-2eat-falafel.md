2eat Falafel & Hummus is ranked the number one vegan restaurant in Riga by reviewer praise, and it earns it the simple way: falafel and hummus done properly, with fresh salads, warm pita and good sauces. It is a small, fast, affordable spot on Terbatas street, the kind of place where the food is the whole event.

It is the quick-lunch pick on this list. Order a falafel plate, add hummus and pita, and enjoy the best-value meal in Riga.
