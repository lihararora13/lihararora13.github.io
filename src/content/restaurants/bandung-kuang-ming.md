Kuang Ming is a small vegan Chinese-Indonesian buffet on Sudirman Street, with more than thirty types of vegetarian meals and more than fifty types of vegetarian snacks, plus a small store selling ramen and veggie meats. The seating area is small and the service is fast, and Mandarin is spoken at the counter.

Order a buffet plate with grilled rice or liwet rice. It is a vegan buffet, open weekdays from 8am to 4pm, so come for lunch rather than dinner, and bring cash.
