Pizzeria Zac is the answer to the question nobody asks in Montreal anymore: can vegan pizza actually be good? House-made dough, fresh ingredients, and vegan cheese that genuinely pulls like mozzarella have made this little Plateau counter a 4.8-star local legend, with reviewers calling it the best vegan pizza in town. The Mile End and EL DIABLO pies are the headline acts, and the pepperoni calzone is a sleeper hit.

It is small, casual, and priced for students: $5 foldable slices, seitan mac, calzones, and parfait for dessert. Delivery and takeout run through the apps, the staff is friendly, and true vegans keep coming back. Grab a slice, sit on a picnic bench, and watch Duluth go by. This is vegan comfort food at its most joyful.
