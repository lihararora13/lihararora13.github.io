Veg on the Edge is Santa Cruz's only fully vegan restaurant and its anchor: a Black-owned African-fusion stall in Abbott Square Market, established in 2017, with a mostly gluten-free menu that runs on rice and quinoa bowls, soups, burgers, wraps and toast, with separate weekday and weekend menus. It is the one place in the city where everything on the menu is vegan, and it is the pick that local vegan guides and the VegNews Vegan Chef Challenge both lead with.

Hours are Monday to Thursday 10am to 9pm, Friday to Saturday 10am to 10pm and Sunday 10am to 9pm. Eat in the market hall, order a bowl, and note the separate weekend menu.
