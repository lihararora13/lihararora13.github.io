Falafel To Go is exactly what it says on the tin: a small hole-in-the-wall on Hope Street, just off Sauchiehall Street, serving freshly made falafel and falafel wraps. It is fast, cheap and very good, the kind of place you duck into for lunch and then recommend to everyone you meet.

Order a falafel wrap, add hummus, and eat it walking. The simplest pleasure on this list, and one of the best.
