Vegan World Peace is probably the most well-known fully vegan spot in Reykjavik, a relaxed restaurant on Adalstraeti in downtown Reykjavik serving pan-Asian comfort food: pho, Korean noodles, mapo tofu and Kung Pao dishes. Founded by Buddhist practitioner and chef Nara Lee, it embodies the principle that peace begins on your plate, and the food is good enough that locals recommend booking ahead for dinner.

It is the first stop on any Reykjavik vegan list. Order the Peace Bowl, add the chocolate cake made with Icelandic sea salt, and settle in.
