Shamballa is the special occasion in Da Nang's vegan scene: a vegetarian rooftop restaurant in a greenhouse setting that became the first vegetarian restaurant in the city to receive a Michelin Bib Gourmand in 2025. The cooking is refined and seasonal, with plenty of vegan plates if you ask, and the rooftop setting makes dinner feel like an event.

Book ahead, come at sunset, and let the kitchen guide you. This is the one for the best meal of your trip.
