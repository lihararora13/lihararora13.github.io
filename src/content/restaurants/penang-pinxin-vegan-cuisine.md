Pinxin is one of HappyCow's ambassador picks for Penang, and it earns the title: a fully vegan Chinese-Malaysian restaurant serving generous local plates in a calm, welcoming dining room. The menu leans into the flavours Penang does best, savoury, spicy and fresh, with dim sum, noodles and chef's specials that change with the seasons.

Come for lunch or dinner, ask for the chef's special, and order dim sum to share. This is the one for a proper sit-down vegan meal in the old town.
