Puro Cocina Honesta is a vegetarian restaurant in San Antonio built on honest cooking, and it earns its place as the vegan-friendly anchor of this list: the kitchen cooks a vegetarian cazuela de orellanas without seafood, using a viche sofrito, and the daily plates adapt easily to vegan. It featured on Cali's Petronio veggie route, which is a good sign of how seriously the kitchen takes plant-based cooking.

Order the cazuela de orellanas and ask which of the day's plates are vegan. It is open afternoons and evenings, later than most of Cali's lunch-focused vegan kitchens, which makes it useful for dinner.
