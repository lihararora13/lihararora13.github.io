Hakata Hiiragi is the sweet surprise of the list: a taiyaki shop near Akasaka Station with a sign saying no egg and no milk are used for the taiyaki. The fish-shaped cakes are a Fukuoka street snack, and here they are fully vegan.

Grab a couple while they last, they sell out. The red bean and soybean flour versions are the picks.
