Vita Rama is Sofia's fully vegan restaurant with a shop and a bakery, cooking high-carb, low-fat food with nothing fried, no sugar and no alcohol. The daily-changing menu runs to soups, salads, dips, breads and peppers, and the roasted eggplant over rice and the blueberry cheesecake are the dishes visitors name.

It is open Monday from 11am to 4pm, Tuesday to Saturday until 10pm, and Sunday until 6pm. Come for the daily menu at lunch, and leave room for something from the bakery.
