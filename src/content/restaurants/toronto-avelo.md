Why we love it — Avelo is the answer to anyone who ever asked "but can vegan be fancy?" Set in a heritage townhouse on a quiet Yorkville street, it guides you through 5 or 8 courses of seasonal small plates with the kind of quietly elegant service that earns a perfect 5.0 on OpenTable. Come hungry and budget three hours; the portions are small and the evening is long, in the best possible way.

This is the anniversary spot, the proposal spot, the impress-your-parents spot. For a looser night with the same inventive kitchen, head upstairs to Bar Avelo for tapas and cocktails under the domed ceiling.
