Rad Radish is what happens when one of the city's biggest restaurant
groups decides vegan food should be the fun option. Adriano Paganini's
Hayes Valley spot serves comfort food with a healthy-but-not-preachy
twist: spicy jackfruit sausage pizza, a truffled mac and cheese so creamy
it does double takes at non-vegans, and fresh locally sourced salads for
when you feel like balancing things out.

It shows up on InsideHook's short best-of list, VegNews's neighborhood
guide, and HappyCow's top rankings, which is about as close to unanimous
as SF gets. The room is very photographable, the probiotic sodas are
great, and it works for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.
