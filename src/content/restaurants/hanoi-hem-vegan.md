Finding Hem is half the fun: down a narrow Old Quarter alley off Cau Go, steps from the water puppet theatre, behind a doorway most people walk straight past. Inside it is simple and unpretentious, and the menu is anything but, a beautiful range of lesser-known and beloved Vietnamese dishes, all vegan and built on the familiar chay foundation.

The star is the hotpot. Fresh vegetables, tofu, mushrooms and noodles laid out to build your own bowl, simmering away in a broth that sings umami. Come back the next morning for breakfast and dip fresh banh mi into a curry-style stew like the regulars do.
