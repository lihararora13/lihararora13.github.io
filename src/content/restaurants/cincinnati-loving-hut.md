Loving Hut is the reliable, budget-friendly anchor of Cincinnati's vegan scene: an all-vegan cafe on Montgomery Road in Pleasant Ridge with a wide global menu, from pho and spring rolls to sloppy joes, veggie grilled chick'n with rice and salad, and raw specials like the Raw Taco Supreme. The Sunny Burger is the dish reviewers call the best plant-based burger they have had, and the Cincinnati-style chili is the local nod worth ordering. The carrot cake is a fixture too.

Service runs Tuesday to Saturday with separate lunch and dinner windows, 11am to 3pm and 4pm to 7pm, and the cafe is closed Sundays and Mondays. It is casual, quick and takeout-friendly, with outdoor seating in good weather. A good first stop if you want a lot of choice for very little money.
