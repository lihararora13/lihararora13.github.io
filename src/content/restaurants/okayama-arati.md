Arati is an Indian restaurant in Higashi-ku, east of the city centre, serving vegetable curry, dal, chana masala, rice, fried potatoes and edamame, several of which are vegan or can be made vegan. Specify no butter, ghee or yogurt when you order, as the kitchen cooks both vegetarian and vegan dishes and the default can include dairy.

The chana masala and the vegetable curry are the strongest vegan choices, with dal and rice alongside. It is a little out of the centre, so it suits an evening when you have time for the trip, and it is open for both lunch and dinner.
