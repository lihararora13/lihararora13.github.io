Vegan Planet is where Italian grandmas' recipes get the plant-based glow-up they always deserved. The pink-sauce gnocchi is the headliner, but the marsala, the calamari, and the espresso-soaked tiramisu all earn standing ovations from actual Italian reviewers.

The room has high ceilings, fresh flowers, and a cute patio, plus a deep vegan wine list at friendly prices. Parking out back is a Miami miracle. Order generously, the portions are built for sharing.
