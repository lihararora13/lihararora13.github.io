Spice Villa is the reliable workhorse of Piplod dining: big room, big menu, and a veg section that runs deep. It is the sort of place where a mixed group can all eat happily, and where the vegan in the party just asks for the ghee held and the curd swapped out.

The North Indian dishes are the move here. A dal tadka made without ghee, a mixed vegetable curry and hot tandoori rotis make a proper Surat dinner, and the location behind Iscon Mall keeps it an easy stop after shopping or a film.
