Barbar is Hamra's late-night institution, the place Beirut goes for falafel and shawarma after midnight. The falafel is the vegan order, with hummus and salads alongside, and the room is always busy, always fast, always good.

Come late, order a falafel sandwich, and soak up Hamra at its liveliest. It is the perfect end to a Beirut night.
