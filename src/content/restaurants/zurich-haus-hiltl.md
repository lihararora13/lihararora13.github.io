Haus Hiltl is not just Zurich's best vegetarian restaurant, it is the oldest vegetarian restaurant in the world, open since 1898 and in the Guinness Book of Records for it. The Sihlstrasse flagship has a buffet, a self-service counter, an a la carte menu, a bar-lounge, a cooking studio and a shop, and the menu is 90 percent vegan.

It is a 4.5-star HappyCow favourite with more than two hundred reviews. Go for the buffet, try the house-made meatballs and the Bolognese bowl, and take a moment to appreciate that you are eating in a piece of history.
