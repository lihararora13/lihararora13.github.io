Pusu Teahouse is Xi'an's calmest vegan table: a fully vegan, Buddhist-owned teahouse on Jianxi Street, near Yanpingmen metro, open for over ten years and rated 4.5 on HappyCow. The room is quiet and zen, the kitchen cooks spicy stews and regional specialties, and the afternoons are for tea, so it works as well for a slow lunch as for a long sit with a pot.

The menu is in Chinese only, so bring a translation app or point at the dishes other tables are eating. Open 9:30am to midnight. The pin is triangulated from HappyCow search distances, accurate to about 60 metres.
