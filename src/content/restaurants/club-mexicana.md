Why we love it — Club Mexicana has survived the great London vegan
squeeze that claimed so many independents, and it did it by being
absolutely impossible not to love. The Carnaby and Shoreditch outposts
are gone, but the brand is alive and well inside Seven Dials Market
and Battersea's Arcade Food Hall, slinging the same gloriously
over-the-top tacos that made it famous: beer-battered tofish, crispy
fried chick'n, jackfruit carnitas.

It is the fun night out of this list. Order the tofish tacos, the
loaded cheezy fries, and a frozen marg, then finish with the churros
dipped in rich chocolate. Nobody leaves hungry and nobody leaves
unhappy.
