Black Coffee is a Black-owned coffee shop on Vaughn Boulevard, a warm community room with vegan pastries, avocado toast, and strong coffee. It is the coffee stop on this list, and the east-side anchor that the Magnolia crawl does not reach.

Come for a pastry and a coffee, and linger: the room is built for it.
