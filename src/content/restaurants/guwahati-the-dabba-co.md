The Dabba Co. is Guwahati's pure-veg cloud kitchen, cooking out of the ground floor of Hotel Rajmahal on A.T. Road and serving its meals in bowls, which it affectionately calls dabbas. It is delivery and takeaway only, which makes it the practical pick on this list for anyone staying in the city rather than eating out.

Order a veg dabba bowl and ask for no paneer and no curd. It is open 11:30 to 10 daily, and it is the closest thing Guwahati has to a modern plant-based delivery kitchen.
