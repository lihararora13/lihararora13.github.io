Oasis Vegan Treats holds a special place in this guide: the first all-vegan outlet in Vadodara, founded in 2022, and still the city's only dedicated vegan bakery and kitchen outside the Greenr chain. It is a small operation in Diwalipura, with a fresh menu and baked goods that travel well on Zomato and Swiggy.

Order whatever bake is fresh that day and the vegan meal of the day. It is a delivery-friendly stop as much as a visit, and a good way to end a Vadodara vegan crawl with something sweet.
