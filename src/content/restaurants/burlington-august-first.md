August First is Burlington's neighbourhood bakery, a big South End room built around a wood-fired oven, with vegan pastries and avocado toast on a menu that leans heavily on local grain and produce. It is the best morning coffee-and-pastry stop on this list.

Come early for the best selection, and linger: the room is built for slow mornings.
