Purple Acai Wiesbaden is a fully vegan acai shop on Wilhelmstrasse, next door to Alebrijes, offering customisable acai bowls with fruits, nuts, cereal, seeds and sauces, plus coffee, porridge bowls and pancakes. It is open every day from eleven to eight, with outdoor seating.

Order a custom acai bowl and a coffee or a pancake, and treat it as breakfast or a light lunch rather than dinner.
