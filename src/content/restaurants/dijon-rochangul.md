Rochangul is a Uyghur restaurant on Rue des Godrans, and its vegan lagman, hand-pulled noodles with vegetables, is one of the most interesting vegan dishes in Dijon.

Order the vegan lagman and vegetable dumplings. A good evening choice for something different.
