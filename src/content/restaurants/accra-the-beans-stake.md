The Beans Stake is a small La spot doing what its name promises: bean-based Ghanaian cooking, with waakye and fried plantain in the mix. It is a weekday lunch place, open Monday to Friday, and it is the kind of local favourite that does not shout about itself.

Go at lunchtime, order the bean stew with plantain, and keep expectations simple: this is honest, cheap, plant-based neighbourhood food.
