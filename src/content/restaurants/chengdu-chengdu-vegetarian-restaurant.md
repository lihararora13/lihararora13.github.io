Chengdu Vegetarian Restaurant has been a local favourite for years, and the menu shows why: an extensive spread of plant-based versions of traditional Sichuan dishes, all without animal products, at family-friendly prices.

Order the vegetarian mapo tofu first, then dumplings and seasonal vegetables. It is the classic Chengdu vegan meal.
