Seitan's Realm is Columbus's vegan fast-food deli, and it is a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow for a reason: Philly cheesesteak sandwiches, gyros, roast beef sandwiches, jalapeno poppers and a fried chiken burger, all of it plant-based and all of it made to satisfy a craving. It started as a food truck, opened its Clintonville shop in 2021, and never looked back.

It is the pick for the hungry, the hungover, and the friend who insists vegan food cannot do a Philly. Order the cheesesteak, add poppers, and bring napkins.
