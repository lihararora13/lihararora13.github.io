Why we love it — Gracias Madre has been LA's vegan Mexican standard-bearer
for years, and the formula still works: an organic, fully plant-based menu
served on one of the best patios in West Hollywood. The potato pimiento
flautas are the order people write home about, the vegan fish tacos taste
suspiciously like the real thing, and the agave-forward cocktail program
keeps the tables loud and happy late into the night.

It holds 4.3 stars across 750-plus reviews and shows up on every credible
LA list we cross-checked. Come for brunch on the patio, stay for the
margaritas, and do not skip the flautas.
