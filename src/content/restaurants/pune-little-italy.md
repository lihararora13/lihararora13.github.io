Little Italy made the list for a reason. The beloved vegetarian Italian chain with a wide vegan menu: plant-based pizzas, pastas and desserts that work for the whole table. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Bund Garden Road, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan pizza, Vegan pasta, Dairy-free dessert if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
