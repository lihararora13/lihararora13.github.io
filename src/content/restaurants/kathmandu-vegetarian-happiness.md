Vegetarian Happiness made the list for a reason. A Chinese-run vegetarian restaurant by Boudhanath Stupa, with a stupa view from the windows and a good spread of vegan dishes. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Boudhanath, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Veg noodles, Tofu stir-fry, Veg momo if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
