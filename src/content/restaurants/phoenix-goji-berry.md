Goji Berry is the sunny cafe half of the Grand Avenue vegan double act, sharing a building with Earth and holding its own with ease. The menu leans lighter and brighter: acai bowls piled with fruit, Caesar salads, avo toast, a Cuban panini regulars swear by, and a bakery case full of churro donuts and guava-and-cheese pastries. The baked mac and cheeze is the comfort-food anchor, and the matcha limeade is the drink to order with it.

It is the kind of place Phoenix's vegan community treats as a living room: easy lunches, long coffees, and specials that change with the season. Come for a bowl and a pastry, stay for the people-watching on Grand Avenue.
