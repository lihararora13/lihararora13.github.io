Sixta is a very popular vegetarian lunch and brunch spot on Rue de Bayard, with outdoor seating and plenty of indoor seating, perfect for a warm day. On Saturdays it runs a brunch-only set menu with either a vegetarian or a vegan option, and the star of the show is the vegan creme brulee dessert, which one guide calls the best vegan version of the French classic it has ever tried.

It is listed as vegan-friendly, as the menu is vegetarian with vegan options. Order the brunch set menu and the vegan creme brulee.
