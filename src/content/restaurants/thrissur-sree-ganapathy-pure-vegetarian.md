Sree Ganapathy is the pure-veg restaurant at Swaraj Round North, in the Bini Heritage building, and its distinction is also the clock: open from 7:30 in the morning to midnight. It is the late-night tiffin room on the Round, for the evenings when the temple festival runs long.

Order the masala dosa and the veg meal, and ask for them without ghee or curd. It is the Round's night owl, and the city needs it.
