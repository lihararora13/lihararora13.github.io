Vinaigrette is the lightest stop on a Santa Fe vegan crawl, a salad restaurant in a converted space near the Railyard where the menu turns with the seasons. The chopped salads and grain bowls are the core of it, and the kitchen is happy to build them vegan with tofu, beans, and nuts doing the heavy lifting.

It is a good reset between heavier meals, and a reliable lunch when the bigger kitchens are booked out. Ask what is seasonal, and build from there.
