Lolaparoli is Aachen's fully vegan café-bar on Friedrichstraße, a performance and event space that cooks vegan burgers and rolls and pours rum, beer and cocktails late into the night. The kitchen also does vegan catering for events, which is how most Aacheners first meet it, but the bar itself is the draw: a small, lively room with a changing programme and a fully plant-based menu.

Hours are evenings only: Wednesday and Thursday from six until ten, Friday until eleven, and Sunday from five until midnight, with Monday, Tuesday and Saturday closed. Come for a late vegan burger and a drink, and check the programme before you go.
