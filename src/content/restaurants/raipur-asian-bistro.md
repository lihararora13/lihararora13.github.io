Asian Bistro is the quiet one on this list: a serene dining room in Civil Lines that locals kept suggesting whenever a best-of roundup left it out. The menu is pan-Asian, and the vegetable dishes, dim sum, noodles, stir-fries and soups, are the reason to come.

Confirm no egg or fish sauce when you order, and the kitchen will look after you. A calm, unhurried end to the Raipur list, and a good place to go when the busier cafés feel like too much.
