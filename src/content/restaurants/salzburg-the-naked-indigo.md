The Naked Indigo is a vegan and vegetarian fast-food spot in the Europark shopping centre, serving bowls, curries and porridges, all meat-free and customisable. The bean and corn stew with rice and sprouts is the bowl to order, and it is the mall stop on this list.

Order a bowl, a curry and a porridge, and ask for the vegan version, since some dishes run vegetarian with dairy or egg. It is closed on Sundays.
