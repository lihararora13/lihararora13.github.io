Govindas is the ISKCON restaurant in Nyali, on Moyne Drive, serving Indian and Gujarati food with a huge selection for the area. Reviewers call it the best place in Mombasa for vegan food, with large portions, friendly staff, and Saturday and Sunday buffets that are a feast.

Order a dal and a vegetable curry, and ask which dishes are vegan, as not all are. The weekend buffet is the time to go if you are hungry.
