FLFL is a vegan falafel restaurant on Servetstraat, and the top-ranked fully vegan spot in Utrecht on Vegan Map. The menu is falafel, hummus and pita, with salads and wraps, and HappyCow featured it in a world best vegan falafel round-up.

It is open daily from late morning to evening. There is also a FLFL Centraal at Stationspassage 7, so use the Servetstraat address on this page. Order the falafel with hummus and pita.
