Maison Bambou is Bordeaux's eco street-food answer: a local, healthy, eco-responsible kitchen on the Quai de Brazza that feeds omnivores, vegetarians and vegans from the same generous menu, with an eye on sustainable ingredients and packaging. The food is balanced, refined and good value, and the vibe is festive and neighbourhood-y.

It is open for lunch most days and late on Fridays, and it is at its best when the weather is good. Order the vegan bowl, add a juice, and eat it like the locals do.
