Saigon Kingdom is a Vietnamese restaurant at Steamer Wharf and in Frankton, with a separate vegan menu. It is a comfortable, modern room on the waterfront, and it suits a dinner in the centre.

Order from the separate vegan menu, and try the pho and the spring rolls. It is central and reliable.
