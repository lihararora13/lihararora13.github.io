Flower Crossing Rice Noodles is the table to book first in Kunming, because it does the city's signature dish fully vegan: crossing-bridge rice noodles, or guoqiao mixian, served with a choice of soup bases and sides. The shop is run by Shidachu (施大厨), a vegan food company whose flower rice noodles were named on Kunming's 2025 wild mushroom food recommendation list, and it holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, with the listing last updated in December 2025.

Order the tomato-based noodle soup, which reviewers call their favourite, plus a mock-meat side and the vegan yoghurt for dessert. It is midrange by Kunming standards, and the staff are friendly and helpful. The chain has a few locations across the city; this page is the Xiyuan Road store.
