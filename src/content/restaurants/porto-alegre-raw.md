Raw Porto Alegre is a 5.0-rated all-raw and all-vegan restaurant in Moinhos de Vento, serving a daily set menu of three courses, a salad or starter, a main and a dessert, with optional freshly made juice and complimentary flavoured water and tea. All food is organic and gluten, lactose and sugar-free, and a small grocery shop inside sells raw vegan goods made in the kitchen.

Order the set menu and a fresh juice. It is open Tuesday to Saturday, 9 am to 7 pm, and it relocated from Rua Tomaz Flores, so use the Padre Chagas address.
