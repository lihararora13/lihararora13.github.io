Plant Base is where Lisbon goes for vegan junk food done properly: a bubblegum-pink bar and restaurant by Carmo with pizzas, burgers, tacos, momos, nachos and fish and chips, all plant-based. VegNews picks the BBQ Cheese Vacon Burger and the nopales tacos, Veggies Abroad calls it the place to go when the craving hits, and HappyCow counts more than 570 reviews.

It is fun, filling and gloriously unpretentious, with local beers and cocktails alongside. Perfect for a group where everyone wants something different, and nobody wants a sensible salad, though you could probably get one if you insisted.
