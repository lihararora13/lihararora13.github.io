Apple & Eve is the comfort-food stop on this list: a fully vegan restaurant in Grindel where the burgers, fries and kumpir are the draw, and the lunch menu changes with dishes like stuffed eggplant, chickpea curry and rice plates. It is one of two locations, this one opened in 2023, and it has quickly become a local favourite.

Come hungry, order a burger and a kumpir, and settle in. This is vegan food that does not ask for permission, it just tastes good.
