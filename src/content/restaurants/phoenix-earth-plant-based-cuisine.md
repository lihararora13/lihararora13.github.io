Earth is the restaurant that makes people rethink everything they thought they knew about vegan food. The Aguiler family built it on Mexican-inspired comfort cooking, and the hits are gloriously over the top: shrimp tacos that taste like shrimp, quesabirria with proper dunkable consomme, flautas de papa, a pulled-seitan BBQ sandwich, and vegan bone-in wings that one reviewer called the best in the world. The monster shakes are a dessert and a show in one.

It sits on the artsy stretch of Grand Avenue, so the room hums with the neighbourhood's creative energy. Order the tacos, save room for a shake, and don't skip the chocolate ice cream: one diner was silently worried it was regular ice cream, it was that convincing.
