Elia Greek Kitchen sits by Pub Street, and it is the Mediterranean stop on this list: dolmades, zucchini fritters and gyros with falafel and hummus, all with vegan options clearly available. It is the place to land when New Leaf's kitchen has closed and you still want a proper dinner near the action.

Order the falafel gyros and the zucchini fritters. It is a meat-serving Greek restaurant with a good vegan bench, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it is open late, which counts for a lot on Pub Street.
