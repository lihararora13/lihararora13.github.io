With HÄRT grew from a small operation into a full-scale vegan patisserie, and walking in feels like stepping into a boutique bakery. Everything is fully vegan and artisanal: freshly baked goods, cakes and custom wedding cakes, made with the kind of pastry craft that ends the old joke about dry vegan brownies. The Biscoff layer cake alone has a fan club.

Note the address: the bakery relocated from Sunninghill Village to its current home inside the Moove Motion Fitness Club on Kikuyu Road, and some older guides still list the Greenside address. Order the Biscoff layer cake, a seasonal fruit-custard pastry, and a box of macarons to take home.
