Nabati Eatery is a vegetarian Jordanian street food kitchen on Keizerstraat in the centre, cooking falafel, hummus, mezze and bowls to family recipes from Amman. Every dish can be made vegan, and the falafel, fatteh, hummus, muhammara and baba ganoush are the core of the menu.

It is a cheap, fast lunch pick, open midday to early evening. Say you are vegan when you order, and start with the falafel and the fatteh.
