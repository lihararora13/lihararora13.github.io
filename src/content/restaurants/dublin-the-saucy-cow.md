The Saucy Cow is the place that comes up on top of every Dublin vegan list, and one visit explains why. Tucked into Temple Bar, it skips every clean-eating cliche and goes straight for the good stuff: towering smashburgers, fried chicken sandwiches slathered in jalapenos and spicy barbecue sauce, and Parm chicken rolls with smoky vegan bacon.

Come hungry and come ready to share, or not. Order the Big WAP, add the salt and chili hashbrowns, and wash it all down with a creamy shamrock shake. This is vegan comfort food with zero apologies and maximum joy.
