Slutty Vegan's Birmingham location in Woodlawn is an all-vegan burger joint where everything on the menu is plant-based by default. The room is loud yellow and black, the burgers carry provocative names, and the food is unapologetically greasy comfort rather than virtuous salad.

Order the One Night Stand burger with hooker fries and a cup of banana pudding. Come hungry and expect a line at peak hours.
