FTP Vegan Eats is a food truck at the Rotorua Night Market on Tutanekai Street, serving vegan dishes. The name alone is not proof of vegan status, and HappyCow gives no veg status for it, so it is filed vegan-friendly, not all-vegan. Check the night market days before you go, as the truck moves with the market.

Order the vegan dishes and ask for the vegan options by name. It is inexpensive and central, and it suits a market evening.
