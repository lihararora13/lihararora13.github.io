JOLA is the place every Vienna vegan guide points to first when the occasion calls for something special, and one visit explains why. Since March 2022, chef Jonathan Wittenbrink and Larissa Andres have served a fully plant-based surprise menu in a relaxed first-district room, cooking root to leaf, knowing their producers by name, and wasting as close to nothing as a kitchen can manage. Gault Millau gave it three toques, a first for a vegan restaurant in Austria, and reviewers keep calling it the best meal of their lives.

Come for a celebration, book ahead, and surrender to the menu. The courses change with the seasons and the market, the open kitchen lets you watch the cooking happen, and the wine or non-alcoholic pairing is half the fun. This is vegan dining at its most confident.
