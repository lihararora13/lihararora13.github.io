humus x hortense is the destination restaurant on this whole list: Belgium's first vegan Michelin-starred restaurant, and the first 100 percent plant-based fine dining restaurant in the country. Chef Nicolas Decloedt cooks a tasting menu that changes with the year's 24 micro-seasons, using hyperlocal organic produce, while mixologist Caroline Baerten pairs it with zero-waste botanical drinks.

It is open for dinner Wednesday to Saturday, with lunch on Friday and Saturday. Book well ahead, order the tasting menu, and let the kitchen show you what vegan fine dining can be.
