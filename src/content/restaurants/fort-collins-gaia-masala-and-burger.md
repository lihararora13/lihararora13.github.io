Gaia Masala & Burger is Fort Collins' Indian-American fusion pick: a restaurant on South College Avenue near the university with vegan momos, chili tofu, a vegan burger and a falafel sandwich. The momos are the dish to order, and the fusion of Indian and American fast food makes it the most unusual table in this guide after Tasty Harmony. It opened in January 2026 and stays open late, daily 10am to 3am, which makes it the late-night pick in this guide.

Order the vegan momos and the chili tofu. The vegan dishes are marked, so ordering is straightforward.
