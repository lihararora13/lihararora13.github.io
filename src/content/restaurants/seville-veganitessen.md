Veganitessen has been vegan since 2009, making it the oldest vegan spot in Spain, and it still lives inside the Arenal market hall. Burgers, tapas, tortillas and a pastry counter that will ruin you for other bakeries, all fully vegan, all handmade.

It closes at 15:30, kitchen until 15:00, and it is closed Sundays and Mondays, so plan lunch, not dinner. Order the tortilla, a seitan burger, and a tiramisu, and eat it at a market table like a local.
