The Common is a 100% vegan brunch cafe and zero-waste refill shop in Jung-gu, Daegu, with a wall painted MANKIND IS KIND, bulk jars of grains and spices, kombucha on tap and two resident rescue cats, Wangja and Howa, who roam the dining room. The menu lists a tempeh plate, falafel, pasta dishes, oat brownies and oat lattes, and visitors in 2026 call it the city's essential vegan stop. The owner is warm and the room is the kind of place you linger in.

Order the tempeh plate and an oat brownie, and bring a container if you want to use the refill shop. It is the strongest all-vegan pick in Daegu and the natural first stop for anyone arriving in the city.
