Berner and Brown is Magdeburg's tapas bar, a Mediterranean room on the Breiter Weg where the vegan offering runs to dips, appetizers, main dishes and a vegan dessert, on a menu that changes with the seasons. It is a meat-serving restaurant, so the vegan dishes sit alongside the rest, but they are a deliberate part of the kitchen's cooking rather than a grudging add-on, and the wine list is worth exploring.

It opens for lunch on weekdays and dinner most evenings, and reservations are wise. Order a spread of vegan tapas for the table and share. This is the pick for a slow, wine-led evening in the Altstadt.
