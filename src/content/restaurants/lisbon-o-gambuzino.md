O Gambuzino is the playful, ferment-loving heart of Intendente: a fully vegan restaurant and bar that makes its own kimchi, kombucha, tempeh, pickles and marinades, and builds a slow-food menu from around the world around them. HappyCow rates it 5.0, and VeganHaven loved the bibimbap and the mushroom scallops in pea foam.

Come in the evening for small plates, a proper main or the tasting menu, and settle in for the happy hours at the bar. It is creative without being precious, and reservations are a smart idea, this little spot is popular for very good reasons.
