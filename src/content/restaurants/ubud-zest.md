Zest is the name every Ubud vegan guide leads with, and it earns that spot by refusing to be a salad bar. The kitchen is wholly plant-based and gluten-free, and the menu is hearty comfort food with a Balinese accent: juicy shiitake mushroom burgers, jackfruit empanadas, peanut miso ramen, coconut calamari and a mushroom schnitzel parmigiana, finished with desserts like cucumber ice cream and apple cheesecake. It sits on the Penestanan hill in an open-air room built around a big tree, next to a temple, and it draws yogis, digital nomads and curious meat-eaters in equal measure.

It opens daily from 8am to 10pm and gets busy at lunch, so book ahead if you can. If you only have one vegan meal in Ubud, make it dinner here and let the jackfruit fillet steak do the talking.
