Vegenarang is the Busan vegan meal to plan a sunset around: Buddhist temple-style food on the 9th floor near Gwangalli Beach, with sea views, lotus-leaf rice sets, fried oyster mushrooms and bean cutlets that mimic tonkatsu.

Book a sunset table if you can. The lotus-leaf rice set and the bean cutlet are the dishes to order.
