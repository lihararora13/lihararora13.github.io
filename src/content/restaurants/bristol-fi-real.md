Fi Real is a burst of colour in Old Market, a Caribbean-inspired vegan cafe that has been going since May 2014 and still holds a 5.0 on HappyCow. The menu is veggie food and cakes plus smoothies and Caribbean drinks: curry with rice and beans, lentil stew and whatever the kitchen is feeling that day, all of it eat-in or takeaway.

It is small, friendly and unpretentious, the kind of place where the food does all the talking. Order the curry, add rice and beans, and finish with a slice of cake.
