Netherworld is the late-night end of Brisbane's vegan scene. Part retro diner, part drinking hole, part arcade, this Fortitude Valley institution is plant-based first: the menu is vegan by default, with non-vegan options only as add-ons, so the karaage tofu, Hellmouth fries, Beelzebun burger and gravy-smothered Pegasus poutine are all yours without a second thought.

Come for dinner, stay for the pinball and the jukebox, and order the poutine. It is the rare vegan-friendly room that stays open late, pours a proper drink, and never makes the plant-based table feel like an afterthought.
