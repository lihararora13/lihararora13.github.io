Bodhi Vegan Living's St. Georg restaurant sits on the Lange Reihe, one of Hamburg's best streets for an evening out, and it does what the Bodhi group does best: fully vegan Asian food, from sushi and tapas to bowls, all freshly made and generously portioned. It is buzzy, modern and reliably good.

Order the sushi set, add a few tapas, and then wander the Lange Reihe afterwards. This is Hamburg's vegan scene at its most social.
