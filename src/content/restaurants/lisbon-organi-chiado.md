Organi Chiado has been doing seasonal, local, organic vegan cooking in Chiado since 2016, fully vegan since 2018, with no refined sugars and a menu that follows the market. VegNews highlights the lentil curry, feijoada and lasagna, HappyCow lists close to 600 reviews, and the little terrace on the steps is one of the sweetest lunch spots in Baixa-Chiado.

Order the plate of the day if you want to taste what the kitchen is proudest of, add the trio of pates to start, and save room for the pecan pie or berry cheesecake that reviewers keep mentioning. Fresh juices and organic wines complete a very Lisbon lunch.
