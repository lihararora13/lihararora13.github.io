Vegetarian Kedai Kungfu is a vegetarian cafe on Jl. Kapasan Dalam II in Simokerto, with Indonesian vegetarian dishes and a strong Instagram presence. It is a modern, casual kitchen, and it suits a quick lunch in the east of the city.
