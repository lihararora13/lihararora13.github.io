Kesariya sits on Sevoke Road beside the Vishal Cinema Hall, and it is the pure-veg restaurant Siliguri's food lovers photograph most: club kachori straight from the streets of Kolkata, Amritsari chole kulche, palak paneer and honey chilli potato, all served in a bright dining room that calls itself an experience.

Order the club kachori and a masala dosa, ask for no paneer and no butter, and you have the best veg lunch on Sevoke Road. It is open 11:15 to 11 daily.
