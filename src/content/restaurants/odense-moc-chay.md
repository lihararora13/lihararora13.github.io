Moc Chay is a vegan Vietnamese restaurant on Norregade, rated 5 on HappyCow, serving pho, banh mi, fresh summer rolls, fried noodles and rice dishes made with tofu, soy protein and mushrooms. It is the Vietnamese pick on this list.

Order the pho, the banh mi and the fresh summer rolls, and come Tuesday to Saturday, since it closes Sundays and Mondays.
