River Music Garden is the prettiest table in Old Manali: a garden restaurant on the riverbank near the Old Manali bridge, tables under trees and prayer flags with the river running past, and a covered garden for the evenings. The German bakery counter does cakes and pies, and the menu runs from momos and thukpa to sizzlers and Israeli plates.

It is open 8:30 AM to 11 PM every day, with free Wi-Fi. Order the veg momos and a slice of the bakery cake, and sit by the river.
