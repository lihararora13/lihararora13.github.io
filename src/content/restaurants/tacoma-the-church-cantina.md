The Church Cantina is Tacoma's Mexican vegan pick: a cantina on South Tacoma Way with a separate vegan menu, a Vegan Cubano, a Green River Wrap, vegan tacos and a plant-based burrito. It is the most generous vegan menu in the city outside Quickie Too, and it is open long hours, Monday to Thursday 11am to 9pm, Friday to Saturday 11am to 10pm and Sunday 10am to 9pm, with outdoor seating. The room is a converted church, which gives it the name and the atmosphere.

Ask for the vegan menu on arrival. Order the Cubano and a taco, and come on a Sunday for the 10am opening.
