Prana Vegetariano is the obvious lunch stop on a Botanical Garden morning: five minutes from the entrance, relaxed and green, a bit hippie in the best sense, with a garden feel that matches the neighbourhood. The menu is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but the vegan options are genuine rather than one token dish: soya meatballs, Indian-inspired plates, big salads and fresh juices, plus kombucha, teas and smoothies. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow from 47 reviewers.

Lunch runs roughly midday to 4pm, with snacks after and dinner in the evening. The staff are patient with questions about ingredients, which matters if you have restrictions. Order the soya meatballs and a fresh juice, then wander into the gardens.
