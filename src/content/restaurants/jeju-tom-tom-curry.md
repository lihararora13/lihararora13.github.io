Tom Tom Curry is the east coast's cheap and cheerful vegan-friendly stop: a small vegetarian Japanese curry house on the Haemajihaean-ro coastal road in Gujwa-eup, not far from Woljeongri Beach and Five Seventh. The menu is short and the curry is the point, a mild, savoury Japanese-style curry rice that can be made vegan, served in a plain room at prices that undercut the island's cafe scene. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow from a small set of reviews.

It is the simplest pick in this guide, so keep expectations in line: come for a quick, satisfying bowl between beaches rather than a destination meal, and pair it with Five Seventh down the road if you want the full east-coast vegan afternoon.
