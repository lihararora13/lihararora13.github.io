The World Storytelling Cafe is the city's first exclusively plant-based restaurant, and it is a must-visit for any vegan in Marrakech. The menu is Middle Eastern and African cooking at its most generous: stewed beans, aubergine with garlic and coriander, pumpkin with raisins, soups, dips and smoothies, all fully vegan. It is also a venue space, so the room has a story to it.

Come for lunch, order across the stews and dips, and linger. This is the one fully vegan kitchen on the list, and it earns the top spot.
