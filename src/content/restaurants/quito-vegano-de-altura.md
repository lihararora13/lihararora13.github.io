Vegano De Altura, vegan at altitude, is exactly that: an all-vegan restaurant in La Floresta with a small, imaginative menu of vegan Ecuadorian dishes, plus ice cream, juices and cakes that reviewers keep praising. The room is calm, the staff are welcoming, and the food is beautifully presented.

Order a vegan Ecuadorian plate and save room for cake. It is the most charming dinner on the Quito list, and the one to take someone who thinks vegan food in Ecuador means a sad salad.
