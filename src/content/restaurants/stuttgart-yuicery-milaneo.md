Yuícery Milaneo is a fully vegan bowl bar on Mailänder Platz in the Europaviertel, up by the Milaneo shopping quarter. The pumpkin falafel bowl is the signature, with a tex-mex bowl, avocado toast and vegan pulled pork alongside, all of it quick and all of it plant-based.

It is the shopping-day lunch on this page, and the bowl to order is the pumpkin falafel. Note: the venue is listed as vegan on HappyCow, while the places data describes it as serving vegetarian food, so the all-vegan label here rests on the HappyCow categorisation.
