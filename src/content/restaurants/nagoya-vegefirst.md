Vegefirst is the quick lunch of the list: a small weekday-only vegan bento counter in Marunouchi, a few minutes from Hisaya-Odori Station or Nagoya Castle. Grab a bento and eat it in the castle grounds.

It is weekday lunch only, so plan a castle day around it. The bento is the whole order.
