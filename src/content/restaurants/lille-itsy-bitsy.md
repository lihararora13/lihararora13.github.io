Itsy Bitsy is one of only two fully vegan spots in Lille, a small bakery-café in the Centre turning out cookies, cheesecake, hummus and sandwiches. It is the sweet stop on this page and the one locals name when visitors ask where to eat vegan in the city.

Everything is plant-based, so the case is safe to graze. Come for a cookie and a coffee, stay for a sandwich, and take a cheesecake slice for later.
