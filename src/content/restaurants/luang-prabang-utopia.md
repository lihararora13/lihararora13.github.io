Utopia is a relaxed garden bar and restaurant in Ban Aphai, with vegan-friendly Western and Lao dishes and a riverside garden. It is a popular sunset spot, and the garden setting makes it a good place to linger over a long, slow meal.

Order the salads and vegetable dishes, and ask for the vegan options by name. Its pin is approximate to Ban Aphai.
