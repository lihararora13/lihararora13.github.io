Estravagario describes itself as an organic, fair-trade, queer and anti-speciesist vegan restaurant, and HappyCow reviewers gave it 4.9. The menu is Bolognese comfort food made plant-based: seitan, lasagne, cappellacci, crostata and tiramisu, with a long lunch service and evening hours on Thursdays to Saturdays.

It is open from early morning, so it works for breakfast, lunch or dinner. Order the lasagne and the tiramisu, and expect a warm, political, proudly independent room.
