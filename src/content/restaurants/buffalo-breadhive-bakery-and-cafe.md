BreadHive is Buffalo's worker-owned cooperative bakery, a small West Side room on Connecticut Street that bakes its own bread and builds labelled vegan sandwiches daily from tempeh and house-made seitan. It is a bakery first, so the vegan offering is sandwiches and baked goods rather than a full menu, but the quality is high and the cooperative model is part of the appeal. Outdoor seating in season.

Hours are Thursday to Sunday 8am to 3pm, closed Monday to Wednesday. Come for a late breakfast or lunch, and check the daily sandwich board: the vegan options rotate.
