The Helmand is a Mount Vernon institution serving Afghan food in a white-tablecloth room on Charles Street. Its vegetable dishes, pumpkin, spinach, leeks, and dumplings, are the vegan draw, and the kitchen is used to adapting them.

Order the pumpkin and the spinach with leeks, and ask for the vegan preparation of the yogurt sauce, since the standard version is dairy. Book ahead on weekend evenings.
