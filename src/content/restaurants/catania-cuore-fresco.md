Cuore Fresco is the vegan restaurant behind Piazza Duomo, and Catania's vegan anchor. The menu board lists focacce and panini with vegan and gluten-free options, the pink beetroot-bun burger with fragrant fries is the signature, and the 15-euro vegan aperitivo, a super tagliere with focaccia, vegan paninetti, plant-based pulled pork and pistachio fries plus a cocktail, is one of the best deals in the city.

Order the vegan burger on the beetroot bun, the Vegania salad with spinach, quinoa, avocado and almonds, and the vegan aperitivo if you arrive in the evening. It sits steps from the cathedral, so it is the easiest first stop in the city.
