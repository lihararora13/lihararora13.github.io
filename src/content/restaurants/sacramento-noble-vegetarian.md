Noble Vegetarian is the kind of place every city wishes it had: an all-vegan Vietnamese restaurant that has been quietly excellent for years, serving pho, spring rolls and clay pot dishes at prices that make you double-check the bill. The room is simple, the food is generous, and the regulars are loyal for a reason.

It is a drive from downtown, and it is worth it. Order the pho, add spring rolls, and settle in for one of the best-value vegan meals in California.
