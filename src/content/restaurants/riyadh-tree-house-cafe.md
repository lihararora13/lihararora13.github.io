Tree House Cafe is a small bistro on Prince Mohammed Ibn Salman Road in Ar Rabi which is willing to adapt dishes to suit diets, and has its own organic farm supplying most of its ingredients. Vegan dishes include fresh salads, a famous falafel sandwich, plant milk and an extensive dessert menu.

It is open daily from 11:00am to midnight. Order the falafel sandwich and a salad, and save room for dessert. Note the pin is at street level in Ar Rabi.
