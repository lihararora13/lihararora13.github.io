The Raw Place is Abu Dhabi's raw and plant-based cafe, doing juices, smoothies, nut milks, bowls and light vegan meals. It is the wellness counterweight to the city's heavy hotel dining, and a good reset after a big night.

Order a green juice and a smoothie bowl, and treat it as a light lunch or a mid-afternoon stop. It is the freshest pick on this list.
