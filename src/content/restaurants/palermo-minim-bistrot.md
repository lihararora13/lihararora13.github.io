MiniM Bistrot is a plant-based bistrot on Via Antonio Gagini, a slow-food room with an orange garden where the kitchen moves with the seasons. The cooking is vegetal and creative rather than doctrinaire, and the bistrot opens at ten in the morning most days.

Order the seasonal vegetable plates and the daily specials, and come for a slow lunch in the orange garden. It runs late on Thursdays and Fridays and opens Saturday afternoon.
