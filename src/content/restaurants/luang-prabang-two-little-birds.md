Two Little Birds is a vegetarian cafe on Kingkitsarath Road, offering smoothie bowls, light bites, lunch bowls and mains, and it will adapt dishes for vegans on request. It is reported fully vegetarian as of October 2022, and it is a bright, modern cafe in the centre of town.

Order a smoothie bowl and a lunch bowl, and ask for the vegan version. It is a useful brunch and lunch stop, and it sits a short walk from Reunion and The Happy Cat.
