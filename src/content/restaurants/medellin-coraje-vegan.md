Coraje Vegan is the table every guide in Medellín agrees on: HappyCow's highest-rated vegan restaurant in the city at a perfect 5.0 from 63 reviews, and a fixture in the Veggies Abroad and medellin.com round-ups too. It is a small vegan café and bakery hidden down a pedestrian passage in Manila, next to the Landmark Hotel, with a short menu executed with real care: arepitas topped with smoked eggplant, mushrooms and a spicy ricotta sauce, tofu tacos, sourdough and a pastry case that regulars swear by.

The café recently moved to a new, slightly larger space on the same passage, and recent reviewers say the food is still excellent. Come for brunch or lunch, order the arepitas Veggies Abroad called the best they ever had, save room for the chocolate cake or a cinnamon roll, and say hello to the black café cat if it wanders over.
