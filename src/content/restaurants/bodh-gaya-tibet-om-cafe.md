Tibet Om Cafe has fed Bodh Gaya since 1986, and its red sign still says VEG. & VEGAN in big yellow letters. It is rated 4.4 from 118 TripAdvisor reviews, the second-best restaurant in town, and it serves momos, thukpa, thenthuk and tingmo at prices barely in the modern era, 200 to 400 rupees a person.

Order the steamed momos and the thukpa. Note that it runs on the pilgrimage season, October to March only, and that it moved premises recently, so confirm the location on the day rather than trusting an old map pin.
