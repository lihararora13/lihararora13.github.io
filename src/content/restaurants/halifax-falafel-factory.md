Falafel Factory is Halifax's all-vegan falafel shop, out on the Bedford Highway in the Larry Uteck area, and it holds a perfect 5.0 HappyCow rating. The menu is short and does one thing well: falafel sandwiches, falafel platters, fatteh with chickpeas, toasted pita and tahini, and fries.

It is open daily from 9am to 9pm, which makes it the reliable cheap vegan meal in the north of the city, and it does delivery and take-out as well as dine-in.
