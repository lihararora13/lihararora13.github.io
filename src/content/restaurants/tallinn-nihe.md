Nihe is Rohe's sister cafe in Telliskivi Creative City, and reviewers call it the most spacious vegan cafe in town, suitable for vegans and for those reluctant to try vegan food, because Nihe does not advertise itself as a vegan cafe, it just is. The menu is breakfast and lunch with a bakery heart: cardamom buns, chocolate buns, croissant sandwiches, focaccia and vegan cakes.

It sits near the Fotografiska museum with nice outdoor seating, open every day from 9 to 9. Order a croissant sandwich for breakfast, a colourful quinoa and smoked carrot dish for lunch, and a cake to finish.
