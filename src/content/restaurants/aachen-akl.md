AKL is a Lebanese restaurant on Pontstraße, Aachen's student strip, and the vegan move is to build a meal from the clearly labelled choices: mezze plates, falafel sandwiches, hummus and tabbouleh, all of it served fast and cheap in a room that fills with RWTH students at lunchtime. The kitchen is mixed, so order from the vegan-labelled dishes.

It is open Monday to Saturday from half past eleven until eleven, with Sundays closed. Order the mezze plate to share, add a falafel sandwich, and treat it as Aachen's best-value vegan-friendly lunch.
