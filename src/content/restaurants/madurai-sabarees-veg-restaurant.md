Sabarees is the high-volume pure-veg restaurant near Madurai Junction, the one the crowds pile into for banana-leaf meals, ghee roast and veg kothu parotta. It is loud, fast and generous, and the meal at 145 rupees is one of the best-value lunches in Tamil Nadu.

Order the banana-leaf meal without curd and the veg kothu parotta. It is the working lunch of Madurai, and it tastes like it.
