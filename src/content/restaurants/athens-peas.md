Peas is one of those places where the noise drops the moment the door shuts: a small, cosy, tucked-away spot in Koukaki, and one of the city's earlier fully vegan places, open since 2017. The food is fully vegan and made from scratch, from Greek dishes and Greek pitas to burgers, sandwiches, main dishes, salads and desserts, with raw and sugar-free options alongside fresh juices, smoothies and organic fair-trade coffee.

It sits a six-minute walk from the Acropolis Museum and a minute from Syngrou-Fix metro station, which makes it a perfect quiet lunch after the crowds. Try the seitan gyros wrap, the Pita Pita, and save room for a raw dessert.
