Shree Anandhaas is the family tiffin favourite at Lakshmi Mills Junction, the pure-veg restaurant Coimbatore locals name alongside Annapoorna when asked where to eat. It does South Indian and North Indian, tiffin and sweets, from 7 in the morning to 10 at night.

Order the masala dosa and the veg meal, and ask for the ghee to be held. It is a big, reliable, always-busy room, and the food is consistent in the way only a decades-old institution can be.
