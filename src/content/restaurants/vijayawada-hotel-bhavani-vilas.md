Hotel Bhavani Vilas is the newly opened pure-veg restaurant behind the Jyoti Convention on Silver Palace Road, and the June 2025 review calls out the budget pricing, the ample parking and the unusual dishes: idiyappam, dhokla, poha, whole-wheat paratha, chole puri and pongal dosa, with perugu vada as the community recommendation.

Order the perugu vada and the idiyappam. It is the budget pure-veg find in Vijayawada, and the menu is more interesting than the room suggests.
