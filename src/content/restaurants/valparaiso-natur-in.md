Natur In is a 4.4-rated vegetarian restaurant and smoothie and juice bar on Avenida Colon, serving a daily set menu with juices and smoothies. It is a lunch-only kitchen, open Monday to Friday from 12:30 to 4:30 pm, and it has been feeding the Almendral lunch crowd for years.

Order the set menu of the day with a fresh juice. It is simple, cheap and fast, and the best option in the area when you want a proper lunch rather than a cafe snack.
