Manna Haven is a little pocket of calm in the middle of Byron's busiest street: a not-for-profit vegan cafe run by the local church community, with a peaceful garden courtyard and a rotating menu that changes with the seasons. Hot mains like Thai peanut curry, lasagne and spiced wedges sit next to a big spread of salads, desserts and hot drinks, including a turmeric latte regulars rave about. It is budget-friendly, welcoming and gloriously unhurried.

Come for lunch, take your time, and ask what is fresh that day. This is the one for a slow, wholesome lunch between beach sessions.
