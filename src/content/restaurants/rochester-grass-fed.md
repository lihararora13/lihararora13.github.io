GRASS fed is Rochester's first vegan butcher and the top-rated fully vegan spot in the city: a deli on Monroe Avenue that makes and sells a range of vegan and plant-based proteins, then builds them into sandwiches for dine-in, takeout or delivery. Gluten-free choices are available across the menu, and the counter doubles as a shop for taking proteins home. It is the practical, everyday counterpart to the Red Fern's cafe cooking, and local guides rank it first in the city.

Hours are Monday, Thursday and Friday 10am to 6pm, Saturday 11am to 3pm and Sunday 10am to 6pm, closed Tuesday and Wednesday. Check the daily sandwich board, the butcher sandwich of the day is usually the best thing on it.
