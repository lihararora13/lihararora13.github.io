Gardurinn is a Reykjavik legend, a vegetarian restaurant that has been serving the community since 1999, making it one of the longest-running vegetarian spots in the city. The format is a lunch buffet: hot food prepared daily, legume stews, roasted vegetables, grain salads, soups and baked items, with the menu changing daily based on what is available seasonally.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but the vegan options are plentiful and clearly marked, and at around ISK 2,200 to 2,800 it is one of the most affordable sit-down meals in Reykjavik. Fill your plate once, add a soup, and enjoy a proper home-cooked lunch.
