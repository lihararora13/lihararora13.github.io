Foodstock is a small vegan bistro on Klobucnicka, rated 4.5 from 241 reviews, serving gyoza, hummus, falafel, ramen, kimchi and miso, plus a street-food stand with vegan gyoza. It turned vegan in 2018. It is the gyoza pick on this list.

Order the gyoza, the ramen and the falafel, and come for lunch or dinner.
