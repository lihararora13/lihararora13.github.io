Kynd Baker, also listed as Loving Hut's artisan vegan bakery and cafe in Jakarta, is the city's sweetest vegan stop: a proper bakery turning out cakes, breads and desserts alongside pizzas, pasta, salads and smoothies, all fully vegan.

The room is bright, child-friendly and easy to linger in, with free Wi-Fi and card payments. Order a pizza or a pasta, then let the dessert case make the decisions.
