The Five Elements is Nashik's modern café entry: a Mahatma Nagar spot with a Continental-leaning menu, salads, pastas and grilled things, and a room that feels a world away from the city's thali halls. It is a good change of pace in the middle of a Nashik food crawl.

The vegan play is the pasta and salads, with the dairy held, and the grilled vegetable sandwich as backup. Ask what is plant-based on the day, because the menu leans seasonal.
