Pure Lotus is the reliable mid-range pick Beijing regulars keep coming back to: classic Chinese vegetarian cooking, a calm room, and a menu with plenty that is vegan or easily made so.

Order a spread of classics and ask the staff which dishes are fully vegan. It is a comfortable, unhurried meal after a day of sightseeing.
