Don Vegan is the one fully vegan restaurant in Quebec City, and it carries the whole scene on its shoulders with style. Open since 2019 in the Old Port, it serves an international menu with a full bar: carbonara pasta, miso ramen, beet tartare, almond ricotta ravioli, dumplings and a beyond burger, plus cashew raspberry and beet cheesecake for dessert.

Reviewers call it the best vegan restaurant in Quebec City and a must-visit for any vegan passing through. Book a table, order the beet tartare and the ramen, and save room for the cheesecake.
