vhy! is a fully vegan restaurant on Reinsburgstraße in the West, cooking European and German dishes plant-based: bao buns and gnocchi for the main, apfelstrudel and churros for dessert. It is the comfort-food pick on this page, and the room is relaxed enough for a long evening.

The gnocchi and the apfelstrudel are the orders. Everything is vegan, so the menu is safe to graze.
