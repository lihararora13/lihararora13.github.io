Hotel Pavner is the Rajarampuri favourite: a pure-veg restaurant in Appa's Complex that locals choose for an uncomplicated North Indian dinner. No themes, no fuss, just a dining room that has earned its regulars.

Order the dal tadka without ghee, a mixed veg sabzi and hot rotis, and you have a proper, cheap, satisfying meal. It is the quiet, reliable heart of this guide.
