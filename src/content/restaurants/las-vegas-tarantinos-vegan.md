Tarantino's Vegan opened right before the pandemic, the kind of timing that ends most restaurants, and the Vegas community simply refused to let it die. One forkful of pasta in lemon garlic butter explains why. This family-run spot makes everything in-house: sauces, cashew-based cheeses, dough, desserts.

The menu is classic Italian comfort done entirely plant-based: chicken-style parm, lasagna, meatball sandwiches, eggplant parm, plus a brunch service and dairy-free desserts like tiramisu. A 2026 ranking named it one of America's ten best vegan restaurants, and with a 4.8 Google rating from hundreds of reviewers, the locals agree.
