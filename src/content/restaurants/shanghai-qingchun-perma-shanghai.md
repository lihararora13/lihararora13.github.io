Qingchun Perma brings a refined, sustainability-focused approach to contemporary Chinese vegan cooking, and the Michelin Bib Gourmand agrees. The dim sum plates are the draw, delicate, inventive and entirely plant-based.

It is the best reason to cross the river to Pudong. Order the soup dumplings and the black truffle fried rice, and book ahead at weekends.
