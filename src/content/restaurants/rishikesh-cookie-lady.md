Cookie Lady is Rishikesh's only fully vegan kitchen and its most beloved: reported to be the first all-vegan place in the area when it opened in 2018, it still holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow, with a review updated in April 2026 praising the cheesecakes, the coconut caramel cake and the fully vegan protein shakes. The menu is small and made fresh daily, vegan cakes, muffins, scones, donuts, bagels and chocolates, plus breakfast plates and high tea, with soya, almond and oat milk for the coffees and hot chocolate.

Seating is just a handful of tables, so come early. The old Neelkanth Road location is permanently closed; the bakery now trades from Deccan Valley Gate on Badrinath Road in Tapovan, near the Green Hills Hotel. Order the blueberry cheesecake, the avocado toast with mushrooms and a green smoothie.
