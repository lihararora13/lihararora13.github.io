Why we love it — Chay Long is a Berlin institution for a reason. The
menu is entirely vegan and draws on traditional Vietnamese recipes done
right: fragrant pho brimming with herbs and tofu, fresh and crispy
spring rolls, coconut curries that cling to the spoon. Prices are
extremely reasonable for the quality, and the portions are generous
enough to make you rethink your plans for the afternoon.

It is a relaxed, no-frills kind of place that has become a regular haunt
for Berlin's vegan community. Go for lunch, order the tapas spread of
bao buns, summer rolls, and satay with peanut dip, and watch an hour
quietly become three.
