Dolcezza, also signed Dolcezza Vegan, is a vegan cafe and bakery in the Borgo district, an institution for locals since 1984. The tavola calda runs on vegan Sicilian pastries, arancini, cartocciate, pizzette and cipolline, with croissants, cinnamon rolls and iris pastries for breakfast, granita with brioche col tuppo in summer, and a dessert case of individual cakes and tiramisu cups.

Order the pistachio cannoli, a vegan arancino, the iris pastry with a cappuccino, and the granita with brioche in summer. Come early for the breakfast pastries, which sell out.
