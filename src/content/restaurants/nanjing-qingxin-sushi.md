Qingxin Sushi is the vegan buffet of the Confucius Temple: an all-you-can-eat spread of some 30 dishes, all vegan, in the basement of the Maoye mall at No. 2 Jiankang Road, for 26 yuan a head. It holds a 4.0 on HappyCow, with reviews from September 2026, and it is frequented mostly by locals.

Go early, as the dishes run down before closing and the food can sit cold late in service. It relocated from 199 Zhanyuan Road, so older guides point at the old address; the current spot is on the B2 floor of the Maoye mall, reached by the escalator near the crossroads. Open 11am to 2pm daily and 5pm to 8pm Monday to Saturday.
