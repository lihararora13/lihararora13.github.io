Somkid Vegetarian is a vegan jay buffet in Krabi Town, open mornings and lunch, and the plant-based anchor for the inland town. It serves the classic jay buffet: ready-cooked vegetable dishes, curries and tofu over rice, with no meat, no fish sauce and no pungent vegetables.

Come for lunch and fill a plate from the buffet, as the kitchen is a morning and lunch operation. Its pin is approximate to Krabi Town, so ask locally for the current location.
