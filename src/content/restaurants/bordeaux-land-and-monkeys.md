Land & Monkeys is the Bordeaux outpost of the Paris vegan bakery that vegans cross town for, and it brings the same promise: everything is plant-based, from the breads and croissants to the cakes, viennoiseries, sandwiches and salads. It is a bakery first and a café second, which means the coffee is good and the pastry case is the main event.

Come for breakfast, grab a croissant and a sandwich for the road, and stock up on bread. It is the simplest pleasure on this list, and one of the best.
