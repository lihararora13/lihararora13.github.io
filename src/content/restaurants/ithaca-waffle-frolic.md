Waffle Frolic is a waffle restaurant on East State Street in downtown Ithaca, doing vegan waffles and brunch plates. It is the breakfast pick on this list, and the vegan waffle with fruit is the headline order.

Order the vegan waffle with fruit toppings and a tofu scramble. Expect a weekend wait, since the room is popular for brunch.
