Jaa Nu' Rosticceria Vegana is a 100% vegan deli and eatery down a laneway in the historic centre, and it is Genoa's vegan anchor. The counter runs on Ligurian-inspired dishes: focaccia, tempeh, hummus, panini and seitan, with a vegan tiramisu that visitors in September 2025 ate with visible enthusiasm. Recent posts from the kitchen in October and November 2026 show the daily counter still going strong.

Order the focaccia, the tempeh plate, a panino and the vegan tiramisu. It is open Tuesday to Saturday, ten to seven, so plan it as a lunch stop rather than dinner.
