Veganika has been Ljubljana's vegan anchor since 2018, and it still tops the city's HappyCow list with a perfect 5.0 from hundreds of reviewers. The menu changes every two to three months with the seasons, built around local produce, house-made ferments and tofu made in the kitchen, and the room on Gornji trg is small, warm and worth booking for dinner.

Order whatever is seasonal, save room for a brownie or a cinnamon roll, and ask about the bio wines. This is the table to book when you want Ljubljana's vegan scene at its most thoughtful.
