Glas is Dublin's special-occasion vegan-friendly table. This intimate, completely gluten-free and plant-forward restaurant near Grafton Street earned Michelin recognition in 2022 for its innovative approach to vegetable cooking, and it has not slowed down since.

Book ahead and go for the three-course tasting menu, which leans hard into the seasons: saffron poached fennel in a chili broth, grilled beetroot with potato rosti, and whatever the kitchen is most excited about that week. Not every dish is vegan, so confirm the vegan menu when booking, but this is the room to choose when dinner should feel like an event.
