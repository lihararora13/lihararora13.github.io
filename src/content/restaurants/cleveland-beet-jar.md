Beet Jar is Ohio City's all-vegan juice bar and takeaway counter, and the Bravocado sandwich is still number one: a colourful, crunchy, creamy sandwich that regulars call the best vegan sandwich in town. The breakfast burrito is the morning move, the acai bowls are the summer move, and the cold-pressed juice is the always move.

It is a second counter inside Van Aken Market Hall in Shaker Heights now too, but the Ohio City original is the one to hit. Order the Bravocado, add a juice, and enjoy Hingetown.
