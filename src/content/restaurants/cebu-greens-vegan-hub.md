Greens Vegan Hub is the fun, filling stop on F. Ramos Street: a vegan restaurant doing a wide variety of Filipino plant-based dishes, a delicious mushroom burger and fries, and a vegan all-you-can-eat buffet on Sundays.

Order the mushroom sisig, fried lumpia and the mushroom bulaklak. Everything is tasty, and the Sunday buffet is a local institution.
