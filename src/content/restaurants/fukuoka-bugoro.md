Bugoro is the smallest room on this list, a vegan ramen counter with seating for only three guests, serving a fully vegan Hakata-style ramen as a set with a drink and dessert. It is the only other confirmed spot for vegan Hakata ramen in the city.

Arrive early or expect a wait. The ramen set is the whole order, and it is worth it.
