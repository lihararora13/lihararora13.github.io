Racines is a fully vegan, gluten-free cafe and take-away in the old town, open since 2016, serving vegan cuisine and cold-pressed juices that are 100% organic and vegan. It does sourdough pizza, vegan shepherd's pie, pancakes and cheesecake. It is the fully vegan pick on this list.

Order the sourdough pizza, the shepherd's pie with sunflower seeds and the vegan cheesecake, and come Tuesday to Saturday, since it closes Sundays and Mondays.
