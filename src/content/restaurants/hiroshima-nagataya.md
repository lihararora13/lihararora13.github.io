No trip to Hiroshima is complete without okonomiyaki, and Nagataya, at the end of the Hondori arcade directly next to Peace Park, is the place vegans go for it. The menu has a separate vegetarian and vegan section with three entirely plant-based versions of the layered savoury pancake, plus vegan gyoza as a starter, and the staff will cook with vegetable oil instead of lard and hand you the vegan sauces if you say you are vegan.

Expect a queue at peak hours: each pancake is cooked to order on the teppan in front of you, and it takes a while. Ask for the vegan English menu booklet, and note the house is closed on Tuesdays.
