Namma Illam is the heritage-themed veg cafe that a November 2026 visit documented in detail: a circular exterior sign, bright green digital menu boards, filter coffee in metal davara-tumbler sets, plain and masala dosa, medu vada with coconut chutney, and a butter-topped thattu idli with sambar, all under a large Kerala mural of Hindu deities.

Order the plain dosa and a medu vada, and drink the filter coffee the proper way. It is the prettiest veg room in Alleppey, and the mural alone is worth the visit.
