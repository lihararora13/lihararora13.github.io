The Vegan Chef is the name Durban vegans give first, and the Umhlanga branch at The Pearls Mall is its slickest outpost. Everything is fully plant-based and proudly indulgent: American and Indian comfort food side by side, from burgers and wraps to curries, poke bowls, cakes and thick milkshakes, with gluten-free options on the menu too. It is the rare vegan spot that works just as well for a quick mall lunch as for a proper sit-down dinner.

Come hungry and order across the menu: a burger for the table's sceptic, a curry for the spice lover, and a milkshake to finish. It is open seven days a week, takes cards, and does catering as well, so it doubles as the easy answer for group meals where not everyone eats the same way.
