Bep Chay Thien Binh is a casual central buffet on Nguyen Chi Thanh in Dalat, with rice, tofu, mushrooms and vegetable dishes. It is a straightforward, inexpensive lunch kitchen, and it fills a useful gap in the centre when the bigger buffets are busy.

Order a rice plate with tofu and mushrooms, and ask which dishes are vegan. Its pin is approximate to Nguyen Chi Thanh.
