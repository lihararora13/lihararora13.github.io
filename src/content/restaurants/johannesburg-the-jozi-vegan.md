The Jozi Vegan opened in early 2026 and has quickly become the darling of the Parkhurst strip, a vegan bakery and coffee shop with a focus on clean indulgence. It holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow, and its trick is inclusivity: gluten-free, soy-free and nut-free options without sacrificing taste, a menu that changes frequently, and baked goods that span pastries from all over the world.

It is airy and chic, with plenty of sidewalk seating for people-watching on one of Joburg's best cafe strips. Order the signature vegan omelette, made with a special mix of legumes, a milk tart, a croissant, and one of the savoury burgers if you are staying for lunch.
