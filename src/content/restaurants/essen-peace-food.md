Peace Food is a fully vegan Vietnamese kitchen on Huestraße in Schonnebeck, a small restaurant with a five-star following and a short menu done properly: gỏi cuốn, chả giò, satay, seitan skewers with lemongrass and tofu bowls with sesame. Everything is plant-based, the portions are generous, and the prices are the lowest on this list.

Two things to know before you go: it takes cash only, and it opens in the evenings on weekdays, from five until half past nine, with lunch service added at weekends from one. Come for an early dinner, order the seitan skewers, and bring euros.
