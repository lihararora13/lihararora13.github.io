Sen Vegan Cuisine is Bilk's Vietnamese vegan table: tapas, noodle bowls and sushi, all of it plant-based, in a calm and modern room a short walk from the centre. It is the kind of restaurant you can rely on for a good dinner any night, and the tapas are made for sharing.

Order a few tapas for the table, add a noodle bowl, and finish with sushi. Düsseldorf's Little Tokyo influence, served vegan.
