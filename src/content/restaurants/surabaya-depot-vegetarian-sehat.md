Depot Vegetarian Sehat, also called Sehat, is a vegan depot near Pasar Besar Wetan in central Surabaya, listed as vegan on HappyCow, serving Indonesian vegetarian dishes. It is a market-area kitchen, and it is one of the oldest vegan kitchens in the city.

Order the Indonesian vegetarian dishes and tempeh with rice, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. Its pin is approximate to Pasar Besar Wetan.
