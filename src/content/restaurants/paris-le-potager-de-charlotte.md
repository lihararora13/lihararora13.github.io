Le Potager de Charlotte is the name that tops nearly every best-of list
for a reason. Founded by brothers David and Adrien Valentin, it serves
gourmet, seasonal plant-based dishes in a warm dining room, with local
organic produce and a menu that actually changes with the market.

Sunday brunch here is a small event in itself, so reserve ahead. Come for
the polished French technique, stay for the dessert course that will make
you forget butter was ever a thing.
