Zodiac has been Essen's vegetarian restaurant since 1987, a Südviertel institution on Witteringstraße that was organic before organic was a selling point. The kitchen is vegetarian with a long vegan side: penne bolognese, lasagne, pizza and tofu dishes in vegan versions, cooked with organic ingredients and served in a quiet, old-fashioned dining room.

It is an evening place, open Wednesday to Saturday from six and Sundays from five, with Monday and Tuesday closed. Order the vegan penne bolognese, confirm the vegan preparation with the staff, and treat it as Essen's most traditional vegan-friendly table.
