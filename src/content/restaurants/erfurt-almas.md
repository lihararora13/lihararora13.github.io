ALMA's is Erfurt's Middle Eastern kitchen on Grafengasse, a warm old-town restaurant where the vegan dishes sit alongside the meat ones and hold their own. The plating runs to shawarma with hummus, cucumber-tomato salad and caramelised onions, and the kitchen is generous with the plant-based plates. It is open Tuesday to Saturday from noon to eleven, which makes it one of the few vegan-friendly tables in town that runs properly late.

Ask for the vegan dishes when you order, as the menu mixes meat and plant-based freely. Order a shawarma plate with hummus and salad, and take your time. This is the evening pick when the rest of the old town is winding down.
