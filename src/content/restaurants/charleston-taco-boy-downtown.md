Taco Boy is a Charleston-born taqueria group, and the downtown room on Huger Street is the one to visit: a large back patio, a kids menu, and a vegan bench that covers guacamole made from scratch, vegan black beans, build-your-own nachos and burritos, and the crispy avocado taco.

It is the most casual dinner on this list, and the best group table. Order the crispy avocado taco and a round of nachos for the table.
