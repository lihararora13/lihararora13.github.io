Poke All Day on Geumnam-ro in Dong-gu is a fast-casual Korean chain with customisable poke bowls on a vegan soba noodle or brown rice base, with tofu and mock meat as proteins and avocado, asparagus, radish, tomato and corn as add-ins, plus vegan sauces. It is open Monday to Saturday, closed Sundays.

Build a bowl on soba noodles with tofu and avocado, and ask for a vegan sauce. It is a useful central lunch stop, and its vegan labelling is clear.
