Stone Soup is Burlington's community cafe, a vegan buffet on College Street where the pay-by-weight hot bar does most of the talking. The vegan club and the seitan pot pie are the dishes regulars name first, and the soups, cauliflower cheddar dill among them, are made in-house.

It keeps shorter hours than the rest of this list, Tuesday to Saturday, so check the day. Come hungry and build your own plate.
