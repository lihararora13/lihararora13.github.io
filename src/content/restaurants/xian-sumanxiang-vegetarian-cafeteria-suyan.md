Sumanxiang Suyan is Xi'an's cheapest fully vegan meal: an all-you-can-eat buffet at Minleyuan, off Jiefang Road, with a huge choice of classic Chinese dishes, desserts and drinks for 21 RMB at dinner, about five euros for two. It is rated 4.5 on HappyCow, with reviews as recent as May 2026, and it was featured in HappyCow's November 2026 video on Xi'an as one of the city's standout vegan stops.

Come hungry and come early, and keep expectations honest: one recent review notes the food can be oily and of uneven quality, which is the trade-off at this price. The chain's website is sumanxiang.cc.
