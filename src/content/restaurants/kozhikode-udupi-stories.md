Udupi Stories is one of the newest veg-friendly openings in Kozhikode, featured in the September 2026 roundup of the city's new vegetarian cafes and restaurants. The storefront and the vada and chutney plates are the draw, with Mangalore buns, filter coffee and dosas on the menu board.

Order the Mangalore buns and a masala dosa, and ask for the dosa without ghee. It is the bypass stop for anyone driving into the city from the south.
