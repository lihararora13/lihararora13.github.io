If Slutty Vegan hasn't popped up on your feed by now, congratulations on living under a very thick rock. Pinky Cole's burger empire started right here on Edgewood and turned the whole South plant-curious, with lines out the door and menu names that make your grandma blush.

This is fast food with a wink: towering Impossible burgers, crispy plant-based chicken, and boozy Slutty Slushies that absolutely qualify as a meal-adjacent activity. The Edgewood flagship is the mothership, so come hungry, bring your loudest friends, and don't skimp on the Slut Sauce.
