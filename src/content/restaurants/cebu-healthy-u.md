Healthy U is the affordable vegan food stop Cebu locals recommend first: a vegetarian sattvic restaurant near Southwestern University, serving super-delicious and healthy food in a simple setting, and making its own tofu and soy milk.

It is cheap, cheerful and genuinely good. Order a tofu plate and a lemongrass dish.
