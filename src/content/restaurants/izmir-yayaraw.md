YayaRaw is a raw vegan kitchen in Alsancak specialising in raw food, including dehydrated crackers and raw cheesecakes and savoury dishes like zucchini spaghetti with avocado pesto, plus juices and smoothies. It is gluten-free and refined sugar-free, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, and open Monday to Friday until 6:30pm and Saturdays from 11:00am.

Order the zucchini spaghetti and a raw cheesecake. Note it was reported to no longer be fully vegan in August 2026, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly, not all-vegan.
