A Coruja calls itself the revolution of churrasco, and it earns the slogan: Brazil's beloved barbecue ritual, rebuilt entirely from plants, served all-you-can-eat style in Lapa. Think smoky grills, hearty regional sides and desserts to finish.

Go with a group, go hungry, and treat it as an event rather than a quick lunch. It is one of the most Sao Paulo experiences on this whole list.
