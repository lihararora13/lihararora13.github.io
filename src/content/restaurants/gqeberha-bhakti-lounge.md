Bhakti Lounge is Gqeberha's vegetarian and vegan lounge, an Indian-inspired room on Alexander Road serving curries, dhal and thali-style plates. It is the city's most reliable sit-down vegetarian meal, and most of the menu can be made vegan on request.

Order a vegetable curry with rice and ask for the vegan version of the thali. Confirm no ghee or dairy when you order, as the kitchen cooks vegetarian by default.
