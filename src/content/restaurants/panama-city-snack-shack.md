Snack Shack is the casual patio in Casco Antiguo, on Avenida A between Calles 4 and 5, with fresh bowls, juices cold-pressed every day and full-flavoured dishes. It is the friendliest menu in the old town for eating vegetarian without feeling like you are on a diet, and it serves all-day breakfast.

Order the vegetarian chili, the house's most popular dish, along with the quinoa burger, the avocado toast or the La Avenida salad, and a watermelon juice. It is moderately priced, open every day from 7:30 am to 9 pm, and worth booking on weekends. Note that fish dishes share the menu, so say you want the vegan build.
