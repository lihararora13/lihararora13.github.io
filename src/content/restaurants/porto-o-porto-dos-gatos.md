O Porto dos Gatos is a vegan cafe with rescue cats, and it is exactly as delightful as that sounds. Fully vegan since 2018, serving lunch plates, cakes and coffee in a room where the cats are available for adoption and very good at their jobs.

Order a lunch plate and a cake, and stay for the cats. It is the most charming stop on this list, and the food is genuinely good, not just a side note to the cats.
