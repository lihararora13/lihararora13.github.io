Arepas.World is a tiny arepa bar on the Untere Straße, doing one thing and doing it well: griddled corn cakes split and filled, with vegan fillings among the options and guasacaca, the Venezuelan avocado sauce, to finish. It is a counter rather than a restaurant, open seven days a week, and it fills the gap between a snack and a meal exactly the way a good street-food spot should.

Order a vegan arepa with plantain, add the guasacaca, and eat it standing in the old town or take it down to the Neckar. It is one of the cheapest lunches on this list and one of the most satisfying.
