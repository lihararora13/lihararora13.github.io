Abu Jbara is a vegetarian institution in Amman, serving traditional Jordanian food of which most is vegan. There is a large sit-down section and a takeaway counter, and the hours are famously long, often running to two in the morning. It is the reliable, everyday answer to eating veg in the city.

Order foul, falafel and hummus, and ask which dishes are vegan that day. It is the kind of place that has fed generations of Amman families.
