Hops Burger Bar is a burger bar on Spring Garden Street, with vegan-friendly burgers on a mixed menu. It is the second burger pick on this list, and the best place for a casual evening with a pint.

Order the vegan burger and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since the standard burger uses cheese or mayo.
