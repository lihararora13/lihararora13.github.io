Kartoffelkogge is a vegetarian restaurant in the KTV quarter with a focus on potato dishes, where the vegan items include kumpir, bowls, soups and dessert. The baked potatoes come with seitan bouletten on millet salad, barbecue tofu, hummus and baba ganoush, and it is rated 5 on HappyCow.

Order the kumpir, the seitan bouletten on millet salad and the barbecue tofu, and come for lunch or the Sunday afternoon service.
