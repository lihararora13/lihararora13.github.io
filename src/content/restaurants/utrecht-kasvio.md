Kasvio is a vegan restaurant with a Nordic-inspired menu, recently moved into a smaller, intimate space at Wittevrouwenstraat 22 in the heart of Utrecht. The cooking blends Nordic techniques with Dutch foraging traditions, using wild herbs from a regional garden and sourdough from a nearby bakery, with a charcoal-grilled lion's mane mushroom in seaweed-tomato sauce and a spinach pasta in wild-garlic oil and nettle dashi among the standouts.

It is open Wednesday to Sunday evenings only, with wine pairings and house-made kombucha and teas. Book ahead; this is the special-occasion pick in Utrecht.
