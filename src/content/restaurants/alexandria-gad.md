Gad is an Alexandria institution, a big multi-cuisine restaurant on El-Gaish Road by Stanley Bridge that stays open nearly round the clock. The vegan end of the menu is long: soup, hummus, tahini, babaganoush, falafel, fuul, dolma, fries and Neapolitan pasta.

Order falafel, fuul and hummus, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is the reliable 2am answer in the city, though parking on the main street is a known headache.
