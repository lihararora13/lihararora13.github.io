Casa Macro is the San Pedro stop on this list, a vegan restaurant in Del Valle cooking macrobiotic-inspired plates with a modern touch. It is worth the short ride from Centro for a calmer, more polished vegan dinner.

Order a macrobiotic bowl and a dessert. It is the most polished room on the Monterrey list, and a good pick for a proper night out.
