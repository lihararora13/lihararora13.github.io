The Grain Cafe is Long Beach's vegan living room: a fully vegan cafe on Retro Row with an all-day breakfast menu, Mexican-inspired burritos and burgers, and a cake case full of organic cakes. The Waffle Deluxe is the signature, the mole burrito is the sleeper, and the breakfast burrito is the reason to come before noon.

It is open from early morning to late night, which makes it the most useful restaurant on the list. Come for breakfast, stay for cake, and remember that Retro Row is the best street in Long Beach to wander after.
