Eisdiele Frohnatur is the sweet stop on this list: a young, owner-run, certified-organic ice cream parlour where every flavour is vegan and gluten-free, made from plant-based drinks with the flavouring ingredient front and centre. Pumpkin seed is the house favourite, the cones, waffles and sauces are homemade, and there are winter ice creams in the cold months, so this is not a summer-only address.

It sits at the city-centre end of Wilhelmshöher Allee, a few minutes from the Königsplatz. Order a scoop of the pumpkin seed, try whatever seasonal flavour is on the board, and take the walk up the avenue with a cone in hand.
