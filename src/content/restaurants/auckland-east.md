East is this guide's one vegan-friendly pick, and it earns the exception: an entirely vegetarian modern Asian dining room where roughly 75 percent of the menu is vegan, cooked by chefs drawing on Japan, China, Thailand, India and Sri Lanka, and recognised by the Michelin Guide for New Zealand.

Order to share and order plenty: sticky jackfruit bao, gochujang cauliflower with a proper kick, water chestnut wontons and a massaman curry for the table. If you can time it, Sunday brings a dedicated vegan yum cha from noon to 3.
