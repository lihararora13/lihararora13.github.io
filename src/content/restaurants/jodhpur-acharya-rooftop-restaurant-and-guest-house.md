Acharya is the old city's other rooftop: a vegetarian restaurant and guest house near Nagori Gate, with fort views and a calm, traveller-friendly room. It is the quieter alternative to the stepwell cafés, and a good place to eat slowly while the city turns blue in the evening light.

The North Indian veg mains are the move, with dal tadka and mixed veg sabzi leading, and the kitchen is used to vegan requests from its guest-house travellers. Ask for the ghee held and the meal turns plant-based.
