Tökmag has been doing vegan street food since 2017, and it shows in the confidence of the menu: burgers, sandwiches and soups, all fully plant-based, served fast and without fuss on Hollán Ernő utca. It holds a 4.5 from more than 200 HappyCow reviews, which is a lot of love for a small street-food counter.

This is the stop for when you want something quick, filling and a bit messy between sights, ideally eaten while still warm. It is also the rare Budapest vegan spot on the Buda side of the centre's orbit, in the likeable Újlipótváros neighbourhood.
