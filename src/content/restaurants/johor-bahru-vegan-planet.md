Vegan Planet is one of the few fully vegan restaurants in Johor Bahru, and Vegan Food Quest likes it for exactly that reason: in a city where most vegetarian rooms serve egg and dairy, here everything is vegan. The cooking is local-style, and the two dishes the guide names are the rendang, very good and full of flavour, and the laksa, one of the best they have had in Johor. It sits in Taman Mount Austin, the city's food hub, a short drive from the centre.

It is open Wednesday to Monday, 11am to 8:30pm, and closed on Tuesdays. The pin on this page is neighbourhood-level for Mount Austin, as the geocoder could not resolve the 8/6 street number, so follow the address and look for the shophouse row.
