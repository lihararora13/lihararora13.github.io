BareSoul is a vegetarian and vegan cafe in the old town, open weekday mornings to early afternoon, serving breakfast and lunch dishes in a small room near the university. It is the early-morning pick on this list.

Order the breakfast bowl, avocado toast and a coffee, and come before two, since it closes in the early afternoon.
