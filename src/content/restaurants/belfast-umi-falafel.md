Umi Falafel's Botanic Avenue branch is the quick, cheap, generous pick on this list: falafel platters with quinoa, hummus and salad, made fast and priced for students. It sits in the Queen's Quarter, a short walk from the university and the Botanic Gardens.

Order the falafel platter and a hummus side. It is a vegetarian chain with a deep vegan bench, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it is the best lunch stop between the Ulster Museum and the city centre.
