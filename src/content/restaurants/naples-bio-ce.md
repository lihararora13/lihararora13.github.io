Bio C'e is an organic vegetarian and vegan restaurant and grocery at Piazza dell'Immacolata, with a second counter at Piazza Dante, sourcing directly from local organic producers. The offer is simple: cooked vegetables, salads and takeaway dishes, with a small grocery alongside.

It is more deli than restaurant, so come for a light lunch or to stock up rather than a full dinner. The produce is the point here, and the cooked vegetables of the day are the best order.
