Moonrise Comida Vegana is a fully vegan mexican spot in Zona 4. Moonrise is a fully vegan kitchen on the pedestrian street in Zona 4, in front of the TEC, cooking vegan tacos al pastor, churrasco and Cali-style burritos.

Order the Tacos al pastor veganos, Churrasco vegano, Burrito estilo Cali, and Kombucha. The tacos al pastor are the signature; the churrasco is the second plate.
