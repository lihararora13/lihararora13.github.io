Nu Kitchen is a health-food cafe on Chandler Street, doing bowls, salads, and sandwiches with a strong vegan bench. It is the lighter lunch pick on this list, and the best stop for a quick, customizable meal.

Order the vegan bowl and a smoothie. Ask the staff to confirm the dressing is vegan, since a few contain honey or dairy.
