Happy Veggie sits at the very top of HappyCow's Budapest ranking with a perfect 5.0, and it earns it the old-fashioned way: an all-vegan Chinese kitchen near Keleti station doing stir-fried noodle dishes, rice with vegetables, dumplings, gyoza, Chinese pancakes, soup and bubble tea, all in a calm, peaceful dining room. It was reported fully vegan in March 2026 and has held the top spot since.

After a run of heavy Hungarian meals, this is the lighter, brighter counterpoint: proper comfort food with onion- and garlic-free options, plant-based milks for the bubble tea, and dumplings that even meat-eaters rave about. It is open daily from 10am to 8pm, so it works for an early dinner before a train.
