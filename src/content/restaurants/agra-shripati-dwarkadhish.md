Shripati Dwarkadhish is one of Agra's top-rated vegan restaurants by Google reviews, a vegetarian restaurant on Kariappa Road doing North Indian thalis and plates for the Taj crowds.

Order the veg thali without ghee and the dal tadka. It is a solid, no-fuss table, and a good one when the South Gate lanes are packed.
