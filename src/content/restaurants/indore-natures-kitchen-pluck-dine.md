Nature's Kitchen is the closest thing Indore has to a farm on a plate. The restaurant grows its own organic vegetables on site, and the signature move is exactly what the name promises: you wander the garden, pluck what catches your eye, and hand it to the chef, who cooks it into your meal. It is a vegetarian kitchen rather than a strictly vegan one, so ask for dishes without ghee, butter, curd or paneer, and the kitchen is used to the request.

Come for the novelty and stay for the freshness. The salads and seasonal vegetable mains taste like they were picked an hour ago, because they were, and the calm garden setting is a world away from Indore's gloriously chaotic street-food lanes. Book ahead if you can, and bring an appetite for whatever the beds are offering that day.
