Little World Kitchen is a 100% plant-based lunchroom and catering kitchen in a former concierge house on Vondellaan, run by Brechje van der Zwan, who returned from running a Little World cafe in India. The cooking is colourful and Indian-inspired: tiffins, dosa masala, puri bhaji, cheese naan and masala chai lattes, made from scratch.

It is open Wednesday to Saturday, daytime only, so plan it as a lunch stop. Order the tiffin and a dosa masala.
