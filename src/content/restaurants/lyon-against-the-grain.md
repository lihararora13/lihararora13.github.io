Against the Grain is Lyon's vegan sweet tooth, a tea room and canteen where the donuts are the headline and the savoury lunch is the bonus. Crème brûlée donuts, cinnamon rolls, pumpkin spice lattes: everything is plant-based, everything is homemade, and the little Guillotière room fills up fast on Saturdays.

It is open for lunch and afternoons, Tuesday to Saturday, so time your visit for a mid-afternoon sugar stop. Order a donut, take a second one for the road, and do not pretend you were only passing by.
