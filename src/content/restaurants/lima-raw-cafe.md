Raw Cafe is the name at the very top of HappyCow's Lima ranking, and it earns the spot the friendly way: a small all-vegan restaurant and organic store that has been pioneering plant-based food in Miraflores for years. The menu covers breakfast, lunch, tea time and early dinner, from raw food and crackers to pizzas, burgers and a dessert case full of raw cakes, while the shop shelves carry Peruvian superfoods like spirulina, camu camu, cacao nibs, maca and yacon syrup.

Come for breakfast or a long lazy lunch, because this is the rare vegan cafe that works equally well as a healthy pit stop and a proper sit-down meal. The portions are generous, the atmosphere is warm and unpretentious, and the raw cakes alone are worth the walk from Parque Kennedy.
