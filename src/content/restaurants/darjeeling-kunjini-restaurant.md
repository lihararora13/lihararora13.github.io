Kunjini is a small veg restaurant on Laden La Road below the State Bank of India, open 11 to 9 daily. It is a quiet, central option for thalis and North Indian food, a short walk from Chowrasta and the Mall.

Order the veg thali and ask for no ghee and no paneer. It is not a destination, but it is a dependable veg room in the town centre, and in winter, when some cafes cut their hours, that reliability matters.
