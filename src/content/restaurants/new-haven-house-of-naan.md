House of Naan is an Indian restaurant on Howe Street in downtown New Haven with curries, dals, and breads that adapt well to vegan orders. It is a neighborhood lunch stop, and the chana masala is the reliable pick.

Order the chana masala and dal tadka with vegan roti, and confirm which breads are dairy-free, since most naan is brushed with butter or ghee.
