Soul Kitchen is a vegan, raw vegan and vegetarian restaurant in the historic centre, and the most ambitious vegan-friendly kitchen in Genoa. The menu follows the seasons and is produced in-house: seitan cutlets with kimchi mayonnaise, seitan braised in Barbera with polenta, lapsang-marinated tempeh and smoked tofu bao in tempura.

Order the seitan cutlet with kimchi mayonnaise, the brasato di seitan with polenta, and the bao with smoked tofu. It serves lunch and dinner Wednesday to Sunday, and the room is small, so book on weekends.
