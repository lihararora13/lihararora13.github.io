Croustille is a cute little vegan cafe on Rue Varsovie in Saint-Cyprien, a short walk from Pont Neuf bridge, rated 4.0 on HappyCow. The food is wholesome and tasty, and the style and feel of the place are part of the draw, with a small, warm room and a short menu.

Note the limited hours: weekdays between 11:00 and 15:00. It is a lunch-only spot, so plan it as a midday stop, and order the daily plate.
