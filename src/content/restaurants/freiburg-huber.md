Huber is a café unlike any other in Freiburg: part coffee house, part comic shop, part ceramics gallery, where you drink your coffee at a high wooden table with a graphic novel in one hand and a piece of homemade cake in the other. The food is simple and organic, soup, quiche, filled rolls and cake, with vegan options, and the room invites you to linger, browse the shelves, and take your time. It is the most Freiburg café on this list, in the best sense.

It sits on the Wentzingerstraße and keeps short daytime hours, Wednesday to Saturday with a Sunday morning opening. Order the soup and a slice of cake, buy a comic on the way out, and treat it as a morning rather than a meal.
