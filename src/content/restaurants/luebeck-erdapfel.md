Erdapfel is a baked-potato café right on the Markt, and it is the trickiest pick on Lübeck's list. Every potato variation can be made vegan on request, and the location could not be better. But the vegan handling is sloppy: multiple reviews report being served cream or dairy after ordering vegan, the vegan labelling is poor, and the place now serves meat as well, despite once being listed as vegetarian.

It stays on the list because the veganised potatoes are good when they arrive as ordered, and the Markt location is genuinely useful. Say vegan clearly, repeat it, and double-check what arrives before you eat. This is the table to approach with your eyes open, not the one to trust blindly.
