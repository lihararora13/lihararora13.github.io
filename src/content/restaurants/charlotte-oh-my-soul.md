Oh My Soul is the restaurant Charlotte vegans name first, and one look at the menu explains why. It runs from French toast and waffles to mozzarella stix, nachos, BBQ seitan ribs and boozy milkshakes, with South African specials nodding to owner Tallulah Duffin's homeland: the Big Ben stacks crispy chick'n on a waffle base, the Makhulu Vurger brings house-made boerewors-style patties, and the Aikona Waffles are the brunch move. The patio under the old oak tree is one of NoDa's happiest rooms.

It is also the rare vegan spot that doubles as a proper night out: a full bar, vegan cocktails and wine, live music, and a dessert case piled with cheesecake, cupcakes and banana bread. Save space, bring friends, and order more than you think you need.
