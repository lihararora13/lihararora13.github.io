Snoogies is a 100% vegan cafe in Mainstreet Arcade on Grafton Street in Cairns, open Monday to Friday from 9am to 2pm, serving vegan cafe dishes. It is a weekday lunch kitchen, and it is the best vegan lunch in the centre on a weekday.

Order the vegan cafe dishes and a coffee, and come at lunch, when the kitchen is at its fullest. It is central and reliable.
