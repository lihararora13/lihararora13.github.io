Why we love it — Blu Bar is the Poblenou living room every beach
day deserves: nautical decor, cocktails, and a fully vegan menu that
omnivores order without noticing anything is missing. The pizza oven
runs overtime, the buns are baked to order from pizza dough, and the
cashew-mozzarella game is genuinely excellent. Tripadvisor once ranked
it among the top ten restaurants in all of Barcelona, vegan or not.

Go for the terrace, stay for the carbs. The Margherita with garlic and
arugula pesto is a classic for a reason, the pulled pork sandwich is a
beautiful mess, and the tiramisú closes the deal. Eat, drink, chill,
surf.
