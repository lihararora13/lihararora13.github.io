Juicy Brew is the Beretania juice bar that Honolulu vegans swear by: fully vegan, doing tofu musubi, mochi, fresh juices and soy and almond milk lattes from a bright little room in the Hale Pawa'a medical building. The tofu musubi is the cult item, and the juices are the reason to come back.

It is a daytime spot with a rotating daily menu, so the selection shifts. Order the musubi, grab a juice, and enjoy the most refreshing stop on the list.
