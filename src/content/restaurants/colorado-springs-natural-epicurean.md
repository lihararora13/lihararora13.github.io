Natural Epicurean is a natural-foods cafe on Lake Avenue near the Broadmoor, serving smoothies, bowls, sandwiches, and croissants with a vegan bench. It is the lightest meal on this list, and the best stop for breakfast or a post-hike lunch.

Order the smoothie bowl and a vegan sandwich. Ask the staff to confirm the vegan preparation, since some bowls are finished with honey or yogurt.
