Not Just Snacks is the little East Side cafe that rounds out a Hope Street vegan crawl. It sits a few doors from Like No Udder and the Vegan Deli, serving vegan baked goods, sandwiches and coffee in a friendly neighbourhood room.

Stop in for a cookie and an oat milk latte between the bigger meals. It is the kind of place that makes a vegan day in Providence feel effortless.
