The Vegan Table has been Phuket Town's vegan anchor since 2018, and with 293 HappyCow reviews it is the most-reviewed vegan restaurant on the island. The menu is wide: smoked tofu summer rolls, fried mushrooms, sourdough toast with avocado and mushrooms in balsamic reduction, soup in a bread bowl, pizza with vegan cheese, a full English breakfast, a burger with fries, nut roast and a granola bowl with fresh fruit, plus fresh juices and dessert cheesecakes. The dining room is air-conditioned, which matters in Phuket Town at midday.

It was reported under new ownership in June 2026, so the kitchen may still be settling in, but the listing remains live and well reviewed. A 2026 guide calls it Phuket's most reliable fully vegan restaurant and gives Thursday to Tuesday, 1pm to 10pm, so check the current hours before you go.
