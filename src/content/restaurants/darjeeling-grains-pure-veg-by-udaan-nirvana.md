Grains by Udaan Nirvana is the Mall branch of Darjeeling's pure-veg chain, below Chowrasta on C.R. Das Road, which makes it the most central veg dining room in town. It runs the same broad pure-veg menu as its Hill Cart Road sister, open 7:30 to 10:30 daily.

Order the veg thali or the veg noodles, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you have a plant-based lunch steps from Chowrasta. It is the easiest veg meal in Darjeeling to find.
