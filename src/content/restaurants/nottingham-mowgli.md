Mowgli's Nottingham branch sits on Stoney Street in Hockley, and it keeps a beautiful separate vegan menu alongside the main one, with veg-packed curries, tiffin boxes and street-food small plates. The room is warm and busy, and the vegan menu is gluten-free friendly too.

Order a tiffin box to try a spread, and add the fenugreek kissed fries. It is a chain with a meat menu, so file it as vegan-friendly, but the dedicated vegan card is one of the best of its kind in the city.
