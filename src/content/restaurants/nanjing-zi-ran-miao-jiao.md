Zi Ran Miao Jiao is the organic buffet near Mochou Lake: a vegetarian buffet restaurant at 329 Mochou Road, formerly known as Qing Xin Organic Vegetarian, offering vegan-friendly Chinese dishes of tofu, vegetables, soups, dessert and fruit. It holds a 4.0 on HappyCow, and a September 2026 Nanjing guide names it as one of the city's two dedicated plant-based venues.

Order the dumplings with vegan meat filling, which one reviewer calls the best thing they ate in China, plus the fish imitates and a tofu dish. The restaurant can be hard to spot from the street, so use the exact address. Open daily 10:30am to 8:30pm.
