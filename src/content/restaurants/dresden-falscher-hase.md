Falscher Hase, the false hare, was Dresden's first vegan restaurant, and it still cooks like a place with something to prove. The menu leans German and European: seitan burgers, soups, pasta, and a rotating choice of mains and desserts that changes daily, with local beer and wine to match. The room doubles as an art gallery, with exhibitions, occasional live music and a reading corner, so dinner here feels more like an evening out than a quick bite.

It opens in the late afternoon and runs into the evening, closed on Tuesdays, which makes it a dinner pick rather than a lunch stop. Order the seitan burger if it is on, ask what the rotating main is, and stay for dessert. This is the table for a slow evening in the Neustadt.
