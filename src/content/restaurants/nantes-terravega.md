TerraVega is Nantes' everyday vegan canteen: fully vegan, with a raw and gluten-free bent, serving bowls, tartines and salads that are generous rather than austere. The Bowl délicieux and the Tartine et Salade gourmande are the regulars' orders, and the house terrine végétale and the Fogra, a plant-based foie gras, show the kitchen likes a challenge.

Note the address: TerraVega has a second listing at 2 Rue Mathelin Rodier, but the restaurant is the one at 22 Rue Baron. Prices are the kindest on this page, making it the best lunch stop in the city.
