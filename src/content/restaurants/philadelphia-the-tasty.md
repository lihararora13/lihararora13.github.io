The Tasty is South Philly's answer to the question of whether a diner can be all-vegan and still nail breakfast: yes, loudly, with 654 reviewers at 4.7 stars backing it up. Buffalo wingz, deluxe burritos loaded with tots, tofu scrambles, and blueberry pancakes cover every diner craving.

It is the perfect bookend to a Philly vegan weekend: fancy tasting menus at the top of the list, and a plate of pancakes at the bottom that tastes like Sunday morning should. Cash-strapped travelers, this one is for you.
