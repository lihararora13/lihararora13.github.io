Oowee Vegan brings its Bristol-born junk-food formula to Market Street in the Lanes, and Brighton laps it up. The menu is fully vegan American diner food: smash burgers, wraps, loaded fries, onion rings, fried chickn strips, buffalo cauliflower wings and milkshakes, with seitan made in-house and outdoor seating for sunny days.

It is casual, no-reservations and open late, which makes it the perfect end to a day by the sea. Order a smash burger and the buffalo cauliflower wings, add a shake, and walk it off along the seafront.
