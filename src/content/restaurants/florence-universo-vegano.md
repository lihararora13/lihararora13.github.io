Universo Vegano is the fully vegan fast-food-style restaurant near the Ponte Vecchio, on Via delle Terme in the Centro Storico. Burgers, wraps and bowls are the menu, all plant-based, with mains at about eight to twelve euros, and it is open daily.

It is the quick central vegan meal in Florence: fast, cheap and fully vegan, a few minutes from the river.
