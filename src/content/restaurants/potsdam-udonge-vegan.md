Udonge Vegan is Potsdam's fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant, a calm and elegant room on Lindenstrasse where the cooking is organic, additive-free and entirely plant-based. The signatures are a tofu and vegetable stir-fry served in a matte black clay pot, a fragrant orange coconut-curry soup with dumplings, and a colourful green papaya salad with peanuts and fried wontons. The room, velvet booths, marble tables and warm light, is as considered as the plates.

It opens for lunch and dinner most days, closed Tuesdays, and does not take reservations for small groups, so arrive early at peak times. Order the stir-fry and the soup, share the papaya salad, and linger. This is the table to take someone who thinks vegan food cannot be elegant.
