Why we love it — Mallow was created by the Mildreds team as their
playful younger sibling, and the family resemblance shows. The Borough
Market room runs on fresh, market-driven, sustainable small plates,
and the brunch has developed a small cult following of its own, with
tiramisu french toast and a beetroot tempeh smash burger leading the
charge.

It is the perfect Borough Market pit stop: colourful, generous,
unfussy, and fully plant-based. If Mildreds is the institution, Mallow
is the institution having more fun. Desserts are non-negotiable here.
