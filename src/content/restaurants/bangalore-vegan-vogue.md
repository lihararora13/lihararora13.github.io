Vegan Vogue is the new star of Bangalore's vegan scene: a fully plant-based restaurant on 80 Feet Road in Indiranagar, rated 4.6 on Swiggy and 4.4 from over 2,000 Google-era ratings, with a menu that refuses to sit still. Italian, Mexican, Rwandan and Indonesian dishes share one kitchen, the noodles and pastas are handmade, the microgreens are grown in-house, and even the menu is printed on biodegradable paper. The owner's pitch is simple: vegan food should be fun and accessible, not a lecture.

The signatures are the beetroot and cashew croquettes and the zucchini fritters to start, then the pesto linguine or the mushroom risotto, and if you're feeling adventurous the Rwandan agatogo with seared plantains. Finish with the vegan chocolate mousse and a fresh-pressed pineapple, green apple and mint juice. Prices sit at the premium end for Indiranagar, but this is the table to book when you want to show a sceptic what vegan food can do.
