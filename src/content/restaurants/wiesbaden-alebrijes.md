Alebrijes is the first fully vegan cafe, restaurant and bar in Wiesbaden, on Wilhelmstrasse, specialising in traditional Latin American dishes, drinks and vibes. The menu runs from breakfast pancakes, chilaquiles and scrambled veggs to pozole, elote, nachos, yuca frita and tacos of nopales, birria, al pastor and mole, plus enchiladas, arepas and empanadas. It is cash only and rated 4.5 on HappyCow.

Order the tacos, the pozole and the chilaquiles, and come in the evening Tuesday to Friday or for a long Saturday lunch. It closes on Mondays.
