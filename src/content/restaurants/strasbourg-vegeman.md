Végéman is Strasbourg's fully vegan kebab counter, on Rue des Bateliers in Petite France, and it is the table the city's vegans name first. The seitan kebab is the signature, with dürüm, burgers and cheezy sticks alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it built for a proper feed after a walk along the canals.

It is fast food done with care, and the kebab is the order. Everything on the menu is vegan, so there is nothing to negotiate.
