Köstlich Vegan is an all-vegan fast-food restaurant on the Lange Gasse, in the heart of the old town, doing köfte, börek, falafel and hummus at counter prices. It is open Monday to Saturday, half past ten to nine, and it fills the same role as the best fast-food counters do: quick, cheap, generous, and entirely plant-based. For a student town, it is exactly the right restaurant.

Order a köfte plate, add falafel and hummus, and treat it as the fast lunch that happens to be fully vegan.
