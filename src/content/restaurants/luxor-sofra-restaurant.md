Sofra is a long-running East Bank restaurant serving Egyptian and Middle Eastern food in a traditional dining room. It is not a vegan restaurant, but the Egyptian end of the menu, ful, falafel, salads and vegetable dishes, is naturally plant-based.

Order ful, falafel and a vegetable tagine, and ask for no ghee. It is the dependable East Bank dinner when Aroma's rooftop is full.
