Green Roll proves that vegan sushi is not a compromise, it is a cuisine of its own. Since 2014 this little spot near Allenby has been rolling fresh, creative sushi with no fish in sight, and reviewers keep coming back for the variety: rolls of every persuasion, plus egg rolls, salads, dumplings and dim sum.

It is affordable, unfussy and generous, the kind of place where a combination plate lets you taste half the menu in one sitting. Order a platter if you are with friends, and save room for a vegan dessert.
