Cantina NCL is a contemporary Mexican restaurant in Heaton that runs a fully plant-based kitchen, and almost every dish on the menu can be made vegan and gluten free, with a nut-free kitchen too. The room is warm wood and soft light, the kind of place built for date nights.

Order the blue-tortilla tacos and the tempura cauliflower, then the chocolate cake. Vegans, coeliacs and nut-allergy diners all eat from the same menu here, which is rare enough to be worth the bus ride out to Heaton.
