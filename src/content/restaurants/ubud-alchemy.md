Alchemy is the pioneer that started Bali's raw food scene. Open since 2011 in Penestanan, it is the island's first 100% raw, organic and vegan restaurant, a sunlit white sanctuary with an iconic self-serve salad bar, fresh greens, cashew cheeses and homemade dressings. The menu runs from laksa and raw pizzas to waffles, smoothie bowls and a raw chocolate pie, and the little market by the door sells raw, organic and vegan goods to take home.

It opens daily from 7am to 9pm. Walk uphill from the Blanco Museum and look for it on the left, and bring an appetite for the salad bar, it is the main event.
