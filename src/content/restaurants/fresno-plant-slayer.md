Plant Slayer is a fully vegan comfort-food restaurant inside the Galleria at Civic Center in downtown Fresno, open for four years and serving 100 percent plant-based versions of burgers, sandwiches, animal-style fries, and milkshakes. The room is industrial, with a giant skeleton greeting diners on the patio, and orders go in on a self-service kiosk.

Order the Campfire Burger on a pretzel bun and the animal-style fries. Note the split hours: lunch, then a dinner window on some days, so check the board before an evening trip.
