dean and david is the salad, bowl and juice chain on the Neuer Markt, open daily, with hot wraps, sandwiches, bowls and smoothies, several of them vegan. It is the reliable centre-square stop when the smaller vegan rooms are closed.

Order a bowl, a hot wrap and a smoothie, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan options, since the menu also runs dairy and egg.
