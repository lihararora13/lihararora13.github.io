Palmera is a vegetarian restaurant and grocery on Garibaldi, in the centre, serving lunch plates on weekday lunchtimes, 11:30 am to 2:30 pm. The attached store sells natural products, and the kitchen is a straightforward lunch counter rather than a destination.

Order a lunch plate and a salad, and ask which dishes are vegan. It is a useful fallback when the more famous kitchens are closed.
