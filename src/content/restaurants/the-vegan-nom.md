The Vegan Nom was doing all-vegan tacos out of a trailer on Cesar
Chavez when most of Austin still thought "vegan taco" was a prank.
Thirteen years later the lines are still long, the breakfast tacos
still sell out, and the Birdie Sanders still converts carnivores by
the dozen. It is cheap, fast, and absurdly good.

Come hungry and order more than you think you need. Nobody in
Austin regrets a second taco, and the tempeh bacon has started more
arguments about "wait, this is vegan?" than any brunch in the city.
