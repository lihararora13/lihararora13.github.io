Non Viet Vegan is Edinburgh's fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant, trading at 23 Brougham Place under its current name after starting life as Sen Viet Vegan. The menu is the classic Vietnamese spread, done entirely plant-based: special pho, tofu summer rolls, crispy spring rolls, Hue spicy noodle soup, stir-fried noodles and vegetables, and a hotpot for sharing. It is the only fully vegan Vietnamese table in the city, and the aromas alone make choosing difficult.

It sits a short walk from the Meadows, open for lunch and dinner every day. Order the special pho if it is your first visit and add the tofu summer rolls. This is the table for a steaming bowl of noodles on a grey Edinburgh day.
