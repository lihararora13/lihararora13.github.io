King's Joy is the jewel of Beijing's plant-based scene: a serene courtyard restaurant across from the Lama Temple, holding two Michelin stars and a Green Star for its organic, seasonal cooking. The tasting menus work remarkable depth out of mushrooms and seasonal vegetables.

Book ahead and ask for the vegan dishes when you reserve. This is the splurge of the list, and the truffle soup dumpling is the dish people talk about for years.
