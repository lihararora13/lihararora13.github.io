Curry n Jerk is an authentic Caribbean counter in downtown Memphis, and its vegan-friendly bench is deep: jerk tofu, curry vegetables, rice and peas, and plantains, all cooked the way the cuisine intends. It is the most flavour-forward meal on this list after Plant Based Heat.

Order the jerk tofu with rice and peas, and add the plantains.
