RKs is the quick stop in Umhlanga Ridge: a veg fast-food counter doing burgers, wraps and curries at prices that make it an everyday lunch rather than a treat. The menu is vegetarian with vegan options throughout, and it is open daily from morning to early evening.

Use it for a fast, cheap plant-based lunch between the mall and the beach. The veg burger and the vegetable curry are the reliable orders.
