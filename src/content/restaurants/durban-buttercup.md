Buttercup is a Durban institution built on one glorious idea: vegan food that tastes like the fast food everyone grew up with. The headline is KFV, seitan fried up KFC-style and served with mash and gravy, alongside tikka burgers, BBQ chops and roti wraps. It is cheap, cheerful and unapologetically comfort-first, and locals swear by it.

The Greyville shop is the original, a short hop from the city centre, with a second branch in Umhlanga for the north coast. Order the KFV rounder if it is your first visit, add the tikka meal if you like heat, and do not skip the mash and gravy.
