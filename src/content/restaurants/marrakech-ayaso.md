Ayaso sits on Place Rahba Kedima, the spice square, with three floors and a rooftop made for long lunches between the souks. The kitchen is farm-to-table and organic, with vegan breakfasts, granola and plant milk coffees in the morning, and vegetable tajine, couscous and curry later. It is open daily from late morning to ten.

Go for the rooftop, stay for the tajine. The bissara here is a good benchmark, and the rooftop mint tea at sunset is the Marrakech moment.
