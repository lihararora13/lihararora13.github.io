The Jalandhar Vegetarian at Ishmeet Singh Chowk is a multi-cuisine pure-veg restaurant that has been serving affordable buffets since 2016. Dal makhani, chana masala, kadhai paneer, soya chaap gravy and Punjabi kadhi fill the menu, and the buffet is the value play.

Ask for the dal makhani without cream and the soya chaap gravy as it comes. It is family dining at its most generous.
