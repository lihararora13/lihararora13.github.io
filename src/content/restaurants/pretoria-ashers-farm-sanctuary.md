Ashers Farm Sanctuary is the one place in Pretoria where everything is vegan, a farm sanctuary open to the public with a fully vegan restaurant alongside a shop selling vegan products. The menu is simple and comforting, toasted sandwiches, burgers and desserts, and the setting is the point: you eat surrounded by rescued animals on a plot out in Boschkop, Pretoria East.

It is open Wednesday to Saturday, 9:00am to 3:00pm, so plan it as a weekend outing rather than a weeknight dinner. Order a toasted sandwich and a burger, leave room for dessert, and allow time to walk around the sanctuary after lunch.
