Baobab Wholefood Cafe grew out of a juice and smoothie bar and is now the Eastern Cape's best-known fully vegan cafe, with a menu built around whole foods rather than mock meats. The much-loved macadamia and amaranth porridge leads a short list of wraps, toasted sandwiches and smoothies, and the room is bright, unpretentious and easy to linger in.

Order the porridge if it is your first visit, then a wrap and a smoothie. It sits on Main Road in Walmer, an easy stop between the beachfront and the city centre, and it is the safest first-night choice in Gqeberha.
