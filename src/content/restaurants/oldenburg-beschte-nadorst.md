BESCHTE! Nadorst is a döner and fast-food counter up the Nadorster Straße, and it is the quick vegan-friendly stop on Oldenburg's list: vegan döner, burgers and fries, served daily until nine. It is a fast-food room rather than a restaurant, and the vegan offering is a real part of the menu rather than a single token item.

It sits in Donnerschwee, a short walk north of the city centre. Order a vegan döner with fries, add a burger if you are still hungry, and enjoy a menu where the vegan options are taken seriously. This is the table for a fast, cheap vegan meal in Oldenburg.
