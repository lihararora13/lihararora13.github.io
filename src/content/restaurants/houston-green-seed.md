Green Seed Vegan has been holding down the corner of Almeda and Wheeler since long before vegan went mainstream. It started as a food truck with a legion of loyal fans and grew into a cozy cafe with a raw bar, an elixir bar, and a cold-pressed juice program. The whole menu is soy-free and gluten-free-friendly, with everything wrappable in collard greens or servable over microgreens.

The buckwheat-quinoa burger with jalapeno, the Porta-panini, and the dill sweet potato fries are the greatest hits. It is the kind of place where regulars have a four-year relationship with the food and the food is still getting better.
