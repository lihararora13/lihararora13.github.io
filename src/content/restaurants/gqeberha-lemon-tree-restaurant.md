The Lemon Tree is a relaxed, sunny restaurant with a wooden deck and garden seating, and its chalkboard regularly features a vegetarian quiche with salad. It is a meat-serving room, so vegans are ordering from the vegetarian end of the menu rather than a dedicated plant-based list.

Ask for the vegetarian quiche and check it is made without egg or dairy that day, or fall back on the salads and flatbreads. Come for the deck and the sea air as much as the food.
