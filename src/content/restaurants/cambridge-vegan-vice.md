Vegan Vice is Cambridge's other all-vegan burger joint, on East Road near the Grafton Centre, part of a small chain with a second site elsewhere in the country. The formula is burgers, loaded fries, shakes and desserts, all plant-based, all generous.

Order a burger with loaded fries and a shake. It sits a short walk from Thrive, so the two make a natural pair for a Norfolk Street and East Road crawl.
