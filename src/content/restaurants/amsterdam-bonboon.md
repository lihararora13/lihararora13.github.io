Bonboon is where you go when you want vegan food to feel like an occasion. Tucked inside Hotel Mercier in the Jordaan, this intimate dining room serves a chef's tasting menu that changes every eight weeks with the seasons. Expect dishes like Thai-inspired potato and aubergine terrine, plated with a serious flair.

The five-course menu plus an optional wine flight makes for a genuinely romantic, grown-up evening out. Reservations are required, so plan ahead and let the kitchen show off.
