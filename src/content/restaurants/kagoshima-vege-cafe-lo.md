Vege Cafe Lo is a vegetable cafe on the ground floor of the Meizan Building, in the Tenmonkan area, describing itself as a place for people too busy to eat well. The menu runs to warm vegetables, vegetable salads and Japanese-style, tomato and lemon-cream pastas, with lunch from 9am to 3pm and dinner from 5pm to 10pm. It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so ask what is vegan on the day.

The warm vegetable plate and the tomato pasta are the safest vegan choices. It is a useful central stop, open most of the day, and the room is small and friendly rather than formal.
