The Planters is the fully vegan sister restaurant of Plant-erian, closer to Ao Nang beach on Ao Nang Road, with almost the same menu. Vegan travel site Veggies Abroad called it the best vegan spot in Ao Nang in 2026 and singles out the khao soi and a breakfast sandwich with tofu scramble and avocado.

Order the khao soi and the breakfast sandwich, and come for breakfast or lunch, when the beach is at its best. It is the closest fully vegan kitchen to the sand, and its pin is approximate to Ao Nang Road.
