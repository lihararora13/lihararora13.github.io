Tsan The has been serving vegetarian food in downtown Floripa since 2003, and it is a local institution: an ovo-lacto vegetarian restaurant of Taiwanese origin, with various vegan and gluten-free plates, no garlic or onion, and a 4.9 rating from reviewers. The food is nourishing, varied and economical, with a concept of vegetarianism all its own.

Come for lunch, order a Taiwanese buffet plate, and ask which dishes are vegan. This is the one for a wholesome, affordable lunch in Centro.
