Govinda Gopal is Quito's Hare Krishna lunch, a set menu in the Old Town that costs about three dollars and includes a main, soup, salad, juice and dessert. It is the cheapest proper meal on this list, and one of the most wholesome.

Go for lunch between noon and three, Monday to Saturday. Order the set menu, eat it slowly, and enjoy the calmest room in the Old Town.
