The Kitchen Table is a cosy eatery and bakery on Pearce Street, breakfasts, lunches and occasional dinners, with sourdough baked in-house. The falafel bagels are the vegan-friendly pick on a menu that also runs the full meat and fish range.

Order the falafel bagel and ask what else can be made vegan that day. It is a morning and lunch room, with dinner only on some evenings.
