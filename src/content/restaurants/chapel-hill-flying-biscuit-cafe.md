Flying Biscuit Cafe is a breakfast cafe on South Estes Drive, doing vegan French toast, tofu scramble, and hash browns. The address here is 201 South Estes Drive; some listings say South Columbia Street, which is wrong.

Order the vegan French toast with hash browns and a tofu scramble. Expect a weekend wait, since the room is popular for brunch.
