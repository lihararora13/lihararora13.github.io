Archway is what happens when a brunch spot takes plants completely seriously. The Verdun cafe is 100% vegan, woman-owned, and dressed up like a design magazine: stylish decor, a relaxed patio, and a menu that swings from baklava pancakes with pistachio cream and apple honey to chick'n waffles, udon tempeh, and maitake Thai. Reviewers keep calling it the best vegan brunch in Montreal, and the gluten-free pancakes have a small but fervent cult following.

Service is friendly and knowledgeable, the matcha lattes are excellent, and the wine and cocktail list makes it a solid dinner pick too. It is the kind of place omnivores wander into by accident and then recommend to everyone. Head south on the green line to Verdun; the brunch is worth the ride.
