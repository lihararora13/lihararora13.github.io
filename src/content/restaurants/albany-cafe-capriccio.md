Cafe Capriccio is an Italian restaurant on Grand Street in downtown Albany, with a strong vegan bench of pastas and vegetable dishes. It is the most polished room on this list, and the one to book for a nicer dinner.

Order the vegan pasta and the roasted vegetables, and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since some pastas are finished with cheese or butter.
