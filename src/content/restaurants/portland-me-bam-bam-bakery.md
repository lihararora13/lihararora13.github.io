Bam Bam Bakery is a fully gluten-free bakery on Brighton Avenue, and its vegan case is the reason it makes this list: cookies, cupcakes, and whoopie pies that are plant-based as well as celiac-safe. It is a bakery, not a restaurant, so come for the baked goods and a coffee rather than a full meal.

It sits a short drive from downtown, so pair it with a Congress Street stop rather than treating it as a standalone trip.
