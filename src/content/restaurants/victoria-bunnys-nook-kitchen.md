Bunny's Nook Kitchen is Victoria's plant-based bakery across the harbour in Esquimalt: a 100% plant-based bakery and coffee bar on Esquimalt Road, serving a vegan breakfast sandwich, a B.E.L.T., a vegan breakfast burrito and a vegan cinnamon bun. It is takeout only, Tuesday to Saturday, and it is the one pick in this guide that requires a short trip out of downtown, by bus, bike or car across the Johnson Street Bridge.

Order a breakfast sandwich and a cinnamon bun. Check the days before you go: it is closed Sunday and Monday.
