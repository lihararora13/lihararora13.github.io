Santosha is the calm one in Gokulam's cafe cluster, the kind of place where the yoga crowd lingers over cold-pressed juice after class. The 2026 mindful-dining guide names it among the quarter's top sattvic, vegan and organic institutions, and the menu leans into plant-based South Indian curries and light bowls.

Order a curry and a juice and settle in. This is a slow-morning cafe, not a quick stop, and it rewards the unhurried.
