Timeless Coffee is Oakland's all-vegan bakery-cafe, and the croissants are the reason to set an alarm: they sell out fast, and regulars plan around them. Beyond the pastry case there is Sunday brunch, vegan pizza, and coffee roasted in-house, all of it plant-based and all of it excellent.

It is the morning stop on the list, and the one to hit early. Order a croissant, stay for brunch, and take a loaf home if there is one left.
