Nilgiri Express is the place to try authentic Badaga cuisine in Ooty, the food of the Nilgiri hills, with a buffet and a la carte menus, balcony seating and live cooking. The guide names it first for Badaga food, and the hill-potato and double-bean curries are the dishes that define it.

Order the Badaga potato and bean curry with ragi mudde, and ask for the ghee to be held. It is the only Badaga meal on this list, and the most Ooty thing you can eat.
