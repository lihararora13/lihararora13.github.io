Seitan Urban Bistro is the chef's table of Lima's vegan scene: a bistro run by Santiago Santolalla, a Peruvian chef who trained in France and came home with new techniques and a new love for vegan food. The menu mixes international influences with Peruvian traditions and local ingredients, from seitan ceviche and anticuchos to French-inspired mains, with a daily set menu at lunch and a proper vegan pisco sour at night.

Come for the set lunch or a full dinner, because this is the rare vegan bistro that works equally well as a quick affordable meal and a special night out. The seitan dishes are the house specialty, the desserts and cakes get singled out by reviewers, and the pet-friendly patio makes it an easy stop between Miraflores sights.
