Spice Venue is an Indian restaurant on Asylum Street in downtown Hartford with curries and vegetable dishes that adapt to vegan orders. It sits two doors from Dhaba Wala, giving downtown a small Indian cluster for plant-based diners.

Order the vegetable curry and the chana masala, and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since some curries are finished with cream or butter.
