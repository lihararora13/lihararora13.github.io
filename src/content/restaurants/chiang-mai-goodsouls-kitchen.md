Goodsouls Kitchen is the name every Chiang Mai vegan guide leads with, and it has earned that spot by doing everything well. The menu is famously big, Thai classics like khao soi, pad Thai and massaman curry sit next to falafel pitas, veggie burgers, penne pasta and Burmese tea-leaf salad, and the all-day breakfast runs from tofu scramble to pancakes with coconut whipped cream. It is upmarket by Chiang Mai standards but still gentle on the wallet, and the house on Singharat Road is the kind of room you settle into.

It sits in the northeastern corner of the Old City, an easy walk from anywhere inside the moat, and it opens daily from 8am to 10pm. If you only have one vegan meal in Chiang Mai, make it lunch here and let the khao soi do the talking.
