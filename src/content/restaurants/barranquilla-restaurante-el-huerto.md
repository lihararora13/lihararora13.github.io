Restaurante El Huerto is a vegan-friendly colombian spot in El Prado. El Huerto is the vegetarian garden restaurant in El Prado, and its sancocho vegetariano and falafel in guiso criollo are the most Caribbean plates on this list.

Order the Sancocho vegetariano, Falafel en guiso criollo, and Ensalada de la casa. The sancocho is the signature; the falafel is the second plate.
