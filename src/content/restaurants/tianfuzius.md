Why we love it — Tianfuzius might make you question whether what you are
eating is definitely vegan, which is the highest compliment a Sichuan
kitchen can earn. The Schöneberg branch is fully vegetarian (the rest of
the Tianfu group is not), with a huge, clearly labeled vegan menu of
authentic Sichuan dishes: numbing mapo tofu, tender eggplant, cold
noodle salads, and faux-meat small plates built for sharing.

It is not the cheapest, but half portions of many mains let you order a
wide spread for the table. Bring friends, order too much, and discover
that vegan Sichuan hits every bit as hard as the original.
