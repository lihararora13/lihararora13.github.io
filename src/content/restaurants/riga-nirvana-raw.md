Nirvana Raw is a cosy family restaurant serving gluten-free and sugar-free vegan and raw vegan food, and reviewers confirm it is 100% vegan, everything is vegan, even the milk. The menu changes daily, so check the page or the Wolt app before you go, and the desserts are all raw vegan.

It is the healthiest and most unusual table on this list. Order the daily raw main, add a raw cheesecake, and enjoy food that tastes nothing like a compromise.
