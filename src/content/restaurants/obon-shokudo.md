Why we love it — Obon Shokudo is the rare vegan Japanese spot that doesn't
feel like it's missing anything, because it isn't. Handmade udon with real
chew, okonomiyaki that hits every savory note, and onigiri worth planning
your day around. It sits on every Portland vegan essentials list for the
simple reason that it earned it.

The Combo is the move for first-timers: two onigiris, curried korokke,
kakiage, and salad, which is less a lunch and more a parade. Come hungry,
leave converted.
