Tabliyit Massaad is a Gemmayzeh institution with branches across Lebanon, and its Gemmayzeh branch is the one to visit. The menu is Lebanese barbecue at heart, but the vegan shawarma, falafel and hummus are real dishes, not afterthoughts, and the room is buzzing until midnight.

Order the vegan shawarma, add falafel and hummus, and enjoy the Gemmayzeh street outside. It is open noon to midnight every day.
