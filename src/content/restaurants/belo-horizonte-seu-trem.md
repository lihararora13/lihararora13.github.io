Seu Trem is Belo Horizonte's fully vegan burger bar: a range of burgers, garlic bread, snacks including mushrooms, onion rings and hot dogs, plus kafta and more. It is the kind of place where a group of friends can eat very well for very little, and the beer list helps too.

Come hungry, order a burger and garlic bread, and add onion rings for the table. This is the one for a big, cheap, satisfying vegan feed.
