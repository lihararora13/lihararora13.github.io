Edible Eden is a vegetarian cafe at the LifeShine Wellness Centre on the corner of Klein Helderberg Road and the R44, specialising in functional foods for optimal health. Vegan dishes include smoothie bowls, porridge, a breakfast bowl, a garden burger with vegan mayonnaise and a raw wrap.

It sits outside the town centre, so it is a drive rather than a walk, and it is best for a slow breakfast or lunch. Order a smoothie bowl and the garden burger.
