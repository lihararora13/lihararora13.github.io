Al Mounia is a classic Moroccan restaurant on Rue du Prince Moulay Abdellah, one of the city's long-running traditional tables. Vegan options are the salads and a vegetable couscous, and you should specify vegan when ordering.

It is the most traditional room on this list and the most expensive, open daily. Order the vegetable couscous with salads to start.
