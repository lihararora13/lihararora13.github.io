Sattva Vegan is the mindful lunch stop on this list, and locals clearly agree: it is one of the best-loved vegan rooms in the 3rd district. The European and Indian-inspired menu changes daily, typically soup, main and dessert, prepared fresh from 99 percent organic and regional ingredients, with no processed products and plenty of house-made sweets.

Sit in the bright, unpretentious room with a view of the open kitchen and order the daily menu. Think teriyaki tofu stir-fry, lentil dal or a medicinal broth, balanced, hearty and kind to the afternoon ahead. Note the lunch-only hours on weekdays.
