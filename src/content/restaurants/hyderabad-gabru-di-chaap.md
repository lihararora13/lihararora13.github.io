Gabru Di Chaap made the list for a reason. Soya chaap done properly in Madhapur, with vegan-marked options that give plant-based eaters a proper North Indian feast. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Madhapur, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Soya chaap, Vegan curry, Tandoori soya if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
