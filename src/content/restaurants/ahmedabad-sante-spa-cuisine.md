Sante Spa Cuisine is Ahmedabad's polished vegan-friendly table: a health-focused vegetarian restaurant on Sindhu Bhavan Marg with an extensive separate vegan menu, rated 4.6 on Justdial from nearly 2,000 ratings, and posting actively on its own Instagram as of November 2026. The menu runs from smoothie bowls and quinoa upma to farm-fresh pizza on a ragi-bajra crust and vegan desserts.

Order the Vegan Raw Buddha Bowl, the vegan farm-fresh pizza, the avocado dynamite sushi roll and the Vegan Valrhona Chocolate Pie. Note the address: the restaurant has moved from the White Crow building listed in older guides to Sindhu Bhavan Marg beside the Ahmedabad Racquet Academy, so use the address on this page.
