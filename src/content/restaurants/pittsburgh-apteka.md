Apteka is the restaurant that put Pittsburgh's vegan scene on the national map: a fully vegan Central and Eastern European restaurant and bar in Bloomfield, named one of the 50 best restaurants in America by the New York Times in 2022, with James Beard-nominated chefs and pierogi that locals call the best in the Burgh. The pierogi are the headliner, the potato dumplings are the sleeper, and the borscht is the soul.

It is dinner only, Thursday through Sunday from 5pm, and the menu changes with the seasons, so book ahead and ask what is new. Order the pierogi, add dumplings, and save room for whatever the kitchen is excited about.
