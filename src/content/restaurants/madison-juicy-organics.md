Juicy Organics is a plant-powered juice bar and vegan cafe a block from Capitol Square, built on 100% organic, additive-free ingredients. The Veryberry acai bowl and the berry antioxidant smoothie are the signatures, and the spicy jackfruit sandwich and raw vegan cheesecake make it a proper lunch stop rather than just a juice run.

It is a quiet, calm room with takeout and delivery. Come for a bowl, stay for the tea list.
