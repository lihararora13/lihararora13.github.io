Green Farmer's does exactly what it says: generous, unpretentious vegan
comfort food. Think plant-based burgers with real heft, loaded fries,
and desserts that never taste like an afterthought, all at friendly
prices.

The Faubourg du Temple location is the spacious one, with room for a
proper sit-down feast. Fun, filling, and open late, it's the answer when
you're starving and nobody wants a tasting menu.
