The Mitten Brewing Company is a brewery in a former fire station on Leonard Street Northwest, pouring house beer and serving pizza with vegan options. It is the best group stop on the west side, with a big room and a menu that works for mixed diets.

Order the vegan pizza with a flight of house beers. Ask the server to confirm the vegan cheese and crust, since the standard pie is dairy.
