Plantivoros is the one vegan restaurant to eat at in Playa del Carmen if you eat at one: an all-vegan Mexican kitchen in a breezy open-air palapa setting, run by a vegan chef with a long history in town. The menu is Mexican made vegan, and HappyCow reviewers name the tacos, enchiladas, sopes, pozole and the torta cubana, with tamales on top.

It has moved, so use the Calle 14 Norte bis address on this page, not the old corner of Calle 32 and Avenida 40 in older listings. It opens mid-afternoon from 2:30pm and is closed Wednesday and Sunday, so plan on dinner.
