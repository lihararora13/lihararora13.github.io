Why we love it — Crossroads is the restaurant that proved upscale vegan dining
could be a date night, not a lecture. Chef Tal Ronnen's Mediterranean menu
runs on refinement rather than imitation: stuffed zucchini blossoms with
panko crunch, artichoke oysters topped with kelp caviar, and a truffle
mushroom pizza that anchors every best-of list in the city. The room feels
like a classic steakhouse, the cocktail program is all-vegan, and nothing
here needs a dietary disclaimer to impress.

It sits at the top of every 2026 roundup we cross-checked, from review
aggregators to food press, and it has held that position for years. Order the
blossoms and the artichokes to start, the pizza or bolognese for the main
event, and book ahead on weekends.
