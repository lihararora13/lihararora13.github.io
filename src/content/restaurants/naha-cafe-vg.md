Cafe VG is a small open-style vegetarian café near Miebashi Station, and it is the lighter lunch in this guide: specialty coffee with beautiful latte art, an organic wine list, and affordable vegetarian food, with the taco rice drawing the praise, a completely different taste from other stores, according to one reviewer.

It is open Wednesday to Sunday, lunch 11:30 to 14:30 and dinner 17:00 to 20:00, closed Monday and Tuesday, and one guide advises calling ahead to confirm it is open before making the trip. Come for a casual lunch or an early dinner between Kokusai-dori and the monorail.
