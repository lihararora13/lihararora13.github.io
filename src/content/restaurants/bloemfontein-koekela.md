Koekela is a day-time cafe on Reid Street in Westdene, all-day breakfast and lunches with cakes baked fresh in-house daily. It is a meat-serving cafe, but vegan options are on the menu and the baking is the draw.

Ask which cakes are vegan that day and order a plant-milk coffee. It is a good slow-morning stop, and it closes in the afternoon.
