Govinda's is the temple restaurant at ISKCON Bhubaneswar, and it cooks the way temple kitchens always have: fresh, simple, sattvik food served as prasadam. The thalis are the draw, a rotating plate of dal, sabzi, rice and roti, and the no-onion-no-garlic cooking means the food is already close to what a vegan traveller wants.

Ask for the sattvik thali without ghee and curd and you get a clean, honest meal at temple prices. Come for lunch, when the kitchen is at its freshest, and pair it with a walk around the temple complex.
