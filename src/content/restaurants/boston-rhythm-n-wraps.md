Rhythm 'n Wraps is what happens when a food truck dreams big and a neighborhood shows up. What started rolling around Boston in 2012 is now a brick-and-mortar Allston hangout where the walls are covered in graffiti art, the old-school hip-hop never stops, and every single dish is fully vegan. The wraps are named like mixtape tracks and hit just as hard.

The menu is maximalist comfort food: burgers stacked with mac 'n' cheese, crispy oyster mushrooms, jerk-spiced wraps, and smoothies that regulars call joyful. It is also a community pillar, having delivered healthy meals to Boston neighborhoods through the pandemic, which makes every bite taste a little better.
