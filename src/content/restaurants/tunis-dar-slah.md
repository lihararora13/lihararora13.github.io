Dar Slah is a Tunisian restaurant on Rue de la Kasbah in the medina with a daily changing menu. It can prepare vegan dishes on request, including salads, couscous and rice with vegetables, and it is open Monday to Saturday from noon to 3:30pm.

Order the couscous with vegetables and a salad. It is a lunch-only stop, so come at midday.
