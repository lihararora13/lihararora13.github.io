The Wooden Monkey is downtown Halifax's farm-to-table restaurant, cooking local and organic food with a menu that labels its vegan dishes clearly. The vegan seitan donair with roasties and the rice bowl are the mains to order, and the chocolate tofu pie is the dessert visitors keep naming as one of the best in the city.

Use the Grafton Street address on this page: the restaurant also has a second location across the harbour in Dartmouth above the ferry terminal. Open daily for lunch and dinner.
