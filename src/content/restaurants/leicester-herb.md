Herb is a vegetarian and vegan Kerala restaurant on Granby Street, cooking the food of India's south-west coast. The vegan dishes are a real part of the menu, and the coconut curries and dosa are the draw.

Order the vegan Kerala thali and a masala dosa. Book ahead at weekends, as the small room fills fast.
