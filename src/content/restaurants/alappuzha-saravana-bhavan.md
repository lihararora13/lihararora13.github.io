Saravana Bhavan on Madurai Road is the Alleppey branch of the chain every South Indian knows, and the one the town's where-to-eat reels name among the vegetarian stops. The menu is the full tiffin and meals repertoire, and the kitchen is consistent.

Order the masala dosa without ghee and the veg meal without curd. It is the reliable fallback in town, open and vegetarian to the core.
