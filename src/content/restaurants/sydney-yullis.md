If Sydney had to pick one vegan restaurant to represent the whole city, Yulli's would win in a landslide. Open since 2008, this quirky Crown Street bar-restaurant was doing creative plant-based food back when it was still a niche, and it has only gotten better: Vietnamese mini pancakes with crispy tofu skin, silky steamed mapo tofu, mango tapioca pudding, all designed for sharing and washed down with craft beer. It's the kind of room that converts picky non-vegan friends without trying.

The $51 banquet is the move for first-timers: outstanding value, huge amount of food, and the easiest way to tour the menu in one sitting. In mid-2026 they even ran a limited vegan yum cha collab with chefs from Bodhi, Sydney's beloved former plant-based yum cha institution, which tells you where this place sits in the scene's heart.
