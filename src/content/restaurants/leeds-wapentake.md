Wapentake is the pub on this list, and it proves the point: yes, you can still have a Sunday roast, and yes, the vegan one rivals the traditional one. The Kirkgate pub does a vegan roast that locals rate highly, alongside vegan options across a proper pub menu, with local ale to match.

Come on a Sunday, order the roast, and settle in for the afternoon. This is Leeds' vegan scene at its most northern, and its most comforting.
