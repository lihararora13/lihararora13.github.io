NIJIYA is seven counter seats, two women cooking in front of you, and the highest-rated vegan restaurant in Kyoto: a perfect 5.0 from 354 HappyCow reviews. The course runs through yuzu tofu and fresh yuba, soy-meat yakitori and yakiniku, a five-dish obanzai assortment, osuimono soup, grilled miso rice balls and chocolate yokan, all built on a plant-based dashi in the shojin tradition.

The catch is the queue: evening service runs just two seatings, so arrive 15 to 30 minutes before the 5:30pm opening or expect a long wait. Get the timing right and it is one of the most memorable vegan meals in Japan.
