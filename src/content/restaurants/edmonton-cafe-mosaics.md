Cafe Mosaics is Old Strathcona's vegetarian cafe, a no-sign, street-number-only room that locals have loved for years. The menu is short and honest: veggie burgers, noodle soup, juices and falafel on Fridays, with vegan options throughout.

Look for the street number rather than a sign, and go with the flow. Order the burger and a juice, and you will understand why the regulars keep coming back.
