Let Them Eat Vegan is the top-rated vegan spot in Dunedin, a perfect 5.0 from 110 HappyCow reviews, and it is the city's only fully vegan deli and cafe. It hides down Albion Lane, at the side entrance of Mall 218 off Great King Street, and everything is hand-made from scratch in a tiny kitchen: the Not Meat range including Not Bacon, Not Chook, Not Salami and Not Chorizo, the Not Cheese range including cheddar, camembert and smoked gouda, plus daily cafe specials, loaded burritos, baguettes and a cabinet of cakes and doughnuts. The deli range is posted nationwide and stocked in shops from Auckland to Dunedin, and the Not Bacon won Supreme Winner at the 2025 Vegan Sausage and Deli Awards.

The cafe is open Tuesday to Wednesday 11:00am to 3:00pm, Thursday to Friday 11:00am to 8:00pm, and Saturday 9:00am to 3:00pm, with outdoor seating in the lane only. Its November 2026 menu, posted on its own Instagram, lists eight hot toasted burritos and five baguettes, all 100 percent plant-based. The pin is street-level for Albion Lane, as the lane has no street number.
