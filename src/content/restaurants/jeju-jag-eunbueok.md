Jag Eunbueok, "Small Kitchen", is the most personal table on Jeju: a two-table kitchen inside the owner's house at Seonheul-ri in Jochon-eup, where one chef cooks a five-course set menu, mostly Korean, with greens and edible flowers grown in the garden outside. Reviewers call it the best vegan meal they have had on the island, and the room, all thoughtful decor and soft Ghibli music, feels more like a dinner party than a restaurant. The set menu runs around 30,000 to 40,000 won per person, with brown rice tteokbokki or bibimbap available a la carte.

Book at least a day ahead by phone, same-day reservations are not accepted, and tell them when you book if you do not eat honey. It is open Tuesday to Saturday, 9am to 3pm, with two lunch seatings a day and one dinner a week. The pin on this page is district-level for Seonheul-ri, so follow the address and call if you are unsure of the turn.
