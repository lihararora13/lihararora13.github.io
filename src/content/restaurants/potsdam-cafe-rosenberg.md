Cafe Rosenberg has been a Potsdam fixture since 2017, a cafe on Dortustrasse with vegan food and cakes and space to use a laptop, and it is still one of the city's most reliable vegan-friendly tables. A note on its status: it was previously listed as fully vegan, but was reported in May 2026 to serve eggs and dairy, so it is vegan-friendly now, not all-vegan. The vegan cakes and dishes are still on offer, and the room is as welcoming as ever.

It opens daily, ten to six on weekdays and Saturdays, eleven to five on Sundays. Order a vegan cake and a coffee with plant milk, settle in with a book, and treat it as a working lunch. This is the comfortable, central pick for a slow afternoon.
