Green Lotus is a Vietnamese vegetarian restaurant in the heart of Little Saigon that makes its own tofu and marks its vegan dishes clearly, which is exactly the kind of quiet competence that keeps a place packed for a decade. The vegan pho is the draw, the tofu dishes are the sleeper hit, and the spring rolls are the opener.

It is a vegetarian restaurant rather than a fully vegan one, so ask and the kitchen will steer you right. Come for the pho, stay for the tofu, and remember that Vietnam Town is one of the best food neighbourhoods in the Bay Area.
