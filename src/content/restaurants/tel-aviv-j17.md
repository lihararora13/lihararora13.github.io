J17 is the quiet achiever of this list: a small, homey vegan kitchen that has held a perfect HappyCow rating since 2019 by doing simple food extremely well. The menu covers the Israeli canon, chickpea stew, hummus, tabbouleh, and then rotates specials like buckwheat meatballs with root puree, stewed tofu on couscous, and beet Cuba on rice.

It is also one of the most allergy-friendly kitchens in the city, with gluten-free, peanut-free and sesame-free options clearly available, and delivery via Wolt if you would rather eat at home. Come for a wholesome lunch that tastes like someone's grandmother learned to cook all over again, this time without the meat.
