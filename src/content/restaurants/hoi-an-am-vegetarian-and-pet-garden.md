Am Vegetarian and Pet Garden is a garden restaurant out in Hoi An Tay ward, shared with the owners' rescue cats and dogs, and rated 4.7 from 447 Google reviews. It moved from 33D Ly Thai To to Bau Oc Ha 1, and many lists, including the old version of the guide, still send people to the old address. The chef cooks alone, so food takes time, but diners call it some of the best vegetarian food in Hoi An.

Order the vegetarian set and the seasonal vegetables, and allow time for the ride and the cooking. It is 3.9 km out of town, about 11 minutes by scooter, and it is the most peaceful vegan-friendly meal in Hoi An.
