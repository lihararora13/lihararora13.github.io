Namaste Village is a plant-based Indian restaurant in Oxford's Castle Quarter, and its menu is the widest on this list: Mumbai street food, South Indian dosas, curries and a vegetarian afternoon tea, all cooked without animal products. Reviewers call the room warm and welcoming and the menu huge.

The masala dosa is the highlight, and the black house daal is the comfort pick. It is a plant-based kitchen, so file it as all-vegan, and come hungry, because the portions are generous and sharing is the way to eat here.
