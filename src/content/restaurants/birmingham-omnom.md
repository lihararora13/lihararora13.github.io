OMNOM is Birmingham's newest big vegan-friendly opening, an Indian vegetarian and vegan restaurant on Fleet Street in the Jewellery Quarter that opened in November 2025. The cooking is Ayurveda-inspired and made without onion or garlic: chaat, saag aloo, beetroot kebab, gobi manchurian and jackfruit biryani, served in a calm, elegant room that feels like a proper night out.

It is vegetarian with an extensive vegan menu, so flag that you are vegan when you book. Order the jackfruit biryani, add the beetroot kebab, and finish with whatever the kitchen recommends.
