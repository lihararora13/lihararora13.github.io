Mad Cheese began as a fully vegan online store and is now a brick-and-mortar shop in Madisonville, making small-batch artisan cheeses alongside plant-based meats and charcuterie boards. It is a shop rather than a restaurant, so think of it as the stop for a picnic or a dinner-party board: taste through the cheeses, pick up a board for the evening, and ask the staff what is newest. The 5.0 HappyCow rating reflects how seriously the regulars take it.

The shop is open Tuesday to Friday 11am to 6pm and Sunday noon to 4pm, and closed Mondays and Saturdays. It is a small room, so the visit is short, but it is the only dedicated vegan cheese shop in the city.
