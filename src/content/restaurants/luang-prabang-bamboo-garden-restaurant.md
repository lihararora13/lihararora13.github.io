Bamboo Garden Restaurant is a Thai and Lao restaurant on Visounarath Road, serving vegan options including vegan larb with no fish sauce, fried rice with vegetables and no egg, curries with rice, and noodle dishes. It relocated from Wisunalat Road, so older guides send people to the wrong street, and it is open Monday to Saturday, 11am to 3:30pm and 6pm to 9:30pm, closed Sundays.

Order the vegan larb and specify no fish sauce, and ask for fried rice with vegetables and no egg. It is the best Lao vegan meal in the centre.
