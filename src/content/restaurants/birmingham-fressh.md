Fressh is the home of delicious, healthy, plant-based food in Birmingham city centre, a vibrant and trendy vegan eatery tucked into the Victorian City Arcade on Union Street. Everything on the 100% vegan menu is made fresh in-house daily, including all the salad dressings and burger sauces, which is why it became a favourite so quickly after opening.

It is right in the middle of the shopping district, which makes it the perfect lunch stop between shops. Order a burger or a buddha bowl, add a fresh juice, and enjoy a cafe that takes freshness literally.
