Nirvana is the family-run vegan trattoria on Via Il Prato, west of the station, cooking traditional Italian dishes with a few Asian touches, with Italian craft beer and Tuscan wines to go with them. The truffle carbonara is the dish to order, with lasagna, pizza and tiramisù behind it, and gluten-free pastas are available too.

It is open for lunch and dinner, Tuesday to Sunday. Book for the evening, and ask which wines are vegan, because fining agents are not listed on Italian wine lists.
