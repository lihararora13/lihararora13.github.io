Modernark Pharm Cafe has been Sannomiya's vegetarian café for years, a five-minute walk from JR Sannomiya and Kobe-Sannomiya stations, cooking brown rice and vegetables into a wide menu where the vegan items are clearly flagged. The plates to order are the curry with brown rice and the chili-bean burrito, and the cake case, tiramisu, gateau chocolat and carrot cake, is worth saving room for.

Not everything on the menu is vegan by default, so confirm honey and cheese are left off when you order. It is the flexible everyday café in this guide: open daily, central, and genuinely good at feeding vegans without fuss.
