Flower Burger is the vegan fast-food chain famous for its colourful burgers made with natural plant-based ingredients, and its Verona branch sits on Corso Porta Nuova south of the Arena. It is the informal, reliable fallback on this list: you know what you are getting, and it is fully vegan.

Order the protein burger and the colourful house burgers, and come when the independents are full or you want something fast and informal.
