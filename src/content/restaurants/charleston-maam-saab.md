Ma'am Saab is a Pakistani restaurant on Meeting Street, at the corner that long held Jestine's Kitchen, and Charleston City Paper readers have voted it Best Indian/Pakistani in 2024, 2025, and 2026. The menu marks gluten-free, vegetarian, and vegan dishes, and the curries and lentil dishes are the vegan-friendly core.

It is the most polished dinner room on this list after Three Girls. Order a curry, add the lentils, and ask which breads are vegan.
