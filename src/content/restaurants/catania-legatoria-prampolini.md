Legatoria Prampolini is a combined bookshop and cafe on Via Vittorio Emanuele II, with bookshelves, seating and wine bottles, and it is the slow afternoon stop on this list. It is a community space as much as a cafe, and it promotes the change Catania's vegan scene is part of.

Order a coffee and whatever vegan cakes and snacks are on, and stay a while with a book. It is a daytime and early-evening spot.
