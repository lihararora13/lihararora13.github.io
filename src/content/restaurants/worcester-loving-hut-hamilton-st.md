Loving Hut on Hamilton Street is an all-vegan restaurant doing Asian-inspired plates, curries, and noodle dishes with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the second of two Loving Hut locations in Worcester, and the Hamilton Street room is the smaller of the two.

Order the vegan curry and a noodle dish. Use the Hamilton Street address, 56 Hamilton, not the Chandler Street location.
