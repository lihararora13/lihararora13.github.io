Beetschwester is a vegetarian and vegan eatery on Tibusplatz, opened in June 2020 and ranked the city's top vegan-friendly spot by local reviewers. The menu is breakfast and lunch: vegan pancakes, breakfast bowls, hummus bowls, flatbreads and lunch dishes, with regional and seasonal ingredients and a bright modern room that fills fast at weekends.

It is a vegetarian kitchen with a strong vegan menu, so order the vegan-marked dishes. Open Monday to Saturday from nine until five, with Sundays closed, it is Münster's best vegan-friendly breakfast table.
