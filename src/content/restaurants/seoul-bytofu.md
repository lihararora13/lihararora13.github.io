ByTOFU does exactly what the name promises: tofu in every costume, baked, whipped, ricotta-ed and moussed, in a pocket-sized cafe partway up Namsan. It holds a perfect five stars on HappyCow with fresh reviews into mid-2026, and the Korea Times put it on its best-vegan-in-Seoul list.

The eggless egg sandwich is the best-seller and the correct first order; the Sunrise Bowl is the virtuous second. Seats are few (sixteen or so) and it closes Tuesday and Wednesday, so treat it as a planned pilgrimage, not a backup plan.
