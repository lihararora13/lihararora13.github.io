Kulturküche is a vegetarian lunch kitchen on the Kaiserstraße, and it keeps the shortest hours on this list: Tuesday to Friday, noon to two, and nothing else. The menu is short and seasonal, a lunch plate and a soup, with vegan options, and the room is a canteen rather than a restaurant. It is the working lunch on this list, and it is worth planning around rather than dropping in on.

Go at noon, order the seasonal lunch plate, and treat it as the quick, honest, meat-free lunch that Karlsruhe's office workers rely on.
