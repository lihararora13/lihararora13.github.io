Hungry Bowl is the North End's build-your-own bowl spot on Cunard Street, cooking made-to-order meals from organic and nutrient-rich ingredients. The menu runs Mediterranean and Mexican, and whether you are vegan, vegetarian or just hungry, the bowl format means you choose exactly what goes in.

It is a quick, casual meal rather than a long dinner, and it pairs well with a walk around the North End's Gottingen Street vegan strip afterwards.
