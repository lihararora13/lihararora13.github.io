Aloe Vega is a vegan health food restaurant on Avenue des Minimes, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, with creative vegan bowls, home-made plant yoghurt, carrot cake muffins and turmeric crackers. It is a daytime spot, open mornings to early afternoon, with a set lunch and dessert.

It is a little out of the centre, so pair it with a walk north rather than squeezing it between Capitole sights. Order a curry bowl and a carrot cake muffin.
