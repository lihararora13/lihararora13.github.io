2 Phat Vegans moved to Decatur Street in the French Quarter and brought the city's best late-night vegan option with it: the kitchen stays open until 1 AM every night. The Black-owned deli runs on plant-based whole foods and vegan meats turned into New Orleans classics, and the hot sausage po'boy with Impossible meat is the order that converts skeptics.

The room has a laid-back island vibe with board games and art, and the menu is deep: buffalo chicken sandwiches, mushroom po'boys, vegan pizza, jerk wraps, milkshakes, and sea moss smoothies. Prices are friendly, portions are generous, and at midnight on a Friday it might be the best decision you make all week.
