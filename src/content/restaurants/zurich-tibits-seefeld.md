Tibits is Zurich's beloved vegetarian buffet, and the Seefeld branch is the one to visit. More than forty homemade salads, hot dishes, soups, sandwiches and desserts are prepared fresh daily and sold by weight, with around 80 percent of the food vegan and labelled, and Tuesdays fully vegan.

It is open from breakfast through dinner. Fill a plate, add a samosa and a tahini brownie, and bring your own container for a discount on takeaway.
