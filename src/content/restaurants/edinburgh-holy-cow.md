Holy Cow is the snug, 100% vegan cafe down Elder Street, a couple of minutes from Waverley station, and the one Edinburgh locals send visitors to for a casual plant-based lunch. The menu is built around its Muu Burgers: pulled jackfruit, oyster mushroom steak, Vietnamese tofu, plus a Reuben sandwich with real bite, homemade soups, salads and a root veggie bowl. It also runs a fancy vegan and gluten-free afternoon tea, which needs booking in advance.

The room is small, with a courtyard for sunny days, and it hums from breakfast through dinner. Order the pulled jackfruit burger if it is your first visit, or the Reuben if you want to test how good vegan deli food can get. This is the table for a no-fuss, genuinely delicious lunch between sightseeing stops.
