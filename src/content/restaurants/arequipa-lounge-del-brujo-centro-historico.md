Lounge del Brujo Centro Histórico is a fully vegan peruvian spot in Centro Histórico. Lounge del Brujo's historic-centre branch sits inside the Casona de San José shopping centre's courtyard, and it is fully vegan, serving vegan versions of Peruvian classics including anticuchos, lomo saltado and chicharrón. Recent reviewers call it their favourite vegan dinner in Arequipa.

Order the Tallarín saltado vegano, Spring rolls, and Risotto vegano. The tallarín saltado is the plate to order; the spring rolls are the starter.
