Çiğköftem Ulm is a small counter on the Albert-Einstein-Platz, by the station, serving fresh daily çiğ köfte alongside falafel wraps and hummus bowls. The shop describes its food as high-quality vegan dishes, and the çiğ köfte itself is plant-based by tradition, bulgur, walnuts and spices, made fresh every day. It is open Monday to Saturday until eight, which makes it the quick lunch or early dinner by the station.

Order a çiğ köfte dürüm, add a falafel wrap if you are hungry, and treat it as the fastest proper vegan meal in the city centre.
