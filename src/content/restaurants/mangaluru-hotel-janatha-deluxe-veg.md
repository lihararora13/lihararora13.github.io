Hotel Janatha Deluxe is the old Hampankatta veg hotel that a May 2026 food reel rated 4.5 out of 5 for its coastal Karnataka-style meals on banana leaf: hot rice, sambar, rasam, chutney and papad, served in a crowded, brightly lit dining hall. It is affordable, hygienic and unpretentious, which is exactly what a veg meals hall should be.

Order the unlimited coastal veg meal and a filter coffee. Ask for the curd to be left off the leaf and the meal stays plant-based and generous.
