Délice Végétal is a fully vegan bakery on the Quai de la Fosse, and the case is the draw: almond croissants, cinnamon rolls and pistachio donuts, all plant-based and all made to be eaten warm. It is the morning stop on this page, and the one Nantes vegans point visitors to first thing.

Go early for the best selection. The bakery sits a short walk west of the centre along the Loire, an easy detour on the way to the Machines de l'île.
