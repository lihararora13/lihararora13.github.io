Matsa Caffè is the Bastide's vegetarian canteen with Italian soul: focaccia, matcha lattes, chai lattes, golden lattes and affogato, served in a bright, easy room across the river from the old centre. The vegan options run through the menu, and the coffee program is serious.

It is a weekday lunch spot, open Monday to Saturday until early afternoon, so cross the bridge for it. Order the focaccia, add a matcha latte, and enjoy the slower side of Bordeaux.
