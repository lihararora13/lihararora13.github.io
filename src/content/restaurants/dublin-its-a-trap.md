No vegan trip to Dublin is complete without a morning stop at It's a Trap. The warm aroma of cinnamon practically wafts out the door of this entirely vegan bakery and coffee house on Aungier Street, its current home after relocating from Denmark Street Great.

The cinnamon rolls are the headliners, slathered in cashew cream cheese, with rotating varieties like a matcha pandan roll topped with thick pandan frosting and pistachios. For something savoury, the all-day breakfast wrap stuffs seitan sausage, baked beans, mushrooms, hashbrowns, sundried tomatoes and barbecue sauce into one glorious bundle. There is also a well-stocked grocery corner of vegan cheeses and treats to take home.
