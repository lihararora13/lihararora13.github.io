Pearl at Swarnabhumi is the prettiest pure-veg restaurant in Ranchi, at least according to the creator who filmed it in February 2026: a spacious, well-lit dining room with light green chairs, wooden tables and soft lighting, serving spring rolls, noodles, fried rice and curries with bright mocktails. It is the one to book for a family dinner or a celebration.

Order the spring rolls and the veg noodles, and ask the kitchen to skip the paneer and dairy in the curries. It is a calm, modern room, and a good contrast to the older thali houses on this list.
