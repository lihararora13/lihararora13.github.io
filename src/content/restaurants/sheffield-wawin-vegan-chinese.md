Wawin is Sheffield's all-vegan Chinese restaurant, an evening spot on John Street that rebuilds the takeaway classics without a trace of animal products. The crispy aromatic duck is the signature, and the salt and pepper chicken and chow mein are the plates regulars come back for.

It opens in the late afternoon and runs to 10.30pm, so this is a dinner pick. Order the duck, the salt and pepper chips and a chow mein, and share across the table.
