Chayfood wears its chay identity proudly and delivers it with a modern, quietly confident touch. The menu jumps between Vietnamese classics, Thai, Japanese and Korean dishes, with the occasional curveball like tacos or pasta, and somehow it all holds together, every dish carrying that subtle chay twist in ingredients and technique. It opens in the morning and doubles as a cafe, with plant-based milk options and a pretty little terrace.

Order the Hanoi-style bun rieu if you only order one thing: fresh, comforting, and the dish that shows off what this kitchen can do. A no-brainer stop for hungry vegans in the Old Quarter.
