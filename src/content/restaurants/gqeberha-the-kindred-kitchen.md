The Kindred Kitchen was the very first plant-based restaurant in Gqeberha, set among the restaurants and boutiques of Richmond Hill, and it still draws the city's best reviews for a vegetarian room. The food menu is fully vegan, which is why it leads this list, but honey appears in some drinks and desserts and cow's milk is offered as a coffee option, so vegans should say so when ordering.

Ask for the vegan plate of the day and confirm no honey in your drink or dessert. It is a small, friendly room, so come for lunch rather than expecting a big dinner service.
