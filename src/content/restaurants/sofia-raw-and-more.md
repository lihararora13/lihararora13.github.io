Raw & More is Sofia's all-vegan raw dessert shop on San Stefano Street, selling cakes, bars, brownies and truffles prepared by dehydration at low temperatures. The raw chocolate cheesecake is the slice the vegan guides name, and the brownies and truffles are the takeaway orders.

It is open daily from 9am to 8pm. This is a dessert shop, not a restaurant, so come for cake and coffee rather than a full meal.
