Ama Cafe on Rajpur Road is one of Dehradun's best healthy food spots, open from 8 AM, with a menu that runs from salads and bowls to toast and smoothies. It is the kind of cafe where a morning run ends and a slow breakfast begins.

Order the salad bowl and an avocado toast, and ask for the vegan version. It is a calm, bright room, and a good start to a Doon day.
