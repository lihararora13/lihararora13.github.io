Nabati is Jeddah's first fully vegan restaurant, founded by Ola Kayal, all-vegan, gluten-free and organic, with a zero-waste philosophy and an earthy, eco-conscious room on Al Kayal Street. The signature dishes are the Nabati Benedict, mushroom mac n cheese made with a cashew-based sauce, and vegan ice creams in salted caramel, rose pistachio and cookie dough.

Order the Nabati Benedict and the mushroom mac n cheese, and finish with the salted caramel ice cream. It is the flagship vegan table in Saudi Arabia.
