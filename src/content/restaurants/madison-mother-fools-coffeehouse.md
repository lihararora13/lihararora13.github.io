Mother Fool's Coffeehouse is a Williamson Street institution, a coffeehouse and bakery where the vegan donuts are the draw and the tofu scrambles and baked goods fill out a menu that has fed the neighbourhood for years. It is the coffee stop on this list, and the room, warm and bookish, invites lingering.

Come for a donut and a coffee, and stay for a second cup.
