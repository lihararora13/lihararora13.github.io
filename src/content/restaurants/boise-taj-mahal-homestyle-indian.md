Taj Mahal Homestyle Indian is a downtown Indian restaurant on North 8th Street serving homestyle curries, dals, and breads. The vegan bench is strong, with chana masala and vegetable dishes that the kitchen prepares to order.

Order the chana masala and baingan bharta with vegan roti, and confirm which breads are dairy-free, since some are brushed with ghee.
