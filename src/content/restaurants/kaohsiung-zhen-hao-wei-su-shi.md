Zhēn Hǎo Wèi SùShí, 真好味素食, is the vegan-friendly stall to know in Liuhe Night Market, the night market that runs down Liuhe 2nd Road a few steps from Formosa Boulevard MRT station, and it is the only place in this guide that cooks after dark. Look for it on the left just after Liuhe 2nd Road crosses Nantai Road, and watch for the Buddhist swastika on the sign, which marks vegetarian restaurants in Taiwan. The family-run stall cooks to order: stir-fried greens, sesame paste noodles, mock duck, braised beancurd, soup noodles, fried noodles, veg mutton soup pots and sushi, all of it cheap, with most dishes around NT$50 to NT$170.

It is listed here as vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan: one English guide calls it all-vegan, but local reviews note that some dishes are egg or milk vegetarian, so ask for the vegan dishes and the staff will steer you. The stall opens at 5:30pm and runs to midnight, seven days a week, with outdoor seating, and it was still trading in August 2025 and in a night-market guide updated in November 2026. Cash only.
