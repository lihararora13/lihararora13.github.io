Edify Cafe is the NCR cafe-circuit pick in Noida: warm lighting, cane chairs, and a menu that leans properly plant-friendly. The chickpea salad and the hummus platter with pita and falafel are the orders the vegan guides keep naming.

It is a good work-or-meet cafe, calm and unhurried. Ask for the vegan version of the platter and the kitchen will keep it dairy-free.
