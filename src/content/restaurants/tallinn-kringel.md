Kringel is the vegan cafe that Tallinn needed and finally got. Located at Koidu 101 in the Uus Maailm neighbourhood, it combines a full bakery programme with a proper lunch menu, and everything, every pastry, every sandwich, every cake, is 100% plant-based, egg-free and dairy-free. The menu runs from fresh pastries and breakfast dishes to lunch and dinner items ranging from pasta to pancakes, with coffee, smoothies and cocktails.

It is the sweet-tooth stop on this list. Order a pastry with your coffee, come back for lunch, and try the desserts, which reviewers say are the thing to order.
