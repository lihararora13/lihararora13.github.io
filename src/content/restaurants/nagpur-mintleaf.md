Mintleaf is the hotel dining room on this list, inside the Tuli Imperial on Central Bazar Road in Ramdaspeth, and it plays that role well: air-conditioned, unhurried, and reliably good for a proper sit-down meal. It is the pick for when you want comfort rather than adventure.

The North Indian veg mains are the move, with dal tadka and mixed veg sabzi leading, and the kitchen is used to dietary requests. Ask for the ghee and cream held and the meal turns properly plant-based without a fuss.
