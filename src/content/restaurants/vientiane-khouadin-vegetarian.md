Khouadin Vegetarian, also called Kwadin Restaurant, is a vegan buffet restaurant in central Vientiane, with Lao dishes and a daily lunch buffet. It is inexpensive and central, and it is one of the oldest vegan kitchens in the city.

Come for the lunch buffet and fill a plate with Lao vegetable dishes and tofu. Its pin is approximate to the city centre, so check the address before you go.
