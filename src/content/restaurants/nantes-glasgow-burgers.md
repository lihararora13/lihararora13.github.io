Glasgow Burgers is Nantes' fully vegan burger counter: a short menu, done properly, with the Glasgow burger as the headliner and homemade fries on the side. It is the city's plant-based answer to a late lunch or a post-sightseeing refuel, a couple of minutes from the Place du Bouffay.

Everything is vegan, so there is no need to negotiate the menu. Order the Glasgow burger, add fries, and you have the measure of the place.
