Vegan Heaven is the reliable workhorse of the Chiang Mai vegan scene, open since 2016 and still going strong with fresh reviews through October 2026. The menu is huge and deliberately crowd-pleasing: Thai noodles and curries, pineapple fried rice, gyoza, burgers and fries, pesto pasta, an English breakfast and waffles with ice cream, all 100 percent vegan with no MSG and no service charge. It is the place you take a mixed group when everyone wants something different.

It sits on Loi Kroh Road by the Night Bazaar, open daily 9am to 10pm, which makes it the easiest late dinner on this list. Big portions, fair prices, zero fuss.
