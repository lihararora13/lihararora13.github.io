Plant is where every Seoul vegan guide seems to start, and one visit explains why: a bright, friendly room above Bogwang-ro serving the burgers, pastas and wraps that visitors plan layovers around. It is fully vegan, endlessly recommended, and still collecting glowing reviews through autumn 2026, with a second branch in Yeonnam-dong for Hongdae-side grazing.

Come hungry and work the whole menu: a burger, something creamy and pasta-shaped, a smoothie bowl if the sun is out. Then surrender to the bakery case, because the cakes are the part people write home about.
