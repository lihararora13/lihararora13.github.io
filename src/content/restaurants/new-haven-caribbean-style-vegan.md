Caribbean Style Vegan, also listed as Ninth Square Market Too Caribbean Style, is an all-vegan Caribbean kitchen on George Street in the Ninth Square Market. The menu is Jamaican plates, stews, and sides, all plant-based, and it is the only all-vegan restaurant on this list.

Order the jerk tofu plate with rice and peas and plantains. A caution: sources conflict about Tuesday service, so confirm the day before a special trip.
