Ouzeri Tou Likarou is the historic taverna in the lively Ladadika neighbourhood, with a large selection of mezzés to share. The staff know the substitutions well: stuffed vegetables and dolmades and chickpea-based dishes can be made without animal products, and the room is built for a long evening of sharing plates.

It is open Tuesday to Sunday from 1pm to 11pm and closed Monday. Aim for early evening, from 6pm to 8pm, for a friendly atmosphere without long waits.
