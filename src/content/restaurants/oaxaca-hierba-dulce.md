Hierba Dulce is a small all-vegan restaurant in Centro cooking Oaxacan flavours plant-based, with fresh local ingredients and a menu that changes with the market. It is the kind of place where the food tastes like the region, not like a substitute for it.

Order the Oaxacan plate and a fresh juice. It is a calm, nourishing lunch, and a good stop between the busier tables on this list.
