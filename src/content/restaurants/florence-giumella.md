Giumella is a vegan gastronomia, the Italian deli that sells ready-made dishes by the portion, east of the centre on Via Lorenzo di Credi in the Campo di Marte area. The counter changes daily with the season, and the move is a lunch box of whatever is on, Tuscan-inspired and freshly made, to take down to the Arno or eat in.

It is open Monday to Saturday from 10:30am to 8pm. Go before 1pm for the full choice at lunch, since this is where locals buy dinner on the way home.
