Bep OM Chay, also listed as Nguyet Quang Vegan, is a fully vegan cafe in an alley off Nguyen Tu Luc in Dalat, serving fully vegan Vietnamese dishes, open 7am to 2pm and 4pm to 8pm. It is a small, local kitchen, and it is the most fully vegan of the Dalat cafes after Vegan Heaven and Au Lac.

Order the Vietnamese dishes and rice plates, and come at lunch, when the kitchen is at its fullest. Its pin is approximate to the alley, so check the address before you go.
