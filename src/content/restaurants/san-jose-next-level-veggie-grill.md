Next Level Veggie Grill is the grown-up version of fast food: everything on the menu is 100 percent vegan, all organic, non-GMO and responsibly sourced, served counter-style in the middle of Santana Row's shopping and strolling. The Santa Fe Crispy Chickin sandwich and the Beyond burger are the headliners, and the sweet potato fries are the move on the side.

It is fast, loud, family-friendly and open late, which makes it the easiest vegan meal in the South Bay when nobody can agree on anything. Come hungry, order at the counter, and take the leftovers for a walk around the Row.
