Alterna is the vegan cafe Birmingham's reviewers rate highest, a Jewellery Quarter spot that does breakfast burritos with tofu scramble, tofu sando and bakes, all of it fully plant-based. It is the kind of cafe that works for a slow morning as well as a quick lunch, with proper coffee and a menu that changes with the seasons.

It sits on Caroline Street, a short walk from St Paul's Square. Order the breakfast burrito, add a bake, and take your time over the coffee.
