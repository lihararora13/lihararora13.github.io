Fuente Rica Rica is a vegan fuente de soda, the classic Chilean sandwich counter, rebuilt entirely around plants. Everything comes on bread baked in-house: sandwiches, burgers and hot dogs, all served with fries, plus empanadas and other Chilean street food, and a cocktail list for the evenings.

It is the most fun kind of casual eating: unfussy, filling, and proudly ethical about where its food comes from. Come for a seitan sandwich and stay for a pisco sour, and you will understand why it sits on HappyCow's top ten for the city.
