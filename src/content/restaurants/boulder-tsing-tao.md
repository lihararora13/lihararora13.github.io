Tsing Tao is a local Chinese restaurant with a secret that Boulder vegans all know: on Monday and Thursday evenings the buffet goes fully vegan. Soy orange chicken, sesame dishes, vegan dumplings and plenty more, all you can eat, in a quiet South Boulder room.

Even outside buffet nights there is a full vegan menu, so any evening works. Time your visit for a Monday or Thursday if you can, arrive hungry, and try the dumplings first.
