Soul Miner's Garden started as a modest food truck roaming the Queen City and grew into one of Charlotte's most loved vegan kitchens, now settled into Camp North End. Chef Sasha Quinn's menu is elevated comfort food with an allergen-friendly streak: loaded nachos with house-made queso, collard greens regulars call their favourite in town, a Shroomin' Philly, buffalo ranch fries, and a Reuben that one reviewer declared the best in town, full stop.

It is a safe space in every sense, welcoming, kid-friendly, and proud of it, with beer, coffee and dessert on the menu too. Come for the nachos, stay for the peanut butter pie.
