Pure Kitchen's Elgin Street branch is the downtown flagship of Ottawa's favourite vegetarian mini-chain, and it is where to go for vegan poutine done right, with vegan cheese or curds. The menu is huge: fusion bowls, acai bowls, salads, burgers, sandwiches, wraps, dumplings and vegan cheesecake, plus a juice bar.

It is open daily until 9pm and always busy. Order the poutine, a fusion bowl, and the buffalo cauliflower to start. This is the crowd-pleaser on the list.
