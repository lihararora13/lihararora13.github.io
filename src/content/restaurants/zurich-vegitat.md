Vegitat is Zurich's vegan doner spot, a fast-food takeaway on Langstrasse serving seitan doner and wraps with cigkofte, the bulgur, tomato and herb classic. Sweet options run to tiramisu and baklava, and the shop was reported fully vegan in December 2024.

It is the late-night pick on this list. Order a seitan doner, add a cigkofte wrap, and finish with the baklava.
