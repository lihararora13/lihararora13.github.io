Tacuara Ciudad is the plant-based kitchen to start with in Mendoza: a natural-food restaurant on Avenida Colon, rated 4.3 from 130 reviewers, serving lomo de seitan on sourdough focaccia, milanesa de seitan, burgers, fries and bombitas de papa stuffed with melted cheese. It describes itself as plant based and real food, and it is open Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 10 pm, which makes it the most flexible table in the city.

Order the lomo de seitan on focaccia and the bombitas de papa. It is the priciest pick on this list, and the one most worth a proper sit-down meal. The Godoy Cruz branch is a separate location, so use the Colon address here.
