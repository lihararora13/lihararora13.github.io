Darikhana is Jodhpur's fine-dining entry: the restaurant of RAAS, the boutique hotel at Toorji Ka Jhalra, where Rajasthani cooking gets the white-tablecloth treatment. It is the most polished meal on this list, and the one to book for a special evening in the old city.

The Rajasthani thali is the order, with the ghee held and the curd left off, and the dal baati done the same way. The pin is approximate to the old-city area around the stepwell, so navigate by the RAAS hotel.
