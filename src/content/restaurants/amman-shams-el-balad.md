Shams El Balad is set in a restored downtown building with rooftop seating and a farm-to-table focus, and its menu is mostly meat-free, treating vegetables as the main ingredient rather than a filler. It is a proper sit-down dinner spot rather than a quick lunch counter.

Order a falafel wrap and a hearty salad, ask what is vegan that evening, and enjoy the rooftop. It is the grown-up dinner on this list.
