Thyme & Whisk tops Vadodara's vegan-friendly list for the same reason it tops Surat's: a bright, leafy room, a menu that takes plant-based eating seriously, and a kitchen that knows the difference between veg and vegan. The Race Course Road branch sits at Chakli Circle, right in the city's café heartland.

Order a vegan pasta and a smoothie bowl, save room for the vegan dessert, and you will understand why this is the first name every local vegan mentions. It is the safest first stop in the city, and a good one.
