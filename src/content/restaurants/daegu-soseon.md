Soseon is a Korean restaurant on Dalgubeol-daero in Jung-gu, open from 8am to 3pm most days, with vegan-friendly dishes among its Korean menu. It is a daytime kitchen, so it suits breakfast and lunch rather than dinner, and the vegan dishes need asking for, as the menu is not fully plant-based.

Order the vegetable rice and tofu dishes, and confirm no egg or fish sauce when you order. It is inexpensive and central, a useful morning stop before the cafes open.
