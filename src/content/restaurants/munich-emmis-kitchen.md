Emmi's Kitchen is where Munich goes for a slow vegan breakfast, and its enchanted backyard in the Glockenbachviertel is one of the city's happiest places to sit with a coffee. The kitchen went fully vegan in 2018 and the menu reads like a greatest hits of brunch: tofu scrambles, Beyond burgers, salads, pots, muffins and cinnamon rolls, plus smoothies and juices. It opens at nine, the kitchen closes at four, and the room fills up fast on weekends.

Order the tofu scramble and a cinnamon roll, grab a table outside if the sun is cooperating, and settle in. This is the gentle, sunny counterweight to Munich's schnitzel-heavy heavyweights, and it belongs on any list of the city's best vegan tables.
