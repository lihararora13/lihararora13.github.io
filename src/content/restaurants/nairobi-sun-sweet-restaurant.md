Sun Sweet is a small CBD vegetarian Indian restaurant with a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow and a very welcoming owner. The cooking is classic Indian lunch food, dishes, sweets and savouries, with many plates vegan or vegan on request. It is the kind of unassuming spot that regulars guard jealously.

Go for lunch, ask what is vegan today, and let the owner guide you. The sweets counter is worth a look on the way out.
