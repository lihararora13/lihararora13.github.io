Cowvana is Magdeburg's newest vegan cafe, opened in late 2025 by a young local gastronome in a small passage off the Breiter Weg, and it has already become the city's sweet spot. The speciality is handcrafted 100% vegan cinnamon rolls baked fresh daily with local spelt flour from Magdeburg, from the classic cinnamon-sugar roll to wilder varieties like Schmand-Mandarine and savoury pizza rolls. Vegan soft serve ice cream, specialty coffee roasted nearby in Tangermunde, and seasonal winter drinks like gingerbread lattes round out the menu.

It opens Tuesday to Saturday, nine to six, so this is a daytime pick. The rolls are the reason to come, and they sell out, so earlier is better. This is the cafe to take anyone who still thinks vegan baking tastes like a compromise.
