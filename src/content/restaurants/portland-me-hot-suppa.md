Hot Suppa is Portland's breakfast-and-brunch room, a bright spot on Congress Street with a Southern lean and a vegan-friendly menu that goes beyond the usual fruit plate. The tofu scramble and the vegan breakfast sandwich are the orders, and the home fries are worth adding.

Weekend mornings get busy, so come early or on a weekday. It is the best breakfast on this list.
