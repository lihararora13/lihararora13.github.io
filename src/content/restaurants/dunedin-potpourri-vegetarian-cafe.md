Potpourri Vegetarian Cafe is a vegetarian cafe at 97 Stuart Street in central Dunedin, a short walk from both the Octagon and the railway station, rated 4.0 on HappyCow, and it has been feeding the city from the same site since the 1970s. The kitchen is entirely meat-free, with a substantial vegan section running through the menu: a Vegan Big Breakfast on rye sourdough with smokey beans and vegan sausage, a vegan burger, soup of the day, beans on toast, and vegan cabinet food including salads, pies, muffins, scones and cakes. Coffee and tea come with plant milks, and there is a separate gluten-free menu alongside the vegan options.

It reopened under new ownership in April 2022 and is still trading on the same site. It is open Monday to Friday 8:00am to 2:00pm and Saturday 9:00am to 2:00pm, so it is a breakfast and lunch room only. Say vegan when ordering, as some dishes such as the creamy mushrooms are modifiable rather than vegan by default.
