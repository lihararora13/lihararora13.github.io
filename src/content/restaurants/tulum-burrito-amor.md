Burrito Amor is the burrito to skip nothing else for in Tulum, and it has moved since the older guides were written: it now sits in La Veleta, a short ride from Centro, not on its old downtown corner. The vegan burrito comes wrapped in banana leaves with nopal, chaya, black beans, rice, pico de gallo and avocado with epazote dressing, and coconut-flour tortillas are available on request.

It is open daily from about 8am to 10pm. Use the La Veleta address on this page, not the old Centro address in older guides.
