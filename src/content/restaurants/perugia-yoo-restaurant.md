Yoo Restaurant is a vegan restaurant in Pretola, east of the centre, serving only dishes with no animal products, from organic or biodynamic agriculture, with an eye to eco-sustainability. The a la carte menu runs across antipasti, primi, secondi, contorni and dolci, presented with care for composition and colour, and every Friday the menu turns into vegan pizza night.

Order from the a la carte menu and come on a Friday for the vegan pizza night. It opens for dinner Tuesday to Sunday, reservations are welcome, and it is closed Mondays.
