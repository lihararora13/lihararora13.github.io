Zirael is the smallest room on this list and one of the warmest: about eight counter seats down a narrow Gion alley, run by a Russian chef and a tiny team who cook in front of you and ask about your allergies before you order. The food is Russian and Ukrainian leaning, dumplings, hamburg steak, ginger-fried soy meat, all served with rice and a huge salad of Kyoto vegetables with a dressing reviewers keep writing home about.

Go early, because they close when the food runs out, and expect a queue at lunch. It is the kind of place people eat at every night of their Kyoto trip and still miss afterwards.
