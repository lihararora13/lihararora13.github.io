Sanctuary Bistro is the special-occasion answer to Charlotte's vegan scene, and it earns the occasion. Chef Barry Horton's seasonal menu is entirely vegan and entirely gluten-free, and it reads like a dare: a charcuterie plate built from house-made cashew cheeses, crispy oyster mushrooms with spicy kale, seared lion's mane steak, butternut squash lasagna, and a dessert case that regulars call the best vegan desserts in Charlotte. The fall menu landed in September 2026 and the room, all exposed brick and big windows, stays intimate.

Book ahead, the bistro has only a handful of tables and reservations are highly recommended for brunch and dinner. It is the one spot on this list to dress up for, and the one that proves vegan fine dining is not an oxymoron.
