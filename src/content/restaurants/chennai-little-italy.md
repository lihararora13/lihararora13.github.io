Little Italy made the list for a reason. The vegetarian Italian chain's Nungambakkam outpost, with vegan pizza and pasta options that keep mixed groups happy. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Nungambakkam, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan pizza, Vegan pasta, Bruschetta if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
