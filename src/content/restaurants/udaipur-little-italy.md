Little Italy is Udaipur's Italian vegetarian restaurant, a long-running Ashok Vihar dining room that has fed the city's pasta lovers for years. It is the most Italian meal on this list, and the kitchen is happy to hold the cheese.

Order a vegan pasta or a pizza without cheese, start with bruschetta, and you have a proper Italian dinner in the middle of Rajasthan. It is a good family pick, with something for every generation at the table.
