Brown Bread Bakery has been the German bakery of Varanasi for decades, a bakery, cafe, restaurant and guesthouse rolled into one by Pandey Ghat, run alongside a learn-for-life empowerment project. Travellers have been coming here for coffee and croissants since the backpacker trail was young.

The breads are the draw, wholemeal loaves baked the German way, and the coffee is famously good. Plenty of the counter is dairy-free if you ask, and the apple pie is worth the walk alone.
