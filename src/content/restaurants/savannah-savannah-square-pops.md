Savannah Square Pops makes small-batch ice pops named for the city's historic squares, and there are vegan, dairy-free, and gluten-free flavours in the case every day. The shop also runs tours and pop-making demonstrations, and it marked its ten-year milestone in September 2026.

It is the lightest sweet stop on this list, and the most Savannah-specific one. Get a pop named for your favourite square.
