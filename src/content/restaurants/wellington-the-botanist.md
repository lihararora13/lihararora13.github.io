The Botanist is the south-coast stop on the list, a plant-based all-day cafe and restaurant on Onepu Road in Lyall Bay with a sheltered courtyard, views of the bay, and the highest HappyCow review count of any Wellington vegan spot. The cooking is inventive rather than worthy: a dirty cheeseburger, bang bang cauliflower, buckwheat and Oreo pancakes, and a drinks list of local organic wines, botanical cocktails and craft beer on tap. Bookings are recommended, and dogs and kids are both welcome.

It is the easy, breezy pick of the guide, the one for a long brunch that turns into lunch by the sea. Come for the pancakes in the morning or the cheeseburger and a botanical cocktail in the evening, and save it for a clear day so the courtyard earns its keep.
