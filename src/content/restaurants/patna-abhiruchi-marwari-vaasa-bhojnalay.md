Abhiruchi has served pure-vegetarian Marwari thalis on Fraser Road since 1931, which makes it the oldest kitchen on this list and one of the oldest in Patna. It is a bhojnalaya in the old sense: a simple dining room, a fixed thali, and food cooked the way it has been cooked for four generations.

Order the Marwari thali and ask for no ghee and no curd. The dal baati is the signature, and the gatte ki sabzi is worth asking about, though the curd in the usual preparation needs to be skipped.
