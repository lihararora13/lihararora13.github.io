Lamai Veggie is a long-running fully vegan Thai restaurant on Lamai Beach, rated 4.5 from 99 HappyCow reviews. It sits on Had Lamai 4, a little north of the gas station and the morning market on the same side of the street, and it serves a daily changing menu of Thai dishes with mock meat options. Honey was removed from the menu in 2016, so the kitchen is fully vegan.

There is no fixed menu to plan around, which is the charm and the catch: ask what is cooking that day and take whatever the kitchen has made. It is one of the cheapest proper vegan meals on the island, and it anchors the Lamai end of the scene alongside Pure Vegan Heaven a short walk away.
