Small Mart is the Bywater's all-day hangout, a mostly-vegan cafe that locals lean on for breakfast, lunch, and coffee. The vegan bagels with vegan cream cheese are a morning lifesaver, and the curry and chaat bowls bring Indian flavors into the po'boy capital with total confidence. Pond Coffee pours the lattes inside, so the caffeine game is strong.

It is casual, cheerful, and unfussy, the kind of place where you become a regular by accident. New Orleans' own tourism guide calls out Small Mart as a vegan must, and it earns the spot: great food, kind prices, and a menu that makes mornings easier.
