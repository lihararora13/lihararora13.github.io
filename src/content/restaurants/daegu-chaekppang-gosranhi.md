Chaekppang Gosranhi is a vegan bakery and bookshop in Samdeok-dong, Jung-gu, open Thursday to Saturday only, and known for its rice-salt bread. It is a small, bookish room where the baking is the point: vegan breads and pastries, coffee, and shelves to browse while you eat.

Come for the rice-salt bread and whatever is fresh that day, and do not plan a visit outside Thursday to Saturday, as the bakery is closed the rest of the week. Its pin is approximate to Samdeok-dong.
