Hotel Sree Sabarees is the pure-veg hotel restaurant on Travelers Bungalow Road, near the station and the old city, open from 6 in the morning. It is the family-dining version of the Sabarees name, with a broader menu that runs to North Indian as well as South.

Order the masala dosa or a North Indian veg curry, and ask for the dairy to be held. It is the comfortable sit-down option when the tiffin rooms feel too rushed.
