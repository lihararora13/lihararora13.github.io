Il Vegetariano is one of the oldest vegetarian restaurants in Florence, operating since 1981 near San Marco, with a self-service format and daily-changing dishes. About 80% of the menu is vegan, and a full meal costs about ten to fourteen euros, which makes it the best-value plant-based dining in the city.

It is cash only and closed Sunday. Go for lunch or dinner, pick from the counter, and pay at the till.
