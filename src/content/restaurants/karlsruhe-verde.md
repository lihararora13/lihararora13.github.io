Verde is a fully vegan restaurant and ice cream manufactory on the Kaiserstraße, a modern, eco-friendly room with a green hummingbird logo and a menu that takes vegan cooking seriously. The standouts are the roast bombe, a crispy potato croquette filled with braised plant-based schnitzel and roast gravy, and the tagliatelle frutti de mare, with a spicy wild-garlic cream sauce and grilled herb-mushroom calamari, followed by vegan ice cream made in house. It is the most ambitious cooking on this list.

Hours shift between lunch and dinner service, with a weekend brunch, so check the day before you go. Order the roast bombe, share a tagliatelle, and finish with the ice cream.
