Ecologica is a bio shop and bistrot on Via Chiavettieri in the old town, combining a low-price organic grocery with a kitchen that serves lunch and dinner daily. The menu runs from vegetarian lasagne to soy kebab with salad and vegan apple and chocolate cakes.

Order the soy kebab with salad, the vegetarian lasagne and a slice of vegan chocolate cake, and pick up organic groceries on the way out. It opens at half eight most mornings.
