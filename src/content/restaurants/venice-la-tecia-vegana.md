La Tecia Vegana is the one place in Venice where the entire menu is yours, an organic all-vegan kitchen in Dorsoduro about ten minutes on foot from the station. The cooking is Italian with the occasional turn east, the room is small and fills every night, and reservations are not a suggestion but a requirement.

Order the aubergine lasagne, the seitan with polenta, the ravioli if they are on, and leave room for the tiramisu or the raw cashew pistachio cake. Book before you fly, go early in the trip so there is room to go twice, and come hungry.
