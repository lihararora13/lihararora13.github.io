If you want a vegan slice that rivals New York, Bambino is the stop. This tiny, hugely popular slice shop on Stephen Street Lower turns out massive thin-crust slices with a proper crunch, and the queue moving down the pavement is part of the experience.

Go for the vegan hot honey pepperoni slice with melty mozzarella, sliced red chilies and drizzles of plant-based hot honey, and do not skip the vegan garlic sauce for dipping. Seating is minimal, so plan to eat standing up or on the curb like everyone else, ideally with the two-slice-and-a-drink meal deal in hand.
