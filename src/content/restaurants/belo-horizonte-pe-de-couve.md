Pe de Couve is a Belo Horizonte pioneer: the first vegan buffet in the city, offering more than 35 kinds of vegan meals in a family restaurant that makes everything in house. It is a feast, and the sheer choice is half the fun, from buffet plates to coxinha and warming caldos.

Come hungry, take a slow walk past the buffet before you choose, and go back for seconds. This is the one for a big, joyful vegan buffet lunch.
