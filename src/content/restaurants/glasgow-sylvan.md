Sylvan is the West End's health-focused vegetarian café and bar, and it is the table for a long, slow evening: a selection of natural wines, local independent beers and a menu with a strong vegan range, all of it served in a calm, modern room on Woodlands Road.

Come for dinner and a glass of natural wine. Order the vegan plate of the day, ask what is pouring, and enjoy one of Glasgow's most thoughtful rooms.
