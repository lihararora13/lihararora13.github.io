Botanica Lab is a plant-based fine-dining restaurant in the centre, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, and the most polished table on this list. The menu is built on fresh vegan pasta, gnocchetti with cashew cream, a large raviolo with cashew burrata, and seasonal desserts, with a chocolate cheesecake that earned it a place on a world best vegan cheesecake round-up.

Book ahead for dinner. This is the special-occasion pick in Bologna, so order the pasta and save room for dessert.
