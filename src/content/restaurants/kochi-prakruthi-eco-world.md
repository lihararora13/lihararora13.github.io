Prakruthi Eco World is Fort Kochi's millet kitchen: a 100% pure-vegetarian restaurant and eco wellness cafe cooking millet-based Kerala food in coconut oil, open daily from 8am to 9pm, with a 4.7 on Google from 219 reviews. The menu lists vegan dishes alongside the paneer specials, and the kitchen will customise dishes on request.

Order the millet biriyani, a millet dosa and a Ganoderma coffee, and expect homecooked food at very low prices with a wait, everything is cooked to order and regulars warn of thirty minutes or more. It sits at Thamaraparambu in Fort Kochi, a short walk from the beach.
