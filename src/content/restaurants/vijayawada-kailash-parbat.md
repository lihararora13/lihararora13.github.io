Kailash Parbat on DV Manor Road is the famous pure-veg restaurant that won the Food Connoisseurs India Awards 2025 for Best Restaurant in Andhra Pradesh, and the October 2026 video shows why: a huge menu from chaat and tandoori to Italian, Chinese, biryanis and desserts, with chole bhature, pav bhaji and a handi of vegetable biryani on the table.

Order the chole bhature and the vegetable biryani, and ask the kitchen to hold the paneer and dairy. It is the big pure-veg dining room in Vijayawada, and the award is on the wall for a reason.
