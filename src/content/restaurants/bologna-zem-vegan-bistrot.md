ZEM is a vegan bistrot on Via Mascarella whose mission is to cook with love and sell organic, local, plant-based food. The menu is the Bolognese canon made vegan: tortellini, tortelloni, tagliatelle, cotoletta alla bolognese and passatelli in vegetable broth, with a small shop alongside.

It is open for lunch and dinner every day. If you eat one vegan tortellini in Bologna, eat it here, then order the cotoletta too.
