Basic Kitchen is the sister restaurant to the Post House Inn, and it cooks seasonal, local produce to be nourishing, with a wide range of vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free dishes and staff trained to guide you through allergens. The Basic Bowl is the signature, the corn ribs are the fun order, and the buffalo cauliflower wings are the ones people photograph.

It serves brunch, lunch, and dinner daily. Order the bowl, add the sweet potato fries with vegan ranch, and stay for a zero-proof cocktail.
