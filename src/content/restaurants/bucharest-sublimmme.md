Sublimmme is the vegan and raw-vegan bistro-patisserie on Calea Victoriei, with burgers, wraps, soups, salads, smoothies and more than a dozen raw, gluten-free and refined-sugar-free cakes and desserts. The Buddha bowl is the main to order, and the raw cakes are the reason to save room.

It is open daily from 11am, until 10pm at this branch, which makes it the reliable Sunday vegan meal in Bucharest. A second location sits in the Old Town on Splaiul Independenței.
