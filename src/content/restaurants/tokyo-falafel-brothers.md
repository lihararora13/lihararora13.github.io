When Harajuku hunger hits late, Falafel Brothers is the hero on the sixth floor of Harakado. Everything is vegan, the falafel is made to order, and the menu stretches happily into burritos, salads, karaage, pizza and cake, so it works for a quick bite or a proper refuel.

Get the full sandwich set, enjoy the view over Jingumae, and save room for something sweet. It stays open to 11pm, which in vegan travel terms is basically a superpower.
