Cafe Affenbrot is a vegetarian café on Kanalstraße, and it is one of the most reliable vegan-friendly rooms in Lübeck: the vegan dishes are clearly marked, the vegan Vöner plate with seitan is the dish regulars order, and the Grünkernburger is the other one to know. The room is warm and old-town in feel, the prices are low, and the kitchen takes its vegan cooking seriously.

It sits a short walk from the Markt, open daily. Order the vegan Vöner plate, add a Grünkernburger if you are sharing, and ask about the vegan cakes. One caution: some of the cakes contain gelatine, so it is not strictly vegetarian despite the label, but the marked vegan dishes are the safe ones. This is the table for a solid, unshowy vegan meal in Lübeck.
