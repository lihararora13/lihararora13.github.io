Jax Beach Brunch Haus is a brunch restaurant on 3rd Street South in Jacksonville Beach, doing breakfast tacos with potatoes, tofu scrambles, and avocado toast with vegan options. It is the beach-day pick on this list, and the best place for a plant-based breakfast before the sand.

Order the breakfast tacos with potatoes, asking for the vegan preparation, and the avocado toast. Note that it sits in Jacksonville Beach, not Jacksonville proper, so plan the drive east.
