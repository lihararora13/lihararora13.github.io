Kentucky Native Cafe is a cafe inside Greyline Station, the market hall on West Loudon Avenue, doing salads, sandwiches, and bowls with vegan options. The market-hall setting makes it a good group stop, since everyone can order from a different stall.

Order the vegan salad and a grain bowl. The pin is the market-hall point, so navigate to Greyline Station rather than expecting a standalone storefront.
