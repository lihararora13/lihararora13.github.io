Sardarjee is Chapel Allerton's vegetarian Punjabi street-food spot, and it is gloriously specific: traditional, authentic Punjabi food, with a large variety of vegan pizzas, plus vegan curries, tandoori chick'n and burgers. It is a takeaway at heart, so order big and take it home.

Order a vegan pizza, add a curry, and try the tandoori chick'n. Punjabi street food, vegan, in the suburbs, and all the better for it.
