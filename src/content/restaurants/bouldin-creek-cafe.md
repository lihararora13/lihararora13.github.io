Bouldin Creek Cafe has been Austin's bohemian living room since
2000, the kind of vegetarian cafe where the lavender mocha is
famous and the patio never hurries you. The menu leans lacto-ovo,
but the vegan options are so deep it earns its spot on a vegan
list without apology.

Come for breakfast, stay for the patio. The Veggie Royale and a
scrambled tofu sandwich are the classics, and the vegan omelette
with chorizo is proof this kitchen takes plant-based seriously.
