Blue Nile is Ann Arbor's Ethiopian table, a downtown room where the vegan dishes are the point rather than the accommodation: a veggie combo of misir wot, gomen, and other wots, all scooped up with injera. It is the most shareable meal on this list.

Order the combo for the table and eat with your hands, as intended.
