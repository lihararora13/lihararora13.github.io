Greens Cafe is Bhubaneswar's answer to the modern veg cafe: a fourth-floor room in Saheed Nagar with a menu that wanders from Indian to Italian to Thai, a salad bar, a juice bar and delivery. HappyCow lists it as the city's main veg-options restaurant, and it is the easiest place in town to build a vegan meal from salads, stir-fries and Thai curries.

Ask for the Thai veg curry and the garden salad bowl, hold the cheese on the pizza, and finish with a fresh juice. It is a good laptop-and-lunch spot, and the rooftop feel of the top floor makes it one of the more relaxed rooms in the city.
