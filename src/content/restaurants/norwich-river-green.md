River Green is Norwich's destination vegan restaurant, set in Trowse just outside the city, in a converted space by the river. The cooking is seasonal and ambitious, and the Sunday roast has a loyal following.

Book ahead, especially for Sunday lunch. The tasting plates are the best way to see what the kitchen is doing that week.
