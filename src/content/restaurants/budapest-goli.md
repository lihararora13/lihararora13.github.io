Goli is the fanciest table on this list and the most likeable one too, literally: it was voted Dining Guide's most likeable restaurant in 2024 and holds a Michelin Bib Gourmand for 2024 and 2025. Chef Harel Zakaim's open-hearted kitchen near St. Stephen's Basilica cooks Middle Eastern food with no eggs or dairy anywhere on the menu, so the vegan plates are not adaptations but the point of the place.

Order the pickled black garlic with burnt bell peppers, the yellow zucchini on a stick, and the caesar salad made with soy milk, and let the kitchen's warmth do the rest. It is the one reservation to make if you want a proper dinner out rather than a quick bite.
