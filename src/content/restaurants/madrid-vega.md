VEGA, on Calle de la Luna just steps from Gran Via, is the creative heart of this guide: a 100 percent vegan kitchen turning out mushroom ceviche, plant-based burrata, eggplant teriyaki, tacos, pizzas and poke, with the menu changing as new ideas land. A second spot on Calle Alamo gives you a backup plan nearby.

The weekday lunch special is one of central Madrid's better bargains. Go for that if you can, stay for the zucchini lasagne in tomato cream if it is on, and expect to want to come back.
