Bungalow No. 6 looks like it was designed to be lingered in: tiered sage-green seating, soft light and a calm that feels a world away from LIG Road outside. The café is pure vegetarian, and the brunch plates, salads and pastas all bend vegan with a quick word to the kitchen about butter and cheese.

It rounds out this list as the pretty one, the place to end an Indore food day rather than start it. Come for a late brunch, order light, and let the room do the rest.
