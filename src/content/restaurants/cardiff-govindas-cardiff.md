Govinda's Cardiff is a vegan-friendly Indian restaurant in the Capitol Centre on Queen Street, formerly called The Atma Lounge. The thali is the signature, with a bhaji wrap, turmeric chai latte and mango lassi alongside, in a bright food-court room that suits a quick lunch.

One honest note: it was previously listed as vegan, but was reported in March 2026 to serve dairy, with paneer on the menu, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly. Order the vegan dishes clearly, and the thali is the pick.
