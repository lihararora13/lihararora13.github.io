Inside the Nashville Farmers Market sits a small taqueria with a big claim, Nashville's first plant-based taqueria, and the tacos back it up. Run by Adriana from Mexico City and Ronald from Costa Rica, Succulent serves proper Mexico City-style tacos with house salsas, plus tamales, enchiladas and weekend specials.

Go for the al pastor with pineapple, the asada, and a cup of esquites, then wander the market with a Jarritos in hand. Lunch only, closed Mondays, and popular, so arrive before the noon rush.
