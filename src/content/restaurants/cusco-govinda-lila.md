Govinda Lila is a tiny vegan Hare Krishna restaurant tucked in a corner of San Blas market, and it is one of the best-value meals in Cusco: a complete meal of soup, hot drink and main entree, served at a couple of small tables inside the market. It is simple, hearty and deeply local, and the 5.0 rating tells you the regulars love it.

Come for lunch, order the set meal, and wander the market afterwards. This is the one for a proper, cheap, local Cusco lunch.
