Jheel's Ginger Coffee Bar & Bakery is the old city's coffee stop: a small, charming room inside Chandpole, where the coffee is good, the bakes are fresh, and the lake is a short walk away. It is the best pause in a day of old-city wandering.

Ask for coffee with plant milk and whatever vegan bake is on the counter that day. It is a light stop rather than a full meal, and exactly right for a mid-morning break.
