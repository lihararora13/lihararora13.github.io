Bageecha is Bhopal's garden escape, a bistro about three kilometres past the airport where the city finally loosens its grip. Thatched cabanas, artificial turf, a little bridge over a water feature and a flower-decked wooden deck set the scene, and the kitchen is pure vegetarian, serving pizza, pasta, hot veg kothi and crispy dosa from 2:30 PM into the night.

It is the most transport-worthy pick on this list, and the most romantic. Order the dosa and the kothi, ask for no cheese on the pizza, and stay for the mocktails as the garden lights come on.
