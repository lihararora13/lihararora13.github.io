Haldiram Bhujiawala is the family default of Patna vegetarian dining: a sweets, bakery and restaurant chain whose Kankarbagh and Rukanpura branches top the city's pure-veg guides. The main branch is a confectionery store with a dine-in restaurant, and the fast food is the draw, dosa, chole bhature and pav bhaji alongside the sweets counter.

Order the chole bhature or a masala dosa, and ask for the pav bhaji without butter. It is open until 11 at night, and with nearly 7,000 ratings behind it, it is one of the most-reviewed veg spots in the city.
