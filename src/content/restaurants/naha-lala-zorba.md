LaLa Zorba is Naha's fully plant-based ethnic kitchen, near Miebashi station, cooking Thai, Indian, Vietnamese and Nepalese dishes with no meat, fish, egg, dairy or white sugar. The plates to order are the vegan taco rice, the pumpkin coconut curry and the dal soup, and the banana cheesecake and smoothies are the finish reviewers remember.

Sources disagree sharply on its schedule, some list a dinner-only service closed Sunday and Monday, others a mixed lunch and dinner schedule closed Tuesday and Wednesday, so treat any specific hours as unconfirmed and check directly with the restaurant before visiting.
