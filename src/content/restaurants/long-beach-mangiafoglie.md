MangiaFoglie is Retro Row's vegan Italian spot: a fully vegan restaurant and coffee shop doing pasta, salads, pastries and pizza, rated 5.0 by HappyCow reviewers. The pizza is the headliner, the pasta is the sleeper, and the pastries are the reason to come for coffee.

It is small, charming, and very Long Beach, the kind of place where dinner turns into an evening. Order the pizza, add a pastry, and enjoy the Row.
