Fasoule is the name Beirut guides give for fresh mezze platters done properly: traditional ingredients, authentic flavours, and a spread that makes a meal out of small plates. Foul, tabbouleh, hummus and salads are all vegan by tradition here.

Order a mezze platter for the table and let it be the meal. This is Lebanese vegan eating at its most natural.
