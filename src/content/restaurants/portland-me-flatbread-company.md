Flatbread Company is the Old Port's wood-fired pizza room, and its vegan pizzas, built on dairy-free cheese and the same thin, blistered crust as everything else, are a proper dinner rather than a compromise. The house salads and the local beer list round it out.

It is a big, busy room on the waterfront, so it works well for groups where not everyone eats vegan.
