Ngoc Chi is the local favourite that makes downtown Da Nang worth the trip: a vegan restaurant serving banh mi, com tam and Vietnamese classics at local prices, in a simple room full of regulars. It is the opposite of the beachside expat cafes, and that is exactly the point: this is how Da Nang eats vegan every day.

Come for lunch, order a banh mi and a com tam, and pay less than the price of a coffee back home. This is the one for a proper local meal.
