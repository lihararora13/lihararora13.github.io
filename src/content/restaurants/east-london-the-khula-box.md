The Khula Box is a 100% plant-based eatery inside the Ashmel Spar Centre in Vincent, with lime-green walls, bulk jars of grains and seeds, and a chalkboard menu of burgers, wraps, Buddha and poke bowls, juices and smoothies. Regulars swear by the chickpea burger, the sweet beet burger and the Verde juice, and it is the first choice for eating out in the city.
