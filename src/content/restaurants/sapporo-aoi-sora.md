Aoi Sora, which means blue sky, is the bright, airy organic cafe of the Maruyama list: plant-based Japanese plates with miso soup, green salad, brown rice and three rotating dishes, plus soy ice cream and grain-based coffee.

Come for lunch and arrive early, it gets busy. The lunch plate and the soy ice cream are the order.
