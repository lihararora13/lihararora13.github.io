Gokul Brindavan, Reddy's to regulars, has been pouring filter coffee and frying medu vada on WHC Road since 1960, and it shows in the confidence of the cooking. This is the cheapest meal on Nagpur's list, around 150 rupees for two, and one of the most satisfying.

The medu vada and masala dosa are the classics, with upma and idli sambar close behind. It closes early on Thursday afternoons, so plan around that, and bring cash-ready expectations rather than fine-dining ones.
