Miss Margarita is Byron's Mexican party spot, and it is a dream for vegans because every single menu item is labelled vegan or can-be-made-vegan. Tacos, guacamole, margaritas and a buzzing cantina atmosphere make it the obvious pick for a fun night out with a mixed group: nobody has to compromise, and the vegans eat just as well as everyone else.

Come for dinner, order tacos and guacamole to share, and wash it down with a margarita. This is the one for a loud, happy group dinner.
