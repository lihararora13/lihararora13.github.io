Frary's sits on a fondamenta a minute from the Basilica dei Frari, the enormous Gothic church where Titian is buried, and it has been serving Middle Eastern food beside it long enough to count as an institution. The plant-based half of the menu is the larger half, and the prices are fair for San Polo.

Order the mezze when there are two of you, because you get the whole range for less than two main courses: falafel, hummus, vine leaves, spiced rice, and a vegan dessert to finish. It works as lunch on either side of seeing the Titians.
