Nom Nom is the all-vegan counter in the Carnivale Food Market on Splaiul Unirii, a space to nourish mind, body and spirit with an exclusively plant-based menu and no preservatives, artificial flavours or sugars. The Buddha bowl and the quesadilla are the standing dishes, with soups and daily mains alongside.

It is open Monday to Friday from 11am to 8pm and Saturday until 6pm, and closed Sunday. Come for a healthy lunch in the food hall, not a dinner.
