When the craving is dirty vegan junk food, Bristol heads to Oowee Vegan on Baldwin Street. This award-winning plant-based eatery has been serving American diner-style comfort food since 2018: burgers with seitan made in-house, wraps, loaded fries, fried chickn strips, buffalo cauliflower wings and thick milkshakes, all of it fairly priced and fully vegan.

It is casual, loud and exactly what you want after a long day. Order a burger and the buffalo cauliflower wings, add a shake, and do not try to be restrained about it.
