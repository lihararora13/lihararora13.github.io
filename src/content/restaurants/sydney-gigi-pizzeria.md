Gigi's is the pizzeria that proves you don't need to lose anything when you go fully vegan: it converted its entire menu to plant-based back in 2015 and then earned AVPN certification, the real-deal Naples stamp of authenticity, for its wood-fired Neapolitan pies. A traditional pizzeria since 2008, it keeps the Italian methods strict and just makes the mozzarella from plants. The room is simple, warm, and designed around watching dough get shaped and slid into the oven.

There's no reservations, so arrive early and expect a queue, especially on weekends. Work through the pizza list, then save room for the hazelnut cake, a patron favourite described as creamy and decadent, and the vegan Pastiera, a cashew-cream take on the traditional Neapolitan Easter cheesecake that surprises even Italians.
