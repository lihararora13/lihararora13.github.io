Ginger Mule moved into the space vacated by Houston's old favorite Verdine and promptly became the new favorite. The menu roams the globe: jackfruit birria tacos, hearts of palm "fish" tacos, cozy Thai curry, sancocho soup, and a butternut squash lasagna that regulars talk about like an old friend. Everything is made fresh on site, no processed faux anything, and the brunch lineup runs from scrambles to pancakes to french toast.

Save room for dessert or live with regret. The vegan tres leches, made with oat flour and coconut milk and glazed with warm guava sauce, got a dedicated write-up from Eater Houston. The cacao ceremony at the bar from 2 to 6 on weekday afternoons is the kind of thing other cities simply do not have.
