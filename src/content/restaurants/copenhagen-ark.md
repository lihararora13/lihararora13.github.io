Ark is the headline act: the first vegan restaurant in the Nordics to win a Michelin Green Star, built entirely around local, seasonal and foraged ingredients, with mushrooms from its own farm and ferments in almost every course. VegNews calls it 100 percent vegan Michelin-quality fine dining, and every Copenhagen guide puts it first for a special night out.

Come for the full tasting experience and let the kitchen surprise you, the menu is not published in advance and changes with the seasons. Reviewers and listings in 2026 still show it open for dinner and thriving, elegant without being stiff, and very much a dinner that feels like an event. Book well ahead.
