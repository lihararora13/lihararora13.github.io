Keiai, written 桂愛, is a vegetarian Chinese restaurant in Nakamachi with a 5.0 rating on HappyCow and a menu that is almost entirely vegan: only a few dishes contain egg, and it uses no garlic or chives. The daily lunch set, around ¥980, rotates through dishes like mapo tofu and soy sauce ramen, and the a la carte menu runs to mock Beijing duck, deep-fried karaage, jaja-men topped with fried lotus root, and vegetable dumplings.

Reviewers in November 2026 call it a pure vegan and vegetarian discovery, and the room, all red couplets and lanterns, is a warm change from the city's Japanese tables. Confirm with staff on the few egg dishes, and you are set.
