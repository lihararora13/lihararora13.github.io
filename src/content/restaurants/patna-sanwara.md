Sanwara is a pure-vegetarian restaurant on S.P. Verma Road that keeps it simple: dosas, idli, vada and uttapam, done well enough to earn a 4.6 rating and the claim of serving the best vegetarian food in Patna. The masala dosa and Mysore dosa are the dishes to order, and the idli vada is the light breakfast move.

Dosas are naturally close to vegan as served, so this is one of the easiest plant-based meals in the city. Ask for no butter on the dosa and you are set.
