Crave Fit is Lucknow's answer to eating clean without eating boring. Every parcel is labelled with its macros, and the vegan Mexican superfood bowl has become the signature order for the city's gym-and-yoga crowd.

It is a delivery-first healthy kitchen rather than a linger-over-coffee cafe, so order in. The veg protein overdose salad and the quinoa bowl round out a menu that takes plant-based eating seriously instead of treating it as a side quest.
