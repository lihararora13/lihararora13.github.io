Ānanda is a fully vegan Indochine tapas and sushi bar on the Sankt-Peters-Weg, the kind of place that makes Regensburg's vegan scene feel modern rather than dutiful. The menu runs from vegan sushi and maki through tapas plates to curries, and the kitchen is confident with all of it. It keeps generous hours, lunch and dinner seven days a week, which makes it the reliable evening table when the old town's other vegan kitchens have closed.

Order a vegan sushi set and a plate of tapas to share, add a tofu curry if you are hungry, and treat it as the night out on this list rather than the lunch stop.
