Frambuesa is the sugar stop on this list and it takes the job very seriously. Founded in 2012 as a delivery-only project, it opened its own storefront in 2018 and has been Bogotá's only fully vegan bakery ever since, a bubblegum-pink room decorated with flowers and sprinkles where the dessert case is filled every day with donuts like chocolate peanut, passionfruit and Oreo cookie, plus cakes, brownies, cookie jars and a separate gluten-free case. The food menu runs to burgers, pasta and sandwiches too, for anyone who needs something savoury before the sugar.

Do not skip the limonada de coco, the creamy coconut lemonade that is traditionally made with cow's milk and is vegan only at spots like this one. It is tart, sweet and the perfect chaser to a Matilda cake.
