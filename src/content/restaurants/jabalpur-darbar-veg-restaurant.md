Darbar is a pure-veg family restaurant in the heart of Napier Town, the kind of place that describes itself, with feeling, as making love edible. The menu is North Indian comfort food: chole bhature, dal makhani, paneer tikka, Kashmiri pulao and veg biryani, served in a family dining room from 11 AM to 11 PM.

Order the chole bhature and the veg biryani, ask for the dal makhani without butter, and you have a proper Jabalpur family meal. Unpretentious, filling and central.
