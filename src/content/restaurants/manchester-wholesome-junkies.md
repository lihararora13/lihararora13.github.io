Wholesome Junkies is the award-winning plant-based junk-food flip with street-food attitude, tucked into the Hinterland Bar on Turner Street in the Northern Quarter. The room is subterraneanly cosy and funky, the menu runs from brunch through dinner, and reviewers call the truffle burger insane, the Korean tofu bites so tasty, and the Kentucky Fried Cauli burger a proper hit with a generous helping of vegan mac and cheese, tater tots and house fries on the side.

It is open Wednesday to Saturday with Sunday brunch, closed Monday and Tuesday, and it does takeout and delivery too. Order the truffle burger, the Korean tofu bites, the kimchi tater tots, and the KFC if you are feeling brave. This is the table for the friend who thinks vegan food cannot do comfort food.
