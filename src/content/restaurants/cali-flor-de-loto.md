Flor de Loto is a 4.5-rated vegan spot in the centre of Cali, serving tacos, burritos and bowls on an outdoor patio. The food is bright and generous, the kind of place where a taco order turns into a second taco order without much discussion.

Order the vegan tacos, add a burrito if you are hungry, and sit outside. It is a straightforward, cheerful kitchen, and one of the most reliable vegan lunches downtown.
