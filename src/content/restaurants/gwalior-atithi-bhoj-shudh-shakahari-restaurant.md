Atithi Bhoj is the old city's budget stalwart: a pure-vegetarian restaurant on Nai Sadak, near Delite Talkies, where ₹300 covers two people and the menu is North Indian through and through. It holds a 4.1-star Google rating, and the dining room is the kind of unpretentious, fairly priced place that locals rely on daily.

Order the North Indian thali without ghee, add dal and seasonal sabzi with roti, and you have the cheapest proper sit-down veg meal in Gwalior. A fitting, honest end to a list that leans on the old city as much as the new cafés.
