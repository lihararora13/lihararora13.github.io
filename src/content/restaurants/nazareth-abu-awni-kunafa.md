Abu Awni is an Arab dessert bakery on Al-Bishara Street with a vegan pastry named nemura. It is a bakery rather than a restaurant, and the vegan pick is specific: the nemura is vegan, the baklava is not.

Order the vegan nemura and an Arabic coffee. Do not assume the rest of the counter is vegan, and ask before you buy.
