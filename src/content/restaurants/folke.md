Why we love it — Folke is what happens when a kitchen commits fully to
vegetables and never looks back. The menu changes with the seasons, the
share plates reward curiosity (beet tartare, chickpea tofu with za'atar),
and the no-tipping, hospitality-included model makes the bill refreshingly
drama-free.

This is the date-night pick for people who read menus like novels. Book the
tasting menu, sit at the bar if you can, and let the kitchen do the talking.
