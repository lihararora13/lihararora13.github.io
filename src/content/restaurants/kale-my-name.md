Kale My Name is the vegan restaurant that acts like a party you were
lucky to get invited to. The menu bounces from the Chicago Burger to
jackfruit lox bagels to cauliflower wings that one reviewer swore were
the best they had ever had, and the brunch has won citywide awards.
Everything is 100 percent plant-based, the portions are generous, and
the room is always buzzing.

This is the place you take the skeptic in your life. Two bites of
that burger and the "but where do you get your protein" conversation
simply never happens.
