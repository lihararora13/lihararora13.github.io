Falafel etc does exactly what the name promises, and does it very well. Three kinds of falafel, hummus, sweet potato fries and flatbread made in house, in a cosy little room south of the centre.

Order falafel by the piece if you want to taste all three, or go straight for a large sandwich. It is cheap, fast and the kind of place you will want to return to the next day.
