The Shaka Cafe is the coolest room on this list: a pure-vegetarian Asian cafe on the first floor of Pushpak Complex in Prahlad Nagar, from the Shakahari by Awadhpuri group, with a menu by a globally travelled chef and a long list of vegan dishes. It is open daily from 11am to 11pm and its own site is live and trading.

Order the dim sum sushi, the white dumpling and the orange and peppermint dim sum, the chimichurri, and a Tokyo ramen bowl or the Himalayan Yeti Ramen Bowl. Note that it is pure vegetarian with vegan options, not a fully vegan kitchen despite what some blogs claim, so ask for the vegan dishes by name.
