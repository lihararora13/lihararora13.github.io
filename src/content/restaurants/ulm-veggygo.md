VeggyGO is a vegetarian and vegan street-food eatery on the Marienstraße in Neu-Ulm, just across the Danube from Ulm's old town, doing burgers, tacos, burritos, salads and more. The menu is built for the fast-food crowd that happens to eat meat-free: veggie burgers, vegan nuggets, kimchi fries and wraps, all of it served quickly and cheaply. It is the casual counter on this list, and the one for a hungry evening rather than a slow lunch.

It is open Monday to Saturday, eleven to nine. Walk across the river, order a veggie burger with kimchi fries, and treat it as the easy dinner after a day in Ulm.
