Yonder is a vegan hotspot on Church Street in Queenstown, serving pancakes, vegan breakfast butties and rice bowls. It is a bright, modern cafe in the centre, and it is the natural first stop for any vegan visiting Queenstown.

Order the pancakes and a vegan breakfast butty, and try the rice bowls. It is central and reliable, and it suits a morning coffee or a light lunch.
