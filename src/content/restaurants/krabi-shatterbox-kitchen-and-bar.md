SHATTERBOX Kitchen and Bar is a restaurant in Ao Nang with a broad menu that the kitchen can adapt for vegans: hummus and falafel, vegan burgers, vegan tacos and vegan pasta, with pad Thai and many other Thai dishes also preparable vegan, and dishes normally containing cheese can be made with vegan cheese. It is not exclusively vegan, so make it clear that you are vegan when ordering.

Order the vegan burger and the hummus and falafel, and ask for the pad Thai vegan. It is a good choice for a mixed group, and its pin is approximate to Ao Nang.
