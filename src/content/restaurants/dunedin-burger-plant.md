Burger Plant is an entirely plant-based burger joint at 654 Great King Street in North Dunedin, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, and it is the only fully vegan place in the city open late. The kitchen started life at the Otago Farmers Market under the name Straight Up Vegan, selling house-made vegan sauces and burgers, before moving into a permanent shop run by owner Thomas White. The menu is built to win over sceptics: eight burgers including the That Fungi burger with grilled mushrooms in sticky Szechuan sauce and the VChicken burger with a pineapple ring, plus loaded fries, four types of loaded donuts, three types of vegan chicken bites, thick shakes, cookie pies, and an Asian menu with ramen, gyoza, mapo tofu and Eden curry.

It is open seven days, 11:00am to 10:00pm, later than most cafe kitchens in the city, which makes it the default vegan dinner in Dunedin. Seating is very limited, so treat it mostly as takeaway.
