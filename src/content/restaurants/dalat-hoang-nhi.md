Hoang Nhi, also called Quan Hoang Nhi, is a vegetarian restaurant on Phan Dinh Phung in central Dalat, open roughly 6am to 9pm, with mushroom pho, vegetarian mi Quang, banh mi, tofu, spring rolls and a substantial selection of other Vietnamese dishes. It is a practical choice for anyone staying around the market and Hoa Binh, and it is the best early vegetarian breakfast in Dalat.

Order the mushroom pho and a vegetarian banh mi, and ask for the vegan dishes by name, as the kitchen is vegetarian. It is inexpensive and central.
