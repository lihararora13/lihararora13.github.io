Vegan Valley is the reason Kozhikode leads this guide: a cafe that calls itself Kerala's first vegan cafe, and a November 2026 video shows exactly why, with a vegan burger and wedges, a quinoa and avocado Buddha bowl, green pesto pasta with garlic bread, a wrap and a dessert served in a coconut shell, all in a bright modern room.

Order the burger and the Buddha bowl. The food is free from sugar and additives as well as animal products, and the cafe is the single best vegan meal in the city.
