Yadokari is the headline act of Fukuoka vegan food: a fully vegan counter shop doing the thing you would assume impossible in Hakata, a plant-based version of the city's rich tonkotsu ramen. It is also Muslim-friendly and skips the five pungent vegetables.

It is a short walk from Yakuin-Odori Station and the counter is small, so arrive early. Order the tonkotsu-style ramen and prepare to be converted.
