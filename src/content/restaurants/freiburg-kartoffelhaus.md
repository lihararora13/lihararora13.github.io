Das Kartoffelhaus takes the potato seriously, which in Freiburg means very seriously: rösti, gratins, sweet potato curry, tempeh and seasonal specialities, with an extensive menu of vegetarian and vegan, lactose-free and gluten-free dishes and a clear note of where everything comes from. It is a proper sit-down restaurant rather than a fast-food counter, and the vegan dishes are full plates rather than afterthoughts.

It sits on the Basler Straße in the Stühlinger, open for dinner every evening and for lunch at weekends. Order the sweet potato curry or a gratin, and treat it as the hearty, Black Forest answer to a vegan dinner.
