Life Alive is an organic cafe in Harvard Square serving grain bowls, salads, soups, and smoothies, with a menu that leans heavily plant-based. The bowls are the draw, built from grains, roasted vegetables, and house sauces, and the room is bright and fast.

Order the Rainbow Harvest bowl and a green smoothie. Ask the staff to confirm the dressing is vegan, since a few contain honey or dairy.
