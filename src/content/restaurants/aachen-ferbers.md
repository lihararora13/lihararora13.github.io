Ferbers is a café and restaurant in Burtscheid on Kapellenstraße, and its claim on this list is a named vegan breakfast served in its daily morning service: a substantial plant-based start to the day with Mediterranean plates and brunch dishes alongside. The wider menu is mixed, so order the named vegan breakfast rather than improvising from the regular menu.

It is open every day from nine, until ten on weekdays, midnight on Friday and Saturday, and ten on Sundays. Come for a slow Burtscheid morning, order the vegan breakfast, and walk it off around the Burtscheid spa quarter.
