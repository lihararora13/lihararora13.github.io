Vegan International Co. Kitchen and Market is an all-vegan kitchen and market on North Nebraska Avenue, doing plates, sandwiches, and grocery staples with no animal products anywhere in the room. It is the strongest pick in Tampa, and the place to eat and stock up in one stop.

Order the vegan plate and a sandwich. Note the distance: North Tampa is a drive from downtown, so plan it as a north-side stop.
