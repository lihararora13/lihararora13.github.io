Mayan Cafe is an upscale Mayan restaurant on East Market Street in NuLu, with specifically noted vegan options on the menu. The sikil pak, a pumpkin-seed dip with tortilla chips, and the fried plantains are the vegan signatures, and the room is the most polished on this list.

Order the sikil pak and the fried plantains with a vegan entree. Book ahead, since it is the priciest room on this list.
