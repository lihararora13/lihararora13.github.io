Lord of the Fries made the list for a reason. The Australian vegan fast-food franchise on Hindley Street: burgers, hot dogs, nuggets, onion rings, fries and shakes, all plant-based. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Adelaide CBD, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan burger, Nuggets, Loaded fries if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
