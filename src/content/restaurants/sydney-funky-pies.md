Nothing says Australia like a meat pie, and Funky Pies has been quietly veganising the whole tradition from a little shop near Bondi Beach for years. Every pie is hand-made on the premises, the flavours roam the globe, from a No Wurry Curry with chickpeas and lentils to a Spicy Thai with chic'n chunks and a Rustic Bolognese built on organic tempeh, and the gluten-free range gets its own loyal following. Even the milkshakes, cupcakes and carrot cake are all plant-based.

This is the cheapest, cheeriest stop on the guide: grab a Funky Chunky, mash and peas on the side, and take it to the beach. The whole operation exists to make food that treads lightly on the planet, and each bite apparently cuts your carbon footprint by a whopping 90%. Pie-based climate action, at your service.
