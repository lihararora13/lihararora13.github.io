Why we love it — Viet Chay is the kind of place locals guard jealously: a cozy North York room where every Vietnamese classic arrives plant-based and bursting with authenticity. The pho is a rainy-day hero, the spicy Bun Bo Hue warms from the inside out, and the fried king oyster mushrooms are dangerously snackable. Reviewers call it award-worthy and the owner is famously warm.

It is a bit of a trek from downtown, but worth every subway stop. Go hungry, try the vegan lobster special, and leave with a new favorite restaurant.
