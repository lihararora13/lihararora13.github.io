Nepenthe Brewing Co is a Hampden brewpub with a taproom and a kitchen that keeps vegan options on the menu. It works as the evening stop in a Hampden crawl: beer brewed on site, a relaxed room, and plant-based plates that hold up on their own.

Order the vegan burger with a flight of house beers. Check the board for the vegan appetizer of the day, since the kitchen rotates it.
