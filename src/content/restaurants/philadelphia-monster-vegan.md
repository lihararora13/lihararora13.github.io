Monster Vegan is owned by horror-film devotees, and it shows: neon signage, monster movies on loop, and a private screening room with horror marathons on Thursdays. But the scares stop at the decor, because the comfort food is deadly serious and beloved, with a 4.7 rating across 1,200-plus reviews.

The cheesesteak is the headliner, built on seitan or vegan ribeye, but the General Tso's cutlet hoagie and Korean BBQ wings have their own cult followings. It is the rare themed restaurant where the theme is the least interesting thing about it.
