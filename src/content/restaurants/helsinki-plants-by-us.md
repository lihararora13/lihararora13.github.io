Plants By Us is the practical hero of this list, a fully vegan cafe on the third floor of the Forum shopping centre in Kamppi. The menu is sandwiches, smoothies and bowls, all of it plant-based, and it is the easiest vegan lunch in the city centre when you are out shopping or between meetings.

It is not fancy, and it does not need to be. Order a sandwich and a smoothie, sit by the window, and recharge before heading back out.
