Col' Cacchio's Simonsplein Centre branch on Plein Street is Stellenbosch's vegan pizza stop, with vegan cheese and a few labelled vegan pizzas on the menu: margherita, bianca and lazio. You can also build your own.

It is open daily from noon to 9:00pm. Order the vegan margherita or the lazio, and note the pin is at the Simonsplein Centre on Plein Street.
