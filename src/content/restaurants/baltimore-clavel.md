Clavel is a Remington mezcal bar and taqueria that grinds its own masa in-house, which is the detail that makes its tacos worth the trip. The room is loud, warm, and built for mezcal, and the vegan tacos sit comfortably alongside the meat versions rather than as an afterthought.

Order the vegan tacos and the guacamole, and ask the staff which taco fillings are vegan that day, since the menu rotates. Come early or expect a wait on weekend nights.
