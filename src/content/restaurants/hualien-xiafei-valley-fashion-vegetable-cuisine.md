Xiafei Valley Fashion Vegetable Cuisine is a vegetarian restaurant in Hualien offering noodles, mock meats, soups, rice dishes, tea and glutinous rice cakes. Most dishes are vegan, though you should ask the staff to confirm, as the kitchen is vegetarian rather than fully vegan.

Order the noodles and the mock meat dishes, and finish with glutinous rice cakes. Its pin is approximate to the city centre, so check the address before you walk over.
