Vegangster is a vegan cafe and fast-food spot in the KTV quarter, open since February 2014, serving burgers, voner, langos, pakora and soup, with cakes, tortes and tiramisu for dessert. It is rated 4.9 from 238 reviews and is the loudest, most loved vegan room in Rostock.

Order a burger, the voner and a langos, and finish with a cake or the tiramisu. It opens Wednesday to Sunday, noon to eight.
