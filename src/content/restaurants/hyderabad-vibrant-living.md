Vibrant Living made the list for a reason. An all-vegan organic shop and cafe in Jubilee Hills with a health-focused Indian menu, juices and breakfast, open from 7am. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Jubilee Hills, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Millet bowl, Vegan thali, Cold-pressed juice if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
