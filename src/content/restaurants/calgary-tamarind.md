Tamarind has been fully vegan since 2015, which makes it one of Calgary's longest-running all-vegan kitchens. The menu is classic Vietnamese done without compromise: vegetable pho, savoury crepes, lemongrass tofu, curry soup, pad thai and fried spring rolls. It relocated to 14th Street SW and was reported open in March 2026.

It is a small, friendly room, and the pho is the order. Add the spring rolls and the lemongrass tofu and you have a perfect Vietnamese dinner.
