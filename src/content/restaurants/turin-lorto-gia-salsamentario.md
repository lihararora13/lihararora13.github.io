L'Orto Gia Salsamentario is a restaurant that is 100% plant-based, cooking free of animal products in San Salvario. The menu is the most ambitious on this list: agnolotti del l'orto, seitan roast cooked in Barbera, stuffed zucchini flowers with smoked tofu cream, eggplant parmigiana and a matcha tiramisu.

It relocated from Via San Dalmazzo to Via Monferrato, so use the current address. It is open for dinner most nights and lunch on Saturdays only, so book ahead and plan the evening around it.
