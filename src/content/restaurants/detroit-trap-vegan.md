Trap Vegan is the Livernois corridor's vegan comfort-food spot: fully plant-based, doing loaded cheeseburgers with their special Trap sauce, nachos, tater tots and fresh smoothies, all of it bold, indulgent and designed to trap you into coming back. The cheeseburger is the signature, and the tots are the sleeper.

It is a feel-good, fast-casual room, and the one to hit after a drive up Livernois. Order the cheeseburger, add tots, and finish with a smoothie.
