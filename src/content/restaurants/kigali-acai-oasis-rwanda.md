Acai Oasis is a fully vegetarian acai bar on KG 637 Street, offering vegan acai bowls, chia seed puddings and fruit salads. Vegan options include berry acai bowls with fresh fruit, granola, coconut and peanut butter, plus smoothies and waffles.

Order a berry acai bowl, and ask for plant-based milk and protein, and no honey. It is open daily 7am to 8pm, and it is the breakfast stop on this list.
