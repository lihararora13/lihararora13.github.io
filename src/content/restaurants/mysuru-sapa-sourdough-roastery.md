Sapa is Mysuru's sourdough obsessive, a roastery and pastry counter where the bread is slow-fermented and the vegan options are labelled rather than hidden. It sits on the VV Mohalla border of Gokulam, an easy walk from the yoga quarter, and it keeps civilised hours: 8:30 in the morning to 6:30 in the evening, closed Tuesdays.

Buy a loaf, eat a pastry, and drink the coffee. The sourdough is the reason to come, and the vegan bakes are the reason to stay.
