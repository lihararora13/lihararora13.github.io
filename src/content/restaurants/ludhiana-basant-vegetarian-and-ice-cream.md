Basant Vegetarian and Ice-Cream tops Ludhiana's vegetarian rankings by Google ratings and review counts, a sweets and food counter in Feroze Gandhi Market that locals have been eating at for years.

Order the veg thali without ghee and the chole bhature without dahi. The ice cream is for your dairy-eating companions, and the savoury side is the reason to come.
