Why we love it — Hand-pressed tortillas, a house cashew quesillo that behaves
exactly like the real thing, and seitan al pastor with actual char. Mis
Tacones is queer, Chicano-owned, and Eater Portland's answer to the question
of where the best vegan tacos in town are. The answer is here.

Get the seitan taco trio so you can stop pretending you have a favorite
between the al pastor, the asada, and the cilantro-lime. If you're still
standing after that, the al pastor torta is the encore.
