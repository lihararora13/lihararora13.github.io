Los Muchitos is Oaxaca's vegan cafe for a proper, filling lunch: tacos, fajitas and a burger, all vegan, all served in a friendly room in Centro. It is the kind of place where the menu is short because everything on it works.

Order tacos and fajitas for the table. It is cheap, fast and satisfying, and the best refuel between sightseeing stops.
