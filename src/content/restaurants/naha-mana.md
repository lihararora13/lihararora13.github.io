Mana is the gem of Naha's vegan scene: a small all-vegan café tucked into an alley in the Tsuboya pottery district, a few minutes from Kokusai-dori, serving a brown-rice set lunch built on organic, pesticide-free Okinawan vegetables. The plates run to dal curry, tempura pumpkin and seasonal small dishes, with desserts like soy-lemon cheesecake, and lunch is roughly ¥1,000.

Sources disagree on the exact hours and closed days, and the café serves lunch only, so confirm the current hours before you go. It is worth the small effort: reviewers call it a beautiful, cosy place and the food all delicious.
