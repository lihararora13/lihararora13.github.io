Ops! is the easiest lunch in Rome and the cheapest on this list. The buffet turned fully vegan in 2013 and has been packing the room ever since: lasagne and cannelloni, risotto and couscous, ravioli and cheese plates, salads, soups and a dessert spread with tiramisu, all of it vegan, all of it pay by weight. Reviewers keep coming back two days running and calling it the best buffet they have ever eaten.

The room is scruffy and cheerful, the kitchen runs lunch and dinner with a break in between, and the whole thing is a masterclass in how good vegan food gets when nobody is trying to impress anyone. Fill the plate, weigh it, sit down happy.
