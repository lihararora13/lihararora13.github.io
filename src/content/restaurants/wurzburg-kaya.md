Kaya Salat Feinkost is a salad and fine-food deli on the Nürnberger Straße, east of the old town, doing fresh salads, bowls and healthy deli food with vegan options. It keeps long daytime hours, seven to eight Monday to Saturday, which makes it the early-morning and lunch stop on this list, the place to go when you want something light and fresh rather than a full sit-down meal.

Order a vegan salad bowl, add a fresh juice, and treat it as the healthy counterweight to Würzburg's burgers and döner.
