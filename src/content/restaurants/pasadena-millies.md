Millie's is Pasadena's breakfast cafe with a proper vegan menu: a cozy room on East Washington Boulevard with a full vegan menu insert that covers brunch and lunch, soyrizo benedict, tofu scramble, tofu wrap, sandwiches, avocado toast, baked goods and an acai bowl. HappyCow lists it vegan-friendly with a full vegan menu, and local guides call it a vegan-friendly favorite. It is the morning pick in this guide, open 8am to 3pm daily.

Ask for the vegan menu insert on arrival. Order the soyrizo benedict and the pancakes, and check the pastry case for vegan baked goods.
