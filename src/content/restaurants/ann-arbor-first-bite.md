First Bite is a bright, health-focused cafe on Main Street, open 9am to 3pm daily, and it is primarily geared towards vegans, with almost the whole menu available gluten-free. The beet burger and the avocado toast are the lunch orders, and the blueberry pancakes are the brunch one.

It is a calm, quiet room that works well for a slow morning. Come for brunch, and ask what can be made vegan if it is not marked.
