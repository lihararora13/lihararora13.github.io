Miyazaki is a Japanese restaurant on Evergreen Street with a vegan selection across ramen, sushi and gyoza. It is a good evening choice in the centre.

Order the vegan ramen and vegetable sushi. Ask for the vegan options to be made without egg or fish, which the kitchen does on request.
