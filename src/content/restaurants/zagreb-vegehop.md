Vegehop is the Zagreb local favourite, a warm, unhurried vegan restaurant where the daily menu does the talking. Soups, pastas, seitan and tempeh, curries, fresh bread, salads and cakes, all cooked from whole grains, legumes and local produce, with eco wine and fresh juices alongside.

Note that HappyCow flags it as closing soon, so go while you can and check hours before you walk over. Order the daily menu, add a soup, and finish with a cake made with agave or dried fruit instead of refined sugar.
