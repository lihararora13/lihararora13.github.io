Meshek Barzilay has been feeding Tel Aviv's vegans since 2013 and went fully vegan in 2018, and it still feels like the warm heart of the scene. The menu wanders happily from Israeli breakfasts to Indian thali to beetroot gnocchi, all organic, with gluten-free options marked and a deli shop where you can take the flavours home.

Come for a weekend brunch and book ahead for Friday evening or Saturday, when the leafy patio fills fast. It is the rare restaurant that works equally well for a lazy breakfast, a family lunch, or a first stop for visitors who want one place that explains Tel Aviv vegan food in a single meal.
