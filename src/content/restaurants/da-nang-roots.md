Roots is the An Thuong cafe that expats recommend first: fully plant-based, bright and welcoming, with smoothie bowls, tofu scrambles, vegan burgers and cakes, all a short walk from My Khe Beach. It is the kind of place where you come for breakfast and end up staying through lunch, laptop open, coffee refilled.

Come for breakfast, order a smoothie bowl and a tofu scramble, and settle in. This is the easy, everyday vegan cafe in the beach neighbourhood.
