Hans im Gluck is the burger grill and bar on Arndtstrasse, in the heart of the centre between Bahnhofstrasse and Karl-Eilers-Platz, with a large vegan range of burgers, salads, bowls and snacks. The chain was ranked second most vegan-friendly in Germany by ProVeg in 2024, and it is the burger pick on this list.

Order a vegan burger, a salad and a bowl, and stay for a cocktail at the bar in the evening. It opens daily at noon.
