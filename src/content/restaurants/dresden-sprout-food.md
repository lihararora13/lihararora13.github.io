SPROUT FOOD is Dresden's healthy lunch counter, a small Neustadt room where you build your own bowl from warm and cold salads, grains, spreads and dips, then add soups, wraps, salads, smoothies and desserts. The food is always vegetarian and mostly vegan, made from organic and regional ingredients, with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options clearly labelled. It is fast, fresh and filling, and the small backyard seating area is popular in summer.

The catch is the hours: lunch only, Monday to Friday, roughly midday to three, closed on weekends, no reservations. Arrive early, because the room fills quickly and food can run low after one. This is the weekday lunch pick, not a dinner plan.
