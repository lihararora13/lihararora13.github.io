100% Bio sits in the shadow of the Pyramid of Cestius, a few steps from Piramide metro, and it is the most useful all-day stop on this list. Breakfast means vegan croissants and cappuccinos, lunch is a pay-by-weight buffet of organic dishes, and the evening aperitivo spread is one of the best fully plant-based ones in Rome. Everything is certified organic, the room is clean and modern, and the tiramisu gets its own fan club.

It has been fully vegan since June 2024 and the 2026 guides all back it up. Get there early for the widest breakfast choice, and leave room for the jam-filled crostata.
