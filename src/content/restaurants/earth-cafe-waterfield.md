Why we love it — Earth Cafe is Mumbai's vegan answer to the neighborhood
cafe, and the Waterfield Road original is where it started. The entire menu
is 100% vegan: all-day breakfast, burgers, kimbap, pasta, and a dessert
counter that regulars plan visits around. Reviewers rave about the staff and
the cozy, work-friendly room.

The avocado bruschetta and the Vietnamese cold coffee are the twin pillars
of a visit. Come for breakfast, stay for the desserts.
