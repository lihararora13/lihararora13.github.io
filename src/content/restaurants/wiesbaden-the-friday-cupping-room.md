The Friday Cupping Room is a vegetarian coffee shop on Nerostrasse, open Wednesday to Sunday, with vegan cakes, wraps, sandwiches and plant milk for coffee. It is the quiet coffee stop on this list.

Order a vegan cake or wrap with an oat-milk coffee, and come in the daytime, since it closes at six.
