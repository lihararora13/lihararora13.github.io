Caapora is part restaurant, part grocery store, part neighbourhood hangout in Campeche: a vegetarian and vegan spot serving chapati, puri, sangas and healthy plates, with live music and a warm, easy atmosphere. It is the kind of place where you come for lunch and stay for the music, and the grocery shelves are worth a browse too.

Come for lunch, order a chapati and a sanga, and check what is playing. This is the one for a relaxed Campeche afternoon.
