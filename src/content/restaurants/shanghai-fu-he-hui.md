Fu He Hui is the meal Shanghai's vegan scene is built around: two Michelin stars, a place on Asia's 50 Best list, and a kitchen that cooks with the seasons instead of leaning on substitutes. Chef Tony Lu's tasting menu changes through the year and every course is plant-based.

Book well ahead, dress up a little, and let the kitchen lead. This is the special-occasion pick, and it earns every bit of its reputation.
