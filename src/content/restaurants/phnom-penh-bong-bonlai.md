Bong Bonlai, meaning Vegetable Friend, is an all-vegan restaurant tucked inside the courtyard of the YK Art House guesthouse, with an innovative menu spanning Mediterranean and Asian flavours: moussaka, keftedes, French toast and a king mushroom Aussie burger.

Most Sundays it offers a brunch where all plates are one dollar, which is the best-value vegan meal in the city. It is fully vegan, it is open daily from 7.30am to 8.30pm, and it is the most creative kitchen on this list.
