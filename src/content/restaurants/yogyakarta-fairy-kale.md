Fairy Kale is a vegetarian cafe on Dewi Sartika, and it is one of the most-loved vegetarian rooms in Yogyakarta, with a varied menu of vegetable pizza, pasta and fresh salads. The room is bright and casual, and the pizza is the dish visitors photograph first.

Order the vegetable pizza and a salad. It is a vegetarian cafe with vegan options rather than an all-vegan one, so file it as vegan-friendly, and confirm the cheese is vegan when you order.
