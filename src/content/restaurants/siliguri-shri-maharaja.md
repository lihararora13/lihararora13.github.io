Shri Maharaja sits on Station Feeder Road below the SBI Bank, and it is the workhorse veg restaurant of the station area: open from 8 in the morning, serving thalis, dosas and North Indian staples to travellers and locals alike.

Order the veg thali or a masala dosa, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you have a cheap, reliable meal a short walk from the station. It is a simple room, and that is the point.
