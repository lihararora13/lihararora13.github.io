Avit is the modern one: a plant-based restaurant near Syntagma that blends Mediterranean soul with a modern edge, and it holds a perfect 5.0 from 235 HappyCow reviews. Mornings bring a brunch line-up with the Avit Breakfast of fluffy toasted brioche, scrambled tofu, vegan bacon and avocado, plus maple French toast topped with strawberries, Chantilly cream and basil. Dinner moves to starters like beef tomato tartar and mushroom calamari fritti, and hearty mains like bucatini bacciatore, pepper steak and the famous truffle cheeseburger.

Every dish is made in house and finished with tasty sriracha, lemon mayo or Avit's infamous pink sauce, and the drinks list runs to vegan wines, craft cocktails and specialty coffee with oat, soy or pea milk. Save room for the baklava cheesecake or the red wine velvet cake.
