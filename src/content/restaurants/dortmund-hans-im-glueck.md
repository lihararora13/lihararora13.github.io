Hans im Glück is the German burger chain that has spent over fifteen years building out its vegan menu, and the Dortmund branch on Wißstraße is the reliable late-night option on this list: plant-based burgers, sweet potato fries, salads and bowls, open Monday to Thursday until midnight and Friday until one in the morning. The chain was ranked among Germany's most vegan-friendly by ProVeg, and the Dortmund kitchen carries the full plant-based range.

Order a plant-based burger with sweet potato fries, ask for the vegan sauces, and treat it as the safe late dinner when the Kreuzviertel cafés have closed. It is a chain, but it is a chain that takes its vegan menu seriously.
