Bonsai Plant Kitchen tops HappyCow's Brighton list with a perfect 5.0, and it is the restaurant the city's vegans are most excited about right now. The kitchen is fully vegan and Southeast Asian in spirit: small plates, curries and bao sliders, with daily desserts and cocktails, cooked with the kind of flavour-first confidence that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food.

It sits on Baker Street by London Road, a short walk from the station. Order a spread of small plates and bao sliders for the table, and save room for dessert.
