Hummus Abo Adnan is the hummus specialist the guides send people to when they want the dish done creatively rather than as a side. Hummus is vegan by nature, and here it is the main event, with foul and fresh bread alongside.

Order hummus with your choice of toppings, add foul, and mop it all up with bread. Simple, cheap, and very Beirut.
