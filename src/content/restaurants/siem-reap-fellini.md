Fellini is Siem Reap's Italian restaurant with a vegan menu, believe it: starters, pastas, pizza with vegan mozzarella, dessert and wine. It is a bit pricey by Siem Reap standards, but it is the place if you are looking for Italian food after a week of Khmer cooking.

Order a vegan pizza and a pasta, and save room for dessert. It is a meat-serving Italian restaurant with a dedicated vegan menu, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note the pin is neighbourhood-level for Bamboo Street.
