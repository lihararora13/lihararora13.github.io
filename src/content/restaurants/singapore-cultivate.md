Cultivate is the calm one: a fully vegan, gluten-free and GMO-free cafe tucked into the Maxwell Reserve, where lunch comes with soaring ceilings, shelves of books and food that treats vegetables like the main event rather than the apology.

The Earth Bowl is the signature, the hummus and broccolini make a lovely shared start, and the juices and wellness shots keep the whole thing feeling like a reset. Come for a slow lunch, leave plotting a return for cake.
