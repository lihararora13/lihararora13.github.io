XiaoQiao DaMei is the polished vegetarian table near Xinjiekou: a restaurant on the third floor of the JLC Jinling Zhonghuan mall on Wangfu Street, serving a wide selection of Chinese dishes, many of which are naturally vegan. It holds a 4.0 on HappyCow and was last updated in August 2026.

Order the black truffle fried rice, the mushroom dumplings, the tofu and mushroom stew and the braised eggplant, and finish with mung bean pastries. Note that a few tofu dishes contain egg, so confirm ingredients when ordering. Open 11am to 2pm and 5pm to 9pm on weekdays, 11am to 9pm at weekends.
