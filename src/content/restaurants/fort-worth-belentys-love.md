Belenty's Love is widely considered the top fully vegan restaurant in Fort Worth, a vegan Mexican kitchen near TCU in Blue Bonnet Circle, bright with Frida-inspired art. The birria tacos are the dish people drive across town for, the crispy cauliflower wings are the starter, and the birria ramen and birria pizza are the experiments that work.

It is open daily and pet-friendly, with outdoor seating. Order the birria tacos, add the wings, and come hungry.
