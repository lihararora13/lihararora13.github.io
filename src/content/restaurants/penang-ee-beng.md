Ee Beng is Penang vegan eating at its most joyful and most affordable: a small restaurant on Lebuh Dickens serving traditional Chinese-Malaysian meals, an economic rice buffet, different fried mee, fried rice, a large choice of steam buns, banana leaf curry and more, plus a big choice of drinks. It is the kind of place locals eat at every week, and the value is extraordinary.

Come for lunch, point at what looks good, and fill your plate. This is the one for a proper, cheap, delicious Penang lunch.
