Hearts Choices is Calgary's family-run vegan institution, and the northeast cafe-market is its beating heart. The menu runs Thai and Western comfort side by side: khao soi, pad thai, cauliflower wings, corn dogs and a seitan beef dip, plus a shelf of vegan groceries to take home.

Reviewers call it the best meal ever and keep coming back for the ginger beef. It is takeout-focused, open Monday to Saturday. Order the khao soi, add the cheeze fries, and grab a chocolate bar for later.
