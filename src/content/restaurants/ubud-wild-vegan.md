Wild Vegan is the quiet overachiever of Ubud's vegan scene. The little restaurant on Jalan Sugriwa serves creative vegan Balinese and Indonesian cooking in a calm, natural space: breakfast plates, burgers, pasta, rendang and raw dishes, all made with fresh local ingredients. HappyCow's community rates it a perfect 5.0, and it was reported fully vegan again in June 2026, which is why it lands on this list now.

It opens daily from 10am to 10pm, right in central Ubud. Come for a relaxed lunch between temple visits, and let the rendang do the convincing.
