Greentable is Hamburg's vegan Asian comfort spot: bowls, rice, noodles, spring rolls, crispy baked wontons, pho and red curry with tofu, plus desserts like coconut panna cotta with raspberry-lime sauce and black sticky rice with mango. It turned fully vegan in July 2025 and the kitchen has not looked back.

It sits right in the Altstadt, easy to reach from anywhere central. Order the pho, add the wontons, and finish with the panna cotta. Warm, fragrant and very Hamburg.
