Why we love it — An inn has stood on this Homerton site since the
1700s, and in 2018 it became something even better: the first fully
vegan pub in London. The beers are brewed without animal finings, the
cocktail list rotates, and the kitchen treats Sunday lunch with the
reverence it deserves. The Beet Wellington has a devoted following,
and regulars call it one of the best Sunday roasts in the city, vegan
or otherwise.

It is warm, dog-friendly, and full of actual regulars on a Wednesday
night, which is the highest compliment a pub can earn. Go for the
roast, stay for the quiz, order a Bloody Mary made with vegan
Worcester sauce.
