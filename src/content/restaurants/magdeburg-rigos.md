Rigo's is a neighbourhood German pub deep in Stadtfeld Ost, a mom-and-pop room full of locals, card games and good beer, where the vegan dishes live on a chalkboard that changes with the kitchen. The cooking is honest home food at honest prices, and the vegan options, soups and mains among them, are a real part of the menu rather than a token. Dogs are welcome, the whisky list is long, and the atmosphere is the point as much as the plate.

It opens for lunch Tuesday to Friday and dinner most evenings, closed Mondays. Ask what is on the vegan chalkboard when you arrive, because it changes. This is the pick for a low-key local evening, the Magdeburg that residents actually eat in.
