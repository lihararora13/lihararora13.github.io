Anthony Vegetarian Restaurant is Melaka's reliable Chinese-Malay vegetarian workhorse: a buffet and a la carte menu, air conditioning, outdoor seating, and a room that fills with locals at lunchtime. It has been trading since April 2017 on Jalan Laksamana Cheng Ho, the street where the city's vegetarian supply shops and a temple cluster, and it is the kind of place where you point at the buffet, take what looks good, and eat well for very little.

It is vegetarian with vegan options rather than fully vegan, so ask about egg and dairy in the buffet dishes. It is open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday to Sunday, 10am to 8:30pm, and closed on Wednesdays. Its neighbour Hui Yuan, at 6B on the same street, runs a similar daily buffet if Anthony is full.
