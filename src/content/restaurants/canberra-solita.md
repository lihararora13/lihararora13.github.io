SoLita is Canberra's temple of true Neapolitan pizza: the only pizzeria in town accredited by the Naples association, with hand-stretched dough, a wood-fired oven and a menu that takes vegan diners seriously. Pizzas can be made vegan with vegan cheese, and the menu marks vegan and vegan-option dishes clearly, from bruschetta and salads to pasta and a peppers-and-potatoes pizza. The room is warm and rustic, with mosaic tiles and wood panelling.

It is a proper Italian night out that happens to work beautifully for vegans. Book ahead, order a Margherita with vegan cheese, and finish with the artisan sorbet.
