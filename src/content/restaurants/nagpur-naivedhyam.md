Naivedhyam is Nagpur's South Indian institution: a busy, no-nonsense dining room at Rani Jhansi Square where the thatte idli and chole have their own fan club. It is cheap, fast and consistently good, which is why it has lasted.

Order the thatte idli and a masala dosa, skip the ghee roast, and you have one of the best vegan breakfasts in the city for very little money. Come early, because the lunch crowd is real.
