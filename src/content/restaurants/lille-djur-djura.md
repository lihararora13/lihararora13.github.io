DjurDjura is an Algerian restaurant on Boulevard Jean-Baptiste Lebas, and it brings the most distinctive cooking on this page: couscous, tagine and falafel, with vegan dishes that lean on vegetables, pulses and spice rather than substitutes. It is the table to pick when the French bistros start to blur together.

The kitchen is vegan-friendly, so confirm the vegan options with the staff. The couscous is the order, and the tagine runs it close.
