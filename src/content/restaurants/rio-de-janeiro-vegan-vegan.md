Vegan Vegan is the kind of place you would never find without someone telling you: a small house at the end of a dead-end street off Rua Real Grandeza, with a garden, fruit trees and shade. It has been cooking vegan Brazilian food since long before it was fashionable, it holds a 4.9 from more than 150 reviewers, and almost everything is made from scratch with organic ingredients where possible. The menu leans into the classics done properly: palm heart lasagna, feijoada, tofu and tempeh dishes, seasonal gluten-free specials, fresh juices and homemade desserts, some sugar-free.

It is mostly a lunch place during the week, with dinner later in the week, and it is closed on Mondays, so it is worth calling ahead. Order the palm heart lasagna, take the soup or salad starter and the tea of the day, and sit in the garden. This is Botafogo's quiet masterpiece.
