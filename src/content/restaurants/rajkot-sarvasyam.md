Sarvasyam is Rajkot's South Indian fine-dine restaurant: an authentic dining room on Kalawad Road in Virda Vajdi, where the banana-leaf thali is the event. It runs on Sundays from 11am to 4pm, at 550 rupees plus tax, and it is worth planning your week around.

Order the banana-leaf thali without curd, add a masala dosa, and you have the most South Indian meal on this list. Come on a Sunday, come hungry, and come ready to eat with your hands.
