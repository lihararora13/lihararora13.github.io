La Botanica is the Strip's all-vegan Mexican restaurant and bar: a big covered patio, a 100 percent vegan menu of nachos, burgers and tacos, and a beer and cocktail list that makes it a proper night out. Reviewers call the food creatively delicious and the atmosphere a gem, and the 4.4 stars across more than 800 reviews back it up.

It is the party stop on the list, kid-friendly and dog-friendly, with DJs and community events. Order the vegan nachos, grab a cocktail, and settle into the patio.
