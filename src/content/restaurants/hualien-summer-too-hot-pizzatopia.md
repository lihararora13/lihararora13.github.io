Summer Too Hot Pizzatopia is a vegetarian pizzeria on Zhongmei Road serving vegan items such as ice cream, pizza with non-dairy cheese, hummus, salads and wraps. It is a lacto-ovo vegetarian kitchen, so the vegan dishes are a subset of the menu, and you should ask for the vegan pizza by name.

Order the vegan pizza with non-dairy cheese and the hummus, and finish with vegan ice cream. It is the best vegan pizza in Hualien and a useful change from noodles and buffets.
