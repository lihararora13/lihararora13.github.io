Padmanadi is a family-run Indonesian vegan restaurant with a devoted following across Alberta, and the Calgary branch is every bit as good as the Edmonton original. The menu is a tour of Indonesian and pan-Asian comfort: ginger beef, bakmi goreng, rendang, dendeng, roti canai and satay, all plant-based.

Reviewers say the ginger beef tastes like the real thing and the portions are generous. Order the ginger beef and the bakmi goreng, and save room for the roti canai.
