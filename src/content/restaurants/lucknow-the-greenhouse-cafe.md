The Greenhouse Cafe is the leafy, light-filled answer to Lucknow's vegan question up in Janakipuram Extension. Grain bowls, avocado toast, fresh salads and berry smoothie bowls fill the menu, and the room itself is the kind of green, calm space that makes a slow brunch feel like a small holiday.

Order the quinoa and roasted vegetable bowl, add an avocado toast, and finish with a berry smoothie bowl. It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so say the word and the kitchen will keep the cheese and dairy off your plate.
