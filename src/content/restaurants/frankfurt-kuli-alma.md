Kuli Alma tops HappyCow's Frankfurt list with more than two hundred reviews, and it earns them with Mediterranean and Israeli food that is all made in-house. The sharing plates are the way to eat here, hummus, falafel, salads and warm breads, in a relaxed Nordend room that has been going since March 2019.

Come with friends, order a spread for the table, and let the kitchen do the work. This is Frankfurt's vegan scene at its most generous.
