Backyard Cafe is the sister cafe to Vibe, a bright, airy, plant-filled room specialising in vegetarian, vegan and raw food. The menu runs from earth bowls and quinoa, pumpkin and hummus salad to smoothie bowls, cold-pressed juices and organic coffee, and the interior is one of the most stylish in the city.

Order an earth bowl and a cold-pressed juice, and come for brunch or an afternoon work session. It is a flexitarian cafe with strong vegan options rather than an all-vegan one, so file it as vegan-friendly, and like Vibe it donates 10 percent of profits to the Good Vibe Foundation.
