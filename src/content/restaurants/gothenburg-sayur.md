Sayur is a small Indonesian warung doing 100% plant-based food, and it is one of Gothenburg's most beloved lunch spots. The menu is Indonesian comfort: gado gado, nasi campur, tempeh, bakwan jagung and nasi kuning, all of it vegan, all of it inexpensive, and all of it made with care.

It is open Tuesday to Saturday, lunchtimes mostly, so check the hours before you go. Order the gado gado, add tempeh, and enjoy a taste of Indonesia in Linné.
