Casa Lėlytė is the one to book when the evening calls for something special. Hidden inside a boutique hotel of the same name in Chapinero, it has been quietly serving Bogotá's most creative vegan cooking since 2017, a menu that changes with the seasons and folds Mediterranean, Asian and Colombian flavours together using mostly organic produce from small producers. Every dish arrives like a small work of art, and reviewers keep coming back to the same two: a pecan ragu slow-cooked for eight hours and finished with macadamia buttermilk cheese, and a watermelon ahi tuna that should not work and absolutely does.

It is the priciest table on this list and the most refined, open for lunch and dinner with a proper bar. Come hungry, come curious, and let the pecan ragu do the talking.
