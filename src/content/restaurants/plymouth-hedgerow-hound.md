Hedgerow Hound trades from Plymouth Market, cooking seasonal plant-based small plates and bowls. The menu changes with what is available, and the stall is a market favourite.

Order the seasonal small plates and ask what is foraged this week. Market hours apply, so come at lunch rather than dinner.
