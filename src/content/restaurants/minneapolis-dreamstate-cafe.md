Dreamed up by the crew behind Reverie, Dreamstate is Uptown's plant-based cafe and bar where mushrooms get the star treatment. Lion's mane steaks, scallop-style linguine, and mushroom dashi ramen share the menu with espresso drinks and a cocktail program that includes plenty of zero-proof fun.

The space is bright, the patio is breezy, and the vibe is date-night ready. It's the kind of place that makes vegan look easy and effortless, because here it is.
