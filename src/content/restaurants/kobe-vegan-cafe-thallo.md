Vegan Cafe Thallo is Kobe's only fully vegan restaurant, and it is entirely gluten-free too, run since 2017 by an owner with a background in animal-welfare advocacy, a few minutes on foot from Motomachi Station. The menu is short and careful: vegan quiche sets in tomato, spinach or curry, enzyme brown rice, rice-flour muffins and gateau chocolat, with no chemical seasonings and no microwave in the kitchen.

It opens weekends only, roughly 12:30 to 18:00, and lunch reservations are required, so book ahead rather than turning up. That small effort is the price of the best vegan table in Kobe, and the 5.0 rating says it is worth it.
