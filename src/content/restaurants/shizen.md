Shizen did not just open a vegan sushi restaurant in 2015. It invented the
category. Before it, plant-based sushi meant sad avocado rolls; after it,
rolls like the Proud Peacock layered shiitake, tofu, and eggplant under
blankets of pickled mango and roasted pepper, and the Michelin Guide took
note. The izakaya side of the menu keeps the hits coming too: spicy garlic
miso ramen, tempura brussels sprouts, karaage that crunches like it means
it.

Every major 2026 roundup we checked puts Shizen at or near the very top,
and VegNews ranked it the best vegan restaurant in the world. It is the
rare pioneer that never got complacent. Book ahead, order the ramen and a
specialty roll, and watch a skeptic convert in real time.
