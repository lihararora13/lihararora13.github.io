Green Republic is a small, cheerful vegan cafe and juice bar out towards the Supernova shopping centre, and it has quietly earned a perfect HappyCow score. Smoothies, poke bowls, sandwiches and raw cakes, all light, fresh and made to order.

It is worth the short trip out of the old town if you want something raw and bright. Order a poke bowl and a raw cake, and treat it as the lightest meal of a very full Ljubljana day.
