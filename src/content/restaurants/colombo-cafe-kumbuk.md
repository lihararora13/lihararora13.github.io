Cafe Kumbuk made the list for a reason. A garden cafe in Colombo 7 promoting healthy, sustainable living with local seasonal produce and a generous vegan menu. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Colombo 7, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan English breakfast, Jackfruit tacos, Hummus bowl if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
