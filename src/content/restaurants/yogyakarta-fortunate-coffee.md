Fortunate Coffee Jogja is a haven for vegetarian and vegan food near Malioboro, serving a diverse menu of plant-based dishes from soto and burritos to Vietnamese rolls, teriyaki rice and vegan cheese fettuccine. The room is cosy, the prices are affordable, and the vegan breads and Javanese tea are the extras to try.

Order the teriyaki rice and a soto soup. It is a fully vegan cafe, open daily from noon, and it is the best lunch stop between Malioboro and the station.
