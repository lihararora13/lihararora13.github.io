Auflauf is a regional restaurant on the Peterplatz, near the Residenz, cooking Franconian food with vegan options on the menu. It is the sit-down alternative to the Juliuspromenade counters: a proper room, a proper menu, and dishes that change with the season rather than a fixed fast-food list. For visitors who want to eat regional food without eating meat, it is the most useful table in this part of the city.

Order the vegan Auflauf of the day or the seasonal vegetable main, and treat it as the dinner that shows Würzburg's traditional side can make room for plants.
