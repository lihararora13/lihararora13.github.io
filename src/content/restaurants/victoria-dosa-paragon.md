Dosa Paragon is Victoria's South Indian pick: a vegetarian restaurant on Yates Street in downtown Victoria, with masala dosa, vegetable uttapam, sambar and chutney, and filter coffee made with oat milk. It is the cheapest proper meal in this guide, and the dosa is the dish to order. HappyCow lists it vegetarian, so specify vegan when ordering: the dosas are naturally vegan or easily made so, but the coffee and some accompaniments need the oat milk request.

Order a masala dosa and a filter coffee with oat milk. It is downtown, so it pairs with an afternoon at the Inner Harbour.
