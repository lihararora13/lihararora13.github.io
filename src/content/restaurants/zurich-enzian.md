Enzian is Zurich's vegan bakery, a Niederdorf favourite serving flaky croissants, cakes and pastries, all plant-based. It is the sweet stop on the Zurich list, and the croissants are the ones to beat.

Order a croissant and a slice of cake, take a pastry for the walk along the Limmat, and enjoy the fact that Zurich does vegan baking this well.
