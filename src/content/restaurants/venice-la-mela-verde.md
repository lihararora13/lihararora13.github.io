La Mela Verde is an artisan gelateria on the Fondamenta de l'Osmarin in Castello, and the sorbets are why people cross the city for it. The dairy-free flavours sit in the same cabinet as everything else, so you ask which is which, and the staff will let you taste before you choose.

Order the vegan sorbets: chocolate, fig and watermelon are the ones people rave about. Check the cone as well as the gelato, because the cone is where dairy tends to hide, and sit on the public bench outside, a rare free seat in a city where almost every chair belongs to a cafe. Open 11 to 10, closed Wednesdays.
