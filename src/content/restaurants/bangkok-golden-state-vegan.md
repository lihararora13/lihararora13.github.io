Golden State answers the question nobody expected Bangkok to ace: where is the great vegan sushi? In Ekkamai, it turns out, where this Californian-inspired spot rolls up colourful maki, nigiri and vegan seafood with real swagger.

The Rainbow Sushi Party is the table-filling move, the bento is the sensible-but-fun one, and the crab cakes prove the kitchen is not a one-trick roll. Add a retro cocktail, some vinyl on the speakers, and dinner becomes a night out.
