Casa Umbigo is more than a cafe: it is a vegan cultural space hosting brunches, pasta nights, burger festivals and other events, with a menu of sandwiches, pancakes, bread, cakes, quiche and muffins. The room is warm and welcoming, and the events programme makes it worth checking before you visit.

Come for brunch, order pancakes and a sandwich, and see what is on. This is the one for a slow, happy vegan morning in BH.
