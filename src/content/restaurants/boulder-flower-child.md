Flower Child is Boulder in restaurant form: bright, healthy, and built around vegetables without ever feeling like a lecture. Everything is made from scratch, and the menu is designed so vegans can build exactly the bowl or wrap they want, from the sweet-potato-noodle Glow Bowl to the Mother Earth Bowl.

It is open daily for lunch and dinner, with a dog-friendly patio. Come for a quick, genuinely good lunch that leaves you full but not heavy. The avocado smash toast is the sleeper hit.
