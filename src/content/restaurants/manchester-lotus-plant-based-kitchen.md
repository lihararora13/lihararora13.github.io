Lotus Plant Based Kitchen is the fully vegan Chinese restaurant on Wilmslow Road in Withington, about half an hour south of the centre by bus, and it is worth the ride. The dining room is spacious and welcoming, the portions are big, the menu runs from dim sum and bao to sizzling mock meats, and reviewers call the food amazing and the staff lovely. The popcorn tofu, the pad thai and the sizzling Cantonese beef get named again and again, and the battered ice cream is the pudding to finish on.

It is evenings only and closed on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, so call to book before you come, especially Friday to Sunday, when tables can be scarce. Order the popcorn tofu, the buttery prawns, the pad thai and the sizzling Cantonese beef, and bring your own bottle. This is the table for the group that wants a feast rather than a snack.
