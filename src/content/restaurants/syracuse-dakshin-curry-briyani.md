Dakshin Curry and Briyani is an Indian restaurant on Walton Street in Armory Square, doing curries, biryanis, and dals with a solid vegan bench. It is the Indian pick on this list, and the chana masala is the reliable order.

Order the chana masala and the vegan biryani, and confirm which breads are dairy-free, since most naan is brushed with butter or ghee.
