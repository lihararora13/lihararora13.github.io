Glass Onion is the prettiest vegan-friendly cafe on this list, a veggie and vegan spot on Allerton Road with an enclosed garden for sunny days. Everything is house-made, from the seitan steaks and soups to the sandwiches, quiche, sharing platters, pancakes and full English breakfasts, and most of the menu can be made vegan with plenty of gluten-free options too.

It is a little way out of the centre in Mossley Hill, which makes it a lovely slow-morning stop rather than a dash between sights. Order the seitan steak, save room for cake, and linger in the garden if the sun is out.
