Jumon is one of the few city-centre restaurants where vegans get the whole menu rather than a corner of it. The kitchen pulls in flavours from Malaysia, Japan, Thailand and beyond: laksa, ramen, pad thai, dim sum-style small plates and bao buns, with a serious cocktail list. It is loud, modern and confident, and it draws a mixed crowd rather than a niche one.

Order the vegan laksa or the bao buns, plus whatever the seasonal dim sum special is on the night. There is a second site at 402 Newtownards Road in East Belfast, but the Fountain Street original is the one to book first.
