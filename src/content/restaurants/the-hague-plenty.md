Plenty is a coffeehouse and plant-based kitchen on Piet Heinstraat in the Zeeheldenkwartier, established in May 2019, with exclusively 100% vegetable dishes on the menu. The room is a white, atmospheric living room, and the menu runs from specialty coffee, pancakes, fresh breads and bakery treats to sandwiches, salads and avocado toast, with Middle Eastern influences throughout.

It is open every day from 09:00 to 16:00. Order a cinnamon roll, the dish the city guide singles out, and a lentil soup.
