Ganga Laffa & Falafel is Pushkar's best cheap vegan meal: a small stand near Narsingh Ghat serving falafel and laffa, the soft flatbread that makes a wrap worth eating. It is fast, filling and properly plant-based, which is why it has a queue at lunch.

Order a falafel laffa, add a hummus plate, and eat it by the lake. It is the most Pushkar meal on this list, and the cheapest too.
