Very Good Vegan Gua Bao is a small shop on Linsen Road in the East District selling vegan gua bao, steamed buns filled with vegetables, mushrooms and herbs, and listed as a MICHELIN Selected restaurant in the 2026 guide. It is a quick, cheap meal rather than a sit-down dinner, and it is one of the cheapest vegan eats in the city.

Order a couple of gua bao and eat them on the go. It is open Wednesday to Saturday, 11am to 8pm, so do not plan a Sunday to Tuesday visit.
