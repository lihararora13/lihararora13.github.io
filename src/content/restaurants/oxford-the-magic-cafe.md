The Magic Cafe is a relaxed community vegetarian cafe on Magdalen Road, all mismatched furniture, twinkly lights and local art, with live music on Saturday lunchtimes and monthly exhibitions. The food is homemade, budget-friendly and generous: mushroom risotto, masala potatoes, veg lasagna and tofu stir-fry.

Regulars call it the most amazing vegetarian food in Oxford, and it is a favourite with local parents for its chilled room. It is a vegetarian cafe with strong vegan options, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note it is open Tuesday to Saturday, 10am to 5pm.
