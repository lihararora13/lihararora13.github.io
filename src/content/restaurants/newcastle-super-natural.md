Super Natural sits on Lisle Street, two minutes from Central Station, and it is the city-centre pick for a big vegan-friendly brunch. The menu leans on full veggie breakfasts, brunch burgers and breakfast sandwiches, with a country brunch skillet for the hungriest table.

It serves meat too, so this is a vegan-friendly stop rather than an all-vegan one, but the plant-based breakfasts are a proper part of the menu rather than an afterthought. Come hungry and come early at weekends.
