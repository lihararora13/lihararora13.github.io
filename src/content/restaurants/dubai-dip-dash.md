Dip Dash tops the ratings for a reason: it is the rare health-focused kitchen that never tastes like a compromise. The Pan-Asian menu runs from sushi and dumplings to soba noodles and curries, all made from scratch in-house, with no white sugar, fillers or refined white flour, and gluten-free options across much of the menu.

It is vegetarian rather than strictly all-vegan, but the owners confirm no egg is used and most of the menu is vegan-friendly, so vegans eat very well here. Order the soba noodles, a round of rainbow sushi and the khao suey, and save room for the chocolate fudge dessert that reviewers keep name-checking.
