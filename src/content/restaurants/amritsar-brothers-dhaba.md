Brothers Dhaba is the budget legend of Amritsar: a pure-veg dhaba near the Golden Temple where a full meal costs pocket change and the Amritsari kulcha is, by one traveller's account, love. Dal, chhole, kadhi, bharta, the whole Punjabi bench is here.

Order the kulcha and the dal makhani, ask for no ghee and no cream, and eat like the city eats. It is simple, fast, and deeply satisfying.
