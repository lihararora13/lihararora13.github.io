The Burnt Tongue is Hamilton's soup shop, and the Locke Street location is the one to use: a small counter where the black bean burger is explicitly vegan and the coconut curry lentil soup is the current vegan soup on rotation. The soup selection changes, so the vegan choice of the day is whatever is on the board.

Use the 182 Locke Street South address on this page; the shop also has a Cannon Street East location. It is a lunch counter, so go midday.
