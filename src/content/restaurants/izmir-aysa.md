Aysa is a Michelin Bib Gourmand awarded restaurant in the Kemeralti bazaar, in the Abacioglu Hani on Anafartalar Caddesi, serving home-cooked traditional Turkish cuisine. It features a wide selection of vegan mezes, including lentil kofte, kisir, filled grape leaves and green beans in olive oil.

It is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00am to 6:00pm. Order a spread of the vegan mezes, starting with the lentil kofte and kisir.
