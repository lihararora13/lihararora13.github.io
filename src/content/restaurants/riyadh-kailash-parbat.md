Kailash Parbat is a vegetarian Indian restaurant on Al Imam Ali Ibn Abi Taleb Branch Road offering North and South Indian, Thai and Chinese dishes. Vegans should specify no butter, ghee, cream or curd when ordering.

It is open daily until 11:00pm. Order a North Indian dish and a Thai curry, and note the pin is at street level.
