Why we love it — Tucked into a quiet Eixample passageway, Modoki
is the kind of tiny spot that builds a fanatical following one roll at
a time. Everything is vegan: the spicy maguro with flame-torched
pear, the mushroom yakitori, the Osaka-style okonomiyaki, even the
kimchi they sell in jars to take home. It is the sushi fix for people
who thought sushi was off the table.

The space is small and the vibe is cozy, so book ahead. Work through
the rolls, save room for the dorayaki pancakes, and thank the plant
gods for watermelon that tastes like tuna.
