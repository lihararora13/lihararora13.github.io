Loving Hut is the casual, cheap option in Reykjavik, part of the international Loving Hut chain, so if you have been to one before you know what you are getting: fully vegan Asian comfort food, noodles, burgers and rice dishes, with no fuss and no big bill. The Laugavegur restaurant's menu includes noodle soups such as pho and tom yum, including pho with vegan chicken or beef alternatives.

It is the quick-lunch pick on this list. Order the pho, add a burger if you are still hungry, and keep the change for a coffee.
