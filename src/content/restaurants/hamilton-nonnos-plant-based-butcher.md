Nonno's is Hamilton's fully-vegan Italian butcher and sub shop on York Boulevard downtown, the kind of place where the counter matters as much as the menu. Deli-style sandwiches and subs lead the way, with the Tuscan Pollo and the Forgettaboutit as the names to order, and the fridges sell packaged vegan deli meats, cheeses and sausages to take home.

It is a small shop with limited hours, open Thursday to Saturday from 10am to 4pm, and it can cater six-foot subs for a group. Go early in the day, not late.
