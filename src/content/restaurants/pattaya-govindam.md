Govindam is a 100% pure vegetarian Indian restaurant on Pattaya Klang Road, the go-to for Indian vegetarians, particularly those observing Jain principles with no root vegetables. The kitchen serves rich curries, dosas and thalis, and vegans should ask for dishes without dairy, as the kitchen is vegetarian rather than fully vegan.

Order the dal and a vegetable curry with a dosa, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is inexpensive and central, and it is the deep bench of Pattaya vegan eating.
