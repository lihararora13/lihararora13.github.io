Na Tlali is the thoughtful counterpoint to the taco crawl: a calm, pretty room in cobblestoned San Angel where the cooking looks backward to pre-Hispanic and regional traditions instead of imitating meat. Moles, hoja santa, huitlacoche and heirloom corn do the heavy lifting, and they do it beautifully.

Time it with the Saturday San Angel market, order the enchiladas with all three moles, and settle in. This is the meal people write home about.
