El Jardin Botanico sits in a courtyard garden in the Walled City, and its menu is substantially vegan, though not exclusively so: the kitchen uses no red meat or pork and most of the menu is plant-based. The gazpacho fria, the stuffed peppers with quinoa and Colombian black beans, and the fresh ceviche made with palmito are all worth ordering.

Dinner mains run about COP 35,000-55,000, which makes this one of the smarter evening tables in the old town for a mixed group. Ask which dishes are vegan when you order, as a few plates still use dairy or egg.
