GIARDÍ is the easiest first or last meal of a Milan trip: a small, minimal, fully vegan café a few minutes from Stazione Centrale, serving plant-based food all day, every day. Breakfast brings the Not Avocado Toast, hummus plates with crunchy focaccia, granola bowls and fresh juices; lunch adds the Uao Burger, a hemp bun piled with plant cheddar and BBQ sauce, and roasted cauliflower; dinner adds gnocchi and Mondegreen, legume polpette inspired by Lombard home cooking.

Diners rave about the bowls, the juices and both desserts, a chocolate mousse and the Ricordo di una cheesecake. Dinner runs Monday to Friday only, the café closes at 4pm on Saturdays and Sundays, and the owners host guest-chef dinners called Ah Tavola, so check their Instagram before you go.
