The Flat is Exeter's evening pizza room, vegetarian and vegan, on Fore Street. It only opens Wednesday to Saturday evenings, which is worth knowing before you plan around it.

Order a vegan sourdough pizza and check the specials board. Book ahead at weekends, as the small room fills quickly.
