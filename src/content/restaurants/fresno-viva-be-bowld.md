Viva Be Bowld is a bowl shop on North Cedar Avenue doing acai bowls, smoothies, and breakfast and lunch bowls with a strong vegan bench. It is the morning pick on this list, and the best place for a light start before a heavier lunch.

Order the acai bowl and a green smoothie. Ask the staff to confirm the vegan preparation, since some bowls are finished with honey or dairy.
