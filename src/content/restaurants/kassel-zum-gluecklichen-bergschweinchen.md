Zum glücklichen Bergschweinchen, the happy little mountain pig, is Kassel's vegan institution in snack-bar form: 100% vegan, organic and regional, and rated 4.9 stars across nearly two hundred reviews. It is a proper Imbiss rather than a restaurant, with a big car park and a short menu done well, falafel and burgers at its heart, served Thursday to Sunday in a friendly neighbourhood setting a short walk from the Fulda.

It sits in Unterneustadt, east of the old town across the river, which makes it the stop to pair with a walk along the Fulda or the Karlsaue. Order the falafel plate if it is your first visit, and note the hours before you go: this is a Thursday-to-Sunday spot, closed Monday to Wednesday.
