La Coco C is a trendy small health cafe in Pretoria East with vegan dishes clearly labelled across breakfast and lunch. Vegan choices include acai breakfasts, porridge and pancakes in the morning, and bowls and rice paper wraps at lunch, plus juices and smoothies.

It is open Monday to Friday until 5:00pm and Saturdays until 2:00pm, so it is a daytime stop. Order the acai breakfast or a lunch bowl with a fresh juice.
