Casa la Familia is a family-run vegetarian pizzeria with Mediterranean flavours, and it is the comfort-food stop on this list. The pizzas are cooked in an authentic wood-fired oven, the desserts and ice creams are made with organic products, and the vegan pizzas and pastas are the draw, with the beetroot pizza the must-try.

It is vegetarian with vegan options, and the lunch deal is good value. Order the beetroot pizza, add a pasta to share, and enjoy a proper family meal.
