Orto is a fully vegan restaurant in Daeheung-dong, Jung-gu, in Daejeon's old centre, listed as vegan on HappyCow. It serves plant-based Western dishes in a neighbourhood that also holds the city's other old-centre vegan spots, and it is the best dinner option in the centre when the Yuseong-gu kitchens are too far away.

Order the vegan pasta and the seasonal vegetable plates, and save room for dessert. It is a small room, so come early at dinner or be ready to wait.
