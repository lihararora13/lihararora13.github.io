Vegan AF Ramen is Krakow's plant-based ramen bar, and the name tells you everything about the attitude. Tantanmen, donburi, katsu and kakigori fill the menu, with breakfasts served in the morning too, all vegan.

It is a bright, modern room on Krupnicza, open daily. Order the tantanmen, add the katsu, and finish with the kakigori if you have room.
