Celebration Inn is a pure-veg restaurant above Vishal Mega Mart in Spencers Plaza on Burdwan Road, with a live kitchen and free home delivery. It is rated 4.3 from 38 reviews, and the aloo dum, thali and biryani are the dishes its regulars order.

Order the aloo dum or the veg thali, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you get a solid Bengali-leaning veg meal. It is open 11 to 11 daily, and it also runs a fine-dining room at Hotel Central Park on Sevoke Road.
