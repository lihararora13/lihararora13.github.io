Sage has been one of Ubud's best-loved vegan spots for years, and it still feels like a local secret. The fully vegan menu leans Mexican, with burritos and flautas next to nourishing vegetable bowls, hearty burgers and homemade cakes, and whole cakes and cookies can be ordered to take away. The team is famously kind, the room is calm, and it is the kind of place you settle into for a long lunch.

It sits in Nyuh Kuning, a long walk south of central Ubud, and opens daily from 8am to 9pm. Go in daylight if you are walking, the lane back is dark at night, or take a scooter and make an evening of it.
