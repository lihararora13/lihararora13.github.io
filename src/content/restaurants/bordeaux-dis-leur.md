Dis Leur is Bordeaux's vegan bistro, and it takes the French bistro seriously: seasonal, organic and local cooking, plant-based only, served in a warm room a short walk from the cathedral. The menu reimagines the classics, tartare, rillettes, beignets, strawberry gazpacho, and finishes with a vegan canelé, the city's signature sweet.

It is open for dinner Tuesday to Saturday, plus Saturday lunch, so plan your evening around it. Order the tartare, ask what is seasonal, and finish with the canelé. French bistro cooking, without the butter.
