Kommune is Penang's modern plant-based dining room: a bright, conscious-lifestyle spot on Lebuh Victoria serving bowls, burgers and cold-pressed juices, all fully vegan. It is the newer face of the city's vegan scene, polished and thoughtful, and a nice contrast to the old-school buffet joints a few streets away.

Come for lunch, order a bowl and a juice, and enjoy the calm. This is the one for a fresh, modern vegan meal in the old town.
