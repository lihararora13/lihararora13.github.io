Mother Vegan Bistro is the home-cooking stop on this list: an artistic vegan bistro in Koukaki, est. 2020, offering a calm and welcoming atmosphere and wholesome organic plant-based food made in house using local ingredients. The menu features traditional Greek dishes such as pastitsio, moussaka and savoury pies, plus daily rotating specials, and the house-made desserts run to apple pie, chocolate crumble souffle and banana protein cake.

It is the best home cooking in the city according to the guides, and the drinks menu keeps pace with specialty coffee, wine, beer, cocktails and organic biodynamic spirits. Come for a slow dinner, order a pie and a dessert, and let the evening stretch.
