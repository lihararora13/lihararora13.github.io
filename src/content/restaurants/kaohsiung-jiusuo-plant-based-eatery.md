JiuSuo, 玖所, is a plant-based eatery on Chongshang Street in Zuoying, one MRT stop from the high-speed rail station, and it is the warmest room in this guide: a bright space with rescue cats waiting for adoption, a self-service ordering machine at the door, and a menu that runs Chinese and Western side by side. The dishes to order are the thick soy-milk ramen, the monkey-head mushroom rice, the spicy tofu hotpot and the mushroom chicken leg, with Thai-style stir-fries, pasta, risotto, spring rolls and a pineapple cake behind them, and papaya and avocado milkshakes made with plant milk. There is a minimum spend of one dish or drink, or NT$120.

The restaurant opened in late 2023 and its HappyCow listing was updated in November 2025, with a 2026 Zuoying food guide giving it a 4.8 Google rating. Hours are Tuesday to Sunday, 11:30am to 2:30pm and 5:00pm to 8:30pm, though one recent guide says Tuesday is the rest day and the afternoon runs to 3:00pm, so check before you go. Cash only. One practical note: the entrance is easy to miss, and Google Maps has shown a paediatric clinic at the address, so look for the restaurant sign rather than the map pin.
