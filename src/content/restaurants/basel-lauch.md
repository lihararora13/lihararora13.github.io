Lauch is a fully vegan fine-dining restaurant in Kleinbasel, open since 2021, with 13 Gault-Millau points and a garden. It serves two four-course tasting menus made entirely from plants, using seasonal, mostly regional ingredients, plus a lunch menu for 25 francs. It is the dinner pick on this list.

Order the four-course tasting menu, or the lunch menu on a weekday, and book ahead, since it opens for dinner Wednesday to Sunday and lunch Wednesday to Friday only.
