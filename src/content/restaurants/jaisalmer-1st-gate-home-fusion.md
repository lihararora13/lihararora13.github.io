1st Gate Home-Fusion sits by the fort entrance on Shiv Road, a primarily vegetarian restaurant that cooks with organic ingredients and a home-style touch. It is the healthiest meal on this list, and the most traveller-friendly, with a menu that explains itself in plain language.

Order a fusion veg bowl and a fresh juice, and you have a light lunch before or after the fort. It is a good reset between Jaisalmer's richer thalis.
