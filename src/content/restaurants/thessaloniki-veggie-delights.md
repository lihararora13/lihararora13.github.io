Veggie Delights is the cosy plant-based bistro in the lively Valaoritou district, specialising in quick dishes, quinoa bowls, vegetarian wraps and vegan pastries. The market bowl made with seasonal local vegetables is the value order, with small plates from three to six euros and mains from seven to thirteen.

It is open Tuesday to Saturday from noon to 10:30pm and closed Sunday and Monday, and it gets busy, so arrive before 1pm on weekends to avoid the queue.
