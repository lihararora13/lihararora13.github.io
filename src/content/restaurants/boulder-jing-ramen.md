Jing Ramen is a small, cozy shop on 28th Street with a big claim: every ramen broth is 100 percent plant-based. The shoyu and spicy miso vegan ramens have earned it a devoted following, with reviewers calling it the best vegan ramen they have ever had.

Seating is limited and it fills up fast, so arrive early. Order the spicy miso if you like heat, the shoyu if you do not, and add a vegan bun while you are at it.
