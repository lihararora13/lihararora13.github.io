Café-Bistro Corba is a small fully vegan café-bistro on Huestraße, four doors down from the Katzentempel, and the quietest table on this list. The offer is simple: a daily vegan lunch, a case of vegan cakes and coffee with plant milk, in a room that feels more like a neighbourhood living room than a restaurant.

Public information is thin: no hours are published in the current listings, so check before you go or pair it with a Katzentempel visit next door. Order the cake of the day, take a window seat, and treat it as Bochum's calmest coffee stop.
