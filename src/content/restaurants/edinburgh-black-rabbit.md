Black Rabbit is Edinburgh's fully vegan deli and coffee shop, a tiny Brougham Street room that has been a local favourite since 2019 and still draws a queue on weekend mornings. Everything is baked in-house: breakfast bagels and rolls, tofu Benedict, haggis and cheese toasties, salads and sandwiches, plus cakes, scones and pastries, and a deli counter selling vegan cheeses and mock meats by weight. The Green Rabbit, avocado, grilled artichokes and homemade pesto on toasted sourdough, is the sandwich to order, and the pistachio croissant is the one to take home.

It is very small, with only a few tables, so expect to take away or wait a little at busy times. Order the Black Rabbit bagel if it is your first visit and grab a pastry for the road. This is the table for a proper vegan breakfast in Edinburgh, no compromises and no card tricks.
