Welcome Moon Black Tofu is a black-tofu restaurant in Sinchang-dong, Seo-gu, open daily from 9am to 9pm, serving typical Korean dishes built around black tofu. A lot of the dishes are vegan, though the kitchen also serves meat, so ask which plates are plant-based when you order.

Order the black tofu dishes and the vegetable sides, and confirm no egg or fish sauce. It is close to the highway, so it suits a stop on the way in or out of the city as well as a local dinner.
