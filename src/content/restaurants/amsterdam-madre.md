Madre brings the fiesta to the Jordaan with a fully vegan Mexican menu that is as playful as it is delicious. Jackfruit tacos, mushroom quesadillas with shiitake and truffle oil, cauliflower tacos smothered in chipotle sauce, and birria tacos with vegan pork: this is comfort food with a sombrero on.

The cocktail list is the co-star, from margarita sours to ice-cold granita made with mezcal, lime, and ginger. It is loud, lively, and built for a night out with friends who think plants are boring. They are wrong.
