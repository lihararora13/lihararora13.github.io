Shukla Bhojnalay in Barra's KDA Market is the veg spot Kanpur locals vote for first: kadhai paneer, paneer butter masala and kadhi pakoda on a menu that regulars defend fiercely in the comments. It is the city's most-loved vegetarian kitchen, and the thalis are the way in.

Order the kadhi pakoda and the arhar daal, ask for no ghee and no dahi, and skip the paneer dishes. It is hearty, oily in the best way, and unapologetically Kanpur.
