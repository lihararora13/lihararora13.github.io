Fortunate Coffee Bandung is a small, cosy vegan cafe near the city train station, established in 2015, and it is the fashionable-cafe counterpart to the city's buffet canteens. The Eastern menu covers Chinese and Indonesian food like fried bihun and fried rice, with satay made of gluten or seitan, and the Western menu has pasta dishes that can be veganised on request.

Order the satay and a fried rice. It is a vegan cafe, confirmed open in April 2024, and it is the best plant-based coffee stop in the city centre.
