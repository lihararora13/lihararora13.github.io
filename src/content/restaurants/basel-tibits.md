Tibits is the vegetarian and vegan buffet in the old town, rated 4.6 from 229 reviews, serving over 40 homemade salads, hot dishes, soups, fresh juices and desserts from around the world. It is the buffet pick on this list, with a Sunday brunch to half two.

Order from the buffet by weight, the tandoori tofu with aubergine and the zucchini fries, and come for the Sunday brunch or a quick take-away.
