Arth is the soul-food cafe of this list: an airy dining room with terrazzo floors, wooden slats and yellow accents, built around the idea that food should feed more than hunger. Local guides keep naming it among Gurugram's best vegan-friendly spots.

Order the Buddha bowl and the hummus platter. One honest note from local diners: the oat milk situation has been patchy, so confirm your plant milk when you order coffee.
