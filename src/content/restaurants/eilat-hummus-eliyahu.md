Hummus Eliyahu is the national hummus chain's Eilat branch, serving traditional hummus and tahini dishes as well as a vegan shawarma, salads and falafel. It was previously listed as vegetarian but was reported in December 2025 to serve meat, so it is filed vegan-friendly rather than vegetarian.

Order the vegan shawarma and a hummus plate. It is closed on Saturdays, open Sunday to Friday, and it is the cheapest proper meal on the promenade.
