Veggies Express is the surprise of Trichy: a modern vegetarian restaurant on Thillai Nagar 7th Cross that serves the city's first veg Mandi biryani, alongside Thai red curry with pineapple tempeh, shiitake 65, luffin vindaloo with parotta and paloma rosa pasta. A June 2025 food vlog documents the full spread, and the tempeh dishes are the ones that make it a vegan pick rather than just a veg one.

Order the Thai red curry with pineapple tempeh and the veg Mandi biryani. It is the most interesting veg kitchen in Trichy, and the only one cooking with tempeh.
