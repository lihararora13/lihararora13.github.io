Magu is Helsinki's vegan fine dining table, a laid-back restaurant in Punavuori focused on non-animal flavours and the bright tastes of nature. The kitchen cooks with organic, locally sourced and foraged ingredients, and umami is the key: risotto, kimchi, soup, puttanesca and seasonal dishes that change with what the land is offering, with matcha tiramisu for dessert.

It is the special-occasion pick on this list, open Wednesday to Saturday evenings. Book ahead, order whatever is seasonal, and save room for the tiramisu.
