Tisch is the insider tip of this list: a small, cozy room in Josefstadt with upcycled furniture, gentle lighting, a relaxed bar and a summer terrace, serving creative vegan soul food that changes with the seasons. The menu ranges from reimagined classics to international surprises, and past favourites include carbonara with a tomato egg yolk, crispy plant schnitzel and oysters made from black salsify.

Hours are quirky, evenings on weekdays and all day at weekends, so check before you go. That irregularity is part of the charm: Tisch feels less like a restaurant chain and more like a friend's very good kitchen.
