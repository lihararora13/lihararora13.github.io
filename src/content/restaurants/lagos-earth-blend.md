Earth Blend is the calm, bright cafe in Rade Mall that Lekki's wellness crowd swears by. Smoothies, fresh juices and light vegan-friendly snacks are the point here, and it is the place to reset after a heavy Nigerian dinner the night before.

Order a green smoothie and a snack bowl, and treat it as a light lunch or a mid-afternoon stop rather than a big dinner.
