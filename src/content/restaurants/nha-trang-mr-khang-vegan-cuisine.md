Mr Khang Vegan Cuisine is a small vegan restaurant on Ngo Gia Tu in Nha Trang, described as small but high-quality, with fried rolls, salads, braised tofu and noodle soup. It is a local kitchen rather than a tourist restaurant, and it is one of the most affordable vegan meals in the city.

Order the fried rolls and the braised tofu, and try the noodle soup. It is inexpensive and central, and it suits a quick lunch.
