VegBox sits on Piazza Terracina by the river at Haven Banks, a plant-based cafe that makes a good destination for a waterside lunch. It is a short walk from the centre and quieter than the Fore Street rooms.

Order a brunch bowl and a cake, then walk the quay. A good pick for a slow morning away from the shopping streets.
