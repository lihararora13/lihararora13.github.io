Rainbow is where Tel Aviv vegans go when they want junk food done right. Opened in 2016 by the team behind the Green Cat pizzeria, it sits near Rabin Square and serves burgers and kebabs made of soy and mushrooms, seitan shawarma, and even pho and ramen, all for very friendly prices.

This is the stop for a quick, satisfying, gloriously un-healthy-tasting meal between beach and bar. Grab a burger, load up on fries, and remember that in this city, the fast food line is often the vegan one.
