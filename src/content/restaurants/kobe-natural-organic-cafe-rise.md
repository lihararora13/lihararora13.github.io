Natural & Organic Cafe RISE is a small café in Nakayamatedori that cooks Thai food from pesticide-free, additive-free produce: pad thai, khao man gai, gapao rice, green curry and khao soi. It is rated 4.8, the highest of the cafés in this guide, and it runs as a café with in-store pickup and takeout alongside dine-in.

It is vegetarian-leaning with vegan dishes across the menu, so confirm the vegan options when you order. Open Tuesday to Saturday 12:00 to 20:30 and Sunday afternoons, it is a good lunch stop on the walk up toward Kitano.
