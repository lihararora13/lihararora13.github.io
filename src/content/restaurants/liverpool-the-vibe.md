The Vibe is the cafe Liverpool's vegans keep coming back to, sometimes twice in one weekend. It is the only all-vegan restaurant in the city that serves breakfast, and the room is spacious and stylish with a small outdoor area for sunny days. The menu runs from breakfast through lunch: Veggs Royale with vegan carrot salmon, hoisin seitan bowls, burgers, wraps and sandwiches, all of it fresh and properly made.

It is the most versatile stop on the list, equally good for a slow brunch or a quick lunch between shops. Order the Veggs Royale if you are there in the morning, and the Hoisin Energy Bowl if you are there at lunch.
