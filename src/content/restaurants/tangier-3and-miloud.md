3and Miloud is a medina spot listed for bissara with vegan options, the fava-bean soup with bread that is vegan by nature. It is a small, local room rather than a tourist restaurant.

Order the bissara with bread and a mint tea. It is the cheapest, most local vegan meal in Tangier, and a good breakfast before the medina.
