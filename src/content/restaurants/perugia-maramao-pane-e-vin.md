Maramao Pane e Vin is a small creative restaurant on Via Dei Priori, two minutes from the main square, cooking local kilometre-zero food with natural wines and craft beers. The menu carries many vegetarian and vegan dishes made with care and imagination, and a February 2026 review called it highly recommended for vegetarians in the heart of Perugia.

Order the vegetarian tris, the bruschette and the vegan dishes of the day, and ask the staff, who are flexible about veganising plates. It is open until nine in the evening and the room is small, so book.
