La Korrigane is Saint-Roch's beloved craft brewpub, pouring unfiltered, unpasteurized house beers in a warm neighbourhood room. The kitchen will veganize dishes on request, and the vegan poutine, fries with vegan gravy and plant-based cheese, is the one to order, alongside the vegan pasta and the jalapeno pretzel with warm vegan cheese sauce.

It is the evening pick on this list. Order the poutine, try a house beer, and settle in. The room is pure Quebec City: local, seasonal and unpretentious.
