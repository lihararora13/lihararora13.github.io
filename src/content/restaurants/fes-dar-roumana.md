Dar Roumana is a fine-dining riad restaurant near Batha, a refined Moroccan room with a courtyard and a careful kitchen. It is a meat-serving restaurant, but the kitchen will cook vegan dishes on request, and the vegetable tagine and salads are the picks.

Book ahead and ask for a vegan menu when you reserve. It is the one table in Fes for a proper evening out.
