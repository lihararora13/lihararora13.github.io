Ashoka's Veg is the Nirala Bazar favourite: a pure-veg restaurant opposite Kohinoor Plaza, near M P Law College, with a 4.5 star rating and a loyal local crowd. It is the kind of place where the menu never changes because it never needs to.

Order the dal tadka without ghee, a mixed veg sabzi and hot rotis, and you have a proper, cheap, satisfying dinner. It is the quiet, reliable end of the Cidco and Usmanpura circuit.
