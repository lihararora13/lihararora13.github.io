Falafel Vegan Munch is the budget hero of this list, a Digbeth takeaway serving delicious falafel wraps and smoothies, all of it fully vegan and all of it cheap. It is the kind of place that proves vegan food does not need a tasting menu to be great: fresh falafel, good bread, proper sauces, eaten on the move.

It sits on Digbeth High Street, right in the middle of the city's creative quarter. Order a falafel wrap, add a smoothie, and keep the change for a coffee.
