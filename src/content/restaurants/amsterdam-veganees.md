Veganees takes Asian street food on a plant-based world tour and brings it home to Oost. The menu of 25 creative dishes spans Japanese mushroom gyoza with ponzu, Korean cauliflower with gochujang, Thai tom kha soup, Indonesian jackfruit rendang, pho, bibimbap, and bao buns. It is stylish, chill, and seriously fun.

The three-course dinner deal for 38.50 EUR is the move if you want to taste your way through the menu. Save room for the mochi and the grilled pineapple, because dessert is not optional here.
