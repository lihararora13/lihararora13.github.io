Far West Fungi is the most Santa Cruz restaurant in this guide: a mushroom shop that also cooks, with foraging goods and homewares on the shelves and a menu built entirely around fungi. The kitchen is committed to vegan and vegetarian dishes, a mushroom patty over rice smothered in porcini gravy in the mushroom moco loco, truffle grilled cheese, porcini arancini with mushroom pepper marinara, and fried wontons, all celebrating how hearty mushrooms can be. Browse the shop, then eat.

Hours are Monday 11am to 5pm and Tuesday to Sunday 11am to 6pm. Ask for the vegan preparation on the grilled cheese and arancini, and try the huckleberry spritzer with the meal.
