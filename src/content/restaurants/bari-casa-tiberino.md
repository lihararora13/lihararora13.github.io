Casa Tiberino is a refined local spot in Bari Vecchia with a wonderful, complete vegan menu for lunch and dinner, and the best full vegan meal in the city. A Puglia vegan guide found homemade focaccia with fried peppers, orecchiette with broccoli, pancetta and cherry tomatoes, a vegan salmon that tasted identical to the real thing, and a chocolate semifreddo for dessert.

Order the orecchiette, the vegan salmon and the chocolate semifreddo, and call ahead to reserve, especially in high season, since the room is small and the vegan menu is popular.
