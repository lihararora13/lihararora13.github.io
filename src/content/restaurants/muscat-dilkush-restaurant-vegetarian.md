Dilkush is an Indian vegetarian restaurant on Al Alam Street in Muttrah with many options that are naturally vegan, like Dal Fry, Channa Masala, Idly and Plain Dosa, along with vegetable fried rice and noodles. It is rated 5.0 on HappyCow and open daily from 7:00am to 3:00pm and 5:30pm to 10:30pm.

Order the Dal Fry with a Plain Dosa and the Channa Masala.
