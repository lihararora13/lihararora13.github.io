City O' City is the sister spot from the Watercourse Foods crew,
and it might be the most Denver restaurant in the city: an art-covered
Capitol Hill diner that pours a 7am latte and serves poutine at 11pm,
all fully vegetarian with a huge vegan lineup. The chicken and waffles,
built on bourbon-brined chicken-fried cauliflower, is the headline
act, but the seitan wings and BBQ mac bowl have their own fan clubs.

Rotating local art on the walls, a full bar, gender-neutral restrooms,
and a dessert case that never sits still. It is the rare spot that
works for a first date, a 2am rescue meal, and brunch with your mom.
