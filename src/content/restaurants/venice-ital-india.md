Ital India sits on the Fondamenta de le Capuzine in Cannaregio, a short walk from the Jewish Ghetto, and after La Tecia Vegana it is the most dependable full meal in the city. Indian menus carry more naturally plant-based cooking than anything else in Venice, and the kitchen is used to the vegan question and answers it straight.

Order the chana masala and a dal with rice, and a vegetable curry to share, asking for them without ghee or cream. Portions run large by Venetian standards and the prices sit well below the restaurants near the water.
