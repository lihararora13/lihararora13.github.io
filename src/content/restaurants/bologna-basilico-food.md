Basilico Food is a vegetarian and vegan takeaway on Via Massarenti, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, with a counter of vegan cakes, mousses, biscuits, muffins and a vegan burger. It is a daytime spot, open mornings to mid-afternoon on weekdays and Saturday mornings, so it works as a lunch or a cake stop.

This is not a fully vegan restaurant, so confirm the vegan dishes when you order. The cakes are the reason to come, and the burger is the savoury pick.
