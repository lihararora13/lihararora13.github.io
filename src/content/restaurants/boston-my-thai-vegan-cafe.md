My Thai Vegan Cafe has been quietly perfecting vegan Thai food since before vegan Thai food was cool. Perched on the second floor above Chinatown, this plant-filled loft has fed generations of Boston vegans its enormous, wallet-friendly plates of pad thai, curries, and spring rolls, all made without a trace of animal products. It calls itself the oldest all-vegan restaurant in Greater Boston, and nobody is arguing.

Regulars swear by the lunch special, a steal that comes with soup and dumplings, and the tom yum soup gets shouted about in reviews like it is a local celebrity. Come for a quiet pre-theater dinner or the monthly vegan meetup, and leave a little happier than when you walked in.
