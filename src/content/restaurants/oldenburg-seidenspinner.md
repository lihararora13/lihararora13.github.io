Seidenspinner, full name Bio-Restaurant Seidenspinner, is an organic restaurant on Nadorster Straße, and it is the most grown-up dinner on Oldenburg's list: seasonal German cooking, with vegan dishes alongside vegetarian and meat options, and a piano on Thursday evenings. The kitchen is organic through and through, the menu changes with the seasons, and the vegan dishes are cooked with the same care as everything else.

It opens on dinner evenings, Wednesday to Sunday, so book ahead and plan the evening around it. Order the seasonal vegan main, add a vegan starter, and take a glass of organic wine. This is the table for a special evening in Oldenburg, and the one to pick when you want a proper restaurant rather than a café.
