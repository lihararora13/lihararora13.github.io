Crunchy Kebab Vegan opened on Goethe-Allee in October 2025 and was immediately a big day for Göttingen's vegans: one of only two fully vegan kitchens in the entire city, with a completely plant-based menu of kebabs, falafel, rice plates and salads. The seitan and pea-protein skewers are the signature, the sauces and toppings are generous, and it stays open until three in the morning on Fridays and Saturdays.

It sits a few doors down from Chay, which makes Goethe-Allee the vegan street of the old town. Order the crunchy kebab, add fries, and you have the city's best-value vegan dinner, any night of the week.
