Tap The Roots made the list for a reason. A cold-pressed-groundnut-oil kitchen in C-Scheme with vegan options, millet bowls and wraps at honest prices. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in C-Scheme, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Millet bowl, Vegan wrap, Cold coffee, plant milk if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
