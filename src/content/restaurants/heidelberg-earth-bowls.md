Earth Bowls is the tropical counterpoint to Heidelberg's old-town cafés: a small counter in the Altstadt doing smoothie bowls, cold-pressed juices and a handful of savoury bowls, everything on the menu fully plant-based. It is the lightest meal on this list and the most refreshing on a warm day, the kind of place you stop at between the castle and the river rather than plan an evening around.

Two practical notes: it is cash only, so bring euros, and it closes on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Order an açaí bowl and a juice, take it to the Neckar bank if the weather allows, and treat it as Heidelberg's answer to a beach shack lunch.
