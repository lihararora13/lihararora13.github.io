Saravana Bhavan is the South Indian vegetarian chain that vegans in the Gulf know by heart, and the Abu Dhabi branch downtown is the real thing: dosa, idli, sambar, thali and curries, with vegan dishes across the menu if you ask about ghee. It is cheap, fast and open long hours.

Order a masala dosa, add idli with sambar, and ask for the thali without ghee. It is the budget backbone of this list.
