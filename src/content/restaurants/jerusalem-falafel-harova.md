Falafel HaRova is a small all-vegan falafel shop on the eastern side of the main square of the Jewish Quarter, and it is the only fully vegan pick on this list. It is a walk-up counter: falafel in pita with salads, nothing else, done well.

It is open Monday to Thursday and Sundays from 10:00am to 9:30pm, and closed Fridays and Saturdays. Order the falafel in pita and eat it in the square.
