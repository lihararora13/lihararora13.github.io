Vrinda is a natural restobar in the centre of Rosario, vegetarian and vegan, with ready-to-eat meals, a natural grocery and delivery, and it is open from 9:30 am to 11:30 pm, which makes it the most flexible table on this list. The mushroom burger in curry potato bread is the dish reviewers keep naming, alongside the protein crunch bowl and the vegan wholemeal medialunas.

Order the mushroom burger, add a coffee, and finish with the coconut flan with date dulce. It is the best first stop in Rosario, and the only pick here that reliably serves dinner.
