Aujourd'hui Demain is part restaurant, part grocery store, part fashion
boutique, and entirely vegan. It's the perfect one-stop hangout: eat a
proper meal, then browse the shelves for vegan groceries and sustainable
goodies on your way out.

The Croque-Monsieur Forever tastes almost unfairly authentic, and the
weekend brunch pancakes draw a crowd. It's small and very popular, so
make a reservation and plan to linger.
