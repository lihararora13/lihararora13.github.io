Tasty+ is a casual vegan-friendly cafe a short walk from Okayama Station, with a menu built around soy meat and soy milk: Bolognese, carbonara, spinach curry and vegetable curry, plus smoothie bowls and a tofu salad bowl. It is not a fully vegan kitchen, so say you eat vegan when you order, but the vegan dishes are clearly part of the menu rather than an afterthought.

The soy meat Bolognese and the soy milk carbonara are the two dishes regulars name first, and the smoothie bowls make it a good breakfast stop. Prices are modest, and the room is bright and relaxed rather than formal.
