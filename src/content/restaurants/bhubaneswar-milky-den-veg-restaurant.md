Milky Den is a small, no-fuss pure-veg restaurant at Ram Mandir Square, right by the Unit 4 market. It is the kind of place locals drop into for a quick thali between errands, and HappyCow keeps it on the Bhubaneswar list for exactly that reason: reliable, cheap, and always open.

Order the veg thali or dal fry with roti, and ask them to skip the ghee. It is not a destination restaurant, but it is a dependable one, and in a thin scene that counts for a lot.
