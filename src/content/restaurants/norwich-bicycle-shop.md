The Bicycle Shop is half bike shop, half cafe-bar, on St Benedicts Street, and the kitchen cooks a solid vegan selection alongside the regular menu. It is a lively evening room as well as a daytime cafe.

Order a plant-based burger with a local beer. Good for an evening when you want a drink as well as dinner.
