Julian's is Providence's beloved brunch spot, a quirky, art-filled room on Broadway where the vegan options are not an afterthought. The Vegan St. Jamez Benedict, the tofu scramble and the General Tso's cauliflower all have their own following, and the full bar does proper cocktails.

It is busy at weekends, so arrive early or be ready to wait. Order a benedict, share the cauliflower, and linger over coffee. This is the brunch pick on the list.
