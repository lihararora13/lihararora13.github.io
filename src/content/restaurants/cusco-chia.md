Chia is Cusco's organic vegan restaurant, and its motto says it all: eat consciously, drink happily. Fully vegan since 2018, it cooks with vegetables, quinoa, brown rice and other healthy ingredients, and the set menu is the way to go: salad bar, soup, main course, drink and dessert. There is also a terrace, organic Peruvian coffee, fresh juices and smoothies, and even cooking classes.

Come for lunch, order the set menu, and linger on the terrace with a coffee. This is the one for a proper, healthy San Blas lunch.
