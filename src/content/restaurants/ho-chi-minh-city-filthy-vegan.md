Filthy Vegan is the cure for the day you want something gloriously unserious. A vegan Brit's comfort-food kitchen in District 1, it serves fish and chips, bangers and mash, cottage pie, hot dogs, burgers and a full English breakfast, all 100% vegan, with every faux meat made in-house rather than unwrapped from a packet. The bakery turns out cheesecakes, muffins, apple pies and a brownie with custard that reviewers call the best dessert menu in the city.

It is also a good citizen of a restaurant: biodegradable packaging, recycling on site, local ingredients, and 10% of profits donated to a vegan charity. Come hungry, order the fish and chips, and finish with the brownie.
