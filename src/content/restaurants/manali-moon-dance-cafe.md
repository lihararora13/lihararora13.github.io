Moon Dance Cafe is one of the long-running names on the Old Manali lane, listed on every cafe map of the village. Momos, thukpa, pasta and pancakes fill the menu, and the room has the easy, backpacker feel the lane is known for.

Order the veg momos and the thukpa. Check winter hours before walking up, as the lane cafes shorten their days in the cold months.
