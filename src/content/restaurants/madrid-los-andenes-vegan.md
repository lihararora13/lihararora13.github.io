Los Andenes brings something no other pick on this list does: a fully vegan Peruvian kitchen, serving ceviche, lomo saltado, mock-meat stews and seitan noodles in Chamberi since 2018. HappyCow reviewers put it among Madrid's top-rated vegan spots, with the ceviche getting called out by name.

The daily menu, two starters and a dessert, is the weekday move. Otherwise order the ceviche first, the lomo saltado second, and save room for cake or ice cream.
