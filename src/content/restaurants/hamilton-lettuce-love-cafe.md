Lettuce Love is the Ontario plant-based café chain's Westdale location, sharing a bright space on King Street West with Heal Wellness since March 2025. Everything on the menu is plant-based and largely gluten-free: Happy Buddha, Macro and Goddess bowls, falafel bowls, BBQ tacos, sandwiches and burgers, with a weekend brunch from 10am to 2pm.

It is open Monday to Friday from 11am to 8pm and weekends from 10am to 8pm. Order a bowl, add a smoothie from the Heal side, and sit on the patio in summer.
