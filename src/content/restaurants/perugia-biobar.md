BioBar sits in the lower floor of NoiBio, a small organic supermarket in Ponte San Giovanni near the Collestrada mall, and it serves a vegan buffet every lunch from 12:15 to 2:30 for eight euros. The buffet is often themed, Moroccan, Japanese, Turkish or Mexican, and at weekends there is an Aperivegan, a rich buffet and a drink for fifteen euros.

Order the lunch buffet and come hungry, since it is all you can eat, and return on a Friday or Saturday evening for the Aperivegan. Drinks, coffee and dessert are not included in the buffet price.
