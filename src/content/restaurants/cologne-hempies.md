Hempies is the Belgian Quarter's vegan café and bistro, and it is the kind of place you plan a morning around: croissants, brownies, Berliners, cinnamon rolls and sandwiches with fillings like carrot salmon, tempeh and tomato or vegan mozzarella. Everything is plant-based, everything is casual, and the coffee is good.

Come for breakfast or a light lunch, order the carrot salmon sandwich, and take a cinnamon roll for later. The Belgian Quarter's best vegan morning.
