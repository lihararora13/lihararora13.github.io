MADENITALY did something gloriously cheeky: it opened a 100 percent vegan Italian restaurant and then moved it into the old Meatpacking District. Fresh pasta made in house, pizza romana, rich lasagna and a tiramisu that reviewers call perfection, plus gelato and a full bar. HappyCow rates it 5.0, its highest in the city.

Reviewers in September 2026 were still converting sceptical friends here and raving about the lasagna and the fried oyster mushrooms. Book ahead at weekends, it fills the room fast, and whatever you do, order the tiramisu.
