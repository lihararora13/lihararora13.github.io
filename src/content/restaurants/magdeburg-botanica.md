Botanica is the restaurant Magdeburg vegans are proudest of: a small, plant-filled room cooking a changing seasonal menu from regional and organic ingredients, with house ferments, kimchi, sauerkraut and kombucha, and a drinks list of natural wines and spirits from small producers. Everything is vegan, the menu turns with the seasons, and reviewers keep coming back for the creativity as much as the cooking. It is fine dining without the stiffness.

It is dinner only, Tuesday to Saturday evenings, and reservations are required, so plan ahead. The room is tiny and the menu is short, which is the point. Order whatever the season is offering, and let the staff pick the drinks. This is the table for a proper evening out in Magdeburg.
