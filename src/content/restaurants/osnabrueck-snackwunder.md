Snackwunder is a vegetarian cafe and juice bar on Redlingerstrasse, open daily, serving sweet potato waffles, banana bread, nicecreams, smoothies and juices, all homemade without industrial sugar or additives. It is the sweet stop on this list.

Order the sweet potato waffles, a power porridge and a nicecream, and come for a late breakfast or an afternoon snack.
