NBTAHOUSE, also listed as Nebta, is a tranquil guesthouse and a soulful plant-based restaurant tucked into the medina, with a courtyard, natural light and greenery. The kitchen celebrates local ingredients with a creative take on vegan-vegetarian cuisine, colourful mezze, fragrant tagines and herbal infusions, and Planted Maps lists it as 100% vegan.

Order the mezze platter and the mushroomawarma, and the almoncado pastilla if it is on. One recent review found the execution uneven, so stick to the mezze and the best-reviewed dishes, and note it is closed on Mondays.
