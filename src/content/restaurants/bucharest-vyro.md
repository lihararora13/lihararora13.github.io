Vyro is the plant-based raw and vegan bistro on Strada Transilvaniei, with raw soups, vegan pasta, raw lasagna, vegan sushi, cold-pressed juices, smoothies and raw cakes. The daily menu is the order at lunch, and the raw lasagna and vegan sushi are the dishes to try for dinner.

It is open Monday to Friday from 10:30am to 7pm and Saturday from 11am to 4pm, and closed Sunday. The room is small and elegant, so it works as a refined lunch rather than a big night out.
