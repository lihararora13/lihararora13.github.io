Awash has been feeding Geneva's Ethiopian community and its UN crowd since 1995, and it shows in the confidence of the cooking. Five vegan dishes sit in the vegetarian section of the menu, all of them properly vegan, and the degustation pulls them together into one long, generous meal on injera. Portions are big, flavours are bold, and the bill is gentle by Geneva standards.

It is a family-run room with communal tables and a loyal following, so it feels like a local secret even though it sits minutes from the station. Order the degustation, add an avocado salad, and finish with Ethiopian coffee. A classic for a reason.
