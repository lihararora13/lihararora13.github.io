CHAO brings the wok party: a laid-back Da'an stir-fry joint from the team behind BaganHood, serving the sizzling, share-it-all plates of a Taiwanese rechao, entirely vegan. Metal tables, cold teas, dishes landing fast and furious, it is dinner as a good time.

The pineapple shrimp is the dish reviewers cannot stop talking about, with beef-style noodles and mushroom tempura close behind. It stays open later than most vegan spots in town, which makes it the hero of hungry evenings. Bring friends, order too much, regret nothing.
