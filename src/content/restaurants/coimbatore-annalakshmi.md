Annalakshmi is Coimbatore's most unusual dining experience: fine-dining vegetarian food served by volunteers, professionals from various fields who donate their time, with a significant portion of the revenue going to charity. The menu covers South Indian, Chettinad and multicuisine, and the room has the calm of a place that is not trying to sell you anything.

Order the Chettinad veg curry and a thali, and ask the volunteers about the vegan options. It is the most memorable meal in the city after Padayal, and the money does good.
