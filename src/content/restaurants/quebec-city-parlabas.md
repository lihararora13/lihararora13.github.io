Parlabas is a small vegetarian cafe-boutique with a loyal local following, serving salads, wraps and a breakfast cookie, with plenty of vegan options across the menu. It is a quiet, friendly room, and a good morning stop if you are staying north of the centre.

Order a vegan wrap and a breakfast cookie, and browse the boutique while you are there. It is the gentle, neighbourhood end of the Quebec City list.
