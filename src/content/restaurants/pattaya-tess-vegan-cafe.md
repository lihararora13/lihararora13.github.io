Tess Vegan Cafe is a 100% vegan cafe up in the north Pattaya and Naklua neighbourhood, on Na Kluea 16, Soi Pho Samphan, serving stir-fried noodles, pasta and vegan fish and chips. It reworks Thai food without meat, dairy or eggs while keeping the bold Thai flavour, with pad Thai, pad kaprao, tom yum, mushroom dishes and curries, and it is open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 9pm, closed Sundays.

Order the pad Thai and the vegan fish and chips, and try the tom yum. It is the best vegan cafe in north Pattaya and the natural stop for anyone staying in Naklua.
