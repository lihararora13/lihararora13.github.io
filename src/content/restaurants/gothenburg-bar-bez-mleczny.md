Bar Bez Mleczny is Gothenburg's vegan milk bar, a fully plant-based Polish kitchen on Kungsgatan that calls itself a vegetable restaurant: hyper-local, micro-seasonal, and gloriously eclectic. The menu runs from dosa and kimchi to borek, burrito and jollof rice, changing with what is in season and what the kitchen feels like cooking.

It is inexpensive, central and open for lunch and dinner, which makes it one of the easiest vegan stops in the city. Order the dosa, add a borek, and let the kitchen surprise you.
