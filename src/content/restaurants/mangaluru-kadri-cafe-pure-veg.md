Kadri Cafe is the pure-veg cafe Mangaluru's food pages keep coming back to, and the reason is simple: crisp dosa, good chutney, and a room full of locals who treat it as their daily canteen. The cone dosa photographs well, but the plain masala dosa is the dish that earned the reputation.

Go for breakfast or the evening session, order the masala dosa without ghee, and finish with filter coffee. Some regulars grumble about small portions and prices, so order a second dosa rather than expecting a feast.
