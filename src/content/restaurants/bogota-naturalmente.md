Naturalmente has been feeding Bogotá's vegans longer than anyone else in the city, since 2005, and it still does the job the same unshowy way: a daily lunch set menu of soup, main course, a sweet, a fruit and a juice, everything made in-house from natural ingredients, for the price of a bus ride and a coffee. The two-floor spot near the National Museum serves a vegan version of Spanish paella, seitan chicharrón, ajiaco santafereño, tamales, arepas and buñuelos, and not a single egg, dairy product or drop of honey crosses the kitchen.

It only opens for lunch, Monday to Saturday from noon to three, so plan around it. Pair it with a morning at the museum next door and you have the perfect Bogotá afternoon.
