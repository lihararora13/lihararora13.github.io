Saraza is Kalavad Road's modern dining room: a popular multicuisine restaurant opposite Cosmoplex Cinema, in the city's newer western quarter. It is the kind of place that works for a family dinner, a business lunch, or a hungry post-cinema meal.

The North Indian mains are the move, with dal tadka and mixed veg sabzi leading. Ask for the ghee and cream held and the plate turns plant-based without losing any of its comfort.
