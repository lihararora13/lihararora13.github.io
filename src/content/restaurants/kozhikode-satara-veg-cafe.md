Satara Veg Cafe is the new veg cafe near the KSRTC bus stand on Mavoor Road, and a February 2026 review called it the best breakfast place in Calicut, with a chef rolling masala dosa on the tawa in full view. The benne masala dosa is the signature, though vegans will want the benne, butter, held back.

Order the masala dosa and an idli, and expect to pay a little more than the old lunch homes charge. The dosa is worth it, and the bus stand location makes it the traveller's breakfast.
