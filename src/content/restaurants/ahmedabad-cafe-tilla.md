Cafe Tilla is the Paldi cafe with the design-studio soul: a pure-vegetarian restaurant on Narayan Nagar Road, opposite Union Bank, sharing its space with the Tilla design studio near the National Institute of Design, with outdoor seating and a 4.0 on HappyCow. The vegan dishes are named on the menu, which is rare in this city.

Order the Tibetan vegetable thupka for dinner, the vitamin bhel and sattu paratha, and finish with the vegan Classic Chocolate Truffle Cake. It trades Tuesday to Sunday from noon to 11pm, and the mini idlis and waffles are the other vegan picks worth knowing.
