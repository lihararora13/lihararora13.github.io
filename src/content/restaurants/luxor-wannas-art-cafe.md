Wannas Art Cafe calls itself Luxor's first vegetarian and vegan restaurant, a mud-brick art cafe on the West Bank near the ferry, with art, music and events alongside the food. It reopened after the summer holidays in September 2026 and is trading now.

Order the vegan plate of the day and stay for the art. It is the West Bank's social hub as much as a restaurant, so evenings are the time to go.
