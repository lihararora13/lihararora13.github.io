Izz Café is a Palestinian street-food spot on George's Quay, serving falafel, wraps and mezze with plenty of vegan options. It is fast, cheap and the best quick lunch in the centre.

Order a falafel wrap and a mezze plate. A good lunchtime stop by the river.
