Cairo Kitchen is a small Egyptian spot in Moreleta Park with a menu that leans naturally vegan: dips, wraps, snacks and platters, many of them vegan as served. It is a takeaway-friendly place, open Monday to Saturday from 10:00am to 5:00pm.

Order a falafel wrap with a side of dips, or a sharing platter if you are in a group. Note the pin is at neighbourhood level for Moreleta Park, as the listing gives no street number.
