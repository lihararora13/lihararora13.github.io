Thammachat Vegetarian Food is a long-running jay kitchen in North Pattaya, doing the classic point-and-choose trays from early morning. It is one of the reliable anchors of the Pattaya scene as of mid-2026, and it is the cheapest vegan breakfast in the city.

Come early for the trays, and note the jay-shop custom of closing around the 1st and 16th of the lunar month. Its pin is approximate to North Pattaya, so ask locally for the current location.
