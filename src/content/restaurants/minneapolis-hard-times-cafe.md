Hard Times has been worker-owned and weird since 1992, and the Cedar-Riverside original is a Minneapolis rite of passage. The all-vegetarian menu leans heavy on vegan options, the prices are delightfully cheap, and the kitchen stays open late for the night owls, punks, and post-shift crowd.

Bring cash, grab a seat in the scruffy dining room, and order the vegan helter skelter with hash browns. It's greasy-spoon perfection, animal-free.
