sattgrün is Essen's fully vegan buffet, a self-service restaurant in the Theaterpassage off Rathenaustraße where everything is plant-based and you pay by plate size. The buffet runs to soups, salads, warm vegetable dishes and fine desserts, cooked fresh from natural organic ingredients, cholesterol- and lactose-free, and open every day from twelve until ten.

It is the easiest vegan meal in Essen: no menu to decode, no questions to ask, just a plate and a buffet. Take a small plate for a light lunch or a large one for a proper dinner, and check the operator notice for public-holiday closures, the one day of the year it shuts.
