Monkey Mood is the restaurant Bordeaux's vegans are proudest of: a fully vegan café and gallery in Saint-Michel where the cuisine travels the world each week, with a standing focus on Indonesian food. The Chick'n Nasi Goreng, the seasonal vegetable and soy protein curries and the vegan burger are hearty, colourful and packed with flavour, and the desserts are just as good while staying low in sugar.

The jungle-style interior is half the fun, and the Sunday brunch is worth booking ahead for. Order the Nasi Goreng, save room for dessert, and let the place whisk you east for an hour.
