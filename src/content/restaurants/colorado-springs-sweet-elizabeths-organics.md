Sweet Elizabeth's Organics is an all-vegan and gluten-free bakery on West Uintah Street, doing cakes, cookies, cinnamon rolls, and seasonal pastries. It is the city's dedicated vegan bakery, and the place to order a whole cake for a celebration.

Order a cake slice and a cookie, and ask what is fresh that day, since the case rotates. Go early on weekends, when the popular items sell out.
