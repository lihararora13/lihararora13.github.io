Why we love it — Finding Cookies Cream is half the adventure: you
follow signs through a parking garage courtyard at the Westin Grand,
ring a bell, and suddenly you are in a Michelin-starred dining room.
Since 2007 this has been the proving ground for vegetable-first fine
dining, holding its star every year since 2018. The kitchen builds an
entirely vegan tasting menu (five, six, or seven courses) that never
feels like a compromise.

The vegetable truly rules here. Dishes like carrot, chard, and sea
buckthorn show how far technique can carry a beet, and the wine and
alcohol-free pairings are fantastic. Book weeks ahead, arrive hungry,
and enjoy Berlin at its most gloriously secretive.
