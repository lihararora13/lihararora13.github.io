The Little Sisters Cafe and Bakery on Manu Temple Road is the coffee-serious cafe of Old Manali: espresso, pour-overs, French press, AeroPress and moka pot, iced coffees and matcha, with breakfast running all day. It keeps a separate plant-based, gluten-free card of pancakes and smoothies sweetened with banana, jaggery and maple.

It is open 8 AM to 11:30 PM every day, with free Wi-Fi and all-indoor seating. Order the plant-based pancakes and a falafel platter, and take your time over the coffee.
