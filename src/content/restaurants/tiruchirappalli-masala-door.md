Masala Door is the artisanal vegetarian restaurant on Williams Road in the Cantonment, named on the September 2026 list of the city's top five veg breakfast spots. It is the modern one in Trichy: a cafe-style room rather than a tiffin hall, and the one that treats breakfast as a craft.

Order the artisanal dosa and a filter coffee, and ask about the vegan options. It is the Cantonment breakfast, and the most likely room in the city to have a vegan plate already in mind.
