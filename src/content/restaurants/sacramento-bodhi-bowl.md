Bodhi Bowl is a vegetarian Vietnamese restaurant in South Sacramento that has been quietly rated 5.0 by HappyCow reviewers, with a menu that leans vegan-friendly and a pho that regulars swear by. Ask for the vegan version and the kitchen knows exactly what to do.

It is the south-side stop on the list, and the proof that Sacramento's vegan scene stretches well beyond Midtown. Order the vegan pho, add spring rolls, and enjoy the drive.
