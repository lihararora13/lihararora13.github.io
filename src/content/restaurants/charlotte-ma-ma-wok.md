Ma Ma Wok is the rare find every vegan city guide dreams of: a Chinese restaurant that went fully vegan in late 2017 and never looked back. The menu looks standard at a glance, lo mein, sweet and sour fish, steak with broccoli, sesame chicken, and then you notice that every single meat is plant-based, across more than a hundred entrees. Spring rolls, dumplings, noodles, fried rice and tofu dishes all come exactly as you remember them, minus the animals.

It holds a rare 5-star HappyCow rating, and it is the pick on this list for the table that cannot agree on anything else. Order family-style and let the dumplings do the talking.
