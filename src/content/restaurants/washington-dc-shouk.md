Why we love it — Shouk does one thing perfectly: bold, craveable Israeli
street food that happens to be 100 percent plant-based. The Shouk Burger packs
fifteen vegetables into a patty that has won RAMMY awards and converted legions
of skeptics.

It's the perfect Union Market pit stop: quick, affordable, and wildly
flavorful. Get the burger in a pita with tahini dripping everywhere, add the
falafel if you're smart, and thank Carla Hall later.
