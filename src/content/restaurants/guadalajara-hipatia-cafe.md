Hipatia Café is the vegan cafe of Colonia Americana, a bright room on La Paz where breakfast and brunch are the main event. It is the easy morning stop before a day of exploring Chapultepec.

Order a vegan breakfast and a fresh juice, and linger. This is the calmest table on the Guadalajara list, and the one to start your day at.
