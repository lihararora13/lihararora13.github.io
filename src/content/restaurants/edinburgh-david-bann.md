David Bann has been Edinburgh's meat-free landmark since 2002, a stylish but relaxed dining room in the Old Town that predates the city's vegan boom by two decades and still leads it. The menu is vegetarian with a deep vegan bench: smoked and marinated tofu, beetroot risotto, beetroot and cauliflower curry, and the vegan haggis with whisky sauce that converts sceptics at the table. Reviewers call it the best vegetarian and vegan restaurant in Scotland, and the Michelin Guide agrees enough to list it.

It sits on St Mary's Street, a short walk from the Royal Mile, which makes it the easiest first stop for anyone arriving in the city. Book ahead for dinner, order the haggis if it is your first visit, and save room for dessert. This is the table to take someone who thinks vegan food in Scotland means a sad side salad.
