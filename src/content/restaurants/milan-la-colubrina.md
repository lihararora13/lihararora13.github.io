La Colubrina has been cooking vegan food on Via Felice Casati since 1983, which makes it older than a fair number of its customers and the longest-running fully vegan restaurant in Milan. It is an all-female, family-run place that makes nearly everything in house, and the house obsession is cheese: the kitchen ferments and ages its own, so start with the cheese plate and buy a wedge to take back to your hotel.

The rest of the menu is old-school trattoria cooking with a contemporary touch: cacio e pepe that keeps coming up in reviews, risotto with oven-cooked eggplant, plane tree gnocchi with zucchini and basil cream, wholegrain pizzas and daily desserts, with organic wines and craft beers. The chef likes to stop by tables to talk through the menu, and the room feels like a retro neighbourhood trattoria a short walk from Porta Venezia. Closed Mondays.
