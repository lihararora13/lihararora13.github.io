Gulmohar is the Karaparamba pure-veg restaurant, named on the November 2026 list of the city's five best veg spots, with thali and a grilled platter as the dishes the list highlights. It is a family dining room rather than a tiffin counter, and the menu runs broader than the beach cafes.

Order the veg thali and ask for the curd to be skipped. It is the Karaparamba dinner pick, and the grilled platter is the dish to share.
