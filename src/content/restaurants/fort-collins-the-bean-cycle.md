The Bean Cycle is Fort Collins' coffee pick: a roaster and cafe on North College Avenue in Old Town with numerous vegan cookies, muffins and smoothies alongside oat milk lattes. It is the morning and working pick in this guide, open Sunday to Thursday 7am to 7pm and Friday to Saturday 7am to 9pm, with a cafe room that suits a laptop and a pastry case that always has a vegan option.

Order a vegan cookie with an oat milk latte. The vegan items are a small part of the menu, so ask which cookies and muffins are vegan that day.
