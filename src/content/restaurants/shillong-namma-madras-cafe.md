Namma Madras Cafe is the quick-and-easy pick in Police Bazar: South-meets-North dosas and thalis at 400 to 600 rupees for two, listed in the August 2026 roundup of Meghalaya's pure-veg spots. It is a fast, casual cafe, good for a dosa between errands.

Order the masala dosa or the veg thali, ask for no butter on the dosa, and you have a cheap, reliable South Indian meal in the middle of Police Bazar.
