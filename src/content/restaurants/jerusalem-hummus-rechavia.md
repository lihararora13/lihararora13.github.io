Hummus Rechavia is a hummus restaurant in Rechavia offering hummus with pita bread, falafel, sabich, soup, salads and sides such as rice with beans, fries and stuffed vine leaves. It is mostly vegan apart from an option to add boiled eggs to dishes.

It is open Monday to Thursday and Sundays from 10:00am to 9:00pm, Fridays until 5:00pm. Order hummus with falafel and a sabich without egg.
