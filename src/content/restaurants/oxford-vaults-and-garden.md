Vaults & Garden sits in the vaulted congregation house of the University Church, a 1320s building opposite the Radcliffe Camera, and it serves organic, locally sourced vegetarian food with a beautiful outdoor terrace. The full Oxford breakfast and the Oxford rarebit are the dishes to order, with a vegan version of the breakfast on the menu.

It is a vegetarian cafe rather than an all-vegan one, so file it as vegan-friendly. Come for a slow lunch between the Bodleian and the Covered Market, and take the terrace if the sun is out.
