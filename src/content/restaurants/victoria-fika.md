Fika is Victoria's most unusual cafe: a fully vegan Scandinavian art cafe on Cook Street in Fernwood, founded by artist and environmentalist Praveen Ponraj, with oat milk as the default and a menu that mixes onigiri, tortina, payasam and cardamom caramel coffee. It is an art space as much as a cafe, and it is the morning pick in this guide, open daily 9am to 1pm. Note that other Fika menus found online, including fika.coffee, belong to a different Nordic cafe chain and are not this restaurant.

Order an onigiri and a cardamom caramel coffee. The hours are short, so go in the morning.
