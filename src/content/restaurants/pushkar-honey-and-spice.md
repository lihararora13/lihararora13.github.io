Honey & Spice is Pushkar's family-run vegan institution: a small, beloved all-vegan and organic café in Laxmi Market near Rangji Temple, where the smoothie bowls and gluten-free bakes have their own following. It is the kind of place where the owners remember you by your second visit.

Order a smoothie bowl and a gluten-free bake, and settle into the market buzz. It is the warmest room on this list, and the most Pushkar.
