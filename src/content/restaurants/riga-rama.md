Rama is, by its own account, the only purely vegetarian restaurant in Riga, a small Indian restaurant and bookshop on Krisjana Barona iela where the chefs cook first and second courses, snacks and desserts from vegetables, soya and dairy. The menu runs from Bengali sabji and kichari to salads, pizza, stromboli and gutabi, with a large tea selection, fresh juices and the traditional Indian yoghurt drink lassi.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so ask which dishes are vegan when you order. Order the kichari, add a gutabi, and browse the bookshop while you wait.
