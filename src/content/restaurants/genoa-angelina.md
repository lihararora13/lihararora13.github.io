Angelina is a vegetarian and vegan spot on Piazza Colombo, open for lunch Monday to Saturday, and the budget pick on this list. The kitchen specialises in vegetarian and vegan cooking with room for different diets, and locals call it a pleasant discovery with honest prices.

Order from the daily vegetarian and vegan dishes, the crostata and the cakes. It is a daytime-only spot, so come for lunch rather than dinner.
