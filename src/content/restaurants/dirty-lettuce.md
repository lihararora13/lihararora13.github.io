Why we love it — Mississippi-born cooking, fully vegan, winning readers'
polls against every soul food joint in town, vegan or not. The seitan fried
"chicken" sandwich is the kind of thing you think about days later, mid-meeting,
while staring out a window. The Cajun mac 'n' cheeze does not taste like a
compromise. Nothing here does.

Order the ribs, the sandwich, the mac, and the konjac "shrimp", then accept
that you have a new favorite cuisine situation. Your wallet will be pleased
too: this is some of the best-value eating in the city.
