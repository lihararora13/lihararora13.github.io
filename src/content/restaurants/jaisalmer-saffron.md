Saffron is Jaisalmer's fine-dining entry: the restaurant of Hotel Nachana Haveli at Chhangani Chowk, with fort views and a polished menu that leans Rajasthani and North Indian. It is the most elegant meal on this list, and the one to book for a special evening in the golden city.

The Rajasthani thali is the order, with the ghee held and the curd left off, and the seasonal sabzi behind it. Come for sunset, when the fort turns the colour of the restaurant's name.
