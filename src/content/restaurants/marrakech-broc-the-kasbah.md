Broc the Kasbah is a concept store turned vegetarian restaurant, and almost all of its starters and mains are vegan-friendly. The menu runs from vegetable tagine and tajina, which sounds like tagine but is very different, to sweet chilli rice, a vurger with potatoes and samosas. It is fun, quirky and very Marrakech.

Order the vurger if you want comfort, the tagine if you want tradition, and browse the shop after. It is the playful end to the list.
