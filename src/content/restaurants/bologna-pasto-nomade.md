Pasto Nomade is an artisan lab of plant-based and vegan takeaway cooking, catering and courses, a little north of the centre in Bolognina. The menu runs from seitan souvlaki and sardinian gnocchetti to eggplant parmigiana, briam and a carbonara nomade, all of it vegan.

It is open for lunch on weekdays, so treat it as a lunch stop or a catering pick rather than a dinner. Order the carbonara nomade and the seitan souvlaki.
