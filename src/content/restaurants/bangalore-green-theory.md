Green Theory is the pause button in the middle of Bangalore: a charming old bungalow on Convent Road in Richmond Town, wrapped in a small garden with a library inside, and one of the city's most dependable vegetarian restaurants for years. It is not fully vegan, but vegans are well looked after here, with a global menu that runs from Italian to Asian to Middle Eastern, and most dishes can be made vegan on request. A second branch in Indiranagar serves the same menu.

Come for a slow lunch under the trees. The broccoli almond soup (made with almond milk) and the spicy tofu pizza are the house signatures, the Thai curry bowls are a regular favourite, and the Mexican tortilla soup is the thing to order on a rainy day. It is the kind of place mixed-diet groups default to, because everyone leaves happy.
