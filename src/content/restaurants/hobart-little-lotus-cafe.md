Little Lotus Cafe is a cafe on Victoria Street in Hobart, serving eggplant curry, avocado sourdough with scrambled tofu, and apple pancakes. It is a bright, modern cafe a short walk from Little Poet, and it suits a brunch or a light lunch.

Order the eggplant curry and the avocado sourdough with scrambled tofu, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is central and reliable.
