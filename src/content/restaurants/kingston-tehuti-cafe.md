Tehuti Cafe is a 4.5-rated plant-based and raw cafe in the Loshusan Barbican Shopping Center, offering salads, pizzas, wraps and desserts. It relocated from 3 Lyncourt, so use the Barbican address on this page.

Order a raw pizza and a quinoa bowl with avocado, plantain and stuffed tomato, and save room for a raw cake from the display case. It is cash only, open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm.
