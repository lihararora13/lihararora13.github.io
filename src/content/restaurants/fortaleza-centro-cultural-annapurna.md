Centro Cultural Annapurna is a vegan-friendly brazilian spot in Iracema. Annapurna doubles as a cultural centre, so lunch comes with exhibitions and events rather than just a menu board.

Order the Prato vegetariano do dia, Sopa do dia, and Chá da casa. A good lunch stop if you are already wandering Iracema's galleries.
