Padayal is the most unusual restaurant on this whole list: a South Indian restaurant where no dish has ever been near a flame. Founder Dr. R. Padayal Sivakumar opened it in 2021 and has built more than 2,500 recipes using only soaking, grinding and hand-binding, with no oil, no boiling, no onion, no garlic and no dairy. Homegrown magazine called it the world's first exclusive South Indian raw vegan restaurant, and the team was still serving its banana-leaf spreads at a green business event in Vietnam in November 2026.

Order the full raw vegan meal and the aval biryani. This is not a gimmick kitchen, it is a philosophy you can eat, and it is the single best reason for a vegan to visit Coimbatore.
