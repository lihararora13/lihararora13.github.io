Tiny Tiny is the current name of the business that began in 2010 as Flat Caps Coffee, widely credited as Newcastle's first speciality coffee shop, and the rebrand kept the same team and the same Carliol Square site. The kitchen roasts its own coffee and cooks breakfast and brunch properly, with vegan dishes across the menu and nearly everything doable gluten free.

The vegan shakshuka and the huevos rancheros with vegan feta are the plant-based headliners, and the counter of bakes, including a pecan and apple cinnamon bun, is worth a look on the way out. It serves meat as well, so file this one as vegan-friendly.
