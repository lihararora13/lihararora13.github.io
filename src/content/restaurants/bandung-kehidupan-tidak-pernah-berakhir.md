Kehidupan Tidak Pernah Berakhir, meaning Life Never Ends, is the biggest and most economical vegetarian restaurant in Bandung, a buffet on Pajajaran Street in the city centre where both locals and tourists eat. All dishes are cooked without MSG, coconut milk or meat, and the top row of the buffet is the slightly pricier pick.

Order the mushroom sate sticks, around 6,500 rupiah each, and build a buffet plate around them. It is a vegan buffet, open daily from 6.30am to 9pm, and it is the cheapest proper vegan meal in the city.
