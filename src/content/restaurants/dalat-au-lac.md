Au Lac, also called Com Chay Au Lac, is the vegan kitchen of Dalat's most famous chay restaurant, on Phan Dinh Phung in the centre, open daily from 6am to 8pm. Every dish, from pho to spring rolls to banh cuon, is 100% plant-based, and the room has air conditioning and card payment, which makes it one of the most comfortable vegan meals in town.

Order the pho and the spring rolls, and try the banh cuon. It is inexpensive, central and open early, which makes it the best vegan breakfast in Dalat.
