Andrum is a green oasis in the middle of Gothenburg, a vegetarian buffet restaurant on Ostra Hamngatan, a short walk from the central station. The format is all-you-can-eat: two hot mains, two soups and a salad bar, with a wide selection of vegan food from both the buffet and the kitchen, and a few desserts that are vegan too.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but the vegan spread is generous and the price is kind. Go hungry, fill your plate from the vegan side of the buffet, and save room for dessert.
