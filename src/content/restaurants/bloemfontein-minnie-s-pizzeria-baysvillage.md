Minnie's Pizzeria in Bays Village is the neighbourhood pizza option, a casual Italian room with vegan pizza options and delivery. It is the evening fallback when the cafes have closed.

Order a vegan pizza and confirm the cheese is plant-based. It is a short hop from MelG's Kitchen in the same suburb.
