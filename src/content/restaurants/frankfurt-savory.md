Savory is Frankfurt's vegan bistro-bakery, an industrial-style room in Bockenheim where the menu covers sandwiches, burgers, hot dogs and salads, the daily specials run to chili, soups and waffles, and the cupcakes and cakes are the reason to stay for a second coffee. There is live music on Saturday evenings, and the whole place is companion-animal friendly.

Come for lunch, order a burger and a cupcake, and come back on Saturday for the music. This is the neighbourhood bistro every city wishes it had.
