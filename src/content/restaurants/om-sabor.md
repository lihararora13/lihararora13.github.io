Om Sabor might be the most fun dinner in this guide. The fully vegan
kitchen lives inside Phonobar, an audiophile cocktail lounge in Hayes
Valley, where live DJs spin while the chefs send out cosmic Latin plates:
loaded carne asada nachos, jackfruit "crab" arancini with salsa macha
aioli, oyster mushroom calamari that converts seafood lovers, and a vegan
clam chowder reviewers describe as the closest thing to the real deal they
have found.

The buzz is current and strong, with happy hour from 5 to 7 on weekdays
and a whole city of food writers raving about the pesto mac and cheese.
Come for the cocktails, stay for the beats, and order the chowder because
when in San Francisco.
