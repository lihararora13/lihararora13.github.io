Velo Vege is a fully vegan hot dog stand situated just outside the Jardin des Plantes, on Allees Jules Guesde, and one of the most wholesome foodie experiences in Toulouse. The menu is plant-based hot dogs and street food, served from a stand rather than a restaurant, so it is a lunch or afternoon snack rather than a dinner.

The pin on this page is at street level, as the stand has no house number. Order a vegan hot dog and eat it in the gardens.
