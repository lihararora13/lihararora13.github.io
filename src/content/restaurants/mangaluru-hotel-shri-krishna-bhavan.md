Hotel Shri Krishna Bhavan is the Bendoorwell pure-veg hotel restaurant, a Kankanady workhorse open from 7:30 in the morning to 9:30 at night. The menu is South Indian tiffin and meals, served to the office and hospital crowd that passes through the circle all day.

Order the masala dosa or the veg meal and ask for it without ghee. It is a solid, central, always-open option when the smaller cafes have closed.
