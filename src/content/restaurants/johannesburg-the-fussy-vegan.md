You cannot talk about vegan food in South Africa without The Fussy Vegan, and the Greenside cafe is the one to head for. Voted the Best Vegan Restaurant in Joburg in 2021 and 2022, it holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow across all three of its branches, and its philosophy is simple: vegan food should never feel like a compromise. The menu is pure comfort, deep-fried burritos, smoky facon subs, seitan ribs and biltong, with kombucha and kefir on the side and cruelty-free body products on the shelves.

It is casual, urban and unpretentious, the kind of place you take a sceptic for lunch and watch them go quiet halfway through a chimichanga. Order the Texan Chimichanga, a deep-fried burrito stuffed with plant-based goodness, the Tofu Facon Sub for that smoky salty crunch, the seitan ribs, and the nachos originale to share. The Blairgowrie and Fourways branches serve the same menu if you are closer to those.
