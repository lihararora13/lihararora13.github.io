Ramma is Barra's natural kitchen: a health-food buffet with a myriad of vegan and vegetarian options, fresh fruit juices and vegan desserts, a short walk from the beach. It is the obvious lunch stop after a morning on the sand, generous, healthy and easy.

Come after the beach, order a natural buffet plate, and finish with a vegan dessert. This is the one for a beach-day lunch in Barra.
