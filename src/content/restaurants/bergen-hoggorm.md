Hoggorm is a vegan-friendly pizza bar on Nygårdsgaten, south of the Sentrum, and it is the pizza pick on this page. The vegan pizza of the day is the signature, with garlic bread and salad alongside, in a room that suits a casual evening.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so order the vegan pizza clearly. The pizza of the day is the order, and the garlic bread is the thing to share.
