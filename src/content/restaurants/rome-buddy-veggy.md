Buddy is the workhorse of Rome's vegan scene: 1,300-plus HappyCow reviews, fully vegan since 2022, and open from breakfast to late dinner right in the middle of the historic centre. The morning menu is the draw, a vegan omelet with bacon, beans and toast or a stack of pancakes under house-made hazelnut cream, and by lunch it shifts into soups, salads, pastas and pizza without missing a beat.

It is also the most convenient vegan table in Rome, a short stroll from the Pantheon and Piazza Navona, with a relaxed room that works just as well for a quick slice as for a long sit-down dinner. If you only eat at one vegan spot in the centre, this is the one.
