Sweet Alchemy is Church Street's ice cream shop, making small-batch scoops with dairy-free options alongside the regulars. The vegan flavours change with the season, and the cones and sundaes are the way to eat them.

It is a dessert stop rather than a meal, and it pairs naturally with an evening walk down Church Street.
