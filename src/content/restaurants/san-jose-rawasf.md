RawASF, short for raw and super foods, is San Jose's youngest vegan star: opened in April 2023, rated a perfect 5.0 by HappyCow reviewers, and doing the thing almost nobody else in town does, which is raw pizza and burgers and smoothies that actually taste like food rather than homework. The room is bright and modern, the menu is fun, and the smoothies are the real deal.

It is the pick for the health-curious friend who still wants a burger. Order the raw pizza, grab a smoothie, and enjoy the rare vegan spot that feels brand new.
