Tamatebako is Naha's vegan Buddhist table: a small restaurant on Makishi cooking Okinawan food without animal products, using local ingredients like sea grapes, island tofu and Okinawa soba. The menu rotates, running through Okinawan laksa, Taiwanese dumplings and seaweed plates, and regulars call it super tasty.

It opens in the evenings only, roughly 18:00 to 22:00, and the hours change month to month, so check its social pages before visiting. Come for dinner, order the laksa, and settle in: this is the most Okinawan meal in the guide.
