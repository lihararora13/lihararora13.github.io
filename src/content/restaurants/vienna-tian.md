TIAN has held its Michelin star since 2014, making it Austria's only Michelin-starred vegetarian restaurant, and it is brilliantly accommodating to vegan diners: ask when booking and the whole tasting menu arrives entirely plant-based. Chef Paul Ivic and team build each plate from seasonal, regional, mostly organic produce, using the whole vegetable from root to leaf and fermenting, smoking and pickling along the way.

This is the splurge of the list and it earns it. Expect an elegant, minimalist room, a serious natural wine list, and courses that make vegetables feel like the main event rather than the side dish. Mention vegan when you reserve so the kitchen can plan your menu properly.
