Greenr is the real deal: a fully plant-based café from the chain that fans call the best plant-based café in India, set in the Alembic City art district in Gorwa. Everything on the menu is vegan, so ordering here is the most relaxing meal in Vadodara.

The signature bowls and the vegan burger are the draw, and the art-district setting makes it a destination rather than just a lunch stop. If you only visit one place on this list, make it this one.
