Le Georges is a French and international restaurant with a large menu curated by professional chefs, and a decent selection of vegan-friendly dishes. It is a classic, white-tablecloth kind of room.

Ask the kitchen for the vegan dishes of the day and order a vegetable pasta. It is a good business-lunch or evening option in the centre.
