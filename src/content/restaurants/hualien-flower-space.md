Flower Space is a vegetarian tea room and restaurant in Hualien serving a mix of Taiwanese and Western dishes, including pasta, udon noodles, regional dishes and dessert. It uses egg and dairy, so vegans should check for vegan options, but the kitchen is used to the question and the vegan dishes are clearly part of the menu.

Order the vegan pasta or udon noodles and ask about the desserts. Its pin is approximate to the city centre, so check the address before you walk over.
