nami is the dessert counter Phoenix didn't know it needed until it had it. The bakery case alone could stall your decision for ten minutes: cinnamon rolls, oat cookie cream pies, chocolate raspberry bundt cakes, cheesecake, tarts, cupcakes and doughnuts, all vegan and much of it gluten-free too. Then there is the tSoynami soft serve, the house-made swirl that reviewers double-check for dairy because it tastes too good to be plant-based.

It opens at 7am daily, so it doubles as a breakfast spot: the Big Ole Breakfast Burrito, biscuits and gravy, and the Cheeze-Rex breakfast quesadilla all arrive before most of the city is awake. Come for a coffee and a donut, leave with a cake you didn't plan on buying.
