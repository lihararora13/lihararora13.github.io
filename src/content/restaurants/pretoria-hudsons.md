Hudsons is a small burger chain with a separate vegan menu, and the Hazelwood branch is Pretoria's best bet for vegan comfort food. The vegan mains include Beyond Meat patties, vacon nachos, a few kinds of pizza, vegan wings and milkshakes, and vegan mains are discounted on Mondays.

It is open daily from 11:30am to midnight. Order the Beyond burger with a side of vacon nachos, and save room for a milkshake.
