La Gastronomia Veg has been cooking vegan food in Turin since 2000, originally as Gastronomia Vegetariana, and it is still an all-vegan deli and grocery. The counter offers a wide range of all-vegan dishes and fresh baked goods: seitan, tofu, tempeh, salads and lasagne, plus chocolate walnut cake and chocolate-covered figs.

Come for a takeaway lunch or to stock up on deli dishes rather than a seated dinner. It is one of the oldest vegan addresses in the city, and the lasagne is the order.
