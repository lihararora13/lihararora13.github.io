Hearth is the kind of place where the music is as carefully plated as the food. This intimate Oost spot fuses European and global flavors into fully vegan dishes that look like modern art and taste like a party. The beetroot sushi is the headline act, but the whole menu is a confident flex of plant-based creativity.

With natural wines, creative cocktails, and weekend brunch on top of dinner service, it works for date night, group dinners, and that friend you are trying to impress. Book ahead for weekends, because this little gem fills up fast.
