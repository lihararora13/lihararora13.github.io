Vrohstoff is a concept that is unique in Germany, as its own website puts it: vegan, gluten-free and low-allergen, all under one roof. The Theaterstraße restaurant does breakfast, lunch and dinner, from caprese bagels and avocado focaccia through pancakes, waffles, cakes and tortes, and every dish on the menu is plant-based and gluten-free. It is the most careful kitchen on this list, and the food does not taste careful at all.

It sits on the Theaterstraße in the Innenstadt, open for breakfast from half past nine on Sundays and Saturdays and for lunch and dinner through the week. Order a caprese bagel, add a slice of cake, and treat it as the table that proves vegan and gluten-free can both be generous.
