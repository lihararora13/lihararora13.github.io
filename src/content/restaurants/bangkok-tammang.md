Tammang makes history taste like dinner. This fully vegan Isaan spot in Sathorn was listed as MICHELIN Selected in the 2026 guide, the first fully vegan restaurant in Thailand to get that nod, and it did it with everyday prices and big, punchy northeastern flavours.

The kitchen makes its own vegan pla ra and fermented fish sauce from plants, so grilled dishes, larb and som tam land with the funk and fire you came for. Come hungry, order to share, and let the table fill up. Fancy recognition, gloriously un-fancy bill.
