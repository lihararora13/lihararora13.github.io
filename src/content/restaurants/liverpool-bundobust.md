Bundobust on Bold Street is arguably the best vegetarian restaurant in Liverpool full stop, and its Gujarati street food holds its own against any Indian restaurant in the city. The format is a craft beer bar serving small plates: chaat, okra fries, curries and snacks, most of which are vegan or easily made so, with plenty of gluten-free options too.

It is walk-in only, upstairs on the bottom half of Bold Street, and it fills up fast at weekends. Go with a friend, order the vegan combo for two, and add a craft beer from the bar downstairs.
