Eko Lyfe made the list for a reason. A fully vegan, zero-waste cafe in Alwarpet with a ramen bar, open sandwiches, vegan sushi and chocolate gelato. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Alwarpet, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan sushi, Ramen, Open sandwich if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
