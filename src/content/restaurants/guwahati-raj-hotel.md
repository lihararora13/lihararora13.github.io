Raj Hotel is one of the old vegetarian hotel restaurants on A.T. Road, ranked second in Guwahati's vegetarian guides and still going strong. It is a straightforward dining room opposite the Vishwaratna Hotel, open from 9 in the morning to 10:30 at night, serving the North Indian vegetarian staples that have kept it busy for years.

Order the veg thali, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you get a solid, cheap, plant-based meal in the middle of Fancy Bazar.
