Why we love it — Aharveda proves that vegan food can be both deeply Indian
and strictly whole-food plant-based: everything is oil-free, sugar-free,
gluten-free and 100% plant-based, which is vanishingly rare anywhere in the
world. Near Andheri station, it serves thalis, pizza, khousuey and a vegan
gajar halwa that should not work but does.

Order the Aharveda thali for the full tour, the coconut buttermilk to drink,
and the banana walnut cake if you saved room.
