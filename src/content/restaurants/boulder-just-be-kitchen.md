Just BE Kitchen is the rare spot that takes gluten-free seriously without making the food boring, and vegans are well looked after too. The menu is built around comfort: burgers, breakfast bowls and baked goods, all gluten-free, with plenty of plant-based options clearly marked.

It is a friendly, unfussy room on 30th Street, and a great pick for mixed groups where someone needs gluten-free and someone else needs vegan. The burger and the breakfast bowl are the orders to beat.
