Lotus Moon is the restaurant Boulder's vegan scene rebuilt itself around. It opened in late 2024 in the old Black Pepper Pho space, run by owners Thuy Steward and her husband, both vegan, who built the menu around the Vietnamese flavours their own family missed. Everything is plant-based: pho with rich broth, banh mi, vermicelli bowls, curry, dumplings and spring rolls.

It is the top-rated vegan spot in town on HappyCow and the easiest recommendation in Boulder. Come hungry, order the pho, and save room for the dumplings. This is the one to take someone who thinks vegan Vietnamese food is a contradiction.
