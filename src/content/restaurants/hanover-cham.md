Cham, full name Cham The Vegan Kitchen, is Hannover's fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant, and it is the best first stop in the city. The phở is the dish to order: a proper broth, fresh herbs, and tofu and seitan that actually taste of something. Udon, dumplings and a changing set of specials fill out a menu that is entirely plant-based, in a bright modern room in the List that feels more like a good neighbourhood restaurant than a health-food café.

It sits on Wedekindstraße, a short walk from the city centre. Order the phở if it is your first visit, add the seitan dumplings to share, and save room for whatever special the kitchen is running. This is the table that shows Hannover's vegan scene at its best.
