Quintal Vegan is Belo Horizonte's top-rated vegan restaurant, and it earns the perfect score: an all-vegan, sustainable restaurant that grows most of its own produce, including seasonings and leaves, in its garden. The menu runs from hamburgers and sandwiches to not dogs and appetizers, and you can order on iFood, Rappi or Uber Eats if you would rather eat at home.

Come for lunch or dinner, order a burger and a not dog, and taste the garden in every bite. This is the one to take someone who thinks vegan food in BH means a sad salad.
