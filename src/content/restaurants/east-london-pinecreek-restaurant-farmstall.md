Pinecreek is a family restaurant and farmstall out at Holm Hill, with a kids' play area, a garden setting and a big breakfast and lunch menu. It is a meat-serving room, but vegan-friendly dishes are available on request.

Call ahead and ask what vegan dishes the kitchen can do that day. It is a drive out of town, so it suits a slow morning rather than a quick stop.
