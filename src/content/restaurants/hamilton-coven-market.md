Coven Market is Hamilton's fully vegan deli and bakery on Main Street East, a small counter serving breakfast sandwiches, plant-based deli sandwiches and baked goods. The Breakfast Sandy is the morning order, and the deli case offers tofu-based Megg Salad and jackfruit-and-chickpea Toona by the sandwich.

It is a deli first and a sit-down spot second, so plan on a counter meal or take-out. The menu is fully vegan throughout, so there is nothing to decode.
