To1980 Vegan is the plant-based version of Düsseldorf's popular Vietnamese restaurant, right in the middle of Little Tokyo on Immermannstraße. Everything is vegan, the bowls come small or large, and the vegan burger with sweet potato, steamed yeast buns, cucumber, soybeans, mango and teriyaki-mango sauce is the dish people cross town for.

It gets busy, so book if you can. Order a large bowl, add a fresh lemonade, and enjoy the best Vietnamese street food in the city, entirely plant-based.
