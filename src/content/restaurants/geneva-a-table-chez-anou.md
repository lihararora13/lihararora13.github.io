À table chez Anou is not a restaurant in the usual sense: you book a seat at a shared table in the chef's contemporary home and eat a six-course vegetarian meal together with strangers who become dinner companions. The cooking revives vintage Indian ingredients with modern international flavours, and a full vegan tasting menu is on offer alongside the vegetarian one.

It is the most special meal on this list and the most expensive, so book ahead and come ready to linger over champagne, tea and conversation. The masala dosa and the vegan thali are the dishes to remember, but the evening itself is the real draw.
