Holy Llama is the best-known vegan pastry shop in Athens, and for good reason: a fully plant-based bakehouse that also does breakfast, brunch and light lunch, with a perfect 5.0 from 550 HappyCow reviews. The counter runs from croissants and cakes to raw desserts, savoury brunch plates like tofu scramble, fresh juices and smoothies, and bio coffee the staff will happily talk you through until you find your order.

It sits on Nikis Street, a short walk from Syntagma metro station, so it is easy to fold into a day of sightseeing, shopping or walking around the centre. It is not the cheapest vegan spot in town, but the pastries are worth it, and the outdoor seating makes it a lovely slow-morning stop.
