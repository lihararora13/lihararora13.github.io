Akoya is an Asian restaurant on the Ludwig-Erhard-Allee in the Südstadt, doing sushi, curries and noodles with vegan options across the menu. It keeps long hours, lunch and dinner seven days a week, which makes it the reliable Asian table when the fully vegan kitchens have closed for the day. The room is modern, the service is quick, and the vegan sushi is the dish to order.

Order a vegan sushi set, add a tofu curry, and treat it as the late, easy Asian dinner in the Südstadt.
