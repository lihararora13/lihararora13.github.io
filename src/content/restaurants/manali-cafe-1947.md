Cafe 1947 is one of the Old Manali lane's long-running names, and it earns the spot on every Manali cafe list. Wood-fired pizza, pasta, momos and thukpa, and the apple pie that travellers keep ordering with ginger-lemon-honey tea.

Order the veg pizza without cheese and the apple pie. It is a lane cafe, so expect a climb and a wait at peak times, and check winter hours before walking up.
