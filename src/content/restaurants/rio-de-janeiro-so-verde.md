Só Verde is Botafogo's answer to the question of what to eat when you want something fun, fast and fully vegan. It is a bar and restaurant in one, it holds a 4.5 on HappyCow, and the menu is exactly what a hungry evening calls for: burgers, onion rings, croquettes, fries, plus traditional Brazilian dishes for when you want something a bit more substantial. It is one of several Só Verde locations around the city, and the Botafogo one is the most central for visitors.

Prices are low, the room is casual, and it is open daily from late morning into the evening. Order a burger and onion rings, add a Brazilian plate if you are still hungry, and enjoy the rare pleasure of a fast-food joint where everything on the menu is fair game.
