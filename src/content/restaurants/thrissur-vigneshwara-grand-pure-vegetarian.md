The Vigneshwara Grand is the pure-veg restaurant on Chembottil Lane, and its distinction is the clock: open from 7 in the morning to 11:30 at night, the latest veg kitchen in central Thrissur. The menu covers South Indian and North Indian, and the room is built for family dinners that run long.

Order the masala dosa or a North Indian veg curry, and ask for the dairy to be held. It is the dinner pick when everything else has closed.
