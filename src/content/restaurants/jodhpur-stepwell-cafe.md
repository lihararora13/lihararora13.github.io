Stepwell Cafe is the café every Jodhpur visitor ends up at: a bright room on the edge of Toorji Ka Jhalra, the 18th-century stepwell in the walled city, with blue walls, fort views and a menu that takes vegan requests in its stride. It is the most photographed meal on this list, and the food earns the attention.

Order a vegan thali or a grilled vegetable sandwich, ask for dairy-free on request, and stay for the light changing over the stepwell. Come early for a quiet table, because the sunset crowd is real.
