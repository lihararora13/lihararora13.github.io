Viva! Vegetarian Grill is Eugene's all-vegan food cart: a cart on Willamette Street in downtown Eugene serving the fast-food classics that vegans usually miss, a tempeh reuben, a veggie dog, a cheesesteak and tempeh satay, all plant-based. It is takeout only, cheap, and the most direct answer to a craving in this guide. HappyCow lists it all-vegan, and local guides call it the cart to find first.

Hours are Monday to Saturday 11am to 4pm, closed Sundays. Order the tempeh reuben and eat it on the go: there is no dining room.
