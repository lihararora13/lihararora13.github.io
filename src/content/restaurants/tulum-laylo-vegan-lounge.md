Laylo is Tulum's vegan lounge, a small fully vegan restaurant and bar in Centro with a cool, laidback vibe. The beetroot burger is the signature when it is on the menu, and the power bowls and the plate of three tacos are the other orders, with burgers running about 220 to 250 pesos.

It is an evening place, open Wednesday to Sunday from about 5pm to 10pm and closed Monday and Tuesday. Ask about the beetroot burger when you arrive, since it is not always listed.
