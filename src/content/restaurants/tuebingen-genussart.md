GenussArt is a vegetarian and vegan bistro café on the Eisenbahnstraße, by the station, in a modern glass-fronted room with an industrial-chic interior, wooden floors and hanging plants. The menu runs from Bircher muesli and filled rolls through salad bowls with tofu to homemade cakes and matcha affogato, and it is open seven days a week, which makes it the reliable all-week table in Tübingen. It is also the venue for the town's silent reading club on Sunday mornings.

Order a green salad bowl with tofu and a matcha affogato, add a slice of homemade cake, and treat it as the easy brunch that works any day of the week.
