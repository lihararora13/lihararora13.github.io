Why we love it — Bubita is where vegan travellers in Barcelona
fall in love with Spanish food all over again. Steps from the Picasso
Museum, this little Born gastro-bar veganizes everything: calamari
rings, aged cashew cheeses, seafood paella, all 100% plant-based. It
used to be BarCeloneta, but the rebrand brought it to the Born and
made it the city's go-to for vegan tapas.

Order the paella, a pile of croquetas, and a pitcher of the
fruit-loaded homemade sangria, then watch the old town go by. This is
tapas culture with zero compromise and maximum fun.
