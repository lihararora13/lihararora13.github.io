Chay Viet Tadilen is a Vietnamese restaurant on the Juliuspromenade, and its food menu is presented as fully vegan: phở, rice noodles, curries, vegan tapas and tofu dishes, with Vietnamese drip coffee to finish. It is open seven days a week from eleven to ten, which makes it the most reliable vegan dinner table in Würzburg, and the kitchen is confident rather than cautious with its flavours.

One note: the food menu is vegan, but individual drinks are worth a quick check, as milk or honey can appear there. Order the phở, add a plate of vegan tapas, and treat it as the evening anchor of the Juliuspromenade.
