BABA Handmade Cafe is a fully vegan Polish café on Georg-Schwarz-Straße in Lindenau, and it is the sweetest room on this page. The piroggen are the signature, handmade and filled, with pistachio lattes, matcha cake and stollen french toast alongside, all of it plant-based.

It is the cheapest pick on this page and the one to choose for a slow morning. Order the piroggen and a pistachio latte.
