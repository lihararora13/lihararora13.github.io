Ginko is a vegetarian and vegan restaurant in the centre, founded in what was then Austria's only vegetarian restaurant in 1976, and 100% vegan since 2021. It runs a self-service buffet priced by weight plus Thai curry, burgers, lasagne and vegan-bio cakes, and it is rated 4.8 from 210 reviews.

Order from the buffet by weight, the Thai curry and a vegan brownie, and come Monday to Saturday, since it closes on Sundays.
