Chay Chay is Münster's fully vegan Asian fusion restaurant on Frauenstraße, run by the royals and rice team and specialising in Vietnamese and Korean dishes. The menu runs to phở, bibimbap, summer rolls and Korean fried dishes, with delivery and takeaway alongside the small dining room, and a lunch and dinner split that keeps the kitchen focused.

It is open Monday to Saturday with a lunch service from twelve until three and dinner from half past five, and Sundays from one. Order the phở first, share the summer rolls, and treat it as Münster's best vegan Asian table.
