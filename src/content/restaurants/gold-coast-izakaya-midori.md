Izakaya Midori made the list for a reason. The Gold Coast's only plant-based Japanese restaurant: sushi, ramen, karaage and even sashimi, reimagined with inventive vegan ingredients. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Reedy Creek, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan sushi, Vegan ramen, Karaage if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
