Kusa, which long-time fans may remember as SUPE Vegan, is the Bukit Bintang answer to the question of whether vegan sushi can be a special-occasion food. HappyCow rates it among the city's top vegan tables, and the hand rolls, maki and tempura are made with the kind of care that makes non-vegans stop asking questions.

Splurge a little: the sushi is the headline, the thick, spicy Kusa ramen with silken tofu is the comfort act, and the seaweed fried rice is the better-value crowd-pleaser. Closed Tuesdays, and worth planning around.
