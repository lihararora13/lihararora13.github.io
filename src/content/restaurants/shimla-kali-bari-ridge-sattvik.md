Kali Bari Ridge Sattvik sits below the Kali Bari temple on Mall Road West, a 100% pure-vegetarian, egg-free kitchen doing hing-kachori, cholar dal and luchi-aloo dum with no onion or garlic, open 7:30 AM to 9 PM. Jain and Navratri vrat platters are available on request.

Order the hing-kachori and the cholar dal. It is the most devotional table on this list, and the food is cooked with a care that shows.
