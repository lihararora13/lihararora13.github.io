Saarangaa Bhojan Shala is a South Indian vegetarian restaurant in Al Khan, next to the Petrofac Towers, and reviewers praise it for listening: order a dosa fried without oil or butter and it arrives as a healthy vegan dish. The price, cleanliness and quality are all rated high.

Order a dosa without oil or butter and specify no ghee. It is the best South Indian meal in Sharjah, and the staff are used to vegan requests.
