Saravanaa Bhavan is part of the international chain of South Indian vegetarian restaurants, with a branch on Prince Faisal Bin Turki Bin Abdulaziz Street in Al Murabba at Riyadh Avenue Mall. It serves dosas, idlis and more, and uses dairy, so let staff know if you are vegan and do not want yogurt, ghee or cream.

It is open daily from 8:00am to midnight and rated 5.0 on HappyCow. Order a dosa with sambar and an idli. Note the pin is at street level.
