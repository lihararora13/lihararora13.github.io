Common Ground Cafe on Temple Road does Tibetan and Western food well, with vegan and gluten-free choices across the menu. It is one of the reliable all-day tables in McLeod Ganj, and the guides name it alongside the best in town for Tibetan food.

Order the veg thukpa and the veg momos, and take a Tibetan tea with it. It is a good meeting point before a walk up to Bhagsu.
