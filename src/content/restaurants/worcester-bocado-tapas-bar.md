Bocado Tapas Bar is a tapas bar on Winter Street in downtown Worcester, doing small plates with vegan options. It is the evening pick on this list, and the room is built for sharing plates over drinks.

Order the vegan tapas and the patatas bravas, asking for the vegan preparation. It is a good stop for a group that wants to share.
