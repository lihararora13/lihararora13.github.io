Rango de Planta is a delightful haven for plant-based food lovers, tucked across from Cine Passeio in Centro. The menu is mostly burgers, including the Big Veg burger with large fries, alongside dishes like plant-based meatballs with rice, an Arabic platter, casserole and stroganoff. It is cosy, attentive and entirely vegan, and reviewers call it a must-visit for vegans in Curitiba.

Come for lunch or dinner, order the Big Veg burger, and add the Arabic platter to share. This is the one for a proper vegan burger in the historic centre.
