Reunion Cafe Luang Prabang is a vegan restaurant near Dara Market, rated 5 cows on HappyCow, serving ramen, noodle soups, fried rice, curries, fresh salads, stir-fries and a variety of mock meat dishes. It is part of the same group as Reunion Cafe in Vientiane, and it is open Monday to Friday and Sunday, 8am to 8pm, and Saturday 3pm to 8pm. Order by call or Facebook, as the kitchen takes orders that way.

Order the ramen and a curry, and try the mock meat dishes. It is the only fully vegan restaurant in Luang Prabang and the natural first stop for any vegan visiting the city.
