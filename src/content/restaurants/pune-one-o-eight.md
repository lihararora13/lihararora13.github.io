One O Eight made the list for a reason. Koregaon Park's breezy vegetarian cafe with a generous vegan side: smoothie bowls, nourishing mains and three kinds of plant milk. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Koregaon Park, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Smoothie bowl, Vegan pasta, Cold coffee with oat milk if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
