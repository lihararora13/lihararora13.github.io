Breads On Oak is the kind of bakery that makes non-vegans do a double take. The Oak Street flagship is 100 percent plant-based and organic, turning out artisan breads, breakfast biscuits, soups, sandwiches, and pastries that have earned it shoutouts from national outlets and a spot on Eater's vegan map. Start your day here, full stop.

The Egg O'Vegan, a sandwich of tofu scramble, vegan bacon, and melted cheese on a homemade buttermilk biscuit, is the headline act. Come Carnival season, the vegan king cake is the thing to order, and yes, it counts as breakfast. The cafe stays busy with locals, which tells you everything: this is a neighborhood bakery first, a vegan landmark second.
