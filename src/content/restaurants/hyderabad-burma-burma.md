Burma Burma made the list for a reason. The Burmese chain's Hyderabad outpost, with clearly marked vegan options: tea leaf salad, khow suey and coconut curries. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Jubilee Hills, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Tea leaf salad, Vegan khow suey, Tofu curry if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
