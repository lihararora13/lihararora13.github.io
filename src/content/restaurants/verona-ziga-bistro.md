Ziga Bistro is a vegetarian bistro on Via IV Novembre, ideal for brunch and vegan breakfasts, and the alternative to La Magnolia on the other side of the city. It is a daytime spot with a vegan thread through the brunch menu.

Order the vegan breakfast dishes and the brunch plates, and come in the morning, when the bistro is at its best.
