Sakura is a Japanese restaurant on Rwanda Street in Bole with a separate vegan menu section, a rarity in Addis. It lists vegetable rolls, avocado rolls, pan-fried yakisoba, vegetable dumplings, spring rolls and steamed buns with vegetable filling.

It is open Monday to Saturday for lunch and dinner. Order the avocado rolls and the yakisoba, and note the pin is at street level on Rwanda Street.
