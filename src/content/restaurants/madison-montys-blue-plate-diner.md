Monty's Blue Plate Diner is a retro 1950s diner drenched in blue, serving breakfast all day every day on Atwood Avenue, with a dedicated vegan menu section. The meatless loaf is the dish locals name first, even the non-vegetarians, and the tofu scramble and the Sheldon sandwich cover the breakfast side, while the scratch-made pies close the meal.

It is open daily from 7:30am to 9pm. Come for breakfast at any hour, and ask for the vegan menu.
