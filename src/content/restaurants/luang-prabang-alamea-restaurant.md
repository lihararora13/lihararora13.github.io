Alamea Restaurant is a Lao restaurant in Ban Phonehueng, serving local Lao cuisine with vegan dishes including tofu curry, Mekong seaweed and mango sticky rice. It is a neighbourhood kitchen rather than a destination, and it is the most easterly pick in this guide.

Order the tofu curry and the Mekong seaweed, and finish with mango sticky rice. Its pin is approximate to Ban Phonehueng.
