Gopal's has been serving vegetarian street food in Chandigarh for over 50 years, and the chole bhature alone has a fan club. The thalis are a good way to sample several dishes at once, and the sweets counter is the local favourite it has always been.

Ask for the chole bhature without dahi and the pav bhaji without butter, and you have one of the city's great cheap vegan-friendly meals. It is busy, loud, and exactly right.
