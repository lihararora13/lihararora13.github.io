Like An Elephant is the restaurant Lyon's vegans send their friends to first, and it has held a near-perfect HappyCow score since opening in 2017. The cooking is bistronomic in the best French sense: seasonal, local, organic, cooked from scratch, with a menu that changes and a vegan cheese board that visitors still talk about years later. Three courses for around €38 is a steal for cooking this good.

The room is cosy and relaxed on the slopes of Croix-Rousse, the natural wine list is thoughtful, and the kitchen is just as happy feeding lifelong vegans as it is converting sceptics. Book ahead for dinner, order the cheese board, and let the menu surprise you.
