Kurumi is the cafe Da Nang's long-term expats swear by: a bright, work-friendly, 100 percent vegan spot a block back from My Khe Beach, serving healthy international and Vietnamese dishes alongside raw cakes, smoothies and good coffee. The mi quang and cao lau are the local stars, the pesto pasta and mushroom stroganoff cover the international side, and the raw cakes are worth saving room for.

Come for brunch or a long afternoon, order the mi quang, and stay for a cake and a coffee. This is the one you will end up returning to.
