Broccoli Revolution helped start Bangkok's modern vegan wave, and its Sukhumvit 49 flagship still feels fresh: high ceilings, lots of green, and a globally curious menu that hops from Thai curries to quesadillas to mezze without losing the plot.

The broccoli quinoa burger in its charcoal bun is the signature, best chased with a cold-pressed juice or a smoothie bowl. It is socially conscious, reliably tasty, and an easy first stop for anyone new to vegan Bangkok.
