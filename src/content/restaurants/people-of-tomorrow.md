Why we love it — Everything here is vegan and nothing about it feels like a
compromise. The kitchen is run by chefs who treat plants as the main event:
yam and zucchini kebabs that land like galouti, buffalo-style broccoli
with a soubise that replaces blue cheese without apology, pizza cheese
made from the almond meal left over from their own almond milk.

The room helps: low light, cactus-leather seats, coasters cut from
recycled wine corks, and an omakase cocktail bar for when the meal turns
into an evening. The founder's philosophy is "conscious, not preachy,"
and the food carries that confidence. This is the dinner you take a
skeptic to.
