Manjericao is one of Salvador's classic health-food buffets: a myriad of vegan and vegetarian options alongside fresh fruit juices, served in a simple room where locals eat every day. It is the kind of place that does not need to shout, because the regulars already know.

Come for lunch, fill a buffet plate, and order a fresh juice. This is the one for a generous, everyday Salvador lunch.
