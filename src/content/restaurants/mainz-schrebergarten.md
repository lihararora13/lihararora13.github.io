Schrebergarten is a vegetarian snack bar in the Neustadt, rated 5.0 on Google and 4.5 on HappyCow, serving kumpir, soups, rolls, waffles, salads and lemonades, all vegetarian and partly vegan. The kumpir are big oven-baked potatoes, mashed inside with butter, salt and cheese, then filled with vegetarian toppings and sauces, and the vegan versions are made to order.

Order the vegan kumpir, the soup of the day and a salad, and ask for the vegan fillings, since the standard kumpir uses butter and cheese. It is open daily, noon to half eight.
