Counter Café is Kassel's fully vegan neighbourhood café, a small counter-service spot on Westring by the university where the whole menu is plant-based: sourdough with vegetables, overnight oats, an olives and bread plate, and vegan cakes, with oat milk for the coffee. It is rated five cows on HappyCow and has become the daytime hangout for the Nordstadt's student crowd.

It is open Wednesday to Sunday from noon until eight, closed Monday and Tuesday, and it doubles as an event space, so check the programme before you go. The overnight oats and a slice of cake make the simplest good lunch in the area.
