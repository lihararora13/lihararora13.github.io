El Huerto has been feeding Santiago's vegetarians since 1980, which makes it older than most of the city's vegan scene combined, and it still draws a crowd on its quiet Providencia side street. It is a vegetarian restaurant rather than an all-vegan one, but about half the menu is vegan, clearly labelled, and the vegan dishes are the ones regulars rave about.

Order the ceviche de cochayuyo, the seaweed ceviche that fans call out by name, then a pumpkin soup or a burrito, and finish with something from the dessert list. The room is warm and family friendly, the portions are generous, and the organic wine list is a nice bonus for a long dinner.
