Uttar Dakshin is the Naivedhyam family's answer to the question of what to eat in Sadar: a clean, comfortable dining room on Mount Road Extension that does both North and South Indian cooking well. It is a favourite for family dinners and business lunches alike.

The South Indian side is the vegan play, with masala dosa and idli sambar that need no modification at all. The thali works too, with the curd and ghee held, and the room is calm enough for a long, unhurried meal.
