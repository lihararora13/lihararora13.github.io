Xin Cheng Vegan in Jiangning is the second fully vegan pick on this list: a naturally vegan restaurant at 55 Liyuan South Road, serving fried rice, hand-pulled noodles and a variety of tofu dishes, along with other traditional Chinese plant-based specialities. It was added to HappyCow in August 2026, with private dining rooms and meeting rooms for group gatherings.

Order the fried rice, the hand-pulled noodles and a tofu dish. Note that this is the Jiangning branch: the Xinbai store in Xinjiekou closed in 2026, so do not follow older guides to No. 1 Zhongshan South Road. Reservations are required and it is cash only. Open daily 10am to 9pm.
