Seva Table is the place Dubai vegans describe like a secret they almost do not want to share. Tucked into a villa garden off Jumeirah Beach Road, it feels less like a restaurant and more like a deep breath: leafy, calm, a little bit Bali, with a shop, a library corner and wellness sessions running alongside the kitchen.

The food keeps the same gentle promise, fully plant-based, gluten-free, no refined sugar, no GMOs, and still genuinely craveable. Come for a slow breakfast in the garden, order the chickpea omelette and an acai bowl, and plan to linger far longer than you meant to.
