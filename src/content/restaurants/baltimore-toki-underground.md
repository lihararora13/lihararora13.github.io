Toki Underground is a small, loud ramen shop in Station North with a from-scratch vegan ramen and bao on the menu. The broth is built for vegans rather than adapted from a meat stock, which is the difference that matters, and the room fills fast at dinner.

Order the vegan ramen with a bao on the side. The pin for this entry is address-level, so use the street address rather than the map pin alone when navigating.
