Linfa is the table to book first in Milan, and the guides agree: it tops both the HappyCow community ranking and the 2026 Your Vegan Passport list, with a perfect 5.0 from HappyCow reviewers. Everything on the menu is plant-based and gluten-free, from the Cashew & Pepe, a cacio e pepe rebuilt on cashews, to the sushi counter's uramaki, gunkan and nigiri, and the dining room sits on the lower floor of the Visionnaire design showroom, all white seating and backlit bar, with low and no-alcohol cocktails.

In August 2026 Linfa moved from its old Via Bergognone address to Piazza Cavour 3, by the Giardini Montanelli, so use the new address. Book dinner a few days ahead, order the gnocchi or the garganelli with shiitake ragù, and save room for the tiramisu, which regulars keep naming as one of the best vegan versions in the city.
