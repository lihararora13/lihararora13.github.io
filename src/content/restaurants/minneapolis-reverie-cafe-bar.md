Reverie is the heart of the Twin Cities vegan scene, a sunny Powderhorn cafe and bar where everything on the menu is plant-based and everything on tap is local. Brunch runs lazy and generous, dinner slides into cocktails, and the pet-friendly patio might be the happiest place in Minneapolis on a warm day.

Order the crispy cauliflower tacos, grab a seat outside, and save room for the rotating cashew cheesecake. The kimchi BLT with tempura tempeh bacon is a house legend for a reason.
