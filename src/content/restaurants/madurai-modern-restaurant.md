The Modern Restaurant is the heritage tiffin hall on Netaji Road, a short walk from the Meenakshi temple, and the one the old city recommends for appam and banana-leaf meals. The room is plain and the food is not, which is the correct ratio for a temple-town tiffin room.

Order the appam and a banana-leaf meal, and ask for the curd to be left off. It is the most atmospheric pure-veg meal in Madurai, and the temple is right there afterwards.
