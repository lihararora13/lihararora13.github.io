Souley Vegan is back, and Oakland is better for it. Chef Tamearra Dyson's Louisiana soul food institution reopened in 2025 in a bigger Jack London Square home after a two-year closure, and the comeback is the real thing: chik'n and waffles, Creole mac, red beans and rice, and cornbread good enough to have its own song.

It is generous, loud, joyful food, the kind that lulls you into a nap after lunch. Order the chik'n and waffles, add the Creole mac, and do not skip the cornbread.
