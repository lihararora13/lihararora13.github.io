New Gokul is the Kankanady bypass pure-veg room, open from 7 in the morning and popular with families heading through the junction. It is a straightforward tiffin-and-meals kitchen: dosa, idli and veg meals, served fast and priced for everyday eating.

Order the masala dosa or the veg meal and ask for it without ghee or curd. It is the dependable stop on the bypass when Kadri's cafes are full.
