Espacio Samsara sits in the heart of Montevideo's Ciudad Vieja, and it cooks the way the Old City feels: eclectic, colourful and full of surprises. The menu is mostly vegan, with inventive plant-based dishes inspired by global cuisines, from hearty lentil tacos to creamy cashew risottos. The room is cosy and the atmosphere is warm.

Come for lunch or dinner, order the lentil tacos and the cashew risotto, and ask what is seasonal. This is the one for a creative vegan meal in the Old City.
