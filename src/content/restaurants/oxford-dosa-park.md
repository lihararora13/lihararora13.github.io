Dosa Park is a tiny South Indian restaurant and takeaway on Park End Street, next to the station, and its appearance belies its quality: some of the most mouth-watering authentic South Indian food in Oxford, at student prices.

Order the masala dosa and an aubergine curry, and wash it down with a filter coffee. It is a small, cheap, fast room rather than a destination restaurant, and it serves meat too, so file it as vegan-friendly, but for a quick vegan dosa before a train it is hard to beat.
