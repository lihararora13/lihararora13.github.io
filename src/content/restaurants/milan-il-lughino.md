Il Lughino is a vegan lasagneria, and it does exactly what that promises: lasagne and typical Italian dishes, everything house-made by the chefs from selected organic plant ingredients. The menu changes with the seasons, running from tortelli di zucca alla mantovana and zucchini carpaccio to seitan, rice and nettle flan and savoy cabbage rolls, and the tiramisu has its own following.

It sits on Via Solferino in Brera, which makes it the easy stop after the Pinacoteca di Brera or a wander through the design district, and it is open every day from 11:30 to 23:00 with outdoor seating. Come hungry, order the lasagne, and finish with the dessert that vegan travellers keep photographing.
