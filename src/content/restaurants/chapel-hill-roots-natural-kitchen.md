Roots Natural Kitchen is a fast-casual restaurant on West Franklin Street, Building A Suite 115, doing build-your-own grain bowls with a strong vegan bench. It is the quickest lunch on this list, and the best stop for a customizable meal.

Order a build-your-own vegan bowl with a grain base and roasted vegetables. Ask the staff to confirm the dressing is vegan, since a few contain honey or dairy.
