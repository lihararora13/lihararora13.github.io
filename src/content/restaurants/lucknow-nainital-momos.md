Sometimes the best vegan meal in a city is the cheapest one. Nainital Momos is a small counter in Gomti Nagar steaming plate after plate of veg momos, with thukpa for when the evening turns cool.

The momos are the whole point: thin skins, generous vegetable filling, fiery chutney on the side. Order the steamed veg momos, add a bowl of thukpa, and you have dinner for the price of a coffee elsewhere.
