Plant Kitchen is Winnipeg's all-vegan bakery, working out of a commercial kitchen in Windsor Park and selling at the Wolseley and South Osborne farmers' markets as well as by pre-order for pickup. The range covers baked goods and deli items, all plant-based, and the market stalls are the easiest way to try it without planning ahead.

Pre-order for pickup at Autumnwood Drive if you want something specific; otherwise catch the stall at a weekend market and take home whatever is fresh that day.
