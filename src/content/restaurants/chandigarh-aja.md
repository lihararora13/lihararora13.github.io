AJA in Sector 11 is the falafel-and-hummus stop on this list: a cafe with nicely cooked falafels, hummus, organic plates and vegan options across an American and Western menu, plus a juice bar for good measure.

It is a casual, student-friendly room, and the falafel with hummus is the order. Ask for the vegan pizza without cheese and the kitchen will build it for you.
