La Magnolia is a 100% vegan and organic pastry shop in the San Zeno area, from the same owners as La Lanterna, and the vegan breakfast in Verona you do not expect. A first-hand visit in October 2026 found a cannoli, a croissant with peanut cream, a strawberry tart and a savoury pastry, all for 17.90 euros for two including cappuccinos.

Order the cannoli and the croissant with peanut cream first, then the strawberry tart, and focus on the sweets, which are on another level. They also make cakes to order through their website.
