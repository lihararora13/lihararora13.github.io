Al Quds is the downtown Jordanian restaurant the guides name for mujadara, the lentil and rice classic, and for baba ghanoush done properly. It is authentic, cheap and unpretentious, and the vegan dishes are the ones locals order anyway.

Order the mujadara and the baba ghanoush, add a fattoush, and eat like a local. It is the quiet, reliable end to the list.
