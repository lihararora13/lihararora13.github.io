KF erde is Austria's first vegan university canteen, run by the Cafe Erde team in the ReSoWi building at the University of Graz, open weekday mornings to half two. It is the student-lunch pick on this list.

Order the vegan lunch of the day and a coffee, and come on a weekday before half two, since it closes in the early afternoon.
