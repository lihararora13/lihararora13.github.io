Apna Indian Kitchen is the most budget-friendly Indian vegan option on State Street: a restaurant with a variety of clearly marked vegan dishes, including samosas, curries, aloos, naan and rotis, with outdoor seating and a dining room that stays busy at lunch. The vegan marks on the menu make ordering simple, and the prices are the lowest of the three Indian restaurants in this guide.

Hours are Tuesday to Saturday 11:30am to 2:30pm and 4:30pm to 9pm, and Sunday noon to 2:30pm and 4:30pm to 9pm, closed Mondays. Order a samosa to start and a vegetable curry with roti.
