Bunk is a wings and cocktails bar on Stoney Street, all neon and leather booths, and the vegan wings are the dish Nottingham's vegan creators name first when they talk about the city. The chain runs sites across the Midlands, but the Nottingham original is the Hockley one.

Order the vegan wings and a round of loaded fries, and stay for a cocktail. It is a meat-serving bar with a vegan wing menu, so file it as vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan.
