The Vegan Kitchen is the table to book first in Ahmedabad, and the only fully vegan restaurant in the city: every dish is 100% vegan, with separate Desi Vegan and World Vegan menus, and it holds a 5.0 on HappyCow and a 4.3 on Justdial from over 1,200 ratings, open daily from 11am to 11pm. The kitchen describes its food as healthy, farm-fresh soul food for animal welfare and a cruelty-free lifestyle.

Order the tofu butter masala and soya bhurji wrap from the Desi side, a Quinoa Tabbouleh and the Dal Makhni Combo, and save room for the vegan cheese pizza. It sits in the Yogi Complex on Drive In Road by Reliance Smart; older listings disagree on the floor, so head for the complex itself.
