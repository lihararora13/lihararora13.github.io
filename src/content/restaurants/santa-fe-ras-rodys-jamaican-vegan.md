Ras Rody's is Santa Fe's only fully vegan Jamaican kitchen, a food truck on Agua Fria Street with shaded outdoor seating and a daily combo plate of curried chickpeas, spiced veggie protein, brown rice, and banana pancakes. Reviewers call it the best vegan meal they have had in town, and the fresh juices are worth adding.

It keeps short hours, Wednesday to Saturday, so check the day before you go. The combo plate is the move, with pepper pot soup if it is on.
