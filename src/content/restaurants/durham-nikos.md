Nikos is Durham's plant-based date-night pick, a Mediterranean small-plates room in Brightleaf where the menu clearly labels which dishes are vegan. The hummus, the horta, and the roasted eggplant are the dishes to share, and the room is built for lingering over a spread.

Order several small plates rather than one main, and let the table pick across them.
