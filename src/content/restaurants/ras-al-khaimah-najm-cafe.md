Najm Cafe is a pure-veg Indian cafe in Al Jazirah Al Hamra, and its own website says the whole menu has no meat, fish or egg, which is a rare claim in this emirate. The menu is North Indian, South Indian and fusion, with dosas, idlis, paneer butter masala and chaat.

Order a dosa and specify vegan, no ghee or dairy. It is a drive out to Al Hamra, so confirm hours and reserve through the website before travelling.
