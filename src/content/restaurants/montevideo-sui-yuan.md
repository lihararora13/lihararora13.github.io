Sui Yuan is Montevideo's vegetarian Chinese buffet, and it is a happy, affordable sprawl of a place: a long list of dishes, plenty of them vegan or easily made so, from chop suey and spring rolls to tofu dishes and vegetable plates. It is the kind of restaurant where you point, pile your plate, and go back for more.

Come for lunch, ask which dishes are vegan, and fill your plate. This is the one for a cheap, generous Chinese feast.
