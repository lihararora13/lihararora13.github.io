BailwalG Pure Vegetarian Restaurant is a pure vegetarian Indian restaurant on Tutanekai Street in Rotorua, with an extensive vegan-friendly menu and vegetarian thalis. It is the strongest vegetarian pick in Rotorua, and it is the natural first stop for any vegan visiting the city.

Order a vegetarian thali and ask for it vegan, and try the vegetable curries and dals. It is central and reliable, and it suits a dinner.
