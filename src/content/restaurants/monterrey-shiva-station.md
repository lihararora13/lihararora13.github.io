Shiva Station has been feeding Monterrey's vegetarians since 2014, and its mixed menu is the point: Indian, Mexican and American dishes in one room, with vegan options across the board. Samosas, empanadas, burgers, tacos and a vegan lasagna.

Order a samosa to start and the vegan lasagna as a main. It is the easy group dinner in Mitras, and the room is warm and welcoming.
