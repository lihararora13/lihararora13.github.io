If The Fussy Vegan is the king of comfort, The Vegan Chef is the queen of gourmet fast food, right in the heart of South Africa's financial hub. The Sandton branch at Benmore Centre is rated 4.5 on HappyCow from 25 reviews, and the menu is a masterclass in meat-mimicry: spicy chicken-style patties, vegan prawns, ribs, and a bakery case, all 100% plant-based and built for people shifting to a plant-based diet rather than preaching to the converted.

It is sleek, modern and efficient, the kind of place that works for a quick Sandton lunch or a first vegan meal with a curious friend. Order the Zingy Burger with its spicy chicken-style patty, the Crispy Chickn Burger, a pile of loaded nachos, and the vegan prawns. The menu changes regularly, which is half the fun of coming back.
