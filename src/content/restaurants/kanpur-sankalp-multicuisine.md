Sankalp in Kidwai Nagar Market is the family restaurant Kanpur keeps going back to: a special masala uthappa, a dosa platter, and North Indian gravies that families have been pulling up chairs for over years. It is open 10:30 AM to 11 PM.

Order the special masala uthappa and the dosa platter, and ask for the veg kolhapuri without paneer. It is the South Indian anchor of this guide.
