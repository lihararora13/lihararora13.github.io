Easy Street Diner is the diner breakfast stop in Halifax's Fairview neighbourhood, an American-style diner with a daily vegan special alongside its mixed menu. The vegan breakfast plate combines hash browns and vegetables with beet hummus, salad and toast, and it is the dish to order in the morning.

Call ahead for the current vegan special, since it changes daily, and note the diner is closed Monday and Tuesday. Open Wednesday to Saturday from 9am and Sunday until 3pm.
