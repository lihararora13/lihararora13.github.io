Vleischerei is Leipzig's fully vegan fast-food counter, on Eisenbahnstraße in Volkmarsdorf, and it is the table to start with in the city. The Vöner is the signature, a vegan döner, with Thüringer bratwurst, gyros and gulasch alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it hearty.

It is the German fast-food canon, rebuilt vegan, and the bratwurst is the order. Come hungry, and order the gulasch too.
