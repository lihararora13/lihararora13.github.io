Veganitas is Switzerland's first vegan fast-food restaurant, and it does exactly what the title promises: plant-based burgers, fries and shakes, served fast in the Kreis 4 district. It is the quick, cheap, cheerful pick on the Zurich list.

Order a burger and fries, add a shake, and you have the classic vegan fast-food meal, Swiss style.
