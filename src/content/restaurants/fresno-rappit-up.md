Rappit Up is an all-vegan wrap and bowl shop on West Bullard Avenue in north Fresno, doing roasted cauliflower and chickpea bowls, wraps, and salads. It is the lighter, faster option on the north side, and the one to pick when the burger shops feel too heavy.

Order the roasted cauliflower bowl with chickpeas, avocado, and radishes. It is counter service with takeout as the main format.
