The Henna Art Cafe is the best-value vegan-friendly stop in the medina, and the clearest: vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free dishes are all marked on the menu, so there is no guessing. Prices are budget-cafe level, and local artists do natural henna on site if you fancy it.

Order a vegan tagine or a bowl of bissara, the fava-bean soup that costs about 40 MAD at the good places, and a mint tea. It is the easy, honest lunch between souks.
