Veda House is the teahouse on William Gladstone Street that also serves light vegan and vegetarian food, a non-smoking, relaxed room with books for reading. It is the quiet stop in the guide: come for tea and a light vegan soup or salad, not a big dinner.

It is open daily from 10:30am to 10pm, with outdoor seating in summer. The food is light, so treat it as a café break between sightseeing.
