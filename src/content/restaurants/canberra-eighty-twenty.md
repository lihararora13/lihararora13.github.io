Eighty Twenty is the kind of cafe that makes healthy food feel like a treat rather than a chore. The Civic branch sits right in the middle of town, the menu leans fresh and colourful, and the vegan options are some of the best in the city centre: a buckwheat waffle that eats like dessert, soft tacos, and avocado with mushroom on good toast. It is a weekday lunch spot at heart, open mornings and afternoons.

Come for breakfast or a working lunch, order the waffle if you have a sweet tooth, and grab a cold-pressed juice for the walk back. This is the easy, everyday vegan stop in central Canberra.
