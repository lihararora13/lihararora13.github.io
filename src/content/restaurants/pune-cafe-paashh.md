Cafe Paashh made the list for a reason. A calm Kalyani Nagar cafe with a thoughtful vegan-friendly menu and a garden feel, a quieter alternative to the Koregaon Park rush. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Kalyani Nagar, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Buddha bowl, Avocado toast, Cold-pressed juice if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
