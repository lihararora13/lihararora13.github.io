Crossroads Kitchen is the touchstone of modern vegan dining, and its Las Vegas outpost at Resorts World is the only high-end vegan restaurant on the Strip. After more than a decade, chef Tal Ronnen's menu still covers an absurd amount of ground: inventive meat and seafood takes, handmade pastas, indulgent sides, creative desserts, and a brunch in Vegas that goes especially big.

Order the grilled lion's mane steak, a beautiful mushroom that mimics a nice cut of meat, finished with truffle potatoes and a divine mushroom bordelaise. June 2026 diners are still raving on OpenTable, and the eighth straight VegNews win says the rest.
