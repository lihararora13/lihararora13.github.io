Odete Bakery is a vegan cafe and bakery that takes its croissants as seriously as its coffee. Sandwiches, cakes, croissants, pain au chocolat, specialty coffee and teas, all vegan, all made with care, in a small room with limited seating.

Order a croissant and a sandwich, and drink the coffee slowly. It is the quiet, delicious morning stop before a day of walking Porto's hills.
