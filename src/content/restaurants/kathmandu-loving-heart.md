Loving Heart tops the list for a reason. Kathmandu's 100% vegan home base in Thamel, doing homestyle Nepali cooking, meat substitutes and the first vegan trail in the Himalayas. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Thamel, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan dal bhat, Tofu curry, Mock meat momo if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
