Depth N Green is Gokulam's bakery, and the one that takes dietary labels seriously: vegan and gluten-free choices across the cakes, breads and sandwiches, plus kombucha brewed in house. It is the rare Mysuru cafe where the vegan option is a first-class citizen rather than a subtraction.

Order a vegan sandwich and a slice of whatever vegan cake is in the case, and take a loaf of bread for the road. The kombucha is the quiet star, sharp and properly fizzy.
