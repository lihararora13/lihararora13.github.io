BaganHood earns its hype the honest way: big, layered flavours in a handsome room with an open kitchen, right by Songshan Cultural Park. Even sceptical reviewers who arrived side-eyeing that sky-high rating left converted, and regulars call it an automatic stop on every Taipei trip.

This is vegan comfort food with ambition, crispy chicken burgers, creamy rigatoni that channels a fish dish you thought you had lost, meatballs in a spiced mole sauce. Allium-free versions are available too. Finish with the Thai mylk tea, poured hot over frozen tea balls, because theatre tastes good.
