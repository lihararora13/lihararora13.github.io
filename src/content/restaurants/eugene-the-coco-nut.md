The Coco Nut is Eugene's raw-food cafe: a fully raw, vegan, organic, oil-free and gluten-free room on Chambers Street, serving raw tacos, falafel wraps, coconut soft-serve and green smoothies. It is the purest menu in this guide, and the one to choose if you want food that is entirely uncooked and entirely plant-based. The coconut soft-serve is the dessert to order.

Hours are Monday to Saturday 10am to 5pm, closed Sundays. Order a raw taco and the soft-serve, and check the daily specials board.
