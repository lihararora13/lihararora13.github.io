Cafe Baba is a tiny, famous cafe in the Kasbah, a Tangier institution with a few tables and a view over the medina. Mint tea and light bites are the order.

Order mint tea and bread with olive oil. It is a tea stop, not a meal, and the smallest room on this list, so come early or late to get a seat.
