Suan Eakaparp Vegetarian is a traditional vegetarian restaurant in a garden setting in South Pattaya, on the Jomtien-Sukhumvit road, serving Thai dishes in a quiet, green room. It is a good stop for anyone staying in Jomtien, and the garden setting makes it a calmer meal than the central kitchens.

Order the Thai vegetable dishes and curries, and ask which plates are vegan. Its pin is approximate to Jomtien-Sukhumvit, so check the address before you go.
