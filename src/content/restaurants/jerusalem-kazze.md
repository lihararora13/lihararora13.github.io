Kazze, also written Kaze, is a vegetarian restaurant on Betzal'el Street offering vegan pizzas, pasta and salads, with many gluten-free choices. Vegan pizzas are made with cashew cheese, and the kitchen is used to vegan orders.

It is open Monday to Thursday and Sundays from 8:30am to 10:00pm, Fridays until 3:00pm. Order a vegan pizza with cashew cheese and a salad.
