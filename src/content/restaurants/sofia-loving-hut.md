Loving Hut is the Sofia branch of the international vegan chain, on Rakovski Street near the National Opera, and it is the cheapest vegan lunch in the centre. Everything it serves is vegan, from the döner to the cakes, which come in gluten-free and sugar-free versions too, and the menu runs to vegan mac and cheese, lasagne, pizza, quiche and raw brownies.

It is more a small snack bar with limited seating than a restaurant, and it is open weekdays from 11am to 6pm and Saturday until 5pm. Come for a weekday lunch, not a long dinner.
