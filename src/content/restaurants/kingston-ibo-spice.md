Ibo Spice is a 4.5-rated Ital food spot on Orange Street in downtown Kingston, serving burgers, mixed platters, sips and juices. It is cash only, open late nights, and the advice is to call beforehand to see what is left and what time it closes.

Order a mixed platter and a natural juice. Note that the listing reports caged birds on site, which some visitors will want to know before they go.
