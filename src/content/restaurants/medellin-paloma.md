Paloma is the grand dame of Medellín's vegan scene, fully plant-based since September 2018 and still the most-reviewed vegan restaurant in the city. Longtime visitors knew it as KAIME; it took the Paloma name under new ownership in August 2026, keeping the same boho-chic dining room, woven pendant lights and open kitchen in El Poblado, and the same shareable global menu. Recent visitors in September 2026 call it beautifully designed and refined, and one HappyCow regular calls it simply the best vegan restaurant in Medellín.

The menu mixes small and large plates built for sharing: cauliflower wings, gazpacho, hummus, potato and heart-of-palm croquettes, oyster mushroom flautas, milanesa and shawarma, plus the orange chick'n that won over a sceptical omnivore on the Veggies Abroad visit. There is a bakery case of cakes, cookies and cheesecakes for the way out, and plenty of gluten-free options throughout.
