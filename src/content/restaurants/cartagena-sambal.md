Sambal is a Getsemani bistro built on natural, homemade and fresh products, with several vegetarian and vegan options alongside its Argentinian-leaning menu: wraps, burgers, milanesas, arepas and a daily menu corriente. The room is relaxed, the kitchen is open daily from noon to 10:30 pm, and the vegan dishes are cooked to order rather than adapted from a meat plate.

Order a vegan wrap or the vegan burger, and check the daily menu corriente, which often includes a plant-based main. It is a good fallback when the fully vegan kitchens have closed for the afternoon.
