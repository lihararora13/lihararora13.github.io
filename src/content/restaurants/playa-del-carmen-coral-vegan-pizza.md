Coral Vegan Pizza is the pizza night in Playa: an all-vegan pizza spot on the corner of Calle 10 Norte and Avenida 20 Norte, with a choice of wheat or gluten-free crust and cashew cheese. My Vegan Travels recommends the al pastor-style pie, topped like Mexico's spit-roast pork tacos but made vegan.

The vegan cheeses are not cheap, My Vegan Travels warns, but the pizza is the best vegan slice in town, with empanadas and breakfast on the menu too.
