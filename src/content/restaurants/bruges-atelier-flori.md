Atelier Flori is a vegan winebar with vegan food and live music on Jozef Suveestraat, opened 2019 and since relocated, serving all-vegan homemade finger food on an etagere. It is the evening pick on this list.

Order the tapas etagere, the arancini and the muhammara, and come Thursday to Sunday from three to eleven.
