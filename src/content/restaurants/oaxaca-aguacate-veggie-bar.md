Aguacate Veggie Bar sits on the first floor of Casa de Barro in Reforma, and it is the fresh, healthy stop on this list. Falafel, vegan meatballs, hummus, avocado toast, smoothies and shakes, all made from natural ingredients.

Order falafel and avocado toast, and drink a smoothie. It is a bright, easy lunch, and a good break from Oaxaca's richer moles and tlayudas.
