The Holy Donut is a Portland institution: potato-based donuts, fried fresh, with vegan flavours in the case every day alongside the regulars. The Exchange Street shop is the one to visit, right in the Old Port.

Come early, because the popular flavours sell out. It is a snack stop, not a meal, and it is the cheapest pleasure on this list.
