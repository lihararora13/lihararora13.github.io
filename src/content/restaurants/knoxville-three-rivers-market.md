Three Rivers Market is a co-op grocery and deli on North Central Street, with a deli counter doing vegan sandwiches, salads, soups, and prepared bowls alongside the grocery aisles. It is the most practical stop on this list, good for a quick lunch and for stocking up.

Order the vegan sandwich and a deli salad. Check the deli board for the vegan soup of the day, since it rotates.
