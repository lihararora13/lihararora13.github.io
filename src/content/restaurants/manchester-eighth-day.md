Eighth Day has been going since 1970, a vegetarian health food shop on Oxford Road with a cafe whose whole food menu is vegan, run as a workers' co-operative. The lunch menu changes daily, the kitchen serves from 11am to 3pm, Monday to Saturday, and there is no cow's milk for coffee at all. It is the kind of place that has been quietly feeding the city for longer than most of the Northern Quarter has existed.

The shop stocks thousands of vegetarian and vegan products too, which is handy if you are staying in an apartment and want to cook. Order the daily vegan lunch plate, a soup and sandwich, a slice of vegan cake, and an oat milk flat white. Come for lunch, stay for the shelves, and leave with a bag of something you did not know you needed.
