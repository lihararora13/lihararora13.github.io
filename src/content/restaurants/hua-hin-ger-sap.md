Ger Sap, also written Ger Soup and Jersup, is a Thai restaurant in Hua Hin with a two-page vegan menu, filmed by visitors in October 2026. The vegan page lists dishes at about 50 to 90 baht with large portions, including a creamy coconut-based soup with tofu, tomatoes, herbs and mushrooms served with rice, and the restaurant also serves meat, fish, chicken, beef and pork, so ask for the vegan menu by name.

Order the coconut tofu soup and a vegan curry, and note the portions are large enough to share. Its pin is approximate to central Hua Hin, so check the address before you go.
