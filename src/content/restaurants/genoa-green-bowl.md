Green Bowl sits on Piazza Matteotti in the historic centre, a deli-cafe built on smoothie bowls, nourish bowls and smoothies. It is the lightest stop on the list and the one to pick when the rest of Genoa's cooking has caught up with you.

Order a nourish bowl and a smoothie, and note the split hours: lunch, then a break, then dinner. Sundays run lunch and evening only.
