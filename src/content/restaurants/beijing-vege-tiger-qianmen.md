Vege Tiger, known in Mandarin as Su Hu, is the casual vegan anchor of the Qianmen area: plant-based versions of the Chinese comfort food everyone actually wants, dumplings, noodles and hearty plates, at everyday prices.

It is the easiest vegan meal in the old city, a short walk from Tiananmen. Order dumplings and noodles and keep it simple.
