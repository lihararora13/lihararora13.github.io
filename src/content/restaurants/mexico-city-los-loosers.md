Los Loosers started by delivering vegan food by bike and became, as one guide puts it, possibly the most famous vegan restaurant in the city. The trick is mushrooms: fermented, battered, grilled and ramen-ed, they anchor nearly every plate, and somehow never get boring.

Expect a wait, a leafy patio and chilaquiles people describe as to die for. Arrive early, order the ramen and a burger to share, and enjoy one of the great CDMX originals.
