Veg Veg is the restaurant Curitiba's vegan scene is built around: an all-vegan cafe and diner going since January 2013, serving vegan hamburgers, wraps, hot dogs, coffee, cakes, milkshakes and desserts, all house-made. The famous version of Brazilian street food, the coxinha, is the thing to try first. There is also a mini market stocking all-vegan products, from frozen meals to vegan cheese, and the place is dog friendly with indoor and outdoor seats.

Come for lunch, order a coxinha and a hamburger, and finish with a milkshake. This is the one to take someone who thinks vegan food in Curitiba means a sad salad.
