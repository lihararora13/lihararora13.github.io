Smokey Jo's is the anchor of Udaipur's vegan scene and the only fully vegan café in the city, a fact it wears proudly. It moved from Chandpole Bridge to a greener home in Palri Village in 2026, and the new space is calmer, leafier and worth the short trip out of the old city.

Everything on the menu is plant-based, so order freely: vegan pancakes, smoothie bowls, a vegan burger and a plant-based dessert. Note the hours, Tuesday to Thursday from 4pm, Friday to Sunday from noon, closed Monday, and note the pin is decoded from the café's published plus code, so confirm the location before you set out.
