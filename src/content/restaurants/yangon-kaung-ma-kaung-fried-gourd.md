Kaung Ma Kaung Fried Gourd, also listed as Halal 786, is a vegetarian fried gourd shop on Anawrahta Road in downtown Yangon, describing itself as a vegetarian fried gourd restaurant. It is a small, local kitchen, and it is the most distinctive vegan-friendly snack in Yangon.

Order the fried gourd and the vegetable dishes, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is inexpensive and central, and it suits a quick snack.
