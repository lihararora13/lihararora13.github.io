Bar Alfalfa is a classic Sevillian tapas bar in the quarter of the same name, and it is the most old-school room on this list. Traditional tapas, a lively crowd, and vegan-friendly options including the Andalusian classic of spinach with chickpeas.

Order the espinacas con garbanzos, a few vegan tapas, and a glass of something cold. It is Seville as the locals live it, noisy, generous and very good value.
