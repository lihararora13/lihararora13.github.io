Gowri Parvathi Bhavan is the pure-veg restaurant on 8th Cross Street in Thillainagar, named on the September 2026 list of the city's top five veg breakfast spots. It is a neighbourhood tiffin room, and the dosa is the dish the list highlights.

Order the masala dosa and an idli, and ask for the dosa without ghee. It is the Thillainagar breakfast, and the room is full of regulars for a reason.
