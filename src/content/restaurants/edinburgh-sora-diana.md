Sora Diana is Edinburgh's fully vegan Roman restaurant, run by two Italian women who cook the food of Rome without a scrap of animal product: handmade pasta, supplì, arancini, penne alla vodka and a tiramisu reviewers call the creamiest they have ever had. When its sister restaurant Sora Lella closed in June 2025, the pizza oven and the beloved pasta dishes moved here, so the menu now carries both restaurants' greatest hits in one warm, wood-detailed dining room.

It sits on Causewayside in Marchmont, a short walk from the Meadows, and it is small and cosy, so reservations are a good idea. Order the bucatini cacio e pepe if it is your first visit and save room for tiramisu. This is the table for anyone who thinks vegan Italian food stops at a margherita with the cheese left off.
