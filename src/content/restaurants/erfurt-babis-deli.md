babi's DELI is Erfurt's vegan sandwich shop, and its menu reads like a world tour in four sandwiches: the Sloppy Joe, an American-style tomato sandwich with plant-based mozzarella; the Banh Mi Jack, Vietnamese-style with banana blossom, kimchi and chilli salsa; the Lovely Egg, a broad bean omelet sandwich with caramelised onion marmalade; and the Pink Black, a lentil stew sandwich with fermented carrots and probiotic dressing. Everything is vegan, and the shop does delivery and takeaway too.

The live branch is the one on Kettenstrasse, open Monday to Saturday from half past eleven to half past eight. The second branch on Thomasstrasse is temporarily closed until further notice, so do not go there. Order one of each and share, or commit to the Sloppy Joe.
