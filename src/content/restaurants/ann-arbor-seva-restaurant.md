Seva has been Ann Arbor's vegetarian destination since 1973, and it shows in the confidence of the menu: General Tso's cauliflower that is vegan and gluten-free, a tempeh Wellington, and a tiramisu that reviewers call some of the best dessert in town. The room is a relaxed bistro with a full bar and a weekday happy hour.

It sits in the Westgate Shopping Center, a short drive from downtown, so it is worth the trip rather than a walk-by. Order the cauliflower and save room for the tiramisu.
