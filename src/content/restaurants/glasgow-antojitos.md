Antojitos is Finnieston's vegan Mexican kitchen, tucked inside The 78 Bar on Kelvinhaugh Street, and it is the most fun table on this list: tacos, quesadillas, birria, loaded nachos, padrón peppers, elote ribs, chilaquiles and guacamole, with plenty of gluten-free options across an extensive menu.

Come hungry and order a spread for the table. The birria tacos are the headline, the nachos are the support act, and the whole thing is best eaten with friends and a drink.
