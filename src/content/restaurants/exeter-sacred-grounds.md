Sacred Grounds is Exeter's fully vegan cafe, set inside the McCoys Arcade off Fore Street. The menu is brunch-led with cakes made in-house, and the arcade setting makes it feel like a find rather than a shopfront.

Order the brunch plate and a cake. It is the natural first stop for anyone arriving in the city centre.
