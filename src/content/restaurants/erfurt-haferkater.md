Haferkater is the porridge bar at Erfurt's main station, and it is the earliest vegan-friendly breakfast in the city, open from six on weekdays and seven at weekends. The porridges come vegan or veganizable: Powerkater with peanut butter, banana, apple and dates, Apfelkater with apple sauce, walnuts and cinnamon, Waldbeerkater with forest berries and coconut flakes, plus seasonal bowls, energy balls, cakes, coffee, matcha and chai. It is a chain, but a good one, and the station location makes it the default first or last meal of a trip.

Order at the counter, take a seat or take it to go. This is the practical pick: early trains, late arrivals, and mornings when the old town is still waking up.
