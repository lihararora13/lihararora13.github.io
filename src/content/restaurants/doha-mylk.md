MYLK is the highest-rated vegan spot in Doha on HappyCow, a 5.0 cafe at Gate Mall doing brunch, ice cream, chocolate, cookies, pastries and cakes, all fully vegan, with coffee to match. It is the brunch pick, the dessert pick and the cake pick in one.
