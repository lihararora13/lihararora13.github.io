ViaVia is a long-running restaurant in Prawirotaman, renowned for its international and Indonesian vegetarian cuisine, organic ingredients, cage-free eggs and a ban on palm oil and MSG. The menu runs from salads, fresh bread and fruit plates to veggie burgers, pasta, falafel, sate, gado gado and sayur lodeh.

Order the gado gado and the falafel, and specify clearly that you are vegan, as the staff know what vegan means but the menu mixes vegetarian and vegan dishes. It is the priciest table on this list and the most established, so file it as vegan-friendly.
