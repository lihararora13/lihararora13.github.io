27 Adentro is a Latin restaurant on 6 Avenida Sur serving local ingredients and traditional recipes from the region, and it is the most Guatemalan table on this list. Vegans can choose from a vegetable pepian, portobellos in subanik, the Mayan ceremonial stew sauce, vegan cheesecakes, avocado soup and mushroom ceviche.

Order the vegetable pepian and the portobellos in subanik. It is open Wednesday to Sunday, noon to 9 pm, and it is the pick for anyone who wants to eat Guatemalan food rather than international vegan food.
