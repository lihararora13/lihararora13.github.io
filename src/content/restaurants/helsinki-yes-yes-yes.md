Yes Yes Yes is the fun night out on this list, a vegetarian bar and restaurant in a former McDonald's on the corner of Iso Roobertinkatu, where the food just happens to be vegetarian. The menu is veggie tapas and small plates, with cocktails and a bustling room that fills up fast, and the vegan dishes are clearly marked and genuinely good.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so confirm the vegan options when you order, and book ahead at weekends. Order a spread of tapas for the table, add a cocktail, and enjoy the liveliest room in Helsinki.
