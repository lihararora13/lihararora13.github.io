Guan Shi Yin Organic Lifestyle, 觀世音有機生活館, is a healthy lifestyle shop with a restaurant attached, a short walk from Taichung station in the Central District, and it tops the HappyCow community ranking for the city with a perfect 5.0. The kitchen serves set meals, hot pot, coffee and tea, and the shop sells snacks, oils, dried goods and fresh produce. Reviewers praise the fried king oyster mushroom with rosemary and soy sauce business meal, served with rice, soup and two sides, and the vegetarian black pepper steak, a seitan cutlet with a crisp exterior, and note that the staff speak good English and will tell you clearly what is and is not vegan.

The food is reported to be vegan but the restaurant also offers dairy, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan: ask the staff, who are happy to steer you. Hours are 10:00am to 7:30pm daily, with the kitchen opening for lunch and dinner. Note the listing's last HappyCow update was in August 2024, but the listing shows live hours and it appears on the current HappyCow print page and on spinach.guide's Taichung list, refreshed 25 November 2026.
