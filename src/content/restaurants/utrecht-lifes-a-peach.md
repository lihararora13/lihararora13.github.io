Life's a Peach is a vegan bakery and cafe on Hardebollenstraat, praised in a world top-ten HappyCow round-up, and the sweetest table on this list. The glass case is full of savoury tarts, quiches and sweet pastries, with an orange-topped cake slice as the signature, and the pink peach-shaped sign outside is hard to miss.

Come for breakfast, lunch or an afternoon cake stop. Order the orange-topped cake and a savoury tart.
