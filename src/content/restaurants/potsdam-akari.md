Akari is the Vietnamese restaurant in Babelsberg, the film-studio quarter, and its vegan menu is clearly labelled and generous: tofu sushi bowls, mango sauce with tofu or vegan chicken, peanut paradise with tofu or vegan chicken, Akari curry with tofu, tofu fried noodles and udon curry. It is a meat-serving restaurant, so the vegan dishes sit within a wider menu, but they are a deliberate part of the kitchen's cooking.

It opens daily from half past eleven to ten, which makes it the most reliable dinner table in Babelsberg. Order the Akari curry with tofu and a tofu sushi bowl, and take the short trip out from the centre. This is the Babelsberg dinner pick.
