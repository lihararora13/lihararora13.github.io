Terra is a food concept restaurant on Rua Camara Pestana, open for dinner Tuesday to Saturday, and it is the most ambitious kitchen on this list. The menu moves between Greek, Peruvian and Asian influences, and the vegan dishes are built as dishes in their own right rather than subtractions.

Ask what is vegan on the evening's menu; the green curry and the vegetable plates are the usual standouts. Book ahead, as the room is small and it only opens in the evenings.
