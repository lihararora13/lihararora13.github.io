TULA is the rare restaurant that manages to be a pioneer and a perfectionist at the same time. Opened in 2023 as what was likely Montreal's first 100% vegan Indian restaurant, this colourful Mile End spot reimagines the classics: butter chicken without the butter or the chicken, tikka masala that converts lifelong omnivores, and a jackfruit biryani people cross the city for. Owner Abhishek Arun named the place for balance, and the menu lives up to it.

The weekday thali lunch platter is the smart move for first-timers: a tasting tour of curries, dals, and rice that reads like a greatest-hits album. Weekend brunch is vegan Indian, which is apparently a thing only TULA does in this city, and the Indian poutine is exactly the beautiful cross-cultural chaos Montreal deserves. Everything is made in-house, the service is famously warm, and you will leave planning your next visit before the door closes behind you.
