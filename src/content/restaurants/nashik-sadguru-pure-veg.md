Sadguru is the highway restaurant on this list, a pure-veg stop on the Nashik-Pune Road that has fed generations of travellers heading out of the city. It is simple, fast and honest, the kind of place where the food arrives quickly and tastes like it was made that morning.

Order the dal tadka without ghee, a mixed veg sabzi and hot rotis, and you have a proper roadside meal. The pin is approximate to the Nashik-Pune Road area, so watch for the Hotel Sadhguru signboard as you drive.
