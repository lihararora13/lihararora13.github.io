Peace 'n' Spice is the Indian restaurant that local vegan explorers scored 100 out of 10, the highest mark in their Padua guide. It sits on Via Dondi dall'Orologio in the historic centre, and the vegan dishes are the point rather than an afterthought.

Order the vegan curries, the dal and the vegetable dishes, and ask the kitchen to hold the ghee and cream, as with any Indian menu. It is the strongest full meal in the centre after GingerGi.
