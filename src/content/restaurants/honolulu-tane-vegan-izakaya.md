Tane Vegan Izakaya is the restaurant that converted a lot of Honolulu meat-eaters into plant-based believers: a fully vegan izakaya on South Beretania doing ramen, small plates and tempura in a warm, lively room that lands on best-restaurants-in-Honolulu lists, vegan or not. The ramen is the headliner, and the small plates are the reason to bring friends.

It is dinner-focused and popular, so book ahead. Order the ramen, share the small plates, and enjoy the rare vegan restaurant that food lovers of every stripe agree on.
