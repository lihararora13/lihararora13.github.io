Dyen Sabai Restaurant is a riverside Lao restaurant in Ban Phan Luang, with vegan-friendly Lao dishes and a relaxed setting by the water. It is a good choice for a slow evening meal, and the riverside location makes it worth the trip out of the centre.

Order the Lao vegetable dishes and tofu with rice, and specify no fish sauce. Its pin is approximate to Ban Phan Luang, so check the address before you go.
