Dar Zaki sits in the old medina, a haven of Moroccan cooking in a traditional house, with friendly service and a menu that runs from apricot chicken to fragrant tagines. Vegans eat from the vegetable side: tagines, couscous and salads, cooked the Moroccan way.

Order the vegetable tagine and confirm it is cooked without meat stock or butter. It is the most atmospheric table in Rabat, and the medina setting is half the meal.
