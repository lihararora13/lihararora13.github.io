Lola Conscious Cafe is a small smoothie and juice bar on Main Road in Walmer, built around intentional living and light, nutritious food. It is a breakfast-and-lunch kind of place, with smoothies, juices and vegan-friendly light meals.

Order a green smoothie and the vegan light meal of the day. It is a good morning stop before the beachfront, and it pairs naturally with Baobab a few doors down the same road.
