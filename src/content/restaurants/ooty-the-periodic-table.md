The Periodic Table is the most serious kitchen in Ooty, a 150-year-old heritage building on Commissioner's Road where the menu is a dialogue between European and Oriental cooking, from Thailand to Japan. TripAdvisor rates it 4.8 across 324 reviews, and the listing carries vegetarian-friendly and vegan options.

Order the tofu eggplant dish and the fried idli, and ask about the vegan tasting options. It is the ambitious dinner in Ooty, and the chef's table is the one to book.
