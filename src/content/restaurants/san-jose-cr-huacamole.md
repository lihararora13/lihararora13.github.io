Huacamole, formerly Huaca Mole Sodita, is a fully vegan restaurant in Barrio Escalante, and it does vegan junk food as well as anyone in San Jose: burgers, pizza, tacos, bowls and smoothies, with the Crispy Cheddar Vegan Chick'n burger as the signature. The kitchen moved and rebranded, keeping the same energy, and HappyCow was last updated by the restaurant in November 2026, with visitor reviews through September 2026.

Order the Crispy Cheddar Vegan Chick'n burger. It is one of the biggest and best vegan burgers in the city, and the trip across from downtown is worth it.
