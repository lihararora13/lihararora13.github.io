Arcana is the lighter lunch in Playa, a café in the residential Gonzalo Guerrero neighbourhood north of the centre, with tonics and elixirs alongside the food. My Vegan Travels recommends the saffron rice bowl with tofu, and Roam and Thrive points to the vegan shawarma pita and the reishi or maca elixirs.

Its day off is Monday, not Sunday, so it is an easy Sunday breakfast or lunch, open from 8am to 4pm. It is a café for a light meal, not a big dinner.
