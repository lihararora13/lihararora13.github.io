Why we love it — Virtuous Pie makes its own cashew mozzarella in-house, and
it shows. The pizzas have names like Stranger Wings and Ultraviolet, the
crust has real chew, and skeptics leave converted. Every pie can go gluten-
free, and the ice cream and brownie skillet mean dinner is never just dinner.

Order the garlic knots to start and do not skip dessert. This is the rare
vegan pizza place that locals recommend to non-vegans without a single
qualifier.
