Medialuna is the strongest recommendation in Valparaiso, and the one every local vegan guide names first: a cafe-bakery that is 100% vegan and gluten-free, run by pastry chef Belen Godoy in a three-storey old house a few steps from the Museo del Inmigrante on Cerro Concepcion. The pastries are the point, medialunas, croissants and Danish pastries, executed so well that most visitors never notice nothing animal went into them.

Order a medialuna rellena, the hummus toast with romesco, tomato cherry, pesto and seed parmesan, and a coffee from the local women-run roaster Sorbo. The cafe also serves a mechadito sandwich on brioche and a croissant bon o bon, and it stays open into the evening on weekends.
