Kaon Nilumad is the Filipino comfort-food cafe of the list: classic dishes served buffet-style, plus a burger, cakes, pastries and ice cream, with many vegan options. Note that some drinks and desserts contain dairy or honey, so ask when you order.

Order a buffet plate and a slice of cake. It is a warm, easy meal, and the five-star HappyCow rating backs it up.
