City Silo Table + Pantry is a bright East Memphis restaurant and pantry built around healthy, clean eating, with vegan bowls, salads, and smoothies across the menu. It is the lightest lunch on this list after Raw Girls, and the best one for a group that wants healthy options without giving up flavour.

Order a bowl and a smoothie, and browse the pantry on the way out.
