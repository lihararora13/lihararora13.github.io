La Terra is a straightforward Italian on John Street that takes its vegan menu seriously rather than treating it as an afterthought. Pasta, pizza and starters all have plant-based versions, and the staff can talk through the options.

Order the vegan pasta and a dairy-free pizza to share. It is a reliable evening choice when the fully vegan rooms have closed for the day.
