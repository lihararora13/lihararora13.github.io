Indo Ceylon Cafe - Dosa King made the list for a reason. A mostly-vegan South Asian locals' spot on Galle Road, doing dosa, idli and vada the way Colombo has done them for years. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Kollupitiya, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Masala dosa, Idli, Vada if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
