Peacefood Café is Montpellier's fully vegan cafe, on Rue Saint-Firmin in the old centre. The menu is brunch and bowls, all plant-based, and the room is a calm daytime stop.

Order the brunch plate and a cake. It is the natural first stop for anyone eating vegan in Montpellier.
