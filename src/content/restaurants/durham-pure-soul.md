Pure Soul is Durham's vegan soul food star, a fast-casual room on Durham-Chapel Hill Boulevard that Yelp ranked the number eight vegan restaurant in the country in 2024. Everything is 100% vegan: the NC BBQ sandwich, the soul rolls, the Small Soul Mac, and the vegan ice cream that reviewers call the best in the South.

It is the first table to book in Durham, and it is also the most fun. Order the BBQ sandwich and a side of greens, and save room for the ice cream.
