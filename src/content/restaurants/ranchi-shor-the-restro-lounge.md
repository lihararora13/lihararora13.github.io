Shor is a pure-vegetarian restro lounge on Circular Road, beside the State Bank of India and opposite the Apsara Hotel. It is a modern, late-opening dining room, running 11 in the morning to 11:30 at night, which makes it the useful evening pick on this list.

Order the veg thali or the veg Manchurian, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you will eat well. It is a lounge-style room, so come for a relaxed dinner rather than a quick thali.
