Shangri-La Vegan on Linden is the West Oakland branch of the macrobiotic chain, and it leans buffet: soups, salads, entrees and baked goods, with a daily menu that changes with the season. It is the more casual sibling of the Telegraph location, and the one to hit for a quick, wholesome lunch.

It is simple, affordable, and genuinely good for you, which is the whole point of macrobiotic cooking. Order the buffet plate, check the baked goods, and take the calm with you.
