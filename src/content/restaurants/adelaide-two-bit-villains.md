Two-Bit Villains made the list for a reason. A retro soda bar and diner above Adelaide Arcade, serving vegetarian Americana since 2011, with everything veganisable. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Adelaide CBD, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan beef burger, Vegan chicken burger, Vegan hot dog if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
