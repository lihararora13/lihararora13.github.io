Tai Lang is Tainan's vegan ramen secret: a small counter on Xinmei Street, 80s tunes on the speakers, an owner who speaks some English, and a menu of exactly two fully vegan ramens, a miso soy-milk bowl and a soy sauce bowl. Reviewers call it very good and absolutely worth the wait, and worth noting that the place is hard to find, tucked just around a corner, so allow time to locate it.

Come in the evening, expect a short queue at busy times, and pick between the creamy miso soy-milk ramen and the cleaner soy sauce version. Both are cheap, filling and entirely plant-based.
