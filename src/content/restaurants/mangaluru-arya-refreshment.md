Arya Refreshment is one of Falnir's old veg rooms, a refreshment counter and bakery that has been serving Balmatta Road for years. The format is classic coastal Karnataka tiffin: dosa, idli, vada, and a bakery case for the walk home.

Order the masala dosa and a vada, and ask for the chutney without curd. It is a quick, cheap breakfast stop rather than a destination dinner.
