Sonu Sonu is the back-pocket pick every vegan traveller in Fukuoka needs: a fully vegan cafe in Tenjin that opens for breakfast from around 8am, when almost nothing else vegan is open. Keema curry, pizza, acai bowls and a vegan cheeseburger, ordered by QR code.

Go early for breakfast or for a late lunch. The keema curry is the signature.
