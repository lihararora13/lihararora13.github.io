Emmi's Kitchen is a fully vegan restaurant on the Kirchgasse, doing brunch, bowls and seasonal plates in a bright room in the old town. It keeps generous hours, nine to ten Monday to Saturday with a shorter Sunday service, which makes it the other reliable all-day vegan table in Augsburg alongside Dreizehn. The cooking is international and unfussy, the kind of food that works for breakfast, lunch or an early dinner without ever feeling like a compromise.

Order a tofu scramble or a stack of vegan pancakes for brunch, or a seasonal bowl later in the day. It is an easy, friendly room, and a good second stop if Dreizehn is full.
