The Roti Shack is a Trinidadian roti shop on Lake Street in Cairns, serving vegan potato curry, chickpea curry and dal in roti wraps. It is a quick, casual lunch kitchen, and it is the most distinctive vegan-friendly meal in Cairns.

Order a vegan potato curry roti and a chickpea curry roti, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is inexpensive and central.
