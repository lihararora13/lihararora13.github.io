Darlene's Veg is Eugene's vegetarian diner: a room on West 7th Avenue with rice bowls, burritos, Buddha bowls and a breaded tofu plate, most dishes vegan or easily made so. It is a diner in the best sense, generous, unpretentious and open long hours, Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm and Saturday to Sunday 9am to 3pm. The Buddha bowl and the breaded tofu plate are the vegan core.

Specify vegan when ordering, no dairy and no honey mustard. The kitchen is used to the request, and the bowls are the best value in the guide.
