LOHAS is the Tanukikoji arcade stop: a raw and macrobiotic restaurant where nearly everything is vegan by default, including handmade soba and vegan nut sushi. Ask which items are vegan and the staff will guide you.

Order the soba or the lunch set with cake and tea. It is the most central vegan-friendly meal in Sapporo.
