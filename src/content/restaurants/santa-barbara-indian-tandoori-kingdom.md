Indian Tandoori Kingdom is the Indian restaurant in this guide with its own separate vegan menu: mains, appetizers, soups, salads and sides, all in one place on State Street, with outdoor seating. That separate menu is what earns it the rank: ordering is a matter of opening the vegan pages rather than negotiating substitutions. The room is a classic State Street Indian restaurant, open long hours, 11am to 10pm daily.

Order the chana masala and a vegetable biryani, and use the vegan menu rather than the main one: the vegan dishes are grouped there.
