Down the Hatch is the obvious starting point for any vegan tour of Liverpool, and it tops the list for a reason. The kitchen is 100% plant-based and the format is unapologetic vegan junk food: burgers, loaded fries, dim sum, bao buns, plant-based fried chicken strips and vegan hot dogs, plus a cocktail menu that takes vegan drinking seriously, with no honey, no isinglass and no animal-derived finings.

It sits on Duke Street in the Ropewalks, right in the middle of the city's best eating strip, and the portions are famously generous. Go hungry, order the seitan burger and a pile of loaded fries, and save room for whatever the specials board is doing that week.
