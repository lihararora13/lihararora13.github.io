Raiz Vegetariana is a vegetarian kitchen on Salta, open Tuesday to Friday for lunch, with a longer service on Fridays. It is a small, local kitchen, and the menu is built around the day's cooking rather than a fixed card.

Order the lunch plate of the day and ask which dishes are vegan. It is closed on Mondays and weekends, so plan it as a weekday lunch.
