Why we love it — Detroit-style pizza lives or dies on the caramelized crispy
cheese edge, and Boxcar does it without a single dairy product in the
building. Nearly a thousand reviewers agree, which is a consensus you don't
argue with. Tucked inside the Portland Zipper food hall, so you can pair
your pie with whatever your tablemates are having.

The Jimmy Pesto is the signature, the Bianca is the sleeper hit, and the
vegan drumstick wings will make you do a small double-take mid-bite. Pizza
night is officially back on the menu.
