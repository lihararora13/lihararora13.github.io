Bödhi is a fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant on Karl-Liebknecht-Straße, the Karli, in Südvorstadt, and it is the pho stop on this page. The pho is the signature, with summer rolls, tofu curry and bánh mì alongside, all of it plant-based.

Note: this Bödhi is a different venue from Bodhi in Nuremberg, a different spelling and a different operator. The pho is the order, and the summer rolls run it close.
