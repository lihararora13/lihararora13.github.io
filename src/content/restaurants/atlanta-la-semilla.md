La Semilla started as a scrappy pop-up called Happy Seed and grew up into one of the most beloved vegan dining rooms in the city. The space is basically a greenhouse with cocktails: plants everywhere, rum-heavy drinks, and a menu that proves Latin food never needed meat in the first place.

The Crunchwrap is the legend, a hexagon of seitan beef that will genuinely upstage its drive-thru inspiration. Come for date night, stay for the queso, and fight politely over the last bite of chochoyotes.
