Bunnie Cakes has been Miami's vegan bakery darling since way before it was cool, and Cupcake Wars came calling for a reason. The guava and passion fruit cupcakes are little pink-hearted masterpieces that taste like the city itself.

Gluten-free, allergy-free, and kosher options mean nobody gets left out of dessert. Swing by the Brickell location for a mini-cake or a box of cupcakes, and try to share. You won't.
