Trendy Vegan has cornered the market on vegan buffets in Houston. The all-you-can-eat Chinese buffet, around sixteen and a half bucks, is the headline: mapo tofu, dan dan noodles, fried king mushroom bites, red bean sesame balls, all of it fully vegan and all of it refillable. The Infatuation went back for seconds on principle.

Not in a grazing mood? The bento boxes are made to order and just as good: salt and pepper tofu crisped to order, General Tso's tofu, spring rolls, dan dan noodles, and rice in one neat package. The Greenway room is understated with one giant mural, because the food is the wallpaper.
