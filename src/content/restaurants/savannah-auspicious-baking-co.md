Auspicious Baking Co. opened in Sandfly in 2017 as a Sundays-only bakery, and it has grown into a bakery counter and a brunch restaurant, Bread & Butter, in the same building. The vegan pastries and sourdough are the draw, and the brunch dishes are the reason to stay.

It sits out in Sandfly, a short drive from downtown, so pair it with a drive rather than a walk. Come for the pastries and a coffee.
