Glonojad is one of Krakow's longest-running vegetarian restaurants, a friendly room by Plac Matejki with a big menu of Polish and international dishes and plenty of vegan options. It has been feeding the city's veggies for years, and the portions are generous.

Order the vegan plate of the day, add a soup, and enjoy one of the most reliable vegetarian rooms in the Old Town.
