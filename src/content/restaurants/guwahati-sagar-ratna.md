Sagar Ratna has served authentic South Indian food in Guwahati since 1986, and its Paltan Bazar branch near Nepali Mandir is the one the food bloggers visit. The North Indian fixed thali, at 343 rupees, is the dish everyone photographs, and the masala dosa with three chutneys is the South Indian classic.

Order the fixed thali without ghee and paneer, or go straight for the dosa and a filter coffee. It is open 8:30 to 10:30 daily, and it is one of the most reliable veg meals in the city.
