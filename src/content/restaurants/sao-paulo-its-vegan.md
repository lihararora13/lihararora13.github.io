It's Vegan completes a perfect Consolacao crawl: bakery at number 93, fast food here at 89, pizza at 144, all on Rua Fernando de Albuquerque. The pitch is simple, schnitzel sandwiches, burgers and fries, all vegan, all satisfying.

Closed Mondays and open from midday other days according to current listings, but hours have wobbled in past guides, so check its Instagram before a special trip. For a casual lunch near Paulista, it is an easy yes.
