Rübenrot is the quietest of Augsburg's fully vegan restaurants, a seasonal kitchen in Hochfeld, the residential district south of the old town, cooking local and changing dishes with the seasons. It is the grown-up table on this list: no burgers, no bowls, just careful plant-based cooking in a calm room, and the kind of place Augsburgers keep for a proper evening rather than a quick lunch.

It is a short tram ride from the centre, and worth the trip for the seasonal main alone. Order what the kitchen is proudest of that week, and treat it as the dinner that shows what Augsburg's vegan scene can do when it slows down.
