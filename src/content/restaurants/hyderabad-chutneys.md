Chutneys made the list for a reason. A Hyderabad institution for South Indian food: dosa, idli and vada that are vegan as long as you skip the ghee. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Begumpet, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Plain dosa, Idli with sambar, Masala dosa, no ghee if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
