Chakra House has been feeding Mysuru's yoga crowd since 2002, which makes it older than most of the city's vegan scene and more sure of itself than any of it. The room is bohemian, murals and prayer flags, and the menu is yogic: no artificial flavours, plenty of ragi, tofu and kombucha, and a chalkboard that lists vegan dishes without fuss.

The ragi pancakes are the move, stacked with dark chocolate or fruit, and the nasi goreng is the savoury anchor. Ask for the vegan version of anything with egg or dairy and the kitchen knows exactly what you mean.
