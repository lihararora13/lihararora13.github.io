Taco Vado is a taco restaurant on West Northwest Boulevard, doing vegan tacos and sides. It is the Mexican pick on this list, and the best place for a quick, cheap lunch.

Order the vegan tacos and the guacamole, and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since some tacos are finished with cheese or sour cream.
