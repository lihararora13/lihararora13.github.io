Xuxu is a 100 percent vegan, bio and gluten-free cafe on Avenue de la Reine Nathalie, a short walk from the Grande Plage. Everything is plant-based and gluten-free.

Order the brunch plate and a cake. A good pick for anyone avoiding gluten as well as animal products.
