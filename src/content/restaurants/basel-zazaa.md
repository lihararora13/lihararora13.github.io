ZaZaa is a Lebanese soul-food restaurant on Gerbergasse, serving hot and cold mezze, most of it meat-free, with aromatic vegetarian dishes. It is the mezze pick on this list.

Order the hot and cold mezze, the falafel and the hummus, and ask for the vegan dishes, since the menu also runs meat.
