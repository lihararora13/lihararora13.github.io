Veggie Vegan Buffet is a vegan buffet in Tambon Ban Du, offering a large selection of made-to-order dishes posted on the wall, including pizza alongside Thai dishes. It is a little out of the centre, so it suits a trip rather than a walk, but the made-to-order menu gives more choice than the central carts.

Order from the wall menu, mixing a Thai dish with the pizza if you want a change. Its pin is approximate to Ban Du, so check the address before you go.
