Zrno is Zagreb's organic institution, cooking since 2013 from Croatia's first organic farm of the same name. Everything is certified organic, the meals follow macrobiotic principles, and the seitan cordon bleu has been winning people over for a decade.

It is a calm, two-room restaurant a short walk from the main square. Order the cordon bleu, a quinoa salad on the side, and one of the cakes, the kitchen bakes them properly.
