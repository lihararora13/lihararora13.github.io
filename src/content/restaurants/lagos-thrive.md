Thrive, tucked away in Lekki, is Lagos' first fully vegan restaurant, and it shows in every detail. The cooking leans alkaline and wellness-focused, with vegan egusi, mushroom suya, amala and other Nigerian classics done entirely plant-based. It is calm, bright and deliberate, the opposite of a noisy fast-food counter.

Come for a proper sit-down meal rather than a rushed lunch. The mushroom suya is the dish to order, and the amala with vegan egusi is the comfort plate.
