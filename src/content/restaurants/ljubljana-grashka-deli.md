Grashka Deli is the everyday hero of Ljubljana's vegan scene: a bright, fast-casual bistro and deli where everything is plant-based and most of it comes from regional producers. Sourdough pizzas, hearty bowls, soups and stews, plus a brunch spread and the curiosity that is chickpea ice cream.

It is an easy lunch stop east of the centre, with takeaway and delivery if you are in a hurry. Order a pizza to share, a bowl for yourself, and do try the ice cream, it is stranger and better than it sounds.
