The Southern V proves Nashville's signature dish needs no chicken at all: Nashville Guru and NASHtoday both point hot-chicken hunters here for seitan chick'n with the full spicy punch, served the proper way with mac, greens and all the Southern sides.

Hours are proudly weekend-only, Saturday and Sunday, and locals will tell you to call ahead or order curbside if the dining room is full. It is Black-owned, family-run since its farmers market baking days in 2015, and absolutely worth planning your weekend around.
