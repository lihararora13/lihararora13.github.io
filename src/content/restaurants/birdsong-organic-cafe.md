Why we love it — Birdsong is not all-vegan, but it has been on PETA
India's vegan-friendly lists for a decade for a reason: the vegan menu is
real, not an afterthought. The mushroom risotto swaps cream for cashew
paste, and the egg-free dessert counter is famous in its own right.

A calm, plant-filled hideout on a back lane off Hill Road. The gooey
chocolate cake is the order, full stop.
