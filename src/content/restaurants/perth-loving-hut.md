Loving Hut is Perth's vegan living room: part cafe, part corner store, all plant-based. The Wembley spot does a proper brunch trade with v-egg benedict, breakfast bagels and mochi waffles, and the Sunday buffet is the stuff of local legend, fourteen dishes deep with rendang, laksa noodles, satay sticks and cauliflower wings.

Stay for the food, then browse the vegan mart on the way out: handmade Aussie vegan pies, dairy-free cheddar, chocolates and ice cream to stock the fridge. It is the rare restaurant that feeds you twice, once at the table and once at home.
