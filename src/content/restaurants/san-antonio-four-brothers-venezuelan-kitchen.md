Four Brothers is River North's Venezuelan kitchen, cooking grandmother's recipes into stuffed arepas and bowls, including the vegan Vegana arepa and the Pabellón bowl. The food is naturally gluten-free, the portions are generous, and reviewers call it truly fantastic.

It is a meat-serving restaurant with a real vegan arepa, so it lands as vegan-friendly, but the Vegana arepa is a proper dish rather than an afterthought. Order it, add the Pabellón bowl, and enjoy the most transportive meal on the list.
