Yellow is the answer to the question nobody could ask until recently: what would Australia's best vegetable cooking taste like in a fine-dining room? Created by the team behind Bentley, this Potts Point institution went fully plant-based for dinner and never looked back, collecting two Good Food Guide hats along the way. Under head chef Sander Nooij, formerly of Quay and London's Michelin-starred Pollen Street Social, the rotating menu turns NSW small-farm produce into dishes that diners describe as genuinely life-changing.

Book it for a special occasion and commit to the six-course tasting: it runs $125 a head, with a three-course lunch at $75 for the lighter option, and the natural wine list is as thoughtfully built as everything else here. Yellow is proof that vegan fine dining in Australia isn't a trend, it's the standard.
