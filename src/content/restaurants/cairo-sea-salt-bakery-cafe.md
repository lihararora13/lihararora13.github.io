Sea Salt is Maadi's gluten-free bakery, and its vegan cakes and breads are clearly marked, which is half the battle in a city where dairy creeps into pastries. It is a cafe as well as a bakery, so it works for a coffee and cake stop or a takeaway loaf.

Order a vegan cake, take a dairy-free loaf home, and ask what is fresh that day. It is the sweet stop on this list.
