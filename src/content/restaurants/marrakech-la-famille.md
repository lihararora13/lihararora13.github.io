La Famille is one of the only fully vegetarian restaurants in Marrakech, and a long-time favourite with travellers. The garden setting is the draw: a green, calm courtyard for lunch away from the souk crowds, with vegetarian cooking that vegans can navigate easily.

Order a tagine and a garden salad, and treat it as the peaceful lunch in the middle of a busy medina day.
