Vevi is the Vietnamese room Vienna's vegans keep returning to, and the HappyCow community agrees: it carries a rare 5.0 rating across hundreds of reviews. The menu rotates but the classics stay, pho with soy fish balls or mushrooms, fresh summer rolls, bun bowls, crispy banh mi and house creations, all made fresh with regional, fully plant-based ingredients.

The Stollgasse original in the 7th district is bright and modern with an open kitchen and outdoor seating, and there are sister branches in the 8th and 20th districts if you are wandering. Come hungry, order the pho, and add a round of summer rolls for the table.
