Umeke Market is the downtown lunch gem most visitors never find: a Surfrider Foundation-certified Ocean Friendly Restaurant serving fresh, organic vegan plates with a focus on sustainability. The tofu plates are the draw, the wraps are the quick move, and the daily special is the reason to ask what is new.

It is a lunch spot, so come midday. Order a tofu plate, enjoy the downtown buzz, and feel good about the ocean-friendly bit too.
