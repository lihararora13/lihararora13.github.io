Idem Con Patate is an all-vegan bistrot in the centre, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, that does comfort food properly: carbonara, lasagne with pumpkin, mushrooms and plant sausage, Beyond Meat meatballs in sauce with potatoes, a Redefine Meat tagliata and a veg burger, plus crepes for dessert.

It is open late, with brunch-style dishes at weekends, so it works as a dinner after a day in the centre. Order the carbonara and a seitan skewer to share.
