Kayak Kafe is a locally owned, healthy cafe with two Savannah locations, and the Broughton Street room in the Historic District is the one to visit: readers' pick for vegan food in 2026, with vegan tater tot nachos, tiger tofu tacos, and a vegan shawarma sandwich on a menu that is consistently rated the best in town for fresh salads and gluten-free options.

Order the tater tot nachos with guac, then the tiger tofu tacos. It is the best lunch on this list.
