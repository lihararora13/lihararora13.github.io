Meals From the Heart Cafe sits inside the historic French Market and answers the most important question in New Orleans vegan dining: where do you get vegan beignets? Right here. The Black-owned cafe runs on a heart-healthy philosophy (low sodium, low sugar, no trans fats) with a clearly marked vegan menu that includes gumbo, crab cake tacos, and blueberry pancakes.

It is technically a vegan-friendly spot rather than an all-vegan one, but the vegan dishes are the stars, and Yelp once ranked it the city's top heart-healthy restaurant. Come for breakfast, sit at the open-air counter, and eat beignets in the French Market. That is a sentence that sells itself.
