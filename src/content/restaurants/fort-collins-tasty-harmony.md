Tasty Harmony is Fort Collins' only fully vegan restaurant: a cafe on West Oak Street in Old Town, reported fully vegan in April 2025, serving a tempeh Reuben, a Nashville Chxn sandwich, a fried oyster mushroom po' boy and buffalo mac 'n cheese. It is the anchor of this guide and the pick that local vegan guides lead with, with a menu that runs from sandwiches and bowls to desserts. It is open Monday to Saturday 11am to 8pm, closed Sundays.

Order a tempeh Reuben and the buffalo mac 'n cheese. It is the one place in the city where everything on the menu is vegan, so it is the safe first stop.
