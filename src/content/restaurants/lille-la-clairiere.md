La Clairière is a bright, vegan-friendly bistro on Boulevard de la Liberté, serving a burger végétal, a veggie bowl, pad thaï and houmous. It is the easy central lunch on this page, a short walk from the Grand-Place.

One honest note: one guide calls it 100% vegan and organic, while the vegan-map listing files it under vegan options, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly. The vegan dishes are clearly marked; check the menu on the day.
