Crepe Pancake Hut is the breakfast favourite of McLeod Ganj, with a 4.6 Tripadvisor rating, the highest in town, and strong vegan and gluten-free options. The vegan banana bread at 10 AM is the order the guides keep naming.

Come early, order the vegan crepe or the vegan pancakes, and take your time. It is one of the two strongest breakfast spots in town, and the vegan bench is the reason it makes this list.
