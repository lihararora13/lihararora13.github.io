Big Bad Bagels is an American-style bagel shop in the centre of Riga, and it is the quick-breakfast pick on this list. Fresh bagels, spreads and fillings, with vegan builds that hold their own against any bagel shop anywhere, and a central location that makes it easy to fold into a day of sightseeing.

It is not a dedicated vegan restaurant, so ask for the vegan build when you order. Order a bagel with hummus, add a coffee, and keep walking.
