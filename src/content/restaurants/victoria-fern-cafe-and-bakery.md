Fern Cafe and Bakery is Victoria's all-vegan cafe in Fernwood: a bright room on North Park Street serving breakfast and lunch, a Western Scrambler, a Big Bad Breakfast Sandwich, a vegan burger and a bakery case of house-baked goods. It is the breakfast pick in this guide, open Monday to Friday 8am to 3pm and Saturday to Sunday 9am to 3pm, and it is the place to go for a proper vegan breakfast in the city.

Order the Western Scrambler and a baked good. The bakery case is the second reason to go: the goods are made in-house.
