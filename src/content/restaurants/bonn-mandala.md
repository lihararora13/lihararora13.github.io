Mandala is the table Bonn's vegan scene is built around: a fully vegan restaurant doing Japanese-Vietnamese fusion a few minutes from the Hauptbahnhof, open Tuesday to Sunday from late morning until eleven at night. The menu runs from bao buns and vegan salmon nigiri to Bún bò Huế, coconut soup and vegan chicken wings, with the kind of careful, colourful plating that makes it as popular with omnivores as with vegans.

It is the easiest first stop for anyone arriving in Bonn, and the room stays busy, so come early or book ahead for dinner. Order the nigiri if it is your first visit, share a couple of small plates, and save room for the coconut soup. This is the pick that shows how far Bonn's plant-based cooking has come.
