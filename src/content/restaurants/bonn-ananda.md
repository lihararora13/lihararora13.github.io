Ananda sits in Bonn's pedestrian zone on Acherstraße, a vegetarian restaurant with a long vegan-friendly streak: salads, Asian dishes, soups, juices and breakfast foods, with many vegan options across the menu. The dishes to ask for are the pumpkin soup with coconut and ginger, the vegetable tagine with salted lemon, couscous and hummus, and the oven-baked aubergine with sweet potato and turmeric sauce.

It is not a fully vegan kitchen, so ask the staff about specific dishes before ordering, as HappyCow's own listing advises. Come for a light lunch between the shops, take a table outside when the weather allows, and treat it as Bonn's dependable everyday vegetarian stop.
