Tianchu Miaoxiang was founded by a chef who became a Buddhist monk, and the cooking carries that seriousness: no egg, no alcohol, no alliums, just careful vegetarian food at honest prices. The Bib Gourmand is well earned.

It is the best-value pick in Beijing, and the Chaowai branch is the easiest to reach. Order a spread of classics and share them family style.
