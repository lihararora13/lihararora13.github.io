Cake or Death is Exeter's fully vegan bakery, a small shop on Bartholomew Street West where everything is plant-based. The brownies and celebration cakes have a devoted following.

Buy brownies and a traybake to take away. Whole cakes are worth ordering ahead, especially for weekends.
