Why we love it — Vegan dim sum that takes the cart seriously. Jade Rabbit is
worker-owned and queer-run, and chef Cyrus Ichiza cooks with the kind of
confidence that lands a spot on best-new-restaurants lists. The chili oil
wontons are the thing people cross town for, and the cha siu bao are
pillow-fights you get to eat.

Note: the restaurant recently relocated to Hawthorne Blvd (reopened August
2026), so update your mental map and your bookmarks. Worth the trip wherever
it lands.
