GustaV is a charming vegan cafe on Wolf-Dietrich-Strasse, run by owner Denise, serving sandwiches, salads, soups and homemade cakes, plus veganised traditional Austrian dishes, everything predominantly organic. It is the breakfast pick on this list, but it is closed most days of the week, so check its hours.

Order a vegan sandwich, a soup and a homemade cake, and check the opening days first, since it is closed most days of the week.
