Living Food and Juices is a 4.5-rated vegan kitchen on South Avenue at Constant Spring Road, making plant-based cuisine both raw and cooked, with full-course meals and raw cakes alongside organic cold-pressed juices, smoothies and wellness shots. It also offers detox packages, workshops and food preparation classes.

Order a full-course meal and a raw cake, with a cold-pressed juice. It is open Monday to Saturday, 7 am to 8 pm, and Sundays from 11 am to 5 pm.
