Planted Foods Cafe is Midtown's sunny little vegan cafe: harvest sammies, sweet potato sliders, chickpea tuna wraps and adaptogenic brews, all of it plant-based and all of it made to be eaten outside in the California sun. The sweet potato sliders are the cult favourite, and the chickpea tuna wrap is the sleeper.

It is the lunch spot for the health-curious and the hungry alike, open weekday daytime hours, so plan it as a lunch rather than a dinner. Order the harvest sammie and a brew, and take the long way home.
