The Hummus Connection is a fully vegan Lebanese restaurant on Äußerer Laufer Platz in the Altstadt, and the hummus is the whole point. The basilikum-hummus is the signature, with falafel and an oyster mushroom shawarma alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it fresh.

It is a short walk from the Hauptmarkt, and the hummus plate is the order. Come hungry, and order the shawarma too.
