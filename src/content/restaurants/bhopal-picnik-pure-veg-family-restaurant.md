Picnik is the family-restaurant end of Bhopal's veg scene: open from 7 AM to 11 PM, well rated by locals, and built for unhurried family meals rather than café posing. The menu covers North Indian mains and café dishes, and the kitchen handles no-ghee, no-butter requests as a matter of course.

It is a useful all-day anchor for the list. Come for a proper lunch thali without ghee, or a low-key evening meal when the fancier cafés feel like too much effort.
