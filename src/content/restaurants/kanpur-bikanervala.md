Bikanervala in Kakadeo is the chaat stop: chhole bhature that could start a fan club, a tandoori platter, puri sabzi, creamy dal makhni with rice, and a pav bhaji that never misses. It is open 8 AM to midnight, which makes it the late-night veg option too.

Order the chhole bhature without dahi and the pav bhaji without butter. It is fast, filling, and the most fun meal on this list.
