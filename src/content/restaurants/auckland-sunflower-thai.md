Sunflower has been feeding central Auckland for years from its High Street home, and it remains one of the happiest bargains in the city: a fully vegan Thai menu built on mock meats and vegetables, cooked without garlic, onion or MSG, and priced for a proper weekday feast.

The salt and pepper tofu is the table's first argument, followed closely by prawn fried rice made entirely from plants and a green curry with real warmth. It is closed on Sundays, so plan your Sunflower run for lunch or dinner any other day.
