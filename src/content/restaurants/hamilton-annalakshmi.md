Annalakshmi is Hamilton's South Indian vegetarian restaurant on Main Street West, with many clearly labelled vegan options and more that can be made vegan by asking to omit ghee and yogurt. The dosas are the draw: plain, masala, paper and podi, with idli, vada and sambar alongside.

It is open daily for lunch and dinner, with a break in the afternoon on weekdays. Ask for the vegan dishes by name, since the menu labels them, and the kitchen is used to the request.
