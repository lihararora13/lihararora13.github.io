Double Dutch serves proper Dutch pancakes on Church Street, and the vegan version of the batter is available across the menu, sweet and savoury. It is a small, cheerful room that suits a mid-morning stop.

Order a savoury pancake first, then a sweet one to share. Tell the staff you want the vegan batter, as the standard batter contains egg and milk.
