Pizzi & Dixie proves a pizzeria needs no cheese from a cow to be brilliant: a fully plant-based Italian kitchen in Malasana since 2017, with creative pizzas topped with pistachio cream, veggie mortadella and vegan gorgonzola, plus house-made pastas that regulars cross town for.

Start with artichoke flowers, commit to the truffle pizza, and finish with tiramisu or the sweet Nutella-style dessert pizza. Lunch and dinner sittings fill up, so timing your arrival to opening is a smart move.
