Sei Vegan is a vegan bakery on Daejong-ro in Jung-gu, listed as a vegan bakery on HappyCow, selling plant-based breads, cakes and pastries. It is a takeaway-first kitchen, good for stocking up before a day out, and it is one of the few places in the old centre where everything on the shelf is vegan.

Buy bread and a cake for later, and ask about the day's pastries. It is inexpensive and central, and it pairs well with a coffee from a nearby cafe that stocks oat milk.
