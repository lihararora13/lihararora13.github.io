6 Ballygunge Place made the list for a reason. A Bengali vegetarian classic: shukto, mochar ghonto and a proper veg thali that is vegan-friendly without the ghee. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Salt Lake, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Veg thali, Shukto, Mochar ghonto, vegan-style if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
