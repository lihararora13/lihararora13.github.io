The GoVeg Project is the one fully vegan kitchen on this list, and in Bodh Gaya it is a quiet gem: a proper vegan thali near Kalachakra Ground for 100 to 600 rupees a person, the cheapest sit-down meal in the town's vegetarian guides. It exists for the pilgrims and long-stay travellers who want a thali with no dairy in it at all.

Order the vegan thali and ask what is seasonal. It is the only pick in the East region where you do not need to ask the kitchen to hold anything, and that alone earns it its place.
