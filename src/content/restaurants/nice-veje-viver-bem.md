Veje Viver Bem is a small, convivial Brazilian restaurant on Rue Miron, run by a chef who has been vegetarian all his life and serves a good selection of vegan Brazilian dishes, with a few European-inspired dishes alongside. The portions are generous, no reservations are possible but the staff do their best to accommodate everybody, and the homemade chilli sauces range from mildly spicy to burning hot.

It is open Tuesday to Saturday for lunch and dinner, closed Sundays and Mondays. Order a Brazilian plate and try the chilli sauces.
