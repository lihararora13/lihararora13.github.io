Copper Branch is a plant-based cafe on Steenweg in the centre, open every day from 10:00 to 21:00, Sundays from noon. The menu is the same colourful comfort food as the Rotterdam branch: rainbow burgers, rendang loaded fries, loaded nachos, an Impossible burger and a doner pita.

It is the fast, central pick on this list. Order the rainbow burger and the rendang loaded fries.
