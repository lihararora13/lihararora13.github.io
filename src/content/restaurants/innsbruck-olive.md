Olive is a fully vegan restaurant south of the old town, describing itself as 100% plant-based and rated 4.8 from 140 reviews. The menu runs on kimchi bowls, cannelloni with fresh cheese and spinach, pumpkin-quinoa dumplings with nut cream cheese, creamy parsley root soup and spelt vegetable lasagne.

Order the kimchi bowl, the cannelloni and the pumpkin-quinoa dumplings, and come for lunch or dinner, Wednesday to Saturday and Sunday lunch.
