Detroit Vegan Soul is the city's vegan soul food institution: fully plant-based, woman-owned, and doing a cornmeal-battered catfish tofu sandwich, creamy mac and cheese, soul food platters and Southern sweet tea that reviewers call surprisingly good. The West location on Grand River is the one to hit, with takeout via a self-service kiosk.

It is the pick for the comfort-food purist, and the one to take someone who thinks vegan soul food is a contradiction. Order the catfish sandwich, add mac and cheese, and bring patience, the food is worth the wait.
