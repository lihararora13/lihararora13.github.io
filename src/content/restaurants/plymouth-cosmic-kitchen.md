Cosmic Kitchen is a fully vegan bistro and bar in a converted chapel on Palace Street, run by two sisters, and it is the best vegan table in the south-west by most accounts. The food is generous and the room turns into a dance floor late on Fridays and Saturdays.

Order the moussaka, the beetroot falafel wrap and a vegan scotch egg. Book ahead, as it is busy most nights. Website: cosmickitchen.co.uk.
