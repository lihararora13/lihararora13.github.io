Restaurant Hiller is a piece of German food history: founded in 1955 as Germany's oldest vegetarian restaurant, and fully vegan since May 2012. The room is grand and old-fashioned, the cooking is seasonal German fine dining, and the Saturday vegan buffet is a local institution. This is not a café that happens to serve vegan food; it is a proper restaurant that has been cooking without meat for seventy years.

It sits on Blumenstraße in the Mitte, near the Opernplatz. Come on a Saturday for the buffet if you can, or book a table for the seasonal menu, and order dessert. This is the table for a special evening, and the one to take someone who thinks vegan food is a new idea.
