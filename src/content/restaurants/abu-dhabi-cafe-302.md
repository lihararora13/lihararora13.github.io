Cafe 302 is one of the longest-standing go-to spots for vegan food in Abu Dhabi, inside the Al Maha Arjaan by Rotana on Hamdan Street. Its plant-based menu covers hearty vegan breakfasts, burgers, bowls, mains and desserts, including tofu benedict, a white bean burger and vegan superfood bowls, and it has run entirely plant-based buffets on some nights.

Order the tofu benedict for breakfast or the white bean burger for lunch. It is the reliable, central, all-day vegan-friendly pick.
