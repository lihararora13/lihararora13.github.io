Mike's is the no-fuss, all-vegan grill Charlotte reaches for when the craving is classic: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches and fries, done properly and served late. The Hola Amigo burger and the Carolina burger are the headliners, Fiesta fries are the side that comes with everything, and the mango iced tea is the drink regulars pair with both. It is casual, quick and open until midnight on weekends.

It is also one of the most consistently top-rated vegan spots in the city, with a 4.5 HappyCow rating and a ThreeBestRated nod. Come for a burger, stay for the fries, and do not overthink it.
