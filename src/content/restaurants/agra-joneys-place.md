Joney's Place is the old reliable of Taj Ganj: a traveller cafe about 150 metres from the South Gate, open from 6 AM to 10:30 PM, doing breakfast, thalis, Continental plates and masala chai for the early Taj crowd.

Come here before the Taj opens, order the veg thali without ghee, and you are set for the morning. It is the most practical table on this list, and the one every guidebook mentions for a reason.
