Why we love it — Planta is the restaurant that turned vegan dining into a scene, and Planta Queen is where it all started. The Asian-leaning menu is stacked with crowd-pleasers: crispy Bang Bang Broccoli glazed in chili sweetness, a kelp-noodle Caesar with smoky mushroom bacon, and weekend dim sum that will convert the skeptic at your table. The room is beautiful enough that Curiocity put it on a most-beautiful-restaurants list.

It is popular, polished, and proudly upscale without being stuffy. Come for the Instagram-worthy interior, stay because the food actually backs it up.
