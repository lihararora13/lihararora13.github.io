Bistro Maha sits right on Preseren Square and takes whole food seriously: fresh, seasonal ingredients, no industrial shortcuts, dishes built to actually fuel you. The menu is short, changes with the market, and leans macrobiotic without ever tasting like a lecture.

It only opens for lunch on weekdays, so plan around it. Order the daily plate, ask what is seasonal, and keep it in mind as the healthiest lunch in the old town.
