Aunty Mena is the budget hero of the guide, a small vegetarian cafe on Cuba Street that has been feeding Wellington's plant-based crowd rich, hearty Southeast Asian food for years. The laksa is the dish the city talks about, and the rest of the menu follows the same generous logic: nasi lemak, roti, dumplings and deep-fried entrees that are always a crowd-pleaser. At 4.8 stars it is one of the best-reviewed vegan-friendly rooms in the capital, and one of the cheapest.

Come hungry, order the laksa first, and treat the deep-fried starters as mandatory rather than optional. It is open daily from noon to 9pm, which makes it the easiest late lunch or early dinner on the list.
