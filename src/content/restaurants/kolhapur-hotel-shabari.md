Hotel Shabari is the Rajarampuri pure-veg restaurant opposite Naivedya Pure Veg Thali, a straightforward local favourite for North Indian vegetarian meals. It rounds out this guide the way Kolhapur itself does: simply, honestly and without fuss.

Order the dal tadka without ghee, a mixed veg sabzi and hot rotis, and you have a proper, cheap, satisfying dinner. It is the quiet end to this guide, and sometimes the quiet ones are the best.
