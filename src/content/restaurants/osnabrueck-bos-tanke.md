Bos Tanke is a coffee shop in a converted old petrol station on Karlstrasse, open Wednesday to Sunday, serving filter coffee, cold brew tonic, homemade lemonade and carrot cake, with a farm-shop corner selling seasonal produce. It is the character pick on this list.

Order a cold brew tonic, a filter coffee and the carrot cake, and ask about plant milk and the vegan bakes, since the menu also runs dairy cakes.
