Swaagata South 19 is the newest spot in town and already turning heads: ragi idli, neer dosa, a ghee roast spring masala dosa, butter mushroom uttapam, South Indian kadhi and tomato rice, a menu that means serious business. It is open noon to 11 PM, on the first floor near South X Mall.

Order the ragi idli and the neer dosa, and ask for the masala dosa without ghee. It is the most exciting South Indian table in Kanpur right now.
