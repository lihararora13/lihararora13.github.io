MARE Cafe and Bar is a cafe bar on the Markt, open Tuesday to Saturday, serving sandwiches, hummus, coffee and cocktails in a sunlit room on the market square. The veggie sandwich runs on hummus, sauteed carrots, paprika mix and red onions, and every sandwich can be paired with a chip basket.

Order the veggie sandwich with hummus and a chip basket, and ask the staff to confirm it is vegan, since the menu also runs prosciutto and cheese boards.
