Sharma Bhojnalaya is a Police Bazar bhojnalaya in the old style: lunch from 11 to 3:30, dinner from 6 to 9, and home-style North Indian veg food in between. It is a small, local room, and it is the kind of place Shillong's vegetarian residents have eaten at for years.

Order the veg thali and ask for no ghee and no paneer. Mind the split hours, and come for lunch if you want the fullest menu.
