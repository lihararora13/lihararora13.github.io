The Tune Up Cafe is Santa Fe's neighbourhood all-day cafe, a funky, low-key room a short walk from the Railyard where breakfast runs all day and the vegan options are a real part of the menu rather than an afterthought. The tofu scramble and the vegan breakfast burrito are the regulars' orders, and green chile shows up where you would want it to.

It is the easiest, least fussy breakfast on this list. Walk in, order at the counter, and take your time.
