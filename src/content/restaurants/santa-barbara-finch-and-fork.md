Finch & Fork is Santa Barbara's fine-dining vegan-friendly room: the restaurant inside the Kimpton Canary Hotel on Carrillo Street, with a clearly labelled vegan section that reads like a tasting menu in miniature, mushroom dandan noodles, kung pao cauliflower, house focaccia and gnocchi with butternut romesco and pistachio pesto. It is the priciest table in this guide and the one to book for a special evening.

Hours are 7am to 2pm and 4pm to 9pm daily, with reservations recommended. Ask for the vegan dishes by name: they are marked on the menu, and the kitchen is used to cooking them.
