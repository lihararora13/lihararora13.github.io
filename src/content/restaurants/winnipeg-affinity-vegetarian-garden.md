Affinity has been Winnipeg's vegan anchor since 2001, a Taiwanese-Chinese restaurant downtown with a pay-by-weight lunch buffet on weekdays and a long a la carte menu in the evening. The cooking uses soy-protein faux meats and house-made gluten, and the buffet is the move: pile a plate with the crispy oyster, kung pao gluten and black mushroom, and pay for what it weighs.

Lunch buffet runs weekdays from 11:30am to 2pm, with dinner service Monday to Saturday from 4:30pm. It is closed Sundays, and the dining room is simple and unpretentious, so come for the food, not the decor.
