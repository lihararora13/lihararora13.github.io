Seven Mile Cafe is a bright all-day cafe on the North Freeway, and it is the family-friendly vegan-friendly bet in Fort Worth: vegan breakfast dishes, avocado toast, and salads on a menu that works for everyone at the table. It is the most northern stop on this list, so pair it with a north-side day rather than a Magnolia crawl.

Order the avocado toast and a smoothie, and settle in.
