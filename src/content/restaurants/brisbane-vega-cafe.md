Vega Cafe is the quiet overachiever of Brisbane's vegan scene. A short hop from the CBD on Boundary Street in Spring Hill, reachable on the free Spring Hill Loop bus, it cooks Thai-flavoured plant-based food with serious care: the vegan omelette with marinated plant-based meat, vegan parmesan, tempeh and roasted vegetables is the dish regulars come back for, and the menu keeps changing, so there is always something new to try.

Come for brunch or dinner, bring the dog, and order whatever the kitchen is excited about that week. Consistently praised for quality and creativity, Vega is the kind of neighbourhood cafe that makes eating vegan feel effortless.
