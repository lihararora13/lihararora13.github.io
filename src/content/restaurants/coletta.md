Coletta answers a question nobody asked with total confidence: what if
Italian fine dining, but every single thing is vegan? Wood-fired pizzas,
handmade pasta, a proper craft cocktail list, and desserts that have no
business being this good. Eater's editors once wrote about its butternut
squash ravioli with real longing.

It is the date-night pick of this list and the one to book when the
occasion calls for it. Mixed-diet tables are Coletta's specialty: the
skeptics order the "steak filet" and go quiet.
