Greens and Beans is a healthy cafe on Bahureksa Street in the Citarum area, and it is one of Bandung's vegan-friendly picks for a lighter meal: grain bowls, salads, smoothies and fresh juices in a bright, casual room.

Order a grain bowl and a fresh juice. It is a vegan-friendly cafe rather than an all-vegan restaurant, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it is the best light-lunch pick on this list after the heavier Indonesian cooking elsewhere in the city.
