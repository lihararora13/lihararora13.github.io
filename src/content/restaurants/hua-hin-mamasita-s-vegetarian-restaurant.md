Mamasita's Vegetarian Restaurant is a long-running vegan and vegetarian kitchen in central Hua Hin with strong Thai and Western menus, and dishes running about 180 to 300 baht. It is closed on Sundays, and it is one of the most established sit-down vegan-friendly kitchens in town.

Order the Thai curries and a Western dish, and ask which plates are vegan. Its pin is approximate to central Hua Hin, so check the address before you walk over.
