Zen Chay is the fully vegan Asian restaurant Cologne's vegans book for a proper dinner: sharing plates, sushi, bao buns, crispy tofu, rice noodles with vegetables and rolls, a sushi bowl and red coconut curry, all of it plant-based, with vegan cocktails to match. It sits on Apostelnstraße near Neumarkt, and it is open every day until ten.

Order the bao buns, add the crispy tofu, and share a ramen. This is the table for a group night out, and the one that converts the sushi sceptics.
