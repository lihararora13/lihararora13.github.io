Why we love it — Rare Earth started as an all-vegan store in 2016 and added
a cafe in 2019, run by owner Shami Sethi, whose dog greets regulars at the
door. The menu is entirely plant-based: pizzas on khapli wheat crust with
cashew cheese, Indian comfort food, and desserts people cross the city for.

Get the vegan butter chicken at least once, then stock up in the store on
the way out. Saturday nights add karaoke and live music.
