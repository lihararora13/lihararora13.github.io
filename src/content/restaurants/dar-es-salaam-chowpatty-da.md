Chowpatty Da is a lacto-vegetarian restaurant on Temple Street in Kisutu, established in 2011, serving everything from a quick pani puri to a light plate of idli or a full paneer butter masala. It has vegan options and others that can be made vegan on request.

Order the pani puri and an idli, and ask which dishes can be made vegan. It is the heart of the Kisutu cluster, and the cheapest proper meal on this list.
