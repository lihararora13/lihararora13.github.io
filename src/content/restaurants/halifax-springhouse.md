Springhouse is the North End's licensed plant-based restaurant, a few doors from Real Fake Meats on Gottingen Street, serving classic dishes with a plant-based spin. Breakfast burritos are the morning draw, the vegan club with onion rings is the lunch order, and the fried vegan chicken wings are the dish that gets talked about on vegan food tours of the city.

It is primarily dine-in with takeaway available, open Wednesday to Friday for dinner, Saturday for lunch and dinner, and Sunday for brunch. Previously called Springhouse Market.
