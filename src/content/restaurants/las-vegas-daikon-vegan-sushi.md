Daikon proves sushi night does not need fish. This all-vegan spot builds its rolls and nigiri from vegetables, tofu, mushrooms, fruit, and carefully seasoned plant-based ingredients, and it keeps earning perfect HappyCow scores from the community. A second location on Windmill Lane means the northwest Lake Mead original is not your only option.

The menu runs well past sushi into ramen, rice dishes, appetizers, and Japanese-inspired entrees, plus fresh-squeezed juice and smoothies. It is one of the highest-rated vegan restaurants in the whole city, and a genuinely fun night out for skeptics too.
