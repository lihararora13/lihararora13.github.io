Banu Vegan describes its menu as conscious comfort food, and it delivers: the OG Chikin sandwich and the Philly Fake Steak are the dishes regulars name first, the Mackin' cheeze is the side, and the jerk burrito is the sleeper. Everything is vegan, the prices are low, and the room is fast-casual.

It is open Tuesday to Saturday. Order the OG Chikin sandwich and ask for all the salsa.
