Juicy Vegan started as a counter inside a fitness studio and moved into its own storefront in the Waterloo Arts District, and it is now Collinwood's vegan anchor: Philly cheesesteaks, plant-based burgers, smoothies and juices, all of it plant-based. The Philly is the signature, and the burger is the sleeper.

It is the north-side stop on the list, in the arts district, which makes it the perfect pre-gallery meal. Order the Philly, grab a smoothie, and wander Waterloo Road after.
