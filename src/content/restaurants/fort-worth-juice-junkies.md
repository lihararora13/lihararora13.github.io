Juice Junkies is Fort Worth's first cold-pressed juice bar, a vegan juice bar and cafe in the Cultural District built on organic juices, superfood smoothies, and gourmet vegan bites. The loaded nachos are the dish reviewers call the best plant-based nachos in town, and the Better Than Tuna wrap and the Green River juice round out a menu that is as much lunch as it is juice.

It is open daily with outdoor seating, takeout, and delivery. Order the nachos and a green juice.
