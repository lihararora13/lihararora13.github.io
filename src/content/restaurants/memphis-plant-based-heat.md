Plant Based Heat is Memphis's vegan comfort-food star, a Black-owned restaurant near the University of Memphis that VegNews named among the country's best vegan soul food spots. The oyster mushroom burger is the dish reviewers call the absolute best, the BBQ jackfruit nachos are the shareable one, and the vings, with their house hot sauce, are the ones to order with friends.

It is open six days a week for lunch and dinner, with takeout and delivery. Order the burger, add the nachos, and come hungry.
