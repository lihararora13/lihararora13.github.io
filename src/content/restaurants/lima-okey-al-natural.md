OKEY Al Natural is the budget hero of this guide: a simple all-vegan restaurant in the Breña neighborhood that has been quietly feeding Lima since 2013. The formula is unpretentious and generous, a meal of the day plus an a la carte menu of sandwiches, veggie burgers, empanadas and rice plates, with seitan, tofu, anticuchos and chorizo all made in house, and a chocolate cake to finish.

Come hungry and bring cash, because this is the rare vegan spot where a full meal costs less than a coffee in Miraflores. The meal of the day is the order to make, the empanadas are the snack to add, and the whole place runs on the kind of neighborhood warmth that keeps regulars coming back for over a decade.
