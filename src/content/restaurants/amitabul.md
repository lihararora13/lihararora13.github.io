Amitabul is a quiet, incense-scented room on the Far Northwest Side
serving Buddhist-inspired vegan Korean food, and it has been doing it
for twenty years. Chef Bill Choi cooks the kind of healing food that
makes you feel like you have done something good for yourself: the
famous Dr. K's Cure All Noodle Soup, plump mandoo dumplings, kimchi
pancakes with a kick, and the beloved Buddha bop.

This is the soulful opposite of a scene. No oil-heavy indulgence,
no gimmicks, just generous, restorative plates from a kitchen that
has earned every bit of its loyal following.
