The Giriama Village Lounge is a vegan restaurant, cafe, library and culture and arts centre in a village setting near the Giriama Catholic Church, serving high-class vegan Swahili and Kenyan cuisine: potato curry, spaghetti stir fry and sauteed lentils with warm rice and chapatis. It has been open for three years and also provides accommodation.

Order the potato curry with rice and chapatis. It is a community space as much as a restaurant, so come with time to eat, learn and connect, and take a taxi, it is outside the city.
