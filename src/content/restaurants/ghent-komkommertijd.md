Komkommertijd serves entirely plant-based tasting menus in a converted warehouse near the Design Museum, and it is the most inventive table on this list. Expect a cauliflower steak with more technique than many meat kitchens apply to a filet, plus seasonal courses that change with the market, in a four-course format.

It is a dinner-only spot with limited days, so book ahead. Note the address: some guides give Hoogstraat 42, but the Bijlokekaai address on this page is the one to use.
