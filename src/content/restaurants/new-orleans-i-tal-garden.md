I-tal Garden sits under the Claiborne Expressway in Treme serving some of the most joyful plates in New Orleans. The entirely plant-based menu channels Ital principles through a NOLA lens, which means you get coconut curry black-eyed peas next to Creole greens, cauli-wings next to cornbread, and it all makes perfect sense. The crabless crab cakes and jackfruit ribs are the famous flexes, but the breakfast plate with chickpea scramble and seitan sausage is the sleeper hit.

Everything here tastes like it was cooked by someone who loves feeding people. The colors are bright, the portions are kind, and the room has that neighborhood warmth that turns first-timers into regulars. This is a local favorite for a reason: it eats like comfort food and feels like a community project.
