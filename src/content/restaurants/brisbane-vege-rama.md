Vege Rama is where Brisbane's vegan scene eats lunch. Since 2009 this Adelaide Street counter has been the affordable, no-fuss answer to "where can we eat, all of us": browse the rotating selection of rich chickpea masala, hearty lasagna, salads and crowd-pleasing burgers, pay at the counter, and find a seat among the office workers and students who keep it packed on weekdays.

It is only open on weekdays, so plan around that, and do not skip dessert: the Ferrero cups and Oreo cakes are the reason regulars linger. Cheap, cheerful and fully vegan, it is the city's most reliable plant-based lunch.
