Bagseu is a vegetarian and vegan-friendly Korean restaurant in Daesin-dong, Jung-gu, with plant-based dishes among a wider Korean menu. It is a neighbourhood kitchen rather than a destination, and the vegan dishes are best found by asking the staff what can be made without animal products.

Order the vegetable and tofu plates and check about fish sauce and egg. Its pin is approximate to Daesin-dong, so allow a little time to find it.
