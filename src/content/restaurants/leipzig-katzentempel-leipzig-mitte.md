Katzentempel Leipzig Mitte is the city's fully vegan cat café, on Katharinenstraße in the Zentrum, and it is exactly what it sounds like: breakfast, a daily plate, açaí bowls and cakes, all plant-based, with cats in residence. It is the most charming room on this page.

Use this address: there is a second Leipzig branch in the Ost, on Nürnberger Straße. The breakfast is the order, and the açaí bowl runs it close.
