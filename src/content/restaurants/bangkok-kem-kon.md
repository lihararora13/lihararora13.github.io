Kem-Kon is the Khao San area spot reviewers keep going back to two days in a row. It sits just off the backpacker buzz on Tani Road, calm and contemporary, with a menu that happily sprawls from Thai stir-fries and noodle soups to pasta, waffles and cake.

Order the khao soi first, it is the bowl people daydream about, then graze through mushrooms, salads and something sweet. Everything is vegan, portions are generous, and the flavours are dialled all the way up. Old Town Bangkok, sorted.
