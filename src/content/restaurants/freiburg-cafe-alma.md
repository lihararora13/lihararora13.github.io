Café Alma opened in 2023 and has quickly become Freiburg's breakfast café, a cosy room near the Hauptbahnhof where almost everything is made in house, from pastries and bread to seasonal specials. It is a vegetarian café with a serious vegan range: vegan breakfasts, panini, several cakes and croissants, and good specialty coffee, served with outdoor seating in the warmer months. Regulars say to reserve at weekends, when the room fills early.

It is closed on Wednesdays and keeps morning-to-early-evening hours otherwise. Order the vegan breakfast, add a croissant, and treat it as the proper start to a day in the Black Forest city rather than a quick coffee.
