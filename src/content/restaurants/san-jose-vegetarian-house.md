Vegetarian House has been San Jose's vegan anchor since 1994, and it still eats like a place with something to prove. The menu roams from China to Vietnam to Thailand to India to the Middle East, all of it cooked from scratch with organic, non-GMO ingredients, and the dining room is big enough for a whole crew. Order the pad Thai, the pho, or the Thai green curry, and save room for the raw strawberry cream cake.

It is the rare old-guard vegan spot that never went stale: gluten-free, nut-free and raw options all over the menu, solar panels on the roof, and a team that has been doing this longer than most of the city's tech companies have existed. This is the first stop on any San Jose vegan crawl, and the one to take someone who thinks vegan food is a new invention.
