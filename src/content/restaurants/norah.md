Why we love it — Date night has a new address. Norah is an all-vegan
Pan-Asian restaurant and cocktail bar, BIPOC women-owned, and the kind of
place that makes you forget the menu is vegan because nobody mentioned it and
nobody needed to. The mushroom linguine in coconut-galangal sauce is lush in
a way pasta rarely is.

Go for dinner, stay for a cocktail, order the roasted cauliflower curry
somewhere in between. There is a second location on Alberta if the east side
is more your scene.
