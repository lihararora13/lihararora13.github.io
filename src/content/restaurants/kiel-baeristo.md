Bäristo Vegan Deli is a small, fully vegan deli on Schülperbaum, and it is the quirkiest stop on Kiel's list: tacos, quesadillas, bagel sandwiches and curry, cooked by a crew that also runs a food truck over in Hamburg. The food is quick and generous, the room is tiny, and the whole place runs on a lunch-only rhythm that makes it feel like a secret worth knowing.

It opens Tuesday to Thursday, noon to two, so plan around it. Get there early, order tacos and a bagel sandwich, and do not expect dinner. This is the table for a fast, excellent vegan lunch in Kiel, and the hours are the whole point of the warning.
