Die Vegane Fleischerei is the most Munich idea on this list: a vegan butcher shop. The Frauenstraße counter by the Viktualienmarkt sells fully vegan, GMO- and palm-oil-free sausages, spreads, roasts and cheeses to take home, and the little bistro seating serves a daily changing selection of soups, sandwiches and snacks to eat in. The vegan Leberkässemmel is the signature move, a Bavarian classic rebuilt without the meat.

Come for lunch, order the Leberkässemmel and the soup of the day, and stock up on sausages for later. Note that the second branch on Häberlstraße is permanently closed, so the Frauenstraße shop is the one to visit.
