La Lanterna is Verona's first vegan restaurant, 100% vegan and organic, in Piazzetta Portichetti near the Arena. The food is made in the kitchen with care and organic ingredients, and a first-hand visit in October 2026 found the vegan carbonara the standout, with appetisers, first courses, mains and desserts all plant-based.

Order the vegan carbonara first, then the appetisers and a dessert, and expect to pay for the organic ingredients: food and drinks for two ran to about 73 euros in October 2026. Skip the radicchio dishes if you dislike bitter flavours.
