Apple & Eve is a fully vegan fast-food counter in the Ostertor doing the whole comfort-food range: burgers, gyros, pizza and currywurst, all plant-based, all served daily until 21:30. It is the kind of place where the menu reads like a dare and the food quietly delivers, and it has become a fixture of the quarter for vegans and curious omnivores alike.

It sits on Wielandstraße, a short walk from the Steintor. Order a burger or a gyros plate, add a pizza to share if you are with friends, and enjoy a menu where every single item is fair game. This is the table for a hungry evening when you want everything.
