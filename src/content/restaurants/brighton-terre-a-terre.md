Terre a Terre on East Street in the Lanes is the benchmark for vegetarian fine dining in Brighton, and arguably in the south of England, and it has been open since 1993, long before plant-based eating was fashionable. The cooking is contemporary and multi-ethnic, with an a la carte menu, children's menu, afternoon teas, organic wine and sweets, and the vegan options are serious rather than token.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so check the menu when you book, but it is the special-occasion table on this list. Reserve ahead for weekends, order whatever is seasonal, and save room for dessert.
