Vega is Warsaw's no-fuss vegan cafeteria, a simple room with plenty of seating and a buffet of dishes with a strong Indian influence, plus cakes. It turned fully vegan in 2016 and has been a reliable, cheap lunch ever since.

It is the budget pick on this list. Fill a plate from the buffet, add a cake, and you will leave full for very little money.
