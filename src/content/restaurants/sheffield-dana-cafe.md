Dana Cafe has been pouring coffee and cooking vegetarian brunch in Crookes since 2017, and it runs its own vegan micro-bakery alongside. The room is warm and green, the vibe is relaxed, and the vegan breakfasts are the draw.

The vegan full English is the order, with scrambled tofu and tempeh bacon, and the vegan cream cheese bagel is the lighter option. It is a vegetarian cafe, so file it as vegan-friendly, but the vegan menu is extensive and clearly marked.
