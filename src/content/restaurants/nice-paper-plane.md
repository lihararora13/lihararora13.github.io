Paper Plane is a vegetarian restaurant on Rue Gubernatis, near the old town, serving breakfast, lunch and a Sunday brunch, with a menu that changes monthly and seasonal, organic produce. The dishes include a vegan Caesar-style salad, a falafel galette, a creamy polenta, a vegetable bourguignon and a vegan burger, with smoothies and good coffee.

Its own site describes the menus as essentially vegetarian, so it is listed as vegan-friendly, not fully vegan. Confirm which dishes are vegan on the day you visit, and come for an early lunch, as it closes at 15:00.
