Dahon Plant Kitchen is an all-vegan kitchen inside the COATI Uprise food hall on South Tejon Street, serving Asian-inspired bowls, tofu plates, and noodle dishes. The food-hall setting makes it a good group stop, since everyone can order from a different stall.

Order the vegan bowl of the day and the spring rolls. The pin for this entry is address-level, since the kitchen sits inside the food hall, so navigate to the Tejon Street address.
