Super ZaZa in Santa Croce is a comic shop with a bar attached, which sounds like a gimmick and works far better than it should. The sign and the locals say Zaza. Come for a drink and a snack and a browse, because the atmosphere is the point more than the food, and Santa Croce is the quietest of the six districts.

Order the vegan snacks and a drink, work through the shelves of fumetti, and treat it as a stop for an hour between other things rather than a destination. The walk over from San Polo in the hour before sunset is one of the nicer ones in the city.
