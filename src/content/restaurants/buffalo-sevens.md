Sevens is Buffalo's all-vegan bakery cafe: a cozy, pet-friendly room in the Old First Ward with free street parking, house-made cold foam, kombucha on tap and a pastry case that regulars plan around, lemon-poppyseed cruffins, cinnamon buns and caramel apple cruffins. The savory side is just as considered, with a vegan Reuben and a miso eggplant sandwich, and the iced pistachio milk matcha is the drink to order. It holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow.

Hours are Monday to Friday 7am to 4pm, Saturday 8am to 5pm and Sunday 8am to 4pm. It is laptop-friendly, with thoughtful outlet placement at the bar seating, and it is the best breakfast or working-lunch stop in this guide.
