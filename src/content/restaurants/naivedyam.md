Why we love it — Some restaurants earn their place through innovation;
Naivedyam earned it through 27 years of never missing. The dining room
feels like a temple, devotional music and all, and the welcome rasam
arrives before you have finished deciding what to order.

South Indian food is a vegan's cheat code: dosas, idlis, sambar, and
chutneys are plant-based by default, and the staff here know exactly how
to keep the thali dairy-free if you ask. Eight thousand ratings do not
lie. This is the meal that costs less than a movie ticket and feeds you
like family.
