Mission Burger Co. is what happens when a vegan burger joint
decides to have everything. Forty-plus plant-based options cover
the greatest hits: a double burger with special sauce, a New York
chopped cheese, a Reuben, and a mushroom brisket smoked for five
hours in the land of actual brisket. Bold.

Order the brisket plate at least once for the principle of it.
Then come back for the chopped cheese and the animal-style fries,
because forty options means forty reasons to return.
