Vida Atemporal is the calm, healthy vegan table in Centro, serving bowls, salads and seasonal plates that feel good to eat. It is the lighter lunch on this list, and a good counterpoint to Monterrey's pizza-heavy vegan scene.

Order a bowl and a dessert. It is a straightforward, nourishing meal, and the room is a pleasant place to slow down for an hour.
