Cinnaholic is Rochester's all-vegan bakery stop: a cinnamon roll shop on Monroe Avenue in Brighton where every roll is plant-based and built to order, with frosting and toppings layered over a classic roll. The Old Skool, a classic cinnamon roll with vanilla frosting, is the baseline; the Cookie Monster, with cream cheese frosting and cookie dough, and the caramel apple pie roll are the extravagant ones. Cookies and other baked goods fill out the case.

It is open Monday to Saturday 10am to 9pm and Sunday 10am to 7pm. A dessert stop rather than a meal, but an essential one: it is the sweet end of Rochester's all-vegan run, and the rolls travel well.
