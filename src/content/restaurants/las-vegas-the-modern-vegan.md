The Modern Vegan reads like a chicer retro diner gone plant-based: high-back vinyl booths, graphic black-and-white wallpaper, and a menu so expansive it borders on overwhelming. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner are all covered, with themed days like Taco Tuesday and a mostly-raw Monday in the mix.

Start the day with the Todd, a golden Belgian waffle buried under bananas, chocolate chips, peanut butter, and chocolate. End it with the lobster-stuffed portobello steak, a melty, herby, briny creation that is weird and wonderful in equal measure. It has been a local anchor for years and even spawned a Detroit outpost.
