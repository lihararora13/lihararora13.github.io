Veganski Kiosk is exactly what it sounds like, a small vegan kiosk just off the city's main shopping street, and it is perfect for a walking lunch. Lasagna, sandwiches and quick plates, all vegan, all cheap, all made to be eaten on the move.

Grab a lasagna and keep walking down Kneza Mihaila. This is the pick for the day you are sightseeing and do not want to sit down for an hour.
