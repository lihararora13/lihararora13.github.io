Miào Shàn GōngZhǔ, 妙善公主素食養心鍋, is a hot pot restaurant on Ziqiang 3rd Road in Qianjin, est. 2019, and it is the most devotional room in this guide: Buddha artwork and calligraphy on the walls, a banner reading "天下第一湯" over the counter, and a soup recipe the house attributes to Guanyin Bodhisattva. The signature is the medicinal nourishing hot pot, 藥膳養心鍋, a steaming clay pot of carrots, corn, cabbage, mushrooms, tofu and greens, served as a single set for NT$329 or a double set for NT$977, alongside a five-elements vegetable hot pot, a princess veg oyster omelette set for NT$150, handmade toona dumplings, curry fried rice and sesame dry noodles.

The restaurant describes itself as fully vegan, with no egg, no milk and no alliums, and it is listed here as all-vegan on that basis, though HappyCow still carries an older Ovo tag. It is open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00am to 3:00pm and 4:30pm to 8:00pm, closed on Mondays, and a visit in November 2026 confirms it is trading.
