Zest made the list for a reason. A bohemian garden cafe on Agonda's beach road with gluten-free vegan pizza, smoothie bowls and okra fries, and a loyal following. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Agonda, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Gluten-free vegan pizza, Smoothie bowl, Buddha bowl if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
