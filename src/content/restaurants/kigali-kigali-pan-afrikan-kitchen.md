Kigali Pan-Afrikan Kitchen is a Pan-African restaurant on KN 4 Avenue in the city centre, with dishes from all over the continent: jollof rice, jerk tofu, agatogo and more. Reviewers praise the vegan soul platter with extra BBQ tofu and the Kenyan sukuma, and the prices are cheap.

Order the vegan soul platter with extra BBQ tofu. It is in the car-free zone with lovely outdoor seating, and it is the best-value vegan meal in Kigali.
