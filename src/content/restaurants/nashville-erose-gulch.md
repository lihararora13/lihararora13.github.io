E+ROSE is the bright, feel-good counterweight to all that hot chick'n: founded by a registered dietitian, with a string of cafes across Nashville, and a menu built on whole, plant-based ingredients with no seed oils or cane sugar. HappyCow lists the Gulch spot as vegan-friendly because honey appears in a few bowls; everything else, juices, smoothies, wraps, salads and toasts, is vegan.

It is the perfect pre-show or post-workout stop in the Gulch, grab a Pro Bowl and a Supernova smoothie and you will glow a little. NASHtoday and HappyCow both rate it among the city's best vegan eats.
