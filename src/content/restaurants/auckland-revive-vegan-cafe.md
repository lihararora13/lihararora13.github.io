Revive is the CBD lunch hero: a counter of steaming hotpots, dahls and bright salads that changes every day, all of it vegan, with nothing deep-fried and no animal products used on the premises at all. Its own menu page says so in capital letters, which is the kind of clarity we like.

It only opens Monday to Friday, 10:30 to 4, so this is a workday treat rather than a weekend plan. Grab the hotpot of the day, add a salad box, and finish with an orange and cacao slice for the walk back.
