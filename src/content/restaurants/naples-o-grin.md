O' Grin is an informal lunch choice on Via Mezzocannone, in Naples' university area, built around a changing daily plate. Expect a breaded cutlet with greens, couscous and tomato stew, or whatever the kitchen cooked that morning, plus a simple dessert, all of it fully vegan and priced for students.

It is a lunch spot first, so arrive before the early afternoon rush and do not expect a long evening menu. The daily plate is the order; ask what it is and take it.
