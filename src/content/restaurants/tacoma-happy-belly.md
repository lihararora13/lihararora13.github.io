Happy Belly is Tacoma's vegetarian cafe on Market Street, open since 2014, with many vegan items across the breakfast and lunch menu: a Buena Dia Burrito, Ultra Avocado Toast, a Mango Paradise smoothie and a Tex grain bowl. It is the morning pick in this guide, open Monday to Friday 8am to 3pm and Saturday to Sunday 9am to 3pm, with a bright, casual room and a juice bar. The vegan items are marked, so ordering is straightforward.

Order the Buena Dia Burrito and a smoothie. It is downtown, so it pairs well with a morning at the museums.
