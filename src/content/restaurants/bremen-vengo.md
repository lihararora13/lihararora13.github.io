Vengo is the Ostertor's answer to the question of what vegans eat after dark: a fully plant-based fast-food counter doing burgers, loaded fries and currywurst, open late into the evening and until midnight on Fridays and Saturdays. It is quick, cheap and unpretentious, and it is exactly the kind of place a vegan scene needs once the sit-down restaurants have closed their kitchens.

It sits on Ostertorsteinweg, right in the middle of Bremen's going-out quarter. Order a burger and loaded fries, add a currywurst if you are still hungry, and enjoy the fact that the whole menu is yours. This is the table for a Friday night, not a white-tablecloth dinner.
