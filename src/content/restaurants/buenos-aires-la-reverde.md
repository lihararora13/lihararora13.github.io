If you want to eat like an Argentine, vegan style, this is the stop. La Reverde has been firing its plant-based parrilla since 2017, building its chorizos and steaks from seitan and serving them with salad, fries and chimichurri, exactly as tradition demands.

Order the grill for two and let it arrive sizzling at the table. Hours can be irregular, so check their Instagram before you go, then go hungry.
