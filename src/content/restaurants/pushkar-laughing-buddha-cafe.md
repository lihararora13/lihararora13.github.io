Laughing Buddha is a Pushkar classic: a cheerful café on Main Market Road in Badi Basti, where travellers have been eating falafel wraps and drinking fresh juice for years. It is the kind of place that feels like it has always been there, and probably has.

Order a falafel wrap and a fresh juice, and watch the market go by. The pin is approximate to the Main Market Road area, so follow the road and the laughter rather than the map dot.
