Falafel House is Nairobi's falafel fix: a fast, friendly counter doing falafel in several forms, including vegan and gluten-free, plus customisable veggie burgers. It is the kind of place that makes a plant-based lunch easy rather than a project, with delivery and takeaway for busy days.

Order a falafel wrap, build a vegan burger your way, and add hummus if you are sharing. Simple, quick, and reliably vegan-friendly.
