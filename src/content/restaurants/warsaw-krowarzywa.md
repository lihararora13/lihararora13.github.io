Krowarzywa is Warsaw's iconic vegan burger chain, open since 2013 and now a local institution. The Plac Konstytucji branch is the central one to know, serving plant-based burgers, fries and shakes in a fast, friendly room.

It is cheap, cheerful and very good. Order a burger and fries, add a shake, and you have the classic Warsaw vegan lunch for next to nothing.
