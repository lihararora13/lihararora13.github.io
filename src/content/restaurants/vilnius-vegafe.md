Vegafe is the creative vegan kitchen in Vilnius' Old Town, the place local guides name when they want inventive plant-based food rather than the familiar. The menu changes with the seasons and the kitchen's ideas, and it is fully vegan from top to bottom.

It sits on Augustijonu street, a short walk from the cathedral. Order whatever the chef's special is, add a seasonal main to share, and let the kitchen show off.
