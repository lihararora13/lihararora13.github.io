H&S Brothers Meal is a small healthy fast-food takeaway on the Guntramstraße, doing vegetarian and vegan bowls, wraps and juices for the weekday lunch crowd. It is not a place to linger, it is a place to eat well quickly: fresh ingredients, clear labelling, and prices that suit a student budget. Think of it as Freiburg's answer to a lunch break that happens to be entirely meat-free.

It keeps weekday hours, mostly noon to six. Order a vegan bowl or a falafel wrap, add a fresh juice, and take it to the nearby Stadtgarten if the sun is out.
