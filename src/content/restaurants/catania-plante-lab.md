PLANTE Lab is a fast-casual vegetarian restaurant on Via Sant'Anna, open noon to half eleven every day, and the loudest vegan-friendly room in Catania. The menu runs on plant-based arrosticini skewers, tacos, panelle, vegan burgers and gyros, and a November 2026 vegan tour found the burger and fries the plate to order.

Order the vegan arrosticini, the vegan burger and the gyros, and come hungry: the portions are generous and the prices are low.
