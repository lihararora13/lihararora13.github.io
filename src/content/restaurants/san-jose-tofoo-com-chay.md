Tofoo Com Chay is the little downtown shop that San Jose vegans whisper about like a secret, even though it has been fully vegan since 2018. The room is small, the menu is Vietnamese Buddhist chay cooking, and the sesame chicken sandwich is the thing to order first, followed by steamed buns and a rice plate.

It is cheap, fast and unfussy, the kind of place where the owner knows the regulars and the food tastes like it was made by someone who cares. Go at lunch, order the sandwich, and thank us later.
