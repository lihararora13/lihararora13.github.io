Lablebi is a take-out shop at 67 Rue Bab El Khadra offering Tunisian chickpea stew, lablabi, and it is open daily from 11:00am to 8:00pm, which makes it the all-day version of Badra's breakfast-only stall around the corner.

It is cash only. Order the lablabi and eat it while walking the medina.
