Malaguetta Restaurante Vegano is a fully vegan brazilian spot in Benfica. Malaguetta is the city's full-service vegan restaurant, cooking Brazilian comfort food without animal products in the Benfica district.

Order the Feijoada vegana, Lasanha vegana, Picadinho com vatapá, and Cheesecake vegano. The feijoada is the signature; the picadinho with vatapá is the second plate to try.
