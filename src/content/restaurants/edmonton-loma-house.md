Loma House is Edmonton's Pan-Asian vegan spot, serving plant-based bowls, curries and stir fries in the south of the city. It is a quieter, calmer room than the Whyte Avenue picks, and the food is fresh, colourful and generous.

Order a Pan-Asian bowl and a curry, and you have a proper vegan dinner without the downtown crowds. It is the south-side anchor on this list.
