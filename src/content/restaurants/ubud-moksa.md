Moksa is the Ubud experience, not just a meal. The restaurant sits beside its own permaculture garden on a quiet lane off Jalan Raya Sayan, and founder and chef Made Runatha builds the menu around what the garden grows: raw lasagne, jack rendang, tempeh spare ribs, black pepper beet steak and jumbo plant platters for sharing. The dining room looks onto the garden, the site hosts a Saturday farmers' market and raw-food cooking classes, and the whole place runs on a slow, sustainable rhythm.

It opens daily from 10am to 9pm. One honest note: Moksa still lists a few vegetarian items, so ask for coconut syrup instead of honey and confirm your dish is vegan when you order. It is a short scooter ride west of central Ubud, down a narrow lane past the rice fields, and it is worth every bump.
