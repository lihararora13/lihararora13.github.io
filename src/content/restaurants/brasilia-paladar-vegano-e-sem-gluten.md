Paladar Vegano e Sem Glúten is a fully vegan brazilian spot in Asa Norte. Paladar Vegano is the city's vegan-and-gluten-free buffet, a pay-by-weight style spread where everything on the counter is plant-based and wheat-free.

Order the Torta de mandioca, Lasanha de abóbora, and Strogonoff de shitake. The torta de mandioca and the strogonoff de shitake are the two dishes to load up on.
