Green Owl Cafe calls itself Madison's only vegetarian and vegan restaurant, and it has earned the title: a relaxed Atwood Avenue cafe where the menu runs from a Nashville Hot chicken sandwich to a Big Owl Burger, an oyster mushroom po'boy, and a coconut cream pie that reviewers call the best they have ever had.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so check the cheese on your order, but the vegan dishes are the heart of the menu. Come for the burger, stay for the pie.
