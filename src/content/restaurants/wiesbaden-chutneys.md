Chutney's is an Afghan restaurant at the LuisenForum on Schwalbacher Strasse, with labelled vegan options including lentils with rice, aubergine, okra, samosas and a vegan bowl. It is open Monday to Saturday, eleven to nine.

Order the lentils with rice, the aubergine and okra, the samosas and the vegan bowl, and come for lunch, when the menu is at its fullest.
