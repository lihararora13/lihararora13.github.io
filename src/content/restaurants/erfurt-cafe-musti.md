Cafe Musti is a small, friendly cafe on the Lange Brucke, one of Erfurt's prettiest old-town streets, with seats outside and a vegan offering that covers the day: a vegan breakfast, at least one vegan soup and vegan cake, alongside coffee and the rest of the menu. It opens at nine every morning, which makes it a reliable start to a day of sightseeing.

The vegan dishes are the minority of a wider menu, so ask for them by name. Order the vegan breakfast, take a table outside if the weather allows, and ease into the day. This is the gentle morning pick in the old town.
