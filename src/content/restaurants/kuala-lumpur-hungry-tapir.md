The Hungry Tapir is where every Kuala Lumpur vegan guide starts, and the pink shophouse on Jalan Petaling earns it: rattan, jazz, a full vegan bar, and a kitchen that treats Malaysian classics with real respect. Vegan Food Quest's October 2026 update confirms the whole menu, desserts and drinks included, is now fully vegan, and the room stays packed for good reason.

Order like you mean it: lion's mane mushroom satay to start, the nasi lemak with its mushroom rendang, and a beetroot burger if anyone at the table is still pretending to be restrained. Book ahead, then wander Kwai Chai Hong's lantern-lit alley afterwards.
