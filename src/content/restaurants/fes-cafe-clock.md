Cafe Clock is more than a meal, a cultural cafe with a rooftop, cooking classes and storytelling nights, and the vegan food is genuinely tasty. The rooftop views over the medina are a big part of the charm, and the spiced cappuccino is the house drink.

Order the vegan lunch plate and a spiced cappuccino, and stay for a cooking class if you have an afternoon. Come for lunch and leave with plans.
