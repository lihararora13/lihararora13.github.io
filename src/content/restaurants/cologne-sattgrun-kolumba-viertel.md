Sattgrün is Cologne's vegan buffet institution, a few minutes from the cathedral on Ludwigstraße, where you help yourself to hot dishes, salads, soups and cakes and pay by the size of your plate. It is one of several Sattgrün outlets, and this one, in the Kolumba quarter, is the most central and the most loved, with 447 HappyCow reviews behind it.

Come hungry, take a medium plate, and work your way through the salads, the curries, the pasta and the cakes. It is the easiest good lunch in the city centre, and the best way to try a dozen dishes at once.
