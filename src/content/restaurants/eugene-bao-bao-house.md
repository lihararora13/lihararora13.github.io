Bao Bao House is Eugene's Chinese vegan pick: a restaurant on West Park Street in downtown Eugene with vegan bao, potstickers, mapo tofu and mushroom fried rice. It is a small, casual room, open Monday to Saturday 11am to 8pm, and it is the place in this guide for a quick, satisfying Chinese meal with vegan dishes that are part of the menu rather than an afterthought.

Order the mapo tofu and a vegan bao. Ask about the day's vegan specials: the menu rotates.
