Veggietude is Nagpur's plant-forward café, a Ring Road spot in Trimurti Nagar that speaks the language of bowls, salads and cold-pressed juice. It is the lightest meal on this guide, and a welcome counterweight to the thalis and dosas elsewhere on the list.

Build a vegan bowl, add a juice, and you have a clean lunch that still feels like a meal. There is also a Civil Lines branch, so check which is closer before you set out.
