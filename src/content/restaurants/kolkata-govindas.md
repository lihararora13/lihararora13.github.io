Govindas made the list for a reason. A pure-vegetarian institution where the thali, dal and sabzi are vegan-friendly with a word to the kitchen. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Phool Bagan, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Veg thali, Dal, Seasonal sabzi if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
