Poornatva is Noida's organic bakery with a serious vegan streak: the Belgian chocolate cake is 100% vegan, the brownies are 100% plant-based, and the signature BBQ tofu pizza is the order regulars rave about. The room is calm, the music is soothing, and the DIY pizza option lets you build your own and have it delivered hot.

One honest note: the cafe itself also serves non-veg, so it is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, and it is closed on Mondays. Order from the vegan menu, start with the BBQ tofu pizza, and finish with the Belgian chocolate cake.
