Non La is a Vietnamese restaurant on Knuckey Street in Darwin, serving eggplant curry, pho and vegan-friendly Vietnamese dishes. It is a modern, comfortable room in the centre, and it suits a dinner.

Order the eggplant curry and a pho, and ask for the vegan dishes by name, as the kitchen uses fish sauce. It is central and reliable.
