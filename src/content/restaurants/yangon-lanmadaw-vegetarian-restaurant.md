Lanmadaw Vegetarian Restaurant is a vegetarian restaurant on Pyay Road in Lanmadaw, open daily from 8am to 8pm, serving Burmese vegetarian dishes. It is a neighbourhood kitchen rather than a destination, and it is the best Burmese vegetarian meal in the west of the city.

Order the Burmese vegetable dishes and tofu with rice, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. Its pin is approximate to Pyay Road.
