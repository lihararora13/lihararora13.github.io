Aunty Sophie is a cafe restaurant on Noordwal Wes in the town centre with a solid vegan bench: avocado toast, mock chicken toasties, beans on toast and salads, with outdoor seating and free Wi-Fi. It is open daily from 7:00am to 4:30pm.

Come for breakfast or a light lunch. The avocado toast and the mock chicken toastie are the vegan orders to start with.
