The Vegan Life made the list for a reason. A 100% vegan cafe in Sangamwadi doing mock-meat burgers, sandwiches and vegan milkshakes at prices that make vegan food feel everyday. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Sangamwadi, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan burger, Vegan milkshake, Mock meat sandwich if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
