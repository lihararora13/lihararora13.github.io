Joia is the special-occasion booking in Milan, and one of the most important restaurants in the history of plant-based fine dining. Opened in 1989 and the first vegetarian restaurant in Europe to win a Michelin star in 1996, it still holds that star under chefs Sauro Ricci and Raffaele Minghini, who took over from founder Pietro Leemann in 2024. The kitchen uses no eggs and no cheese made with animal rennet, and every tasting menu can be cooked fully plant-based, so say so when you book.

Dinner runs as two tasting menus of small, painterly plates with names like Col favore della luna, and recent guests single out the asparagus courses and the seasonal wine pairings. Lunch is the friendlier way in: the Piatto Quadro serves five small tasting dishes together for a shorter, lighter visit. Book one to two weeks ahead for dinner; closed Sundays and Mondays.
