Samsara is a health-focused vegetarian kitchen on 6a Calle Poniente, rated 4.5, making food with world influences, from falafel and beans to pancakes and Indian seasonings, with more than half the menu vegan. An attached store sells coconut oil, natural toothpaste, jewellery, raw bars, coffee and kombucha.

Order a falafel plate or the pancakes, and browse the store after. It is open every day, 8 am to 10 pm, which makes it the most flexible table in Antigua.
