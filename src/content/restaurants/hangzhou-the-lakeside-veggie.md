The Lakeside Veggie is the West Lake dining room on this list: a more up-market all-vegan restaurant on a side street off Nanshan Road, on the side of the lake, with an English menu and warm, friendly staff. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow and a 4.6 on Menuweb from 37 reviews, and the brand has since expanded to Shanghai, where its outpost holds a Michelin Bib Gourmand.

Order the sweet and sour lotus root, the basil eggplant and the mushroom daoxiao noodles, and the Yunnan mushrooms and almond sweet soup. Reviewers note the location can be tricky to find, as Apple Maps shows it incorrectly; use the Chinese address on this page. Open daily 11am to 8:30pm.
