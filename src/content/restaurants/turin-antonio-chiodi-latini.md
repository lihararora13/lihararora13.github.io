Antonio Chiodi Latini is a small all-vegan pastry and pasta spot in the centre, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, and the desserts are the reason to come. The tortellini and spaghetti in green sauce are the savoury anchors, but regulars keep naming the chocolate cream dessert topped with whipped topping as the dish to save room for.

It is a compact room, so it works best as a lunch or an afternoon pastry stop rather than a big group dinner. Order the tortellini, then the chocolate dessert.
