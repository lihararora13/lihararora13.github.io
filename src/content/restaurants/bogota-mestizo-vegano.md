Mestizo Vegano has been mixing up Latin America on a plate since 2016, and its menu reads like a tour of the continent: mushroom ceviche, tortas, nachos, tacos, empanadas, burgers, pasta and lomo saltado, with the kitchen making its own seitan steaks and cakes for dessert. The room in the Edificio Alaska, a short walk from the historic centre, is warm and unpretentious, and on some Friday and Saturday nights it fills with live music.

It only serves lunch, noon to four, so treat it as the centrepiece of a slow afternoon downtown. The mushroom ceviche is the opener, the seitan steak is the main event, and the yuca waffles are the reason to save room.
