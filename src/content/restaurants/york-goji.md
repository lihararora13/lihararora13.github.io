Goji has been feeding York's vegetarians for years from its Goodramgate cafe, and the vegan options are plentiful and clearly marked. It is a calm, old-fashioned cafe rather than a scene, and all the better for it.

The vegan breakfast and the soup of the day are the safest orders. It is a good rainy-day stop between the Minster and the Shambles.
