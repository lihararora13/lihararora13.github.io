Sat Nam is a vegetarian restaurant on 4 Calle Oriente, rated 5.0, offering 17 vegetarian and vegan hot dishes that fuse East and West flavours. The room is small, reservations are required, and the cooking is careful rather than fast.

Order a vegan hot dish and ask which of the 17 are vegan that day. It is open for lunch and dinner with a break in the afternoon, and it closes earlier on Fridays and Saturdays, so book ahead.
