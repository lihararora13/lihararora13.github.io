Kleines Waffelhaus is a waffle house on Fleischhauerstraße, and it is the sweet stop on Lübeck's list: vegan waffles with vegan ice cream and vegan chocolate cream, made properly rather than as a token option. The room is small and old-town in feel, the waffles are crisp and warm, and the vegan range is a real part of the menu.

It is not a fully vegan shop: non-vegan waffles are sold alongside, including salmon and pastrami versions, so order from the vegan side and say so clearly. It opens Tuesday to Saturday afternoons. Order a vegan waffle with vegan ice cream, add chocolate cream, and enjoy the most charming dessert in Lübeck.
