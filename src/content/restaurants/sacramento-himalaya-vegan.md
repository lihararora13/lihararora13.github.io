Himalaya Vegan brings Nepalese and Indian cooking to Sacramento's vegan scene, and the momos are the reason to go: vegetable dumplings made from scratch, served with chutney, alongside dal bhat and chana masala that taste like they come from a family kitchen rather than a steam table.

It is the most transportive meal on the list, and the one to take someone who thinks vegan food is all burgers and bowls. Order the momos first, then the dal bhat, and do not rush.
