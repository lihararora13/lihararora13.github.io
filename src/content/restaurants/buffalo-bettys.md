Betty's is Buffalo's brunch institution and one of its most vegan-aware mixed restaurants: the menu marks vegan dishes with a small V in a heart, and the vegan bench is real, vegan chik'n wings, a black bean quesadilla with vegan cheese and sour cream, an entirely vegan salad, vegan sides and toasts with vegan butter, plus Just Egg across the breakfast menu. The room is a classic Allentown cafe, busy at weekends and best before the rush.

Hours are Tuesday to Friday 8am to 3pm, Saturday 9am to 3pm and Sunday 9am to 2pm, closed Mondays. Order the wings and the quesadilla, and bring non-vegan friends: the menu works for everyone.
