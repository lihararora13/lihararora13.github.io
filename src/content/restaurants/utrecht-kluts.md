KLUTS is a vegan bakery and coffee bar on Achter de Dom, behind the Dom cathedral, serving freshly baked cookies, cakes and pies next to the beautiful Dom church. The bakes are all vegan, from a traditional apple pie to a pumpkin orange cake, with good coffee and a tabby cat as the cutest regular.

It is a small, cosy room, so come for coffee and cake rather than a big group lunch. Order a loaf cake and a cinnamon roll.
