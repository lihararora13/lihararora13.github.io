Daxingshan Temple Vegetarian is the table to book first in Xi'an, and the one the guides keep coming back to: a fully vegan canteen inside the Daxingshan Temple grounds, near the Giant Wild Goose Pagoda, rated 4.5 on HappyCow with reviews as recent as November 2025. The menu is classic temple vegetarian cooking at canteen prices, about 60 RMB a person, from the vegan duck and fungi soup that reviewers single out to walnut baozi, Buddha soup, Kung Pao chicken, sizzling fish, ma po tofu and mock goose.

The temple grounds close at 5pm but the guard lets restaurant visitors in after that, so an evening meal is fine. The same owner runs a larger sister branch, Tianlong Baoyan, on Ci'en West Road, so treat that as the same operation rather than a separate restaurant. Order the vegan duck and the fungi soup first, and save room for a baozi.
