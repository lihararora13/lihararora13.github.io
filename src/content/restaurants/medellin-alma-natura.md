Alma Natura is Laureles at its most low-key: a small vegan kitchen serving healthy almuerzos, the set lunch that is the backbone of Colombian eating, rebuilt entirely from plants. The plates are wholesome and colourful, four-component plated meals with grains, vegetables and plant protein, and the juices are fresh. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow and a loyal local following.

Come at lunchtime, order the almuerzo del día, and treat it as the calm, nourishing counterpoint to the city's louder tables. It is open daily from noon, Sundays until six.
