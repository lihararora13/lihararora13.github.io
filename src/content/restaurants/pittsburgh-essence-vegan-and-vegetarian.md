Essence is the South Side's farm-to-table vegan restaurant: opened in 2023, fully vegan, and fusing West African, Korean, Middle Eastern and Indian cooking with modern American vegan fare. The West African plates are the signature, the Korean dishes are the surprise, and the Indian curries are the comfort move.

It is the most ambitious new vegan restaurant in Pittsburgh, and the one to watch. Order a West African plate, add a Korean dish, and enjoy the range.
