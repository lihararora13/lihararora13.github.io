Strawberry Blonde Bakery is Ottawa's vegan bakery, and it is also gluten-free and nut-free, which makes it a lifesaver for mixed-diet groups. Cupcakes, cookies, cakes and seasonal treats come out of the Westboro shop, all plant-based.

It is the dessert stop on this list. Order a cupcake and a cookie, and ask about custom cakes if you are celebrating something in town.
