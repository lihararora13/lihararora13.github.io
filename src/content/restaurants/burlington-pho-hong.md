Pho Hong is Burlington's Vietnamese standby, a no-frills room on North Winooski Avenue where the pho chay and the tofu banh mi have been feeding students and locals for years. It is the cheapest sit-down meal on this list, and the fresh spring rolls are the right starter.

Order the pho, ask for it vegan, and add the banh mi if you are still hungry.
