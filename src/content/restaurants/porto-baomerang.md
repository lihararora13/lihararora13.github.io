Baomerang is Porto's vegan bao bar, a fully vegan Asian-fusion street-food spot across the river in Gaia. Teriyaki portobello bao, crispy tofu bao, Korean-style cauliflower, all of it bright, bold and made to be eaten with your hands.

Order two bao and the cauliflower to share. It is the fun, fast dinner on this list, and the desserts are worth saving room for.
