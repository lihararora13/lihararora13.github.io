Govinda's is a 100% vegetarian, vegan and Jain restaurant on the second floor in Ao Nang, offering an extensive variety of Indian cuisine alongside falafel, hummus and an aloo tikki burger. It is open daily from noon to 11:15pm, and it is the most reliable vegetarian Indian kitchen in Ao Nang.

Order the dal tadka and the falafel with hummus, and ask for vegan dishes by name, as the kitchen also serves paneer. It is inexpensive and central, and it suits a group with mixed diets.
