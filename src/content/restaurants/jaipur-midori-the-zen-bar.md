Midori - The Zen Bar made the list for a reason. A matcha bar and all-veg C-Scheme kitchen with vegan ramen, tofu katsu and oat-milk lattes in a calm, zen room. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in C-Scheme, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Matcha latte, oat milk, Vegan ramen, Tofu katsu if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
