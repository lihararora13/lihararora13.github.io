East Village Cafe is the cafe Bristol's vegans call the best in the city, a calm Clifton spot that does brunch, bowls, cakes and proper coffee, all of it fully vegan. It is the quieter end of the list, the place to go when you want a slow morning rather than a feast, and the food is thoughtful rather than showy.

Boyces Avenue is a short walk from the Downs, so pair it with a stroll if the weather is kind. Order the vegan brunch, add a cake, and take your time.
