Hot pot is Chengdu's defining meal, and Miao Wang makes it fully plant-based: build your own hot pot from tofu, mushrooms and fresh vegetables, and let the mala broth do its work. No meat stock, no compromises.

Come with friends and make an evening of it. The mushroom and tofu selection is the heart of the meal.
