June is Coimbatore's newest pretty cafe, opened in June 2026 in RS Puram, and it wears its dietary labels on its sleeve: 100% vegetarian, Jain-friendly options, vegan options, and every dessert eggless. The room is sage green and checkered floors, the coffee is specialty, and the tagline is come for the food, stay for the feeling.

Order a vegan pasta and an eggless dessert pastry, and ask the staff which dishes are fully plant-based. It is the most vegan-aware cafe in the city after Padayal.
