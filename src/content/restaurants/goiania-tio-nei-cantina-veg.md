Tio Nei Cantina Veg is a fully vegan brazilian spot in Setor Leste Vila Nova. Tio Nei is a vegan cantina, the kind of unpretentious lunch counter where the daily plate does all the talking.

Order the Prato do dia vegano, Feijoada vegana, and Salada da casa. The daily plate is the order; it changes with the market.
