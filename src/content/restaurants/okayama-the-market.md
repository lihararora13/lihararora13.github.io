The Market is a market-style restaurant in central Okayama with a seasonal Japanese and Western menu that includes vegan-friendly dishes. It is the kind of place where the vegan options change with what is in season, so ask the staff what can be made plant-based on the day rather than relying on a fixed vegan menu.

The seasonal vegetable plate and any vegan pasta on the day are the safest bets, with a salad bowl and soup as backups. It is a useful central fallback when the fully vegan kitchens are full or closed, and its pin is approximate to the city centre.
