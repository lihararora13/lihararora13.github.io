Flora & Fauna is the brunch that built Perth's vegan scene. Born in a Northbridge laneway in 2014 and now settled into a sunny courtyard in Fremantle's Atwell Arcade, it does the classics turned gloriously plant-based: truffle mac and cheese, pistachio pancakes, chick'n waffles and a mushroom bagel that regulars call their favourite in the city. Everything on the plate is vegan; only the coffee offers cow's milk, and the oat flat white is the move anyway.

Come on a weekday if you can, because weekends fill up fast and the wait is real. Book ahead, order the pancakes, and linger in the courtyard with a strawberry matcha: this is the table that converts sceptics one brunch at a time.
