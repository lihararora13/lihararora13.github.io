Anna e Paolo is a long-running Italian restaurant in the city centre, a classic Tangier table with pasta, salads and vegetable dishes. It is a meat-serving room, but the kitchen does vegan pasta on request.

Order a vegan pasta and ask for no cheese or butter. It is the Italian fallback when the medina rooms are full.
