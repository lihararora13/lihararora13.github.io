1936 is the grown-up table on this list: a fine-dining Italian room inside the Saraca hotel on Mall Avenue, all polished service and white tablecloths. It is not a vegan restaurant, but the kitchen is used to building vegan plates and does it properly rather than as an afterthought.

This is the pick for a celebration dinner. Ask for the vegan pasta or a wood-fired pizza without cheese, and let the staff guide you through the seasonal vegetables. Book ahead and mention vegan when you do.
