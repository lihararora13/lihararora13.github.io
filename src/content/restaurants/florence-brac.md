Brac is the bookshop-restaurant near Santa Croce, a literary room full of art where the kitchen cooks creative vegan dishes from Tuscan seasonal ingredients: grain salads, vegetable tarts and vegan pasta, with mains at about twelve to sixteen euros.

It is open daily for lunch and dinner, and it is one of the most pleasant dining experiences in Florence, especially in the evening when the bookshop closes and the restaurant takes over.
