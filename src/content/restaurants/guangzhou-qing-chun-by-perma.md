Qing Chun by Perma is the dim sum room to book in Guangzhou: a fully vegan dim sum restaurant on the fifth floor of the IGC mall at 222 Xingmin Road, listed in the Michelin Guide, with all-vegan versions of the classics from dumplings to deep-fried dishes. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, and a November 2026 video tour shows the red signage, the long lantern-lit dining room with a view of Canton Tower, and steamed dumplings, mapo tofu and glazed lotus root coming out of the open kitchen.

Order the steamed dumplings, the mapo tofu, cold noodles with chili oil and glazed lotus root. At peak hours expect a wait at the entrance; you take a ticket and are guided in when your number is called, and staff look out for foreign visitors. Note that a second Perma restaurant, Qing Chun By Perma, trades from Shop 501 in the same building; this entry is the L5 dim sum room, store L5025. Open daily 11am to 9pm.
