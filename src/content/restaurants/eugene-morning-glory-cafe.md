Morning Glory Cafe is Eugene's breakfast institution: a fully vegetarian cafe on Willamette Street in downtown Eugene, with many vegan options across the breakfast menu, vegan French toast, tofu scramble, biscuits and gravy and a vegan omelet. It is the morning pick in this guide, open Monday to Friday 7am to 2pm and Saturday to Sunday 8am to 2pm, and it is busy at weekends, so arrive early.

Ask for the vegan preparation: the kitchen is vegetarian and knows the vegan versions well. Order the French toast and the tofu scramble.
