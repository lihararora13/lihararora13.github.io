Monsoon is a plant-based new Hakka restaurant in Zhubei that held a Bib Gourmand in Taipei, moved to Zhubei in July 2025 and was named a new Bib Gourmand in Hsinchu County in the 2026 guide. It serves set menus by booking only, from NT$1,500 per person for eight dishes and two drinks, and it is the most ambitious vegan table in the Hsinchu area.

Book ahead through its website and expect an irregular schedule: sources disagree on open days, and the restaurant itself says to confirm when you book. It is cash only, and it suits a special evening rather than a walk-in lunch.
