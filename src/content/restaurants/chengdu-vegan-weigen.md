Vegan, known in Mandarin as Weigen, is a Zen-themed restaurant where every dish is vegetal, delicate in look and taste, with faux meats made for flavour rather than gimmick. No tobacco, no MSG, just careful cooking.

It is a calm, thoughtful meal, best taken slowly. Order a set and let the kitchen show off its delicate side.
