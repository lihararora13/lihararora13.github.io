The Sage of Cambridge sits at the front of The Fellows House hotel on Milton Road, a wellness-inspired cafe with cosy corners, good Wi-Fi and a pastry counter that regulars praise highly. The menu runs from vegan pancakes and oat bowls to vegan chilli and a build-your-own bagel, with gluten-free pastries and speciality coffee.

The vegan pancakes with berries are the breakfast order, and the jacket potato with vegan chilli is the lunch pick. It is a hotel cafe with a strong vegan section rather than an all-vegan room, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it doubles as a good working space by day.
