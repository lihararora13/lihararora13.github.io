Kimbap Spot is Bochum's fully vegan Korean counter on Ostring, a small city-centre room built around kimbap rolls and Korean rice bowls. The hours are unusual and worth checking: evenings from Wednesday to Saturday, a Thursday and Friday lunch slot, and a Sunday service that runs morning and evening, so it rewards a planned visit rather than a passing one.

Order the kimbap rolls first and a rice bowl second. It is the only fully vegan Korean table in the Ruhr on this list, and the cheapest of Bochum's all-vegan picks.
