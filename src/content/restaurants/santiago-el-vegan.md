El Vegan keeps things simple and does them well: an all-vegan eatery in Providencia where the menu is mostly burgers, sandwiches and hot dogs, with tofu and seitan doing the heavy lifting alongside rice and vegetables. It is the kind of place you drop into when you want something fast, filling and entirely plant-based, no questions asked.

The freezer case is a bonus: gyozas, seitan, spring rolls and onion rings to take home, plus custom cakes pre-ordered for special occasions. It is not fancy, and that is exactly the point.
