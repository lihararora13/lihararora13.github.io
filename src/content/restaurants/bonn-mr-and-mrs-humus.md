Mr and Mrs Humus is a small Middle Eastern restaurant on the corner where Kölnstraße meets Kaiser-Karl-Ring, built, as the owners put it, out of a love of hummus. The room is cosy and the menu is short and confident: hummus classic, baba ghanoush, pita falafel and shakshuka, with warm pita and the kind of unhurried service that makes a simple lunch feel like a break.

It is a mixed menu, so stick to the plant-based plates: the hummus classic is the one to order first, the baba ghanoush is the smoky second, and the pita falafel rounds it out. Evenings run until eleven, and the weekday opening at half past four makes it a good early-dinner stop.
