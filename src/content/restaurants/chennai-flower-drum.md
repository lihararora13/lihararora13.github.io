Flower Drum made the list for a reason. A pure-vegetarian Chinese and Thai favourite where most of the menu is inherently vegan: lotus stem, dimsums and curries. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Adyar, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Crispy lotus stem, Vegan dimsums, Thai veg curry if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
