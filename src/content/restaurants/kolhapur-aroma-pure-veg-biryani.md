Aroma is Kolhapur's pure-veg biryani specialist: a Takala restaurant built around vegetable biryani, the dish the city does best in vegetarian form. It is the most biryani meal on this list, and the one most likely to convert a sceptic.

Order a veg biryani, skip the raita or ask for it plant-based, and add a dal tadka without ghee. It is cheap, filling and properly Kolhapur.
