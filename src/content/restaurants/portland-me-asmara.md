Asmara is a small Eritrean restaurant a block off Congress Street, and it is one of the most naturally vegan-friendly rooms in Portland: the vegetable dishes use no dairy or egg, and the meal arrives on communal teff bread. Order the vegetable combo and let the lentils, potatoes, and spiced greens do the work.

Monday is by reservation only, and the room is small, so book ahead. Ask for separate breads if you are sharing with non-vegan diners.
