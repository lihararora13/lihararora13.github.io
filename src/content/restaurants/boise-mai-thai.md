Mai Thai is a downtown Thai restaurant on West Main Street with curries, noodle dishes, and tofu preparations that adapt to vegan orders. It is the evening pick on this list, open later than the downtown cafes, and the kitchen is used to vegan requests.

Order the green curry with tofu and the pad see ew, asking for the vegan preparation and confirming the fish sauce is omitted. The pin for this entry is address-level, so use the street address when navigating.
