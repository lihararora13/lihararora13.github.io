269 Two Six Nine is Frankfurt's vegan kosher restaurant, opened in early March 2024 in the middle of the city, and it has quickly become one of the most-loved tables in town. The menu covers hummus, falafel, shawarma, salads, pasta and a vegan schnitzel with fries, all of it plant-based and kosher, and the room is warm, modern and companion-animal friendly.

Order the shawarma plate, add hummus, and save room for the schnitzel. This is the table that proves vegan kosher food can be a destination in its own right.
