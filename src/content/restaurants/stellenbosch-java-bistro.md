Java Bistro on Church Street is one of the few Stellenbosch spots with a separate vegan and vegetarian menu, which makes ordering straightforward. Vegan options include two pizzas, stir-fry, curry pasta, smoothies, a lentil crunch wrap, a smoothie bowl, breakfast dishes, a pesto-mushroom sandwich, a hummus sandwich, a burger and dessert.

It is open daily from 8:00am to 10:00pm, Sundays from 9:00am. The lentil crunch wrap and the curry pasta are the dishes to start with. Note the pin is at street level on Church Street.
