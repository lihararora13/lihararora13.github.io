La Jacka is an all-vegan Mexican taco truck doing tacos, enchiladas, guacamole, and nopales, and it shows up in nearly every local vegan roundup of Fresno. The food is Mexican-inspired and fully plant-based, with dried chilies and fresh salsas doing the heavy lifting.

Order the vegan tacos and the enchiladas. Note that La Jacka is mobile, so the pin is the truck's listed base at Van Ness Avenue rather than a fixed dining room; check its current location before a special trip.
