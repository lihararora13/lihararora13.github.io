Little Aladdin is a small cafe on High Street that went fully vegan around 2023, and it is the cheapest option in this whole guide. There is little decor, just formica tables and big portions, but the star is the rice and three: rice with three curries of your choice, a Northern Quarter lunch tradition that goes back decades. Regulars swear by the mixed wrap with mango chutney, the chickpea curry and the cabbage curry, and the samosa chaat and vegan doner kebab round out a menu that never tries to be anything it is not.

It is open Monday to Saturday, roughly 11:45am to 8pm, closed on Sundays. Order the rice and three for about seven or eight pounds, add a samosa chaat, and you have had the most Manchester lunch there is. Cash in your pocket, appetite ready, no booking needed.
