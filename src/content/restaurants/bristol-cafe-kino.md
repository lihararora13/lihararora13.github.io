Cafe Kino has been a Bristol institution since 2011, and in August 2026 it went fully vegan, which makes this non-profit community cafe and workers cooperative one of the most ethical eats in the city. The menu is all-day comfort: breakfasts, BLTs, house-made bean burgers, soups, salads and speciality sandwiches, with sweet treats like chocolate brownie, choc chip cookies, chai cake, carrot cake and banana bread.

It doubles as an independent events venue, so there is often something on in the evening. Order the bean burger, save room for the chai cake, and enjoy a cafe that puts its values where its menu is.
