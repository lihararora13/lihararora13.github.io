Vegan East is proof that the sweetest part of any trip to Minneapolis is dessert. This all-vegan bakery bakes rotating cupcakes, cakes, and cinnamon rolls fresh every day, and the scratch-made caramel alone is worth the drive to Nokomis.

Birthdays, celebrations, or just a Tuesday that needs fixing: grab a box of cupcakes and watch them disappear. Pre-ordering is smart, since the case moves fast.
