Grandma Bob's is Corktown's handmade deep-dish pizza spot, and it has an amazing amount of vegan options: the gyro pizza, with vegan meat, feta, onion, tomatoes and fresh dill, is the signature, and the vegan deep-dish is the comfort move. It is a meat-serving pizzeria with a serious vegan bench, so it lands as vegan-friendly.

It is the Corktown pizza stop, and the one to hit after Street Beet for a second round. Order the gyro pizza, add a salad, and enjoy the deep dish.
