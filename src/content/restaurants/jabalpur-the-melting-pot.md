The Melting Pot is Jabalpur's continental café, a Paras Building room near the bus stand that stays open until midnight and serves the pasta, pizza and café plates the city's younger crowd gathers around. It is a vegetarian-friendly kitchen with a broad menu, and the vegetable-led dishes need only a no-cheese word to turn vegan.

Come for a late dinner when the thali houses have closed, order the pasta and a pizza without cheese, and enjoy one of the few kitchens in the city still cooking at 11 PM.
