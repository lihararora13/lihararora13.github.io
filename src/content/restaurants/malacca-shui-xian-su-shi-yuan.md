Shui Xian Su Shi Yuan is the kind of place Melaka does best: a simple, welcoming local vegetarian restaurant on a quiet street near Jonker Walk, with a wide menu of vegan dishes at prices that make it one of the cheapest meals in this guide. The vegan laksa and the vegan fish head soup are the dishes reviewers keep naming, alongside daily specials and chee cheong fun, and the room is friendly and unpretentious, the sort of spot locals eat at every week.

Hours are unreliable, so check before you go, and come at lunchtime when the kitchen is in full swing. It is a short walk from Jonker Street but feels a world away from the tourist crowds.
