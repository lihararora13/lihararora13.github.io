Cafe Chaos is a vegetarian cafe on Muhlstrasse, east of the centre, serving cakes, coffee and lunch dishes with vegan options. It is the daytime cafe pick on this list.

Order the vegan lunch dish, a cake and a coffee with plant milk, and ask which cakes are vegan, since the menu also runs dairy and egg.
