Cornucopia has been feeding Dublin since 1986, and it celebrated 40 years in 2026 as the city's grand old wholefood restaurant. What started as a health-food shop and cafe run by Deirdre and Neil McCafferty is now a multi-floor institution in handsome Georgian rooms just off Grafton Street, and it has moved from vegetarian to primarily vegan over the years.

The menu rotates with the seasons, so regulars never quite know what they will find, but the cakes are a constant: berry silken torte, chocolate fudge cake, and a pastry case worth planning a visit around. Note that a small number of dishes still use cow's milk and butter, so vegans should check the menu, but the plant-based heart of this place is beyond doubt.
