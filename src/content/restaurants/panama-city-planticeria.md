Planticeria works like a plant-based butcher's shop, with a deli-style fridge of plant-based meat alternatives, dips, desserts and ready meals, plus a casual kitchen that translates Latin and international classics into vegan form. Chef Hidemi leads the kitchen, with house-made plant meats, sourdough bread and fully vegan pastries, and a catering service for meat-free events.

Order the Best Vibes Reuben, the pastrami sandwich and the Llopo Tenders, and take something home from the fridge or the bakery. It is moderately priced and open every day except Monday.
