The Vikram's Kitchen is Lakadganj's pure-veg fine-dining room: marble tables, warm lighting, and a menu that runs from North Indian thalis to Indo-Chinese fried rice. It is rated 4.5 stars and describes itself as tradition, taste and trust, which is about right for a family dinner spot.

The North Indian thali is the signature, with the paneer dishes swapped or held for a plant-based plate. The Indo-Chinese side is the backup, with veg fried rice and schezwan noodles that veganise easily.
