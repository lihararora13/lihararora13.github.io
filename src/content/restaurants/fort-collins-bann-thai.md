Bann Thai is Fort Collins' Thai vegan pick: a restaurant on Council Tree Avenue in the Harmony Corridor with vegan dishes across the menu, pad Thai with tofu, fresh rolls, fried tofu and bean thread soup. It is the furthest south of the eight, a short drive from Old Town, and It is open Monday to Friday 11am to 3pm and 4pm to 9pm, and Saturday to Sunday noon to 9pm.

Specify vegan when ordering: no egg, and no fish or oyster sauce. The pad Thai with tofu is the dish to order.
