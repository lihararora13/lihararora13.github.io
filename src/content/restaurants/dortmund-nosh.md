nosh is Dortmund's bowl bar, a small Kreuzviertel counter on Saarlandstraße building bowls on purple rice with vegetable toppings and house-made sauces. Nearly everything is vegan: the marinated-tofu bowl, the tofu-walnut balls bowl and the vegan meat bowl are all marked vegan, the sauces are all vegan, and the udon soups come in vegan variations with tofu or dumplings.

One bowl on the menu is marinated salmon, so stick to the bowls marked vegan. It is open Monday to Friday until seven, a quick healthy lunch rather than a long dinner, and the miso udon with tofu is the warming order for a grey Ruhr day.
