Wildseed is the vegan restaurant you bring your non-vegan friends to when
you want them to stop asking questions. The Cow Hollow room glows, the
menu rides the seasons, and everything from the beet poke to the rigatoni
bolognese arrives looking like it was styled for a magazine. It tops
HappyCow's community rankings and anchors every local best-of list, and
the cocktail program built on fresh juices and herbs deserves its own
paragraph.

Yes, waits can stretch long on weekends, and yes, it is worth it. Start
with the poke, split a pizza or the bolognese, and do not skip the Meyer
lemon agave cheesecake with whipped coconut cream.
