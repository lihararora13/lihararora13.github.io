Loving Vegan is the place you go when you want comfort food and you want it vegan: gyros, pizza, burritos, sandwiches, Korean spicy noodles, mac and cheese with cashew cheese, teriyaki tofu and the well-loved Loving Burger. It is fully plant-based, no dairy, eggs or honey, and HappyCow users consistently rank it among the best vegan restaurants in Da Nang.

Come hungry, order the Loving Burger and a gyro to share, and finish with something sweet. This is the one for a big, satisfying, unapologetically fun meal.
