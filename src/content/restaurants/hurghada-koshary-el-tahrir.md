Koshary El Tahrir is a koshary house in Sakkala with vegan dishes marked on the menu, and koshary is vegan by birth: rice, lentils, chickpeas, pasta, tomato sauce and fried onions. It is the most reliable vegan meal in Hurghada.

Order the koshary with chilli sauce. It is cheap, fast and open late, and it is the first stop for any vegan arriving in town.
