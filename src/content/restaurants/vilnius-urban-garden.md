The Urban Garden is the bright, healthy end of this list, a newer spot on Basanaviciaus Street serving smoothie and buddha bowls and fresh juices for breakfast, brunch, lunch and dinner. Local guides call it heaven for a healthy body and soul, and it is the place to go when you want something light after a heavy few days of eating.

Order a buddha bowl, add a fresh juice, and recharge before heading back out into the Old Town.
