Seventh Heaven is a multi-cuisine vegetarian restaurant on Hazaribagh Road, with its own website and a steady 3.8 rating from 99 reviews. The South Indian side is the strongest: benne dosa, idli and medu vada, with chole bhature and chilli paneer on the North Indian side.

Order the benne dosa without butter, or the idli and medu vada, and you have a clean vegan breakfast or lunch. It is open 9 to 10 daily.
