Nick's Italian Kitchen is possibly even more celebrated in traveller circles than its guest house: a spacious dining room with large windows over the market, a mini library, and an extensive pure-vegetarian menu. Homemade pasta and pizzas, Indian curries and chapattis, Tibetan momo and thukpa, quiches and savoury pies.

The spaghetti aglio olio without cheese and the veg momos are the orders. It is open 7:30 AM to 9 PM, and the view is one of the better ones in the market.
