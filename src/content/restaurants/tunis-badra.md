Badra is a vegetarian shop by the main arc of Bab el Khadhra that sells exclusively lablabi for breakfast. You get a bowl of chickpea soup with spices as a basis, after tearing up the bread, with olive oil and eggs optional extras that you pay for, so order it without egg and it is vegan.

It is cash only and open Monday to Saturday from 6:00am to 11:00am, and in winter you should go before 10:00am. Ask people around for lablabi Badra if you cannot find it. Note the pin is at street level on Rue Bab el Khadhra.
