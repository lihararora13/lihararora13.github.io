Zur Goldenen Kaper is Regensburg's flagship vegan restaurant, a fully plant-based kitchen on the Rote-Löwen-Straße cooking seasonal, locally focused food in one of the old town's prettiest lanes. It is the table that shows what the city's vegan scene is capable of when it cooks properly: changing dishes, local produce, and a dining room that feels like a restaurant first and a vegan restaurant second. Outdoor seating in the warmer months makes it better still.

It opens from noon on its service days and closes on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, so plan around that. Order the seasonal main, take your time, and treat it as the dinner to book first in Regensburg.
