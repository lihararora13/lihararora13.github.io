Ji Ming Temple Vegetarian Diet is the temple table of Nanjing: the vegetarian restaurant of Jiming Temple, known as Baiweizhai, where Buddhist vegetarian culture in China began. It holds a 4.0 on HappyCow from 20 reviews, and Trip.com lists it as operating with current hours and recent visits praising the Guanyin blessing noodles and the vegetarian roast duck.

Order the Guanyin blessing noodles at 28 yuan, the vegetarian roast duck, the dumplings and the Jixiang mixed noodles. There are two restaurants, one at the top of the temple and one outside it, and the menu has English translations. Cash only. Open 10:30am to 3pm and 4:30pm to 8pm daily.
