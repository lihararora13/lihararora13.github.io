Penne is a fully vegan restaurant in Bo Phut with a perfect 5.0 from 74 HappyCow reviews, mixing Italian and Thai cooking in one menu. Thai vegan starters and mains sit alongside curries with mock meats, smoothies, and desserts, so it works for a group that cannot agree on one cuisine. It was previously called Pena and relocated from its old Bo Phut address; HappyCow confirmed it open at the current address in November 2024.

Use the current address, 26/33 Moo 3 in Bohut, not the old one that still appears in older guides. It is a mid-priced room in the Fisherman's Village area, an easy stop on a Bo Phut evening crawl.
