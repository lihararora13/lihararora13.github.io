AYI Café is the calm meeting place inside the Ashtanga yoga studio on the Safranberg, and its whole menu is plant-based: bowls, soups, smoothies, cakes, tea and coffee, with a vegan brunch buffet every other Sunday that needs a reservation. The food is meant to nourish and stay light, bowls inspired by different cuisines, hearty vegetable soups, golden milk, and it works just as well for non-yogis as for practitioners. The room is bright, the service is warm, and the pace is deliberately slow.

One practical note: parts of the grounds are still a building site, and access is currently via the rear entrance through Leimgrubenweg 29. Come for a bowl and a tea, and treat it as the quietest table in Ulm.
