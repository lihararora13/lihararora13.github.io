Tacos Veganos started as a food truck with a simple mission: prove that traditional Mexican street food can be vegan and tasty. It worked so well that the truck grew into a proper restaurant, and the menu is now the Valley's benchmark for plant-based tacos: jackfruit birria with rich consomme, birria ramen for the truly hungry, Baja cauli tacos, and al pastor that holds its own against any meat version. The oat milk horchata and the $2.75 all-day tacos keep the regulars coming back daily.

It is casual, fast, loud in the best way, and gloriously cheap for the quality. Order the birria tacos and the ramen, and wash them down with horchata: zero guilt, all flavour.
