Flavor of India is the third Indian restaurant in this guide and the one furthest up State Street, in San Roque: a restaurant with a vegetarian and vegan menu available on request, covering more than ten entrees plus appetizers, with paneer dishes substitutable with tofu. It is a buffet restaurant at lunch, so ask for the vegan menu rather than the buffet if you want the vegan dishes made to order.

Hours are Monday to Saturday 11am to 3pm and 5pm to 9pm, closed Sundays. Ask for the vegan menu on arrival, and order a vegetable curry with a tofu substitution.
