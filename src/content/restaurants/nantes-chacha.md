Chacha is a casual, vegan-friendly bistro tucked into Rue de la Bleterie, with a short menu that leans on vegetables rather than meat substitutes. The buckwheat and mushroom burger is the signature, with vegetable tacos and black bean tempeh alongside, and a purple carrot cake to finish.

It is not a fully vegan kitchen, so check the day's menu, but the vegan dishes are the ones the guides keep naming. Prices are friendly and the room is relaxed.
