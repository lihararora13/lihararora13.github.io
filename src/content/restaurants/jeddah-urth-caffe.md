Urth Caffe is the California cafe chain's branch in Ash Shati on the north Corniche, with a hummus platter and vegan-friendly dishes. It is a cafe rather than a restaurant, and the hummus platter is the vegan pick.

Order the hummus platter and a coffee with oat milk. It is the Corniche fallback, open long hours with a sea view.
