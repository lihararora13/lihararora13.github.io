Sherpa Garden is a Nepalese and Tibetan restaurant on West Colorado Avenue with dals, vegetable momos, and curries that adapt well to vegan orders. It is a warm, family-run room and a good counterpoint to the downtown burger and bar picks.

Order the dal bhat and the vegetable momos, asking for the vegan preparation, since some momos are served with dairy-based sauces.
