Dobra Tea is Squirrel Hill's bohemian tea room: a vegetarian light menu of hummus plates, cookies, cakes and chocolate, alongside tea tastings and classes in a room that feels like a different century. It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, with honey in some dishes, but the vegan options are clear and the tea list is the point.

It is the calmest stop on the list, and the one to hit when you want to slow down. Order a hummus plate, add a cookie, and book a tea tasting if you can.
