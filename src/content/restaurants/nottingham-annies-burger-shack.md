Annie's Burger Shack is a Hockley freehouse with a huge variety of vegan options, from burgers to American-style breakfasts. It is the kind of place where the vegan menu is as long as the meat one, and the portions are built for appetite.

Order a vegan burger with loaded fries, or come for the vegan breakfast. It is a meat-serving burger joint with a serious vegan bench, so file it as vegan-friendly.
