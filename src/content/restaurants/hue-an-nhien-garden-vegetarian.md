An Nhien Garden Vegetarian is a garden vegetarian restaurant on the outskirts of Hue, on Tinh lo 10 in Phu Thuong, open daily from 10am to 9:30pm. It is a modern, natural space with a garden setting, and the menu covers bun bo Hue chay, pho chay, goi cuon chay, bun cha chay and pineapple fried rice. It is inexpensive and calm, and it suits a slow lunch away from the tourist streets.

Order the bun bo Hue chay and the goi cuon chay, and ask for the vegan dishes by name, as the kitchen is vegetarian. It is a little out of the centre, so allow time for the ride.
