Cigkoftem is an all-vegetarian Turkish snack bar by the station, rated 4.9 from 96 reviews, serving cigkofte made from bulgur, tomato, herbs and spices, plus falafel. If you stay away from the drinks and desserts, all of the dishes are vegan.

Order the cigkofte and the falafel, and skip the drinks and desserts, since those are the only non-vegan items. It opens Monday to Saturday.
