Gioia is the splurge that visiting vegans put first, and locals agree: a fully plant-based kitchen inside one of the city's grandest hotels, plating small dishes that look like jewellery and taste like a garden party. The Hyatt's own page describes a menu created without animal ingredients, and recent listings show it open daily for lunch and dinner.

Book the patio if the weather behaves, share everything, and do not skip the corn croquettes. This is Buenos Aires showing off, and for once the plants get the palace.
