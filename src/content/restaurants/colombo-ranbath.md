Ranbath tops the list for a reason. Colombo's fully vegan, organic Sri Lankan restaurant: heirloom rice and curry, a juice bar, and buffet-style plates in Havelock Town. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Colombo 5, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Heirloom rice and curry, Gotukola sambol, Woodapple juice if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
