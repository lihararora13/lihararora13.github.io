Por Siempre Vegana is the consensus number one for a reason: these are proper Mexico City street tacos, just made entirely from plants. The al pastor will make you forget meat ever existed, the salsa bar is generous, and there is almost always a happy queue.

Go to the sit-down spot on Manzanillo when you want a table and time to work through the menu, or the nearby stand when you want tacos the classic way, standing up. Either way, order more than you planned. Everyone does.
