La Oveja Negra, the black sheep, is Lavapies at its best: a vegan queer bar and tavern with posters and murals on the walls, breakfast until 1pm, and then tapas and comfort food until late. City Life Madrid and HappyCow both single it out for its diverse, playful menu and welcoming room.

Go for cheesy nachos and croquettes to start, a burger or jackfruit tacos in the middle, and chocolate cake to finish. It is friendly, LGBTQ+ welcoming, and exactly the kind of place you end up staying longer than planned.
