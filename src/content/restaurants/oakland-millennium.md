Millennium is the restaurant that made vegan fine dining a serious idea in America, and its Rockridge home is still the special-occasion pick for the whole Bay Area. The menu changes with the markets, the five-course tasting menu is the move on weekends, and the almond-ricotta gnocchi, buffalo cauliflower and mochi brownie are the dishes people remember.

It is dinner only, Tuesday through Saturday, and reservations are strongly recommended, especially for the patio. Order the tasting menu if you can, ask what is seasonal, and save room for dessert. This is the one to take someone who thinks vegan food cannot be fine dining.
