Prana is Lyon's health-conscious vegan table, and it wears that label with pride: a macrobiotic restaurant using organic and local products, fully vegan since April 2023, cooking French classics revisited for vegans alongside world dishes. The signature is the macrobiotic health plate, a balanced, colourful spread that leaves you feeling better than when you sat down.

It is calm, bright and genuinely nourishing, the kind of lunch that powers an afternoon of walking the city. Order the health plate, ask what is seasonal, and enjoy the rare feeling of a restaurant meal that is both a treat and a reset.
