Tan Fulin Xuan is the restaurant Johor Bahru locals recommend when they want vegetarian food done properly: a Chinese vegetarian room in Taman Sentosa, run by a cook with more than twenty years behind her, specialising in mock meats from chicken to fish and pork, with the mock fish alone cooked steamed with minced ginger, claypot curry or asam style. The menu marks egg with an icon and the kitchen will cook vegan, and the dishes reviewers name are the otak-otak, the monkey head mushroom with sliced ginger, the four-colour mushroom, the crispy roasted duck, the claypot asam mock fish and the spicy and sour soup. The signature dessert is the Mud Pie, an eggless chocolate-coffee-vanilla ice-cream cake.

Its curries are cooked Indian-Chinese style without coconut milk or dairy, so they run spicier than usual, and the Lo Hon Chai claypot is the milder vegan pick. The restaurant moved here from Taman Sri Pelangi, so older guides give the old address; use 54 Jalan Sulam.
