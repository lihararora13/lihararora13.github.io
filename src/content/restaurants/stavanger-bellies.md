Bellies is a cozy plant-based, all-vegan restaurant in an old canning factory on the east side of town, rated 5 on HappyCow and listed in the Michelin Guide, with a wood-fired oven, a vegan tasting menu and pizzas. It is the fine-dining pick on this list.

Order the vegan tasting menu and a pizza, and book ahead, since it opens Wednesday to Saturday from four to ten only.
