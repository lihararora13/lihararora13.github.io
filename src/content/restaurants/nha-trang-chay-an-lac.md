Chay An Lac is a vegetarian restaurant on Ly Thanh Ton in Nha Trang, open daily from 6:30am to 9pm, serving local home-style dishes. It is a neighbourhood kitchen rather than a destination, and it is the most northerly pick in the central cluster.

Order the vegetable and tofu dishes with rice, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is inexpensive and open early, which makes it a good breakfast stop.
