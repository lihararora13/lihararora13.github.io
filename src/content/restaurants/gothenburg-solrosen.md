Solrosen is the oldest purely vegetarian restaurant in Gothenburg, and one of the places in town that has been going longest with the same owners and staff. It sits in picturesque Haga, and the menu is several daily dishes, at least one of which is always vegan and gluten-free, with vegan stew, gratin potatoes and patties among the regulars.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so ask which daily dish is the vegan one. Order the vegan stew, add a local beer, and enjoy a piece of Gothenburg history.
