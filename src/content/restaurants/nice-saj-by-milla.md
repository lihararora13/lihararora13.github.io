SAJ by Milla is a vegetarian Lebanese-inspired restaurant on Rue Droite in Vieux Nice, with vegan variations of its flatbreads and dishes. The menu runs from saj flatbreads and hummus to lentil salads, fruit bowls and a tiramisu cheesecake, with a useful dinner service in the old town.

It is a vegetarian restaurant with vegan options, so tell the staff you are vegan before ordering. Order a saj flatbread and the hummus, and check the opening days, as it is closed on Tuesdays.
