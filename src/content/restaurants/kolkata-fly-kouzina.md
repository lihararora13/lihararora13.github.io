Fly Kouzina made the list for a reason. A big global menu in Salt Lake with Continental plates, Asian bowls and dairy-free starters for vegan diners. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Salt Lake, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Continental plate, Asian bowl, Dairy-free starter if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
