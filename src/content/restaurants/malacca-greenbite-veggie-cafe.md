Greenbite Veggie Cafe is the newest pick in this guide, opened in June 2025 in a cream-and-green shophouse a short walk from Jonker Street, and it is the most convenient vegan-friendly breakfast and lunch in the old town. The menu leans Hainanese: nasi lemak, wan tan mee, vege hakka mushroom pork noodles, curries and noodle soups, plus daily specials and Malaysian kuih on the counter for a travel snack. Vegan Food Quest calls it an excellent option for local veggie food in the centre of the old town and says they will most certainly return.

It is vegetarian with vegan choices, so ask for dishes to be made vegan where they are not already. It is open Thursday to Tuesday, 7:30am to 3:30pm, and closed on Wednesdays, which makes it the early-morning pick in this guide.
