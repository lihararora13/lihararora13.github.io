Ashvita Nirvana made the list for a reason. A quaint lounge-cafe a short walk from Elliot's Beach, with Continental and Asian plates that are vegan or easily made so. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Besant Nagar, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan lemon chocolate cake, Buddha bowl, Asian stir-fry if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
