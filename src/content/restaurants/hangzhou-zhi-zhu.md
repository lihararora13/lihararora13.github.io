Zhi Zhu is the noodle shop at the foot of Lingyin Temple: a tiny shop with a garden and rustic decor, on the 2026 Michelin Bib Gourmand list, serving vegetarian noodles and dumplings. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, and a guide checked on 25 November 2026 confirms the spinach noodles in mushroom broth and the vegetarian potstickers with crispy skirts.

Order the spinach noodles in mushroom broth and the potstickers, and finish with the peach resin and honey locust sweet soup. Note that Michelin lists it as vegetarian rather than vegan, and local guides warn that vegetarian cooking here may include egg, so confirm dishes are egg-free when you order. Open 7am to 3pm and 4:30pm to 8:30pm on weekdays, 7am to 9pm at weekends.
