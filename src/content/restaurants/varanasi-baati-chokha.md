Baati Chokha is the most honest meal on this list. Litti roasted over coals, chokha of roasted brinjal and tomato mashed fresh, sattu parathas, all of it the everyday food of the region, vegan long before anyone thought to call it that.

It is cheap, filling, and deeply Banarasi. Order the baati chokha as it comes, ask them to go easy on the ghee, and you have the city's most grounding lunch.
