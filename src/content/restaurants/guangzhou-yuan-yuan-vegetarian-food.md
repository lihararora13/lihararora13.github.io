Yuan Yuan Vegetarian Food is the temple buffet of Guangzhou: a small Buddhist eatery next to Guangxiao Temple on Jing Hui Road, with a fixed-price all-you-can-eat vegan buffet for around ten to twenty yuan a head, two small floors, and leftover food donated to the homeless. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow from 24 reviews, and the Guangzhou travel guide lists it as a small Buddhist eatery popular with locals.

Come for lunch between 10:30am and 2pm or dinner from 5pm to around 7:20pm, and note that HappyCow's listed hours are wrong; the buffet runs about eight vegetable dishes, rice and two soups, and reviewers say to bring your own container if you want takeaway, as only disposable plates are provided. Find it just to the side of the temple's main entrance.
