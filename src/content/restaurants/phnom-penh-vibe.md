Vibe is Phnom Penh's first 100% vegan cafe, in the cool Russian Market neighbourhood, and it is the place everyone knows: a natural-feeling rooftop with swinging chairs, a popular work spot, a cookbook, and 10 percent of profits going to a foundation feeding schoolchildren.

The Live Sandwich is the dish to order, and the vegan omelette and the Explorer quesadilla with sweetcorn, pickled beets, black beans, cashew crema and guacamole are the other headliners. It is fully vegan, it is open Tuesday to Sunday, and it is the first table to book in the capital.
