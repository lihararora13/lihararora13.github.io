FOOD and bottled is a cafe, restaurant and bar on the Osterberger Reihe, open daily from nine, serving breakfast, a changing lunch menu and cocktails from five. The burritos, burger tacos and rigatoni include vegan options, and it sits on the city's only experience playground, so families are welcome.

Order the breakfast, a burrito and the burger tacos, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan options, since the menu also runs meat and dairy.
