Vida's Plant Based Butcher is Columbus's vegan butcher: a deli on Parsons Avenue doing plant-based deli meats, sandwiches and charcuterie, all of it vegan and all of it made to be taken home or eaten on the spot. It is the most unusual shop on the list, and the one that makes vegan charcuterie a real thing.

It is the pick for the picnic, the party platter, and the friend who misses a good deli. Order a sandwich, build a charcuterie board, and take something home.
