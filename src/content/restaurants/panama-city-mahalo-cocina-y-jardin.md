MAHALO Cocina y Jardin is a garden restaurant on the ground floor of the Hotel Las Clementinas, on Avenida B at Calle 11 in Casco Antiguo, founded in 2016 by Canadian sisters Emma and Brit. The menu goes well beyond salads, with crispy cauliflower, grilled mango with burrata, a falafel bowl, coconut curry and flatbreads, and the vegetarian and vegan options are clearly marked.

Order the beet carpaccio, the gnocchi alla zucca and the falafel bowl, with a smoothie or a watermelon mimosa. It is fairly expensive, open every day from 7:30 am, and worth booking on weekends.
