Junk Soul trades from a street-food container inside SPARK:York, the shipping-container yard on Piccadilly, and does fully vegan junk food without apology: burgers, dirty fries and shakes.

Go for a loaded burger with dirty fries. It is a fun evening stop, especially when SPARK has music or an event on.
