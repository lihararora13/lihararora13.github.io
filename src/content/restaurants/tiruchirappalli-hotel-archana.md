Hotel Archana is the pure-veg hotel restaurant at No 1 Tolgate, named on the Trichy highway veg list for a delightful veg menu that always amazes visitors. It is the tollgate stop, the first veg room for anyone arriving from Chennai and the last for anyone leaving.

Order the masala dosa and the veg meal, and ask for them without ghee or curd. It is the highway pick, and the location is the point.
