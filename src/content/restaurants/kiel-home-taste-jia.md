Home Taste Jia is a fully vegan Taiwanese restaurant on Holstenstraße, Kiel's main shopping street, and it cooks the kind of food that rewards a slow meal: handmade dim sum, ramen in proper broth, and a hot pot on Wednesdays that regulars plan their week around. Everything is plant-based, the dumplings are made by hand, and the room is warm and unpretentious.

It sits right on the Holstenstraße, an easy stop between the harbour and the station. Order the dim sum, add a ramen, and come on a Wednesday if you want the hot pot. This is the table for a proper Taiwanese meal in Kiel, and one of only three fully vegan anchors in the city.
