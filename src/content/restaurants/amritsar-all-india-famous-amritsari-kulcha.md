All India Famous Amritsari Kulcha lives up to the name on Maqbool Road: a top-rated kulcha stop where the stuffed kulcha and chole are the whole point. It is the kind of place where the queue is the review.

Order the Amritsari kulcha with chole, ask them to hold the makhan, and eat it hot off the tandoor. It is breakfast, and it is perfect.
