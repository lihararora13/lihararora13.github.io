Korean Mother's Meals is a small Hintonburg takeout spot serving house-made vegetarian Korean food, with plenty of vegan options. Kimchi, hotteok, noodles and hot and cold dishes change with the season, and everything is made with the care the name promises.

Order online for takeout, with some outdoor seating in good weather. The kimchi and the hotteok are the orders to beat, and the noodles are a close second.
