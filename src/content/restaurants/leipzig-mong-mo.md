Mong Mo is a vegan-friendly Vietnamese restaurant on Könneritzstraße in Plagwitz, and it is the neighbourhood pick in the west. The pho is the draw, with spring rolls and noodles alongside, and the room is relaxed enough for a long lunch.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so confirm the vegan dishes when you order. The pho is the order.
