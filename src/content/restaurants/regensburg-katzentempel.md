Katzentempel Regensburg is a fully vegan all-day restaurant in the heart of the old town, and several temple cats live there, which is the whole point. Breakfast is served all day, alongside lunch, coffee and cake, and a relaxed dinner menu, all of it plant-based, in a bright room with a large window front and a terrace for warm days. The Beyond burger, the planted pulled pork and the vegan tiramisu are the dishes regulars come back for.

It sits on the Obermünsterstraße, open seven days a week from half past nine or half past ten. Come for breakfast at any hour, order the breakfast plate, and stay for cake among the cats.
