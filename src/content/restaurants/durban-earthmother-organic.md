Earthmother Organic is Glenwood's calm corner: an organic cafe and health shop that has been feeding Durban's plant-based crowd for years. The menu leans wholesome without being worthy, with vegan breakfast bagels, wraps, cakes and plant milks, plus a shop full of organic pantry goods to take home. It is the kind of place where regulars linger.

Come for a slow morning rather than a rushed lunch. The Glenwood streets around it are Durban at its most walkable, and the cafe's community feel is half the point.
