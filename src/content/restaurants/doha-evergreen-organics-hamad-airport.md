Evergreen's airport outlet is the rare thing: a fully vegan option airside, in Concourse C opposite gate C. It does grab-and-go wraps, sandwiches, soups, desserts, juices and snack packs, plus jars of nut butter, and it is open round the clock.

Grab a wrap and a juice before your flight, or a snack pack for the plane. It is the best vegan airport food in the Gulf, and it is on this list because travellers need it.
