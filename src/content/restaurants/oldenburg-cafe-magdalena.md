Café Magdalena is a vegetarian café on Kurwickstraße, and it is the calmest stop on Oldenburg's list: homemade cakes, breakfast and soups, with vegan dishes on a wider vegetarian menu. The room is warm and old-town in feel, the cakes are baked in-house, and the kitchen takes its vegan dishes seriously rather than treating them as an afterthought.

It sits a short walk from the Markt, open Tuesday to Saturday. Order a vegan breakfast, add a slice of homemade cake, and take your time over coffee. This is the table for a slow morning in Oldenburg, and the one to pick when you want cake with your coffee.
