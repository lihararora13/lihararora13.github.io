Parada Vegana is Cusco's vegan all-rounder: a small restaurant open since 2021, serving all-day breakfasts with pancakes, scrambled tofu and sandwiches, plus lunch and dinner items including pizza, pastas, lasagna, bun-less bean burgers, soups and desserts. It is the kind of place that covers every craving, and the 5.0 rating says it does them all well.

Come for breakfast or dinner, order pancakes in the morning or a pizza at night, and save room for dessert. This is the one for a big, satisfying vegan meal near the centre.
