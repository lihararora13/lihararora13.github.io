Allah's Kitchen is Rochester's most eclectic all-vegan kitchen: established in 2019, it builds its menu from Jamaican and Indian inspiration, with veggie burgers and plantains alongside bowls that pull from both traditions. It relocated from Jefferson Avenue to Park Point Drive in Henrietta, and the current room is a bright, casual counter with takeout and delivery. The 5.0 HappyCow rating puts it among the city's best-loved vegan spots.

Hours are Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday noon to 7pm, Thursday 1pm to 7pm and Saturday 11am to 7pm, closed Sunday and Monday. Order a bowl, add plantains, and use the address on this page: older guides still list the Jefferson Avenue location.
