Comune is the Short North's vegan restaurant: a bright, modern room on High Street doing seasonal plates, bowls and sandwiches, all of it plant-based and all of it cooked with the seasons in mind. It is the most polished meal on the list after Seitan's Realm, and the one to take a date.

It is the Short North stop, easy to reach and easy to love. Order the seasonal plate, add a bowl, and enjoy the High Street buzz after.
