The Seeds is a hidden alley cafe on Tran Cao Van, shoes off at the door, floor cushions downstairs and a few more tables upstairs, cooking entirely plant-based with little oil. The kitchen covers Vietnamese plates such as vegan chicken rice, cao lau, banh xeo and fresh spring rolls, as well as tempeh salad, falafel and desserts, and it is one of very few places in town with a proper vegan cinnamon roll and chocolate cake. It is rated 4.9 from 377 Google reviews.

Order the vegan chicken rice and a cinnamon roll, and note it is closed on Thursdays and Fridays. It is the most hidden vegan cafe in Hoi An, and it is worth finding.
