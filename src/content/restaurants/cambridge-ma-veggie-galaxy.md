Veggie Galaxy is a vegetarian diner in Central Square with a big, playful menu of breakfast plates, burgers, and shakes, and the kitchen says it can make almost everything vegan. It is the most fun room on this list, and the best place to bring a mixed group of vegans and omnivores.

Order the vegan breakfast plate or the vegan burger with a vegan milkshake. Tell the server you are vegan up front, since some dishes need the cheese or egg swapped.
