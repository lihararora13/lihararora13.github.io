Bangkok Taste is a downtown Thai restaurant on Ionia Avenue Southwest with curries, noodle dishes, and tofu preparations that adapt to vegan orders. It is the evening pick on this list, and the kitchen is used to vegan requests.

Order the green curry with tofu and the pad see ew, asking for the vegan preparation and confirming the fish sauce is omitted. The pin for this entry is address-level, so use the street address when navigating.
