Caoba Farms is a restaurant in the outdoor gardens of an organic farm at the end of 5 Avenida Sur, and all the produce served comes from the farm. The lunch menu features salads, sandwiches and pizza, with several vegan choices in every category, and the breads are made in-house. The farm store sells natural food, including vegan cheese made from almond and pumpkin seed, and a farmers market runs on Saturdays.

Order a farm salad and a vegan pizza, and visit on a Saturday if you can, for the market. It is open Tuesday to Sunday, 8 am to 4 pm, and it is the most expensive lunch on this list.
