Kaf is the sweet heart of Norrebro: a cosy, entirely vegan cafe and bakery where the croissants are flaky, the cheesecake is famous, and nobody can quite believe none of it contains dairy. Its own site promises vegan breakfast, lunch and house-made cakes, and local guides call its pastries unforgettable and fairly priced.

Come for a slow brunch, a cinnamon cruffin and a plant-based cappuccino, then take a cake for later, you will want one. Current listings show it open daily on Birkegade, with outdoor seats in summer and candles and heat lamps when Copenhagen turns chilly.
