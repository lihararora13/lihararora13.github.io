Veggie House is a quiet corner in Gemmayzeh, the hidden gem the guides mention for vegan-friendly salads, wraps and desserts. It is smaller and calmer than the big Lebanese restaurants, which makes it a good lunch stop when Gemmayzeh gets loud.

Order a salad and a wrap, save room for dessert, and enjoy the slower pace. It is the gentle pick on this list.
