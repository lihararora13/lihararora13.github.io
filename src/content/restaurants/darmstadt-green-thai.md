Green Thai is a Thai restaurant on Wilhelminenstrasse, serving curries, noodles and salads with vegan options. It is the Thai pick on this list, sharing a building with L'Osteria at the same number.

Order a vegan curry, the noodles and a salad, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan dishes, since the menu also runs meat and fish sauce.
