Mimoni's is a small food stand in the Machane Yehuda market serving vegan and vegetarian versions of traditional Middle Eastern offerings, like cauliflower shawarma, beet carpaccio, hummus stew and a lentil and wild rice kebab. Everything can be made vegan except the desserts that contain dairy.

It is open Monday to Thursday and Sundays from 11:00am to 11:00pm, Fridays until 4:00pm and Saturdays from 7:00pm. Order the cauliflower shawarma and the lentil kebab.
