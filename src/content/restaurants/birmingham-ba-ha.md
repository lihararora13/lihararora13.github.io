Ba-Ha has really made a name for itself on the Birmingham vegan scene, serving innovative, homemade vegan food from its home at The Rainbow in Digbeth. The menu changes with the seasons and the kitchen's mood, which is exactly why regulars keep coming back: this is cooking that treats plant-based food as a creative starting point rather than a set of restrictions.

Digbeth is Birmingham's creative quarter, and Ba-Ha fits right in. Order whatever the chef's special is, add a burger if it is on, and enjoy one of the city's most inventive kitchens.
