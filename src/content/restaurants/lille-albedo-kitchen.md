Albedo Kitchen is Lille's flagship fully vegan restaurant, on Rue Léon Gambetta in Wazemmes, and it cooks northern France vegan: the headline is vegan moules-frites, the dish every Lille visitor should try once, alongside risotto, couscous and a granola bowl for the morning crowd.

Wazemmes is the city's market quarter, so arrive hungry and walk the market first. Albedo is the table to book for a proper sit-down vegan dinner in Lille.
