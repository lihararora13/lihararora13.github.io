Morfys Vegetariano on Mitre is the vegetarian restaurant whose whole menu can be made vegan except the lasagne. Do not confuse it with a second nearby venue of the same name without the Vegetariano suffix, which serves meat. Its pin is approximate.

Order the Plato vegetariano del día, Tarta vegana, and Ensalada. Ask for the vegan version of the daily plate; skip the lasagne.
