Bansi Vihar is the Patna pure-veg restaurant that locals reach for when the day has been long: a Fraser Road dining room where the Bansi thali, a full plate of everything the kitchen does well, is the default order. Recent reviews from late 2026 call it the simplest cure for Patna tiredness, and the masala dosa and uttapam are the other regulars.

Order the Bansi thali and ask the kitchen to hold the ghee and curd. It is open 8 in the morning to 11 at night, and it is the most-loved pure-veg room in the city centre.
