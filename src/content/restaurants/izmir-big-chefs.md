Big Chefs is an upscale chain restaurant on Ataturk Kultur Caddesi in Alsancak with a dedicated vegan menu page and labelled allergens on all pages. Vegan dishes include schnitzel, mac and cheese, falafel and spaghetti Bolognese.

It is open daily from 11:00am to 11:00pm and takes reservations. Order the vegan schnitzel or the mac and cheese.
