Linglong pairs Sichuan flavours with a modern, upscale dining room, and it is the chicest vegetarian meal in Chengdu. The kitchen does plant-based versions of Sichuan classics with fresh vegetables, tofu and gluten-based proteins.

Order the vegan kung pao chicken, a plate of seasonal vegetables, and something from the mock-meat side of the menu.
