Homemade.mmr is a pure-veg Indian kitchen on Lower Pazundaung Road, with a 100% vegetarian kitchen, daily tiffin service, bulk orders and home delivery, open 7:30am to 6:30pm. The menu covers veg biryani, sweets such as bundi laddu, gulab jamun and kheer, and snacks, and it is the best Indian vegetarian kitchen in Yangon for vegans who ask for dairy-free dishes.

Order the veg biryani and the daily tiffin, and ask for vegan dishes without dairy. It is inexpensive and central, and it suits a takeaway lunch.
