XinAn Vegan is a small, pure fully vegan restaurant in Haizhu, south of the Pearl River, with one of the widest menus in the city: authentic dumplings and noodles, plus Western food such as hamburgers, pizza and croissants, and oat brown sugar tea. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow from 24 reviews and is listed in the Guangzhou vegetarian guide for foreign travellers.

Order the dumplings and handmade noodles, or a burger and a croissant from the Western side. It is a tiny spot with two tables and limited seating, so call ahead to reserve a place, and note that one older review found the food mostly reheated and pricey; the menu is on WeChat. Open daily 11am to 8pm.
