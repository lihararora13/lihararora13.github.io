Cinnaholic is an all-vegan cinnamon roll bakery on South Northshore Drive in West Knoxville, doing build-your-own rolls with a long list of frostings and toppings. It is the sweet anchor of this list, and the only all-vegan restaurant on it.

Order a cinnamon roll with vanilla frosting and one with maple frosting, and add a brownie. It is a drive from downtown, so plan it as a west-side stop.
