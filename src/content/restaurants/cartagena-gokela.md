Gokela is one of the most established names in Cartagena for health-oriented, customisable food: wraps, bowls, salads and plant proteins that let vegetarian and vegan diners build a complete meal rather than ordering side dishes. It is especially useful for casual meals, takeout, and anyone staying in Bocagrande.

Build a bowl with lentil or tofu protein, add the coconut rice after confirming it was made without fish stock, and you have a fast, cheap, reliable vegan meal. It is not a destination restaurant, and it does not need to be.
