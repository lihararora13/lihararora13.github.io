Flower Burger is the vegan burger chain famous for its rainbow buns, and its Bari branch on Via Principe Amedeo is the city's only all-vegan restaurant. It is fully vegan, informal and reliable, with colourful burgers, panini, nuggets and pasta.

Order the colourful house burgers and the nuggets, and come when you want something fast, filling and certain.
