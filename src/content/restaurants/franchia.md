Franchia has been quietly running one of the most beloved all-vegan Korean
menus in the country for years, and it shows in every plate. The crispy
chili tofu has a loyal following, the kimbap is textbook, and the sizzling
bulgogi plates arrive hissing like a fireworks show. An entire page of
Korean teas finishes the job.

The room feels like a calm garden break in the middle of Midtown, which
makes it the rare vegan spot that works for a power lunch, a quiet date, or
dragging your non-vegan friends somewhere they will actually thank you for.
