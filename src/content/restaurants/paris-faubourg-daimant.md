Faubourg Daimant is where Paris eats its vegetables like they are the
main event. Chef Alice Tuyet's all-vegan bistronomy plays with bold
flavors, a seasonal menu, and a curated wine list, plus herb-based
mocktails for the non-drinkers at the table.

Tuyet now runs three Paris addresses, which tells you something about
how well this concept landed. Book ahead, the room is intimate and the
regulars are loyal.
