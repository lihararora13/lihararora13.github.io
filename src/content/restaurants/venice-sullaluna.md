Sullaluna is a bookshop with a bistro attached on the Fondamenta de la Misericordia in Cannaregio, and the canalside tables are the reason to time your visit for late afternoon. It is a vegetarian kitchen, so the vegan dishes are a proper subset done well: falafel, hummus, puccia, crostini and salads built on local produce, with vegan wine on the list.

Order the falafel and hummus, the puccia and the crostini, and ask which cakes are vegan on the day, as the baking changes and honey turns up in unexpected places. Dairy, eggs and honey appear elsewhere on the menu, so order from the vegan side deliberately.
