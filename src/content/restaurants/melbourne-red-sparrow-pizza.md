Open since 2017, Red Sparrow proved that vegan pizza isn't a compromise, it's the main event. Blistered wood-fired crusts, melty house-made cheeses, and more than a dozen creative pies mean the hardest part of your night is choosing.

The rooftop bar is made for long summer evenings. Grab a Patatas Bravas pie, a cold vegan lager, and watch the sun go down over Smith Street like the absolute genius you are.
