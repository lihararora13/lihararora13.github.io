Asa Vegan Kitchen and Studio is the gentle start to a Chiang Mai day. Open since 2018 in a quiet lane off Thapae Road, it serves Western and Thai dishes side by side, burgers and sandwiches, curries and stir-fries, noodle dishes and breakfast bowls, with hot and iced coffee, teas and smoothies. The kitchen leans on locally sourced, sustainable ingredients, and there is extra seating and a yoga studio upstairs.

It is a calm, unhurried room near the eastern moat, the kind of place you end up returning to every morning without planning to. Good coffee, honest food, no rush.
