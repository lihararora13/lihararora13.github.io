Sai Krushna sits in the Urmee Complex on NH-5 at Rasulgarh, which makes it the natural stop for anyone arriving or leaving Bhubaneswar by road. It is a big air-conditioned pure-veg dining room with a broad menu: South Indian, North Indian, Indo-Chinese, plus a salad and juice bar.

The masala dosa is the safe bet, and the Indo-Chinese plates veganise well if you ask for no butter and no paneer. HappyCow lists it as vegan-friendly, and it is open from late morning to late evening every day.
