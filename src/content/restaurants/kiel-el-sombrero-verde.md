El Sombrero Verde, Verde for short, is Kiel's vegetarian Tex-Mex restaurant, and it has been feeding the city for years: burritos, tacos and nachos in a colourful dining room a short walk from the harbour. Everything on the menu can be ordered vegan, which is the point of including it here, but the kitchen is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, and cheese and sour cream are the defaults, so say vegan clearly when you order.

It sits on Kleiner Kuhberg, open late on weekends. Order a vegan burrito, add tacos to share, and ask for the vegan version of whatever catches your eye. This is the table for Tex-Mex in Kiel, and a good late-night option.
