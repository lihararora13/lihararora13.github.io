Ubuntu Eat is the top-rated vegan restaurant in Phuket, a perfect 5.0 from 180 HappyCow reviews, and it is Phuket's first fully vegan Indian restaurant, launched on 7 July 2024 at 7 past 7 in the evening. The menu is big and crowd-pleasing: vegan paneer, biryani, sausages, shawarma, crispy prawns, masala, raita, lababdar and roti, plus a Thai menu with curry, tom yam and khao soi, and smoothie bowls. It also has a branch in Kolkata and a global community behind it, and it delivers on Food Panda and Grab.

It sits on Phangnga Road in Phuket Town, the old town's easiest area for a vegan crawl. It is closed on Thursdays, so plan around that, and it is open Monday to Wednesday and Friday to Sunday, 11:30am to 9:30pm.
