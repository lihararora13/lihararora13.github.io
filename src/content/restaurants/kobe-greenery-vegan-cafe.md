Greenery Vegan Cafe sits on Kaigandori between Kobe and Motomachi stations, a small green-tabled room with a flagstone patio and a menu built on superfoods: nine smoothie bowls, including the famous Mermaid Lagoon and the acai bowl, plus vegan scones in flavours like pineapple and coconut and cashew and white chocolate, fruit salads and savoury sandwiches. Everything is vegan, and reviewers praise the artful smoothies, the friendly owner and the lavender oat-milk latte.

Come for breakfast or a light lunch, order the Mermaid Lagoon, and expect a short wait at busy times: the room is small and the bowls are made to order.
