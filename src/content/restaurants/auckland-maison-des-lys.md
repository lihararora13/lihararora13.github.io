Maison des Lys does the thing people said could not be done: real French patisserie, laminated, flaky and glossy, made entirely from plants. Croissants in plain, almond, Biscoff and matcha, pain au chocolat, macarons and chocolate mousse entremets, with coffee poured only with plant milks.

The catch is the hours: Thursday and Friday mornings, and weekend mornings, and then it is gone until next week, often with a queue and sell-outs. Treat it like a farmers market for butter-free butter pastry and arrive early.
