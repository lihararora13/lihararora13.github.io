The Hare Krishna buffet is Monterrey's cheapest proper vegan-friendly lunch: a set menu of soup, salad, main, juice and dessert, served in a calm room in Centro. It is simple, wholesome food, and the price is hard to beat.

Go for lunch, eat the set menu, and browse the little shop. It is the most practical, generous meal on this list.
