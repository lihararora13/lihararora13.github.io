Dreizehn is the most useful table in Augsburg: a fully vegan café in the old town that is open every day from nine in the morning to ten at night, covering breakfast, lunch and dinner in one room. The menu moves through the day, overnight oats and brunch dishes in the morning, curry at lunch, cakes and Apfelstrudel in the afternoon, and it has the easy, lived-in feel of a neighbourhood café rather than a concept. Regulars rate it five stars, and the room earns it.

It sits on the Barfüßerstraße, a short walk from the Rathaus. Come for breakfast, stay for the curry, and treat it as the default stop in Augsburg, the place that is always open and always vegan.
