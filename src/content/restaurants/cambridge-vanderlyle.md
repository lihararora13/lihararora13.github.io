Vanderlyle is Cambridge's fine-dining vegan pick: an intimate, Michelin-selected restaurant on Mill Road with a seasonal ten-course vegetable tasting menu, dishes like carrot tartare, miso parfait with cherries and courgette with beurre blanc, and a relaxed, sustainable dining room with thoughtful drinks pairings.

It is a vegetarian menu that cooks vegan when you ask at booking, so file it as vegan-friendly and book ahead, mentioning vegan when you reserve. It is the most expensive table on this list and the most special, the place for a celebration rather than a quick lunch.
