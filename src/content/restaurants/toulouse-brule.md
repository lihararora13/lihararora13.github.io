BRULE, also written Cafe Brule, is a vegan coffee shop at Place Saint-Georges, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, with many homemade pastries, a couple of options for lunch and brunch during the weekend. It is one of the most popular vegan spots in Toulouse, and the pastries are the reason to come.

It has limited opening hours, so check before you go, and note that it closes for holidays, as one recent visitor found. Order the pastries and a coffee, and come early.
