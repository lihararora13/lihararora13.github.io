Glo is technically a health food restaurant rather than strictly vegan, but the menu is overwhelmingly plant-based, and it is widely regarded as one of the best lunch options in Reykjavik for all dietary preferences. The Engjateigur restaurant serves a wide variety of vegan and gluten-free plates, bowls and desserts, and there is a second location by the Old Harbour too.

It is the healthy-lunch pick on this list. Order a vegan bowl, add a raw cake, and feel virtuous without feeling deprived.
