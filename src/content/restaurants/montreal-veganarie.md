Veganarie is proof that the suburbs sometimes hold the city's best-kept secret. This family-owned, 100% vegan Greek comfort counter in Pierrefonds-Roxboro tops Montreal's vegan ratings charts with gyros, souvlaki skewers, spanakopita, dolmadakia, and a tzatziki that reviewers keep calling the best they have ever had, dairy version included. The owner is a fixture at the counter and a sweetheart, according to basically everyone.

The menu reads like a tour of guilty pleasures: fried chick'n poutine with cheezy mac, vegan filet-o-fish, bang-bang shrimp, mozzarella cheeze sticks, and baklava made with apple honey. Prices stay friendly, the room is a cozy 20 seats, and takeout runs hot. It is a bit of a metro ride from downtown, but this is the rare comfort-food spot worth the trip out west.
