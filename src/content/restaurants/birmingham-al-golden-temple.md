Golden Temple is a natural grocery and cafe in Five Points South, part health-food store and part lunch counter. The cafe serves vegetarian and vegan plates, smoothies, and baked goods, and the grocery aisles make it a useful stop for stocking up as well as eating.

Order the vegan plate of the day and a smoothie. It is a daytime stop, so plan it for lunch rather than dinner.
