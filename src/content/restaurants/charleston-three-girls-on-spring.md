Three Girls on Spring is Charleston's only fully vegan kitchen, a plant-based Italian delicatessen run by a mother and her two daughters, who came from Connecticut with some 800 family recipes. The lasagna is the dish people cross town for, the chicken parmigiana sandwich is the lunch order, and the tiramisu, big enough to share, is the dessert.

It opened in February 2024 and is already the first table to book in the city. Order the lasagna, add the focaccia, and save room for the tiramisu.
