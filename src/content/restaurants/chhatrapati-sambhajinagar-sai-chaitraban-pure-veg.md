Sai Chaitraban sits out on the Mumbai-Nagpur Highway at Karodi, a pure-veg highway restaurant that travellers plan their drives around. The draw is the Shevaga Handi, a drumstick curry that is the signature dish of the region, and the reason this guide stretches out to the highway at all.

Order the handi with bhakri, add a dal tadka without ghee, and you have a properly Maharashtrian meal. It is the most local dish on this list, and the best reason to leave the city centre for dinner.
