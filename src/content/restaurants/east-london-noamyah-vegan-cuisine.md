Noamyah Vegan Cuisine is a vegan caterer and restaurant in Mdantsane, open daily, serving plant-based meals rooted in local cooking. It is a community operation rather than a tourist restaurant, and it is one of only two fully vegan kitchens in the city.

Call ahead and ask what the plate of the day is, then order it. It is best approached as a caterer that also feeds walk-ins, so a phone call first is wise.
