L'Aubergine has been Cairo's vegetarian standby longer than anyone, a Zamalek institution with a broad vegetarian and vegan menu and a lively bar upstairs. It is the reliable choice: the kitchen knows vegan cooking inside out, the room is warm, and the location on leafy Zamalek is one of the prettiest in the city.

Order a vegan mezze plate to start, a vegetable main, and save room for dessert. Book on Thursday and Friday nights, when Cairo goes out.
