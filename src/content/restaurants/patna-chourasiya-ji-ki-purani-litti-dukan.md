Litti chokha is Bihar's gift to plant-based eating: roasted sattu-stuffed wheat balls with mashed brinjal, tomato and potato, and Chourasiya Ji's old shop on New Market Station Road is one of the places Patna goes for it. The shop sits on the first floor by pillar number six, open 11 to 7, and it does one thing well.

Order the litti chokha and make sure the litti is roasted without ghee brushed on top. It is the most authentically vegan meal on this list, and it costs almost nothing.
