Simple Plant is a garden kitchen and artspace in Bantul, south of the city, and it is the most committed vegan kitchen in Yogyakarta: all vegan, no palm oil, no MSG, with homemade sauces, dips and nice creams. The menu runs from rendang and sate to pempek and sambal matah, cooked in a garden setting.

Order the rendang and the sate, and save room for a nice cream. It keeps short hours, Thursday to Sunday from 1pm to 9pm, so plan around them, and expect a quiet, art-filled room rather than a busy cafe.
