Bean is where Charlotte learned to eat vegan, and it has barely needed to change the formula since 2012. The menu is a love letter to Southern comfort food rebuilt from plants: BBQ, bratwurst, Reubens, meatloaf, crab cakes and pimento cheese, with about a dozen burgers and sandwiches and a little grocery market tucked inside. The Cowboy Burger and the fried pickles are the orders regulars swear by, and the mac and cheese is the side that converts sceptics.

Weekend brunch is the other reason to come: crisp waffles, rich tofu scrambles and a room full of families who have been coming for years. It is not a first-date spot, hands get messy, and that is exactly the point.
