Back Forty Beer Company is an Avondale brewery with a big taproom and a kitchen serving pub food with vegan options. It is the best evening stop on this list, pairing house-brewed beer with plant-based plates in a room built for groups.

Order the vegan burger with a flight of house beers. Check the kitchen board for the vegan special, since it rotates with the season.
