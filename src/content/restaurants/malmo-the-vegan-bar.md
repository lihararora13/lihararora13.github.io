The Vegan Bar is Malmö's fully vegan burger bar, on Södra Skolgatan in Möllevången, and it is the fast-food flagship on this page. The mustard and mayo burger is the signature, with sweet potato fries on the side, all of it plant-based.

It is a small room with a short menu, done properly. Order the burger, add the fries, and you have the measure of the place.
