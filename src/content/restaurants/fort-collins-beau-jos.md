Beau Jo's is Fort Collins' Colorado-style pizza: a restaurant on North College Avenue in Old Town serving thick, honey-sweetened crusts with vegan cheese, sauces and plant proteins. It is the second pizza pick in this guide after Slyce, and it is the one to choose for a heartier, Colorado-style pie. It is open Sunday to Thursday 11am to 9pm and Friday to Saturday 11am to 9:30pm.

Order a vegan cheese pizza on a whole wheat crust, and add plant-based pepperoni. Ask the staff to confirm the vegan preparation: the crusts and sauces vary.
