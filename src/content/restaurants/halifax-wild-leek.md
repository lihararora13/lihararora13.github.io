Wild Leek has been Halifax's 100% vegan comfort-food spot since May 2013, a small West End café and gluten-free bakery cooking seasonal, local food. The Wild Bowl with tofu, rice and vegetables is the signature, the seitan donair wrap is the Halifax classic, and the Dragon fries and the tofu Nomlette at brunch are the other orders to make.

Seating is limited and the Saturday brunch gets crowded, so call ahead or plan on take-out. Fresh-pressed juices, smoothies and rainbow cupcakes round out the menu.
