El Árbol is the health-oriented café in García Ginerés, a residential neighbourhood north-west of Centro, with vegan dishes among a health-food menu. It is the quietest pick in the guide, a neighbourhood spot rather than a destination restaurant, and it rounds out the Mérida list for anyone staying on the north side of the city.

Go for a bowl or a breakfast, and treat it as a café meal rather than a full dinner.
