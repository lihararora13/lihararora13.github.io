Grumpy & Runt is the little deli Cape Town refuses to let die, and thank goodness for that. After earlier closures, it reopened on Wale Street next to Honest Chocolate, and HappyCow reviewers in 2026 are still calling its donuts the best vegan donuts they have ever had, with a perfect 5.0 rating to match.

Go early, because the donuts sell out. Order a sando, grab a huge donut in whatever wild flavour the week brought, and take your coffee through to the Honest Chocolate side to sit down. Small shop, big heart, zero animal products.
