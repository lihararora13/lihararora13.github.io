EDO is in the Michelin Guide and runs a dedicated vegan menu it has been refining since 2019. The kitchen is wood-fire-led and the format is small-plates-to-share, so a vegan group can order across the whole menu rather than picking around a meat-heavy card.

The falafel fritters, the confit butternut squash with vegan cheese and almonds, and the potato gnocchi with wild mushrooms and truffle are recurring favourites. It is the priciest table on this list and the most refined, so book it for the special night of a Belfast trip.
