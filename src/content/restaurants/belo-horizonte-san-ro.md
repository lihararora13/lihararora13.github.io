The 5.0 HappyCow rating tells you the regulars love it, and the Savassi location puts you in the middle of BH's prettiest neighbourhood.

Come for lunch, ask for the vegan-labelled dishes, and fill your plate. This is the one for a generous Chinese buffet lunch.
