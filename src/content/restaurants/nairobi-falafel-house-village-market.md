The Village Market branch brings Falafel House to Gigiri's mall food court, and it is rated a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow. The menu covers falafel, hummus, sweet potato fries, tabbouleh, avocado salad, burgers, breakfast and desserts, with vegan dishes clearly in the mix. It is the easiest vegan lunch in the Gigiri area.

Good for a mall day or a break between errands: grab a falafel plate, add sweet potato fries, and finish with something from the dessert list.
