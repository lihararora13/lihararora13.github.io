KUKI is TTDI's quiet showpiece: you follow a little garden path into Menara KEN and find a serene, upscale vegan Japanese dining room built around shojin ryori, the Buddhist temple tradition of cooking plants with total attention. A July 2026 review still found the dishes great and the room lovely, and HappyCow gives it a perfect score.

Book the set menu if you can, five courses for a gentle tour or eight for the full journey, and let the handmade noodles and pressed sushi do the talking. It is the KL vegan dinner to dress up for, Mondays excepted.
