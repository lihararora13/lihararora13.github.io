Chickpeas is Ottawa's top-rated vegan spot, and it keeps things simple: falafel and hummus, done properly, in sandwiches and salads. The falafel is crisp outside and fluffy inside, the hummus is smooth, and the waffles are an unexpected bonus.

It is a small, friendly takeout spot, and the five-star HappyCow rating is well earned. Order a falafel sandwich, add a hummus plate to share, and you will understand the fuss.
