Beyond Sushi has been rolling since the mid-2010s and basically built the
NYC vegan-sushi playbook: rice rolls layered with roasted sweet potato,
shiitake, avocado, and pickled mango, all wrapped in nori and good vibes.
With several locations across Manhattan, it is the easiest win on this list
when you just need excellent sushi-adjacent comfort fast.

The shiitake truffle dumplings are the sleeper hit regulars swear by.
Affordable, fast, and fun, this is your weeknight-hero pick.
