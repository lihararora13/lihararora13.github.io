Mr. Arancino is an arancino bar in the Borgo district with a proper vegan menu, and the street-food pick on this list. A September 2025 vegan tour found vegan mozzarella in carrozza, arancini and sfincione for 17.90 euros, plus a vegan pistachio cannoli, and a November 2026 tour found a mushroom-filled arancino with the vegan menu on display.

Order the mushroom arancino, the vegan mozzarella in carrozza, the sfincione and the pistachio cannoli. It is a counter, not a dining room, so eat standing or take away.
