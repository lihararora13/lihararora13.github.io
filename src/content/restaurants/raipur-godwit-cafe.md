Godwit is the café Raipur's food lovers argue about, which is usually a sign it matters. It is pure vegetarian, famous for its coffee and desserts, and unusually adventurous for a tier-two city café: sushi, dim sum, continental mains and Asian plates all share the menu. Local guides put it at the top of the city's pure-veg fine-dining lists, and the dining room stays busy for it.

Vegans should steer toward the sushi, dim sum and vegetable-led continental dishes, and ask about the desserts rather than assuming. Come for the buzz, stay for the coffee, and book a table if you are visiting on a weekend.
