Murray Hillbilly is an all-vegan Southern comfort restaurant on Edgewood Avenue South in Murray Hill, run as a local vegan establishment with a back patio and live music on select nights. The menu is plant-based comfort: buffalo ranch chickun sandwiches, wingz, shrimp po boys, and mac and cheese, all vegan by default.

Order the Buffalo Ranch Chickun Sandwich and the wingz with Carolina tangy gold sauce. Note the hours: closed Mondays, with a shorter Tuesday service.
