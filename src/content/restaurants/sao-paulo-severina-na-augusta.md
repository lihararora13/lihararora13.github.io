Severina na Augusta won visiting hearts with one thing first: the waffles. Crispy outside, chewy inside, absolutely smothered in sauces and ice cream, with the peanut and chocolate combo singled out as heavenly.

It is now a Thursday to Sunday spot, so save it for a weekend-leaning brunch on Rua Augusta. Order the savoury tofu breakfast too, it comes with sweet waffles on the side, because of course it does.
