Agadir Burger is a burger place on David Pinski Street in Central Carmel with a dedicated, house-made vegan burger made from mushrooms and soy protein. Vegan burgers are handled in a separate workspace and fried on a separate grill, vegan cheese is available, and the fries are vegan, though the onion rings are not.

It is open daily from noon to midnight, later on Thursdays and Fridays. Order the house-made vegan burger with fries, and note that the order may arrive with packets of non-vegan sauces, so check before using them.
