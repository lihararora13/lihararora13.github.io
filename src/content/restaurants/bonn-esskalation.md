ESSKALATION is Bonn's fully plant-based café, tucked into Poppelsdorf on Clemens-August-Straße, and its weekend brunch is the city's best excuse to sleep in and then eat well. The brunch menu runs à la carte rather than buffet: scrambled tofu, a plant-based Full English, French toast and pancakes alongside breakfast platters, with flammkuchen, bánh mì, mezze and bao burgers on the regular menu and proper speciality coffee throughout.

Brunch runs Saturday and Sunday from half past nine until two, so book ahead for the weekend rush. Weekday lunches are quieter, and Thursday to Saturday evenings stretch until ten or half past ten. This is the table for a slow Bonn morning.
