Kong sits at the top of HappyCow's Lisbon list with a 5.0 rating and more than 1,300 reviews, and VegNews and VeganHaven both put it first too, for good reason: this is Portuguese comfort food, lovingly veganised and made entirely in house, from the breads and cheeses to the ice creams. The francesinha, the bitoque and the bacalhau com natas all land with real swagger.

Come for a celebratory dinner and do not rush it. Reviewers in August and September 2026 were still raving about the croquettes and the seitan ribs, the room is cosy and classy without being stuffy, and there is even a little play corner for kids. Book ahead at weekends, it fills up fast.
