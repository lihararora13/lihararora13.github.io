SOIL is where a Brazilian chef's bold instincts meet Dutch gezelligheid. Crafted by chef Gustavo Bottino, the menu is a fusion feast of big flavors: mushroom bitterballen, laksa ramen, bulgogi cheese steak, and vegan cod fish balls that have won over skeptics by the dozen. Prices stay friendly at around 20 EUR a head.

The Oud-West location is cozy, dog-friendly, and buzzing with energy, with cold artisanal beer and natural wine on tap. Note that the Javastraat east branch has permanently closed, so head to Bilderdijkstraat for the real deal.
