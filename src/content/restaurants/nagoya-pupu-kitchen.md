Pupu Kitchen is the health-food-store cafe of the list: an entirely vegan, macrobiotic menu with a Hawaiian flair, served for lunch only inside a health food shop near Nagoya Castle.

Go for lunch, browse the shop, and enjoy one of the calmest meals in Nagoya. The macrobiotic lunch plate is the pick.
