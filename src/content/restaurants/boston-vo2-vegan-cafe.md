VO2 Vegan Cafe is the little Mass Ave cafe that could. Owner Mimi Loureiro has been slinging vegan comfort food here for years, calling it "carnivore-tested" because the goal was never just to feed vegans, it was to make food anyone would love. The chuna melt, the vegan New England clam chowder, and the legendary tofu breakfast sandwich suggest the mission worked.

It is small, friendly, and open weekdays, the kind of place where regulars plan their yoga-and-lunch days around it and visitors leave five-star reviews calling it the best vegan food in Mass. Grab a smoothie, snag a brownie, and try not to become a regular.
