Goc Da is a modern Vietnamese restaurant on Sonnenberger Strasse with a large range of creative vegan dishes and a separate vegan menu. The choices include Vietnamese crepes, fried oyster mushrooms, curries and pho, and it is rated 4 on HappyCow.

Order the Vietnamese crepes, the fried oyster mushrooms and a curry or pho, and ask for the separate vegan menu, which the kitchen keeps apart from the meat dishes.
