Cafe Harvest has been feeding Vienna's vegans for years from its retro, cozy room by Karmelitermarkt, and it still feels like the neighbourhood's living room. The menu reimagines Austrian comfort food, cheese spaetzle, goulash, soy schnitzel, club sandwiches, alongside burgers and homemade lemonades and cakes, everything 100 percent plant-based, organic and fair-trade where possible.

Time your visit for the weekend All Vegan Brunch buffet if you can: tofu scramble, spreads, hot dishes and cakes, popular with vegans and meat-eaters alike. On weekdays it opens in the afternoon, so plan it as a late lunch or dinner stop after the market.
