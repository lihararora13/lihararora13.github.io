Avant Garden has been one of the city's best destinations for upscale
plant-based dining since 2015. Velvet banquettes, moody lighting, and a
menu of seasonal vegan dishes that the Michelin Guide once summed up as
"excellent food that just happens to be vegan." The toast dishes and
seasonal pastas are the move, with natural wines and craft cocktails to
match.

It is the special-occasion anchor of the NYC list. Book ahead, dress up a
little, and let the kitchen show off.
