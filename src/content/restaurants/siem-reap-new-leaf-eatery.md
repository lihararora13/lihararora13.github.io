New Leaf Eatery is a social-enterprise restaurant a few minutes from Pub Street, and it donates 20 percent of its profits to a selected charity partner each year, supporting education in Siem Reap Province. The menu runs from brunch to dinner, with contemporary options like Beyond Meat burgers alongside international and Khmer classics.

Order the Beyond Meat burger and a beet-chip salad, and come for brunch or dinner rather than a late night, as the kitchen closes earlier than Pub Street. It is a vegetarian-friendly spot with extensive vegan options, so file it as vegan-friendly.
