The Good Food is a newly opened pure-veg outlet in Lalpur, on the first floor of Aikat Arcade on Circular Road, specialising in Indian and Chinese cuisine. Its early reviewers rate it 4.8, which is the highest on this list, and the Indo-Chinese plates are the reason.

Order the veg Manchurian or the hakka noodles, ask for no paneer, and you have a quick, cheap, plant-based lunch. It is open noon to 10:30 daily.
