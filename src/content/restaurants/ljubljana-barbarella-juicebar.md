Barbarella is Ljubljana's bright, buzzy vegan juice bar, right on Slovenska cesta in the middle of everything. Savoury bowls, salads, vegetable soups, smoothies and acai bowls, plus matcha and turmeric lattes for the slow mornings.

It is the easiest healthy breakfast or light lunch in the centre. Order a smoothie bowl, grab a juice to go, and use it as the refuelling stop between sightseeing and dinner.
