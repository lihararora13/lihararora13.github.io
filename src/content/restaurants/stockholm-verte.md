Verte sits in the heart of Gamla Stan, a few steps from the cathedral, cooking vegetables first and everything else second: the menu is predominantly vegan, with a small number of lacto-ovo vegetarian dishes, which is why it lands here as the guide's vegan-friendly pick. A Day In's Stockholm guide praises its seasonal cooking, food-waste focus and thoughtful drinks list of craft beers and organic wines.

Choose the set menu for the full tour or graze the buffet, where vegan Swedish meatballs, dumplings and lasagne show up alongside global flavours. Indoor tables plus summer outdoor seating make it an easy Old Town dinner.
