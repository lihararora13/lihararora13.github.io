Mad Mad Vegan sits at the top of HappyCow's Madrid list with a perfect score and hundreds of reviews, and it earns it the fun way: big, messy, proudly vegan fast food in the heart of Chueca. Burgers are the headline, with tacos, currywurst, nachos and nuggets piling in behind them.

Come hungry and do not skip dessert. The brownie and tiramisu finish things properly, the craft beer is made for the house, and there is a second spot in Lavapies if Chueca is rammed, which it often is. Open daily from lunchtime until midnight.
