Kaidi Kitchen made the list for a reason. A pure-veg kitchen in Phool Bagan doing Indo-Chinese and Pan-Asian plates that veganise happily. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Phool Bagan, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Veg fried rice, Chilli tofu, Hakka noodles if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
