Johnson's Cafe on Circuit House Road is the sit-down institution of Manali: a calm garden setting, good for older couples and long lunches, and famous for its apple crumble. It is the table the travel guides send you to when the lane cafes feel too hectic.

The apple crumble is the order, and the veg sandwich and pasta are the savoury backup. Ask for the vegan preparation and the kitchen will sort you out.
