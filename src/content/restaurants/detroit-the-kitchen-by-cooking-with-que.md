The Kitchen by Cooking with Que is New Center's health and wellness hub: an event space and restaurant created by Quiana Que Rice, featured on season 18 of The Great Food Truck Race, doing plant-based plates, smoothies and bowls with a focus on food as fuel. It is the most wellness-forward stop on the list, and the one to hit when you want to feel good after lunch.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, but the plant-based plates are the point. Order a bowl, add a smoothie, and enjoy the Woodward Avenue buzz.
