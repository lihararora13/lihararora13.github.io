The Gator Club made the list for a reason. A 100% vegan cafe in College Park, home of Chlo's Vegan Club, wholesaling vegan goodies across Adelaide and baking for weddings. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in College Park, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan pastry, Loaded toast, Oat latte if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
