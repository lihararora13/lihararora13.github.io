Le's Cyclo is Potsdam's Vietnamese street-food room, a warm and inviting space on Lindenstrasse with bamboo screens, paper lanterns and wooden tables. The vegan dishes are clearly marked on the menu, and the signature is a surprise vegan platter where the chef selects a range of small vegan dishes for two people to share, with vegan desserts to finish. It is open Tuesday to Sunday from noon to ten.

Order the surprise platter if there are two of you, it is the best way to taste the kitchen, and add fresh spring rolls to start. This is the pick for a shared, unhurried Vietnamese dinner in the city centre.
