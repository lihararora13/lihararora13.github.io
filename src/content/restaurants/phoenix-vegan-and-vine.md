Vegan & Vine brings a touch of elegance to Phoenix's vegan scene without ever feeling stuffy. The Moon Valley cafe and wine bar covers the whole day, from breakfast and lunch through dinner and weekend brunch, with a menu built around comfort done properly: buffalo cauliflower with real bite, a portobello burger, creamy mac and cheese, and chef-made cheeses that show up across the menu. The wine list is the other half of the name, and it earns it.

It is the table to book when the occasion calls for something a little nicer than a taco stand but just as plant-based. Come for brunch, linger over a glass, and let the mac and cheese do the talking.
