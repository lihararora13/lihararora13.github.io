Govindas Vegan is Concepción's all-vegan comedor, a small dining room on Cochrane where the tofu burgers, seitan asado, empanadas and sopaipillas are all plant-based.

Order the Hamburguesas de tofu, Asado de seitan, Empanadas veganas, and Sopaipillas. The seitan asado and the tofu burger are the two plates to try.
