Mishhh is Raipur's big, friendly new pure-veg restaurant, all woven dome booths, warm pendant lights and a kids' play zone that keeps families lingering. The menu is enormous: continental starters, pizzas, pastas, Chinese dishes, tandoori starters and a long Indian main-course list, with Jain-marked dishes called out on the menu.

Service can be slow when it is busy, say the reviews, so come unhurried. Order the farm-fresh veg pizza without cheese, the veg handi and the pav bhaji, and you have a proper family feast at a fair price.
