Casa Azul is a bright, family-run taqueria on Allens Avenue with a whole Verde menu of vegan dishes. Vegan chorizo tacos, jackfruit tinga, potato flautas, a huge vegan wet burrito and vegan horchata sit alongside the regular menu, and the birria tacos have their own cult following.

Reviewers call the vegan tacos a revelation and the tamarind margarita the best in Rhode Island. Order the chorizo tacos and the wet burrito to share, and do not skip the horchata.
