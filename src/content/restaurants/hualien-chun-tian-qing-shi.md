Chun Tian Qing Shi is a vegetarian restaurant on Dean 1st Street offering hotpot, salads and veggie burgers. It is a Western-leaning vegetarian kitchen, and the vegan dishes need asking for, as the menu includes egg and dairy.

Order the veggie burger and a salad, and ask which hotpot broth is vegan. It is a useful change from the Taiwanese buffets, and it is open for lunch and dinner.
