Hans im Glück is a German burger chain, and its Oldenburg branch on the Lange Straße is the reliable vegan-friendly burger in the city centre: vegan burgers, fries and cocktails, with a proper vegan range rather than a token patty. It is a chain, and it feels like one, but the vegan offering is serious, and the Lange Straße location makes it the easiest burger in Oldenburg.

It sits right in the middle of the old town's main street. Order a vegan burger with fries, add a cocktail if you are in the mood, and enjoy a menu where the vegan section is a real section. This is the table for a burger in Oldenburg when you want something reliable.
