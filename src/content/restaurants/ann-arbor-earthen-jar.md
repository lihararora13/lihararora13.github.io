Earthen Jar is a mostly vegan, all-vegetarian buffet on South 5th Avenue, priced by the pound, and it is the cheapest hot meal on this list. The chana masala and saag dal are the staples, the samosas are the add-on, and the whole thing is fast, filling, and genuinely good.

It keeps short hours, Tuesday to Saturday until 7pm. Go hungry, build a plate, and pay by weight.
