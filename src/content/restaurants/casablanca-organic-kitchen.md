Organic Kitchen is an organic whole-food restaurant in Gauthier with plenty of vegan and gluten-free options, a small shop inside selling organic products and baked goods, and staff who help translate the French menu into English. Vegan options include a falafel bowl, cauliflower wings and quinoa sushi, plus vegan desserts.

It is open Monday to Saturday until 10:30pm and Sundays until 5:00pm. State vegan when ordering, as some labels have been reported incorrect, and order the falafel bowl.
