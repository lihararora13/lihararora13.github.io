VeganBurg started in Singapore in 2010 with a simple, slightly audacious idea: a burger joint where everything, patties, buns, sauces and soft serve, is plant-based. The Eunos flagship is still the pilgrimage site, with thousands of reviews and a queue of loyal regulars.

The local legends are the ones to order: a chilli krab burger with proper Singapore heat, a char-grilled satay number, and seaweed fries on the side. Fast food, but make it a hometown hero.
