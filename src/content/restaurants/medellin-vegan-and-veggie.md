Vegan & Veggie is the El Poblado café that turns first-timers into regulars: one reviewer ate here every day of a six-day visit, and Veggies Abroad brought two omnivorous Colombians along and won them over too. The small dining room in Manila serves house-made burgers, colourful bowls, tacos and burritos, and on weekends a special line-up of cazuelas, hearty veganised Colombian stews cooked in terra-cotta pots.

Do not skip the limonada de coco, the coconut-lime classic rebuilt entirely without dairy, which was one of the few vegan versions in the city. Sunday brunch is on, and there is a second Laureles branch on Carrera 77 if you are on that side of town.
