Why we love it — In a city that sometimes treats veganism as a
lifestyle brand, 222 Vegan Cuisine is gloriously old-school: whole,
unrefined ingredients, cooked to order, in a neighbourhood spot that
feels like a secret. The lunchtime buffet is the move, a generous
spread of tofu stews, warm grains, and baked plantains at a price that
feels like 2019.

Come for the value, stay for the stroganoff and the raspberry
cheesecake. This is comfort food that happens to be vegan, not the
other way round.
