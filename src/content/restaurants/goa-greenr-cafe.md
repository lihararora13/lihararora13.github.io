Greenr Cafe made the list for a reason. A plant-forward cafe in historic Fontainhas doing smoked jackfruit earth bowls, mushroom pizza and vegan tiramisu in a leafy courtyard. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Fontainhas, Panaji, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Austin smoked jackfruit earth bowl, Mushroom pizza, Vegan tiramisu if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
