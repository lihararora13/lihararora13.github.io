The Willow Cafe is the hidden gem of Sector 10: a quiet-street cafe with fascinating decor and a relaxed atmosphere, the ideal spot for a lazy brunch or an intimate dinner. Local guides keep naming it among the city's best veg restaurants.

Order the avocado toast and a vegan pasta, and take your time. It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so mention dairy-free when you order.
