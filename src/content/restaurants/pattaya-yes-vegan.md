Yes! Vegan is a small restaurant and guesthouse in the Pratumnak area, the residential hill south of Walking Street and north of Jomtien, with a menu running Thai, Chinese and Western dishes, all vegan. It is a quiet, residential kitchen, and it suits anyone staying on the hill rather than in the centre.

Order the Thai curries and a Chinese dish, and stay for the guesthouse if you want a vegan base in Pattaya. Its pin is approximate to Pratumnak Hill, so check the address before you go.
