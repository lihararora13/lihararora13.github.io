Pure Gourmet, 蔬食本色素食坊, is a big pay-by-weight buffet one block from Taichung station in the East District, and it is the easiest lunch in this guide: take a tray, load it with what you like from a spread of more than fifty dishes, and pay by weight, usually about NT$170 for a decent meal, with soup included and rice a small extra. The vegan dishes are clearly set aside from the vegetarian ones, and the few dishes with egg or milk are labelled, which is why reviewers keep calling it out as a buffet vegans can actually use.

Spring rolls, mushroom dishes, vegetable dishes and rice dishes make up most of the spread, and the kitchen also does bento boxes and set meals for groups. The room is wheelchair accessible, cash only, and there is a car park with one hour free when you eat in. Hours are Monday to Saturday 10:30am to 2:00pm, with a dinner service Monday to Friday 4:30pm to 7:30pm; Saturday is lunch only and Sunday is closed. One honest note: the tongs are shared, so there is cross-contamination risk for anyone with an egg allergy, and the food is not always served warm.
