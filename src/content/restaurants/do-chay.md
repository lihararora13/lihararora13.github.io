Why we love it — Do Chay takes Vietnamese street food and rebuilds it around
plants, in a room that looks better than it needs to. The pho broth is deep
and aromatic, the share plates (Uncle Hing's Wings, the Monsoon Roll) are
built for the table, and the cocktails keep the mood easy.

The menu is fully vegetarian with vegan options across it, so confirm with
staff if you're strict. Either way, this is one of the most fun nights out
on the vegan-adjacent map.
