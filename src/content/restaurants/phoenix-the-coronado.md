The Coronado is the neighbourhood spot Phoenix's plant-based crowd keeps to itself, until they don't. Set in the historic Coronado district since 2015 and fully vegan since 2020, it does Southwest-inspired cooking with a bar attached: fried guacamole that regulars call the hero dish, cauliflower tacos, a beet and quinoa burger, and a chimichanga worth the trip alone. Brunch runs Friday through Sunday, and the pancakes are famously the fluffiest vegan ones in town.

The patio is dog-friendly, the cocktails are proper, and the room glows in the evening. Come for brunch, stay for a drink, and order the fried guac first: it is the dish that converts the table.
