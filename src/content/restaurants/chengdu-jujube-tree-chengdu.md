Jujube Tree runs modern Chinese vegetarian cooking with an English menu and plenty of vegan-friendly plates, in a calm room that makes ordering easy for visitors. The mock meats are there, but the best orders lean into seasonal vegetables, tofu skin, beans and fungi.

Order the jujube jelly and mashed Chinese yam, then a round of seasonal vegetables. It is a relaxed, reliable meal.
