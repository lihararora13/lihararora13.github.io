Mr Fero is Freiburg's other fully vegan spot, a small shop on the Franziskanerstraße serving vegan çiğ köfte in its tastiest form, as the owners put it: 100 percent plant-based, fresh daily, and very cheap. The menu runs from wraps and plates with chips to mixed sushi plates, and everything is made to be eaten on the move or at one of the few seats inside.

It is open seven days a week until seven or half past, which makes it the reliable late-afternoon stop in the old town. Order a çiğ köfte wrap with chips, add a vegan sushi plate if you are hungry, and enjoy one of the best-value vegan meals in the city.
