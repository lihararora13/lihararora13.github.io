ALKALIME is the closest thing Abu Dhabi has to a true vegan-conscious kitchen: a wellness-focused restaurant on the Saadiyat Cultural District waterfront, built around alkaline, plant-forward cooking with clearly marked gluten-free dishes and dairy alternatives like oat milk. Reviewers specifically note celiac awareness and lactose-free substitutions, and it holds 4.6 stars across 640 reviews.

Order a plant-based bowl, ask about the dairy-free options, and enjoy the waterfront. It is the priciest meal on this list, and the most deliberately plant-based.
