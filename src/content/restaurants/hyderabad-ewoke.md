e'woke tops the list for a reason. An all-vegan cafe in Sainikpuri doing mock-meat pizza, vegan haleem and a full plant-based menu, pickup and delivery daily. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Sainikpuri, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Mock meat pizza, Vegan haleem, Garden salad bowl if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
