Loving Hut presents itself as Panama's first vegan restaurant, part of the international chain of the same name, and it is 100% vegan, with cooking that combines Western and Asian dishes with Vietnamese touches. The service comes with jasmine tea and homemade hot sauce, and there are chilled vegetarian products to take home.

Order the vegetable fried rice, the sweet and sour mushrooms and, for dessert, the vegan tiramisu. It is inexpensive, open every day except Sunday, 10:30 am to 8:30 pm, on Calle Manuel Espinosa Batista in the banking district.
