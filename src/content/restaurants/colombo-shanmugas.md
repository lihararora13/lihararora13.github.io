Shanmugas made the list for a reason. One of Colombo's oldest and best-known vegetarian Indian restaurants, famous for dosas and vadai, with a lunch buffet vegans love. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Wellawatta, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Masala dosa, no ghee, Vadai, Lunch buffet, vegan picks if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
