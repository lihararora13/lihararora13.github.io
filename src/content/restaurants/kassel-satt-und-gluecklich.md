Satt & glücklich, full and happy, is the café to start a Kassel morning in. The menu is mixed, but the vegan side of it is serious: a vegan breakfast of breads and spreads, fruit and vegetables, a weekend brunch spread with vegan yoghurt, granola, salads, dips and vegan cheese, plus vegan pancakes and four or five homemade vegan cakes on the counter every day.

It sits on Pestalozzistraße in the Vorderer Westen, a short walk from the rest of the quarter's vegan cluster. Come at the weekend for the fullest vegan breakfast, and save room for cake: the vegan slices are the reason regulars keep coming back.
