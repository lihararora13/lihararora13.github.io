Linh is the restaurant its name promises: not just a restaurant, but a small fairytale. The Vinohrady dining room is all warmth and whimsy, and the kitchen turns out fully vegan Vietnamese cooking that regulars rave about, from pho and pad Thai to banh mi and burgers, plus house-made desserts and raw cakes.

Order the Vietnamese family meal for two if you come as a pair. The vegan pork belly is the dish people write home about, melting and crisp at once, and the whole menu is safe to order from without a single question.
