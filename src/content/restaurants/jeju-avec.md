Avec sits on the second floor above the Aewol coastal road, and its house rule is printed on the wall: no meat, no fish, no milk, no egg. The cooking is vegan comfort food done properly, open sandwiches, burgers and pasta, including a meatball rose pasta and a cream pasta made with Jeju's broken rice, plus Ghost Magpie Brewing beers. It is pet-friendly, and the room is bright and unhurried, a good stop between the coastal cafes of Aewol.

It is open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday to Sunday, 11am to 8pm, and closed on Wednesdays. Come for lunch rather than a late dinner, and expect the menu to change with the season.
