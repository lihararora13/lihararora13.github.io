WholeLifeGo is Calgary's clean-eating vegan spot, and it takes the brief seriously: vegan, oil-free and gluten-free, with sushi, soups, salads, bowls and baked goods made fresh daily. There is a small grocery section too, and limited seating for those who want to stay.

It is a five-star HappyCow favourite and the lightest meal on this list. Order the vegan sushi and a rainbow bowl, and you will leave feeling like a better person.
