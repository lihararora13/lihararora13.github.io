Church - Temple of Fun is Sheffield's vegan flagship: a 100% vegan bar, eatery, games arcade and clothes shop inside the listed Osborn Works in Kelham Island, owned by Bring Me The Horizon's Oli Sykes. Since last summer it runs its own in-house kitchen, after its partnership with Make No Bones came to an end, and the food has only got better for it.

The vegan beef wellington and the Hex Mex burger are the plates to order, the Sunday roast is the weekend draw, and the golden sticky toffee pudding closes the meal. Every drink on the list is vegan too. Book ahead for Sunday, and expect arcade machines, PS4 pods and a photo-booth confessional alongside dinner.
