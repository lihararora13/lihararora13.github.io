Free Bird Cafe is the meal that feels good twice over. It is a non-profit cafe run to support Thai Freedom House, which teaches language and arts to refugees from Burma, and the kitchen cooks a fully vegan menu of Burmese, Shan and Thai dishes alongside Western breakfasts. The Burmese tea-leaf salad is the signature, the vegan pancakes are regularly called the best in town, and there is a thrift shop and donation centre to browse after you eat.

It sits on a quiet street near Nimman, closed on Mondays, open late Wednesday to Saturday. Come for the salad, stay for the bookshop, and leave knowing the bill did more than feed you.
