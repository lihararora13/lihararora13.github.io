Pie-Sci is Woodbridge's creative pizza joint, and it is the vegan-friendly pizza stop on the list: almost every pizza can be made vegan with dairy-free cheese, and gluten-free options are on the table too. The seasonal specials are the reason to ask what is new, and the creativity is the point.

It is a meat-serving pizzeria with a real vegan game, so it lands as vegan-friendly, but the vegan pizzas are the ones people write about. Order a vegan pie, add a seasonal special, and enjoy the most creative pizza in Detroit.
