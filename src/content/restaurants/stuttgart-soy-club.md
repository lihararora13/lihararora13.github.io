Soy Club is a fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant on Schloßstraße in the West, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, and it is the most fun room on this page. The bao buns are the signature, with avocado tofu fries, vegan shrimp and pho alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it built for sharing.

It is a short walk west of the Schlossplatz, and the bao bun is the order. The vegan shrimp is the dish regulars keep naming.
