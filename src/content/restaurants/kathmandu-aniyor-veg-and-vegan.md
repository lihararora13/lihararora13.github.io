Aniyor Veg & Vegan Restaurant made the list for a reason. A pure vegetarian and vegan restaurant in Thamel with a separate vegan menu covering Nepali, Indian, Jain, Thai and Continental food. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Thamel, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan dal bhat, Seitan curry, Thai green curry, vegan if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
