red has been Heidelberg's vegetarian anchor for years, a buffet restaurant where you build your own plate from a long "gedeckter Tisch" of salads, antipasti, gratins and bakes, plus a wok dish cooked to order in front of you. Much of the cooking is organic and regional, the vegan dishes are marked, and the room has the easy, garden-party feel of a place the whole city trusts for lunch. It is the safest bet in Heidelberg for a group with mixed diets.

It sits on the Poststraße in the Weststadt, a few minutes from the Hauptbahnhof. Go hungry, take your time at the buffet, and save room for the homemade cakes, including the vegan nougat cream torte. Everything is available to take away in compostable bowls if you would rather eat by the river.
