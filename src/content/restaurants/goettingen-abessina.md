Abessina has been serving Eritrean and Ethiopian food on Ritterplan for about a decade, a traditional restaurant opposite the city museum where the vegan options are clearly marked and genuinely good. The move is the vegan platter: four or five vegetable dishes with injera, rice and couscous, best ordered for two, with sambusa to start and an Ethiopian coffee ceremony to finish if you have the time.

It is open Tuesday to Sunday from five in the evening, closed on Mondays. Book ahead if you can, the room is small and popular, and come hungry: the platter portions are ample.
