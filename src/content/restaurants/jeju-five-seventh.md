Five Seventh, styled 5/7 and subtitled "As Vegan As Possible", is the east coast's vegan cafe, a bright modern room a short walk from Woljeongri Beach in Gujwa-eup. Everything is plant-based, dairy-free and egg-free: vegan burgers with purple cabbage slaw and roasted kabocha, focaccia baked daily, grain bowls layered with Jeju-grown seasonal vegetables, and a vegan porridge that regulars come back for. There is also a small shop shelf of sustainable goods, from granola to bamboo toothbrushes.

Opening hours are short and they shift, so check the cafe's Instagram before going: the listing gives Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Friday and Saturday 11am to 8pm, closed Wednesday and Thursday, while another guide lists 12pm to 4pm. It is also fully halal-safe, which makes it an easy stop for Muslim travellers exploring the east coast.
