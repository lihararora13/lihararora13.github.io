burgerme's Südstadt branch is the chain option on this list, a fast-food burger joint on Reinhäuser Landstraße where the vegan order is the Veggie Crunchy: a spicy breaded vegan patty with tomato and vegan mayonnaise in a handmade potato bun. Note the other veggie burger on the menu, the Veggie Peppery, comes with cheddar, so the Crunchy is the one to ask for.

It is open every day until ten, with delivery and takeaway alongside dine-in. Order the Veggie Crunchy with fries, and you have a reliable vegan burger south of the old town.
