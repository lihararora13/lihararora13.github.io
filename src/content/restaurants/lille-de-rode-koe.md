De Rode Koe sits on Rue des Trois Mollettes in Vieux-Lille, the old town's prettiest quarter, and serves a vegan-friendly menu with a tikka masala and curry at its heart and a gâteau to finish. It is the dinner pick for a wander through the old streets.

The room is small and the menu is short, which is usually a good sign. Vegan dishes are marked; ask the staff if anything is unclear.
