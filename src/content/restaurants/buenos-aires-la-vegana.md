La Vegana is the neighbourhood cantina every vegan city guide secretly runs on: a small, fully vegan kitchen in Monserrat turning out seitan and chickpea milanesas, empanadas and daily homestyle plates without fuss or big prices.

It keeps evening hours Wednesday to Sunday and takes cash only, so plan accordingly. Simple, tasty and genuinely local, a lovely final stop on a Microcentro wander.
