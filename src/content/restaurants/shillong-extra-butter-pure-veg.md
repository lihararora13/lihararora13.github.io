Extra Butter is the new pure-veg star of Upper Shillong: a multi-cuisine restaurant inside Hotel Wild Cherry at Mawnianglah, on the road to Sohra, with a modern dining room, a bar and a menu that runs from veg galouti kebab to vegetable dum biryani. Travel guides call it the crowd favourite, and the October 2026 vlogs show a busy, beautiful room.

Order the veg galouti kebab on naan and the vegetable dum biryani, ask for no paneer and no dairy, and you have the most ambitious veg meal in Shillong. It is 15 kilometres from the city centre, so pair it with a Sohra day trip.
