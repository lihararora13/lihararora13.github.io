Plant has been flying the vegan flag in Cape Town longer than almost anyone, and the Camps Bay branch is the scenic way to meet it: a fully vegan menu on The Promenade, a short stroll from the beach, running from breakfast smoothies to dinner and dessert.

Cape Town Tourism calls it one of the city's vegan gems, and the menu is a world tour with a local hero: the vegan bobotie, full of Cape Malay spice, no meat and no egg. Sit on the terrace, watch Camps Bay do its thing, and order the crepes for pudding.
