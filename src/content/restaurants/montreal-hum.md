Hum is the quiet overachiever of the Plateau vegan scene. The menu is 100% plant-based and draws on Southeast Asian and Japanese traditions: handmade dumplings with real chew, ramen with a broth that tastes like it simmered all day, and vegan sushi rolls that lean creative rather than imitative. The room is warm, green, and calm, the kind of place where a weeknight dinner turns into a long conversation.

Tastet's editors keep it on their best-of lists for good reason: every dish is crafted with care and the mindful approach shows on the plate. It appeals equally to plant-based regulars and curious omnivores, which is the highest compliment a vegan kitchen can earn. Order the dumplings first, whatever the ramen special is second, and let the sushi round out the table.
