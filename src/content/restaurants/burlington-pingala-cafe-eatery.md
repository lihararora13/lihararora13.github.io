Pingala is Burlington's vegan flagship, a 100% plant-based cafe in the Chace Mill overlooking the Winooski River, with a second room on North Avenue. The menu is comfort food done properly: the Crunchwrap Supreme is the signature, the French toast puffs are the brunch order, and the buffalo mac crunchwrap is the one people photograph.

It is open daily, and it gets busy at weekend brunch. Come for the crunchwrap, stay for the room, which still keeps its food-truck-inspired ordering counter.
