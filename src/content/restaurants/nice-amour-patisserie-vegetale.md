Amour Patisserie Vegetale is the clearest vegan recommendation in Nice: a fully vegan and gluten-free patisserie with large-format creations, brunch and a tea room at 2 Rue Foncet. The counter is full of pastries, with a plant-based omelette, a vegan quiche and salad, and a breakfast bowl alongside, and everything is homemade.

It is open Monday to Saturday from 09:00 to 18:00, Sundays from 10:00 to 14:00. Come for pastry and brunch, and order the omelette and a pastry to take away.
