Kaernehuset is a members vegetarian club on Nedergade, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, where volunteer cooks make vegetarian food, some days entirely vegan, for 65 kroner a meal. It is open to the public as a trial member. It is the community pick on this list.

Reserve a seat at least an hour before dinner at six, and come Tuesday, Wednesday or Friday, since it opens three afternoons a week.
