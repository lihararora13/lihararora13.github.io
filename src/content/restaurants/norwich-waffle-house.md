The Waffle House has been serving waffles on St Giles Street for decades, in one of Norwich's oldest buildings, and the vegan waffle options cover sweet and savoury. It is an institution rather than a trend.

Order a sweet waffle with vegan toppings. A good dessert stop after dinner in the Lanes.
