Crusty Kuhna is Ljubljana's vegan fast-food fix, and it does one thing brilliantly: fried oyster mushrooms treated like the star they are. Burgers, wraps, loaded fries and chocolate pancakes, all of it fully vegan and all of it built for a hungry, happy mess.

It is a short walk north of the centre and very much a casual counter, not a date spot. Order the oyster mushroom burger, add loaded fries, and save the chocolate pancakes for the walk home.
