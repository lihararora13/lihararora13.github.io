Padmanadi is Edmonton's vegan institution, a family-run Indonesian restaurant downtown that has been winning awards and converting sceptics for years. The menu is a love letter to Indonesian and pan-Asian comfort: ginger beef, curry chicken, bakmi goreng, satay, roti canai and a monthly vegan buffet with more than twenty options.

Reviewers say the ginger beef tastes like real beef and the buffet is great value. Order the ginger beef and the bakmi goreng, and time a visit for the buffet if you can.
