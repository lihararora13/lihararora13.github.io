Social Kitchen, Taste of Udupi, is the fast-casual Udupi stop in Kadavanthra: self-service kiosks, food in two or three minutes, and a menu built on masala dosa, sambar idli and filter coffee, with a 4.4 on Justdial from over 2,000 ratings. The chain has since added outlets in Mamangalam and near Changampuzha Park, but this Jawahar Nagar original is the one to visit.

Order the Udupi Schezwan masala dosa or a plain masala dosa, sambar idli and an avocado shake, and ask for no ghee. It is affordable, quick and open late, until 11pm, which makes it the practical dinner pick on this list.
