Chez Lulu is a French cafe and bakery in Mountain Brook with a garden patio and a menu that includes vegan-friendly salads, soups, and vegetable plates. It is a calm, pretty lunch stop, very different in energy from the burger and brewery picks on this list.

Order the vegan salad and the soup of the day, confirming the vegan preparation with the server. Save room for sorbet if it is on the board.
