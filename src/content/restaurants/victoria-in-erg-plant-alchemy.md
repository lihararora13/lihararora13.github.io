In Er G Plant Alchemy is Victoria's raw juice bar: a fully vegan, raw, organic and gluten-free room on Fisgard Street in Chinatown, with no processed sugars, serving smoothies, juices and raw desserts. The Conscious Creator smoothie, the Golden Turmeric Twist and the Blue Unicorn juice are the drinks to order, and the Raw Strawberry Cheezecake is the dessert. It is open Monday to Saturday 10am to 5pm, closed Sundays.

Order a smoothie and the cheesecake. It is the lightest meal in this guide, and the best stop after a walk through Chinatown.
