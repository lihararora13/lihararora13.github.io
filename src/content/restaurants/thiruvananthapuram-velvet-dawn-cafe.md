Velvet Dawn is the Trivandrum cafe that did something unusual: it veganized its dinner menu. A 2025 visit documented the creamy mushroom stroganoff, stir-fried vegetables with cashew nuts and two kinds of fried rice, every dish free of meat, dairy and eggs, in a warm, plant-filled room down a quiet lane in Sasthamangalam.

Order the stroganoff and the cashew stir-fry. This is the closest Trivandrum gets to a dedicated vegan dinner, and the cafe earned it by listening to its customers.
