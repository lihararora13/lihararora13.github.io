Shanty Man is a 5.0-rated Ital Jamaican restaurant on Gordon Town Road, serving local dishes like ackee and plantain alongside porridge, sandwiches and drinks. It is cash only, with outdoor seating, and it is open Monday to Friday and Sundays, 10 am to 9 pm.

Order ackee and plantain, and try the porridge. Gordon Town is a taxi ride from central Kingston, so pair it with a trip out to the hills.
