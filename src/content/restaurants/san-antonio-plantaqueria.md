Plantaqueria is downtown San Antonio's plant-based taqueria, and it is a flavour bomb: a Latina-owned, 100 percent vegan spot doing birria quesatacos, jackfruit tacos and breakfast tacos on handmade corn and flour tortillas. The La Lucha taco is the signature, the jackfruit tacos are the sleeper, and the agua de pepino is the refreshment.

It is open daily from 7am to 7pm, which makes it the breakfast taco stop as well as the lunch stop. Come hungry, order the La Lucha, and bring someone who thinks vegan tacos are a contradiction.
