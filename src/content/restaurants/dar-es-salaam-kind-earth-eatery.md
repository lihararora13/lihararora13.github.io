Kind Earth Eatery is a Jamaican-influenced vegetarian restaurant on Kahama Road in Masaki, rated 4.5, with 80% of the dishes vegan or able to be made vegan, plus raw food. It houses two separate kitchens and no meat is cooked in Kind Earth's, and it has outdoor seating.

Order the Jamaican vegan plate and a fresh juice. It is cash only, open Monday to Saturday 1pm to 10pm, and it is the best vegan table in Dar es Salaam.
