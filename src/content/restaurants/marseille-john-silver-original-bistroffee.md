John Silver does not pretend to be anything it is not: it takes non-vegan dishes, makes them vegan, and makes them better than the original. The kebab is the legend here, praised by travellers who had ranked Warsaw's vegan kebabs first until they ate this one, and the bao, toast and desserts fill out an all-day menu that never stops.

It is a small, cool bistro in Le Panier with a name that makes you smile and food that makes you stay. Order the kebab, add a bao, and thank us later.
