Veggie Vie made the list for a reason. A fully vegan cafe off Gawler Place since 2015, doing toasts, burgers, bowls, smoothies and pizza in a funky green room. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Adelaide CBD, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan burger, Brunch toast, Nourish bowl if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
