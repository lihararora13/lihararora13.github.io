The Tree Rooms took over the former Vegano spot on Blandford Square and kept the neighbourhood's vegan cafe going, with a calm, cosy room, a dog-friendly welcome and bakes reviewers call divine. Days run on brunch, Fridays and Saturdays add an evening service.

The Sunrise Stack with its signature vegan dippy egg is the signature plate, and the carbonara with shiitake mushroom pieces is the one to order at dinner. The cakes are baked in house, so leave room.
