Udupi Gangotri Dakshin on Chakrata Road is Dehradun's South Indian anchor: a pure-veg cafe doing Mysore masala dosa, rava onion-free uttapam and medu vada, open 7:30 AM to 9:30 PM, with sattvik and Jain options on request.

The Mysore masala dosa is the order, and the rava uttapam is the backup. Ask for no ghee and the dosa comes out properly vegan.
