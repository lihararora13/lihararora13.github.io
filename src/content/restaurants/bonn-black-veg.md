Black Veg is Bonn's fully vegan café-bistro, a small Nordstadt room on Adolfstraße that cooks a changing savoury menu alongside coffee and cake. The kitchen leans hearty rather than health-food: Käsespätzle, ciabatta with vegan cheese and seitan, toasties, and a cake case that regulars plan around, with the room turning into a bar on Friday evenings.

Hours are limited and worth checking before you go: Wednesday and Thursday afternoons, Friday until ten, Saturday until eight, and Sunday from ten until six. Come for a late lunch or a Sunday coffee and cake, and ask what the kitchen is cooking today rather than expecting a fixed menu.
