The Red Hot is Tacoma's hot dog shop on Sixth Avenue, and it is the most fun pick in this guide: vegan-labelled dogs and burgers, a North End Not Dog, a Goodburger, vegan fries and a vegan shake, all in a casual, counter-service room with outdoor seating. It is open Monday to Thursday 11am to 9pm, Friday to Saturday 11am to 10pm and Sunday 11am to 8pm, and it is the cheapest proper meal in the guide after Quickie Too.

Order a Not Dog and a shake. The vegan items are labelled, so ordering is easy, and the Sixth Avenue location makes it a good stop between downtown and the Hilltop.
