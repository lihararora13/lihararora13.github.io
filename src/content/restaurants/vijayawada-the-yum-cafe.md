The Yum Cafe opened in Patamata in September 2026 billed as the new biggest pure-veg cafe in Vijayawada, and the video shows a large modern room with high ceilings, green marble tables, self-order kiosks and a very busy dining floor. The food is thatte idli with red podi, thali and roti with curry.

Order the thatte idli and the thali, and ask for the curd raita to be skipped. Some commenters call it overpriced, so order the idli rather than the full spread.
