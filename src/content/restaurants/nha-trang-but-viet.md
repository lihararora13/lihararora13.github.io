But Viet, also called Nha Hang Chay But Viet, is a vegetarian restaurant on Dong Da in Nha Trang, with a quiet space and Buddha decor, known for tofu and mushroom hotpot and vegan bun Hue. It is open daily, 10:30am to 2pm and 4:30pm to 9pm, and it is the best sit-down vegetarian dinner in the centre.

Order the tofu and mushroom hotpot and the vegan bun Hue, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is a calm, traditional room, and it suits a slow evening meal.
