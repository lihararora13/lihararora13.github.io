Squatcho's is Rochester's vegan pizza party: a fully vegan pizzeria on East Main Street, decked out in Sasquatch murals with a life-size Bigfoot statue, serving five house pizzas plus build-your-own, hot subs like meatball and buffalo chicken, plates, fried dough, wings and milkshakes. The Let's Go Buffalo pizza, topped with buffalo-style plant chicken and vegan blue cheese crumbles, is the signature, and the crispy garlic parm wings and garlic knots are the sides regulars insist on. Reviewers call it the best vegan pizza they have had, with a crust that stays chewy rather than heavy.

Hours are split, Tuesday to Friday 11am to 2pm and 4pm to 7pm, Friday evenings to 8pm, and Saturday noon to 8pm. It is takeout-friendly, budget-friendly and kid-friendly, with limited parking downtown, so plan a short walk.
