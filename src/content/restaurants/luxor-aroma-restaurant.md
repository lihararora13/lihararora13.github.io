Aroma is the East Bank rooftop with a dedicated vegan menu, Egyptian and international dishes, a vegan tagine and fresh juices, all with Nile views. It is the most-cited vegan address in Luxor and the only one with a menu written for vegans.

Order the vegan tagine and a fresh juice, and come at sunset for the rooftop. It is a tourist-facing room, so prices run higher than the street, but the vegan menu is genuine.
