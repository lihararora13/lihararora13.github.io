Mas Veggies Taqueria is Pasadena's all-vegan taco counter: a fast-food taqueria in the Lake Cordova Building at 200 South Lake Avenue, open 11am to 10pm daily, serving vegan tacos, burritos, quesadillas and nachos. It shares its building with Next Level Veggie Grill, which makes this address the densest vegan corner in the city, a burger place and a taco place under one roof, both fully vegan.

Order tacos and a burrito. The room is quick-service and takeout-friendly, and the 4.0 HappyCow rating reflects a solid, unfussy taco.
