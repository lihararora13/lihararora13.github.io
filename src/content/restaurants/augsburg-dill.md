DILL is Augsburg's answer to the question of what Bavarian food looks like without any animal products: schnitzel burgers, gyros, mushroom tortellini, roulade and chocolate mousse, cooked in a small restaurant near the cathedral in the old town. It is fully vegan, it is serious about the cooking, and it is the table to take someone who thinks vegan food in Bavaria means a side salad. The dining room is intimate, so the short hours and small size are part of the charm rather than a flaw.

Mind the service times: evenings Wednesday to Saturday, with lunch on Saturday and Sunday, and closed Monday and Tuesday. Book ahead, order the schnitzel burger if it is your first visit, and save room for the mousse.
