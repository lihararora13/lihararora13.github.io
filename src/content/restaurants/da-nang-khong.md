Khong is one of An Thuong's fully vegan kitchens, and it is a lovely one: salads, soups, pastas, rolls and healthy bowls, all plant-based, in a bright space a short walk from My Khe Beach. It sits in the thick of the beach neighbourhood's vegan cluster, so it is easy to combine with a swim and a coffee next door.

Come for lunch, order a salad bowl and spring rolls, and keep the afternoon open. This is the one for a light, fresh meal by the beach.
