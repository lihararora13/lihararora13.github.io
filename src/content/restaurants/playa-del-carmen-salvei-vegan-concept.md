Salvei is the pick for a proper night out in Playa: a minimalist all-vegan dining room right on 5th Avenue in Zazil-Ha, with cocktails, wine and kombucha alongside an Asian-leaning menu. HappyCow reviewers in 2026 praise the satay ramen and the mole chilaquiles, and the fresh rolls and pad thai are the other orders.

It opens at 9am every day, and a June 2026 reviewer says it now serves breakfast too, so it works for a morning on 5th Avenue as well as dinner. It is somewhat pricey by Playa standards, and tofu costs extra on some dishes.
