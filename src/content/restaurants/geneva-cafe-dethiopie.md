Café d'Éthiopie is the third of the Pâquis area's great Ethiopian kitchens, and it leans hardest into authenticity: the injera is made from Ethiopian teff, the coffee is taken seriously, and the menu is full of naturally vegan dishes that need no adapting. It is the kind of place where the food arrives looking like a celebration and disappears fast.

Come for the injera platter, stay for the coffee, and treat it as your introduction to Ethiopian food if you have never had it before. Between the three Ethiopian picks on this list, Geneva might quietly be one of Europe's best cities for it.
