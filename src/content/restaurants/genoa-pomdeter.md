Pomdeter is an organic vegetarian and vegan restaurant and grocery in the Foce district, east of the centre, with a perfect five-star HappyCow record. The cooking is Ligurian and organic: farinata, focaccia, minestrone, Russian salad and beetroot hummus, with a shop counter for takeaway.

Order the farinata, the focaccia, the minestrone and the beetroot hummus. It opens early for breakfast and runs through lunch most days, with a Thursday dinner service, so check the day before going.
