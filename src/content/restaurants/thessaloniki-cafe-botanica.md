Café Botanica is the small café inside Modiano Market, offering vegan croissants, egg-free muffins and an outstanding espresso in a rustic room with herb shelves and wooden tables. It is the perfect stop after browsing the market stalls, and the market itself sells olives, bread and produce to round out a picnic.

It is open Monday to Saturday from 8am to 7pm and closed Sunday. Pick up olives and fresh bread at the market to go with your pastry.
