La Moulinette is a co-op café-cantine on Boulevard Victor Hugo in Lille-Moulins, serving a döner végétal, homemade fries, pancakes and sourdough bread at the friendliest prices on this page. It is a neighbourhood place with a community feel, and the cheapest lunch on the list.

The kitchen is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, and its own description does not claim 100% vegan, so check the board. The döner végétal is the order.
