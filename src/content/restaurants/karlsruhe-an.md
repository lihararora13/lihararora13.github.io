An is Karlsruhe's first Asian vegan restaurant, and it sets the standard for the city: a fully vegan menu of Vietnamese cooking, fine vegan sushi and Indochina-inspired interiors, open every day from eleven to ten. The cooking is balanced and calm, phở, pad thai, udon and rolls, with a dining room that feels considered rather than themed. It is the table to book first in Karlsruhe, and the one that shows what the city's vegan scene is capable of.

Order the phở, add a plate of vegan sushi, and treat it as the dinner that anchors a Karlsruhe evening.
