SURN YI has been serving vegetarian food since 1998, just behind the Royal Palace and a short walk from the riverside and the National Museum. The menu is wide: noodles, rice, burgers, deep-fried dishes, soups, hotpot and a long list of teas, bubble teas, smoothies and desserts.

Order a noodle soup and a hotpot. It is a vegetarian restaurant with plenty of vegan options, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it is open daily from 7am to 8pm, which makes it the early-breakfast pick on this list.
