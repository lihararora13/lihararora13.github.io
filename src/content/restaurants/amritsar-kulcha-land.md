Kulcha Land in Ranjit Avenue is the breakfast stop: crisp, multi-layered Amritsari kulchas stuffed with potato, gobi or aloo, served with mild spiced chole and a pomegranate-anardana chutney. It is open 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM, and it is a morning institution.

Ask for the kulcha without white makhan and the chole as it comes. It is the most Amritsari thing you can eat before noon.
