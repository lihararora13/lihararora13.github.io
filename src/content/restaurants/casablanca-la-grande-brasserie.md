La Grande Brasserie is a brasserie on Rue Normandie in Maarif with clearly marked vegan dishes: chia bowl, acai bowl, mezze, chickpea salad, a burger and pita bread with falafel, plus plant milk for coffee. It is open daily from 7:00am to 1:00am, which makes it the most flexible hours on this list.

Order the chia bowl for breakfast or the mezze and falafel pita later in the day. Note the pin is at street level on Rue Normandie.
