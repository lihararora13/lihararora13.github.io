Detroit Street Filling Station is Ann Arbor's vegan showpiece, a retro lounge in a converted 1925 gas station in Kerrytown with a sign in the window that says it plainly: we are 100% vegan. The buffalo cauliflower wings and the World's Greatest Wrap are the dishes people travel for, and the patio and full bar make it a proper evening out.

It is open seven days a week for lunch, dinner, and drinks. Order the wings, then the wrap, and plan on staying a while.
