Kissa Saeki is an organic café and kitchen in Kamiyacho, founded in 1976, two minutes from Kamiyacho-higashi station, and one of Hiroshima's best vegan-friendly tables. The menu is clearly marked vegan and gluten-free in places: a vegan katsu cutlet sandwich on bread from a nearby bakery, lentil hamburg sets, tempeh, vegan croquettes and vegan curry, plus a raw sugar-free chocolate tart and vegan scones for dessert, all from seasonal local ingredients with no chemical or GM ingredients.

One caution from a HappyCow reviewer: the rice here is grown with the aigamo duck method, so strict vegans may prefer the sandwiches and sweets over the rice sets. Meat dishes are also on the menu, so this is a mixed table that happens to cook vegan food very well.
