Marae, full name Marae Kaffee and Zauberei, is a fully vegan café in Lübeck's old town, and it has been fully vegan since March 2021. Raw cakes, bircher muesli, wraps and burgers come out of a bright little room on the Engelsgrube that lives up to the magic in its name, and the food is proper cooking rather than a health-food lecture. The cake counter is the reason regulars keep coming back.

It sits a short walk from the Holstentor, closed on Mondays. Order a raw cake, add a bircher muesli or a wrap, and take your time. This is the table for a slow morning in Lübeck, and the second fully vegan anchor of the list.
