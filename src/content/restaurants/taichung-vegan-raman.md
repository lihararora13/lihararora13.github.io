Vegan Raman, 旭麥蔬食拉麵, is a joint venture between Wu Su Zhai and Xu Mai, and it is the most modern bowl in this guide: the broth is built on oat plant milk, the noodles are made with low-GI barley for a chewy bite, and there is no MSG, vegetable oil or shortening, just canola or sunflower oil. Every bowl of ramen comes with a piece of crispy fried enoki mushroom, which regulars say is the thing to look forward to, and the menu also runs wontons and a mapo-style barley rice. The white broth ramen, a soy milk broth that drinks like a vegan tonkotsu, is the signature, with yuzu white broth and a spicy hell ramen as the other two to try.

The shop sits on Kangle Street in the West District, near Taichung Girls' Senior High School, about an 18-minute walk from the station. Hours differ between sources: the iCook guide lists Monday and Thursday to Sunday, 11:30am to 2:00pm and 5:00pm to 8:00pm, closed Tuesday and Wednesday, while other guides list Wednesday only as the rest day, so check the shop's social page before you go. Cash only.
