Blackbird is Gothenburg's vegan paradise, a punky all-vegan restaurant and bar on Stigbergsliden that started as a cooperative of five friends from the punk scene and the animal rights movement. The concept is simple: no animal products at all, and top quality food, with much of it made from scratch, from seitan and tempeh to the house-made vegan cheeses that fill the deli counter.

The bar pours vegan beer, wine and cider, and the room plays punk and metal with occasional live DJs. Order the seitan schnitzel or the chilli cheese, add a cheese board to share, and stay for a drink.
