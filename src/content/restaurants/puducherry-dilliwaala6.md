Dilliwaala6 is the White Town North Indian vegetarian restaurant, named among the must-try vegetarian restaurants in Pondicherry in a February 2025 guide, with a glass-doored entrance, checkered tiles and arched doorways. It is the North Indian counterpoint to the French Quarter cafes, and the room is built for a proper dinner.

Order a veg thali and a North Indian curry, and ask for the dairy to be held. It is the White Town dinner pick when the cafes feel too light.
