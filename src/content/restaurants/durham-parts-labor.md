Parts & Labor is Durham's thoughtful pub, a Central Park district room where the vegan and vegetarian options are the best pub grub in the city rather than a token salad. The menu changes with the seasons, so ask what is vegan that day, and pair it with something from the beer list.

It is the best evening pub stop on this list. Come hungry and order across the table.
