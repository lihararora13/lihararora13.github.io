Officina Vegana is a small, casual takeaway on Via Sedile di Porto, in the university area, and one of the cheapest proper meals on this list. The menu is short and fully vegan: filled breads, gnocchi in tomato sauce, stews and salads, made to take away or eat standing at the counter.

Come here for a fast lunch between sights rather than a long dinner. It is the kind of place regulars return to weekly, and the daily specials are worth asking about.
