Jinglianju Vegetarian is the temple-side table on West Lake: a restaurant beside Jingci Temple at 56 Nanshan Road, directly opposite Leifeng Pagoda, where you eat wholesome vegan noodle soups in a zen-like room with monks dining nearby. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow from 13 reviews, and recent visitors describe a minimalist menu of five noodle bowls, self-service from the kitchen counter, with clean ingredients and no artificial flavourings.

Order a noodle soup bowl, or the set menu at 68 yuan a head. Note that the format has changed over time, from a la carte noodle soups to a set menu and back to noodle bowls, and one reviewer reports dairy in the dessert, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly and worth confirming the dessert. A closure reported in May 2023 predates the current reviews, which show it trading. Open daily 9am to 7:30pm.
