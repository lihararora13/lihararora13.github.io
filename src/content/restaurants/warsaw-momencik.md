Momencik is Warsaw's vegan Mexican fix, a Spanish-owned cafe that has been rolling burritos since 2015. Five kinds of burrito, gluten-free tacos, tostadas, nachos, gringas and flautas, plus a different soup every day and fresh lemonade, all plant-based and made without artificial additives.

It is a quick-service room with a 4.8 rating from more than six hundred reviewers. Order a burrito al pastor, add the nachos, and try the soup of the day.
