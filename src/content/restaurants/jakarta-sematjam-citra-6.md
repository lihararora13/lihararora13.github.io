Sematjam is the neighbourhood vegan cafe done right: western and Indonesian dishes side by side, smoothie bowls and coffees in the morning, nasi goreng and pastas later, and ice cream and sweet treats whenever. Everything is vegan, and the room is relaxed enough for a long lunch.

It is out west in Kalideres, which makes it a destination rather than a drop-in, but the food earns the trip. Order the nasi goreng, save room for ice cream.
