Sri Gowri Krishnaa is the pure-veg restaurant on 7th Cross Street in Thillainagar, rated 4.5 across twenty reviews, and run by the Gowri group that pioneered the pure-veg restaurant in Madurai in 1982 before opening in Tiruchirappalli in 2011. The watchword, in the group's own words, is quality in whatever they do.

Order the masala dosa and the veg meal, and ask for them without ghee or curd. It is the polished Thillainagar dinner, and the lineage shows.
