Rocca is the one for people who want Japanese home cooking rather than substitutes. It is a quiet, minimalist counter restaurant in Tanabe, a residential neighbourhood south of Tennoji, serving a single fixed vegan Japanese set meal for around ¥1,300 at lunch, tea included. There is little English and no choice beyond the set, which is exactly the point.

The kitchen uses no animal ingredients, no chemical seasonings and no sugar, cooking with organic vegetables and specially cultivated rice. It is out of the way for most visitors, so pair it with a morning at Shitennoji or Abeno Harukas, and remember it closes on Sundays.
