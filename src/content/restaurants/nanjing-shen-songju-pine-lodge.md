Shen SongJu, or Pine Lodge, is the vegetarian restaurant of Linggu Temple: a small spot at 2 Linggusi Road, east of the Songfeng Pavilion in the Zhongshan scenic area, recommended for its noodles. It holds a 3.0 on HappyCow, and current Ctrip guides to Linggu Temple still point visitors to it for a vegetarian meal.

Order the noodles and the temple-style vegetable dishes. It is the quietest pick on this list, deep in the pine forest of Purple Mountain, so plan it as lunch on a Zhongshan scenic area day rather than a downtown stop.
