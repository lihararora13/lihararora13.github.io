Kaibur Coffee is Polish Hill's vegan-centric coffee shop: a cosy neighbourhood cafe doing vegan breakfast sandwiches, house-made vegan donuts and lavender cold brew, alongside a pescatarian menu that now includes fish, so it lands as vegan-friendly. The vegan breakfast sandwich is the signature, and the donuts are the reason to come on a Tuesday, Saturday or Sunday.

It is the morning stop on the list, and the one to hit early. Order the breakfast sandwich, add a donut, and enjoy the best vegan breakfast in the Burgh.
