Qing Ya Cha Su is the tea house of the Lingyin cluster: a fully vegan food and tea house at No. 193 Baile Bridge, where the friendly owners grow their own organic vegetables and serve tasty, flavourful dishes and teas. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, and a November 2024 Trip.com visit calls it a favourite among the Lingyin vegetarian choices, praising the white-chocolate passion fruit dessert, the soy fish and the cashew mousse with pomelo.

Order the Longzhu Tanhai, the soy fish and the cashew mousse with pomelo, and take tea with the meal. It has indoor and outdoor seating and is wheelchair accessible. Open daily 9:30am to 9pm.
