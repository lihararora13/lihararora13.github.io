Stella's Lounge is a downtown burger bar on Commerce Avenue, home of a burger once named the best in America, and it keeps veggie sandwiches, salads, and vegan sides on the menu. It is the loudest, most bar-like room on this list, and the best evening stop.

Order the veggie sandwich with the vegan sides. Ask the server which sides are vegan, since some are finished with butter or cheese.
