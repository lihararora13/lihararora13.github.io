Hae Tteuneun, also listed in English as House of the Rising Sun, is Gwangju's only fully vegan restaurant, on Daenam-daero in Juwol-dong, Nam-gu. It serves vegan Korean food in a neighbourhood restaurant setting, open for lunch and dinner most days, and it is the natural first stop for any vegan visiting the city.

Order the vegan Korean set and the tofu dishes, and come at lunch when the kitchen is at its fullest. Its coordinates come from its map listing, so the pin is close but worth confirming on arrival.
