Cafe Stepping Stone is worth the drive out to Strathnairn for the setting alone: a vegetarian cafe inside a gallery homestead at the arts centre, with plenty of outdoor seating for sunny days and an open fire for cold ones. The menu is small and made in-house, soup, salad, toasties, pies and cakes, with clearly labelled vegan options, and the cafe provides employment and training for migrant and refugee women. Friday evenings bring woodfired pizza, and the whole place runs on local produce and zero waste.

It is a slow, sunny detour from the city, and a lovely one. Come for lunch, wander the gallery, and stay for a slice of cake in the garden.
