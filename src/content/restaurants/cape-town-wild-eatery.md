Wild Eatery keeps it simple and gets it right: a small vegan kitchen on Harrington Street turning fresh local produce into wraps and bowls, with gluten-free options and a perfect 5.0 rating from its listing reviews. A recent city guide puts it at 4.9 from 184 reviews, which is a lot of happy lunchers.

Plan around the hours, because this is a lunch spot: weekdays until 3:30pm, Saturdays until 3pm, Sundays off. The falafel bowl plus tempura cauliflower is the order.
