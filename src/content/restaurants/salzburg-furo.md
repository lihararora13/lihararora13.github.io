Furo is a vegetarian Levantine restaurant on Elisabethstrasse, a hidden city oasis with a garden and a playground, serving mezze, hummus, falafel, shakshuka and sourdough focaccia with natural wine. The muhammara focaccia with Salzburg mountain cheese and truffle pesto is the signature, so vegans should order the hummus, falafel and cauliflower instead.

Order the mezze, the hummus and falafel, and the spiced cauliflower, and ask for the vegan dishes, since the signature focaccia runs on cheese.
