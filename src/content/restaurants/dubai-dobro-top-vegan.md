Dobro Top Vegan is the JLT local that plant-based Dubai swears by: a delivery favourite turned sit-down restaurant with a menu so wide it feels like a dare. Pitas stuffed with plant-based meats, pizzas, sushi, burritos, bowls, pancakes and crepes all share one fully vegan kitchen.

It is unfussy, generous and great value, the kind of place you end up ordering far too much from because everything sounds good. The vegan caviar toast is the conversation piece, but the pitas and pizzas are the reason you will come back.
