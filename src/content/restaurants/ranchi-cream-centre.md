Cream Centre is the name Ranchi food creators reach for first when they list the city's top vegetarian restaurants: a pure-veg restaurant since 1958, now on the second floor of Matrix Mall on Circular Road, with a special no-onion-no-garlic Jain menu alongside its regular menu. A July 2026 food vlog shows the pani puri tower, the sizzling platters and the green parathas that keep families coming back.

Order the pani puri tower for the table and a Jain thali for yourself, asking the kitchen to skip the ghee and paneer. It is the best-known pure-veg room in the city, and it is built for family meals and celebrations.
