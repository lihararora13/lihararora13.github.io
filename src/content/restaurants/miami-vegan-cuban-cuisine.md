Yes, vegan Cuban food is a thing, and yes, it absolutely slaps. This family-run kitchen in Sunset serves the classics you grew up craving: a stacked cubano, crispy ham croquetas, papa rellena stuffed with plant-based picadillo, and flan for the finish.

The oat-milk cafe con leche means your ventanita ritual stays fully intact. Reviewers call it Cuban food that hits every time, and at these prices you can order the whole menu and still feel virtuous.
