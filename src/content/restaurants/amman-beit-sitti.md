Beit Sitti means my grandma's house, and it is exactly that: a homely kitchen on Rainbow Street where you choose your ingredients and cook your own meal in daily classes at breakfast, lunch and dinner. The view is panoramic, the vibe is warm, and the kitchen is happy to keep things vegan.

Book a cooking class for the full experience, or just come for a meal and order the mezze. It is the most memorable food experience in Amman.
