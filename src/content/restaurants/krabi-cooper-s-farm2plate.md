Cooper's Farm2Plate is a vegan kitchen in the Anana Ecological Resort's Permaculture Art Farm, serving nutritious vegan food and drinks from organic and natural ingredients harvested fresh daily from the resort's farm and associated farms. The kitchen processes the food as little as possible to preserve vitamins, minerals and enzymes, and the menu covers salad plates, burgers and seasonal dishes.

Order the salad plate and the vegan burger, and come for lunch or dinner, as the kitchen is open 12pm to 3pm and 6pm to 9pm, Monday to Saturday. It is a short ride from Ao Nang beach, and it is the most farm-direct vegan meal in Krabi.
