Foodhisattva is the small, fully vegan Asian fusion kitchen in Cleveland Heights that locals rate as one of the best vegetarian restaurants in the city: Bangkok tacos, Korean BBQ tofu wings, mushroom scallops and harvest bowls, all of it made from scratch with plant-based, ethically sourced ingredients. The tofu wings are the signature, and the mushroom scallops are the surprise.

It is a carryout cafe, so order ahead and pick up. Get the tacos, add the wings, and enjoy the most creative vegan cooking on the east side.
