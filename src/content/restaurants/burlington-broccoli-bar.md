The Broccoli Bar started as Pingala's roaming vegan food cart and now has a home base inside the Marketplace on the second floor of UVM's Davis Center, open to everyone, not just students. Everything on the menu involves broccoli in some form: the customisable Broccoli Bowl, crunchy tempura florets with maple-sriracha sauce, and tofu-mushroom dumplings in coconut red curry.

Hours follow the university calendar, Monday to Thursday 11am to 4pm and Friday until 2:30pm, and it closes for breaks, so check before a holiday visit. The bowl is the order.
