Happy Chicks is Cincinnati's vegan bakery, and one of the most trusted in the country: a small independent shop on Hamilton Avenue in Northside where everything is made from scratch in a dedicated egg- and dairy-free kitchen. The case rotates through cupcakes, brownies, cookies, croissants and pies, and the bakery is known for custom wedding and celebration cakes, it made PETA's top-10 list for vegan wedding cakes. Savory options include croissant sandwiches, salads and a lunch special.

The shop is open Tuesday to Friday 9am to 4pm and Saturday 9am to 3pm, closed Sunday and Monday. Go for a bourbon cherry cupcake and a cinnamon bun, and order celebration cakes ahead. Street parking is on Hamilton and Blue Rock, with pay lots nearby.
