Ojas is a quaint secret-garden cafe on Chocolate Road, and it is the prettiest room on this list: a relaxed patio, a pink cocktail or two, and a menu of tempeh bowls, salads, pizza and cakes. The owners combine health, flavour and creativity, and the miso peanut and tempeh bowl is the dish to order, hearty enough to leave room for dessert.

It is a vegan and vegetarian eatery rather than an all-vegan one, so file it as vegan-friendly, and come for a slow evening rather than a quick lunch.
