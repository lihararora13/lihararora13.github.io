Falafel Sahyoun is a downtown Beirut legend, the falafel counter that locals and visitors alike queue for. The falafel is crisp outside, green and herby inside, served in a sandwich or as a plate, and it is vegan without anyone having to ask.

Order a falafel sandwich, eat it standing like everyone else, and add a mint tea. This is street food at its best.
