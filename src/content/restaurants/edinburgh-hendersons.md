Hendersons is the reborn version of an Edinburgh institution: the original Hanover Street restaurant opened in 1962 as an outlet for the Henderson family's organic East Lothian farm, and after Covid closed it, the founder's grandson reopened it at Barclay Place near Bruntsfield Links. The cooking is vegetarian with a strong vegan side: vegan haggis in whisky sauce, lion's mane mushroom steak, pumpkin gnocchi, Cullen skink and Biscoff cheesecake, all built on local, seasonal and organic ingredients. It has won Best Vegetarian Establishment at the Food Awards three times running and is Michelin Guide-listed.

The room is cosy and stylish, open every day from noon to ten. Order the lion's mane mushroom steak if it is your first visit, or the Sunday roast with its homemade vegan Yorkshire pudding. This is the table for a long, unhurried lunch that shows off what Scottish vegetables can do.
