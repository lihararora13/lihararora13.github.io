Vegetarian Food Studio is a vegan-friendly Indian restaurant on Penarth Road in Grangetown, and it is the dosa pick on this page. The masala dosa is the signature, with a thali and samosa alongside, all of it hearty and all of it cheap.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so confirm the vegan dishes when you order. The masala dosa is the order.
