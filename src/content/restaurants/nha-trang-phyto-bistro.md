Phyto Bistro is a small hidden restaurant in Nha Trang city serving fresh, healthy and 100% plant-based food, with salad falafel, hummus, cashew cheese, chebureki and kombucha. It is a calm room with big portions, and it suits a relaxed lunch of hummus or a wrap. It is open Monday to Saturday, 11am to 3pm and 5pm to 9pm, closed Sundays.

Order the salad falafel and the hummus, and try the kombucha. Skip it if you want a Vietnamese menu, which this kitchen does not serve.
