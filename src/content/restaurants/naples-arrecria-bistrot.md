Arrecria Bistrot is the Vomero address on this list, a fully vegan bistrot that earned a 5.0 on HappyCow's 2026 Naples round-up. The menu is seasonal and Italian first: pasta, baked dishes and plant-based reworkings of Neapolitan classics, plated simply and priced for a neighbourhood dinner rather than a special occasion.

It is a short ride up from the centre, so pair it with an evening in Vomero rather than squeezing it between historic-centre sights. Book ahead at weekends, when the small room fills fast.
