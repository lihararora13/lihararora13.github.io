Earth Cafe has been Marrakech's meat-free address since 2007, hidden down a lane off Rue Riad Zitoun el Kedim. Follow the signs. The kitchen is fully vegetarian, with several vegan plates daily and produce from the owners' own farm, and it has outlasted every trend that followed it.

Order the vegan plate of the day and a fresh juice. It is the old soul of the medina vegan scene, and the one locals send their friends to first.
