Veganitas is a vegan restaurant in the lower level of the station at the Food-Corner, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, serving freshly baked pitas with smoked plant salmon, tempeh, seitan, jackfruit, Beyond Burger and Planted chicken, plus desserts and coffee. It is the station pick on this list.

Order a pita with tempeh or seitan, a dessert and a coffee, and come any day, since it opens daily in the station food court.
