Veggie Park is a vegetarian restaurant in People's Park on Pyay Road, open daily from 9am or 10am to 7pm or 7:30pm, serving mala xiang guo, curry, pad Thai and potato curry. It is a park restaurant with a relaxed setting, and it suits a slow lunch after the park.

Order the mala xiang guo and the potato curry, and ask for the vegan dishes by name, as the kitchen is vegetarian. It is inexpensive and green, and it is the best park meal in Yangon.
