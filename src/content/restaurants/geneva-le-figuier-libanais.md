Le Figuier libanais rounds out the list with the thing Geneva does best after Ethiopian food: generous, shareable mezze. The vegan spread is the order, a table full of hummus, falafel, salads and warm homemade bread, built for groups and for lingering over.

It is a neighbourhood restaurant rather than a destination, and that is why it works: honest cooking, fair prices, and a menu where vegans can order freely. Bring friends, order the mezze, and let the table do the talking.
