Steffenhagen has been Dresden's vegan anchor since March 2016, opened by the owner of Der Dicke Schmidt, and it still tops every local ranking. The kitchen cooks the food Saxony is known for without a scrap of animal product: schnitzel, seitan steak with ratatouille and sage polenta, vish 'n' chips, red beet tartare, and a daily menu that changes with the season. The room is relaxed and unpretentious, the bar stays open late, and the kitchen serves until ten.

It sits on a quiet Neustadt corner a short walk from the heart of the district, which makes it the easiest first dinner for anyone arriving in the city. Come in the evening, order the schnitzel if it is your first visit, and save room for dessert. This is the table to take someone who thinks vegan food in Saxony means a sad side salad.
