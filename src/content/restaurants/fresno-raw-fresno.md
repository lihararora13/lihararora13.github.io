Raw Fresno is an all-vegan raw food counter in the Galleria at Civic Center, two doors down from Plant Slayer in the same building. The menu is raw tacos, wraps, and juices, the lightest meal in downtown Fresno, and a useful counterweight to the comfort food next door.

Order the raw tacos with avocado, cabbage, and sauce. Pair a visit with Plant Slayer, since the two share the Galleria hallway.
