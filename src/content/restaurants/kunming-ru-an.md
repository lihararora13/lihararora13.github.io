Ru An (儒安·中华植物料理) is Kunming's vegan fine-dining table, on the third floor beside the Dianyue Homestay on Huizhan Middle Road, near the Dianchi International Convention Centre and 521 metres from Dayu Park. The cooking is seasonal plant-based Chinese cuisine, mushroom broth, tofu dishes, root vegetables and steamed vegetable dumplings, and the room is built around the culture of ancient Chinese saints and sages, with a classic reading class on Sunday afternoons.
