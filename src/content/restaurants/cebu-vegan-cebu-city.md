Vegan Cebu City is the weekend buffet pick: a small wholefoods plant-based cafe on Salinas Drive serving fully vegan food, from pasta and mushroom dishes to tofish, desserts and kombucha, with an all-you-can-eat buffet every weekend.

Come on a weekend for the buffet, and save room for dessert. The vegan cakes are famously good, even non-vegans love them.
