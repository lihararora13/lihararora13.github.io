Café Botanik sits beside the botanical garden in the Neuenheim university quarter, a calm, green corner of the city that most visitors never reach. The café serves lunch bowls, homemade pasta variations and creative salads, with vegan options across the menu, and the open kitchen lets you watch the food being made. It is a daytime place, open weekdays, and it suits a slow lunch after the garden rather than a rushed one.

Walk the botanical garden first, then settle in for a bowl and a slice of cake. It is the quietest table on this list, and a good reminder that Heidelberg's vegan scene reaches well beyond the old town.
