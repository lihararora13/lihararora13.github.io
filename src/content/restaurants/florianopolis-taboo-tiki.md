Taboo Tiki Bar is the fun one: a tiki bar in Lagoa da Conceicao with a vegan and vegetarian menu that reviewers call a haven for plant-based lovers, from flower wings and cauliflower wings to tofu dishes and the vegan grill. It is not exclusively vegan, but the vegan and gluten-free cooking is the star, and the tiki atmosphere makes it a night out rather than just a meal.

Come for dinner, order the flower wings and a tofu dish, and stay for a cocktail. This is the one for a fun vegan night by the lagoon.
