Area Four is a wood-fired pizza bar in Kendall Square's Technology Square, a modern, buzzy room with an open oven and a patio. The menu marks vegan and vegetarian options, including a vegan pasta and seasonal pizzas, and the mushroom and fontina pizza is the standout to adapt.

Order the casarecce alle verdure made vegan and ask about the day's vegan pizza. Book ahead on weekends, since the room fills with the Kendall Square crowd.
