Kara Nichha's is Durban's beachfront vegetarian chain, and the North Beach branch puts you right on the Golden Mile. The menu is classic South African Indian vegetarian: curries, breyani, samoosas and snacks, with vegan dishes across the board if you ask. It is open long hours, seven days a week, and works for everyone from families to solo travellers.

Eat here after a morning on the beach. The vegetable breyani is the signature, the bean curry is the vegan workhorse, and the samoosas make a perfect seaside snack.
