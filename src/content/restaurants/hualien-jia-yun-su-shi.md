Jia Yun Su Shi is a simple vegetarian eatery on Ziyou Street with the kitchen out front and seating inside and out, serving kimchi and a variety of noodles and rice dishes. HappyCow reports it fully vegan as of April 2024, and it is cash only and inexpensive, the kind of place locals eat at daily.

Order the noodles and a rice dish with kimchi, and come at lunch or dinner, as the kitchen closes on Wednesdays. It is the cheapest fully vegan meal in Hualien.
