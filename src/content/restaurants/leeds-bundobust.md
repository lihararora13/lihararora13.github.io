Bundobust is Leeds' best-known vegetarian restaurant, and it is the one every visitor should eat at: a craft-beer bar serving Gujarati-style Indian street food, entirely vegetarian and mostly vegan, with okra fries, vada pav, bundo chaat and bhel puri on a menu built for sharing. The decor is recycled wooden doors, the beer is brewed in-house, and the atmosphere is buzzing.

Come with a group, order a combo for the table, and try a bit of everything. This is Leeds' vegan scene at its most joyful, and the best cheap eats in the city centre.
