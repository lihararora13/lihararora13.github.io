Beatles Cafe, also known as 60's Cafe Delmar, is the retro heart of Laxman Jhula: a 1960s rock-themed vegetarian restaurant on Paidal Marg with river views, a wide menu of Indian, continental, vegan and gluten-free dishes, and a 4.5 on HappyCow backed by nearly a thousand TripAdvisor reviews. It is the best-reviewed restaurant in town by several local guides.

Breakfast is the move: the vegan high-protein pancakes and soy milk coffee are the classic order, followed by scrambled tofu, a falafel platter with hummus, and a cold-pressed juice. Come for a long, leisurely meal rather than a quick stop.
