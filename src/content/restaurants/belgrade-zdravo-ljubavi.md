Zdravo Ljubavi, which means hello love, is Belgrade's all-vegan fast-casual favourite. Salads, pasta, sushi, burgers, soups, pancakes, ice cream and raw cakes, all cooked fresh in minutes from local and organic ingredients.

It is quick, bright and genuinely good for a fast lunch. Order a burger, a raw cake, and the hot chocolate that regulars keep mentioning in reviews.
