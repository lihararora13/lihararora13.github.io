Imagine Vegan Cafe is a family-owned, all-vegan cafe in the heart of Cooper-Young, built on the idea that you should never have to worry about what is in your food. The chicken and waffles are the signature, the BBQ sandwich is the lunch order, and the mozzarella sticks and mac and cheese are the sides that make it a proper Southern meal.

It serves breakfast through dinner, seven days a week. Order the chicken and waffles, add the mozzarella sticks, and save room for dessert.
