Full Bloom is a dog-friendly cafe at the old harbour, on Rue Emmanuel Philibert, that happens to be vegan. The stars of the show, in addition to the dogs, are the coffees, with vegan croissants and pain au chocolat early in the day and quiches, omelettes and brunch dishes throughout the day, with indoor and outdoor seating.

It is open Tuesday to Friday from 08:30 to 17:00, and Saturday to Monday with shorter hours. Come early for a croissant, and order the quiche for lunch.
