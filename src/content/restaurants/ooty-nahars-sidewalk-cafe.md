Nahar's Sidewalk Cafe is the Charing Cross institution, one of the most revered vegetarian restaurants in Ooty, and the one the guides call the regulars' pick. The wood-fired pizzas are the draw, with Nilgiri Special and Margherita on the board, and the kitchen explicitly offers gluten-free and vegan options alongside the Italian menu.

Order the Nilgiri Special pizza without cheese and the spaghetti Neapolitan. It is the best-value sit-down in Ooty, and it stays open through the winter season.
