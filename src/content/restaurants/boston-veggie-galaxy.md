Veggie Galaxy is the place you bring your most skeptical friend, the one who says "but where would I get a burger?" This classic American diner in Central Square does everything a diner should do, pancakes to Reubens to pie, with an entirely vegetarian menu that can be made fully vegan on request. Guy Fieri featured it on Diners, Drive-Ins, and Dives, which tells you everything about the comfort-food credentials.

Do not skip the towering dessert case: the on-site vegan bakery turns out pies and Taza chocolate cakes that rotate by the counter like edible carnival rides. Breakfast, brunch, lunch, dinner, late-night slices of pie, Veggie Galaxy covers every meal of the day and every craving that comes with it.
