Amar's is the neighbourhood pick on this list: a family-run pure-veg restaurant on Gangapur Road, opposite Someshwar Temple, that locals choose for an uncomplicated North Indian dinner. No themes, no fuss, just a dining room that has earned its regulars.

Order the dal tadka without ghee, a mixed veg sabzi and hot rotis, and you have a proper, cheap, satisfying meal. It is the quiet end to this guide, and sometimes the quiet ones are the best.
