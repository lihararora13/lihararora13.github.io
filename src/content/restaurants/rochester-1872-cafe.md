1872 Cafe is the mixed-menu pick in this guide and the practical one: a downtown cafe on West Main Street with a separate vegan menu, so ordering is straightforward rather than a negotiation. The vegan offerings cover the cafe canon, a burger, bagels with vegan cream cheese, sandwiches, soup and a vegan plate, at budget prices and with morning hours that suit an early start. It is the only non-vegan kitchen in the Rochester eight, included because its vegan menu is distinct and well used.

Hours are Monday to Friday 8am to 2pm and Saturday to Sunday 8am to 1pm. Come for breakfast or an early lunch, and ask for the vegan menu on arrival.
