Manda de Laos is an upscale Lao restaurant by the pond in the centre of Luang Prabang, with vegan-friendly Lao dishes and a vegetarian sample platter that can be made vegan. It is the most formal Lao table in the city, and it suits a special evening.

Order the vegetarian sample platter and ask for it vegan, and try the tofu dishes with sticky rice. Its pin is approximate to Chao Fa Ngum Road.
