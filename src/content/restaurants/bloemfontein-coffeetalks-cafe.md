CoffeeTalks is a small cafe with a clearly labelled menu, which is rarer than it should be in Bloemfontein: a vegan falafel meal, pita with olive oil and za'atar, vegan shawarma, vegan muesli and a customisable mezze plate with hummus and tabouleh.

Order the falafel meal or build a mezze plate. Note the hours, it closes Mondays and Saturdays, and it is a daytime cafe rather than a dinner spot.
