Combi is tucked just behind Byron's main street, and its chic, Instagram-ready decor is only half the story. The vegan toasted sandwich is the thing people write home about: soft pumpkin, melted vegan cheese and crispy oiled toast, pure comfort food. The rest of the menu is just as thoughtful, with acai bowls, fresh juices and healthy cafe plates that taste as good as they look.

Come for breakfast or a light lunch, order the toasted sandwich, and grab a juice for the beach. This is the pretty little cafe that actually delivers on the food.
