Azur is the restaurant in the Four Seasons Hotel Tunis in Gammarth, and it is the most serious vegan menu in the city after Inward: a four-page vegan menu covering breakfast, Tunisian local dishes, Middle Eastern dishes, pasta and risotto. It is open daily from noon to 7:00pm.

Ask for the vegan menu on arrival and order a Tunisian dish with pasta or risotto. It is the priciest table on this list, and the one to book for a proper sit-down meal.
