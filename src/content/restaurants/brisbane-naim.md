NAÏM is the table for the mixed group, and the vegans eat best of all. Perched in a charming old worker's cottage on a Paddington hill, this Middle Eastern restaurant runs two dinner menus: one entirely plant-based, one for omnivores, so nobody compromises. The plant-based side leans on grilled chicken schwarma, baba ghanouj tartlets, hummus bowls and a plant-based eggplant and braised beef moussaka, all built on fresh, locally sourced ingredients.

Come for brunch or dinner, book ahead on weekends, and order the schwarma. It is the rare vegan-friendly room where the plant-based menu feels like the main event, not the afterthought.
