Bhoj Thali is the thali house on this list: a Samarth Nagar dining room near the Central Bus Stand where the whole point is a full plate, dal, sabzi, roti, rice and salad, for very little money. It is the most complete meal in the city for the least rupees.

Ask for the thali without curd and ghee, and the plate is properly plant-based. Come hungry, because the thali does not believe in small portions.
