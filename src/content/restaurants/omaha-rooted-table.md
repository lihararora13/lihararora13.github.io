Rooted Table is a from-scratch, plant-based cafe on Shamrock Road in Countryside Village, serving cold-pressed juices, smoothies, and meals. The Mane Street Crab Cakes, lion's mane mushroom patties with vegan tartar, are the signature, and the Choco-Banana Bliss Bowl and Crystal Blue juice round out the menu.

Order the Mane Street crab cakes and the Choco-Banana Bliss Bowl. It is a daytime stop, open Monday through Saturday 8am to 4pm.
