Annapurna's is the table Santa Fe vegans have relied on for years, an Ayurvedic cafe where the menu leans on warming spices, coconut, and dosa bread instead of cheese and cream. The roasted cauliflower tacos are the dish regulars name first, and the kitchari, chapati, and cardamom blueberry pancakes round out a menu that works for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

The Santa Fe cafe moved to 1620 St. Michael's Drive, so use that address rather than the older Don Diego Avenue listing. Order the tacos, add a chai, and save room for the coconut desserts.
