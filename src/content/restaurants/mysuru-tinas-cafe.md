Tina's is one of the small cafes that make Gokulam walkable: a main-road stop where the yoga crowd grabs breakfast before class and the menu covers the vegan basics without making a performance of it. It is not the fanciest room in the quarter, which is exactly its charm.

Order the vegan breakfast plate and a fresh juice, and you will be fed and out the door in time for the morning. Simple, cheap, reliable.
