CLEAN FOOD is a health-focused café on the Dr.-Gessler-Straße, west of the old town, built around nutritional information and a wide range of vegan, vegetarian, allergen-friendly and diabetic-friendly options. The menu runs from falafel and hummus through avocado toast to tiramisu and crème brûlée, and the room is bright, modern and open seven days a week until nine. It is the most health-conscious table on this list, and it wears that plainly.

It is a short bus ride from the centre, and worth it for the falafel bowl alone. Order the bowl, add a vegan tiramisu, and treat it as the light lunch that still feels like a proper meal.
