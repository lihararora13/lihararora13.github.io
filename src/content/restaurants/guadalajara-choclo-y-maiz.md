Choclo y Maíz is a small, cheerful all-vegan restaurant where the tacos do the talking. Jackfruit birria, mushroom carnitas, chilaquiles, burritos and acai bowls, all made fresh, all very reasonably priced.

Order the jackfruit birria tacos and a burrito to share. Reviewers keep saying the same thing: even the meat-eaters at the table were won over.
