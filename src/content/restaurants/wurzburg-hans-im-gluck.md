Hans im Glück has been serving vegan burgers for over fifteen years, and the Marienplatz branch in Würzburg carries the full range: plant-based burgers, salads, bowls and snacks, plus cocktails in the evening. The chain was ranked among Germany's most vegan-friendly by ProVeg, and the Würzburg branch is the reliable table for a group that wants burgers, beer and a proper sit-down meal rather than a counter.

It sits on the Marienplatz, in the middle of the old town. Order a vegan burger with sweet potato fries, add a salad bowl to share, and treat it as the easy evening that pleases everyone.
