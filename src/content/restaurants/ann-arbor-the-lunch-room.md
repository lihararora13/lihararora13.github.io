The Lunch Room is Ann Arbor's all-vegan bakery and cafe, tucked into Huron Towers near North Campus, and it punches above its size: bagel sandwiches like the Hangover Cure and the Billie Jean Biscuit, a vegan bibimbap, a banh mi chay, and a bakery case that regulars plan around.

It is a weekday lunch spot, open Monday to Friday until 2pm, so plan it as a lunch rather than a dinner. The bagel sandwiches are the move.
