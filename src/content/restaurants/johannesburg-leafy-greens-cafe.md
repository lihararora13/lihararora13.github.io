Leafy Greens has been flying the flag for plant-based eating in Johannesburg for years, and it is still one of the best fully vegan restaurants in the city. It sits on the Casalinga organic farm out in Muldersdrift, a short drive from the city, and this is farm-to-table in its truest form: whole-food cooking that does not lean on processed meat substitutes, a health shop and deli on the same premises, and a weekend buffet that is a kaleidoscope of colour.

It is rustic, serene and restorative, more wellness retreat than restaurant, and worth the drive for the setting alone. Order the Shakshuka Stack and the Chili Protein Bowl, go for the weekend buffet if you can, and save room for the raw chocolate cake. Open Wednesday to Sunday, 9am to 4pm, closed Monday and Tuesday.
