Michel MaBelle is Bordeaux's first fully plant-based pastry shop, and it is exactly as charming as the name suggests: a cosy café in Saint-Michel where everything is homemade with local, seasonal products, from banoffee tarts and madeleines to chocolatines, praliné tartelettes and scones, with gluten-free options and coffee roasted in Bordeaux.

It is open daily and it is the sweet ending this list needs. Order a banoffee tart, add a chocolatine for the road, and do not be surprised if you come back tomorrow.
