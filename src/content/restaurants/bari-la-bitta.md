La Bitta is a small intimate bar-restaurant in a narrow Bari Vecchia alley, run by Onofrio and Patrizia, with a full vegan menu on display. A September 2025 vegan dinner there ran to a puccia sandwich with soy shreds, a pinsa with arugula, tomatoes and vegan cheese, and a vegan gluten-free gianduia cheesecake the owner added to the menu on the spot after it was praised.

Order the puccia with soy shreds, the pinsa with vegan cheese and the gianduia cheesecake, and book ahead, since the room is tiny and the long table fills fast.
