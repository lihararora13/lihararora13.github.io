Balino Yoga Café is Seville's healthy vegan cafe, all vegan and gluten-free friendly, with bowls, salads and smoothies that look like they were styled for a magazine and taste even better. The staff know their allergies, the room is calm, and the food is genuinely nourishing.

Order a bowl and a smoothie, and use it as the light lunch between heavier tapas crawls. It is the reset button Seville's vegan scene needed.
