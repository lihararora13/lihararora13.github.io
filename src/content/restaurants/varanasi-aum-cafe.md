Aum Cafe is the soul of this guide. Sixteen years ago Sangeeta Awasthi opened a vegan cafe in a small lane near Assi Ghat, run entirely by women cooks, servers and artisans, and it is still going strong. The menu is alive with pictures and community stories, and the chocolate banana pancakes have a following all of their own.

Come for breakfast after the morning aarti, order the daal chilla and the vegan chocolate cake, and stay for the calm. It is fully vegan, fully women-run, and the rare cafe that feels like a small act of resilience served warm.
