Halo by Loving Earth is the second act from the Loving Earth team: a plant-based cafe, gelato bar and spa that opened on Peter Celli Street in September 2026, behind a bright-pink facade with warm orange walls, wooden beams and marble tables inside. It is listed as vegan on HappyCow and holds a 4.8 on Justdial, with the venue's own Instagram showing it open daily from 9am to 10:30pm.

Order the Notzerella Sticks, breaded vegan mozzarella with marinara, the Mrs Rosemary Pizza with plant-based meat and cashew cheese, and a Coconut Affogato served in a coconut shell, then finish with a scoop from the gelato case. It is a short walk from the original Loving Earth cafe on Napier Street.
