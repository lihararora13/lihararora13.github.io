NIYA is Morocco's first fully vegan restaurant, five years running, working with local organic produce on a seasonal, ever-changing menu, with filtered water instead of bottled water and a small zero-waste shop. The menu covers burgers, pasta alla barba, Musakhan rolls and desserts, and the restaurant also hosts concerts, talks and workshops.

It is open Tuesday to Friday from noon to 8:00pm, Saturdays from 10:00am, and Sundays until 5:00pm. Order a burger and the pasta alla barba. Note there are two NIYA listings in Casablanca; the Rue Ain Asserdoun address on this page is the one to use.
