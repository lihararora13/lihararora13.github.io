The Heaven Cafe and Restro sits four floors up in MP Nagar, and the climb is part of the point: a veg multicuisine café with a rooftop feel, open late, serving Chinese platters, continental mains and coffee until past midnight. It is a young crowd's hangout, buzzy rather than hushed, and the kitchen is used to vegetarian orders of every kind.

Vegan ordering here is straightforward: pick the vegetable-led Chinese and continental dishes, ask for no butter, cheese or egg, and the plates come back as ordered. Come for the view and the late hours as much as the food.
