Kailash Veg is the neighbourhood workhorse of New Usmanpura: a pure-veg restaurant on Railway Station Road that locals choose when they want a solid North Indian dinner without any fuss. It is close to the station, which makes it an easy first or last meal in the city.

Order the dal tadka without ghee, a mixed veg sabzi and hot rotis, and you have a proper, cheap, satisfying plate. It is the unpretentious heart of this guide.
