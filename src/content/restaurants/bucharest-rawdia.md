Rawdia is one of Bucharest's raw-vegan pioneers, a raw food restaurant with a light menu of raw vegan dishes, smoothies and juices, all gluten-free, lactose-free and refined-sugar-free. The daily menu is the order, and the raw cakes are the reason to come.

Everything is vegan except for honey in some cakes and drinks, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan: ask whether a dish contains honey. Hours vary by day and it closes early on Saturday, so check before you go.
