Vegan Bunker calls itself Chile's first vegan restaurant, and after more than a decade in Barrio Italia it still feels like the heart of the scene. The colourful house on Fresia serves the full comfort playbook: burgers, a daily menu of homestyle dishes, and a cake selection that visitors keep writing home about.

The dish to share is the vegan chorrillana, the classic Chilean pile-up of fries, onions and plant-based meat, reimagined without a single animal product. It is the table that best explains why this place became a Santiago institution, and why the 2026 city guides still send every plant-based visitor here first.
