Oak is the new name for the room that was Acorn, Bath's Michelin-starred vegetable restaurant, and it keeps the same ambition: tasting menus built around vegetables, with vegan courses available throughout. It is the most grown-up table on this list.

Book the tasting menu and say vegan when you book so the kitchen plans around it. This is the special-occasion pick, and the one to choose for a proper night out.
