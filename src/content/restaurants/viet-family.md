Why we love it — Commercial Drive has no shortage of Vietnamese kitchens,
but Viet Family earns its 4.8 stars by doing everything plant-based without
losing a drop of the flavor the cuisine demands. The pho broth is rich, the
portions are generous, and the room feels like family.

This is the spot for a rainy-night bowl of something restorative. Order the
pho, add the spring rolls, and thank us on your way out.
