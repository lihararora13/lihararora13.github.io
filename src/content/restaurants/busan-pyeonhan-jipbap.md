Pyeonhan Jipbap is the comfort-food twin of Soban Vegan: another home-style vegan restaurant run by a grandmother, serving hearty soups, noodles, dumplings and soy chicken. It is pure comfort, and very affordable.

Order a soup and a plate of dumplings. It is the warmest meal on the Busan list.
