The Ruined Garden is all atmosphere, a flower-filled hideaway set inside the ruins of an old riad. It is not fully vegan, but it is famous for its pastries and Moroccan desserts, and the savoury side has a smoked aubergine salad, vegan-friendly soups, salads and tagines, with tapas that are vegan.

Order the smoked aubergine salad and keep ordering the vegan tapas. It is the prettiest room in Fes, so come for lunch when the light is good.
