Bageri Leve is a bakery on Östra Rönneholmsvägen in the Centrum, and it is the morning stop on this page. The case holds croissants, cinnamon rolls and pistachio donuts, with plenty of vegan options among them, and the cinnamon roll is the order.

One honest note: the guide says it has lots of vegan options, and a commenter noted it serves dairy too, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan. Check the vegan case before ordering.
