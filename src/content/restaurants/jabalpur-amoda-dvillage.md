Amoda D'Village is the most atmospheric room on this list, and possibly in the whole region: a pure-veg restaurant built like a village, with thatched roofs, wooden bridges, stone paving, hanging lanterns and marigold garlands, tucked behind Dadda Parisar near Madan Mahal. Visitors call it a little escape from time, and the comments under its reels are unusually affectionate, one awarding it seven stars out of five.

The food is pure-veg Indian, served in the slow village spirit, so order the thali without ghee and settle in for the evening. Come at dusk, when the lanterns come on and the place earns its reputation.
