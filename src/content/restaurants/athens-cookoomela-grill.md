Cookoomela Grill was the first vegan souvlaki shop in Greece, and it still runs like a proper Athens grill, with outdoor tables made for watching Exarcheia go by. The kitchen builds its whole menu around the mushroom, serving gyros, pita wraps, kebabs and dessert on a Mediterranean plant-based diet, in biodegradable packaging, and it holds a perfect 5.0 from more than 400 HappyCow reviews.

Order the mushroom gyros, which comes with fresh potato wedges, and do not skip the red one if you like a little heat. It is a small, busy, beloved spot, and the kind of place locals dream about living next door to.
