Vege Fino Za Sve, which means everything for everyone, is a vegan fast-food counter and organic shop in one. Burgers, dogs, sausage burgers and seitan bolognese, all made with organic ingredients and a lot of good cheer.

It is a weekday lunch spot, open until six, so plan an early dinner elsewhere. Order the burger, add fries, and browse the little organic shop on the way out.
