Plumeria is proof that mock meat can be a thing of beauty. The flower cups with plant-based tuna, the frizzled mock duck, the tempura "chicken" in tangerine sauce: dish after dish, it delivers the full sweet-sour-salty-spicy Thai playbook with zero fish sauce and no animal products anywhere (egg is an optional add-on in a few dishes). The review pages are full of carnivores insisting they would never have guessed.

It is an eco-conscious operation too: bring your own takeout containers and get a dollar off. Casual, kind, and consistently excellent, it is the kind of neighborhood spot every city wishes it had.
