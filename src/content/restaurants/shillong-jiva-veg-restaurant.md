Jiva Veg is the name every Shillong vegetarian gives first: a South Indian pure-veg restaurant on G.S. Road in Police Bazar, above the Barbeque Restaurant, that local guides call the city's favourite. It is open 8:30 to 9:30 daily, serving veg thali, mini idli, sambar and puri sabzi at moderate prices.

Order the veg thali or the mini idli with sambar, ask for no ghee and no curd, and you have the most reliable veg meal in Shillong. It is the anchor of the Police Bazar veg cluster.
