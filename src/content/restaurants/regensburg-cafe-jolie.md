Cafe Jolie is a café on the Pfauengasse doing waffles, ice cream, hot dogs and bistro dishes, with vegan options across the menu and a bright, friendly room in the old town. It opens at nine seven days a week, which makes it a useful breakfast and cake stop, and the waffles are the dish regulars recommend. It is not a vegan restaurant, it is a café that takes its vegan guests seriously, and that is enough.

Order a vegan waffle and a coffee, add a scoop of vegan ice cream if the sun is out, and treat it as the sweet stop on a walk through the old town.
