Kowloon City is Hong Kong's Thai quarter, and this unassuming South Wall Road eatery is its plant-based anchor: no frills, no bookings, just a long menu of Thai classics cooked vegetarian and happily adapted for vegans. Green Queen counts it among the city's best 100 percent vegan-friendly kitchens for a reason.

Order the tom yum properly hot, the fried monkey head mushrooms crisp, and a pomelo salad to cool things down, then wander the wet market streets outside. It is the cheapest thrill in this guide, and one of the tastiest.
