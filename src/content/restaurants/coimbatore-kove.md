KOVE is the modern veg dining room on the second floor of Hotel Annapoorna in RS Puram, the upmarket sibling to the tiffin hall below. It does the North Indian and international veg repertoire in a quieter, more polished room, and it appears on the city's pure-veg directories as a current favourite.

Order a veg platter and a North Indian curry, and ask for the dairy to be held. It is the dinner pick in RS Puram when the tiffin rooms feel too hurried.
