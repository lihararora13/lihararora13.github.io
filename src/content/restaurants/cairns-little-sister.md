Little Sister is an Asian street food restaurant on the Esplanade in Cairns, with a vegan and vegetarian Feed Me Banquet. It is a lively, modern room on the waterfront, and it suits a group dinner.

Order the Feed Me Banquet and ask for the vegan version. Its pin is approximate to the Esplanade.
