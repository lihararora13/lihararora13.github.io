Tea by the Lock is the outlier on this list and worth the trip: a fully vegan cafe beside the River Ouse at Naburn, a pleasant cycle or bus ride from the centre. The room is small, the cakes are the draw, and the lock-side setting does the rest.

Order cake and a cream tea, and plan the visit as a half-day outing rather than a quick stop. Check opening days before you go, as the cafe keeps shorter winter hours.
