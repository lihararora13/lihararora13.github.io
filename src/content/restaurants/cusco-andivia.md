ANDIVIA is Cusco's newest vegan star: a 100 percent vegan Peruvian restaurant reimagining traditional dishes with Andean ingredients, from the team behind VIDA Vegan Bistro. It sits on the second floor on Calle Palacio, steps from the Plaza de Armas, with outdoor seating and a menu that treats Andean food with real respect.

Book ahead, order the Andean tasting plate, and try the quinoa risotto. This is the one for the most exciting new vegan cooking in Cusco.
