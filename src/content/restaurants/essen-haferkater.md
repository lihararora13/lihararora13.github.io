Haferkater is the porridge counter inside Essen Hauptbahnhof, the breakfast stop for anyone catching an early train. The range is porridge with a choice of toppings and milks, and the vegan move is simple: choose the plant-based porridge toppings and plant milk explicitly, as the wider range is vegetarian and vegan rather than fully vegan.

It is the earliest vegan-friendly breakfast in Essen and the most practical pick on this list for travellers. Order a porridge with oat milk and fruit toppings, eat it on the platform, and treat it as fuel rather than a destination meal.
