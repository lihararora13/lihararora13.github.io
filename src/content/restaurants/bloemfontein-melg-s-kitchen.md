MelG's Kitchen is a vegetarian home kitchen in Bayswater cooking Indian food with a daily vegan menu, and reviewers call the pakoras excellent and the dal fry perfect. It is takeaway only and orders need at least a day's notice, placed via WhatsApp or Instagram.

Order a day ahead, ask for the vegan menu, and do not forget the homemade chilli sauce. This is the most-loved vegan cooking in Bloemfontein, but it rewards planning.
