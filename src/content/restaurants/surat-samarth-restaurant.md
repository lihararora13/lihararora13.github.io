Samarth is the old-school anchor of this list: a pure-veg North Indian restaurant on Ring Road that Surat locals have trusted for years, with a 4.4 star rating to show for it. No themes, no gimmicks, just solid vegetarian cooking done the way it has always been done.

Vegans order the classics and ask for the ghee held: dal tadka, mixed veg sabzi, hot tandoori rotis and a veg pulao. It is the cheapest, most unpretentious meal on this guide, and sometimes that is exactly the point.
