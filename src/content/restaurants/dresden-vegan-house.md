Vegan House is Dresden's all-vegan East Asian kitchen, and its format is the fun part: more than twenty small bowls designed for sharing, two or three per person, so a table can taste its way across the menu. The signatures are the Green Tofu Stick, crispy tofu skewers in green rice flakes with teriyaki and raspberry sauce, a vegan burger on a steamed bun with soy patty, mango and hoisin, and seitan satay, alongside soups, larger bowls and desserts. Everything on the menu is vegan, confirmed again in January 2026.

The Neustadt branch on Alaunstrasse is the one to visit, with a garden area out back that fills up in summer, so book ahead for dinner. There is a second location at Schillerplatz across the river, but this is the original and the livelier of the two. Order a spread of small bowls, share everything, and let the table argue over favourites.
