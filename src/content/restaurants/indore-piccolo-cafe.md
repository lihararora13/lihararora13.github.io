Piccolo is the café you photograph before you sit down: hanging tassels, soft light, and the kind of room that makes an ordinary coffee feel like a small event. The menu leans Italian, which is good news for vegans, because tomato-based pastas, bruschetta and no-cheese pizzas need almost no translation at all.

Ask about plant milk before ordering the cappuccino, and keep the order simple: the kitchen does its best work when the dish is built around vegetables, herbs and good olive oil rather than a pile of cheese. A lovely stop for a slow afternoon in Vijay Nagar.
