The Sprout is the vegetarian cafe at Verite in Auroville, open from 7 in the morning to 4 in the afternoon, and the 2026 reviews read like a love letter to slow mornings: the view, the coffee, no rush, and a tiramisu one reviewer calls literally the best. The menu lists lattes, cappuccino, tiramisu, chia bowl and matcha latte.

Order the chia bowl and a matcha latte, and ask about the vegan options. It is the most expensive cafe on this list, and the most unhurried.
