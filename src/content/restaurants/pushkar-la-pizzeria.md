La Pizzeria is Pushkar's pizza stop, a small room at Varah Ghat where the ovens run hot and the lake is steps away. It is the most Italian meal on this list, and the easiest vegan order in town: a pizza without cheese, loaded with vegetables.

Add a bruschetta and a garden salad, and you have a proper lakeside dinner. It is simple, satisfying, and exactly what a Pushkar evening calls for.
