Bistro57 sits by the Botanical Garden metro station and does exactly what a commuter cafe should: quick, cheap, cheerful plates of pizza, sandwiches and cold coffee, with outdoor seating and delivery for the rest of the day.

It is a vegetarian cafe with a vegan-friendly bench, so order the veg pizza without cheese and the cold coffee with plant milk. The staff are famously cheerful, and the prices are student-friendly.
