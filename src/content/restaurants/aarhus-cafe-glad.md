Café Glad is a vegan-friendly café on Frederiks Allé in Frederiksbjerg, south of the centre, and it is the neighbourhood pick on this page. The lunch plate is the signature, with cakes and coffee alongside, in a calm room that suits a slow afternoon.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so check the vegan dishes on the menu. The lunch plate and a cake are the orders.
