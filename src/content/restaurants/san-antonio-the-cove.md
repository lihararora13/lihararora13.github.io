The Cove is San Antonio's gloriously weird vegan-friendly stop: a laundromat, car wash and cafe in Monte Vista where you can throw in a load of laundry, order a vegan club and a vegan bacon cheeseburger, and get your car washed. The vegan menu is clearly defined, the burgers are homemade, and the fries are cut in-house.

It is kid-friendly, dog-friendly, and very San Antonio. Order the vegan club, add fries, and enjoy the only vegan meal on the list that comes with clean laundry.
