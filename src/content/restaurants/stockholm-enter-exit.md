Enter/Exit does the joyful, messy end of vegan eating: a fully vegan fast-casual kitchen inside the Bacchi Syre building at Jarntorget, all burgers, comfort plates and organic coffee. HappyCow rates it 4.5 and lists the menu clearly, and reviewers in 2026 describe enormous mac and cheese and lunches that sell out by one, which is always a good sign.

Go for the Say Cheese Burger or the No-Cluck, or the Something Fishy take on fish and chips, and time a weekend visit for brunch. It is the Old Town's easiest vegan lunch, with outdoor seating when the Stockholm sun cooperates.
