Soul's is the breakfast café at the northern edge of this list, up in Kaiserswerth: vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free, with the motto food for your soul. The drinks are the draw, iced matcha lattes, dirty chai, golden milk, espresso tonics, and the breakfast plates are generous and kind.

Come for a slow morning, order a matcha latte and a breakfast plate, and enjoy the quieter side of Düsseldorf. Food for your soul, indeed.
