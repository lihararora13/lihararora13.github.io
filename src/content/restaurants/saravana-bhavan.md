Why we love it — Yes, it is a chain. It is also the single most reliable
dosa in Delhi, served in a no-nonsense dining hall on Janpath where the
line at peak hours is part of the ritual. The menu is pure vegetarian,
most of it is vegan or one substitution away, and the prices belong to
another decade.

The move is the mini tiffin: a greatest-hits platter of idli, pongal,
kesari, and a small masala dosa that lets you sample the whole kitchen in
one sitting. Tourists find it, locals swear by it, and the filter coffee
is non-negotiable. Some institutions are institutions for a reason.
