Nicezeit is an ice cream parlour in Linden with the largest vegan selection in Hannover, and it is the dessert stop on this list. The vegan range is serious: scoops, sundaes and waffles, made properly rather than as a token flavour or two. It is not a fully vegan shop, though: dairy ice cream is sold alongside the vegan range, so order from the vegan side and say so clearly.

It sits on Velberstraße in Linden-Nord, a short walk from Rüpel. Order a couple of vegan scoops, add a sundae if you are in the mood, and enjoy the fact that a proper ice cream parlour in Hannover takes its vegan range this seriously. This is the table for dessert, and a good end to a Linden evening.
