Furahaa is a fully vegan fast food restaurant on Rue Lafaille, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, hiring exclusively deaf staff. The new Furahaa Express opened on 17 December 2025 at the same address, and the menu is the vegan version of the major fast food chains, but better quality: burgers, cheesy fries, nuggets and milkshakes.

It is the junk-food pick on this list, and the most fun dinner in Toulouse. Order a burger and the cheesy fries.
