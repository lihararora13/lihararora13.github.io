Harilal's has been a Patna name since 1996, and its outlets now spread across the whole city, from Kankarbagh to Boring Road. It is renowned for sweets and hygiene, and the restaurant side serves the pure-veg food that lands it in the city's top veg guides, with a 4.1 rating from around 6,000 reviews.

Come for the thali and stay for the sweets counter, though most of the mithai is dairy-based, so vegans should treat it as a look, not a lunch. The Kankarbagh Road branch is the one to visit.
