The Coconut Tree brings Sri Lankan street food to St Clement's: tapas-style plates built for sharing, signature cocotails and a relaxed island vibe, with extensive vegan and gluten-free options across the menu.

Order the cashew curry and the kotthu, and share a parotta rotti across the table. It is a meat-serving restaurant with a strong vegan bench, so file it as vegan-friendly, and book ahead on Friday and Saturday nights.
