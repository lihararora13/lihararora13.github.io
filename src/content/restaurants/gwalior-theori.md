Theori is Gwalior's newest fine-dining arrival, and it arrived with intent: a bright third-floor room in City Centre, teal chairs, draped fabric ceilings, woven pendant lights and a leaf logo that says vegetarian bliss. The headline is a ₹499 unlimited buffet, which the city's creators have been filming with undisguised delight since the doors opened.

The buffet is the move, and the kitchen's Jain-friendly approach means vegetable-led dishes are easy to find. Ask about ghee and butter on the curries, load up on the noodles, salads and stir-fries, and enjoy the most polished room in the city at the friendliest price on this list.
