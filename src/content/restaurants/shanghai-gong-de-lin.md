Gong De Lin has been serving mock duck on Nanjing Road since 1922, out of a Buddhist tradition that turned wheat gluten and tofu skin into meat centuries before anyone had heard of a veggie burger. The three-floor heritage building is a Shanghai institution, and its vegetarian technique is on China's national intangible heritage list.

Go for breakfast, when the doors open at 7am, or for a classic family-style lunch. Order the mock duck first, then the vegetarian crab roe and the sweet and sour pork that is not pork.
