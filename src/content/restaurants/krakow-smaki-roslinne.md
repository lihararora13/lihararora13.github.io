Smaki Roslinne is Krakow's daily-menu vegan restaurant, and it does one thing beautifully: a short, fresh menu that changes every day, with soup, a choice of mains and dessert, served from noon until the food is gone.

It is a five-star favourite and the most seasonal meal on this list. Arrive early, order whatever is on the board, and trust the kitchen. The pierogi ruskie and the lemon risotto are regular stars.
