Harvest Clean Eats is the quick wholesome meal in downtown Halifax, a health-food restaurant with clearly labelled vegan bowls, salads and toasts. The Harvest bowl, the Habibi salad and the avocado toast are the vegan orders to make, and the Market Street location is the central one to use.

It is open daily from 7:30am to 9pm, which makes it the early-breakfast and late-evening vegan-friendly fallback downtown. A second location sits across the harbour in Dartmouth on Portland Street.
