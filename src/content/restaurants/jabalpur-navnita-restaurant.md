Navnita has been feeding Jabalpur's vegetarians since 1986, and it shows in the confidence of the room: a 4.5-star institution opposite Anand Cinema, where the chole bhature and the thalis are the dishes people drive across town for. The tagline is destination for veggies, and forty years of regulars suggest it earned the claim.

Order the chole bhature and a veg thali without ghee, and you are eating the city's classic vegetarian meal in the room that defined it. A must-visit, and the historical heart of this list.
