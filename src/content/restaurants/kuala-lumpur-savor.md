Savor is the Hartamas kitchen for people who read ingredient lists for fun: a fully plant-based restaurant built on whole foods and bold flavour, where the founders' lifestyle philosophy shows up in every bowl. Vegan Food Quest called the food simply outstanding after a recent delivery feast, and The Yum List's updated 2026 coverage agrees it bridges healthy and joyful like few places manage.

Work through the signatures: Sarawak laksa, the tofu and soy protein Laab Nam Tok, a Nasi Kerabu you will not want to share, and the silken tofu and dark chocolate Silky Decadence to finish. Just do not plan it for a Saturday, when Savor closes for community work.
