KAUAI is South Africa's island-inspired health chain, and the Henry Street branch is the most reliable vegan bet in central Bloemfontein: vegan items are denoted on the menu, from avocado toast and the spicy avo greens bowl to the plant power salad or wrap and the No-Bull burgers, with plant milk for drinks.

Order the plant power salad or a No-Bull burger. There are two more branches at Preller Square and Langenhoven Park if this one is out of your way.
