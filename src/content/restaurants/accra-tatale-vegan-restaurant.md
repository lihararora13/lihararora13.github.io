Tatale is the restaurant that puts Accra on the vegan map. It is fully vegan, run by a chef who cooked at London's renowned 222 Vegan, and the menu reimagines Ghanaian food without losing it: fufu with palm nut soup, egusi with boiled yam, coconut daal, tofu stir-fry, plus kosayi fritters and pineapple crumble cake. Reviewers keep coming back for the sharing platter, and for good reason.

Book ahead, order the Tatale Garden platter for the table, and finish with the pineapple crumble and a sobolo or bissap drink. This is the one to take someone who thinks Ghanaian food cannot be vegan.
