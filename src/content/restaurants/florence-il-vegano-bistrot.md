Il Vegano Bistrot is the fully vegan bistro on Via San Gallo near San Marco, formerly Veggy Days, serving lasagne, pasta, burgers and tiramisù with alcoholic drinks alongside. The lasagne is the dish the guides name, and the pasta with tomato sauce and pesto plus fries is the other order.

It is open Monday to Saturday for lunch and dinner, from noon to 3pm and 6:30pm to 10pm. Book ahead in the evening, since the room is small.
