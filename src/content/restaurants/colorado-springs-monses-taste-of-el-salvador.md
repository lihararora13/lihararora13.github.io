Monse's Taste of El Salvador, also listed as Monse's Pupuseria, is a Salvadoran kitchen on South 25th Street doing pupusas, curtido, and bean and rice plates. The vegan pupusas are the draw, and the room is a small, family-run counter rather than a polished dining room.

Order the vegan pupusas with curtido and a bean and rice plate. Confirm the vegan filling when you order, since the standard pupusa uses cheese.
