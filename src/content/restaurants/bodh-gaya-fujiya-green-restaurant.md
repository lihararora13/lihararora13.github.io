Fujiya Green is the Japanese end of Bodh Gaya's international cafe scene: a restaurant near Kalachakra Maidan doing Japanese, Chinese and Indian under one roof, popular with Japanese pilgrims. The spinach momos and hot and sour soup are the dishes the guides name, and it is rated 3.6 out of 5.

Order the spinach momos and the hot and sour soup, and ask the kitchen to skip the egg in the noodles. It is the most international kitchen on this list, and a good change from thalis and momos.
