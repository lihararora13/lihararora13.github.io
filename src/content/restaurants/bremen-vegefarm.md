Vegefarm has been Bremen's vegan Taiwanese-Chinese table for years, and it cooks the kind of food that converts sceptics without trying: soya mock meats with real texture, peanut noodles with depth, udon in proper broth, all of it fully plant-based. The room is warm and old-school, the portions are generous, and the menu is long enough that regulars have their own orders and newcomers need a minute to choose.

It sits on Hamburger Straße in the Östliche Vorstadt, an easy walk from the Steintor quarter. Order the peanut noodles if it is your first visit, add a soya mock-meat dish to share, and do not skip the udon. This is the table to take someone who thinks vegan food cannot be hearty.
