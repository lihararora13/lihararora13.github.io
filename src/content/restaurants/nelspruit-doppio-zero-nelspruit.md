Doppio Zero is a continental-style cafe, restaurant and bakery on Belladonna Street in Sonpark, open from breakfast to late dinner. The menu runs from oven-fresh croissants to clay-oven pizzas, with vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals throughout, and the artichoke soft shell tacos are the most vegan-friendly headline dish.

Order the artichoke tacos and ask for a vegan stone-oven pizza. It is open seven days and is the most reliable all-day option in the city.
