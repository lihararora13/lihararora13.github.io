Bloom Sushi is vegan sushi as a night out. The Quartier des Spectacles dining room is pristine and bright, with flowers hanging from the ceiling, and every maki on the menu is fish-free: konjac salmon sashimi that fools people nightly, crispy-rice pieces topped with mushroom and avocado, kamikaze inari, and the Nami for two tasting platter. Reservations are bookable, the presentation is gorgeous, and reviewers rave about the quality and the loaded toppings.

Note: the old Saint-Paul Street location closed, so head to the Quartier des Spectacles room. It is the perfect downtown stop before a show: date-night pretty, fully plant-based, and proof that botanical sushi can hold its own against the traditional kind. Book ahead on weekends.
