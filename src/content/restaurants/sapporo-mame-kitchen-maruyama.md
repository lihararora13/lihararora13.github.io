Mame Kitchen is not a fully vegan restaurant, but its vegan versions are worth the trip: bean onigiri, soy-meat chili beans and bean pizzas with plant-based cheese, in a cozy Maruyama cafe near the park station.

It opens Wednesday to Sunday and closes Monday and Tuesday. Order the bean onigiri and a chili bean plate.
