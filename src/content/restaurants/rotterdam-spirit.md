Spirit is an organic vegetarian buffet in the Green Passage on Mariniersweg, where you walk along the buffet, ladle up what you like and pay by weight. About fifty vegetarian dishes are ready every day, a large proportion of them vegan, with hot dishes, salads, soups, bread, cake and other sweets, all organic and seasonal.

It is open from early morning to evening, so it works for breakfast, lunch or dinner. This is a vegetarian restaurant, so it is listed as vegan-friendly; the vegan dishes are clearly marked, and the roasted carrots, sushi roll and seaweed salad are the picks.
