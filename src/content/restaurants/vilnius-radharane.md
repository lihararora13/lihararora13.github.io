Radharane is Lithuania's first vegetarian restaurant, and the Gedimino Avenue location is the one to visit, a long-running dining room that blends traditional and contemporary flavours. The menu runs from falafel and potato pancakes to spinach and paneer stew, with tiramisu and panna cotta for dessert, and vegan options across the board.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so confirm which dishes are vegan when you order. Order the falafel, add potato pancakes, and finish with tiramisu.
