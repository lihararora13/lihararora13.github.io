Udum is the quieter, more refined side of Cologne's vegan Asian scene: a fully vegan restaurant doing sushi, tapas and bowls in a calm, modern room in the city centre. It shows up on the city's fine-dining lists for a reason, the cooking is careful, the flavours are clean, and the sushi is genuinely good.

Come for a proper sit-down dinner, order the sushi set and a few tapas, and enjoy the rare pleasure of a vegan restaurant that feels grown-up without feeling stiff.
