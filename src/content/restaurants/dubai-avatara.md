Avatara is the special-occasion pick, and quite possibly the most ambitious vegetarian cooking in the Gulf. Dubai's first and only Michelin-starred vegetarian degustation restaurant seats just 24 guests facing the kitchen, and builds a tasting menu of a dozen-plus courses from humble seasonal Indian vegetables, jackfruit, lotus root, bitter gourd and all.

It is vegetarian rather than all-vegan, so flag vegan when booking and the kitchen will plan around it, with dairy-free courses and thoughtful non-alcoholic pairings. Book well ahead, dress smart, and come ready for a slow, theatrical evening where vegetables get the star treatment.
