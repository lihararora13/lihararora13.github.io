Orenda is Beirut's plant-based space in Bourj Hammoud, a bar and restaurant doing bolognese pasta, burgers, scrambled tofu, koshari, croissants and falafel, all vegan, plus a small shop of vegan goods. It is pet-friendly, open late, and the most deliberately vegan room in the city.

Order the burger or the bolognese, add a cocktail if it is evening, and browse the shop on the way out. This is the top of the Beirut list for a reason.
