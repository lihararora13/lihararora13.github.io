Çiğköfte Vegan Land is Bonn's fully vegan Turkish counter, a no-fuss spot on Kölnstraße where everything on the menu is plant-based and most of it costs less than a tenner. The draw is the çiğköfte itself, the spiced bulgur classic, served as a wrap, a burger or a plate, alongside içli köfte, falafel and lahmacun, with the counter open every day from eleven until eleven.

It is fast, cheap and genuinely filling, which makes it the best budget stop on this list. Order the wrap if it is your first visit, add a falafel on the side, and take it away for a walk along the Rhine. No booking, no ceremony, just good street food.
