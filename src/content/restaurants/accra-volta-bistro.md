Volta Bistro brings the flavours of Ghana's Volta Region to La, with fresh, locally sourced ingredients and a menu built on jollof, fufu, wagashi and fried plantains. It is not a vegan restaurant, but the kitchen is happy to steer vegans to the plant-based plates, and the setting is a proper sit-down bistro rather than a counter.

Order the jollof and the fried plantains, ask for the vegan-friendly preparations, and enjoy one of Accra's prettiest neighbourhood dining rooms.
