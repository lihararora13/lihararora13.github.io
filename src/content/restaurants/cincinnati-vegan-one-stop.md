Vegan One Stop is Cincinnati's late-night vegan kitchen: a globally inspired, fully plant-based restaurant on Colerain Avenue that cooks the comfort-food canon without animal products, including vegan breakfast, burgers, Philly cheesesteak hoagies, vegan steak, wings, ribs and mac and cheese, plus cheesecake and sea moss fruit juices. It is one of the few vegan spots in the city that keeps evening and late hours, which makes it the practical pick after a show or a long day.

The venue updated its own listing in September 2026 and trades daily, but the late-night window varies, so check the day's hours before a late run. Order a hoagie and the mac and cheese, and expect generous, unpretentious portions at a fair price.
