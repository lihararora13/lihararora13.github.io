BODHI's Winterhude restaurant is the northern sibling of the St. Georg original, and it keeps the same formula: all-vegan Asian cuisine, sushi and Vietnamese dishes freshly prepared daily, plus a drinks list that is vegan through and through. The atmosphere is a little calmer up here, which suits a long dinner.

Order the sushi, add a Vietnamese bowl, and try one of the house drinks. Winterhude's best vegan table, and a good reason to ride north.
