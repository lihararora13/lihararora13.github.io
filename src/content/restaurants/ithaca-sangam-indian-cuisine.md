Sangam Indian Cuisine is an Indian restaurant on Eddy Street in Collegetown, doing curries, dals, and breads with a solid vegan bench. It is the Indian pick on this list, and the chana masala is the reliable order.

Order the chana masala and dal tadka with vegan roti, and confirm which breads are dairy-free, since most naan is brushed with butter or ghee.
