When you are busy sightseeing and only have time for a quick lunch, Umi Falafel is the answer. This Palestinian-owned chain's George's Street Arcade branch sits right in the middle of the action, turning out super fresh, filling Middle Eastern food at a pace that keeps a sightseeing day on track.

Build your own bowl and pile it high: crispy falafel, super smooth hummus, stuffed grape vine leaves drizzled with tangy pomegranate molasses, citrusy tabouleh and a generous pour of tahini. The restaurant is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but the vegan orders here are clearly labelled and plentiful.
