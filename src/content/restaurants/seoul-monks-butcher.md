The name is a wink: nothing at Monk's Butcher was ever butchered. This is Itaewon's fancy vegan night out, a multi-floor space with a rooftop terrace where lion's mane mushrooms get the steakhouse treatment and natural wine flows next to the burgers. CatchTable keeps it in the neighbourhood's top ten, and 2026 Seoul vegan guides keep sending people up the stairs.

Book for the terrace if the weather plays along, share the fried lion's mane and whatever the season is showing off, and let the lemon tofu chicken make its case. Date night, sorted.
