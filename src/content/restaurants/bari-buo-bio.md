BUO Bio is a modern gastronomia-bistrot with a dedicated vegan menu of antipasti, primi, secondi, contorni and dolci, plus a separate pastry case with vegan sweets. A Puglia vegan review in April 2025 scored it 7 out of 10, praising the berlingot pasta filled with fava beans and chicory and the grilled seitan with ratatouille.

Order the berlingot pasta with fava beans and chicory, the grilled seitan with ratatouille, and a vegan chocolate-almond cookie from the pastry case.
