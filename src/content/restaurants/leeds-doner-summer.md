Döner Summer is the vegan junk-food joint Leeds is proudest of: 100% vegan, Berlin-inspired, on Call Lane, with its own mock döner meat and chick'n, smash patties, loaded fries, tater tots and cocktails. The Bad Girl Kebab is the signature, the Berliner Döner loaded fries are the side to fight over, and the portions are famously enormous.

Come hungry, order the Bad Girl Kebab, and add a cocktail. This is the best fully vegan restaurant in the UK according to some very happy reviewers, and the most fun table on this list.
