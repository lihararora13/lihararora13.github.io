4th and State is downtown Columbus's 100 percent vegan diner, and it is exactly what a vegan diner should be: breakfast, lunch and dinner, all of it plant-based, all of it generous. The Vrunchwrap is the signature, the bananas and cream pancakes are the brunch move, and the stuffed-crust pizza is the dinner surprise.

It is also nut-free and labels for soy and gluten, which makes it the most allergy-friendly spot on the list. Order the Vrunchwrap, add pancakes, and enjoy the rare diner where everything is safe.
