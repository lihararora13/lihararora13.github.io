Vegan Center Cafe is a fully vegan cafe and restaurant in Ban Kaoyod, Sisattanak, top rated in Vientiane, with a lunch buffet from 8am to 2pm and a la carte ordering from 3pm to 9pm, closing from 2pm to 3pm every day. Reviewers call it the best vegan buffet in Southeast Asia, with two types of rice, many vegetables, tofu and fruits on the buffet, and teriyaki bowls and a duck volcano red curry on the dinner menu. It relocated from Kualuang, so older guides send people to the wrong address.

Come for the buffet at lunch, when it costs about 80,000 to 90,000 kip with green tea and water included, and try the teriyaki bowls at dinner. It is cash only, and it is the essential vegan meal in Vientiane.
