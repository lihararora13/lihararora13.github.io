Mumokuteki is the one every Kyoto vegan guide starts with, and for good reason: a big, light-filled, all-vegan cafe above its own lifestyle shop on Teramachi-dori, serving the kind of generous Japanese set meals that make first-timers relax. The signature soy-meat karaage set comes with brown rice, miso soup and pickles for around 1,600 yen, and the menu runs on to katsu lunch sets, omurice, curry, cakes and parfaits.

Come for lunch, expect a queue at peak times, and go upstairs through the shop. It is the easiest, friendliest first vegan meal in Kyoto, and the one locals point visitors to before anything else.
