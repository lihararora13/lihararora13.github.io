Hotel Bharath is the Thrissur dosa institution, the Chembottil Lane restaurant whose dosa the city's food pages call iconic and pure bliss. The menu lists dosa, panunga and a pulao with jackfruit, pineapple, tutti frutti, cherry and apple, and the room has been feeding the Round for decades.

Order the masala dosa without ghee and the jackfruit pulao. It is the benchmark veg meal in Thrissur, and the one the locals measure everything else against.
