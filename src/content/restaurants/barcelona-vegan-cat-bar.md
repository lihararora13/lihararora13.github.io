Why we love it — Vegan Cat Bar is a Barcelona institution:
moody, punky, plastered with cats, and serving vegan burgers since
before it was cool. The British owners worked with local brewers to
build an all-vegan craft beer menu, and the house-made burgers with
the works are the perfect sidekick to a pint. It is the kind of bar
where dinner turns into one more round.

The Champion Cat Burger with its mushroom-flecked patty is a legend
for a reason, and the bravas are among the city's best. Cheap,
cheerful, and gloriously unpretentious.
