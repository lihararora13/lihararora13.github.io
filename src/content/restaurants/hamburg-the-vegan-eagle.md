The Vegan Eagle has been quietly excellent since 2014: a small, fully vegan restaurant with a garden in Fuhlsbüttel, cooking a weekly changing international menu that keeps regulars coming back to see what is new. It is a five-minute walk from the U1, and it feels like a neighbourhood secret even though the reviews are stellar.

Come for the weekly menu, sit in the garden when the weather allows, and enjoy one of Hamburg's most enduring vegan tables. A classic that never tries too hard.
