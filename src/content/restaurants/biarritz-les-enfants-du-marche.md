Les Enfants du Marché is a restaurant at the Halles de Biarritz, cooking seasonal food with vegan dishes on the menu. The market setting is the draw, and the cooking follows what is on the stalls that day.

Order the seasonal plates. Best at lunch, when the market is in full swing.
