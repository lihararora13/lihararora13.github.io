NADI, also listed as Nadi Eco Saludable, is a 5.0-rated fully vegan restaurant cooking Afro-Pacific Colombian food in Miraflores, and it is the most culturally specific kitchen on this list. The vegan bandeja paisa is the signature, and the menu reads like a love letter to Pacific ingredients: plantain, coconut, herbs and slow-cooked aromas, described by the kitchen as food that is medicine.

Order the vegan bandeja paisa, try a plantain dish in yellow sauce, and save room for dessert. The room is small and personal, with the hosts cooking behind a wooden counter, so go for lunch and take your time.
