Moon Shell is a vegan restaurant and cafe in Ar Rawdah, serving plant-based dishes and smoothie bowls in a minimalist setting, open daily 4pm to 4am with outdoor seating. The signature molten midnight dessert pairs raw cacao with local dates.

Order the lentil pancakes and the molten midnight dessert. It is the late-night vegan room in Jeddah, and the hours make it the fallback after everything else closes.
