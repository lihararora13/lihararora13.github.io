Obuthan is the Nampo pick: an all-vegan restaurant close to Busan Tower, serving traditional bansang meal sets for lunch and more Western dishes for dinner. It is a handy stop on a tower and market day.

Order the bansang set for lunch. It is the most traditional meal on the list after Vegenarang.
