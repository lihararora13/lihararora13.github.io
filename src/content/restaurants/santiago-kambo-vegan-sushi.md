Kambo is the kind of small restaurant that inspires devotion: reviewers call it the best sushi they have ever had, vegan or not, and then immediately warn you about the wait for a table. Every roll on the menu is fully plant-based, and the kitchen treats the craft seriously, with generous portions, beautiful presentation and flavours like the sweet potato roll that regulars say they have found nowhere else.

The All-Stars Combo is the move if you are deciding for a table, with crispy tofu cubes on the side and a strawberry or passionfruit daiquiri if you are in the mood. The room is small, so arrive early or be ready to wait ten minutes for a table that is worth it.
