Why we love it — Sticky Fingers is a DC vegan institution: a two-time Cupcake
Wars winner that proved plant-based baking could outdo the originals. The
bakery moved to Takoma and kept the magic going with cupcakes, cookies, and
breakfast pastries worth the trip uptown.

This is the celebration stop. Stock up on Cowvin cookies for the office, grab
a box of cupcakes for literally any occasion, and try to leave without
eating one on the way out. You won't.
