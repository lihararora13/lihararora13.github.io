TELAX is a vegetarian restaurant on 46th Street in downtown Yangon, open daily from 6am to 6pm, serving Burmese vegetarian dishes. It is a morning and lunch kitchen, and it suits an early breakfast or a quick lunch in the downtown area.

Order the vegetable and tofu dishes with rice, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is inexpensive and central.
