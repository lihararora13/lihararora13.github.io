Knol & Kool is a vegan bistro with its own plant-based shop, on Heilige Geeststraat, rated 5.0 on HappyCow. Everything on the menu is plant-based, organic and homemade: brunch and lunch, baked oyster mushrooms, seitan, tofu scramble, vegan gyros and cashew cheese, with snacks for the train from the shop.

It is open Wednesdays to Saturdays for lunch and dinner, and Sundays and Saturdays for brunch. Come for brunch, and stock up in the shop on the way out.
