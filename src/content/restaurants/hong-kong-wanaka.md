Wanaka tucks into King Sing Street beside Wan Chai's famous Blue House, and it has quietly become the city's vegan brunch answer. It is small, sunny and unhurried, with courtyard seating and a menu that treats breakfast like the main event.

The all-day breakfast, tofu scramble and all, is the order regulars defend, with sushi bowls and gnocchi for the lunch crowd. Save room for the tarts and the lemon basque cheesecake; HappyCow reviewers were still raving about them in March 2026, and so will you.
