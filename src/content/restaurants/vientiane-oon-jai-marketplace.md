Oon Jai Marketplace is a marketplace in Phonexay Hom 15, with a menu in four categories: Mexican, burgers, sandwiches and bowls, and the food is all vegan. One drink on the menu contains honey, which is the only thing that stops it being classified as a vegan restaurant, so order food, not that drink.

Order a vegan burger and a bowl, and skip the honey drink. It is a good change from the Lao buffets, and it suits a casual lunch.
