Subculture is an all-vegan cafe on Lark Street in the Center Square neighborhood, doing plates, sandwiches, and desserts with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the cafe anchor of this list, and the place to linger over a coffee and a pastry.

Order the vegan plate and a dessert. It is a daytime stop, so plan it for lunch rather than a late dinner.
