Planet Vegis is one of the pioneers of plant-based dining in Cebu, and it still does what it has always done: a canteen-style eatery near Capitol serving very affordable Filipino veggie dishes like tortang talong and ginataang gulay.

It is a daily-meal spot rather than an occasion, and that is its charm. Order a plate of Filipino classics.
