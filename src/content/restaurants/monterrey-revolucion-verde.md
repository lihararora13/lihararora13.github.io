Revolución Verde is Monterrey's plant-based Mexican comfort-food spot, and its Sunday buffets have become a ritual for regulars. Chicharrón tacos, pirata, gringa, discada and the Checo Burger, all vegan, all generous.

Order a mega pirata and a torta de chicharrón. It is the most Monterrey meal on this list, big, bold and very satisfying.
