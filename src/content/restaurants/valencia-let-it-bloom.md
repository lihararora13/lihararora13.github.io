Let It Bloom is Valencia's vegan brunch darling, a bright cafe near Mercat de Russafa that opened in August 2024 and shot straight to the top of the city's HappyCow list. Spanish toast, avocado toast, chickpea omelettes, croissants and tofu scramble, with proper specialty coffee.

Go for brunch and order a set. The chickpea omelette is the dish to try if you have never had one, and the baked goods are worth taking away for later.
