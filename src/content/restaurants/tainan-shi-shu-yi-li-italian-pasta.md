Shi Shu Yi Li is a street vendor with seating at the counter on Minzu Road, and it is the most casual pick in this guide: a mix of western and Taiwanese vegetarian dishes, oven-baked casserole, spaghetti and noodle soup, with an English menu and a 4.5 rating on HappyCow.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so say you eat vegan when you order and the cook will steer you to the right dishes. Come for a quick, cheap lunch between temples, sit at the counter, and order the casserole.
