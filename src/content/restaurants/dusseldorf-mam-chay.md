Mâm Chay is the newest Vietnamese table on this list, opened in August 2023 in Unterbilk, and it cooks vegetarian food inspired by Buddhist recipes, with clearly labelled vegan dishes, separate menus for lunch and dinner, and all desserts vegan. The rice with veggies and tofu and the bánh mì are the dishes to start with.

It is calm, thoughtful and a short walk from the Rhine. Order the bánh mì, add a soup, and finish with a vegan dessert. Buddhist cooking, done with care.
