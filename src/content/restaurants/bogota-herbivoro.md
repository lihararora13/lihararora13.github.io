Herbívoro is the workhorse of Bogotá's vegan scene, and the rare spot that takes breakfast seriously. The Chapinero branch on Calle 71 opens early with a giant bowl of super-creamy oatmeal topped with tart apples, an oat milk latte, pastries and a yogurt parfait, then rolls into lunch and dinner with everything from arroz criollo to kung pao seitan and pizza. The whole operation runs on a vision of ethics, ecology and health, and the room doubles as a bulk market selling grains, legumes, seeds and ecological household goods.

With multiple locations across the city, it is the easiest vegan meal to find wherever you wake up. Start the day here, stock up on bulk goods, and save the pizza for the evening.
