La Flaca is Guadalajara's vegan Mexican anchor, a plant-filled patio restaurant where the torta ahogada, the city's signature drowned sandwich, is fully vegan and, according to regulars, just as good as the original. Pozole, birria, enmoladas and a tortilla soup that gets its own fan club.

Order the torta ahogada and the sopa de tortilla. It is the table to book when you want to eat Guadalajara's food, not a vegan approximation of it.
