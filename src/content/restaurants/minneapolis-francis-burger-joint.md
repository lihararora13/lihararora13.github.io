Francis is where vegan junk food dreams come true. This Northeast burger joint does what nobody else in Minnesota dared: a fully plant-based Juicy Lucy, the state's iconic cheese-stuffed burger, plus chicken sandwiches, loaded fries, and milkshakes, all 100% vegan with a full bar to wash it down.

The menu is short, the smashburgers are legit, and non-vegan friends routinely leave stunned. Skip the FOMO, order the Juicy Lucy, thank us later.
