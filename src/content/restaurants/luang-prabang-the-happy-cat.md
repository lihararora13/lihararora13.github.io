The Happy Cat is a sustainable veggie cafe on Kingkitsarath Road, with a large vegan offering, 100% biodegradable takeaway packaging, and a range of cat-themed and sustainable souvenirs. Part of the income goes to help cats in need in Laos, and it was previously called Luangprabang Cat Cafe. It is rated 5 cows on HappyCow, and it is open daily from 8:30am to 10pm.

Order a vegan breakfast or a smoothie bowl, and ask for the vegan dishes by name, as the kitchen is vegetarian. It is a calm, friendly room, and it suits a slow morning.
