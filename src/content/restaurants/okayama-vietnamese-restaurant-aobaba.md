AOBABA is a Vietnamese restaurant in the basement of the Daiichi Sentoraru Building on Honmachi, one of the closest vegan-friendly kitchens to Okayama Station. The menu covers pho, fresh rolls, tofu and noodle dishes, and the kitchen will make them vegan if you ask for no fish sauce and no egg. It is a straightforward, inexpensive neighbourhood restaurant rather than a destination, and that is its charm.

Order the pho with tofu and vegetables, the fresh spring rolls, and a lemongrass tofu if it is on. Ask clearly about fish sauce, as with any Vietnamese kitchen in Japan, and come at lunch when the room is at its liveliest.
