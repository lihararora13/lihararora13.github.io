KIRAS is Vilnius' vegan dessert bakery, a cafe in a tiny wooden building on Totoriu Street that looks like it belongs in a fairy-tale book. It was previously known as Chaika, and it specialises in vegan food and desserts: plant-based cakes, sweet treats, hot sandwiches and drinks, with plant-based milks available without an extra charge and very good matcha.

The banana and raspberry cake is the one locals order for birthdays, and the poppy seed cake is the other classic. Order a cake and a matcha, and take your time.
