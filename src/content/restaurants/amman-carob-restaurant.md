Carob is the reason Amman is on the vegan map at all. It is fully vegan, rated a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow, and was ranked among the ten best vegan restaurants in the world, with a menu that runs from Thai curry and pad kee mao to burgers with avocado dip, salads, soups and a dessert of the day. It sits inside a hostel, which keeps the vibe young and the breakfast vegan too.

Come for dinner, order the Thai curry and a burger to share, and save room for dessert. This is the meal to plan an Amman evening around.
