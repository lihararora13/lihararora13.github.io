Happy Valley is Darjeeling's oldest tea estate, and its 1854 cafe at the estate gate serves first-flush Darjeeling tea and steamed veg bao and tingmo from a 100% pure-vegetarian, egg-free kitchen. It is verified for November 2026, open 8 to 6 daily, and the tea tasting is the reason to come.

Order the first-flush tea and the steamed veg bao, and ask for the bao without ghee. It is a short walk from the town centre, and it is the most scenic veg stop on this list.
