Modak Food is a vegetarian and vegan cloud kitchen in Kazhakkoottam, run by Arun Anirudhan, who went from graphic design to food entrepreneurship starting with idli sales. The local Reddit thread praises the filling portions, the idlis, the desserts and the Bhutanese sweet dish ema datshi, and the parents of the original poster are fans.

Order the idli and the ilayada dessert. It is delivery-only, so this is the pick for a night in, and the ema datshi is the adventurous order.
