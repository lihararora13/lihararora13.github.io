The Cocinita is the friendly corner spot Brickell never knew it was missing: 100% plant-based, Venezuelan soul, Middle Eastern flourishes, and prices that won't make you wince. The choricachapa, a golden sweet-corn pancake hugging a chorizo-style filling, is the move that regulars swear by.

Burrito Tuesday deals, outdoor seating, pets welcome, open daily till 10. Big portions, fast food, zero animals. What's not to love.
