Heart and Soy, with its sibling Roots, is a Bardstown Road vegetarian institution: two adjoining restaurants, one casual with Asian street food and one more upscale with small plates. It is the longest-running vegetarian name in Louisville, and the kitchen is deeply experienced with vegan orders.

Order an Asian street food plate at Heart and Soy and small plates at Roots, and tell the server you are vegan up front, since some dishes need the egg or dairy swapped.
