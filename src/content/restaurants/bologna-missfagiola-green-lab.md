Missfagiola Green Lab is a 100% organic and vegan kitchen on Via San Felice, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, cooking everything from sweets to sandwiches, salads, sides and seasonal extracts. The savoury anchors are a vegan lasagne and a ragu alla bolognese, and the desserts include a sacher vegan cheesecake and a chocolate mousse.

It is a lunch-first spot, open midday on weekdays, so plan it as a lunch stop rather than a dinner. Order the lasagne and the cheesecake.
