Terracanta is a natural cafe with an attached shop specialising in organic and kilometre-zero products, open for breakfast, lunch and aperitivo from half nine to ten. It is more a cafe than a restaurant, and it is the daytime vegan stop on this list.

Order the vegan breakfast and lunch dishes and browse the bio shop. It is open all day, which makes it the flexible pick when the dinner-only restaurants are shut.
