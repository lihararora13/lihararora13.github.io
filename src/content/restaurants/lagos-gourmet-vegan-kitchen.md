Gourmet Vegan Kitchen brings a fresh, fun twist to vegan dining in Ikoyi, with everything on the menu entirely vegan. It is the comfort-food entry on this list: generous plates, familiar flavours, and a kitchen that treats plant-based cooking as a pleasure rather than a restriction.

Order across the menu and share. It is a good second stop after VeggieVictory if you want to compare how two Ikoyi kitchens veganise the same classics.
