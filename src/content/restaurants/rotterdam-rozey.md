Rozey is an all-you-can-eat tapas restaurant on Wijnhaven, part of a small Dutch franchise chain, and one of the most-loved vegan-leaning rooms in Rotterdam. Almost the entire menu is vegan, with a few dairy options clearly marked, and the small plates run from mini burgers and fried sushi rolls to sweet potato wedges and desserts.

It is a dinner-first spot, so book ahead at weekends. This is listed as vegan-friendly because of the few dairy dishes; order the vegan tapas and check the markings.
