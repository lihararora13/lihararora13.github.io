Cafe Mokka is a vegan cafe in the Sudstadt, run by the Rostocker Fraueninitiativen, serving a daily changing homemade lunch, house-baked cakes, organic coffee and fresh iced tea, with gluten-free options. It is rated 5 on HappyCow and takes reservations, which are required.

Order the daily changing lunch, a house-baked cake and an organic coffee, and book ahead, since the room is small and reservations are required.
