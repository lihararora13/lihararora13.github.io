Qing Chun by Perma is the table to book first in Hangzhou: a Zen-inspired sanctuary in the misty tea fields near Lingyin Temple, listed in the Michelin Guide, where stone pathways, bamboo groves and soft lantern light frame 100% plant-based Buddhist cooking. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, and Cathay Pacific's 2026 guide to the city's plant-based scene names its osmanthus Lipu yam and black truffle fried rice as must-orders.

Order the black truffle fried rice, the cumin lamb skewer, the quinoa ma po tofu and the pan-fried truffle buns, and finish with osmanthus-stewed taro and a hazelnut-brown rice milk. Dim sum is handmade to order. It is the priciest table on this list, so book it as the special meal. Open daily 11am to 9pm.
