Sopitas is a 4.5-rated all-vegan soup cafe in Barrio Escalante, on Calle 29 at Avenida 7, diagonally opposite Parque Francia. The kitchen serves a rotating selection of soups and focaccia using locally sourced ingredients, with limited seating, and everything is gluten-free and vegan, which makes ordering easy.

Order a roasted red pepper soup or the Greek lentil soup, add the gluten-free focaccia with cherry tomatoes and olives, and ask about the vegan and gluten-free banana cake. It is open daily from 11:30 am into the evening.
