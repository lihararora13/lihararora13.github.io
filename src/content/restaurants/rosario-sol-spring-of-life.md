Sol Spring of Life is a vegetarian rotiseria on Cordoba, open weekday lunchtimes, and it is the kind of place Rosario locals rely on rather than tourists discover: a lunch plate, a salad, a milanesa, eaten quickly and cheaply. The kitchen is vegetarian with vegan dishes on the menu, so ask which plates are vegan when you order.

Come between 10:30 am and 2:30 pm, Monday to Saturday, and order the lunch plate of the day. It closes early on Saturdays and does not open on Sundays.
