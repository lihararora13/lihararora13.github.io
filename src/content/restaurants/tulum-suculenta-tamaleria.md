Suculenta is Tulum's vegan tamalería, right across the road from El Bajón on Calle Sol Oriente, and it is the best vegan tamale stop in town. The tamales are traditionally steamed in plant leaves, and the mole and papadzul are the ones to order, at about 95 pesos on delivery menus.

It is a daytime spot, with listed hours starting around 9am, so go before evening. Pair it with tacos from across the road and you have the best cheap vegan lunch in Tulum.
