India Gate is Buffalo's Indian standby on Elmwood Avenue, with a second location on Hertel Avenue, and it is the most reliable Indian vegan option in the city: many dishes with tofu, lentils, eggplant and potatoes, curries and rice that are vegan or easily made so. Some vegetarian dishes may have dairy, so ask when ordering and specify vegan. The Elmwood room is the one listed here, with outdoor seating in season.

Hours at the Elmwood location are Monday to Wednesday and Friday to Sunday 11:30am to 10pm, closed Thursdays. Order a tofu curry and a dal, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan preparation.
