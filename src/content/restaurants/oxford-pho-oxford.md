Pho Oxford sits at the top of the Westgate Centre and does what the chain does well: fragrant pho, curries, wok-fried noodles and salads, with over a third of the menu vegan and nearly all of it gluten free, accredited by Coeliac UK.

Order the three mushroom pho or the curry pho with tofu and mushrooms, and start with the vegan spring rolls. It is a meat-serving chain with a serious vegan section, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it is open daily from noon.
