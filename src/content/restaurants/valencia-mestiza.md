MESTIZA is the beachside vegan table Valencia locals keep to themselves, a fusion restaurant in El Cabanyal where the menu changes regularly and everything is designed to share. Pad thai, breaded cauliflower, mushroom ceviche, and a vegan tinto de verano that reviewers keep mentioning.

Go after a day at the beach and order several small plates for the table. It is the most relaxed dinner on this list, with live music some evenings and the sea a short walk away.
