The Wild Chive is the brunch spot Long Beach vegans plan their weekends around: a former market vendor turned brick-and-mortar in Belmont Heights, doing plant-based comfort food that reviewers call some of the best vegan food they have ever tried. The Southern fried chick'n sammie is the headliner, the Monte Cristo is sandwiched between caramelised French toast, and the French Quarter beignets are mandatory.

It is only open Thursday through Sunday, so plan ahead, and come hungry. Order the sammie, add beignets, and bring someone who needs converting.
