The Garden Mbombela is a garden restaurant and lounge with indoor dining, outdoor patios and lounge seating, and it is one of the first names locals give when asked where to eat in the city. It is a general restaurant rather than a vegan one, but the kitchen does vegan-friendly plates.

Ask what vegan dishes are on that day and order a garden plate. It suits an evening out more than a quick lunch, and it is busiest at weekends.
