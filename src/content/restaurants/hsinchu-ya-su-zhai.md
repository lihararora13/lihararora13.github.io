Ya Su Zhai is an everyday Taiwanese vegetarian restaurant on Guangfu Road Section 2 in the East District, near National Tsing Hua University, serving rice bowls, ramen, dry noodles and dumplings. It is inexpensive, local and unpretentious, the kind of place students eat at daily, and the vegan dishes need asking for, as the kitchen is vegetarian.

Order a rice bowl and the dry noodles, and ask about egg and dairy. It moved here from Jinshan Street, so older guides send people to the wrong street, and it is closed on Mondays.
