Only in Cape Town: a butcherie where nothing on the counter ever mooed. Romeo and Vero craft their cuts from mushrooms, soya and chickpeas, spice them properly, and serve them as ribs, kebabs, fillets and sandwiches, with a shop section so you can take vegan biltong home for the road.

HappyCow classes it fully vegan with a 4.5 rating, and the kitchen is gluten-free too, which makes it an easy crowd-pleaser. Order the Mexican pizza, add the wings, and leave with a packet of biltong like a proper local.
