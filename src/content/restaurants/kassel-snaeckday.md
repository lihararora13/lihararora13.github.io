Snäckday is Kassel's fully vegan coffee shop, a small daytime café on Wilhelmshöher Allee where everything on the menu is plant-based: cakes, coffee and snacks, with outdoor seating when the weather allows. It is a simple, honest stop rather than a destination restaurant, and that is its charm.

It sits on the long avenue that climbs from the centre to the Bergpark Wilhelmshöhe, which makes it the natural coffee break on the way up to the Hercules monument or back down again. Come for a slice of vegan cake and a coffee, and note it closes on Sundays.
