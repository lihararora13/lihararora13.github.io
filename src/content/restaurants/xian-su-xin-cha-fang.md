Su Xin Cha Fang is the original branch of a six-branch Xi'an teahouse chain, on the second floor at 15 Nanyuanmen, just inside the South Gate. The set meal is 48 RMB and runs to mushroom dishes and soy mock meats, and the room is a quiet, tea-house setting that suits a slow meal. It is rated 4.0 on HappyCow, with a review from a diner who found it open late in the evening.

It is listed as vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan because some dishes contain egg, though many have the egg served on the side, so vegans should ask when ordering. The other branches are at Xiaozhai, Zhongmao Plaza, Longhu Xingyue Hui, Datang West Market and Tai'ao Plaza; this page is the Nanyuanmen original.
