The Hearty Hooligan is Hamilton's all-vegan junk-food café, on Ottawa Street North, and the room lives up to the name: pizza pockets, a seitan Philly cheesesteak, a Mac & Cheese Bake and an all-day breakfast, plus a bakery case of custom-baked goods. It holds a 5.0 HappyCow rating and is the first table to book in the city.

It is open Monday and Thursday to Sunday from 11am to 9pm, and closed Tuesday and Wednesday. Come hungry, order the Mac & Cheese Bake, and save room for something from the bakery.
