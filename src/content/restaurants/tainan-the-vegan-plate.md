The Vegan Plate is tucked down an alley off Chenggong Road in the North District, and reviewers call it their favourite place in Tainan, a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow. The menu jumps between continents: crepes and risotto on one side, Japanese-style plates on the other, with the Omni pork katsu with rice, the gyokuro mushroom with rice and the G-nuggets drawing the most praise, and everything made with natural seasonings and local small-farm produce.

Opening hours have shifted over time and recent reviews note it is closed on some weekdays, so check the current hours before you go. The alley address is part of the charm and part of the challenge: use the lane number, not just the road, and you will find it.
