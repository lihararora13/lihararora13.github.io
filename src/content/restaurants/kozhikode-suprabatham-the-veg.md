Suprabatham The Veg sits on Beach Road and leads the November 2026 list of Kozhikode's five best veg restaurants, with pav bhaji and dosa as the signature dishes. It is a straightforward pure-veg room, and the beach is right there for the walk afterwards.

Order the pav bhaji without butter and the masala dosa. It is the beach-road lunch, and the city recommends it first.
