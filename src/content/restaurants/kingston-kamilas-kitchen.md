Kamila's Kitchen is a fully vegan Ital restaurant doing island fusion, serving burgers, nachos, soup, Buddha bowls, salads, pizza and wraps. The Constant Spring Road location is the second location for the kitchen, and the original sits on Skyline Drive, so use the Constant Spring address on this page.

Order a Buddha bowl or the vegan BBQ jackfruit bowl, and try the KK curry chickpea. It is open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm.
