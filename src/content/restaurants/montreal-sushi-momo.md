Sushi Momo was doing vegan sushi before vegan sushi was cool, and it shows. This Plateau institution serves a fully plant-based Japanese menu with the kind of creativity that makes you forget fish was ever involved: caramelized-onion maki topped with pan-fried jackfruit tartare and flambéed at the table, tempura-vegetable rolls with spicy mayo, and imitations of salmon that convert skeptics nightly. Tourisme Montreal calls it the premiere vegan sushi spot in the city, and the regulars agree.

The dining room is intimate, reservations are accepted, and the sister takeout counter Komomo (3603 Rue Saint-Denis) handles the grab-and-go crowd with artful boxes and the same bold rolls. Come for the Fuji flambe, stay for the panna cotta. This is the stop that proves Montreal's vegan scene has been ahead of the curve for years.
