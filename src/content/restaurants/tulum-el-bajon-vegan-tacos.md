El Bajón is Tulum's best budget vegan taco stand, a small Centro spot on Calle Sol where every taco is plant-based and costs around 25 pesos. The soy-spinach ball tacos are the top pick, with chickpea and jackfruit tacos alongside, and the guides agree it is the taco stand to start with.

It is open Monday to Saturday from about 11am, closed Sunday, and it is cash-friendly street food, so bring pesos. Suculenta Tamalería sits right across the road, which makes this corner the best cheap vegan lunch in Tulum.
