Lemon Tree Co is a downtown Mediterranean cafe on North 10th Street with bowls, salads, and sandwiches that adapt well to vegan orders. Falafel, hummus, and vegetable plates are the reliable picks, and the room works for a quick downtown lunch.

Order the falafel bowl with the hummus plate. Ask for dressings on the side, since some contain dairy.
