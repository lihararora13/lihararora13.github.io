Pickles is a vegan-friendly bistro on Rue Solférino, serving accras, pea beignets with vegan mayo and seasonal sharing plates built for the table. It is the sharing-plates dinner on this page, and the room suits a group.

Use this address: the original Pickles at 25 Rue Pierre Mauroy is permanently closed, and the Solférino location is the live one. Book ahead on weekends.
