SnackWallah is the loudest little corner of the Grainger Market: a fully vegan Indian street-food stall where the samosa chaat lands piled with chutneys, chickpeas, tamarind and vegan yoghurt. Reviewers call it a mouthful taste explosion, and at around five pounds a plate it is the cheapest great lunch in the city centre.

Go for the samosa chaat first, then the pani puri and a bhaji wrap. Seating is tight at lunchtime in the busy market hall, so expect to perch rather than linger.
