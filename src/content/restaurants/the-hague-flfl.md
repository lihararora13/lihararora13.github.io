FLFL in The Hague is a vegan falafel shop on Spuistraat, part of the same chain as the Utrecht original, serving falafel balls, wraps and salads. It is a fast, cheap lunch pick in the centre, with the same falafel, hummus and pita formula that earned the Utrecht branch a world best falafel mention.

Come for lunch or a quick dinner. Order the falafel wrap with hummus.
