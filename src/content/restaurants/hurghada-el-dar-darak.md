El Dar Darak is an Egyptian restaurant in Sakkala cooking traditional food with a vegan koshari on the menu. It is a local room rather than a tourist one, and the food is straightforward Egyptian home cooking.

Order the vegan koshari and a lentil soup. It is the second koshary anchor in town, and the two together mean you are never far from a vegan meal.
