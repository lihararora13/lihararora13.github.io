Mahatma Gourmet is Curitiba's chef-led buffet: ten hot dishes, prepared by a chef who draws on Spanish, Japanese, French, Italian, Thai and other cuisines to build a menu that changes daily. The ingredients are fresh, the seasoning is special, and there are always some vegan options on the spread.

Come for lunch, take a slow walk past the buffet before you choose, and finish with dessert. This is the one for a world tour on one plate.
