Pizzeria da Michele is the most famous pizzeria in Naples, and it belongs on a vegan list for one simple reason: the marinara, tomato sauce, garlic and herbs with no cheese at all, is naturally vegan and is one of the best pizzas in the city. The menu is short and the room is famously no-frills.

Expect a queue, a ticket system and a fast turnaround. Order the marinara, skip the margherita unless you have confirmed a vegan cheese is available that day, and treat this as a pilgrimage lunch rather than a long dinner.
