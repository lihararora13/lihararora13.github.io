Roots has been Thessaloniki's plant-based restaurant since 2014, in the cobblestone Athonos alley in the city centre, serving familiar flavours in a new direction: vegan gyros, vegan moussaka, legume-based burgers, vegan meze and cashew-cream desserts. Reviewers call the gyros the best they have had and the cheesecakes exceptional, and the menu is clearly marked vegan and vegetarian.

Use the Mpalanou 4 address on this page: the restaurant moved from its old Vassilissis Olgas address. The kitchen runs Monday to Thursday from 2pm to 11pm, Friday and Saturday from 1pm, and Sunday from 2pm to 10pm, with drinks until midnight.
