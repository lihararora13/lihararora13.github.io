Little Kim is the fast-casual little sister to Miss Kim, created by Chef Ji Hye Kim with the Zingerman's community, and it is one of the most exciting vegan-friendly counters in Michigan: a fried tofu sandwich with seaweed mayo, chilled tofu noodles, beet hummus, and Bombay bhel, all built for grab-and-go.

It is a lunch spot, open until 6pm, with a patio in Kerrytown. The fried tofu sandwich is the order, and the noodles are the second.
