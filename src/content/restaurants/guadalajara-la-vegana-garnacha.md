La Vegana Garnacha is Guadalajara's vegan street-food counter, a small spot in Colonia Americana where antojitos are the whole point. Garnachas, tacos, tortas, all vegan, all fast, all very cheap.

Order a spread of garnachas and tacos for the table. It is the quick, joyful lunch on this list, and the one that feels most like eating on a Guadalajara street.
