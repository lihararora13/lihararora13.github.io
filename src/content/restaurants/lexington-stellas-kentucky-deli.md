Stella's Kentucky Deli is a deli on Jefferson Street in downtown Lexington doing sandwiches, salads, and sides with vegan-friendly options. It is a quick downtown lunch stop, and the vegan sandwich is the reliable order.

Order the vegan sandwich and a salad. Ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since the standard sandwich uses cheese or mayo.
