BLACKOUT is dinner theater with the lights literally off: a pitch-black room, servers in night-vision goggles, and a seven-course plant-based mystery menu where guessing the food is half the fun. The restaurant does not advertise its vegan status loudly on its own site, but the food is 100 percent plant-based, and reviewers keep confirming it.

February and March 2026 diners on OpenTable give it straight 5s, calling it one-of-a-kind and a must for vegans and non-vegans in Vegas. Reservations are strongly recommended. You are not there just for the food, but the food holds up.
