Chandan Shree is the pure-veg thali house at Hanuman Circle: a 100% vegetarian kitchen serving Rajasthani and Gujarati thalis, the two great thali traditions of the region, on one menu. It is the most traditional meal on this list, and one of the cheapest.

Order a Rajasthani thali with the ghee held, add a dal baati done the same way, and you have a proper desert-city dinner. The kitchen cooks with ghee as a matter of course, so say the word when you order.
