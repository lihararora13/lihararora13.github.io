Legume is Asia's first vegan Michelin-starred restaurant, merging precise French technique with Korean produce, and it recently relocated from Jangchung-dong to the Dongdaegu Station neighbourhood. Visitors in 2026 praise a large oyster mushroom cooked to mimic meat and a tasting menu built entirely from plants, and the room is small, calm and deliberately fine dining.

Book the tasting menu and expect to pay for it: this is the special-occasion table in Daegu, not a casual lunch. Confirm the current address when you book, as the move is recent and older guides still list Jangchung-dong, and the pin here is approximate to the station area.
