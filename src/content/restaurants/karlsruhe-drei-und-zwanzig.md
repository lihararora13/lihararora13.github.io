drei&zwanzig is a fully vegan café on the Blumenstraße, a small, wheelchair-accessible room with outdoor seating, doing cakes, sandwiches and coffee, all of it plant-based. It keeps daytime hours, Tuesday to Saturday from ten to six, and it is closed on Sundays and Mondays, which makes it a weekday and Saturday stop rather than a weekend one. The cakes are the reason to come.

Order a slice of vegan cake and an oat cappuccino, and treat it as the quiet café pause on a walk down the Kaiserstraße.
