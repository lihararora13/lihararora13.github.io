Stella is a Glenwood local with a surprisingly serious vegan menu for a sports-bar setting. The kitchen does a pizza with vegan cheese, Beyond burgers and sausages, poke bowls, toasted sandwiches and wraps, so vegans are not stuck with one sad option while everyone else orders freely. It is lively, generous and open all day.

This is the easy group pick in Glenwood: vegans eat well, omnivores are happy, and nobody has to negotiate the menu. The vegan pizza and the Beyond Burger are the safest bets.
