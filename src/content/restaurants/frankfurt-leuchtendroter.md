Leuchtendroter is the all-vegan sibling of Seven Swans, run by the same team on the ground floor of the Lindley Lindenberg hotel in the Ostend. The food is tapas and sharing platters, the ingredients mostly come from the owners' own farm and in-house bakery, and the whole thing is fully vegan since June 2021.

Reservations are required and dietary restrictions should be mentioned when booking, but the reward is one of Frankfurt's most thoughtful vegan meals. Order the sharing platter, add the house bread, and let the farm do the talking.
