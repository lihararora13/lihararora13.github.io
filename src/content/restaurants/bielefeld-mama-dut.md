Mama Dut is a vegetarian Middle Eastern and Turkish spot on Stapenhorststrasse, serving warm chickpea wraps, falafel, bowls, vegan lahmacun and vegan-labelled stuffed vine leaves. The menu also lists halloumi and tuna, so order explicitly vegan, including the sauces.

Order the warm chickpea wrap, the falafel and the vegan lahmacun, and confirm the sauces are vegan, since one vegan-titled dish describes yoghurt and butter sauce.
