Oh K-fée d'MJ is a cosy café a short walk from the Jardin des Plantes, serving an Amaryllis plate alongside black sesame and hojicha lattes. It is the quiet coffee stop on this page, and the one to pick when the group wants to linger.

One honest note: the local guide says it keeps a vegetarian label because the milk choices for drinks need separate attention, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan. Confirm the milk before ordering a latte.
