Every good vegan city guide needs a bakery, and Bangalore's is Crave by Leena, the city's only 100% vegan patisserie. What started as an online bakery in 2019 now has a bright little café above the Airtel store at Maharaja Signal in Koramangala, and the case holds more than 30 varieties of vegan cakes, 30-plus cupcakes, cheesecakes, cinnamon rolls, doughnuts, brownies and cookies, with gluten-free, sugar-free and vegan keto options alongside. It is PETA-awarded, Sharan-certified, and rated 4.9 from its reviewers and 4.5 on HappyCow.

Come for a slice of chocolate truffle cake or mango gateaux, a raspberry tiramisu cupcake, or the vegan cream-cheese cinnamon rolls the regulars swear by. Everything is made fresh, so order a few hours ahead for whole cakes, and save the savoury side of the menu, the tempeh sandwiches and wraps, for a second visit.
