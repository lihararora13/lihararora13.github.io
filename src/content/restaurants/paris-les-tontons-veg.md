Les Tontons Veg is the neighborhood vegan burger joint Paris needed,
homemade street food with a Levantine twist from chefs Amir and Vincent.
Burgers arrive cut in half so you can go 50/50 with a friend, and the
sauces are all made in-house.

The Katsu and Cebolla burgers are the fan favorites, and the herb fries
with basil lemonade make the perfect side. Friendly, filling, and
wallet-friendly.
