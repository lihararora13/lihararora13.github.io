Sara Green is the vegan and healthy-food stop by Piața Victoriei, with burgers, wraps, salads, poke bowls, gluten-free options, sweets and drinks. The Vitality Bowl is the dish to order, and the burgers start from 39 lei. The Lascăr Catargiu bistro is the one to use: it is open Monday to Friday from 9am to 8:30pm and weekends from 11:30am.

A second branch in Pipera is a weekday office-lunch spot, open weekdays only. Come here for a healthy daytime meal rather than a late dinner.
