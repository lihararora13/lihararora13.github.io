Golden West Cafe is a Hampden staple on West 36th Street, the Avenue's main drag, serving breakfast and lunch with a solid vegan bench. Tofu scrambles, vegan pancakes, and breakfast potatoes give plant-based diners a real brunch rather than a side salad.

Order the tofu scramble with breakfast potatoes and an oat milk latte. Expect a weekend wait, and bring the dog, since the patio is friendly.
