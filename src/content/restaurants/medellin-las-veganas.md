Las Veganas is the place to go when you want the Antioquian classics without compromise: a fully vegan restaurant in Conquistadores, Laureles, rated 5.0 on HappyCow and famous for its bandeja paisa vegana. The kitchen works with fresh, organic produce and turns out a daily menu of the dishes Medellín grew up on, chicharrón vegano, chorizo vegano, seitan and picada vegana, alongside desserts.

It is inexpensive, unfussy and open from breakfast through dinner, which makes it the easiest first stop for anyone arriving in Laureles hungry. Order the bandeja, add a picada to share, and finish with whatever is in the dessert case.
