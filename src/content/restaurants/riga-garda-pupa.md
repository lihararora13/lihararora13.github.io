Garda Pupa is a beloved vegetarian lunch spot in Riga, holding a 5.0 from HappyCow reviewers for its tasty vegetarian dishes served every weekday from 11 to 3. The menu is simple and daily-changing, with vegan options across the board, and it is the kind of unpretentious local lunch that visitors rarely find on their own.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, and it only opens on weekdays at lunch, so plan around it. Order the daily vegan lunch, add a soup, and enjoy one of Riga's best-value meals.
