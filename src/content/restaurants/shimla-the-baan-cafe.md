The Baan Cafe is Shimla's first and only 100% vegan cafe, and it wears the title proudly: the sign says so, the wall art says powered by plants, and the menu is 100% vegan food and products. It sits in Village Upper Kelty, 1.2 km from RKMV College in Longwood, about ten minutes from Mall Road, open 10 AM to midnight, with live music, a rooftop, and parking.

The vegan pizza, the penne pasta in red sauce, and the spinach, tofu and mushroom momos are the orders. It has just celebrated its first year, which in this business is a real achievement, and it is the table to plan a Shimla evening around.
