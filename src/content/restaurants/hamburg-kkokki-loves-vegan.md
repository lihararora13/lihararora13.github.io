Kkokki Loves Vegan is the Schanze's Korean street-food party: chicken tofu wings, miso soup, tteok-bokki, bibimbap, cauliflower wings and kimbap, all of it vegan and all of it built for sharing. It is loud, colourful and exactly the right amount of messy.

Come with friends, order a spread, and eat it all with your hands if you like. This is the funkiest table on the list, and the one you will crave again the next day.
