Rhino Vegan Beat, also known as Vegan Beat Athens, is the name at the very top of HappyCow's Athens ranking, and the numbers back it up: a perfect 5.0 from more than 1,200 reviews, the biggest review count of any vegan spot in the city. It is a 100% plant-based street food restaurant a short walk from Syntagma Square, doing vegan gyros, Greek souvlaki, burgers, salads and homemade desserts in an open-air setting, with gluten-free options and homemade drinks on the side.

Come hungry and come casual, because this is Athens vegan food at its most joyful: proper grill-shop energy, generous portions, and gyros that regulars rate as the best in the city, vegan or not. It is open seven days a week until late, which makes it the easiest first stop after a day around the Acropolis.
