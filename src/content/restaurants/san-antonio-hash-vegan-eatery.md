HASH, short for Heal And Spread Healing, is the Southside's vegan dry bar and comfort eatery: family-owned, sober-friendly, and doing phish tacos, jackfruit pozole and a jalapeño cheddar cornbread waffle that regulars call the best vegan brinner on the Southside. The mocktails are the other half of the point, and the vibe is genuinely welcoming.

It is the pick for a night out that does not revolve around alcohol, with live music and a Saturday vegan night market. Order the phish tacos, add the cornbread waffle, and stay for the scene.
