Mitti is Indore's earthy answer to the café scene, all warm wood, plants and an outdoor patio that fills up fast on pleasant evenings. The menu leans healthy without leaning boring: big salads, smoothie bowls, wholegrain sandwiches and herbal teas, with plenty that is vegan as served or one small swap away from it. Ask for no cheese or butter and the staff know exactly what you mean.

It is the kind of place that works for a slow brunch, a working lunch or a post-Sarafa reset. The food is light, the coffee is good, and the patio is one of the nicest places in Saket Nagar to linger over a second pot of tea.
