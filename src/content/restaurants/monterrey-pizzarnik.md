Pizzarnik is a vegan pizzeria with a poet's soul: slow fermentation, wood-fired ovens, and pizzas with names like Opera Prima, Best Seller and La Borderline. The menu is small, the dough is serious, and the room at Jardin 860 is a lovely place to spend an evening.

Order the Opera Prima, it is the house classic. Go Wednesday to Sunday evenings, and book if you can, it fills up.
