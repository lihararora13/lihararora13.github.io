Falafel en Hummus is a Middle Eastern falafel and hummus shop on Raamstraat in the centre, open daily from 11:00 to 23:00. All the options are vegetarian and some are vegan, with falafel, hummus, baba ganoush, falafel sandwiches and wraps on the menu.

It is a vegetarian restaurant, so it is listed as vegan-friendly; say you are vegan when you order. Order the falafel sandwich and the hummus.
