Jungle Cafe leans into its theme with real enthusiasm: greenery, animal prints and a room that feels like a small escape from VIP Road. It is the most fun room on this list, and the multicuisine menu means everyone at the table finds something.

The veg section is generous, so vegans can graze across Indo-Chinese noodles, sizzlers and pastas with a quick dairy check. Come with a group, order a spread, and let the jungle do the entertaining.
