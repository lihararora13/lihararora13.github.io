El Vegano is a straightforward all-vegan restaurant in Obrera, the kind of place locals eat at rather than tourists photograph. Plant-based Mexican and international plates, generous portions, honest prices.

Order the daily special and a dessert. It is a good, unpretentious dinner, and a useful stop if you are staying south of the centre.
