Dosa etc is a South Indian restaurant at 10 Hanover Street, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, and it is the best vegan dosa in Dunedin. The signature masala dosa, a rice and lentil crepe filled with potato, onion and spices, is naturally vegan and gluten-free, served with coconut chutney and sambar, as are the Mysore masala dosa with spicy chilli garlic chutney and the plain dosa. Idli and vada round out the vegan options. The room was a groovy vegetarian restaurant true to its roots when Cuisine magazine reviewed it, and it has since added meat to the menu, in July 2024, so it is now a veg-options restaurant rather than a vegetarian one.

It is open Tuesday to Sunday for lunch and dinner, closed Mondays, and the lunch service ends at 2:00pm or 2:30pm. Order the masala dosa first; it is the best seller and the dish the room is built around.
