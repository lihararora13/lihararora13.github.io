Yueye is an Asian dessert café on Bergstraße, and it is the sweet stop on Kiel's list: mochi, matcha soft serve, bubble tea and Asian desserts, with the ice cream, waffles and mochi all vegan. It is not a fully vegan café, though: dairy milk is still served alongside the vegan drinks, despite some listings calling it fully vegan, so order from the vegan side and say so clearly.

It sits a short walk from the Dreiecksplatz, open daily from eleven. Order the mochi, add a matcha soft serve, and take a bubble tea to go. This is the table for dessert in Kiel, and a good end to a walk through the city centre.
