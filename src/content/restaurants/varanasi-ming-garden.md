Ming Garden is Varanasi's answer to a vegetarian Chinese craving: an authentic, all-vegetarian Chinese restaurant in Ravindra Puri, near Durgakund and Assi Ghat, with a cosy vintage-vibe dining room and a loyal local following.

The Hakka noodles and momos are the orders regulars swear by, and the sizzling brownie is the sweet finish. Ask for the vegan preparation and the kitchen will happily cook without egg or dairy.
