Off Track Ice Cream is a scratch ice cream shop just off King Street that makes both traditional and vegan ice cream, with the vegan base built from raw cashews, organic coconut cream, organic avocado oil, and cane sugar. The scoops are the point, and the sundaes are the way to eat them.

It is the sweet stop at the end of a Charleston vegan crawl. Ask which flavours are vegan that day, and get a cone.
