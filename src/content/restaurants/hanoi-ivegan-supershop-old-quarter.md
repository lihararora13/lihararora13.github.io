iVegan is Hanoi's friendly face of Western-style vegan eating: a bright, health-focused cafe where the menu runs from smoothie bowls and Buddha bowls to wraps, hummus on toast and burgers, with cold-pressed juices and kombucha to wash it all down. The Old Quarter branch sits on Hang Dieu, right in the thick of the sightseeing, and there is a little shelf of packaged plant-based groceries to take away.

It is the easy, reliable stop when you want something familiar and definitely vegan between temples and markets. Come for brunch, linger over a juice, and stock up on snacks for the road.
