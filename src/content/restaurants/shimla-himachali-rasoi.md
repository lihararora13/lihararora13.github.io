Himachali Rasoi on Mall Road near Scandal Point is the place for traditional Himachali food: chana madra, babru flatbreads, and the dham plates that define the hills. It is quick, budget-friendly, and right where you are already walking.

Order the chana madra without dahi and the babru. Skip the ghee on the siddu if you order it. It is the most local table on this list.
