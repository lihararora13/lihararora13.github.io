Lun-haw is the Cebu vegan meal to start with: a fully vegan cafe in Lahug doing inventive Filipino cooking, jackfruit adobo, vegan kare-kare and tofu sisig, built on local vegetables and simple cooking methods.

It is a go-to for health-conscious students and professionals, and the seasonal menu keeps things fresh. Order the jackfruit adobo and the kare-kare.
