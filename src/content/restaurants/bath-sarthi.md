Sarthi opened on Walcot Street in spring 2025 and quickly became the area's go-to for vegetarian and vegan Indian food. The menu marks vegan dishes clearly, and the kitchen is used to cooking without dairy on request.

The vegan thali is the best way in, with dosa and chana masala close behind. Ask about the day's vegan specials, which rotate with the seasons.
