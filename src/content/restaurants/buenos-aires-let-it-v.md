Let It V is one of the city's best-known plant-based names, and the Manduca location on theatre-row Corrientes is the easy central stop: sushi, noodle dishes, salads and fusion plates made with seasonal organic ingredients, all vegan and gluten-free.

The food is fresh, bright and prettily plated, the kind of place that converts sceptical friends over one ceviche. Save room for the panna cotta.
