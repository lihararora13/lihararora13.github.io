Green Ganga Cafe sits right at Assi Ghat, and it does the simple thing well: vegetable-based plates, honest prices, and a front-row seat to the ghat. Travellers keep recommending it for vegetarian and vegan eaters who want to eat well without leaving the river.

Order the veg thali and a masala chai, and watch the ghat do its thing. It is the kind of unpretentious cafe that makes Banaras feel easy.
