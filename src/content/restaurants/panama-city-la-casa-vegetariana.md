La Casa Vegetariana is one of the oldest vegetarian restaurants in Panama City, with almost two decades of history under a family of Chinese heritage, in Plaza Colonial, El Dorado. It works as a buffet with a dozen preparations that change every day, always with a complimentary soup, and there is takeaway, breakfast and brunch.

Order the watercress balls, the breaded eggplant, the potato salad and the veggie sushi. It is inexpensive, open every day from 7 am, and the best-value vegetarian breakfast in the city.
