Purezza started in Brighton in 2015 as one of Europe's first fully vegan pizzerias, and the Manchester branch on High Street, open since 2021, is the most established all-vegan pizzeria in the city centre. The bases are sourdough, the mozzarella is made in-house, much of the menu comes gluten-free too, and the room is all vintage interiors and an open pizza oven with a cosy downstairs. Recent diners in November 2026 call it the best pizza they have ever eaten, and the festive set menu running into December is entirely plant-based.

It sits round the corner from Afflecks, a few minutes from Piccadilly Gardens, which makes it the easiest first stop for anyone arriving in the city. Order the black truffle pizza, the pepperoni and agave, the parmigiana if it is on, and save room for the tiramisu. Book ahead on Friday and Saturday nights, and come hungry: the carvery calzone alone is a roast dinner folded into pizza.
