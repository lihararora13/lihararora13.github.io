Charlie Hong Kong is Santa Cruz's street-food favorite: an Asian-fusion counter on Soquel Avenue that has served healthy, vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free food for 25 years, using mostly organic ingredients from local farmers. The menu is mostly vegetarian and labelled, so vegan ordering is easy, and the Spicy Dan, a spicy noodle dish loaded with fresh vegetables and balanced by a creamy organic coconut peanut sauce, is the dish the local tourism board singles out. Sesame noodle salad, tofu rice bowls and Thai soup round out the vegan core.

Hours are 11am to 9pm daily, takeout only in the current setup with outdoor seating. It is quick, cheap and perfect for eating seaside at Seabright Beach, just down the road.
