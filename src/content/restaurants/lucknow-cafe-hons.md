Cafe Hons runs on a simple promise: house of no sugar. The Gomti Nagar cafe builds its smoothie bowls, toasts and bakes without refined sugar, which makes it a natural home for plant-based eaters who still want dessert to taste like dessert.

The smoothie bowls are the headline, and the oat milk cold coffee is the quiet star. Ask for the vegan version of anything on the board and the kitchen knows exactly what you mean.
