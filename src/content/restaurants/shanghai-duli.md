Duli is the modern face of Shanghai vegan food: a fully vegan bistro with Sichuan roots and ideas from everywhere else. Mapo tofu hummus, tom yum wontons, gnocchi with dandan sauce and gongbao pizza all share a menu, and somehow it works.

It is pet-friendly, open daily and easy to love. Order the mapo tofu hummus to start and the gongbao pizza if you want the full fusion experience.
