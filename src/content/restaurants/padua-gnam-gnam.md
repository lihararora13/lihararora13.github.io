Gnam Gnam is an artisan organic gelateria on Piazza dei Signori, in the middle of the historic centre, certified organic since 2006 and pouring vegan gelato alongside its dairy flavours. It is the dessert stop on this list.

Order the vegan gelato flavours of the day and ask which are dairy-free, since the cabinet mixes both. The square is the place to eat it, preferably at sunset.
