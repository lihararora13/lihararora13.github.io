Jiayuan is the alley restaurant that earned a Michelin Bib Gourmand: a fully vegan eatery tucked at the end of a small lane off Guangxiao Road, close to Ximenkou metro station Exit C, after relocating from RenMin North Road. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow from 24 reviews, and a November 2026 guide calls it a Michelin Bib Gourmand vegetarian spot averaging eleven to thirteen dollars a head.

Order the plant-based Dongpo pork belly, the tangerine sweet potatoes with cashews, the lion's mane mushroom and the eggplant with truffle rice. It is a little hidden, so show the Mandarin name and address to someone nearby if you cannot find the alley, and ask for the English menu. Open 9am to 2:30pm and 5pm to 9pm.
