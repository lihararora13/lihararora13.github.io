Veggie Tales is a small all-vegan kitchen in central Hua Hin, on Hua Hin 43 Alley, with creative daily specials and a vegan pad Thai that reviewers single out. It is open Monday and Thursday to Sunday, 10am to 7:50pm, closed Tuesday and Wednesday, and it is cash only.

Order the vegan pad Thai and the daily special, and bring cash. It is the second fully vegan kitchen in Hua Hin and the best small-plate vegan meal in town.
