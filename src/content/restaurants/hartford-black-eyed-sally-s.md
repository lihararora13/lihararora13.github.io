Black-Eyed Sally's is a Southern and barbecue restaurant on Asylum Street in downtown Hartford, with live blues and a menu of vegetable plates and sides that vegans can work with. The collard greens and black-eyed peas are the reliable picks.

Order the vegetable plate with collard greens and black-eyed peas, and ask which sides are vegan, since some are cooked with meat or butter.
