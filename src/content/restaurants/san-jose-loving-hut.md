Loving Hut is the reliable chain option that never lets a vegan traveller down: everything is plant-based, the portions are generous, the prices are gentle, and the menu covers pho, golden fried fish, spring rolls and American comfort dishes in one sitting. The Blossom Hill location sits inside Westfield Oakridge Mall, which makes it the easiest vegan lunch in South San Jose.

It is not fancy, and that is the point. When you need a solid vegan meal with zero menu decoding, this is the call.
