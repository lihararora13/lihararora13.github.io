BioHarmony is a vegetarian takeaway further down the same boulevard, and it is the healthiest fast food on this list. Fresh juices, made-in-house meals, burritos, cakes, ice cream and bread, all from mostly organic, non-refined ingredients.

It has no fixed hours, so check before you go. Order a burrito and a fresh juice, and take it to go, this is Belgrade vegan food at its most practical.
