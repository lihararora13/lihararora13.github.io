Gladys started as a food stall and grew into a James Beard-nominated
restaurant, and the food tastes like it has something to prove in
the best way. Inside Edgewater Public Market, the kitchen builds
everything around vegetables with enough confidence to make a celery
root Reuben the signature dish.

The charred Caesar with miso-Parmesan routinely outshines the dairy
versions around town, and the turnip cassoulet is the kind of dish
that makes you wonder why turnips never got top billing before.
5280's readers voted it their favorite vegan and vegetarian spot in
2026, and they were right.
