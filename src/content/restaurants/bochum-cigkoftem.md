ÇiğköfteM is Bochum's fully vegan çiğköfte house on Massenbergstraße, in the heart of the city centre. The menu is short and plant-based throughout: çiğköfte wraps, lahmacun, falafel wraps, hummus and burritos, with the counter open Monday to Saturday from eleven until ten and Sundays from two until nine.

It is fast, cheap and genuinely filling, which makes it the best budget stop on this list. Order the çiğköfte wrap if it is your first visit, add a lahmacun on the side, and take it away for a walk around the Bermuda3eck.
