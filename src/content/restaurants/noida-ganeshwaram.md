Ganeshwaram is the neighbourhood pick: a pure-veg restaurant in Sector 45 that locals rate at the top of the city's vegetarian lists. It is not fancy, and that is the point, honest South Indian and North Indian plates at honest prices.

The masala dosa and idli sambar are the vegan-friendly orders. Ask for no ghee and no dahi, and the kitchen will look after you.
