Jeilbanjeom is a Korean-Chinese restaurant on Geumnamno in Dong-gu with a separate vegan menu, open daily from 10:30am to 8:30pm. The vegan menu covers kung pao vegan shrimp, fried rice, jjajangmyeon, jjamppong and mapo tofu with rice, which makes it one of the most useful vegan-friendly kitchens in Gwangju for anyone craving noodles.

Order the vegan jjajangmyeon and the mapo tofu with rice, and ask for the vegan menu by name. It is inexpensive, central and open every day.
