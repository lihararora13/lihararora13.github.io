Gaea is the Greek bistro on Aristotelous Square, serving a wide range of mezzés, reworked Greek salads and hot dishes from a terrace built for people-watching. Ask for versions without cheese or honey to turn the classics vegan: grilled vegetables, gigantes, fava, dolmades and an excellent roasted pepper salad.

Combine three or four mezzés for a full meal, at about twelve to sixteen euros per person. It is open daily from noon to midnight.
