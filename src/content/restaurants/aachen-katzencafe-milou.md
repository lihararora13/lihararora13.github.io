Katzencafé Milou is Aachen's cat café on Alexanderstraße, a small room where the resident cats set the pace and the menu carries vegan cakes and snacks alongside coffee. It is the gentlest stop on this list: no rush, no ceremony, just a slice of cake and a cat asleep on the windowsill.

It is open Wednesday to Sunday from eleven until seven, with Monday and Tuesday closed. Order a vegan cake, take a window seat, and treat it as Aachen's calmest coffee break.
