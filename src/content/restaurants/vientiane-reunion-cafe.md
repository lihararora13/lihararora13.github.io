Reunion Cafe is a fully vegan restaurant on Luangprabang Road in Chanthabouly, open daily until 9pm, doing vegan variations of typical Lao dishes using mock meats, such as stir-fried basil with pork belly for about 75,000 kip. It is the go-to vegan kitchen for digital nomads in Vientiane, and it is open every day, including New Year's Day.

Order the stir-fried basil with mock pork and a noodle soup, and come for dinner, when the kitchen is at its best. It is inexpensive, central and reliable, and it is the second fully vegan anchor in the city.
