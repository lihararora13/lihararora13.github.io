Maki & Ramen is the Meadowhall answer to a vegan lunch: a Japanese-style eatery in the shopping centre with pink cherry-blossom decor and an expansive menu with extensive vegan and vegetarian selections. Friends order different dishes and share across the table, from sushi rolls and tempura to ramen bowls.

The vegan ramen is the pick, with a sushi roll or two on the side. It is a chain-style spot inside a mall rather than an independent, but the vegan range is genuinely broad, and it is the best plant-based option for a Meadowhall day.
