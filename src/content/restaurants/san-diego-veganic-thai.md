Some restaurants chase the new thing; Veganic Thai Cafe has been quietly serving some of San Diego's best Thai food, all vegan, for over ten years. The massaman and pumpkin curries are rich and proper, the drunken noodles bring the heat on a 1-to-10 scale you get to choose, and the khao soi with egg-free noodles has a devoted following among people who claim it as their favorite dinner in the entire city.

The faux duck and crispy soy chicken can upgrade nearly anything on the menu, and the coconut-milk Thai iced tea is the move. It is a cozy, unfussy Hillcrest room that just keeps delivering.
