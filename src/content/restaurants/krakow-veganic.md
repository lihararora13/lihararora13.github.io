Veganic is the restaurant Krakow vegans call the best vegan place in the city, and the reviews read like love letters. Polish classics get the fine-dining treatment: cabbage rolls with smoked tofu and cheesy sauce on purple potato puree, pierogi ruskie, Silesian rolls in demi-glace, and a Napoleon cake to finish.

Reviewers say the quality equals fine dining and you would never know it is vegan. Book a table, order the cabbage rolls, and save room for the cake.
