Rico Rico Vegano is the Grand Lake vegan Mexican spot that HappyCow reviewers rate a perfect 5.0, and the itaya tacos are the reason: fully vegan, properly seasoned, and served with coconut horchata that tastes like a holiday. The quesadillas and burritos round out a menu that does not miss.

It is a neighbourhood gem rather than a destination, which is exactly why it belongs on the list. Order the itaya tacos, get the horchata, and enjoy the quiet win.
