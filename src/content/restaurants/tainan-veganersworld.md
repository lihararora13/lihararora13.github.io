Veganersworld is the table Tainan's vegan community rates highest, a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow, and it earns it with a very specific idea: 100% plant-based Indian cooking built on Taiwanese vegetables, with no added sugar in the salads, curries, drinks or desserts. The menu runs through curries with quinoa rice, whole-wheat roti and chapati, samosas and chai, and reviewers keep coming back to the spinach-chickpea curry and the tempeh greens.

The kitchen was previously called Swarakchit, so older guides may list it under that name; the address and the cooking are the same. It is closed on Tuesdays, and it delivers through Uber Eats and Foodpanda if you would rather eat in. Order a curry with quinoa rice, add a roti, and finish with one of the unsweetened desserts.
