Basta is Magdeburg's Neapolitan pizzeria, a wine shop, pizzeria and events space tucked into a rear courtyard on Halberstadter Strasse, and reviewers call its pizza the best in the city. The vegan offering is two labelled pizzas, Classico Vegan and Vegana, plus marinara, bruschetta and mixed olives, and other pizzas can be made vegan with plant-based cheese and salami. The wine list is the other half of the point, with Monday discounts a local ritual.

It is an evenings-only spot, Monday to Saturday from five, kitchen until ten, closed Sundays, and reservations are required. The pizzeria is in the courtyard at numbers 51-53; the wine shop has its own entrance at number 49. Order a Classico Vegan, a glass of something red, and settle in.
