La Cucina dei Colori is a vegetarian and vegan restaurant a few steps from Via Etnea, with a 4.7 HappyCow rating across 343 reviews. The format is a buffet counter where you build your own mixed plate from up to four dishes, two for 12 euros, three for 14, four for 16, with hot dishes from the counter and cold ones from the menu, plus a chocolate pan di cielo with vegetable cream for dessert.

Order a four-dish plate: the vegan pulled, the rice with artichokes, peas and fava beans, and whatever Sicilian vegetables are on the counter, then the chocolate pan di cielo. It serves lunch and dinner six days a week.
