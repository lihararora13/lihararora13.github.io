The Vegan Kitchen made the list for a reason. A 100% vegan home restaurant serving Sri Lankan breakfasts, veggie burgers, sweets and snacks, pre-order a day ahead. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Boralesgamuwa, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Sri Lankan breakfast, Veggie burger, Vegan sweets if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
