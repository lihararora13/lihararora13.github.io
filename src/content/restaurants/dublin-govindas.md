Govinda's has been a Dublin fixture since 1997, a long-running Hare Krishna vegetarian restaurant that has fed generations of city-centre wanderers. The room is simple, the welcome is warm, and the cooking is the kind of honest Indian comfort food that never goes out of style.

The Govinda's Special is the must-order: fragrant stewed vegetables, steaming basmati rice and tender tofu in a savoury tomato sauce, with freshly fried samosas to start and a tropical pineapple coconut punch to sip alongside. The kitchen uses dairy but not eggs, so vegans should ask about the day's vegan dishes, of which there are always several.
