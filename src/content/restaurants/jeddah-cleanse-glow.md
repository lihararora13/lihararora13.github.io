Cleanse and Glow is a juice bar and kitchen at the Mosaidiah Center offering abundant vegan options, zoodles, sweet potato pizza, black bean burgers, smoothies and raw desserts. It is strongly vegan-oriented but not consistently 100% vegan, so it is filed vegan-friendly.

Order the sweet potato pizza and a black bean burger. It is the healthiest room on this list, and the raw desserts are worth saving room for.
