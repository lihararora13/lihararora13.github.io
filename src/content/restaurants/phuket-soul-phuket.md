Soul Phuket, also listed as Soul I Vegan Cafe & Restaurant, is Karon's fully vegan restaurant and cocktail bar, reported fully vegan since October 2022 and holding a 4.5 from 160 HappyCow reviews. The menu runs Thai and international, pizza, bowls, burgers and Mediterranean dishes, with child-friendly options and cocktails, and it stays open later than most vegan rooms on the island, until 9:30pm.

It is the Karon pick in this guide, on Karon Road near the beach. Note that card payment carries a 3% surcharge. The pin on this page is neighbourhood-level for Karon, as the geocoder matched the wider Karon area rather than the street number.
