Le Artisan Boulangerie is the bakery stop on this list: a French boulangerie with branches in Bodakdev and Thaltej, and this Bodakdev branch in the Magnifico building opposite the Avalon Hotel is the one the vegan guides cite. Selected vegan dishes are on the menu, and the bakery trades daily.

Order the chia sourdough lavash with the spinach and cashew vegan dip, and the vegan orange and chocolate slice for dessert. It is a bakery first, so come for a light lunch or an afternoon bake rather than a full dinner.
