Santo Falafel is the cheap eat in Florence: a fully vegan falafel spot in the Oltrarno, open from 11:30am to midnight every day, which makes it the late-night vegan option in the city. Everything is vegan, the motto is In Hummus We Believe, and the falafel wrap, hummus and dolmas are the orders.

Take your wrap to Piazza Santo Spirito, a few minutes' walk away. There is a second branch at Borgo Allegri near the Sant'Ambrogio market.
