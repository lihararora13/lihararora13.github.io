Buddhachay is Oldenburg's fully vegan Vietnamese, Asian and Japanese restaurant, right on the Markt, and it is the second all-vegan anchor of the list. Phở, noodle bowls, sushi rolls and wok dishes come out of a modern dining room in the middle of the old town, all plant-based, and the menu is long enough that regulars have their own orders and newcomers need a minute to choose.

It sits on the Markt itself, open Monday to Saturday from eleven. Order the phở if it is your first visit, add sushi rolls to share, and save room for a wok dish. This is the table for Asian food in Oldenburg, and the easiest vegan meal in the city centre.
