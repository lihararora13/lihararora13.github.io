Tofu Vegan is a small, focused joy: a fully vegan Chinese restaurant tucked into Meeting House Lane, holding a 5.0 on HappyCow. The menu is built around tofu done properly, with king oyster and shiitake mushrooms, mapo tofu in spicy Sichuan-style sauce, gong bao king oyster mushrooms with peanuts and chilli, and dim sum like steamed vegetable dumplings and wontons in house special sauce.

It is the kind of place where the food is the whole event, no fuss and no noise. Order the mapo tofu, add the dumplings, and let the kitchen show off what tofu can do.
