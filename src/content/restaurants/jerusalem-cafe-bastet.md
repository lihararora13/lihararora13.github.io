Cafe Bastet is a little vegetarian cafe on Heleni Hamalka in the city centre serving fresh juice and coffee, breakfast foods, sandwiches, salads, soups and rice plates. The owner is a long-time vegan, and the kitchen is happy to adapt dishes.

It is open daily from 9:00am to 5:00pm. Order breakfast or a sandwich with a fresh juice.
