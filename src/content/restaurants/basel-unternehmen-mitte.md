Unternehmen Mitte is an organic cafe, bar and restaurant in the old town, open daily from eight to one in the morning, serving lunch menus, coffee and cakes with vegan options. It is the late-night coffee pick on this list.

Order the lunch menu, a coffee with plant milk and a cake, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan dishes, since the menu also runs meat and dairy.
