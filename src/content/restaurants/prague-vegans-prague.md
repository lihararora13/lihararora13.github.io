Vegan's Prague is the place every Prague vegan guide points to first, and one visit explains why. Tucked into a 16th-century house on the cobbled climb up to Prague Castle, it serves the Czech classics other restaurants will not touch: goulash, svickova, meatballs with dumplings, all fully plant-based, alongside international dishes and a pastry case of raw cakes.

Come for lunch after the castle and aim for the rooftop terrace if you can. Order the goulash, save room for a fruit dumpling or raw chocolate cake, and be ready for the stairs: three flights up, and entirely worth the climb.
