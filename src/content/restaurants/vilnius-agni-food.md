Agni Food is a vegetarian restaurant at a cosy and welcoming yoga ashram in the Old Town, a space to nurture body and soul where most dishes are also made in a vegan version. The menu runs from buddha bowls and focaccia to arancini, tofu and ciabatta, with lunch, sweets, coffee and tea.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so ask for the vegan version when you order. Order a buddha bowl, add arancini, and enjoy the calmest room on this list.
