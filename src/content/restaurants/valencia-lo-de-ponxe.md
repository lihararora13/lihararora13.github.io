Lo de Ponxe is Valencia's most low-key vegan gem, an informal food bar with a weekly menu and a perfect HappyCow score. A few choices per course, starter, main and dessert, all vegan, all cooked that week, all very good value.

It is lunch only, Monday to Saturday, so plan around it. Order the full weekly menu, it is the cheapest proper vegan lunch in the old town, and the one locals actually eat at.
