Bapu Ki Kutia started in Bhopal and now runs fourteen outlets across Madhya Pradesh, which tells you most of what you need to know: this is the city's trusted pure-veg workhorse. The kitchens are live, so you can watch your food being made, and the staff happily adjust spice levels and leave out ghee, butter and cream on request.

Order the special veg thali without ghee, add dal tadka and tandoori roti, and you have the classic Bhopal vegetarian meal, veganised. It is unpretentious, filling and fair-priced, the kind of place you return to twice in one trip.
