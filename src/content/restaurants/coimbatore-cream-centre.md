Cream Centre has been a pure-veg legacy brand since 1958, and the Coimbatore outpost on Race Course carries that reputation well. The menu is global veg: sizzling brownie, signature biryani, macaroni magic, paneer cheese lollipops, and the kind of Indo-Chinese that defined a generation of Indian vegetarian dining.

Order the signature veg biryani and the macaroni magic, and ask the kitchen to hold the paneer and cheese. It is the special-occasion veg restaurant in Coimbatore, and the room knows it.
