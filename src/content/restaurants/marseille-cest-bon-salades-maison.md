C'est Bon Salades Maison is the Eastern Mediterranean answer to a Marseille lunch: a friendly restaurant in the Cinq Avenues quarter with a long list of vegan options, headlined by a seitan shawarma that regulars swear by. The mezze plates are generous, the salads are fresh, and the whole thing is very good value.

It is a takeaway-friendly, everyday kind of place, perfect for a quick, satisfying meal between the markets and the museums. Order the shawarma, add a salad, and keep walking.
