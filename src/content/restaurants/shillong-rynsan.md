Rynsan is the most celebrated restaurant in Shillong, named among India's 50 best, and it is the one place on this list where Khasi cooking takes centre stage. The dining room is elegant, stone-walled and art-filled, and the plates are built from rice, greens, beans and wild fern salad with fermented soybean chutney.

It is the priciest pick in the city, so come for a proper dinner. Much of the Khasi menu is plant-based by tradition, so tell the kitchen you eat vegan and let them guide you through the seasonal greens.
