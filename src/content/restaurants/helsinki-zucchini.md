Zucchini is the cafe Helsinki's vegans name when they want healthy food, a vegetarian cafe in Kaartinkaupunki with a strong vegan menu of salads, bowls and wholesome plates. Local guides call it the best healthy vegan food in the city, and the cooking is fresh, light and properly satisfying rather than virtuous for its own sake.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so check the menu when you order, but the vegan options are the stars. Order the vegan bowl of the day, add a soup, and feel good about lunch.
