Young Vegan is a small, much-loved spot with a perfect HappyCow score: baked goods, desserts, smoothies, pasta and pizza, with gluten-free options, from a team that knows vegan baking properly. Seating is limited, so it is also a great takeaway stop.

Order a pizza or a pasta, then raid the bakery case. The desserts are the reason regulars come back.
