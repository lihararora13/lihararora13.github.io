Refúgio Veg started as a delivery kitchen in 2021 and grew into a little dining room that now holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow, and the reason is simple: it does vegan comfort food properly. There are nine different plant-based burgers, vegan cheese appetizers, and Brazilian plates like strogonoff, seitan dishes and feijoada on rotation, plus a self-service buffet priced by weight from noon to 4pm. Portions are large, the staff are friendly and will adapt dishes for you, and it sits a short walk from Arpoador and the Copacabana Fort.

It opens in the evenings, and the closing days are unusual, so check before you walk over. Ask what is on that day before you sit down, because the rotation changes, and then order a burger. This is the best plant-based burger in Copacabana, and it is not close.
