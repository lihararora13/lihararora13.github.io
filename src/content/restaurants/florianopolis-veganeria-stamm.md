Veganeria Stamm is Floripa's fully vegan restaurant with a conscience: big on the environment and on doing business sustainably, serving pizzas, burgers and a variety of other plant-based plates by the lagoon. It is the kind of place where the values and the food line up, and the lagoon-side setting does the rest.

Come for dinner, order a vegan pizza to share, and enjoy the lagoon air. This is the one for a proper vegan night out in Lagoa.
