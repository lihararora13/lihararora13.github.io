New Ethic is upstate New York's first 100% vegan pizzeria, and it cooks Sicilian-American pizza without compromise: the Buffalo Chickpea Pizza, the pickle-topped Pickle Pizza, the One Justice Garbage Plate pizza, calzones, burgers and jackfruit wings, with vegan milkshakes to finish. It trades inside 2 Ton Tony's Pizza on Titus Avenue, and reviewers call it the best vegan fast food they have had, praising the garlic lemon pepper sauces and the sheer size of the sauce list.

Hours are short and evening-only, Wednesday to Saturday 4:30pm to 7:30pm, so plan around them. Dine in, take out or get it delivered, and order the Garbage Plate pizza at least once: it is Rochester's signature dish, veganized twice over in this guide.
