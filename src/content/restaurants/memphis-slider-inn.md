Slider Inn calls itself the Mecca of Mini, and its vegan bench is better than a slider bar has any right to offer: a vegan Triple B, a falafel slider, and spicy vegan wings with house-made hot sauce and ranch. The downtown room on Mulberry Street is the one to visit.

Order a spread of sliders and the wings, and share them across the table.
