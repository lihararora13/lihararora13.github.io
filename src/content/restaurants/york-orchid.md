The Orchid is the first place to send anyone eating vegan in York: a fully vegan cafe a short walk from the station, with a brunch-led menu and cakes made in-house. It has become the city's plant-based meeting point.

Order the brunch plate and save room for cake. It is busy at weekends, so arrive before noon or expect a short wait.
