Koka is Gothenburg's Michelin-starred table, a modern West Swedish restaurant in Linné where the tasting menus run to five or eight courses and the whole menu can be made vegetarian or vegan. The kitchen cooks with 90% organic produce from local growers, fishers and foragers, and the spring and summer menus are formed largely around plant-led dishes.

It is not a vegan restaurant, so tell them when you book, but the vegetarian tasting menu is superb and the vegan version is well balanced and full of flavour. Book ahead, order the tasting menu, and enjoy the finest meal in Gothenburg.
