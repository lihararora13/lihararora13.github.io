Fusion Organic Cafe is a small organic cafe on Arundel Street, a short walk from the station, serving vegetarian food with plenty of vegan options, organic coffee and home-baked cakes. It is a weekday lunchtime spot, open Tuesday to Friday from 10am to 2pm.

Order the avocado toast and the chocolate cheesecake, and take a matcha cake for the road. Check the hours before you go, as the window is short, and note it is vegetarian rather than all-vegan.
