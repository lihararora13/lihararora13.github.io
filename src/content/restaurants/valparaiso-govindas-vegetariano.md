Govindas Vegetariano is the long-running Hare Krishna kitchen in El Almendral, rated 4.8 from over 200 reviewers, serving thalis, sabji de coliflor en crema de coco, chapati, seitan and tofu, with a small grocery store attached. The food is described by the kitchen as food for the soul, offered daily on the altar, and the prices are among the lowest on this list.

Order the thali, add the cauliflower sabji, and take chapati on the side. It is open daily for lunch into the early evening, and it is the most reliable cheap vegan lunch in the flat part of town. Do not confuse it with the separate Govindas Hare Krishna kitchen on Morris.
