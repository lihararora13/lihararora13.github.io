Quore di Panna is a gelateria and yogurteria in Corciano, just outside Perugia, with vegan gelato flavours made on soy or rice milk. It is the dessert stop on this list, and the place to finish a day that started at L'Erbavoglio.

Order the vegan gelato flavours of the day and ask which are made on soy or rice milk. It is a short drive from the centre, so pair it with the Ellera stop.
