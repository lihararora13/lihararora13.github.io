Mangolds is a vegetarian and vegan restaurant and cafe near the Kunsthaus on the Mur, rated 4.6 from 35 reviews, serving daily changing lunch dishes, a salad buffet, a vegan bowl bar, pinsa, burritos and homemade cakes. It is the salad-buffet pick on this list.

Order the lunch dish of the day, a Greek bowl and a burrito, and ask the staff which cakes are vegan, since the menu also runs dairy.
