Trio is comfort food at its most joyful: crispy cauliflower wings, jackfruit riblets, mac and cheeze, and collard greens that taste like somebody's grandmother is back there supervising. As Minnesota's first Black-owned fully vegan restaurant, it's a community anchor on East Lake and a pilgrimage spot for traveling vegans.

The soul food mac bowl and Boolin buffalo wrap are the moves. Bring an appetite, bring friends, and expect the cozy room to be full of people who clearly come here a lot.
