Kuku Coffee Shop is the café inside Jaisalmer Fort, a small room near Hari Om Jewellers in Chougen Para that travellers plan their fort days around. It is rated 4.7 stars across 567 reviews, and the coffee is the best in the city.

Order a coffee with plant milk and a vegan cake, and sit with the fort's golden walls around you. It is the most atmospheric coffee on this list, and the natural first stop in Jaisalmer.
