Aroydee is a Thai restaurant on Flachstrasse, the second branch of a Frankfurt restaurant, with marked vegan meal choices across the Thai menu. It is open for lunch and dinner daily, with a late Sunday start.

Order the marked vegan Thai dishes, the curries and the noodles, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan marking, since the menu also runs meat dishes.
