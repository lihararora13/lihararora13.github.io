Kaze Bento is the Beaufort Street local that vegans whisper about. It is a proper Japanese restaurant first, but its vegan menu is no afterthought: the vegan katsu chicken bowl, the vegan big mac sushi and the eggplant unagi all get the same care as everything else on the pass, and the 4.8-star reviews read like love letters.

It is cheap, cheerful and family-friendly, with bento boxes, udon and curry laksa for cold nights. Ask for the vegan menu when you sit down and order the katsu bowl; it is the dish people cross town for.
