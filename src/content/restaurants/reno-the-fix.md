The Fix Falafel and Things is an all-vegan falafel counter inside West Street Market in downtown Reno, a former food truck now serving plant-based comfort food from a market stall. The menu runs from falafel wraps and asada tacos to the chopped cheez sandwich and wings, all vegan by default.

Order the falafel wrap and the asada tacos. Note the hours: Wednesday through Sunday, noon to 8pm, and the pin is the market address rather than a surveyed entrance.
