The Clever Rabbit is Edmonton's vegetarian waterhole, a cozy cafe on 124th Street that has been feeding the city's veggies for years. The menu is simple and good: veggie burgers, noodle soup, juices, and falafel on Fridays, with plenty of vegan options.

It is busy on weekends and loud with music, which is part of the charm. Order the veggie burger and a juice, and come on a Friday if you want the falafel.
