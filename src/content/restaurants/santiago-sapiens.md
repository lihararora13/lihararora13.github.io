Sapiens is the name at the very top of HappyCow's Santiago ranking, and it earns the spot the friendly way: a health food cafe in Barrio Italia that has been doing this since 2017, with a perfect review score across 164 reviews. The menu covers the whole day, from buddha bowls and burritos to personal pizzas, green smoothies and a dessert case that tempts everyone who walks past it.

Come hungry and come curious, because this is the rare vegan cafe that works equally well for brunch, a quick lunch between antique shops, or a sweet stop at the end of a Barrio Italia wander. The space is animal friendly, the portions are generous, and the burritos get singled out again and again by visitors.
