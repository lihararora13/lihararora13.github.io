Spiral Diner & Bakery has been serving down-home vegan cooking in Fort Worth since 2002, and it is the city's comfort-food anchor: a retro diner on Magnolia Avenue where the Spiral Burger, the Nacho Supremo, and the Frito pie are the orders, and the bakery case, cakes and ice cream, is the reason to save room.

It serves breakfast, brunch, lunch, and dinner, and it is kid-friendly with outdoor seating. Order the burger, add the elote nachos, and finish with a slice of cake.
