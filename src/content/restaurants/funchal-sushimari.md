SushiMari is a proper Japanese restaurant inside the Monumental Lido shopping centre, and it is the one sushi bar on the island that vegans plan around rather than settle for. The room is calm and precise, the service is used to dietary requests, and reviewers call its vegan platter one of the best meals they have had in Madeira.

Ask for the vegan freestyle sushi, which is not printed on the menu: the kitchen builds it from fruit and vegetable combinations, with vegan gyoza and tempura alongside. It is open daily from late morning to late evening, and the platter for two is the move if you are sharing.
