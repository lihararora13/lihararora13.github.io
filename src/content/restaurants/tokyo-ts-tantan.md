Every vegan trip to Tokyo seems to start, end, or pass through T's TanTan. Hidden inside Tokyo Station near the Keiyo Line, this cheerful counter proved years ago that a completely plant-based tantanmen could be rich, spicy, and deeply sesame-satisfying, and travellers have been queuing happily ever since.

The Golden Sesame bowl is the classic order, with gyoza on the side if you are doing it properly. It is fast, affordable, and inside the gates, so plan it around a train and thank past you for the excellent scheduling.
