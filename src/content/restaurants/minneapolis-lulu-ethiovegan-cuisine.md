Lulu EthioVegan blends traditional Ethiopian cooking with plant-based everything, and the result is one of the most delicious cultural tables in the city. Chef Tigist Feyisa's kitchen sends out wots fragrant with berbere, tibs sizzling on gluten-free injera, and a veggie sampler made for sharing with your whole crew.

Eating here is a communal joy: everyone digging into the same injera, tearing off pieces, passing plates. Order the sampler if you can't decide, then decide you need to come back tomorrow.
