OR2K made the list for a reason. A three-floor vegetarian institution in Thamel, famous for hummus, falafel and mezze, with vegan options across the menu. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Thamel, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Hummus, Falafel, Veg mezze if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
