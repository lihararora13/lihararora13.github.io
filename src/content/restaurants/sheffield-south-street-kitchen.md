South Street Kitchen is tucked into the Park Hill flats with huge windows over green park views, and it serves some of the best brunch food in Sheffield. The kitchen leans Middle Eastern, with its own pickles and ferments, and most of the menu is vegan.

The South Street hummus plate is the order, with falafel and muhammara alongside, and the Friday and Saturday mezze evenings are the time to come for dinner. It is a vegetarian cafe rather than an all-vegan one, so vegans should check the egg dishes, but the vegan options are the heart of the menu.
