Palo Verde is the gentle, plant-filled bistro that Prague vegans start their day at. Open from 7:30 every morning, it does the full arc from breakfast waffles topped with coconut whipped cream to evening pasta, fusion bowls and Spanish tapas, with organic wines and a daily case of bakery treats in between.

Come hungry in the morning and linger. The house-made pasta is the move at dinner, the croissants are the move at any hour, and the enclosed patio makes it an easy all-weather stop in the New Town.
