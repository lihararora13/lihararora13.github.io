Itadakizen is the traditional counterweight to Veggy Way's ramen: a fully vegan restaurant in Maruyama cooking tofu dishes and veggie plates in the quiet, balanced style of traditional Japanese cuisine, minus any dashi worry.

It is a calm, unhurried meal. Note that hours can shrink in the coldest winter months, so check ahead from December to February.
