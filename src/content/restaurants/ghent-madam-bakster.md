Madam Bakster is a plant-based bakery on Brabantdam, with no refined sugar, known for cake and coffee. It is a small, calm room with a checkered floor, and the cakes change with the season, so regulars come back to see what is in the case.

It is open Wednesday to Sunday, so plan it as a midweek or weekend cake stop rather than a Monday visit. Order cake and coffee, and take a slice for the train.
