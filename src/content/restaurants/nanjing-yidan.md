YI'DAN is the mountain table of Nanjing: a vegetarian restaurant at the east entrance of the Niushou Mountain scenic area, at No. 18 Ningdan Avenue, where the dishes are relatively rich and many are vegan. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, with a review from April 2026 calling the sweet and sour lotus root life changing, and Trip.com's Niushou guide names its Mountain Zen Vegetarian Feast, with Arhat Vegetarian, Lotus Root Fortune Bags and bamboo fungus mushroom soup.

Order the sweet and sour lotus root, the oyster mushroom sweet and spicy and a set menu. It sits right outside the scenic area, with parking, so plan it as the meal after the Buddha Summit Palace. Open daily 10:30am to 7pm.
