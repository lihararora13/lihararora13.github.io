Chacha Cafe is Bhopal's friendly budget café, the kind of place that serves parathas, momos, Chinese noodles and South Indian snacks under one roof and charges the same price whether you dine in or order online. The food is pure vegetarian, the prices are gentle, and the room is bright and welcoming rather than fancy.

Vegans should order the parathas, momos and noodles plainly and skip the curd and butter that come alongside. It is a great low-key lunch stop, honest food at honest prices in the middle of MP Nagar.
