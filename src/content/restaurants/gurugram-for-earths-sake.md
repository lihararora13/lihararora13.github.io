For Earth's Sake is a zero-waste store and cafe in DLF Galleria, the kind of place where the shopping and the lunch share the same philosophy. The cafe serves planet-friendly, plant-forward food in a space that takes sustainability seriously rather than as a slogan.

Order the Buddha bowl and a cold-pressed juice, and browse the store after. It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so confirm the dairy-free version when you order.
