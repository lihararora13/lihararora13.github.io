La Hoja Verde is the sit-down taco meal in Tulum, right on Avenida Tulum at Calle Beta Sur, and it is the place to go when the street stands are not enough. The pastor and barbacoa soy-meat tacos come stacked with grilled mushrooms and guacamole, and reviewers in 2026 are still happy with the food.

It is almost entirely vegan, but a few items use honey or dairy, so ask when you order. It costs more than the street stands, but the loaded plates are worth it at least once.
