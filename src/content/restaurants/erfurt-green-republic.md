Green Republic is Erfurt's all-vegan anchor, the highest-rated vegan spot in the city, and it cooks the comfort food people actually crave: burgers, doner, wraps, seitan gyros, salads, weekly special soups and drinks, with tiramisu to finish. The whole menu is vegan, the room is friendly and unfussy, and the food is the kind that wins over sceptics without a lecture.

It sits on Johannesstrasse in the old town, open Tuesday to Friday from half past eleven to eight and Saturdays from half past twelve. Come for lunch, order the seitan gyros or the sticky tofu wrap, and save room for the tiramisu. This is the first table for any vegan arriving in Erfurt.
