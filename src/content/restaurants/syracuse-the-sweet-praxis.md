The Sweet Praxis is an all-vegan bakery and cafe on East Water Street, doing pastries, cakes, and brunch plates with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the sweet anchor of this list, and the place to order a whole cake for a celebration. The address here is 203 East Water Street; some listings say 2041 East Genesee Street, which is wrong.

Order a vegan pastry and a cake slice, and ask what is fresh that day, since the case rotates.
