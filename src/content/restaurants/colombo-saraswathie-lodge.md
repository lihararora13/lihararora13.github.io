Saraswathie Lodge made the list for a reason. A long-running South Indian vegetarian eating house on Galle Road, loved for its value, where a little money buys a lot of food. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Bambalapitiya, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Masala dosa, Idli sambar, Filter coffee, black if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
