Hari Om Cafe is the traditional Indian vegetarian stop in Bodh Gaya's cafe row: thalis, curries, parathas and soups at affordable prices, listed in the town's travel guides as the place for authentic Indian vegetarian cuisine. It is a simple, calm room, and a good counterpoint to the Tibetan and continental cafes around it.

Order the veg thali or a paratha with sabzi, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you have a solid, cheap, plant-based meal near the temple.
