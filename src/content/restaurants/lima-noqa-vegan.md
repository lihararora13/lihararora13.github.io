Noqa Vegan is the highest-rated vegan spot in Lima, and finding it is half the adventure: a stall on the second floor of the hectic Polvos Azules shopping center, up the escalators and past the gauntlet of vendors to the back corner of the food court. The reward is a huge menu of Latin American cooking, Peruvian, Venezuelan, Mexican, Colombian and Cuban dishes, plus a 12-sol set menu that might be the best-value vegan lunch in the city.

Come hungry and come curious, because this is the rare perfect-scored kitchen that locals and travellers both swear by. The hallacas and arepas get singled out again and again, the seitan dishes are generous, and the whole place runs on the kind of warmth that makes a shopping-center food court feel like a family kitchen.
