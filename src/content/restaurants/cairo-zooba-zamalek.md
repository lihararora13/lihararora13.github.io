Zooba is the chain the guide points first-timers to, because every dish is tagged for diet and the vegan koshari and taameya are clearly marked. No guesswork, no awkward conversation, just modern Egyptian fast-casual done well. The Zamalek branch is the prettiest, with Maadi and New Cairo branches too.

Order the vegan koshari, a taameya sandwich in baladi bread, and foul if it is breakfast time. It is the easiest vegan meal in Cairo, and one of the cheapest.
