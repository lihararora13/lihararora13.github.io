Smith's Alternative is part cafe, part bar, part live-music venue and part creative hub, and the food matches the energy: mostly vegan and vegetarian, generous, and made for lingering. The sweets are all vegan, including a tiramisu that regulars swear by, and there are plenty of gluten-free plates too. It is open late, which makes it one of the few places in Civic where you can eat well and catch a gig in the same evening.

Come for lunch or stay for the music, and ask what the kitchen is cooking that day. This is the one for a long, lazy evening that starts with food and ends with a band.
