Loving Hut is the comfort-food stop of Brisbane's vegan scene. Out on Logan Road in Mount Gravatt, this branch of the international vegan chain cooks a menu that wanders the globe: Chinese, Italian, Thai and Singaporean dishes, all vegan, from the signature Loving wrap to a mushroom carbonara that converts the sceptics and a lasagna regulars insist you order.

It is family-friendly, generous with portions, and closed on Sundays, so plan around that. Come for lunch or dinner, order the wrap and the lasagna, and save room for the mini muffins and cake waiting at the counter.
