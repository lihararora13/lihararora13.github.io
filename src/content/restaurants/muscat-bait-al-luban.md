Bait Al Luban is a traditional Omani restaurant on the Muttrah Corniche with a separate vegetarian menu. Vegan dishes include vegetable curries, a variety of appetizers, lentil soup and a fruit platter for dessert, and reservations are required.

It is open daily from noon to 11:00pm. Order the vegetable curries with lentil soup, and note the pin is at street level on the Corniche.
