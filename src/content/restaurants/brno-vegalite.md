Vegalite is a vegan restaurant and bar on Slovakova, rated 4.6 from 528 reviews, open since 2009 and focused on vegan cuisine with gluten-free options, brewing five of its own beers and pouring others from Czech mini-breweries, with live D.I.Y. bands in the evenings. It is the top pick on this list.

Order the lion's mane steak, the seitan and the tofu teriyaki noodles, and come for lunch or a late evening, since it opens to midnight.
