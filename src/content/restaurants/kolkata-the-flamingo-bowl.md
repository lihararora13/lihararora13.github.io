The Flamingo Bowl made the list for a reason. A Pan-Asian restaurant with an easy-to-read menu that flags vegan, vegetarian and Jain options, one of the city's most accessible plant-based stops. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Bhawanipur, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Asian bowl, Tofu stir-fry, Vegan noodles if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
