Franz Soupmarine is a small, cheerful soup bar in the Altstadt, the kind of place students and old-town workers rely on for a fast, honest lunch. The menu is short and changes with the day: soups, börek, cakes and coffee, all of it vegetarian with plenty vegan, served at counter prices in a room that seats only a handful. It is not a destination restaurant, it is a neighbourhood habit, and a good one.

It sits on the Märzgasse, just off the Hauptstraße, and keeps daytime hours Monday to Saturday. Order the soup of the day with a börek on the side, add a slice of vegan cake, and you have one of the cheapest proper lunches in the old town.
