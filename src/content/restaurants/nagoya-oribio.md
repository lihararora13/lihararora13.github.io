ORIBIO is Nagoya's most dedicated fully vegan restaurant, and it cooks like it means it: organic vegetables grown on the restaurant's own farm, no meat, fish, eggs or dairy, and no onion or garlic either, following Buddhist shojin principles. The vegan katsu bowl with tofu egg is the dish people remember.

Dinner runs Wednesday to Saturday evenings, with Sunday lunch, and the restaurant is closed Monday and Tuesday. Book ahead for dinner.
