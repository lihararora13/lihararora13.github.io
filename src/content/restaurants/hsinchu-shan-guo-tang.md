Shan Guo Tang is a creative vegetarian restaurant on Zhuangjing South Road in Zhubei, in a bright wooden room, drawing on Taiwanese, Sichuan and Jiangzhe cooking, and a MICHELIN Bib Gourmand in the 2026 guide. The Tourism Administration's vegetarian dining guide lists vegan, ovo-lacto, ovo and lacto dishes, so say which you eat when you order, as the kitchen is vegetarian rather than fully vegan.

Ask for the vegan dishes by name and order the seasonal vegetables and tofu dishes. It is open Tuesday to Sunday, closed Mondays, and it takes cards.
