Govinda's has been feeding Perth's plant-based crowd since the 1980s, and it still might be the best value meal in the city. Ten dollars buys an all-you-can-eat buffet of dahl, curries, basmati rice and vegetables, cooked with devotion and served in a calm, humble room on William Street, with the samosas earning their own fan club.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so ask the staff which dishes are vegan on the day; there is always plenty. Come early, come hungry, and leave room for the halva.
