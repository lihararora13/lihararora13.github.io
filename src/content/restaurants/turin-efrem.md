efrem is the ultimate fully vegan Italian pastry shop, and HappyCow reviewers gave it a perfect 5.0. The menu changes daily, with a large variety of fresh pastries, plus savoury dishes and vegan cheeses to take home, in a cafe, restaurant and pastry shop rolled into one.

It relocated from Corso Vittorio Emanuele II to Via Almese 15/E in Cit Turin, so use the new address. Come for breakfast, order the pistachio croissant, and save room for whatever is in the case that day.
