Falafel Wisam Jabali is a falafel restaurant on HaGalil Street that serves falafel only, and HappyCow rates it 5.0. It is the purest vegan meal in Nazareth: chickpea falafel, tahini, pickles and pita, done one way and done well.

Order the falafel in a pita with tahini. It is cheap, fast and open long hours, and it is the single best vegan meal in the city.
