Nirvana is an Indian restaurant on Massachusetts Avenue in North Cambridge with a menu that vegans can work through comfortably. Curries, dals, and vegetable dishes are the core, and the kitchen is used to vegan requests.

Order the chana masala and dal tadka, and ask which breads are vegan, since most naan is brushed with butter or ghee.
