Chay Corner is a small, beloved vegan spot near the beach, and the dumplings are the reason people keep coming back: delicate Vietnamese dumplings, made fresh, alongside noodle soups and banh mi chay, all for local prices. It is simple, quick and deeply satisfying, the kind of place you stumble into once and then plan your day around.

Come for a snack or a light lunch, order dumplings and a noodle soup, and thank the chay tradition for existing. This is the one for dumpling lovers.
