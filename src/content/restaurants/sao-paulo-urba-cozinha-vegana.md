Urba, listed as Urbã Cozinha Vegana, is the little neighbourhood spot that visiting vegans rave about most: a short menu, a dish of the day, and cooking that feels personal rather than performative. Lunch only, so plan it as your midday mission.

Order both mains if you can, the mushroom pasta especially, and do not leave without the chocolate fudge or an alfajor for later. Friendly, buzzy and a little out of the centre, in the best possible way.
