The Grand Thakar is Rajkot's thali institution: a Gujarati dining room on Jawahar Road, by the Jubeli vegetable market, where the thali is the whole point and the room is full of families who have been coming for years. It tops this list on local love alone.

Order the Gujarati thali with the ghee held and the curd left off, and the plate is properly plant-based. Come hungry, because the thali does not believe in small portions.
