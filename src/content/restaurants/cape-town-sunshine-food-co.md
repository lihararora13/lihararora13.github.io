Sunshine is the pocket-sized proof that a short menu done properly beats a long one done lazily. Everything is organic and vegan, the greens and sprouts are homegrown, and the signature burger arrives as a tower of patty, avo, black bean salsa, hummus and crispy onions, with a side of dhal where the fries would normally be.

ProVeg calls it a tiny, fully vegan restaurant with big ideas, Cape Town Tourism lists it among the city's plant-based gems, and reviewers keep going back two days in a row. Affordable, quirky and properly delicious.
