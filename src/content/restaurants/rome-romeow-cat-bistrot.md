Romeow is the meal people tell stories about when they get home. The food is 100% vegan and genuinely creative, Italian dishes with Indian and Mexican accents, a raw menu after 8pm, and a seasonal tasting menu that regulars call the best vegan food they had in their life. Then there are the cats: six residents who wander the room, nap on chairs, and supervise dinner.

Come for breakfast and the pancakes, or book dinner days ahead, reservations are usually needed five or more days out for the evening service. It is the rare restaurant that is both a gimmick and the real deal, and the food would hold up with the cats nowhere in sight.
