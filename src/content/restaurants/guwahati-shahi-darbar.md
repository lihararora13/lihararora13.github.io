Shahi Darbar has been Fancy Bazar's go-to for classic North Indian vegetarian food for twelve years, and it is one of the three spots local food creators name when they list the city's best pure-veg restaurants. The dining room is busy and family-oriented, the tandoor runs all day, and the vegetarian Indian and Chinese menu is broad.

Order a thali or tandoori roti with dal, and ask the kitchen to skip the paneer and butter. It is a good second stop on a Fancy Bazar veg crawl after Gopal Maharaj.
