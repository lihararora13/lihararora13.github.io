Veganes Cigkofte is a mostly vegan Turkish fast-food spot on Bahnhofstrasse, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, with the only exceptions being dairy in the ayran and some desserts. It serves cigkofte made of bulgur, red peppers, walnuts, tomatoes and spices with fresh mint, lemon and parsley, and it was previously called My Cigkofte.

Order the vegan cigkofte with mint, lemon and parsley, and skip the ayran and the dairy desserts. It is a fast, cheap, filling stop by the station.
