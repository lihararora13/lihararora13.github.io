CassiusGarten has been Bonn's vegetarian and vegan restaurant for over thirty years, a buffet on Maximilianstraße where you build your own plate from the dishes labelled vegan and pay by weight. The spread changes daily but the shape stays the same: gyros, Bolognese, smashed potatoes with beetroot-bean purée, seasonal vegetables, tiramisu and vegan soft-serve, all of it in a bright, unfussy dining room a short walk from the old town.

It is not a fully vegan kitchen, so follow the vegan labels rather than assuming everything on the buffet qualifies. Come hungry, take a little of a lot, and treat it as the reliable everyday lunch it has been for three decades.
