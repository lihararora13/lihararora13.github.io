dean&david's Hafengasse branch is the salad and bowl chain doing what it does everywhere: hot wraps, sandwiches, curries, bowls, juices and smoothies, with a solid vegan range and clear labelling. It is not the most distinctive table in Ulm, but it is reliable, fast and open six days a week, and it fills the gap for a light, healthy lunch in the old town.

Order a vegan bowl or a hot wrap, add a fresh juice, and treat it as the practical lunch that never lets you down.
