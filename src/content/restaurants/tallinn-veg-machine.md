Veg Machine is great vegan fast food at the Balti Jaama market, right opposite the railway station, and it holds a 5.0 from HappyCow reviewers. The menu is smash burgers, kimchi burgers, loaded fries, soft serve and Beyond burgers, all of it only ever vegan, served from a stall on the market's food street.

It is the quick, fun, affordable stop on this list. Order a smash burger, add loaded fries, and finish with a soft serve while you browse the market.
