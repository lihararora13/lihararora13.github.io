Kintsubo Shokudo is a small family-owned Taiwanese vegetarian buffet cafeteria in the Tsuboya pottery district, and it is the cheapest sit-down meal in this guide: ¥600 all-you-can-eat, about ten fresh dishes including vegetables, pickles and tofu, Japanese rice and black bean rice, soup and pale porridge. The chimaki, rice dumplings wrapped in leaves with peanuts and vegan spam, are what regulars rave about.

Most dishes are vegan, but the menu changes daily, so tell the owner you eat vegan and he will point out what you can eat. It opens at 8am and closes in the early afternoon, so come for breakfast or an early lunch, and note the map pin on some listings points at the wrong parallel street.
