Plantiful is Bangkok's whole-food reset button: a calm Sukhumvit 61 cafe doing plant-based eating with unprocessed, nutrient-dense ingredients and zero sense of punishment. Salads are hearty, bowls are colourful, and the lasagna with cashew cheese is properly comforting.

Come for a post-explore lunch, a smoothie, or a slice of banoffee that still leaves you feeling sprightly. It is the guide's healthiest stop, and one of its prettiest, pets welcome and all.
