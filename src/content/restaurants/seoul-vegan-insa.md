Ikseon-dong's hanok lanes are Seoul at its most strollable, and Vegan Insa is the lunch that completes the wander: a fully vegan Korean kitchen with a perfect five-star HappyCow score across hundreds of reviews and a spot on the city's top-ten lists in 2026 guides.

The headline act is bibimbap topped with a fried vegan egg that actually looks and breaks like the real thing. Add tteokbokki with proper chew, a plate of fried chick'n, and someone you like across the table.
