Cafe Jeju 901 is the vegan cafe inside the Jeju901 wellness stay at the foot of Hallasan, on the 1100-ro mountain road in Nohyeong-dong. The cafe sits on the second floor with a rooftop deck looking out over the mountain greenery, and everything is fully vegan and organic: a 901 Vegan Brunch set of sandwich, salad and soup, a tofu hamburger sandwich, detox green smoothies, soy and oat lattes, and cakes under glass domes, including a Jeju sweet pumpkin cake. The same building runs meditation and yoga day classes, so the room is calm by design.

It opens Wednesday to Sunday, 9am to 6pm. Note the address: HappyCow lists the cafe at 1100-ro 288-39, while Korea's official tourism site and other guides give 2977-10 1100-ro for the same property; use 2977-10, which matches the published map pin.
