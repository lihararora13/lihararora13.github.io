My Vegan Sweet Tooth is an all-vegan bakery on Garrison Place doing cakes, cupcakes, and cookies with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the sweet anchor of this list, and the place to order a whole cake for a celebration.

Order a vegan cupcake and a cake slice, and ask what is fresh that day, since the case rotates.
