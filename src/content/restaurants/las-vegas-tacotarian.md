Tacotarian is the spot locals tell every visiting vegan to hit first, and with five locations across the valley plus one on the Strip inside Miracle Mile, it is hard to miss. Everything is 100 percent plant-based, from hibiscus flower and mushroom asada tacos to seitan carne asada, with a bright, colorful interior that matches the food's energy.

The downtown Arts District location is the one to hit: photogenic, hopping, and the heart of the operation. Get the quesabirria, the queso fundido, and whatever agua fresca is on. April 2026 reviewers are still calling it one of their favorite restaurants in the Arts District, vegan friends or not.
