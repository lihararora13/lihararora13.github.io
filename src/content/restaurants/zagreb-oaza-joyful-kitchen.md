Oaza, which means oasis, is exactly that: a small, joyful vegetarian kitchen tucked into the Upper Town, cooking seasonal plates that are mostly vegan and always made with care. It is the kind of place where the menu is short because the kitchen only cooks what is good that day.

Order the seasonal plate and a soup, and treat it as the quiet, wholesome lunch between the busier stops on this list. It is a lovely counterpoint to Zagreb's burger-heavy vegan scene.
