finefine is a health-food restaurant on Bismarckstraße in the Südviertel, a bright salad-bar room doing build-your-own bowls, seasonal warm dishes and fresh smoothies. The menu is built around choice rather than a fixed list, which suits vegans well: pick the plant-based bases, add the vegan toppings, and skip the dairy dressings.

It is a mixed menu, so choose the plant-based options explicitly. Lunch runs from eleven until two with dinner service from five until ten, and Sundays from twelve until ten. Come for a light, fast lunch between the Südviertel errands.
