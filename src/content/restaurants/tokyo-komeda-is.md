KOMEDA is is what happens when a giant Japanese coffee chain decides to do a plant-based cafe properly. Everything here is vegan, from the morning toast sets to the burgers, sandos, pancakes and soft serve, and it opens at 7am, which makes it a rare and wonderful Tokyo breakfast find.

Order on the tablet, settle into the calm Ginza room, and work through the comfort-food greatest hits. Reviewers were still happily returning in October 2026, and one visit tends to turn into a daily habit on a trip.
