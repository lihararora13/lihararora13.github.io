Fern & Fika began life as Sthlm Raw and grew into Hornstull's greenest little cafe: everything plant-based, naturally gluten-free and made without refined sugar, served in a room full of plants. Visit Stockholm lists it among the city's vegan restaurants, and Thatsup praises its light-hearted take on clean eating and its big spread of vegan pastries.

This is fika, upgraded: overnight oats and smoothies for breakfast, bowls and daily soups at lunch, and raw cakes, cookies and buns that prove free-from never has to mean flavour-free. The scrambled tofu sandwich gets special love from regulars, so start there.
