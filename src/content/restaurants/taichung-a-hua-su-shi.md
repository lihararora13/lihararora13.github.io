A-Hua Su Shi, 阿華素食, is a food stall inside Taichung Second Market, the century-old market that takes up a whole block in the Central District, and it is the cheapest breakfast in this guide: a vegan buffet, pay by weight, with complimentary rice and soup. Go in through the market entrance just north of 383 Taiwan Boulevard and the stall is straight ahead on the left. The daily spread is freshly stir-fried seasonal vegetables, with white rice, five-grain rice and congee to choose from, and the fried noodles are the dish old hands order first: one visitor's whole meal came to five NT$10 coins.

This is a morning place, open Monday to Friday and Sunday from 6:00am to 1:30pm and closed on Saturdays, so go early and go hungry. Note the HappyCow reviews are old, but the stall is on the current HappyCow print page, appears in a 2026 Second Market guide, and was photographed trading in February 2026.
