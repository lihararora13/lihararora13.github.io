Monte de Olivos is a vegetarian rotiseria on San Juan, in the centre, serving lunch plates on weekday lunchtimes, 11:30 am to 2:30 pm. It is a quiet local lunch counter, and it rounds out this list with a dependable weekday option.

Order a lunch plate and a salad, and ask which dishes are vegan. It is lunch-only and closed on weekends.
