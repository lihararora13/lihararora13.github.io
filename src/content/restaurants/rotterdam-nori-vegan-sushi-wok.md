Nori is a vegan sushi and wok restaurant on Pannekoekstraat, with a simple vision: high-quality vegan dishes that bring the flavours of Asian cuisine to life. The menu runs from vegan sushi, dragon rolls and hand rolls to gyoza, crispy tofu and wok dishes, all of it plant-based.

It is open for lunch and dinner, with evening-only service on Mondays to Wednesdays. Order the dragon roll and the gyoza.
