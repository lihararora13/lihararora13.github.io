Heavenly Buffaloes is a wing joint where the vegan wings are not an afterthought: local North Carolina soy protein nuggets with fourteen vegan dry rubs and sauces, every bit as good as the chicken wings they sit next to on the menu. It is the most fun, least formal meal on this list.

Order a spread of wings across several sauces and share them. The dry rubs are the way in.
