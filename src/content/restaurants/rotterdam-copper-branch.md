Copper Branch is a plant-based cafe on Korte Hoogstraat, a few doors from Backyard, open every day from 10:00 to 21:00, Sundays from noon. The menu is colourful comfort food: rainbow burgers, rendang loaded fries, loaded nachos, an Impossible burger and a doner pita, with bright salad bowls alongside.

It is the fast, central pick on this list, and the Rotterdam branch is one of two in the Netherlands, the other in Utrecht. Order the rainbow burger and the rendang loaded fries.
