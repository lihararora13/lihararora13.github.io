Corina Bakery is Tacoma's bakery pick: a downtown bakery on Fawcett Avenue with vegan breakfast sandwiches, a tofu and potato burrito, vegan cakes and pastries. It is open Monday to Friday 7am to 3pm and Saturday 8am to 3pm, closed Sundays, and it is the morning pick in this guide after Happy Belly, with the advantage of a proper bakery case. The vegan items are a small part of the menu, so ask which pastries are vegan that day.

Order a vegan breakfast sandwich and a pastry. It is downtown, so it pairs with a morning walk to the waterfront.
