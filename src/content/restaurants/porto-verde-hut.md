Verde Hut is Porto's vegan buffet, and it is the answer to the question of what to eat when you are very hungry and cannot decide. Appetizers, salads and warm dishes, all vegan, all self-service, all very good value.

Go for lunch, fill a plate, and go back for more, that is the whole point. It is the most practical, generous meal on this list, and the one locals use on busy weekdays.
