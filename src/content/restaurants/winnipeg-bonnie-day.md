Bonnie Day is Wolseley's candlelit comfort-food room, a restaurant with a mixed menu where the vegan dishes are clearly labelled and genuinely good. The gnocchi bowl is the dish the vegan guides keep naming, and the not-crab cakes and roasted eggplant are the other plates to order, with a chocolate-peanut-butter torte to finish.

It is a dinner spot, open Tuesday to Saturday from 4:30pm, and reservations are recommended. Ask for the vegan menu when you sit down, since the labelling does the work for you.
