Green Heaven sits in Stadtamhof, the old town's sister district across the Stone Bridge, and it is the evening spot on this list: a vegetarian restaurant and bar with plant-based burgers and steaks, cocktails, beer and a kitchen that runs until midnight. The room is lively, the menu is generous, and the location, just over the Danube from the cathedral, makes it the natural end to a day of walking the old town.

It opens in the afternoon on weekdays and from eleven at weekends. Cross the bridge, order a plant-based cheeseburger and an Aperol spritz, and stay for the evening.
