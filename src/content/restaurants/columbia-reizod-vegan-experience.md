The Reizod Vegan Experience is an all-vegan restaurant on Two Notch Road in northeast Columbia, doing plates, sandwiches, and comfort food with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the strongest pick in Columbia, and the room is casual, counter-service, and built for a hearty lunch.

Order the vegan plate and a sandwich. Note the distance: Two Notch Road is northeast of downtown, so plan the drive.
