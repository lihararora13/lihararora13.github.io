Momo's is a vegetable kebab shop on the Blücherstraße, east of the old town, doing grilled vegetable kebabs, falafel plates and salads at counter prices. It is a vegetarian kitchen with a proper vegan range, and it fills the same role as the best kebab shops do everywhere: fast, cheap, generous, and open six days a week until nine. The grilled vegetables are the point, not an afterthought.

Order a Gemüsekebab with everything, add a falafel plate if you are hungry, and treat it as the quick lunch that keeps Augsburg's vegan scene honest.
