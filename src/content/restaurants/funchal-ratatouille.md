Ratatouille is a long-running Italian restaurant near the old town, open daily from late morning to eleven at night, and it is the dependable pasta-and-pizza stop when the vegan cafes have closed for the day. The kitchen is Italian first, with vegan dishes across the menu rather than a single token plate.

Ask for the vegan pasta and pizza options and the vegetable dishes the kitchen does well; the staff are used to the question. It is a good fallback for a late dinner after a day out, when most of the brunch spots have shut.
