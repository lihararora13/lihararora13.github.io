Greentrees is Düsseldorf's all-vegan health café: fresh juices, cold-pressed juices, superfood smoothies, signature smoothie bowls, salads, soups and raw snacks, plus matcha pancakes and avocado sandwiches that regulars plan their mornings around. It is bright, cheerful and very Unterbilk.

Come for breakfast or a light lunch, order the açaí bowl, and add the matcha pancakes. This is the freshest start to a Düsseldorf day.
