Nha Tam is a vegetarian buffet on Tran Phu in central Dalat, with a fixed price of 99,000 dong per adult, drinks and dessert included, open 7am to 2:30pm and 4pm to 8:30pm. It is a casual, family-style buffet, and it is the best-value vegan-friendly lunch in Dalat if you want to try many dishes at once.

Fill a plate from the buffet and ask which dishes are vegan, as the kitchen is vegetarian. It is inexpensive and central, and it suits a group.
