Govinda's sits on the campus of Houston's ISKCON temple, which means the meal comes with spires, bansuri music, and the calmest dining room in the city. It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but the buffet goes all-vegan on Wednesdays, Fridays, and Sundays, and the vegetarian days are already heavy with vegan dishes. Everything is served without meat, fish, or eggs, as temple tradition demands.

The saag paneer is the most requested dish in the house, and the tofu curry, chana masala, and dal hold their own. At about $12.95 for all-you-can-eat, it is the best-value meal on this list, and the mint-ginger lemonade is worth the drink upcharge.
