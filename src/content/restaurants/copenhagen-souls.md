Souls started the Copenhagen vegan comfort-food wave and its Torvehallerne stand is still the tastiest stop in the market halls: house-made burgers, mushroom everything, snacks, cocktails, beer and wine, all al fresco and all vegan. Lonely Planet, via local guides, says it smashes the vegan stereotype, and the mushroom and truffle burger is the one everyone names.

HappyCow reviewers in September 2026 said they would eat the schnitzel burger again and again, and praised the friendly owner tips. Come hungry between market browsing, grab a burger and a seat outside, and thank us later.
