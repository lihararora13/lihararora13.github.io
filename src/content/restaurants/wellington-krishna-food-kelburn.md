Krishna Food is the campus canteen that happens to be one of the best-reviewed vegan rooms in Wellington, a fully vegan cafeteria on Level 3 of the Kirk Building at Victoria University, open to the public as well as students. The counter runs on curries and rice, lasagna, salads, sausage rolls, samosas, ice cream and sweets, with meal deals that keep it the cheapest proper lunch on the list. Its 5.0 HappyCow rating comes from a small but unanimous crowd.

Take the cable car or the walk up the hill to Kelburn, order the curry of the day with a sausage roll on the side, and treat it as the low-key, high-value end of the guide. Hours are daytime only, roughly 10am to 3pm, so plan it as lunch.
