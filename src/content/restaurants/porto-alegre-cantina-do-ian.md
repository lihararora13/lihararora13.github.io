Cantina do Ian is a 5.0-rated fully vegan restaurant in Cidade Baixa offering homemade Brazilian comfort food for lunch and dinner. The menu features traditional dishes such as feijoada prepared with plant-based ingredients, vegan hot dogs with creative toppings, and a rotating homemade meal.

Order the feijoada and a vegan hot dog. It is open for lunch and dinner most days, with a break on Wednesdays, and it is the best Brazilian comfort food on this list.
