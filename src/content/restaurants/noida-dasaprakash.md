Dasaprakash is the South Indian workhorse of this list: a long-running pure-veg kitchen doing dosas, idlis and uttapams the traditional way. For a vegan eater it is one of the easiest tables in Noida, since most of the menu is a single request away from plant-based.

Ask for the masala dosa without ghee and the idli sambar as it comes. It is family dining, fast and generous, and the filter coffee is the proper finish.
