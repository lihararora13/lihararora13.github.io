Pfannenzauber is Aachen's fully vegan cultural restaurant, open since January 2017 on Suermondtplatz: a seasonal, regional kitchen in a room that doubles as a stage for local artists, with a piano for anyone brave enough and a home for foodsharing. The menu changes with the seasons, but the shape holds: sticky orange tofu, spicy tofu burrito with yoghurt salad, Thunvish club sandwich, batata harra potatoes and asparagus risotto when it is in season.

Hours are evening-led: Monday and Thursday from half past five, Friday until half past nine, Saturday from twelve, and Sunday from twelve until nine, with Tuesday and Wednesday closed. Come for dinner, check the changing menu, and treat it as Aachen's most thoughtful vegan table.
