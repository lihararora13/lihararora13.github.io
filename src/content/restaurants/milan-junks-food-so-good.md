Junk's Food So Good does vegan junk food properly: kimchi sandwiches, various burgers, loaded fries, fresh salads and a shelf of sweets, all 100% plant-based, and HappyCow reviewers give it a perfect 5.0. The house patty is the King Kong Kimchi, and the Birthday burger is the one regulars tell you to start with.

The spot on Piazza Risorgimento comes with a good story too: the team is linked to the world of Stella, Italy's Michelin-starred vegetarian restaurant, which is why the cooking is more careful than the name suggests. Open Tuesday to Sunday, lunch and dinner, with the kitchen running late on weekends.
