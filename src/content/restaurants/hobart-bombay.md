Bombay is an Indian restaurant in Blackmans Bay, south of Hobart, serving gobi butter, dosas and vegan-friendly Indian dishes. It is a neighbourhood restaurant, and it is the best Indian vegan-friendly meal in the Hobart area.

Order the gobi butter and a dosa, and ask for the vegan dishes without dairy. Its pin is approximate to Blackmans Bay.
