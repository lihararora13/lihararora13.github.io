Hong Yi Su is the Cantonese dim sum teahouse of Liwan: a fully vegan restaurant in a beautiful old building on Baoyuan Road, reported fully vegan in March 2026 and rated 5.0 on HappyCow from 14 reviews. A November 2026 guide lists it as a Cantonese dim sum teahouse averaging nine to sixteen dollars, and reviewers confirm the staff state no eggs or milk are used.

Order the vegan chrysanthemum siu mai with mock egg yolk, the spicy soup dumplings, the bean curd rolls and the soy pancake, and try the congee and fruity teas. It is also listed as Hongyisu Cantonese Vegan Teahouse, the same 131 Baoyuan Road building opposite the Hengbao Garden bus terminus, about a hundred metres from Changshou Road metro Exit B. Come at lunch or dinner service; between services the menu narrows to steamed options.
