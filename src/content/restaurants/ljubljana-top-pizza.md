Top Pizza is the vegetarian pizzeria Ljubljana's pizza lovers keep recommending, and its vegan pizzas are the reason it makes this list. The style sits somewhere between Napoli and New Haven, the dough is serious, and the vegan tiramisu is worth asking about even if it is not on the board.

It is a friendly, unfussy room south of the river. Order a vegan pizza to share, ask about the tiramisu, and enjoy the one Ljubljana table where nobody has to compromise on pizza night.
