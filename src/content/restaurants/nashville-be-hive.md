The BE-Hive began as band-house vegan potlucks, turned into beloved buffets and a seitan company, and landed as East Nashville's plant-based deli and market, open seven days a week. Veggies Abroad calls it possibly the best vegan junk food in Nashville, and that is not hype.

Order the hot chick'n, a crunchwrap or the gyro with house meats, then browse the market shelves stocked with their own pepperoni, bacon and deli slices to take home. Fun, generous and very East Nashville.
