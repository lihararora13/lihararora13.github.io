Coromandel Cafe is the White Town favourite at La Maison Rose, the pink-walled cafe one guide calls highly hyped and rates 10 out of 10, and a December 2025 vegan traveller ate yellow pumpkin pasta with cashew cream there, followed by a chocolate walnut brownie with tea. The beet pasta is the other dish the guides name.

Order the pumpkin pasta with cashew cream and ask for it vegan, and finish with the brownie. It is the most romantic room on this list, and the cashew cream is the vegan trick that makes it work.
