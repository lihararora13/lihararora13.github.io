Koshary Ala Sokhon serves one dish, koshary, and it serves it perfectly: rice, pasta, lentils, chickpeas, fried onion, garlic water and tomato sauce, in a small room in Al Attarin. HappyCow rates it 5.0, the highest vegan rating in Egypt, and it is fully vegan by nature rather than by adaptation.

Order the koshary with chilli sauce and extra chickpeas. It is cheap, fast and open late, and it is the single best vegan meal in Alexandria.
