Ogen is a Lebanese restaurant in central Hua Hin that does a good job of reproducing the flavours of the Lebanese kitchen, with decent prices for its location. The mezze, hummus, falafel and tabbouleh give vegans a solid meal, though the kitchen also serves meat, so ask which dishes are plant-based.

Order the hummus and falafel with tabbouleh as a mezze. Its pin is approximate to central Hua Hin, and it is a useful change from Thai food.
