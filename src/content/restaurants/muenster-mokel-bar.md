Mokel Bar is Münster's fully vegan bar on Rosenplatz in the Kuhviertel, a trendy urban bar and restaurant with an entirely plant-based menu: flammkuchen with tofu feta, vegan burgers, baked potatoes, sweet potato fries with hummus, and cocktails, wine and beer, with the kitchen closing at ten. HappyCow rates it five out of five, and the reviews agree: the flammkuchen in particular is fantastic.

It is an evening place, open Tuesday to Thursday from six until eleven, Friday from five until one, and Saturday from twelve until one, with Sunday and Monday closed, and reservations are recommended. Order the flammkuchen with tofu feta first, a burger second, and treat it as Münster's best vegan night out.
