Maa Ki Rasoi sits by the railway overbridge on Station Road, which makes it the first pure-veg kitchen a train traveller meets in Ranchi. It is a simple, home-style place, open 8 in the morning to 11 at night, serving the North Indian vegetarian staples at station-side prices.

Order the veg thali and ask for no ghee and no paneer. It is not fancy, but it is exactly the kind of dependable veg kitchen a thin scene needs.
