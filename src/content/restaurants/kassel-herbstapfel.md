Herbstapfel is one of the most sustainable restaurants in Kassel, a small and cosy dining room in Wehlheiden where the kitchen cooks predominantly vegan food with a few vegetarian dishes alongside, matched with selected craft beers. The menu leans on fresh regional ingredients and organic staples, turned into bowls, burgers and chilli sin carne, with desserts like baked-apple tiramisu that play on the restaurant's name.

It is an evenings-only spot, open from six on weekdays and from one at weekends, closed on Tuesdays, so plan it as dinner rather than lunch. The burger bowl is the signature, and the room is small enough that booking ahead on a Friday or Saturday is wise.
