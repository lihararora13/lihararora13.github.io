Juezhengjing on Minsheng Street is the restaurant a November 2026 vegan guide to South China calls the place that struck it most in Kunming: a vegan restaurant specialising in typical Yunnan dishes and other Chinese regions, with plant-based meat substitutes, tofu in a thousand variations, noodles and dumplings, in sumptuous traditional interiors frequented almost exclusively by locals. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow, with reviews from August 2026, and Ctrip lists it at about 71 yuan a person.

Order by scanning the QR code on the table; the menu has many pictures, and tea is free. The fried mushrooms are the dish Ctrip reviewers rave about, and the menu runs from sweet and sour mock pork and Fugui noodles to a signature fruit pizza. It is listed as vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan because HappyCow notes it as possibly 100% vegan and asks diners to verify with staff. Close to Wuyilu metro station. The pin is triangulated from HappyCow search distances, accurate to about 30 metres. Do not confuse it with the Xishan Courtyard branch, a separate restaurant across town.
