De Broers van Julienne has been cooking tasty and colourful veggie and plant-based food since 1994, with take-out dishes, a second room called Juliette and cooking workshops by chef Nina. The menu runs from flatbreads with aubergine croquettes and gluten-free croquettes to quiche and a mezze platter.

This is a vegetarian restaurant with strong plant-based options, so it is listed as vegan-friendly. Order the mezze platter and a flatbread, and expect a long-running, much-loved neighbourhood room.
