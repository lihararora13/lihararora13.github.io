Gatorta is street food theatre: a small stand, punk stickers everywhere, and tortas so large that reviewers warn each one is really two meals. The cubana, piled with milanesa, sausage, ham-style slices and cheese, all vegan, is the headline order.

Bring cash just in case, bring an appetite definitely, and eat standing up like a local. This is exactly what vegan street food should be.
