Why we love it — From the team behind Miami's beloved Minty Z, Men & Beasts
brings a 100% plant-based dim sum menu to Echo Park, so you never have to
ask what has meat in it. Seitan char siu, king-oyster gua bao, and "monkey
buns" (char siu seitan meets monkey bread) anchor a menu that honors Chinese
tradition while playing with it. The adjoining tea room, with its own
entrance, makes this a dinner-and-tea night out in one.

Eater LA name-checked it as part of the city's white-hot Chinese summer, and
Echo Park has adopted it as a neighborhood anchor. Reserve for dinner; the
tea room takes walk-ins.
