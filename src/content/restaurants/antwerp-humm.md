HUMM is a modern vegan restaurant on Dageraadplaats in Zurenborg, with an adjoining takeaway deli next door at number 31. The team removed all meat and dairy in June 2021, and the menu is now fully vegan: burgers, Middle Eastern inspired sharing dishes, natural wines, craft beer and coffee, with hummus with harissa, sabich bowls and stuffed cabbage rolls among the favourites.

It is open from late morning to late evening, so it works for lunch, dinner or a long afternoon. Order the sharing plates and a natural wine.
