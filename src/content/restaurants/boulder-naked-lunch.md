Naked Lunch is Boulder's cheerful lunch counter, tucked inside The Peloton on Arapahoe Avenue, and it does exactly what the name promises: fresh, nakedly good food with nothing to hide. Sandwiches, salads and grain bowls are built around vegetables, with vegan options across the menu.

It is a quick, bright stop rather than a long dinner, and perfect before or after a ride or a run. Order a vegan sandwich and a seasonal salad and you are set.
