Vegan Heat is Bangalore's newest fully vegan fine-dining room, and it takes the scenic route: check in at the Nova Regency reception behind Sony World Signal, take the lift to the 6th floor, and settle into open seating with a menu of tempeh, wok-fired plates and seasonal salads. The kitchen cooks to order, the same menu delivers across the city from 7am to 10:30pm, and HappyCow rates it 4.5. It also makes its own fermented foods, water kefir, kombucha, sauerkraut, sourdough and tempeh, which show up across the menu.

Order the roasted tempeh tikka in makhani gravy with paratha, the makhani pizza with fried tempeh, or the palak tofu masala, and start the day with the millet masala dosa or the mixed berry pancakes if you're here for breakfast. Book ahead on WhatsApp; the room is small and the word is out.
