Bodhi looks and feels like a traditional Bavarian tavern, wooden tables, beer-stein decor, hearty plates, except the entire menu is vegan. It opened in December 2013 as Bavaria's first vegan Wirtshaus and it still does the classics better than anywhere else in the city: schnitzel, mushroom ragout, tempeh rouladen, gyros, Käsespätzle and Kaiserschmarrn, plus burgers, salads and house-made desserts. The Westend location, a short walk from the Oktoberfest grounds, makes it the natural stop after a day at Theresienwiese.

It gets very busy at peak dinner times, so book ahead, and note the evening-only opening on weekdays. Order the schnitzel and the Kaiserschmarrn in the same sitting and you will understand why this place tops so many Munich lists. This is the one for the full Bavarian experience, no compromises, no side salad.
