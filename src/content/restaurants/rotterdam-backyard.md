Backyard is a fully vegan all-day restaurant on Korte Hoogstraat, in the middle of the centre, and the top lunch pick in Rotterdam. The menu is Dutch snacks made vegan, bitterballen and kapsalon, alongside a spicy kimchi burger, falafel burgers, cauliflower wings, avocado toast and hummus, with acai bowls for breakfast.

It is open all day, so it is the reliable centre pick on this list. Order the kimchi burger and the vegan bitterballen.
