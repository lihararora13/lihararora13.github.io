Café Nomad is the Weststadt's neighbourhood café, sourcing produce from a local market garden and bread from a Heidelberg organic bakery, and its brunch plate is the best plant-based morning in the city: smoked organic tofu, a house-made paprika spread, butter-bean miso dip and pickled vegetables, with a matcha-açaí bowl for anyone who wants something cold. The room is calm, the coffee is good, and the whole thing feels like a café that belongs to its street.

It takes no reservations, so arrive early at weekends, when the brunch crowd queues. Order the brunch plate, take your time over it, and treat this as the slow start to a Heidelberg day rather than a pit stop.
