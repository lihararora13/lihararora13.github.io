Cafe Ceci is Potsdam's small all-vegan cafe, a quiet room on Hegelallee with outdoor seating and a short menu of mostly housemade food: cakes, bowls, soups and potato salad. It is the kind of place that does a few things well rather than many things adequately, and the cakes are the reason regulars keep coming back. It opens Tuesday to Sunday, ten to five.

Come for an afternoon slice and a coffee, or a light lunch bowl between palace visits. This is the gentle, unhurried pick in the city centre, and the only fully vegan cafe on this list.
