Tayibat is a Lebanese delicatessen and fast-food counter on Albersloher Weg in Gremmendorf, a butcher's shop and snack bar in one: homemade Lebanese pastry specialities, grill sandwiches, fresh bread and Lebanese groceries, with parking right in front of the house. The vegan side is strong: vegan bowls, sandwiches, salads, falafel and vegetable dishes, with the falafel sandwich the order to start with.

It is not a fully vegan restaurant, despite some listings saying otherwise: it is a butcher's shop that serves meat, so order the vegan dishes. Open Monday to Saturday until half past nine and Sundays until half past eight, it is worth the trip out of the centre.
