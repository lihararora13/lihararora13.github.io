RoseHip is Vilnius' vegan bistro and the first place any local will send you, a fully vegan restaurant near the Old Town with a second location on Pylimo Street. The menu is bowls, burgers and plant-based versions of familiar dishes: beetroot soup, buddha bowls, Caesar salads, burgers, sandwiches, croissants and desserts, with main dishes starting around 8.50 euros.

It is the most useful starting point for a fully vegan meal in the city. Order the beetroot soup, add a pulled jackfruit burger, and save room for dessert.
