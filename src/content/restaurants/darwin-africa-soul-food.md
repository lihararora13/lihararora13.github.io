Africa Soul Food is a 100% vegan and gluten-free market stall in Darwin, at Nightcliff Pools on Thursday and Friday from 5pm to 8:30pm and at Bundilla Beach on Sundays, serving egusi stew, fufu, jollof with tofu and vegetable dishes. The operator confirms the food is 100% vegan and gluten-free, and it is the only fully vegan kitchen in Darwin.

Order the egusi stew and the jollof with tofu, and check the market days before you go, as the stall moves between markets. It is the essential vegan meal in Darwin.
