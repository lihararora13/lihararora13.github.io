Indo Vegan keeps things simple and does them well: homestyle Indonesian cooking, fully vegan, at prices that make it an everyday stop rather than an occasion. It is the kind of place where the food tastes like someone's family recipes, because it probably is.

Go for the Indonesian classics, tempeh and tofu dishes, vegetable stews and fried rice, and expect a filling, unfussy meal.
