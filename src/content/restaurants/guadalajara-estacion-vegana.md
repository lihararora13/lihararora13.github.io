Estación Vegana is a tiny taqueria with eight seats, a big heart and very good tacos, a short walk from the Expiatorio church. It is an animal-lovers haven, full of spicy aromas and regulars who clearly love the place.

Order three tacos each, like the reviewers did, and enjoy the camaraderie. It is the smallest room on this list, and one of the most memorable.
