Frutas Prohibidas has been doing all-day vegan brunch in Conde Duque since 2016, and Madrid keeps showing up for it: stacks of pancakes, a proper eggless eggs Benedict, acai bowls, burgers and burritos for lunch, and cakes from its own bakery. Everything is made from scratch.

It opens Wednesday to Sunday, 10 to 6, takes no reservations, and gets busy for excellent reasons. Arrive early, order the Benedict, and take a whole cake home if you have any sense.
