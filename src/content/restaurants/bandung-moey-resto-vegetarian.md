Moey Resto Vegetarian is a casual Chinese-food restaurant on Pajagalan Street, and it is famous for its nasi tutug oncom, vegetarian rendang, vegetarian satays and salads, alongside Indonesian favourites. The room is casual and local, and the prices are low.

Order the nasi tutug oncom and the vegetarian rendang. It is a vegetarian restaurant with vegan-friendly dishes rather than an all-vegan one, so file it as vegan-friendly, and confirm dishes are vegan when you order.
