NORI WAY bills itself as the first fully plant-based sushi restaurant in Italy, and HappyCow reviewers rank it the top vegan restaurant in Milan. The sushi chefs build uramaki, futomaki, gunkan and nigiri from marinated and fermented vegetables, with vegan versions of salmon, tuna and prawns, plus gyoza, ramen, udon and mochi. Give the team a budget and let them build you a menu, then let them explain every piece as it lands.

The restaurant relocated from its old Piazza Bruno Buozzi address to Via Federico Confalonieri 8/N01 in Porta Nuova, a bigger room with a veranda looking out at Bosco Verticale, the two apartment towers planted with hundreds of trees. Ask for a veranda table, reserve a few days ahead for Fridays and Saturdays, and note it opens for dinner only, Tuesday to Saturday from 7pm to midnight.
