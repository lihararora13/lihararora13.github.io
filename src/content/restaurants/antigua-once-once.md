Once:Once is a small all-vegan restaurant, rated 5.0, serving plant-based food with different influences: tacos, enchiladas and ravioli alongside dumplings and wonton soup, with everything, including the pasta, made in-house. It relocated from 6a Calle Poniente to 2 Calle Oriente, so use the address on this page.

Order the dumplings and wonton soup, or the homemade ravioli, and try a tea infusion. It is open Tuesday to Saturday for lunch and dinner, and it is the most creative vegan kitchen in Antigua.
