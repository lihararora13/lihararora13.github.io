Paxil answers a question nobody thought to ask: can a tiny Roma Norte stall recreate a Mexican beach shack, entirely from plants? Tuna-style tostadas, gobernador tacos and aguachile later, the answer is a cheerful yes.

It is casual, affordable and fun, the kind of place where you order one taco, then immediately order two more. Seafood fans and sceptics both leave happy.
