Next Level Veggie Grill is Pasadena's all-vegan fast-food stop: a 100% vegan menu made with organic fresh vegetables and responsibly sourced, non-GMO ingredients, on South Lake Avenue. The burgers and chick'n sandwiches are the core, with fries, salads and shakes, and the room is fast, casual and open late, Monday to Thursday 10:30am to 10pm, Friday to Saturday to 10:30pm and Sunday to 10pm. It is the reliable pick when you want vegan food quickly and without a fuss.

Order a burger and sweet potato fries. The restaurant shares the 200 South Lake Avenue building with Mas Veggies Taqueria, also in this guide, so use the business name as well as the address.
