Herbies has been serving vegetarian food on North Street since 1990, and the vegan options are a proper part of the menu rather than a concession. It is a relaxed, old-school cafe that locals have used for decades.

Order the vegan breakfast or the soup of the day. It is the most reliable daytime vegan-friendly stop in the city.
