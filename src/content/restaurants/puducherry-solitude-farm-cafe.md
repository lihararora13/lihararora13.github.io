Solitude Farm Cafe is the farm-to-table cafe at Solitude Farm in Auroville, where the food comes from the fields around the table. A December 2025 travel carousel shows the thali, the dosa with chutneys and chips, and the farm itself, all in the unhurried Auroville way.

Order the farm thali and whatever the season is growing. It is the most Auroville meal on this list, and the one that tastes most of the place.
