The Gardeners Arms is the original 100% vegetarian and vegan pub in Oxford, a historic Jericho local with a plant-filled beer garden and a rear games room. The menu is meat-free pub classics done properly: mushroom pie, kebab bowls, dirty fries and calzone, with a strong drinks list.

The kebab bowl is the dish regulars name first, and the medley mushroom pie is the pub-classic pick. It is a vegetarian pub with a deep vegan bench, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note it opens late afternoon on Mondays and Tuesdays.
