Veggy Way is the Sapporo vegan meal that matters most: a fully vegan cafe in Maruyama doing the city's signature dish, miso ramen, entirely plant-based, with homemade koji amazake and miso, and gluten-free noodles on request. It also skips onion-family vegetables and garlic.

It is two minutes from Maruyama-koen Station. Order the miso ramen, then a parfait if you have room.
