Ranjit's Kitchen is a family-run Sikh Punjabi café on Pollokshaws Road, and it is the Southside's comfort food: all vegetarian, with the kitchen happy to remove dairy and make dishes vegan on request. It is small, warm and fiercely local, the kind of place where regulars are greeted by name.

Order a thali, add dahl, and ask what is vegan today. It is the gentlest, kindest meal on this list, and the best way to end a day in Shawlands.
