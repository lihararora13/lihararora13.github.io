Doppleganger started as a pop-up and grew into Cambridge's top vegan burger joint, a small unit on Regent Street with an American diner-style vibe and a menu built entirely around burgers. The burgers are rich and filling, the loaded chips are the side to order, and the shakes and desserts close the meal.

Order the Crunch, the house favourite, or the Hot Chirpy if you like heat, with a side of jack fries. It is fully vegan, it is fun, and it is the city's best answer to a junk-food craving.
