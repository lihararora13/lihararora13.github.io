Liners is Hannover's vegan fast-food counter, and it does the whole comfort-food range without a scrap of animal product: smash burgers, currywurst, vegan fish and chips, chili cheese nuggets. It is quick, cheap and loud in the best way, and it has become the city-centre stop for vegans who want a proper burger rather than a lecture about health.

It sits on Andreaestraße, right in the Mitte. Order a smash burger and the chili cheese nuggets, add the vegan fish and chips if you are sharing, and enjoy a menu where everything is yours. This is the table for a hungry afternoon, not a white-tablecloth dinner.
