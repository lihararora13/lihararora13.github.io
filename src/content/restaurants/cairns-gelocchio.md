Gelocchio is a gelato shop on the Esplanade in Cairns, with a vegan gelato and sorbet section. It is a waterfront dessert stop, and it suits an afternoon break or an evening dessert.

Order the vegan gelato and a sorbet, and ask which flavours are vegan today. It is central and reliable.
