Earthlings is a calm, plant-based cafe built from upcycled wood on Buckingham Street, and everything it serves is dairy free and refined sugar free. The menu runs from thali and momo dumplings to cold-pressed juices, hemp mylks and speciality lattes, with cakes baked in house and plenty of gluten-free options.

The momo dumplings are the dish regulars name first, and the thali is the move if you want to try a bit of everything. Evenings bring kirtans and community talks, so check the programme if you want the full Earthlings experience.
