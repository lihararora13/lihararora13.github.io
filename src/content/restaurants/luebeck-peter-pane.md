Peter Pane is a German burger chain, and its Lübeck branch on Breite Straße is the reliable vegan-friendly option in the old town: a separate vegan menu section, with every burger menu veganisable, and fries and shakes to match. It is a chain, and it feels like one, but the vegan offering is serious rather than a token patty, and the city-centre location makes it the easiest burger in Lübeck.

It sits on Breite Straße, a short walk from the Markt. Order a vegan burger with fries, add a vegan milkshake, and enjoy a menu where the vegan section is a real section. This is the table for a burger in Lübeck when you want something reliable.
