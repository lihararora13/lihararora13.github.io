JAJANA is the fully vegan café in Barrio de Santiago, on Calle 70 a few doors from Muchas Vidas, and the two of them make the street the vegan corner of Centro. Avocado toast with tomatoes is the dish the guides name, with bowls and baked goods alongside, in a small café that works for breakfast or a light lunch.

It is a daytime café, so go in the morning or early afternoon. Pair it with a walk around Santiago, one of the oldest barrios in the city.
