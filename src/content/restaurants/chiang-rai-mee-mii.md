Mee Mii is a simple vegan shop on Phao Kun Road at Phokun Soi 3, with a buffet cart of ready-cooked dishes, usually with vegetable, mock meat, soup, and spicy and mild options. It is a small, straightforward kitchen, and it is the kind of place where you point at what you want and eat well for very little.

Order from the buffet cart, mixing a spicy dish with a mild one, and take the soup if it is on. It is inexpensive and central, and it suits a quick lunch.
