Rakuen Cafe sits on the second floor of the Ryubo Department Store next to Kencho-mae monorail station, which makes it the most convenient vegan-friendly stop in this guide: step off the train, walk into the department store, and you are there. The menu has a good selection of clearly marked vegan options, from breakfast and rice bowls to vegan pancakes, with the purple sweet potato pancakes, the plant-based burger, the fried tofu croquette and the soft-serve sundae drawing the praise, plus baked goods from Detox Cafe Felicidad.

It is a modern café with vegan options rather than a vegan restaurant, so look for the vegan labels on the menu. Open 10:00 to 20:30, it works for a shopping-break lunch or a first meal after arriving in the city.
