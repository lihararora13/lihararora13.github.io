Natraj is the Station Road pure-veg restaurant: a straightforward dining room at Hanuman Circle that locals choose for an uncomplicated North Indian dinner. No themes, no fuss, just a room that has earned its regulars.

Order the dal tadka without ghee, a mixed veg sabzi and hot rotis, and you have a proper, cheap, satisfying meal. It is the quiet, reliable end of the Hanuman Circle circuit.
