Pure Kitchen started on Richmond Road in Westboro in 2015, and the original still has the neighbourhood feel the chain is loved for. The menu matches the Elgin branch: vegan poutine, fusion bowls, acai bowls, burgers, wraps and vegan cheesecake, with a juice bar for good measure.

It is the Westboro local's pick, open daily. Order the poutine and an acai bowl, and save room for the cheesecake.
