Yours is a worker-owned and managed cafe and venue at 43 Moray Place, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, and it is the most unusual room in this guide. It imagines a better food system and provides space for radical action, music, art and cooperation, and it is pay as you like: it accepts cash, dish washing, bean shelling, potato scrubbing, gifts, and whatever else you can offer. The food is sourdough bread, salads, sweet and savoury cabinet food, soups, curries and other full meals, with a menu that changes daily, plus a monthly Hari Rasai community feast on the third Thursday.

It is a vegetarian cafe, not a fully vegan one, so ask which dishes are vegan on the day. Its hours disagree across sources, with HappyCow listing Wednesday to Friday 2:00pm to 9:00pm, Saturday 12:00pm to 9:00pm and Sunday 12:00pm to 6:00pm, while its own social posts give a simpler Wednesday to Sunday 12:00pm to 6:00pm, so check before you go. Note the 5.0 rating rests on only four HappyCow reviews, so it is the thinnest rating in this guide.
