The Beer Engine is a bright, cosy pub on Cemetery Road with eighteen cask and keg lines and a food menu that changes monthly, built around global tapas and a proper Sunday roast. The roast comes in three versions, meat, vegetarian and vegan, and the vegan one is pub food at its best, with braised red cabbage and celeriac mash.

Come on a Sunday for the roast, or any other evening for the tapas, with the Korean BBQ cauliflower wings and the spicy mushrooms leading the vegan side of the menu. It serves meat too, so this is a vegan-friendly pub, and allergy sufferers should flag their requirements clearly with staff.
