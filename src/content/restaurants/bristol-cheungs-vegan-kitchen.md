Cheungs Vegan Kitchen proves that a takeaway can be a destination. This fully vegan Chinese takeaway in Brislington serves a modern twist on traditional Chinese cuisine: mock-meat dishes, noodles, curries and specials, all of it plant-based and all of it earning a 5.0 from HappyCow reviewers.

It is takeaway-first, so order ahead and eat it somewhere with a view, or take it home and make an evening of it. Order the mock-meat noodles and a curry, and add whatever special the kitchen is running that week.
