Katzentempel is Munich's temple of cats, a fully vegan café in Maxvorstadt where rescue cats wander between the tables while you eat. The food is proper café cooking rather than an afterthought: breakfasts, soups, salads, sandwiches, daily plates, cakes and tarts, with oat and soy milk for every coffee. It is the most charming room on this list, and one of the most popular, with more than two hundred HappyCow reviews behind it.

Come for breakfast, order the breakfast plate and a slice of cake, and let the cats set the pace. This is the pick for a slow morning, and the one your non-vegan friends will ask to go back to.
