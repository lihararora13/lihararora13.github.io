Sneaky Pickle has been one of New Orleans' most creative kitchens for years, and the daytime menu is almost entirely vegan, which is frankly doing vegans a huge favor. The Bywater spot slings tacos, fried buffalo tofu, burgers, and the kind of comfort food that makes you forget you are eating vegetables. By night the space becomes Bar Brine, which is also excellent.

Everything is locally sourced and the menu shifts with the seasons, so there is always something new to try. The room is casual and kid-friendly, the staff are warm, and the fried pickle appetizer is legally required ordering. Sneaky Pickle is technically vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, but you would never know it from the plate.
