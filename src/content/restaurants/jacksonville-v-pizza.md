V Pizza is a pizzeria on Hendricks Avenue in San Marco with vegan pizza options on the menu. The pies are Neapolitan-style, and the vegan cheese and marinara options give plant-based diners a real pizza rather than a cheese-less compromise.

Order the vegan pizza and a salad. Ask the server to confirm the vegan cheese, since the standard pie is dairy.
