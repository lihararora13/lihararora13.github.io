Happy Food & Health is a cafe and restaurant on Maashavenweg, south of the centre, and it is all vegan and all gluten-free. The menu runs from coffee and cakes to Mediterranean food: pistachio tiramisu, limoncello tiramisu, blueberry pancakes, lasagne and Thai curry, with HappyCow reviewers giving it a perfect 5.0.

It is a little out of the centre, so pair it with a walk around Maashaven rather than squeezing it between centre sights. Order the pistachio tiramisu and the lasagne.
