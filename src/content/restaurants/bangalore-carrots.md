Carrots is the reason Bangalore's vegan scene exists at all: India's first fully vegan restaurant, open since long before plant-based was a trend, and still going strong as a cloud kitchen in Ejipura after its Koramangala dining room closed. The menu is 100% plant-based, eggless and dairy-free, with a raw food section, gluten-free options and a bakery case that takes advance orders for whole vegan cakes. HappyCow rates it 4.5, and it was verified active on Swiggy in 2026.

There is no dining room anymore, so order in: the Bombaat Mockmeat Tofu Wrap is the signature, the vegan Greek salad and Carrots Special Manchurian are the old favourites, and the OCD Brownie is, in one reviewer's words, chocolate heaven on earth. WhatsApp the kitchen directly if you want a cake, and ask about the Good Karma Meal project, which provides free vegan food to anyone who's hungry.
