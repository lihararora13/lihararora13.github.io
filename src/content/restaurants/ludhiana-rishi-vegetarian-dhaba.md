Rishi Vegetarian Dhaba is the other great pure-veg dhaba on this list, on Income Tax Road in Rishi Nagar, doing the Punjabi canon without fuss: dal tadka, chana masala, aloo gobi, tandoori roti.

It is a working dhaba rather than a destination restaurant, which is exactly its charm. Ask for no ghee and the kitchen will cook your dal the vegan way.
