Ci Yun Zhai is a down-to-earth vegan buffet on Wuyi Road, in the Xingjie Building, and one of Kunming's best-value meals: 19 to 22 yuan a person for all you can eat, with fried vegetables, boiled vegetables, steamed buns, biscuits, cakes, fruit, tea and coffee. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow, with reviews updated in October 2026, and a November 2026 vegan guide to South China names it as a very affordable Chinese vegan buffet.

The ma po tofu is the dish reviewers keep praising. One older review mentions bugs at the payment counter, so go in with that in mind, though the recent reviews are warm about the food and the friendly staff. Open daily 11:30am to 2:00pm and 5:00pm to 8:00pm.
