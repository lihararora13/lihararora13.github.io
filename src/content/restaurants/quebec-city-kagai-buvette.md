Kagai Buvette is Quebec City's vegan sushi bar, and it is the surprise hit of the scene. Maki, nigiri and specialty rolls are built with inventive plant-based alternatives to fish, and the menu runs on to vegan pad thai, fried rice, mushroom risotto, wok noodles and poke bowls.

Reviewers say the sushi is mind-blowing and come back twice in a single weekend. Order a sushi set, add a specialty roll, and finish with the pad thai if you are still hungry.
