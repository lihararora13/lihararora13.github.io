Kozy Eats is the entirely vegan restaurant Riga's vegans keep coming back to, a block from the centre on Blaumana iela. The menu runs from salads and burgers to pasta dishes, with a full-service cafe pouring coffee drinks with plant milk, draft kombucha and to-go sweet and savoury pastries, all of it vegan and all of it in English.

It does not serve alcohol, but the food and the kombucha more than make up for it. Order a burger, add a pasta to share, and take a pastry for the road.
