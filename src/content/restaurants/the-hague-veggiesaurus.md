Veggiesaurus is a modern vegetarian and vegan dining destination on Binckhorstlaan, bringing innovative vegetarian cuisine with authentic Indian spices: exotic biryanis, street food and beloved curries reimagined for a refined dining experience. The favourites are samosa chaat, butter unchicken, garlic naan, satay and a shawarma roll.

It is a vegetarian restaurant, so it is listed as vegan-friendly; order the vegan dishes, and ask the staff which curries are made without dairy. It is open evenings only, from 16:00.
