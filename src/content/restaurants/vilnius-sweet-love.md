Sweet Love Caffe is a cosy vegetarian and vegan haven in the heart of Vilnius, on Totoriu Street just around the corner from KIRAS. The menu celebrates wholesome, delicious meals from hearty brunches to flavourful lunches and pizza dinners, with desserts and aromatic coffee for any time of day, including vegan cinnamon buns.

It is vegetarian with strong vegan options, so confirm the vegan dishes when you order. Order the vegan brunch, add a cinnamon bun, and settle in.
