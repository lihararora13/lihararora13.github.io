Hot pot is the meal vegans miss most, and Yangege fixes it properly: a fixed price, a copper pot of your own, and a buffet of wild Yunnan mushrooms, tofu, greens and mock meats. Everything is plant-based, so there is no negotiating with the broth.

Come hungry and take your time. The mushroom selection alone is worth the trip, and the copper-pot ritual makes it feel like a proper Shanghai night out.
