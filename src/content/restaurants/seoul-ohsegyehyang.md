Ohsegyehyang has been fully vegan since 2007, long before it was fashionable, and Insadong regulars treat it like a family kitchen. Everything is plant-based, broths and sauces included, the portions are famously generous, and the prices stay firmly in treat-yourself-weekly territory. Recent reviews run through 2026, and the owners even posted their Chuseok holiday hours, which is about as still-open as evidence gets.

Order the bubbling stone-pot bulgogi, something sweet-and-sour and crispy, and dumplings for the table. Closed Thursdays, so time your Insadong wander accordingly.
