Veg Kourt is the pure-veg all-rounder near the palace, the place that covers pasta, momos, kulcha and South Indian tiffin under one roof. It keeps split hours, lunch until 3:30 and dinner from 7, and the kitchen is used to vegans asking for the dairy held back.

Order the penne arrabbiata or the margarita kulcha and ask for it plant-based. It is the most flexible pure-veg room in central Mysuru, and the one to pick when the group can't agree on a cuisine.
