Heaven's Kitchen is a fully vegan restaurant on Theodor-Heuss-Straße, and it cooks the widest menu on this page: croffles and shakshuka for brunch, linsen-curry mit Spätzle for the Swabian in you, and orecchiette bolognese for dinner. It is the all-day pick, and the room suits a long lunch.

The linsen-curry with spätzle is the dish that shows what this kitchen is about: a Swabian classic, rebuilt plant-based, without losing the comfort. Book ahead at weekends.
