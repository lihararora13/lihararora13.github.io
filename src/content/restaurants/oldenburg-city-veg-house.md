City Veg House is a vegetarian Indian restaurant on Nadorster Straße, and it is the Indian stop on Oldenburg's list: thalis, dal and vegetable curries, with plenty of vegan dishes on a wider vegetarian menu. The kitchen is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so say vegan clearly when you order, but the vegan dishes are a real part of the cooking, not an afterthought.

It sits up the Nadorster Straße in Donnerschwee, a short walk from the city centre. Order a thali, add a dal, and ask which curries are vegan that day. This is the table for Indian food in Oldenburg, and a good evening stop.
