Lords is the Kasturba Road dining room: a long-running multicuisine restaurant in the old city, where families have been eating for years. It is the kind of place where the menu never changes because it never needs to.

The North Indian veg mains are the move, with dal tadka and mixed veg sabzi leading. The pin is approximate to the old-city area around Kasturba Road, so navigate by the street rather than the map dot.
