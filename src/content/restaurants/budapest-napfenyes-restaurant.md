Napfényes has been Budapest's vegan anchor for years, and the review counts prove it: a 4.5 from more than 1,100 HappyCow reviews, the biggest review count of any vegan spot in the city. It is a fully vegan restaurant with its own confectionery, serving Hungarian classics like smoky bean goulash with csipetke noodles, a mountainous Napfényes platter of veganised breaded cheese and stuffed cabbage, plus pizza, a salad bar, daily specials and a whole counter of vegan cakes, right by Ferenciek tere metro.

Come for lunch and the daily menu is one of the best-value vegan meals in the city, and come back for the cakes, because the bakery case is half the reason locals keep returning. It is the rare spot that does both hearty Hungarian comfort food and delicate pastry properly, all under one roof.
