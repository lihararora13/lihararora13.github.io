Anjo Verde is a vegetarian restaurant on the old square, Largo da Praca Velha, with a menu that runs heavily vegan, from beetroot tartare to cheesy baked dishes, plus cocktails on the terrace. It is the evening pick on this list.

Order the beetroot tartare, the cheesy baked dish and a cocktail, and ask the staff which plates are vegan, since the menu also runs dairy dishes.
