Helene is Göttingen's smash-burger spot, a small place on the Kornmarkt with outdoor seating in the middle of the old town. The menu is built around beef smash burgers, but the falafel burger is the vegan order, and it has earned its place on the list as the most central burger in town.

It is open every day from noon, with the kitchen running until half past eight. Order the falafel burger with fries, grab a table outside if the weather allows, and you are eating in the prettiest square in the city.
