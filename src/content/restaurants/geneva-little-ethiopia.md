Little Ethiopia tops every ranking of Geneva's vegan food for a simple reason: it cooks Ethiopian food the way it is meant to be cooked, and most of that food happens to be vegan. The mesob is the move, a big round platter of injera loaded with little mounds of misir wat, shiro, gomen, cabbage and carrots, built for sharing and for eating with your hands. It is generous, deeply spiced and quietly one of the best-value meals in a famously expensive city.

The room is small and unfussy, the welcome is warm, and the injera is properly tangy teff, naturally gluten-free. Come hungry, order the sharing plate, and do not skip the Ethiopian coffee if it is going. This is the table to book first when you land in Geneva.
