The Doshi House is the rare place that is both a neighborhood institution and a destination. Housed in a charming old building on Emancipation Avenue in the historic Third Ward, it runs as a 100% vegan coffeehouse and cafe: no dairy anywhere, oat, soy, and almond milk standard, pastries and quiche from the case, and a lunch menu that hops from Mumbai street sandwiches to Korean BBQ tacos to three-bean chili.

The pistachio latte has a following of its own, and the Friday-night fajita dinner is a standing ritual. It is the work-from-here cafe the Third Ward built around itself, plants on every shelf, community on every table.
