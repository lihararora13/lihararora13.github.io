Thanh Lieu Vegetarian is a popular vegetarian restaurant among backpackers and young travellers in Hue, serving inexpensive home-style dishes. It is a casual, friendly kitchen, and it fills a useful gap for anyone wanting a cheap, central vegan-friendly meal.

Order the vegetable and tofu dishes with rice, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. Its pin is approximate to the city centre.
