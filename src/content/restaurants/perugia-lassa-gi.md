Lassa Gi is a bar in Ellera di Corciano, just outside Perugia, that runs a weekly Aperivegan with the Scuola Comica di Cucina Veg, a vegan apericena with a didactic-comic twist. The buffet runs on fried vegetables, chickpea fritters, vegan Russian salad, couscous, falafel with hummus, spelt salad, bruschette, vegan burgers and pasta, for ten euros with a drink included.

Go for the Aperivegan evening and check the schedule first, since it runs weekly rather than daily. It is a community event as much as a meal.
