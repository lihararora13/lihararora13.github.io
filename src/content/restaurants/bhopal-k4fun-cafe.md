K4FUN is Bhopal's pure-veg fast-food stop, all pizza, burgers, pasta, sandwiches and mocktails, with dine-in, takeaway and delivery through Swiggy and Zomato. The café advertises itself as 100% pure veg, and the menu is the familiar comfort-food lineup done without meat and, on request, without cheese or mayo.

It rounds out the list as the fun, cheap, late-night-friendly option. Order the pizza without cheese, add a mocktail, and enjoy a café that takes its vegetarian promise seriously even at fast-food speed.
