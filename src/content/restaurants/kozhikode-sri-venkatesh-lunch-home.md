Sri Venkatesh Lunch Home is the old lunch home at Calicut Beach, the kind of place that has been serving vada and tea to beachgoers for longer than most of the city's cafes have existed. The November 2026 list of the city's best veg restaurants names it for exactly those two things.

Order a vada and a tea, and eat them looking at the sea. It is the simplest pick on this list, and one of the best.
