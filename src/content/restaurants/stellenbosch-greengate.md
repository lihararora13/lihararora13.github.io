Greengate is a small restaurant on Andringa Street built around a daily lunch buffet, the harvest table, with vegan options like salads and fresh cut fruit. You take what you like and pay by weight, with plant milk for coffee and tea and a courtyard to sit in.

It relocated from Church Street to Andringa Street, so use the address on this page. It is open Monday to Friday from 7:30am to 3:30pm, a lunch stop rather than a dinner one. Check for a vegan dessert before you leave.
