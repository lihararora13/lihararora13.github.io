Jardim das Cerejas, the cherry garden, is Lisbon's beloved vegan buffet: a small Chiado room where the hot dishes, salads and soups change every day, with a strong Indian lean, pakoras, vegetable dumplings, pasta, bean and chickpea curries, brown rice and cake to finish. It turned fully vegan in 2015 and locals have been filling plates here ever since.

The deal is simple and joyful, a fixed price, eat well, try a bit of everything, and recent HappyCow reviews in September 2026 called the dinner buffet delightful and great value. It is the perfect refuel between Chiado sightseeing and an evening up in Bairro Alto.
