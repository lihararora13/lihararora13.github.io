Peace Pies has been doing raw vegan since before raw vegan was a thing you said out loud. The Ocean Beach original is the flagship now (the Leucadia location closed in March 2026, so go to Voltaire Street), and the menu is a greatest-hits parade of things you would never guess are raw: mushroom stroganoff over kelp noodles in cashew alfredo, bliss burgers, magical mango curry wraps, and a dessert case of raw cheesecakes that ends arguments.

Everything is gluten-free and soy-free too, made with produce from local farms they actually name. Grab a table on the garden patio, order the cosmic coconut cream pie no matter what, and let the surfers walk past in a haze of jealousy.
