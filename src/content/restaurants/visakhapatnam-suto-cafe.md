Suto Coffee is the modest vegetarian cafe in Madhurawada that a January 2026 visit priced at just 1,100 rupees for a full meal: mushroom soup, pav bhaji, paneer sandwich and Alfredo pasta, in a bright blue-and-white room with board games and a neon sign. A commenter calls it ideal for vegetarians, and the April reel agrees.

Order the mushroom soup and the pav bhaji, and ask for the pasta without cheese. It is the budget veg dinner in Vizag, and the room is made for lingering.
