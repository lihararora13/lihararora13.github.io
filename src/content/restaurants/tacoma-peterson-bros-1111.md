Peterson Bros. 1111 is Tacoma's late-night vegan pick: a 21+ bar in the Hilltop with a kitchen that serves vegan food late, a Vegan Buffalo sandwich, a vegan burger, fries and a soy curls plate. It is open Monday to Thursday 3pm to 11pm, Friday to Saturday 3pm to midnight and Sunday 3pm to 10pm, and it is the only place in this guide where you can get a proper vegan meal after 10pm. The room is a bar first, so come for a drink and stay for the sandwich.

Note the 21+ rule: it is an adults-only evening pick. Order the Buffalo sandwich and a soy curls plate.
