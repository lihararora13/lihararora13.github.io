Casa de Nicolas is a vegetarian restaurant and grocery on Mendoza, rated 4.8, offering food for vegetarians and vegans who want healthy, natural eating. It is open lunch and dinner, 11 am to 3 pm and 6:30 to 10 pm, Monday to Saturday, which makes it one of the more flexible tables in the centre.

Order a lunch plate with a salad and a natural juice. The attached grocery is useful for stocking up on vegetarian and vegan staples.
