Sopanum Eat Veg is the Red Cross Road pure-veg room near the beach, named on the November 2026 list of the city's five best veg restaurants for its large dosa and breakfast spreads. It is a morning place, built for the beach walkers and the early risers.

Order the large dosa and a filter coffee, and ask for it without ghee. It is the beach breakfast, and the spread is generous.
