Mamacam is the vegetarian table Bordeaux Tourism itself recommends, a seasonal kitchen on Cours Victor Hugo cooking vegetable-led plates with care and a light touch. The menu follows the market, the vegan dishes are a real part of it, and the room is calm and welcoming.

It is a good lunch stop between the centre and Saint-Michel. Order whatever is seasonal, ask about the vegan options, and enjoy a quiet, thoughtful meal.
