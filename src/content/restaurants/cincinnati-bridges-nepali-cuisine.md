Bridges is Cincinnati's Nepali kitchen: a friendly, family-operated counter in Northside where the vegetarian items are suitable for vegans and clearly labelled on the menu. The vegan momos, juicy steamed dumplings, are the dish regulars order every time, alongside vegan samosas, marinated mushrooms and potatoes, mixed salads and a vegan bowl built on brown rice with a choice of curries. The staff are happy to talk through the vegan options.

Bridges is open Monday to Saturday, 10:30am to 9pm or 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays, and closed Sundays. The counter is a short walk up Hamilton Avenue from Melt Revival, at number 4165.
