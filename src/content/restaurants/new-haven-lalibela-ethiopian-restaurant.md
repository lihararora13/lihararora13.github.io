Lalibela Ethiopian Restaurant is an Ethiopian restaurant on Temple Street in downtown New Haven, doing lentil, chickpea, and collard green wots served on spongy injera. The combination platter is the way to eat here, shared family-style.

Order the vegetarian combination platter and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparations, since some wots are finished with niter kibbeh butter.
