Govinda's at ISKCON Ulubari anchors Guwahati's pure-vegetarian trail with freshly cooked sattvik food in an eggless, alcohol-free dining room. The Maharaja Sattvik Thali costs 190 rupees, the chana dal payesh is the sweet to order, and the kitchen honours Jain and fasting customisations without a fuss.

It is open 8 in the morning to 9:30 at night, and the zero-onion-garlic cooking means the food is already close to plant-based. Ask for no ghee and no paneer and the thali is vegan.
