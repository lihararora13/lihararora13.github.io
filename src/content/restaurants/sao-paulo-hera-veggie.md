Hera Veggie is the bakery stop every Sao Paulo vegan itinerary needs: fresh breads, focaccia and a vegan take on pao de queijo, Brazil's beloved cheesy roll, with none of the cheese and all of the chew. Sweet case rotates daily, so repeat visits are rewarded.

It opens at 9am Tuesday to Friday, a genuine rarity in this city, and 10am on Saturday. Closed Sunday and Monday, so make it a weekday breakfast with a book and a coffee.
