Willow is where vegan comfort food puts on its nice shoes. The menu reads
like a greatest hits album of American cravings: truffle mac and cheese,
buffalo cauliflower wings, a crispy gochujang mushroom burger, plus a banana
pudding that reviewers rave about. The full plant-based cocktail bar does
not hurt.

Open since 2021 and still packed on cold weeknights, it is the rare vegan
bistro that works as well for a rowdy group dinner as for a proper date.
Come hungry, order the wings, and do not skip dessert.
