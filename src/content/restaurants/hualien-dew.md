DEW is a vegan brunch and natural wine restaurant and bed and breakfast on Fu'an Road, close to the west side of Hualien train station, serving international fusion meals: focaccia sandwiches, burrito wraps, korma curry, soup, hummus, chia pudding, coconut yogurt, rice pudding, kombucha, herbal tea and nut milk tea. It is expensive by Hualien standards, and it is open Friday to Sunday, 10:30am to 3pm, so it is a weekend brunch rather than a daily kitchen.

Order the focaccia sandwich and the korma curry, and try the kombucha. Come on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday morning, and book the bed and breakfast if you want to stay over.
