Kitchen Table is a downtown restaurant on Farnam Street doing homemade comfort food, including sandwiches and all-day breakfast, with vegan options on the menu. The hummus plate and the poppy-seed sandwich are the reliable picks.

Order the hummus plate and a vegan sandwich, and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation of the breakfast dishes, since some use egg or dairy.
