The Zenith is the South Side's vegetarian cafe and antique gallery: a weekly changing menu, an extensive herbal tea list, live events some evenings, and a Sunday brunch buffet that regulars plan around. It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so it lands as vegan-friendly, but the vegan options are serious and the brunch buffet is the draw.

Hours can be irregular, so call ahead. Order the brunch buffet on Sunday, browse the antiques, and enjoy the weirdest, most wonderful room on the list.
