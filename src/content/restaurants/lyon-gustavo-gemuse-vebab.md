Güstavo is where Lyon's vegans go when they want a kebab, and the Vebab is a registered trademark for a reason: spiced, juicy, properly messy, and entirely plant-based. The Guillotière fast-food spot does kebabs, falafel sandwiches, French tacos and bowls, with a menu that is mostly organic, local and homemade.

It is cheap, fast and fun, the perfect counterpoint to Lyon's tasting menus. Order the Vebab, add fries, and eat it like you mean it. Nobody leaves hungry.
