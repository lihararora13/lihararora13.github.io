Veg Aroma is one of Gangapur Road's most recommended pure-veg dining rooms, the kind of place Nashik families return to week after week. The menu is long and vegetarian through and through, which makes it a comfortable stop for a vegan who knows what to ask for.

The North Indian mains are the move: dal tadka without ghee, mixed veg sabzi, and hot tandoori rotis. It is straightforward, satisfying food at fair prices, and a good second stop after Thyme & Whisk.
