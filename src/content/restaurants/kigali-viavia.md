ViaVia is a restaurant, bar and guesthouse in quiet Kibagabaga, part of a chain, open daily 7:30am to 9:30pm. The chefs are open to changing recipes to make them vegan, and the reported vegan items include scrambled tofu, a trio of hummus with chapati, falafel salad and a beetroot burger.

Order the scrambled tofu for breakfast and the falafel salad for lunch. Ask the staff to confirm each dish is vegan, as HappyCow notes it cannot confirm all of them.
