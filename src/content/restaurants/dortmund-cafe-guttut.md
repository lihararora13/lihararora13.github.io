Café Guttut is a small organic vegetarian café on Lindemannstraße, a Kreuzviertel institution that cooks everything fresh à la minute from seasonal, mostly regional organic produce. The menu is vegetarian with much of it vegan: vegan gyros, sauerkraut with mushroom cream sauce, smashed potatoes with baked beetroot and vegan tzatziki, plus a cake case and alcohol-free drinks made from organic ingredients.

Hours are daytime and worth noting: Monday to Friday until four, Thursday until eight, and Sunday afternoons from two to five, with Saturday closed. Come for a weekday lunch, order the gyros, and treat it as Dortmund's calmest vegan-friendly table.
