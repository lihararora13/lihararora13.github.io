Swing Kitchen is the fast, fun, no-fuss stop on this list, and it has fans well beyond the vegan crowd. The handcrafted burgers, wraps, salads, chick'n nuggets and vegan schnitzel come with the sweet potato fries regulars swear by, plus tiramisu or cheesecake for dessert, all served with CO2-neutral, biodegradable packaging.

Head to the Schwedenplatz branch in the 1st district: it is open daily and confirmed open now, unlike the Operngasse branch, which was reported closed in January 2026. Watch the running tally on the wall counting the animals, water and carbon saved, then order a second burger if the tally inspires you.
