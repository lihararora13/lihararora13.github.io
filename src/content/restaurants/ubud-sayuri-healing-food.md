Sayuri is where Ubud's raw food scene lives. The cafe and its plant-based chef academy serve a fully vegan menu that is mostly raw living food, with some cooked dishes and plenty of gluten-free options: the laksa bowl runs every day, specials rotate through the week from banana flower fish tacos to Saturday's raw Mexican lasagne, and the dessert case is the part regulars keep coming back for. Nuts and seeds are activated and dehydrated below 48 degrees, the miso and tempeh are organic, and the room doubles as a community space for kirtan, cacao ceremonies and cooking classes.

It opens daily from 8am to 11pm, and delivery runs by WhatsApp within Ubud. Come for a slow breakfast or a raw dessert and stay for the courtyard calm.
