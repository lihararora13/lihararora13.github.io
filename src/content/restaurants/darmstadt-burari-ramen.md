Burari Ramen is a ramen bar on the Schustergasse, a narrow old-town lane, serving ramen with vegan options alongside the meat broths. The vegan ramen runs on a vegetable broth with tofu and vegetables, and it is the noodle pick on this list.

Order the vegan ramen with tofu and vegetables, and ask for the vegan broth, since the standard broths run on meat.
