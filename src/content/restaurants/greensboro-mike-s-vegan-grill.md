Mike's Vegan Grill is an all-vegan grill on West Gate City Boulevard, doing burgers, sandwiches, and Southern plates with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the strongest pick in Greensboro, and the room is casual, counter-service, and built for a hearty lunch.

Order the vegan burger and the Southern plate. It is a good stop for a group, since the plates are built to share.
