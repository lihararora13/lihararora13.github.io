Towzen claims to be the first vegan ramen restaurant in Japan, founded in 2004, and it still feels like a secret: a small traditional house in a quiet residential street near Shimogamo Shrine, shoes off at the door, tatami floors, and a short menu built on shojin principles. The two bowls are the classic Musashi soy-milk ramen with mushrooms and nori, and a tantanmen, plus eggplant sushi, yuba and homemade vegan cakes.

It is a fifteen-minute walk from Matsugasaki Station and dinner reservations go through LINE, so plan the trip north as a little expedition. The broth is rich enough to convert sceptics, which is exactly why it has lasted twenty years.
