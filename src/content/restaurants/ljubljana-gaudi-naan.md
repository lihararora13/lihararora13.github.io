Gaudi&Naan is Ljubljana's special-occasion vegan table, a compact bistro where dinner is a proper tasting menu and lunch is a four-course affair. The kitchen moves between Indian and Chinese influences with confidence, and the brunch from nine to noon is one of the best slow mornings in the city.

Book ahead for dinner, the room is small and the menu is in English. Come hungry, order the lunch tasting if it is running, and finish with whatever dessert the kitchen is proud of that week.
