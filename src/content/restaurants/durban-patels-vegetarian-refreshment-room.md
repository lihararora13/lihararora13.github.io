Patel's has been serving vegetarian food in Durban since 1911, and it calls itself the bunny chow kings with good reason. The bean and lentil bunny chows are the draw: a hollowed loaf filled with curry, eaten with your hands, plus dals, vegetable curries and jalebi from the sweets counter. It is history you can eat, for very little money.

It opens early and closes early afternoon, so go for breakfast or lunch. Order the bean bunny chow, ask which curries are dairy-free that day, and save room for jalebi.
