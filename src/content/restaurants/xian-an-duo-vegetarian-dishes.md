An Duo sits directly opposite Guangren Temple, the city's only Tibetan Buddhist temple, at 136 Xibei First Road inside the old city wall, and it is the natural stop after a temple visit. The cooking is fully vegan, with no eggs, dairy or honey in the dishes, and the menu runs from All Stars Surrounding the Moon Soup to Tian Su Roast Goose, vegetarian noodles and a vegetarian Dongpo Pork, at about 110 RMB a person.

The room is elegant and bright, with a zen feel to match the neighbourhood, and the service is attentive. Open 10:30am to 9:30pm, phone 029-87330205. The pin is at the Guangren Temple coordinates, directly across the street from the restaurant.
