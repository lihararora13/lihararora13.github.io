Paprica Vegan is Curitiba's vegan fast-food favourite: hamburgers, hot dogs, cakes and desserts, all plant-based, in a casual spot right in Centro. It is the kind of place where the food is fun first and vegan second, which is exactly why it works for mixed groups.

Come hungry, order a burger and a hot dog, and save room for cake. This is the one for a big, fun vegan feed downtown.
