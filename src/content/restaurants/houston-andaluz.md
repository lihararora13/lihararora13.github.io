Andaluz is the hole-in-the-wall proof that Houston's vegan map has no edges. Out on Cypress Creek Parkway, this family-run spot serves a fully vegan Mexican menu with an absurd number of protein options: jackfruit carnitas, mushroom fajitas, hibiscus, plant-based queso, the works. The quesabirria tacos and the enchiladas verdes with jackfruit are the dishes people drive across town for.

The room is modest, the prices are kind, and the horchata is non-negotiable. It is exactly the kind of neighborhood treasure this guide exists to surface.
