La Vegan is the casual, friendly face of Luohu's Buddhist restaurant strip: a diverse international menu, a buffet at lunch and dinner, and staff who make first-timers feel welcome. It is a simple date-night spot rather than a business dinner room.

Order from the buffet if you want to try everything, or point at the pictures on the menu. The Korean stone pot and the spaghetti both work.
