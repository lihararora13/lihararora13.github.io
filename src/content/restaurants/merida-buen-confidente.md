Buen Confidente is the fully vegan café in Montes de Amé, north of Centro, a bright neighbourhood spot that travellers keep naming on vegan tours of the city. Wraps with chips and fresh orange juice are the order visitors photograph, with bowls alongside.

It is a residential-neighbourhood café rather than a downtown restaurant, so take a taxi or a ride north from Centro. The food is light and café-style, best for breakfast or lunch.
