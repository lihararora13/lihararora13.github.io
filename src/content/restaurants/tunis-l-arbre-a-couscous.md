L'Arbre a Couscous is a small restaurant on Rue Mami in La Marsa offering a rare vegan twist on the traditional North African dish. A buffet at the back holds several kinds of couscous, including spinach, whole wheat and the highly spiced harissa, all prepared without animal products, with stewed vegetables, beans and a choice of mild or spicy sauce.

Be sure to ask for the vegan spinach boulettes, which regulars single out. The room seats about twenty-four, so it fills up but rarely needs a booking.
