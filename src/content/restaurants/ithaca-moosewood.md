Moosewood is a vegetarian restaurant on North Cayuga Street that has served seasonal, organic food since 1973, and its cookbooks made it famous far beyond Ithaca. The menu rotates with the seasons, with vegan dishes sitting alongside vegetarian ones, and the kitchen is deeply experienced with vegan orders.

Order the vegan entree of the day and the roasted vegetables, and tell the server you are vegan up front, since the kitchen uses eggs and dairy in some dishes.
