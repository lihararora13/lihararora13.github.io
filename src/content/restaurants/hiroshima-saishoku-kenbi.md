Saishoku-Kenbi is as close as Hiroshima gets to a temple kitchen: a 100% vegetarian restaurant in a residential part of the city, up the hill behind Nishi-Hiroshima station, cooking shojin-style food with okara-konnyaku, wheat gluten and soybean protein. The menu runs to richly flavoured ramen, gourmet sandwiches filled with salad and veggie meat, crispy spring rolls and gyoza, with generous portions and reasonable prices, and there is a mini grocery section selling natural foods to take away.

Getting there takes effort: a taxi from Nishi-Hiroshima station, about ten minutes, or the local bus up the hill. It is open for lunch, roughly 11:00 to 16:00, and closed one weekday, so check the current closed day before you go.
