Gypsy has been feeding Sardarpura since 1991, and it shows: a big, confident pure-veg dining room on 9th C Road where the thali is the order and the room is full of families who have been coming for decades. It is Jodhpur's classic vegetarian institution.

Order the veg thali without curd and ghee, add a dal makhani without cream, and you have the most traditional meal on this list. Come hungry, because the thali does not believe in small portions.
