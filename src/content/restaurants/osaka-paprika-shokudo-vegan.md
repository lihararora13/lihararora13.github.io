Paprika Shokudo Vegan is the answer to the question every vegan visitor asks at 7pm on a Sunday: where can I actually eat tonight? It is open for lunch and dinner every day, a rarity in this city, in a cosy rustic room a short walk west of Shinsaibashi. The menu leans into comfort food with a Japanese and Italian accent: set meals built around tempeh, soy-meat karaage and other meat substitutes, plus pizza, pasta and vegan cakes.

Mains start at around ¥800, and there is a ¥300 seat charge in the evening, which is normal for restaurants of this kind in Japan. It takes credit cards, the kitchen cooks with organic vegetables and no additives, and it is the obvious dinner plan when Green Earth has closed for the day. Closed Wednesdays.
