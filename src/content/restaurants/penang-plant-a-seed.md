Plant A Seed is Penang's vegan Japanese restaurant, and it is a delight: vegan sushi, hand rolls, tempura, fried rice and noodles, all of it fully plant-based, served in a calm room where you can sit on a tatami mat or a regular chair. The restaurant has been going since 2013 in various forms, and the sushi is the draw, fresh, inventive and properly satisfying.

Come for dinner, order a spread of sushi and hand rolls to share, and finish with tempura. This is the one for a Japanese night out in George Town.
