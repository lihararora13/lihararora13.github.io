YEARS started in Sham Shui Po and quietly grew into a small plant-based empire, but the Fuk Wah Street original is still the one locals rank first for vegan eating in the neighbourhood. It is a calm, Japanese-tinged art cafe in the middle of fabric shops and electronics stalls, and it fills up fast at lunch.

The menu roams happily: a proper cheeseburger, katsu curry over risotto, green pastas and colourful salads, all plant-based and protein-forward. Come early, order the cutlet sandwich if it is on, and wander Sham Shui Po's streets afterwards, happily fed.
