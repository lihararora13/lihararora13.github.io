The Buckingham is Edmonton's all-vegan punk pub, and it is exactly as fun as that sounds. The kitchen serves an all-vegan pub menu, the Buck Crunch Wrap, vegan wings, poutine, queso and jackfruit tacos, while the room hosts live music, karaoke and dance parties until 2am.

It is the late-night pick on this list. Order the wings and the crunch wrap, stay for the bands, and thank the kitchen for making a dive bar fully vegan.
