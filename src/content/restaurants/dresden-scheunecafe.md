Scheunecafe has been part of Dresden's Scheune cultural centre for more than twenty years, a vegetarian cafe with a generous beer garden and a seasonal international menu that marks its vegan dishes clearly. The cooking leans on regional suppliers, the weekly specials change, and weekend mornings bring breakfast. It is a neighbourhood institution as much as a restaurant, the kind of place where lunch stretches into an afternoon.

Note the move: the cafe has relocated from Alaunstrasse 36-40 to Gorlitzer Strasse 20, so do not go to the old address. Hours are limited, evenings Tuesday to Friday and mornings at the weekend, so check before you go. This is the relaxed, beer-garden pick for a slow Neustadt day.
