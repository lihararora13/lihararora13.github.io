La Kombuchería is the small vegan kitchen inside the Kuka y Naranjo hotel in Centro, serving organic vegan meals, snacks and light meals with house kombucha in a cosy, plant-filled courtyard. The kombucha is the draw, with a club for regulars, and the food is light rather than a full dinner.

It is a hotel restaurant, so the room is small and calm, and it works as a snack and kombucha stop between museums rather than a big evening meal.
