La Temeraria is Montevideo's vegetarian rock-and-food joint: fresh, home-style cooking, imported and craft beers, juices and smoothies, and a menu that runs from brunch through to one in the morning. The vegan stars are the chivito de seitan, the falafel burrito and the empanadas, and the whole place has the relaxed buzz of a neighbourhood favourite.

Come for brunch or a late dinner, order the chivito de seitan, and stay for a craft beer. This is the one for a fun, loud, late night out.
