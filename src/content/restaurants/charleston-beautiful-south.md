Beautiful South is the Cantonese sister of Kwei Fei, and more than half of its dishes are vegetarian or vegan-friendly, which is rare for a Cantonese room. The scallion oil noodles and the Shanghai-style shumai are the dim sum orders, and the family-style General Tso's, sweet and sour, and black pepper dishes all come with tofu.

Order family-style and let the table share. The noodles are the dish to start with.
