Param Food Complex is a Gwalior family-run institution, serving Indian vegetarian food to the city since long before the café wave arrived. It holds a 4.4-star rating across dozens of reviews, and it is open from 8 AM to 11 PM, which makes it one of the most dependable veg kitchens in the old city.

Order the veg thali without ghee, add a seasonal sabzi and roti, and finish with jalebi and chai. It is not fashionable, and that is precisely the point: this is Gwalior's everyday vegetarian cooking, served without fuss.
