True Food Hua Hin is a plant-based cafe in central Hua Hin with smoothie bowls, vegan burgers and gluten-free options, running about 200 to 350 baht, and walk-in friendly. It is the most casual plant-based cafe in town, and it suits a light lunch or a breakfast bowl before the beach.

Order a smoothie bowl and a vegan burger, and ask about gluten-free options if you need them. Its pin is approximate to central Hua Hin.
