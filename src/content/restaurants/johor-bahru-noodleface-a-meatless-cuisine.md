Noodleface is Mount Austin's meatless noodle house, a vegetarian restaurant doing Chinese, Korean and Western comfort food with the emphasis on noodles, mushrooms and tofu. The signature is the stone pot noodles, kamameshi served piping hot in a stone pot, alongside a black pepper sizzling mushroom steak, a charcoal tofu burger, milk curry kamameshi and fried abalone mushroom. Reviewers praise the sesame oil soup base with kolo mee and the crispy mushroom burger, and note the cooking is free from garlic and onion.

It is vegetarian and serves eggs, but the menu marks egg and dairy clearly and has a no-egg-no-milk page, so almost the whole menu can be made vegan; note that of the three noodle choices, only the handmade banmian is vegan. It is closed on Mondays. The pin on this page is for the Akademik Suite complex, as the geocoder matched Block A rather than Block B.
