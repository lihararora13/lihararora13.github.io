Village of OM is the neighbourhood vegan spot Sacramento locals keep to themselves: small, warm, all-vegan, and the kind of place where the daily bowl and the seasonal soup are the whole point. The menu leans wholesome without getting preachy, and the room feels like a community living room.

It is not trying to be a destination, which is why it is one. Come for a quiet lunch, order the Buddha bowl, and enjoy the rare vegan restaurant that feels like a exhale.
