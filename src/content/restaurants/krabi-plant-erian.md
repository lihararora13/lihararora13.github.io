Plant-erian is a fully vegan cafe and restaurant in Ao Nang, about a 15 to 20-minute walk inland from the beach, open daily from 10am to 10pm. According to 2026 reviews it has a huge menu of Thai and European dishes, from curries and fried rice to burgers, pasta, salads, smoothie bowls and cakes, and it is the safest bet for dinner in Ao Nang.

Order the green curry and the khao soi, and try a burger or a smoothie bowl if you want a change. It is the strongest all-vegan pick in Krabi and the natural first stop for anyone staying in Ao Nang.
