Eatsa is a mixed-menu fast-casual spot on Rue Pastorelli, near the centre, offering build-your-own bowls and pitas with vegan and vegetarian choices. It is the quick-lunch pick on this list, useful when the smaller vegan rooms are closed or full.

It is listed as vegan-friendly, not fully vegan: the menu also includes chicken, so confirm the protein, sauces, cheese, yoghurt and honey when you order. Build a bowl with a vegan protein and check the sauces.
