Woo Tan Scran is Liverpool's all-vegan Chinese takeaway, operating from the same Duke Street space as Down the Hatch, which means one address covers two very different cravings. The menu is fast-food fun with a Chinese accent: dan dan noodles, salt and pepper tofu, dumplings and the kind of takeaway classics that taste even better knowing no animal was involved.

It is takeaway-first, so grab your order and eat it while wandering the Ropewalks, or take it back to your hotel. Order the dan dan noodles and a side of dumplings, and thank the city for putting its two best vegan takeaways under one roof.
