Godt Brød is a bakery café at Muséplassen in the Sentrum, and it is the morning stop on this page. The sourdough toast is the signature, with vegan pastries and cinnamon buns alongside, all of it baked in-house.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so check the vegan pastries in the case. The cinnamon bun is the order, and the sourdough toast runs it close.
