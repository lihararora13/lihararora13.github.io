Anna's Mess is the South Indian counter in Tapovan, a simple vegetarian mess near Aloha on the Ganges that holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow. The cooking is authentic South Indian, dosa, idly, mini idlies, malabar parota, medu vada and paniyaram, and much of it is vegan as served or can be made vegan by leaving out the ghee and curd.

This is the cheapest proper meal on the list and the best breakfast in town for a vegan: order a dosa with sambar and chutney, an idli and a medu vada, and ask for no ghee. It sits on NH 7 by the main Tapovan road, easy to reach on foot from the Tapovan cafes.
