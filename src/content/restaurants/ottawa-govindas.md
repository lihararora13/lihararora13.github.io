Govinda's is Ottawa's beloved Hare Krishna-run vegetarian restaurant, a non-profit near the University of Ottawa serving a hot and cold all-you-can-eat vegan buffet. It is simple, hearty and very cheap, which is why students and locals have loved it for years.

Come hungry and cash-ready. The buffet is the whole point: dhal, vegetable curries, rice and salads, as much as you like. This is the best-value meal on the list.
