Vincent V does exactly what it says on the sign: burgers, fries and shakes, all plant-based, right on Bahnhofsplatz at the main station. It is the easiest vegan meal in Bremen, the one you can eat between trains, and it is better than a station meal has any right to be. The burgers are properly smashed, the fries are crisp, and the shakes are thick enough to need a proper straw.

It sits at the Hauptbahnhof, which makes it the natural first or last stop in the city. Order a smash burger with fries and a shake, eat it standing at the counter or take it with you, and move on with your day. This is fast food done properly, with nothing animal in it.
