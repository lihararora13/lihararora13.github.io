Mizuna is a farm-to-table restaurant on North Howard Street in downtown Spokane, with a strong vegan bench of seasonal vegetable dishes. It is the most polished room on this list, and the one to book for a nicer dinner.

Order the vegan entree of the day and the roasted vegetables. Book ahead, since it is the priciest room on this list.
