Gustav Grün is a Mediterranean health-food restaurant on Haarenstraße, and it is the freshest stop on Oldenburg's list: falafel, sweet chilli bowls and vegan salads, served daily until nine. The cooking is light and bright rather than heavy, the room is modern and calm, and the vegan dishes are a real part of the menu.

It sits a short walk from the Markt, in the middle of the old town. Order a falafel plate, add a sweet chilli bowl, and enjoy a menu that makes healthy food feel like a pleasure rather than a duty. This is the table for a light, fresh lunch in Oldenburg.
