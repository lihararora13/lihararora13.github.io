Harmony Bakery is Hampden's all-vegan bakery, a small counter turning out cinnamon rolls, cakes, and cookies with no animal products anywhere in the case. It is the city's best stop for a sweet course after a savory meal, and a reliable place to pick up a whole cake for a celebration.

Order a cinnamon roll warm from the case and a slice of whatever cake is on the board. Go early on weekends, when the popular items sell out.
