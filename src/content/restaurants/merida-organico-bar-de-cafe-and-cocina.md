Orgánico is the quaint daytime café in Centro, a terrace spot on Calle 62 serving organic coffee and vegan and vegetarian bites, with a 4.7 rating across more than 600 reviews. Visitors who have been several times praise the consistency of the food and the kindness of the staff, and the menu changes at night, with burger nights among the events.

It is a casual daytime hangout, best for breakfast, coffee or a light lunch, and the vegan options are clearly part of the menu rather than an afterthought.
