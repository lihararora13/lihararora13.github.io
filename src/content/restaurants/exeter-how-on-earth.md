How on Earth is a fully vegan deli and shop on South Street, part grocery and part lunch counter. It is the place to stock up as well as to eat, with vegan cheeses, pantry goods and a rotating lunch menu.

Eat at the counter, then browse the shelves. The deli plates change daily and the vegan cheeses are worth trying.
