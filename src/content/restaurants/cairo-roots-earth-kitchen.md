Roots Earth Kitchen is Heliopolis' all-vegan answer to the bowl trend: build-your-own salads, hearty bowls, nachos and vegan ice cream, all plant-based. It is bright, modern and easy, the kind of place that works for lunch with friends who eat in different ways.

Build a bowl with everything you like, add nachos to share, and finish with the vegan ice cream. It is the most relaxed fully vegan meal in the city.
