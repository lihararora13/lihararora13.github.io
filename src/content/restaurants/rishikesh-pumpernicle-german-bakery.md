Pumpernicle German Bakery is the riverside bakery of Laxman Jhula, a vegetarian bakery cafe by the Ganges near the post office that also serves vegan baked treats and raw food. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow and has been a fixture of the Jhula strip for years.

Come for fresh bread and vegan baked treats with a coffee, and sit with the river below. It is a simple, no-fuss stop, and a good one to pair with a walk across Laxman Jhula itself.
