Easy House made the list for a reason. An all-vegan Mermaid Waters favourite for authentic yum cha and Cantonese comfort food, and a must-visit for dumpling lovers. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Mermaid Waters, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan yum cha, Dumplings, Tofu claypot if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
