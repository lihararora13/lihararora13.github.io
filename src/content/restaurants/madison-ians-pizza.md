Ian's Pizza is Madison's slice shop, famous for its mac n cheese pizza, and the vegan version of it is the one to order, along with the buffalo cauliflower slice. The State Street room stays open until 2:30am on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday, which makes it the late-night stop on this list.

It is cheap, fast, and best eaten by the slice. Get the mac n cheese slice and a salad from the bar.
