Hotel Anupam is the pure-veg hotel restaurant on Palace Road in Chembukkav, open from 7 in the morning to 8:30 at night. It is the quiet standby near the palace grounds, the place to go when the Round's restaurants are full and the family wants a calm table.

Order the masala dosa and the veg meal, and ask for them without ghee or curd. It is not the flashiest room in Thrissur, and that is its virtue.
