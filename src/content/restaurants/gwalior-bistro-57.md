Bistro 57 is the café Gwalior's young crowd has made its own: grey upholstered booths, brick-textured walls, industrial sconces and a big red sign glowing over Patel Nagar. The menu is proudly pure vegetarian, spanning North Indian, Chinese, Italian and café dishes, and the house pushes its friendship table, a spread of burger, pizza, pasta and paneer dishes meant for sharing.

Vegans should build around the peri peri fries, the veg exotic pizza without cheese and the basil pesto pasta, and finish with a mint mojito. Come for the vibe, stay for the table, and bring friends.
