Rotiseria Urkitza is an oriental vegetarian rotiseria on Urquiza, rated 5.0, serving a remarkable spread at lunch: sushi, chau fan, rice noodles, chop suey, spring rolls, tofu stew and textured-soy dishes, plus western pastas, milanesas, empanadas and more than 25 salads. It is takeaway-focused and open weekday lunches, 11:30 am to 2:30 pm.

Order the tofu stew, add sushi and a salad, and ask which dishes are vegan, as the kitchen also uses dairy. It is one of the cheapest and most generous vegan lunches in Mendoza.
