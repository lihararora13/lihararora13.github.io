Tumbleweed is Nantes' fully vegan CBD coffee shop, a small Centre-Ville counter with a playful drinks list: Snickers and peanut butter lattes, plus vegan cinnamon rolls and chocolate chip brioche. It is the sweet-coffee stop on this page and the cheapest one too.

The CBD angle is part of the concept, so expect a relaxed room and a menu that leans sweet. The pastries are all vegan, and the lattes are the reason to come.
