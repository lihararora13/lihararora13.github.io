Moravid, full name Moravid Sonnenküche, is a Moroccan restaurant on Gutenbergstraße, and it is the newest face on Kiel's list: vegan tajine, couscous and a falafel sandwich alongside meat dishes, with an owner who happily veganises on request. The room is warm and small, the cooking is North African and generous, and the vegan dishes are the ones the kitchen is proudest of.

It sits at the address that used to house Kitty Rock Belly Full, which is gone, and it opens Wednesday to Sunday evenings. Order the vegan tajine, add couscous, and ask what the kitchen can veganise that day. This is the table for Moroccan food in Kiel, and a good evening stop.
