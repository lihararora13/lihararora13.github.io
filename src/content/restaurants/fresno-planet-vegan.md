Planet Vegan started as a food truck in 2019 and opened its north Fresno restaurant on North Palm Avenue two years ago, run by three siblings. The menu is vegan versions of favorite foods: Impossible burgers, buffalo chicken sandwiches, and loaded waffle fries with plant-based meat, cheese, and aioli.

Order the Impossible burger and the loaded waffle fries. It is a drive from downtown, so plan it as a north-side lunch rather than a downtown stop.
