Grains by Udaan Himalayan calls itself Darjeeling's largest pure-veg restaurant, and it sits in the perfect spot for it: on Hill Cart Road by the toy train station. It is a big, modern dining room with a broad pure-veg menu, open 7:30 in the morning to 10:30 at night, which makes it the reliable all-day veg stop in town.

Order the veg thali or the veg Manchurian, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you have a solid plant-based meal before or after a toy train ride. It is the backbone of Darjeeling's veg scene.
