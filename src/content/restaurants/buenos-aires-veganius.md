Veganius is Palermo lunch done right: comforting plates, proper variety of vegetables, and a bakery counter that understands the sacred medialuna ritual. Their own description is proudly 100% vegetal, with no animals or derivatives in anything.

Grab the milanesa, add a salad, and finish like a local with espresso and a medialuna on the terrace. Friendly, filling and fairly priced.
