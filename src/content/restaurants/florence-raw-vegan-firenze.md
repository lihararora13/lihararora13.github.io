Raw Vegan Firenze is the small fully vegan and gluten-free café on Via Sant'Agostino in the Oltrarno, two minutes from Piazza Santo Spirito. Order the pizza: it comes on a gluten-free buckwheat base, raw or baked, topped with cashew mozzarella and almond cheese, and it is the thing to try here. Poke bowls, wraps, smoothies and handmade chocolates round out the menu.

Ask for the pizza baked if you want it warm. It works well for a mixed group with different dietary needs, and it is closed Monday.
