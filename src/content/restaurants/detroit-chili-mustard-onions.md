Chili Mustard Onions is the first plant-based Coney Island kitchen in Detroit, and it is exactly as good as that sounds: vegan coney dogs, chili fries and vegan chili, all of it plant-based and all of it a love letter to the city's coney tradition. The coney dog is the signature, and the chili fries are the sleeper.

It is the most Detroit meal on the list, and the one to take someone who thinks a coney cannot be vegan. Order the coney, add chili fries, and enjoy the Motor City classic, remade.
