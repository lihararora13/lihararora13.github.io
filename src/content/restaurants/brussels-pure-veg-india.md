Pure Veg India is the Royal Quarter's vegetarian buffet, and it is a vegan-friendly gem. Curries, breads, chaat, samosas and a salad bar fill the buffet, with many dishes vegan, and the owner understands veganism and will guide you to the right plates.

It is a handy lunch stop near the centre. Fill a plate with curry and samosas, add some chaat, and ask the owner what is vegan today.
