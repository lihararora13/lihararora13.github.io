Pizzeria Vaatika claims to be India's first pizzeria, opened in 1992 after the owner was inspired by Italian music students living along the Ganges, and the wood-fired brick oven is still the heart of the place. The garden seating looks out towards the river, and the wait for a table can stretch past an hour at peak times.

Order the wood-fired veg pizza and the pink sauce penne, and save room for the cinnamon-scented apple pie, the dish that made the cafe famous. Ask for the pizza without cheese and it comes out properly vegan.
