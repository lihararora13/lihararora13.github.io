Espaco Natural is Salvador's healthy one-stop shop: a natural restaurant and store on Avenida Antonio Carlos Magalhaes, serving healthy Brazilian plates, smoothies and salad bowls, with vegan options across the menu. It is bright, modern and easy, the kind of place you stop at for lunch and leave with a bag of healthy snacks.

Come for lunch, order a natural plate and a smoothie, and browse the shop. This is the one for a quick, healthy Salvador lunch.
