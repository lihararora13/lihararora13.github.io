Tribe is the modern, Western-leaning counterweight to Beijing's temple restaurants: salads, bowls and healthy cafe cooking in the heart of Sanlitun, handy when you want a break from Chinese food.

Order a bowl and a juice, and enjoy the people-watching. It is the lightest meal on the list, in a good way.
