Hibiscus is a 100% vegetal restaurant open since 2014, hidden behind the Igreja dos Terceiros in the historic centre, serving a lunch buffet that changes every day plus dinner on Fridays and Saturdays. The mostly Indian-inspired buffet runs on cold and warm dishes with a soup and unlimited tea included, and it fills fast, so come early.

Order the lunch buffet, the vegetable sushi and the moussaka, and finish with the tiramisu de copo. Lunch runs Monday to Saturday, dinner Friday and Saturday only.
