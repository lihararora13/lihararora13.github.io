Klorofull is a fully vegan Mediterranean restaurant offering a selection of appetizers, mains and desserts, with a menu that changes seasonally and a 5.0 HappyCow rating. The kitchen calls itself a vegetable restaurant, hyper-local and micro-seasonal, and the cooking runs from dosa and kimchi to borek, burrito and jollof rice depending on what is in season.

It is the most creative table on this list after V. Check the menu online before you go, order whatever is seasonal, and let the kitchen show off.
