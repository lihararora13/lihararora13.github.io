Dhaba Wala Indian Kitchen is a downtown Indian restaurant on Asylum Street with curries, dals, and breads that adapt well to vegan orders. It is a quick, central lunch stop, and the chana masala is the reliable pick.

Order the chana masala and dal tadka with vegan roti, and confirm which breads are dairy-free, since some are brushed with ghee.
