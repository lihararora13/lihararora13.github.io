Espaco Nutrir has been feeding Campeche and Lagoa for ten years, and it is Floripa's vegan buffet at its best: a 100 percent vegan kitchen, all-you-can-eat or pay-by-weight, fry-free and mostly gluten-free, with a variable menu that changes with the seasons. On special dates the Lagoa unit hosts the Cafe Colonial, a bountiful table of sweets, savouries, breads, cakes, pies, coffees and teas, all vegan.

Come for lunch, fill a buffet plate, and ask about the Cafe Colonial. This is the one for a big, joyful vegan buffet in the south of the island.
