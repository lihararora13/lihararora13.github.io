Anandam Pure Veg near Clock Tower on Rajpur Road is the thali house of this list: brass-thaal Doon Basmati thalis, slow-simmered dal, and pure-ghee kesari jalebi for the dairy eaters. It is the kind of place where lunch is a proper event.

Order the Doon Basmati deluxe thali without ghee and the dal tadka. It is cheap, generous, and deeply Doon.
