Kind Kones Oman is an all-vegan ice cream parlour on the first floor of Souq Al Madina Mall, serving a variety of plant-based flavours like Almond Brittle Fudge, Black Forest, Blue Planet, Charcoal Vanilla, Chocolate Sorbet, Cookies and Cream, Madagascan Vanilla Bean, Mango Raspberry Swirl, Pandan Gula Melaka, Peanut Butter and Jelly, Pulut Hitam and Salted Chocolate Chip. It is rated 5.0 on HappyCow and open daily from 10:00am to 10:00pm.

Order the Almond Brittle Fudge and the Mango Raspberry Swirl. It is the only fully vegan pick in Muscat, and the sweetest stop on this list.
