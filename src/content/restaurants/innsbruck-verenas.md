Verenas is a cute cafe just south of the old city, open Monday to Friday seven to six, serving vegan cakes, vegan rice pudding and breakfast options with hot drinks and plant milk. It is the breakfast pick on this list.

Order the vegan rice pudding, a vegan cake and a coffee with plant milk, and come in the morning, since it closes at six.
