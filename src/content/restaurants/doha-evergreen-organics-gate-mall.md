The Gate Mall branch is the highest-rated Evergreen on HappyCow at 5.0, with the same seasonal, organic, fully vegan menu as The Pearl: smoothie bowls, salads, sandwiches, burgers, pancakes, French toast and cakes. This location also has a small shop for vegan cosmetics and household goods attached.

Order the French toast for brunch or a sandwich for lunch, and browse the shop after. It sits in the same mall as MYLK, so the two make an easy vegan crawl.
