Vegan King is Accra's wellness-first vegan spot, built around alkaline ingredients grown locally. The menu is high-end vegan meals, smoothies, juices and frozen desserts, and the kitchen also runs meal plans for regulars. It is the place for a clean, deliberate plant-based meal rather than a quick snack.

Order the meal of the day, add a smoothie, and save room for a frozen dessert. It is open six days a week in Osu, close to Tatale, so the two make an easy vegan crawl.
