The Summer Tea House, also known as Jingyi Tea House, is one of Shenzhen's best vegetarian restaurants, frequented by Buddhist monks from China and Tibet as well as locals. The menu is extensive, mainly Guangdong style, with faux meats, tofu and sweet corn dishes.

It reopened in October 2024 at its new Luohu address. Order the mock chicken drumsticks and the sweet corn and pine nut dish.
