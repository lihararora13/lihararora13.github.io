True Food Kitchen is Pasadena's health-driven, seasonal American restaurant: a bright room on West Colorado Boulevard in Old Pasadena, built around Dr. Andrew Weil's anti-inflammatory food pyramid, with vegan dishes clearly marked across the menu. Hummus with pita, seasonal vegetable bowls, ancient grains bowls and vegan pizza are the vegan core, and the kitchen is used to cooking for dietary restrictions. It is the group pick in this guide, open Monday to Sunday 11am to 10pm.

Ask for the vegan dishes by name: they are marked on the menu. Order the hummus to share and a seasonal vegetable bowl as a main.
