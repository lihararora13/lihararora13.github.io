Papilla is an ice cream parlour on Rue Saint-Francois in Vieux Nice, with a fully vegan ice cream cabinet alongside its classic flavours. The vegan flavours include hazelnut, mango sorbet, rice-milk matcha and strawberry sorbet, and it is open daily from noon to 22:30, so it is the dessert stop after dinner.

It is listed as vegan-friendly, as the shop also serves dairy ice cream. Order the hazelnut gelato and the mango sorbet.
