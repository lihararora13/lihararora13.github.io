VeggieVictory has been changing the game in Lagos since 2013, turning familiar Nigerian dishes meat-free without losing their soul. The menu covers vegan jollof rice, efo riro, amala, plantain chips and vegan shawarma, with a buffet on offer, and it is the restaurant other Lagos vegan spots measure themselves against. Pre-ordering is recommended, as delivery is a big part of the business.

Order the jollof and the efo riro for the classic experience, and the shawarma if you want something handheld. This is the first stop on any vegan trip to Lagos.
