Ice Shack is the fully vegan dessert parlour on Wilmslow Road in Withington, a short walk from Lotus Plant Based Kitchen, which makes the two of them a perfect one-two for an evening out. The menu is ice cream, milkshakes and cakes, all of it vegan, with gluten-free cones and plenty of gluten-free cakes too. It is closed on Mondays and otherwise open daily.

Order a sundae, a milkshake, and a slice of cake to share, and do not rush it: this is the kind of place that turns a good dinner into a proper night out. Pair it with Lotus up the road and you have a fully vegan evening, starters to scoops, without leaving Wilmslow Road.
