Curry and Noodle is an Asian noodle restaurant in Robinhood Village, doing curries, noodle dishes, and tofu preparations with vegan options. It is the noodle pick on this list, and the vegan curry is the reliable order.

Order the vegan curry and a noodle dish, and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since some dishes are finished with fish sauce or egg.
