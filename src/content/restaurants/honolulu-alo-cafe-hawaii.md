ALO Cafe Hawaii is Waikiki's vegan cafe: fully plant-based, doing bowls, sandwiches and smoothies a short walk from the beach, and quickly a favourite with visitors and locals alike. It is the Waikiki stop on the list, and the one to hit between the sand and the shops.

It is bright, casual, and very Waikiki. Order a bowl, grab a smoothie, and take it to the beach.
