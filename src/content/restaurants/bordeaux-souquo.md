Souquo is the café Bordeaux's coffee lovers swear by, and the kitchen keeps up: a vegetarian, engaged cuisine with a long list of vegan dishes, from beetroot gravlax and panisse fries to creamy polenta and a vegan panna cotta. The specialty coffee is the draw, the food is the reason to stay for lunch.

It sits on the Quai Sainte-Croix by the river, open for breakfast, lunch and evening service on Thursdays and Fridays. Order the gravlax, add the polenta, and finish with the panna cotta. Coffee and plants, done properly.
