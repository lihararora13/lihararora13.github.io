Simi's World Vegan Cafe made the list for a reason. A fully vegan cafe, community and wellness hub in Film Nagar with burgers, pizzas, pastas and wraps, plus yoga and holistic-living events. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Film Nagar, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan pizza, Vegan burger, Hummus wrap if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
