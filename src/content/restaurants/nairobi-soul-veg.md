Soul Veg is the highest-rated veg restaurant in Nairobi on HappyCow, a franchise of the Atlanta original, and it brings American comfort food to the city centre. The menu runs on mac-and-cheese, BBQ twists, cakes, cookies and pastries, with most dishes vegan and honey available only on request. It is a cafe, bakery and caterer in one, so it works for a sit-down lunch or a takeaway box.

Order the mac-and-cheese first, then work through the bakery case. This is the pick for anyone who thinks vegan food in Nairobi means only dosa and dal: it does not.
