Doppeltsolecker 2.0 is the Kreuzviertel restaurant of Dortmund's vegan-focused Doppeltsolecker brand, cooking Ukrainian and Georgian food that is vegan and vegetarian by design. The menu is the most ambitious in the city: vegan beef wellington with mushroom duxelles, seitan duck breast with cherry sauce, vegan roulade with vegan bacon, vegan camembert and shakshuka, with cakes and desserts that regulars cross town for.

The kitchen is vegetarian with a large vegan side, so order from the vegan-marked dishes. Come for dinner rather than a quick lunch, book ahead on weekends, and save room for the cookie dough torte. This is the table to take someone who thinks Eastern European food cannot be vegan.
