Oh Donuts & Coffee is ranked the top vegan spot in Belfast by reviewer praise, and it is easy to see why: the counter always has at least two vegan donut flavours, with the lemon poppyseed and the chai and apple among the favourites, alongside good coffee.

Order a vegan donut and a coffee, and take a box for the road. It is a donut shop that sells vegan donuts alongside regular ones, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note there is a second branch at Stranmillis if the city-centre queue is long.
