Joy Healthyfood is a fully vegan and gluten-free cafe on Rue Estelle, in the Gambetta quarter. Everything is plant-based and gluten-free, which makes it a safe table for coeliacs as well as vegans.

Order the brunch plate and a cake. A good pick for anyone avoiding gluten as well as animal products.
