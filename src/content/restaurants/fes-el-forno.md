El Forno is a cheerful, colourful Moroccan restaurant on Rue Talaa Kbira with vegan options and a slightly international lean. The vegetable tagine is the must-order, and the Moroccan salad is a sampler of four dishes, split pea soup, white beans, eggplant and lentils.

Order the vegetable tagine and the Moroccan salad. It can feel touristy, but the food makes a strong case for staying.
