Flames & Pots opened in Vijayawada on 19 November 2026 as the city's first purely plant-based Asian restaurant, and the launch video is explicit: dishes from fifteen-plus countries, Indonesian, Malaysian, Korean and Thai, with veg chicken and veg mutton starters, curries and biryanis designed to taste like the originals while being completely vegetarian. The room is modern, with wooden tables, blue booths and a large Buddha mural.

Order the veg chicken starter and the veg mutton biryani. It is a week old, it is the only fully plant-based restaurant in the city, and it is the most exciting opening on this list.
