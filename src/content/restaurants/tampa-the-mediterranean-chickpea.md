The Mediterranean Chickpea is a vegetarian Mediterranean restaurant on South MacDill Avenue, doing falafel, hummus, and plates with a strong vegan bench. It is the Mediterranean pick on this list, and the falafel is the reliable order.

Order the falafel and the hummus, and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since some plates are finished with dairy.
