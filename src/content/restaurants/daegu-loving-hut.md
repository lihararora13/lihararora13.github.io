Loving Hut in Dong-gu is the Daegu branch of the international all-vegan chain, open for lunch from 11am to 3pm, Monday to Saturday. The menu covers mock-meat Korean dishes, mala noodles and vegan bulgogi at very low prices, and it is the most reliable all-vegan lunch in the city outside Jung-gu.

Order the vegan bulgogi and the mala noodles, and come at lunchtime, as the kitchen closes at 3pm. It is inexpensive and unpretentious, and it fills a useful gap for anyone staying near Dongdaegu Station.
