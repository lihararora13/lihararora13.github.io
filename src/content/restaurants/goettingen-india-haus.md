India-Haus is the old town's Indian restaurant, a mixed kitchen with vegetable curries and vegan-capable choices on the menu. The vegan order needs a word with the staff: ask for an explicitly vegan preparation and confirm the cooking fat, sauces and breads, since ghee and dairy turn up in a mixed Indian kitchen.

It sits on Kurze-Geismar-Straße in the heart of the Innenstadt, open evenings through the week and for lunch on Sundays. Order a vegetable curry with dal and basmati rice, confirm it is vegan when you order, and you have a solid, affordable dinner.
