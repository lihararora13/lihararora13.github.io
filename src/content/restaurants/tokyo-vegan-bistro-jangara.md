Vegan Bistro Jangara takes a beloved Fukuoka ramen name and gives it a fully plant-based Harajuku spin. Upstairs on Jingumae, the menu happily jumps from creamy tonkotsu-style ramen to tacos, burgers and curry, which makes it ideal when everyone in the group wants something different.

The spicy Karabon ramen is the headline, especially with karaage and vegan mayo on the side. It is fun, filling, and right in the middle of Harajuku wandering territory.
