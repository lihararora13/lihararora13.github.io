Chee Is Thai is a Thai restaurant on Rue Hafid Ibrahim near the Twin Centre, serving vegan dishes such as gyoza, tofu with satay sauce, spring rolls, tom yum with tofu, pad Thai and curries. Specify vegan when ordering.

It is open daily from noon to 11:00pm, later on weekends. Order the tofu satay and the pad Thai.
