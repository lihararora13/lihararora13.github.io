Maratonga is a farm food cafe and restaurant at Medinet Habu on the West Bank, a family operation that dates to 1881, with salads, tagines, fresh juices and karkade. It is a meat-serving room, but the vegan end of the menu is real, and it runs cooking classes and delivery.

Order a vegetable tagine with salad and warm Egyptian bread, and a fresh juice. It is open from 6am to 8pm, so it covers breakfast after an early Valley of the Kings start.
