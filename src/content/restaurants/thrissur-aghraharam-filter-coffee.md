Aghraharam Filter Coffee is, by its own fans' description, the tiniest filter coffee cafe in Thrissur, and a September 2026 night visit shows why people love it: a very small, warm room with hand-painted wooden dolls, vintage clocks, framed photographs, and a queue at the counter for filter coffee in metal cups. The sign reads pure vegetarian restaurant.

Order the filter coffee and a South Indian tiffin, and ask for the coffee without milk if you want it plant-based. It is the smallest room on this list, and the most charming.
