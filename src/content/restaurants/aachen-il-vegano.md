Il Vegano is Aachen's fully vegan street-food counter on Adalbertsteinweg, five minutes from Rothe Erde station: essentially a vegan kebab shop, with a green-signed storefront and a menu board of vegan döner, burgers, pizza and falafel. The draw is the döner dürüm, the falafel pizza and the lahmacun, all of it plant-based and most of it under ten euros.

It is open every day from twelve until nine except Mondays, which makes it the most reliable fully vegan lunch in Aachen. Order the döner dürüm first, add fries, and take it away for a walk toward the old town.
