O Vegan is the real deal and the only fully vegan kitchen in Lucknow: a small cloud kitchen in Indira Nagar that cooks plant-based food with coconut and almond milks, no white sugar, and a lot of care. The menu reads like a healthy-eating diary, red rice poha, quinoa bowls, millet upma, ragi laddoos, chia pudding and banana date shakes, and the regulars rate it 4.9 out of 5.

One practical note: this is delivery and pre-order only, with no dine-in seating, so order a couple of hours ahead and eat at home. The chia pudding in coconut milk and the moong dal chilla with gur and imli chutney are the orders to start with, and the kitchen also cooks pure sattvik Jain food on request.
