Verdechiaro is a vegetarian and vegan restaurant in the north of the centre with a 4.9 HappyCow rating across 282 reviews, the highest-rated pick on this list. The kitchen is Sicilian and Italian, with millet meatballs, green ravioli, falafel with rocket cream, vegan sausage with turnip tops and potatoes, and fresh Sicilian durum pasta.

Order the millet meatballs, the falafel with rocket cream and the vegan sausage with turnip tops. It serves lunch and dinner most days, with Sunday hours and a Tuesday evening service.
