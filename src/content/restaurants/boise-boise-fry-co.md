Boise Fry Co is a downtown fry shop on North Capitol Boulevard doing hand-cut fries with a long list of toppings and sauces, several of them vegan. It is the budget pick on this list and the best late-afternoon snack stop between downtown meals.

Order the hand-cut fries with a vegan sauce and ask which loaded toppings are vegan that day. It is counter service, so eat and move on.
