The Harbinger Cafe & Bakery is a cozy downtown cafe with vintage charm, a vegetarian kitchen with a strong vegan bench, and a back patio that makes it a popular remote-work brunch spot. The avocado toast is the morning order, the house-made pastries are the reason to linger, and The Best Friend, a vegan chocolate-chip cookie, is the takeaway.

It is open for breakfast and lunch daily. Come for the toast, stay for the coffee, and take a cookie for the road.
