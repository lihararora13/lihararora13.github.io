Oh Natuurlik Cafe sits on President Steyn Avenue in Westdene, a health-focused cafe with vegan options, smoothies and takeaway. It is a light, daytime room rather than a full restaurant.

Order the vegan bowl and a smoothie. It is a useful lunch stop between the Westdene cafes, and it does takeaway if you are heading out of town.
