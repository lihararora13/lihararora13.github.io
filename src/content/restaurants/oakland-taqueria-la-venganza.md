Taqueria La Venganza is the taqueria that converts sceptics: fully plant-based Mexican street food, with mushroom pastor tacos, vegan asada burritos and carnitas that regulars put up against any meat taqueria in the East Bay. The San Francisco outpost closed in March 2025, so the Oakland shop on Telegraph is where the action is now.

It is casual, cheap, and very good, the kind of place where you order too much and regret nothing. Get the mushroom pastor tacos first, then the asada burrito, and bring a friend who says vegan tacos cannot work.
