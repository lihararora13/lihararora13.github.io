Obrador la Osa is a tiny bakery in Triana that punches far above its weight. Organic empanadas made with stone-ground flour and good olive oil, for about three euros each, plus breads and pastries that sell out early.

Go in the morning, buy several empanadas, and eat them walking along the river. It is the cheapest, most delicious vegan snack in Seville, and the one you will tell people about at home.
