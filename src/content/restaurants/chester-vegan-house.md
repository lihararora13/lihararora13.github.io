Vegan House is the fully vegan stall in Chester Market, cooking plant-based street food in the middle of the city's food hall. It is a quick, cheap lunch and a good reason to visit the market.

Order a burger with loaded fries. The market hall has shared seating, so it suits a casual lunch.
