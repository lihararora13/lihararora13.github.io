Donna Jean is the rare vegan restaurant that made the whole city pay attention. Chef Roy Elam built it in memory of his mother, with a mission to make plant-based food so good nobody thinks about the "plant-based" part, and San Diego's dining scene responded by handing it Best Overall Restaurant and Best Pizza honors. This is not a health food restaurant; this is a pizza-and-pasta celebration that happens to be 100 percent vegan.

Now settled into its new North Park home on El Cajon Boulevard, the menu runs on seasonal, locally sourced ingredients: fennel-sausage tempeh pies, cast iron mac and cheese that HuffPost once praised, Nashville hot mushrooms with a cult following, and a weekend brunch anchored by the famous Breakfast in Bread calzone. Book the patio, order too much pizza, and leave a convert.
