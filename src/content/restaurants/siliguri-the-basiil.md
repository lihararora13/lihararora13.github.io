The Basiil is the pure-veg restaurant on the first floor of City Centre mall, above Domino's, and it is the mall crowd's veg default: thalis, stir-fried noodles, red-sauce pasta and chocolate mousse, served in a wooden-panelled dining room with booth seating. Its August 2026 posts show a busy, well-loved room.

Order the veg thali or the stir-fried noodles, ask for no cheese and no paneer, and you have a mall lunch that works for vegans. It is the most modern room on this list.
