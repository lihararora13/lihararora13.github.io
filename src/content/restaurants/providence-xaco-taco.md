Xaco Taco is downtown Providence's quick taco fix, and vegans are well catered for. The menu builds tacos, burritos and bowls around plant-based fillings, with fresh guacamole and salsas made to order.

It is fast, cheap and cheerful, open late enough for a post-show bite. Order a couple of vegan tacos and a burrito bowl and you will leave happy.
