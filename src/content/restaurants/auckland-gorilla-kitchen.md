Gorilla Kitchen on Karangahape Road swings happily between virtuous and naughty: sprouted, fermented wholefoods on one side, and fried vegan chicken, loaded burgers and pancakes on the other. Everything is free from dairy, eggs, honey and animal products, and the room has that relaxed K'Road energy.

Start with cauliflower wings, commit to the Deluxe Royale or the very Kiwi Phush N Chups, and save room if pancakes are calling. It is closed on Mondays, and the kitchen stays open late later in the week.
