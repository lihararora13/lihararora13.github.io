Kafe MeeTup by Dr Su is the newest pick in this guide and the only one in the old town: a fully vegetarian Malaysian cafe on Jalan Tan Hiok Nee, the heritage street a short walk from the CIQ border crossing, opened in October 2026 as a 100% plant-based space for food, conversation and learning. Nothing on the menu contains meat, poultry or seafood, and anything that looks like meat, fish or egg is plant-based. The cafe publishes a named list of vegan dishes, so ordering is easy: kampung rendang poke tup, golden curry mee, laksa spaghetti, nasi lemak rendang, mee rebus Jawa, rendang biryani and tomato fettuccine.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, because some dishes use dairy, so order from the vegan list. It is the most convenient pick in this guide for anyone arriving from Singapore on foot.
