The Red Fern is Rochester's vegan destination and has been since 2013: a 100% vegan, plant-based restaurant in a converted house on Oxford Street, with a basement dining room, a full bakery case, fresh juices and smoothies, and a menu of American classics rebuilt without animal products, nachos, mac and cheese, quesadillas, Caesar salad and a steak bomb sandwich. The Compost Plate is the must-order, a healthy version of Rochester's notorious Garbage Plate with seitan, mac salad and sweet potato salad, and the house-baked desserts, lemon ginger donuts and chocolate mint Oreo cake, are a second reason to go.

The kitchen is unusually allergy-aware, with gluten-free, nut-free, soy-free, onion-free, garlic-free and oil-free menus available on request. It is open Tuesday to Thursday 11am to 9pm and Friday to Saturday 11am to 9:30pm, closed Sunday and Monday, and reservations are always recommended: the dining room is small and requires navigating six steps down. Free parking is in the back lot until 5pm on business days.
