Plant A Pizza is Belgium's first 100% plant-based pizzeria, on Geldmunt, a minute from the Gravensteen, and rated 5.0 on HappyCow. The pizzas are the whole story: a classic margherita with cashew mozzarella, pesto with arugula and cherry tomatoes, and seasonal specials, all of it fully vegan.

It is open evenings only, from late afternoon, so plan it as a dinner. Order the margherita first, then a seasonal pizza to share.
