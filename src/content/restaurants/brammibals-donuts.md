Why we love it — Brammibal's has been making handmade vegan donuts
since 2015, long before the trend caught up, and the flavors are still
the ones everyone talks about. The Bienenstich is the signature: a
fluffy ring filled with homemade vanilla custard, dunked in vanilla
glaze, and crowned with roasted slivered almonds. The Boston cream and
pistachio hold their own, and the seasonal specials rotate through all
year.

With several locations across the city (including one at the
Hauptbahnhof for traveling snack emergencies) and genuinely good
specialty coffee, this is the happy ending every Berlin food day
deserves. Nobody leaves unhappy.
