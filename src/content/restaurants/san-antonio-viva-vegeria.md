Viva Vegeria is the Southside's dedicated gluten-free vegan restaurant: a family-owned Tex-Mex kitchen doing tacos, enchiladas and Tex-Mex plates from scratch, all of it vegan and all of it gluten-free. Regulars call it their favourite restaurant in San Antonio, and the food is consistently great.

It is the pick for the gluten-free vegan who is tired of asking questions. Order the Tex-Mex plate, add tacos, and enjoy the rare kitchen where everything is safe and everything is good.
