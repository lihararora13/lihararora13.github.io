Angelhearts Donuts, also listed as Angelhearts Diner, is an all-vegan donut shop on North Aurora Street in downtown Ithaca, doing raised, cake, and filled donuts with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the strongest pick in Ithaca, and the place to buy a box for the road.

Order a raised donut and a filled donut. The pin for this entry is address-level, so use the street address when navigating.
