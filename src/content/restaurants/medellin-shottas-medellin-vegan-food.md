Shottas is the second Laureles location of a proudly Colombian vegan kitchen, and it holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow from 29 reviews. The brief is simple and ambitious: traditional Colombian gastronomy made with 100% plants, from every region of the country, served in a casual purple-walled spot with outdoor seating. Think empanadas, stuffed potatoes, typical rice, chicken-imitation paste and a pile of fries, alongside crispy tacos and a mushroom burger that one visiting vlogger rated "80 out of 10".

Come hungry and order across the menu; the fries with the house white sauce are the sleeper hit. It is open for lunch and dinner Tuesday through Saturday, and Sundays until early evening.
