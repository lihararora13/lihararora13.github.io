Bio Bistro Spicy Spices is a vegetarian bio restaurant on Wolf-Dietrich-Strasse, serving Indian-inspired curries, chickpeas, samosas, salads and drinks. It is the Indian pick on this list.

Order a curry, the chickpeas and the samosas, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan dishes, since the menu also runs dairy.
