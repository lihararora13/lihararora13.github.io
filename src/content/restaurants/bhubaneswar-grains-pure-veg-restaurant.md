Grains is the closest thing Bhubaneswar has to a vegan restaurant: a big, stylish pure-veg dining room on Cuttack Road with a chalkboard wall that literally says VEGAN, and a vegan meals section that local food bloggers keep photographing. The room is all teal booths, hanging greenery and a vintage Ambassador car front as decor, and the menu is proudly all-vegetarian with plenty that is plant-based as served.

Order from the vegan side of the menu and ask the kitchen to skip the dairy: the kulhad chaat, Yangzhou fried rice and Chinese fondue are the dishes regulars keep coming back for, and the buffet counter makes it an easy family stop. It is open late and busy on weekends, so come early if you want a booth.
