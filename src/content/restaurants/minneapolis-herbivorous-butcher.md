The Herbivorous Butcher put Minneapolis on the vegan map, and one bite of their deli case will tell you why. Brother-and-sister team Aubry and Kale Walch have spent years perfecting meat-free meats that fool even lifelong carnivores, all out of a sunny Northeast storefront that feels like a magic trick you can eat.

Go for the stacked sandwiches and stay for the retail case, where you can stock your own fridge with their cheeses and cold cuts. This is the souvenir shop for vegans.
