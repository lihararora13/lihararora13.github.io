Hashem has been serving falafel in downtown Amman since 1952, and it is entirely vegetarian. The menu is tiny, falafel, hummus, foul, french fries and pita, eaten at shared tables that spill into the alley, often standing. It is not a vegetarian restaurant in the modern sense; it has just always been meat-free, which is part of why it works.

Go at any hour, order falafel and foul, and eat with the crowd. This is the most Amman meal on the list.
