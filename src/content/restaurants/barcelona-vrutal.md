Why we love it — Vrutal is where Poblenou goes to party: a
high-energy vegan burger bar and coctelería where the comfort food is
as loud as the crowd. The cauliflower wings and chilli cheese fries
have a genuine cult following, and the craft beers and cocktails keep
the tables buzzing long after the beach crowd rolls in. One hundred
percent vegan, zero percent boring.

Order the BBQ jackfruit burger, a mountain of loaded fries, and
something fizzy from the bar menu. This is vegan food as a night out,
not a sacrifice.
