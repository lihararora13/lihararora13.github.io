Pinchito is a Spanish vegetarian and vegan food stall on Market Hill, right in the city centre, serving churros, bravas potatoes and signature sandwiches. It is small, cheap and cheerful, with a five-star rating from reviewers and a queue at lunchtime.

Order the patatas bravas and a Spanish vegan sandwich, and save room for the churros with chocolate. It is a vegetarian stall with a strong vegan bench, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note it is open Fridays to Sundays.
