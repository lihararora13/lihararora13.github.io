Kind Greens is the headline of this guide: Dehradun's first all-vegan cafe, opened in 2026 at 281/174 Rajpur Road inside Always Fresh n Easy. The room is bright and plant-filled, the espresso machine is a proper La Carimali, and the menu boards list matcha and ube, smoothie and parfait, and blue pea tea.

Everything is 100% plant-based, which makes it the easiest table in the city for a vegan eater. Order the matcha latte and a smoothie bowl, and bring your own cup if you like, the cafe is thinking about a discount for it.
