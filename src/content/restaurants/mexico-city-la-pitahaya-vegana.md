Yes, the tortillas are pink, dyed with beet juice, and yes, you will photograph them. But La Pitahaya backs up the looks with real cooking: local, organic ingredients, everything made from scratch, and flavours reviewers keep calling revelations rather than substitutes.

Order a trio of tacos to taste your way across the menu, save room for dessert, and do not skip the seasonal specials. Pretty and delicious is a fine combination.
