Galley and Garden is a Southern restaurant on Highland Avenue with a menu built around seasonal produce and vegetable plates. Vegans can build a full dinner from the vegetable sides and the kitchen's adaptable mains, and the room is the most traditional on this list.

Order the vegetable plate with collard greens and seasonal vegetables, and ask which preparations are vegan, since some sides are finished with butter.
