Desi Thaat Veg earned its place the honest way: locals kept pointing out that the city's best-of lists were incomplete without it. It is a straightforward pure-veg restaurant, no café gloss, just hearty desi thalis, dal and seasonal curries served the way Raipur likes them.

Order the veg thali without ghee, add a seasonal curry and roti, and you have a filling, fair-priced meal that tastes like the city itself. A good reminder that the best lists are written by the people who eat there.
