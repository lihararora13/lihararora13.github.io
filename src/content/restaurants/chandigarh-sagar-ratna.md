Sagar Ratna is the first name on every Chandigarh vegetarian list, and for good reason: a family-friendly, pure-vegetarian restaurant doing South Indian, North Indian and Chinese food with real consistency. The Sector 17 branch is the one to head for.

The Mysore masala dosa is the signature, and the idli sambar and vegetable uttapam are the vegan-friendly orders. Ask for no ghee and the kitchen will happily oblige.
