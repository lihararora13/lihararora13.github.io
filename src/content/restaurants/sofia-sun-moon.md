Sun Moon is the vegetarian restaurant and bakery on 6th September Street with a separate vegan menu, and it is where to book when vegetarian friends want a sit-down dinner. The bakery makes salty vegan banitsa, dips like hummus and pesto, wholegrain and gluten-free sourdough breads, cookies and cakes.

It is open daily from 9am to 10pm. Ask for the vegan menu when you sit down, since the bakery is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, and the vegan dishes are the ones to order.
