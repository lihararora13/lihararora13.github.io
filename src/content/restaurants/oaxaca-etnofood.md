Etnofood is the Oaxacan vegan restaurant on this list, a kitchen that cooks the region's food plant-based: tostadas, avocado salad, mezcal and chocolate, all organic, all rooted in Oaxaca's own ingredients and traditions.

Order a tostada and an avocado salad, and try the chocolate. It is the most Oaxacan meal on this list, and the one that shows what vegan food can be here.
