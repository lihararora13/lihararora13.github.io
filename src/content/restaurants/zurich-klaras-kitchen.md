Klara's Kitchen is Zurich's organic takeaway, a bright room near the university serving smoothies, curries, tofu dishes, pancakes and cakes, with vegan options across the menu. It is a five-star favourite with the student crowd, and the food is fresh, colourful and honest.

It is open weekdays for lunch. Order a tofu curry and a smoothie, add a slice of vegan cake, and you have a proper Zurich lunch.
