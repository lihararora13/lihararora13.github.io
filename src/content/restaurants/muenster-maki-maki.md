Maki Maki sushi green is Münster's fully vegan sushi restaurant on Hammer Straße, a small room doing what most sushi bars will not: an entire menu without fish. The sets and maki rolls are built from vegetables, tofu and seasoned rice, with miso soup on the side, and the kitchen takes the craft seriously rather than treating vegan sushi as a novelty.

It is an evening place, open Tuesday to Friday from five until ten and Saturday and Sunday from three, with Mondays closed. Order a sushi set first, add a miso soup, and book ahead: the room is small and the word is out.
