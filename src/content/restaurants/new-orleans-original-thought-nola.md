Original Thought NOLA started as an herbal medicine and ancient foods stand in the 7th Ward and grew into a Gentilly fixture locals swear by. The all-vegan kitchen, tucked behind Earn Dat Body's Gym, serves comfort food with a wellness bent: smoky BBQ jackfruit burgers, loaded nachos, vegan gumbo, and the legendary Better Than Popeyes sandwich.

This is a true original, a Black-owned business that Eater's vegan map has followed from food truck to storefront. The sea moss smoothies and herbal teas are part of the ethos, and the ratings are sky-high. It is a little off the tourist path, which makes it taste even better.
