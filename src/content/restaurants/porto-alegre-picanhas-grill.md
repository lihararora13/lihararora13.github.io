Picanha's Grill is a 5.0-rated vegan restaurant in the Centro Historico offering a vegan buffet, with vegan alaminuta during the day and vegan xis burgers at night. It has served no meat since June 2020, and it relocated from Avenida Plinio Kroeff to Rua General Lima e Silva, so use the address on this page.

Order a buffet plate at lunch, or come back at night for a xis burger. It is open Monday to Saturday for lunch and dinner.
