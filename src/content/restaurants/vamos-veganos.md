Why we love it — Doner is to Berlin what the bagel is to New York, and
Vamos Veganos serves the city's finest vegan version. The Fiöner is
expertly seasoned smoked tofu shaved like the real thing, loaded into
crispy Turkish flatbread with classic garlic, herb, and spicy sauces,
and the almond feta add-on is the pro move. Reviewers call it the best
vegan doner of their lives, and we are not arguing.

The cozy deli sits on leafy Arminplatz with outdoor seating for sunny
days, and they also do a cracking Neapolitan pizza and tangy vegan
froyo in summer. Small, friendly, and fully plant-based: this is Berlin
street food at its peak.
