NI-Vegan is Lübeck's fully vegan sushi kitchen, and it is the best first stop in the city. The sushi is the dish to order: properly made, properly seasoned, and entirely plant-based. Seitan duck noodle bowls and gyoza fill out a menu that is entirely vegan, in a small, modern room on the Mühlenstraße in the old town.

It sits a short walk from the Markt, open most days from late morning. Order the sushi, add the seitan duck noodle bowl, and save room for gyoza. This is the table that shows Lübeck's vegan scene at its best, and the genuine all-vegan anchor of the list.
