Simone's Kitchen is Antwerp's oldest plant-based home-restaurant, a non-profit serving a moderately priced four-course set menu that changes every three weeks, with seasonal organic ingredients, plus a weekend buffet brunch in the dining room or on the garden terrace. Reviewers praise the kind staff and fresh, tasty food, calling the place cosy and warm.

Reservations are preferred, and the menu is a surprise, so book ahead and come hungry. This is the most personal meal on this list, and the set menu is the order.
