Chowpaty is one of the oldest and most prestigious pure vegetarian restaurants in Kenya, and the Westlands branch is its most polished. The menu is South Indian at heart: masala dosa, chaat, curries and fresh juices, served in a comfortable dining room that suits families and first-timers alike. Vegan options are not always labelled, so ask and the kitchen will point you right.

The masala dosa is the order, with dahi sev chaat on the side if you like tang and crunch. It is open late, takes cards, and has been doing this since long before vegan was a trend.
