O Macrobiotico is Porto's macrobiotic veteran, a calm vegetarian restaurant in Bonfim cooking wholesome, seasonal plates with plenty of vegan options. It is the healthiest dinner on this list, and the one your body will thank you for after a week of francesinhas.

Order the macrobiotic plate and a soup. It is quiet, nourishing food, and a lovely counterpoint to Porto's louder vegan scene.
