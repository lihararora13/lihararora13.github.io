La Vegan Taquería is the newer fully vegan taquería in Centro, and it is already one of the highest-rated taco spots in town, with steady reviews through early 2026. Tacos run roughly 40 to 45 pesos each, and the pastor, chorizo and barbacoa are the ones to order.

It is a different place from El Bajón, whose Facebook page is called "vegan.taqueria": different address, different phone, different page. Listed hours are about 2pm to 10pm daily.
