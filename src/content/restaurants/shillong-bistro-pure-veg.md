Bistro Pure Veg calls itself the one and only pure-veg fine-dining restaurant in Shillong, and its early reviewers give it a full 5.0. It sits on Thana Road in Police Bazar, a budget pick at 400 to 600 rupees for two, serving kofta, paratha and paneer in a proper dining room.

Order the veg kofta or a paratha with dal, ask for no paneer and no ghee, and you have a fine-dining veg meal at street prices. It is open noon to 10:30 daily.
