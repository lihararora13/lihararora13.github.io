Toorji's Stepwell Café sits on the same stepwell edge as its famous neighbour, and it offers the same view with a slightly quieter room. It is the second stepwell café on this list, and the one to choose when the first is full.

The menu runs café-style, with pasta, sandwiches and coffee, and the vegan-friendly dishes are easy to find. It shares the stepwell pin with Stepwell Cafe, so follow the stepwell itself rather than the map dot.
