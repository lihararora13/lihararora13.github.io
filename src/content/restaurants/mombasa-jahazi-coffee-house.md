Jahazi Coffee House is a restaurant and cafe in the Old Town on Ndia Kuu Road, with Swahili vegan choices that may include sweet potato in coconut sauce, beans in coconut sauce, bhajia with coconut chutney and mahamri. It is open daily 9am to 5pm.

Order the sweet potato in coconut sauce and the beans in coconut sauce. It is the Old Town pick on this list, and the Swahili cooking is the draw.
