The Holy Tree is Ludhiana's most plant-forward table: a vegetarian cafe and organic store on Guru Gian Vihar Main Road in Jawaddi, with a calm, poetic vibe that leans into plant-based food, mindfulness and superfoods. It is open 10:30 AM to 11 PM, seven days a week.

The plant-based bowls and vegan sandwiches are the orders, and the organic store downstairs is worth a browse. It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so confirm the dairy-free version when you order.
