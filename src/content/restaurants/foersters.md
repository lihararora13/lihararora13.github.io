Why we love it — It would be a shame to visit Germany and skip German
food, and Försters makes sure vegans never have to. This cozy,
cabin-like spot tackles the great challenge of veganizing the national
cuisine: schnitzel pounded thin and fried golden, meaty roulade with red
wine sauce, king oyster mushrooms with potato gratin. Each dish comes
with a wine recommendation, and there is live piano in the evening.

Established in 2019, it uses organic, regional ingredients and keeps the
atmosphere warm and family-style. Monday is schnitzel day, which tells
you everything about how seriously they take their mission. German
comfort food with heart and soul, minus the animals.
