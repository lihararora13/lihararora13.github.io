Sanskruti sits at the top of HappyCow's Liverpool list with a perfect 5.0 from more than a hundred reviewers, and it earns it. This award-winning vegetarian Indian restaurant cooks dishes from all across India on a completely meat-free menu, and the vegan options are the headline rather than the afterthought: thali, dosa, North Indian curries and chaat snacks.

It is a proper sit-down restaurant in the Commercial District, a short walk from the waterfront, and it does delivery across the city too. Order a thali to taste your way across the menu, and add a dosa if you are still hungry.
