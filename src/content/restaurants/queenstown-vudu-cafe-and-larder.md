Vudu Cafe and Larder is an organic cafe and larder on Earl Street in Queenstown, serving vegan cabinet food and coffee. It is a bright, modern cafe in the centre, and it suits a breakfast or a light lunch.

Order the vegan cabinet food and a coffee, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is central and reliable.
