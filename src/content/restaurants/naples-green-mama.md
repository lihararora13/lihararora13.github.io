Green M'ama is the table to start with in Naples, and the HappyCow community agrees: it tops the city's 2026 list with a perfect 5.0. Everything in the kitchen is plant-based, from the Mama Bun, a bean-and-oat patty with homemade carrot bacon, plant-based provolone, sun-dried tomatoes and garlic yoghurt sauce, to the bao buns, the vegan cotoletta and the tiramisu.

It sits on Via San Giacomo, a short walk from Via Toledo, with a dining area inside, so a quick meal need not mean eating on the move. It is closed on Mondays, open for lunch and dinner the rest of the week, with continuous service on Saturdays.
