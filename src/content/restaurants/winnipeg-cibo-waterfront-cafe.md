Cibo sits on Waterfront Drive in the Exchange District, a Mediterranean fusion restaurant with a vegan section that is clearly labelled on the menu. The vegan pasta and pizza are the mains to order, with bruschetta and salads to start, and the room looks out toward the river.

It is open daily for lunch and dinner, and it works well as the easy, central, sit-down vegan-friendly meal in Winnipeg, especially on a summer evening when the patio is open.
