Mosh Beach is a vegetarian beach restaurant and club on Derekh Mitsrayim, the north beach road, serving Indian, Mediterranean and Middle Eastern food with vegan-friendly dishes. It is open and trading, and it is the most vegan-friendly room on the beach.

Order a vegan curry and a hummus plate, and eat with your feet near the sand. It is a beach club as much as a restaurant, so come for a long lunch.
