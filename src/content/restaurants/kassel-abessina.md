Abessina brings Ethiopian and Eritrean cooking to central Kassel, a mixed restaurant with a solid vegan side: vegetable stews, lentils and greens served with injera, the spongy sourdough flatbread that doubles as plate and cutlery. It is open every day from noon until half past eleven, which makes it the most reliable late dinner on this list.

It sits on Kurt-Schumacher-Straße, a short walk from the Königsplatz. Order the vegan sharing platter if there are two of you, and ask the staff to point out the vegan stews: the menu is mixed, so ordering by name is the way to go.
