Sen Vang is a cheap vegetarian noodle kitchen in central Nha Trang, about $5 for two mains and two drinks, open from 6am to 8pm. It is a morning kitchen, and it suits an early breakfast before the beach or a day trip.

Order the vegetarian noodles and a tofu dish, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. Its pin is approximate to the city centre, so check the address before you go.
