L'Erbavoglio is a strictly vegan restaurant in the historic centre, cooking genuine simple food from organic kilometre-zero ingredients, with its own bread and pasta made in-house. The menu runs on vegan recipes across antipasti, primi, secondi and dolci, and a first-hand visit praised the summer ravioli, the gnocchi with seitan ragu, the meatballs in pink sauce and the seitan al cartoccio.

Order the gnocchi al ragu di seitan, the summer ravioli, the seitan al cartoccio with potatoes and mushrooms, and the tiramisu or the strawberry cream cake. It opens for dinner Tuesday to Saturday and for Sunday lunch, and it is closed Mondays.
