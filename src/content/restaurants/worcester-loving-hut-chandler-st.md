Loving Hut on Chandler Street is an all-vegan restaurant doing Asian-inspired plates, curries, and noodle dishes with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is one of two Loving Hut locations in Worcester, and the Chandler Street room is the larger of the two.

Order the vegan curry and a noodle dish. Use the Chandler Street address, 415 Chandler, not the Hamilton Street location.
