Claire's Corner Copia is a vegetarian restaurant on Chapel Street that has served organic, kosher-certified food since 1975, sourcing sustainable ingredients and paying fair wages for decades. The menu rotates, with the Kindness Dinner served from 4 to 6pm every evening, and vegan dishes sit alongside vegetarian ones.

Order the Kindness Dinner and the vegan huevos rancheros, and save room for the Lithuanian coffee cake. Tell the server you are vegan up front, since the kitchen uses eggs and dairy in some dishes.
