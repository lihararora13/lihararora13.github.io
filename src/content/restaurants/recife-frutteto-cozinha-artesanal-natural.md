Frutteto Cozinha Artesanal Natural is a vegan-friendly brazilian spot in Graças. Frutteto cooks artisanal, natural food with a clear dairy-free thread running through the menu, and it is one of the prettiest rooms on this list.

Order the Burrata frita, Parmigiana di melanzane, and Falafel salad. The burrata frita and the parmigiana are the two plates regulars name first.
