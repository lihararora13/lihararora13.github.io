Bahir Zaf is a social business in Haya Hulet that trains marginalised youth, with a menu that spans Ethiopian and international cooking. Vegan choices include a spicy chickpea hot-pot soup, green bean and leek curry with potato and kale dumplings, lentil and mushroom samosas, potato wedges and a fasting platter, plus fresh juices.

It is open Tuesday to Sunday from 11:00am to 10:30pm. Order the fasting platter and the samosas, and note the pin is at neighbourhood level for Haya Hulet.
