Damaskus Express is Potsdam's famous falafel imbiss, a tiny Babelsberg shop serving just falafel and halloumi in a pita plus some sweets and tea, and it has a devoted local following. The falafel is small, homemade and very good, the hummus-tahina sauce with a chilli option is the thing regulars rave about, and the standard falafel option is vegan, with cheese offered as an extra. The owners are famously friendly, and the whole meal costs a few euros.

It opens Monday to Friday, half past eleven to eight, and it is cash only. Note the halloumi is dairy, so order the falafel plain vegan. This is the cheap, cheerful Babelsberg pick, and the one locals send visitors to first.
