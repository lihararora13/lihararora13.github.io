MuYe, 木葉粗食, is a farm-to-table cafe in an 80-year-old house on Gunan Street in the old Hamasen quarter of Gushan, and it is the most principled kitchen in this guide: founded on Earth Day 2015, it cooks whole-food plant-based food with no additives, sources organic ingredients from its own farm and other local farmers, and puts "let us rethink eating" on the wall. The seasonal menu runs western-fusion, with a signature red sauce pulled king oyster mushroom burger, a salad bowl that reads like a small garden, sweet-sour dry wide noodles with close to ten vegetables and nuts, teriyaki maple tempeh skewers, soups, sandwiches and pita, plus dessert chocolate pie, brownies and ice-cream, and single-origin coffee with macadamia or almond milk. Gluten-free and Buddhist items are labelled.

The cafe reopened here in May 2023 after a move from Linquan Street, and it has survived a pandemic closure and a typhoon since. Hours are roughly Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 11:30am to 2:30pm and 5:00pm to 7:30pm, weekends 11:30am to 7:30pm, closed Wednesdays, but the hours are flexible, so call ahead. There is a minimum spend and no service charge, and cards are accepted.
