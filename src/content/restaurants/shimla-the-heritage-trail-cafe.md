The Heritage Trail Cafe is a museum-inspired heritage cafe on Mall Road, in the Jain Ashram building near the lift: vegetarian and eggless, with coffee, food, desserts and heritage hi-tea in a room full of Shimla history. It is open 10 AM to 9:30 PM.

The eggless banana chocolate pancakes are the signature, and the Vietnamese coffee is the companion. Ask for the vegan version of the pancakes and the kitchen will keep the dairy out.
