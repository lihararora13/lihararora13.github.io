Cafe Pasqual's is an institution: a small, art-covered adobe one block off the Plaza, serving organic, locally sourced New Mexican and global food since 1980. It is not a vegan restaurant, but the kitchen is famously accommodating, the menu is marked vegan-friendly, and several dishes veganise cleanly, including the huevos rancheros and the Oaxacan tamales.

It is also the priciest room on this list and the hardest to walk into without a wait, so book ahead, especially for dinner. Go for the mole, the green chile, and the room itself.
