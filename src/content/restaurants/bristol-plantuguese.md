Plantuguese is the most original restaurant on this list: a fully plant-based take on classic Portuguese dishes, right in the heart of Stokes Croft. The menu reimagines the canon without a single animal product, from the layered Francesinha sandwich with vegan meats and rich tomato-beer sauce to Bife a Casa, marinated plant-based steak with mushrooms and peppercorn sauce, and Bacalhau a Bras, shredded vegan cod-style protein with potatoes and onion.

It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, and it earns it dish by dish. Order the Francesinha if you only try one thing, and go hungry, because the portions are generous and the small plates are hard to stop eating.
