SZY is a vegan restaurant on KG 5 Avenue in Kacyiru, rated 4.5, offering an upscale menu with brunch dishes like mushroom waffles and burritos, plus lunch and dinner options including soups, salads, burgers, pizzas, pastas and loaded fries. It has outdoor seating, is wheelchair accessible and takes cards.

Order the mushroom waffles for brunch and a vegan burger for dinner. It is the only fully vegan restaurant in Kigali, and it is the reason this list leads with it.
