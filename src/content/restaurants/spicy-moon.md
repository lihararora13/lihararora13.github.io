Spicy Moon is proof that vegan food does not need to apologize for flavor.
The Szechuan menu hits all the classics: dan dan noodles that fans call
heavenly, wontons swimming in chili oil, kung pao tofu with real crunch,
and a mapo tofu that brings the tingle. There is even a crabless rangoon
for the skeptics.

The room is small and moody with a cocktail program built to cool the burn,
which makes it a natural date-night pick or a group feast where everything
lands in the middle. Spice lovers, this is your church.
