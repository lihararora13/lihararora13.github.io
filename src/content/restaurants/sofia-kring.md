Kring is the health-food restaurant on Tsar Simeon Street, with vegan dishes and cakes alongside its vegetarian menu. Dishes that have dairy are labelled, so the vegan choices are easy to spot, and the owner has reported not using egg since 2014.

It is open Monday to Saturday from 11:30am to 7pm. Come for lunch and a cake, and check the daily board for the vegan main.
