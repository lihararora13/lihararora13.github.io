Besharam is our one vegan-friendly exception, and it earns the spot on
ambition alone. Chef Heena Patel's Dogpatch restaurant is a Michelin-starred
Gujarati kitchen inside the Minnesota Street Project gallery space,
plating drunken pani puri (yes, with gin in the pani), khandvi, and pav
bhaji puffs with serious craft. The whole menu is vegetarian, and the
kitchen is famous for accommodating vegan diners, with plenty of dishes
that go fully plant-based.

It is the priciest table on this list and worth dressing up for. Tell the
staff you are vegan when you sit down, order the tasting menu if you want
the guided tour, and save room for the shreekhand cheesecake.
