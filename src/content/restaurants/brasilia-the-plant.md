The Plant is a bright natural cafe in Asa Sul, but its all-vegan status is disputed: one guide calls it fully vegan while another lists meat, chicken, pork and fish on the menu and its own offerings include a chicken wrap, so it is filed vegan-friendly.

Order the Açaí, Kombucha, and Torta de mousse de chocolate. The açaí and the chocolate mousse torta are the vegan-safe picks; check the savoury menu item by item.
