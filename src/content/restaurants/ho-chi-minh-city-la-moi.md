La Moi is the District 1 plant-based restaurant that locals keep busy and visitors keep recommending, a bright room full of Asian dishes cooked entirely from plants. The signature rainbow roll, the LAMOI Plant-Based Rice (a vegan take on Hoi An chicken rice), mushroom pho and stir-fried rice are the dishes that come up again and again in reviews, alongside service that travellers rate well beyond full marks.

It is a touch pricier than the quan chay canteens, and worth it for a first night in the city when you want something reliable, fresh and central. Order the rainbow roll to start and the plant-based rice as your main, and let the staff talk you through the menu.
