Root Vegan might be the hardest working small kitchen in Central: one modest first-floor dining room, and a menu that cheerfully tours the world, Thai curry here, Taiwanese noodles there, a burger with real ambition in between. Reviewers keep using the word inventive, and the 4.6-star rating backs them up.

The entrance is easy to miss, which only makes finding it sweeter. Order the khao soi or the tom yum orzo, ask about allium-free versions if you need them, and absolutely leave room for cake: the rotating bakes, double chocolate especially, have their own fan club.
