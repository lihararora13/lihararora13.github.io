Qiu Dou Ru La Mien, 萩豆乳拉麵, is the ramen bar to start with in Kaohsiung, and it is the most single-minded bowl in this guide: the owner trained at the Kyoto soy milk ramen shop Tozen, came home, and has been building broths from soy milk, mushrooms, kelp and brewed soy sauce ever since, with no animal products, no egg, no milk and no alliums anywhere in the kitchen. The five flavours all start from the same soy milk base, with miso, sake lees, kimchi and spicy dan dan variations, and the broth comes in a light or a thick version for twenty NT more. The original soy milk ramen, with bean curd sheets, corn and wakame, is the one to order first, and the spicy soy milk dan dan noodles are the one to order second.

The shop sits on Songjiang Street in Sanmin, a five-minute walk from the Houyi MRT station and near the Kaohsiung Medical University quarter, in a quiet residential street with an Edo-style interior: a four-seat counter downstairs and a roomier second floor. Bowls run about NT$120 to NT$180, with set meals adding rice and small dishes, and a cup of hot burdock tea comes with the meal. Cash only, and no takeaway. Closed on Tuesdays.
