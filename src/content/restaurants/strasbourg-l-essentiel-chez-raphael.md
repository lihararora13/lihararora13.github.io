L'Essentiel chez Raphaël is an organic, vegan-friendly buffet out in Cronenbourg, and it is a lunch-only weekday stop: a buffet lunch and a pause café, cooked from organic produce. It is the furthest out on this page, so plan it as a deliberate lunch rather than a passing stop.

Check the opening days before you go, as it is weekday lunch only. The buffet is the order, and the organic credentials are the draw.
