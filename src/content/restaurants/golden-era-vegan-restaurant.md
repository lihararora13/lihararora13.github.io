Golden Era has been quietly dishing up pure-veg Asian cuisine in downtown
San Francisco for over 15 years, and it has the review count to prove the
city noticed. This is the workhorse spot: fast, affordable, and beloved
for lemongrass soy protein, crispy pot stickers, spring rolls with peanut
sauce, and a banh xeo that one reviewer called nostalgic in the best way.
It holds a Travellers' Choice award and a HappyCow Top Rated badge, which
is not a combination you fake.

The menu is huge, the service is lightning fast, and the prices are
gentle. Come hungry, order the lemongrass deluxe and a pile of rolls, and
take dessert from the vegan sweets case on your way out.
