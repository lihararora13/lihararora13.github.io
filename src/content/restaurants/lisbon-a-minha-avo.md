A Minha Avo means my grandmother, and that is exactly the cooking on offer, just fully vegan: traditional Portuguese dishes made with love near Parque Eduardo VII. HappyCow lists duckless rice, bitoque with a strikingly real-looking vegan egg, francesinha, creamy codfish and meat-style croquettes, and VegNews closes its Lisbon list with the promise that you will not go wrong with anything you order.

VeganHaven recommends it as the top-rated replacement for a much-missed neighbour that closed in 2025, and the set lunch, soup plus the daily main, is a local bargain. Booking is advised, grandma is popular.
