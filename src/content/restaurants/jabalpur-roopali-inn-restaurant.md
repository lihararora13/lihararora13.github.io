Roopali Inn has been serving Jabalpur since 1989, and the dining room carries that history lightly: a 4.6-star veg restaurant in South Civil Lines, known for thalis, sizzlers, aromatic rice and family feasts. It is a hotel restaurant in the old sense, steady, generous and unhurried.

Order the veg thali without ghee, add an aromatic rice and a curry without butter, and you have a classic, comfortable end to the Jabalpur list. A fitting bookend to Navnita, the city's other great old veg room.
