Addis in Dar is an Ethiopian restaurant on Ursino Street with a vegan and vegetarian section on the menu, including misir wot, slow-cooked split red lentils with berbere, kek alicha wot, split yellow chickpeas, and shimbera asa, house-made chickpea cakes simmered in berbere sauce.

Order the misir wot and the shimbera asa with injera. It is the Ethiopian anchor on this list, and the vegan section is clearly marked.
