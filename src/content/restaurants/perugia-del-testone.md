Del Testone is an Umbrian institution where the vegan move is the torta al testo, the flatbread of the house, customised with seasonal vegetables. The meat-first menu means the first course of the day is a surprise and may contain meat, so vegans should order the torta and the vegetable sides rather than gamble on the primo.

Order a torta al testo veg with seasonal vegetables and the grilled vegetable sides. It is open daily from eleven to midnight and a five-euro torta with a drink is the cheapest meal on this list.
