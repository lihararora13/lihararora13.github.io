Kahvila Rakastan is an absolute must for visitors, a vegan cafe in the heart of Helsinki that serves vegan versions of a traditional Finnish delicacy: the Karelian pie, filled with potato or barley. It is the most Finnish stop on this list, and the one that turns a coffee break into a proper taste of the country.

It sits on Mannerheimintie, the city's main street, so it is easy to fold into a day of sightseeing. Order a Karelian pie, add a cinnamon bun, and take an oat latte to go.
