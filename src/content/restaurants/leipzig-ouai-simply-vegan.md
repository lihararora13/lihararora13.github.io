OUAI, Simply Vegan, is a fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant on Münzgasse in Zentrum-Süd, and the menu names are half the fun. The tofu in pyjamas is the signature, with rolling tofu, dumplings and a red ninja noodle soup alongside, all of it plant-based.

It is a small room, so book ahead at weekends. Order the tofu in pyjamas and the red ninja noodle soup.
