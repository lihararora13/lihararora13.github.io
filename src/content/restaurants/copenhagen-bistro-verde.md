Bistro Verde is the crowd-pleaser in the heart of town, just steps from Nyhavn: a fully vegan bistro that gleefully veganises the classics, from creamy carbonara and a Juicy Marbles steak to open sandwiches and proper Danish cream puffs for dessert. VegNews and HappyCow both rank it near the top of Copenhagen, with a 4.5 rating and a menu that changes quarterly.

Come for lunch or dinner any day of the week, start with the chickpea pancake or a smorrebrod, and save room for something sweet. Current listings show it open daily, bright, welcoming and perfect between sightseeing stops.
