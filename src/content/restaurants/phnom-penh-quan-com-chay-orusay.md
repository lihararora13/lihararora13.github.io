Quan Com Chay Orusay is a small vegetarian canteen on Street 141, the budget pick on this list: simple Khmer and Vietnamese vegetarian food at local prices, served in a no-frills room.

Order a vegetarian rice plate and a noodle soup. It is a vegetarian canteen with vegan options rather than an all-vegan restaurant, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note it is a mornings-only spot, open 7am to noon, so come for breakfast or an early lunch.
