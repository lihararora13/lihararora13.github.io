Village Juice and Kitchen is a juice bar and kitchen on South Stratford Road, doing cold-pressed juices, smoothies, and bowls with a vegan bench. The address here is 205 South Stratford Road; some listings say 901 West Fourth Street, which is wrong.

Order a cold-pressed juice and a smoothie bowl. Ask the staff to confirm the bowl toppings are vegan, since some include honey or granola with dairy.
