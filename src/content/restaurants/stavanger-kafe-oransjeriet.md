Kafe Oransjeriet is a fully vegan and plant-based cafe in Stavanger Ost, rated 5 on HappyCow, serving breakfast and savoury meals in the historic Oransjeriet building overlooking Lervigtunet Park. It is the cafe pick on this list.

Order the tofu scramble, the mushroom stroganoff and the vegan pancakes, and come any day from half seven to six.
