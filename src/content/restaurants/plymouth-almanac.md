Almanac Cafe sits in a historic warehouse at Royal William Yard, the old naval victualling yard, and serves a plant-led brunch and lunch menu. The setting, by the water among the stone warehouses, is one of the best in Plymouth.

Order a buddha bowl and a cake, then walk the yard and the harbour. Best on a dry day, when the outside tables are in use.
