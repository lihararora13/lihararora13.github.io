Wise Boys started as a food truck built from recycled materials and grew into Auckland's go-to vegan burger name. The Grey Lynn original closed in August 2026, so head for the Commercial Bay stand downtown, which HappyCow lists as open and top-rated, and which Denizen's recent burger guide calls its Auckland home.

The Spicy Buffalo Chick'n is the headline: crispy vegan chick'n, buffalo sauce, apple slaw and vegan ranch, with jackfruit loaded fries and a thick oat-milk shake to complete the very correct food-court feast.
