Vegan Street is Inglewood's vegan party: tacos, burritos, quesadillas and margaritas, all plant-based, in a bright room with outdoor seating. It is the comfort-food pick on the Calgary list, and the five-star reviews back it up.

Come with friends, order tacos to share, and add a margarita. This is the fun dinner on the list, and the patio is the place to be when the sun is out.
