Vapiano is the Italian chain right next to the Triumphal Arch, doing pasta, salads and pizzas made to order, with a few marked vegan options. The vegan pesto made from basil and pine nuts is the one to order.

Order the vegan pesto pasta, a vegan pizza Margherita with eggplant and artichokes, and a salad, and ask for the marked vegan options.
