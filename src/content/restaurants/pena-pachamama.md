Peña Pachamama is the only restaurant on this list where dinner might turn
into a dance party. This 25-year-old North Beach institution sits in a
former speakeasy where Clark Gable once wooed Carole Lombard, and tonight
it is an organic all-vegan Bolivian restaurant with nightly live Andean
music and dance performances. The food holds its own: baked kale chips
reviewers call leagues above the rest, empanadas, a beloved vegan lasagna,
and coconut cream pie for the finish.

Recent OpenTable reviews from summer 2026 are glowing, and the room still
runs four nights a week with shows. Order the empanadas, get the lasagna
for the table, and do not fight the urge to dance.
