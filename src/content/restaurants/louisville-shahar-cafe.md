Shahar Cafe is a cafe on Logan Street in Shelby Park doing vegan burgers, chicky sandos, tortas, and loaded stacks with fries or tots. It was formerly a 100 percent vegan restaurant, and its vegan items remain on the menu, cooked separately according to the owners.

Order the vegan cheeseburger and the crispy chicky sando. A caution: Shahar added meat to its menu in August 2026, according to Louisville Business First, so it is filed vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, and the vegan items should be confirmed as vegan when ordering.
