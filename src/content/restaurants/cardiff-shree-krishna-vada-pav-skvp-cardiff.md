Shree Krishna Vada Pav, SKVP, opened its Cardiff counter on Queen Street in June 2026, and it is the street-food pick on this page. The vada pav is the signature, with papdi chaat, uttapam and chole bhature alongside, all of it quick, cheap and properly spicy.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so confirm the vegan dishes at the counter. The vada pav is the order, and the papdi chaat runs it close.
