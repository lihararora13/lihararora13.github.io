Sunami is the new star of Taipei vegan dining: a smart, relaxed room on Civic Boulevard where pan-Asian plates arrive looking like art and tasting even better. Local guides have crowned it the city's top fully vegan restaurant, and reviewers in October 2026 were still going back for third visits.

The menu roams happily, Korean bibimbap here, Japanese-style sushi there, satay skewers everywhere. Order the mushroom and yuba skewers and the tangy orange cauliflower to start, leave room for a skillet cookie or tiramisu-style cheesecake, and book ahead: there can be a queue before the doors even open.
