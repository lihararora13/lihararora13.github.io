Vegetariano Natural is a vegetarian takeaway on Urquiza, rated 5.0, serving oriental and occidental dishes and desserts for lunch on weekdays. The counter mixes eastern and western cooking, and the dessert case is part of the draw.

Order a takeaway lunch plate, try an oriental dish, and ask which desserts are vegan. It is lunch-only, Monday to Friday, 11 am to 2:15 pm, so come early.
