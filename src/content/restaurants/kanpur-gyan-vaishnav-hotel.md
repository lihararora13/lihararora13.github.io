Gyan Vaishnav Hotel at Gumti No. 5 is the thali stop Kanpur locals name first: arhar daal, baigan bharta and aloo masala cooked the slow way, served in a dining room that has been doing this for years.

It is a pure-veg kitchen, so the vegan eating is a matter of asking for no ghee and no dahi. Order the arhar daal and the baigan bharta, and eat like the neighbourhood eats.
