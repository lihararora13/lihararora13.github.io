Speicher is a zero-waste shop and vegan café on Beim Nonnenhaus, in the old town, where the bistro sits inside a shop that sells everything unpackaged. The café runs a rotating lunch menu made from local produce, Tuesday to Friday from noon to three, alongside vegan cakes and drinks with plant milk, and the shop around it grinds its own hazelnut and almond butter. It is the most sustainable table on this list, and the lunch is genuinely good rather than merely virtuous.

It is closed on Sundays and Mondays. Order the Mittagstisch, add a slice of vegan cake, and take a jar of freshly ground nut butter home with you.
