Swastik is a vegetarian restaurant and dessert shop on Pramukh Swami Street in Kisutu, offering vegan Indian, Italian and Indochinese dishes. Reviewers call it the lowest budget Indian food in town, and the chef once created a new dish, an aloo paratha pizza roll, for a vegan regular.

Order the black dhal fry and the aloo paratha pizza roll. It is open daily 11am to 11pm, cash only, with outdoor seating.
