Apoteka is a fully vegan specialty coffee café on the Friedrich-Miescher-Straße, up by the Tübingen AI centre, and it is one of those coffee shops you are unlikely to find in other cities: a plant-filled room with a view over the hills, knowledgeable owners and baristas, and a menu of homemade treats made from organic ingredients. The lemon poppy pancakes are the dish creators travel for, the vegan quiche is the surprise, and the weekend brunch is the time to come.

Mind the hours: it opens Thursday to Saturday and on Sundays, and is closed Monday to Wednesday. Come for brunch, order the pancakes, and treat it as the slow morning Tübingen does best.
