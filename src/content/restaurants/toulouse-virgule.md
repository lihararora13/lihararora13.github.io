Virgule is a vegan coffee shop just behind Place du Capitole, on Rue des Lois, rated 5.0 on HappyCow. The menu is small and cafe-style: coffee, salad, toast and pastries, with a cute room that works as a morning or lunchtime stop.

It is a daytime spot, so plan it as a breakfast or lunch rather than a dinner. Order coffee and toast, and sit in for the calmest hour of a Toulouse day.
