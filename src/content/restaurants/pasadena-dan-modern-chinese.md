Dan Modern Chinese is Pasadena's dumpling pick: a modern Chinese restaurant on South Lake Avenue with a separate vegan menu covering vegetable dumplings, dan dan noodles, salt and pepper tofu and sesame noodles. The room is a polished, family-friendly dining room, open 11:30am to 9pm daily, and it is the place in this guide for a proper sit-down Chinese meal with vegan dishes that were designed as vegan rather than adapted.

Ask for the vegan menu on arrival. Order the dumplings and the dan dan noodle, and add the salt and pepper tofu.
