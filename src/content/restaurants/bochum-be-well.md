be.Well is Bochum's fully vegan restaurant in the Unicenter at the Ruhr University, run as Armbrusts Vegan Kitchen on Querenburger Höhe. The menu is built for students and staff: daily changing specials, vegan döner, burgers, falafel bags, cakes and coffee, all of it plant-based and priced for a campus lunch.

Hours follow the university rhythm: Monday to Friday from twelve until half past seven, with a split lunch and evening service, and Saturday until five. Come for the daily special, order the vegan döner if it is on, and treat it as the best-value vegan lunch in the Ruhr.
