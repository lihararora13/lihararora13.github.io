Cafe Roya sits on Chilwell Road in Beeston, and it is the local recommendation when Nottingham vegans want something a little more special. The menu is vegetarian and vegan, with Persian-inspired cooking and a proper cake counter.

It is the fancy-vegetarian pick on this list rather than a daily cafe, so save it for a slow brunch or a celebration. File it as vegan-friendly, and check the day's specials board when you arrive.
