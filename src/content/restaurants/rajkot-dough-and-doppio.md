Dough & Doppio is Rajkot's most modern café: a specialty coffee and Neapolitan pizza room in Kotecha Nagar, with a vegan almond caramel praline coffee on the menu and Jain-friendly options. It is the most café-culture meal on this list, and the one most likely to surprise a visitor to Rajkot.

Order the almond caramel praline coffee and a pizza without cheese, and settle into the city's best coffee room. It is a good working lunch, a good date spot, and a good end to a day of thalis.
