El Cantaro is proof that vegan Mexican food does not need a qualifier: fully vegan, rated 5.0 by HappyCow reviewers, and serving tacos, burritos and quesadillas that regulars put up against any taqueria in town. The al pastor tacos are the headliner, and the burritos do not skimp.

It is a neighbourhood taqueria in Rosemont, not a downtown showpiece, which is exactly its charm. Go hungry, order tacos, and bring cash for the second round.
