Nirvana The Veg Cafe is the number one restaurant in Bodh Gaya, 4.6 out of 5 from 242 TripAdvisor reviews, opposite the Thai Temple. It is a calm, polished dining room with a huge menu, vegan and gluten-free options, and a September 2026 post showing it currently open with a 4.2 rating from 697 reviews.

Order the masala dosa and the cauliflower in black pepper sauce, and skip the pizza, which regulars say is the one weak dish. It is open 9 to 10:30 daily, and it is the best-regarded vegetarian kitchen in town.
