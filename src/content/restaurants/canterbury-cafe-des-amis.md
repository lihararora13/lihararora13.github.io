Café des Amis has been serving Mexican food on St Dunstans Street for decades, and the vegan dishes are a real part of the menu. The room is famously colourful and the atmosphere is part of the draw.

Order vegan enchiladas and guacamole. A good evening pick with character, and one of the city's oldest restaurants.
