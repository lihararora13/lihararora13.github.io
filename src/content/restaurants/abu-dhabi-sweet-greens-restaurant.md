Sweet Greens is the healthy bowl spot at Rihan Heights, doing salads, grain bowls, wraps and soups with an emphasis on fresh, seasonal produce. Reviewers single out the Tempeh Kale rice bowl, and the kitchen is happy to customise for vegan diets.

Order the Tempeh Kale rice bowl, add a wrap, and treat it as the light, modern end to the list. It is the pick for a fresh, uncomplicated plant-based lunch.
