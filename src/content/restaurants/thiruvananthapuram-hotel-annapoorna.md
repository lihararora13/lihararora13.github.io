Hotel Annapoorna sits by the Padmanabhaswamy Temple in West Fort, and its Mysore masala dosa is the dish Trivandrum regulars say they will never forget. The room is a classic pure-veg hotel restaurant, open from 7:30 in the morning, and the filter coffee is the proper finish.

Order the Mysore masala dosa without ghee and a filter coffee. It is the temple-side breakfast, and it has been for years.
