Masa Luna is a New Mexican-ish bar on South 13th Street in Little Bohemia, with a menu of tacos, tortas, tamales, and chips and dips that is largely vegan. It is the most casual evening stop on this list, and the best place for a group that wants to share plates.

Order the vegan tacos and the tamales, and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation of the torta, since the standard version uses cheese.
