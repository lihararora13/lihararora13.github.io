Rincón Verde is Córdoba's all-vegan anchor, a kitchen that describes its food as free of fats, eggs and dairy, and its lentil burgers and spinach-and-tofu ravioli are the plates the city knows it by.

Order the Hamburguesas de lentejas, Ravioles de espinaca y tofu, and Lasagna integral. The lentil burger and the ravioli are the two orders; the wholewheat lasagne is the third.
