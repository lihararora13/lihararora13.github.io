Xu Xiang Zhai is the fun, filling option near the Lama Temple: an all-you-can-eat vegetarian buffet of mock meats and classic dishes that has been a local favourite for years.

Come hungry and graze. It is the best value way to try a dozen dishes at once, and the mock meats are the stars.
