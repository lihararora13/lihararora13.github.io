Has Antep Cigkofte is a Turkish fast-food shop in the east of the centre serving cigkofte, wraps and plates, described by the venue itself as vegan, fresh and spicy. Cigkofte in Germany is made without meat, from fine bulgur, walnuts, tomato paste and spices, so the whole menu is plant-based, and it is the only fully vegan kitchen on this list.

Order a cigkofte durum, a cigkofte plate and a party platter to share, and go to the Franz-Lenz-Strasse shop, since the Neumarkt branch is temporarily closed. It opens daily, late on Saturdays.
