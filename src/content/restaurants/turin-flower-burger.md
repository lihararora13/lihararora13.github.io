Flower Burger is the original rainbow vegan burger bar, and the Turin branch sits on Via Bertola, a short walk from the other centre picks. The burgers come in colourful buns, yellow, pink and more, with fries and edamame on the side, and everything is plant-based.

It is the fun, fast pick on this list rather than the fine-dining one. Order the Cherry Bomb, the house signature, and expect a bright, loud room full of first-timers and regulars alike.
