Ruean Thong Vegetarian House is a jay and vegetarian restaurant near Chatchai Market, open for more than 30 years across three generations, and the first vegetarian restaurant in Hua Hin. It serves traditional Thai food from all four regions, with house-made chilli pastes, curry pastes and sauces, and dishes built on local ingredients such as pineapple, palm heart and lotus stem. It is open daily from 6am to around 4pm, and it sells out fast.

Come early for the rice-and-curry in the morning and the noodles at midday, and try the pineapple curry and the yen ta fo noodles. Some dishes include egg for vegetarians, so vegans should ask for the jay dishes, which leave out egg and the five pungent vegetables.
