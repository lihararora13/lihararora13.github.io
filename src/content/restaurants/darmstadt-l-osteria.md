L'Osteria is the Italian chain on Wilhelminenstrasse, doing pizza and pasta made to order, with vegan pizzas and pastas across the menu. It shares a building with Green Thai at the same number, so check the door sign.

Order a vegan pizza and a vegan pasta, and ask for the vegan menu, since the kitchen also runs meat and dairy.
