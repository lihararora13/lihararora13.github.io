Veggie Bros is a 100 percent vegetarian street-food counter on the Juliuspromenade, and its vegan range is the broadest fast-food offering in Würzburg: falafel wraps, bowls, salads and loaded fries, with homemade sauces and the vegan brownies that regulars call legendary. Everything is made fresh daily from organic chickpeas and Franconian organic potatoes, and the falafel is the dish the whole city knows.

It is open Monday to Saturday, half past eleven to eight. Order a falafel wrap, add loaded fries, and finish with a vegan brownie, which is the correct order of operations here.
