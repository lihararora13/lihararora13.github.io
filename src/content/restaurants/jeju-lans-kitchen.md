Lan's Kitchen is the table Jeju's vegan travellers plan their west-coast day around: a fully vegan bistro in a white wooden house at Haga-ri, rated a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow, where the menu runs from Vietnamese rice noodle bowls and banh mi to Korean hotpot and tteokbokki, finished with house-made gelato as the sun drops over the sea. It is a small, owner-run room, so the cooking is personal and the produce is Jeju-grown where the season allows.

The kitchen moved from its old address at 57 Hagwidongnam 1-gil to 137 Haga-ro, so use the new address, not the one in older guides. It closes on Wednesdays, and the July 2026 reopening posts on its own Instagram show it trading again after a break, so check the feed before a special trip out.
