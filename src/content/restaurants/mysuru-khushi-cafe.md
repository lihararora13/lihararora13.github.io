Khushi is the budget breakfast stop in Gokulam, a small cafe where the coffee is organic French press and the porridge comes with soy milk as a matter of course. Energy balls and fresh juices fill out a menu that reads like it was written for people who just finished yoga, because it was.

Order the soy milk porridge and a French press, and keep the change. This is the cheapest good breakfast in the quarter.
