Vegan Khunnay is the most-reviewed vegan restaurant on Koh Samui, 4.5 from 257 HappyCow reviews, and it is one of the few fully vegan places on the island that is open for dinner. The draw is the all-you-can-eat Thai buffet for 120 THB, with stainless steel serving pots of stir-fried vegetables, curries, tofu dishes and unlimited wild rice, which regulars call the best they have had on the island. It is the second branch of the eatery, relocated from Bo Phut, and it sits in Mae Nam on the north coast near Bang Por beach.

Come hungry and come for the buffet, which is the whole point of the place. It is a simple open-air room with bamboo walls and floral tablecloths, not a polished cafe, and the price makes it the cheapest proper vegan meal on the island.
