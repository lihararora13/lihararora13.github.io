My Veggy is a vegan-friendly takeaway counter on Bahnhofsplatz, right by the station, and it is the train-day pick on this page. The menu is wraps, bagels, smoothies and shakes, made to take away and eat on the move.

Do not expect a sit-down dinner here; it is a counter, and the wraps are the order. Useful on arrival or departure, when the station is where you are.
