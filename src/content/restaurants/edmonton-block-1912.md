Block 1912 is Whyte Avenue's cafe for dessert lovers, and vegans get a proper look-in: six or more vegan gelato flavours, vegan fruit crisp, a vegan smoked tofu grilled sandwich and a daily vegan soup.

It is open late, which makes it the after-dinner stop on this list. Order a scoop or two of vegan gelato, add the fruit crisp, and linger on Whyte Avenue.
