Why we love it — Lucky Leek is the restaurant that proves vegan fine
dining is not a contradiction in terms. The Kollwitzstraße dining room
earns a Michelin Bib Gourmand for its seasonal tasting menus, and the
dishes read like poetry: tandoori cauliflower with passion fruit yogurt,
iced beetroot soup with yogurt foam and capers, green pea soup with
chili-mint oil. Every plate looks like a garden exploded in the best
way.

Book it for your special-occasion night. The three-course menu is
genuinely affordable by fine-dining standards, and the terrace in summer
is one of Berlin's loveliest places to linger. This is the table that
converts skeptics, and our number one in Berlin.
