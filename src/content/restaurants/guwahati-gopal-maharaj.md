Gopal Maharaj has been Guwahati's pure-veg institution since 1969, and the Assam tourism board itself spotlights it as the city's vegetarian landmark. It has won Zomato awards for vegetarian food, and local food creators call it one of the city's OG pure-veg spots, a busy family dining room where the naan, dal and vegetable curries have barely changed in fifty years.

Order a thali and finish with the elaichi peda from the sweet counter, which is famous in its own right. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee and paneer and the thali stays plant-based.
