Chez Mara is a vegetarian French restaurant in the KTV quarter, rated 5 on HappyCow, where most of the menu is vegan as it stands. The vegan items include braised seitan, fried eggplant, onion soup and chocolate tart, served in a traditional French room.

Order the braised seitan, the fried eggplant and the onion soup, and finish with the chocolate tart. It opens Tuesday to Saturday, two to ten.
