Abyssinia is Buffalo's Ethiopian kitchen on Grant Street, and it takes its vegan diners seriously enough to print a separate vegan menu. The dishes are the classic spiced and seasoned stews of beans, lentils, potatoes and vegetables, shiro wot of finely ground split peas in berbere sauce, kay sir of potatoes and beets sauteed in onion, garlic and ginger, and ya abesha gomen of ginger, spinach, garlic and onions in a mild sauce. Order the vegan platter to taste across them.

Hours are Tuesday to Thursday 11am to 7pm and Friday to Saturday 10am to 8pm, closed Sunday and Monday. It is a small, family-feeling room on the West Side, and the vegan menu is the thing to ask for.
