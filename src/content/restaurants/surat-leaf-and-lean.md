Leaf & Lean does exactly what the name promises: fresh, light food that leaves you feeling good rather than weighed down. The Gymkhana Road counter is a natural stop for a post-gym lunch or a clean-eating reset between Surat's richer meals.

Build a garden salad bowl or a quinoa bowl, add a cold-pressed juice, and you have the healthiest plate on this list. It is simple food done properly, and the vegan options are front and centre rather than hidden at the back of the menu.
