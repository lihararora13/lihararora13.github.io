Plantan is Reykjavik's vegan bakery and cafe, opened in 2021 by sisters Maria and Anna Jonsdottir, and it has quickly become a community hub for the city's vegan scene. The menu is coffee, pastries, sandwiches and soup, all of it fully vegan, with house-made baked goods that regulars swear by.

It is right in the centre, a short walk from Hlemmur, which makes it a lovely slow-morning stop. Order a pastry and a coffee, add a sandwich, and enjoy the calmest room on this list.
