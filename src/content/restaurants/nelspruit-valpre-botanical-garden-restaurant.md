Valpre is a thatched-roof restaurant inside the botanical gardens, with stone walls, a covered patio and views over dense trees and a waterfall. It is a lodge restaurant rather than a vegan one, but the kitchen does vegan-friendly plates on request.

Ask the kitchen for a vegan plate and eat on the deck. It is a destination for the setting as much as the food, and it suits a slow lunch.
