Reform Kafe is the quiet star of the Old City, a garden restaurant tucked inside the Green Tiger House guesthouse near the northern moat. It has been fully vegan since 2017, it holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow across nearly 400 reviews, and the menu moves easily between Thai dishes, burgers and pasta. Come for the calm as much as the food: tables under the trees, a slow pace, and a kitchen that has been doing this longer than almost anyone in town.

It is the same team behind Goodsouls Kitchen, so the quality bar is no surprise. The garden setting makes it the best slow lunch on this list, and it is open to the public even if you are not staying at the guesthouse.
