Somebody People is the argument-ender for anyone who thinks vegan
food can't do fine dining. The South Broadway room runs a seasonal,
Mediterranean-influenced menu built around zero-waste principles,
with house-made pastas and a 60-bottle vegan wine list that would
impress anyone who drinks wine, period.

The Sunday Supper is the move: six courses built from whatever the
week left behind, around thirty-eight bucks, and usually the best
meal you will eat that week. Book the tasting Tuesday through
Saturday if you want to plan ahead, or walk in for Sunday Supper.
