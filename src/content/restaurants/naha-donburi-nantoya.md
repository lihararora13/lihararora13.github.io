Donburi Nantoya is the safety net at the start and end of an Okinawa trip: a food-court stall on the fourth floor of Naha Airport's domestic terminal, open since March 2019 with a dedicated vegan menu cooked in separate oil from its other dishes. The vegan picks are a seaweed-and-vegetable tempura rice bowl, a veggie curry rice, a mapo tofu bowl and soy-meat dishes.

One caveat: the miso soup that comes with set meals may not be vegan, so ask staff to leave it out if you are strict. Otherwise this is a genuine first or last meal, open daily from around 9am to 8pm, though airport hours can shift.
