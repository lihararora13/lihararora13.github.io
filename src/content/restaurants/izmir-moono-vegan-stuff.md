Moono Vegan Stuff is an all-vegan bakery and cafe in Karsiyaka, describing itself simply as Vegan Stuff, with a menu of tres leches, enchiladas, tacos, tiramisu and muffins. It is open Tuesday to Friday from noon to 10:00pm, Saturdays until 9:00pm and Sundays from noon to 9:00pm.

Order the tres leches and the tiramisu, and take a muffin for the road.
