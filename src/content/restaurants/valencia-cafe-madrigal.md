Cafe Madrigal is Valencia's vegan cafe institution, a small room in Ruzafa that has been fully vegan since 2018 and still holds a perfect HappyCow score from hundreds of reviewers. Breakfast, lunch and dinner, with a menu del dia that locals swear by.

Order the Spanish tortilla, it is the dish that proves eggs were never the point. Stay for the Thai curry, drink the house lemonade, and plan to come back the next morning.
