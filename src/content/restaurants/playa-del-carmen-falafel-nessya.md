Falafel Nessya is the cheap filling lunch in Playa: a falafel spot in Centro where a wrap costs 80 pesos and a plate 120, per Nomads Beyond's December 2025 update. Order a falafel plate or a pita with hummus and Israeli salad, and expect the guides to keep recommending it for exactly that reason.

It is open from noon to 11pm, so it works for lunch or a casual dinner, and it is one of the lowest-priced vegan meals in the centre of town.
