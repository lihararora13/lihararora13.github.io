Sinfull Bakery started when founder Dylan Carnes got an idea on a hike, built it from her parents' kitchen, and within six months was selling at Whole Foods. Sixteen years later the Midtown storefront is a retro, coffee-slinging vegan bakery where the guests constantly ask "is this vegan?" and the staff grins: everything, always. Kolaches are the star, from sausage-egg-and-cheese to breakfast taco to the cult "Big Mac"-style one.

It is inexpensive, allergy-conscious, and dangerously close to a coffee bar that will talk you into a cinnamon roll latte. VegNews name-checked it in its Texas city roundup, and the nationwide shipping page means people far from Houston already know the name.
