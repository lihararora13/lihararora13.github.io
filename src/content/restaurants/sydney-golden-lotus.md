Golden Lotus is what happens when a Saigon family's love of flavour meets a 100% plant-based kitchen: a menu so enormous it might genuinely be the biggest all-vegan menu in Sydney, spanning pho, laksa, crispy duck pancakes, vegan fish clay pots, and rendang, all in a warm room on King Street. Regulars are almost aggressively loyal, with some visiting three or four times a week for years, and the pho alone has a devoted following.

Go for the laksa, which one reviewer describes as divine and orders every single time, and don't skip the Vietnamese iced coffee: reviewers call it an absolute highlight and worth the caffeine rush that keeps you up all night. It's cheap, it's cheerful, and it's exactly what a neighbourhood vegan institution should feel like.
