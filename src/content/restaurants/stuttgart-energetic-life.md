Energetic Life is a fully vegan and gluten-free café on Schulstraße in the Mitte, a short walk from the Schlossplatz. The menu runs to falafel, tacos, quiche and pfannkuchen, with a health-food bent that never quite becomes austere.

It is the gluten-free safe harbour on this page, and the falafel plate is the order. Good for lunch between sights.
