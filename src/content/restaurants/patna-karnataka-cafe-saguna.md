Karnataka Cafe Saguna is one of the newest pure-veg spots in Patna, a South Indian, North Indian and Chinese veg restaurant that opened on Saguna-Khagaul Road in 2026 with a bright green Pure Veg banner and a balloon-arch launch. The dining room is modern and family-friendly, and the menu covers dosas, noodles, curries and chaat.

Order the masala dosa or the veg noodles, and ask the kitchen to skip the dairy. It is a useful stop on the western side of the city, near the Saguna More junction.
