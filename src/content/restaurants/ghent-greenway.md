Greenway is a plant-based restaurant on Nederkouter, open daily from 11:00 to 21:00, cooking vegetarian versions of the classics: burgers, pasta, rice, salads and wraps, with a pita kebab and a vegan stoofvlees among the favourites. It is the easy, everyday pick on this list, and the prices are kind.

It is listed as vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, as the chain's menu is vegetarian with vegan options. Order the pita kebab and the vegan stoofvlees.
