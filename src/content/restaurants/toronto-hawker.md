Why we love it — Seven tables, a garden patio, and some of the most artful vegan plating in the city. Hawker takes the chaos of a Southeast Asian street-food centre and distills it into small, gorgeous plates: fermented chickpeas, smoked cabbage, and a plant-based ras malai ice cream that reviewers write love letters about. Book ahead, because a room this small and food this good does not leave seats empty.

This is the date-night pick and the food-nerd pick in one cozy room. Order the tasting menu, grab the Cardamom and Curry Old Fashioned, and wander Kensington Market after with full bellies and zero regrets.
