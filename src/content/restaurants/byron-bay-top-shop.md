The Top Shop sits on top of a hill a few blocks behind Byron's touristy areas, and it is the kind of neighbourhood cafe that rewards the walk up. The vegan burger is the star, a proper, satisfying thing that surprises even committed carnivores, and the rest of the menu is classic Byron cafe fare: avocado toast, smoothies and good coffee. It is local, laid-back and gloriously un-touristy.

Walk up the hill, order the burger, and enjoy the view back down. This is the one for a proper local breakfast away from the main street.
