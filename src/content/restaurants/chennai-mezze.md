Mezze made the list for a reason. A charming blue-walled Middle Eastern retreat in RA Puram with falafel, smoked tofu salad and dukkah mushroom on the vegan list. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in RA Puram, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Falafel sandwich, Smoked tofu salad, Dukkah mushroom if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
