The Green Tongue is an authentic Mexican vegan food trailer on East Berry Street, trading from a food court shared with six other trailers. The street tacos are the dish to start with, the Tex-Mex bowl is the filling one, and the tamales and mole are the reasons to come back.

It keeps short hours, Thursday to Saturday evenings and weekend lunches, so check the day. Order the tacos and eat them in the courtyard.
