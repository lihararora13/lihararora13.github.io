Verte Cafe is a vegetarian cafe in Gubeng, with many vegan options, including a vegan burger and vegan cakes. HappyCow lists it at Cisadane 26, while a guide lists it at Jl. Jambi 47, so check the address before you go, and use Cisadane 26 as the primary address.

Order the vegan burger and a vegan cake, and ask for the vegan dishes by name, as the kitchen is vegetarian. It is a modern cafe, and it suits a light lunch or a coffee break.
