Durgiana Sannidhi sits outside Hathi Gate by the Durgiana temple complex, a pure-veg, egg-free kitchen doing sattvik breakfasts from 6 AM: fluffy poori and bhatura with pindi chana, tangy aloo launji, and desi-ghee jalebi for the dairy eaters.

It is the quietest, most devotional table on this list, and the Jain and vrat platters show a kitchen used to dietary care. Ask for the poori-chana without ghee and you have a proper temple breakfast.
