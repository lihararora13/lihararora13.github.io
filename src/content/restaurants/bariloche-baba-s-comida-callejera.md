Baba's Comida Callejera is a vegan-friendly indian spot in Centro. Baba's is Bariloche's Indian street-food counter on 20 de Febrero, with marked vegan choices including broccoli tikki, gochujang tempeh and tofu palak. Its pin is approximate.

Order the Tikki de brócoli, Tempeh gochujang, and Tofu palak. The tikki and the tofu palak are the two vegan plates to try.
