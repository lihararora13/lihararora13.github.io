The Vegan Room tops the list for a reason. Jaipur's first and only 100% plant-based restaurant: Thai millet bowls, Tom Yum soup, open sourdough sandwiches and Italian pasta, all vegan. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in C-Scheme, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Thai millet bowl, Tom yum soup, Open sourdough sandwich if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
