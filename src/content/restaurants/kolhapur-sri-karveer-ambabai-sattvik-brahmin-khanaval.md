Sri Karveer Ambabai is the temple-town khanaval: a 100% pure-veg, zero onion-garlic sattvik dining hall on Mahadwar Road in Shivaji Peth, steps from the Ambabai temple. It is where pilgrims eat, and where the food is at its most traditional.

Order the sattvik thali with the ghee held, and you have a properly plant-based temple meal. It is simple, cheap and deeply Kolhapur, and the best lunch in the old city.
