Vegetiger, full name Toraya Vegetiger Second Room, is the fun dinner of the Nagoya list: a fully vegan izakaya-style restaurant in Shinsakae doing South Asian and Asian fusion, from soothing curries and laksas to mapo tofu.

It closes for a couple of hours each afternoon, so plan around it. Order a curry and a laksa and share them.
