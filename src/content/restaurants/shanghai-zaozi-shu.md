Zaozi Shu, also known as Jujube Tree, has been cooking pure vegan Buddhist food since 2001, and it shows in the confidence of the kitchen. No egg, no alcohol, no garlic or onion, just careful Chinese cooking built on mushrooms, tofu, gluten and seasonal vegetables.

It is the all-rounder of the list, broad enough for a mixed table and traditional enough to feel like old Shanghai. Order the sweet and sour pork and whatever is seasonal.
