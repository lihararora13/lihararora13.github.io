OzyMex is the budget hero of Byron's vegan scene: a casual Mexican spot where the vegan house-cooked pinto beans are actually cheaper than the meat options, which tells you everything about the priorities here. Burritos, tacos and nachos all come in vegan versions, the portions are generous, and the whole thing is fast, fun and easy on the wallet.

Come hungry after the beach, order a burrito and tacos, and save your money for an ice cream afterwards. This is the one for a cheap, satisfying feed.
