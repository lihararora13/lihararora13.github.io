Agarwal's Kitchen is a small veg kitchen on N.C. Goenka Road, open 9 to 9 daily, serving homely North Indian vegetarian food at budget prices. It is the kind of family-run room that Darjeeling's vegetarian visitors find by word of mouth rather than by signboard.

Order the veg thali and ask for no ghee and no paneer. It rounds out the Chowrasta cluster of veg options, and it is a good fallback when the bigger rooms are full.
