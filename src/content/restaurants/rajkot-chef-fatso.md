Chef Fatso is Rajkot's open-air café: a Kalavad Road spot in Virda Vajdi where smoothie bowls, pesto pizza and burrito bowls are eaten under the sky. It is the most outdoors meal on this list, and the most fun.

Order a smoothie bowl and a burrito bowl, veg version, and enjoy the evening air. The kitchen is used to plant-based requests, so ask for the cheese held and the meal turns vegan.
