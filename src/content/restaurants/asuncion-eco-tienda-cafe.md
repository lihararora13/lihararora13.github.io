Eco Tienda Café is Asunción's fully vegan eco cafe in Barrio Herrera, a bright minimalist room that is also a shop for frozen food, supplements and bio-cosmetics. Its pin is approximate.

Order the Sándwich de falafel, Waffles veganos, Empanadas veganas, and Helados veganos. The falafel sandwich is the signature; the waffles and the ice creams are the second round.
