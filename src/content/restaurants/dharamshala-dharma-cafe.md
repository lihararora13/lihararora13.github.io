Dharma Cafe is the plant-based heart of McLeod Ganj: a cafe inside the Lha Ahimsa House on Temple Road, with Tibetan prayer flags along the ceiling, thangka paintings on the walls, and a menu that is fully plant-based. The plant-based burger with fries and purple cabbage slaw, the stir-fried vegetable bowl, and the big bowl of noodle soup are the orders.

It is open 9 AM to 6:30 PM, and it is the easiest vegan table in the hills. Come for lunch, stay for the herbal tea, and let the prayer flags do the rest.
