Rango Vegan is the restaurant that proves Bahian food was always meant to be vegan: moqueca built on palm heart, feijoada without the pork, bobo and vatapa, all of it plant-based and all of it delicious. It sits in Santo Antonio, one of Salvador's prettiest historic neighbourhoods, and it is rated 4.7 from over a thousand reviewers.

Come for lunch or dinner, order the moqueca vegana, and take a walk around Santo Antonio afterwards. This is the one for a proper taste of Bahia, minus the meat.
