Backstage Vegan Kitchen is the vegan kitchen at Rotown, the music venue on Nieuwe Binnenweg, serving a changing plant-based menu in the afternoon and evening. It is a dinner-first spot, open from 12:30, with later hours on Fridays and Saturdays, and the menu changes with the season.

Come for dinner before or after a show, or as a standalone evening meal on the Binnenweg. Ask what is on that day; the kitchen cooks to the season.
