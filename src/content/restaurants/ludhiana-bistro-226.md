Bistro 226 is the breather on this list: a relaxed bistro in the Ghumar Mandi area with plant-filled interiors and outdoor seating, doing pizza, pasta and salads for travellers who want a break after several days of dhaba food.

The vegan pizza without cheese and the pasta arrabbiata are the orders. It is a comfortable, modern room, and a good one for a long lunch.
