GreenGrill House sits on the same Lekki street as Earth Blend and does the heartier half of the wellness job: grain bowls, grilled vegetables and smoothies in a bright, modern room. It is a good pick when you want something more substantial than a juice but still light.

Order a grain bowl, add a grilled vegetable plate to share, and finish with a smoothie. It pairs well with a Lekki afternoon.
