Cleveland Vegan is the OG: a vegan catering company that grew into a full cafe and bakery in Lakewood in 2012, and still the best-known all-vegan spot in the city. The seitan gyro is the signature, the mac and cheese is the comfort move, and the vegan cupcakes are the reason to save room.

It is the trailblazer of the Lakewood dining scene, and the one to take someone who thinks Cleveland cannot do vegan food. Order the gyro, add a cupcake, and enjoy the bakery case.
