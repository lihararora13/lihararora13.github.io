L'Ethiquête is the table to book first in Nantes: a fully vegan French restaurant a short walk from the Château des ducs de Bretagne, cooking bistro food without the animal products and without making a fuss about it. The Tofish and Chips is the signature, alongside a proper burger, a tartare, and a hummus plate that works as a lighter start.

The room is small and the menu changes with the seasons, so book ahead on weekends. It is the highest-ranked vegan table in the city across the local guides, and the one Nantes vegans name first.
