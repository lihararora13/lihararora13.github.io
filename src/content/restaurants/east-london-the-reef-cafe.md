The Reef Cafe sits on the vegetated cliff above Nahoon Reef, a white-roofed building with a boardwalk down to the beach and long views along the coast. It lists vegetarian and vegan options alongside its regular cafe menu, and the setting is the draw.

Ask for the vegan options of the day and eat on the terrace. It is a view-first stop, so keep expectations on the food modest and the camera ready.
