vevi Café is the Belgian Quarter's healthy vegan café, and it takes the brief seriously: breakfast and lunch built on nutritious ingredients and low-waste preparation, with croissants, bowls, brownies, soups, sandwiches, raw cakes, smoothies, juices and waffles, always with gluten-free and sugar-free options on hand.

Come for a slow breakfast or a working lunch. Order a bowl, add a raw cake, and enjoy the calmest corner of the Belgian Quarter.
