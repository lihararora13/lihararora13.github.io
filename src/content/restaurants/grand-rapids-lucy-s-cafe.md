Lucy's Cafe, also listed as Little Lucy's, is a neighborhood cafe on Plainfield Avenue Northeast doing breakfast and lunch with a vegan-friendly bench. Tofu scrambles, avocado toast, and breakfast plates give plant-based diners a real morning meal in a residential part of town.

Order the tofu scramble and an oat milk latte. It is a daytime stop, so plan it for breakfast or lunch.
