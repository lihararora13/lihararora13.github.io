Foo Dog Curry Traders is a curry shop on Stockton Street in Riverside doing noodle soups and curries with tofu and vegetables. The vegan bench is strong, and the noodle soup with tofu is the headline order.

Order the noodle soup with tofu and the fresh spring rolls. Ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since some broths contain fish sauce or egg.
