El Jardín de Jazmín is one of Lima's most popular vegan tables, and the most reviewed on this list: a plant-based, entirely gluten-free restaurant in the heart of Miraflores that blends Peruvian fusion with Western comfort food. The a la carte menu runs from sandwiches, tacos and burgers made with lentils, garbanzos and black beans to salads and bowls, and the room doubles as a venue for poetry readings, art exhibits and live music.

Come for lunch or a late dinner, because this is the rare vegan restaurant that stays open until midnight on weekends and still feels relaxed. The lentil burger with guac and sprouts is the dish regulars swear by, the buffalo cauliflower is the appetizer to share, and the pisco drinks are worth ordering alongside.
