Otis! is the funkiest room in this guide: a family-run Tex-Mex restaurant that doubles as a live music venue, a few minutes south of Peace Park, with posters and vinyl lining every inch of the walls. The menu is clearly labelled, the chef is happy to substitute ingredients, and the vegan dishes, chili, pizza, nachos and enchiladas, all come loaded with melty homemade vegan cheese, with vegan cake for dessert and a lentil curry plate if Tex-Mex is not the mood.

It is not high-end dining, it is comfort food in a friendly room, and it is great if you want a change of pace from Japanese cuisine. Open noon to 11pm Monday to Saturday, and from 5pm on Sundays and holidays.
