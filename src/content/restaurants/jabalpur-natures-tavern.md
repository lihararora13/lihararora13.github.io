Nature's Tavern is the pick Jabalpur's listings agree on: a vegetarian café in North Civil Lines with a 4.7-star rating, a calm room, and a menu of pizzas, burgers, rolls, pasta and fresh juices. Reviewers praise the white sauce pasta, the club sandwiches and the fast, friendly service, and the café runs two outlets in the city, this Civil Lines one being the flagship.

It is a café rather than a thali house, so order the pasta without cheese, the veg club sandwich and a fresh juice, and treat it as the relaxed, modern start to the Jabalpur list. Open 11 AM to 11 PM daily.
