Goorsha has been a Durham mainstay for years, and Ethiopian cuisine is naturally kind to vegans: the menu has a dedicated vegan section, and the combo arrives with injera for scooping. The misir wot and gomen are the anchors, and the room, in the Brightleaf district, is one of the most pleasant in the city.

Order the vegan combo for the table and share it family-style.
