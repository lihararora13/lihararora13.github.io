The Arty Vegan is the highest-rated café in Pushkar, with a perfect 5.0 stars across 212 reviews, and it is fully vegan. That combination is rare anywhere, and it makes this the first stop on any Pushkar vegan crawl.

Everything on the menu is plant-based, so order freely: a signature bowl, vegan pancakes, a smoothie bowl and a plant-based dessert. The room is art-filled and relaxed, the way Pushkar's best cafés always are.
