Ratanada Vinayak is Jodhpur's South Indian and chaat counter: a 100% pure-veg spot in High Court Colony, Ratanada, with Jain options and a menu built around dosa, idli and chaat. It is the most casual meal on this list, and one of the cheapest.

The masala dosa and the chaat are the orders, with the curd held on the chaat. One honest note: the kitchen uses pure cow ghee in some dishes, so say the word and they will hold it. The pin is approximate to the High Court Colony area.
