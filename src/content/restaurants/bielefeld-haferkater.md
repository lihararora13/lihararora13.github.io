Haferkater is the early-opening station stop in the entrance hall of Bielefeld Hauptbahnhof, beside McDonald's, serving porridge cooked with water and salt and finished with a choice of toppings, plus bowls, wraps and banana bread. The range is vegetarian-vegan, so choose the vegan toppings and plant milk.

Order the porridge with vegan toppings, a bowl and a banana bread, and come early, since it is the breakfast pick before a train.
