Truptee has been feeding Buddha Nagar for years, and its regulars are loyal enough to give it a five-star rating. It is a straightforward pure-veg restaurant: thalis, Indo-Chinese plates, North Indian curries and the kind of generous portions that make it a family default.

The mushroom manchurian and veg mix fried rice are the dishes to order, and the thali is good value. Ask the kitchen to cook without ghee and paneer and the menu opens up nicely for vegans.
