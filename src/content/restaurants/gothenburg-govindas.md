Govindas is Gothenburg's meditative lunch, a Hare Krishna vegetarian restaurant on Karl Johansgatan in Majorna that serves a simple, soulful menu at lunchtime on weekdays. Dal, sabji, samosa, papadam and mango lassi, all of it inexpensive and all of it made with care, in a calm room that feels a world away from the street outside.

It is vegetarian with vegan options, so ask which dishes are vegan that day. Order the dal, add a samosa, and enjoy the cheapest proper lunch in Majorna.
