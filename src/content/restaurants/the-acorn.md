Why we love it — The Acorn is the restaurant that put Vancouver's plant-based
scene on the fine-dining map, with a MICHELIN nod and a zero-waste philosophy
built around BC-grown produce. The kitchen treats vegetables like headliners,
not sides, and the vegan tasting menu runs deep.

Book the tasting menu and come hungry. Everything here is vegetarian with
strong vegan options throughout, so it's the rare splurge room where a vegan
diner gets the full experience instead of the afterthought.
