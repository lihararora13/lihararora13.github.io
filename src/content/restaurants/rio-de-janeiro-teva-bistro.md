Teva Bistrô is the restaurant Rio's vegan scene is built around, and it tops HappyCow's city list with a perfect 5.0 from more than two hundred reviewers. It has been fully plant-based and organic since 2016, it is a certified B Corp, and the kitchen cooks with seasonal, local ingredients rather than leaning on packaged substitutes. The dining room is small and a couple of blocks from the sand at the Leblon end of Ipanema, and the food is ambitious in the way that converts sceptics: Korean seitan, truffled mushroom lasagna, hummus with chermoula, plus burgers, seasonal classics and proper desserts.

It is open all day, every day, which makes it the easiest special meal in the city, but book ahead for dinner because the room is tiny. Order the Korean seitan if you eat gluten, ask what is seasonal, and save room for dessert. This is the one to take someone who thinks vegan food in Rio means a sad salad.
