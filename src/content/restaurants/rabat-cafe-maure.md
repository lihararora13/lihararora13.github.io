Cafe Maure is the famous cafe in the Kasbah des Oudayas, a clifftop room with views over the Bou Regreg river and the Atlantic. Mint tea and pastries are the order, and it is one of the most photographed spots in Rabat.

Order mint tea and bread with olive oil, and check which pastries are vegan that day. Come for the view at sunset, and treat it as a tea stop rather than a meal.
