Tassili's is the kind of place people drive 45 minutes for and then stand in line another 45, happily. This historic West End café serves 100% raw vegan food with 100% maximum flavor, built around kale wraps so legendary they have names like the Punany Wrap and the Mandingo.

Nothing here touches a stove, yet somehow everything tastes like a hug. Expect enormous portions, bold seasoning, and a crowd of true believers who will talk your ear off about kale if you let them. Let them.
