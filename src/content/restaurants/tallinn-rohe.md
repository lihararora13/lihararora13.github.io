Rohe Kohvik is the vegan restaurant you end up at three times in a week without meaning to. Located at Kopli 4, near the Balti Jaam train station and the entrance to Kalamaja's restaurant scene, it sits perfectly between cafe and restaurant: casual enough for a quick lunch, substantial enough for a proper dinner, with coffee, bowls and cakes on the menu.

The cooking leans Asian, with miso ramen, tom kha, tofu bowls and tempeh, and the tiramisu is the dessert to save room for. Order the ramen, add a bowl, and come back tomorrow.
