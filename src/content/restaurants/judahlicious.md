Judahlicious is the kind of neighborhood gem that makes you wish you lived
within walking distance. Tucked out in the Outer Sunset near Ocean Beach,
this vegan and raw food spot has built a loyal following on nutrient-rich
bowls, fresh juices, and smoothies, all made with a stated commitment to
local growers and community. It lands on every aggregator's top-10 list
and anchors the Sunset side of the city's vegan map.

It is also the friendliest check on the wallet in this guide. Grab a juice
after a beach walk, eat something that makes you feel like a better person,
and know you are supporting one of the longest-running plant-based
independent spots in the city.
