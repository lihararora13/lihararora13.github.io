Why we love it — Every city needs a late-night answer to the chicken
shop, and London's is Temple of Seitan. The "chicken" is seitan,
seasoned and deep-fried exactly like the stuff it replaces, served as
burgers, wraps, wings, or the classic two-piece with fries. It has
converted carnivores since opening, and the queues at the Hackney
original say everything.

It is fast, cheap, and gloriously unpretentious, the kind of place
that proves vegan junk food is not a compromise but a destination.
Vegan or not, this is worth the trip east.
