Bikanervala on Fatehabad Road is the vegetarian-only chain that never lets you down: chhole bhature, puri sabzi, chaat and sweets, open 9 AM to 11 PM, about 1.6 km from the South Gate.

Order the chhole bhature without dahi and the puri sabzi. It is fast, cheap, and the most reliable veg meal in Agra.
