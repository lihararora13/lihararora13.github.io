The Vegan Club is Shaker Square's Southern-leaning vegan spot: 100 percent plant-based since November 2020, doing vegan krab fries, haute chick'n sandwiches and comfort plates with cocktails on the side. The krab fries are the signature, and the chick'n sandwich is the one reviewers rave about.

It is the east-side anchor of the list, and the one to hit for a proper dinner rather than a quick lunch. Order the krab fries, add the sandwich, and stay for a cocktail.
