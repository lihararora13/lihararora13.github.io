Bread and Butterflies started in 2015 as a craft tearoom run by twin sisters Linsey and Lisa, and after six years they made the whole menu 100% vegan. The room is vintage-inspired and dog-friendly, the cakes are baked on the premises, and most dishes can be made gluten free.

Book ahead for the vegan high tea, served on china crockery, and order the scrambled tofu and a slice of almond raspberry cake. It is one of the few fully vegan tearooms in the country, and the cakes alone justify the trip across the Trent.
