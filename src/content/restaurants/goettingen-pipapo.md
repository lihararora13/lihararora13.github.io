Pipapo is Göttingen's vegan-pizza answer, a pizzeria on Bühlstraße with a clearly labelled menu where vegan cheese and mock meats are available for the pizzas. The rest of the menu is pizza, pasta and fries, and the vegan options are marked rather than hidden, which is more than most pizzerias manage.

It is open Tuesday to Sunday, closed on Mondays, and it does delivery as well as dine-in. Order a vegan pizza with vegan cheese, add fries, and you have the easiest vegan dinner in the eastern old town.
