60 seconds to napoli is a Neapolitan pizza restaurant on the Karlstraße, doing proper Neapolitan pizza with a vegan range, open every day from noon to midnight. It is the late table on this list, the place that is still serving when the rest of Karlsruhe has closed, and the vegan pizzas are a real part of the menu rather than a single token topping. The room is casual, the ovens are hot, and the pizzas come fast.

Order a vegan pizza, add a marinara, and treat it as the evening that does not need a reservation.
