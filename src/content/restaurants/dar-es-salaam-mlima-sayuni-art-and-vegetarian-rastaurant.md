Mlima Sayuni is a vegetarian restaurant in Mji Mwema, Kigamboni, rated 4.5, with many vegan options including cake, seasonal and regional healthy wholefood meals, and ingredients from the owner's organic garden. Menu meals need to be ordered a day ahead, otherwise you get the daily special with a fresh juice.

Order a day ahead for the menu, or take the daily special with a fresh juice. It is cash only and a ferry ride across the harbour, so plan the trip.
