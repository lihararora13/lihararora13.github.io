Kaffi Vinyl has hands down the most chill vibe in Reykjavik, a vegan cafe and vinyl record shop where the art, the records and the diverse crowd are all part of the meal. The coffee uses plant-based milk as standard, the cakes and pastries are house-baked, and the menu rotates through sandwiches and plant-based hot dishes.

It was the city's only fully vegan restaurant for years, and it still feels like the heart of the scene. Order a cake and an oat latte, browse the records, and stay a while.
