Milas is one of the pioneer vegetarian restaurants in Yogyakarta, in the Prawirotaman backpacker quarter, and it cooks savoury vegetarian meals with organic ingredients and no MSG. The spicy tempe salad is the dish to order, and the room is a quiet, long-running local favourite.

It is a vegetarian restaurant rather than an all-vegan one, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note the pin is neighbourhood-level for Prawirotaman. Come for a slow lunch between the batik shops and the cafes of the quarter.
