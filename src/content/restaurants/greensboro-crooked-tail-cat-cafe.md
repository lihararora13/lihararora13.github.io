Crooked Tail Cat Cafe is a cat cafe on South Elm Street, doing all-vegan plates alongside a room of adoptable cats. It is the most unusual stop on this list, and the best place to eat lunch while meeting the cats.

Order the vegan sandwich and a coffee. Book the cat room ahead if you want to sit with the cats, since space is limited.
