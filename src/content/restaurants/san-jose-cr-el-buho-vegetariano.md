El Buho Vegetariano is a colourful vegetarian spot on Calle de la Amargura in San Pedro, near the university, with bowls, tacos, wraps, pancakes and pastries. English menus are available, and vegan versions of most dishes are straightforward to order.

Order a brunch bowl or the pancakes. It is a daytime place, with weekday lunch into the early evening, shorter Saturdays, and closed Sundays.
