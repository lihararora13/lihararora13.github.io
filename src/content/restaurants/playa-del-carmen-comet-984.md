Comet 984 is the diner stop in Playa: a quirky 1950s American diner a few blocks off 5th Avenue where the whole menu is plant-based, milkshakes included. Get a burger with loaded fries, or a hot dog or corn dog, and the Snickers milkshake that both My Vegan Travels and Roam and Thrive call out, with gluten-free buns available.

It is open from 1pm to 9pm and closed Thursday. When you want a burger and a shake instead of tacos, this is where you go.
