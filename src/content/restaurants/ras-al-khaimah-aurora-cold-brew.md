Aurora Cold Brew is a cold-brew coffee shop in the north of the emirate, towards Dibba, with plant-based milk options. It is a coffee stop rather than a restaurant.

Order a cold brew with oat milk. It is the softest pick on this list, included because the emirate has so few options, and it suits a drive up the coast.
