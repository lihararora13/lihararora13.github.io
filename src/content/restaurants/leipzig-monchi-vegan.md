Monchi Vegan is a fully vegan Asian fusion restaurant on Beethovenstraße in Zentrum-Süd, and it is the fun dinner on this page. The phở and ramen are the anchors, with a bao burger and dumplings alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it built for sharing.

The bao burger is the order, and the dumplings are the thing to share. It is a short walk south of the centre, and the room is lively.
