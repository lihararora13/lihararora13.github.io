Ba Baou Ge, in Anping near the old fort and the canals, is a Taiwanese vegetarian restaurant that takes vegan diners seriously: many dishes are vegan as served, and others can be made vegan on request. The headline plates are the vegan braised pork rice, the vegan beef noodles and the dumplings, and it holds a 5.0 rating on HappyCow.

It is a vegetarian restaurant, not a fully vegan one, so mention you eat vegan when ordering. It is a good stop after Anping's sights, with a big menu and take-out if you want to eat by the water.
