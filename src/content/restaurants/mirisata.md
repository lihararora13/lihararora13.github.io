Why we love it — There is exactly one all-vegan Sri Lankan restaurant on the
entire planet, and it happens to be in Portland. Mirisata is worker-owned,
which feels right for a place cooking food this generous: rice-and-curry
plates piled with sambals, curries, and fried things you didn't know you
needed in your life.

Order the rice-and-curry plate and the hot chili pol roti, then save room for
the watalappan coconut custard, because skipping dessert here is a rookie
mistake you only make once. Number one on our list and nobody came close.
