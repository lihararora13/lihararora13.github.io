Salrim Vegetarian Buffet is an all-vegetarian buffet in Juwol-dong, Nam-gu, near the South District town hall, serving classic Korean food: soups, porridges, vegetable sides, tofu and veganised main dishes, plus a salad bar and desserts of rice cakes, waffles and toast with jam. Reviewers say the kitchen makes most food on site and does not use dairy or eggs, with one exception: the pizza is topped with store-bought cheese, so skip it.

Fill a plate with the namul banchan, tofu and mock meat dishes, and the soups, and leave the pizza alone. It is spacious, inexpensive and easy to find, and the best-value vegan-friendly meal in Gwangju.
