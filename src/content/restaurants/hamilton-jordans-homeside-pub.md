Jordan's Homeside Pub is Hamilton's plant-based pub, out on Main Street East, serving sandwiches, pasties, burgers, subs and wraps with a pint. It is the only all-vegan pub in the guide, and the most relaxed vegan meal in the city: no decoding, no asking, just order.

It opens in the late afternoon, Tuesday to Saturday from 4pm, with later hours on Thursday and Friday. Come for dinner and a drink, not for lunch.
