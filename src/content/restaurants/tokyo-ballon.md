Ballon is small, sunny, and exactly the kind of place Nakameguro does best. This little falafel stand has been serving completely vegan wraps and soft serve since 2017, loading crisp falafel with grilled mushrooms, eggplant, herbs and bright pickled vegetables.

There are only a handful of stools, so take your wrap for a stroll along the canal like a local. Finish with the soft serve, come back the next day, and repeat. Some Tokyo rituals are very easy to adopt.
