Oteate is the neighbourhood cafe of the Nagoya list: a vegan cafe a few minutes from Higashiyama-koen Station, in a quiet corner of Chikusa Ward, doing thoughtful cafe cooking and desserts.

It is a good stop after the zoo or the park. Order the lunch plate and a dessert.
