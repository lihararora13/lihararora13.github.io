7 Basiks is a 5.0-rated vegan kitchen on Deanery Road serving home-cooked Ital food and healthy treats, with a wide variety of fresh juices such as sea moss spirulina, ginger lemon and lemongrass herb. The menu features escovitch mushrooms, sweet and sour veggie balls, black bean burgers and wraps, with soy ice cream and pastries for dessert.

Order the escovitch mushrooms and a black bean burger, with a sea moss spirulina juice. It is open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm, and Saturdays from 10 am to 5 pm.
