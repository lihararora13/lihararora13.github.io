Pots & Bowls is a vegan-friendly breakfast and lunch spot in Rohrmoser, at Sky Garden on Calle 78, and it is the useful pick for anyone staying west of downtown. The menu runs to bowls, burgers and breakfast items, with coconut pancakes, acai and quinoa bowls, a vegan burger and falafel among the plant-based options, and reviews include January 2026 visits.

Order the vegan burger, the falafel, or a customisable bowl, and ask what is plant-based, as this is not a fully vegan kitchen. It is a trek from Barrio Amon, so pair it with a morning on the west side.
