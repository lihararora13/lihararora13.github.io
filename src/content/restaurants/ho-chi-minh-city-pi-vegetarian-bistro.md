Pi has been feeding Saigon's vegetarians since 2013, and its rooftop garden room in Da Kao is still one of the city's happiest places to eat. The menu is built for sharing: family-style Vietnamese dishes, curries, steamed and stewed plates, and two sizes of hot pot, plus cold-pressed juices, smoothies and teas. Reviewers rave about the green mango salad and lemongrass tofu, and about food so creative you would never guess it was meat-free.

Book ahead, because rooftop seating is limited and sunset here is popular. Order Pi's platter for the table, add a hot pot, and settle in for a long evening above the District 1 traffic.
