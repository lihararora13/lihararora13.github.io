UZU is not really a ramen shop, it is a show: sixteen seats, black polished tables, teamLab artwork drifting across the walls, a drop of essential oil on your wrist as you sit down, and then a multi-course vegan ramen meal built around soy sauce with fermented mugwort or spicy miso with sake kasu. It has held a Michelin Green Star for two years running and a Bib Gourmand for five, which makes it the most decorated vegan restaurant in Japan.

This one needs planning: reservations are essential, the course runs around 7,000 yen, and it is closed several days a week. Book it as the special meal of the trip, and arrive hungry and on time.
