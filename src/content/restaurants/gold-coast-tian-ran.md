Tian Ran made the list for a reason. One of the Gold Coast's OG vegan restaurants, doing classic Asian dishes: stir-fry, hot pot, noodle soup and vegan seafood. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Mermaid Beach, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Hot pot, Noodle soup, Stir-fry if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
