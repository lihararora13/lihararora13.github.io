Why we love it — Stefano's took the classic greasy-spoon diner and rebuilt it from plants, and the result has Foodism and VegNews calling it Toronto's best vegan restaurant. The menu is a love letter to comfort: meatball subs, rigatoni bolognese, a smash burger Toronto Life raved about, and house-made milkshakes. Weekend brunch brings eggs benny and brioche French toast that you will not believe are vegan.

Grab a bar stool, order the fried chicken sandwich, and save room for lemon cake. It is casual, cozy, and the kind of place you immediately want to bring everyone back to.
