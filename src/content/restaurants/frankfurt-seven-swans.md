Seven Swans is the headline of Frankfurt's vegan scene and one of the most famous vegan restaurants in the world: the first vegan restaurant ever to earn a Michelin star, cooking a seasonal tasting menu on the Mainkai in the Altstadt. Chef Ricky Saward builds the menu from his own permaculture farm, the Braumannswiesen, with house-made miso, ferments and pickles, taking each vegetable from seed to fruit and root to flower.

It is open Wednesday to Saturday evenings only, and it is expensive, but it is also one of the great meals in Germany. Book well ahead, order the tasting menu, and let the vegetables have their moment.
