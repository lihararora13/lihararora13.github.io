Happy Maki is a vegan sushi lover's dream, an all-vegan sushi bar in North Laine that has been going since its brick-and-mortar venue opened in January 2017. The signature is the sushi burrito: a big, handheld maki roll packed with fillings like hoisin mock duck, Thai sweet potato, beet and basil, and crispy chicken, all of it made to raise awareness of ocean overfishing.

It runs on a gift-economy spirit and a loyal local following, and it holds a 5.0 on HappyCow. Order the hoisin mock duck burrito, add a second roll to share, and enjoy sushi that never needed the fish.
