High Note Cafe went fully plant-based in 2019 and is now Boise's only all-vegan restaurant, a downtown cafe in the Chandlee Building with a scratch kitchen, house-made breads, seitan, and sauces. The all-day brunch menu runs from breakfast burritos and tofu scrambles to gyros, loaded nachos, and rotating soups, with beer, wine, and cocktails alongside.

Order the vegan breakfast burrito, the house specialty, and the loaded nachos. Note the hours: closed Mondays, with dinner service Thursday through Saturday only.
