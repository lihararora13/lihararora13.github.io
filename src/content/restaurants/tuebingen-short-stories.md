Short Stories is a bowl bar on Am Lustnauer Tor, in the old town, doing build-your-own bowls and salads with vegan options, open Monday to Saturday from eleven to nine. It is the healthy counter on this list, the place for a fresh, light lunch between the old town's heavier tables, and the bowls are generous rather than precious. The room is small and modern, and the service is quick.

Order a signature bowl, add a seasonal salad, and treat it as the fresh lunch that keeps a Tübingen day moving.
