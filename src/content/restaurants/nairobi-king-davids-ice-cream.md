King David's is Nairobi's only fully vegan pick on this list, and it is a joyful one: a premium ice cream parlour doing scoops and soft serve, all plant-based, with house-made cones and gluten-free options. It is rated 5.0 on HappyCow, and it earns it.

Come in the afternoon, when it opens, and try whatever flavours are on that day. For events, order at least four days ahead, but walk-ins are the whole point.
