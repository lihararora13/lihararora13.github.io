Krsna calls itself the godfather of all the vegetarian restaurants in the vicinity, and with a 4.5 rating from 263 reviews, Main Road seems to agree. It sits in the GEL Church Complex on Main Road, it thrives on deliveries as much as dine-in, and its dosas are the dish the regulars order.

Order a masala dosa or a thali, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and in mango season ask about the aamras. It is open 10 to 10:30 daily.
