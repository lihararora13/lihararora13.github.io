The Plot's whole pitch is eating simply and letting others simply eat, and it backs it up with a genuinely zero-waste kitchen where chefs pick herbs and vegetables from their own patio garden. The menu reads like a dare: Spam kimchi bowls, meatless loaf, chorizo-topped polenta, cheesy truffle fries, and a rotating cast of seasonal dishes that reviewers consistently call unforgettable.

It is the rare place where fine-dining technique and comfort food share a table without arguing, and the garden fall dinner series under the market lights is one of the best nights out in North County. Worth the drive up the coast, and then some.
