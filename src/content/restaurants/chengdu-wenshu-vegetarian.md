Eating inside Wenshu Monastery is the Chengdu experience: a vast, affordable buffet of tofu, greens, mushrooms, noodles and faux meats, served in the calm grounds of a historic Buddhist temple. It is a meal and a visit in one.

Come for lunch, take what you like, and leave time to walk the temple grounds. The Buddhist stew with tofu and mushrooms is the dish to build your plate around.
