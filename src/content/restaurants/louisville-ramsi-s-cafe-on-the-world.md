Ramsi's Cafe on the World is a Bardstown Road cafe doing world cuisine, with a menu that runs from Middle Eastern plates to curries and a strong vegan bench. It is a long-standing Highlands favorite, and the kitchen is used to vegan requests.

Order the vegan world plate and the hummus. Ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since some dishes are finished with dairy.
