Rota Cafe is the macrobiotic heart of Tenjin: a 100 percent vegan cafe built on Kyushu's organic produce, with no refined white sugar and no additives. The lunch plate with miso soup, vegan protein, rice and salad is the move, and the vegan ice cream is a rare treat in Fukuoka.

It is closed Wednesdays and the room is generous, so it is a good group stop. Order the lunch plate and a dessert.
