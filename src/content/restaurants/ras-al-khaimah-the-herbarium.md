The Herbarium is the emirate's first dedicated vegan restaurant, inside the Pullman Marjan Island resort, and the tourism board promotes it as plant-based and thoughtfully crafted, with fresh ingredients and mindful cooking. It is open daily 8am to 11pm, and the menu runs from herbal teas to a vegan mac and cheese.

Order the vegan mac and cheese and a herbal tea. It is a resort restaurant, so expect resort prices, and it is the one table in Ras Al Khaimah that is vegan by design.
