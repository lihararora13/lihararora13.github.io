FIRKI is Udaipur's dress-up dinner: a pure-veg fine-dining room on Saheli Marg in Panchwati, where the cooking is careful, the room is calm, and the vegetarian menu is the whole point rather than an afterthought. It is the pick for a special evening in the city.

The seasonal vegetable mains are the vegan play, with dal and pulao behind them, and the kitchen is comfortable with dairy-free requests. Ask for the ghee and cream held and the meal turns properly plant-based.
