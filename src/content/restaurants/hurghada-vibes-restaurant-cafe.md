Vibes is a restaurant and cafe in Sakkala with a dedicated vegan menu, promoted with a discount in August 2026: falafel sandwiches, quesadillas, mushroom fajitas, penne arrabbiata, vegetable pizza and a colourful vegan grain bowl with chickpeas, sweet potato, red cabbage, avocado and greens.

Order the vegan grain bowl and the mushroom fajitas. It is the only dedicated vegan menu in Hurghada proper, so it earns its place near the top.
