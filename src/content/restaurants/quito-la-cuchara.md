La Cuchara turned fully vegan in 2018, and it has held a perfect HappyCow score ever since. It sits in the north of the city, a little further out than the Mariscal cluster, and it is worth the trip for a proper vegan dinner.

Order the daily special and a fresh juice. It is the northern outpost on this list, and the one to pair with a day exploring Quito's north.
