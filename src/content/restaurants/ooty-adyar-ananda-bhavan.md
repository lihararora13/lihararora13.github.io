Adyar Ananda Bhavan, the A2B at Charing Cross, is the central pure-veg dining hall and sweet house in Ooty, and the one the town's veg guides name for morning tiffin, banana-leaf lunches and Jain North Indian dinners. The ghee roast dosa is the signature, though vegans will want it without the ghee.

Order the roast without ghee and the banana-leaf meal without curd. It is the Charing Cross tiffin room, and it is open through the winter.
