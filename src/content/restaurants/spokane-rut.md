Rut is an all-vegan cafe on West 14th Avenue on the South Hill, doing burgers, sandwiches, and brunch plates with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the strongest pick in Spokane, and the room is casual, counter-service, and built for a hearty lunch.

Order the vegan burger and the breakfast sandwich. Come hungry, since the portions are large, and check the hours before a Sunday trip.
