Amara is Pasadena's Venezuelan chocolate cafe: a cafe on South Raymond Avenue in Old Pasadena serving artisanal Venezuelan chocolate, coffee and food, with vegan dishes clearly marked on the menu. The vegan arepas and the Impossible burrito are the savory picks, the plant-milk lattes and avocado toast the cafe picks, and the chocolate itself is the reason to come in the first place. It is open Monday to Friday 8am to 4pm and Saturday to Sunday 9am to 4pm.

Order a vegan arepa and a plant-milk latte. The vegan options are marked, so ordering is straightforward.
