Green is where Phoenix learned to eat vegan. Chef Damon Brasch opened the city's first vegan restaurant on 7th Street back in 2010, and the formula has barely needed changing since: American comfort food rebuilt from scratch with plant-based cheeses, mock meats and unrefined organic ingredients, served in a cheerful room that welcomes vegans and sceptics alike. The Big Wac is the burger that built the legend, the buffalo wings and thyme fries are the sides regulars fight over, and the house-made vegan soft serve is still made fresh in the kitchen every day.

Come for lunch or Sunday brunch, and bring an appetite: portions are generous and the menu is long. Its little sister bakery nami sits two doors down, so the correct move is dinner at Green followed by soft serve next door.
