Unterfreunden is a vegetarian restaurant and wine bar on the Mühlenbrücke, and it is the most ambitious vegan-friendly room in Lübeck: seasonal sharing plates built around fermented ingredients, a toasted sandwich that local guides keep photographing, and a wine list to match. The vegan options are clearly part of the cooking, and the rest of the menu can be made vegan on request, so say so when you book.

It sits by the water at the edge of the old town, open Tuesday to Sunday evenings. Come for the sharing plates, order the spätzle with celery, and ask what the kitchen can veganise that day. This is the table for a special evening in Lübeck, and the one to book ahead.
