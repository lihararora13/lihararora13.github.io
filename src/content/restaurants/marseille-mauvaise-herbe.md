Mauvaise Herbe is Marseille's special-occasion vegan table: a sleek, minimalist restaurant with a seasonally changing menu built entirely on local produce, served as a three-course set. The cooking is delicate, French and quietly ambitious, the kind of meal that makes you forget the word vegan entirely and just enjoy dinner.

It is the restaurant visitors dream about afterwards, so book ahead and come ready to be surprised by the menu. Order the set, trust the kitchen, and let Marseille's best produce do the work.
