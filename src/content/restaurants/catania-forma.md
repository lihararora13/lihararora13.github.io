Forma is a Catania bakery-cafe where a September 2025 vegan tour found savoury hummus cornetti, the kind of thing that makes a vegan breakfast in Sicily feel normal rather than improvised. It sits in the centre, a short walk from the Duomo.

Order the hummus cornetti and the savoury pastries, with a coffee, and come in the morning when the baking is fresh.
