No Bones is Krakow's vegan comfort-food kitchen, a cheerful Old Town room serving Polish classics without the meat: pierogi, vegan fish fillet, gyros, bigos and katsu curry, with vegan beer and wine on the side.

It is the hearty dinner on this list. Order the pierogi and the katsu curry, add a vegan beer, and settle in. The gyros is the sleeper hit.
