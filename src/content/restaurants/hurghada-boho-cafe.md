Boho Cafe sits at Abu Tig Marina in El Gouna, a marina cafe with hummus and falafel and a clear menu. It is a good lunch stop between the boats and the beach.

Order hummus and falafel. It is seasonal like the rest of El Gouna, so confirm hours outside the winter months.
