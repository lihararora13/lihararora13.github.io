SAMSARA is where Lima's vegan scene goes out for the evening: a fully vegan restaurant and bar, merged with Madre Planta, that takes on every Peruvian standard and wins. Ceviche, causa, tamales, lomo saltado and chaufa all get the plant-based treatment, alongside pasta, vegan seafood, burgers, wings, cocktails and desserts, all served in a lively Miraflores spot with outdoor seating.

Come for dinner and stay for a drink, because this is the rare all-vegan kitchen that works equally well as a date-night table and a group night out. The causa acevichada is the dish to order first, the lomo saltado tastes like the original, and the cocktail list means nobody has to rush home after.
