Mothers Veg Plaza is the Trivandrum restaurant that serves a full Kerala sadya on a banana leaf all year round, not just at Onam, and the reviews read like love letters: from the first pinch of salt to the last drop of payasam, pure bliss. It also claims more than a hundred dosa varieties, which is either a boast or a challenge.

Order the sadya and ask for the payasam to be skipped or made without milk, and try a dosa you have never heard of. It is the essential Trivandrum meal.
