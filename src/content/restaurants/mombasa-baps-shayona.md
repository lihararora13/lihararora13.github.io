BAPS Shayona is a vegetarian Indian restaurant on Beach Road in Nyali, with a large selection of dishes and outdoor seating. It is open six days a week, closed on Mondays, and it is the biggest vegetarian room in Mombasa.

Order a vegetable curry and specify no dairy when ordering. It is the most reliable Indian meal in Nyali.
