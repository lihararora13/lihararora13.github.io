Cinema Cafe is a medina cafe near Batha that mixes American comfort food with Moroccan classics, and vegetarians get plenty of choice, with a few solid vegan options too. It is an easy win when you are wandering the medina hungry and overstimulated.

Order a vegan option from the board and save room for dessert. It is a good first-night stop, central and unpretentious.
