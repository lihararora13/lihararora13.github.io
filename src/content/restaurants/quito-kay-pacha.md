Kay Pacha is a small vegan restaurant in La Mariscal with a perfect HappyCow score, and it does one thing beautifully: a daily vegan almuerzo, soup, main and small dessert, for a fixed price, served at lunchtime on weekdays.

Order the almuerzo, it is the whole menu and the whole point. It is the best-value vegan lunch in La Mariscal, and the one locals queue for.
