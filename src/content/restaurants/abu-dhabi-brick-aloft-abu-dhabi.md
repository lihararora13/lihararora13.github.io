Brick is the rooftop terrace kitchen at Aloft Abu Dhabi, and it has an entire section of the menu only for vegans: flame-baked vegan pizzas with vegan cheese, lentil shepherd's pie, baked sweet potato and other vegan mains. It is a bar as well as a kitchen, so it works for a long evening.

Order the vegan pizza, add the lentil shepherd's pie to share, and enjoy the terrace. It is the fun night-out pick on this list.
