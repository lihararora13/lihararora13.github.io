Bury Me Standing is a 100% vegan bagel and pastry shop on Spence Street in Cairns, open Friday to Sunday from 7am to 1pm, serving vegan bagels and pastries. It is the only fully vegan bakery in Cairns, and it is the natural first stop for any vegan visiting the city.

Order the vegan bagels and a pastry, and come on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday morning, as the shop is closed on weekdays. It is central, bright and reliable, and it suits a weekend breakfast.
