Dosa Corner is the small, simple South Indian shop that Columbus vegetarians swear by: masala dosa, idli, vada and sambar, all of it vegetarian and much of it vegan or easily made so. It is the cheapest meal on the list and one of the most satisfying.

It is a counter-service spot, not a scene, which is exactly its charm. Order the masala dosa, add idli, and enjoy the best-value vegan-friendly meal in town.
