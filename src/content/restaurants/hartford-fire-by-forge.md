Fire by Forge is a wood-fired restaurant on Broad Street in downtown Hartford, with pizza and vegetable plates that adapt to vegan orders. The oven is the draw, and the vegan pizza is the reliable pick in a room that also serves meat.

Order the vegan pizza and the roasted vegetables. Ask the server to confirm the vegan cheese and crust, since the standard pie is dairy.
