Les 4 Jeudis is a vegan bistro in the Dansaert quarter, and it sits in Brussels' HappyCow top ten for good reason. The cooking is seasonal, plant-based bistro food, served in a relaxed room that feels like a neighbourhood favourite rather than a destination.

Order the seasonal plate, ask what the soup is today, and save room for dessert. It is the quiet, confident end to the Brussels list.
