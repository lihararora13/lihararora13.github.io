The Glad Cafe is Shawlands' vegan arts venue: a performing arts space with a café whose kitchen went fully vegan in April 2022, serving Mediterranean-influenced small plates and pizza, with brunch at weekends. It is the Southside's answer to Mono and Stereo, a little calmer, a little more grown-up, and just as good.

Come for a show, stay for dinner, or come for the weekend brunch. Order the small plates, add a pizza, and enjoy the Southside at its best.
