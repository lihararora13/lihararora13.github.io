The Harbour is a seafood restaurant and takeaway on Sutton Harbour, and it would not normally belong on a vegan list, except that its vegan menu is genuinely good: banana-blossom fish and chips, oyster-mushroom squid rings and a Moving Mountains burger, all verified on the current menu.

Order the banana-blossom fish and chips with mushy peas, and the salt-and-pepper squid rings to start. It is the best vegan chip-shop experience in the city.
