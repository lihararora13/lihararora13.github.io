Mo Mo is Brussels' Tibetan dumpling spot, and it went fully vegan in August 2023. The Saint-Gilles branch serves two kinds of dumplings, steamed and fried, plus soup, in a simple room that lets the food do the talking.

It is a four-and-a-half-star HappyCow favourite and one of the city's most-loved vegan kitchens. Order both kinds of dumplings, add a soup, and do not rush: the dumplings are made to be savoured.
