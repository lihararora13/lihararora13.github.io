New Alka has been feeding Old Padra Road since 2012, a 100% vegetarian multicuisine dining room where the must-order is the rava masala dosa. It is a big, busy, family kind of place, the sort where three generations share a table and nobody is in a hurry.

Vegans do well here with the South Indian side and the dal and pulao, asking for cream and ghee held. A few recent reviews grumble about service and consistency, so come for the dosa and the classics rather than expecting fine dining.
