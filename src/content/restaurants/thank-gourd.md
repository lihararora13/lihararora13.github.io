Why we love it — Thank Gourd's name gets a groan, but the food does not.
This all-vegan kitchen in Versova's Aaram Nagar neighborhood leans into
pan-Asian fusion, think Thai curries, bibimbap-style bowls, and tempura, with
pasta and flatbreads for the Italian half of the menu. The cozy room with
outdoor seating has become an Andheri weeknight staple.

The beetroot and tofu ravioli is the order that converts skeptics. Ask the
staff for recommendations; the regulars say Anurag never misses.
