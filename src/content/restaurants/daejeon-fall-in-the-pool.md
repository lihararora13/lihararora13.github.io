Fall in the Pool is a dessert cafe in Yuseong-gu, Daejeon, where all the desserts are vegan and most drinks can be made vegan. Reviewers in 2026 call it a lifesaver in a city where vegans otherwise struggle, praising the matcha cheesecake, Dubai chocolate pudding and tiramisu pudding, and the corn cream latte. One recent review notes the menu has narrowed to one vegan cake at a time, so the choice on the day may be small, but what is there is fully vegan.

Order the matcha cheesecake if it is on, the Dubai chocolate pudding, and a corn cream latte made vegan. It is the strongest vegan pick in Daejeon and the natural first stop for anyone arriving in the city.
