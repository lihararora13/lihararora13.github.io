Mesopotamia is a vegetarian and vegan Middle Eastern restaurant on the Metgebergasse, doing falafel, hummus and vegan döner in a small old-town room. It opens in the evening, five to eleven on weekdays and noon to ten on Sundays, which makes it one of the few vegan-friendly dinner options in the old town that is reliably open every night. The food is straightforward and generous, and the falafel plate is the dish to order.

Come for dinner, order a falafel plate with hummus and a vegan döner to share, and treat it as the easy evening meal after a day on the Danube.
