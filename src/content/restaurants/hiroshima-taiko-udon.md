Taiko Udon is the udon stop in this guide, and the one that thought hardest about vegans: the soup in udon is usually made with dashi fish stock, but Taiko created a plant-based version, and all the vegan dishes are listed on a separate menu. The picks are the vegan oroshi bukkake udon, the fried tofu udon, the soy-milk dandan, and the sides, sweet potato tempura, deep-fried eggplant and lotus root croquettes, with toppings like deep-fried tofu, wakame, yuzu and daikon.

The noodles are handmade with natural ingredients and no additives, the staff are friendly about vegan requests, and the room is open for lunch and dinner, closed on Mondays. The broth is rich and a little seaweed-forward, which is exactly the point.
