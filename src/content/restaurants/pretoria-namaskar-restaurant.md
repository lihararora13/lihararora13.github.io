Namaskar is a long-running Indian restaurant in Colbyn with a wide range of vegetarian and vegan starters and main courses, and dishes can be made mild, medium or hot to suit your taste. It also offers meals with no onion and garlic on request.

Order a spread of vegan starters followed by a vegetable curry. It is open Monday to Saturday until 10:00pm and Sundays until 3:00pm.
