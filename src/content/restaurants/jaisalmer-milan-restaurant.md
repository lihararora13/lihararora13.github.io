Milan sits on Fort Road opposite Hanuman Circle, a pure-veg restaurant that does something useful for this list: Jain gravies on request, which means a kitchen already comfortable cooking without onion, garlic and dairy. That makes it one of the easiest vegan orders in Jaisalmer.

Ask for a Jain veg gravy and a dal tadka without ghee, and the meal is properly plant-based. It is a good stop for travellers heading to or from the fort.
