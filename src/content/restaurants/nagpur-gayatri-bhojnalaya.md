Gayatri Bhojnalaya has been serving Nagpur the same honest Maharashtrian thali since 1972, and the room still fills up at lunch with regulars who have been coming for decades. It is a simple two-floor dining hall behind Sudama Theater, with unlimited thalis for about 200 rupees and a 4.9 star rating across more than 8,000 reviews.

The thali is the order: roti, dal, rice, two sabzis and salad, with the curd left off for a fully plant-based plate. Come on a weekday to skip the weekend queue, and come hungry.
