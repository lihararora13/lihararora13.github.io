Galani Coffee is a cafe in Jacros on Salite Mehret Road with clearly marked vegan dishes: a smoothie bowl with muesli and fruit, tabbouleh, lentil salad, a beet and avocado sandwich, and fresh juices and smoothies. Above the cafe is a cultural space for Ethiopian art, crafts and events.

It is open Tuesday to Sunday from 8:00am to 8:00pm and takes cash only. Order the smoothie bowl or the beet and avocado sandwich.
