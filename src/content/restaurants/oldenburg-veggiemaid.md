Veggiemaid is Oldenburg's fully vegan café and bakery, and it is the best first stop in the city. Vegan Berliner, cheesecake, quiche and burgers come out of a small, bright room on the Damm, all baked fresh and all plant-based. The bakery counter is the reason regulars keep coming back, and the café also hosts the city's vegan meetup, which tells you what kind of room this is.

It sits a short walk from the city centre, open weekday mornings to early evening and Saturday until mid-afternoon. Order a Berliner and a slice of cheesecake, add a quiche if you want lunch, and take your time. This is the table that shows Oldenburg's vegan scene at its best.
