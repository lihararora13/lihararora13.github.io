Benne Bhavan is the divine veg restaurant on 2nd Cross East in Thillainagar, named on the September 2026 list of the city's top five veg breakfast spots, and praised by the commenters for its dosa. Benne means butter, which is the house signature, so vegans should order the dosa plain and ask for the butter to be held.

Order the dosa without benne and an idli, and drink the filter coffee. It is the Thillainagar breakfast the locals defend, and the dosa is the reason.
