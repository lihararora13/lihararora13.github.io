Asian Herbivore fills the one gap Joburg's vegan scene had: proper, high-energy Asian cooking with no compromises. Rated 4.5 on HappyCow, it serves dumplings, baos, ramen and stir-fries that are just excellent Asian food that happens to be vegan, generous portions, bold flavours, and a neon-lit room that makes it the place to be seen on a Friday night.

It is the trendiest table on this list and the one to book for a date night. Order the spicy ramen, the vegan mi goreng, a plate of light-as-air tofu bao buns, and the dumplings to start. It gets busy on weekends, so book ahead.
