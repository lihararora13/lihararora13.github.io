Jardin Vegetariano is a self-service cafeteria inside the Via Veneto shopping centre in El Cangrejo, about 100 metres from Via Espana, run for years by a Taiwanese family. The menu mixes Asian and Panamanian cooking, with more than 30 preparations including soy proteins, vegetables, plantain, potatoes, rice dishes, soups and salads, and you pay by the portion.

Order the vegetable ball, the baked empanadas and a soup, with a fresh orange or carrot juice. It is inexpensive, open every day except Sunday, and one of the oldest vegetarian options in the city.
