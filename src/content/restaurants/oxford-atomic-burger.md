Atomic Burger is Cowley Road's over-the-top American diner: comic-book walls, huge burgers, bigger sides and a wide vegetarian selection with endless topping combinations. It won the local best veggie burger contest, and the veggie patty with a soft warm bun is the dish to order.

Order a veggie burger with sweet potato fries and an onion ring stack. It is a meat-serving burger joint with a strong veggie bench, so file it as vegan-friendly, and come hungry, because the portions are famously large.
