The Bissara Shop is a tiny medina shop serving one dish, bissara, the thick fava-bean soup eaten with bread, and HappyCow rates it 5.0, the highest vegan score in Morocco. The dish is vegan by nature, fava beans, olive oil and bread, and the shop serves little else.

Order the bissara with bread and olive oil, and a mint tea. It is a breakfast and a lesson in one bowl: the simplest food in Fes is also the best vegan food in Fes.
