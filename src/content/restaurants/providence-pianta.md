PiANTA is the restaurant that changed how Providence thinks about vegan food. Opened in 2021 on Atwells Avenue, the heart of Federal Hill, it takes Italian-American comfort dishes and rebuilds them entirely from plants: steak and cheese egg rolls with chipotle aioli, penne alla vodka, ziti and broccolini in a four-cheese sauce, plus brunch waffles on weekends.

Reviewers call it the best vegan food in the country, and non-vegans keep coming back for the egg rolls. Book for dinner, order the egg rolls to share, and save room for the banana bread pudding.
