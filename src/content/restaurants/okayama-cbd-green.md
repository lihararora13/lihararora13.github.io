CBD Green is a fully vegan cafe that also retails CBD products, open since 2021 near Okayama Station, and HappyCow reports it fully vegan since May 2023. The kitchen serves two daily set meals plus ice cream, coffee and beer, with bento boxes on weekday lunchtimes, and the room turns into a hookah lounge at night. It moved from its original Honmachi address to Kuwadacho, so older guides send people to the wrong street.

Come for the daily set at lunch, when the bento boxes are also running, and try the vegan ice cream. It is open daily from 11:30am to 8pm, takes cards, and has free Wi-Fi, which makes it a useful base as well as a meal.
