Cavalier Marino is a vegetarian and vegan cafe and restaurant in the Albaro district, open from seven in the morning to one at night, and it is the all-day option on this list. The kitchen does vegetarian and vegan cooking with organic products: focaccia, paccheri, ravioli and a vegan tiramisu.

Order the paccheri, the ravioli and the tiramisu, and come for a long lunch or a late dinner, since the hours run far past every other pick on this list.
