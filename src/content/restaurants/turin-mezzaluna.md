Mezzaluna offers vegan and organic cooking in a 19th-century building in the heart of the Quadrilatero Romano, with a terrace on the piazza in summer. The kitchen works from Piedmontese traditions and looks outward too, with a macrobiotic eye for balanced, seasonal plates: agnolotti del plin, culurgiones, seitan, bagna cauda and fried polenta.

It is open for lunch and dinner most days, closed on Sunday evenings and Monday evenings, so check the day before you go. The agnolotti del plin is the dish to order first.
