Sushimar Vegano proves vegan sushi is not a compromise, it is its own art form: inventive, beautifully fresh rolls and delicate tofu dishes in lovely Jardim Paulista. Closed Mondays, with lunch and dinner services the rest of the week.

One useful warning from every guide: the non-vegan Sushimar sits a few doors down at number 1307 on the same street. Head for 1287, the vegan one, and enjoy the wander around Jardins afterwards.
