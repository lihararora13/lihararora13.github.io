Planet Terra is the kind of neighbourhood cafe every neighbourhood wishes it had. Everything is made in-house, organic and seasonal, with no artificial sweeteners, refined sugars or GMOs, and the menu swings happily from comfort food to clean eating without ever feeling preachy about it.

The tofu makhani and yasai ramen are the dishes regulars swear by, creamy and rich in a way that makes you double-check there really is no dairy involved. Come for an easy all-day meal, stay for a cold-pressed juice, and treat it as your base camp for a Greens-side wander.
