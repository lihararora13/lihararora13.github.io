Padmashree Hotel sits at the Pumpwell junction on the Bangalore highway, which makes it the natural first or last veg stop for anyone driving in or out of Mangaluru. The restaurant is pure veg, and the tiffin is the standard coastal spread: dosa, idli, meals.

Order the masala dosa and ask for it without ghee. It is a highway-junction hotel restaurant, so set expectations accordingly, but it is open, reliable and vegetarian to the core.
