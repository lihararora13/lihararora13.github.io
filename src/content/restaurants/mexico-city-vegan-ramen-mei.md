A fully vegan ramen bar sounds like a niche, until you taste the broth. Vegan Ramen Mei makes its noodles in-house, builds deep tonkotsu-style and shoyu bowls, and tops HappyCow's Mexico City list with a perfect score for it.

The Condesa shop on Tamaulipas is the one to aim for. Go for a Red Tonkotsu, add the orange chicken everyone raves about, and finish with a Japanese dessert. Comfort in a bowl, no compromises.
