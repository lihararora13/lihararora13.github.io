Extra Extra is Buffalo's vegan-friendly pizza and wine bar: a small room on West Utica Street where the pizzas come with vegan cheese, vegan pepperoni and vegetables of choice, and the garlic cheese bread has a vegan version too. It is a natural-wine-bar atmosphere with a short, focused menu, and it suits an evening slice and a glass better than a big group dinner.

Hours are Monday to Thursday 5pm to 10pm and Friday to Saturday noon to 10pm, closed Sundays. Build your own pie with vegan cheese and pepperoni, and add the garlic cheese bread.
