Soul Garden Bistro made the list for a reason. A multi-cuisine Alwarpet bistro with a strong explicit vegan menu, from BBQ tofu to pasta arrabbiata and vegan ice creams. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Alwarpet, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the BBQ tofu, Zucchini chips, Pasta arrabbiata if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
