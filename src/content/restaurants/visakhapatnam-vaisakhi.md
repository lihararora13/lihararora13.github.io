Vaisakhi is the Andhra restaurant inside Hotel Dasapalla at Jagadamba Junction, and a March 2026 family video put it on its list for the Andhra thali, the crispy dosa and the starters, with ras malai for dessert. It is the hotel's regional room, and the thali is the dish that justifies the visit.

Order the Andhra thali without curd and the crispy dosa without ghee, and skip the ras malai. It is the best Andhra veg meal in the city centre.
