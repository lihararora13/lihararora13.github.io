Jajaja brought its New York plant-based street-food swagger to Wynwood and Miami said muchas gracias. Zero animal products, maximum flavor: watermelon stands in for tuna, coconut becomes queso, and the birria tacos will convert the most skeptical meat eater at your table.

The room is pure vacation vibes with latino music, playful cocktails, and a serious tequila and mezcal lineup. Recent reviews keep raving about the birria tacos and the spicy margaritas, so plan on staying a while.
