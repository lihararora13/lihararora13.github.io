V-Grand Restaurant is a vegetarian and vegan restaurant in Tambon Nang Lae, offering a variety of vegan options including pad Thai, tofu dishes, soy meat options, rice dishes and noodle dishes. It is an ovo-vegetarian kitchen, so vegans should ask about egg, but the vegan dishes are a real part of the menu.

Order the pad Thai and the tofu dishes, and ask for no egg. It is a little out of the centre, and its pin is approximate to Nang Lae.
