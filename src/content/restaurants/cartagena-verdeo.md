Verdeo is the Cartagena outpost of the mini-chain established in Medellin, adapted to local ingredients: bowls built on native grains and vegetables, cold-pressed juices, and no processed substitutes. Here the produce leans Caribbean, with name, yuca, ahuyama, corozo and guanabana doing the work that Andean grains do up in Medellin.

A lunch bowl with juice runs about COP 24,000-38,000, the WiFi is good, and it has become a digital nomad favourite in Getsemani. Order a bowl, add a corozo juice, and you have the most reliable vegan lunch in the neighbourhood.
