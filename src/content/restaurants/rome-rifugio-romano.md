Rifugio Romano is the pick every 2026 Rome vegan guide leads with, and one plate explains why. This is a proper Roman trattoria in every way except one: the whole menu is vegan. The carbonara, cacio e pepe and amatriciana are the dishes visitors come back for, the cannelloni and seasonal pastas round out a menu built on forty years of kitchen experience, and the tiramisu closes the argument.

It feels like a classic neighbourhood restaurant because it is one, just one where you never have to ask what's safe to eat. Gluten-free options are strong, the room is warm and unpretentious, and it sits a short walk from Termini, which makes it the easiest first dinner in the city. Book ahead on weekends.
