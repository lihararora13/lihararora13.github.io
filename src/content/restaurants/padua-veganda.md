Veganda is a bistrot that is 100% plant-based, right beside Prato della Valle, sitting between a proper restaurant and a fast-casual spot. The menu reworks Italian tradition in vegan form, and local vegan guides rate it a solid stop even when they score it below the city's best.

Order the vegan Italian classics of the day, the plant-based pasta and the vegan desserts, and come for a relaxed lunch by the Prato.
