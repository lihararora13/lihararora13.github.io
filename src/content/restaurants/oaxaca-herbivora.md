Herbívora sits in Jalatlaco, Oaxaca's most colourful neighbourhood, and it cooks the way the neighbourhood looks: bright, fresh and full of life. Bowls, salads and healthy plates, all vegan, all made from local ingredients.

Order a bowl and a fresh juice, then walk Jalatlaco's painted streets. It is the prettiest lunch on this list, and the freshest.
