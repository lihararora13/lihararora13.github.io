Yafel is Marseille's chickpea temple: a vegetarian street-food spot where everything revolves around the humble chickpea, served as falafel, in pitas, salads and quinoa bowls, with a hummus bar offering four flavours to dip with toasted or fried pita. The vegan options are clearly marked and plentiful, and the fried sweet potatoes and panisses are the sides to fight over.

It is fast, cheap and cheerful, right by Cours Julien. Order a falafel pita, work through the hummus bar, and finish with the chia pudding. Street food, done right.
