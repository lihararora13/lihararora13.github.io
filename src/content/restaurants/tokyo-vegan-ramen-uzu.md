Only in Tokyo would one of the city's best vegan ramen bowls come with a side of immersive digital art. UZU, which started in Kyoto, serves its plant-based ramen at teamLab Planets, where you can eat surrounded by swirling calligraphy and light after wading through the exhibits.

The miso bowl is rich and warming, the Tokyo-only specials are worth the detour, and the vegan ice cream makes a very civilised finish. Book the museum, keep an appetite, and let lunch become part of the artwork.
