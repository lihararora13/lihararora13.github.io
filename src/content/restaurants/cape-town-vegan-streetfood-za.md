Vegan Streetfood started as a roving truck and now has a fixed home at the SALT Food Market in the City Centre, which makes it the easiest plant-based fast food in the middle of town. Cape Town Tourism points you here for loaded wraps, burgers and sandwiches, and the truck still pops up at markets and festivals.

The pulled jackfruit burger is the headline: smoky, spicy and properly hearty, with biodegradable packaging for the planet points. Check their food truck calendar if you want to catch them out and about instead.
