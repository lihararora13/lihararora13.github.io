Sai Kung is Hong Kong's breezy seaside escape, all seafood restaurants and hiking trails, and 2084 is the plant-based pioneer that made sure vegans get a table in the square too. The whole menu is vegan, tapas-style, and built for sharing under the open sky.

Graze through Peking-style oyster mushroom tacos, momos, spicy mapo tofu and meatballs in rose harissa, with vegan beer and wine if the afternoon is going that way. It is the detour pick of this guide, and worth every minute of the journey out.
