Yxi's Arepas and Gordito is an arepa restaurant on Taughannock Boulevard on the Ithaca waterfront, doing vegan arepas and gorditas. It is the Latin pick on this list, and the vegan arepa is the reliable order.

Order the vegan arepa and the plantains, and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation of the gordita, since the standard version uses cheese.
