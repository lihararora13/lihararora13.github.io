Venn Canteen is a modern vegan canteen in the heart of Porto, cooking seasonal plates that change with the market. It is the kind of place where the menu is short because everything on it is good that day.

Order the daily special and a dessert. It is a calm, bright lunch stop between the busier, louder tables on this list, and a good place to plan the rest of your day.
