Careys Bay Historic Hotel is a historic pub and seafood restaurant at 17 Macandrew Road in Careys Bay, rated 4.0 on HappyCow, and it is the only place in this guide with a full separate vegan menu. The vegan menu runs to battered banana blossom fish and chips with vegan tartare, a tempura vegetable main with rice and miso soup, jackfruit roti with hummus, coleslaw and vegan aioli, Thai vegetable curry, an all-day vegan breakfast, and vegan desserts including sticky date pudding with coconut ice-cream and a poached pear. The hotel's own current menu page lists the same vegan dishes, and it labels its vegan wines and beers as well.

It is a drive out of the city centre, about 15 minutes to the harbour at Port Chalmers, and reservations are required. One warning: there is no dedicated vegan fryer, so the fried dishes share the fryer with fish and meat; strict vegans should favour the soup, the jackfruit roti, the curry and the desserts, and ask the staff, who know the vegan menu well.
