Ike's is Cincinnati's answer to the question of whether barbecue can be vegan: yes, and smoky. The Evanston restaurant runs a fully plant-based menu built around the smoker, with jackfruit ribs, vegan oxtails, mushroom chicken and Salisbury steak, plus the sides that make a barbecue plate, creamy vegan mac and cheese, green beans and peach cobbler. Reviewers keep coming back to the ribs and the cobbler, and the portions are generous.

Ike's opens in the afternoon, Tuesday to Sunday from 1:30pm to 8:30pm, and is closed Mondays. It is takeout-friendly and family-friendly, and the dining room is warm and unpretentious. Order the rib dinner with mac and cheese and save room for dessert.
