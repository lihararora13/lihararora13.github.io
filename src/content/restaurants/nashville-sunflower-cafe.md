Look for the giant sunflower in Berry Hill and you have found one of Nashville's happiest lunch spots. Sunflower Cafe serves everything cafeteria-style from a cute house with a big patio, and City Cast confirms the whole menu at both the cafe and its Donelson Bakehouse sibling is vegan.

Burgers, wraps, potato bowls, nachos and daily specials like the Moroccan chickpea bowl rotate through, with separate menus for gluten-free, soy-free and oil-free diners, so nobody gets left with the sad salad. The Infatuation rates it, locals adore it, and the resident cats may say hello on the porch.
