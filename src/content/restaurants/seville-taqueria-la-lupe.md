Taquería la Lupe is the Alameda's Mexican spot, and its vegan menu is a proper menu, not an afterthought. Tacos, gorditas and huaraches made with Beyond Meat, in a lively room that fills up fast on weekend nights.

Order tacos across the table and share. It is the late-night, loud, fun dinner on this list, and the vegan options are as good as the regular ones.
