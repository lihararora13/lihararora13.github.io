Dar El Medina is a riad restaurant in the heart of the medina, a sumptuous house with an elegant patio and Moroccan cooking. It is famous for chicken couscous, but the vegan options, salads and fruit parfait, are there for those who ask.

Order a vegetable couscous and the salads, and finish with the fruit parfait. It is the grandest room in the medina, so book ahead.
