Banhmi Bandits is a vegan-friendly bánh mì counter on Ny Banegårdsgade in the Midtbyen, near the station, and it is the fast lunch on this page. The bánh mì is the signature, with spring rolls alongside, all of it quick and all of it cheap.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so confirm the vegan fillings at the counter. The bánh mì is the order.
