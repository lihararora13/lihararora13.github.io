Basic Bistro is a long-running bistro on Church Street with a vegetarian and vegan menu available on request. Vegan options include a veggie burger, stir fry and more, and the kitchen is used to adapting dishes.

It is open daily until 10:30pm, which makes it a useful late option in the centre. Ask for the vegan menu and order the veggie burger or the stir fry. Note the pin is at street level on Church Street.
