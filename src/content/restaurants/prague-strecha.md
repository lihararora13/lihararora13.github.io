Strecha is the rare restaurant where the story is as good as the lunch. A social enterprise run on cooperative principles, it employs people coming out of homelessness or prison, and it plates up Czech comfort food with real care: vegan schnitzel, goulash, potato dumplings and potato salad, plus fair-trade coffee and local draught beer.

Come for the daily lunch menu, which is famously good value, and stay for dessert. The vegan cakes are a proper draw, and the whole room runs on the warm idea that good food and a fair chance belong on the same table.
