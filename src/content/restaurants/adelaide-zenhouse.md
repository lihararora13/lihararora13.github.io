Zenhouse made the list for a reason. A long-running plant-based favourite in West Hindmarsh, doing yum cha-style favourites and nourishing Asian-inspired dishes since 2006. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in West Hindmarsh, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan yum cha, Tofu stir-fry, Noodle soup if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
