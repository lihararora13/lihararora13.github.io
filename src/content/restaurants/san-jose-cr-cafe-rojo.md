Cafe Rojo is a vegan-friendly cafe in Barrio Amon specialising in Vietnamese-Costa Rican fusion, with a plant-filled room, old-school architecture and friendly English-speaking staff. You start with a vegan base, a bowl, sandwich, salad or healthy casado, then add extras: the vegan toppings on the current menu are shiitake and caramelised eggplant, while trout, chicken and pork toppings are also on the board, so say you want the vegan build.

Order the banh mi with shiitake or eggplant. The official site is live and reviews run through October 2026, and the cafe is still one of the best vegan-friendly spots in San Jose. Do not stay out late downtown after dark.
