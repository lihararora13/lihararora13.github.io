The Food Co-op is a Canberra institution: a member-owned shop and cafe that sells bulk foods, fresh produce and plant-based goodies, and serves a simple, honest lunch in the middle of it all. The cafe plates are vegan-friendly and dairy-free friendly, the baked goods are house-made, and every drink can be made with dairy-free milk. On Thursday evenings during semester the place turns into dinner, drinks and live music.

It is the kind of place where you come for lunch and leave with a bag of bulk oats and a new favourite snack. Come for the daily lunch, stay for the shop, and check the Thursday evening programme if you are around.
