Sucursal Vegana, also listed as La Sucursal Vegana, is a 5.0-rated, 100% vegan burger joint in San Antonio, and it is the fun end of Cali's scene: burgers, salchipapas, seitan and hot dogs, served Wednesday to Sunday from the afternoon into the night. The Burger Tentacion is the dish to order first.

Come in the evening, order the burger with potato wedges, and add the salchipapas if you are sharing. It is casual, loud and cheap, and exactly what a vegan burger joint should be.
