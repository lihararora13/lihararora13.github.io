Chay Vegan Asian Kitchen has been a steady Altstadt favourite since 2021: a fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant doing bowls, tapas and larger plates with care and consistency. It is the kind of place you can rely on for a good dinner any night of the week, and the room is calm enough for a proper conversation.

Order a noodle bowl, add a couple of tapas to share, and enjoy one of the most reliable vegan kitchens in the city centre.
