Leopold's is Savannah's ice cream institution, a parlour on Broughton Street that labels its vegan flavours on the menu. The sundaes and banana splits are the way to eat them, and the room itself, all chrome and history, is half the pleasure.

It is the sweet stop on this list, and it gets busy on weekend evenings. Ask which flavours are vegan, and get a sundae.
