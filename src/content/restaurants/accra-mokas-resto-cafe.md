Moka's is a Lebanese resto-cafe in La, open noon to late seven days a week, and its falafel platter is the vegan order. The menu mixes grills and pizzas with mezze, so vegans should stick to the falafel, hummus and salads, but there is plenty there.

Come for a relaxed lunch or a late dinner, order the falafel platter to share, and add hummus and bread. It is the Lebanese counterweight to Accra's Ghanaian vegan spots.
