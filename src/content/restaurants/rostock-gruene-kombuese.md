Grune Kombuse is a vegan restaurant in the old town serving regional German dishes from natural, regionally grown ingredients, with a seasonally changing menu plus a dish of the day and a daily lunch deal. Choices run to carrot lox with horseradish, jackfruit fritters with pumpkin, and tagliatelle in madeira-miso-cashew cream, and it is rated 4.9 from 98 reviews.

Order the carrot lox, the jackfruit fritters and the tagliatelle in madeira-miso-cashew cream, and come for the lunch deal Tuesday to Friday or the long Saturday service.
