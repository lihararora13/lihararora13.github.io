Portia's Cafe is Clintonville's vegan cafe: a warm, neighbourhood room doing sandwiches, bowls, smoothies and baked goods, all of it plant-based and all of it made to be eaten slowly. It is the quieter sibling of the High Street heavyweights, and the one locals keep for themselves.

It is the lunch stop for the neighbourhood, and the baked goods are the reason to come back. Order a sandwich, add a bowl, and save room for something sweet.
