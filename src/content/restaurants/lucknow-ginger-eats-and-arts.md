Ginger Eats & Arts is the kind of cafe where the art on the walls matters as much as the food on the plate. Tucked into Mahanagar Extension, it keeps vegan milks behind the coffee bar and a menu that can be made vegan on request, which is exactly the flexibility a plant-based traveller needs in Lucknow.

Order the hummus platter and a vegan pasta, and take the oat milk latte with it. It is a good work-from-cafe spot too, calm, unhurried, and happy to have you stay a while.
