Staazi & Co tops the list for a reason. A small, family-owned vegan Greek street-food eatery, voted one of the best vegan food trucks in the world by Lonely Planet. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Adelaide CBD, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan yiros, Loaded AB, Greek salad if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
