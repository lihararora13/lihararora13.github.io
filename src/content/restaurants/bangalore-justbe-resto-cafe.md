JustBe is the table every Bangalore vegan guide agrees on: the city's top-ranked plant-based café, set in an 80-year-old heritage house in leafy Sadashivanagar. The founding principles are printed right on the philosophy, healthy, natural, vegan, whole and guilt-free, and the kitchen lives up to them: no refined flour, no refined sugar, no preservatives, produce sourced locally, and the nut milks, vegan cheeses and breads all made in-house. The menu wanders happily between Italian, Mexican, Mediterranean and desi, and there is a sound-healing room and a little store upstairs if you want to linger.

Recent visitors in 2025 call it one of the best plant-based spots in the city, and the dining room still fills up for its festival menus and weekend brunches. Order the beetroot hummus platter, the methi nachos with tulsi salsa and almond cheese dip, a superfood bowl, and save room for the choc chia pudding. Come hungry, come unhurried, and bring a friend who thinks vegan food is all salad.
