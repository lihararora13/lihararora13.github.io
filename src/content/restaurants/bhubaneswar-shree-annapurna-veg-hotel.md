Shree Annapurna is the budget hero of this list: a veg hotel on Cuttack Road opposite the Bomikhal Durga Mandap, where a regular veg thali costs 100 rupees and almost everything on the menu sits under 150. Local food bloggers call it the best budget veg hotel in the city, and its regulars rate it a full 5 out of 5.

The veg pulao and the veg biryani are the dishes the bloggers recommend, and the mushroom thali is good value too. Ask for no ghee and no paneer and the thali stays plant-based. It is on Swiggy and Zomato, and it is open noon to 11.
