Mercado Verde is Montevideo's healthy-eating hub: a health shop with a juice bar and kitchen, serving vegetarian and vegan food with raw options, fresh juices and vegan pastries. All the prepared food is vegan, even though the shop also sells vegetarian items, so it is a safe and happy place to eat. The Punta Carretas branch sits in one of the city's prettiest neighbourhoods.

Come for breakfast or lunch, order a raw bowl and a fresh juice, and browse the shop. This is the one for a bright, healthy start to the day.
