Chutnej is the treat-yourself stop in Letna, a cozy two-floor restaurant near Letenska Plan park where the menu has grown fancier and more creative over time. Think lion's mane mushroom cooked like a steak, reimagined Czech dishes, daily brunch and shared dinner menus, with desserts and pastries from its sister bakery Krafin.

Come for a slow brunch or a calm evening meal. The room is airy and plant-filled, the ingredients are thoughtful, and it is the pick when you want Prague vegan cooking at its most inventive.
