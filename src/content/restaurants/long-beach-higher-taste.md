Higher Taste is downtown's plant-based cafe: bright, casual, and built around breakfast bowls, smoothies and sandwiches that taste like a fresh start. Formerly known as Zephyr Cafe, it is the downtown stop for a light, wholesome meal before a day at the beach or the Aquarium.

It is the gentlest meal on the list, and the one to hit when you want something clean and quick. Order a breakfast bowl, grab a smoothie, and take on the day.
