Grains, or Miho's Kitchen, is the most flexible dedicated vegan option in Nagoya: a cozy eatery near Nagoya Station built around Japanese grains, with rice bowls, veggie burgers, tofu dishes and cakes. Dinner after 5pm needs a reservation, by phone or Instagram DM.

Order a rice bowl for lunch, or book ahead for dinner. It is the handiest vegan stop if you are passing through the station.
