Restaurante Vegetariano Cañadú is a vegetarian restaurant on Plaza de la Merced, next door to Byoko, with a vegan selection across the menu. It is a reliable daytime and evening room.

Order the seasonal plates and a buddha bowl. A good fallback if Byoko is full.
