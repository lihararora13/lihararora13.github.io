Shou-Yu Vegetarian Buffet is a pay-by-weight buffet on Zhongxiao 1st Road in Xinxing, part of a chain with locations in Pingtung, Taipei, Taichung and Taitung, and it is the big lunch table of this guide: 35 to 50 dishes, mostly cooked, with sprouts, greens, seaweed, roots, gourds, fungi, noodles, fried rice and soups, plus pot-sticker dumplings, oven-baked onion cakes and salads. The kitchen uses no alcohol or eggs in its cooking, the price is by weight at about NT$100 to NT$135 for a large plate, and a free iced sweet black tea comes with the meal. Dine in or take away.

It is listed as vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, and with a warning worth heeding: a current guide notes that some dishes may contain eggs despite the no-egg claim, and that dishes labelled vegan were not always vegan, so check with the staff if you are unsure. The staff are not always sure about ingredients themselves, so ask specifically. Open Monday to Sunday, 10:00am to 1:30pm and 4:00pm to 8:00pm. Cash only.
