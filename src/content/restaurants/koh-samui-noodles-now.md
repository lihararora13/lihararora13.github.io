Noodles Now is a fully vegan restaurant and bar in Bo Phut, rated 4.5 from 48 HappyCow reviews, and it is the only noodle specialist in this guide. The menu is regional noodle dishes, Thai street food snacks and Thai-inspired sweets, with everything clearly labelled, and it was reported fully vegan in July 2023. It is a bar as well as a restaurant, so it works as an evening stop rather than a lunch-only cafe.

It sits at 9/62 Moo 2 in Bophut, in the Fisherman's Village area, a short walk from Penne. Order a noodle bowl first and save room for the sweets, which are the part of the menu other Samui vegan rooms do not cover.
