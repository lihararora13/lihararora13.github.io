The Vegetarian Health Food Center is one of Accra's older veg institutions, a simple health-food spot near the 37 Military Hospital junction. The food is straightforward vegetarian cooking with vegan plates available, and it is open weekday lunchtimes.

This is a practical lunch stop rather than a destination dinner: go midday, order the plate of the day, and ask which dishes are vegan.
