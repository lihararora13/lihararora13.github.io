Penelope's was the first all-vegan taqueria in Chicago, and it still
tastes like a place with something to prove. The soy al pastor tacos
are the headline act, the quesabirria dunks beautifully, and the
salsas are bright and punchy. Everything is plant-based, the room is
colorful and loud in the best way, and the prices are easy.

Reviewers call these the best vegan tacos in Chicago, full stop. With
three locations across the city, there is genuinely no excuse to miss
it.
