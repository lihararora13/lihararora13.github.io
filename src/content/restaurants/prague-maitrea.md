Maitrea has been Prague's calm vegetarian heart for years, a two-floor dining room of carved wood and quiet corners just off Old Town Square. The menu is fully vegetarian with many vegan dishes, from international plates to Czech classics like svickova and black beer goulash, and vegans should ask which dishes can be made fully plant-based.

Book ahead at peak times, and note the kitchen break in the late afternoon. Order the Maitrea selection for two as a sharing starter, then a creamy peanut curry or vegan udon, and you will see why this is the Old Town table locals keep returning to.
