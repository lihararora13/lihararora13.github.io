Legumi proves vegan sushi is not a compromise, it is a party. HappyCow rates it 5.0 from hundreds of reviews, VegNews puts it second in the city, and reviewers rave about the sheer variety: rolls, boxes and full sushi boats for sharing, with plant-based salmon and shrimp that genuinely surprise people.

Everything is made by hand to order, portions are generous, and the family-run warmth is part of the charm. It opens for dinner only, Monday to Saturday, so plan an evening in Santos and arrive hungry, the boat is bigger than you think.
