Florencia is the homestyle heart of Barranco's vegan scene: a small, colorful, fully vegan restaurant and cafe in a beautiful old house, where the cooking feels personal and the menu covers the whole day. Breakfasts, lunches, dinners, desserts and vegan ice cream all get the same careful treatment, from soups and pastas to sandwiches and a bakery case, with gluten-free and organic dishes throughout.

Come for a slow brunch or a relaxed dinner, because this is the rare vegan kitchen that feels like eating at a friend's house. The causa furai, fried potato cake with mushroom ceviche, is the signature to order, the carrot cake is the dessert to save room for, and the açaí bowls make a fine start to a Barranco wander.
