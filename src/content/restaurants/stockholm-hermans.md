Hermans is the Stockholm classic every guide agrees on: a vegetarian institution since the early 1990s that went fully vegan in 2015, perched on Fjallgatan with a greenhouse, a lush garden and a knockout view over the harbour and Gamla Stan. Visit Stockholm calls it beloved, HappyCow puts it at the top of its Stockholm list, and recent reviews in October 2026 are still raving about going back for thirds.

The move here is simple: book ahead, take the buffet, and pace yourself through salads, lasagne, chilli, curries and breads, then save room for cake and a fika in the garden hammocks if the sun is out. Student discounts sweeten the deal, and the motto says it all: Give Peas a Chance.
