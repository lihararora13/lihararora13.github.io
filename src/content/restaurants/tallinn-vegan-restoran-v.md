Vegan Restoran V is the restaurant that put Tallinn on the plant-based map. Open since 2014, it was Estonia's first fully vegan restaurant, and it remains the best, holding a 5.0 from more than six hundred HappyCow reviewers. The menu is fusion: potato casserole with seitan, a vegan seafood platter, tofu-filled pasta shells, tempeh salad, spicy tomato soup, risotto and chocolate cake, plus smoothies and juices.

It is small and books out days in advance, so reservations are not optional. Order the seafood platter, add the Snickers cake, and book as soon as you know your dates.
