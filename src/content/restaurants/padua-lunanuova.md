Lunanuova has carried Padua's vegetarian culture since 1986, becoming a proper restaurant in 2011, and it is the most historic address on this list. It sits out in Pontevigodarzere, north of the centre, and it is worth the short trip for a classic vegetarian kitchen with a vegan thread.

Order the vegetarian dishes of the day and ask what is vegan; the kitchen is used to the question and the menu changes with the seasons.
