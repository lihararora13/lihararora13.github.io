Gratitude made the list for a reason. Run by the same team as Bean Me Up, a tucked-away Anjuna cafe with a yoga space, an eco thrift store and a fully vegan menu. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Anjuna, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Buddha bowl, Smoothie bowl, Vegan cake if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
