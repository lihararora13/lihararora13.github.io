Love & Greens is the Binjiang pick on this list: a vegan and allium-free organic restaurant at No. 120 Jucai Road in Changhe, across the Qiantang River from the West Lake cluster, with outdoor seating and delivery. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, and the menu runs from an organic alfalfa sprout sushi roll to slow-cooked tomato and herb pasta and seaweed noodles.

Order the alfalfa sprout sushi roll, the tomato and herb pasta and the seaweed noodles, and the cashew, Chinese yam and goji berry drink. It is the only pick south of the river, so plan it as a Binjiang stop rather than a West Lake walk. Open daily 11am to 8:30pm.
