Planttscape is the real anchor of this guide: a vegan kitchen crafting a desi plant-based landscape, with broccoli kulcha and chhole, tofu steak and vegetables, Madras curry vegetables and mushroom stroganoff, all made with minimal processed ingredients. Delivery is available, which makes it the practical everyday vegan option in Noida.

The menu is proudly Indian rather than an imported imitation of one, which is exactly what makes it good. Order the broccoli kulcha with chhole and the tofu steak, and you will see why the regulars keep coming back.
