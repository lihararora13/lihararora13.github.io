Halapua by Kapuhala is the top-rated vegan restaurant on Koh Samui, a perfect 5.0 from 99 HappyCow reviews, and it is the island's first high-end fully plant-based restaurant. It sits inside the Kapuhala Boutique Wellness Resort on the hills above Chaweng Noi, with an infinity pool and a rooftop mixology lounge overlooking the sea. The menu is a small, seasonal Sicilian-Asian set menu inspired by the story of two islands, Koh Samui and Sicily, both home to Kapuhala, cooked from produce grown on the resort's own natural farm. Dishes change with the season, but the black bean and walnut polpette in tomato sauce with vegan cheese is the signature, alongside a vegan quesadilla and Cuban tacos.

It is expensive even by Samui standards, and reservations are required. HappyCow lists it open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:30am to 7:00pm, but other sources give different hours, including evening service to 8:30pm, so confirm directly before going. The pin is neighbourhood-level for Chaweng Noi rather than street-level.
