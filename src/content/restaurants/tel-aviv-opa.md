Opa is the table to book when you want to see what vegetables can really do. Chef Shirel Berger, who previously ran the beloved Miss Kaplan, builds a tasting menu of around ten courses in the middle of old-school Levinsky Market, aging and fermenting produce for months to coax out flavours you would never expect from a carrot or a lychee.

This is dinner as theatre, not dinner as fuel: small, beautiful plates arrive without explanation, and the story comes only after you have tasted. Book well ahead, come hungry for adventure rather than volume, and save it for a night when the meal itself is the occasion.
