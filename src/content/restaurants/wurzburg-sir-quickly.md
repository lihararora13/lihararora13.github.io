Sir Quickly is a small vegetarian and vegan imbiss on the Herzogenstraße, the kind of lunch counter that office workers and students rely on: a short menu, a quick service, and prices that suit a weekday. It keeps lunch hours only, Monday to Friday from eleven to three, and it does not pretend to be anything more than it is, which is a good, fast, meat-free lunch in the old town.

Order the vegan lunch plate or the soup of the day, eat it quickly, and get on with your afternoon. It is the most practical table on this list, and sometimes that is exactly what you need.
