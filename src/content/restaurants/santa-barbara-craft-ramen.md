Craft Ramen is Santa Barbara's ramen pick: a restaurant on State Street with a labelled vegan veggie ramen and a vegan fried meat appetizer, and staff who will talk through the other dishes. It is a small, focused menu, and the vegan ramen is the dish to order rather than a modified version of a meat ramen. Reservations are recommended at dinner.

Hours are Monday to Friday 11:30am to 2:30pm and 4pm to 9pm, and Saturday to Sunday noon to 2:30pm and 4pm to 9pm. Order the vegan veggie ramen and the fried appetizer, and ask about the other vegan dishes.
