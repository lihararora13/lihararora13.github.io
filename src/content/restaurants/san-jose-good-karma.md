Good Karma is where San Jose's vegan scene goes to hang out: a fully vegan cafe and brewpub pouring its own artisan ales and stouts, with live music on Thursdays and a menu that jumps from Thai curry to dal to burritos without missing a beat. The chickpea salad sandwich is the quiet classic, and the Chile verde rice plate is the one regulars swear by.

It is casual, a little scruffy in the best way, and genuinely a community hub rather than a restaurant that happens to be vegan. Come for a beer, stay for dinner, and do not skip the live music night.
