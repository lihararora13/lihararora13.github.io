Why we love it — The only all-vegan Andean tapas spot and pisco lounge in
town is a sentence we enjoy saying. Epif serves small plates with real Andean
roots, baked empanadas with aji verde that disappear in about ninety seconds,
and mushroom ceviche that will start an argument about what ceviche even is.

The mixed-berry pisco sour is the move while you wait for a table. Intimate,
colorful, and unlike anything else on this list, which is exactly why it's
on it.
