Madras Mania is a pure-vegetarian South Indian restaurant that opened recently in Rohrbach, and it is already one of the best-rated tables in the city. The menu is built around dosas, idli, vada and uttapam, with a clearly marked vegan selection, and the cooking is confident and properly spiced rather than toned down for a German audience. The room is modern and uncluttered, a long way from the usual kitsch, and the kitchen is proud of its recipes from the mothers of Madras.

Two practical notes: it is cash only, and it is closed on Tuesdays. Order a Mysore masala dosa and a plate of medu vada to share, add a filter coffee or a mango lassi, and expect to want to come back for the 70mm dosa.
