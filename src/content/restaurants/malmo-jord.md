Jord is a fully vegan café on Falsterbogatan in Möllevången, and it is the other great brunch on this page. The tofu scramble is the signature, with vegan pancakes, french toast and a blue spirulina smoothie bowl alongside, all of it plant-based.

Möllevången is the city's food quarter, so walk the square before or after. The tofu scramble is the order, and the pancakes run it close.
