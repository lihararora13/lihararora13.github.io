Oasis Vegetaurant is a large vegetarian restaurant at Sirikorn Market on Paholyothin Road, about 150 yards behind the bus station, with a vegan buffet of Thai and Chinese dishes. It is the biggest vegan-friendly room in Chiang Rai, and it suits a first meal on arrival, as it is a short walk from the buses.

Fill a plate from the buffet with vegetable curries and tofu dishes, and come at lunch when the choice is widest. It is inexpensive and easy to find.
