Margo's is a Mexican restaurant on Church Street in Queenstown, with a full vegan menu of Mexican-inspired dishes. It is a lively, modern room in the centre, and it is the only fully vegan menu in Queenstown.

Order the vegan tacos and a burrito, and try the nachos. It is central and reliable, and it suits a casual dinner.
