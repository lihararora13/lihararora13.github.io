Kemara Cuisine made the list for a reason. A plant-based-first cafe on Reid Avenue doing vegan peppers lasagna, cashew-cheese pizza and fresh juices with real intent. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Colombo 7, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan peppers lasagna, Grilled peppers pizza, cashew cheese, Fresh juice if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
