Mariposas captures everything lovable about Marseille and puts it on a plate: a fully vegan, fully organic restaurant on the city's coolest square, cooking world cuisine from local, seasonal produce. The menu wanders the globe, the burger and the roti are the regulars' favourites, and the chocolate fondant is the reason to save room.

Come for dinner, stay for the tea salon in the afternoon, or book the Sunday brunch. It is the warmest room on Cours Julien, and the most Marseille way to eat vegan in the city.
