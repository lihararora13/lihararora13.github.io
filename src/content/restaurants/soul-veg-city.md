Soul Veg City has been cooking exclusively with plants since 1981,
longer than any other restaurant in the Chicago area, and it is the
beating heart of plant-based soul food in the city. The Black- and
family-owned spot on the South Side runs a hot bar, a salad bar, and
a menu of Southern classics gone vegan: BBQ ribs, mac and cheese,
greens, cornbread, and a black-eyed pea burger with real fans.

This is Chicago food history you can eat. Come hungry, go back for
seconds, and do not skip dessert.
