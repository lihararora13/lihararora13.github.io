Veganí is what happens when someone takes vegan cooking seriously and keeps the menu small. Thirteen dishes, all seasonal, all local, all Andalusian at heart, in a stylish room near the Alameda that feels like a proper restaurant, not a health-food counter.

Book ahead, the room is small and the menu changes with the seasons. Order whatever the kitchen is proudest of that week, and let the tortilla be your benchmark, it is ridiculously fluffy.
