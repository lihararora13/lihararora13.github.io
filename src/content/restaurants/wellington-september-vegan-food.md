September Vegan Food is the newest name on the list, a 100% vegan Asian restaurant on Manners Street that has already made HappyCow's top ten for the city. The menu reads like a greatest-hits tour of Chinese and Southeast Asian comfort food, all of it plant-based: hotpot, mapo tofu, Hainan "chicken" rice, bak kut teh and fish-fragrant eggplant. It is open for lunch and dinner most days, with a shorter Sunday service.

This is the pick for a group that cannot agree on anything, since the hotpot alone covers most arguments. Start with the mapo tofu, share the eggplant, and save room for the Hainan "chicken" rice.
