Why we love it — Chef Rob Rubba built one of the country's most acclaimed
sustainable restaurants around vegetables, plates made from recycled wine
bottles, and induction stoves. The tasting menu is almost entirely plant-based,
with oysters as the sole concession.

It's vegetable cooking treated with the seriousness usually reserved for dry-aged
beef. Book for the experience, stay for the mushroom dishes that people describe
in religious terms, and don't miss the apple dessert.
