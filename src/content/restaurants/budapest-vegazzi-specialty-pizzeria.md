Vegazzi is a Neapolitan-style pizzeria with a wood-fired oven, a courtyard to eat in, and a perfect 5.0 from more than 340 HappyCow reviews, the highest review count of any 5.0 spot in the city. It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but the vegan pizzas are the stars: mushroom pesto, tofu ricotta, mexicano, margherita and rotating specials, all on proper wood-fired dough.

Regulars call it one of the best pizzas they have ever had, vegan or not, which is exactly the kind of claim a curated list should take seriously. It has moved to Paulay Ede utca 6 from its old spot on the same street, so head to the new address.
