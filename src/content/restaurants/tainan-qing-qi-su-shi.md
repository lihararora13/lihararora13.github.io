Qing Qi Su Shi sits diagonally opposite the Eastern Vegetable Market on Cingnian Road, and it is exactly the kind of place that makes Tainan's vegetarian scene so deep: a neighbourhood buffet and dim sum eatery, cheap, busy, and very local. The dim sum are vegan, and the buffet runs through seasonal vegetables and tofu.

It is a vegetarian restaurant with vegan dishes rather than a fully vegan kitchen, so check the made-to-order dishes, some of which are vegetarian rather than vegan. Come early, the buffet is at its best in the morning, and pair it with a wander through the market across the road.
