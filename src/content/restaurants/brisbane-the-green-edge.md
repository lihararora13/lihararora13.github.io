The Green Edge has been making being vegan easy in Brisbane since 2008, long before plant-based was fashionable. The Windsor cafe-grocer pairs a full vegan menu with a health food supermarket, so one visit covers lunch and the pantry: katsu curry, the Origin burger, Korean BBQ pork bowls and lamington pancakes in the cafe, then a wander through tofurkey, coconut yoghurt and every specialty staple a vegan kitchen could want.

It is the city's long-running, iconic plant-based dining room, four kilometres from the city heart, and the community vibe is the point. Come hungry, order the burger, and leave with a bag of groceries you cannot find anywhere else.
