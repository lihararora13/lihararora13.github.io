The Vegan Temple is Cusco's most atmospheric vegan meal: walk up an ancient staircase to reach it, then dine at low tables with colourful cushions, eating curry, tofu dishes, burgers and brownies. It is a proper little adventure just getting there, and the food rewards the climb.

Come for dinner, order a curry and a tofu dish, and finish with a brownie. This is the one for a memorable, slightly magical Cusco evening.
