Haiku is an organic vegan restaurant on Via Ricasoli, and Palermo's vegan anchor. Its own description calls it a biological vegan restaurant serving international and Sicilian cooking in a vegan key, with a 4.8 HappyCow rating across 142 reviews, and an aperitivo from seven in the evening with organic wine, craft beer and vegan bites.

Order the vegan carbonara, the parmigiana, the pasta with courgette flowers and the hazelnut tiramisu. It serves lunch and dinner Tuesday to Sunday, and the aperitivo is the move on a warm evening.
