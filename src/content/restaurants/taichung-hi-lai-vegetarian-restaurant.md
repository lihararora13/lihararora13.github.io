Hi-Lai Vegetarian Restaurant, 漢來蔬食, sits on the 16th floor of the Kuang San SOGO department store on Taiwan Boulevard, and it is the polished table of this guide: a two-star Green Vegetarian Guide restaurant in 2023, with an open kitchen, a honeycomb wood entrance and a menu that runs from dim sum to stir-fries, rice, noodles and desserts. The menu labels pure vegan, egg and milk dishes clearly, so vegans can order with confidence, and the dishes to start with are the quinoa eight-treasure rice, the house signature, the XO sauce pan-fried flaky pancake, the pan-fried turnip cake, the BBQ pork pastry and the Kung Pao lion's mane mushroom, with a wooden bucket of bean curd dessert to finish.

Expect about NT$400 to NT$700 a person, plus a 10% service charge, and a 90-minute seating limit. Book ahead on weekends: the dining room fills up, and the restaurant takes online reservations. Hours are weekdays lunch 11:30am to 2:30pm and dinner 5:00pm to 9:00pm, weekends lunch 11:00am to 3:00pm and dinner 5:00pm to 9:00pm.
