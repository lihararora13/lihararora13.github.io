Br00m44 sits right on Dolac, Zagreb's famous market, and it is the city's best vegan-friendly breakfast. Seasonal ingredients, specialty Prolog coffee, fresh juices and smoothies, all in a charming little spot above the market stalls.

Go in the morning, shop the market first, then sit down for brunch. Order whatever is seasonal, drink the coffee properly, and enjoy the best view of Zagreb waking up.
