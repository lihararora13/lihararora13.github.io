Anagha Pure Veg is a vegetarian restaurant in the Al Qasimia Plaza building in Al Mahatta, serving South Indian dishes. It is a pure-veg room, so the kitchen understands vegetarian cooking, and dishes can be made vegan on request.

Order a dosa and specify no ghee or butter. It is a good lunch stop in the Al Mahatta area.
