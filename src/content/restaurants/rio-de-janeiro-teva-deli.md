Teva Deli is what every Copacabana morning needs: the same kitchen behind Teva Bistrô, loosened up into a deli, bakery and café that opens at 8am and runs until 9pm every day. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow from 125 reviewers, which makes it the second-highest-rated vegan spot in the city, and it earns it the easy way, with good coffee, flaky vegan croissants, gluten-free tapioca snacks, sandwiches, cakes, house-made cheeses and spreads, and hot dishes when you want a full meal.

This is the pick for travellers staying in Copacabana who want a proper vegan breakfast without committing to a sit-down restaurant. English is spoken, the shelves are full of things to take to the beach, and the pastries alone justify the walk. Grab a croissant, a tapioca and a cake for later, because later you will wish you had.
