Flipstones is the riverfront stop on this list: a cafe on the Sabarmati Riverfront approach in Usmanpura, near the Natarani Amphitheatre, with a leafed green V marking the vegan items on its menu. It is open daily from 11am to 9pm and holds a 4.2 on Justdial from over 300 ratings.

The vegan options run across filler bowls, bread, nachos, fries, earth bowls and sandwiches, and the Purple Noodles are the dish regulars name first. Come for the view as much as the food, and note there is a second Flipstones branch in South Bopal; this is the riverfront original.
