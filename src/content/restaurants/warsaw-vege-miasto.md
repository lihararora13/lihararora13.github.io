Vege Miasto is the restaurant Warsaw vegans send everyone to first. Open since 2014 near the Old Town, it is 100 percent vegan, with a daily changing lunch, Polish classics like pierogi and pancakes, a big cake counter and plenty of gluten-free dishes. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow from more than five hundred reviewers.

Come for lunch, order the pierogi and a pancake, and do not leave without dessert. The mango cheesecake and the banana toffee pie are the ones to beat.
