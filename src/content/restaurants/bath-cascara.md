Cascara is the second room from the Green Rocket team, set up as a brighter daytime cafe a few minutes from the Abbey. It shares the same kitchen philosophy: fully vegan, seasonal, and cooked from scratch.

Come for a slow brunch and a proper coffee. The bakes change daily and sell out, so the earlier the better, and the salads are the lightest lunch in the city centre.
