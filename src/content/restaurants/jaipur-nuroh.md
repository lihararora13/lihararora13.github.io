Nuroh made the list for a reason. A specialty-coffee C-Scheme cafe with vegan options across the menu, cooked in olive oil and served in a bright, easy room. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in C-Scheme, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Avocado toast, Vegan sandwich, Specialty coffee, oat milk if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
