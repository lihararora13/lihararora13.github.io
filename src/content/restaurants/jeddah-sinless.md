Sinless adds a fast-food twist to Jeddah's vegan scene, a fully vegan spot in Ar Rawdah with cashew cheese pizza, lentil and mushroom burgers, kale Caesar salad and desserts such as cinnamon buns and bounty bites. The vegan brownies are the dish locals photograph.

Order the cashew cheese pizza and the cinnamon buns. It is the fun, fast end of Jeddah's vegan scene, and the one for a late-night craving.
