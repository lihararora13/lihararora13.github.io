Green Point is the restaurant Cusco's vegan scene is built around: fully vegan since 2013, with a 5.0 rating from over 550 reviewers and a large menu of international dishes plus juices, cakes and baked goods. It sits in San Blas, the artists' quarter above the Plaza de Armas, and it is open all day, which makes it the easiest good meal in the city.

Come for breakfast, lunch or dinner, order a quinoa bowl and a slice of cake, and enjoy the San Blas air. This is the one to take someone who thinks vegan food in Cusco means a sad salad.
