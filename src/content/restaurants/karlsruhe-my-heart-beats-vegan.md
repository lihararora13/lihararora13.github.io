My Heart Beats Vegan has been fully plant-based since 2015, the first such restaurant in Karlsruhe, and it has been doing the same thing well ever since: towering vegan burgers, wraps, global plates, salads and rotating desserts, in a pink-walled room that wears its heart on its sleeve. The Diablo burger and the sloppy joe are the dishes regulars come back for, and the Beyond meat patty anchors the menu. It is the fun table on this list, and the one for a hungry evening.

It opens at five on weekdays and at noon at weekends, closing at ten every night. Order a Diablo burger with fries, add a rotating dessert, and enjoy the restaurant that started Karlsruhe's vegan scene.
