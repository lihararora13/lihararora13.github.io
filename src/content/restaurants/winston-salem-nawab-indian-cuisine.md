Nawab Indian Cuisine is an Indian restaurant on South Stratford Road, doing curries, dals, and breads with a solid vegan bench. The address here is 129 South Stratford Road; some listings say 3908 Reynolda Road, which is wrong.

Order the chana masala and dal tadka with vegan roti, and confirm which breads are dairy-free, since most naan is brushed with butter or ghee.
