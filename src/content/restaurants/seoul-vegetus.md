Vegetus is Haebangchon's old faithful: fully vegan since 2016, holding a near-perfect rating, and feeding the neighbourhood exactly the comfort food it craves. Burgers, lasagna, mac and cheese, burrito bowls, a caprese panini when you want to feel virtuous about lunch.

It is the kind of place expats measure their Seoul years by. Climb Sinheung-ro, claim a table, and order the lasagna first, because tables around you will be doing the same and the smell is a whole argument.
