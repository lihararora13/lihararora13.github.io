BongoRoots is the most distinctive vegan restaurant in Tübingen, and one of the most distinctive in Germany: a 100 percent vegan Afro-Caribbean kitchen on the Eugenstraße, in a room that used to be a butcher's shop, cooking a daily-changing menu from plantains, sweet potatoes, couscous and seasonal vegetables. Weekdays it runs an Afro-Caribbean lunch buffet from half past eleven to three, and on Friday evenings there is a three-course Friday Night Special. The signature plates are jollof rice, stew with chakalaka, seitan kebabs and Rasta pies, and the owner makes his own vegan African Bushman chocolate.

Mind the hours: it is closed on Saturdays and Sundays, the food can run out before closing time, and the Friday evening special is worth booking. Order the lunch buffet plate, and treat it as the meal that makes Tübingen's vegan scene famous.
