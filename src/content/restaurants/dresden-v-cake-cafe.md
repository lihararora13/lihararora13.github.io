V-Cake Cafe is Dresden's vegan bakery, a small Neustadt cafe where everything is made in-house and everything is vegan. The counter runs to cakes, muffins, cookies, sandwiches, soup, quiche and soft ice cream, with a wide range of non-dairy milks for coffee, matcha lattes and hot chocolate. Saturday brings chilli, Sunday brings brunch, and the rest of the week is a steady stream of cake.

It is the sweet stop between the neighbourhood's savoury heavyweights, two doors from SPROUT FOOD and a short walk from Der Dicke Schmidt. Come for an afternoon slice, or time a Saturday visit for the chilli. This is the table for anyone who thinks vegan baking is a compromise.
