Crunchbox is the sweet ending every vegan guide needs. The Malaga dessert bar is entirely plant-based and mostly gluten-free, and it takes dessert very seriously: waffles piled with Snickers, red velvet or pistachio cream, peanut butter bars, raw chocolate slices and cakes that sell out by the weekend.

It started as a labour of love, a chef making better desserts for his mum, and the weekend brunch crowd now travels across Perth for the tofu bacon bagels and smoothie bowls. Come for a waffle, leave with a box of slices.
