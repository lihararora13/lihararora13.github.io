HeyBong is Siem Reap's fully vegan headliner: a big, bright restaurant with a bar on Smiling Circuit, in a residential area away from the Pub Street noise, serving healthy dishes and comfort food. Recent vegan visitors rated it 5 out of 5, with the chickn burger and the avo toast leading the praise.

Order the chickn burger and a tofu katsu, and stay for an iced coffee. The sign reads Stay Cool and Eat Plants, and the kitchen lives up to it: this is the one fully vegan restaurant on the list, and the first table to book.
