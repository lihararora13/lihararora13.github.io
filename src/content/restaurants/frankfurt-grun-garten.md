Grün Garten is Frankfurt's Vietnamese vegan kitchen, a bright, friendly restaurant in the Nordend serving pho chay, bun cha, curries and tofu dishes every day for lunch and dinner. It is the kind of place you can rely on: warm, fragrant, generous and very good value.

Order the pho chay, add a bun cha, and enjoy one of the most comforting vegan meals in the city. A Nordend local, and a visitor favourite.
