Cafe Staywoke in South Point Mall is the aesthetic pick on this list: exotic outdoor seating, wooden interiors, glass walls, and a menu that leans healthy and plant-friendly. It is the kind of cafe where a working lunch stretches happily into the afternoon.

The vegan pasta and avocado toast are the safe orders, and the smoothie bowls are the lighter option. Ask for the dairy-free version and the kitchen will sort you out.
