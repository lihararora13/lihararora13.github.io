Nobiko is the furthest-flung pick on this list and worth the tram ride: a small vegan Japanese noodle bar in a quiet side street in Kalk, re-opened in 2018, making udon noodles in different broths, fried vegetables on rice and teishoku sets that include miso soup. There is cake and cookies, Japanese tea is served, and German, English and Japanese are all spoken.

Come for the udon, stay for the tea, and enjoy one of Cologne's most personal vegan kitchens. Small, sincere and quietly brilliant.
