Why we love it — Eternal Abundance is half cafe, half vegan grocery, and all
neighborhood institution. The daytime menu covers bowls, burgers, and the
quinoa waffles locals swear by, and then you can shop the shelves for
everything you just ate.

Come for brunch, leave with a tote bag full of pantry goods. It's the kind
of place that makes eating vegan feel less like a decision and more like a
habit.
