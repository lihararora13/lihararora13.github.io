En-Champs-Thé is the tea-house sibling of Oh K-fée d'MJ, out in Dervallières on Place Catinat, and it is the calmest room on this page. The draw is a seasonal lunch plate and vegan pastries with a proper speciality tea list, the kind of place where lunch stretches into the afternoon.

It is a short tram ride west of the centre and worth it for the tea alone. Vegan dishes are clearly marked; ask about the day's pastries.
