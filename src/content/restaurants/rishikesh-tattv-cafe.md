TATTV Cafe is the easy hangout cafe of Tapovan: air-conditioned indoor dining, a deck upstairs, Wi-Fi and comfortable seating, two shops before the main corner of Badrinath Road and Laxman Jhula Road, above the Pundit General Health Store. The menu is vegetarian and wide, pizza, Indian, Italian, Western and European dishes, with vegan options available if you ask.

It is the kind of place to settle into for a long afternoon rather than a quick bite. Ask about the vegan options when you order, the kitchen is used to adapting dishes, and the pizza and pasta are the safest bets.
