Drunken Monkey is the most modern room on Nagpur's list: a smoothie and juice café at Zenda Chowk in Bajaj Nagar, built around bowls, fresh fruit and cold-pressed juice. It is the natural first stop for a vegan morning in the city.

Order a smoothie bowl and be specific: some smoothies on the menu contain a little dairy, so ask for the vegan build and the plant milk. Done right, it is the freshest breakfast in Nagpur.
