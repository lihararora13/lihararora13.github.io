Handlebar is where Chicago's vegans go when they want a beer garden,
a late night, and a menu that does not make them order a side salad.
It is a mostly vegetarian spot in Wicker Park, but the vegan side of
the menu is serious: Nashville hot fried "chicken" sandwiches, fried
avocado tacos, a buffalo seitan wrap, and fried pickles that people
describe as the best nachos in the city.

Come for a casual weeknight dinner, stay for the patio out back. In
the summer, that beer garden is the whole point.
