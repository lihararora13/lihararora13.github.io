Green Truck Pub is a casual Starland District pub founded on a simple premise, that Savannahians deserve a place to eat and drink well, and it makes its own ketchup, pimento cheese, salad dressing, and veggie patties in-house. The veggie burger is the vegan order, and the salads are the lighter one.

It takes no reservations and no call-ahead seating, so just walk in. Order the burger and a beer.
