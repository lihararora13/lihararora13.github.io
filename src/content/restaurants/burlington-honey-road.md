Honey Road is Burlington's Mediterranean dining room, a warm, wood-fired spot on Church Street where the mezze and vegetable plates make it an easy vegan-friendly dinner. The hummus and falafel are the anchors, and the seasonal vegetables change with the market.

It is the most polished dinner room on this list, so it works well when the group wants one proper sit-down meal.
