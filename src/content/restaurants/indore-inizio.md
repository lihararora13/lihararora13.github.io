Inizio brings a little industrial polish to New Palasia: exposed staircases, moody lighting and a room that feels designed for long conversations. The menu is continental and Italian-leaning, which keeps the vegan ordering simple, pasta without cheese, pizza without cheese, salads and small plates built around vegetables.

It is a café first and a restaurant second, so come hungry but not expecting a thali. Order a couple of plates, an iced coffee, and settle in: this is a linger-longer kind of place.
