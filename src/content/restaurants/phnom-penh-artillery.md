ARTillery is a cafe and art gallery in an artsy laneway between Streets 240 and 244, and it is a pioneer of Phnom Penh's sustainable food movement: 100% organic Arabica coffee, a creative plant-based menu, and a gallery hosting community art projects, cooking classes and wellness events.

The falafel waffle benedict is the signature, and the build-your-own macro bowl and the sweet potato disco fries with cashew cheese dip are the other orders. It is a vegetarian-friendly cafe with clear vegan labelling rather than an all-vegan room, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note the pin is neighbourhood-level for Street 240.
