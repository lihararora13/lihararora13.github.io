Santos is half organic grocery store, half cafe, and all Byron: shelves stacked with Australian-made vegan cheeses, pestos and snacks, and a little cafe at the back serving vegetarian, vegan and raw meals plus sweets and juices. The garden seating at the rear is a lovely spot to eat, and the shop itself is worth a wander if you are stocking up a holiday rental.

Come for lunch, browse the shelves, and take home a vegan cheese or two. This is the one-stop shop for eating well in Byron.
