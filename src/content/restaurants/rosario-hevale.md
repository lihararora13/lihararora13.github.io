Hevale is a vegan and vegetarian kitchen and deli on Buenos Aires, and it is the most explicitly vegan-named pick on this list, serving lunch and dinner plates alongside a deli counter. The hours are split, lunch from 11 am to 2 pm and dinner from 8 to 11 pm, Tuesday to Saturday, with Sunday dinner only.

Order the deli plate of the day and ask what is vegan, as the kitchen cooks both vegan and vegetarian. It is one of the few Rosario picks that serves a proper dinner, so plan around the evening hours.
