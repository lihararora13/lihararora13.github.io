Mahorgany is a vegetarian and vegan restaurant with a health food store attached, and its menu reads like a greatest hits of Ghanaian comfort food: jollof rice, waakye, seitan, tofu scramble and fufu. It is inexpensive, open long hours seven days a week, and popular with locals as well as visitors.

Order the jollof or the waakye for the classic experience, and the tofu scramble if you are there for brunch. Skip the Afrikiko branch, which is listed as possibly closed.
