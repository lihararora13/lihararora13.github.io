Brown Poodle is Penang's cosy vegan cafe: small, meatless since 2015, fully vegan since 2022, and loved by locals for its cakes, coffee and unhurried breakfasts. The menu is short and sweet, with vegan and vegetarian food, fresh cakes and good coffee, and delivery available across town. It sits up in Tanjung Tokong, a little out of the old-town bustle, which only adds to the calm.

Come for a slow breakfast, order a cake and a coffee, and enjoy the quiet. This is the one for a gentle morning before a day of exploring.
