OhMA, also written Oh!ma, is a mostly vegan breakfast cafe on Gebroeders Vandeveldestraat, listed as vegan on HappyCow. The menu is built for mornings: vegan pancakes, toasts, breakfast bowls and good coffee, in a bright room a short walk from the centre.

Come for breakfast or a late brunch, and order the vegan pancakes. It is the gentle start to a Ghent day on this list.
