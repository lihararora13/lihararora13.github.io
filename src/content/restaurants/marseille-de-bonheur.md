De Bon'heur is the neighbourhood café every street wishes it had: a vegan cantine on Boulevard Baille where the coffee is locally roasted in Aubagne, the breakfast and lunch plates are generous, and the afternoon cakes are the reward for a morning of exploring. It is relaxed, friendly and very Marseille.

Come for a slow breakfast, a working lunch or an afternoon coffee and cake. It is the kind of place that turns into your local within a day, and the kind of place you miss when you leave.
