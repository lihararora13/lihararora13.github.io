Gokul Oottupura is Kochi's temple-style pure-vegetarian institution: 100% vegetarian, egg-free and alcohol-free, built around the banana-leaf Kerala sadhya, dosa and tiffin, with branches on MG Road, in Kadavanthra and here in Panampilly Nagar, all open daily from 7am to 10:30pm. The MG Road branch is the flagship, but this Panampilly Nagar branch is the easiest to reach from the Fort Kochi side.

The sadhya is the order, rice and vegetable curries on a banana leaf, and the ghee roast dosa with three chutneys is the other classic. Ask for the dosa without ghee and the sadhya without curd and it is fully vegan. Pazham pori, the banana fritter, makes a good sweet finish.
