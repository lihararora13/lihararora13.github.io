Slow Cali is a health-food restaurant in Juk-dong, Yuseong-gu, open daily from 11am to 9pm, with vegan-friendly salads, bowls and plant-based dishes. It is a chain-style healthy kitchen rather than a vegan specialist, so ask which bowls are vegan, but the plant-based options are a real part of the menu.

Order a salad or vegan bowl with seasonal vegetables and a smoothie. It is a reliable, central lunch stop in the university district, and its hours are the longest of any pick in this guide.
