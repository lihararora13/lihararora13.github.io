Loving Hut is the world's largest family of vegan restaurants, and the Fresno outpost in the Tower District serves its Asian vegan comfort menu: pho, stir-fry noodles, spring rolls, and curries, all plant-based. It is the chain entry on this list, and the most affordable sit-down vegan meal in the Tower District.

Order the vegan pho and the spring rolls. A caution: this location's freshest sources are old, so confirm it is open and its hours before a special trip.
