Origin is PUR COOP's fully vegan coffee shop on the Grand'Rue, billed as the first vegan coffee shop in Strasbourg, and it is the morning stop on this page. The case holds croissants, cinnamon rolls, quiche and doughnuts, all plant-based, with proper speciality coffee alongside.

It sits on the Grande Île a short walk from the cathedral, so it works as a breakfast before sightseeing or a mid-afternoon coffee. Everything is vegan.
