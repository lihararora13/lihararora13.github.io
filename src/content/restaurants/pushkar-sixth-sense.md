Sixth Sense is the organic café inside Inn Seventh Heaven in Choti Basti, and it cooks the way Pushkar's best cafés do: fresh, local, and heavy on the vegetables. The Buddha bowls and thalis are the draw, with a strong vegan bent that makes ordering easy.

Order a Buddha bowl, add a smoothie, and you have the healthiest lunch in the old town. It is a calm, garden-feeling room, and a good place to plan the rest of your day.
