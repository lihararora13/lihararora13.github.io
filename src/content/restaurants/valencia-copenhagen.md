Copenhagen is one of Valencia's oldest vegetarian restaurants, and its vegan menu is extensive enough to earn it a place on this list. Modern, fun cooking, gluten-free options, a midday menu and a tasting menu, with vegetables bought daily from the Ruzafa market.

Order the tasting menu if you want the full experience, or a seitan special from the a la carte. It is the grown-up dinner in Ruzafa, and the vegan cheesecake is a proper finish.
