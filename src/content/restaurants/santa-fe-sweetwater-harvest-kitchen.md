Sweetwater Harvest Kitchen is Santa Fe's go-to for a long, unhurried plant-based meal: a light-filled dining room in the Pacheco design district, an entirely gluten-free menu, and vegan dishes clearly marked at every service. The smothered vegan breakfast burrito is the brunch order, and the supper menu's chile relleno, stuffed with quinoa, mushrooms, and zucchini under cashew cheese, is the dinner one.

Brunch and lunch run daily from 9am to 3pm, with supper Tuesday to Saturday from 5pm to 8pm. Book ahead on weekends, and leave room for the vegan chocolate pie.
