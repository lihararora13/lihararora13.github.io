Navtara Veg made the list for a reason. A pure-vegetarian Panaji institution for South Indian staples and Goan specials like mushroom xacuti, at family prices. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Panaji, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Mushroom xacuti, Masala dosa, Veg thali if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
