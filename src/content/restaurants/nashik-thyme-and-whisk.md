Thyme & Whisk tops Nashik's list for the same reason it tops the other cities it has reached: a bright, leafy room, a menu that takes plant-based eating seriously, and a kitchen that knows the difference between veg and vegan. The Gangapur Road branch at Prasad Circle is the city's most reliable vegan-friendly café.

Order a vegan pasta and a smoothie bowl, save room for the vegan dessert, and you will understand why this is the first name every local vegan mentions. It is the safest first stop in the city, and a good one.
