Pastel Blumencafe is a flower shop with a cafe inside, a small pastel-coloured room in Stadtfeld Ost where the vegan selection is not a side offering but the centrepiece of the menu. The soups are always vegan, alongside grilled vegan cheese sandwiches, overnight oats and a cake counter that runs heavily vegan, all of it made in-house. Reviewers call it Magdeburg's blooming masterpiece, and the room, flowers, cakes and coffee, lives up to the name.

It opens Thursday to Sunday, ten to six, so this is a late-week pick, and it sits a little outside the centre, worth the short trip. Order a soup and a grilled cheese sandwich, then a cake, and take your time looking around. This is the prettiest table in Magdeburg.
