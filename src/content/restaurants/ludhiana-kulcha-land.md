Kulcha Land in Model Town does one thing and does it well: Amritsari-style stuffed kulchas with chole, griddled hot and served with the chutneys that make the dish. It is the Punjabi breakfast, and it is very good.

Ask for the kulcha without makhan and the chole as it comes. It is cheap, fast, and the most local breakfast on this list.
