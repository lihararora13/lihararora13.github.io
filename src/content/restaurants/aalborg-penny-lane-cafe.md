Penny Lane Cafe is a patisserie and cafe on Boulevarden, rated 4 on HappyCow, making breads, cakes, jams, pesto and hummus, with a hummus sandwich, four kinds of salads and chickpea meatballs for vegans, plus juices, smoothies and plant milk. It is the patisserie pick on this list.

Order the hummus sandwich, a salad and the chickpea meatballs, and ask about the cakes.
