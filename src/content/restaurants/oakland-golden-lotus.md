Golden Lotus is downtown Oakland's fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant, and it does the classics with zero fuss: pho, spring rolls, lemongrass tofu, all of it plant-based and all of it cheap. It is the kind of place where the lunch crowd is mostly regulars, and the regulars are right.

It is the downtown stop on the list, easy to reach and easy to love. Order the pho, add spring rolls, and keep it in your back pocket for every downtown lunch.
