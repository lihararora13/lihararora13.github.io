Natural Spice is a small vegan, vegetarian and raw vegan restaurant on Calle Jose Fabrega in El Carmen, near the university, and it stands out for variety: nachos, burgers, burritos, salads, gluten-free pizzas and a coconut rice, with house-made vegan cheeses and nut-based meat. Chef Marcos D'Zion cooks entirely plant-based, with no eggs or milk.

Order the seitan burger, the vegan nachos and a vegan pizza to share, with a burrito for anyone who wants something heartier. It is inexpensive, open Monday to Saturday, and closed on Sundays.
