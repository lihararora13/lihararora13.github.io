Ammazzacaffe is a 100% vegan cafe tucked into Vicolo Volto Cittadella near the Arena, described by a recent Verona guide as a gem that nobody talks about enough. It is the quiet coffee stop on this list, a small room with a fully plant-based menu.

Order a coffee and whatever vegan cakes and snacks are on the counter, and treat it as the morning stop before the Arena crowds arrive.
