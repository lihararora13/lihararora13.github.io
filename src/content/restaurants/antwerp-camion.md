Camion is a casual fully vegan cafe opened in February 2019 by two friends, Camille and Boquion, hence the name. It serves plant-based breakfast, lunch, coffee and pastry, with sandwiches, salads, daily soup and an extensive apero of sharing dishes, and on Sundays one meal serves as brunch, lunch and aperitif.

It relocated from Paleisstraat 7 to Hospitaalplein 5, so use the current address. Come for breakfast or lunch, and order the kimchi croque.
