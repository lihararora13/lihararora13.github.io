MERCY Vegan Factory is one of the very few places in Osaka where a vegan can eat a proper breakfast without improvising, and that alone would earn it a spot. This is a JAS-certified all-vegan bakery-cafe near Tanimachi 9-chome Station, open from breakfast through dinner, with an all-day menu that runs from a vegan egg sandwich with realistic egg salad on soft bread to Korean gimbap, curry rice, karaage and deep-fried chicken.

The cake case is the real draw after lunch: strawberry pie, muscat cream cake, parfaits and seasonal patisserie, best sampled early in the day before the good stuff sells out. There is a sibling ramen shop in Bakuro-machi if noodles are what you are after, but the Factory is the one for breakfast, cake, and a sit-down meal that covers the whole day.
