Vitto Pitagorico makes vegetable-led reinterpretations of traditional Neapolitan recipes, with pizzas that work as a main course rather than a side dish, and much of the menu is gluten-free as well as vegan. Its setting near the National Archaeological Museum makes it a useful place to sit down for a meal after exploring the collections.

Order the Maria Antonietta, with semi-dried yellow Vesuvio tomatoes, roasted artichokes and fiordilatte, on a base of long-rising dough made with stone-milled flour. The restaurant also has a branch in Aversa, so use the Via Enrico Pessina address on this page.
