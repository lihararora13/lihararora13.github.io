Mukuchi solves the ramen problem beautifully: broths built on oat milk with white fungus and mushrooms, or on sugar cane with tomato and kelp, in a pocket-sized red-brick room just off historic Dihua Street. It is one of the most charming vegan addresses in the city.

Pick your broth, then your style, white miso, fiery Sichuan tantan, or truffle for the full treat. The asparagus bean curd rolls are the side everyone tells you about, and the house puddings finish things gently. Seating is limited, so book through its social channels.
