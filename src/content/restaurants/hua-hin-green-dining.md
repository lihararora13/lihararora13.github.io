Green Dining is a small vegan-vegetarian restaurant in a house in downtown Hua Hin, praised by reviewers for freshness, and described by one local guide as their favourite spot in town. Most of the menu is vegan, with the option to add egg if you want, so vegans should order without egg.

Order the seasonal vegetables and Thai dishes, and check opening hours on the restaurant's page first, as hours change. Its pin is approximate to downtown Hua Hin.
