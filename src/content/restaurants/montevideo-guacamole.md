Guacamole is Montevideo's vegan fast-food institution: all food vegan, all staff vegan, right on the city's main avenue. Burgers, including the Beyond Burger, pizzas, tofu burgers, onion rings and fries, all made to order and delivered across the whole city. It is fast, fun and exactly what you want after a long day walking the rambla.

Come hungry, order a Beyond Burger and onion rings, and add a pizza to share. This is the one for a big, satisfying vegan feed in the centre of town.
