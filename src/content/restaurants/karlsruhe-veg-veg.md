Veg Veg is a small fully vegan restaurant on the Hirschstraße, a quiet all-plant-based table a short walk from the Kaiserstraße. It is the least documented of Karlsruhe's five fully vegan spots, with no published hours, so it is best treated as a walk-in discovery rather than a planned booking: go, see what the kitchen is doing that day, and order the daily plate. The room is small, the cooking is plant-based throughout, and the location is central.

Order the daily vegan plate if it is on, add a seasonal main, and keep it in mind as the fifth fully vegan table in a city that has more of them than most.
