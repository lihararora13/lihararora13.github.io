Sumac Vegano is where Cusco's vegan scene meets Peruvian tradition head-on: mango ceviche, quinoa chaufa, seitan anticucho and spaghetti a la huancaina, all plant-based, alongside international plates like pad thai and smoothies and specialty coffees. It is a 5.0-rated spot right in the historic centre, and the menu is the best argument that vegan Peruvian food is not a compromise.

Come for lunch or dinner, order the mango ceviche and the seitan anticucho, and finish with a specialty coffee. This is the one for a proper taste of vegan Peru.
