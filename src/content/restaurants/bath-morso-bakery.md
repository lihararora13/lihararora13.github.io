Morso is Bath's all-vegan bakery, a small shop on Saville Row where everything from the sourdough to the celebration cakes is plant-based. It supplies several of the city's cafes as well as selling over the counter.

Buy a loaf and a pastry to eat on the walk up Walcot Street. The croissants go early, and whole cakes are worth ordering ahead for a weekend.
