Nissi started as a food truck and graduated to a proper North
Austin storefront, and the regulars followed every mile of the
move. The vegan birria tacos, rich red consome and all, are the
reason people cross the city in traffic. Everything on the menu is
plant-based, from flautas to birria ramen.

Save room for the churro nachos. They are the kind of dessert you
order "for the table" and then quietly defend with your fork.
