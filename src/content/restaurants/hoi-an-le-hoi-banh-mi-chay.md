Le Hoi Banh Mi Chay is a street stall on Nguyen Hue, at the corner of Phan Chau Trinh, where an elderly lady sells vegetarian banh mi from a cart with blue plastic stools. There is no meat on the cart at all, so there is no cross-contamination, and at 20,000 dong it is the cheapest meal in Hoi An and one of the highest rated, at 4.9 from 2,316 Google reviews. It is open 6:30am to 10pm daily, cash only.

Order a vegan banh mi and ask for no egg if you are vegan. It is the cheapest good meal in town, and it is worth going out of your way for.
