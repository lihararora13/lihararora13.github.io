Novapizza is Edinburgh's first fully vegan Roman kitchen, a cosy family-run spot on Howe Street where the pizzas arrive looking like works of art. The Serena is the signature pie, the spaghettoni carbonara is rich and proper, and the menu runs through seitan dishes, cannoli and tiramisu, with a wide gluten-free range that gluten-free vegans rave about. Reviewers keep coming back to the same line: non-vegans would have no idea.

It is a small, intimate venue in the New Town, so book ahead for dinner. Order the Serena if it is your first visit and save room for cannoli. This is the table for anyone who believes pizza is a food group and wants the vegan version done properly.
