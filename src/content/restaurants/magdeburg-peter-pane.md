Peter Pane is the burger grill and bar that Magdeburg's vegans keep coming back to, because the vegan section of the menu is a real section, not an afterthought. Several kinds of vegan burgers with or without buns, green salads, fries and sauces, all clearly labelled, and a vegan dessert of chocolate pudding with coconut ice cream or vegan chocolate cake. Reviewers call the vegan burgers among the best they have had, and the build-your-own system lets you tune every topping.

It sits on the Ernst-Reuter-Allee in the city centre, open daily from late morning into the night. This is the mixed-table pick: meat eaters get their burgers, vegans get theirs, and nobody compromises. Order the Zauberwald red beet patty if it is on.
