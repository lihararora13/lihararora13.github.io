Furna is the vegan bakery on Stefan Stambolov Boulevard, the same street as the Women's Market, and its banitsa has no egg or cheese in it. It opens at 8am, Monday to Saturday, and the vegan banitsa can sell out by 10am, so go early.

It is a bakery counter rather than a restaurant, so take your banitsa to go and eat it walking the market. The leek banitsa is the one the guides name.
