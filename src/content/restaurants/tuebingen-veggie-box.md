Veggie Box is a vegetarian imbiss on the Nauklerstraße, near the university, doing falafel plates, vegan döner plates, vegan shawarma and a vegan shrimp bowl, open seven days a week until ten. It is a student counter in the best sense: quick, cheap, generous, and open when the rest of the town has closed. The vegan döner plate is the dish to order, and the falafel plate is the backup.

Order a vegan döner plate, add a falafel plate to share, and treat it as the late, easy dinner that Tübingen's students rely on.
