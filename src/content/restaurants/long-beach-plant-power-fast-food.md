Plant Power Fast Food is the Long Beach answer to the drive-through: everything 100 percent plant-based, burgers, wraps, tenders, nuggets, fries and shakes, served fast on Pacific Coast Highway with a drive-up window. The Big Zac is the signature, the tenders are the sleeper, and the shake is the finish.

It is the pick for the beach day, the road trip, and the friend who insists vegan food cannot do fast food. It can, and here it does it better than the chains it is winking at.
