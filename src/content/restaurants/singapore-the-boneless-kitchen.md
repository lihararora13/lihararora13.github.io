The Boneless Kitchen answers a very good question: can Korean comfort food, bubbling stews, sizzling stone bowls and all, work without meat? Near Tai Seng MRT, this vegetarian kitchen says yes, loudly, with vegan options clearly marked and most dishes happy to go fully vegan if you ask.

Share an army stew, chase it with a dolsot bibimbap that crackles as it lands, and add kimchi pancakes and kimbap for the table. Friendly prices, generous portions, and a room that fills up fast at lunch for good reason.
