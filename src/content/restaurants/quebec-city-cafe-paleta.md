Cafe Paleta is Quebec City's newest fully vegan cafe, opened in November 2024 on Boulevard Sainte-Anne. The menu is bright and fresh: fried tofu wraps, sandwiches, soups and salads, with a full cafe menu running on oat and almond milk.

It is a five-star HappyCow favourite already, and the friendliest lunch stop on the east side of town. Order the fried tofu wrap and the soup of the day.
