Flower Burger is the vegan burger chain famous for its rainbow buns, and its Palermo branch sits on Via Isidoro la Lumia in Politeama. It is fully vegan, informal and reliable, with colourful burgers, nuggets and fries.

Order the colourful house burgers and the nuggets, and come when you want something fast, filling and certain.
