Plant Cafe is Bahrain's first fully plant-based restaurant, founded by Saudi Prince Khaled bin Alwaleed Al Saud, in Hamala and open daily 8am to 11pm. The menu runs from a mezze board of olives, dips, falafel and flatbreads to curry with flatbread, soup, green sauce, chocolate desserts and pastries.

Order the curry with flatbread and a chocolate dessert. It is the flagship vegan table in Bahrain, and it is worth the taxi out to Hamala.
