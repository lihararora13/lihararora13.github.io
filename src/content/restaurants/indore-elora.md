Elora is one of those cafés that Indore's young crowd has quietly adopted as a second living room: easy seating, a broad continental menu and no rush to turn the table. The kitchen is pure vegetarian, so the vegan move is simple and well understood here: order the pasta or pizza without cheese, the garlic bread without butter, and you are set.

It is not a health-food temple, and that is part of the charm. Come for comfort food done well, a cold coffee, and an evening that stretches out longer than you planned.
