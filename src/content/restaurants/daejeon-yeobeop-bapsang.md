Yeobeop Bapsang is a fully vegan Korean restaurant in Yuseong-gu, Daejeon, listed as vegan on HappyCow. It serves plant-based rice sets and home-style Korean dishes, the kind of everyday vegan cooking that is hardest to find outside Seoul, and it is one of only a few fully vegan kitchens in the city.

Order the vegan rice set and the tofu dishes, and ask about the kimchi, as the kitchen is fully vegan but the kimchi recipe is worth confirming. Come at lunch, when the set is freshest.
