Rad Rabbit is the answer to the question nobody thought to ask in northern Thailand: what about a fully vegan pizzeria? The first of its kind in Chiang Mai, it pours cashew cheese over proper pizza bases, runs pasta and focaccia sandwiches alongside, and does a brunch with vegan eggs Benedict that draws a crowd. The room is small and lively, on a lane off Moon Muang Road, and it feels more like a neighbourhood hangout than a health cafe.

It is the fun pick on this list, the one for an evening with friends, a cold beer and a second slice. Come hungry and order across the table.
