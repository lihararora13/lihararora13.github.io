Harmony Plant Fare is the first stop in Cincinnati and the city's most loved vegan counter: a small deli stall inside Findlay Market, the 1852 public market in Over-the-Rhine, where everything is made in house, from the seitan and plant-based cheeses to the dressings and deli salads. The Italian sandwich on 16-Bricks sourdough is the one regulars call the best vegan deli sandwich they have had, and the buffalo chicken sandwich and vegan gyro are close behind. It is a counter, not a dining room, so eat at the market's shared seating and save room for a pastry from the market halls.

Harmony keeps market hours, Tuesday to Friday 9am to 6pm, Saturday 8am to 6pm and Sunday 10am to 4pm, and it is closed on Mondays. It also sells sauces, dressings and vegan charcuterie to take home, and caters sandwich trays. Come hungry, come early on Saturdays, and pair it with a wander through the market.
