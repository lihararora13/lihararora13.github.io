Shri Sangeetha's is the best family veg restaurant on the Trichy highway list and the first name on the city's top-five veg breakfast list, with a Thillainagar branch that the September 2026 carousel highlights for dosa and chutneys. The format is South Indian tiffin done properly: idlis, uthappams and dosas, clean and simple.

Order the idli and an uthappam, and ask for the chutney without curd. It is the Trichy breakfast, and the family room is the point.
