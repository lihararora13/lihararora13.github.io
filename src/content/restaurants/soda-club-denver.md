Soda Club spent four years earning Michelin's Bib Gourmand in New
York, then packed up and came home. Founder Ravi DeRossi is a
Colorado native, and the RiNo outpost (inside The Ramble Hotel)
serves the same fully vegan, kosher-certified menu of fresh pasta,
Roman-style pinsa, and natural wine.

The lumache with vodka sauce is the order, and the truffle crema
on the gigli will convert pasta skeptics. Brand new as of September
2026, so expect a buzzing room and a well-earned crowd.
