Ristorante Flora is a 100% vegan and gluten-free restaurant a short walk from the Arena, and the most complete vegan kitchen in Verona after La Lanterna. The cooking travels between forgotten ingredients and nutritional balance, with parmigiana di melanzane, arancini, beetroot ravioli, risotto and red bean meatballs all on the menu.

Order the parmigiana di melanzane, the beetroot ravioli and the red bean meatballs, and note the kitchen is fully gluten-free as well as vegan, which makes it the safest table in the city for mixed dietary needs.
