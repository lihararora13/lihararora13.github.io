Spritz is a 100% plant-based Italian trattoria offering full meals, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, with a vegetable-forward Mediterranean menu: vegan carbonara, vegan moussaka, grilled cauliflower steak and hummus boards, with a terrace in sunny months.

It relocated from Oude Koornmarkt to Stoofstraat, and reservations are required. It is open Thursday to Sunday evenings only, so book ahead and plan the evening around it.
