Brocoli is the quiet overachiever of Lyon's vegan scene: a fully vegan restaurant on Rue Masséna in the 6th arrondissement where vegetables get the fine-dining treatment without any of the stiffness. The cooking is refined and seasonal, the room is calm, and the tourist office itself gives it a thumbs up.

It is the table for a proper sit-down dinner when you want plants to be the stars rather than the substitutes. Ask what is seasonal, trust the chef, and let the vegetables do the talking.
