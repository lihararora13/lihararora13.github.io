Alma is a small cafe-restaurant with a fully vegan menu in a colonial house in the Walled City, and it cooks the way the coast eats when the fish is left out: sweet corn arepas with avocado, soups built on yuca and name, and grain bowls with Caribbean-spiced legumes. The fresh juices are excellent, particularly corozo and tamarind.

It closes at 4 pm, so this is a lunch stop, with mains around COP 22,000-35,000. Come for the arepas de choclo, stay for a juice, and plan dinner elsewhere.
