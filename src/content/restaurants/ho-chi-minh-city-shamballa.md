Shamballa is the hidden pearl every Saigon vegan list mentions, a tranquil tea house hidden down a District 1 side street with an inner courtyard that shuts the city out completely. The kitchen cooks without MSG, building its flavours from fragrant rice, vegetables, beans, nuts and fresh fruit, and the menu runs from pineapple fried rice and fried lotus root to generous hot pots made for sharing.

It is a vegetarian restaurant with plenty of vegan dishes, clearly marked on the menu, and reviewers keep calling it a must-visit and the best vegetarian meal in the city. Come for a slow lunch, order the hot pot for the table, and linger over tea in the courtyard afterwards.
