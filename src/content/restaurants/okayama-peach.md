PEACH is a small plant-based restaurant in the K&K Omotecho Building, a few minutes from Okayama Station, and HappyCow's highest-rated listing in the city at a full five cows. The menu changes with the seasons and leans on local Okayama produce, soy meat and a dessert course, with iced and hot coffee alongside. Reservations are recommended by DM on Instagram, and the kitchen is fully vegan, so there is nothing to negotiate at the table.

Order the seasonal set if it is running, ask what the soy meat main is that day, and save room for dessert. It is open late by Okayama standards, which makes it the easiest dinner anchor in the city, and it sits in the Omotecho shopping streets, so it pairs naturally with an afternoon around the castle and Korakuen Garden.
