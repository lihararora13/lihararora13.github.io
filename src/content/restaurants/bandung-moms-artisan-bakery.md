Mom's Artisan Bakery is tucked into a small house in the centre of Bandung, and it is always buzzing with customers. The menu has limited pure vegan options, but you can always ask for sandwiches without cheese, or opt for a falafel pita without tzatziki, and the healthy breads are the stars: sourdough, whole wheat and ciabatta, made without sugar, additives or preservatives.

Order a falafel pita and a big salad, and take a loaf home. It is a meat-serving bakery with clearly labelled vegetarian options, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note the pin is neighbourhood-level for the Progo and Citarum area.
