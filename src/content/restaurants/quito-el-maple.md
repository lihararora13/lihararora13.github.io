El Maple is Quito's beloved vegetarian institution, a big, friendly room in La Mariscal where the menu runs from Ecuadorian to Chinese to Mexican. Veggie burgers, soy steak, potato cakes, beetroot, veggie chorizo and a vegetarian version of encebollado, Ecuador's national fish soup.

Order a veggie burger and a fruit smoothie. It is the easy group dinner in La Mariscal, and the room has been feeding Quito's vegetarians for years.
