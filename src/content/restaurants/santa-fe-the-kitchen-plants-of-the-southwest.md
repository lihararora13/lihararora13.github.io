The Kitchen at Plants of the Southwest is Santa Fe's most unusual lunch: a single daily two-course prix fixe, cooked from whatever the farmers market brought in that morning, served among the nursery plants on Agua Fria Street. The menu changes daily, but roasted eggplant with chickpeas and the lemon ginger spritzer are the kind of dishes that show up again and again.

There is one lunch seating, Wednesday to Saturday from noon to 2pm, and reservations are required. Book ahead, and treat it as the slow lunch it is meant to be.
