HaleLife Bakery is an all-vegan bakery on South Dale Mabry Highway, doing pastries, cakes, and brunch plates with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the sweet anchor of this list, and the place to order a whole cake for a celebration.

Order a vegan pastry and a cake slice, and ask what is fresh that day, since the case rotates.
