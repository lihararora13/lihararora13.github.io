Am Thuc Chay Loc Chien is a vegetarian restaurant in Phu Vang, east of Hue's centre, serving local home-style dishes. It is a neighbourhood kitchen rather than a destination, and it is the most easterly pick in this guide.

Order the vegetable and tofu dishes with rice, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. Its pin is approximate to Phu Vang, so allow time for the trip.
