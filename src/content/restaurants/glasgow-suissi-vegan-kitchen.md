Suissi Vegan Kitchen is the table Glasgow's vegans fight over: an all-vegan Asian restaurant in Partick, opened in 2019 by Mama Lim and her family, cooking Malaysian-inspired dishes with a warm, home-cooked approach. The curry laksa is the dish reviewers compare to versions they ate abroad, the gyoza and tempeh bao are the starters to share, and the banana fritters with syrup and ice cream are the finish.

It is small and popular, so reservations are recommended. Order the laksa, add gyoza, and book ahead. The most ambitious plant-based cooking in Glasgow.
