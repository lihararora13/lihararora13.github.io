Danau Toba is an Indonesian restaurant on Madenge Street with several vegan and vegetarian options, noodles, rice and tempeh. Reviewers call the vegan nasi goreng with tempeh amazing, and the service excellent, with a playground for kids.

Order the vegan nasi goreng with tempeh, and ask for the avocado juice without milk. It is a good family option in Masaki.
