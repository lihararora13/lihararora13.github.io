Nolita is the table every Wellington vegan guide starts with, and for good reason: it is New Zealand's first 100% plant-based pizzeria, right in the heart of Cuba Street, and it treats vegan Italian food as a craft rather than a compromise. The rye sourdough is fermented for 24 hours and hand-stretched, the cashew mozzarella is cultured in-house, and the menu runs from sourdough pizzas and pastas to a brunch of scrambled tofu and carbonara made with house cashew parmesan. Reviewers call it simply the best Italian restaurant in New Zealand, and it happens to be vegan too.

It is a superb brunch spot by day and a pizzeria by evening, small and popular, so book ahead if you can. Order a spread to share: the Pollo pizza with its tangy BBQ sauce and smoky cheddar notes, the truffle burger, a Margherita to judge the dough on its own merits, and the cacio e pepe loaded fries. This is the table to take someone who thinks vegan pizza means a sad compromise.
