ChapiYork is the fast-food end of Chapinero's vegan scene and the locals clearly love it: a 4.8 rating from more than 500 Google reviews for a small menu that punches well above its size. The star is the arepa de huevo, the Colombian egg-stuffed arepa rebuilt entirely plant-based, and reviewers call it incredible. Around it sit chili cheese dogs, vegan wings, mazorcadas loaded with corn and sauces, nachos, empanadas, brownies and ice cream, all fully vegan since March 2023.

It is quick, casual and cheap, the kind of place you hit between errands and end up telling people about. Order the arepa de huevo first, then work your way down the menu.
