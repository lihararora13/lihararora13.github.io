Saude na Panela has been cooking natural food in Salvador since 1997, and it shows: over 400 recipes, from risottos and gratins to grills, stews, pastas, salads and special desserts, all prepared without eggs, meat, butter or fresh milk. The food is wholesome without being worthy, and the carrot cake with chocolate topping is a local legend.

Come for lunch, order a risotto and a gratinado, and finish with the carrot cake. This is the one for a long, wholesome Salvador lunch.
