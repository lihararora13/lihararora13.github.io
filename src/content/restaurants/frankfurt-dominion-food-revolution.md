Dominion Food Revolution is a vegan and kosher fast-food diner with a mission: run by animal rights activists since July 2020, it serves burgers, loaded fries and Middle Eastern dishes like hummus, falafel and shawarma, all of it plant-based and all of it fast. The shawarma burger is the signature, and the loaded fries are the reason to come back.

It is cheap, cheerful and unapologetic, the perfect counterpoint to Frankfurt's fine dining. Order the shawarma burger, add loaded fries, and eat it like a revolution.
