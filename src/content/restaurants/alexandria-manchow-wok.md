Manchow Wok is a Chinese restaurant with a vegetarian section and staff who are transparent about what is in the food, which matters here because the menu does not say vegan anywhere. The vegetable hakka noodle and stir-fried mixed vegetables are the picks, and reviewers loved both.

Ask which dishes have no egg or dairy, then order the hakka noodles. The San Stefano branch is the convenient one for visitors staying on the eastern Corniche.
