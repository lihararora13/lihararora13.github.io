Brindhavan is the dosa hall Kochi locals argue about most passionately: a strictly pure-vegetarian, egg-free kitchen on Civil Line Road in Palarivattom, open daily from 7am to 10:30pm, with a 4.4 on Google from over 16,000 reviews. The dosa is famously large, served with coconut chutney, sambar and chutneys, and the filter coffee is poured tumbler to cup the Kumbakonam way.

Dosa, idli and vada are vegan as served if you skip the ghee, so order them plain and say no ghee. Come early for breakfast or expect a queue at lunch; it is walk-in only.
