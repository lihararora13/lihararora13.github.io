Nana's Kitchen fills the one gap Phoenix's vegan scene used to have: soul food. What started as a food truck in 2018, then a Chandler restaurant in 2021, moved into downtown Phoenix in October 2026, two blocks north of the Arizona State Capitol, and the new room is already the place for generous, comforting plates. The Soul XL platter piles smoked BBQ ribs, honey-hot cauliflower wangz, fried chik'n, collard greens, mac and cheese and cornbread onto one very full plate, and the crispy chik'n sandwich has its own fan club.

Come hungry, because the portions are famously generous and the peach cobbler is not optional. It is the warmest room on this list, and the food tastes like it.
