Farmstand is the physical home of Eat Local Kobe, a grassroots movement connecting nearly 200 local farmers, brewers, bakers and makers, halfway up the slope toward Kitano. The café and grocer serves a daily vegan meal, main dish, soup, salad and bread, from produce sourced within Japan, some of it carrying cards naming the farmer who grew it, alongside homemade breakfast bakes and a shop full of organic vegetables, juices and nuts.

It is a veg-options restaurant rather than a vegetarian one, so ask at the counter and the kitchen will happily make the meal vegan. Call ahead to confirm the day's vegan meal, and allow time for the walk: about seven minutes from JR Sannomiya toward the mountain.
