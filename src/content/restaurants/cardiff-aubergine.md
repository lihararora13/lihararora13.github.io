Aubergine is a fully vegan Caribbean café in Cathays, and it is the most distinctive cooking on this page. The vegan mutton curry is the signature, with a mac and cheese toasty, a New Yorker and banana bread alongside, all of it plant-based.

Use the new address: Aubergine relocated from 26 Clare Street to 36-38 Cathays Terrace, at the Cathays Community Centre, and older guides still show the old one. The mutton curry is the order.
