Why we love it — Âu Lạc is the family-run DTLA restaurant that converts
skeptics nightly. The whole menu is plant-based and organic, and the kitchen
treats mock-meat with rare artistry: the salt-and-pepper "shrimp" are yam
based and uncannily convincing, the garlic basil noodles are a quiet classic,
and the attached jazz lounge makes dinner feel like an event. It has been
doing this longer than most of LA's vegan scene has existed.

At 4.6 stars over a thousand-plus reviews, it is the highest-rated vegan
Vietnamese room in the city by a wide margin. Walk in before a show at the
Music Center, or just walk in.
