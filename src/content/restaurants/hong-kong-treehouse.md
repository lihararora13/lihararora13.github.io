TREEHOUSE is the lunch the whole of Central seems to agree on: a plant-based kitchen in the H Code complex where the sourdough is fermented in-house, the sauces are made from scratch, and the queue moves with cheerful efficiency. At 4.7 stars it is the highest-rated vegan kitchen in the city, and repeat visitors swear by the build-your-own flatbreads.

Go long on a wrap, Cedar if you cannot decide, pile a bowl high with grains and roasted veg, and save room for the brownie. Almost the entire menu is vegan; egg and halloumi exist only as optional add-ons, which is why we label it vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan. Eco packaging and plastic-free everything seal the deal.
