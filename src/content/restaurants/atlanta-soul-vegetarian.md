Soul Vegetarian has been doing this since before it was cool, since before it was a movement, since 1979. This Virginia-Highland institution is a pillar of Atlanta's Black vegan scene, serving the kind of soul food that makes omnivores sit down and rethink their whole life.

The Kalbone (that's kale plus seitan rib, keep up) is the signature, and the mac and cheese, collard greens, and cornbread will ruin every other version for you. Warm, affordable, and nourishing in the way only a 45-year labor of love can be.
