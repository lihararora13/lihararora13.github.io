Malizioso is the Cerro Alegre pizzeria that local vegan guides name for pizza, sitting on the same hill as Medialuna and La Boca del Oso, which makes the Alegre-Concepcion walk a complete vegan circuit. The pizzas are the draw, with vegan cheese and vegetable toppings, and the room is built for an evening rather than a quick lunch.

Order a vegan pizza and a salad to share. Confirm the cheese is the vegan one when you order, as the kitchen also serves conventional pizzas.
