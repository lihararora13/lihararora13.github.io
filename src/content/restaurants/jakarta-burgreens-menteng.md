Burgreens is the chain that made vegan fast food normal in Jakarta, and the Menteng flagship is its best room: bright, busy and built around Indonesian ingredients. The menu runs from smoothie bowls and salads to mini burgers, crispy tofu salad and vegan steak, all of it plant-based and clearly labelled.

It is the easiest first stop in the city, open for breakfast through dinner and happy to do takeaway. Order the tempeh gomashio if you want the Indonesian side of the menu, or the mini burger if you want proof that vegan fast food can be fun.
