Cor del Pilar is Valencia's most ambitious new vegan table, a small old-town restaurant where dinner is a proper occasion. Vegan carpaccio, Thai curry with vegetables and tofu, croquettes, all cooked with a fine-dining touch and served in a room that only seats a few tables at a time.

Reservations are required, and lunch is the easier booking. Order the carpaccio to start, the Thai curry as a main, and trust the kitchen with dessert.
