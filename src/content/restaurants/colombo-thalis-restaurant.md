Thalis Restaurant made the list for a reason. A South Indian vegetarian thali and dosa eatery on Park Road where, apart from a few ghee and curd dishes, the menu is vegan. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Colombo 5, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Veg thali, Dosai, Vadai if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
