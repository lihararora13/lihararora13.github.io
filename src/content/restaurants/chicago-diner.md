The Chicago Diner has been serving meat-free food since 1983, which
makes it older than most of the vegans eating there. It is a vegetarian
diner with a deep bench of vegan options, and it is the single most
cited restaurant on every Chicago vegan list we read. The Radical
Reuben, seitan corned beef on marbled rye, has a genuine cult
following, and the vegan milkshakes are the kind of thing people plan
whole trips around.

This is comfort food with forty years of practice behind it, served
in retro booths to a room that has never once asked you to compromise.
Every Chicago vegan crawl starts here, and ours is no different.
