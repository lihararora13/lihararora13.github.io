Kamat, at Al Wahda Mall, claims more than 360 vegetarian dishes, spanning North Indian, South Indian, Chinese and fusion, and it is one of the biggest vegetarian restaurants in the capital. The menu leans on paneer and ghee, so vegans need to ask, but the dosa, vegetable thali and makki ki roti are all vegan-friendly with a word to the kitchen.

Order the dosa and a vegetable thali, ask about ghee, and enjoy the sheer scale of the place. It is the mall-day pick.
