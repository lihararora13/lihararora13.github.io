Days Kitchen Vegetable House sits on Sakaemachidori in the old merchant quarter, a salad bar and café built around seasonal vegetables, fresh pasta and oil pasta. It is a vegetarian-leaning restaurant with vegan dishes across the menu, open daily 11:30 to 21:00, and it works as an easy lunch or dinner between Motomachi and the waterfront.

Order the salad bar to graze through the season's vegetables, then a fresh pasta. Ask which dishes are vegan when you order, as the menu mixes vegetarian and vegan plates.
