Stereo is Mono's sister venue and Glasgow's vegan party: a café, bar and live music venue in a Charles Rennie Mackintosh building on Renfield Lane, serving an all-vegan menu by day and turning into a club and gig space by night. The food is playful and generous, haggis bon bons, baked celeriac, dan dan noodles, polenta with oyster mushrooms, buffalo cauliflower, plus black forest tiramisu for dessert.

Come for dinner, stay for the gig. Order the haggis bon bons, add the dan dan noodles, and enjoy the most Glasgow night out on this list.
