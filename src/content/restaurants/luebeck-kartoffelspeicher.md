Kartoffelspeicher is a baked-potato counter on the Obertrave, and it is the most Lübeck stop on this list: Kumpir, the Turkish stuffed baked potato, piled with fillings of your choice, eaten by the water a minute from the Holstentor. The staff will help you mix and match a vegan version, and the result is a proper, filling meal.

There is a catch, and it matters: there are no ready vegan dishes here, and cheese goes on by default, so you have to say vegan clearly when you order. Do that, pick mushrooms and vegan dips, and you will eat very well. This is the table for a quick, cheap, satisfying lunch in Lübeck.
