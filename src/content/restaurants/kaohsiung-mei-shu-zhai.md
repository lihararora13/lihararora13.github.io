Mei Shu Zhai, 美蔬齋, is a small mint-green noodle house on Liuhe Road in the Xinxing office quarter, and it is the most technically accomplished kitchen in this guide: the founder and chef, a graduate of the Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism and a silver medallist at the Bodhi Golden Chef vegetarian competition, opened it in 2021 and builds Chinese classics with a strict no-egg, no-milk, no-alliums standard. The house makes its own veggie cutlets, sweet-and-sour pork and minced-mushroom sauce, and the dishes to order are the sesame soy milk ramen, the hot and sour soup noodles, the roselle sweet-and-sour pork rice and the veggie cutlet with minced-mushroom rice, with a vegetable soup curry noodle and a clay pot mapo tofu behind them. Set meals run NT$150 to NT$300, and there is a small dessert case with daily vegan bakes, including a Basque cheesecake.

The room is small and often full, so expect a wait at lunch and dinner. Hours are roughly 11:30am to 1:30pm and 5:00pm to 8:00pm, with irregular closures each month, so check the shop's fan page before you go. The shop was still trading in November 2026, when it posted a closure notice for the 18th of the month.
