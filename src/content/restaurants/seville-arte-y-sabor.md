Arte y Sabor is not a vegan restaurant, which is exactly why it is worth including: a local favourite where the vegan dishes are so good that reviewers come back just to eat there again. Falafel, lentil salad, seitan churrasco and a pea burger that one reviewer called the best burger they had ever had, vegan or not.

Order the pea burger and the seitan churrasco, and ask for the vegan dessert. It is the Alameda dinner that works for a mixed group without anyone compromising.
