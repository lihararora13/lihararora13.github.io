Why we love it — The Green Affair answers the question nobody else in India
had asked: where does a vegan party? The two-storey rooftop lounge at
Breach Candy serves vegan pizzas, burgers, mezze and tapas alongside a bar
built around signature vegan cocktails, with artist performances on some
nights and Jain options on the menu.

Come for sunset on the rooftop, stay for the cocktails. It is the loudest,
glitziest vegan room in the country.
