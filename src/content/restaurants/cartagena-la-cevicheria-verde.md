La Cevicheria Verde is the cleverest idea in Cartagena's vegan scene: a cevicheria that keeps the lime, the onion and the crunch of the city's most famous dish and swaps the fish for heart of palm and mushrooms. It is a separate, fully plant-based operation, not the seafood restaurant of a similar name, and the flavour profile lands genuinely close to the original.

Order the house vegan ceviche, ask for patacones on the side, and treat it as lunch rather than a late dinner, as the kitchen is built around the midday trade. Mains run about COP 28,000-42,000, and the room is small, so it fills up at peak hours.
