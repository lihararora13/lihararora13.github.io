Roots Cafe in Sector 29 is the quiet one: a thatched bamboo roof, a calm room, and a menu that leans healthy and plant-friendly without making a fuss about it.

The falafel wrap and the salad bowl are the orders, and the cold coffee is the afternoon companion. It is a good reset button after the Sector 29 crowds.
