030 Ronin is a fusion spot on the Jakoberstraße with a dedicated vegan sushi menu, which makes it the place in Augsburg for a plant-based sushi evening. The room does lunch and dinner with a split service, cocktails in the evening, and a menu that takes the vegan side seriously rather than treating it as a single roll. It is a small, modern space, and the sushi is the reason to come.

It is closed on Mondays. Order a vegan sushi set, add a maki or two, and stay for a cocktail if it is a Friday or Saturday night.
