VRÜH is Dortmund's fully vegan breakfast café, a bright Kreuzviertel room on Saarlandstraße that does all-day breakfast the way it should be done: Rührtofu, avocado toast, blueberry pancakes, patatas bravas and a banana split for anyone who treats brunch as dessert. It is the city's only fully vegan anchor in this guide, and it earns the top spot on consistency alone, open Tuesday to Sunday from nine until six.

Come for a slow morning rather than a rushed one: the room fills with students and Kreuzviertel locals, and the takeaway store a few streets away on Kreuzstraße covers the same menu to go. Order the Rührtofu first, the pancakes second, and take your time.
