Aromaticus is the prettiest room on this list, a plant-filled bistro in Trastevere where the menu leans on aromatic herbs, edible flowers and sprouts. The cooking is colourful and precise: glazed tempeh, artichoke fettuccine, beetroot risotto, Pad Thai, and a vegan tiramisu, with fresh juices and a curated wine list alongside. Google reviewers give the Trastevere room 4.6 stars, and the 2026 guides all send you here.

One honest note: most of the menu is vegan, but not all of it, so it is the one vegan-friendly pick on this list rather than fully vegan. The vegan dishes are clearly marked and the kitchen is happy to walk you through them. Go for a polished, relaxed dinner with outdoor seating in summer.
