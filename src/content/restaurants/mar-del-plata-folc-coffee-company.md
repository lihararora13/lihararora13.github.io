FOLC Coffee Company is a vegan-friendly cafe spot in Centro. FOLC is Mar del Plata's speciality-coffee house, an all-day brunch spot known for its flat whites and avocado toast, with plant milks and hummus on toast among the vegan-friendly options.

Order the Flat white con leche vegetal, Tostada con hummus, and Bagel vegano. The flat white with plant milk and the hummus toast are the vegan-safe orders.
