Aurora Restaurante Antiespecista is a 5.0-rated all-vegan restaurant, cafe and activism venue in Rio Branco, serving lunch every day on a pay-what-you-think-is-fair model. It relocated from Rua Professor Duplan to Rua Miguel Tostes, and it also offers cookery co-working, which tells you the kind of room it is.

Order the set lunch and ask what the daily special is. It is open Tuesday to Friday for lunch, Saturday and Sunday for a longer lunch, and Saturday evenings, so check the hours before you go.
