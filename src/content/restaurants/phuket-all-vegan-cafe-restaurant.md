All Vegan Cafe' Restaurant is Rawai's fully vegan cafe, open since June 2023 and rated a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow. The menu is the island's most Mexican-leaning vegan offering: burritos, tacos, burgers, panini and toasts alongside Thai food and bowls, with outdoor seating and wheelchair access. It is open daily, 8:30am to 8pm, which makes it the reliable all-day pick on the south coast.

It sits on Soi Samakki 2 in Rawai, among the tourist shops, and it pairs well with Go Vegan Cafe down the road for a Rawai vegan crawl.
