Market is the kind of place locals queue for at lunch and tourists walk straight past, which is exactly why it belongs on this list. The Dizengoff branch turned all-vegan in 2016, and every day brings a different rotation of stews and soups, Indian one day, Mexican or Moroccan the next, alongside sandwiches, burgers and salads.

Expect a line at peak hours, an English menu, and a bill that feels like a bargain by Tel Aviv standards. Go midday, ask what is on today, and eat whatever the kitchen felt like cooking.
