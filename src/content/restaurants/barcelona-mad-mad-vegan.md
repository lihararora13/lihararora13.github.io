Why we love it — Mad Mad Vegan tops the review charts for a
reason: it is the rare vegan burger joint that out-indulges the meat
originals. The Aribau flagship piles veef patties, molten cheddar, and
house sauces into burgers that need a jaw reset, and the sides menu is
a playground of tequeños, buffalo cauliflower wings, and bacon-topped
cheese fries. Loud, fun, and dangerously craveable.

Come hungry and order like there is no tomorrow. The Double Cheese
Bacon and the Nashville chick'n are the heavyweight contenders, and
the tequeños with berry jam might be the best starter in Eixample.
Nobody leaves this place a skeptic.
