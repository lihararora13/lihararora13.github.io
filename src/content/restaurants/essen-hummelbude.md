Hummelbude is Essen's fully vegan restaurant in Frohnhausen, a casual kitchen on Frohnhauser Straße that cooks across the map: birria tacos with jackfruit, gyoza, momos, har gow and arayes, with almost all ingredients in organic quality. The menu changes with the seasons and leans on small plates and sharing dishes, which makes it the most fun table on this list.

Hours are Thursday to Sunday, with Wednesday evenings added, so check before travelling out to Frohnhausen. Order the birria tacos first, share the dumplings, and treat it as the evening meal the city centre buffet is not.
