Greenr Cafe at 32nd Avenue is the most aesthetically complete plant-based cafe in Gurugram: earthy interiors, boho styling, handwritten notes on the walls, and a fully plant-based kitchen behind it. It is the one every local vegan guide agrees on, and the 4.4 delivery rating backs it up.

The hummus platter and the shroom ball spaghetti are the signatures, and the vegan burgers and smoothie bowls fill out a menu that never makes you ask what is safe to eat. Come for brunch and stay for the greenery.
