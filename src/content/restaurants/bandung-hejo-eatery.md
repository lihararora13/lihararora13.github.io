Hejo Eatery is Bandung's fully vegan flagship: an Indonesian and Asian restaurant on Eyckman Street in Pasteur, with outdoor seating, free Wi-Fi and a menu that runs from traditional dishes like nasi padang komplit and sate to curry rice, kimchi fried rice, rendang, tom yum soup and yaki udon.

Order the nasi padang komplit and a sate, and add the vegan chicken katsu curry. It is fully vegan, inexpensive, open daily from 9am to 9pm, and the first table to book in Bandung.
