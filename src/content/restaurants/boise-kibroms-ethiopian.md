Kibrom's is an Ethiopian and Eritrean restaurant on West State Street, and Ethiopian cooking is naturally generous to vegans: lentil, chickpea, and collard green wots served on spongy injera. The combination platter is the way to eat here, shared family-style.

Order the vegetarian combination platter and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparations, since some wots are finished with niter kibbeh butter. Eat with your hands, tearing the injera to scoop.
