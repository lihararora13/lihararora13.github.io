Loving Earth Yoga Cafe is the table to book first in Kochi: Kerala's first vegan cafe, opened before COVID in a white vine-covered house on Napier Street, serving 100% cruelty-free food in a lush garden setting with a yoga studio upstairs. It holds a 4.5 on Google from over 700 reviews and on TripAdvisor, where a March 2026 visit calls it the best plant-based food in Fort Kochi.

The menu runs from vegan sushi and burritos to pizzas topped with the house cashew cheese, plus raw vegan desserts like the passion fruit tart and watermelon tuna. The cafe closed briefly and reopened bigger with the yoga studio, and now trades daily from noon to 9:30pm; one aggregator still shows a stale temporarily-closed tag, but TripAdvisor's live hours and recent reviews show it trading. Order the Veg Out Sushi, the cheese soy minced burrito and a slice of tart.
