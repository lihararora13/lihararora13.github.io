Lyfe is Maadi's health-leaning plant-based kitchen, with mock-beef wraps, big salads and dairy-free options across the menu. It is lighter and greener than the comfort-food spots, and it suits a lunch after a morning at the pyramids or a workout.

Order the mock-beef wrap, add a Buddha bowl, and finish with a dairy-free smoothie. It is the sensible, tasty middle of the Maadi vegan scene.
