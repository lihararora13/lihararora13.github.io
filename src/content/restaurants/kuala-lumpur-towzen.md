Towzen has been perfecting vegan ramen in Kyoto since 2004, and its KL outpost in Menara Milenium is the real deal: soy-mylk broths with proper depth, tantan noodles with a slow burn, and queues at lunch that tell you everything. Vegan Food Quest visited for its October 2026 guide update and rated the ramen and tofu katsu first rate.

Go for the signature soymylk ramen if it is your first bowl, the spicy mala if you like a tingle, and do not skip the curry katsu set. Small, busy and completely vegan, it is a Damansara Heights lunch worth the wait.
