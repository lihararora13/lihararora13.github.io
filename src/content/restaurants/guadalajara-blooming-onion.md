Blooming Onion is Guadalajara's vegan fast-food outpost in Zapopan, and it leans into the fun: burgers, onion rings, loaded fries, all vegan, all made for a hungry crowd. The name is the order.

Order the blooming onion and a burger. It is a short ride west of the centre, and worth it when you want comfort food rather than another taco, excellent as the tacos are.
