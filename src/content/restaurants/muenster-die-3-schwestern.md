Die 3 Schwestern is a cosy vegan café on Wolbecker Straße in the Hansaviertel, built for brunch: avocado toast, banana bread French toast, smoothie bowls, açai bowls, pancakes and a case of vegan cakes that changes daily, with oat milk as the default and matcha and chai lattes alongside. Tables are hard to get at weekends, which says most of what needs saying.

The food menu is vegan, but dairy milk is reportedly still offered for coffee, so ask for oat milk explicitly. Open Tuesday to Sunday from nine, with evening service on Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, it is Münster's prettiest brunch table.
