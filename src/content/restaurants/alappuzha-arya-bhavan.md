Arya Bhavan on Mullakkal Main Road is the Alleppey pure-veg institution, open from 6:30 in the morning by the Rajarajeswari Temple. The ghee roast dosa and the afternoon banana-leaf meal are the dishes the guides name, and the jackfruit chips are the snack to take away.

Order the roast without ghee and the banana-leaf meal without curd. It is the town's central veg room, and the temple is next door.
