Jiu Liu Su Shi, 九六素食處, has been cooking vegan home-style food since 1968, tucked down a narrow lane north of Taichung TRA station, next to a temple. The owner has cooked without egg or dairy for more than fifty years, and the room runs like a neighbourhood canteen: pick dishes from the display, order stir-fried dishes from the menu, and help yourself to free-flowing soup. The signature flat noodles, a bowl of flat noodles with vegan ground meat, vegan meatballs, water spinach, shiitake and Chinese celery, cost just NT$35, and the dumplings and tofu cutlets are the dishes reviewers keep naming as favourites.

Recent visitors in January 2026 paid 410 TWD for nine dishes between them, including fried rice, hot pot, wonton soup, tofu cutlets, dumplings and a vegan meatball rice cake. Bring cash, and bring your own utensils if you want to avoid disposable chopsticks. Open Monday to Saturday, 7:30am to 7:30pm.
