Ambiente is York's tapas address, a busy little bar on Fossgate where the vegan dishes are marked on the menu rather than improvised. It works best as a shared evening meal.

Order patatas bravas, padron peppers and whatever vegan specials are chalked up. Book ahead on Friday and Saturday nights.
