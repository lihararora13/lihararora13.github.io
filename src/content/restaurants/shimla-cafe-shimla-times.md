Cafe Shimla Times on Mall Road is the view cafe: a rooftop terrace with wooden tables, hanging Edison bulbs and a mustard-yellow vintage Ambassador car for decor, overlooking the town. The veggie pizza with olives, peppers and broccoli is the must-try, and the coffee is the companion.

Order the veggie pizza without cheese and take the terrace table. It is the most photogenic meal on this list, and the food holds up.
