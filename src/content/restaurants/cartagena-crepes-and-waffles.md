Crepes & Waffles is a Colombian chain, not a discovery, but its Bocagrande branch earns its place here because the vegan options are clearly marked and genuinely good: the pita en el bosque, salads, and vegan ice cream are all reliable, and the kitchen is used to vegan orders.

It is the safest bet in the city for a mixed group or a late, tired arrival. Order the pita en el bosque, finish with the vegan ice cream, and save the adventurous eating for the smaller kitchens on this list.
