Vasenapoli is the pure-veg tiffin room on RK Beach Road, beside the Victory at Sea memorial and the Ambica Sea Green hotel, open for breakfast and dinner. It is the beach-road dosa stop, and the sea is a minute's walk from the table.

Order the masala dosa and a filter coffee, and ask for the dosa without ghee. It is the beach breakfast, and the location is the point.
