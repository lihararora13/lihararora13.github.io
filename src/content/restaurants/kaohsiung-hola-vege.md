Hola Vege, 歐拉蔬食工坊, is a tiny vegan Japanese counter on Qingdao Street in Sanmin, and it is the most charming small room in this guide: about ten seats, spotless, air-conditioned and quiet, with the vegan mayo on the okonomiyaki made in-house. The menu is Japanese comfort food, all of it vegan and labelled in English: classic okonomiyaki, wasabi okonomiyaki, modern-yaki with noodles folded in, chow mein, red rice with vegetables, tofu balls, dorayaki and laksa. Most dishes cost under NT$200, and the classic okonomiyaki is the dish reviewers keep coming back for.

The shop relocated here from Rehe 1st Street, and it was reported fully vegan in June 2023, with a HappyCow listing update in April 2025 and a food video from September 2026 showing the okonomiyaki still trading. Cash only, and takeaway is popular. Go early or off-peak: the seats fill fast, the last dine-in order is at 6:00pm, seating closes at 6:30pm, and there is no restroom inside, the nearby 7-Eleven is the usual substitute.
