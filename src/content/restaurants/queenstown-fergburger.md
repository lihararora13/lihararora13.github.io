Fergburger is Queenstown's famous burger bar on Shotover Street, open until midnight, serving the Holier Than Thou tofu burger among its menu. It is a late-night institution, and it is the best vegan-friendly burger in Queenstown.

Order the Holier Than Thou tofu burger and fries, and expect a queue at peak times. It is central and reliable, and it suits a late dinner.
