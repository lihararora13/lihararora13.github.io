Why we love it — Buddha's has been feeding Toronto vegans for over 30 years, and it shows in the best way: fast, generous, and deeply comforting. The mock-meat game here is legendary enough to make skeptics double-check their plates; the General Tso's veggie chicken and duck bean curd are the stuff of local legend. Nothing is fussy and everything is affordable, which is exactly the point.

Ignore the humble storefront and judge this place by its portions. Order the chow mein, the hot and sour soup, and whatever sounds like it shouldn't be vegan, then marvel at the bill. Closed Tuesdays, beloved always.
