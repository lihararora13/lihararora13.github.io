vorveg is Hannover's fully vegan breakfast and brunch café, and it is the weekend-morning stop on this list: tofu scramble, pancakes and a proper brunch plate, all plant-based, in a bright room on Gustav-Bratke-Allee. The menu is short and done well, and the room fills up on weekend mornings with people who have made it their regular.

It opens Friday to Sunday, so plan around the weekend. Go for the brunch plate, add pancakes if you are still hungry, and take your time over coffee. This is the table for a slow weekend morning in Hannover.
