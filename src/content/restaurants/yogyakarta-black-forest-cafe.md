Black Forest Cafe is a 100% vegan cafe on Parangtritis Road, all forest murals, wooden tables and a neon sign reading Love Animals, Eat Plants. The menu runs from burgers and Hawaiian pizza to teriyaki bowls and rawon soup, with its namesake black forest cake and a biscoff latte on the drinks list.

Order the vegan burger and a teriyaki bowl, and finish with the cake. It is fully vegan, it is open daily from 8am to 11pm, and it is the most reliable all-day vegan room in the city.
