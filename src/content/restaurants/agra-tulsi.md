Tulsi is the new face on this list: a pure-vegetarian restaurant newly opened at Basant Integral Square on Fatehabad Road, near the Taj East Gate metro station, from India's fastest-growing pure-veg chain. The dining room is big, bright, and built for family dining, dates and celebrations.

The vegetarian spread is extensive, with curries, biryani and mocktails. Ask for the vegan preparation, no ghee and no cream, and the kitchen will sort you out.
