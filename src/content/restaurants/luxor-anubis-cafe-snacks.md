Anubis Cafe and Snacks is a small cafe on the Corniche, a snack spot rather than a restaurant, with vegan-friendly snacks and drinks. It is useful for a quick, cheap bite between temples.

Order a falafel sandwich and a fresh juice. It is the lightest pick on this list, included because Luxor has so few options, so keep expectations modest.
