The V-GAN Restaurant is the highest-rated vegetarian dining room in the city, with 4.7 stars across more than 5,000 reviews, and it sits inside the Ginger Hotel on Railway Station Road. It tops this list on quality and consistency, and the room is comfortable enough for a long dinner after a day at the caves.

One honest note: despite the name, the kitchen is vegetarian rather than all-vegan, with paneer and dairy desserts on the menu. Order the dal tadka without ghee, a mixed veg sabzi and hot rotis, and the meal is properly plant-based. The name is a promise the menu does not quite keep, so this guide says so plainly.
