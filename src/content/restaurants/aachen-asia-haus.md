Asia Haus is an Asian restaurant on Jülicher Straße, a straightforward neighbourhood kitchen with a solid vegan side: noodle dishes, tofu stir-fries and spring rolls on a mixed menu, served fast and cheap. The room is unfussy and the portions are generous, which makes it the practical dinner pick on this list.

It is open Monday to Friday from half past eleven until half past nine and Sundays from two until half past nine, with Saturdays closed. Order the tofu stir-fry, ask for the vegan preparation, and treat it as Aachen's reliable Asian fallback.
