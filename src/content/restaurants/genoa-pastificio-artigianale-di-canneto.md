Pastificio Artigianale di Canneto is a handmade pasta workshop in the old town, and the most Ligurian stop on the list. The menu runs on fresh pasta made that day: gnocchi al pesto, ravioli al pesto and vegan ravioli with walnut ragu, eaten at the counter or taken away.

Order the vegan ravioli with walnut ragu and the gnocchi al pesto, and ask which pasta of the day is vegan, since the menu changes with the kitchen. Pair it with a slice of farinata from Antica Sciamadda nearby, open for over two centuries.
