Ezham Suvai is the authentic vegetarian restaurant in Thiruvanaikoil, named on the September 2026 list of the city's top five veg breakfast spots. It sits on the Chennai Trunk Road near the Srirangam temple route, which makes it the breakfast stop for the temple run.

Order the idli and a masala dosa, and ask for the dosa without ghee. It is the temple-side tiffin room, and the food is honest.
