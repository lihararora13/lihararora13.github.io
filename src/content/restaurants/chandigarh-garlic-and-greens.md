Garlic and Greens is Chandigarh's health-forward table: a restaurant built around fresh produce, Continental cooking and a menu that leans naturally plant-friendly. It appears on every local vegetarian list for good reason.

Order the garden salad bowl and a vegan pasta, and ask the kitchen to keep the cheese off. It is a calm, modern room, and a good reset after the sector markets.
