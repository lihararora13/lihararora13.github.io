Alfanzo is a straightforward, inexpensive pure-veg restaurant on one of Gwalior's main roads, open from 9 AM to 11 PM and built for everyday eating rather than occasions. The menu leans North Indian, with sabudana khichdi and thalis among the dishes regulars name first.

It is the budget anchor of this list. Order the khichdi or a thali without ghee, add roti and a seasonal sabzi, and you have a solid meal for very little money, right in the middle of the city.
