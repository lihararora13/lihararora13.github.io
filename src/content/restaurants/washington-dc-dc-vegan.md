Why we love it — DC Vegan took home the Washington City Paper readers' poll for
best vegan restaurant in both 2024 and 2025, and one bite of the king trumpet
calamari explains why. Italian-American comfort food, a botanical bar, and a
house vegan cheese shop all live under one roof.

It's the rare spot that works for a casual Tuesday dinner and a weekend brunch
with your most skeptical friends. Order the cauliflower wings, take a corner
seat on the balcony, and let the flexitarians convert themselves.
