Yasam Vegan Kafe is Izmir's first vegan cafe, a relaxed all-vegan eatery in Alsancak serving made-in-house home-cooked food using fresh local ingredients, including burgers, vegmacun and puddings. It is rated 4.5 on HappyCow and open daily from noon to 9:30pm, having relocated from 1456 Sokak to 1484 Sokak.

Order a burger and the vegmacun, and save room for pudding. The same business runs the Yasam Foods deli, which describes itself as 100 percent vegan.
