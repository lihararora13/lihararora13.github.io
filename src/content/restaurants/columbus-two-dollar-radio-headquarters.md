Two Dollar Radio Headquarters is the bookstore with a vegan cafe inside, and it is one of the most charming rooms in Columbus: books on the shelves, a 5.0-rated vegan kitchen doing meals, appetizers, coffee and beer, and a crowd that actually reads. The cafe plates change, the sandwiches are solid, and the beer list is the bonus.

It is the pick for the bookish vegan, and the one to linger in. Order a cafe plate, browse the shelves, and stay for a second coffee.
