Pietramala put Philadelphia vegan food on the global map: chef Ian Graye's vegetable-forward kitchen earned the city's first Michelin Green Star in 2025, and he is the only Philly chef who will ever hold it, since Michelin is retiring the designation. In 2026 he added James Beard semifinalist nods, and August 2026 OpenTable reviews are all five stars.

The menu is seasonal and hyperlocal, built from independent organic farms across Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Delaware. Expect fermentation, impeccable produce, and dishes like Buffalo-sauce Murasaki sweet potatoes that turn skeptics into believers. Book ahead: the Michelin bump is real.
