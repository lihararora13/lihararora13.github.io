Leaf has been Boulder's vegetarian anchor for years, and it still sets the standard for a proper sit-down veggie meal on Pearl Street. The globally inspired menu changes with the seasons, vegan dishes are clearly identified, and plenty more can be made vegan on request. Dinner runs Tuesday through Sunday, with brunch on weekends.

Book ahead, because the room fills up. The jackfruit enchiladas and buffalo cauliflower tacos are the vegan headliners, and the weekend tofu scramble is the brunch move. This is the special-occasion pick on the list.
