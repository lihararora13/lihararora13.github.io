Acorn Community Cafe is Eugene's vegan cafe with a conscience: a non-profit, all-vegan community cafe on Monroe Street in the Whiteaker, launched in 2021 with a food-security mission, tip-free and pay-forward, serving seasonal soup, roasted veggie pasta, fresh salads and baked goods. It is the top-ranked vegan spot in Eugene on HappyCow, and it is the pick that local vegan guides lead with. The menu changes with the seasons, so the soup is always the thing to ask about.

Hours are Tuesday to Saturday 9am to 3pm, closed Sunday and Monday. Order the soup and a baked good, and note the pay-forward board: it is part of how the cafe works.
