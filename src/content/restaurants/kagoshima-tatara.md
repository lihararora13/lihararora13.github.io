Tatara is a restaurant that serves both vegan cuisine and wagyu steak, with a dedicated vegan lunch and a six-course vegan dinner menu, and staff who speak English. Lunch runs 11:30am to 2pm and dinner 6pm to 9:30pm, closed Wednesdays and second and fourth Tuesdays, and reservations are required. It is the most formal vegan-friendly table in Kagoshima, and the most expensive.

Book ahead and ask for the vegan lunch or the six-course vegan dinner by name, as the regular menu is built around steak. It suits a special evening rather than a casual lunch, and the vegan cooking is taken seriously rather than being a token menu.
