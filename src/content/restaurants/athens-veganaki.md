Veganaki does something most vegan spots in Athens do not even attempt: a full menu of Greek classics, veganised, without cutting corners. Moussaka, pastitsio, soutzoukakia, gyros and souvlaki all show up here in plant-based form, alongside vegan tzatziki and feta, and everything is baked rather than fried and made in house. It is also one of the city's most gluten-free friendly kitchens, with gluten-free beers and fair-trade coffee with several plant milks.

If you can only visit one vegan restaurant in Athens, several guides say it has to be this one, and the location makes that easy: it sits in Makriyanni, steps from the Acropolis and the Temple of Olympian Zeus, and it is open all day from breakfast through late dinner. Come for the moussaka, stay for the souvlaki.
