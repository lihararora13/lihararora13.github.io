Burma Burma at Elante Mall is the most vegan-literate table in Chandigarh: an all-vegetarian Burmese tea room with comprehensive vegan labelling, so you can order with confidence instead of negotiating. The Burmese falafel, the chilli tangy chickpea tohu, and the Mandalay laphet thoke tea-leaf salad are the dishes to chase.

It is the priciest pick on this list, and the most polished. The grilled tofu and avocado salad is the lighter order, and the tea-leaf salad is the one to remember.
