Mirador de Ulía is a restaurant on the Ulía hill, east of the centre, with a vegetarian tasting menu that the kitchen adapts for vegans. The view over the bay is one of the best in the city.

Book ahead and say vegan when you book. This is the special-occasion pick, and the view is the point as much as the food.
