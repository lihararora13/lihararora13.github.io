The Joint is a plant-forward restaurant on North Tejon Street in downtown Colorado Springs, with a menu built around vegetables, jackfruit, and grains. The jackfruit tacos with avocado crema are the signature, and the room is bright, casual, and central.

Order the jackfruit tacos and a grain bowl. Ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since some dishes are vegetarian rather than vegan by default.
