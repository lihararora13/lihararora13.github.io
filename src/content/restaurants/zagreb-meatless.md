Meatless is Zagreb's vegan street-food fix, south of the river in Novi Zagreb, and it leans into the fun: big burgers with big names, hot dogs, protein-packed wraps and a pasta bolognese for when you want comfort in a bowl.

It is a takeaway-friendly counter, so grab a Karma burger and eat it like you mean it. This is the pick for the group member who still thinks vegan food cannot be filling.
