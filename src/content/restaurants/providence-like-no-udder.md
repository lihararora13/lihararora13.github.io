Like No Udder is Providence's happiest room: a vegan ice cream shop and deli where the soft serve gets whipped with toppings into unicorns, and the hard scoops come in twelve flavours across cashew, peanut and coconut bases. There are floats, shakes, vegan candy bars and jerky, plus savoury deli sandwiches.

It is a five-star HappyCow favourite and a perfect afternoon stop on the East Side. Get a unicorn, try a scoop you have never heard of, and take a candy bar for the road.
