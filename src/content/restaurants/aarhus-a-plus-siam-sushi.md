A+ Siam Sushi is a vegan-friendly sushi bar on Frederiksgade in the Midtbyen, and it is the Japanese pick on this page. The vegan sushi rolls are the signature, with miso soup alongside, made fresh to order.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so order the vegan rolls clearly. The sushi rolls are the order, and the miso soup is the thing to start with.
