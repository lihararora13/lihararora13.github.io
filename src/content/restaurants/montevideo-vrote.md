Vrote is Montevideo's top-rated vegan spot, and it earns the perfect score: a vegan cafe that also sells products and runs workshops, serving hamburgers, salads, pizzas and desserts in a warm room with outdoor seating. It is the kind of place where the food is thoughtful, the staff are lovely, and an afternoon stretches out happily.

Book ahead if you can, order a burger and a dessert, and browse the little shop on your way out. This is the one for a slow, happy vegan afternoon.
