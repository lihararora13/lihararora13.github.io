Balwoo Gongyang is the quiet showpiece of Seoul eating: temple food prepared the way Korean Buddhist monks have eaten for centuries, no meat, no dairy, no egg, not even the five pungent vegetables, served in calm courses on the fifth floor of the Templestay Information Center. It holds Michelin Bib Gourmand and Green Star recognition, a world first for a temple-food restaurant.

Book ahead, arrive unhurried, and let the seasons decide your lunch: pumpkin and brown rice porridge, mushrooms fried crisp in chilli paste, lotus leaf tea to finish. It is the special-occasion pick of this guide, and the meal everyone describes in a slightly softer voice.
