Malhar is the new face of Guwahati vegetarian dining: a fine-dining restaurant from the Makhan Bhog group with arched windows, marble tables and a menu that runs from nadru ke shami kebab to mushroom tortellini and butterfly pea noodles. Food bloggers who visited in early 2026 call it the city's newest vegetarian fine-dining spot, and the plating is a step up from anything else on this list.

It is the priciest pick in the city, so come for a proper sit-down meal. The mushroom tortellini and the vegetable dimsums are the dishes to order, and the kitchen will adapt for vegans if you ask.
