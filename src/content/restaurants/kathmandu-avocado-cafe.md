Avocado Cafe made the list for a reason. A Thamel cafe where the staff actually know what vegan means, doing salads, pastas and vegan meals in a relaxed room. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Thamel, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Avocado salad, Vegan pasta, Smoothie bowl if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
