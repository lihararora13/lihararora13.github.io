Little Africa is a tiny Ethiopian restaurant on Fulton Street East, and it is the only 100 percent vegan restaurant in Grand Rapids. The owner, Tesh Lou, cooks, waits tables, and runs the room himself, and the food is a group platter of wots on spongy injera, eaten with your hands and shared communally.

Order the group platter and the hot tea, and call ahead, since the hours can be erratic. Try the azifa, the mild lentil dish that regulars call their favorite.
