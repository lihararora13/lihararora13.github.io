The Burrowing Owl is a neighborhood lounge with a 100 percent vegan bar food menu, a dim, wood-paneled room with leather booths and a big canned-beer list. The menu is comfort food through and through: vegan mac and cheese, crunchwraps, sloppy joes, burgers, and nachos, all plant-based by default.

Order the vegan crunchwrap and the mac and cheese nachos, and save room for the carrot cake. It is open late, which makes it the best evening stop in the city for vegans.
