Bodhi is Nuremberg's fully vegan Bavarian restaurant, on Pirckheimerstraße north of the Altstadt, and it is the table to book first in the city. The schnitzel Wiener Art is the signature, with gyros, chili-cheeze-nuggets and tempeh rouladen alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it hearty.

HappyCow reviewers praised it in April 2026, and the guides agree it is the city's flagship vegan table. The schnitzel is the order. Note: this Bodhi is a different venue from Bödhi in Leipzig, a different spelling and a different operator.
