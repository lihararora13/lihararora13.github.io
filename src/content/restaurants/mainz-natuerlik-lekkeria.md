Naturlik Lekkeria is a purely plant-based organic bowl bistro, cafe and shop on Franziskanerstrasse, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, relocated from Heidelbergerfassgasse. The savoury bowls run on kimchi, tempeh, carrot lox and cashew cream cheese, plus snacks, smoothies, smoothie bowls, raw and gluten-free cakes, and a shop of house-fermented cashew cheeses, granola, Nutaca and kombucha.

Order the kimchi bowl, the tempeh bowl and the carrot lox, and take home a cashew cheese from the shop. Almost everything is made in-house, gluten and sugar free.
