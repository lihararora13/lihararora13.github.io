Quintal de Casa is the garden restaurant Novo Campeche locals love: an outdoor setting, really good service, and a variety of vegan options alongside the regular menu. It is not exclusively vegan, but the vegan plates are thoughtful and the garden makes lunch feel like a small holiday.

Come for lunch, sit outside, and order the garden lunch with a fresh juice. This is the one for a sunny, slow lunch in the south of the island.
