Why we love it — Veggie Garden has been feeding Barcelona's
vegans, broke students, and hungry wanderers for years, and it is
still the best value in town. The menu is a glorious mashup of
Indian-spiced curries, Mediterranean plates, wraps, and burgers, all
fully vegan and all priced to make you order dessert too. The
colorful dining room is always buzzing.

The thali of the day is the move: a little bit of everything, a lot of
flavor, and change back from a twenty. Follow it with a mango
smoothie and a wander through the Raval. Some things never get old.
