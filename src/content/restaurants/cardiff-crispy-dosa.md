Crispy Dosa is a vegan-friendly South Indian restaurant on St Mary Street in the City Centre, and it is the second dosa pick on this page. The masala dosa is the signature, with gobi manchurian, chilly idly and bhel puri alongside, all of it quick and all of it cheap.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so order the vegan dishes clearly. The masala dosa and the gobi manchurian are the orders.
