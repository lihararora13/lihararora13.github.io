The Batasia Loop tiffin stop is the most charming pick on this list: a strictly 100% pure-vegetarian, egg-free kitchen opposite the Batasia Loop garden gate on Hill Cart Road, verified in November 2026, serving hot aloo-gobhi paratha, Darjeeling spicy aloo-mimi chaat and ginger-cardamom chai from 6:30 in the morning to 7:30 in the evening.

Order the paratha and the chaat, and ask for the paratha without curd. It is a walk-in-only street-food stop with a view of the loop, and it is the best breakfast in Darjeeling for a vegan.
