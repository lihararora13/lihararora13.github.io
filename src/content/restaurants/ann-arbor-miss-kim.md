Miss Kim is Chef Ji Hye Kim's modern Korean restaurant in the Kerrytown Market, and its menu is built for dietary flexibility: vegan, vegetarian, nut-free, dairy-free, soy-free, and gluten-free options are all marked. It is the sit-down counterpart to Little Kim next door, and the more polished dinner of the two.

Book ahead on weekends. Tell the staff you eat vegan and let them steer you through the menu.
