Belén is Wellington's only vegan bakery, and the 5.0 rating across 80 HappyCow reviews says the city knows it. What started as a stall at Newtown Market in 2021 now runs four outlets, and the Lambton Quay original in Masons Lane is a weekday lunch spot for the business crowd as much as for vegans. The cabinet runs from croissants, cronuts, donuts, scrolls and focaccias to sandwiches, sausage rolls and savoury pies, with a menu that changes monthly and Good Fortune coffee alongside.

The famous orders are the raspberry and vanilla crème brûlée donut, the mac and cheese pie, and the vegan "ham" and cheese croissant that turns heads even among non-vegans. It is open weekdays 7:30am to 3:00pm and closed on weekends, so plan it as a weekday treat, and go with a group so you can try a bit of everything.
