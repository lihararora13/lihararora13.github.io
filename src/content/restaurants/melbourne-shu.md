SHU is the kind of place that makes fine dining feel like an adventure rather than a lecture. The whole menu is a plant-based tasting journey built on centuries-old Sichuan flavours, all ma la tingle and slow-building fire, with seasonal Victorian produce doing the heavy lifting.

Book the ten-course degustation and surrender to it. From raw chilled openers to fiery street-food-inspired mains, it's the most exciting vegan table in Melbourne right now, and the wine and cocktail pairings keep pace beautifully.
