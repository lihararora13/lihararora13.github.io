Viegan is a fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant on Neue Brücke in the Mitte, and it is the noodle bar on this page: phở, ramen, kimchi-gyoza and bánh mì, all plant-based. It is the pick for a cold evening, when a bowl of phở is the only sensible order.

The phở and the kimchi-gyoza are the orders. It is a small room, so come early or book ahead.
