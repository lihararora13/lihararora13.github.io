Trap Vegan is Nashville comfort food with the volume up: the dedicated all-vegan kitchen operating alongside Trap Fusion, with its own VEGHEAD menu of Southern and Caribbean plates that Urbaanite singles out in its vegan guide to the city.

Go for the vegan fish and grits, the jerk alfredo pasta, the Memphis Mane, or a BBQ jack sandwich with a side of Henny greens and vegan spaghetti. Big flavours, big portions, and Trap Dust on everything if you know what is good for you.
