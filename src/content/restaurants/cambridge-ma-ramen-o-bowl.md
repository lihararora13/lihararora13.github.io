Ramen O' Bowl describes itself as a 100 percent vegan ramen shop, and it is the clearest all-vegan anchor in Cambridge. The broths are built plant-based from the start, the noodles are vegan, and the gyoza and sides follow the same rule, which means no substitutions are needed at the table.

Order the vegan tonkotsu ramen and the vegan gyoza. It is a small room, so expect a wait at peak dinner hours.
