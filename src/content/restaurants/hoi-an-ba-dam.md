Ba Dam is a local chay buffet in an alley off Phan Chau Trinh, a counter of ready-cooked dishes with a plate of rice, where you point at what you want, and soup comes free. It is the fastest meal on this list and very cheap, which is why local workers fill it at noon, and it is rated 4.6 from 1,220 Google reviews, open 7am to 9pm daily.

Order a rice plate with the ready-cooked dishes and ask the total before you sit down, as some foreign diners report paying more than the price on the sign. Sit upstairs if the ground floor is busy.
