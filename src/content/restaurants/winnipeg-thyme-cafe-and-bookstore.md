Thyme is the warmest room in Winnipeg's vegan scene: a plant-based café inside a second-hand bookshop in Saint-Boniface, cooking home-style comfort food with an emphasis on seasonal ingredients. The menu runs to breakfast bowls, soups and sandwiches, with a Thyme burger and a chickpea-vegetable frittata among the regulars, and the shelves around you are full of used books to browse while you eat.

It is open Tuesday to Saturday until 5:30pm and Sunday until 5pm, so this is a daytime stop, not a dinner one. Order the frittata or the burger, add a coconut date square, and leave time to wander the bookshop.
