Namaste Village is Norwich's vegetarian Indian institution, a big room on Queens Road with a menu that marks vegan dishes clearly. The dosa and thali are the dishes regulars come back for.

Order the vegan thali and a dosa to share. It is the best large-group vegan-friendly table in the city.
