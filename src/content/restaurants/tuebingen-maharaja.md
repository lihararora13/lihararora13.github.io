Maharaja Imbiss is a small Indian imbiss on the Jakobsgasse, describing its cooking as vegan Ayurvedic Indian cuisine, and the menu backs that up: masala dosa, samosa chaat, pakora and samosas, all of it plant-based. It keeps short hours, weekday lunchtimes from eleven to four with an extra Saturday evening service, which makes it a lunch stop rather than a dinner table. The food is fresh, spiced and cheap, and the dosa is the dish to order.

Order a masala dosa and a samosa chaat, and treat it as the quick Indian lunch that Tübingen's old town does best.
