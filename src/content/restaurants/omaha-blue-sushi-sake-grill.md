Blue Sushi Sake Grill is a sushi bar on South 12th Street in the Old Market, with a real vegan bench: vegetable and tofu rolls, edamame, and tempura that the kitchen prepares to order. It is the most polished room on the Omaha list, and the one to book for a nicer dinner.

Order the vegan rolls and the vegetable tempura, and ask the server to confirm the roll sauces are vegan, since some contain mayo.
