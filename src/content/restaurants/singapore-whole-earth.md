Whole Earth has been proving a point deliciously since 2003: Peranakan food, with all its spice, richness and ceremony, does not need meat to sing. The Peck Seah shophouse near Tanjong Pagar MRT is the city's original plant-based Peranakan table, and the MICHELIN Bib Gourmand nods keep agreeing.

Order to share and let the table fill: rendang with mushrooms, a golden Nonya curry, crisp ngoh hiang and the olive rice everyone tells you to get. It is the special-but-not-stuffy meal of the guide, and the one to book for visiting friends you want to impress.
