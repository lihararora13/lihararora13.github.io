Chickpea Eatery is a vegan cafe and cooking school on Phan Chau Trinh, doing vegan Vietnamese and Western food side by side: a tempeh banh mi, a vegan beef burger, smoothies and Vietnamese mains. It also runs one of the best-reviewed vegan cooking classes in town, with a market walk at the start, and it is rated 4.9 from 1,425 Google reviews, open noon to 9pm daily.

Order the tempeh banh mi and a smoothie, and book a cooking class if you want to learn how Vietnamese mock meats are made. It is the best vegan cooking class in Hoi An.
