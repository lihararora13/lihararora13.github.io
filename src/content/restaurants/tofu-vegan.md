Why we love it — London's best vegan Chinese, backed by the Xi'an
Impression team and run by chefs from three different regions of
China, which means the menu reads like a greatest-hits tour of the
country's plant-based cooking. The wontons in house special sauce are
the thing people dream about, the twice-cooked 'fish' is a minor
miracle, and the Peking 'duck' pancakes have converted more than one
sceptic at our table.

The menu is immense, the staff are famously friendly with
recommendations, and it is good enough that Jay Rayner once wrote
love letters to their mushroom salad. Book, or prepare to hover for a
table like everyone else.
