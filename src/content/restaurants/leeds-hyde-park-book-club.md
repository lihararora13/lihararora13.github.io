Hyde Park Book Club is Leeds' vegetarian arts space: coffee, arts, music, food and drinks in a performance space in Hyde Park, with a totally vegetarian menu and an extensive vegan range built on fake meats from the Vegetarian Butcher. Breakfast is served until 5pm, there is a vegan brunch and an evening menu, and the room is always buzzing.

Come for brunch, order the curried scrambled tofu, and stay for whatever is on that night. This is Leeds' student quarter at its best, and the most fun breakfast on the list.
