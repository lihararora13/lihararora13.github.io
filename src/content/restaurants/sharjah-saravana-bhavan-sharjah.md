Saravana Bhavan is the international South Indian vegetarian chain's Sharjah branch on King Faisal Road, serving dosas, idlis and more. It uses dairy, so vegans should say so and ask for no yogurt, ghee or cream.

Order a masala dosa and specify vegan. There are further branches in Al Nahda and Abu Shagara if this one is out of your way.
