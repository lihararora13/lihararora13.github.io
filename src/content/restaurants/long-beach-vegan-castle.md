Vegan Castle is the wildcard of the list and the one people remember: a fully vegan sushi restaurant on the Westside, rated 5.0 by HappyCow reviewers, doing sushi rolls, tacos wrapped in nori and rice paper, vegetable tempura and appetizers. The sushi is the draw, and the nori tacos are the surprise.

Hours are reported to be unreliable, so check ahead before you go. When it is open, it is one of the most fun vegan meals in Southern California.
