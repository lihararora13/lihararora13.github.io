Hummusson is a fully vegan Middle Eastern counter on Södra Förstadsgatan in the Centrum, and it is the cheapest dinner on this page. The mixed plate is the signature, with hummus, baba ganoush and falafel alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it generous.

One note on the address: the guide lists number 57, while the geocoder placed the venue at number 43 on the same street, so the pin is effectively street-level on that block. The mixed plate is the order.
