Vegan Pizza Bar is a vegan pizza bar on Molenstraat in the centre, baking plant-based pizzas for people who never eat plant-based food because they think it does not taste good. The menu runs from pizzas and pasta to antipasti, with outdoor seating in summer, and the room is hip, bright and built for a fun dinner.

It is open for lunch and dinner, with evening-only service on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Order a pizza to share and the antipasti to start.
