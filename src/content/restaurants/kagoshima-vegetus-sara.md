Vegetus Sara is a vegetarian restaurant in Shiroyamacho, near the foot of the castle hill, and the closest thing Kagoshima has to a destination vegan meal. Reviewers praise the vegan set menu, the miso soup with cubes of tofu, the exceptional sticky rice with black salt on top, and the vegan banana cake with brown rice tea. It is open 11am to 5pm most days, and to 8pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

Order the vegan set menu by name: all the menu choices are vegan except one set that adds a side of fish, so the name matters. Come at lunch, when the room is at its best, and allow time, as the set is served slowly and the sticky rice is worth the wait.
