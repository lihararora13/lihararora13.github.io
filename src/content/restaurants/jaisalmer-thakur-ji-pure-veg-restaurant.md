Thakur Ji is the rooftop thali house near Fort Parking on Shiv Road, a pure-veg restaurant with a view over the old city and a menu built around Rajasthani classics. The draw is ker sangri, the desert bean and berry curry that tastes like nowhere else on earth.

Order the ker sangri with a Rajasthani thali, ghee held, and bajra roti, and you have the most Jaisalmer meal on this list. Come for sunset, when the rooftops turn gold.
