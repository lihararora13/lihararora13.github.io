Beam Me Up Bagels - City is a New York-style bagel cafe at 104 Great King Street North, rated 4.0 on HappyCow, and it is Dunedin's vegan bagel stop. The bagels are hand-rolled and boiled using classic techniques, and the menu changes often but usually has around five different vegan bagel options, with Angel cream cheese listed from time to time and the majority of the bagels themselves vegan. Rotating specials include the mung-bean Mung the Merciless, which the shop's own Instagram promoted as its vegan special in June 2026. Plant-based milks are available for coffee.

It is open daily, 8:30am to 2:30pm, so it is a morning and lunch room only. There is a second location at 9 North Road in North East Valley with the same menu, but this guide uses the central Great King Street shop.
