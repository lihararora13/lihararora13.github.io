Cafe Optimi is an Italian and Israeli cafe in the Pninat Center on the promenade, with a large terrace overlooking the Red Sea and a wide vegan selection that reviewers call clearly marked: a vegan hummus omelette, pastas, salads and tofu dishes, with vegan options across the menu.

Order the vegan hummus omelette and a tofu salad. Reviewers note the vegan range is limited in depth, so manage expectations, but the location and the marking are the best in Eilat.
