Abu Shaker is a busy, casual hummus place on Sderot HaMeginim with at least ten kinds of vegan hummus: hummus with fava, hummus with mushrooms and many more. Hummus comes with pita, vegetables and two types of vegan rice dishes, mujadara and a tomato rice, and you can also order falafel balls.

It is open daily from 6:00am to 6:00pm with mostly outdoor seating at large tables. Order a hummus with fava and the mujadara.
