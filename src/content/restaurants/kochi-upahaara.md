Upahaara is the Marine Drive thali stop: a pure-vegetarian restaurant on Cannon Shed Road, opposite the boat jetty, serving a traditional South Indian thali on a banana leaf with rice, dal, sambar, papad and chutneys, plus dosa, North Indian and Indo-Chinese dishes. It holds a 4.4 on Justdial from around 900 ratings.

The restaurant relaunched on 17 August 2026, announced on its own Instagram, after a break, so use the Cannon Shed Road address on this page. The thali is the order, ask for no curd, and the masala dosa was named one of Kochi's top five in October 2026. Finish with a filter coffee and a walk along Marine Drive.
