Fou Shi Yuan is old-school Tainan vegetarian food: a neighbourhood eatery on Chenggong Road, seats about thirty, with a buffet as well as set menus, dim sum, vegetables, mock meats and Chinese medicinal soups. Look for the big green sign outside. It is rated 4.5 on HappyCow and is cash only.

This is a vegan-friendly vegetarian restaurant rather than a fully vegan one, so ask for the vegan dishes when you order. The buffet makes it an easy, cheap lunch, and the medicinal soups are the thing regulars come back for.
