Omphalos Restaurant Vegetariano-Vegano is a vegan-friendly peruvian spot in Centro Histórico. Omphalos is a vegetarian and vegan restaurant in the historic centre, rated 5.0, and it has moved from its old address on Calle Bolívar 107 to Bolognesi 116, which is the address used here.

Order the Seitan, Salchipapas, Hamburguesas, and Chicha morada. The seitan and the salchipapas are the plates to try, with a chicha morada to drink.
