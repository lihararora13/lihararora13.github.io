Katzentempel is Heidelberg's only fully vegan all-day restaurant, and it happens to be a cat café too. The Bahnstadt branch of the German chain serves breakfast all day alongside a broad casual menu of burgers, bowls, cakes and drinks, all of it plant-based, while several rescue cats hold court in the bright, window-fronted room. It is the easiest table in the city for a mixed group: nobody has to negotiate the menu, and the cats do most of the entertaining.

It sits in the Bahnstadt, the new district by the main station, a short walk from the platforms and an easy first stop after arriving. Come for breakfast at any hour, order the breakfast plate if it is your first visit, and stay for cake. Book ahead at weekends, when the cat-loving crowd fills the room.
