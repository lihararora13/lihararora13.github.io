Barsana is the curry house Birmingham's reviewers rate highest, a pure vegetarian and vegan Indian restaurant on Harborne High Street. The menu is South Indian at heart, with dosa, thalis and curries that win over diners who never think about whether their food is vegan, because it simply tastes like great Indian food.

Harborne is a short bus ride from the centre and worth the trip. Order a dosa to start, a thali to share, and a curry to finish, and you will see why it tops the city's curry lists.
