Rai Urban Vege hides in a courtyard off Visokog Stevana, and finding it is half the fun. Inside, the menu runs east and west at once: pad thai, falafel, burgers and vegan sushi, all vegetarian with plenty vegan.

Enter the yard next to Visokog Stevana 5 and look left. Order the pad thai, share the falafel, and try the vegan sushi, it is better than it has any right to be this far from the sea.
