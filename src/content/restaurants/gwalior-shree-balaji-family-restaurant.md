Shree Balaji Family Restaurant is the newest face on this list, a pure-vegetarian family restaurant that opened in Janakganj in 2026 with the old city's blessing and a devotional flourish. It is a small, local opening rather than a chain arrival, and the menu is classic Lashkar vegetarian cooking: thalis, dal, seasonal sabzi and roti.

It is early days, so come with the spirit of discovery rather than a checklist. Order the veg thali without ghee, and you are eating the way Janakganj has eaten for generations.
