Bakmie Jamur is the kind of place locals queue for without making a fuss about it: a simple eatery doing vegan noodles, wontons and meatballs properly. It sits a short walk from Tarumanagara University, which keeps prices low and the room lively.

Do not expect frills, expect a very good bowl of noodles. Order the noodles with wontons and meatballs, add rice if you are still hungry, and enjoy one of Jakarta's best-value vegan meals.
