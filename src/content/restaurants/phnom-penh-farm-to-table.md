Farm to Table is a relaxed outdoor eatery at 16 Street 360 in BKK1, with a lush leafy courtyard, a vegetable garden, free-range hens and a full-size tractor as decor. The kitchen works with local growers and cooperatives, and the menu is seasonal, with vegan options clearly marked.

Order the jackfruit tacos and the veggie quesadilla, and come for a long lunch in the garden. It is a meat-serving restaurant with a strong vegan bench rather than a vegetarian specialist, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it is open daily from 8am to 9pm.
