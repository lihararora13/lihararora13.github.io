Veggie Lunch is a small Ñuñoa bakery-cafe that opened in 2023 and already holds a perfect HappyCow rating, which tells you how seriously it takes its pastry. The menu mixes French bakery and cakes with empanadas and a couple of pasta dishes like ravioli and cannelloni, all fully vegan.

The signature order is the milhojas empanada, the beloved layered pastry, in its vegan version. Come for lunch, leave with a cake, and add Ñuñoa to your mental map of Santiago's vegan neighbourhoods.
