Amavi is a brunch restaurant on Thunstrasse in Kirchenfeld, serving plant-based bowls, toasts, smoothie bowls, porridge, homemade sweets and specialty coffee. It is the brunch pick on this list.

Order a bowl, a toast and a smoothie bowl, and come for a slow brunch, since it opens nine to three on weekdays and nine to four at weekends.
