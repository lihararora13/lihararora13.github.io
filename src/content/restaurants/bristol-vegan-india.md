Vegan India is exactly what it sounds like, and that is the joy of it: a fully vegan traditional Bangladeshi Indian restaurant on Gloucester Road, with around sixty seats for dine-in and a 5.0 HappyCow rating. The menu is the real deal, South Asian curries, breads and classics, cooked without a single animal product and without losing any of the richness.

Gloucester Road is Bristol's great independent eating street, and this is one of its best stops. Order a curry with breads, share a thali if you are with friends, and settle in.
