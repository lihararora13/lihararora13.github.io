Five Star J is a sit-down restaurant on South Pattaya Road near the Third Road junction, fully vegan and vegetarian, with a long Thai menu built around fermented soy protein and konjac-root substitutes. It is open daily except Tuesday, 12pm to 9pm, with dishes at 149 to 179 baht, confirmed on its menu in June 2026, and it is the restaurant most vegetarians find first in Pattaya.

Order the fermented soy protein dishes and a Thai curry, and come for lunch or dinner any day except Tuesday. It is the most reliable sit-down vegan meal in Pattaya.
