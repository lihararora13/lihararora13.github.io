Soedirman Coffee & Eatery started as an online store selling vegan and vegetarian groceries for delivery, and opened a dine-in eatery at its Sudirman Street location in July 2024. The menu is Indonesian vegan cooking: nasi padang, vegan chicken, satay skewers and traditional street snacks.

Order the nasi padang and a satay skewer. It is a vegan eatery, inexpensive, and it is the newest vegan room on this list, so check the social pages for current hours before a special trip.
