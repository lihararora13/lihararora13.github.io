Cafe Felka is a cafe inside the Museum of Cultural History at the Heger Tor, open Tuesday to Sunday, serving breakfast boards, cakes and coffee in a bright villa-style room with a garden and fountain. A recent visitor asked about a vegan breakfast, and the breakfast board runs on bread, olives, dips, flatbread chips and salad.

Order the breakfast board, a cake and a coffee, and ask for the vegan version of the board, since the standard one runs on dairy and egg. Come in spring for the garden seating.
