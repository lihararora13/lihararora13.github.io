Koshari Mama started as a Lowell pop-up and a farmers market favorite before landing a permanent, colorfully cozy home between Union and Porter squares. Mother-and-daughter team Sahar Ahmed and Dina Fahim cook the Egyptian street food they grew up on, except everything on the menu is fully vegan and absolutely packed with flavor. The star is the namesake koshari: a glorious layered bowl of rice, lentils, pasta, chickpeas, and crispy fried onions with house-made sauces.

Beyond the bowl, expect mushroom shawarma wrapped in tahini, crackling falafel, shakshuka fries, and vegan konafa for dessert. Some nights feature live Middle Eastern music, and the hospitality gets raved about as often as the food. Bring friends, order the mezze, and do not leave without dessert.
