Vihar Cafe sits by MP Shah Hospital in Parklands and cooks across North and South Indian, Indo-Chinese and even pizza, which makes it the crowd-pleaser of this list. Vegans need to specify, but the kitchen is used to it, and the Sunday Gujarati thali buffet is the event: pooran puri, undhiyu, dal, kadhi, mutter rice, pickles, salad and papadum.

Plan around Sunday if you can, when the thali is the whole point. Otherwise the dosa and the Indo-Chinese dishes are the reliable vegan-friendly orders.
