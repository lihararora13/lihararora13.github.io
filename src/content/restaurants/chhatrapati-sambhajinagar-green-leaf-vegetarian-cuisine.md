Green Leaf is Cidco's pure-veg anchor, a comfortable dining room in the Town Centre beside the Inox cinema, and one of the city's most recommended vegetarian restaurants. It is the kind of place that works for a family dinner, a business lunch, or a hungry post-cinema meal.

The North Indian mains are the move, with dal tadka and mixed veg sabzi leading. Ask for the ghee and cream held and the plate turns plant-based without losing any of its comfort.
