Great Full Gardens is a health-food cafe on South Virginia Street in Midtown Reno, with a dedicated plant-based breakfast section. The menu names a Vegan Hash, a Vegan Breakfast Burrito, and a Vegan Reuben, and the room is sunny, casual, and built for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

Order the Vegan Hash and the Vegan Breakfast Burrito. Use the Midtown branch at 555 South Virginia Street, Suite 107, not the separate South Reno or Sparks locations, and note the pin does not identify Suite 107.
