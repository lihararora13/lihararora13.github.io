Roots is the plant-based kitchen to know in Zhujiang New Town: a grab-and-go spot inside the Ramada Plaza by Wyndham, a short walk from Canton Tower, serving wraps, salads, stews and open-faced toasts made on the spot, plus coffee and shelf staples to take home. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow and fresh November 2026 reviews on TripAdvisor, and it was formerly known as GoVegan.

Order the plant-based chicken pesto wrap, the hummus platter and the soy protein plate. Seating is limited and delivery is available, so it works as well as a takeaway stop as a sit-down lunch. Open daily 8am to 8:30pm.
