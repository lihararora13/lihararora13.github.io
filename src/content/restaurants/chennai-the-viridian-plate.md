The Viridian Plate tops the list for a reason. A family-run, 100% vegan restaurant doing South Indian comfort food at its best: jackfruit biryani and Chettinad pepper tofu. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Thiruvanmiyur, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Jackfruit biryani, Chettinad pepper tofu, Tofu corn tikka if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
