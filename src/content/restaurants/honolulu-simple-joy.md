Simple Joy is the downtown vegan workhorse: a fully vegan restaurant on South King Street with an extensive menu that roams from Italian to Vietnamese to American, burgers and fries included. It is the kind of place where everyone in the group finds something, and the kitchen has been doing it for years.

It is open for lunch and dinner daily, which makes it the most useful vegan restaurant downtown. Order a burger, add a Vietnamese dish, and enjoy the range.
