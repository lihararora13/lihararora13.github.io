Fruits and Roots has been a Bryanston institution since 1985, a wholefood emporium with a light, airy vegetarian cafe flowing out into the courtyard and a daily buffet that regulars swear by. It is the one vegan-friendly spot on this list rather than fully vegan, the kitchen serves some vegetarian dishes alongside its vegan ones, but the vegan offering is deep: wraps, pizzas, grain bowls, fresh juices and smoothies, and cakes made to order.

It is affordable, unpretentious and genuinely nourishing, the perfect spot for a healthy breakfast or lunch before stocking up in the health store. Order the Thai green curry, the Mexican bean wrap, a slice of vegan pizza, and the vegan carrot cake for the road.
