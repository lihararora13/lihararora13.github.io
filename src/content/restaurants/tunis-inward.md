Inward is the one fully vegan restaurant in Tunis, a conscious-eating kitchen in Les Berges du Lac serving whole, raw, artisanal, gluten-free and refined sugar-free plant-based meals: small plates, cheeses, drinks and desserts. It is rated 5.0 on HappyCow.

It keeps short hours, Tuesday to Friday from noon to 4:00pm, so plan lunch, not dinner. Order a spread of small plates and a raw dessert.
