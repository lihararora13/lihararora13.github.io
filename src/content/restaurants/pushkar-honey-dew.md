Honey Dew is the gentle end to Pushkar's list: a market-road café on Main Market Road in Badi Basti, where breakfast bowls and fresh juice are the order of the day. It is a calm, unhurried room, and a good place to start a morning or end an afternoon.

Order a breakfast bowl and a fresh juice, and take your time. The pin is shared with Laughing Buddha Cafe on the same stretch of Main Market Road, so follow the road rather than the map dot.
