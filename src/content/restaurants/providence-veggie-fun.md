Veggie Fun has been downtown Providence's Pan-Asian anchor since 2012, and it went fully vegan in January 2016 without losing any of its fans. The menu is huge, with a dedicated wheat-free section, and the room is a little more upscale than the average vegan spot, which makes it a good dinner choice.

The lemongrass hot and sour soup with silken tofu is the signature starter, and the tofu teriyaki sizzling platter is the main to beat. Come hungry, because the portions are generous.
