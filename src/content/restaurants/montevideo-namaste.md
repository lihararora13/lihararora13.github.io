Namaste is Montevideo's special night out: a vegetarian restaurant in a fine yet relaxed setting, where guests eat at low tables on wooden floors and the kitchen cooks typical Uruguayan cuisine and international dishes in vegetarian versions. No egg, onion or garlic is used, and at midday a dish of the day is offered. The lentil empanadas and coconut curry are the plates people remember.

Book ahead, come for dinner, and order the dish of the day if it is vegan. This is the one for the most memorable meal in Montevideo.
