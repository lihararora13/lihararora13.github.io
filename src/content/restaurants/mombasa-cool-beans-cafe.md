Cool Beans Cafe is a restaurant and cafe at Danka, off Links Road in Nyali, with a proper vegan list: vegetable curry with rice, Swahili beans with rice, salad, pasta, a red bean burger, hummus with pita and smoothies, and plant milks for coffee. It is open daily 7am to 10 or 11pm.

Order the red bean burger and the Swahili beans with rice, and specify vegan when ordering. It is the best cafe on this list for a long, slow meal.
