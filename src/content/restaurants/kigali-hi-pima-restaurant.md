Hi Pima is a hotel restaurant offering a rotating menu of gourmet plant-based cuisine made with seasonal, locally sourced ingredients: butternut soup, lentil and chickpea creations, tofu-based entrees, baked ratatouille and house-made desserts. It is rated 4.5 and reservations are required.

Order the butternut soup and a tofu entree. Note that it was previously listed as vegan but was reported in September 2026 to serve eggs, dairy and honey, so it is filed vegan-friendly and vegans should confirm each dish.
