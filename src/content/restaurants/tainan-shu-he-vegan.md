Shu He Vegan is one of Tainan's newest fully vegan restaurants, a small wood-tabled room on Xinmei Street where one cook runs the whole show. Every main comes as a set with soup and a traditional tofu pudding drizzled with syrup, and the dishes reviewers single out are the basil green curry over rice, the mushroom and black pepper gratin topped with melted vegan cheese, and the creamy corn soup.

A September 2026 review put two huge portions at NT$510, so this is generous food at a fair price. The room is cosy and the menu is short, which is exactly the point: come for the set, stay for the tofu pudding.
