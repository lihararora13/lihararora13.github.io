Pizzeria Gopau, also known as Gopal, is a lacto-vegetarian pizzeria in San Jose, in business since April 2005, built on a dietetics and herbal background and a love of conscious lacto-vegetarian food. The menu runs to pizzas, pastas and vegetarian combinations, and the kitchen is rated 4.9.

Order a vegetarian pizza and ask for a vegan version without cheese, or pick a pasta. It is open lunch and dinner, which makes it one of the few Mendoza picks that serves a proper evening meal.
