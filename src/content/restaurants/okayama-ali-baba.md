Ali Baba is a Turkish restaurant on Yanagimachi, a short walk from the station, serving a variety of traditional vegetarian dishes that can be made vegan. Specify vegan when you order, as the kitchen uses dairy in several dishes, and stick to the vegetable plates, salads and bread with dips.

It is open evenings only, from 5pm to midnight, cash only, which makes it the late-night vegan-friendly option in Okayama when the cafes have closed. Come for a relaxed, inexpensive dinner rather than a formal meal.
