Apple Vegan Cafe and Restaurant is an all-vegan jay cafe in central Pattaya, near Third Road, covering plant-based steak, pineapple fried rice and stir-fried basil, with Thai and breakfast dishes. It is open Monday to Saturday, 7:30am to 10:30pm, and it is one of the few all-vegan kitchens in the centre that opens for breakfast.

Order the plant-based steak and the pineapple fried rice, and come early for breakfast. It is the most central all-vegan kitchen in Pattaya.
