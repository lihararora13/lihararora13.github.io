JoGeSaYu is the address that changes Hiroshima's vegan story: opened in May 2025, five minutes on foot from Peace Memorial Park, it is a 100% plant-based restaurant with no animal products and no alcohol, supervised by Chef Katsumi Kusumoto of Saido, and confirmed open in November 2026. The menu runs from a tempura vegetable donburi and creamy plant-based ramen at lunch to a ¥5,500 dinner course of vegetable sushi, seasonal soup, a colourful plant-based plate, mini okonomiyaki, a karaage skewer and dessert.

Book ahead, the room is small and takes reservations, and come hungry: reviewers call it the best vegan food they had in Japan, singling out the spicy dandan noodles. Two minutes from Fukuromachi tram stop, it fits easily into a Peace Park visit.
