Restaurant Bambu is a vegetarian restaurant on Independencia in El Almendral, listed in the Municipality of Valparaiso's own vegan guide, and it serves weekday set lunches from 11 am to 5 pm. It is not a destination kitchen, but it is a dependable one, and the official listing is a good sign of how seriously the city takes it.

Order the set lunch and a fresh juice. Come at midday, as the kitchen closes in the late afternoon and does not open on Sundays.
