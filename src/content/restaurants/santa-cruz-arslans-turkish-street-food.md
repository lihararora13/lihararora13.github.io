Arslans is Santa Cruz's Turkish street-food counter: a small room on Walnut Avenue, mostly takeaway with limited seating, serving lahmacun, falafel and hummus with a dedicated vegan menu section. The vegan lahmacun, falafel wrap and falafel box are the core, and the hummus with pita is the simple order that regulars swear by. It is the cheapest proper meal in this guide after Charlie Hong Kong, and the fastest.

Hours are Monday to Thursday 11:30am to 8pm, Friday to Saturday 11:30am to 9pm and Sunday 11:30am to 7pm. Order at the counter, take it to go, and eat it in Abbott Square a block away.
