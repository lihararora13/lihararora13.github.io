Bollywood Bites is an Indian vegetarian restaurant inside the Nyali Cinemax, rated 4.0, serving Indo-Chinese and South Indian specialities like dosa, some of which are vegan and others that can be made vegan on request. Reviewers call it delicious and authentic.

Order a dosa and ask what else can be made vegan. It is open Tuesday to Sunday, and it is a good cinema-night dinner.
