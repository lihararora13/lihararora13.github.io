Why we love it — No eggs, no dairy, no problem. Donut Run has been voted the
best donuts in DC for years on the strength of cloud-light vegan dough and a
rotating flavor board that rewards repeat visits. The line moves fast, so don't
let it scare you.

Go early for the fullest case, especially on weekends. The apple fritter is the
size of a small hat and every bit as good as it looks. Bring a box home or
regret it all week.
