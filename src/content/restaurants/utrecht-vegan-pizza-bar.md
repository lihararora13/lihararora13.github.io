Vegan Pizza Bar is a vegan pizza bar on Jaarbeursboulevard, near the station and the Jaarbeurs, serving plant-based pizzas, pasta and antipasti. It is part of the same small chain as the Hague branch, and the Utrecht room is the one to use on this page.

It is closed on Mondays, open for lunch and dinner the rest of the week. Order a pizza to share and the antipasti to start.
