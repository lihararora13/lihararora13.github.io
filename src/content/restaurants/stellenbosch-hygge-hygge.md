Hygge Hygge sits on Church Street in the centre of town and marks its vegan dishes clearly, which is not a given in Stellenbosch. The vegan list includes avocado and beetroot toast, shakshuka, a quinoa bowl with falafel and hummus, green Thai chickpea curry, burgers and a vegan sharing platter, with plant milks for coffee and smoothies.

It is open daily, with dinner service Wednesday to Saturday. Order the quinoa bowl or the chickpea curry, and ask about the sharing platter if you are in a group.
