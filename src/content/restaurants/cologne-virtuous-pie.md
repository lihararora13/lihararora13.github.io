Virtuous Pie is the restaurant Cologne's vegans recommend first, and it is easy to see why: a fast-casual pizzeria in Ehrenfeld where everything is plant-based, from the pizzas to the mac n' cheese, salads, garlic knots, ice cream and the famous pizza cookie. Dine in, pick up or get it delivered, and bring the kids and the dog.

Order a pizza, add the mac n' cheese, and finish with the pizza cookie. This is vegan comfort food at its most joyful, and the best table in Ehrenfeld.
