Donna Laura Doces e Salgados is a 5.0-rated fully vegan bakery and cafe in the Centro Historico, serving breakfast, lunch, coffee and drinks since it was reported fully vegan in November 2018. The baked goods include vegan calzone and cakes, and the room works as a cafe as much as a bakery.

Order a vegan calzone and a slice of cake, with a coffee. It is open Monday to Saturday, with a shorter Saturday service, and it is the best vegan bakery breakfast in the city.
