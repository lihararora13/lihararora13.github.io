Evergreen is Penang buffet eating at full scale: a very large vegetarian restaurant with over 30 dishes, veggie curries, fried noodles, dumplings, mock-meats, tofu, steam-stuffed bread and noodle soups, self-service and pay for your food by weight. It is a feast, and the sheer choice is half the fun. Note that vegan dishes may not be labelled, so ask the staff which dishes are vegan before you load up.

Come hungry, take a small plate first to scout the spread, then go back for the good stuff. This is the one for a big, joyful buffet lunch.
