Siddhagiri Organic is the temple meal on this list: a 100% pure-veg sattvik bhojanalaya on the Kaneri Math campus, on NH-48 south of the city, serving simple Maharashtrian food in the sattvik tradition, no onion, no garlic, no excess. It is the most peaceful meal in Kolhapur's guide.

Order the sattvik thali with the ghee held, and note the kitchen uses desi-cow ghee as a matter of course, so say the word when you order. The food is simple, honest and deeply local.
