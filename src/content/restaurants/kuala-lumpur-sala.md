Sala has been feeding Hartamas fully vegan Tex-Mex with a Malay accent for the better part of a decade, and it remains the name locals give first when you ask where a hungry vegan should actually go. The name nods to saving the animals, and the portions nod to saving you from ever being hungry again.

The move is a pulled jackfruit burrito or a plate of nasi lemak with mushroom anchovies, a curry laksa if the table can handle sharing, and a peanut butter brownie for the road. Friendly, generous and properly priced, it is a KL staple that keeps earning its spot.
