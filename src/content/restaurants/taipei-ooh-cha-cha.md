Ooh Cha Cha is a Taipei institution: the friendly Guting cafe that proved vegan food here could be fresh, filling and fun, long before it was fashionable. The kitchen skips dairy, refined sugar and refined flour, and still turns out burgers and sandwiches people cross town for.

Order the buffalo tofu sandwich, chase it with a green smoothie, and do not skip the raw desserts. The same team now also runs our number one pick, Sunami, which tells you everything about the pedigree. English-speaking staff make it an easy first stop for visitors.
