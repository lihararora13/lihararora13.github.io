Soda Vegetariana Susbida is a hole-in-the-wall vegetarian soda downtown, next to the Alianza Francesa, and it is the favourite vegan find in San Jose for travellers who have eaten there: an unmarked, grungy little room with a bare menu, huge portions and incredible flavours. The dumpling soup comes with tofu, vegetables and vegetable dumplings, and a meal runs about 3,000-4,000 colones.

Order the dumpling soup and ask if you are unsure what is vegan, as the kitchen is vegetarian rather than strictly vegan. It is a lunch place, open Monday to Saturday from late morning to about 4 pm, closed Sundays, and it is still listed open in 2026 directories.
