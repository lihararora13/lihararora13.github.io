Bodhi Tree is the bakery pick on this list: a 100% egg-free pure-veg bakery and organic cafe on Sujata Bypass Road, verified in November 2026, serving cinnamon-walnut rolls, sourdough avocado-hummus toast and millet khichdi in an eggless, alcohol-free kitchen. It is open 7 to 9:30 daily.

Order the sourdough avocado-hummus toast and the millet khichdi, and ask about the vegan bakes of the day. The bakery case is the draw, and the toast is the most naturally vegan dish in the house.
