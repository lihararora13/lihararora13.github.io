Why we love it — La Stella Nera brings Naples to Neukölln without a
single dairy product in the building. The wood-fired oven turns out
thin-crust Neapolitan pizzas topped with a vegan cheese made in-house
from soy yogurt and nutritional yeast that melts convincingly enough to
fool skeptics. Homemade pasta, antipasti, and a kitchen run as an
egalitarian collective round out the charm.

The dessert case seals the deal: the vegan tiramisu is a legend among
regulars, and the crème caramel with orange sauce has its own fan club.
It is crowded for a reason, so book ahead and settle in for one of
Berlin's great plant-based nights out.
