Soul Vegan is the fully vegan Malaysian restaurant that Edinburgh's plant-based crowd gets dreamy about, tucked onto West Richmond Street just south of the Old Town. The menu runs on big flavours: laksa, satay tofu, salt and pepper lotus root, fried mushrooms, tom yum soup and pineapple fried rice, with a lion's mane mushroom rendang that regulars rave over. One HappyCow reviewer calls it probably the best vegan restaurant in Edinburgh, and the portions are famously generous.

It is a small, cosy room, so book ahead, especially for a weekend evening. Order the laksa if it is your first visit, add the crispy wontons, and finish with banana fritters. This is the table for a cold Edinburgh night, when only a proper bowl of comfort food will do.
