Clover Food Lab is a vegetarian fast-food chain that started as food trucks and now runs brick-and-mortar shops across the Boston area, with a Central Square location on Massachusetts Avenue. The menu rotates with the seasons, built around chickpea fritter sandwiches, BBQ seitan, and rosemary fries, and most dishes can be made vegan on request.

Order the chickpea fritter sandwich and the rosemary fries. Note the history: Clover announced a wind-down in May 2026 and reopened its Boston and Cambridge locations on June 9, 2026, so confirm the Central Square shop's hours before a special trip, and do not use the permanently closed 1075 Cambridge Street site.
