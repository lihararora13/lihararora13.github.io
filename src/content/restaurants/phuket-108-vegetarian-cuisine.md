108 Vegetarian Cuisine is the cheapest pick in this guide and the most local: a simple, fully vegan Thai eatery across the street from Cherng Thalay Wittayakom School in Cherngtalay, formerly known as Kun Na's Kitchen, run from the front of a family home. The food is rice with vegetables, tofu, mock meat and noodles, plus three different curries, all cooked in the morning, and a plate costs around 60 THB. It is a Jay restaurant, so the cooking is without garlic, onion or other pungent roots.

It opens early, around 6am, and closes by the early afternoon, about 2pm, so go for breakfast or lunch and get there early, as dishes sell out. HappyCow lists it open daily, while Vegan Food Quest lists it closed on Sundays, so check ahead on a Sunday. A second listing places it at Soi Cherngtalay 14 with slightly different coordinates, so the pin on this page is approximate for the Cherngtalay area.
