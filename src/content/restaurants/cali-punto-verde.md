Punto Verde is a 4.5-rated vegan kitchen in the centre serving family-style spreads: soups, rice plates and salads, laid out for sharing. It is the kind of place that works best with a group, or with a serious appetite.

Order the soup of the day, a rice plate and something from the falafel side of the menu. The portions are generous, the prices are gentle, and it is one of the most local-feeling vegan rooms in Cali.
