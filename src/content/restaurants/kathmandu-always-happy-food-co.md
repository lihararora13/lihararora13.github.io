Always Happy Food Co made the list for a reason. A Thamel food counter doing build-your-own salad bowls, burrito wraps, biryani, falafel and burgers, all vegan except the cow's milk in drinks. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Thamel, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Build-your-own salad bowl, Falafel wrap, Vegan burrito if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
