Why we love it — Teresa Carles is the restaurant that made
Barcelona take veggie food seriously, and it is still the benchmark
everyone else is chasing. The brick-walled dining room near Sant
Antoni buzzes from noon to night, and the kitchen turns Catalan
comfort classics into plant-based showpieces without losing their
soul. Half the menu is vegan by default, and the rest can be made
vegan without any drama.

This is your fancy-but-chill night out. Order the crispy rice
socarrat paella with vegan "calamari," follow it with the coconut-bacon
Caesar, and finish with the chocolate pastisset. Forty years in, and
it still feels like the future of Mediterranean food.
