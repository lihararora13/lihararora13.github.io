Zeal has been feeding Boulder's health-conscious crowd for years, and it is one of those rare rooms where vegans, vegetarians and omnivores all eat happily at the same table. The menu leans fresh and seasonal, with bowls, salads and juices that change with what is good at the market.

It is a calm, easy stop on the east side of town, open daily. Order a grain bowl, add a fresh juice, and you have the most Boulder lunch imaginable.
