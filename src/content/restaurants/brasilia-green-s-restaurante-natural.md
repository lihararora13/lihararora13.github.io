Green's Restaurante Natural is a vegan-friendly brazilian spot in Asa Norte. Green's is one of Asa Norte's older natural restaurants, a buffet spot where the vegan dishes sit alongside vegetarian ones, including an omelette station.

Order the Salada do dia, Prato vegetariano, and Suco natural. Ask which buffet items are vegan that day before loading the plate.
