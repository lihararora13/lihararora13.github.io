Osteria di Fuori Porta is a natural, organic osteria built on local, seasonal and fair-trade ingredients, with gluten-free and lactose-free options always on the menu. The dishes run from beetroot borscht to chickpea hummus, carrot flan with black olive tapenade, cabbage rolls with seitan and a lentil, quinoa and mushroom burger.

Order the beetroot borscht, the chickpea hummus with crudites, the cabbage rolls with seitan and the lentil burger. It serves lunch and dinner six days a week, closed Sundays.
