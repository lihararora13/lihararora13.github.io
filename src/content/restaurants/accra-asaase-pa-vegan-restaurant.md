Asaase Pa calls itself the oldest local vegan restaurant in Ghana, and it feels like it in the best way: a quiet, unpretentious spot on High Street in Jamestown with large portions of vegan Ghanaian food and a health food shop attached. The menu is not always fully available, so call ahead and ask what is cooking that day.

The vegetable rice and vegan kebab are the reliable orders. Come for lunch, sit outside, and enjoy one of the most genuinely local vegan meals in West Africa.
