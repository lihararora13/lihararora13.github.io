Akimy is the new star of Munich's vegan scene, a modern Vietnamese fusion restaurant that opened in November 2023 and shot straight to a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow. The room by Gärtnerplatz serves phở, summer rolls, bao buns, crispy "duck" and mango "chicken", plus vegan sushi, all of it fully plant-based and a step more refined than the average noodle counter. Reservations are required, which tells you how quickly word got around.

Book ahead, order the phở and a round of summer rolls, and try the bao buns. This is the polished, date-night end of the Glockenbachviertel, and the strongest evidence that Munich's vegan scene is still getting better.
