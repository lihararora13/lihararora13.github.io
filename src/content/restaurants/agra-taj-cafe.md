Taj Cafe sits at South Gate Chowk, about 180 metres from the Taj, open 7:30 AM to 11:30 PM. It is a straightforward cafe doing masala chai, veg thali, lassi and Mughlai plates, rated 4.1 by its reviewers.

The veg thali is the order, and the masala chai is the companion. It is a good second stop if Joney's is full, and it stays open later than most of the lane.
