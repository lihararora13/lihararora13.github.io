Delhi Mistan Bhandar sits on Laitumkhrah Main Road, in the cafe quarter around the Don Bosco area, and it is the sweet-shop-and-cafe stop on this list: samosa chaat, veg sandwiches and a sweets counter. It is a handy snack break between the Laitumkhrah cafes.

Order the samosa chaat without curd, or a veg sandwich, and treat the sweets counter as a look rather than a lunch, since most of the mithai is dairy-based.
