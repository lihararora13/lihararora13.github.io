Puleunsolmalg Eunhyang, whose name translates roughly as "the clear scent of the green pine forest", is a small, fully vegetarian temple-food restaurant beside a nursing home in Nohyeong-dong, with a view the owners are proud of. The cooking is home-meal style: bibimbap with a generous spread of vegetable side dishes, leafy greens, root vegetables, beans and ferments, served without alliums. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow, and regulars praise the kindness of the owners as much as the food.

It is not fully vegan, it is ovo-vegetarian, so say no egg when you order and point at the dishes on HappyCow if the language barrier bites: the menu is in Korean and little English is spoken. It is open daily 11:30am to 6pm. The pin on this page is district-level for Nohyeong-dong, and the entrance is a dark grey building on the left of the road, so look carefully.
