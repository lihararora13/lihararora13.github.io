Ruka Prana is a plant-based kitchen on Manuel Antonio Matta serving empanadas, hummus, burgers, falafel and lentil dishes, with a moderate price and a weekday lunch-and-afternoon rhythm. It is one of the quieter names on this list, and one of the most useful for a straightforward vegan lunch away from the tourist hills.

Order an empanada and the falafel plate, and ask about the lentil dish of the day. The kitchen is open Monday to Friday, 11 am to 7:30 pm.
