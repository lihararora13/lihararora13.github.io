Guac 'n' Roll is one of the most unique restaurants in Liverpool: a punk-rock vegan kitchen in the heart of the Fabric District, near the Royal Hospital, where the tiny flyer-covered dining room doubles as a live-music venue. The menu is international and homemade, built on seitan made in-house: Philly cheesesteak, sticky ribz, banh mi, loaded burgers, creative small plates and a Sunday roast on the first Sunday of each month.

It only opens Thursday to Sunday, so check the hours before you go, and expect a queue when a gig is on. Order the sticky ribz, which regulars call the star of the show, and stay for the music.
