Fuego Verde is a vegetarian kitchen on Derqui in Godoy Cruz, just south of the city centre, serving lunch on weekdays and dinner on Thursday and Friday evenings. That evening service makes it one of the more useful picks in Mendoza, where most kitchens close after lunch.

Order a lunch plate, or come on a Thursday or Friday evening for dinner. Ask which dishes are vegan when you order, as the kitchen cooks vegetarian with vegan options.
