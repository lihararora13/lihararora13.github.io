Sunshine Vegan Eats is Buffalo's vegan heart: a Black-owned, all-vegan cafe on Jefferson Avenue where chef Nikki cooks the Southern-inspired food she wanted to eat in the city, a shrimp po'boy, waffles, grits, soup of the day and health shots and drinks. It is the highest-rated vegan spot in Buffalo, and in May 2026 the mayor visited during Small Business Week and filmed a segment cooking oyster mushrooms with her. The room is bright, the portions are generous and the prices are low.

Hours are Monday 11am to 6:30pm, Wednesday 11am to 8pm, Thursday 11am to 7pm, Friday 11am to 10pm, Saturday 10am to 8pm and Sunday 11am to 5pm, closed Tuesdays. A second location at 1412 Fillmore was announced in 2026, so confirm the address before you go; this guide lists the Jefferson Avenue original.
