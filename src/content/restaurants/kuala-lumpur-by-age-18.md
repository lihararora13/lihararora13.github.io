by age 18 does one thing and does it beautifully: Sanuki-style udon made from Japanese rice flour, which makes the whole menu both fully vegan and fully gluten-free, broths built on kombu, shiitake and vegetables with no alcohol anywhere, not even mirin. SethLui's June 2026 review and HappyCow's perfect rating agree this is a special little spot.

The basil pesto udon is the signature, the tomyam udon is the local handshake, and the curry udon is the hug. Save room for matcha pudding and an oat milk matcha latte, and remember it rests on Wednesdays.
