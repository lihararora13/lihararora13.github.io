Antica Focacceria San Francesco is a Palermo institution, open for over two centuries, and the street-food pick on this list. Its pane e panelle, chickpea fritters in a roll, is one of Sicily's great naturally vegan dishes, and a Slow Food chef put it in his top five vegan dishes of Sicily.

Order the pane e panelle and ask that it be fried in oil rather than lard, since some vendors still use lard. It is a standing, eating-with-your-hands stop, not a sit-down meal.
