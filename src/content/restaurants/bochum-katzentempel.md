Katzentempel is the first cat temple in the Ruhr, a fully vegan café on Huestraße in Bochum's pedestrian zone, a cat's leap from the Hauptbahnhof. The temple cats are named after Ruhr dialect words, and the kitchen serves the full Katzentempel range: scrambled tofu, açai bowls, veggie burgers, burger bowls and a Limiaucello spritz, all of it plant-based, in fluffy company.

It is open every day from ten, until nine on weeknights and ten on Friday and Saturday. Book ahead at weekends, order the scrambled tofu for breakfast or a burger bowl for lunch, and expect the cats to set the pace of the meal.
