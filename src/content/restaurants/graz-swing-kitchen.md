Swing Kitchen is the vegan burger chain's Graz branch on Kaiserfeldgasse, open daily from eleven to ten, serving a fully plant-based menu of burgers, nuggets, salads, wraps and desserts. The bacon burger and the chili cheeseburger are the ones to order, with sweet potato fries on the side.

Order the bacon burger or the chili cheeseburger, the sweet potato fries and a dessert cake, and come for a fast, filling lunch or dinner.
