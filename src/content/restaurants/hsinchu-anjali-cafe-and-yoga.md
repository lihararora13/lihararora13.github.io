Anjali Cafe and Yoga is a plant-based cafe and brunch spot above a yoga studio on Ziqiang 5th Road in Zhubei, with house-made vegan cheese sauce, oat milk and some gluten-free dishes, according to the Commercial Times in May 2026. It is the brunch pick of the Zhubei cluster, and the yoga studio downstairs gives it a calm, unhurried feel.

Order a brunch plate with the vegan cheese sauce and an oat milk coffee. It is closed on Wednesdays, and opening hours differ between sources, so check before you go.
