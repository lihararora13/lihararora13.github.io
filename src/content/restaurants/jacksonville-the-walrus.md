The Walrus is an all-vegan bar and cafe on Edgewood Avenue South in Murray Hill, a daytime vegan cafe that turns into a live music venue and bar by night. The food is pressed sandwiches and plant-based snacks, the drinks are beer and wine, and the room has an arcade and pool tables.

Order a pressed sandwich and a juice. Note that it is a music venue, so it gets loud when a show is on.
