fifteen is a plant-based Western restaurant on Wenxing Road in Zhubei, open since spring 2023, serving rice bowls, sandwiches and pasta with organic produce and no egg or dairy. Mains run about NT$340 to 420, and the room is bright and modern, a good lunch stop in the Zhubei cluster.

Order a rice bowl or a sandwich and ask about the day's pasta. Days off differ between sources, with one review saying closed Wednesday and another saying closed Monday, so check before you go.
