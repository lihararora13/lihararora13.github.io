An Lac Tam is a traditional vegetarian buffet on Pham Ngu Lao in Dalat, with mock meat and seafood dishes, open about 7am to 9pm, and several branches in town. It is a local favourite, and it is the kind of place where the buffet is the meal, with a wide choice of dishes.

Order from the buffet and ask which dishes are vegan, as the kitchen is vegetarian and some dishes use egg. It is inexpensive and central.
