Sprout made the list for a reason. A small all-vegan stall inside the Astrek climbing wall in central Thamel, doing smoothie bowls, Buddha bowls and brownies. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Thamel, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Smoothie bowl, Buddha bowl, Vegan brownie if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
