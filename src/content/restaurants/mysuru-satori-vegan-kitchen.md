Satori Vegan Kitchen is the reason Mysuru earns a place on a vegan map at all: a kitchen with vegan in the name, in the middle of Gokulam, the neighbourhood where the city's yoga students eat. The 2026 mindful-dining guide for the area lists it alongside the quarter's best plant-based institutions, serving plant-based South Indian curries daily.

Come here first and order without negotiating the menu. The curries are the draw, and the kitchen's whole pitch is that nothing on the plate ever came from an animal, which is still rare enough in Mysuru to be worth celebrating.
