Sānjīn Zhì Miàn, 三津制麵, is the Taichung outpost of a three-generation handmade noodle family from Tainan, set in a renovated old house at 249 Zhongshan Road, a short walk from the Second Market, and it is the prettiest room in this guide: wood, soft light and plants, a Zen calm that makes eating a bowl of noodles feel like a small ceremony. The noodles are handmade and springy, served as set meals with soup, braised sides and vegetables, and the dipping noodles are a Taichung-only flavour. The heritage minced-mushroom mixed noodles, the sour-spicy pesto cold noodles and the tomato wild-mushroom dipping noodles are the three to choose between, with classic zhajiang and a Sichuan-spicy version behind them.

Set meals run about NT$210, and the menu is Chinese-only, though some staff speak English and can help translate. Cash only, closed Mondays. Note the hours differ between sources: local blogs list weekdays 11:00am to 2:00pm and 4:00pm to 8:00pm, weekends 11:00am to 8:00pm, while HappyCow lists Tuesday to Sunday 11:00am to 6:00pm, so check before you go.
