Lucciano's is the trendy ice-cream chain with branches all over Mar del Plata, and its vegan flavours include pistachio, dark chocolate sorbet, dulce de leche granizado and mint granizado. The Güemes shop is used here and its pin is approximate.

Order the Helado de pistacho vegano, Sorbete de chocolate, and Granizado de menta. Ask which flavours are vegan that day; the pistachio and the dark chocolate sorbet are the ones to try.
