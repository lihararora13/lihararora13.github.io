Sometimes you do not want a wellness journey, you want a burger, and Vegan Burger delivers exactly that without a single animal product in sight. This is the UAE's first fully plant-powered fast-food brand, and the Dubai Mall counter is its flagship: crispy, cheesy, gloriously unserious comfort food.

The truffle cheese burger with loaded cheesy fries is the classic order, and the crispy bacon cheeseburger is the one that converts sceptics. Perfect for a mid-shopping refuel, and proof that Dubai's vegan scene does indulgence just as well as it does kale.
