Tofurei is a plant-based cafe tucked into St Gregorys Alley in the Lanes, with a tofu-led menu of brunch plates, bowls and cakes. It is small, calm and quietly one of the best daytime rooms in the city.

Order the tofu brunch and a cake. A good quiet stop between the market and the cathedral.
