Aju is the smallest room on this list and one of the most memorable: a one-person vegan izakaya in Nakazakicho, one stop from Umeda, where the owner cooks everything himself at a counter that seats four. The menu is built around soy meats, yakitori skewers, okonomiyaki, Japanese curry, casserole and a carbonara udon, and the whole thing runs at the pace of one pair of hands, so arrive early or expect a wait.

It is fully vegan, confirmed in December 2025, and it takes credit cards. Do not confuse it with the old Aju listing that served meat and closed in 2021; this is the current four-seater, and it is worth planning an evening around. Closed Mondays.
