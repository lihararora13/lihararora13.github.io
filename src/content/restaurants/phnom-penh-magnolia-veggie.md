Magnolia Veggie is a long-running vegetarian restaurant in the city centre, open from 6.30am to 10pm every day, and it is one of the cheapest proper meals in Phnom Penh. The menu is Vietnamese-led, with vegan pho and noodles among the signatures.

Order the vegan pho and a noodle dish. It is a vegetarian restaurant rather than an all-vegan one, so file it as vegan-friendly, and come early, because the room fills at lunch.
