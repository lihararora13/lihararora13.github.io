Kake di Hatti brings Punjabi comfort food to Cidco's Town Centre: rich dals, chole and tandoori breads, served in a room that fills up at dinner. It is the heartiest meal on this list, and the one most likely to leave you happily full.

The dal makhani is the signature, so ask for it without cream and butter, and pair it with chole and hot rotis. The kitchen is used to the request, and the food survives the swap with its flavour intact.
