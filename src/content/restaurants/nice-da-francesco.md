Da Francesco is a pizzeria near Rue de France, a takeaway spot run by its pizzaiolo since 1988, described as a half-vegan pizzeria with a vegan menu. The vegan pizzas include a quattro formaggi that one reviewer called world-class, alongside a marinara and pizza al taglio, and the shop passed 800 Google reviews in 2026 with praise for its vegan options and value.

It is a takeaway counter, so order a pizza and eat it on the beach or in a square. Confirm the vegan cheese when you order, and start with the quattro formaggi.
