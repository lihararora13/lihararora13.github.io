Zest Juice Co is High Street's juice bar: cold-pressed juices, smoothies, acai bowls, tonics and vegan wraps, mostly vegan apart from honey, with vegan cheese and vegan mayo on hand. It is the freshest stop on the list, and the one to hit after a morning in Clintonville.

It is a quick, bright, healthy meal rather than a feast. Order an acai bowl, grab a juice, and take on the rest of High Street.
