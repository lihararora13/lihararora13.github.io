Posillipo is a friendly Italian on the Borough with vegan pasta and pizza options. It is a reliable evening choice when the smaller vegan rooms have closed.

Order the vegan pasta and a dairy-free pizza to share. Ask for the vegan menu, as it is separate from the main one.
