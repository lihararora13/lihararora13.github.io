Soban Vegan is the meal Busan vegans rave about most: a tiny, four-table restaurant run by an ajumma cooking traditional bansang meal sets, the kind of multi-banchan spread you would get in a family home. The fried chicken is, by several accounts, the best vegan fried chicken in the world.

It is small and closes Wednesday and Sunday, with a 19:00 last order. Pair it with a Gamcheon Culture Village visit.
