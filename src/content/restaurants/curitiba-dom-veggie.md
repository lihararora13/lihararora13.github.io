Dom Veggie is Curitiba's themed buffet: an organic vegetarian restaurant where each day of the week has a different style of food, Oriental, Italian, Mexican, Indian and more, including desserts and juices. The buffet is priced per person or per pound, there is free parking, and delivery is available. It is a fun, generous way to eat, and the vegan options are always there if you ask.

Come for lunch, check the theme of the day, and fill a buffet plate. This is the one for a different cuisine every day of the week.
