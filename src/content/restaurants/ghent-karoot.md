KAROOT is a vegan restaurant on Phoenixstraat in Brugse Poort, with a pay-what-you-can daily special and a community dining room that feels more like a shared kitchen than a restaurant. The menu changes daily, built on seasonal vegetables and hearty portions, with outdoor seating in summer.

It is open Tuesday to Thursday evenings and Friday to Sunday afternoons and evenings. Order the daily special, pay what you can, and expect a warm, inclusive room.
