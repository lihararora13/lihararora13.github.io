Bioburger is the organic burger chain's Lille outpost on Place de Béthune, and it does what the chain does everywhere: a veggie burger, fries and a salad, all organic, with a vegan option clearly on the menu. It is the reliable fallback on this page, open when you need it.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, but the veggie burger is a known quantity. Good for a quick central lunch between sights.
