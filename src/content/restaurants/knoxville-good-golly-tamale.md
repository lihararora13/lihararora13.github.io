Good Golly Tamale is a tamale shop on South Central Street in the Old City, doing tamales with a vegan bench alongside the meat versions. It is the most distinctive pick on this list, and the best quick lunch downtown.

Order the vegan tamales, two or three to a plate, with salsa and chips. Ask which tamales are vegan that day, since the menu rotates.
