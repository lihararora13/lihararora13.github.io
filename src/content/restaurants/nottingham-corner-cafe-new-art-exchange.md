The Corner Cafe sits inside the New Art Exchange on Gregory Boulevard, and it is fully plant-based: grilled sandwiches, fries and salads served in a bright room with an orange bar counter, globe lights and bookshelves, under the gallery's contemporary art programme.

Order a grilled sandwich with fries and a slice of cake. It is a gallery cafe first and a restaurant second, so come for a light lunch between exhibitions rather than a big dinner.
