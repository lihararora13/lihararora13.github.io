Elaa Cafe made the list for a reason. A paddy-field cafe in Anjuna with a menu that is 70% vegan, cooking Goan-inspired plates with a light, modern touch. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Anjuna, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Goan vegan thali, Salad bowl, Fresh juice if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
