Dimassi's is a Mediterranean buffet on Camp Bowie Boulevard, and it is the best vegan-friendly bet in Fort Worth when not everyone at the table eats plant-based: falafel, hummus, lentil dishes, and stuffed grape leaves across a buffet where the vegan dishes are clearly part of the spread.

Go hungry, build a plate from the vegan side of the buffet, and take your time.
