Loving Hut is the anchor of Gurugram's vegan scene and, by most accounts, the city's only fully vegan restaurant. No animal products, no exceptions, across a multi-cuisine menu that runs from Asian stir-fries to burgers and thalis.

It sits in Sector 38, rated 4.3 by delivery regulars, and it is the table to book when someone in your group is properly vegan and tired of negotiating menus. Order the tofu stir-fry and the vegan thali, and leave room for a dairy-free dessert.
