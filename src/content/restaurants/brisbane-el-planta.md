El Planta started as a Mexican street-food pop-up and grew into the dinner every Brisbane vegan plans their week around. The Hope Street room is unfussy and homely, folk art on the walls and a cactus garden by the window, and the seasonally changing menu runs on house-made corn and flour tortillas, beer-battered palmito pescado tacos, mushroom dishes with popcorn sauce and pumpkin tacos with sunflower sauce, all washed down with a burnt pineapple margarita.

It is dinner only, Tuesday to Saturday, and weekends fill up fast, so book ahead. Order the banquet, let the server pick the best of the menu, and save room for dessert: this is the table that proves nobody misses meat when the cooking is this good.
