Moody Mike's is Lexington's first vegan food truck, now a cafe on North Limestone, and a minority-owned local favorite. The menu is 100 percent plant-based comfort food: Wingz made from veggie and soy proteins on edible sugarcane bones, a beer batter burger, the Verax fried chicken sandwich, cheeseburger egg rolls, and sixteen house-made sauces.

Order the Buffalo Wingz with Nashville Hot or Mango Habanero sauce and the Beer Batter Burger. Come hungry, since the portions are large, and note the cafe opens at 8am Wednesday through Saturday and 10am on Sunday.
