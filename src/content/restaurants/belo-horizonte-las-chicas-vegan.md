Las Chicas Vegan is Belo Horizonte's vegan feminist restaurant-bar: casual lunch by day, and at dinner the menu expands with burgers, sandwiches, specialty beers, juices and shakes. The burger on brioche, the feijoada and the strogonoff are the plates people come back for, and the room has the warm, welcoming energy of a place with a point of view.

Come for lunch or stay for dinner and a beer, order a burger on brioche, and enjoy the vibe. This is the one for a fun vegan night out in Centro.
