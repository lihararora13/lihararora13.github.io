New Taj Mahal Cafe is the old Hampankatta veg cafe inside Hotel Panchmahal, rated 3.9 across 99 reviews and known for banana-leaf meals, masala dosa, plain dosa and filter coffee. It is one of the city's oldest veg rooms, and the meals still come on the leaf.

Order the banana-leaf meal and a filter coffee, and ask for the curd to be skipped. It is a heritage stop rather than a modern cafe, and the food is better for it.
