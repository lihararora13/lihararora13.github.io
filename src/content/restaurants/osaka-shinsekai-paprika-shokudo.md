Shinsekai is Osaka's loudest neighbourhood, all neon and kushikatsu counters, and this is the vegan way into it. Shinsekai Paprika Shokudo opened in 2023 down an alley in Janjan Yokocho, steps from Tsutenkaku Tower, and it is a fully vegan and gluten-free izakaya: small plates of kushikatsu, takoyaki, noodles, miso and sweet treats, all cheap and all plant-based.

It is the second Paprika Shokudo in the city, the sibling of the Shinmachi original, and the one to pick when you are already south of the centre. The room is small with indoor and outdoor seating, the menu is ordered by QR code, and it closes on Wednesdays, so check the day before you cross town for it.
