Afrika Bite is an African restaurant on KG 674 Street with a buffet and vegan options including rice dishes, stews, chapati, plantains and more. It is a cheap, local room.

Order from the buffet and ask which dishes are vegan. It is the cheapest lunch on this list after the Pan-Afrikan Kitchen.
