Miss Green Bean is Potsdam's cosy health cafe, a warm room with wooden tables, plants and a courtyard, run by a founder who treats healthy food as a pleasure rather than a duty. The menu offers non-dairy milks like oat, coconut and almond for every coffee, vegan muesli and sandwiches, and dishes that are or can be made vegan: banana bread, hummus toast, avocado toast, semolina or oat porridge, and coconut yogurt with chocolate granola. It opens daily from eight in the morning.

The city-centre branch on Brandenburger Strasse is the one to visit; there is a second cafe in Babelsberg on Karl-Liebknecht-Strasse. Order an avocado toast and a matcha latte, and settle in. This is the wholesome breakfast pick in the old town.
