Sri Lanka Kagoshima is a Sri Lankan restaurant in the Tenmonkan district with a dedicated vegan menu on its second floor, advertised on its street A-frame in November 2026. The vegan menu lists a rice curry set, a rice noodle curry set and a vegan jeera rice curry set, all around 1,500 to 2,000 yen, with more dishes beyond. It is a meat-serving restaurant, so the vegan menu is the thing to ask for by name.

Order the vegan jeera rice curry set, the most clearly vegan of the three, and check the board for the rest of the vegan page. It is open late, which makes it one of the few vegan-friendly dinner options in the city, and its pin is approximate to Tenmonkan.
