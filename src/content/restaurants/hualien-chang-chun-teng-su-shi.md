Chang Chun Teng Su Shi is a vegetarian buffet on Fu'an Road, a ten-minute walk from Hualien train station, offering all-you-can-eat for a set price. It is a straightforward buffet kitchen, and vegans should check each dish, as the buffet is vegetarian rather than fully vegan.

Fill a plate with the tofu and vegetable dishes and ask about egg in the buffet items. It is inexpensive and filling, and it suits a quick lunch near the station.
