RAAW Cafe is a neighbourhood cafe in Saar focused on feel-good food, open daily 7am to 10pm, with wraps, grain bowls with grilled protein and sweet potato, a mango dessert and yellow drinks. It is a local favourite rather than a tourist room.

Order a grain bowl and the mango dessert. It is a good lunch stop in Saar, and it pairs well with a visit to For The Livin nearby.
