Hier und Jetzt is an all-vegetarian cafe and fast-food spot on Blucherstrasse, moved from Wilhelmstrasse in January 2024, with a vegan breakfast on homemade bread, vegan burger options with sides, and vegan cakes. Note the cinnamon rolls are not vegan, so ask before ordering the cakes.
