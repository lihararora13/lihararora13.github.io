Woodberry Cafe is a cafe on Haile Selassie Road with vegan Buddha bowls, vegan burgers and vegan snacks, plus gluten-free options and plant milk for coffee. Reviewers note the Buddha bowls are vegan but not labelled as such, so ask, and the vegan breakfast is a solid order.

Order the vegan Buddha bowl and a coffee with plant milk. It has indoor air conditioning and good internet, so it is a good work-from-cafe stop.
