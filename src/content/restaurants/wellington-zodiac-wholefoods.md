Zodiac Wholefoods is the burger stop on the list, a fully vegan kitchen on Taranaki Street whose plant-based burgers take the number one spot in Wellington's vegan burger rankings. The menu is short and confident: burgers and bowls, a vegan wellington, chicken bites and vegan cookies, served in two tight windows at lunch and dinner, Tuesday to Saturday. It is small, it is fast, and it knows exactly what it is good at.

Go for the burger, add the chicken bites, and finish with a cookie. Check the hours before you walk over, since the split lunch and dinner service means it is closed in the middle of the afternoon.
