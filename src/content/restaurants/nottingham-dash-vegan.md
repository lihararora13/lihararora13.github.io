Dash Vegan is a fully vegan fast-food counter tucked into Cobden Chambers on Pelham Street, right in the city centre. It is the quick-bite pick on this list: burgers, loaded fries and shakes, all plant-based, all affordable.

Order a vegan burger and loaded fries. It is a small counter rather than a sit-down restaurant, so this is a lunch-stop pick rather than an evening destination.
