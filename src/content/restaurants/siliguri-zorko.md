ZORKO is the fast-food end of Siliguri's veg scene: a chain outlet on Pranami Mandir Road, open 11 to 10 daily, serving burgers, momos and noodles in a quick-service format. It is the pick for a fast, cheap, plant-based bite between errands.

Order a veg burger or veg momos, and skip the cheese. It is not a dining destination, but it is a useful, reliably open veg option in the city centre.
