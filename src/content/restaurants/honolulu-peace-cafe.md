Peace Cafe has been a pillar of Oahu's vegan community since 2010, and it is still one of the most satisfying plant-based meals on the island. The Heart and Seoul bowl is the signature, the sandwiches are generous, the miso soup is the comfort move, and the dessert case is the reason to save room.

It is the cafe to take your time in, open daily from early morning. Order the bowl, add a dessert, and enjoy the place that has been doing this longer than almost anyone in Honolulu.
