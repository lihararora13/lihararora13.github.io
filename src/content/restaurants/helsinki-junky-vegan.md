Junky Vegan is the place Helsinki's vegans go when they want fast food without compromise, a 100% plant-based burger joint right by the central station. Local guides call it the top vegan fast food restaurant in the city, and the menu is exactly what the name promises: burgers, fries and comfort food, all of it meat-free and all of it satisfying.

It is the easiest stop on this list, a two-minute walk from the station. Order a burger and loaded fries, add a shake, and enjoy junk food with a clear conscience.
