Pure Vegan Heaven in Chalong is the original outlet of a small Thai vegan chain, founded in 2016, and it is the easiest vegan lunch after Wat Chalong and the Big Buddha. The menu is large and entirely vegan, with many gluten-free options: rice paper salad rolls, banana pancakes, nachos, quesadilla, burrito, mushroom larb, burgers with potato wedges and build-your-own salads, plus coffee, smoothies and desserts. Reviewers praise the coconut cappuccino sweetened with coconut blossom juice and the Thai chai tea, and the garden seating with fairy lights.

It is open Monday to Saturday, 9:30am to 8pm, and closed on Sundays, though other sources list different hours, so confirm before you drive over. The address also varies between sources, 45 Soi Ta-ied and 10/120 Moo 1 on the same road, after a move to larger premises; the pin on this page is neighbourhood-level for Chalong, and a sister outlet sits nearby on the same road, so make sure your driver has the right pin.
