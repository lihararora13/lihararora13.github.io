Fidka with Firangi Tadka does exactly what the name promises: foreign food with a desi twist, served in a cheerful Ravindrapuri dining room. Pastas, grills, sandwiches and cold coffees fill the menu, and the veggie side of it is generous.

It is a good mid-range stop between ghat walks, open noon to late. Order the hummus platter and a veg pasta, and ask the kitchen to keep it dairy-free.
