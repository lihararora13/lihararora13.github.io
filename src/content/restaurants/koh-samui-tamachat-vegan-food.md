Tamachat Vegan Food is a simple Buddhist vegan restaurant at the base of the Big Buddha, rated 4.5 from 43 HappyCow reviews, and it is the cheapest proper vegan meal in this guide after Vegan Khunnay. It is a basic vegan buffet with mock meats, plus a la carte options, fresh juices and vegan cooking classes, and it backs onto the ocean with views of Koh Phangan in the distance. To find it, go under the main gate to the Big Buddha, drive onto the island and look to the right of the temple steps; the front looks like a gift shop, with the kitchen cooking fresh dishes in the back, and a big yellow and red Jay sign marks it.

It is open daily, 8:00am to 5:00pm. A recent guide calls the same room Jay Tamachad and notes it moved to the Big Buddha temple grounds, so do not be confused by the two names. The pin is for the Big Buddha temple grounds rather than a street address, as HappyCow lists no street address for it.
