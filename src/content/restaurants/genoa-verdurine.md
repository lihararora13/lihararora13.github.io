Verdurine is a natural and artisanal gastronomia with seats in the central Castelletto area, a vegetarian kitchen with a strong vegan thread: burgers, panini, curry, gyoza and hummus, all made in-house.

Order the burger, the curry and the gyoza, and come at lunch, when the counter is at its best. It closes early on Friday and is shut at weekends, so do not leave it for Saturday.
