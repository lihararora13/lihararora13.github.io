Il Giardino is an Italian bar and grill on Malindi Road in Nyali, offering two vegan pasta dishes. It is open daily 7:30am to midnight, and it is a meat-serving room with a small vegan corner.

Order a vegan pasta and confirm no cheese or butter. It is the Italian fallback in Nyali, and the long hours make it useful late.
