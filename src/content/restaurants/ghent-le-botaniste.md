Le Botaniste is the Ghent original of the small plant-based chain that now has locations in New York and Paris, and the Ghent room remains the best. The menu is entirely plant-based and 99% organic, organised around bowls and small plates, with natural wines alongside: the Tibetan Mama bowl, chilli sin carne, a bowl with hummus and tzatziki, and a groentetajine.

It is open daily from 11:30 to 21:30, so it is the reliable all-day pick on this list. Order the Tibetan Mama bowl and a glass of natural wine.
