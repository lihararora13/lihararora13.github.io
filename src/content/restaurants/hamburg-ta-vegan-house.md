TA Vegan House tops HappyCow's Hamburg list with more than three hundred reviews, and it earns them: a fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant where the food comes as tapas-style small plates to share, alongside larger dishes, all of it fresh, fragrant and generous. There is a second location in Altona, but the Neustadt original is the one to book.

Come hungry and order a spread of tapas for the table. It is the best introduction to Hamburg's vegan scene, and the table every visitor should start with.
