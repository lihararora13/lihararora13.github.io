Streetfood Oriental is a street-food spot in Sakkala serving Egyptian and Middle Eastern dishes, open daily from 10am to midnight. It is a cheap, fast, late-night option.

Order a falafel sandwich and hummus. It is the last stop on this list for a reason, a useful late-night fallback when the koshary houses are full.
