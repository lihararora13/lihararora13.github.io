Cafe Erde is the riverside vegan cafe by the Mur, open every day from half eleven to ten, serving seitan, tofu, falafel, wraps and sandwiches. The soy schnitzel with fries and cranberries costs 14.50 euros and is the dish to order, and the same team runs Austria's first vegan university canteen.

Order the soy schnitzel with fries and cranberries, a wrap and a falafel plate, and come any day, since it opens daily.
