Kesar Da Dhaba is the dhaba every Amritsar guide starts with: a long-running, tucked-away eatery near Hall Bazaar, down a narrow alley you reach by rickshaw, where the dal makhani is an overnight preparation and the missi roti, lachcha parantha and chhole are the supporting cast.

The dal makhani is the order, ask for it without cream and butter, and the palak paneer is the one to skip. A meal for four costs around 750 rupees, which tells you everything about the value here.
