Kanvr Bistro Lounge sits at Sukhadia Circle, the roundabout at the heart of Udaipur's modern quarter, and it plays the role of relaxed all-day café and lounge. It is a good meeting point, a good working lunch, and a good place to recover from a morning of sightseeing.

The Continental menu veganises easily: pasta, salads and grilled vegetable sandwiches, with the dairy held. Come for a slow afternoon coffee and stay for dinner.
