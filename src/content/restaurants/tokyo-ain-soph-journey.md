Shinjuku can be a lot, which is exactly why AIN SOPH. Journey feels like a small miracle. Step off Shinjuku-sanchome into this bright, green, all-vegan space and the city noise drops away, replaced by seasonal plates, gentle curries, and some of the fluffiest pancakes in Tokyo.

Come for lunch, stay for the Heavenly Pancakes, and do not rush the dessert menu. It is the perfect reset button in the middle of a big Tokyo day, and a lovely first vegan stop for sceptical friends.
