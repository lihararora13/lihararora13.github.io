Hydraulic Hearth is Buffalo's brewery-restaurant in Larkinville, a converted industrial space with a big patio, house beer and a menu that makes room for vegans: a vegan pizza, a hummus plate and roasted broccoli, all clearly part of the kitchen's repertoire rather than an afterthought. It is the evening pick in this guide, open Tuesday to Saturday 4pm to 10pm, and the kind of room that works for a group with mixed diets.

Order the vegan pizza and the hummus plate, and pair them with a house beer. The space is wheelchair accessible with outdoor seating in season.
