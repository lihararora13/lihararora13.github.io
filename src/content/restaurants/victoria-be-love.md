be love is Victoria's downtown fusion spot: a restaurant on Blanshard Street whose food menu is vegan, with a satay sandwich, masoor daal, seasonal salads and kombucha on tap. It is listed vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan because some drinks contain honey, so ask about the drinks when ordering. The room is bright and central, open Monday to Saturday 11am to 8pm and Sunday 11am to 5pm.

Order the satay sandwich and a salad, and check the drinks list for honey. The food is entirely vegan, so ordering is easy.
