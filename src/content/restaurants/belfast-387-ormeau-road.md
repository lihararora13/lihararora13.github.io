387 Ormeau Road is the address and the name: a fully plant-based cafe and small grocery in Ballynafeigh, ten minutes south of the city centre by bus. Toasties, wraps, sausage rolls, soups and stews are made on site, and desserts come from local vegan bakery Crumbs.

Order a toastie and a sausage roll, and pick up something from the grocery shelves on the way out. It is one of only two strictly all-vegan venues anchoring the Belfast scene, and it is the casual daytime anchor of the whole list.
