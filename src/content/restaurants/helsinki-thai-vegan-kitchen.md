Thai Vegan Kitchen is the restaurant that put Helsinki's vegan scene on the map for Thai food lovers, a former food truck that has been at its Lapinlahdenkatu home since October 2021. The menu is fully vegan Thai with Korean, Chinese and Japanese inspired daily specials: Beyond meat pad kra prao, khao soi in coconut curry sauce, grilled pork-style with papaya salad, pork satay, pad thai and fried rice.

It is small and popular, so come early or be ready to wait. Order the khao soi, add the satay, and let the daily special surprise you.
