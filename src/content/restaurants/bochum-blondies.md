Blondies is a burger bar on Hattinger Straße in Ehrenfeld, and its vegan Taxiteller is a small piece of Ruhr food history: the classic Taxiteller of fries, currywurst and gyros, reinterpreted fully plant-based, alongside vegan burgers, a truffle burger and even a vegan crème brûlée. The room is lively, the portions are generous, and the menu is almost entirely vegan.

One thing to know: the owners changed in February 2025 and meat is back on the menu, so order from the vegan-marked dishes rather than assuming the whole card is plant-based. Open Tuesday to Saturday evenings from half past five, and Sundays from five, it is Bochum's best vegan-friendly night out.
