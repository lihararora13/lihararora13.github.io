Why we love it — Burma Burma is what happens when a cuisine built on
fermented tea leaves, chickpea tofu, and coconut noodles meets a kitchen
that never has to think about meat. The whole restaurant is vegetarian,
the vegan options are marked, and the flavors are the kind that make
omnivores stop talking mid-sentence.

The Saket room is half the experience: red walls, prayer wheels, Burmese
curios, and a tea list that deserves its own visit. A 4.9 on Zomato across
thousands of ratings is not an accident. Order the tea leaf salad, order
the khao suey, and do not skip dessert.
