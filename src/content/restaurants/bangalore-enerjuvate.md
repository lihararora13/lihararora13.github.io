Enerjuvate is the kind of place Bangalore does best: a yoga and Pilates studio with a café tucked inside, where the food is healthy without being joyless and the prices stay gentle. The Koramangala branch on 7th Cross Road is the one to visit, a cosy, book-lined room where the menu leans on millets, forgotten grains and very little oil, and where almost anything non-vegan can be veganised on request. HappyCow rates it a perfect 5.0, and regulars treat it as a second living room.

Order the orange chilli tofu, the veggie zoodle bowl, or the watermelon delight salad with cashew cheese, and wash it down with the Go Green juice with amla. Stay for a class afterwards if you're feeling virtuous, or just for the board games and the bookshelf.
