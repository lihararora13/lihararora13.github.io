Vege Marche 19 is a vegetable market restaurant in Korimoto, south of the centre, serving vegetable-led lunches daily from 11am to 3pm. It is a daytime-only kitchen with a short, seasonal menu built around farm vegetables, and it is the closest thing in Kagoshima to a farm-to-table lunch.

Come for the vegetable lunch set at midday, when the cooking is freshest, and do not plan on dinner here, as the kitchen closes at 3pm. It is a good stop on the way to or from the ferry port.
