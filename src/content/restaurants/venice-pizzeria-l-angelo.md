Pizzeria L'Angelo is on Calle de la Mandola in San Marco, unpromising territory for value, and it turns out to be the exception. They do vegan pizza with vegan cheese, the staff know exactly what the word means, and it charges local prices in a part of the city that very rarely does.

Order a vegan pizza with formaggio vegano, and add a focaccia if you are sharing. Say senza formaggio if you want no cheese at all, because the two requests get confused constantly, and come at lunch when it is calmer than dinner.
