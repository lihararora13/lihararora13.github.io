Twist Bakery is Dublin's best-kept open secret: a Temple Bar artisan bakery where every croissant, danish and patisserie is made without dairy or eggs, even though the bakery itself barely shouts about it. Head pastry chef Marija Lacic previously ran the vegan bakery Cake My Day, and the craft shows.

The flaky croissants are the must-try, but branch out to the danishes, cruffins and the mind-blowing Ferrero Rocher cake. Twist even catered for Alicia Silverstone while she was filming in Ireland, which tells you everything about how good this pastry case is.
