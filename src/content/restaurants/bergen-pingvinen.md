Pingvinen is a gastropub on Vaskerelven in the Sentrum, and it is the weakest pick on this page, included because Bergen's vegan scene is thin and this is a real, verified option. Only one or two dishes are vegan, a mushroom casserole that can be veganised, squash with pearl barley and vegan cabbage rolls, so check with the staff before ordering.

It is also a pub that serves whale and reindeer, so vegans may prefer to skip it on principle. If you do go, the mushroom casserole is the order, and the cabbage rolls run it close.
