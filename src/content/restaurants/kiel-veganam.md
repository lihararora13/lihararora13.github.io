Veganam is Kiel's first fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant, and it is the best first stop in the city. The phở is the dish to order: a proper broth, fresh herbs, and tofu that actually tastes of something. Bánh mì, spring rolls and a changing set of specials fill out a menu that is entirely plant-based, in a bright room on the Dreiecksplatz that feels like a good neighbourhood restaurant rather than a health-food café.

It sits a short walk from the harbour, open Tuesday to Sunday from noon. Order the phở if it is your first visit, add spring rolls to share, and save room for whatever special the kitchen is running. This is the table that shows Kiel's vegan scene at its best.
