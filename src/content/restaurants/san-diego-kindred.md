Kindred is the most fun dinner in San Diego, full stop. The aesthetic is French gothic meets death metal: skulls, a demon wolf mascot, shock-rocker art, psychedelic metal on the sound system. The food, though, is the real headline: the eggroll burger is the kind of dish people drive across county lines for, and the beer-battered palm tacos, chimichurri skewers, and weekend brunch tofu benedict are all elite.

It is also a proper cocktail bar with mocktails, kombucha on tap, and cold brew, so bring the non-vegan skeptics and watch the room do the converting for you. Opened in 2015 and still the beating dark heart of South Park.
