Michelle Falafel is a small all-vegan falafel restaurant on Derech Yafo, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, with outdoor seating. It is open Monday to Friday from 10:00am to 5:00pm and Sundays the same hours.

Order the falafel in pita. There is a second branch on Wadi Street, but the Derech Yafo address on this page is the main one.
