Pizzeria NO.900 is Quebec City's Neapolitan pizza chain, and the Limoilou branch on 3e Avenue is the vegan-friendly one to know. Vegan cheese is on offer, which means any vegetarian pizza on the menu can be veganized, from the margherita to the mushroom.

It is a reliable, cheerful dinner stop after a day in the Old Town. Order a vegan margherita, add a mushroom pizza to share, and finish with whatever is vegan on the dessert menu.
