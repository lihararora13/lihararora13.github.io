Pure Kitchen is an all-vegan kitchen on West Kennedy Boulevard, doing bowls, sandwiches, and plates with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. The bowls are the headline order, and the room is casual and counter-service.

Order the vegan bowl and a sandwich. It is a good stop for a quick lunch on the Westshore corridor.
