Green Cuisine is Victoria's vegan institution: an all-vegan, pay-by-weight buffet in Market Square on Johnson Street, with a hot buffet, a salad bar, house-made tempeh, pad thai and cornbread. The full buffet is out by 11:30am, and the room is bright and casual, with gluten-free options across the menu. It is the lunch pick in this guide, open Monday to Saturday 10am to 6pm and Sunday 11am to 5pm.

Order a hot buffet plate and weigh it at the till: the pay-by-weight model rewards a sensible plate. Go before 11:30am for the full spread.
