Der Dicke Schmidt is Dresden's vegan doner institution, a 100% vegan shop where you build your own plate from three mock meats: seitan kebab cooked on a proper rotating spit, soy gyros, and pea-protein chicken. Pick a base, pita, wrap or baked potato, then add toppings like herb sauce, tzatziki, hummus, fried onions, olives and vegan feta. Portions are famously large, prices are kind, and the room is canteen-simple, built for a big snack rather than a long sit.

The live shop is the Neustadt branch on Rothenburger Strasse, open daily from late morning to half past nine. The old branch on Rudolf-Leonhard-Strasse is permanently closed, so do not go there. Order the seitan kebab if you can only pick one, and save room for a cinnamon roll from the sweet counter in the same premises.
