Haltestulle is a neighbourhood cafe and cooperative shop on Jungststrasse, open daily, serving flammkuchen, antipasti, quiche, torte and croissants with organic and regional ingredients and good coffee and cake. It is the community cafe pick on this list.

Order the flammkuchen, the antipasti and a torte, and ask which cakes are vegan, since the menu also runs dairy and egg.
