Level Up is one of the strongest vegan comfort-food picks in central Bucharest, with burgers, pizza with plant-based mozzarella, nuggets, fries, vegan wings and desserts, plus veganised versions of Romanian dishes such as sarmale and mici. It holds a 5.0 HappyCow rating and sells local vegan cheeses as well.

It is open Monday to Friday and Sunday from noon to 10pm. Come for a burger and a pizza, and expect fast-food prices rather than fine dining.
