Anastasia is the cafe Tel Aviv vegans grew up with, and it still tops HappyCow's city list with a perfect 5.0 from nearly 200 reviewers. It is a normal, buzzy all-day cafe that happens to be entirely plant-based, which makes it the easiest place in the city to bring non-vegan friends without a negotiation.

Order the vegan Israeli breakfast, the dish regulars say justifies the trip, or a shakshuka with house-made milks in your coffee and a slice of cake to finish. The little shop at the front sells packaged vegan goods, and the patio welcomes dogs, so linger.
