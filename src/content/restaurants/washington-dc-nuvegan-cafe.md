Why we love it — NuVegan is the soul of DC veganism: a Black-owned, family-run
cafe turning out cafeteria-style classics like fried chick'n, lasagna, and
mac and cheese that people drive across the city for. The hot bar changes
daily, so regulars never get bored.

This is comfort food with a mission, and it has been DC's vegan living room
for years. Go hungry, sample before you commit, and save room for one of the
giant cinnamon rolls.
