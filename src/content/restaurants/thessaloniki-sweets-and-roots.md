Sweets & Roots is the small bakery in the centre specialising in 100% vegan pastries, with tarts, cookies and cakes without dairy or eggs. The vegan lemon tart is the must-try, and the viennoiseries run from one and a half to three euros, with cakes from three to six.

It is open Tuesday to Sunday from 9am to 6pm and closed Monday. Come for an afternoon sweet break rather than a meal.
