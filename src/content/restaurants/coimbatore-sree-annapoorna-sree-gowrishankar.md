Annapoorna is the first name on every Coimbatore vegetarian list, and the RS Puram branch is the flagship. The banana-leaf meals and filter coffee are the city's emotional favourites, and the tiffin rooms have been feeding Kovai for generations. It is pure veg, not vegan, but the kitchen is used to vegans asking for the ghee to be held.

Order the banana-leaf meal without curd, a masala dosa without ghee, and the filter coffee with a plant milk if they have it. This is the essential Coimbatore meal, and skipping it would be a mistake.
