The Lakeside Veggie sits beside Longhua Temple, and it feels like it: warm wood, live plants, and a calm that is rare in Shanghai. The kitchen does relaxed family-style vegetarian cooking with plenty of vegan dishes, and the dim sum is the headline.

Pair it with a temple visit for a perfect half day. Order the dim sum and whatever vegetables are in season.
