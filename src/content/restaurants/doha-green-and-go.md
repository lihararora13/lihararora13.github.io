Green and Go is the practical pick: a fully vegan grab-and-go counter at DECC Metro Station in West Bay, doing pre-packaged sandwiches, salads, bowls and juices since 2018. It is inexpensive, quick and open daily, which makes it the easiest vegan lunch in the business district.

Grab a sandwich and a juice on the way to a meeting, or a bowl for a light dinner. It is not a destination restaurant, and it does not need to be.
