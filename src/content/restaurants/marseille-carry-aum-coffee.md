Carry Aum Coffee is the quiet gem of this list: a fully vegan café in the 8th arrondissement serving seasonal plant-based meals, specialty drinks and baked goods, from bowls and savoury tarts to salads and cakes. It is the kind of place you find once and then build your day around.

Come for a slow lunch or an afternoon coffee and cake. Order the bowl of the day, add a tart, and enjoy the rare pleasure of a café where everything on the menu is yours.
