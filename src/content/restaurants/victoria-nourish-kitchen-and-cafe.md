Nourish Kitchen & Cafe is Victoria's heritage-home brunch: a cafe in an 1888 house on Quebec Street in James Bay, serving brunch daily 9am to 1:45pm and dinner Wednesday to Saturday, with a Golden Benny with tempeh, Sleeping Beauty Pancakes, a grain bowl and squash risotto. It is the prettiest room in this guide, with a garden and a fireplace, and it is the place to book for a slow brunch. Reservations are recommended at weekends.

Order the Golden Benny and the pancakes. The vegan dishes are marked, so ordering is straightforward, and the house is worth a look in itself.
