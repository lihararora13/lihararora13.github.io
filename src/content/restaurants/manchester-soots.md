Soots is the pasta place that does not shout about being vegan, which is exactly why it works for bringing friends who think they do not like vegan food. The menu is marked 100% plant based, everything is made in-house from seasonal ingredients, and the room is a tiny independent bistro tucked away on Tib Street. Reviewers call the carrot basque cheesecake a clever little pud and the olive oil ice cream a must, and the truffle ravioli and the rigatoni get named in almost every write-up.

It opens Tuesday to Thursday from 5pm and Friday to Sunday from noon, closed on Mondays, and it is small enough that booking ahead is wise. Order the focaccia for the table, the truffle ravioli, the rigatoni, and finish with the olive oil ice cream. Three plates and a pudding is plenty, and the whole thing reads like a proper Italian evening that just happens to be entirely plants.
