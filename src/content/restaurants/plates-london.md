Why we love it — This is the most important vegan restaurant in the
United Kingdom, and it is not close. Chef Kirk Haworth, a classical
Michelin-trained chef who rebuilt his cooking around plants after a
battle with Lyme disease, earned the country's first Michelin star
for a fully vegan restaurant. The tasting menus are nature-led,
seasonal, and ambitious in the way the best fine dining is, with
mushrooms doing things mushrooms have no business doing.

Book far ahead; tables are famously hard to come by, which is exactly
what happens when 95 percent of your diners are meat-eaters who keep
coming back for the flavour. Save it for a special occasion, and
expect it to recalibrate what you think vegetables can do.
