Thrive is Cambridge's favourite vegan cafe: a down-to-earth, all-vegan bistro and events space at the top of Norfolk Street, up a few steps into a cosy room with fairy lights, blankets over sofas and art for sale on the walls. It runs acoustic music, exhibitions, stand-up and yoga alongside the food, and there is a terrace for the warmer months.

The Big Breakfast is the brunch order, with toasties, scrambled tofu and a plant-based BLT through the day, and the cake display, carrot cake to tiffin, is the reason to stay for coffee. It is fully vegan and great value, and it is the first stop on any Cambridge vegan weekend.
