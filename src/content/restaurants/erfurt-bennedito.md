Bennedito is a family-friendly cafe in the Krampfervorstadt, east of the old town, where a weekly chef cooks seasonal, regional dishes from Wednesday to Friday, with soups, salads, fresh bread and a Friday flammkuchen trio. The vegan options run to avocado toast, a granola bowl, and rotating vegan baked goods such as tarte flambee and banana bread, and the room is built for lingering, with play space for children and a calm, bright feel.

Hours are limited: Wednesday to Friday from half past eleven, Saturday from half past nine, closed Sunday to Tuesday and on public holidays. Check before you go. Order the avocado toast and a slice of whatever vegan cake is on, and treat it as a slow brunch rather than a quick stop.
