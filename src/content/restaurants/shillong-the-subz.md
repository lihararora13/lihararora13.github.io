The Subz is a pure-veg restaurant on the second floor of Anderson Mansion on Jail Road, above the HDFC Bank, and it appears in the November 2026 vegetarian guides to Meghalaya as one of Shillong's three core pure-veg spots. It is open 8 in the morning to 10 at night, which makes it the early-breakfast pick in Police Bazar.

Order the veg thali and ask for no ghee and no paneer. It is a simple, central room, and a good second stop after Jiva Veg.
