Himalayan Organics Cafe on Manu Temple Road, opposite Shiva Garden Bakery, is the organic corner of Old Manali: a cafe and homestay doing plant-based bowls, salads and herbal teas, open 9 AM to 11:30 PM.

Order the organic salad bowl and a herbal tea. It is a calm, green table, and a good one for a slow morning on the lane.
