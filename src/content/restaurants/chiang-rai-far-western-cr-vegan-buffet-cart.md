Far Western CR Vegan Buffet Cart, also listed as Tian Fu, is a vegan buffet cart on Ratyotha Road, just before Madang Korean restaurant, parked in a driveway with two tables of covered outdoor seating. It is a cart rather than a restaurant, and the food is Thai and Korean-influenced, served buffet style.

Order from the cart and eat at the covered tables, mixing the vegetable curries with rice. It is inexpensive and central, and it suits a quick, casual lunch.
