Alfacinha is a vegetarian restaurant on Rua Dom Goncalo Pereira, a quiet street off the centre, serving Portuguese vegetarian cooking with vegan options across the menu. It is the neighbourhood pick on this list.

Order the vegetarian daily plate, a soup and the vegan options of the day, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan dishes, since the menu also runs dairy and egg.
