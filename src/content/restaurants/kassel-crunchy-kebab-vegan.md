Crunchy Kebab Vegan does exactly what the name promises: a kebab shop where everything on the menu is plant-based, from the crunchy seitan kebab that gives the place its name to falafel kebabs, rice plates and vegan skewers with salad. It is fast, cheap, open every day until ten, and it has become the default late lunch in the Vorderer Westen.

It sits on Friedrich-Ebert-Straße, the main drag of the student quarter, surrounded by the rest of Kassel's vegan cluster. Order the crunchy kebab with all the sauces, add fries, and you have the city's best-value vegan meal.
