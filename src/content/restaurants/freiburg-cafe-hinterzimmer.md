Café Hinterzimmer, the back room, is one of Freiburg's two fully vegan spots, a small café on the Grünwälderstraße doing cakes, sandwiches and coffee, all of it plant-based. It is a daytime place, open Monday to Saturday until six, and it works best as a pause in an old-town walk: a slice of cake, an oat cappuccino, and a quiet corner away from the Münsterplatz crowds.

It is the kind of café that does not shout about itself, which is part of the appeal. Order the cake of the day and a cappuccino, and keep it in mind as the reliable all-vegan stop in the city centre.
