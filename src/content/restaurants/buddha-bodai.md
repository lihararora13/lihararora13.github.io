Buddha Bodai has been doing this since 2004, which in New York vegan years
makes it basically a founding father. The steaming baskets of vegan soup
dumplings, shumai, wu gok taro dumplings, and mock char siu bao are the kind
of thing travelers plan entire trips around. It is fully vegetarian,
kosher, and the prices are still gloriously Chinatown.

Service can be brusque and the room is no-frills, but nobody comes here for
cushions. Come before 4 PM for the full dim sum spread, bring cash for a
small discount, and sip the free hot tea while the baskets pile up.
