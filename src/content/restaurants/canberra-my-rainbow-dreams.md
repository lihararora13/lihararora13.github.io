My Rainbow Dreams is the kind of cafe that makes you feel better just by walking in: bright, peaceful and homely, tucked away in Dickson with indoor and outdoor seating and a sunny kids' corner. The food is wholesome and thoughtful, with a daily changing menu of curries, bakes, salads and burgers, and the tempeh-avocado salad and rainbow wrap are the plates regulars come back for. Prices are gentle and the coffee is genuinely good.

It is a vegetarian cafe with plenty of vegan plates, so ask the friendly staff what is vegan that day and they will happily point you to the best of it. Sit in the sun, order a wrap and a coffee, and let the afternoon slow down.
