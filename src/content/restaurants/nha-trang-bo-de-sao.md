Bo De Sao, also listed as Am Thuc Thuan Chay Bo De Sao, is a small, popular local pure-vegan restaurant on Huynh Thuc Khang, with a peaceful vibe, hot pot, spring rolls and rice sets. It describes itself as serving purely vegan food, and it is open daily from 6:30am to 8pm.

Order the pho chay and the goi cuon chay, and try a rice set. It is inexpensive, local and central, and it is the best pure-vegan Vietnamese meal in Nha Trang.
