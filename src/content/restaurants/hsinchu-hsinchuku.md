Hsinchuku is a plant-based bistro and bar in a Japanese-era police dormitory near the back exit of Hsinchu TRA Station, in the East District. The Taiwanese vegetarian guide gives it two stars and says it uses no animal products, and the room pairs Asian small plates with cocktails in a historic building. It is the most atmospheric vegan table in Hsinchu and the natural first dinner stop.

Order a spread of small plates to share and a cocktail, and come in the evening, when the bar side comes alive. Hours differ between sources, so check before you go.
