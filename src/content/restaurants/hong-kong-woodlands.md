Woodlands has been feeding Tsim Sha Tsui since 1981, which in Hong Kong restaurant years makes it practically a monument. It bills itself as the city's first Indian vegetarian restaurant, third-generation regulars still fill the tables, and the menu runs to more than 130 dishes with a dedicated vegan section and Jain preparations.

This is a vegetarian kitchen, so we label it vegan-friendly, but vegans eat very well here: crisp dosas made to order by a dosa specialist, generous thalis, fried idly and tangy chaat, all at prices that feel like a time capsule. Book ahead at peak times; the queue knows.
