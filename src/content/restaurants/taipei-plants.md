Plants is the calm counterpoint to Taipei's buzz: a little lane-side room in Da'an where everything is vegan, gluten-free and built from whole foods, with shelves of good things to take home. It has been quietly excellent since 2016, and the raw cakes in the cabinet have a loyal following.

Come for a macrobiotic bowl or bibimbap, a kale salad that actually excites, and a smoothie made with house activated mylk. Save space for dessert, the raw cheesecakes and macaroons are the point, not an afterthought. Note it closes on Mondays.
