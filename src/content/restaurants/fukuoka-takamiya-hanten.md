Takamiya Hanten is Fukuoka's vegan Chinese option: a small restaurant in Takamiya doing plant-based Chinese cooking in the evenings. It is a useful dinner stop in a city where most vegan kitchens close by early evening.

Check the days before you go, it opens select weekday evenings. Order the mapo tofu and dumplings.
