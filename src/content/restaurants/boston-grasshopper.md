Grasshopper is one of Boston's original veggie restaurants, and it has survived every food trend by simply being excellent. Now parked inside the Super 88 food court in Allston, it serves vegetarian and vegan pan-Asian food with a genius hand for mock meats, drawing regulars from across the state who plan weekends around a visit. The hot and sour soup alone has a fan club.

The menu mixes Chinese, Vietnamese, and Japanese influences, and the monthly Third Sunday buffet is the stuff of local legend. Order the "No Name," fried gluten balls in a sweet-tangy sauce that converts skeptics on contact. It is humble, it is cheap, and it has been doing this longer than most Boston vegans have been vegan.
