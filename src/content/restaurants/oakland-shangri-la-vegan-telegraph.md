Shangri-La Vegan on Telegraph is the Temescal branch of the organic macrobiotic chain, and it is the calmest meal on the list: a fixed-price plate, a daily soup, raw pies in the dessert case, and a serene little room with meditative music. The food is macrobiotic, which means whole grains, vegetables and sea vegetables cooked simply and well.

It is the pick for the friend who wants to feel good after lunch rather than full. Order the full plate, ask what is in the dessert case, and enjoy the quiet.
