Brunchology is a brunch cafe and matcha bar on Rua Joao Tavira in the old town, open every day from nine to six, and it is the easiest vegan-friendly breakfast in Funchal. The vegan options are clearly marked rather than improvised: pancakes, smoothie bowls, a veggie bowl, vegan udon and a vegan shawarma, with plant milks for the coffee.

Order the vegan pancakes, the red smoothie bowl, the vegan udon or the vegan shawarma, and a matcha with oat milk. It is a daytime-only spot, so come for breakfast or a late lunch rather than dinner.
