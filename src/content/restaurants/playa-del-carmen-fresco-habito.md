Fresco Hábito is the vegan breakfast café near the beach in Playa, on Avenida 1 close to the sand, so it fits neatly before a swim. My Vegan Travels recommends the bomblette, an omelette made from chickpea and mung bean, and the smoothie bowls, avocado toast, overnight oats and build-your-own buddha bowls are the other orders.

It is open every day from 8:30am to 5:30pm, Sundays included. If you spot honey on the English menu, staff told My Vegan Travels everything is vegan, but ask if you are unsure.
