Blackbird Pizzeria is the first place one local vegan professional takes every visiting friend, and with good reason: it is an all-vegan pizzeria where everyone finds something, from the cheesesteak pizza to the kale salad. The haymaker pie and seitan wings are the must-orders.

It is the kind of spot that quietly anchors a neighborhood: affordable, unfussy, and better than pizza this creative has any right to be. If you only eat one slice in Philly, make it here.
