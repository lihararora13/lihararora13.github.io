Veganes Gold is Stuttgart's fully vegan fine-dining room, on Schloßstraße in the West, and it is the special-occasion table on this page. The cooking is vegetable-led and precise: roasted romanesco, stuffed sweet potato in puff pastry, avocado toast and tofu salad, plated like a tasting menu.

It is the priciest pick in the city, so save it for the evening you want to dress up for. Book ahead, and order the romanesco.
