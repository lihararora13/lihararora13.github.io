Erdapfel is an organic bistro on the Ochsengasse, in the western old town, doing seasonal lunch plates, soups and cakes with a vegetarian and vegan range. It keeps weekday hours, nine to three with a shorter Saturday service, which makes it a lunch stop rather than a dinner table, and the cooking is the kind of careful, organic food that Ulm does well. It is a quiet room, and a good one for a slow midday break.

Order the seasonal lunch plate and a soup, add a slice of vegan cake, and treat it as the organic counterweight to the tavern food elsewhere on this list.
