Suxin Vegan is the buffet stop on this list: a vegan buffet restaurant on Wenyi Road, on the second floor above its vegan bakery, offering a huge variety including dumplings, dishes with mushrooms and vegetables, plus ingredients to make your own soup. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow from 9 reviews, and reviewers call it a must-try for the variety and the price, around 39 yuan a head.

Go upstairs on the right-hand side of the vegan bakery. The buffet can be semi-warm and the room is plain, but the dumplings, eggplant and potato dish and lotus root are the picks, and there is a second branch at West Lake Culture Square. Open noon to 2pm daily and 5:30pm to 8pm Tuesday to Sunday.
