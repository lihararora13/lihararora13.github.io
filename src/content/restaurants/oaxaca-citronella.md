Citronella is Oaxaca's all-vegan breakfast darling, a courtyard restaurant flooded with natural light where the plant milks are made on site and the vegan ceviche is the dish to order. Salads, sandwiches, smoothies and sweet and savoury breakfast plates, all light, fresh and beautifully presented.

Order the vegan ceviche and a smoothie. It is the prettiest breakfast on this list, and the calmest way to start a day in Oaxaca.
