Pois Chiche is the chickpea's moment in the sun: a plant-based Lebanese restaurant on the Kapellemarkt, where the falafel is made with Belgian-grown chickpeas and the menu runs on hummus, vegan mezze, cauliflower shawarma and smoked aubergine. Almost all main dishes are vegan.

It is a counter-service Levantine food bar with a sunny terrace by the church. Order the falafel plate with four seasonal salads, add the hummus, and try the cauliflower shawarma.
