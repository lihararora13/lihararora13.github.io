Tinh Garden Vegetarian, also called Nha Hang Chay Tinh, is a vegetarian restaurant near Tu Dam Pagoda, on Phan Boi Chau, known for refined presentation and a calm ambiance, with a royal-inspired vegetarian dining experience that reflects Hue's traditional culinary elegance.

Order the royal-inspired dishes and the vegetable plates, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is a good choice for a special, quiet dinner.
