THE TEROTALE is Nashik's dress-up dinner: a two-floor rooftop restaurant opposite City Centre Mall in Untwadi, with lush indoor planting, live music and a 4.7 star rating. It is the most polished room on this list, and the kitchen's Jain menu shows it takes dietary requests seriously.

The Continental side is the vegan play, with veg sushi, hakka noodles and truffle fries, all easy to order without dairy. Note the pin is approximate to the Untwadi and City Centre Mall area, so navigate by the address rather than the map dot. One listing also describes the kitchen as serving non-veg, so confirm the veg menu when you order.
