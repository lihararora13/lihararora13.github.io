China House is a Chinese restaurant in the Green Plaza mall with one of the longest vegan lists in Alexandria: vegetable spring rolls, fried eggplant with sauce, lentil with curry, vegetable rice noodles, home-style tofu with vegetables and ma-po tofu.

Order the ma-po tofu and vegetable rice noodles, and ask about butter in any dish before ordering. It is open daily and has outdoor seating.
