Greenhouse Canteen tops the list for a reason. One of the very best vegan restaurants on the Gold Coast: an entirely gluten-free garden canteen doing Vietnamese pancakes and mushroom cheeseburgers. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Tallebudgera Valley, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vietnamese pancakes, Cauliflower wings, Mushroom cheeseburger if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
