Taste The Divine is a fully vegan cloud kitchen on Boulevard Emile Zola, one of only two fully vegan restaurants in Casablanca. It is known for high-protein, oil-free burgers, and for a relaxed order-in setup where you do not have to check and double-check every ingredient.

Order a burger and a bowl. The listing is a cloud kitchen, so expect takeaway and delivery rather than a full dining room.
