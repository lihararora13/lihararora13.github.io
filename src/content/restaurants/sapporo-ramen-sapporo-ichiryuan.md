Ichiryuan is the second confirmed vegan miso ramen in Sapporo, in the basement of the Hokuren Building a short walk from the station. The vegan ramen uses animal-free noodles, soup and toppings, but only by advance reservation.

Book ahead and confirm when you reserve. It is the station-area ramen fix for vegans catching a train.
