Fat Annie's is the vegan stall in Kirkgate Market, Leeds' great historic market hall, and it is the city's best-value vegan lunch: generous plant-based plates served in the food court, with discounts for students and NHS workers. It sits alongside Manjit's Kitchen's old pitch and the rest of the market's food hall, which makes it the perfect stop on a market wander.

Order the plate of the day, eat it in the hall, and then go and explore the market. Leeds' vegan scene, at market prices.
