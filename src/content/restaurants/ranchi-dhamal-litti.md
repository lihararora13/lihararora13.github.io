Dhamal Litti is a Main Road stall doing one thing: litti chokha, the roasted sattu-stuffed wheat balls with mashed brinjal and tomato that is the most naturally vegan dish in the region. It is open 8 to 10 daily, and it is the cheapest, most authentic plant-based meal on this list.

Order the litti chokha and make sure the litti is roasted without ghee brushed on top. It is street food, eaten standing or on the go, and it is the perfect end to a Ranchi veg crawl.
