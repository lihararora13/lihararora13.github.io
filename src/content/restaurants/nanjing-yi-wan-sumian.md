Yi Wan Sumian is the noodle shop of Qixia Ancient Town: a vegetarian restaurant at Building 82-2, near the west entrance of Qixia Mountain, offering vegan-friendly noodles with mushrooms and vegetables and rice set meals, with mock meats available. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow, and a September 2026 review praises the flavourful broth, the mushroom noodle soup and the dumplings.

Order the mushroom noodle soup with the chilli oil and vinegar on the table, or the single-person set meal of mushroom noodles and dried bean curd for 21 yuan. It is a serene, old-Chinese-style room with outdoor seating, a good stop after the mountain. Cash only. Open daily 7:30am to 8:30pm.
