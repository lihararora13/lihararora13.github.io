Falafelove is Warsaw's falafel favourite, a vegetarian spot on Senatorska by the Old Town with plenty of vegan options. The falafel is crisp, the wraps are generous, and the salads and dips round out a quick, cheap lunch.

It is the Old Town pit stop on this list. Order a falafel wrap and a salad, and you are fuelled for the Royal Castle and the old streets.
