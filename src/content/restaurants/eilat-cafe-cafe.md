Cafe Cafe sits on the ground floor of the Dan Eilat hotel on the promenade, a cafe with pizza, pasta, breakfasts and vegan-friendly dishes. HappyCow shows it open with a 4.0 rating.

Order a vegan pizza or pasta. It is a hotel cafe, so it is open early and late, and it is a good fallback when the promenade rooms are full.
