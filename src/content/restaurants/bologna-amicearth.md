AmicEarth is a wholefood shop and kitchen on Via Andrea Costa, built around cold-pressed oils, stone-ground creams and raw, organic, wholefood cooking. The kitchen serves tortelli, strozzapreti with pumpkin seed protein and a saffron rice pudding, alongside oils and butters to take home.

It is a daytime spot with shop hours, so come for lunch or to stock up rather than a late dinner. Confirm the vegan dishes when you order, as the menu is vegetarian-leaning.
