The Yellow Chill is Lagos' cheerful vegan cafe, with a Victoria Island branch and another in Ikeja GRA. The vibe is bright and lively, the menu runs on bowls, wraps and smoothies, and it is the easiest place on this list to drop into without a plan.

Order a Buddha bowl and a smoothie, and use it as the casual lunch between meetings or beach time. The Ikeja branch is handy if you are staying on the mainland.
