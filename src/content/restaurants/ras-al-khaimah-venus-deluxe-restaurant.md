Venus Deluxe is a South Indian vegetarian restaurant behind Al Safeer Market, rated 5.0, serving paper dosa, masala dosa and idli. It is open daily in two shifts, morning and evening.

Order a paper dosa and specify vegan. It is a short walk from Dosa Kada, so the two together cover the old town's dosa needs.
