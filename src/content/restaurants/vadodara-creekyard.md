Creekyard is Sevasi's other pure-veg star, a spacious café with a waterside calm that makes it a favourite for long lunches and family dinners. The menu runs long across Continental and multicuisine, and the veg section is where the kitchen is most comfortable.

Ask for the dairy held and the pastas and grilled vegetables become properly vegan. The pin is approximate to the Sevasi area, so navigate by the address, opposite Anjana Hospital in Bhayli.
