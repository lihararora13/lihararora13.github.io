Ping An Veggie Time made the list for a reason. Adelaide's first 100% vegan Thai restaurant, doing pad Thai, green curry and spring rolls with a hearty appetite in mind. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Brompton, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan pad Thai, Green curry, Spring rolls if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
