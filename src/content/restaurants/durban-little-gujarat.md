Little Gujarat is a Durban classic: a no-frills vegetarian Indian canteen in the city centre where the food is cheap, fast and deeply satisfying. The curries run on beans, lentils and mixed vegetables, served with rotis, and the sweets counter is a local landmark. Ask about dairy-free options and the staff will steer you right.

This is the place to eat like a local for the price of a coffee. Go at lunchtime when the curries are freshest, and keep it simple: a bean curry, a roti, and something sweet to finish.
