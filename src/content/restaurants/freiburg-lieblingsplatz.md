Lieblingsplatz is the restaurant HappyCow reviewers know as Beb & Bene, the highest-rated vegan-friendly table in Freiburg, now trading under its new name at the same Konradstraße address with the same owners in the kitchen. The cooking is French and fully organic: galettes and crêpes, salads and cakes, coffee, juice and kombucha, with vegan dishes across the menu and a catering arm that serves the whole Freiburg area. It is a bistro in the old sense, small, seasonal, and run by the people who cook the food.

It sits in the Wiehre, the leafy district south of the old town, and keeps lunch and dinner hours with longer evenings on Fridays and Saturdays. Order a champignon galette, save room for a vegan dessert crêpe with apple sauce, and book ahead if you are coming on a weekend.
