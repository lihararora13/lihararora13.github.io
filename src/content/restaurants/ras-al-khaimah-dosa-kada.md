Dosa Kada is a vegetarian South Indian restaurant in Ras Al Khaimah, open daily in two shifts, morning and evening, serving dosa and idli. It is a straightforward, cheap, local room.

Order a masala dosa and specify no ghee or butter. It is the best dosa in the old town, and the split hours mean it closes in the early afternoon.
