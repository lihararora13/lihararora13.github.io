Justveggiez is Madison's 100% vegan comfort-food restaurant, a Black-owned, family-run spot right on State Street serving the dishes Wisconsin is famous for, rebuilt plant-based: vegan cheese curds with chipotle mayo, deep-dish pizza, a mushroom swiss burger, and a barbecue sandwich that has won over non-vegans.

It is open six days a week and also runs a catering business. The cheese curds are the order, and the deep-dish pizza is the second.
