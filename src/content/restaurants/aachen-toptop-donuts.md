TopTop Donuts is Aachen's donut shop on Hartmannstraße, a vegetarian bakery with a long range of vegan donuts and a coffee counter to match. The case changes with the seasons, and most of the donuts are vegan, which makes it the sweet stop on this list and the easiest one to share with a group.

It is a mixed vegetarian menu, so ask which donuts are vegan before ordering. Open Tuesday to Thursday until eight and Friday to Sunday until nine, with Mondays closed, it is a good afternoon break between the Dom and the Pontstraße.
