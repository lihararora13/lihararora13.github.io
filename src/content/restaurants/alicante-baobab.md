Baobab Soul Kitchen & Bar is a vegetarian and vegan fusion restaurant on Avenida Santander, in Playa de San Juan north of the old town, run by three friends from Buenos Aires. The menu borrows from all over, pad Thai, Thai curry, pakora, baba ganoush and pumpkin cannelloni, with plenty of vegan choices, and the room is full of plants.

Order the pad thai, which regulars rate highest, then the vegan ceviche and baba ganoush. Reservations are recommended, and the restaurant is a bus or tram ride from the old town, not a walk.
