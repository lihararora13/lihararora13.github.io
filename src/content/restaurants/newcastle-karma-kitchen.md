Karma Kitchen is a family-run vegan Indian kitchen on the Bigg Market, cooking plant-based twists on North Indian curries with fresh, clean flavours and generous portions. Most of the menu is gluten free as well as vegan, and the same kitchen ships frozen boxes nationwide.

The tofu tikka masala and the seitan madras are the standouts, with a peshwari naan on the side. It is an evening spot, open from 5pm, so plan dinner rather than lunch.
