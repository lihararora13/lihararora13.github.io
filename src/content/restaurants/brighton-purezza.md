Purezza holds a lovely distinction: it was the UK's first fully vegan Italian restaurant when it opened in Kemp Town in 2015, and it is still one of the best. The menu runs from sourdough pizzas and calzones to pastas, lasagna, burgers and raw entrees, with a large drinks list including cocktails, and many dishes available gluten-free.

It is the most booked table on this list, so reserve ahead for evenings. Order a sourdough pizza, save room for the tiramisu, and enjoy the restaurant that proved vegan Italian food could be the real thing.
