Kunda Vegan Vegetarian is a mostly plant-based cafe on Trairat Road, the home of the Kunda vegan patty and Kunda burger since 2011, run by a plant-based chef of more than 21 years. The menu covers burgers, salads, raw dishes and baked goods, with no tofu, no Wi-Fi and no phone orders, and it is an 18-plus eatery. It is expensive by Chiang Rai standards, and it is the most Western table in the city.

Order the Kunda burger and a salad, and try the baked goods. It is a good change from the jay buffets, and it suits a longer, slower lunch.
