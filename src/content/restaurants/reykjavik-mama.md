Mama Reykjavik is the creation of Chef Maria Ingibjorg, a plant-based restaurant that brings global cooking to Reykjavik with a distinctly Icelandic twist. The menu runs from big soups and sourdough to grain bowls, curries, smoothies and raw-style desserts including vegan cheesecake, and the room is warm, communal and welcoming.

Dhal a la Mama is the dish everyone names, red lentils with coconut milk, vegetables and spices, served with wood-fired garlic naan. Order it, add the Mama Curry, and book ahead for dinner in summer.
