Graze is the East Nashville hangout every local guide names first: a plant-based bistro with a full bar, opened in 2016 by the team behind the much-missed Wild Cow, and still packing in brunch crowds seven days a week. City Cast loves the faux-meat breakfast biscuits and pancakes, and HappyCow reviewers keep coming back for thirds.

The menu is pure comfort, Szechuan Brussels, loaded potatoes, patty melts and brownie sundaes, with plenty of gluten-free options and a kids menu too. Brunch until 3, dinner all day, happy hour on weekdays: Graze has the whole day covered.
