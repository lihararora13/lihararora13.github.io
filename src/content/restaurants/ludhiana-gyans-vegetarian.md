Gyan's Vegetarian on Mall Road in Civil Lines is a well-rated pure-veg restaurant, open 11 AM to 11 PM, doing North Indian plates with consistency. It is the reliable mid-range table on this list.

The dal tadka and chana masala are the vegan-friendly orders. Ask for no ghee and no cream, and the kitchen will take care of the rest.
