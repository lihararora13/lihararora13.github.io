Saido is the meal Tokyo vegans tell each other about in a slightly reverent tone. In leafy Jiyugaoka, this small dining room takes familiar Japanese dishes and rebuilds them entirely from vegetables and mushrooms, so you get the crunch of katsu, the gloss of eel, and the comfort of a proper set meal without anything missing.

Book ahead, because walk-ins are not the move here, and let the seasonal set do the choosing for you. It is thoughtful, pretty, and quietly playful, the kind of place that reminds you vegan food in Japan has deep roots, not just good substitutes.
