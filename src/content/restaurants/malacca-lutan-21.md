Lutan 21 is the quieter sibling of the Veggie Planet family, on the same street as Botanist Cafe but with a different philosophy: no mock meat, no MSG, no preservatives, just herbal soups, mushrooms and unprocessed proteins in a modern-rustic room with warm wood and soft lighting. The veggie bak kut teh in a light herbal broth with quinoa brown rice, the Nyonya curry laksa, the lei cha and the yam spring roll are the dishes reviewers name, and the owner is known for coming over to reassure vegan diners about what is safe to eat.

It is vegetarian with plenty of vegan dishes, not fully vegan, so confirm each dish. It is open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday to Sunday, 11am to 9:30pm, closed Wednesdays. The name has caused confusion in older guides, which describe it as previously named Veggie Planet; it is a separate, affiliated restaurant, not Botanist under an old name.
