Veganerie started as a bakery dream and grew into a Bangkok institution, and the Concept flagship behind Benjasiri Park is the fullest expression of it: a big, bright dining room, a huge menu, and desserts that make choosing genuinely difficult.

Go brunch-big with waffles and pancakes, or go savoury with burgers, larb and noodles, then circle back for ice cream. It is 100 percent vegan, family-run in spirit, and the kind of place you plan a second visit to before you have finished the first.
