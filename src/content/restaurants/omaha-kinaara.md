Kinaara is an Indian restaurant on P Street in Millard, far west Omaha, run by chef Ashish Sathyan and highlighted for its jackfruit biryani. The vegan bench is strong, with curries and dals that the kitchen prepares to order.

Order the jackfruit biryani and the chana masala. Note the distance: Millard is a drive from downtown, so plan it as a west-side dinner rather than a downtown stop.
