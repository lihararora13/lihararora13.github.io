My Vegan is Pasadena's vegan landmark: a longstanding, all-vegan Thai restaurant tucked into a strip mall on Arroyo Parkway, on historic Route 66, casually serving an extensive menu of mostly Thai food alongside veggie burgers, wraps and pasta. The pad Thai signature is the dish Visit Pasadena and the HappyCow reviewers both lead with, and the spicy ramen, orange vegan chicken and spicy hot kale bowl are the other regulars. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow under its duplicate listing, and 4.5 under the main one.

Hours are 11am to 3:30pm and 4:30pm to 9pm daily, with a mid-afternoon break. It is casual and quick-service, with parking in the plaza. Note that HappyCow lists it twice, as My Vegan and My Vegan Restaurant, at the same address: it is one restaurant.
