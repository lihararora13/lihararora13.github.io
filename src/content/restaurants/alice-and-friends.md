Alice & Friends' has been holding down Edgewater for decades, and it
shows in the kind of hospitality where the staff remembers you. The
menu is a broad, fully vegan tour of pan-Asian comfort food: tonkotsu
ramen that punches above its weight, the famous "crab" rangoons,
Bibimbap bowls, and a corn chowder that reviewers write love letters
about. There are soy-free, gluten-free, and oil-free lanes too.

It is the rare restaurant that works equally well for a cautious
first-timer and a vegan of twenty years. Warm, easy, and consistently
delicious.
