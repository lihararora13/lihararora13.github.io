The Daily Cafe made the list for a reason. A simple, fresh-ingredient cafe near Lake Market doing vegan salads, wraps, mezze platters and vegan coffee drinks. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Lake Market, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan salad, Mezze platter, Vegan wrap if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
