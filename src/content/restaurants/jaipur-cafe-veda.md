Café Veda made the list for a reason. An Ayurvedic rooftop cafe in a haveli with views of Amber Fort, Jal Mahal and the Aravallis, serving cold-pressed amla juice and sattvic plates. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Brahmpuri, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Amla juice, Millet khichdi, Herbal tea if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
