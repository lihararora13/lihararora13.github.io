Bistro Vonish is the food truck that dresses like a restaurant.
Fire pit, picnic tables, a seasonal menu that changes with the
whims of the chef, and vegan kolaches you will think about for
days. It is the move for a date night that happens to be outside.

The spicy brussels mac and cheese is the dish to judge them by,
and they pass with honors. Sweet potato arepas run a close
second. Sit by the fire, order dessert, stay a while.
