Bao An is the meal that converts people. Tucked down an alley off Tran Hung Dao with a little outdoor kitchen and plastic stools, this family stall does a handful of things and does them perfectly: crackly vegan banh mi, golden banh goi, fried spring rolls, steaming pho, and che, the sweet bean-and-coconut dessert that tastes like a hug. Everything is 100% vegan, no MSG, no preservatives, and it costs next to nothing.

Come early, bring cash, and expect a queue of locals and travellers who all found the same secret. It is the first bowl of pho many visitors eat in Vietnam, and it sets a bar the rest of the trip keeps chasing.
