Joy of Flora made the list for a reason. A fully vegan cafe, art gallery and event space next to West Croydon station, with a leafy secret garden and comfort home-style food. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in West Croydon, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Vegan burger, Smoothie bowl, Raw cake if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
