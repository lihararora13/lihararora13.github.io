Darshan Ooty is the simple vegetarian restaurant at Darshan Hotel on Woodcock Road, with multiple floors of seating and views over the hills and the lake. The guide calls it one of the simplest interiors and the best vegetarian food, with Asian and Indian cuisines and vegan options available, and avare sambhar as the local delicacy to try.

Order the avare sambhar and a veg thali, and ask for the dairy to be held. It is the lakeside lunch, and the views are the point.
