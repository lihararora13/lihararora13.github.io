Warung Ijo is a warm, homely Indonesian warung now at Grantral Mall by Tai Seng, cooking allium-free vegetarian food with a strongly vegan-friendly menu and sambal that means business. Most dishes can be made vegan, and regulars are vocal about the rendang.

Go for the mushroom rendang, build a nasi padang plate, or try the ayam penyet with its plant-based star and fiery chilli. Portions are generous, prices are gentle, and it pairs perfectly with a Boneless Kitchen visit on the same Tai Seng trip.
