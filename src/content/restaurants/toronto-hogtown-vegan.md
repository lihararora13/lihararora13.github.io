Why we love it — Hogtown is where veganism gets gloriously messy. This is Southern comfort food with the volume turned up: crispy un-chicken and waffles, a philly cheezesteak that converts in one bite, and the best vegan mac and cheez in the city, crowned with breadcrumbs. The patio is sunny, the tap list is local, and the portions are unapologetically huge.

Show up hungry and order the Southern combo like everyone tells you to. It is the cure for every bad day and every "but I could never give up cheese" conversation.
