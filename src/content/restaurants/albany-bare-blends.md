Bare Blends is a juice bar on Western Avenue, doing cold-pressed juices, smoothies, and bowls with a vegan bench. It is the morning pick on this list, and the best place for a light start.

Order a cold-pressed juice and a smoothie bowl. Ask the staff to confirm the bowl toppings are vegan, since some include honey or granola with dairy.
