The Lebanese Vegans Social Hub is a vegan cafe with a co-working area in Sin el Fil, run by the Lebanese Vegans community. The menu is unapologetically plant-based comfort: Beyond burgers, plant-based chicken, vegan kebbe, kofta and Philly cheesesteak, all vegan. It is inexpensive and open daily from noon.

Order the vegan kebbe for the Lebanese classic, or the Beyond burger for comfort. It is also the best place in the city to meet other vegans.
