Corum is Indore's dressed-up café, the one locals pick when the evening calls for somewhere a little smarter. The lounge is warm and unhurried, the menu wanders between continental, Asian and Indian, and the kitchen is happy to veganise dishes if you ask up front: no butter, no cheese, no cream, and the stir-fries, salads and noodle plates come through beautifully.

It is the priciest pick on this list, and it earns the splurge on atmosphere alone. Go for a long coffee, a shared spread of small plates, and the pleasure of eating well in a room that feels like a small occasion.
