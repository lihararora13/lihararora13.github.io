The Indian Coffee House on the Mall is the old-school table: white-shirted waiters, filter coffee, masala dosa and vegetable cutlets in a room that has barely changed in decades. It is a piece of living history, and the coffee is still the draw.

Order the masala dosa without ghee and the filter coffee. It is cheap, it is iconic, and it is the most atmospheric breakfast on this list.
