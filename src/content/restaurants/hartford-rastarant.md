RastaRant is an all-vegan Jamaican and Caribbean kitchen on Love Lane in Hartford's North End, doing jerk tofu plates, ital stew, rice and peas, and fresh juices. It is the second all-vegan anchor in a city where most guides count only one, and it is worth the trip north of downtown.

Order the jerk tofu plate with rice and peas and a fresh juice. Note the hours: Tuesday through Saturday, 11am to 5pm.
