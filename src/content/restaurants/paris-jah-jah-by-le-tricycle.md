Jah Jah started in 2014 as a vegan food bike slinging Afro-vegan and
Caribbean hot dogs, and grew into one of Paris's most beloved
plant-based restaurants. The room is colorful and relaxed, the reggae is
on, and everything on the plate is organic and gluten-free friendly.

The jerk tofu and curry lentils are the classics, but the plantain chips
and coconut rice steal the show for first-timers. A must-try for anyone
bored of the usual Paris vegan circuit.
