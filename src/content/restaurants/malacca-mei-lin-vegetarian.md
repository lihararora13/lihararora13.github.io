Mei Lin is one of the largest Chinese vegetarian restaurants in Melaka, a big, plain, family-friendly room in Taman Melaka Raya where the cooking is home-style: stir-fries, mock-meat rice sets, organic ramen and noodle soups, with plenty of vegetable and tofu dishes alongside the mock meats. Reviewers praise the vegetable curry, the tofu soup and the butter lion head mushroom, and a meal for two with four small dishes, rice and tea comes to around RM55, which is cheap by any standard.

It is a little way from Jonker Street, near Mahkota Parade, in a quiet neighbourhood, so it is a locals' room rather than a tourist stop. It breaks between lunch and dinner, 3pm to 6pm, and is closed on Wednesdays. Ask about egg in the mock-meat dishes if you eat strictly vegan.
