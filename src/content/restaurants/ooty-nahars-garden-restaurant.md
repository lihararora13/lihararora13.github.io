Nahar's Garden Restaurant is the Nahar group's garden restaurant on Commercial Road, named in the Ooty food guide alongside Darshan as the place to try Badaga cuisine. It is the sister room to the Sidewalk Cafe, and the one to pick when the pizza queue is too long and a Badaga curry sounds better.

Order the Badaga veg curry and a veg thali, and ask for the dairy to be held. It is the quiet eighth pick, and the garden setting is the point.
