Vegan In Our House is the answer to the question of where to eat fully vegan in Cairo. Tucked into the Pearl Hotel on Street 7 in Maadi, it serves more than 30 all-vegan dishes: lasagne, Beyond burgers, mushroom cheeseburgers, pastas, oriental plates, soups and salads. It is comforting, casual and open late every day.

Order the lasagne, which the guide singles out, then a burger and a big salad to share. This is the cosy fully-vegan dinner to plan a Maadi evening around.
