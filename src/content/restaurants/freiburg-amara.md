Amara is the Stühlinger's late-night answer to vegan eating: döner, Detroit-style pizza, falafel and vegan desserts, served until ten or midnight depending on the day. It is a casual counter with coffee specialities and kombucha on the side, and the vegan dishes are a proper part of the menu rather than a token line. For a group that cannot agree on one cuisine, it solves the argument.

It sits on the Engelbergerstraße, open seven days a week. Order a vegan döner and a slice of vegan pizza, and keep it in mind for the evenings when everywhere else in the old town has already closed.
