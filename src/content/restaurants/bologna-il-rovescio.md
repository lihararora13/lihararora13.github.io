Il Rovescio is a biodynamic osteria and organic pizzeria under the porticoes of Via Pietralata, building its menu around what local small-scale producers have that season. There are plenty of vegan options, clearly labelled, including the Pizza Terra, a vegan pizza on ancient-grain dough, plus melanzane antipasto, pappardelle with mushrooms and a pizza with red chicory cream and potatoes.

This is not a vegan restaurant: it serves meat, so order the labelled vegan dishes. The doughs are the story here, traditional, vegetable charcoal or hemp, leavened for 48 hours.
