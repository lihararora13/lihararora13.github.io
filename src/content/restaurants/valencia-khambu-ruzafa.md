Khambú Ruzafa is the newer, shinier sibling of the Quart original, opened in May 2025 and already holding a perfect HappyCow score. Same plant-based fast-food DNA, burgers, salads, sharing starters and desserts, in a brighter room right in the heart of Ruzafa.

Order a burger, a few starters for the table, and a dessert. If the Quart spot is busy, this is your backup, and honestly it is just as good.
