Friends Dhaba is the vegetarian dhaba that leads Ludhiana's food guides, and it earns the spot: a pure-veg Punjabi kitchen doing breakfast and lunch the way the city likes it, with kulchas, dal tadka and chana masala at the centre of the table.

Ask for the kulcha without ghee and the dal tadka as it comes. It is the most Ludhiana meal on this list, and the cheapest too.
