Pathans is the long-running pure-veg restaurant near the Round, open from 7 in the morning, and the one Thrissur locals name alongside Bharath when asked where to eat vegetarian. The menu covers South Indian tiffin and North Indian veg, and the online menu is still live and ordering.

Order the masala dosa and a North Indian veg curry, and ask for the dairy to be held. It is the old reliable of the Round, and it has earned the title.
