Minf Houze hides up on the third floor of Wisma Cosway, which makes finding it half the fun: a bright, friendly all-vegan cafe serving the greatest hits of Chinese-Malaysian and Malay cooking a short stroll from Pavilion and the Bukit Bintang buzz. With 266 HappyCow reviews at 4.5 stars, plenty of people have made the climb.

Mix and match across the menu: nasi lemak, a tangy assam laksa, mala ramen for the spice brave, and a Hakka leicha thunder tea rice for the virtuous. Cakes and lattes round it out, all plant-based, all fairly priced.
