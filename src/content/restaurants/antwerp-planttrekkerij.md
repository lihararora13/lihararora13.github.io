Planttrekkerij is a cosy vegan restaurant that opened in late 2023 in a corner building behind Den Bell, rated 5.0 on HappyCow. It offers a seasonal three-course dinner menu from Tuesdays to Thursdays, a weekly-changing selection of tapas on Fridays, and lunch on the last Sunday of each month.

The owners' line is that they do not want to convert anyone, they want to charm everyone, and the cooking is varied, accessible and well seasoned, with local products as much as possible. Book the three-course dinner.
