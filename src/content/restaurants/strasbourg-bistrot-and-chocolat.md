Bistrot & Chocolat is a bistro and chocolate house on Rue de la Râpe, a short walk from the cathedral, and the chocolat chaud is the reason to come. The vegan-friendly menu runs to carrot cake, pavlova and a brunch plate, with the chocolate work taken seriously.

It is not a fully vegan kitchen, so check the vegan dishes on the menu. The chocolat chaud and the carrot cake are the orders.
