AIN SOPH. Journey is Kyoto's reliable showpiece: a 100% vegan cafe-restaurant in a beautifully restored century-old building on Shinkyogoku Street, two minutes from Kawaramachi Station and right behind Nishiki Market. The menu leans Western-Japanese fusion, mushroom hayashi rice, taco rice, green curry with brown rice, and a vegan burger, but the real draw is the dessert case: thick, fluffy Heavenly Pancakes, matcha pancakes, vegan cheesecake and creme brulee.

Book ahead, especially at weekends, and note the kitchen sometimes closes for dinner on odd days, so check their Instagram before an evening visit. It is the prettiest vegan table in downtown Kyoto, and the pancakes alone are worth the trip.
