Rüpel is Hannover's vegan fine-dining restaurant, and it is the most ambitious cooking on this list: a seasonal tasting menu built from what is good right now, paired with natural wine, in a small room in Linden-Nord. The menu changes with the seasons, the cooking is precise without being fussy, and the whole evening feels like a proper restaurant that happens to be fully vegan rather than a vegan restaurant trying to be fine dining.

It opens Wednesday to Saturday evenings from 17:30, so book ahead and plan the evening around it. Order the tasting menu, take the wine pairing if you drink, and save room for dessert. Note that the Rüpel Food LAB kiosk is temporarily closed; this, the main restaurant on Kötnerholzweg, is the one to book.
