Khambú started as a food truck called The Vurger and grew into Valencia's favourite vegan fast-food room. Burgers, nachos, wraps, hot dogs and desserts, all plant-based, all generous, all cheap enough to order too much.

It is open lunch and dinner every day. Order a burger and nachos to share, add a dessert, and enjoy the loudest, happiest room on this list.
