Bloom Sushi proves that vegan sushi is not a compromise, it is a cuisine in its own right. The Lyon branch of the Montreal favourite does a full range of plant-based rolls, nigiri-style pieces, salads, miso soup, gyoza and tempura, all of it inventive and all of it vegan. The motto says it all: sea animals are sea life, not seafood.

It sits right on Presqu'île, an easy walk from anywhere central, and it is the perfect lunch when you want something light, pretty and genuinely exciting. Order a set, add gyoza, and save room for whatever is seasonal.
