Why we love it — Chef Tara Punzone did the impossible thing: she made vegan
Italian food that does not taste like a compromise. The Black Magic lasagna,
layered with cashew ricotta, black truffle and cashew mozzarella, is the dish
every reviewer mentions, and the garlic-aioli potato wedges have their own
fan club. Everything organic, everything sustainable, and the wine bar is
fully plant-based too.

It tops Yelp's 2026 LA vegan rankings with 1.3k reviews at 4.6 stars, and
every local guide we cross-checked keeps it in the top two. Sit on the patio,
order the lasagna, and plan to share zero of it.
