Tommy's has been serving veg-friendly food in Coventry Village since 1972, and it is the diner that Cleveland vegans grew up on: homemade veggie burgers of brown rice, adzuki beans and vegetables, tofu scrambles, vegan milkshakes made with tofu, soy or rice milk, and fries voted the best in Northeast Ohio.

It is a meat-serving diner with a deep vegan bench, so it lands as vegan-friendly, but the vegan options are the point for half the room. Order a burger, add fries, and finish with a vegan milkshake.
