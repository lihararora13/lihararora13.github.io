Why we love it — Hey, Sunshine Kitchen is the rare spot that wins both the
rating game and the affordability game: 4.9 stars across 925 reviews, and
an all-day menu where breakfast costs about ten bucks. The kitchen is purely
plant-based with no substitutions needed, and the comfort food is executed
with diner-level confidence. Locals treat it as their everyday vegan, which
is the highest compliment a city this spoiled can pay.

It is the highest-rated restaurant on this whole list by the numbers. Order
the breakfast burrito any time of day and thank us later.
