Sangeetha is the Electra Street South Indian vegetarian restaurant, open from half past seven in the morning until late, and a downtown institution. The menu is dosa, thali, curries and snacks, with vegan options throughout if you ask, and it is the place for a proper South Indian breakfast.

Order a masala dosa, a thali without ghee, and ask about plant milk for your coffee. It is the early-morning pick on this list.
