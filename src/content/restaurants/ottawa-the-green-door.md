The Green Door has been feeding Ottawa since 1988, and it is still the city's best vegetarian buffet. Pay by weight, pile your plate with seasonal organic dishes, and note that most of them are vegan and many are gluten-free. The in-house bakery turns out raw vegan cheesecakes and chocolate cake.

It is open Tuesday to Sunday and the selection changes with the season. Order a bit of everything, especially the tofu broccoli stirfry and the avocado salad, and finish with the cheesecake.
