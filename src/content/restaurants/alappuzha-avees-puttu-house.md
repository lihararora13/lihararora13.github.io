Avees Puttu House is the puttu specialist in the town's where-to-eat guide, a twenty-minute drive from the centre and the place to go when the dosa circuit gets repetitive. Puttu with dark kadala curry is the dish, steamed rice powder cylinders with a chickpea curry that is naturally plant-based.

Order the puttu with kadala curry and a filter coffee. It is the most Kerala meal on this list, and the most vegan-friendly by accident.
