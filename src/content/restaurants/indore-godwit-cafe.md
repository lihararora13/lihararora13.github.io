Godwit is Indore's cheerful all-rounder: bright chairs, a menu that refuses to sit still, and a pure-veg kitchen that covers café, continental, Asian and North Indian in one sitting. That range is exactly what makes it useful for vegans, because there is always a vegetable-led dish to build a meal around, and the staff are used to no-butter, no-cheese requests.

Order across the menu rather than sticking to one cuisine, share a few plates, and treat the shakes as a question to ask rather than an assumption. A solid, happy middle of any Indore vegan day.
