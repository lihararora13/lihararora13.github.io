Green Falafel is the change of pace every long trip needs: a vegetarian restaurant in the historic centre with many vegan options, from falafel and wraps to pizzas and more. After days of quinoa and potatoes, a good falafel wrap tastes like a holiday in itself, and this one is done properly.

Come for a quick lunch, order a falafel wrap and a hummus plate, and take the rest of the afternoon slow. This is the one for when you want something different.
