Hippo Bio Vegan is a fully vegan organic café in the Südstadt, and it is the calmest stop on Hannover's list: a changing lunch plate, homemade cakes, and proper oat-milk coffee, all organic, in a bright room on Marienstraße. The cooking is simple and seasonal rather than showy, and the cake counter is the reason regulars keep coming back.

It sits a short walk south of the city centre. Order the lunch plate, add a slice of whatever cake is out that day, and take your time. This is the table for a slow lunch, and the one to pick when you want organic food without the worthy atmosphere.
