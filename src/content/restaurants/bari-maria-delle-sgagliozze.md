Maria delle Sgagliozze is a Bari Vecchia institution for sgagliozze, fried polenta squares, which are vegan by tradition, served from a small shop on Strada del Carmine in the old town. It opens in the evenings, half five to nine, and it is the cheapest, most Bari stop on this list.

Order the sgagliozze and eat them walking the old town lanes. Ask about the frying oil if you are strict, since recipes vary by vendor.
