Peace & Bowl is the easy healthy breakfast or lunch in Playa, a small all-vegan spot at the very back of Mercado 30 on Calle 30, past the other cafés, so walk all the way through. Have an acai or smoothie bowl in the morning, or a hummus bowl, salad or pita pizza later on, and expect a 4.5 HappyCow rating across more than 50 reviews.

It opens from 8:30am and is closed Sunday. The guides disagree on whether it stays open for dinner, so treat it as a breakfast-and-lunch place and check before planning an evening meal here.
