Isabella De Cham is a restaurant in Naples' loud, vibrant Rione Sanita that serves pizza fritta, deep-fried pizza, and it is the place to try it. Isabella and her all-female team do excellent baked pizzas too, but the fried pizza is the signature, with vegan fillings available alongside the classics.

This is not a fully vegan restaurant, so say you are vegan when you order and pick the plant-based fillings. The Sanita address on this page is the original; there is also an outpost in Montesanto Market and a branch in Aversa.
