Velani answers the question every visitor to Vienna asks: can you eat proper Austrian food without the meat and dairy? Yes, and gloriously. The rotating seasonal menu covers the canon, schnitzel, goulash, onion roast, liver dumpling soup, fried cheese with lingonberry jam, and finishes with the fluffy shredded pancake Kaiserschmarrn that every Viennese childhood remembers.

The room is cozy and modern with a small outdoor area, the prices are fair, and reservations are wise at weekends. Order the schnitzel for the table's first-timer, the goulash for yourself, and save room for dessert, because a vegan Kaiserschmarrn this good feels like a small miracle.
