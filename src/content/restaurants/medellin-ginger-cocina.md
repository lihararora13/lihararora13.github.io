Ginger Cocina is the Laureles spot the locals keep to themselves: a small, owner-run vegan kitchen in a modern-rustic open-air space surrounded by plants, rated a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow. The menu is a joyful mash-up, Mexican tacos and Asian baos and ramen sharing a page with ceviche and daily bowls, and everything is fully plant-based since May 2024.

Order the tacos al pastor de coliflor and the mushroom tacos, add a round of baos and a bowl of ramen, and linger over the greenery. It is the kind of neighbourhood place that makes Laureles feel like the real Medellín, and it is open for lunch and dinner Tuesday through Saturday.
