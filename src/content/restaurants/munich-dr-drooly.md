Dr Drooly is the best vegan pizza in Munich, and regulars will tell you it is simply the best pizza in Munich full stop. The little shop near Goetheplatz turns out 14 different pies, all fully vegan, with cashew parmesan and plant-based mozzarella and toppings that run from the classics to a kimchi pizza that has its own fan club. Seating is limited, reservations are not taken, and the kitchen closes when it sells out, which it does.

Come early, order at the counter, and do not overthink it: the kimchi pizza is the move, and the seasonal special is worth asking about. Card payment only, and takeaway works well if the room is full. This is the fun, loud, late-night end of Munich's vegan scene, and it earns its place at the top of HappyCow's city list.
