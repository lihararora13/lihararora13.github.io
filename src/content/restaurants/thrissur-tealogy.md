Tealogy is the newly opened vegetarian cafe in Chakkamukku, documented in a May 2026 family visit that shows a warm, plant-divided room and a yellow menu booklet built around health-focused eating. It is the new wave in Thrissur: a cafe rather than a tiffin room, and a place families go to linger rather than to eat and leave.

Order a veg bowl and a fresh juice, and ask the staff about the vegan options. It is the city's most modern veg room, and the most likely to understand the word vegan without translation.
