Chafa Cafe and Studio made the list for a reason. A cafe and Iyengar yoga studio in one: relaxed, green, and full of vegan-friendly pizzas, Buddha bowls and salads since 2018. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Koregaon Park, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Buddha bowl, Vegan pizza, Seasonal smoothie if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
