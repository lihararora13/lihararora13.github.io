VEGostices is a fully vegan brazilian spot in Boa Vista. Its tagline is COMIDA NORDESTINA VEGAN, and it means it: the feijoada, the savoury tortas and the brigadeiro are all built without animal products.

Order the Feijoada vegana, Tortas veganas, and Brigadeiro vegano. The feijoada is the dish to judge it by, and the tortas make a good second round.
