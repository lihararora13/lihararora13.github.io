Vega Dimora is the vegetarian restaurant inside the Apollo Dimora hotel, directly opposite the central railway station in Thampanoor. It is the polished, hotel-grade veg option in the station quarter, open for lunch from noon and dinner from seven, and the one to pick when the tiffin rooms feel too hurried.

Order a veg thali and ask the kitchen to hold the dairy. It is the most expensive pick on this list, and the most comfortable.
