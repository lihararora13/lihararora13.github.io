Hook and Press is Santa Barbara's donut shop and cafe: a bright room on East Figueroa Street with a few vegan items that punch above their number, vegan donuts, a breakfast sandwich, an arugula salad and a fruit bowl, plus a vegan egg. It is the morning pick in this guide, open Monday to Friday 7am to 4pm and Saturday to Sunday 8am to 4pm, with outdoor seating.

Order a vegan donut with a coffee and, if you are hungry, the breakfast sandwich. The vegan items are a small part of the menu, so ask which donuts are vegan that day.
