Vegetarian Home is a buffet restaurant on Sanmin Street offering dishes cooked to Zen Buddhist guidelines, including mapo tofu, mock meat, noodles and lots of vegetable dishes, with payment based on the weight of the food you have eaten. It is wheelchair accessible and inexpensive, and it is the most traditional vegan meal in Hualien.

Fill a plate with mapo tofu, mock meat and vegetables, and pay by weight at the counter. It is a lunch and dinner kitchen, and the dishes change through the day, so come early for the fullest choice.
