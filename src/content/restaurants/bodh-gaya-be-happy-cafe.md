Be Happy Cafe is the traveller living room near the temple: pizza, pasta, big breakfasts and real coffee in a garden setting. It announced its seasonal reopening in September 2026, running daily 8 to 8:30, and the travel guides call it one of the most popular cafes among international travellers in Bodh Gaya.

Order a big vegetarian breakfast or a veg pizza without cheese, and settle in with a coffee. Like Tibet Om, it runs on the pilgrimage season, so winter is when it is at its best.
