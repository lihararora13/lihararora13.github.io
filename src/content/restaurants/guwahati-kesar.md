Kesar tops Guwahati's vegetarian restaurant guides, and it does it on the strength of one dish: an aloo tikki chaat that the guides call a burst of flavour. It is a small Kumarpara restaurant in Shivani Arcade, rated a full 5.0 by its reviewers, serving excellent vegetarian recipes at street-food prices.

Order the aloo tikki chaat first, then a thali or chole. Ask for no curd and no ghee and the chaat stays plant-based, which is the move in a city where dairy sneaks into everything.
