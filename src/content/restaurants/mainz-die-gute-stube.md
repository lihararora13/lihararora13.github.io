Die Gute Stube is a small vegan cafe in the old town of Mainz, turned vegan in June 2018 and rated 4.5 on HappyCow, selling cakes and warm dishes, with companion animals welcome. It is the quiet cafe pick on this list.

Order the cakes and the warm dishes of the day, and come for a slow coffee in the old town.
