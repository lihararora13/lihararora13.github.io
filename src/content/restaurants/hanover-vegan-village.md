Vegan Village is Hannover's fully vegan Asian bistro, on Calenberger Straße in the room that used to house the old Loving Hut. The menu is Vietnamese-leaning: phở, noodle bowls, spring rolls and wok dishes, all plant-based, served in a straightforward dining room that cares more about the food than the decor. It is the kind of reliable neighbourhood bistro every vegan scene needs.

It sits west of the city centre, an easy walk from the Mitte. Order the phở, add spring rolls to share, and ask what the kitchen recommends that day. This is the table for a solid, unshowy Asian meal in Hannover.
