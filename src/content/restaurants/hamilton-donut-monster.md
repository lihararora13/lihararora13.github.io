Donut Monster is the bright donut shop on Locke Street South, open since 2018, that started out supplying fresh donuts to indie coffee shops before opening its own doors. At least one vegan donut is available every day alongside the traditional yeast donuts, with milkshakes, coffee and steeped teas.

It is open seven days a week, from 7am on weekdays, so it works as the morning sugar stop before a day of eating around Locke Street. Ask which vegan donuts are on that day, since the selection rotates.
