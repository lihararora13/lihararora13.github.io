Chopsticks on Mall Road is the year-round Tibetan staple: a cosy restaurant doing the copper-pot gyakok hot pot, momos and thukpa, and the table to count on when the Old Manali lane cafes have shortened their winter hours.

Order the veg momos and the thukpa, and the gyakok if you are sharing. It is the most reliable winter table on this list.
