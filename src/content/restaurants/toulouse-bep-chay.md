Bep Chay is a vegetarian Vietnamese restaurant on Rue des Couteliers, with a mostly vegan menu, and the most reliable evening pick in Toulouse: unlike many of the other vegan and vegan-friendly spots, it is open for lunch and dinner on most days. The food is decent, homely Vietnamese cooking, with noodle dishes, spring rolls and curries.

It is listed as vegan-friendly, as the menu is vegetarian with mostly vegan dishes. Say you are vegan when you order, and come here when everything else is shut.
