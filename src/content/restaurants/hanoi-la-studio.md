La Studio is the slow-morning spot. A beautiful, light-filled cafe in Tay Ho where the menu is simple and clearly done with care: smoothie bowls, fresh juices, plant-based yogurt bowls topped with fruit and homemade granola on one side, and big, properly stacked sandwiches on the other, smoky tempeh, roasted veg and creamy spreads tucked into fresh bread.

The vegan salmon lox bagel is the one to order, up there with the best things ever eaten between two pieces of bread, and the coffee holds its own in a country mad about the stuff. Not far from Kiez, so combine the two for a West Lake vegan crawl.
