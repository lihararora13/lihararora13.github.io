Sri Saravana Bhavan on East Veli Street is the Madurai branch of the chain every South Indian knows, open from 6 in the morning to 11 at night. The menu is the full tiffin and meals repertoire, and the kitchen is consistent in the way the chain's kitchens are.

Order the masala dosa without ghee and the veg meal without curd. It is the reliable, always-open fallback in the old city.
