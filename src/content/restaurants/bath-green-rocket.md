The Green Rocket has been Bath's vegan anchor for years, a small cafe off the main shopping streets that built its reputation on careful seasonal cooking rather than gimmicks. The room is simple, the staff know their regulars, and the menu leans on whatever is good at the market that week.

Order whatever is on the specials board first, then the vegan full English if you are there before noon. The cakes are made in-house and the coffee is properly done. Come early at weekends, when the small room fills fast.
