Flower Burger started in Milan and its rainbow buns have become one of the city's vegan calling cards: legume-based patties in pink, green, purple and black buns, all 100% natural ingredients, with seasonal special-edition burgers alongside the regulars. The Viale Vittorio Veneto spot is the one to head for, rated 4.5 on HappyCow from more than 200 reviews.

Order the Cherry Bomb or the Parmigiana burger, add potato wedges and the fried breaded eggplant, and try the newer additions if they are on: the pink Margherose pizza and the house-made vegan Kebroll. It is fast, cheap and cheerful, and the rare vegan spot that kids get as excited about as adults do.
