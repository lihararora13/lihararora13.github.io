Itadakizen is the quiet classic: an all-vegan macrobiotic restaurant with sister spots in London and Paris, set in a traditional Kyoto townhouse north of the downtown buzz. The menu is Japanese comfort food done properly, grilled onigiri, vegan sushi, noodles, tofu steaks and a bibimbap that regulars swear by, all cooked from organic ingredients with forty years of macrobiotic know-how behind it.

Hours are limited and they fluctuate, so call ahead and book if you can. It is the calmest table on the list, and a lovely counterpoint to the downtown queues.
