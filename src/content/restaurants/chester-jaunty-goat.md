Jaunty Goat is a popular Northgate Street cafe that changed from fully vegan to fully vegetarian in late 2025, so dairy now appears on the menu. The vegan dishes are still plentiful and clearly marked, which is why it stays on this list as vegan-friendly.

Order the vegan breakfast or a vegan sandwich, and check the cake cabinet for the vegan options. Do not assume everything is vegan here, as it once was.
