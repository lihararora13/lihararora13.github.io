PURALMA is Escalante's 100% vegan bakery and brunch spot, with pastries, cakes, bowls, burritos and a plant-filled garden. Reviewers through March 2026 praise the muffins, burritos, burgers and coffee, and there are gluten-free options.

Order a brunch plate and the egg muffin, which keeps coming up in reviews, and save room for a house cake. It is closed on Tuesdays.
