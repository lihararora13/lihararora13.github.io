Bar Vegan is an all-vegan bar on Lark Street, doing cocktails, small plates, and burgers with no animal products anywhere in the room. It is the evening pick on this list, and the best place for a drink and a plate after dark.

Order the vegan burger and a cocktail. It is a good stop for a group that wants to share small plates.
