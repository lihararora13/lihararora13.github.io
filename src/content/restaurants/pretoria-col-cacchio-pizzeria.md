Col' Cacchio is the South African pizza chain that takes vegan pizza seriously, with vegan cheese and a menu of vegan pizzas, pastas and salads at its Brooklyn Square branch in New Muckleneuk. Gluten-free pasta and pizza bases are available too, which is handy for mixed groups.

Ask which pizza toppings are vegan when you order, as HappyCow notes some toppings have butter added. The vegan margherita is the safe first order, and the pastas are a solid second choice.
