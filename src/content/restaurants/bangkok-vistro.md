Vistro is the Sukhumvit 24 hideaway that feels like a deep breath: plants everywhere, natural light, and a menu that travels happily from Taiwanese-style dumplings to wraps, tacos, pasta and burgers, all of it vegan.

Start with the dumplings in chilli oil, save room for something chocolatey with gelato, and sip a kombucha or wellness latte on the side. Relaxed, pretty, and quietly creative, it is a favourite for long lunches that drift into afternoon.
