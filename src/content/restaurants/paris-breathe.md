brEAThe took one look at Paris's sushi obsession and asked the obvious
question: why not all of it, but vegan? Since 2020 this little Montorgueil
spot has been redefining sushi with tamago made without eggs, seitan
crunches, and beetroot "tuna" that genuinely surprises.

The Formule Découverte tasting menu is the move for first-timers, and
the cocktail list keeps things lively. A cozy, buzzing room that proves
vegan sushi isn't a compromise, it's a cuisine.
