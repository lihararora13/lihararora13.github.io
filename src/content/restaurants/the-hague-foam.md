FOAM is a healthy food hotspot on Frederikstraat in The Hague, serving a plant-based breakfast, lunch and high tea every day from 09:00 to 16:00. The menu runs from tempeh bacon and a BLTA focaccia to shakshuka with stone-oven pita, gluten-free oat pancakes with salted caramel, a lox bagel and a large variety of vegan cakes, with good coffee and a garden at the back.

It is the breakfast pick on this list. Order the oat pancakes and a lox bagel, and save room for cake.
