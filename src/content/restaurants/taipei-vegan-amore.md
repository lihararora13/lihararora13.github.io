Vegan Amore hides a small party on the third floor of Q Square: colourful, modern and friendly, with a menu that bounces from Italian pasta to Asian risotto to a proper burger. Travellers landing at Taipei Main Station could not ask for a warmer first vegan meal in the city.

Portions run generous, so bring a friend and split the difference between the pumpkin stew and a pasta. Reviewers in September 2026 were still raving about the burgers and that sea salt caramel oat latte. It is mall dining, yes, but the joyful kind.
