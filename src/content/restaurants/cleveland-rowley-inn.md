Rowley Inn has stood at the corner of Rowley Avenue and West 11th Street in Tremont since 1906, and it keeps a dedicated vegan menu alongside its pub classics, which is exactly the kind of old-school hospitality that makes Cleveland great. The vegan entrees are proper dishes, not afterthoughts, and the patio is the summer move.

It is a pub with a vegan menu rather than a vegan restaurant, so it lands as vegan-friendly, but the dedicated menu is the point. Order the vegan special, grab a patio table, and enjoy Tremont.
