26 Vegan Food Project, long known as Ao 26, was one of the first fully vegan restaurants in Lisbon and it remains a benchmark: LisbonLux calls it a favourite you should book ahead for, VegNews praises its juicy mushroom burgers and creamy pasta, and HappyCow reviewers keep coming back for the Portuguese plates. Expect francesinha, bifana and cod-style fritters, all without a trace of animal products.

The daily plates keep regulars interested, the set menu with soup is great value at lunch, and the desserts, cheesecake, chocolate cake, brownies and mousse, are the sort people write home about. Open every day for lunch and dinner in the heart of Chiado.
