Why we love it — Kitchen Garden by Suzette is the calm, earthy cafe where
Juhu's wellness crowd actually eats, and the menu marks vegan items with a
little rabbit icon, making it dead simple to order plant-based. Build-your-
own sandwiches, energy bowls, and cold-pressed juices round out the lineup.

The yellow protein bowl and the chia seed and coconut pudding are the two
orders worth planning around.
