Ekiben started as a food cart at the Fells Point Farmers Market and now runs three small counters across the city, serving Taiwanese curry buns, Thai chicken meatballs, and rice bowls. The vegan buns and bowls are the draw for plant-based diners, built on the same saucy, generous formula as the rest of the menu.

Order the tofu brah bun, the one that shows up at Orioles games as ballpark food, and a tempura broccoli bun if it is on. Confirm the current vegan bun when you order, since recent community posts have debated its status during supply changes.
