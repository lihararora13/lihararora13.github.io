Pom Kitchen is Sheffield's healthy one: a fully vegan cafe on Sharrow Vale Road with a Middle Eastern-inspired menu, a bakery counter and a loyal local crowd. It is inexpensive, it is cheerful, and the food is the kind of thing that converts sceptics.

Order the Tokyo hot dog, a pink pom bagel and a cinnamon swirl from the bakery. It is a daytime spot, open mornings to late afternoon, so plan brunch or lunch rather than dinner.
