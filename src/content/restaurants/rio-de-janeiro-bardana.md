Bardana is where Rio's local vegetarians actually eat lunch, and that is the highest compliment a buffet can get. Everything on the counter is plant-based and labelled, you serve yourself and the plate goes on the scale, and a generous plate lands around R$40. The dishes change with the season but the shape stays the same: palm heart moqueca, soy protein strogonoff, bean stew, ground banana puree with ginger and coconut milk, roasted vegetables, salads and desserts.

The Copacabana branch on Rua Sousa Lima is the one to visit, open daily with lunch as the main event, and there is a second branch in Barra da Tijuca. For anyone avoiding gluten, this format is the easiest in the city, because you can see every dish, read the labels and skip the seitan. Load up on the moqueca and the bean stew, and thank the scale on the way out.
