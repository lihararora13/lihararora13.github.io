Bharawan Da Dhaba has been feeding Amritsar since 1912, a pure-vegetarian dining hall by Town Hall and the Partition Museum that locals call the best vegetarian restaurant in the city. The tandoori kulcha with chana is the dish, and the winter sarson da saag is the seasonal prize.

Ask for the kulcha without ghee and the saag without makhan, and the kitchen will oblige. It is open 7 AM to 11:30 PM, walk-in only, and it fills up fast at mealtimes.
