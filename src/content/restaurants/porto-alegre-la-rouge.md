La Rouge is a 4.5-rated vegan restaurant on Avenida Mariland serving raw lasagnas, truffles and cakes, mostly organic, with a bakery and beer and wine. It previously sat at Avenida Mariland 1587, so use the 1129 address on this page.

Order a raw lasagna and a truffle, and save room for cake. It is open Tuesday to Friday for lunch, Thursday evenings, and Saturday and Sunday lunches.
