Why we love it — Chaia started at DC farmers markets and grew into the city's
beloved taco counter, built around seasonal vegetables and handmade corn
tortillas. Most of the menu can be made fully vegan, and the flavor combos
punch way above their weight.

It's bright, cheap, and exactly what you want for lunch. Grab the taco trio,
add the sweet potato nachos if they haven't sold out, and eat them by the
waterfront like a local.
