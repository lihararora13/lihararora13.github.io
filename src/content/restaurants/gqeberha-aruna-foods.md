Aruna Foods is a small plant-based vegan business in Central that locals rate for depth of flavour rather than novelty, and its vegan cupcakes get singled out by name in review after review. The room doubles as a quiet spot to read, and the cooking leans healthy and vegetable-forward.

Order the cupcakes and whatever brunch plate is on that day. It is a short walk from the Richmond Hill cluster, so it pairs well with a wander through the neighbourhood.
