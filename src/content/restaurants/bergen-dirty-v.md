Dirty V is Bergen's only fully vegan restaurant, a burger bar on Nøstegaten in Nøstet, and it is the table to start with in the city. The Garlicious burger is the signature, with a BBQ Stack, dirty fries and chicken nuggets alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it properly indulgent.

It was formerly called Dirty Vegan, and the current name is Dirty V, so use the name on this page. HappyCow reviews were updated in October 2026, and the burgers are the order.
