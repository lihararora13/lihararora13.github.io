4 Yaar Cafe is Jabalpur's pure-veg hangout, a two-storey café opposite Home Science College with orange brick walls, pizza-paddle signs, a neon-lit music room and a rooftop terrace strung with lights. The menu is built for friend groups: loaded nachos with salsa and cream, grilled sandwiches, garlic bread with olives, pasta and tall colourful drinks.

Vegans should order the nachos, pasta and sandwiches without cheese, butter or cream, and treat the party floor upstairs as a bonus. It is the youngest, loudest room on this list, and the most fun.
