Veg Cafe is Midtown's grown-up vegan bistro: a bright second-floor room above Thai Basil with a globally inspired menu that leans into comfort without getting heavy. The mushroom stroganoff is the dish people write home about, the buffalo cauliflower brings the heat, and the strawberry chocolate sundae is the correct ending.

It is also a proper bar and brunch spot, which makes it the rare vegan restaurant that works as well for a date as it does for a group dinner. Book the window table, order the stroganoff, and stay for dessert.
