Peaches Gastro Girls is Warsaw's vegan fine dining star, a Praga restaurant in the 2026 Michelin Guide selection that calls itself the best plant-based food in Poland. The menu is inventive and seasonal: wontons, salsa macha, pangrattato, labneh and matcha, all plant-based, in a walk-in-friendly room.

It is the special meal on the Warsaw list. Order across the menu, share everything, and let the kitchen show off. This is the one to book for a celebration.
