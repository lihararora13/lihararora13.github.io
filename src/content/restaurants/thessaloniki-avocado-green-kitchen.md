Avocado Green Kitchen is Thessaloniki's 100% vegan breakfast and brunch spot, on Mitropoleos Street in the centre, with a bright Scandi décor that suits a relaxed morning. Banana pancakes, avocado toasts, homemade plant yogurt and green smoothies are the orders, with breakfasts from four to eight euros.

It is open daily from 9am to 11pm. Ask for a compostable cup if you take your coffee to go.
