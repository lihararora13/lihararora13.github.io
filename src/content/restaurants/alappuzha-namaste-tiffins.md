Namaste Tiffins is a pure-veg restaurant in the heart of Alleppey, and its own description is the pitch: pure vegetarian food, classical music, a good ambience and a partner to have a conversation with. The podi masala dosa is the dish the reviewers call crispy, bold and unforgettable, and the puttu kadala is the other name that keeps appearing.

Order the podi masala dosa and the puttu kadala. It is the town-centre tiffin room, and the music is part of the meal.
