Between Cafe and Healthy Food is a small healthy cafe on the same old-town street as Brunchology, open weekday mornings through to early evening. It runs on kombucha, juices, empanadas, lasagne and pancakes, with a vegan thread through the menu and a loyal local following.

Order the vegan empanadas, the vegan lasagne, pancakes and a fresh juice, and ask about the kombucha on tap. It closes early, so treat it as a breakfast or lunch stop.
