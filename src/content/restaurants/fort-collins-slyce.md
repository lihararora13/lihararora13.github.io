Slyce is Fort Collins' vegan pizza pick: a pizzeria on West Mountain Avenue in Old Town with a vegan chef and Follow Your Heart cheese, serving a vegan specialty pizza, a vegan slice of the week and a vegan cream sauce pizza. It is open Monday to Thursday 11am to 9pm, Friday to Saturday 11am to 10pm and Sunday 11am to 9pm, and it is the place in this guide for a proper sit-down pizza with vegan cheese that melts properly.

Order the vegan specialty pizza and ask about the slice of the week. The garden salad is the side if you want something lighter.
