Kulturas is a vegetarian restaurant in downtown Floripa with a loyal following and a 4.8 rating: lasanha, falafel, mole, cakes and a daily buffet, with plenty of vegan and gluten-free plates. It is simple, affordable and generous, the kind of place where office workers and students eat every day.

Come for lunch, order the daily buffet or a falafel plate, and enjoy the downtown buzz. This is the one for a cheap, satisfying lunch in Centro.
