Ukiyo is a Japanese restaurant in the Triangle d'Or with clearly marked vegan dishes: onigiri, salad, wafu pasta, donburi, mochi and a chocolate-tofu mousse. It is cash only and open Tuesday to Saturday until 10:00pm and Sundays until 5:00pm.

Order the onigiri and the wafu pasta, and save room for the chocolate-tofu mousse.
