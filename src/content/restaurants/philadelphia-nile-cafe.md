The Nile Café is where the city's vegan story started for a lot of Philadelphians: a Germantown soul-food institution that has been feeding the neighborhood Caribbean-American comfort food, all of it vegan, long before it was trendy. The 4.6 rating is earned one combo platter at a time.

Order the combo platter and do not skip the plantains, sweet potato, collard greens, and cornbread. It is affordable, it is generous, and it is the kind of place that reminds you vegan food was never a coastal fad.
