V Vegan is a fully vegan restaurant on Nguyen Thai Hoc in the Old Town, with the longest menu of the fully vegan places inside the heritage zone: banh mi, cao lau, wonton soup, bao buns, curry, tofu in sauce and a seaweed salad that diners mention again and again. HappyCow listed it as fully vegan from September 2023, and it is rated 4.8 from 1,235 Google reviews, open 11am to 9:30pm daily.

Order the cao lau and the seaweed salad, and sit outside, where reviewers say the atmosphere is better than inside. It is the best fully vegan dinner in the Old Town.
