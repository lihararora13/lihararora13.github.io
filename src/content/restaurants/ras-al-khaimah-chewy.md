Chewy is a vegetarian shop and cafe on Al Kuwaiti Street, open daily in two shifts, morning and evening. It is a small, local operation with vegan-friendly snacks and meals.

Order a vegan snack plate and a fresh juice. It is a light stop rather than a full restaurant, and the split hours mean it closes in the early afternoon.
