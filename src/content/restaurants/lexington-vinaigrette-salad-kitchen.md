Vinaigrette Salad Kitchen is a salad bar on Palomar Centre Drive doing build-your-own salads and grain bowls with a strong vegan bench. It is the lightest meal on this list, and the best stop for a quick, customizable lunch on the south side.

Order a build-your-own vegan salad with a grain base and roasted vegetables. Ask the staff to confirm the dressing is vegan, since a few contain honey or dairy.
