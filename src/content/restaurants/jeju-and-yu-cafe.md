And Yu Cafe has been Jeju's west-coast vegan anchor since 2018, a British-Korean cafe at Ongpo-ri in Hallim-eup where the menu mixes burgers and sandwiches with soups, cakes and house kombucha. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow across nearly eighty reviews, and the room is the kind of place regulars drive across the island for: relaxed, unfussy, and entirely plant-based.

Note that HappyCow carries the same cafe under two near-identical listings, "And 유 (Yu) Cafe" and "AndYuCafé", at the same address; it is one venue, not two. It is the only fully vegan cafe on this stretch of coast, so it fills up at lunchtime on weekends.
