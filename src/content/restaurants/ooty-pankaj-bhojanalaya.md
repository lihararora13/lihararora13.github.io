Pankaj Bhojanalaya is the honest veg thali restaurant on Commercial Road, named by the 2026 Ooty food guide among the town's best-value sit-downs. It is not fancy, and it is not trying to be: thali, dosa and North Indian veg, served to the Charing Cross crowds at plains prices rather than hill-station ones.

Order the veg thali and ask for the curd to be left off. It is the value lunch in Ooty, and the one the locals use.
