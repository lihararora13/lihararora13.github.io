Basalt is Kolhapur's fine-dining entry: a 100% vegetarian restaurant in Tarabai Park on Station Road, built around the Safar tasting menu, a journey through the region's cooking. It is the most polished room on this list, and the one to book for a special evening.

Ask for the Safar menu veganised, with the ghee and dairy held, and the kitchen will build a plant-based tasting around the season. It is the best meal in Kolhapur, and worth the splurge.
