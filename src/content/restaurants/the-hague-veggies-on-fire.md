Veggies on Fire is a fully plant-based restaurant on Beeklaan, certified 100% organic and palm-oil free, and winner of the 2019 Gaia Green Award as the most sustainable restaurant in the Netherlands and Belgium. The kitchen makes almost everything itself, from bread and burgers to seitan and desserts, and the menu changes every season.

It is open Thursday to Saturday evenings only, from 17:00 to 22:00, so book ahead. Order the seitan and the gado gado.
