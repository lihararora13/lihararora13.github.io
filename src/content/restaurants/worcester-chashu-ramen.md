Chashu Ramen is a ramen restaurant on Franklin Street in downtown Worcester, doing vegan ramen and sides. The vegan ramen is the headline order, and the room is casual and counter-service.

Order the vegan ramen and the edamame, and ask the server to confirm the broth is meat-free.
