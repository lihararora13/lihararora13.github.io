Caribbean Juice Bar is the most fun pick in this guide: a 100% vegan beach bar in a repurposed shipping container on Sandy Beach at Puteri Harbour, about a hundred metres from the main marina development, with a straw canopy, maritime trinkets and a plastic-free, zero-waste ethos. The menu is smoothie bowls, cold-pressed juices, coffees with oat or soy milk, hummus toast and what Vegan Food Quest calls the most decadent vegan cheesecakes, all of it vegan, organic and sustainably sourced. It holds a 4.7 on Google.

It opens in the afternoon and runs into the evening, roughly 3pm to 10pm, later at weekends, but the hours disagree across sources and the closed day is listed as Thursday by one guide and Monday by another, so check the venue's own page before you go. It is the only fully vegan spot on the Puteri Harbour waterfront, and it hosts karaoke nights and beach events.
