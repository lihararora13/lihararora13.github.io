Dana is a love letter to Vietnamese home cooking. The concept is a fully cultural rice warehouse, a restaurant built around the story of rice and the Vietnamese ways of home, and the menu follows through: soups, salads, starters, main courses and desserts, all vegan, all with a modern touch. The colourful rice alongside the tofu dishes is the plate people photograph.

It sits on a quiet Old Quarter lane near Dong Xuan Market, a calm pocket in the middle of the beautiful chaos. Come for a proper sit-down lunch or dinner when the street stalls start to blur together.
