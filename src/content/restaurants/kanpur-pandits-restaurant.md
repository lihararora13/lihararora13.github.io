Pandit's Restaurant near MG Marg on Mall Road is the lunch-crowd thali stop: dal chawal, kadhi chawal and an executive thali, served fast and priced for the working day.

Order the dal chawal and the kadhi chawal, ask for no ghee, and you have a proper Kanpur lunch for very little money. It is simple, and it is good.
