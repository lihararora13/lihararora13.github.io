PXB Life is the most Dubai of ideas done right: a fitness studio, wellness space and rooftop restaurant stacked into Expo City's sustainability pavilion, all running on plant-based fuel. The setting alone is worth the trip out to Expo City, all sky and greenery and good intentions.

Up on the roof, the food is bright and energising rather than heavy: seasonal bowls, juices and plant-based plates built for people who just worked out or are about to. Pair it with a wander around the Terra pavilion and you have a full, feel-good day out.
