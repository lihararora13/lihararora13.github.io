Zhenqingguan is the vegetarian restaurant inside the Zhenqing Taoist Temple on Tuodong Road, a Ming and Qing dynasty complex that is one of the largest surviving temple sites in central Kunming and a national heritage listing. The restaurant serves mostly vegan food, yam soup, tofu, fermented tofu and assorted vegan dishes, for 20 yuan, and it holds a 5.0 on HappyCow.

It is listed as vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan because the listing says mostly vegan rather than fully vegan. The restaurant is open Monday to Saturday, 11:00am to 4:00pm only, while the temple itself opens 8:00am to 5:30pm.
