My Goodness Café sits inside the Quay Co-op on Sullivan's Quay, a vegetarian and vegan cafe with a brunch and bowl menu. It is the daytime heart of Cork's veggie scene.

Order a buddha bowl and a cake. The Co-op shop downstairs is worth a browse afterwards.
