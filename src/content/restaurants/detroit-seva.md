Seva is Midtown's vegetarian institution: a strictly vegetarian restaurant since 2011, tucked into the courtyard at the back of the N'Namdi Center for Contemporary Art, with a vegan option for every single menu item. The breakfast wrap is the signature, the veggie burgers are the lunch move, and the vegan desserts are the finish.

It is the most reliable vegan-friendly room in Detroit, and the one to take a mixed group. Order the breakfast wrap, add a salad, and save room for dessert.
