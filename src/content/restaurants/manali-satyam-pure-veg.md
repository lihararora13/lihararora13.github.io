Satyam Pure Veg on Mall Road is the pure-veg workhorse of the market: a North Indian restaurant open 10 AM to 11 PM, doing dal, chana, aloo gobi and tandoori roti for the crowds coming off the Mall.

Ask for no ghee and the dal tadka as it comes. It is cheap, fast, and the most practical veg meal on Mall Road.
