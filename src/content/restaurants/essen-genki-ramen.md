Genki Ramen is a Japanese restaurant and ramen bar on Dorotheenstraße in Rüttenscheid, a modern, stylish room with clearly marked vegan options. The vegan side covers several ramen dishes and gyoza, which is more than most ramen bars manage, and the kitchen takes the plant-based broth seriously rather than treating it as an afterthought.

It is a mixed menu with meat on it, so order the vegan-marked ramen and gyoza. Lunch runs from twelve until three with dinner from five, and the vegan tantanmen is the order for anyone who likes their ramen with a little heat.
