Rainbow Cafe is a cafe on 7a Avenida Sur, rated 4.0, with vegan options including Israeli falafel with hummus, salad and pita, rice and beans, and a tofu stir fry with vegetables and white rice. It is a straightforward, friendly room, open long hours.

Order the falafel with hummus and pita, or the tofu stir fry. It is open Monday to Saturday with a Sunday afternoon service, and it is a good fallback when the fully vegan kitchens are closed.
