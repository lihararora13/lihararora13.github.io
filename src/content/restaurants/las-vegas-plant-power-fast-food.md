Sometimes you just want drive-thru food that happens to be vegan, and Plant Power Fast Food delivers exactly that. The Las Vegas location on Craig Road serves the full playbook: the Big Mac-rivaling Big Zac, crispy 'chicken' tenders, fish filet sandwiches, breakfast items, fries, and floats and shakes, all plant-based.

Uber Eats and Postmates reviews from 2026 give it 4.7 to 4.8 stars across thousands of orders, with the California burrito and the Big Zac the repeat orders. Open daily from 8am, with a drive-up window for the full midnight-at-the-bellies experience.
