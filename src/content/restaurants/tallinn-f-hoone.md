F-Hoone in Kalamaja's Telliskivi Creative City is one of those rare all-day restaurants that takes every dietary need seriously, and it is the buzziest room on this list. The menu is broad and international, and the plant-based dishes are not afterthoughts, which is why it earns a place here even though it is not fully vegan.

It is the mixed-group pick: everyone eats well, vegans included. Order the vegan main of the day, add a seasonal salad, and enjoy the Telliskivi energy.
