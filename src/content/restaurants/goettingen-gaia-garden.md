Gaia garden is Göttingen's big Asian dining room, a vegan and vegetarian restaurant on Nikolaistraße that is almost entirely vegan: only a couple of dishes on a long menu contain egg, and the sushi list is all vegan. The cooking spans Vietnamese, Thai and Japanese influences, from papaya salad and pho to udon, ramen, curries and colourful bowls, with a weekday lunch menu that keeps mains affordable.

It is open every day, with a lunch service from half past eleven, and the room is far cosier than the street frontage suggests. Order the vegan sushi and a tofu dish, and check with staff on the two egg dishes: everything else on the menu is yours.
