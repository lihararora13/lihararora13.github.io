La Schiscetta is an inclusive bistrot, vegetarian and vegan as well as omnivore, on Via Giovanni Amendola south of the centre. It serves lunch Monday to Saturday and dinner on Fridays and Saturdays, and the kitchen cooks for all diets without making the vegan table feel like an afterthought.

Order the vegan dishes of the day and ask what is plant-based on the board; the kitchen is used to the question and the menu changes with the seasons.
