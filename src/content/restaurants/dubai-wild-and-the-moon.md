Wild & The Moon started in Alserkal Avenue and still feels like the arts district's natural cafe: bright, calm, a little bit gallery, with a menu built on local, seasonal, organic ingredients and nothing refined, processed or dairy in sight. It is slow food served fast, and it works beautifully between exhibitions.

Order a smoothie bowl or a cold-pressed juice, add a raw dessert, and do not skip the date cookie that reviewers have loved for years. Note the branch: the DIFC Gate Avenue location has closed, so head to this original Alserkal Avenue cafe.
