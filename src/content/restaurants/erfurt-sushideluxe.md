SUSHIdeluxe is Erfurt's sushi bar with a vegan streak, a bright restaurant north of the centre whose menu is clearly labelled and whose vegan offering runs to a sushi box, edamame, seaweed salad, summer rolls, an avocado tofu poke bowl, a vegan big roll, and tofu or quinoa gyoza. The house also runs a vegan all-you-can-eat, which is the draw for hungry groups. It is open daily from half past eleven to ten.

The vegan dishes sit within a wider menu that serves fish and meat, so order from the labelled vegan section. Go for the vegan sushi box if you want a tour of the menu, or commit to the all-you-can-eat if you are properly hungry. This is the pick for a sushi night that happens to be vegan.
