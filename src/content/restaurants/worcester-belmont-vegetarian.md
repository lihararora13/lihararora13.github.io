Belmont Vegetarian is an all-vegan restaurant on Belmont Street, doing plates, sandwiches, and soups with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the strongest pick in Worcester, and the room is casual and counter-service.

Order the vegan plate and a soup of the day. It is a good stop for a quick lunch on the Belmont Hill corridor.
