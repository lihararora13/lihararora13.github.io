Soak'd by Veg-O is the new budget pure-veg cafe near Kozhikode Beach, opened in July 2026, and the July food reels show exactly what it does: big masala dosa, fluffy idli, chickpea chaat salad and fried vadas in green coconut chutney, served on banana leaves in a bright orange room. The menu board lists soak'd idlis, rice and dosas.

Order the masala dosa and the soak'd idli, and ask for the chutney without curd. It is the beachside breakfast the city has been waiting for.
