Katzentempel is Bremen's vegan cat café, and it is exactly as charming as it sounds: rescue cats roaming a bright, plant-filled room while the kitchen serves fully vegan breakfast plates, cakes and oat-milk coffee. The cats come from animal shelters and live in the café, and the food is proper cooking rather than an afterthought, which is why the place works as a restaurant and not just a novelty.

It sits on Bahnhofstraße, two minutes from the Hauptbahnhof. Book ahead if you can, order the breakfast plate, and let a cat pick you. This is the table for a slow morning, and the one to take someone who needs convincing that vegan food can be a pleasure.
