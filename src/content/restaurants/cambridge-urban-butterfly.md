The Urban Butterfly is a newer vegetarian and vegan cafe on Regent Street, with big windows overlooking the calm greenery of Parker's Piece and a cute blue butterfly marking the door. It is a bright, calm room for brunch or a coffee between the station and the city centre.

Order a vegan brunch plate and a speciality coffee. It is a vegetarian cafe with vegan options, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it pairs well with a walk across Parker's Piece afterwards.
