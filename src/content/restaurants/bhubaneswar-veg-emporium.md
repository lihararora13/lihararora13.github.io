Veg Emporium sits on Janpath near Kendriya Vidyalaya No. 1, and it is one of the better-rated pure-veg spots in the Unit 9 area, holding around 4 out of 5 across local listings. The menu is the familiar North Indian plus Indo-Chinese spread, served in a clean dining room from lunch through dinner.

It is a solid mid-list pick: order the veg thali or the veg Manchurian, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you will be fed well for about 350 rupees for two.
