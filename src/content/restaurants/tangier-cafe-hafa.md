Cafe Hafa is the legendary clifftop cafe in Marshan, a terraced room above the Strait of Gibraltar where writers have drunk mint tea for a century. It is a cafe rather than a restaurant, and the vegan order is tea, bread, olive oil and pastries.

Order mint tea and bread with olive oil, and check which pastries are vegan that day. Come at sunset for the view, and treat it as a tea stop rather than a meal.
