B13 is the classic Madrid bar, gone completely vegan: a small, busy, no-frills spot just off Gran Via where the tapas are generous, the prices are kind, and the vegan calamari has become a rite of passage. Reviewers rave about portions so large that finishing is a genuine challenge.

Order like a local on day one in the city: bravas, tortilla, a chorizo baguette and the calamari to share, then chocolate cake if there is any room left. This is comfort food for a crowd, and it is all the better for it.
