Vege Creek turns dinner into a treasure hunt: grab a basket, choose your greens straight off the famous vegetable wall, add tofu, mushrooms and your noodle of choice, and hand it all over to be simmered in a gentle herbal broth. The Yanji Street original dates to 2012 and still pulls a perfect score on HappyCow.

It is fast, fun and wonderfully Taiwanese, with a communal table and a condiment station for fine-tuning. Branches now dot the city, including Taipei 101, but the flagship is where the ritual started. Go hungry and grab one more handful of greens than seems sensible.
