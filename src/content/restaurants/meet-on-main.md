Why we love it — Open since 2014, MeeT on Main is where Vancouver learned
that vegan food could be fun. The menu is unapologetic comfort fare: burgers,
fries, onion rings, mac and cheese, and root beer floats for the full
diner-gone-vegan experience.

Bring someone who says vegan food is boring and order the deep-fried bananas
for dessert. The room is warm, the portions are generous, and it stays open
late enough to save a questionable evening.
