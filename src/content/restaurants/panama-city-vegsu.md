Vegsu is a 100% plant-based Asian restaurant in Plaza Condado, in Condado del Rey, and it solves something difficult: Chinese and Japanese dishes whose textures are close to the originals, thanks to seitan, tofu and the house sauces. Some of the food travels round the dining room on a conveyor belt, and jasmine tea arrives on the house.

Order the tempura duck, the seitan pork, the curry fried rice and some dim sum, and take something from the frozen section home for the week. It is moderately priced, open every day except Wednesday, and delivers through Uber Eats and PedidosYa.
