Lil Bao is Brussels' top-rated vegan restaurant on HappyCow, with a 5.0 from more than two hundred reviewers, and it earns every point. The vegan Taiwanese kitchen specializes in bao, fluffy steamed buns filled with plant-based goodness, alongside noodle soups and traditional Taiwanese dishes.

It relocated from Rue des Alexiens and is open daily. Order a couple of bao, add a noodle soup, and you will see why this little room tops the city's vegan list.
