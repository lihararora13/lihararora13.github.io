Java House is Kenya's coffee chain, and the Nyali branch on Links Road offers a vegan burger, vegetable curry, fruit salad, smoothies with no dairy and coffee with plant-based milk. The vegan options are not marked clearly, so specify no dairy, eggs or honey when ordering.

Order the vegan burger and a coffee with plant milk. It is open long hours and is the easiest meeting point in Nyali.
