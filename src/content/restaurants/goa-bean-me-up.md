Bean Me Up tops the list for a reason. Arguably Goa's oldest vegan cafe, in a charming Portuguese villa in Vagator since 1996, doing tofu burgers, tempeh steaks and raw desserts. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Vagator, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Tofu burger, Tempeh steak sandwich, BBQ seitan sandwich if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
