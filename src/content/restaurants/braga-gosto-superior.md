Gosto Superior is a long-running vegetarian restaurant on Praca Mouzinho de Albuquerque, the Campo Novo square, serving a Portuguese vegetarian daily menu with soups, salads and hot plates, several of them vegan. It is the classic lunch canteen of the Braga list.

Order the vegetarian daily menu, a soup and a salad, and ask which hot plates are vegan on the day.
