Yassin's Falafel House is a Middle Eastern restaurant doing falafel plates, hummus, tabbouleh, and lentil soup, with a strong vegan bench. The falafel plate is the reliable order, and the room is casual and affordable.

Order the falafel plate with hummus and tabbouleh. A caution: one guide reported Yassin's as reopened downtown on Walnut Street, but its listing places it on North Peters Road, so the Peters Road address is used here and the possible relocation is flagged.
