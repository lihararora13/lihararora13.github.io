Confidants & Vegans is the set-menu table on Beijing Road: a fully vegan restaurant on the third floor of the Junyi Hui mall, across from Dafo Temple, where you choose seven courses from twenty options. It holds a 5.0 on HappyCow from 11 reviews, and the Spinach guide rates it 4.7 for food and confirms everything on the menu is vegan.

Order the crispy braised pork belly, the golden fungus sashimi, the mushroom soup noodles and the aged tangerine peel bamboo rolls, and try the hand-brewed coffee or an oat milk coffee. The Chinese vegetarian roundup notes the same third-floor spot as a pure vegan restaurant with no egg, no milk and no five pungent spices, built around matsutake broth noodles. Open daily 11am to 9pm.
