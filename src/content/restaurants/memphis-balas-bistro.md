Bala's Bistro is a family-owned African restaurant just down the street from Graceland, and its All About Vegan section is the reason it makes this list: skip the vegan nuggets and go straight for the veganised African dishes, the creamy maafee, peanut stew with carrots and cabbage, and the saka saka, braised spinach with onions. If you cannot decide, the Vegan Plate decides for you.

It is the most distinctive meal on this list, and the one closest to Graceland. Order the Vegan Plate.
