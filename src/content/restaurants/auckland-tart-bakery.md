Tart is vegan paradise with a sign to match: everything in the cabinet is plant-based, from the mince and cheese pie that converts sceptics on the first bite to jam doughnuts, berry danishes and proper sausage rolls. HappyCow rates it among Auckland's very best, and regulars plan their mornings around it.

Go early, go hungry, and do not leave with just one thing. A pie for now, a croissant for the walk, and something sweet for later is the correct order of operations. There is a Commercial Bay outlet too if you are staying central.
