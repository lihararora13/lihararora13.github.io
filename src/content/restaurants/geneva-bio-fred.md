Bio Fred is the one fully vegan kitchen on this list, and it wears that badge lightly: part health-food grocer, part slow-food takeaway, tucked into a quiet Old Town street. Everything is plant-based, around 80% organic or local, and much of it is made to order, from the daily vegan soup to quinoa salads, hummus, vegan cheese toasts and a frankly excellent vegan foie gras.

It is a weekday lunch spot more than a dinner destination, open Monday to Friday until late afternoon, so plan around it. Grab a bowl and a smoothie, eat at the little inside table, and stock up on snacks for the lakefront. Small, sincere and exactly what Geneva needed.
