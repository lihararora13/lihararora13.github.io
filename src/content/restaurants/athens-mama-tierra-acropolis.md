Mama Tierra Acropolis is the smaller, more casual branch of the original Mama Tierra, which has since closed, and it sits right next to the Acropolis Museum, which makes it one of the easiest vegan spots to fit into a sightseeing day. The menu is health-focused vegan street food from around the world: Greek souvlaki, cheeseburgers, hot dogs, falafel and more, all 100% plant-based, with a perfect 5.0 from 269 HappyCow reviews.

It is a takeaway-friendly stop rather than a long dinner, so grab a souvlaki and a fresh juice between the museum and the walk up to the Acropolis. The original dining room is gone, so this Chatzichristou branch is the one to head for.
