Man Yuan Fang has been serving Melaka's vegetarians since 2010, and its menu is the biggest in town: 168 dishes and counting, mostly mock meat, beef, chicken, pork, duck, lamb, fish and shrimp rebuilt from soy and gluten, alongside tofu and vegetable dishes. The plum roast duck, mock beef rendang, mock fish asam and KFC-style mock chicken are the orders regulars name, and the kitchen also does a Man Yuan steam tofu, cereal prawn and a pumpkin and sago dessert, with mix flower tea brewed from dried flowers. It sits across from the Maritime Museum at Plaza Mahkota, air-conditioned and family-friendly.

A note on the status: the restaurant's own October 2026 anniversary posts claim it is 100% vegan, no egg, no dairy, no five pungent roots, but independent guides checked in November 2026 describe a menu with clearly marked vegan dishes alongside others that are not vegan. This guide lists it as vegan-friendly, and vegans should order from the marked vegan dishes and confirm with staff. It is closed Monday and Tuesday.
