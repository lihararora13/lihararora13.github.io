White Rabbit Ice Cream is Edmonton's cutest scoop shop, a small-batch parlour with a pink interior and free taste tests before you choose. The rotating flavours include vegan options like the caramel brookie, ube honeycomb and London fog, and reviewers call it the best ice cream shop in the city.

It is the sweet ending to a vegan day in Edmonton. Taste a few, pick a vegan scoop, and take a pint home if you have a freezer.
