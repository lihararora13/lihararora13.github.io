Blackbird is a fully vegan breakfast, lunch and tea room on Jan van Eyckplein, rated 5 on HappyCow, serving scrambled tofu, acai bowls, bagels and chunky monkey oats in a quiet courtyard garden. It became a vegan restaurant in spring 2021. It is the breakfast pick on this list.

Order the scrambled tofu, the acai bowl and the bagel banh mi, and come for breakfast between nine and half eleven, lunch to half two, or afternoon tea after half two.
