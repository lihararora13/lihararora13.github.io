Dulce is the kind of neighborhood spot that makes Kirkwood locals a little smug about living there. This fully vegan bakery and café does everything from flaky almond croissants to jackfruit BBQ sandwiches, and the allergy-friendly options are baked in, not bolted on.

Mornings belong to the cinnamon rolls and the lavender latte; afternoons belong to whatever just came out of the oven. It's women-owned, queer-owned, dog-patio-approved, and exactly what a vegan bakery should feel like.
