iVegan Supershop is Vietnam's first chain of plant-based cafes, and the Ben Thanh branch is its most traveller-friendly outpost, a short walk from the market with a 2026 Travellers' Choice award to its name. The menu is bright Western-style fusion: smoothie bowls, Buddha bowls, pizzas, sandwiches, vegan burgers, cold-pressed juices and kombucha, plus packaged food to take away.

Reviewers love the generous portions and the value, calling out the Saigon and Tokyo bowls, the falafel and Ohana burgers, and the sweet potato fries above all. Come for breakfast before a market morning, order the Pink Up smoothie bowl, and you will leave full for hours.
