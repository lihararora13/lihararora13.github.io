Greenhouse is Ginko's sister cafe on Herrengasse, a few steps from the main square, serving high-quality directly traded coffee, filter coffee, fresh smoothies, cold-pressed juices, sandwiches, cakes and breakfast and lunch bowls, all homemade, seasonal, regional and mostly organic. It is rated 4.9 from 104 reviews and is open on Sundays.

Order a breakfast bowl, a carrot lox bagel and a cold-pressed juice, and come on a Sunday, when Ginko itself is closed.
