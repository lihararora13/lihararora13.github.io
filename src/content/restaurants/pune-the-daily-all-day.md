The Daily All Day made the list for a reason. A bright all-day cafe with a seasonal vegan menu, vibrant juices and cruelty-free desserts, and one of the best brunches in Koregaon Park. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Koregaon Park, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Seasonal vegan brunch plate, Vegan dessert, Fresh juice if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
