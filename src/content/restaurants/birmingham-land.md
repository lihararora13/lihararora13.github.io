Land is Birmingham's special-occasion vegan table: an intimate, plant-focused dining room in the Great Western Arcade, serving multi-course tasting menus that showcase seasonal produce with global influences. Dishes like squash with kimchi and adobo, kohlrabi with mushroom and soya, and a tom yum one guest called a revelation, all in a small room with an open kitchen, make it one of the most ambitious plant-based restaurants in the country.

It is reservation-only and the six-course experience lasts around two and a half hours, so book ahead and settle in. Order the tasting menu, ask about the wine pairing, and let the kitchen show off.
