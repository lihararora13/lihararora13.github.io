Lien Hoa Vegetarian Restaurant is a legendary vegetarian destination in Hue, famous for an extensive menu of more than 100 dishes that blend royal sophistication with everyday flavours. It is a must-visit for anyone wanting to explore the true essence of Hue's meat-free heritage in a warm, traditional space.

Order across the menu, mixing a royal-style dish with vegetable curries and tofu, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. Its pin is approximate to the city centre, so check the address before you go.
