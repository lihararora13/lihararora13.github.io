List this branch, and this branch specifically: HappyCow describes the Napier Street Conscious Kitchen as a fully vegan restaurant, serving health bowls, wraps, bagels, burgers, bao buns and pasta, while its Kloof Street sibling offers egg and cow's milk add-ons, so Kloof did not make our all-vegan cut.

Napier is the colourful, boho lunch of your Green Point wander: a salmon lox bagel made from plants, a shawarma with proper chew, and a dessert case of raw treats next to the cold-pressed juices. Save room accordingly.
