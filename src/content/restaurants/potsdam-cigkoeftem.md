CigkofteM is Potsdam's cig kofte spot, a small shop near the station serving the bulgur-based Turkish speciality in wraps and plates. Cig kofte is traditionally vegan, a spiced bulgur mix with tomato and pepper paste, and this is one of Potsdam's highest-rated vegan listings. It is a quick, cheap and filling stop, open Monday to Saturday from eleven to eight.

Order a durum wrap to go, or sit in for a plate. This is the fast, casual pick, the one for a quick lunch between the station and the old town.
