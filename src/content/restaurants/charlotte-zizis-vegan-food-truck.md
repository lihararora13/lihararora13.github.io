Zizi's has been feeding Charlotte vegans longer than almost anyone on this list, starting as a takeout counter and re-emerging as a food truck that still shows up around town. The truck parks at Beatties Ford Road with a menu of bold comfort food: the Philly cheesesteak burrito, jerk jackfruit, pulled BBQ jackfruit, a Cajun fried chicken sub and a falafel wrap, all 100% vegan and all built to travel.

It is the takeaway pick on this list, ideal for a night in or a park picnic, and the 4.7-star reviews keep coming. Check the truck's hours before you go, it runs Thursday through Saturday.
