GingerGi is probably the most cited name in Paduan vegan cooking, a restaurant that is entirely vegan, gluten-free and lactose-free, serving kilometre-zero ingredients in small saucer portions designed for tasting more flavours in one meal. A recent city guide calls it the quality benchmark, and its own social posts through autumn 2026 show the kitchen still plating fried mushrooms, green purees and pulled jackfruit.

Order the pulled jackfruit, the celeriac cutlet, the pumpkin flan and the vegetable meatballs with curry sauce, and book the tasting menu if you want the full saucer experience. Closed Mondays.
