Sen Chay is a small, fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant in the Hakaniemi area, tucked behind the Aktia bank and easy to walk past if you are not looking for it. The menu is Vietnamese comfort food with tofu and vegetables: vegan pho, summer rolls, spring rolls, fiery noodles, fried udon noodles and fried rice, all of it inexpensive and all of it good.

It is takeaway-friendly and a local favourite rather than a tourist stop. Order the vegan pho, add summer rolls, and enjoy one of the best-value meals in Helsinki.
