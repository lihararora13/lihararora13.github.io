Dicki's is the brunch that built Brisbane's vegan scene. Tucked onto a sunny corner of Brunswick Street in New Farm, it does the classics turned gloriously plant-based: the sticky duck bao you build yourself with chewy sweet mock duck, fluffy bao buns, slaw and satay sauce, plus breakfast burritos, tofu scrambles and waffles that regulars plan their weekends around. Everything on the plate is vegan, and the unlimited coffee and tea deal on weekdays keeps the brunchers coming back.

Come on a weekday if you can, because weekends fill up fast and the wait is real. Bring the dog, order the bao, and linger on the footpath with a soy chai: this is the table that converts sceptics one brunch at a time.
