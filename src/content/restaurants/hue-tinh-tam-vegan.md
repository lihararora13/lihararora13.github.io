Tinh Tam Vegan is a vegan restaurant on Chu Van An in central Hue, describing itself as a purely vegan restaurant with no MSG, open daily from 9am to 10:30pm. The menu covers bun Hue, fried spring rolls, pho, soy chicken and rice cakes, and it is one of the few fully vegan kitchens in the city centre.

Order the bun Hue and the fried spring rolls, and try the soy chicken. It is inexpensive, central and open late, which makes it the easiest vegan dinner in Hue.
