The Asian Route sits at Tekuna Farm Market, a little out of the temple cluster, and it is the pan-Asian pick on this list: noodles, stir-fries and fried rice in a vegetarian and vegan-friendly kitchen. It is open 4 to 9 daily, so it is an evening stop rather than a lunch one.

Order the veg noodles or the stir-fried vegetables, ask for no egg, and you have a solid Asian dinner. It is the furthest pick from the temple, so pair it with a walk out to Tekuna.
