The Sentient Bean has been brewing coffee and community across from Forsyth Park since 2001, and it is Savannah's most beloved cafe: organic fair-trade coffee, local produce, composting and recycling, and a kitchen that makes most of its food from scratch. The vegan breakfast burrito, with tofu scramble, house vegan sausage, and spicy mayo, is the morning order, and These Tacos are Nutz are the lunch one.

Come for breakfast, sit outside, and watch the park. It is the warmest room on this list.
