Kitsune Burgers is Brussels' vegan burger fix, a few steps from the Grand-Place. The house-made burgers take inspiration from different Asian cuisines, the fries are proper, and Belgian beers are on the menu for those who want one.

It is eat-in or takeaway, with reservations available on WhatsApp. Order a burger, add fries, and enjoy the most central vegan meal in Brussels.
