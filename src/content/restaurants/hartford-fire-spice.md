Fire and Spice Vegan Restaurant, also listed as Fire-N-Spice, is a tiny all-vegan Jamaican cafe on Capitol Avenue, a satellite of a former Sisson Avenue restaurant that became the main event after the mothership closed. The food is cafeteria-style: around fifteen dishes on the board, with small, medium, and large platters, jerk tofu, ital stew, and pumpkin rice leading the way.

Order the jerk tofu and ital stew on a medium platter, and a mango matcha smoothie. Come early, since the cafe sells out, and note the limited hours: Tuesday through Saturday, 10am to 6pm.
