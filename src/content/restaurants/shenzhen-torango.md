Torango is one of the classier stops on Baoan South Road, the strip locals call the Buddhist restaurant row. Meals run about 75 to 100 yuan a head, and the mock carp soup, vegetable dumplings and nongxiang tofu are the dishes to order.

The menu has no English, so point, smile and trust the recommendations. It is worth the small adventure.
