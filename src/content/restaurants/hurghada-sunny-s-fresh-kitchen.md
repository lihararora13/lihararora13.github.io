Sunny's Fresh Kitchen is a small fresh kitchen south of Hurghada towards Makadi Bay, open in the evenings. It is listed as a vegetarian and vegan restaurant, and the cooking is fresh and simple.

Order the fresh vegan plate of the day. It is a drive out of town, so it suits a resort stay in the Makadi area rather than a night in Sakkala.
