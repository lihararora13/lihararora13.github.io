Why we love it — Fresh has been slinging plants on Queen West since 1999, before vegan was cool and long before most of its competition was born. The menu reads like a greatest-hits album of plant-based comfort: towering bowls, crispy buffalo cauliflower, and that mushroom udon that made Destination Toronto's must-try list. It also spawns bestselling cookbooks, so you can try to recreate the magic at home and fail lovingly.

Bring a friend, a juice habit, and zero plans for the afternoon. The sunny patio, the happy hour, the five locations across the city: Fresh is less a restaurant and more Toronto's vegan community living room.
