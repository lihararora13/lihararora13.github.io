Secret Garden Cafe is the quiet one in Tapovan: a cafe-style vegetarian restaurant with a view out toward the jungle, plenty of vegan options across American, Indian, international and Middle Eastern dishes, and a relaxed pace that suits a slow breakfast. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow.

The vegan pancakes topped with fruit are the signature breakfast, and the smoothie bowls and hummus plates round out a menu that leans healthy without feeling austere. It sits in Gulab Nagar, a short walk from the main Tapovan strip.
