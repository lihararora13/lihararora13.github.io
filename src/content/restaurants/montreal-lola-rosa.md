Lola Rosa has been the vegan restaurant non-vegans love since 2004, and the Milton-Parc original is still the heart of the operation. The menu is a world tour of comfort: nachos that regulars call the best they have ever had, hemp burgers with chickpea fries, chorizo burritos, crispy cauliflower, Sicilian meatballs, and a chocolate brownie that has ended countless meals on a high note. The mission is simple: get omnivores to eat less meat by making the alternative too good to resist.

The tables have little drawers full of handwritten notes from past diners, which tells you everything about the vibe: cozy, quirky, beloved. There are now several locations around the city, but the Milton original keeps the neighbourhood-bistro feel. Come hungry, order the nachos, and leave a note in the drawer.
