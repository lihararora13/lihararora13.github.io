Gateway to India is Tacoma's Indian vegan pick: a restaurant on Sixth Avenue with vegan dishes across the menu, chana masala, baingan bharta, lentil dal and vegetable samosas. It is open Monday to Thursday 11:30am to 9:30pm, Friday to Saturday 11:30am to 10pm and Sunday noon to 9:30pm, and it is the evening Indian table in this guide. Some vegetarian dishes may have dairy, so ask when ordering and specify vegan.

Order a chana masala and a dal, and add a samosa to start. The Sixth Avenue location puts it on the same street as The Red Hot, so the two make a good evening pair.
