Avogadro's Number is Fort Collins' university-district hangout: a restaurant and music venue on South Mason Street since the 1970s, with a patio, live music and a menu that includes a house-made tempeh sandwich, an Impossible Burger, a tempeh burger and a garden salad. It is the evening pick in this guide after Tasty Harmony, open Monday to Thursday 11am to 9pm, Friday to Saturday 11am to 10pm and Sunday 11am to 9pm. The tempeh sandwich is the vegan dish to order.

Order the house-made tempeh sandwich and a garden salad. It is a music venue as well as a restaurant, so check the evening program.
