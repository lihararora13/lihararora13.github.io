BEMBA rawfood and plant-based is a raw-food restaurant on Kollarovo namestie, rated 5 from 93 reviews, the first 100% raw-food and plant-based restaurant in Slovakia, est. 2011, serving gluten-free, refined sugar-free a la carte meals: raw starters, soups, main dishes, salads and desserts, with Fairtrade coffee, Amazonian tea, fresh juices and house-made kombucha. It is the raw pick on this list.

Order the raw starters, a soup and a main dish, and come Monday to Sunday from nine to five, since it closes evenings.
