Jade-Imbiss is a small Chinese vegetarian shop in Bilk where the mock meats are the stars: veggie duck, veggie chicken, veggie fish, veggie sausages and veggie meatballs, all made in-house from soy, wheat protein, yam flour, legumes and mushrooms. Most of the food is takeaway, with a little indoor seating, and an English menu is available on request.

Order the veggie duck, add whatever looks good, and enjoy one of Düsseldorf's most unusual vegan-friendly kitchens. Small, specific and very good at what it does.
