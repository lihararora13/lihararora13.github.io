Chickpea is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but it is one of the better spots for a fast, customisable lunch in central Reykjavik. Falafel wraps, grain bowls, everything made fresh, and the vegan options are clearly marked and easy to order.

It is the quick-bite pick on this list, perfect between sights. Order a falafel wrap, add a grain bowl to share, and keep walking.
