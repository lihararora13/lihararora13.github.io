Branzino is an Egyptian restaurant in Al Azaritah with a set of vegan side dishes that add up to a meal: baba ganoush, tahini sauce, roasted eggplant with garlic and other dips served with pita bread. Reservations are recommended.

Order the dips as a spread with pita and build your meal from the sides. It is open late and takes cards.
