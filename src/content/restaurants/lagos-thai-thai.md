Thai Thai is a cosy corner in a renovated flat on Victoria Island, cooking authentic Thai food with a chef from Bangkok. Most dishes can be tweaked to be entirely vegan, which is why it makes this list: ask, and the kitchen will adapt. It is small, personal and a world away from the big hotel restaurants.

Order the pad thai vegan, ask for the green curry without fish sauce, and add a papaya salad. Book ahead if you can, as the room is tiny.
