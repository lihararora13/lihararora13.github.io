Vegan World is Northbridge's plant-based answer to a Southeast Asian hawker hall, run by a family that clearly loves feeding people. The menu is huge and entirely vegan: creamy curry laksa, nasi lemak, pho, tom yum, bao buns and crispy fried "chicken", all with the kind of bold, authentic flavour that fools even the meat-eaters at the table.

It is built for groups and second helpings, with big sharing portions and a relaxed, no-fuss room. Start with the bao buns, split a laksa, and save space for the honey "chickn" that regulars keep coming back for.
