Ahimsa Burgers is Lagoa's vegan burger joint: fully vegan, right in the heart of the lagoon village, serving plant-based burgers, fries and shakes. It is the obvious stop after a day on the lagoon or the beach, fast, satisfying and unapologetically fun.

Come hungry, order the Ahimsa burger with fries, and add a shake. This is the one for a proper burger night in Lagoa.
