Maza Terapija means Little Therapy, and the name is the promise: a cafe whose founders set out to show that vegetarian food can be simple, delicious and appealing, and whose cakes and pastries are the stuff of local legend. The savoury menu is locally inspired too, with cold beetroot soup, solyanka and a non-herring bowl among the vegan-friendly dishes.

It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so check which dishes are vegan when you order. Order the beetroot soup, save room for cake, and feel better, as advertised.
