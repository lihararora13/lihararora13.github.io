Chef Kenny Chye is a legend in the Vegas vegan scene, and his Chinatown restaurant is the go-to for plant-based Chinese classics done boldly. The menu roams from dim sum and sushi rolls to vegan crispy beef, wonton soup ramen, spicy Thai curries, and mock-meat feasts, all served in generous portions.

Reviewers on HappyCow give it a perfect score across hundreds of reviews, and the 2026 guides all point here for Chinese food in Vegas. Do not skip the dim sum, and the arugula salad is the insider order that keeps showing up in reviews.
