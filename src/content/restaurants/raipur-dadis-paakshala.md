Dadi's Paakshala, grandmother's kitchen, does exactly what the name promises: traditional Indian food served family-style, in a room that feels built for long dinners rather than quick bites. It appears on every local pure-veg fine-dining list, and regulars come back for the thalis and the unhurried warmth of it all.

Ask for the thali without ghee and the meal turns vegan without losing its soul: dal, seasonal sabzi and hot roti, cooked the slow way. Come with family or friends, and come hungry.
