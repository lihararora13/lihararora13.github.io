The Warehouse Cafe is Birmingham's original vegetarian restaurant, an award-winning Digbeth institution housed above the Friends of the Earth building on Allison Street, and it now runs a completely vegan menu as BRIG Cafe at the Warehouse. The cooking is healthy, organic and proudly ethical, with dishes like banana blossom fish and chips and a vegan kebab burger that reviewers call fantastic.

It is a short walk from Moor Street station, near the back entrance to the car park. Order the banana blossom fish and chips, add a vegan cheeseburger to share, and enjoy one of the city's most storied rooms.
