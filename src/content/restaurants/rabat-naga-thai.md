Naga Thai is the Thai option in the city centre, curries, noodles and stir-fries with vegan-friendly dishes on the menu. It is a useful change of pace from Moroccan food after a few days of tagines.

Order a vegetable curry and ask for no fish sauce or egg. Confirm the pad thai is made vegan before you order it.
