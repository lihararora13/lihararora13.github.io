Tonic has been quietly doing its thing in Jonction for years: a vegetarian restaurant and café where the cooking follows the seasons and the vegan options are a real part of the menu, not a footnote. Lunch is the sweet spot, with a daily soup, generous salads and vegetable plates that change with the market.

It is calm, reliable and a short walk from the river, which makes it a good reset between museums and meetings. Order whatever is seasonal, and do not rush: this is Geneva at its most unhurried.
