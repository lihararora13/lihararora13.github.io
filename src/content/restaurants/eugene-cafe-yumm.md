Cafe Yumm is the Pacific Northwest bowl chain founded in Eugene, and its Broadway location is the original: a fast-casual room where the signature Yumm bowl, rice and beans with the house Yumm sauce, is made vegan on request with tofu, and tempeh wraps, garden salads and breakfast bowls fill out the menu. It is open Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm and Saturday to Sunday 9am to 8pm, and it is the quick, cheap, reliable pick in this guide.

Order a Yumm bowl with tofu and ask for it vegan: the sauce is the point, and the kitchen knows the vegan version. It is the Eugene original, so this is the location to visit.
