The Itegue Taitu Hotel in Piazza is Ethiopia's oldest hotel, established in 1898, and its restaurant serves a vegan Ethiopian food buffet every day at lunchtime until 2:30pm. It is the most historic room on this list and the easiest way to taste a full spread of fasting dishes in one sitting.

Come for lunch, not dinner, as the buffet is a lunchtime service. The buffet is the order.
