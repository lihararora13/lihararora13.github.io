Farm2Table is Malmö's fully vegan café and brunch spot, on Davidshallsgatan in Davidshall, and it is the table to start with in the city. The spirulina bowl is the signature, with a lox bagel, Belgian choco banana waffles and a golden latte alongside, all of it plant-based.

It is the brunch pick on this page, and the waffles are the order. Come for a slow morning, and save room for the golden latte.
