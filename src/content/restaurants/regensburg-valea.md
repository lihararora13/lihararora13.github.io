Valea is a fully vegan bio-café on Am Brixener Hof, a small organic café and plant-based food shop in the old town doing cakes, coffee and light plant-based food. It is a daytime place, open Monday to Saturday until six, and it works best as a pause between the old town's bigger tables: a slice of cake, an oat milk coffee, and a quiet corner with outdoor seating when the weather allows.

Order the cake of the day and a coffee, and keep it in mind as the all-vegan café stop in the centre of Regensburg.
