Villaveg is a small vegan restaurant in Santa Tereza that does one thing beautifully: a traditional Brazilian dish of the day, plus grilled tofu with vegetables, pasta and plenty of desserts. It is simple, honest and deeply satisfying, the kind of place where the regulars already know what is good before they sit down.

Come for lunch, order the dish of the day, and save room for dessert. This is the one for a proper, homely Brazilian lunch.
