Cinnaholic is the sweet stop in Winnipeg: an all-vegan cinnamon roll bakery where every roll is baked fresh and finished to order with frostings and toppings. The Old Skool roll is the classic, and the cookie and brownie versions are the ones regulars argue about.

It sits on Kenaston Boulevard in the south of the city, open daily from 10am, later on Friday and Saturday nights. Come for a roll and a coffee, and expect to leave with a box.
