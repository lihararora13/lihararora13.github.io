Sage Vegetarian Cafe is a women- and family-owned Persian-inspired restaurant that moved from Chapel Hill to Durham, and about 80% of its menu is vegan. The Osh soup, made with garbanzo beans, pinto beans, and spinach noodles, is the dish to start with, and the bud-m-joon and the soya chicken in coconut curry sauce are the mains.

It is a warm, dimly lit room that works well for a slow dinner. Ask which dishes are vegan, and start with the soup.
