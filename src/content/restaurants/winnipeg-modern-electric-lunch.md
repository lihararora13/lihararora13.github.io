Modern Electric Lunch is the early-morning vegan stop in downtown Winnipeg, a café on Main Street that opens at 7:30am on weekdays. The mixed menu has dedicated vegan choices, and the breakfast plate with toast and potatoes is the one to order if you are starting the day here.

It is a daytime place, so plan it for breakfast or lunch rather than dinner, and expect a casual counter-service room rather than a long sit-down meal.
