No Bones is the restaurant Byron Bay's vegan scene is built around, and it tops the town's plant-based lists with a perfect score. It is 100 percent plant-based, with an ethical and sustainable focus, serving tapas and mains that convert even the sceptics: kalamari, kung pao chick'n, bao buns, shiitake pate and handmade gnocchi, plus a cocktail list that is just as creative. The room is relaxed and alfresco, with happy hour every day from five.

Book ahead for dinner, because the room fills fast. Order the kalamari and the kung pao chick'n, share everything, and stay for a second cocktail. This is the special night out in Byron.
