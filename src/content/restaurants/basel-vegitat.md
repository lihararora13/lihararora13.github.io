Vegitat is a vegan Turkish fast-food spot on Klybeckstrasse, doing a vegan kebab made of bulgur, tomato, pepper and onion topped with a cigkofte spice paste, plus wraps and plates. It is cheap, fast and filling, and it is the street-food pick on this list.

Order the vegan kebab, a wrap and a plate, and come for a quick lunch or a late snack, since it opens daily to nine or ten.
