Thalatheen is a hundred percent plant-based cafe inside the National Museum of Qatar, and it is a lovely place to sit, relax and eat. The menu runs on vegan shawarma, falafel, plant-based burgers, date lattes and acai bowls, and the setting, the museum itself, is one of the most beautiful in Doha.

Order the shawarma and a date latte, and pair it with a museum visit. It is the cultural pick on this list.
