Eesti Pubi Ruuni is a pizza and pancake pub in the heart of Tartu, rated 3 on HappyCow, with the last page of the menu dedicated to vegans: pizza with plant-based cheese, hot pots with coconut milk and salty vegan pancakes, plus gluten, egg and dairy-free cakes. It was previously called Ruunipizza. It is the pizza pick on this list.

Order a pizza with plant-based cheese, a hot pot with coconut milk and the salty vegan pancakes, and ask for chai or coffee with coconut milk.
