Tibits is the vegetarian and vegan buffet by the station, rated 4.9, serving a buffet of homemade salads, poke bowls, gnocchi, katsu curry and desserts from six in the morning to eleven at night. It is the buffet pick on this list.

Order from the buffet by weight, the poke bowl and the katsu curry, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan dishes, since the buffet also runs dairy and egg.
