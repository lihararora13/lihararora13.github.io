Kaschemme is a fully vegan Sichuan restaurant on Vordere Sterngasse in the Altstadt, and it is the spiciest table on this page. The Sichuan dumplings are the signature, with dan dan noodles and mapo tofu alongside, all of it plant-based and all of it properly hot.

It is the pick when the Franconian food starts to feel heavy. Order the dumplings, then the mapo tofu, and keep water handy.
