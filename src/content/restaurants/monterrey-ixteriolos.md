Ixteriolo's is a 100% vegan pizzeria in Mitras with a terrace overlooking the city, and it takes its desserts as seriously as its pizzas. Pizzas, pastas, calzone, and cakes that reviewers keep calling amazing.

Order a pizza, a calzone to share, and a lotus cheesecake. Go at sunset if you can, the terrace is the best view on this list.
