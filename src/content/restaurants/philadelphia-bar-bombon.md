Bar Bombón took the two most beloved food formats in America, tacos and cocktails, and made them fully vegan without a drop of the fun leaking out. The Rittenhouse spot's signature Philly cheesesteak empanadas are the order that converts skeptics, and the jackfruit carnitas tacos, hearts of palm fish tacos, and chorizo-stuffed poblanos each have devoted followings.

Come for a casual taco night, stay for the cocktails and the nachos that one local vegan pro called the ultimate pairing. It is festive, affordable, and proof that plant-based Latin food is one of Philly's quiet superpowers.
