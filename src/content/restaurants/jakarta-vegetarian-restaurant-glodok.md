This is the Jakarta vegan meal people remember: a small restaurant down a narrow Chinatown alley, with a food display at the front and an owner who will happily explain every dish. The menu changes, but the staples are mock pork, mock beef patties, satay and local greens, all made without gluten in the mock meats.

It is basic, cash-friendly and completely charming, and it has held a perfect HappyCow score across dozens of reviews. Go hungry, point at what looks good, and let the owner guide you.
