The Tomato Head is a Market Square restaurant that has been helping Knoxville diners eat their vegetables for a long time, with pizza, sandwiches, house-made veggie burgers, tofu, and plant-friendly desserts. The room is the heart of downtown dining, with a pet-friendly patio and a second location out west.

Order the vegan cheese pizza and the house veggie burger, and save room for the vegan cookies. It is the easiest lunch stop in the middle of downtown.
