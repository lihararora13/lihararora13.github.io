Vrindaa is the special-occasion table on this list, and it earns the splurge. A husband-and-wife team runs a small, 100% plastic-free dining room where organic, Ayurvedic cooking blends Italian and Indian ideas into courses like cashew-cheese flan, potato croquettes with mango cream, and a creamy blueberry pasta. The ten-course tasting menu is the move, and reviewers keep calling it the most refined vegan restaurant in Italy.

Reservations are required and the room is tiny, so book well ahead. Prices are steep, a few diners balk at them, but the consensus is that this is the meal to plan a Rome trip around.
