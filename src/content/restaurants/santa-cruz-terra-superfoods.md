Terra Superfoods is Santa Cruz's superfood cafe: a bright room on Pacific Avenue serving acai bowls, smoothies, cold-pressed juices, salads, wraps and toasts, all vegan except for honey and bee pollen, which is why it is listed vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan. It opens early, 7:30am to 5pm daily, and it is the breakfast and post-surf pick in this guide, with a smoothie bowl that shows up in every local vegan roundup.

Ask for the vegan preparation and skip the honey and bee pollen toppings. The acai bowl with fruit, seeds and cacao nibs is the order.
