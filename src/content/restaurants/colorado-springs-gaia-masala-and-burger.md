Gaia Masala and Burger is a downtown Indian restaurant on South Tejon Street doing tikka calzones, biryani, burgers, and naan. It is a favorite in the city's vegan guides, with a menu that celebrates Indian spice in burger and calzone form.

Order the tikka calzone and the vegetable biryani, and confirm the vegan preparation with the server. This entry is filed vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, since a guide's menu notes mentioned meats, so the all-vegan claim is not taken at face value.
