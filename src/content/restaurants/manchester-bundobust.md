Bundobust is firmly established as one of the city centre's best Indian restaurants, entirely vegetarian with around half the menu suitable for vegans or able to be made vegan, and a beer lover's paradise on top, with great ales largely from across the north of England. The Piccadilly site sits on the walk from the town centre up to the station, which makes it a perfect stop before a train home. The best-seller is the okra fries, okra in crisp chickpea batter, and the vada pav, a Mumbai-style potato burger, is the other signature.

It is open daily until late, and the vegan combo for two covers six dishes from bhajis to tarka dhal and rice, which is the move if you are sharing. Order the okra fries, the vada pav, the tarka dhal, and a couple of local ales. This is the table for a mixed group where nobody has to compromise.
