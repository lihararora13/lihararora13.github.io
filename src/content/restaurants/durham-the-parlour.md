The Parlour is Durham's ice cream shop, and it always has vegan flavours in the case alongside the regulars. The sundaes and floats are the way to eat them, and the downtown room is the sweet stop at the end of a Durham vegan crawl.

Come for a scoop after dinner, and ask which flavours are vegan that day.
