Blos Cafe is the most vegan-forward cafe in Pretoria's eastern suburbs, with a menu that treats vegan dishes as specialties rather than afterthoughts. The vegan list runs from breakfast, vegan French toast, through falafel pizza, vegan tacos, curries, burgers and salads, with soy milk for coffee and outdoor seating.

It is open Monday to Saturday until 11:00pm and Sundays until 3:00pm, which makes it one of the more flexible evening options in the city. Come for the French toast in the morning or the falafel pizza and tacos later in the day.
