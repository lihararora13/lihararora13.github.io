Turlaj Klopsa started as an all-vegan food truck and grew into a stationary favourite, and the Karmelicka branch is the one to visit. You build your own meal: four bases, three sauces, three kinds of meatballs and a pile of toppings, all plant-based.

It is a five-star HappyCow pick and the fun, hands-on meal on this list. Build a bowl, try a sauce you have never heard of, and add as many toppings as fit.
