Geruchtekuch is a plant-based fine-dining restaurant in the old town, serving only a surprise menu of at least six courses for around one hundred euros, Monday to Friday from half six. It describes itself as always regional, always seasonal, always different, and the Gault and Millau guide gave it 15 out of 20 points for 2026.

Book the surprise tasting menu and come hungry, since it runs at least six courses, and bring a budget, since it is the most expensive table in Graz.
