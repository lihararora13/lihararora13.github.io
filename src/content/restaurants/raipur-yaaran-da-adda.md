Yaaran Da Adda, friends' hangout, wears its purpose on its sign: black-and-white patterned tiles, roomy tables and a menu built for sharing. It shows up on both of the city's big pure-veg fine-dining roundups, and locals defend it warmly in the comments whenever anyone questions the list.

Order the North Indian mains without ghee, add a café plate or two, and settle in for the long catch-up the room was designed for. A sociable, easy seventh stop.
