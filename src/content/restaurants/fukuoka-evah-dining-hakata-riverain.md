Evah Dining is Fukuoka's famous vegan chain, and the Hakata Riverain Mall branch is its one sit-down restaurant: a macrobiotic lunch set with rice, soup, vegetable sides and a vegan protein, plus bento boxes and onigiri to take away.

It is the perfect Shinkansen fuel stop. Order the lunch set, or grab a bento for the train.
