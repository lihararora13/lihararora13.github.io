Pop Vegan Food is Sao Paulo vegan comfort in its purest form: generous, affordable and built for repeat visits. Lunch is hearty Brazilian plates, evenings bring the pizza rodizio where slices keep arriving until you surrender.

Come hungry, pace yourself through the pizza flavours, and save a corner for coxinha. This is the spot locals point to when they want fun, not fuss.
