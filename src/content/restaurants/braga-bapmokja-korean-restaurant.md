BapMokja is a fully vegan Korean restaurant on the second floor of Rua do Raio, a short walk from the centre, serving plant-based Korean classics for lunch and dinner Tuesday to Saturday. It is the only Korean table on this list and one of the few fully vegan Korean restaurants in Portugal.

Order the bibimbap, the kimchi stew and the tteokbokki, and book ahead, since the room is small and the lunch window is short.
