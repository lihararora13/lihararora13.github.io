Blue Sushi Sake Grill at the Summit is a sushi bar with a real vegan bench: vegetable and tofu rolls, edamame, and tempura that the kitchen prepares to order. It is the most polished room on the Birmingham list, and the one to book for a nicer dinner.

Order the vegan rolls and the vegetable tempura, and ask the server to confirm the roll sauces are vegan, since some contain mayo.
