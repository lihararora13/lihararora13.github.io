Soul Kitchen has been feeding Rishikesh's yoga crowd since 2014, when it opened as the raw-food eatery Earth Raw above the Om Shanti Om Yoga studio, and it still carries that raw-food DNA alongside cooked Indian and Western dishes, a salad bar and a juice bar. It holds a 4.5 on HappyCow.

Order a raw salad bowl and a fresh juice, or a cooked stir-fry if you want something heartier, and expect a calm, unhurried room full of people coming straight from class. It sits on Laxman Jhula Road in the Swargashram stretch, a short walk from Ram Jhula.
