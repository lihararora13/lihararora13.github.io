Tast Bites is a vegetarian restaurant on the corner of Zanaki and Kisutu Streets, specialising in Bombay street food. Most options are vegan, like usal pav, aloo tikki chaat, chole samosa and batata vada, and it is open Tuesday to Sunday in two shifts.

Order the usal pav and the aloo tikki chaat. It is cash only and the best street-food stop in Kisutu.
