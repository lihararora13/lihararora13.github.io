Sherkaan Indian Street Food is an Indian street food restaurant on Broadway in downtown New Haven, doing chaats, curries, and dosas with vegan options. It is the most casual Indian pick on this list, and the chaat is the headline order.

Order the vegan chaat and a dosa, and ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation, since some chaats are finished with yogurt.
