Qǐ Ruò Yǎ, 起若雅健康蔬食小館, is a small Taiwanese restaurant in Nantun that cooks Chinese and Taiwanese dishes with no onion or garlic, and it is the stir-fry house of this guide: pick a dish each, share them family-style, and let the kitchen send them out fast. The three-cup lion's mane mushroom, the red curry mixed vegetable pot, the sizzling tofu and the salt-and-pepper bitter gourd, which regulars say is not bitter at all, are the dishes to start with, alongside loofah pancakes, tomato and bean curd, and clay pot rice. There is a small garden patio out front and seating on two floors, with self-service tableware and tea.

The shop is open Wednesday to Sunday, 11:00am to 2:00pm and 5:00pm to 8:30pm, closed Monday and Tuesday, and it is cash only. Its Facebook page shows it active and trading, with a 4.9 rating, and a visit in July 2026 confirms the Dadun 18th Street shop is open.
