Plant Food is a fully vegan Italian restaurant on Graven in the Midtbyen, listed as vegan on HappyCow, and it is the pasta pick on this page. The pasta with pesto is the signature, with a cashew sauce pasta, bruschetta and a Beyond burger alongside, all of it plant-based.

It is a small room in the old town, a short walk from the cathedral. Order the pasta with pesto and the bruschetta to start.
