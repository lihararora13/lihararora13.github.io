Naveen's Bapu Ki Kutia is the fourteenth outlet of a brand that started in Bhopal in 2003, and the Gwalior kitchen keeps the formula exactly: a live kitchen you can watch, polite staff who adjust spice to taste, and North Indian food that tastes like a traditional home kitchen rather than a production line. Reviewers praise the paneer kaju masala and dum aloo, and the thalis are the backbone of the menu.

Vegans should order the special veg thali without ghee, with dal tadka and tandoori roti, and skip the paneer-led dishes unless the kitchen can swap them. It is the most reliable sit-down veg meal in the city.
