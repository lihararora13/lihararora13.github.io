Little Turtle does the thing Thai food does best: layer after layer of flavour, all of it plant-based, with zero fuss about it. This Enmore room specialises in vegan versions of Thai classics alongside less common northern Thai-inspired dishes, and the crowd has responded with some of the most consistently ecstatic reviews in Sydney: 4.8 stars across more than 400 verified OpenTable diners, food scoring a 4.9. It's BYO, it's dog friendly, and the prices stay very reasonable.

Order the eggplant curry with coconut rice, the dish reviewers rave about most, and keep an eye out for the pumpkin curry served in two edible bread bowls. Staff are famously friendly, the food lands fast, and even the omnivores in your group will be ordering a second round of the red curry arancini.
