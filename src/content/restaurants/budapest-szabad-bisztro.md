Szabad Bisztro was Budapest's first vegan gastropub, and it still sets the tone for the scene: a 100% plant-based kitchen on Király utca doing burgers, traditional Hungarian dishes, bagels, dips and daily specials, with draught beer and wine alongside. The kitchen serves until late, which makes it the natural landing spot after the ruin bars start filling up.

It is casual, loud in the best way, and reliably good, the kind of place where a vegan burger and a beer after a day of sightseeing feels exactly right. Regulars rate it among their top three meals in the city, and the HappyCow scores agree.
