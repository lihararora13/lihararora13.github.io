Nourish Cafe Yangon is a healthy plant-based cafe on Inya Myaing Road in Bahan, open daily from 8am to 7pm, serving breakfast, lunch, dinner and takeaway meals. It describes itself as a plant-based cafe, and the menu covers falafel pita, hummus, vegan gyoza, gyoza dumpling soup and chickpea tikka masala. It is rated 4.9 from 31 reviews.

Order the falafel pita and the chickpea tikka masala, and try the vegan gyoza. It is the strongest plant-based pick in Yangon and the natural first stop for any vegan visiting the city.
