Distrito Vegano is Mérida's vegan pizzeria a la leña, a wood-fired pizza kitchen in Colonia Francisco de Montejo north of Centro, with a 5.0 rating. The pizzas lead, with gringas, seitan chilaquiles, a burrito and lomito and costillitas de seitán on the menu, and the room is open afternoons and evenings.

It is not downtown, so take a taxi or a ride north. Hours run roughly 4pm to 10pm, closed Monday, and delivery is available.
