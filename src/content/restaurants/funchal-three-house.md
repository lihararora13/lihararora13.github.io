Three House is a hotel, gastrobar and rooftop rolled into one concrete-and-timber building near the waterfront, and its restaurant is one of the most reliable vegan-friendly dinners in central Funchal. The menu is modern and international, with vegan burgers, croquettes and bowls alongside the meat dishes, and the rooftop bar upstairs is worth the lift ride.

Order the vegan burger, the croquettes and the chips, and take a drink on the rooftop after. It serves lunch and dinner daily, and the Sunday brunch runs late morning.
