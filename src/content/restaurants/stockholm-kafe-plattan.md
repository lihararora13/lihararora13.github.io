Kafe Plattan might be the handiest vegan address in Stockholm: right inside Kulturhuset, the city's cultural house on Sergels torg, with everything made in-house and everything vegan. Visit Stockholm lists it among the city's entirely plant-based spots, and HappyCow rates it 4.5 with hundreds of reviews calling it a gem in the heart of the city.

It is fika heaven with substance: vegan semla when the season calls, cinnamon buns and chocolate cake, plus proper light meals like meatballs and mash, Caesar salad, bowls and wraps. Spacious, friendly and cashless, it is the perfect pause between museums, matinees and Old Town wandering.
