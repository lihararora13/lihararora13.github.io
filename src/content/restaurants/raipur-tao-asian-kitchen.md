TAO is where Raipur goes when it wants Asian food done properly: a polished dining room with outdoor seating in VIP Colony, and a menu built around dim sum, noodles, stir-fries and soups. It is not a vegetarian restaurant, but the vegetable dishes are a real part of the menu rather than an afterthought, and the kitchen handles vegan requests cleanly.

Order the veg dim sum and a noodle or stir-fry main, confirm no egg or fish sauce, and you have one of the most satisfying vegan-friendly meals in the city. A strong second stop after Godwit.
