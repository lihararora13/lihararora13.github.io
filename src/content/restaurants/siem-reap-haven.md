HAVEN is a culinary school and restaurant on Chocolate Road with plenty of plant-based options, and eating here supports its training programme for disadvantaged young people. The menu is Cambodian with international touches, and the dining room is one of the more polished in town.

Order a Khmer curry and an amok, and ask about the day's specials. It is a training restaurant with a broad menu rather than a vegan specialist, so file it as vegan-friendly, and book ahead, because it is popular and the room is small.
