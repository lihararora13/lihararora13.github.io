Fitsum Shiro Bet is the one fully vegan restaurant in Addis Ababa, a traditional Ethiopian kitchen in Bole serving vegetables and mock meats alongside soup, tibs, rice with vegetables, sandwiches and spaghetti. It is rated 5.0 on HappyCow and open daily from 8:00am to 9:00pm.

Order the shiro, the vegetable tibs and a fasting platter to share. This is the table to start any vegan trip to Addis at, and the benchmark the rest of the list is measured against.
