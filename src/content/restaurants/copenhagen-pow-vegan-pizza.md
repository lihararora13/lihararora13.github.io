POW is Copenhagen's only vegan pizzeria and it goes all in: crispy sourdough bases, house-made mozzarella and cashew cheese, and a menu of city-named pies from a spinach and asparagus Helsinki to a pepperoni-loaded Berlin. AmityLux calls out the truffle pizza, Veggies Abroad calls it the best melty vegan cheese in town.

Come for an easy Norrebro dinner, sit outside when the sun shows up, and order one more slice than you planned. Current listings show it open daily from late afternoon, active and much loved.
