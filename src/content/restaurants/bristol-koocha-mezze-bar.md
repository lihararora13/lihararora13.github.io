Koocha Mezze Bar is the restaurant Bristol's vegans name first, and it tops HappyCow's city list with a perfect 5.0 from more than two hundred reviewers. It is a fully plant-based cocktail and mezze bar serving Persian-inspired dishes: aubergine dip, hummus, mezze, stews, fries and breads, all of it locally sourced and infused with Middle Eastern flavour, in a buzzy room on Cheltenham Road.

It works as well for a cocktail and a few small plates as it does for a full dinner, which is exactly why locals love it. Order a mezze spread for the table, add the aubergine dip, and let the stews do the talking.
