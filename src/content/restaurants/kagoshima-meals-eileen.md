Meals Eileen is a small South Indian meals kitchen in Higashisengokucho, serving vegetarian thali-style sets on Saturday and Sunday lunchtimes and Saturday evenings. It is a tiny, personal operation with a short menu, and the meals are vegetarian, so vegans should ask what is dairy-free on the day.

Come on a weekend lunchtime for the meals set, and check the Instagram page before you go, as the hours are narrow and the kitchen is easy to miss. It is inexpensive and genuinely local, a long way from the tourist trail.
