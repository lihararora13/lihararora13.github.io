Trilogy Sanctuary is what happens when a wellness center gets a rooftop and decides lunch is sacred too. Up on the fourth floor above Girard Avenue, the cafe serves 100 percent organic, vegan, and gluten-free food with the ocean doing its thing in the background, then you can roll straight into infrared yoga, aerial yoga, or a reiki training downstairs.

The menu covers breakfast through dinner: creative bowls, raw tacos, phenomenal desserts, serious coffee, and fresh cold-pressed juices. It is a TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice winner for a reason. Come hungry, stay for the sunset, leave suspiciously zen.
