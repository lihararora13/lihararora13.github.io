Bansuri is the Bawada Road pure-veg restaurant: a straightforward dining room opposite Jain Boarding that locals choose for a simple vegetarian dinner. It is the kind of place where the menu never changes because it never needs to.

Order the dal tadka without ghee, a mixed veg sabzi and hot rotis, and you have a proper, cheap, satisfying plate. It is the unpretentious end of this guide.
