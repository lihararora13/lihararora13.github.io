Sagar Ratna on GT Road is the South Indian counterpoint to Amritsar's dhaba circuit: a pure-veg restaurant doing dosas, idlis and uttapams with the same consistency the chain is known for across North India.

After two days of kulcha and dal makhani, the masala dosa here is a welcome change of pace. Ask for no ghee and the idli sambar as it comes.
