Limotruck is Bariloche's fully vegan food truck, parked by Lake Nahuel Huapi in season, serving tacos, chips and a cacao-and-dates mousse. It is seasonal, so check it is running before going, and its pin is approximate.

Order the Tacos veganos, Chips, and Mousse de cacao y dátiles. The tacos are the order; the mousse is the dessert.
