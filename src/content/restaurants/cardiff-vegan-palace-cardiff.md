Vegan Palace Cardiff is a fully vegan Chinese restaurant on Albany Road in Roath, and it is the cheapest dinner on this page. The fried rice is the signature, with satay skewers, sweet-and-sour pieces and curry sauce alongside, all of it plant-based.

It is a takeaway-friendly room with a short menu, done properly. Order the fried rice and the satay skewers.
