Arbol de Seda is the Escalante spot reviewers keep going back to: a vegetarian restaurant with labelled vegan options, spanning Spanish, Peruvian, Japanese and Italian influences, with oyster-mushroom burgers, Korean-inspired gnocchi and Costa Rican-style plates. The portions are generous, and HappyCow reviews run through August 2026.

Order an oyster-mushroom dish and confirm which desserts are vegan, as most dishes can include cheese and there is a note about fish at special events. It is closed on Mondays.
