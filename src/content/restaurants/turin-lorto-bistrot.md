L'Orto Bistrot is an all-vegan bistrot on Piazza Palazzo di Citta, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, and the sister spot to the more formal L'Orto Gia Salsamentario. The menu runs from breakfast through dinner: risotto, potatoes and cauliflower, bread, and a cake selection that earned it a place on HappyCow's best vegan bakeries list for Turin too.

Come for a long lunch or an early dinner, and save room for cake. The two L'Orto rooms are different experiences, so do not confuse their addresses.
