AnTeRa made the list for a reason. A creative, traditional-styled Jubilee Hills restaurant where the Telugu vegetarian cooking is naturally vegan-friendly with a word to the kitchen. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Jubilee Hills, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Avakaya pappu annam, Hyderabadi brinjal curry, Millet rice if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
