Go Native is Bangalore's farm-to-table conscience in café form: a beautifully remodelled bungalow in Jayanagar's 5th Block, all hanging lotus lights and open-air tables, with a lifestyle store attached and a kitchen that cooks what nearby organic farms are actually producing. The menu is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but many dishes are naturally free of ghee and cream, and the vegan dishes are the ones regulars come back for. Ingredients come from farms within 500 kilometres, and the menu changes with the seasons.

The raw jackfruit biryani is the dish to order first, followed by the neer dosa with pineapple gassi and the aloo patta chaat. The zucchini zoodles and the spinach neer dosa are the lighter picks, and the seasonal drinks are worth ordering alongside. Go hungry and unhurried: this is a slow-food room, not a fast one.
