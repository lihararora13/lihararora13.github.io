Boda is a Thai tapas and skewer bar on Congress Street, and its menu marks vegan dishes clearly, from tofu tapas to vegan curries. It is a good late-evening stop, with a bar feel that the rest of this list does not have.

Order a spread of small plates rather than one big dish, and let the table share.
