Veggie Campus is a 4.5-rated vegan cafe on Lady Musgrave Road, at the Ragamuffin Hostel, serving vegan soul food: mac cheeze, jerk cauliflower, wraps and more, with fruit juices and smoothies. The vegan lasagna is the dish that keeps coming back on the menu, and the Tuesday two-for-one vegan burger special is the best deal in town.

Order the vegan lasagna with garlic bread and pressed plantain, or a plantain-bun burger on a Tuesday. It is open Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 6 pm, and Sundays from 10 am to 5 pm. Note that recent social posts also list the cafe at 82 Lady Musgrave Road, at Dubwise Cafe, so confirm the door when you arrive.
