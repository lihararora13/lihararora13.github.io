Zhong Shui Xiang is one of the best-known vegetarian restaurants in Chengdu, and its extensive menu of Sichuan vegetarian dishes is the reason. High-quality plant-based ingredients, bold flavours, and a dining room that takes vegetarian cooking seriously.

Order the vegetarian mapo tofu and a round of Sichuan classics. It is a true taste of Sichuan, minus the meat.
