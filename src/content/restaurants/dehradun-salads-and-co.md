Salads and Co. on Patel Road is the lightest table on this list: a salad and smoothie bar doing fresh salads and nutrient-rich smoothies, open from 9 AM, at budget prices. It is the pick for a morning after a heavy thali day.

Order the garden salad and a smoothie. The menu is naturally plant-friendly, and the staff are known for gracious service.
