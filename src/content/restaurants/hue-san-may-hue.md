San May Hue is a traditional vegetarian restaurant and cafe by the Perfume River, in a rustic wooden house, offering a calm, nostalgic dining experience with vegetarian food and a tea ceremony. It is rated 4.8 from 56 reviews, and it is open daily from 7am to 2pm and 4pm to 9pm.

Order the vegetarian set and the seasonal vegetables, and stay for the tea. It is a good choice for a slow, atmospheric meal, and the riverside setting makes it worth the trip.
