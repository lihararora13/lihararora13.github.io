Take the lift to the tenth floor of a Stanley Street office building and step straight into 1950s San Francisco: jukebox colours, vintage clutter, and a menu that went fully vegan back in 2016. Veggie4Love is pure theatre, and the food holds up its end of the show.

The burgers are the headline act, stacked improbably high; try the beetroot, cashew and lentil I Am Fabulous, or The Drama Queen if Korean spice is your love language. Laksa, bibimbap and gado-gado round out the world tour, and the shake is non-negotiable.
