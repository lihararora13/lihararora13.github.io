Love Life Cafe is the beating heart of Miami's vegan scene, and yes, the burger really did win awards. The Wynwood space is all bright colors and communal tables, and the menu reads like a greatest-hits album of plant-based comfort food done right.

Come hungry and don't skip the arepas or the raw desserts. Everything is vegan, most things can be made gluten-free, and the smoothies taste like sunshine with a purpose. This is the first stop you tell every visiting vegan friend about.
