Aricha is a pita-based street food place on Agripas, rated 5.0 on HappyCow, known for sabich, eggplant, hummus, salads and egg in a pita, alongside vegan pita options like a mushroom stuffed pita with salads. It has outdoor seating and is open Monday to Thursday and Sundays from 9:30am to 11:00pm, Fridays until 2:00pm.

Order the mushroom stuffed pita, or a sabich without the egg.
