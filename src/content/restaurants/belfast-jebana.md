Jebana is Belfast's Ethiopian restaurant, on Dublin Road, with a dedicated vegan menu and a room full of Ethiopian ornaments. The move is the vegan combo: nine small dishes of stews on injera, from shiro wot and misr wot to tikle gomen, gomen, key sir and alicha kik wot, eaten with your hands.

Finish with the traditional Ethiopian coffee, served from a jebena pot with popcorn. It is a meat-serving restaurant with a separate vegan menu, so file it as vegan-friendly, and the combo is priced for sharing, so bring a friend.
