Namaste Foodies is one of Patna's newer pure-veg restaurants, a modern restro and cafe on the 70 Feet Bypass Road in Saristabad that pitches itself as taste meeting tradition. The menu covers North Indian classics, South Indian dishes and street-style plates, all cooked fresh with handpicked spices.

It is a good pick for travellers staying on the bypass side of the city. Order the veg biryani or a masala dosa, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you will eat well for moderate prices.
