The Coup has been Calgary's vegetarian living room since the mid-2000s, a bright, art-filled room on 17th Avenue cooking modern fusion food from local and organic ingredients. Curry, peanut satay, veggie sushi and tempeh shawarma share the menu, and most dishes can be made vegan.

It is the veteran on this list and still one of the best rooms in the city for a relaxed dinner. Order the tempeh shawarma, ask what is vegan tonight, and stay for the happy hour.
