Pharm Table is Southtown's apothecary-style health kitchen: locally sourced, globally inspired, plant-forward plates like sweet potato waffles, kitchari and tacu tacu, served in a lush patio setting with botanical cocktails. It is not strictly vegetarian, since animal proteins appear as optional add-ons, so it lands as vegan-friendly, but the vegan dishes are the point.

It is the date-night stop on the list, and the weekend brunch is the move. Book ahead, order the sweet potato waffles, and enjoy the golden hour on the patio.
