Vegan Pub is a vegetarian restaurant on Komarnicka, rated 4.5 from 67 reviews, offering street food where all dishes start out vegan and guests choose between plant and dairy cheeses: burgers, nuggets, pizza, pasta made in-house, Slovakian food, soups and mock meats. It opened in February 2019, originally named Fresh House. It is the pub pick on this list.

Order a burger, a pizza and the Slovakian dishes, and choose the plant cheese, since the menu also runs dairy.
