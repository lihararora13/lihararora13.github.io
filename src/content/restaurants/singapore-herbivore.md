Fortune Centre is Singapore's little vegetarian mall, and Herbivore is its Japanese star: a calm dining room turning out sushi, maki and sashimi-style plates with no fish in sight. It is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, but the vegan range is deep and the kitchen will veganise dishes on request, so it earns its place honestly.

The avocado and crispy floss maki are the fun orders, the ramen and gyoza make it a proper meal, and the whole experience is a neat reminder that sushi night does not need seafood to feel complete.
