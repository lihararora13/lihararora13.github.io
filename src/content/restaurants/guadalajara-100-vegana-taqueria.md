100% Vegana Taqueria does exactly what the name says, on Chapultepec Sur in the heart of the vegan neighbourhood. Golden birria tacos, tofu sausage, jackfruit tamales, veggie burgers and jackfruit steak, all vegan, all made for sharing.

Order the golden birria tacos with consommé, it is the signature. Go in the evening, sit outside, and enjoy the Chapultepec buzz.
