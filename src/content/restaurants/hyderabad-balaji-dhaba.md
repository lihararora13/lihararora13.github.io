Balaji Dhaba made the list for a reason. A vegetarian Indian-Chinese dhaba with a long list of vegan-friendly starters, soups, noodles and curries, open till late. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Malakpet, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Veg noodles, Chana masala, Veg Manchurian if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
