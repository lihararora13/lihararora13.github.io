Veggie Pause is the antidote to tagine fatigue, a veggie bistro on Rue de la Poste with a varied menu and a 4.5 HappyCow rating. Moroccan starters, a Moroccan lasagna, a vegan pizza and gluten-free pasta are the picks, and it is open noon to 10pm daily.

Order the Moroccan lasagna and the vegan pizza. It is the easiest, most reliable stop for a proper veggie-forward meal in the city, and the cat sleeping in the corner is part of the furniture.
