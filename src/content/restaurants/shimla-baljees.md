Baljees sits at the heart of Mall Road, and it is the first name on every Shimla vegetarian list: a pure-veg restaurant renowned for its chole bhature, with a great view of the bustling street from the dining room.

Order the chole bhature without dahi and the veg thali without ghee. It is busy, it is central, and it is the most Shimla meal on this list.
