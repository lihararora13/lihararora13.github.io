PLANTA Queen is where you go when the occasion calls for a room with
a pulse. The River North space is moody and stylish, the menu is 100
percent plant-based, and the cooking is the kind of vegan sushi and
Asian fusion that makes skeptics go quiet. Bang Bang Broccoli is the
signature order, the truffle udon is rich without apology, and the
pressed rolls are legitimately spectacular.

Book ahead, dress a little, and order more than you think you want.
The cocktails are no joke either.
