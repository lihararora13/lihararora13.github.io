Curry Leaves has been feeding Nashik since 1995, and the Jehan Circle dining room shows it: outdoor seating, a banquet hall, live music, and a 4.3 star rating across 10,000 reviews. It is the city's big, confident pure-veg restaurant, open from 7 in the morning till 11 at night.

The misal pav is the order, with rumali khakra alongside, and the North Indian mains behind them. Skip the paneer butter masala or ask for it made without dairy, and the meal turns properly plant-based without losing any of its warmth.
