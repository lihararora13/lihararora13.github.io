Arabeska is an Arabic food restaurant on Luntz Street in Hadar with a variety of vegan dishes prepared on a separate surface and clearly marked on the menu. Vegan offerings include hummus dishes, baba ganouj, tabbouleh salad, other salads and stuffed grape leaves.

It is open Monday to Thursday until 8:00pm, Fridays until 6:00pm and Saturdays until 10:00pm. Order hummus with baba ganouj and stuffed grape leaves.
