Indian Accent is an Indian restaurant on 5a Avenida Sur, rated 4.0, cooking with all-organic ingredients under a chef from New Delhi. The menu has a large vegetarian section, most of which is vegan or can be made vegan on request.

Order a vegetable curry and dal, and ask for vegan naan. It is open every day, noon to 9 pm, and it is the best Indian food in Antigua for vegans.
