House of Nuttmeeg is a fully vegetarian gourmet restro-cafe on Rajpur Road, a unit of Vegetarian Diaries, with a bright blue dining room, arched windows and a menu that runs from avocado toast to pasta, burgers and stir-fried vegetable bowls.

The avocado toast on a slate board and the colourful stir-fried vegetable bowl are the orders, and the mocktails are the companion. Ask for the vegan version and the kitchen will keep it dairy-free.
