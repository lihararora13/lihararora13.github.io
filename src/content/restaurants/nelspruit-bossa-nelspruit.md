Bossa is a South African restaurant chain, and the Nelspruit branch in the Grove Shopping Centre at Riverside Park is the lively, family-friendly option in town. The menu is grill-heavy, but vegan burgers, salads and breakfasts are available.

Order the vegan burger and sweet potato fries. It is open for Christmas lunch and big family meals, so book at festive times.
