Botanist Cafe is the table to book first in Melaka. It began as Veggie Planet and rebranded as Botanist in November 2018, and it tops both the HappyCow community ranking and the Vegan Food Quest guide, with a perfect 5.0 from HappyCow reviewers. The cooking is whole-food and plant-based, no MSG, no preservatives, sweetened only with gula Melaka: monkey head mushroom satay, veggie rendang, sweet and sour, curried tempeh and a daily biryani special, plus nasi lemak, asam laksa and cendol. The room is all potted plants and calm music, a deliberate escape from the Jonker Street crowds.

It is vegetarian, not fully vegan, so say vegan when you order: one reviewer found less than half the menu vegan-friendly and needed a senior staff member to walk through the options. The address is both 41 and 43 Jalan Melaka Raya 8, the cafe spans the two units, which is why guides disagree on the number. It is closed on Tuesdays.
