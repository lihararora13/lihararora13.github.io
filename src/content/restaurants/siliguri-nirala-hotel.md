Nirala Hotel sits at Court More opposite the Head Post Office, and it is the old budget veg hotel of central Siliguri: a simple dining room serving thalis and North Indian staples to travellers, traders and court-goers. It is the kind of place that has fed the same streets for years without ever needing to advertise.

Order the veg thali, ask for no ghee and no paneer, and you have a cheap, honest meal in the middle of town.
