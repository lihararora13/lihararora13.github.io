Geet is Pretoria's upscale Indian option, a fine-dining restaurant in Brooklyn that uses organic, seasonal produce and lists its vegan choices separately on the menu. It is the dressiest table on this list, with a banquet room and a whiskey lounge alongside the main dining room.

Veg choices are clearly marked, so ask for the vegan section and order a thali or a vegetable curry with dal. It is open daily, until 10:00pm Monday to Saturday and 8:00pm on Sundays.
