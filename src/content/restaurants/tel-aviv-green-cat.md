The Green Cat has been converting cheese lovers since it opened as Israel's first vegan pizzeria, and its secret is the cashew mozzarella made fresh in-house every day. The base is thin and crisp, the tomato sauce is rich, and the toppings run from artichoke and mushrooms to seitan strips standing in for pepperoni.

Order by the slice or build a whole pie with different toppings on each half, grab a beer on tap, and if Levontin 7 next door has a show on, you have dinner and a night out in one doorway. Evening-only hours, so plan around them.
