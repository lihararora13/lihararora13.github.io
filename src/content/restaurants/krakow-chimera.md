Chimera calls itself the only romantic fast-food bar in the world, and it is hard to argue: a candlelit vegetarian salad bar in the Old Town, serving soups, salads, kombucha and coffee, with vegan options across the counter.

It is the light lunch on this list, open daily from 10am. Build a salad bar plate, add the soup of the day, and linger over a kombucha.
