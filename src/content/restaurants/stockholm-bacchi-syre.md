Bacchi Syre is not just lunch, it is a whole green headquarters: three floors at Jarntorget in the Old Town holding a vegan cafe and restaurant alongside bars, discussion stages, workspaces and an art gallery. Visit Stockholm singles it out as a green meeting place, and HappyCow, which lists it open with reviews running through August 2026, calls out the kind staff and comfort-food menu.

By day expect sandwiches, soups and pastries, with dinners on weekends and guest concepts popping up in the kitchen. The mac and cheese and the Korean sandwich both get raves, and the location, right in Gamla Stan, could hardly be handier.
