Boonsita Restaurant is a fully vegan buffet on Sathan Payabarn Road, opposite Chiang Rai Regional Hospital, reported fully vegan in March 2023. Walk 30 metres east from the hospital's front entrance and look for the large banner with the owner's photo and the jay signs. The room has a pleasant covered outdoor eating area and a lawn next to it, and it is open Monday to Friday and Sunday, 7:30am to 3pm.

Arrive early for the most food options, as the buffet runs down through the day, and fill a plate with the vegetable curries and mock meat dishes. It is inexpensive and the best vegan lunch in Chiang Rai.
