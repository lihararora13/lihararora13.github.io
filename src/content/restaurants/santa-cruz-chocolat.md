Chocolat is Santa Cruz's most surprising vegan-friendly room: a restaurant and bar on Pacific Avenue with a dedicated vegan menu, not just a few adaptable dishes. The vegan list runs from a Caesar salad and a garlic cheese dipping pot to a shiitake rice bowl, roasted beets, spicy broccoli, vegan mole, deep-dish pizza and desserts, with several kinds of hot chocolate. Pets are welcomed and the room is relaxed, so it works for a long lunch as well as dinner.

Hours are Monday to Thursday noon to 9pm, Friday to Saturday noon to 10pm and Sunday noon to 9pm. Ask for the vegan menu on arrival, and save room for the hot chocolate.
