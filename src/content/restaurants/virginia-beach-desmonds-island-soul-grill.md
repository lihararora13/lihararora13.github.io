Desmond's Island Soul Grill is an all-vegan restaurant on Reon Drive doing Caribbean and Soul food plates with no animal products anywhere in the kitchen. It is the strongest pick in Virginia Beach, and the Soul food plate is the dish locals mention first.

Order the Soul food plate with rice and peas and plantains. Note the distance: Reon Drive is west of the oceanfront, so plan the drive.
