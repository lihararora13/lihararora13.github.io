Minh Hien is the most famous vegetarian restaurant in Hoi An, on Thai Phien, cooking plant-based versions of the town's signature dishes: white rose dumplings normally made with shrimp, cao lau, crispy pancakes and fried wontons, with set menus that put several on one table. Diners also rate the stuffed tofu, fried tofu with passion fruit sauce and banana fritters, and most dishes are vegan and labelled. It is rated 4.7 from 1,672 Google reviews, and it is open 11:30am to 10pm daily.

Order a set menu to try the white rose, cao lau and wontons together, and bring cash, as card payments carry a 3% charge. It is the essential Hoi An vegan meal.
