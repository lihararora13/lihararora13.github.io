Kashew Cheese is the stop that makes Saigon's vegan scene feel genuinely special. Founder Le Na turned Vietnamese cashews into a full artisan cheese range, aged, fresh and cultured, using traditional cheesemaking methods with not a drop of dairy, and the Thao Dien deli is where you taste it all: paninis, Mediterranean-style sandwiches, sourdough pizza, pasta, and boards of cheese with local pepper and chilli.

It is fully vegan, and vegan travel writers call it their all-time favourite place in Vietnam. Order the La Vita Bella panini, build a cheese board, and take a tub of cream cheese home, because you will not find anything like this anywhere else in Southeast Asia.
