Artjuna made the list for a reason. A garden cafe in an old Portuguese house in Anjuna, with a lifestyle shop, yoga classes and a strong vegan menu, open all year. The kitchen cooks with care rather than leaning on shortcuts, and the room, in Anjuna, is the kind of place you end up recommending to friends before you have even finished eating.

Order the Buddha bowl, Hummus plate, Kombucha if you want the greatest hits, and ask what is seasonal or new, the best dishes here are often the ones the kitchen is most excited about that week. This is a table worth planning a walk, a tram ride or a whole afternoon around.
