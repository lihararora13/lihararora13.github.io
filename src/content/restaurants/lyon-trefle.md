Trèfle is the most exciting new opening in Lyon's vegan scene: a fully plant-based gastronomic restaurant that opened in September 2025, run by chef Emily Dader, trained at Institut Paul Bocuse and seasoned in starred kitchens from the Alps to Melbourne. The dinner menu is a fixed multi-course tasting menu that changes every month, built on raw ingredients like herbs, flowers, seaweed, mushrooms, spices and grains.

It is the only experience of its kind in the region, combining the precision of fine dining with a menu that is entirely vegetal, creative and sustainable. Reservations are required, lunch is shorter and simpler, and the wine and non-alcoholic pairings are worth the splurge. Book it for the special night.
