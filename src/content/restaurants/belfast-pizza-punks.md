Pizza Punks is the city-centre pizza pick for a vegan group: a loud, fun room on Waring Street where the vegan pizzas come loaded with meatballs, basil and a proper drizzle, and nobody at the table feels like they are eating the compromise option.

Order a vegan pizza to share and a garlic bread on the side. It is a meat-serving pizzeria with a strong vegan offering, so file it as vegan-friendly, and it is the best pick on this list for a mixed group that cannot agree on anything else.
