Rascal's is the restaurant this guide is built around: an all-vegan eatery on East Haley Street, established in July 2021, and since the closures of Oliver's, Fala Bar and Satellite, the last full-service vegan restaurant in Santa Barbara. The cooking is Mexican, handmade corn tortillas, elote, burrito bowls and tostadas, with a Loteria-card dining room and a Smiths poster on the wall. VegNews filmed it in August 2026 as the city's surviving vegan table, and the elote is the dish everyone photographs.

Hours are Tuesday to Friday 11am to 2pm and 5pm to 9:30pm, Saturday to Sunday 10am to 2pm and 5pm to 9:30pm, closed Mondays. Note the split lunch and dinner service, and that the restaurant no longer accepts Venmo. Order the elote and a burrito bowl.
