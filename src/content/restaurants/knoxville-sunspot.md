Sunspot is a longtime Cumberland Avenue favorite near the University of Tennessee, with a menu influenced by Southwestern, Caribbean, and Latin American flavors. Vegetarian and vegan diners have a real selection here, not just a salad and fries, from the orange blossom special to the black bean burger.

Order the orange blossom special with coconut rice, avocado, pico, and fried wontons, and the chickpea fries. Ask the server to confirm the vegan preparation of the tofu melt, since the standard version has cheese.
