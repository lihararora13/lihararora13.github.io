Khu Khu is where every Auckland vegan list starts, and the Ponsonby room earns it: familiar Thai favourites rebuilt completely plant-based, with organic Tonzu tofu, no fish sauce hiding anywhere, and desserts and drinks that are vegan too. Explore Auckland's October 2026 guide and Neat Places both put it right at the top.

Bring friends and order for the table: green papaya salad and kumara spring rolls to wake everyone up, tempeh pad Thai and the famous duck drunken noodles in the middle, and a curry to mop up. There are sister spots in Milford and Snickel Lane if Ponsonby is packed, which it often is.
