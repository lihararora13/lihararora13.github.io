Crush is Mérida's vegan fast-food restaurant, right on Parque Santa Lucía in Centro, serving vegan versions of local meals alongside vegan fast food. The burger is the dish the guides name first, with birria-style tacos and consomé, a mushroom wrap with potato wedges, and a chocolate brownie with ice cream behind it.

It is a central, casual spot, and one early-2026 review found the kitchen out of several dishes on a quiet day, so go at a busy hour if you can. The patio sits on one of the prettiest squares in Centro.
