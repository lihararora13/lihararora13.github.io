Why we love it — A Michelin star for a fully plant-based Latin American
restaurant in Shaw. That sentence alone deserves a moment of silence. MITA takes
ingredients from across Latin America and turns them into tasting menus so rich
and creamy you'll forget dairy ever existed.

This is the date-night reservation, the anniversary splurge, the place you bring
out-of-towners who think vegan means salad. Start with the tiny arepas, let the
tasting menu do the rest, and leave wondering why more restaurants don't trust
vegetables this much.
