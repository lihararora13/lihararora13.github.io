Lotus is Xi'an's vegetarian fine-dining table, a Black Pearl-recommended room on the second floor of Building F in the Hesheng Jingguang Center on Fenglin Road, in the Gaoxin tech district. Set menus run 399, 599 and 799 CNY a person, served course by course, and the cooking is mushroom-led: Wild Mushroom Garden, Gorgon Fruit with Sweet Peas and Peach Gum, a crispy cone of boletus mushrooms with dried apricots and quinoa, Hundred Mushrooms Braised Rice, and clay pot rice with truffle, plus braised eggplant with taro and walnut shortbread.

It is listed as vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan because the sources disagree: one guide says no eggs, dairy or honey are used, while others list egg custard among the dishes and note some items contain dairy and egg, so vegans should confirm each course when ordering. Do not confuse it with a meat-serving western restaurant also called LOTUS on Furong South Road; the vegetarian one is the Gaoxin address on this page. The pin is at the Hesheng Jingguang Center building, and the Amap rating is 4.8.
