Neue Taverne is Zurich's Michelin-starred vegetable tavern, a gastropub in the Old Town where chef Nenad Mlinarevic cooks a seasonal tavolata tasting menu with a full vegan option. The room is informal, with an open kitchen and a terrace onto a little square, and the cooking is inventive and precise.

It is the special meal on the Zurich list. Book ahead, order the vegan tavolata, and try the tempeh. The portions are small and the prices are high, but the cooking is worth it.
