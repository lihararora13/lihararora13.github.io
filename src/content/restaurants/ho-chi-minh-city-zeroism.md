Zeroism is the Thao Dien cafe that regulars visit pretty much every day, and one menu read explains why. Everything is 100% vegan, made with fresh organic ingredients and no MSG, and the kitchen bakes its own sourdough daily for pizzas and banh mi, whips up its own vegan cream cheese, condensed milk and mayonnaise, and still finds room for pad thai with a tangy fermented kick, a baked lemongrass mushroom burger, and a cheesecake that converts sceptics.

Come for breakfast, lunch or dinner and you will eat something completely different each time. Order the pad thai, save room for the french toast with ice cream if your sweet tooth wins, and grab a vegan condensed milk coffee on the way out.
