Yidaki Cafe is a second-floor room between Sannomiya and Motomachi, up a steep staircase, with big windows and a bright, spacious interior that suits a group. The food is mostly vegan, built around set meals: a mixed plate with rice, salad and soup, a sandwich set, a pasta set, and a small rotating selection of vegan desserts such as walnut tart, banana cake and carrot cake, with oat-milk coffee for non-soy drinkers.

HappyCow lists it as vegetarian rather than fully vegan, and a few dishes use honey or dairy, so specify vegan when you order. It is closed on Thursdays and open 11:00 to 21:00 otherwise.
