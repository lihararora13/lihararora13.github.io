La Vitaminica is a 4.9-rated vegetarian cafe and grocery on Huito, a few steps from the Ascensor Espiritu Santo, and its motto is that food should be medicine. The room serves tortas, coffee and healthy lunches, and the attached store sells natural products.

Order a torta and a cafecito, and browse the store after. It is open weekday daytime only, Monday to Friday, so plan it as a lunch stop rather than a dinner.
