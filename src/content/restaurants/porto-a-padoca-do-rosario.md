A Padoca is Porto's vegan bakery-cafe, and it is the breakfast on this list. Vegan eggs benedict, ricotta toast, shakshuka, and a cake counter with vegan nata, snickers croissants and Sintra pillows, all in a bright room in Cedofeita.

Order a vegan pastel de nata and a savoury dish, and go early, the good stuff sells out. This is the table that proves vegan pastry in Portugal is not a compromise.
