Why we love it — Mildreds has been doing this longer than most London
vegans have been alive. The Soho original opened in 1988, went
completely plant-based in 2021, and now anchors a little empire of six
London spots, but this is the one that started it all. The menu roams
the planet for inspiration, from Sri Lankan coconut curries to
chorizo arancini, and the seasonal refreshes keep regulars on their
toes.

Order the Taste of Mildreds sharing menu if you cannot decide, which
you will not be able to. The mushroom ale pot pie keeps coming back by
popular demand, and the sticky toffee pudding is the correct ending to
every debate. A genuine institution, and our number one in London.
