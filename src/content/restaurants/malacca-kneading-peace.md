Kneading Peace is the most distinctive cafe in this guide: a refugee-supporting training cafe where the kitchen doubles as a classroom, and the small menu runs to vegan pizza, vegan ice cream, drinks and rotating specials. It also hosts live events and baking classes, so the room has more life in it than a standard restaurant, and the money goes to a cause worth supporting.

The menu is small and it changes, so do not come with a fixed order in mind. It sits on the same street as Shui Xian Su Shi Yuan, which makes Jalan Hang Lekiu the best single street in Melaka for a vegan to wander down.
