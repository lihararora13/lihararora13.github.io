Lil' Nonna's is the vegan pizza truck that solved the melty
cheese problem. House-cultured mozzarella that actually stretches,
foldable New York slices, thick square Sicilians, all parked
outside Tweedy's Bar by the university. Pizza and a dive bar is a
combination nobody was complaining about.

The Beeteroni slice is the one to start with. Garlic knots are
mandatory. The Piña Jalapeño is for the brave, and the brave are
rewarded.
