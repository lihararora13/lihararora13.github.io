Brota is the upscale end of Botafogo's vegetarian scene, and it has the Michelin Bib Gourmand to prove it. The restaurant sits in a calm, plant-filled historic house, the cooking is hearty rather than precious, and the kitchen handles vegan requests directly and without fuss. Downstairs you order portions and the daily special; upstairs there is an all-you-can-eat buffet with vegetarian sushi, Thai curry, pasta, salads and veganized Brazilian dishes. Recent reviewers from June 2026 call out the baked pumpkin and falafel, the attentive staff and the cosy, date-night room.

It is closed on Mondays and runs lunch through late evening the rest of the week. Order the baked pumpkin and the falafel to share, ask the waiter what is vegan tonight, and settle in. This is the table to book when you want a proper dinner out.
