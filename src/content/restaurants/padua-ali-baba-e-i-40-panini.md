Ali Baba e i 40 Panini is a panini bar in the historic centre that local vegan explorers scored a perfect 10 out of 10, and it is the fast, cheap, satisfying stop on this list. The vegan panini are built properly rather than assembled from leftovers.

Order the vegan panini of the day and ask what is vegan on the board; the kitchen knows its vegan regulars by now.
