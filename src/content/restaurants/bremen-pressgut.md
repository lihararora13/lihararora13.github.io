pressgut is Bremen's vegan juice bar and café, a small, calm room on Sankt-Jürgen-Straße pressing cold-pressed juices and building smoothie bowls, sandwiches and light plant-based plates. Everything is vegan, everything is fresh, and the whole place runs at a slower pace than the burger counters across town. It is the morning stop on this list, and the one to pick when you want something light.

It keeps unusual hours, closed on Wednesdays and weekends, so check before you go. Order a cold-pressed juice and a smoothie bowl, take a seat by the window, and start the day properly. This is the table for a slow morning, not a big dinner.
