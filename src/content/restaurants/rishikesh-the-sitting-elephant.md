The Sitting Elephant is the table to book first in Rishikesh, and the guides agree: it tops the HappyCow community ranking with a perfect 5.0 and holds a TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice award for 2026, with recent reviews running through October 2026. The rooftop dining room sits on the 5th floor of the EllBee Ganga View Hotel, looking out over a five-kilometre stretch of the Ganges toward mountains that form the illusion of a sitting elephant, and the multi-cuisine menu runs from Garhwali dishes to pasta and Indo-Chinese.

Every dish can be veganised on request, which is the move here: ask for the veg kebab platter, the kurkuri bhindi and the aromatic soya nugget biryani without ghee or curd. Prices run a little higher than the Tapovan cafes, but the view, the live music in the evenings and the attentive service make it the special-occasion pick in town.
