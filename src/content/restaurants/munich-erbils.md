Erbils is a vegan döner shop, and it is exactly as good as that sounds. The Orient Lounge in Haidhausen has been serving seitan kebabs, pita sandwiches, Turkish pide, börek and lahmacun since 2015, with cold dishes lined up in the glass deli case and baklava and cakes for after. It is fast, cheap, generous and fully plant-based, the kind of place you end up returning to twice in one trip.

Order the seitan döner, add a lahmacun, and save room for baklava. This is Munich's vegan fast food at its best, and proof that the city's scene is not all white tablecloths and tasting menus.
