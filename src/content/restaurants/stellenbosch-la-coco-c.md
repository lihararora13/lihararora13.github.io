La Coco C is a healthy juice bar, coffee shop and eatery on Plein Street with clearly marked vegan dishes across the day. Breakfast brings pumpkin flapjacks, oats and granola and tofu scramble on toast; lunch brings bowls, curries, salads, nachos, wraps, a burger and mushroom bao buns, with plant milks for coffee.

It is open Monday to Friday until 5:00pm and Saturdays until 2:00pm. Order the tofu scramble in the morning or the bao buns at lunch.
