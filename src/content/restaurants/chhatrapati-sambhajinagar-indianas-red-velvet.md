Indiana's Red Velvet, also listed as Indiana Veg Restaurant and Cake Boutique, is Cidco's café and cake stop on Jalna Road, next to the New High Court. It is the sweetest room on this list, and the only one where dessert is the main event rather than an afterthought.

The café menu covers pasta, salads and sandwiches that veganise easily, and the cake counter is worth a look for plant-based bakes on request. Ask what is vegan on the day, because the bakes rotate.
