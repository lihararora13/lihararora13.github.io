Vegan Heaven is a dedicated vegan restaurant on Nguyen Van Cu in central Dalat, with Vietnamese breakfasts, noodle soups, hotpots, tofu dishes, vegetables, clay-pot preparations and rice meals. It is open around 9am to 1:45pm and 4:30pm to 8:45pm, and it is the strongest starting point for strict vegans in Dalat, as every dish is plant-based.

Order the noodle soups and a hotpot, and do not count on breakfast at 7am, as the kitchen opens at 9am. It used to be at 62 Phan Dinh Phung, so give your driver 43 Nguyen Van Cu, not the old address.
