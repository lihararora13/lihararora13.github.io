Neko Vegan House is the restaurant Penang's vegan scene is built around, and it tops the city's plant-based lists with a perfect score. Open since July 2022, it serves all kinds of vegan cuisine, local, fusion and international, ranging from rice and noodles to steaks and burgers, all of it fully plant-based. The room is small and friendly, and the kitchen clearly loves what it does.

Come hungry and order across the menu: a steak for the table, a burger for yourself, and noodles to share. This is the one to take someone who thinks vegan food in Penang means a sad salad.
