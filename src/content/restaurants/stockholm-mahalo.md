Mahalo is Stockholm's feel-good brunch headquarters: an all-vegan health cafe with two homes, this bright Odengatan original in Vasastan and a sister on Hornsgatan in Sodermalm. View Stockholm and HappyCow both rank it among the city's best vegan spots, and HappyCow reviews from September and October 2026 praise the fast, friendly service and food worth travelling for.

Come hungry for colour: acai and smoothie bowls, fluffy American pancakes, tempeh salads, loaded fries and a counter of raw cakes and pastries, all washed down with smoothies or an oat matcha latte. It is takeaway-friendly too, which makes it a very easy picnic upgrade.
