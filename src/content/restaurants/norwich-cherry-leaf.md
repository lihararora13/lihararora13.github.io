Cherry Leaf brings Vietnamese flavours to St Giles Street, with vegan pho, banh mi and salads on the menu. It is a bright, quick lunch spot in the Lanes.

Order the vegan pho and a banh mi. Ask for the vegan options to be made without fish sauce, which the kitchen does on request.
