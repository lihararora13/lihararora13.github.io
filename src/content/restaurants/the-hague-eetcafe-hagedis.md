Restaurant Hagedis has been cooking vegan food since 2004, as part of the De Grote Pyr living and working community, using organic, seasonal products. The menu runs from oyster mushroom bitterballen and a cashew fondue to butter bean soup, sage fritters and a pumpkin herb cake, with a relaxed dining room on the canal.

It is open Thursday to Sunday evenings only, from 17:00, so book ahead and plan the evening around it. Order the bitterballen and the cashew fondue.
