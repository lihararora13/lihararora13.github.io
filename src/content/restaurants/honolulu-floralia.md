Floralia is Hawaii's first wood-fired vegan pizzeria, and the Neapolitan-style pies coming out of its Kakaako oven are genuinely exceptional. Plant-based cheese and meat toppings, a proper wood-fired crust, and a room that feels like a neighbourhood pizzeria rather than a health food store.

It is open Thursday through Sunday evenings, so plan around it. Order a pie, add a salad, and enjoy the best vegan pizza in the Pacific.
