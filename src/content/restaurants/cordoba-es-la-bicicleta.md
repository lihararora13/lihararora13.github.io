La Bicicleta is a 100 percent veggie and vegan tapas bar on Calle Cardenal Gonzalez, near the Miraflores Bridge. The menu is tapas, all plant-based, and the room is small and lively.

Order the vegan tapas and the salmorejo without egg. A good evening pick in the old town.
