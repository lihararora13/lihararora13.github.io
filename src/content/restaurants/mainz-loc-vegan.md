Loc Vegan is an Asian restaurant on Bahnhofstrasse, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, offering sushi, wakame salad, spring rolls, summer rolls, Vietnamese noodles and more. It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, and HappyCow notes it was reported serving honey in July 2025, so verify the drinks.

Order the pho, the summer rolls and the vegan sushi, and ask about honey in the drinks before ordering. It serves lunch and dinner Tuesday to Sunday.
