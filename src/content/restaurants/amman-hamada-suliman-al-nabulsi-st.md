Hamada is Amman's fully vegan Middle Eastern restaurant, and it is gloriously unpretentious: foul, hummus, moutabal, baba ganoush, falafel, hummus fatteh, salad, bread, fries and tahini, served from six in the morning until one at night. It was reported fully vegan in November 2023, and it is the place for a proper Jordanian breakfast.

Order foul and hummus fatteh for breakfast, falafel for lunch, and come back for more. It is cheap, fast and exactly what a vegan in Amman needs.
