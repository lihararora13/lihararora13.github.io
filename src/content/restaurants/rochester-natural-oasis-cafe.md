Natural Oasis is Rochester's vegan Ethiopian cafe: a Black-owned room on Monroe Avenue where the daytime buffet lays out four or five dishes plus injera bread and soup until 4pm, and dinner switches to an a la carte menu. Everything is vegan, the stews are the lentil and vegetable dishes Ethiopian cooking does best, and the room is small and friendly. It is one of the six fully vegan places that give Rochester its unusually high vegan density.

Buffet hours are Monday to Friday 11am to 2pm, with dinner Wednesday to Saturday 5pm to 8pm. Come for the buffet if you can: it is the best way to taste the range, and the injera is the vehicle for all of it.
