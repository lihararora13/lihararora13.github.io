Providence Vegan Deli is the savoury sibling to Like No Udder, sharing the same Hope Street address and the same all-vegan kitchen. Opened in June 2024, it sells salads, sandwiches and daily specials, with pickup ordering through its website and delivery on DoorDash.

It is the quick-lunch pick on this list: order ahead, grab a sandwich and a salad, and you are fed in minutes. Check Instagram for the daily specials before you go.
