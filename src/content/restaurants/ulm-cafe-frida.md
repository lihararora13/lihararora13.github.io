Café Frida is a vegan and vegetarian café on the Judenhof, a short walk from the Münster, doing breakfast, a weekday lunch menu, cakes and drinks, all of it fresh and healthy. The bagels, the matcha latte and the carrot cake are the dishes regulars mention, and the room has the easy, bright feel of a café that belongs to its neighbourhood. It is the breakfast table on this list, and the one to start an Ulm day at.

It is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays, and opens at ten otherwise. Order a vegan bagel and a matcha latte, add a slice of carrot cake, and take your time.
