The No Catch Co is the most Brighton restaurant on this list: a fully vegan fish and chip shop right on the seafront, opened in October 2021 by vegan activist Earthling Ed. The menu is a faithful, plant-based take on the traditional chippy: vegan cod and chips, smoked vegan haddock and chips, Tofish burger and chips, tempura prawnz, plus sides and desserts, all of it 100% plant-based.

Grab your order and eat it looking out at the sea, which is the whole point. Order the vegan cod and chips, add the tempura prawnz, and do not skip the mushy peas.
