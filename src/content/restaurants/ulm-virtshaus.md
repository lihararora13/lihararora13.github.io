Virtshaus is the reason to eat vegan in Ulm: a fully vegan tavern cooking the Swabian food the region is famous for, all of it plant-based and house-made, from Zwiebelrostbraten and Kässpätzle to Vleischküchle with mushroom sauce, pretzel dumplings and Kaiserschmarrn. The V in the name stands for vegan, and the kitchen treats that as a starting point rather than a limitation. Reviewers call it some of the best food they have eaten anywhere, vegan or not, and the September 2026 reports back that up.

It reopened under new ownership in April 2026, serves dinner Tuesday to Saturday with a Saturday lunch service, and asks for reservations. Book ahead, order the Zwiebelrostbraten, and save room for the Kaiserschmarrn.
