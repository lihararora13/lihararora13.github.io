BROEI is a 100% plant-based living-room restaurant with a front garden on the Oosterkade, open every day from 09:00 to 23:00. The menu runs from breakfast and lunch to drinks and dinner: oyster mushroom bitterballen and kroketten, crispy cauliflower wings with barbecue sauce, fries with cucumber kimchi, okonomiyaki and curry bowls.

It is the reliable all-day pick on this list. Order the oyster mushroom bitterballen and a curry bowl.
