Loving Hut is an all-vegan restaurant on Jl. Sumbawa in Gubeng, rated 4.5 on HappyCow, open 11am to 9pm, serving vegan Indonesian and international dishes. It is part of the international Loving Hut chain, and it is the strongest fully vegan pick in Surabaya.

Order the vegan Indonesian dishes and tofu with rice, and try the vegetable dishes. It is inexpensive, central and reliable, and it is the natural first stop for any vegan visiting Surabaya.
