The Daily Planet is a downtown Burlington all-day restaurant, the kind of room that has been feeding the city for years, with a vegan-friendly bench that covers burgers, salads, and a rotating daily special. It is not a vegan destination on its own, but it is a reliable, central table when the group needs one.

Ask about the daily special first; it is often the most interesting vegan plate in the room.
