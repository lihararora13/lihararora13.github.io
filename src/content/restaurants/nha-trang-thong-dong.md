Thong Dong is a vegetarian restaurant on Nguyen Thien Thuat in central Nha Trang, with a western-leaning menu of salads, pizza and pasta, open from 9am. It is a useful change from the Vietnamese kitchens, and it is open daily from 9am to 8pm, though it can still be closed after 9:10am, so do not count on it opening on time.

Order the salads and a pizza or pasta, and ask for the vegan dishes by name. It is a calm room, and it suits a relaxed lunch.
