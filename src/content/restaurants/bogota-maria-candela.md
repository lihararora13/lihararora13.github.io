María Candela is the highest-rated vegan restaurant in Bogotá on HappyCow, a perfect 5.0 from over a hundred reviewers, and it earns every point by doing the thing Bogotá does best: Colombian food, fully vegan, with no apologies. The little café in La Candelaria serves ajiaco without the chicken, frijolada, tacos, empanadas and aborrajado, the sweet plantain fritters that usually come drowned in cheese, alongside fruit juices and kombucha. The walls are covered in animal-liberation posters and a bright pink wall of cartoon rats having a feast, so the personality is as loud as the flavours.

It is casual, cheap and open for lunch and dinner, which makes it the easiest first stop for anyone landing in the historic centre. Order the ajiaco, ask for the juice of the day (it might be tree tomato, and yes, it is delicious), and settle in among the posters.
