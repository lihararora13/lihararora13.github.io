Quinua is a sweet little gem in La Mariscal, a mostly vegan restaurant serving a set lunch of soup, main and small dessert. It is simple, wholesome, organic food, and it also runs cooking classes if you want to take the flavours home with you.

Order the set lunch, it is the whole point. Go at midday, eat well for very little, and enjoy one of Quito's best-value vegan meals.
