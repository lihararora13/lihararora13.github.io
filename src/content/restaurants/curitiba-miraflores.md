Miraflores is Curitiba's vegan gastro bar: a fully vegan kitchen pouring cocktails next to plant-based tapas and burgers, in a lively room that feels like a night out rather than a health-food stop. It is one of the best vegan evenings in the city, and a great pick for a mixed group that wants good food and good drinks.

Come for dinner, order tapas to share and a burger each, and stay for a second cocktail. This is the one for a vegan night out.
