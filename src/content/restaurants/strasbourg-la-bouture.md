La Bouture is PUR COOP's fully vegan bar and canteen on the Presqu'île Malraux, the modern waterfront quarter east of the Grande Île. The draw is a seasonal canteen plate, cooked from what the market has that week, with speciality coffee and mocktails, in a room that feels more like a neighbourhood bar than a restaurant.

It is the relaxed lunch or early-evening pick on this page. The menu changes with the seasons, so ask what the plate is today.
