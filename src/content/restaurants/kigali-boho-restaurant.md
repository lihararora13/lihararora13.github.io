Boho is a large rooftop restaurant on KG 623 Street serving African-fusion cuisine, with a vegetarian menu that includes vegan dishes and others that can be veganised on request. The vegan selection includes the chef's vegetable stew, cassava and kidney beans, coconut curry and smoothies made with coconut milk.

Order the cassava and kidney beans and a coconut curry. It is the rooftop evening option in Kacyiru, so come at sunset.
