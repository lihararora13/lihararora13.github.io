Melt Revival is Northside's neighborhood cafe and the city's best vegan-friendly brunch: an open and airy room on Hamilton Avenue cooking sandwiches, flatbreads, soups and salads from scratch with local and organic ingredients wherever possible. The menu is mixed, but the vegan dishes are labelled and serious, the vegan Rachel with seitan, the Artichoke Melt, vegan biscuits and gravy at brunch, and a harvest salad that regulars rate highly. It was voted one of Cincinnati's top three vegetarian/vegan spots in the 2026 Best of Cincinnati awards.

Melt serves breakfast, lunch and dinner Tuesday to Saturday, 9am to 8pm, with Sunday brunch 10am to 2pm, and is closed Mondays. The room is cozy and popular at weekends, so arrive early for brunch.
