La Recontra is the fun one: an all-vegan fast-food stall in a food court built from old shipping containers, sharing the space with a few other vendors and plenty of seating across two floors. The burgers are the draw, especially the La Crispy, and the vegan chicken sandwiches win over even skeptical omnivores, with plant-based takes on Peruvian classics alongside.

Come for a casual lunch or a late-night bite, because this is the rare vegan spot that feels like a street-food hangout. Order the La Crispy burger first and add a vegan chicken sandwich if you are sharing.
