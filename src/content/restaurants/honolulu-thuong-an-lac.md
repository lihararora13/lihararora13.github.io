Thuong An Lac is the Chinatown vegan spot that Vietnamese food lovers seek out: fully vegan, doing pho, spring rolls and rice dishes with the kind of authenticity that reviewers call some of the best Vietnamese vegan cooking on the island. The pho is the draw, and the spring rolls are the opener.

It is a neighbourhood restaurant rather than a tourist stop, which is exactly its charm. Order the pho, add spring rolls, and enjoy Chinatown after.
