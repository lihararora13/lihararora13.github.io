LN Fortunate Coffee is the vegan bakery-cafe Jakarta did not know it needed: a big, comfortable room with coffee made with plant milk, vegan ice cream and cakes, freshly baked breads, and a menu that swings from rice and noodles to burgers, pasta and pizza.

It is a franchise with a philosophy, Loving Nature, and the Duta Mas branch is one of its best-rated. Come for a slow coffee and a pastry, stay for a proper meal.
