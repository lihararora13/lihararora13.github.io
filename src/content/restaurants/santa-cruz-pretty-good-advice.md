Pretty Good Advice is Santa Cruz's vegetarian takeout counter, now with a downtown location on Pacific Avenue as well as the original in Soquel, and it is one of the most vegan-aware kitchens in the city: a vegan burger, tofu scramble sandwich, fries, soups and salads are all marked, the staff understand veganism and can offer suggestions, and the tofu banh mi-style sandwich was a standout of the 2026 Vegan Chef Challenge. The menu is creative, all-day breakfast included, and the prices are kind.

Hours are 9am to 6pm daily. It is takeout-focused with limited seating, so order the banh mi and eat it on the move or at the beach.
