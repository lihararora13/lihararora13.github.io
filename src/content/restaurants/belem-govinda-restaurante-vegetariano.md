Govinda Restaurante Vegetariano is a vegan-friendly brazilian spot in Batista Campos. Govinda is the vegetarian institution on Praça Batista Campos, rated 4.9 from more than 500 reviews, and its moqueca de banana and quiche de jambu are the plates that carry Amazonian flavour into a vegetarian kitchen.

Order the Moqueca de banana, Aloo gobi, Quiche de jambu, and Falafel. The moqueca de banana is the dish to judge it by; the quiche de jambu is the second.
