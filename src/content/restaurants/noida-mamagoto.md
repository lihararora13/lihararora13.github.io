Mamagoto brings the Pan-Asian fun to Sector 18: dumplings, Thai curries, noodles and chilli tofu in a bright, buzzy dining room. It is not a veg restaurant, but the veggie side of the menu is deep and the kitchen is used to vegan requests.

Order the veg dumplings and a Thai green curry made vegan, and ask for the noodles without egg. It is the liveliest table on this list, and a good one for a group with mixed diets.
