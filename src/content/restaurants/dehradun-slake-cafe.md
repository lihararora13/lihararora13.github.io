Slake Cafe on Rajpur Road is one of Dehradun's best healthy food spots, open from 10 AM, with bowls, salads and plant-friendly plates at the top of the range. It is the newer, shinier end of the Rajpur cafe strip.

Order the Buddha bowl and a cold coffee. Ask for the vegan version and the kitchen will keep it dairy-free.
