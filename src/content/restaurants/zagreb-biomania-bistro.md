BioMania sits on Tkalciceva, Zagreb's prettiest street, and it is the city's most complete vegan restaurant: breakfast, lunch and dinner, all fully vegan, all sustainable where possible. Toasts and pancakes in the morning, falafel bowls and pasta at lunch, burgers and Croatian classics at night.

It is the easiest first stop in the city, with two locations and a menu in English. Order the falafel bowl, try a Croatian dish you cannot get anywhere else vegan, and save room for dessert.
