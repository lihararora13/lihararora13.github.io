Pearth, Perth plus Earth, is the city's wholesome breakfast den. The West Leederville cafe has been cooking wholefoods since 2015, no refined sugar, no processed ingredients, and most of the all-day menu leans vegan or vegetarian: mushroom Benedict with crispy avocado and sunflower hollandaise, apple pie pancakes, a vegan burger that keeps getting better, and macro bowls that actually fill you up.

It is a vegan-friendly spot rather than a fully vegan one, so check the menu, but the plant-based options are the stars here. Finish with a slice of vegan cheezecake, which regulars order whole for birthdays.
