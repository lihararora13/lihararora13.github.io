Fo You Yuan Vegetarian is the old soul of this list: a Buddhist vegetarian restaurant in Liwan that has been trading for 34 years, reported open in February 2024 and rated 4.5 on HappyCow from 26 reviews. A September 2026 food vlog filmed inside the busy dining room shows the sweet-and-sour eggplant fish, crispy gold tofu and the signature coconut soup served in a whole coconut with lotus seeds and yams, made without added water or additives.

Order the coconut mushroom soup, the mock char siu and the vegetarian roast goose, and the lion's mane mushroom specialties. It is a vegetarian kitchen, so ask for the vegan dishes by name, and book ahead on holidays. Open 10am to 3pm and 5pm to 10pm.
