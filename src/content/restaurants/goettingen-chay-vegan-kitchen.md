Chay Vegan Kitchen is the table to start a Göttingen visit at: a fully vegan Asian restaurant on Goethe-Allee, a few minutes from the station, cooking Vietnamese-inspired food from original recipes. Udon bowls, bao buns, fried tofu, curries and tapas make up a menu where everything is plant-based, so there is nothing to negotiate and nothing to ask about.

It is open Tuesday to Sunday from noon until ten, closed on Mondays. Order an udon bowl and a round of bao buns, and if it is your first visit, know this is the one kitchen in town where the whole menu is yours.
