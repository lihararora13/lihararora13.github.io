La Bruja Vegan Foods is the top-rated vegan restaurant in Antigua, a perfect 5.0, and it has been all-vegan since 2017, inside the Centro Comercial la Fuente in a historic corner near Tanque la Union. The menu is modern and broad: burgers, wraps, tacos, bowls, empanadas and salads, plus smoothies, yerba mate and coffee, with books and games for guests.

Order a burger, add tacos or an empanada, and finish with a smoothie. It is open noon to 9 pm, closed Wednesdays, and it is the most expensive table on this list, which is still cheap by international standards.
