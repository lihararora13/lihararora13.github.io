Ariya Nivaas is the pure-veg restaurant on Manorama Road by the station, and it is on the local vegan community's own list of Trivandrum rooms where a vegan can eat well. The format is classic station-quarter tiffin: dosa, idli and meals, from 6:30 in the morning.

Order the masala dosa without ghee and the veg meal without curd. It is the station-side standby, and the vegan community vouches for it.
