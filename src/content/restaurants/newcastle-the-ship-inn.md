The Ship Inn is the pub to book first in Newcastle if you eat plants. The whole food menu is vegan, the bar pours local ales, and the room sits in the Ouseburn valley, a short walk from the city centre but a world away from the Bigg Market crowds.

Order the Sunday roast if you land on a Sunday, the beer-battered tofu fish and chips on any other day, and save room for the sticky toffee pudding. Food runs to 5pm on Sundays, so plan lunch rather than a late dinner.
