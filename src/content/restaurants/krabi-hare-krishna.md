Hare Krishna is a plant-based restaurant in Ao Nang with a high Google rating, serving relaxed vegan and vegetarian international dishes. It is one of the newer names in the Ao Nang scene, and it fills a useful gap between the fully vegan kitchens and the Indian restaurants.

Order the vegetable curries and rice dishes, and ask which plates are vegan. Its pin is approximate to Ao Nang, so check the address before you walk over.
