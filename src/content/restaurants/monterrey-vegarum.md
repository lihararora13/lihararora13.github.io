Vegarum has been Monterrey's vegan pizzeria since 2014, and it is still the first name locals give. Pizza, pizzaburgers, hot dogs, falafel, burritos and tacos, all vegan, all in a friendly room in Centro.

Order a pizza and a pizzaburger to share, it is the signature move. This is the table that proves Monterrey does vegan comfort food as well as anyone.
