Manohar Dairy is a Bhopal landmark, the kind of place generations of the city have eaten at without thinking twice. Yes, the name says dairy, and yes, much of the menu leans on it, which is exactly why it earns its place here with a clear instruction rather than a blind recommendation: order the veg thali without ghee, the poha, the samosa and the chaat without curd, and you eat very well for very little money.

It is an institution, not a café, so expect bustle, steel plates and speed rather than soft lighting. Come hungry, order plainly, and enjoy one of the cheapest good meals in the city.
