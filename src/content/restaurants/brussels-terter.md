Terter is a vegan bistro on the Chaussee de Waterloo with a loyal following and a menu built around house-made tofu. Sandwiches, pastries, granola, snacks and desserts fill the counter, and there are guestrooms upstairs if you want to stay the night.

It is open daily and works for breakfast, lunch or an early dinner. Order a tofu dish, add a pastry, and take a sandwich for the road.
