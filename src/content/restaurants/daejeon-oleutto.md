Oleutto is a restaurant in Daeheung-dong, Jung-gu, with vegan-friendly dishes among its menu, open for lunch and dinner from Tuesday to Sunday. It sits in the same old-centre pocket as Orto and Sei Vegan, so it works as a backup when the fully vegan kitchens are full.

Ask the staff which dishes are vegan on the day, and order the vegetable and rice dishes. It is closed on Mondays, so do not plan a Monday visit.
