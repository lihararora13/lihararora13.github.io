Evergreen Indian Cuisine is Eugene's Indian vegan pick: a restaurant on West 7th Avenue with a clearly labelled menu that makes vegan ordering straightforward, vegetable coconut korma, chana masala, aloo gobi and lentil dal. It is the evening Indian table in this guide, open Monday to Thursday 11:30am to 9:30pm, Friday to Saturday 11:30am to 10pm and Sunday noon to 9:30pm.

Order a korma and a dal, and ask the staff to confirm the vegan preparation: the menu is labelled, but the kitchen will happily talk through it.
