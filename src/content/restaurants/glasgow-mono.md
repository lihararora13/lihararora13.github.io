Mono is the institution: Glasgow's most famous fully vegan restaurant, open since 2002, a café, bar and live music venue sharing a roof with the Monorail Music record shop in Merchant City. The menu is comfort vegan at its best, to-fish suppers, mac and cheese, loaded burgers, cheesy pizza, with local beer and home-brewed soft drinks.

It is dog and child friendly, it hosts everything from baby discos to record fairs, and it is the beating heart of Glasgow's vegan scene. Order the to-fish supper, browse the records, and stay for the gig.
