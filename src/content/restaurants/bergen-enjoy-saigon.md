Enjoy Saigon is a vegan-friendly Vietnamese restaurant on Klostergaten in Nøstet, and it is the noodle pick on this page. The vegan spring rolls are the signature, with udon, pad thai and pho alongside, all of it fresh and all of it quick.

It is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so order the vegan dishes clearly. The spring rolls and the pho are the orders.
