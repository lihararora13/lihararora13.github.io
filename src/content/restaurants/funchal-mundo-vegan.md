Mundo Vegan, also signed as Vegan World, is the one place in Funchal where the entire menu is yours. It sits in the Lido area in a low modern block, all plants, bright chairs and hand-painted signs about making a difference, and the room runs on bowls, burgers and baking rather than ceremony. Recent visitors in mid-2026 came back daily for the rainbow bowls and the cakes.

Order the sea rainbow bowl with rice, tofu, avocado and sesame, the white bean hummus on toast, a vegan burger with potato wedges, and the strawberry cheesecake if it is on. It is open for lunch and dinner every day except Sunday, and the terrace is the best seat in the house on a warm evening.
