Bo and Zin is the special dinner on this list: an evenings-only restaurant out on the Route de l'Ourika with a whole separate vegan menu. The vegan courses run from buckwheat and quinoa croquettes and dim sum to green vegetable curry and couscous marrakchi, with vegan desserts to finish. Book a table and a taxi, as it is out of the medina.

This is the place for a proper night out. Order the green vegetable curry, which the published menu lists at 240 MAD, and let the evening stretch.
