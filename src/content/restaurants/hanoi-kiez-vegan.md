Kiez is the one every 2026 guide leads with, and it earns it: a vegan coffee shop, bistro and butcher all in one, tucked into a Tay Ho lane near West Lake. The menu is a joyful pile-up of Western comfort food built on chay techniques, mushrooms standing in for meat, textures thought through, nothing an afterthought. Burgers, kebabs, nuggets, sandwiches, cakes and ice cream, plus Vietnamese dishes and fusion curveballs like a banh mi kebab.

The butchery counter sells burger patties, sausages and spreads to take home, and the American-diner branding against the understated interior gives the place real character. Pair it with La Studio nearby for a full West Lake vegan day out.
