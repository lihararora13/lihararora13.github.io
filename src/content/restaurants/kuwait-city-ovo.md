OVO is a vegan, vegetarian and flexitarian restaurant in Shuwaikh, with a plant-lined outdoor corridor, a sunny patio under a tree and a calm, aesthetic room. It is the most photogenic vegan-friendly room in Kuwait City, and the broccoli salad is the dish reviewers mention by name.

Order the broccoli salad and the vegan main of the day. There is a second branch in Bneid Al Gar, which is closed on Sundays, so check before you go.
