Anavrin is Surat's healthy-eating stalwart, recently moved into a bigger space on Ghod Dod Road and reopened in September 2026. The menu is built around bowls, salads and juices, which puts most of it within easy vegan reach.

It is a good reset button between Surat's richer meals: a grain bowl, a green salad and a cold-pressed juice, eaten slowly in a calm room. Note the paneer dishes are on the menu too, so order from the plant-based side of the board.
