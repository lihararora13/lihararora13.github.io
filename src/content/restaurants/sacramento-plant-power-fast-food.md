Plant Power Fast Food is vegan fast food without the apology tour: burgers, chickin tenders, nuggets, wings, fries and shakes, all of it plant-based, served fast on R Street with a drive-up window. The Big Zac is the signature burger, the tenders are the sleeper hit, and the oat milk shake is the finish.

It is the pick for the road trip, the late lunch, and the friend who insists vegan food cannot do fast food. It can, and here it does it better than the chains it is winking at.
