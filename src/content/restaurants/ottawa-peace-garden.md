Peace-Garden has been Ottawa's calm vegetarian corner since 1992, a coffee shop near Parliament Hill with fountain-side seating and art by Sri Chinmoy on the glass walls. The menu runs from smoothies and fresh juice to daily specials, an Indian thali plate and a veggie burger.

It is the most peaceful meal on this list. Order the thali, add a fresh juice, and take your time. The room doubles as a venue for meditation concerts and poetry readings.
