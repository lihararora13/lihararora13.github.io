Takoyaki is Osaka's defining snack and, by definition, it is made with octopus and fish stock, which is why this tiny shop matters so much. OKO Takoyaki is run by a one-woman army flipping out a veganized version: the batter is made with okara, the leftover pulp from soybeans, which also makes the whole thing gluten-free, and the balls come topped with your choice of sauces, traditional style first if it is your first time.

The room is small, loud, graffiti-covered and always busy, so get there when it opens. Beyond takoyaki there are kushikatsu sets with sauce pairings and small sides like edamame and soft tofu. Note the address carefully: the all-vegan takoyaki shop is the Higashishinsaibashi one, while the Dotonbori OKO okonomiyaki branch serves meat alongside vegan options.
