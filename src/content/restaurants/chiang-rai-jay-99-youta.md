Jay 99, also listed as Youta, is a simple vegetarian eatery and shop on Trairad Road, south of Overbrook Hospital, making local Thai dishes to order, some with vegetable substitutes. It is a small, local kitchen, and the dishes are made to order rather than buffet style, which makes it a good change from the carts.

Order a Thai dish to order with vegetables and rice, and ask for it vegan. It is inexpensive and central, and it suits a quick dinner.
