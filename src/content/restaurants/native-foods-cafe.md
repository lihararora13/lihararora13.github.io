Native Foods is the last Colorado holdout of the all-vegan fast-casual
chain, and it is still holding up its end. The Glendale location keeps
the classics moving: the Gastropub Burger, the Real Nashville Hot
sandwich with serious heat, and shakes that belong in the conversation
about the state's best.

It is the reliable one in this guide. No reservations, no theatrics,
just a hundred-percent plant-based fast-casual menu that works for
lunch, dinner, and that post-hike craving window.
