Krawummel has been Münster's fully vegan restaurant since May 2013, the oldest plant-based kitchen in the city, and still the one every guide starts with. The menu is written on a large chalkboard and runs to bagels, salads, burgers, soups, falafel, hayati, cupcakes and desserts: a falafel sandwich with peanut butter dressing, an avocado roll, chili sin carne and seasonal cakes, with no table service, just order at the counter and take your plate to a seat.

It sits five minutes from the station on Ludgeristraße, open Sunday to Thursday until eight and Friday and Saturday until nine. Order the falafel sandwich first, save room for a cupcake, and treat it as Münster's vegan institution.
