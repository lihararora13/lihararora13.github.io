Bickering Twins is a cafe on North Cayuga Street in downtown Ithaca, doing sandwiches, salads, and brunch plates with vegan options. The address here is 114 North Cayuga Street; some listings say North Aurora Street, which is wrong.

Order the vegan sandwich and a salad. It is a daytime stop, so plan it for lunch rather than a late dinner.
