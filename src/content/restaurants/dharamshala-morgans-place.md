Morgan's Place up in Dharamkot is the view pick: a colourful cafe with one of the better vistas in the hills, doing vegetable pizza and Continental plates from 10 AM to 11 PM. Local guides name it for views and for the walk up.

Order the vegetable pizza without cheese and a salad bowl. It is the table to plan an afternoon around, and the climb is part of the experience.
