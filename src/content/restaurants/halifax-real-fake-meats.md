Real Fake Meats is Halifax's first plant-based butcher shop, opened in January 2019 on Gottingen Street and run by a Top Chef Canada alum. The counter sells handcrafted vegan meats and cheeses to take home, and the kitchen turns them into comfort food: a seitan veggie donair, mac n cheeze, hunni garlic crispy wings and a s'mores cookie are the dishes visitors keep naming.

The menu rotates every couple of weeks, and the shop hosts monthly brunches, so check the current board before you go. It is open Tuesday to Sunday, with late hours on Friday and Saturday.
