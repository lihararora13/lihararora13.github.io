Sidra sits next to the entrance of the Qaitbay Citadel, which makes it the natural lunch stop after the fort, and it serves Lebanese and Mediterranean food with vegan-friendly dishes. HappyCow reviewers rate it 4.0 and it is currently open.

Order hummus, tabbouleh and falafel. Come for the citadel and stay for the mezze, and expect a tourist-facing menu and price.
