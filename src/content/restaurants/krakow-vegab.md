Vegab is Krakow's vegan kebab legend: a tiny hole-in-the-wall on Starowislna Street that calls itself Poland's only 100 percent plant-based kebab shop, and backs it up with a 4.8 rating. Two sizes of wraps, different veggies and sauces, plus soba noodles, falafel and hummus.

The Hamster kebab, with peanut butter and coconut sauce, is the signature order. Get a wrap, add the tzatziki, and eat it standing outside like a local. This is the must-try on the Krakow list.
