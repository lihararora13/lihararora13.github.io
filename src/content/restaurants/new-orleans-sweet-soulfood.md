Sweet Soulfood is the crown jewel of New Orleans vegan eating, and it is not particularly close. The Black-owned Treme kitchen runs a cafeteria-style line with a menu that rotates daily, which means every visit is a little gamble and the house always wins. Staples like cashew mac and cheese, jambalaya, collard greens, and okra gumbo show up most days, while the weekly specials (eggplant lasagna on Tuesdays, BBQ cauliflower on Wednesdays, stuffed bell peppers on Thursdays) are worth planning your week around.

This is the place that proved vegan soul food is not a compromise but a genre of its own. The dining room is unfussy, the portions are generous, and the bread pudding with rum sauce has ended more meals than any dessert in the city has a right to. If you eat exactly one vegan meal in New Orleans, make it this one.
