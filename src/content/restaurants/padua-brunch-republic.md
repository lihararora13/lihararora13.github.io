Brunch Republic is a modern casual chain with a Padua branch on Piazzetta della Garzeria, and it runs a dedicated vegan hour every evening from six to eleven, with a shareable vegan brunch platter, pink and yellow vegan burgers, wraps, pancakes, avocado toast, vegan meatballs and fruit bowls, plus red and green vegan cocktails.

Order the vegan brunch platter for the table, a pink or yellow vegan burger and the pancakes, and come in the vegan hour, when the menu is at its fullest.
