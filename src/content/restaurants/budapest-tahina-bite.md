Tahina Bite is the falafel counter the Jewish Quarter needed: fully vegan since July 2020, everything freshly made, kosher and halal, with falafel plates, bites, sandwiches, salads and bowls plus dips and fries on the side. It holds a 4.5 from nearly 200 HappyCow reviews, and it sits right where you want it, on Wesselényi utca in the middle of the ruin-bar district.

It is fast, cheap and generous, the kind of place that turns a late-night wander into a proper meal. Get the falafel plate if you are hungry, a sandwich if you are walking, and a bowl if you want to pretend you are being healthy about it.
