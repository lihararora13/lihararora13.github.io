Miss Rachel's Pantry is the coziest power move in Philly dining: a supper club where you book a seat at the table and get a five-course, entirely vegan, Italian-leaning feast made with homestyle charm and fine-dining technique. It holds a 4.9 rating because the ricotta-stuffed cappellacci and trumpet mushroom scallops are that good.

It is BYOB, it sells out, and it feels like dinner at your coolest friend's house, if your coolest friend made cashew-truffle cream from scratch. Reserve well in advance and bring a bottle worth the occasion.
