Taste of Vegan Soul is the soul-food sleeper hit of the Charlotte scene, a 100% vegan kitchen tucked inside the City Kitch food hall. The BBQ Ribbz are the dish that made its name, ranked among the best vegan ribs anywhere by one global guide, and the mac n cheez, Royal Smash burger and peach cobbler are the supporting cast that regulars refuse to skip. The menu changes regularly, so the chalkboard is always worth a read.

It holds a 5-star HappyCow rating from travellers who call the chef the sweetest in the business, and it is open Wednesday through Sunday. Come for the ribbz, leave with cobbler.
