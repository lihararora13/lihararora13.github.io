Brother Bon is the family-run spot Northcote locals brag about to their city friends. Every dish is plant-based, but the flavours are so spot-on that even devoted carnivores admit they don't miss the meat for a second.

The menu hops across Asia with total confidence: laksa rich with coconut broth, noodles with real wok hei, and a katsu sando worth crossing town for. Book ahead on weekends, this little gem fills up fast.
