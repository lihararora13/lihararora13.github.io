Vegan Cafe Kiu is the trip out of central Kobe that rewards you: a fully vegan organic café in a renovated old Japanese farmhouse on the owner's family property in Nishi Ward, all tatami seating and calm. The lunch is a one-plate meal, brown rice, a daily main built on seasonal vegetables, salad, soup and small dishes, with gluten-free cakes for dessert, and reviewers praise the Kiu Plate and the vegan hamburger steak with grated daikon.

It relocated here from Kitanocho, so use the Iwaokacho address, not the old central one. It is open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 17:00, and it is a lunch-only kind of place, so plan it as a slow midday stop.
