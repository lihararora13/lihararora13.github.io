Ganga South Indian is the kind of place that never tries to impress you and wins you over anyway: a vegetarian-only counter at Akota Char Rasta, open from 8 in the morning till 11 at night, serving South Indian food at around 200 rupees for two.

Dosa, idli and medu vada are vegan by default, just skip the ghee roast and the curd on the side. It is the cheapest meal on this guide and one of the most satisfying, especially for breakfast.
