Dosa Dosa began as a festival food stand and now holds court in Mala Strana as HappyCow's top-rated vegan restaurant in Prague. The thin, crispy dosas are made the traditional way from idli rice and urid lentils, naturally gluten-free and vegan, and the little bistro pairs them with curries, daily menus and homemade cakes in a cozy hidden garden.

Order the masala dosa first, pick your spice level with care, and finish with a mango lassi and a slice of lemon and blueberry cake. It is a short walk from the Lennon Wall and an easy favourite for vegans and non-vegans alike.
