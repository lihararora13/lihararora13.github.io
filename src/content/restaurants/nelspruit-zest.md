Zest is a Sonpark restaurant that locals recommend for relaxed, romantic dinners, and it is one of the quieter rooms in the city's main restaurant strip. It is a general restaurant, so vegans are asking the kitchen rather than ordering off a vegan menu.

Call ahead and ask what the kitchen can do vegan that night. It is the softest pick on this list, included because Mbombela has so few options, so treat it as a backup.
