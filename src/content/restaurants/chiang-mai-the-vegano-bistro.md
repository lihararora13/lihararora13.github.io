The Vegano Bistro is the one pick worth a Grab ride for. A family opened it in Hang Dong in 2020 after the founder's sister was told to go plant-based for her health, and the kitchen cooks like a family that fell in love with the food rather than a restaurant chasing a trend. The menu runs Thai and Italian side by side: vegan calamari, pesto spaghetti, aglio e olio, carbonara, pad Thai and black pepper tofu, with cakes, slices and dark chocolate brioche to finish.

It is about fifteen minutes south of the Old City, handy on the way to the airport or Doi Suthep, and it holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow. Book ahead if you can, it is a small room and it fills up.
