Abou Tarek is the most famous koshari house in Cairo, and koshari is the dish to build a trip around: rice, lentils, macaroni and chickpeas under fried onions and tangy tomato sauce, with garlicky vinegar and chilli oil on the side. It costs around a dollar, it is filling and addictive, and it is vegan as long as you ask bidoun samna.

Go downtown, queue like everyone else, and order the koshari with extra chilli oil. It is the single most Cairo meal on this list.
