Madreselva holds a perfect 5.0 on HappyCow, and it earns it the friendly way: burgers, arepas, baked goods and fresh juices served in a bright Teusaquillo room with outdoor seating, every day from nine in the morning to four in the afternoon. It comes from the same owners as Flor de Loto, relocated from Cali and an earlier Bogotá address, and the kitchen leans into the comfort-food end of vegan cooking without ever getting heavy-handed about it.

It is the kind of place that makes a neighbourhood feel looked after. Drop in for a late breakfast, grab a burger and a juice, and take a bag of baked goods for the road.
