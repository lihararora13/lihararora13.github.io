The heavyweight champion of Melbourne vegan dining, no contest. Smith & Daughters has been converting sceptics since 2014 with Latin-leaning plates that are so bold and cheesy and unapologetic that omnivores leave quietly cancelling their weekend roast plans.

Settle into the brick-walled dining room (next door to its sibling, Smith & Deli) and let the banquet do the talking. The charcoal 'chicken' and paella fritters are the stuff of local legend, and the cocktail list punches just as hard as the kitchen.
