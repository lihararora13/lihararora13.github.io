Crocus Cafe has been a Lenton institution since 2004, run by volunteers as a social enterprise on eight core values: community, volunteer-led, healthy and affordable, vegetarian and vegan, fairtrade, organic, local and eco-friendly. Any profit goes back into local community projects, and the room, mismatched vintage crockery and wooden tables, feels like it.

The shawarma seitan wrap and the mezze are the plates regulars rate highest, and the rotating global small plates keep the menu moving. It is a vegetarian cafe with a deep vegan bench, so file it as vegan-friendly, and note it is closed Mondays and Tuesdays.
