La Bartola is a proper Sevillian tapas restaurant in Santa Cruz where the plant-based options are clearly marked and genuinely good. It is the kind of place you take someone who wants a classic Seville evening, with vegan food that holds its own next to everything else on the menu.

Order a spread of vegan tapas for the table and share everything. It is the most traditional-feeling dinner on this list, and the one that feels most like Seville itself.
