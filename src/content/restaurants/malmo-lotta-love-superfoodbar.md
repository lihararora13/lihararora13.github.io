Lotta Love Superfoodbar is a fully vegan superfood bar on Davidshallsgatan in Davidshall, a few doors from Farm2Table. The açaí bowl is the signature, with a cajá bowl and grilled sourdough with avocado alongside, all of it plant-based.

It is the light lunch or afternoon bowl on this page. Order the açaí bowl and the grilled sourdough.
