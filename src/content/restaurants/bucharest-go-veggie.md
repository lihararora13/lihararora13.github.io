Go Veggie is the all-vegan and healthy-eating restaurant on Bulevardul Dacia, rated 4.5, serving plant-based mains, bowls and salads at a low price. It is the quiet pick in the guide: a straightforward vegan kitchen without the raw-food framing of its neighbours.

Come for a simple, cheap vegan lunch. The menu is fully plant-based throughout, so there is nothing to decode.
