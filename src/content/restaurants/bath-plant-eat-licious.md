Plant.Eat.Licious sits below street level on Lower Borough Walls, a compact fully vegan cafe that mixes comfort food with raw desserts and juices. It is the kind of place locals use as a weekday lunch stop rather than a special occasion.

The burgers and buddha bowls are the main event, with raw cakes for afterwards. It is small and informal, so expect a quick counter order rather than a long sit-down meal.
