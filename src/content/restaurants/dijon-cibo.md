Cibo is a fine-dining restaurant on Rue Jeannin, and it is listed here as vegan-friendly only, because the regular menu serves pigeon, wild boar, escargots and beef bourguignon. The kitchen will cook a vegan tasting menu on request, and it does it well.

Book ahead and say vegan when you book, so the kitchen plans a full vegan menu. Do not walk in expecting a vegan restaurant.
