Vijayawada's vegetarian scene is having a moment. Flames & Pots opened in November 2026 as the city's first purely plant-based Asian restaurant, doing veg chicken and veg mutton curries and biryanis across fifteen Asian cuisines. The rest of the city is classic Andhra veg: big pure-veg dining rooms like Kailash Parbat and Paushtik, the new Yum Cafe in Patamata, and tiffin rooms that open before dawn.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and one of the eight is fully plant-based. Start with the mock-meat biryani at Flames & Pots, then Kailash Parbat for chaat and chole bhature, The Yum Cafe for thatte idli, and NSM Tiffins for a 6:15 AM breakfast. A note on the traps: paneer, ghee and curd run through the Andhra veg menus here, so the plant-based pick is the safe harbour and the rest need a word with the kitchen.
