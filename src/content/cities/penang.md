Penang already has one of the great food cultures on earth, and its vegan side is just as generous. The best tables cluster in the old streets of George Town, Lebuh Chulia, Lebuh Victoria, Lebuh Dickens and Jalan Nagore, with one seaside detour to Tanjung Tokong, and they cook Malaysian food first: laksas, curries, fried mee, banana leaf spreads and steam buns, alongside sushi, burgers and falafel for when you want a change of pace. This is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than tourist traps, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food at all.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian with a big vegan buffet. Start with a vegan steak at Neko Vegan House, a cake at Brown Poodle, vegan sushi at Plant A Seed, a chef's special at Pinxin, an economic rice plate at Ee Beng, a plant-based bowl at Kommune, a falafel wrap at Falafel Penang, and a pay-by-weight plate at Evergreen.
