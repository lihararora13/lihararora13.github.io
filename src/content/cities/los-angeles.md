Los Angeles did not invent vegan food, but it invented the idea that vegan
food should be excellent. This is a town where a fully plant-based tasting
menu sits next to a $10 breakfast burrito, and both are taken completely
seriously. Every corner of the map has a claim: WeHo for Italian and Mexican,
DTLA for Vietnamese, Echo Park for dim sum, the Valley for pizza.

We ranked eight spots, all of them fully vegan, no substitutions, no apologies.
Bring an appetite, bring sunscreen, and bring friends who think tofu is boring.
They will not survive lunch with their opinions intact.
