Rishikesh is vegetarian by law inside the municipal area, so eating meat-free here takes no effort at all, but eating vegan takes a little asking: most kitchens cook lacto-vegetarian and will happily leave out the ghee, paneer and curd if you say so. The best tables cluster in two pockets, Tapovan up the hill, where the yoga-adjacent cafes sit, and Laxman Jhula by the river, where the older cafes and bakeries have been feeding travellers for decades. Expect dosa and idli for breakfast, thalis and Indo-Chinese for lunch, and rooftop dinners with the Ganges below.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian kitchens with serious vegan options. Start with the veg kebab platter at The Sitting Elephant, a blueberry cheesecake at Cookie Lady, a dosa at Anna's Mess, the high-protein pancakes at Beatles Cafe, and a raw plate at Soul Kitchen. A note on the traps: Cookie Lady's old Neelkanth Road spot is permanently closed, it now trades from Deccan Valley Gate in Tapovan, so use the address on this page, and in the vegetarian spots say no ghee and no curd and the kitchen will know exactly what you mean.
