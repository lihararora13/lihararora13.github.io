Mumbai's vegan scene mirrors the city itself: sprawling, loud, a little
chaotic, and full of surprises. This is the city where a 100% vegan cafe
chain runs a flagship on Hill Road and another in Bandra Kurla Complex, where
a tiny store in Khar turned into the cafe its Jain regulars swear by, and
where you can eat vegan butter chicken one night and dance to it the next at
a plant-based rooftop.

We researched the scene and ranked eight spots that earned it, from
oil-free whole-food thalis to vegan cocktails at 1 am. Eat well, then take
the local train home.
