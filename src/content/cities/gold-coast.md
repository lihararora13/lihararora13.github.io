The Gold Coast has quietly built one of Australia's strongest vegan scenes, and it covers the whole coast from Southport to Reedy Creek. There is a fully vegan bakery, a vegan yum cha house, the Gold Coast's only plant-based izakaya, a gluten-free garden canteen in the valley, and brunch cafes that turn acai bowls into art.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian with a dedicated vegan menu. Start with Vietnamese pancakes at Greenhouse, a filled doughnut at Made With Love, vegan yum cha at Easy House, and ramen at Izakaya Midori. Book Greenhouse ahead, it is small, popular, and worth planning around.
