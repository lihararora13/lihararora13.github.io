Nazareth is an Arab city where vegan food is not a trend but the baseline: falafel, hummus, foul and tahini are the everyday food, and the best vegan meal in the city is a falafel from a shop that serves nothing else. Around that sit the Levantine restaurants of Al-Bishara Street, a sushi bar with marked vegan dishes, a kunafa bakery with a vegan pastry, and the cafe chains, so a vegan eats well from breakfast to midnight.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with a falafel at Falafel Wisam Jabali, hummus and falafel at Abu Ashraf, and the vegan mains at Luna. A note on the traps: Koshari Elmasry on Haifa Road is listed as temporarily closed and is not on this list, and the Bustan Maryam shop is a grocery selling vegan products rather than a restaurant.
