Würzburg's plant-based scene clusters tightly in the Innenstadt, mostly within a few streets of the Juliuspromenade, which makes it one of the easiest cities in this set to eat your way across on foot. The two fully vegan anchors sit almost next door to each other: Vrohstoff, a vegan and gluten-free café-restaurant on the Theaterstraße, and Chay Viet Tadilen, a Vietnamese kitchen whose whole food menu is vegan. Around them are the falafel wraps of Veggie Bros, a lunch-only imbiss, a döner stand, a burger chain with a serious vegan range, a salad deli, and a regional restaurant near the Residenz.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with a caprese bagel at Vrohstoff, phở at Chay Viet, a falafel wrap at Veggie Bros, the lunch plate at Sir Quickly, a vegan döner at Vöner, a vegan burger at Hans im Glück, a salad bowl at Kaya, and the vegan Auflauf at Auflauf. A note on the practicalities: Vöner is a cash-only stand with no seating, Sir Quickly serves lunch on weekdays only, and at Chay Viet the food menu is fully vegan while individual drinks are worth a quick check.
