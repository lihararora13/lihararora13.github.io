San Francisco does not do anything halfway, and that includes plants. This
is the city where vegan sushi stopped being a punchline, where a
Michelin-noted izakaya builds rolls out of tomato and mountain yam, and
where your next taco might come with a side of live Andean flute music.
We cross-checked a dozen local guides, food press roundups, and review
sentiment to land on eight spots that keep winning, from the Outer Sunset
to North Beach. Bring a jacket, bring an appetite, leave your fish excuses
at the Golden Gate.
