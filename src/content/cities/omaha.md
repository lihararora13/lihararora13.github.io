Omaha's vegan scene is spread across the city's neighborhoods rather than concentrated downtown: Benson for the all-vegan bakery, Countryside Village for the plant-based cafe, Blackstone and the Old Market for the bars and sushi, and Millard out west for the Indian kitchen. The list leans on vegan-friendly kitchens with real vegan benches, and the two all-vegan anchors are both small, owner-driven rooms.

Start with the BBQ jackfruit fries at Veg.Edible, a bowl at Rooted Table, and the jackfruit biryani at Kinaara, then work through Kitchen Table, Nite Owl, and Blue Sushi. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan.
