Goa's vegan scene is the oldest and loosest in India, it has been feeding travellers plant-based food since the nineties, and it still runs on garden cafes, yoga spaces and beach shacks rather than chains. The north holds the classic vegan cafes of Anjuna and Vagator, Panaji holds the plant-forward Fontainhas crowd, and the south holds the slow beach cafes of Agonda and Palolem.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian or vegan-friendly kitchens with real vegan menus. Start with a tofu burger at Bean Me Up, a Buddha bowl at Artjuna, a jackfruit earth bowl at Greenr, and a gluten-free vegan pizza at Zest. Goa's kitchens are used to vegan travellers, just say the word and they will cook around dairy without blinking.
