Chiang Rai's vegan scene runs on Thailand's jay tradition: Buddhist vegan cooking with no meat, no fish sauce and no pungent vegetables, served at buffet counters and small shops around the city centre. The anchors are Boonsita, a fully vegan buffet opposite the regional hospital, Mee Mii, a vegan buffet cart, and Oasis Vegetaurant, a large vegetarian restaurant behind the bus station. Around them sit a vegan burger cafe, a jay eatery and a vegan buffet cart, so a plant-based traveller can eat cheaply and well for days.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with the buffet at Boonsita, the cart dishes at Mee Mii, the buffet at Oasis, and a Kunda burger. A note on the traps: Si Trang was reported in December 2024 to have limited vegan options, so it is left out; and the Wiang Pa Pao listings that HappyCow folds into Chiang Rai results are an hour south and are not counted here.
