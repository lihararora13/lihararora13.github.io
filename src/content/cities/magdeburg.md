Magdeburg is not a city that shouts about its vegan food, which makes the finds here all the sweeter. The scene clusters in the Altstadt around the cathedral and the Breiter Weg, with two more gems out in Stadtfeld Ost and one in Sudenburg, and it covers the full range: a plant-based fine dining room, a fully vegan Asian restaurant, two cafes where the vegan baking is the point, and the burger, tapas and pizza spots that keep a mixed table happy. Three of the eight picks are fully vegan, and the rest carry clearly labelled vegan dishes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the seasonal menu at Botanica, the mix maki at Vegan Living, a cinnamon roll at Cowvana, a vegan soup at Pastel Blumencafe, a vegan burger at Peter Pane, the vegan tapas at Berner and Brown, a Classico Vegan pizza at Basta, and the chalkboard vegan dishes at Rigo's. A note on the traps: Botanica is dinner only and wants a reservation, Pastel Blumencafe opens Thursday to Sunday only, Basta is an evenings-only pizzeria in a rear courtyard, and Rigo's vegan offering is a changing chalkboard rather than a fixed menu, so ask what is on.
