New Orleans was built on flavor, and the plant-based kitchens here understood the assignment. This is a city where gumbo, po'boys, red beans, and beignets all have vegan versions so good nobody asks what's missing. Soul food is the backbone of this guide: Treme's Black-owned vegan kitchens are doing some of the most exciting cooking in the country, period.

All eight picks are verified open as of October 2026. The game plan: a rotating cafeteria plate of soul food on North Broad, jackfruit ribs under the Claiborne Expressway, pastries at the all-vegan Oak Street bakery, a midnight hot sausage po'boy in the Quarter, buffalo tofu in the Bywater, a "Better Than Popeyes" sandwich in Gentilly, chaat bowls and bagels in the Bywater, and vegan beignets in the historic French Market. Laissez les bons temps rouler, plants and all.
