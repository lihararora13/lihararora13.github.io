Lagos has one of the most exciting young vegan scenes in Africa, because Nigerian food was halfway there already. Jollof, efo riro, egusi, amala and yam porridge all translate beautifully to plant-based cooking, and the city's dedicated vegan restaurants, VeggieVictory since 2013 and Thrive, Lagos' first fully vegan restaurant, have been proving it for years. Add Lekki's wellness cafes and Victoria Island's Thai and Japanese counters, and a vegan weekend in Lagos is genuinely delicious.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with vegan jollof and shawarma at VeggieVictory, an alkaline plate at Thrive, a smoothie bowl at Earth Blend, and vegan sushi at Shiro. A note on the traps: delivery-only kitchens like Michelle's Vegan Kitchen are wonderful but have no sit-down address, so they do not make this list of places to go.
