Tulum's vegan scene is the densest in Mexico, and almost all of it sits within a few blocks of Avenida Tulum in Centro, with the beach road adding smoothie bowls and pricier cafés. The cheap eats are the story: fully vegan taco stands doing soy pastor, chorizo and barbacoa for under 50 pesos a taco, a tamalería across the road from the best taco stand in town, and a late-night truck that only gets going after dark. La Veleta, the newer neighbourhood south-west of Centro, adds the burrito icon Burrito Amor, which moved there from its old Centro spot.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegan-friendly. Start with the soy-spinach ball tacos at El Bajón, the pastor tacos at La Hoja Verde, the mole tamales at Suculenta, the banana-leaf vegan burrito at Burrito Amor, and a smoothie bowl at Matcha Mama. A note on the traps: Raw Love, the old raw-vegan pioneer, announced its permanent closure in late June 2026 and is not listed here; Co.ConAmor's kitchen may have stopped serving food, so it is not listed either; and Tierra inside Holistika is not fully vegan despite its billing, so it is skipped too.
