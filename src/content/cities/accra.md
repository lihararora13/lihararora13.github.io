Accra is not the easiest vegan city in the world, which is exactly why its vegan spots feel like discoveries. The scene is built on Ghanaian food itself: fufu, waakye, jollof, red red and groundnut soups are plant-based at heart, and the city's dedicated vegan restaurants, led by Tatale in Osu and Asaase Pa in Jamestown, turn them into proper dining. Add a few health-food cafes and Lebanese falafel counters, and a vegan can eat very well here.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the sharing platter at Tatale, a fufu and palm nut soup, a waakye at Mahorgany, and a falafel platter at Moka's. A note on the traps: the Mahorgany branch at Afrikiko is listed as possibly temporarily closed, so only the main Jungle Avenue spot makes this list.
