Greenville does vegan food quietly and well, mostly downtown and along the Stone Avenue and Augusta Road corridors, with a small all-vegan core surrounded by cafes and kitchens that take plant-based orders seriously. The scene is young, led by a fully vegan lunch cafe and a comfort-food shack, and it rewards anyone who looks past the barbecue joints on Main Street.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Start with the wild mushroom pho at Sun Belly Cafe, the Nashville hot chicken sandwich at Vegan House Shack, a build-your-own bowl at Green Fetish, and the falafel plate at Ji-Roz. A note on the traps: Naked Vegan is a food truck with a roaming schedule rather than a fixed dining room, so it is not listed as a sit-down pick; several older guides still list The Anchorage and other closed downtown spots, and Green Fetish is vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, so order the vegan-marked bowls.
