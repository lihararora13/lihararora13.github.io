Charleston is a food-obsessed city, and its vegan scene is finally catching up with its reputation: a plant-based Italian deli that opened in 2024, a vegetable-forward kitchen in Harleston Village, a Filipino restaurant that moved to Rutledge Avenue in 2025, and a Cantonese sister restaurant where more than half the dishes are vegetarian or vegan-friendly. The scene is young, and it is growing fast.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with the lasagna at Three Girls on Spring, the Basic Bowl at Basic Kitchen, the avocado toast at Harbinger, the lumpia at Kultura, the scallion oil noodles at Beautiful South, a vegan curry at Ma'am Saab, the crispy avocado taco at Taco Boy, and a vegan scoop at Off Track. A note on the traps: Annie O Love's, Gnome Cafe, and Neon Tiger all appear on older vegan lists but are closed, Annie O Love's shut in October 2026, Gnome in September 2022, and Neon Tiger in March 2024, so none of them is listed here, and Kultura now trades from 267 Rutledge Avenue, not its old Spring Street address.
