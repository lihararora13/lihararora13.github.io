Bruges eats vegan in its medieval centre, where three fully vegan restaurants anchor a scene of a plant-based boutique hotel breakfast, a vegetarian lunch room and Indian and Tibetan dinners. The picks are all inside the old town, walkable from the Markt, and the local guide The Bruges Vegan has verified each one in person since 2013.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with breakfast at Blackbird, the tapas etagere at Atelier Flori, the vegan menu at Kosmopoliet, and the Indian dishes at Garlic N Greens. A note on the traps: Atelier Flori relocated since it opened in 2019, so use the current Jozef Suveestraat address; Kosmopoliet opened as a vegetarian restaurant in January 2025 and went fully vegan in spring 2025; and the vegan people's kitchen VolXkeuken cooks only on the first Saturday of the month, so it stays off the list.
