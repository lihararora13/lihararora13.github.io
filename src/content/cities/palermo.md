Palermo eats vegan in the historic centre and the Politeama grid, where Sicily's naturally plant-based cooking does a lot of the work: panelle, caponata, pasta with vegetables and granita are vegan by tradition, and the dedicated kitchens cluster around Via Ricasoli, Via Maqueda and Piazza Verdi. The scene runs from a 100% vegan organic restaurant to a plant-based bistrot, a bio shop with a kitchen, and a 200-year-old focacceria.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan carbonara at Haiku, the seasonal plates at MiniM, the millet meatballs at Verdechiaro, and the panelle at Antica Focacceria San Francesco. A note on the traps: panelle are sometimes fried in lard, so confirm the oil before ordering; In Cottura, another Palermo vegan kitchen, could not be pinned to a verified address, so it stays off the list; and MiniM is filed vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, since its own description says plant-based cuisine without confirming the whole menu is vegan.
