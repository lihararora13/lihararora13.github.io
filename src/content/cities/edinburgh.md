Edinburgh does vegan food with the same drama it brings to everything else: cobbled closes, candlelit dining rooms, and a vegan haggis with whisky sauce on more menus than you'd ever expect. The best tables cluster in the Old Town and Southside, around New Town's Howe Street and Elder Street, and down in Bruntsfield and Tollcross, and they cook everything from Malaysian laksa and Vietnamese pho to Roman cacio e pepe and proper Scottish comfort food, all of it plant-based. This is a scene of long-running institutions and fiercely loved small rooms rather than chains, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who came for the castle and stayed for the plants.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan haggis at David Bann, the laksa at Soul Vegan, a bucatini cacio e pepe at Sora Diana, a pulled jackfruit burger at Holy Cow, a Pizza Serena at Novapizza, a Black Rabbit bagel, the lion's mane mushroom steak at Hendersons, and a special pho at Non Viet Vegan. A note on the traps: Sora Lella on Brougham Street closed permanently in June 2025 and its menu moved into its sister restaurant Sora Diana, Seeds for the Soul in Bruntsfield closed in November 2025, and the old Sen Viet Vegan is now trading as Non Viet Vegan at the same Brougham Place address.
