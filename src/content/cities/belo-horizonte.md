Belo Horizonte, the capital of Minas Gerais, is famous for its food, and its vegan scene is built the same way: generous buffets, honest cooking and places that feel like home. The best tables cluster in Centro, Savassi, Santa Tereza and Barro Preto, and they lean heavily on the pay-by-weight buffet, a Minas tradition that happens to suit vegans perfectly, alongside burger bars, a vegan cafe with a cultural programme, and a feminist restaurant-bar pouring craft beer. This is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than tourist traps, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food at all.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with a burger at Quintal Vegan, a burger on brioche at Las Chicas Vegan, garlic bread at Seu Trem, pancakes at Casa Umbigo, the dish of the day at Villaveg, a buffet plate at Pe de Couve, a pay-by-weight plate at San Ro, and a pay-by-kilo plate at Mesa do Sabio.
