Detroit's vegan scene took a hit in the pandemic and is rebounding with real energy: the city lost some terrific spots, but the ones that remain are strong, and new ones keep opening. The food leans comfort, soul food, burgers, pizza, and coneys, with a Corktown and Midtown core and a Livernois corridor worth the drive.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly with serious vegan menus. Start with a catfish tofu sandwich at Detroit Vegan Soul, a loaded cheeseburger at Trap Vegan, a smashburger at Street Beet, a vegan pizza at Pie-Sci, a breakfast wrap at Seva, a plant-based plate at The Kitchen, a coney at Chili Mustard Onions, and a gyro pizza at Grandma Bob's. A note on the traps: Unburger Grill is in Dearborn, not Detroit, and Nepantla Cafe is possibly temporarily closed, so neither makes the list.
