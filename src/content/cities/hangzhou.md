Hangzhou has been cooking Buddhist vegetarian food since the Tang and Song dynasties, and the modern scene has only raised the stakes: Michelin recognition, Bib Gourmands, and a wave of fully vegan kitchens around Lingyin Temple and West Lake. This is a city where a tasting menu and a temple noodle bowl can both be the best meal of the trip, and where mock lamb and truffle fried rice are a craft, not a compromise.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with strong vegan menus. Start with the black truffle fried rice at Qing Chun by Perma, dan dan noodles at Qīng Chún, spinach noodles in mushroom broth at Zhī Zhú, sweet and sour lotus root at The Lakeside Veggie, and the buffet at Suxin Vegan. A note on the traps: Qingchunli at the Kerry Centre, the second-ranked venue on older lists, was reported closed in November 2026, so do not follow older guides there; and there are two Qingchun venues with similar names, so use the exact addresses on this page. At Zhī Zhú and Jinglianju, ask for the vegan dishes by name, as both are listed vegetarian in some guides.
