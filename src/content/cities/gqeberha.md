Gqeberha (still Port Elizabeth to most locals) does not have a vegan district, it has a handful of committed independents scattered between Walmer, Richmond Hill and the beachfront. The anchors are plant-based cafes rather than restaurants: Baobab Wholefood Cafe in Walmer and the Kindred Kitchen in Richmond Hill, the city's first plant-based restaurant, with Aruna Foods and Bhakti Lounge backing them up, and the food leans wholesome rather than junky, porridge and wraps and smoothies alongside curries and burgers.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the macadamia and amaranth porridge at Baobab, a plate at the Kindred Kitchen, a cupcake from Aruna Foods, and the thali-style spread at Bhakti Lounge. A note on the traps: several older guides still list venues that have moved or changed shape, Sixth Nature relocated from Summit Avenue and is pre-order only, and Balkan Burger at the Boardwalk, a good vegetarian-burger fallback, is held as a reserve for this round.
