Calgary's vegan scene is deep, friendly and spread across the whole city, from Inglewood's cafe strip to the northeast markets and the Beltline. Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Chinese kitchens sit next to bakeries and burger joints, and most of them are fully vegan rather than merely vegan-friendly.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan. Start with khao soi at Hearts Choices, sushi at WholeLifeGo, tacos at Vegan Street, pho at Tamarind, ginger beef at Padmanadi, ginger beef noodles at Happy Veggie House, a croissant at Canela, and tempeh shawarma at The Coup.
