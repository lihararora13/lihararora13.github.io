Venice has exactly one fully vegan restaurant, which changes how you plan the whole trip, and this list is built around that fact instead of pretending otherwise. The eight picks cluster in Cannaregio, Dorsoduro, San Polo, Santa Croce, San Marco and Castello, and they run from a bookable all-vegan dining room to a standing-room bacaro, a comic-shop bar and two artisan gelaterias. You eat well here by knowing where to head before you set off, because plenty of places will hand you a side of vegetables and call it dinner.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the aubergine lasagne at La Tecia Vegana (book before you fly), the vegan cicchetti at H2NO, the chana masala at Ital India, and the mezze at Frary's. A note on the traps: Sullaluna is vegetarian, not vegan, with dairy, egg and honey on the menu, so order from the vegan dishes and ask about the cakes; Gelatoteca SuSo, the famous gelateria by the Rialto, is held as a reserve and its vegan Pistacchiotto is worth the queue on a second visit.
