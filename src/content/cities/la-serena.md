La Serena has one of the strongest all-vegan shares in this region: four of its eight picks are fully vegan, including a plant-based restaurant on Amunátegui, a plant-based kitchen in a shared collective space, a vegan food truck and a vegan store. The scene sits in the colonial centre and along Avenida del Mar, and the cooking leans on Buddha bowls, vurgers and empanadas.

The Buddha bowls at Desde la Raiz and the plant-based plates at Salicornia are the two stops to build around, with the 108 food truck and Govindas' vegan empanadas as backups. Two traps were caught: Desde la Raiz's second location at Cuatro Esquinas 100 is temporarily closed, so only the Amunátegui 690 address is used, and Colectivo Mandala shares Salicornia's address and is the same collective space, not a separate pick. Vurgers shares Desde la Raiz's address and is the same venue, and Pies en la Tierra is temporarily closed.
