Tulsa's vegan scene clusters downtown and along Route 66: a 100% plant-based burger joint, an all-vegan juicery with two locations, a Route 66 bakery with vegan donuts, and Thai, Mexican and ramen kitchens with named vegan dishes. It is compact enough to cover in a day.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Start with the OG burger at Ruby D's, the lion's mane mushroom sandwich at Inheritance Juicery, a vegan donut at Big Baby, and the vegan puffy tacos at Elote. A note on the traps: Ediblend closed for good on 31 October 2026, its final trading day, so older guides listing it are stale. The Tropical and The Vault are vegan-friendly with mixed menus, not all-vegan.
