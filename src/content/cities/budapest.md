Budapest splits itself in two across the Danube, and the vegan scene does the same: most of the best tables cluster in Pest, in the walkable triangle between Ferenciek tere, Deák Ferenc tér and the Jewish Quarter, with a couple of gems across the river in Buda. Between them you can eat bean goulash, vegan lángos, falafel, gyoza, wood-fired pizza and proper vegan cakes, all within a few metro stops of each other.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with Napfényes for Hungarian classics by Ferenciek tere, Vegan Garden for a big all-vegan menu near Deák, and Szabad Bisztro for gastropub energy on Király utca, then try Goli for a Bib Gourmand dinner, Happy Veggie for dumplings near Keleti station, Tökmag for street-food burgers, Tahina Bite for falafel in the Jewish Quarter, and Vegazzi for Neapolitan pizza. A note on the traps: Kozmosz closed in September 2025 and Epoch Vegan Burger closed in July 2025, and the Las Vegans truck at Street Food Karaván has shut, so none of those made the cut.
