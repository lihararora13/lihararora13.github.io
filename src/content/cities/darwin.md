Darwin's vegan scene is small and scattered around the city centre, Parap and the night markets, with poke bowls, Vietnamese food, Indian food and a fully vegan African stall. The anchor is Africa Soul Food, a 100% vegan and gluten-free stall at the night markets, with Pokelicious and Fat Tuna in the city centre and Non La and PepperBerry nearby, and Indian, Thai and market stalls filling out the map. The scene is small enough to cover in a weekend, and the best meals are the poke bowls and the market stalls.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the egusi stew at Africa Soul Food, the vegan tofu bowl at Pokelicious, the salad bowls at Fat Tuna, and the eggplant curry at Non La. A note on the traps: Africa Soul Food is a market stall, not a restaurant, so check its market days before you go; and the Indian and Thai restaurants use dairy and fish sauce, so vegans should specify no dairy and no fish sauce.
