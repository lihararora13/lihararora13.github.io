Amman rewards vegans who know the words for it, because Jordanian food is already full of plant-based classics: falafel, hummus, foul, tabbouleh, fattoush and zaatar manakish are vegan as long as no yoghurt sauce sneaks on top. On top of that, the city has a small but serious dedicated vegan scene, led by Carob, which HappyCow ranked among the ten best vegan restaurants in the world, and the fully vegan Hamada.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with Thai curry at Carob, foul and falafel at Hamada, a shared-table falafel at Hashem, and sunset mezze at Wild Jordan Center. A note on the traps: Bayt Sara, once the city's most famous vegan restaurant, is listed as temporarily closed, so it is not on this list, and dairy shows up constantly in Jordanian cooking, so say bidoon samna wala laban, without ghee or dairy.
