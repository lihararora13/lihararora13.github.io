Exeter's vegan scene is small, friendly and centred on Fore Street and the Cathedral Green, with a fully vegan cafe in the old McCoys Arcade, a vegan deli on South Street and a vegetarian institution that has been going since 1990. It is a city where the vegan rooms are independent, daytime-led and easy to walk between.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start at Sacred Grounds in McCoys Arcade, How on Earth on South Street and Cake or Death for bakes. One practical note: The Flat only opens Wednesday to Saturday evenings, so plan it as a dinner rather than a lunch stop.
