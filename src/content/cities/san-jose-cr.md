San Jose is the gateway to Costa Rica, and most travellers treat it as a transit hub, but the capital has a thriving vegan scene worth a short stop. The best tables cluster downtown and in Barrio Amon, where a Taiwanese-run vegetarian soda and a Vietnamese-Costa Rican fusion cafe sit a block apart, and in Barrio Escalante and San Pedro, where fully vegan burger joints, brunch bakeries and vegetarian kitchens have opened in the last few years. The local building blocks are rice, black beans, plantains, avocado, tofu and oyster mushrooms.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with the dumpling soup at Susbida, a banh mi at Cafe Rojo, the Crispy Cheddar Vegan Chick'n burger at Huacamole, an oyster-mushroom dish at Arbol de Seda, a brunch plate at PURALMA, a bowl at El Buho, a soup at Sopitas, and a vegan burger at Pots & Bowls. A note on the traps: at ordinary sodas, always ask whether the rice and beans were cooked with chicken stock, lard or butter; La Esquina Vegana, which appears in older roundups, is in Heredia, not San Jose, so it does not make this list; and Gia Vegan Pastries and Bosque Urbano Cafe are both reported closed.
