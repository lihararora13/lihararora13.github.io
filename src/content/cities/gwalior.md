Gwalior is a city of forts and old-city lanes, and its vegan scene splits neatly in two: the new cafés of Patel Nagar and City Centre, all booths and buffets and peri peri fries, and the old city's thali houses, where ₹300 still covers two people and the cooking has not changed in decades. Seven picks made the list, and together they cover both halves of the city honestly.

All seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, and an honest note: none of the seven is a strictly all-vegan kitchen. Gwalior's scene is pure vegetarian with ghee and paneer at its heart, so every pick here is vegan-friendly, and the ordering move is always the same, no ghee, no butter, no curd, no cheese, no paneer. Start at Bistro 57, take the Theori buffet, and finish in the old city at Atithi Bhoj, where the thali costs less than the coffee did.
