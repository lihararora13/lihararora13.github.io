Hamilton does vegan food with the same energy it does everything else: loud, generous and a little bit rebellious. Ottawa Street North is the anchor, with The Hearty Hooligan's all-vegan junk food across the road from where Sweet Beets used to be, and Locke Street South adds donuts and soup, while Westdale adds plant-based bowls and Main Street West adds South Indian dosas. The scene is compact enough to eat across in a weekend, and the best meals are the comfort ones: a pizza pocket, a French dip, a dosa, a donut.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegan-friendly. Start with the Mac & Cheese Bake at The Hearty Hooligan, a Happy Buddha bowl at Lettuce Love, a Tuscan Pollo sub at Nonno's, a Breakfast Sandy at Coven Market, and a masala dosa at Annalakshmi. A note on the traps: Sweet Beets on Ottawa Street closed permanently in 2022, so do not follow older guides to 295 Ottawa Street North; Democracy on Locke Street is also permanently closed; and The Burnt Tongue has two locations, so use the Locke Street address on this page.
