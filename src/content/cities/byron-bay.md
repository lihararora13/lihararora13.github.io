Byron Bay might be the easiest town in Australia to eat vegan, because here plant-based is not a niche, it is the default. The best tables cluster within a few sunny blocks of Jonson and Fletcher Streets, with one leafy detour out to Ewingsdale Road, and they cook the Byron way: fresh, colourful, a little bit hippie, and genuinely delicious. Burgers, curries, tacos, smoothie bowls and raw slices all show up, and nobody bats an eye when you ask for the vegan version, because it usually is the version.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegetarian or omnivorous with serious vegan menus. Start with the kalamari at No Bones, a Thai peanut curry at Manna Haven, a garden wrap at Folk, a vegan toasted sandwich at Combi, a raw slice at Santos, a burger at The Top Shop, tacos at Miss Margarita, and a burrito at OzyMex. A note on the traps: Elixiba is permanently closed, so it does not make the list despite its old reputation.
