Savannah has one fully vegan kitchen, and it is a good one: Hungry Vegan, a soul food counter on East Lathrop Avenue, is the table to start with, and everything else on this list is a vegan-friendly room that marks its vegan dishes on its own menu. The scene clusters in the Historic District and the Starland District, and it is walkable, compact, and best done on foot.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with the Big Vegan Soul plate at Hungry Vegan, the vegan tater tot nachos at Kayak Kafe, the vegan breakfast burrito at The Sentient Bean, a veggie burger at Green Truck Pub, a vegan bagel at Big Bon Bodega, a vegan scoop at Leopold's, a pastry at Auspicious, and a dairy-free pop at Savannah Square Pops. A note on the traps: Fox & Fig, the city's first all-vegan cafe, closed in late 2023, and any list that still names it is out of date, so it is not listed here.
