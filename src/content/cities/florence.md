Florence does vegan food the way it does everything else: with serious respect for the original dish. The best tables cluster in the Oltrarno across the river, where Santo Falafel stays open until midnight and Raw Vegan Firenze bakes buckwheat pizza with cashew mozzarella, and in the centre, where Il Vegano Bistrot serves lasagne, pasta, burgers and tiramisù and Nirvana cooks a truffle carbonara in a family-run trattoria. The scene is small but confident: six of the eight picks are fully vegan, and the two vegan-friendly ones have been cooking vegetarian food since 1981.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegan-friendly. Start with a falafel wrap at Santo Falafel, the truffle carbonara at Nirvana, a buckwheat pizza at Raw Vegan Firenze, a lunch box from Giumella, and the lasagne at Il Vegano Bistrot. A note on the traps: Tripperia Vegana TAN8's flying kiosk on Viale Guidoni is temporarily closed, so it is not listed here despite topping older guides; and Il Vegetariano is cash only and closed Sunday, so bring euros and plan around it.
