Barcelona has quietly become one of Europe's great vegan cities,
and the scene here refuses to be precious about it. From wood-fired
pizza terraces to a cat-plastered craft beer bar, our eight picks
cover tapas, sushi, thalis, and the best vegan burgers in town.

Grab a beachside table in Poblenou, a sangria in the Born, and a
sushi roll in the Eixample. Barcelona is ready to eat.
