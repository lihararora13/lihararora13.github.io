Leeds does vegan food the northern way: generously, cheaply and without fuss. The scene runs from the city centre's Call Lane and Mill Hill out to Headingley and Harehills, and it is built on Indian street food, vegan junk food, market stalls and pubs, with a strong vegetarian backbone and a growing fully vegan core. It is one of the most fun vegan cities in England, and one of the best value.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with a Bad Girl Kebab at Döner Summer, vegan tiger prawns at Wawin, vada pav at Bundobust, curried scrambled tofu at Hyde Park Book Club, a plate at Fat Annie's, the vegan roast at Wapentake, a vegan pizza at Sardarjee, and sweets at Anand Sweets. A note on the traps: Big Scoffs Diner, Manjit's Kitchen and The Happy Yak Cafe, all once on the top lists, are closed, so none of them make the list.
