Catania eats vegan around Piazza Duomo and Via Etnea, where the scene is young, fast and Sicilian: a vegan restaurant behind the cathedral, a vegetarian fast-casual lab doing plant-based arrosticini, a buffet osteria where you build your own plate, and a vegan bakery doing the island's pastries properly. The picks cluster within a few minutes of the Duomo, and the prices run well below Milan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan burger at Cuore Fresco, the arrosticini at PLANTE Lab, the mixed plate at La Cucina dei Colori, and the pistachio cannoli at Dolcezza. A note on the traps: Agricolab, a vegetarian restaurant on Via Crispi, is listed as temporarily closed, so it stays off the list; Brunch Republic opened in Catania in November 2026 but could not be pinned to a verified street address, so it stays off too; and the granita is egg-free in Catania, but always ask, since recipes vary by bar.
