Chapel Hill's vegan scene clusters on Franklin Street and its side streets, with the university town's cafes, curry houses, and pizza joints each holding a piece. The list runs from an all-vegan bistro to a vegetarian curry house, a fast-casual bowl spot, a Carrboro pub, and a Buffalo wing joint, and the best meals are the ones with a specific cuisine behind them. It is a scene where the strongest meals are the vegan-friendly ones with deep benches, and the one all-vegan room is the bistro anchor.

Start with a plate at Coco Bistro, a curry at Vimala's, and a bowl at Roots, then work through Spotted Dog, QueenWa, and Heavenly Buffaloes. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Spotted Dog is in Carrboro, not Chapel Hill proper, so it is listed with its Carrboro address; Flying Biscuit is on South Estes Drive, not South Columbia Street as some listings say; and Brixx's pin is address-level, so use the street address when navigating.
