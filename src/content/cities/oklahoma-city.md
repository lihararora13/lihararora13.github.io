Oklahoma City's vegan scene is scattered across the Collective Food Hall, the Paseo, and the 63rd Street corridor: a plant-based soul-food stall, a vegan wing shop, a vegetarian cafe, and a set of Thai, ramen and Mediterranean kitchens with vegan dishes. It is a scene of stalls and small kitchens rather than one grand restaurant.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Start with the fungi burger at Enoki Eatz, the vegan wings at The Vegan Capital, the stuffed pepper at The Red Cup, and a burger at The Beet Box. A note on the traps: The Loaded Bowl, long the city's best-known vegan restaurant, was reported closing in June 2026 and is not listed here. Plant, a vegan spot some guides still list, was flagged by locals as one to avoid, so it is left off.
