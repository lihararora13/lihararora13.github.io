Alexandria is a koshary city, and that is good news for vegans: rice, lentils, chickpeas, pasta and fried onions are plant-based by birth, and the city's best vegan meal is a bowl of it. Around that sit the big multi-cuisine institutions on the Corniche, Gad and Branzino and Sidra by the Citadel, plus Chinese and Italian rooms with real vegan dishes, so a vegan can eat well from breakfast to the small hours.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with a koshary at Koshary Ala Sokhon, falafel and fuul at Gad, and ma-po tofu at China House. A note on the traps: La Poire's vegan patisserie is mostly only available during Coptic fasting, so it is not on this list, and Koshary Masrawy is a good reserve with branches in Agami and Bitash.
