Querétaro's vegan scene is centred on the historic centre: a fully vegan taco spot, a vegan restaurant with tacos al pastor, a vegan bistro, and a set of healthy cafes with vegan options. It is a colonial city where the best vegan food is mostly small, independent kitchens.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 5 of them fully vegan. Start with the tacos baja de tofu at Tacogreen, the tacos al pastor at VegCo, the Guacamonster burger at Veggie Happy, and the vegan tacos al pastor de soya at Golden Yo Light. A note on the traps: Veggie Happy is a small vegan eatery that has been described as a hidden gem; hours can be limited, so check before going. La Barra Verde in Juriquilla is a healthy cafe with vegan options, not a dedicated vegan restaurant.
