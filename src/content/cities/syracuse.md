Syracuse's vegan scene splits between Armory Square downtown, where the bars and restaurants cluster on Walton and Franklin Streets, and the Westcott neighborhood, where the cafes and diners sit on Westcott Street. The list runs from an all-vegan biscuit shop and an all-vegan cafe to a vegan bakery, a beer hall, and an Indian kitchen, and the city's annual vegan restaurant week keeps the scene active. It is a scene where the all-vegan rooms are the draw and the Westcott corridor is the place to walk.

Start with a biscuit at NICE, a plate at Strong Hearts, and a pastry at The Sweet Praxis, then work through The Hops Spot, Alto Cinco, and Pastabilities. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Strong Hearts drew community comments in June 2026 mourning a possible loss or closure, but the geocoder lists it active, so it is kept and flagged; The Sweet Praxis is at 203 East Water Street, not 2041 East Genesee Street as some listings say; and Salt City Coffee was held as a backup and is not listed.
