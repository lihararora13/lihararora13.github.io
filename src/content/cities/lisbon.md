Lisbon may be famous for bacalhau and pastel de nata, but its vegan kitchens have gleefully veganised both, and then kept going: francesinha, bitoque, croquettes and cod with cream, all made from plants, all over Chiado, Cais do Sodre and Intendente. This is a hilltop capital that loves a long lunch, a shared plate and a sweet finish, and vegans are very much invited.

All eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Kong for the big Portuguese dinner everyone recommends, 26 Vegan Food Project for a Chiado classic, and Legumi for sushi like you have never had it, then tapas-hop to O Gambuzino and save room for the buffet at Jardim das Cerejas.
