Utrecht does vegan food with the ease of a student city that has been doing it for years. The best tables cluster in the centre around the Dom and the canals, with a couple of gems out towards the station and the Oosterkade, and they cook everything from Nordic tasting plates and Indian tiffins to falafel, pizza and cinnamon rolls, all of it fully plant-based. This is one of the strongest all-vegan line-ups in the Netherlands, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all eight of them fully vegan. Start with an orange-topped cake at Life's a Peach, a charcoal-grilled lion's mane mushroom at Kasvio, oyster mushroom bitterballen at BROEI, a rainbow burger at Copper Branch, falafel at FLFL, a pizza at Vegan Pizza Bar, a tiffin at Little World Kitchen, and a loaf cake at KLUTS. A note on the traps: Kasvio recently moved into a smaller space at Wittevrouwenstraat 22, so use the address on this page, and FLFL also has a second branch at the station, so use the Servetstraat address.
