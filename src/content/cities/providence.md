Providence is a small city with a big appetite, and its vegan scene is concentrated, walkable and full of character. Federal Hill does Italian-American comfort without the cheese, the East Side does ice cream and deli counters, and downtown does Pan-Asian and tacos, all within a short drive of each other.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with steak and cheese egg rolls at PiANTA, a soft-serve unicorn at Like No Udder, a deli sandwich at Providence Vegan Deli, lemongrass soup at Veggie Fun, a vegan benedict at Julian's, vegan chorizo tacos at Casa Azul, a taco at Xaco Taco, and a cookie at Not Just Snacks.
