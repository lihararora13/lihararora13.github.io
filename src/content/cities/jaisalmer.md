Jaisalmer is the golden city, and winter is its season: the desert cools, the fort glows, and the rooftops fill up. This list is built for that winter, with every pick open and serving through the peak months, from the cafés inside the living fort to the pure-veg thali houses around Hanuman Circle.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Jaisalmer's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a coffee at Kuku inside the fort, a Rajasthani thali at Thakur Ji, and a fort-view dinner at Saffron. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
