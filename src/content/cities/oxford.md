Oxford does vegan food the way it does everything else: quietly, well, and with a lot of history. The scene's anchor is a 100% vegetarian pub in Jericho with a plant-filled beer garden, and the rest spreads from the Castle Quarter to Cowley Road: plant-based Indian food, Vietnamese pho, Sri Lankan small plates, a crypt cafe under the University Church and a pop-culture burger joint.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and the rest vegan-friendly. Start with the kebab bowl at The Gardeners Arms, the masala dosa at Namaste Village, the mushroom risotto at The Magic Cafe, a three-mushroom pho at Pho, the cashew curry at The Coconut Tree, the vegan Oxford breakfast at Vaults & Garden, a veggie burger at Atomic Burger, and a dosa at Dosa Park. A note on the traps: Oxford's vegan scene is thin beyond this list, so book the Gardeners Arms ahead on weekends, and note that most of these rooms are vegetarian or vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan.
