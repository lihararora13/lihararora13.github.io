Nagpur does not have a big dedicated vegan scene, and this list is honest about that: it leans on the city's excellent vegetarian kitchens, where the food was mostly plant-based already. South Indian plates, Maharashtrian thalis and modern smoothie bowls do the heavy lifting here, and every pick has real vegan dishes or clearly veganisable ones.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Nagpur's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a smoothie bowl at Drunken Monkey, a thali at Gayatri Bhojnalaya, and a masala dosa at Gokul Brindavan. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
