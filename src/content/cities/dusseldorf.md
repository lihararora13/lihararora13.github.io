Düsseldorf is the city of Little Tokyo, the largest Japanese community in Europe, and its vegan scene shows it: Vietnamese and Asian kitchens dominate the list, alongside the city's beloved Sattgrün buffets and a scattering of cafés and pizza spots. The best tables cluster in Flingern, Bilk, the Altstadt and Kaiserswerth, and the food is as international as the city itself.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with a buffet plate at Sattgrün Flingern, a bowl at To1980, tapas at Sen Vegan, an açaí bowl at Greentrees, mock duck at Jade-Imbiss, a vegan pizza at Fleischfrei, bánh mì at Mâm Chay, and breakfast at Soul's. A note on the traps: Marny's, once a top-rated vegan café, is listed as closed, so it does not make the list.
