Bielefeld eats vegan across four separate stops, the station, Oberntorwall, the Siegfriedplatz area and Stapenhorststrasse, where a fully vegan cat cafe anchors a scene of station porridge, seitan street food and Mediterranean cafes. The picks spread across the centre and the western quarter, so plan by meal rather than by street.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan treats at Miezhaus, the porridge at Haferkater, the seitan doner at Jambu, and the chickpea wraps at Mama Dut. A note on the traps: Cigkoftem at the LOOM is flagged as maybe closed on HappyCow, so it stays off the list; Mama Dut lists halloumi and tuna alongside its vegan dishes, and one vegan-titled dish describes yoghurt and butter sauce, so order explicitly vegan including the sauces; and Miezhaus opens after noon and takes reservations, with an admission cutoff an hour before closing.
