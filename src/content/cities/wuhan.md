Wuhan's vegan scene lives in its temples: Guiyuan, Baotong and Gude temples each have a vegetarian kitchen, and the best cafe in the city sits right by Guiyuan Temple. Say wo chi chun su, I eat purely plant-based, and ask for no egg and no dairy.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Marka Indian has three branches; only the Aeon Mall branch is listed here, to avoid duplicating the same kitchen three times. Pumen Shangsu was previously called Sanbao Vegetarian and relocated; it is listed under its current name and address.
