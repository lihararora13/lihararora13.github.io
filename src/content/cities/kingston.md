Kingston is the home of Ital food, the Rastafari plant-based tradition, and it shapes the whole vegan scene here: stews, callaloo, ackee, breadfruit, plantain, rice and peas, patties and natural juices, cooked without salt or with very little, and served in kitchens that are often small, cash-only and open limited hours. The scene clusters in New Kingston, Half Way Tree, Constant Spring and Gordon Town, and it is best explored by taxi between neighbourhoods.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with the vegan lasagna at Veggie Campus, a raw cake at Living Food and Juices, a mixed platter at Ibo Spice, a raw pizza at Mi Hungry, ackee and plantain at Shanty Man, a Buddha bowl at Kamila's Kitchen, a raw pizza at Tehuti Cafe, and a sea moss juice at 7 Basiks. A note on the traps: most of these kitchens are cash-only, several close early, and Ibo Spice is reported to keep caged birds on site, so go in with eyes open.
