Trondheim eats vegan in the Midtbyen and its edges, where an all-vegan buffet restaurant anchors a scene of a plant-based cafe, a ramen bar, Thai and Indian dinners and a Middle Eastern food hall. The picks spread from the centre to Lade, Tiller and Sluppen, so plan by neighbourhood rather than by street.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the buffet at Mat fra Hagen, the chana masala at Stammen, the spicy ramen at Koie Ramen, and the Thai red curry at Bangkok Cafe. A note on the traps: Daglig deig dropped, since its geocode returned a different business, Daglig Gleder; Maloca dropped, since its pin returned Norway Designs, so it has likely closed or moved; Cafe Ostro is a restaurant in Dalton, Georgia, not Trondheim; and Stammen opens Friday and Saturday only, so check the day.
