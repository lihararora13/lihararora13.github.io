Paris has quietly become one of Europe's best vegan cities, and the old
steak-frites stereotype is officially retired. This is a town where
plant-based bœuf bourguignon sits on a Marais blackboard, vegan sushi
gets a dedicated crowd in the 2nd, and fine dining happens inside a
wellness hotel.

We ate around and ranked nine spots that actually deliver, every single
one fully vegan. Bring an appetite, leave your dairy doubts at the door,
and save room for the chestnut desserts.
