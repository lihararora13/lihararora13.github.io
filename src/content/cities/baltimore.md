Baltimore does vegan food the way it does everything else: unpretentious, filling, and spread across distinct neighborhoods rather than one strip. The strongest tables cluster in Hampden and Remington, with Fells Point, Station North, Mount Vernon, and downtown each holding a piece of the scene. Soul food leads the way, followed by Taiwanese buns, Mexican masa, vegan ramen, and Afghan classics, and the best meals here are the ones that treat plants as the main event rather than a substitution.

Start with vegan crab cakes and BBQ rib tips at Land of Kush, a cinnamon roll and a slice of cake at Harmony Bakery, and a tofu brah bun at Ekiben, then work through Clavel's vegan tacos, Toki Underground's vegan ramen, and the Afghan pumpkin at The Helmand. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Ekiben's vegan bun has been the subject of supply-chain debate in recent community posts, so confirm the current vegan bun when you order; Red Emma's was researched and held as a reserve rather than listed.
