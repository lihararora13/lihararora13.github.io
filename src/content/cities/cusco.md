Cusco sits at 3,400 metres above sea level, and its vegan scene is every bit as high: quinoa, potatoes, Andean grains and tropical fruit, reimagined into the Peruvian dishes travellers dream about, from mango ceviche to seitan anticuchos and quinoa chaufa. The best tables cluster in the historic centre and the hillside artists' quarter of San Blas, most of them a short walk from the Plaza de Armas, and they range from a pioneering vegan restaurant open since 2013 to a tiny Hare Krishna set lunch tucked inside San Blas market.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian with serious vegan options. Start with a quinoa bowl at Green Point, a set lunch at Govinda Lila, a pizza at Parada Vegana, a set menu at Chia, a curry at the Vegan Temple, a mango ceviche at Sumac Vegano, a vegan lomo saltado at ANDIVIA, and a falafel wrap at Green Falafel.
