Nashik is a wine town and a temple town, and its food scene is proudly, stubbornly vegetarian. That is good news for vegans: the city is full of pure-veg dining rooms where dal, dosa and misal pav are the everyday food, and this list picks the best of them, from Gangapur Road cafés to the Untwadi rooftops.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Nashik's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a misal pav at Curry Leaves, a dosa at Krishna Leela, and a rooftop dinner at THE TEROTALE. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
