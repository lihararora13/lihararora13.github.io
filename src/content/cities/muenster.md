Münster is a city of bicycles and students, and its vegan scene has been growing quietly since 2013, when Krawummel opened as the city's first fully plant-based kitchen. The best tables cluster in the old town around Ludgeristraße and Frauenstraße, with a couple of gems out on Wolbecker Straße, in the harbour and in Gremmendorf, and they cover a lot of ground: Vietnamese and Korean fusion, fully vegan sushi, brunch bowls, Lebanese fast food, and a fully vegan cocktail bar in the Kuhviertel. Four of the eight picks are fully vegan, and the rest carry clearly marked plant-based dishes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the falafel sandwich at Krawummel, the phở at Chay Chay, a vegan sushi set at Maki Maki, the vegan pancakes at Beetschwester, the avocado toast at Die 3 Schwestern, a vegan falafel sandwich at Tayibat, a falafel plate at Al Hayat, and a flammkuchen with tofu feta at Mokel Bar. A note on the traps: Tayibat is a butcher's shop and Lebanese fast-food counter rather than a fully vegan restaurant, despite some listings saying otherwise, so order the vegan dishes, and Die 3 Schwestern's food menu is vegan but dairy milk is reportedly still offered for coffee, so ask for oat milk.
