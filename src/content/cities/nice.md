Nice does vegan food the way it does everything on the Riviera: slowly, seasonally and with a view. The best stops cluster in Vieux Nice and around Place Wilson and the port, with a short walk between most of them, and they cook Mediterranean food first: socca, flatbreads, bowls, pastries and gelato, with a fully vegan patisserie as the anchor. This is a thinner scene than the Dutch and Belgian cities on this list, and the honest label matters here: only two of the eight are fully vegan, and the rest are vegetarian or mixed-menu rooms with real vegan dishes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with a pastry at Amour, a bowl at Paper Plane, a saj flatbread at SAJ by Milla, a Brazilian plate at Veje Viver Bem, a quiche at Full Bloom, a bowl at Eatsa, a sorbet at Papilla, and a vegan pizza at Da Francesco. A note on the traps: Paper Plane describes its own menus as essentially vegetarian, Racines Bruno Cirino is a vegetable-focused Michelin restaurant, not a vegan one, so it is not on this list, and at SAJ, Eatsa and Da Francesco, confirm the vegan dishes, sauces and cheeses when you order.
