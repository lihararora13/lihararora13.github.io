Beijing rewards plant-based travellers who know where to look, and the look is worth it: Buddhist temple cooking with centuries behind it, a Michelin-starred vegetarian courtyard, and hutong cafes doing fresh, modern vegan food. The Lama Temple and Qianmen areas anchor the scene, with Sanlitun and Chaoyang covering the modern, Western-leaning end.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegetarian with strong vegan menus, so ask for no egg and no dairy where a dish needs it. Start with the truffle soup dumplings at King's Joy, the monk-chef classics at Tianchu Miaoxiang, dumplings at Vege Tiger, a classic banquet plate at Pure Lotus, the buffet at Xu Xiang Zhai, a bowl at Tribe, Lanzhou noodles at Baiyi, and a vegan pizza at Gung Ho!
