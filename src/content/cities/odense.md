Odense eats vegan in the centre, where two fully vegan restaurants rated 5 on HappyCow anchor a scene of a volunteer vegetarian club, a three-kitchen international restaurant and Mexican, Thai and Italian dinners. The picks cluster around Nørregade and Overgade, walkable from the station, and the whole list is in the centre.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the mushroom toast at Cafe Ojo, the pho at Moc Chay, the volunteer dinner at Kaernehuset, and the vegan menus at Gourmet Workshop. A note on the traps: Birk og Conrad is a party and catering restaurant with a 20-cover minimum, not a walk-in restaurant, so it stays off the list; Kaernehuset is a members club open to the public, so reserve a seat at least an hour before dinner at six; and District 13 was reported in April 2025 to have limited vegan options, so it stays off the list.
