Kansas City does not do anything halfway, and its vegan food follows suit. The scene clusters in Midtown, Westport, the Crossroads and Martini Corner, with a couple of worth-the-drive stops in Riverside and the Plaza, and it runs from chicken-fried seitan to soft-serve, with a bakery that happens to be entirely vegan and gluten-free.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegan-friendly with strong plant-based menus. Start with chicken-fried chicken at The Fix, tacos at Madrina Vegana, a wrap at Gigi's, a cupcake at The Littlest Bake Shop, a buffalo mac wrap at Tree Hugger, a beet burger at Billie's, a scoop at InnaCup, and dumplings at Bo Lings. A note on the traps: Vegan Crave Burgers and Bakery looks temporarily closed, so it does not make the list.
