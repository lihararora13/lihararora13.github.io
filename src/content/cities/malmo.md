Malmö does vegan food with Scandinavian calm: bowls, brunch, burgers and hummus, clustered in Möllevången and Davidshall with a couple of gems in the Centrum, all of it walkable from the central station. The scene leans café and brunch rather than fine dining, with spirulina bowls, lox bagels, vegan pancakes and a proper burger bar, and the quality is high across the board.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegan-friendly. Start with a spirulina bowl at Farm2Table, a tofu scramble at Jord, a mustard and mayo burger at The Vegan Bar, a mixed plate at Hummusson, and a kale salad at Flax. Two notes: Hummusson is listed at Södra Förstadsgatan 57 in the guide, while the geocoder placed it at number 43 on the same street, so the pin is effectively street-level on that block; and Bageri Leve serves dairy as well as vegan bakes, so it is listed as vegan-friendly.
