Kathmandu is a city where vegetarian food is everywhere and vegan food is one careful question away, and Thamel holds most of the dedicated spots. The fully vegan kitchens cook Nepali food first, dal bhat, momos, seitan and tofu, while the long-running vegetarian restaurants add Middle Eastern, Chinese and international plates with clear vegan options.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian kitchens with serious vegan menus. Start with vegan dal bhat at Loving Heart, a smoothie bowl at Sprout, a salad bowl at Always Happy, and hummus at OR2K. A practical note: the word vegan is not always fully understood here, so mention no egg, cheese, butter or milk and the kitchen will take care of you.
