Kigali is one of the cleanest, most orderly cities in Africa, and its vegan scene matches: a single fully vegan restaurant, SZY in Kacyiru, rated 4.5, plus a set of hotel and rooftop restaurants with marked vegan dishes, a Pan-African kitchen with a vegan soul platter, and cafes with clearly labelled vegan food. The eating clusters in Kacyiru and the city centre, and the food leans international rather than Rwandan, though isombe and cassava dishes appear on the vegan menus.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the mushroom waffles at SZY, the vegan soul platter at Kigali Pan-Afrikan Kitchen, and the lentil and chickpea creations at Hi Pima. A note on the traps: Hi Pima was previously listed as vegan but was reported in September 2026 to serve eggs, dairy and honey, so it is filed vegan-friendly, and the Acai Oasis bowls need to be ordered without honey.
