Addis Ababa is the thinnest all-vegan scene in this batch, and also one of the easiest places in the world to eat vegan without trying: Ethiopian Orthodox fasting food is naturally plant-based, and almost every restaurant in the city marks fasting dishes on its menu. The trick is knowing what to ask for, because fasting dishes can occasionally include fish or honey, so check with staff. The one fully vegan restaurant is Fitsum Shiro Bet in Bole, and the rest of the list is built from the strongest fasting menus in town, plus Indian, Japanese and cafe cooking.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with shiro and tibs at Fitsum Shiro Bet, fasting-marked dishes at OM Indian Bistro, the fasting platter at Bahir Zaf, a Buddha bowl at Guac On, the lunchtime vegan buffet at Itegue Taitu Hotel, vegetable rolls at Sakura, a smoothie bowl at Galani Coffee, and the fasting combo at the Grand Palace Hotel. A note on the traps: fasting does not always mean vegan, so confirm no fish or honey in the dish you order, and several addresses here are plus codes or district-level, so use the pins on this page rather than a street number.
