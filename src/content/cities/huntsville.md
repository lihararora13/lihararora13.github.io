Huntsville's vegan scene is spread between Lowe Mill, Stovehouse, Campus No. 805 and MidCity: two all-vegan kitchens, a plant-based food truck, a vegan soul-food pop-up, and a set of ramen, Jamaican, Indian and salad spots with clearly vegan dishes. It is a Rocket City scene that rewards driving between districts.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 4 of them fully vegan. Start with the double Beyond cheeseburger at Vujee Vegan, the Cluckster sandwich at The Veggie, the blackened portobello wrap at Chef Will the Palate, and the vegan oxtail plate at Heaven's Healthy Kitchen. A note on the traps: Heaven's Healthy Kitchen runs limited pop-up hours from a shared kitchen, so check its Instagram before going. The Veggie's main cafe is in Madison on Highway 72, just outside Huntsville proper, and is included as the city's nearest all-vegan cafe.
