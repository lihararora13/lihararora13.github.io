Trivandrum doesn't have a single all-vegan restaurant yet, but it does have something better than a label: a local vegan community that has mapped exactly which pure-veg kitchens will cook a proper vegan feast. The city's strength is the Kerala sadya, served on banana leaf all year round at Mothers Veg Plaza, and the dosa-and-filter-coffee circuit around Thampanoor and Bakery Junction, where the kitchens are used to holding the ghee. Newer arrivals like Velvet Dawn, with its veganized dinner menu, and the plant-based Triple C's Cafe show where the scene is heading.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan. Start with a banana-leaf sadya at Mothers Veg Plaza, the veganized stroganoff at Velvet Dawn, a protein roll at Triple C's, and a dosa crawl through Annapoorna, Ariya Nivaas and Hotel Aruna. A note on the traps: payasam, ghee roast and curd are everywhere in Kerala veg cooking, so order dosa plain and ask about the payasam before the sadya ends.
