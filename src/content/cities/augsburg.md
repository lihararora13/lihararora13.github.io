Germany's third-oldest city turns out to have one of Bavaria's most convincing vegan scenes, and it is concentrated where the old town is at its prettiest: the Lechviertel lanes and the streets around the Rathaus. Four of the eight picks are fully vegan, and they cover the whole day, from breakfast and brunch at Dreizehn and Emmi's Kitchen through a proper sit-down Bavarian dinner at DILL to a seasonal tasting-style evening at Rübenrot out in Hochfeld. Between them sit the falafel and schawarma of Exotiko, the vegan sushi of 030 Ronin, and a vegetable kebab that regulars swear by.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the schnitzel burger at DILL, the curry of the day at Dreizehn, a tofu scramble at Emmi's Kitchen, the seasonal main at Rübenrot, a falafel sandwich at Exotiko, a vegan sushi set at 030 Ronin, a Gemüsekebab at Momo's, and a cinnamon bun at Elvie. A note on the hours: DILL keeps short service, evenings Wednesday to Saturday with lunch on Saturday and Sunday, so check before you set out.
