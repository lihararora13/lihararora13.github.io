Krakow is one of Poland's most vegan-friendly cities, and its Old Town is packed with plant-based kitchens. Vegan kebab, vegan pierogi, vegan ramen and vegan burgers sit within a few streets of each other, and most of them are fully vegan rather than merely friendly.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start with a Hamster kebab at Vegab, cabbage rolls at Veganic, a meatball bowl at Turlaj Klopsa, the daily menu at Smaki Roslinne, tantanmen at Vegan AF Ramen, pierogi at No Bones, a plate at Glonojad, and the salad bar at Chimera. A note on the traps: Mihiderka, the old vegan burger spot, is reported possibly temporarily closed, so it does not make the list.
