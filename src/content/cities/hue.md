Hue is one of Vietnam's great vegetarian cities, with a royal Buddhist cuisine that turns vegetables, tofu and mushrooms into elaborate dishes: bun bo Hue chay, banh loc chay, com am phu chay. The scene splits between peaceful garden restaurants on the outskirts and cheap local kitchens near the Imperial City, and the best meals are the royal-inspired ones, served in wooden houses and gardens.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the bun bo Hue chay at An Nhien, the vegan set at Tinh Tam, the garden meal at San May, and the 100-dish menu at Lien Hoa. A note on the traps: Nha Hang Chay An Dinh is temporarily closed, so it is left out; and several Hue vegetarian kitchens use egg, so vegans should ask for the vegan dishes by name.
