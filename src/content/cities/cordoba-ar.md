Córdoba's vegan scene centres on the historic centre and the Güemes bar district, with a fully vegan kitchen on Deán Funes, a vegan-friendly food court in the Patio Olmos shopping centre, and vegetarian comedores scattered through the old streets. It is a lunch and early-dinner scene, and the strongest cooking is the lentil burgers, spinach-and-tofu ravioli and wholewheat lasagne at the top of the list.

Rincón Verde's lentil burgers and spinach-and-tofu ravioli are the plates to plan around, with the Patio Olmos food court and the Güemes vegetarian tables as backups. Two venues praised in guides could not be used: La Mundi and Planta have no findable address, and Alma Casa Vegana's address may have changed, so none of the three is listed. Rincón Verde's address was also flagged as possibly changed in one guide, but place data confirms it active at Deán Funes 732.
