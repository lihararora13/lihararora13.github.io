Asheville has been a vegan-friendly mountain town for years, and it shows. The scene is concentrated downtown and in West Asheville, with a James Beard recognised vegan restaurant at the top, a beloved soul-food counter, a vegan food truck turned restaurant, and some of the best Indian street food in the country.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegetarian or vegan-friendly with strong vegan menus. Start with the cheese plate at Plant, a tempeh reuben at Rosetta's, avocado toast at Pulp + Sprout, a seitan gyro at The Smokin' Onion, chaat at Chai Pani, a zen bowl at Green Sage, spicy miso ramen at Heiwa, and tea and a snack at Dobra.
