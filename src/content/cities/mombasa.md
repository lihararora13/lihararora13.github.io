Mombasa is a Swahili port city where vegan food is Indian food first: the Nyali suburb holds the vegetarian Indian restaurants, BAPS Shayona, Bollywood Bites and Govindas, and the cafes around Links Road do the lighter end. The standout is the Giriama Village Lounge, a fully vegan restaurant, cafe, library and culture centre in a village setting, serving high-class vegan Swahili and Kenyan cuisine, and it is the reason this list exists.

Eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the potato curry at the Giriama Village Lounge, a thali at BAPS Shayona, and the red bean burger at Cool Beans. A note on the traps: the Giriama Village Lounge is in a village setting outside the city, so a taxi is needed, and the Indian restaurants are vegetarian rather than vegan, so always specify no dairy.
