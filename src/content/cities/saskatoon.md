Saskatoon's vegan scene is centred on Broadway Avenue and downtown: a plant-based Vietnamese kitchen, an Asian fusion spot with a dedicated vegan menu, and a set of fine-dining and casual restaurants with strong vegan dishes. It is a prairie city with a surprisingly serious plant-based bench.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 1 of them fully vegan. Start with the pho at The Greens Kitchen, the vegan lemon chicken at Seasoned Fusion Tastes, the chickpea fritters at Hearth, and the vegan tourtiere at St. Tropez Bistro. A note on the traps: Greens Bistro and Pizza in Dalmeny is a separate business from The Greens Kitchen on Broadway Avenue; the Broadway kitchen is the pick here. Odla, once a vegan-friendly fine-dining spot, has closed, so it is not listed.
