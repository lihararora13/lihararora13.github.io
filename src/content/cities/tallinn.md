Tallinn's vegan spots cluster in three areas: the Old Town has the most dedicated vegan restaurants, Telliskivi Creative City in Kalamaja has the best vegan-friendly dining, and the Uus Maailm and Kalamaja border is home to newer all-vegan cafes. The scene is small but serious, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food at all.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two with serious vegan menus. Start with the vegan seafood platter at V, miso ramen at Rohe, a croissant sandwich at Nihe, a pastry at Kringel, a smash burger at Veg Machine, the seasonal menu at Klorofull, a plate at F-Hoone, and the tasting menu at Fotografiska. A note on the traps: Vegan Inspiratsioon, once a favourite, is permanently closed, so it does not make the list, and V books out days in advance, so reserve ahead.
