Jerusalem is one of the easiest cities in this batch to eat vegan in, because so much of the local food already is: falafel, hummus, sabich without the egg, and the fasting and kosher traditions that keep dairy and meat apart. The scene clusters in three places, the Jewish Quarter, the Machane Yehuda market and the city centre around Hillel and Heleni Hamalka, and the best eating is market stalls and small cafes rather than fine dining.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with falafel at Falafel HaRova, a mushroom pita at Aricha, vegan curries at Ichikidana, a thali at Jeera, a vegan pizza at Kazze, cauliflower shawarma at Mimoni's, breakfast at Cafe Bastet, and hummus at Hummus Rechavia. A note on the traps: many places close early on Fridays and stay closed on Saturdays for Shabbat, so check hours before a Friday or Saturday visit, and at sabich stands order without the egg.
