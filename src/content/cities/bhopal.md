Bhopal is a city of lakes and slow evenings, and its vegan scene matches the mood: unhurried, family-run and generous. The list stretches from a garden bistro past the airport to a rooftop café in MP Nagar, from a Jain kitchen in the old city that makes vegan ordering almost effortless to a beloved dairy institution where the move is simply to order around the dairy. It is a city where vegetarian food is the default, not the exception, and that makes eating well here easy.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and an honest note: none of the eight is a strictly all-vegan kitchen. Bhopal's scene is pure vegetarian with ghee, paneer and curd never far away, so every pick here is vegan-friendly, and the ordering move is always the same, no ghee, no butter, no curd, no cheese, no paneer. Say it up front and the kitchens look after you. Start at Bageecha at dusk, work through the old city, and finish with a late coffee high above MP Nagar.
