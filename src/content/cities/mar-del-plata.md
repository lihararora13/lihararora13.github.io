Mar del Plata is a seasonal city: its vegan scene swells in the summer, when the beach crowds arrive, and quiets in winter. The list is led by a fully vegan kitchen on Belgrano, a vegetarian takeaway that has run since 1991, and the city's speciality-coffee and ice-cream institutions, with vegan options threaded through all of them. Everything below sits in the centre, Güemes or Playa Grande, within a short walk of the seafront.

The stuffed soy milanesa at Un Gusto Superior, the takeaway plates at Greenway and a flat white at FOLC are the three stops to build around, with Lucciano's vegan ice-cream flavours as the dessert. Wara Espacio Saludable was checked and dropped: it is temporarily closed. SAO café was not used either, with only one vegan pastry and no plant milk. Lucciano's pin is approximate, as it has many branches and the Güemes shop is used here.
