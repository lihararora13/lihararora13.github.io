Indore is India's food capital, and its vegan scene is exactly what you would hope for from a city that takes eating this seriously: deep, varied and full of cafés that treat vegetables as the main event rather than the apology. The list runs from a garden you can pluck your dinner from to a premium lounge, a health-food patio and a string of pretty pure-veg cafés across Vijay Nagar, Saket Nagar and New Palasia, with the legendary Sarafa night market waiting after dark for the brave.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and an honest note: none of the eight is a strictly all-vegan kitchen. Indore's scene is pure vegetarian with a heavy dairy habit, so every pick here is vegan-friendly, and the ordering move is always the same, no ghee, no butter, no curd, no cheese, no paneer. Say it up front and the kitchens look after you beautifully. Start at Nature's Kitchen, work through the cafés, and finish the day at Sarafa with bhutte ka kees made your way.
