Tainan is Taiwan's oldest city and its most famous food town, and its vegetarian culture runs deep: temple food, market buffets and family-run eateries where vegan dishes are a normal part of the menu rather than an afterthought. The best picks cluster in the West Central District around the old temples and markets, with a couple of gems out in the East District and Anping, and they cook Taiwanese food first, braised rice, beef noodles, dumplings, dim sum and medicinal soups, alongside a fully vegan Indian curry house, a two-bowl vegan ramen counter and a pasta stall.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly vegetarian. Start with the spinach-chickpea curry at Veganersworld, the miso soy-milk ramen at Tai Lang, the dim sum at Fou Shi Yuan, the basil green curry set at Shu He Vegan, the Omni pork katsu at The Vegan Plate, the vegan dim sum at Qing Qi Su Shi, the oven-baked casserole at Shi Shu Yi Li, and the vegan braised pork rice at Ba Baou Ge. A note on the traps: Xiang Shi Fu Su Shui Jian Bao, which appears on some HappyCow lists, is flagged as maybe closed and is not included here; and several of these kitchens are vegetarian rather than fully vegan, so say you eat vegan (chun su) when you order.
