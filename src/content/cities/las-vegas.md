Las Vegas does nothing small, and its vegan scene is no exception. You can eat seven courses in total darkness, grab dim sum in Chinatown, crush quesabirria in the Arts District, and then go full drive-thru for a Big Zac at midnight. All eight picks here are 100 percent vegan and verified open as of October 2026.

The headline act is Crossroads Kitchen at Resorts World, just crowned VegNews' Best Vegan Restaurant in America for 2026 (its eighth straight win). But the locals' favorites, Tacotarian and Tarantino's Vegan, are where the city shows its true colors. Bring an appetite and maybe sunglasses; you'll need neither indoors at BLACKOUT.
