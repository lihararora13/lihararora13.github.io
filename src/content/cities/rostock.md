Rostock eats vegan between the old town and the KTV quarter, where a regional vegan kitchen, a vegan fast-food cult spot and a vegan cafe anchor a scene that stretches from the Neuer Markt to the seaside district of Warnemunde. The picks cluster around the centre, walkable from the Hauptbahnhof, with one deliberate detour to the beach.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the carrot lox at Grune Kombuse, the voner at Vegangster, the daily lunch at Cafe Mokka, and the braised seitan at Chez Mara. A note on the traps: Cafe Mokka takes reservations and the room is small, so book; Warnefornien sits out in Warnemunde, a tram ride from the centre, so plan it as a beach stop rather than a city-centre meal; and dean and david is a chain, so its vegan bowls are reliable but not local.
