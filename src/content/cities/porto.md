Porto is one of Europe's best vegan cities, and it is also one of the cheapest. The scene clusters in Cedofeita, Bonfim and Santo Ildefonso, all walkable from the centre, and it covers the whole spread: a vegan francesinha, all-you-can-eat buffets, bao buns, pastéis de nata and a cafe full of rescue cats.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian with a strong vegan menu. Start with a francesinha at Kind Kitchen, a vegan nata at A Padoca, a croissant at Odete, a buffet plate at Verde Hut, a bao at Baomerang, a canteen plate at Venn, a coffee with cats at O Porto dos Gatos, and a macrobiotic plate at O Macrobiotico. A note on the traps: Apuro Vegan Bar is reported closed as of January 2026 and daTerra Baixa is reported closed as of July 2026, so neither makes the list.
