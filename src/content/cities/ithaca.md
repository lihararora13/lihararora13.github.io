Ithaca's vegan scene is compact and walkable, clustered around the Commons downtown and Collegetown up the hill, with the Cayuga Street corridor holding the vegetarian institution and the downtown diner. The list runs from an all-vegan donut shop to a fifty-year-old vegetarian restaurant, an Indian kitchen, an arepa truck turned counter, and a bakery, and the best meals are the ones with a specific cuisine behind them.

Start with a donut at Angelhearts, a plate at Moosewood, and a sandwich at Bickering Twins, then work through Sangam, Yxi's, and Ithaca Bakery. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Bickering Twins is on North Cayuga Street, not North Aurora Street as some listings say; and Angelhearts' pin is address-level, so use the street address when navigating.
