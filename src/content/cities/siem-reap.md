Siem Reap feeds its vegan visitors well. The scene runs from a fully vegan comfort-food restaurant on Smiling Circuit to a riverside vegetarian oasis, a social-enterprise brunch spot near Pub Street and a training restaurant that employs disadvantaged young people, with Khmer, Greek and Italian food all covered. Most places sit within a short walk of Pub Street and the Old Market, and prices are backpacker-friendly.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and the rest vegan-friendly. Start with the chickn burger at HeyBong, the miso peanut and tempeh bowl at Ojas, a Khmer set at Peace Cafe, the lotus root ceviche at Banlle, a Beyond Meat burger at New Leaf, a Khmer classic at HAVEN, a vegan pizza at Fellini and the falafel gyros at Elia. A note on the traps: many Siem Reap spots are vegetarian with vegan options rather than all-vegan, so confirm dishes are vegan when you order, especially where menus mark items vegetarian by default.
