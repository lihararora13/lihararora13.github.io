Gwangju's vegan scene is thin and spread across the city's districts, from Nam-gu in the south to Buk-gu in the north, with no single vegan street. The anchor is Hae Tteuneun, the city's only fully vegan restaurant, with an all-vegetarian buffet, a black-tofu specialist, a separate-vegan-menu Chinese restaurant and a vegan bakery filling out the map. Expect to travel between picks, and expect most kitchens to be vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with Hae Tteuneun, the Salrim buffet, the black tofu at Welcome Moon, and the vegan jjajangmyeon at Jeilbanjeom. A note on the traps: Salrim's buffet is vegetarian and mostly vegan, but its pizza uses store-bought cheese, so skip that; the Damyang listings that HappyCow folds into Gwangju results are a different town and are not counted here.
