Burlington is a small city with an outsized plant-based bench, and most of it is walkable: Church Street and the waterfront hold the cafes and bakeries, the university campus holds the Broccoli Bar, and the Old North End holds the pho. Pingala anchors it, a fully vegan cafe that even made it onto Diners, Drive-Ins & Dives, and the scene around it is young, casual, and genuinely committed rather than trend-chasing.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with the Crunchwrap Supreme at Pingala, the vegan club at Stone Soup, a Broccoli Bowl at the Broccoli Bar, the hummus at Honey Road, the pho chay at Pho Hong, a vegan scoop at Sweet Alchemy, a pastry at August First, and a veggie burger at The Daily Planet. A note on the traps: Juice Amour, long listed as a Burlington vegan juice bar, is permanently closed, so it is not listed here, and the Broccoli Bar now trades from its UVM Davis Center outpost at 590 Main Street rather than as a roaming truck.
