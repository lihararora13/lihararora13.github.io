Brno eats vegan in the centre, where a vegan restaurant and bar open since 2009 anchors a scene of a vegan ramen bar, a vegan fast-food chain, a vegetarian canteen and Indian dinners. The picks are all in the centre, walkable from the main square, and the scene runs from seitan steak and own-brewed beer to tantanmen and dosa-style Indian.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the seitan steak at Vegalite, the tantanmen at Vegan AF Ramen, the vegan burgers at Forky's, and the Indian dishes at Dhaba Beas. A note on the traps: Rebio has two branches, Orli and Mecova, so go to the Mecova one, rated 4.6; Soul Bistro runs fish and chips alongside its vegan dishes, so it stays off the list; and Mayada and Zdravy Zivot open weekday lunchtimes only, so they stay off the list too.
