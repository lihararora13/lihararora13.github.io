Corpus Christi's vegan scene is spread between downtown, the bayfront and the south side: a pizza shop opened by a holistic nutritionist, a Thai kitchen with vegan dishes labelled throughout, a Vietnamese spot with a whole vegetarian section, and cafes with vegan bowls and pastries. It is a Gulf Coast scene that rewards a short drive between stops.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. Start with the Radiant Garden pizza at Saucy Mamas, the vegetable crispy rolls at BKK, the fried tofu pho at Pho Life, and the roasted cauliflower steak at Gallery 41. A note on the traps: Pavani Express is now Lotus Indian Cuisine, under a new name; older guides still list the old name. One local guide flagged a concern about Ginger Cafe frying falafel in beef tallow, so it is not listed here.
