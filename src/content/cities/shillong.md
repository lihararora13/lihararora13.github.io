Shillong is the Northeast's cafe town, and its vegetarian food runs from old Police Bazar dining rooms to the new pure-veg restaurants climbing the road to Upper Shillong. Jiva Veg has fed the city's vegetarians for years, Bistro Pure Veg brings fine dining to Thana Road, and Rynsan puts Khasi cooking on the national map with wild fern salad and fermented soybean chutney.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Shillong's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a veg thali at Jiva Veg, a kofta at Bistro, a veg galouti kebab at Extra Butter, and the wild fern salad at Rynsan. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
