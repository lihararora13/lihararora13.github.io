Hua Hin's vegan scene is small and seasonal, with a 100% vegan cafe near the night market, a 30-year-old jay kitchen, and a handful of small vegan and vegetarian kitchens in the centre. The town is a seaside resort, so several spots open and close with the seasons, and the healthy cafes around Soi 88 to 94 turn over often. The reliable core is the vegan cafe, the jay kitchen and the Indian restaurants, with smoothie bowls and markets filling the gaps.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the massaman curry at Hua Hin Vegan Cafe, the jay dishes at Ruean Thong, the vegan pad Thai at Veggie Tales, and the vegan menu at Ger Sap. A note on the traps: the healthy cafes and juice stalls around Soi 88 to 94 open and close fairly often, so treat them as a type rather than a fixed list; and the Cicada Market vegan stalls run Friday to Sunday evenings only.
