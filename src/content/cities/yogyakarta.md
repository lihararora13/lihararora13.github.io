Yogyakarta is Java's cultural capital, and its vegan scene follows the city: Javanese temple food, warung cooking and a creative cafe culture, with Italian and international food alongside. The best tables run from a garden kitchen in Bantul to a forest-themed cafe on Parangtritis Road and a string of Prawirotaman favourites, and prices are among the lowest on the whole vegan.place directory.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and the rest vegan-friendly. Start with the rendang at Simple Plant, a vegan burger at Black Forest, the teriyaki bowl at Fortunate Coffee, a bowl at Veganissimo, a vegetable pizza at Fairy Kale, the spicy tempe salad at Milas, the gado gado at ViaVia and a vegan pasta at Mediterranea. A note on the traps: many Yogyakarta spots are vegetarian with vegan options rather than all-vegan, and dairy appears in Indonesian vegetarian cooking, so confirm dishes are vegan when you order, and note that Simple Plant keeps short hours, Thursday to Sunday afternoons.
