Chandigarh is India's most orderly city, and its food scene is orderly too: grids of sectors, each with its own pure-veg institutions, South Indian chains, and sweet shops that have been going for half a century. There is no all-vegan restaurant here yet, which is exactly why this guide is honest about it: every pick below is vegan-friendly, and the city rewards the traveller who asks for no ghee, no dahi and no cheese.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the vegan Buddha bowl at Back to Source in Sector 17, the dosas at Sagar Ratna, and the chole bhature at Gopal's, and finish with the Burmese falafel at Burma Burma in Elante Mall. The sectors make the city easy to navigate, and the food is the reward for doing so.
