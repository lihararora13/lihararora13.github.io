Rabat is Morocco's capital and its vegan scene is polite rather than pioneering: no fully vegan restaurant, but the medina's riad restaurants cook vegetable tagines and couscous as a matter of course, and the international rooms in Agdal and the centre mark vegan dishes. The best eating is in the old medina, where Dar Zaki and Dar El Medina serve Moroccan food in courtyards, and the rest is Japanese, Thai and French rooms with vegan corners.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan. Order a vegetable tagine at Dar Zaki, the taktouka salad at Le Petitbeur, and the vegetable options at Matsuri. A note on the traps: Rabat has no dedicated vegan restaurant despite what some round-ups imply, and the 24 Camels list this guide draws on is a vegan-friendly list, not a vegan one, so always confirm no butter or meat stock in tagines and couscous.
