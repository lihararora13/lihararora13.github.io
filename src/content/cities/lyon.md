Lyon is France's food capital, and its vegan scene eats like it means it: this is the second-best vegan city in France after Paris, cheaper and calmer, and the cooking here is ambitious rather than apologetic. The best tables cluster on the slopes of Croix-Rousse, around Presqu'île and in Guillotière, and they cover the whole range, from a tasting menu in Ainay to a vegan kebab on Rue Pasteur, via sushi, macrobiotic plates and a bakery full of donuts.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian with a mostly vegan menu. Start with the vegan cheese board at Like An Elephant, a sushi set at Bloom Sushi, the tasting menu at Trèfle, a Vebab at Güstavo, the seasonal plates at Les Mauvaises Herbes, the macrobiotic plate at Prana, a crème brûlée donut at Against the Grain, and whatever is seasonal at Brocoli. A note on the traps: Les Belles Âmes Pâtisserie and AME Café, both once top-rated, are closed, so neither makes the list.
