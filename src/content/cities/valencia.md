Valencia's vegan scene lives mostly in Ruzafa, the city's creative quarter, with a beachside outpost in El Cabanyal and a couple of old-town gems. Expect Spanish tortilla without the egg, Thai curries, burgers, sharing tapas and a proper vegan paella on Sundays, all of it sunny, generous and very reasonably priced.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian with a strong vegan menu. Start with a Spanish tortilla at Cafe Madrigal, a burger at Khambú, a brunch set at Let It Bloom, a Thai curry at Cor del Pilar, a pad thai at MESTIZA, the weekly menu at Lo de Ponxe, and a tasting menu at Copenhagen.
