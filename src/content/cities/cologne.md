Cologne's vegan scene is one of the best in Germany, and most of it sits in the Belgian Quarter, around Neumarkt and in Ehrenfeld, where local magazines sort the vegan restaurants by neighbourhood. The local cooking is famously meat-heavy, so the vegan food here lives in modern restaurants rather than beer halls, and it is all the better for it: pizza, buffets, sushi, noodles, burgers and bakeries, all of it fully plant-based.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with a pizza at Virtuous Pie, a buffet plate at Sattgrün, bao buns at Zen Chay, sushi at Udum, a carrot-salmon sandwich at Hempies, udon at Nobiko, a bowl at vevi, and a burger at Bunte Burger. Come hungry, and spend the afternoon in Ehrenfeld.
