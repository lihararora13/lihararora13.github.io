The Hague does vegan food with the ease of an international city: organic fine dining, Indian-spiced vegetarian cooking, Middle Eastern falafel and a plant-based coffeehouse, all within a short walk of each other. The best tables cluster in the centre and in the Zeeheldenkwartier, with a couple of gems out towards Binckhorst and Beeklaan, and they cook with organic, seasonal ingredients and a lot of care. This is a scene of small rooms and long lunches rather than chains.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start with gluten-free oat pancakes at FOAM, oyster mushroom bitterballen at Hagedis, a seasonal menu at Veggies on Fire, a pizza at Vegan Pizza Bar, falafel at FLFL, a cinnamon roll at Plenty, a samosa chaat at Veggiesaurus, and a falafel wrap at Falafel en Hummus. A note on the traps: Beesechurgers, the vegan burger shop on Koningin Emmaplein, posted in June 2026 that it was possibly in its last week after a conditional sale, and its listing now shows as temporarily closed, so it is not on this list, and Veggiesaurus and Falafel en Hummus are vegetarian with vegan options, so order the vegan dishes there.
