Panama City is enjoying a sweet moment for plant-based cooking, with a new generation of cooks proving that vegan and vegetarian food can be as tasty and hearty as any traditional dish. The scene stretches from Casco Antiguo to San Francisco, Condado del Rey and El Dorado, and it covers the whole spread: meat-free dim sum, Lebanese shawarma with a vegan option, homemade vegan cheeses, raw vegan classics and 100% plant-based smash burgers.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly, and all nine places in the 2026 Out.pa guide were chosen for consistency and verified hours. Start with the seitan burger at Natural Spice, the Best Vibes Reuben at Planticeria, the tempura duck at Vegsu, the beet carpaccio at MAHALO, the vegetarian chili at Snack Shack, a buffet plate at Jardin Vegetariano, the watercress balls at La Casa Vegetariana, and the vegetable fried rice at Loving Hut. A note on the traps: three of the nine places in the guide are closed on Sundays, so check the hours before you go, and parking in Casco Antiguo is scarce, so take an Uber.
