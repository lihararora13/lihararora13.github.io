Bucharest has a small but growing group of fully vegetarian and vegan restaurants, from an Old Town raw-cake café to a vegan burger bar near Piața Victoriei, raw-food bistros with Romanian-style daily lunch menus and a vegan counter in a riverside food hall. Most places here are fully vegan, and the differences are opening days, how much of a meal versus a snack each serves, and gluten-free choice. The centre is walkable, so Level Up, Sublimmme and Vyro pair well with a walk around the Old Town, Universitate and Cismigiu.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegan-friendly. Start with the Level Up burger, the Buddha bowl at Sublimmme, the Vitality Bowl at Sara Green, the daily menu at Vyro, and a raw cake at Rawdia. A note on the traps: opening hours matter more than usual here, since Nom Nom, Vyro and Rawdia close on Sundays and Sara Green's Pipera branch is a weekday office-lunch spot, so plan Sundays around Sublimmme or Sara Green on Lascăr Catargiu; and Rawdia and Seed Cuisine both sweeten some dishes with honey, so ask if you are strictly vegan.
