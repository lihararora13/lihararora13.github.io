Columbus does vegan food like a college town that grew up: the High Street corridor alone carries a diner, a deli, a butcher, a juice bar and a fine-casual favourite, and the scene stretches from downtown to Clintonville to Olde Towne East without losing its nerve. The food leans comfort, diner breakfasts, Philly cheesesteaks, pizza, dosa, and bowls, with prices that still feel like Ohio.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegan-friendly with strong vegan menus. Start with a Philly cheesesteak at Seitan's Realm, a Vrunchwrap at 4th and State, a sandwich at Portia's Cafe, a cafe plate at Two Dollar Radio, deli meats at Vida's, a seasonal plate at Comune, masala dosa at Dosa Corner, and an acai bowl at Zest Juice Co.
