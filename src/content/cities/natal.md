Natal's vegan scene is anchored by three fully vegan kitchens, a burger joint, a vegan kitchen in Neópolis and a daytime vegan restaurant, with the rest of the list made up of vegetarian lunch houses in Tirol and Cidade Alta and vegan-friendly tables in Ponta Negra. It is a lunch-heavy scene: most of the best tables close by mid-afternoon, so plan the day around midday.

The vegan burgers at Goodalinha and the daytime plates at Magia Vegana are the two stops to build around, with Cambuci's kitchen and the Viver Melhor lunch buffet as the backups. Quinoa Alimentos was checked and dropped: it is temporarily closed. Three Ponta Negra and Lagoa Nova pins are neighbourhood-level approximate, as noted in the restaurant entries.
