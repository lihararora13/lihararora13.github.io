Hualien's vegan scene is compact and walkable, clustered around the train station and the old centre, and it leans on Taiwan's Buddhist vegetarian tradition. The anchors are DEW, a vegan brunch and natural wine restaurant, Jia Yun Su Shi, a fully vegan noodle shop, and Vegetarian Home, a Buddhist buffet where you pay by weight. Around them sit vegetarian buffets and a vegetarian pizzeria, so a plant-based traveller can eat well without leaving the centre.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the focaccia sandwich at DEW, the noodles at Jia Yun Su Shi, the buffet at Vegetarian Home, and the vegan pizza at Summer Too Hot. A note on the traps: several Hualien vegetarian buffets serve egg in some dishes, so vegans should check each plate; and the Fuli Township listings that HappyCow folds into Hualien results are an hour south and are not counted here.
