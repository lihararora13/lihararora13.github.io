Perugia eats vegan in the medieval centre and its southern suburbs, where Umbrian cooking meets a small but serious vegan scene: a strictly vegan restaurant in the historic centre, a vegan fine-dining room out in Pretola, an eight-euro vegan buffet in Ponte San Giovanni, and a natural cafe with a bio shop. The picks spread across the hill town and its edges, so plan by meal rather than by street.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the seitan ragu gnocchi at L'Erbavoglio, the Friday vegan pizza at Yoo, the themed buffet at BioBar, and the vegetarian tris at Maramao. A note on the traps: Ferretti, the local dairy chain, has vegan gelato but its own guide says the food menu has nothing vegan, so it stays off the list; Del Testone's vegan dishes depend on the day and the surprise first course, so go for the torta al testo with vegetables; and Lassa Gi's Aperivegan runs weekly, not daily, so check before going.
