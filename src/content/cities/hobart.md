Hobart's vegan scene is compact and walkable, centred on the waterfront and North Hobart, with a plant-based patisserie, a vegan-friendly cafe, and a scattering of restaurants with vegan dishes. The anchor is Little Poet, a 100% plant-based cafe and patisserie on Victoria Street, with Little Lotus Cafe and Mr Good Guy nearby, and Italian, Greek, Indian and Thai restaurants with vegan dishes filling out the map. The scene is small enough to cover in a weekend, and the best meals are the plant-based baking and the vegan curries.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the pastries at Little Poet, the eggplant curry at Little Lotus, the crispy soy tofu at Mr Good Guy, and the vegan pasta at Ottimo. A note on the traps: several Hobart kitchens list vegan dishes that change with the season, so ask what is vegan today; and the Indian and Thai restaurants use dairy and fish sauce, so vegans should specify no dairy and no fish sauce.
