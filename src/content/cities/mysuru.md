Mysuru does vegan the slow, sunlit way. The yoga schools of Gokulam set the tone: mornings start with ragi pancakes, smoothie bowls and kombucha, and the cafes here label their vegan options without being asked, which is rarer than it should be in South India. Beyond the yoga quarter, the old city's pure-veg institutions fill in the gaps with dosa, thali and tiffin that a vegan can happily order with the ghee held back.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and one of the eight is fully vegan. Start at Satori Vegan Kitchen for a fully plant-based meal, then work through Chakra House's ragi pancakes, Depth N Green's vegan bakes, a sourdough loaf from Sapa, and a classic pure-veg spread at Veg Kourt. A note on the traps: most of Mysuru's pure-veg kitchens cook with ghee, paneer and curd, so treat the vegan-friendly picks as places to order carefully, not as all-vegan rooms.
