Quebec City is not a vegan capital, and it does not pretend to be. What it has is small, sincere and very French-Canadian: a fully vegan restaurant in the Old Port, a vegan sushi bar and a vegan cafe sharing one Limoilou street, and a brewpub that takes its vegan poutine seriously. Come for the old stone streets, stay for the food.

All seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with beet tartare at Don Vegan, a sushi set at Kagai, a croissant at Cafe Mila, a fried tofu wrap at Cafe Paleta, vegan poutine at La Korrigane, a vegan pizza at NO.900, and a wrap at Parlabas. HappyCow lists only five vegan-friendly spots in the city, so this is the honest size of the scene.
