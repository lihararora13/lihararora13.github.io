Izmir is an Aegean port city where the traditional food is already half vegan: zeytinyagli vegetable dishes cooked in olive oil, stuffed zucchini flowers, lentil kofte, dolma and gozleme with spinach or potato. On top of that base sits a real dedicated vegan scene in Alsancak and Karsiyaka, with three fully vegan restaurants and a raw kitchen, plus a Michelin Bib Gourmand restaurant in the Kemeralti bazaar with a wide selection of vegan mezes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with iskender at Gibi Vegan, a burger at Yasam Vegan Kafe, tres leches at Moono, zucchini spaghetti at YayaRaw, lentil kofte at Aysa, vegan schnitzel at Big Chefs, a vegan burger at Frida Cafe, and a spinach gozleme at 1912 Gozleme ve Cay Evi. A note on the traps: HappyCow lists no venues under Izmir itself, so this list was verified against the wider Izmir listings and venue pages instead; Tyke Kafe was dropped because its last confirmed open report is from September 2019, and YayaRaw was reported to no longer be fully vegan in August 2026, so it is listed as vegan-friendly.
