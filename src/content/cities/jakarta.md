Jakarta does plant-based food the Indonesian way: tempeh and tofu treated like royalty, mock meats with real swagger, noodles piled high, and smoothie bowls for breakfast. The best tables are scattered across the city, from Menteng and Glodok to Kelapa Gading and Kalideres, and they are almost all fully vegan, which makes Jakarta one of the easiest big Asian cities to eat well in without a single worry.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with a mini burger at Burgreens, satay at the Glodok vegetarian restaurant, a bowl of mushroom noodles at Bakmie Jamur, a pastry at LN Fortunate Coffee, nasi goreng at Sematjam, a cake at Kynd Baker, a homestyle plate at Indo Vegan, and whatever is fresh at Rumah Juliet.
