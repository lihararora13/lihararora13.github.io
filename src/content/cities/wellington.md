Wellington does vegan food the way it does everything else: small, fierce, and slightly windswept, with the best tables clustered along Cuba Street and Manners Street in Te Aro, out on the south coast at Lyall Bay, and up the hill at the Kelburn campus. The scene runs from a proper ten-course tasting menu to a $15 laksa and a campus curry, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who came for the harbour and stayed for the plants.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with a Pollo pizza at Nolita, the tasting menu at Hillside Kitchen, a dirty cheeseburger at The Botanist, a crème brûlée donut at Belén, the laksa at Aunty Mena, a burger at Zodiac Wholefoods, mapo tofu at September Vegan Food, and a curry at Krishna Food. A note on the traps: Sweet Release, the old vegan cafe and bakery on Manners Street, has closed, its website now serves casino spam and TripAdvisor marks it closed, so it is off the list; its crown as the city's vegan bakery now belongs to Belén.
