Wiesbaden eats vegan on Wilhelmstrasse and the streets around it, where the city's first fully vegan cafe-restaurant-bar sits next door to a fully vegan acai shop, and the rest of the scene runs on Vietnamese, Turkish, Afghan and Thai kitchens with proper vegan menus. The picks cluster in the centre, walkable from the Hauptbahnhof, and the two all-vegan anchors carry the list.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the tacos and pozole at Alebrijes, the acai bowls at Purple Acai, the Vietnamese crepes at Goc Da, and the Thai curries at Aroydee. A note on the traps: Cafe Klatsch, the old vegetarian co-op, was reported in January 2026 to function mainly as a bar, with a restaurant reopening only planned, so it stays off the list; and the HappyCow rating for Purple Acai differs between listings, so the 5.0 should not be quoted as settled.
