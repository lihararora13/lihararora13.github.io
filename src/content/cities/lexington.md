Lexington's vegan scene is small and spread across the city's corridors: North Limestone for the all-vegan comfort food, Main Street for the vegetarian diner, Greyline Station for the market-hall cafe, and the suburbs for the salad and juice bars. The list leans on vegan-friendly kitchens with real vegan benches, and the one fully vegan anchor is a former food truck turned cafe with a cult following for its wings and burgers.

Start with the Buffalo Wingz and the Beer Batter Burger at Moody Mike's, a plate at Alfalfa, and the vegan Thai dishes at Archa 9, then work through Kentucky Native Cafe, Vinaigrette, and Stella's Deli. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: FIKA Vegan Cantina closed and was dropped; Vegan Nepal is a personal-chef and catering service rather than a restaurant, so it is not listed; and Alfalfa's listing address says East Main Street while the geocoder returned West Main, so its pin is flagged approximate.
