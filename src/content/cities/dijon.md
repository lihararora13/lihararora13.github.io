Dijon's vegan scene is small, which is why this is a seven-pick city rather than eight. There is one fully vegan restaurant at the top, a vegetarian institution, a fine-dining room with a vegan menu and a handful of smaller vegan-friendly kitchens, all within the compact centre around Rue Condorcet and Rue Jeannin.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at CURIEUSES on Rue Condorcet, Le Shanti and Cibo on Rue Jeannin. The traps were real here: Les Mijoteuses and La Casa Veg are both temporarily closed and are not on this list, and Cibo is not vegan at all, it serves pigeon, wild boar and escargots, so it is listed as vegan-friendly with a vegan menu on request, not as an all-vegan table.
