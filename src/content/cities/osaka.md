Osaka is the city that eats first and asks questions later, and it turns out its famous kitchen has a fully vegan corner worth crossing town for. The scene clusters in the west of Minami, around Shinsaibashi, Shinmachi and Horie, where you can eat your way through the city's own greatest hits, takoyaki, okonomiyaki, kushikatsu and ramen, all rebuilt without a scrap of fish stock or egg. Then there is a four-seat izakaya in Nakazakicho, a quiet set-meal counter down in Tanabe, and a kaiseki lunch that needs booking a day ahead.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and seven of the eight are fully vegan. Start with a daily lunch set at Green Earth, a tempeh set at Paprika Shokudo, takoyaki at OKO, an egg sandwich at MERCY, soy-meat yakitori at Aju, kushikatsu in Shinsekai, the fixed set at Rocca, and the vegan kaiseki at Asai Togei. A note on the traps: the Dotonbori branch of OKO Okonomiyaki serves meat alongside its vegan options, so the all-vegan takoyaki shop is the Higashishinsaibashi one, and the old Aju listing that served meat closed in 2021, the current Aju is the fully vegan four-seater in Nakazaki.
