Bari eats vegan in two worlds: the white stone lanes of Bari Vecchia, where old-town kitchens are learning to cook vegan Apulian food properly, and the Murat grid, where a vegan burger chain, a bio bistrot and Asian counters fill the gaps. The scene is thinner than Palermo or Catania, and it is carried by one all-vegan anchor plus a handful of kitchens with serious vegan menus, including a complete vegan menu at Casa Tiberino and a dedicated vegan menu at BUO Bio.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them all-vegan. Start with the rainbow burgers at Flower Burger, the orecchiette and vegan salmon at Casa Tiberino, the berlingot pasta at BUO Bio, and the sgagliozze in Bari Vecchia. A note on the traps: Rossopomodoro's vegan pizza sits out in Casamassima, outside the city, so it stays off the list; La Bitta's pin is street-level approximate, since the bar sits in a narrow Bari Vecchia alley; and Peppo on Via Melo is a fish-first restaurant with vegan options, so it stays off too.
