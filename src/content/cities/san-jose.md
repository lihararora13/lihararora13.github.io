Silicon Valley runs on code, but San Jose eats like a city that has been doing plant-based food since before it was a trend. The best tables cluster downtown and along Santana Row, with Vietnamese Buddhist chay cooking, all-organic fast food, craft beer and a fermentation bar all within a few miles of each other. This is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than pop-up hype, and the top of the list has been feeding vegans for three decades.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with the pad Thai at Vegetarian House, a burger at Next Level Veggie Grill, a chickpea salad sandwich at Good Karma, a sesame chicken sandwich at Tofoo Com Chay, pho at Loving Hut, a raw pizza at RawASF, vegan pho at Green Lotus, and cashew queso at Fox Tale.
