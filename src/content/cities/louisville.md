Louisville's vegan scene runs along Bardstown Road and into Shelby Park and St. Matthews, with a cluster of vegetarian and vegan kitchens that have been feeding the city for years. The list runs from an all-vegan bakery and an all-vegan comfort-food counter to a two-for-one vegetarian institution, a Mayan fine-dining room, and a Latin kitchen. It is a mature scene with real depth, and the best meals are the ones that treat plants as the cuisine rather than the constraint.

Start with a pastry at Half-Peach Bakery, the loaded fries at V-Grits, and a plate at Heart & Soy, then work through Ramsi's, Mayan Cafe, and Sri Mantra. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Morels Cafe closed and was dropped; Cosmic Bird is a pop-up only and is not listed; and Shahar Cafe, the former all-vegan burger joint, added meat to its menu in August 2026 according to Louisville Business First, so it is filed vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, with its vegan items cooked separately according to the owners.
