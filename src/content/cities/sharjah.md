Sharjah is the UAE's most conservative emirate and its vegan scene is built on the South Indian vegetarian restaurants of Al Khan, Al Qasimia and Muweilah, plus Levantine falafel counters, rather than on dedicated vegan rooms. There are no purely vegan restaurants in the city, according to HappyCow reviewers, so the guide is a mix of pure-veg Indian kitchens where dosa can be made vegan, and Middle Eastern rooms with marked vegan dishes.

Eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan, and that one is delivery-only. Order a dosa without oil or butter at Saarangaa, a dosa at Saravana Bhavan, and the quinoa bowl at Wishi. A note on the traps: Indian vegetarian does not mean vegan, so always specify no ghee, yogurt or cream, and Vegan & Co delivers across Sharjah but has no dine-in room.
