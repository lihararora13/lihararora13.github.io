Chengdu is China's capital of bold flavour, and its vegetarian kitchens have spent generations learning to make mala, mapo and hot pot sing without meat. Temple dining rooms, teahouses and modern restaurants all cook from the same Sichuan playbook: tofu, mushrooms, wheat gluten and seasonal vegetables, turned up loud with chilli and Sichuan pepper.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegetarian with plenty of vegan plates. Start with the buffet at Wenshu Monastery, a teahouse set at Mi Xun, mock meats at Jujube Tree, a build-your-own hot pot at Miao Wang, mapo tofu at Chengdu Vegetarian Restaurant, kung pao at Linglong, delicate plates at Vegan Weigen, and Sichuan classics at Zhong Shui Xiang.
