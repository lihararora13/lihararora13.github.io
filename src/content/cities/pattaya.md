Pattaya is more manageable for vegans than it looks: jay restaurants cover everyday Thai meals, Five Star J is the reliable sit-down choice, and Indian restaurants handle the rest. The dedicated scene is small but real, spread from Naklua in the north to Jomtien in the south, with the centre holding the Indian kitchens and the vegan cafes. The scene turns over, so treat old blog lists with suspicion and check HappyCow before crossing town.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with Five Star J, the pad Thai at Tess Vegan Cafe, the plant-based steak at Apple Vegan Cafe, and the thali at Govindam. A note on the traps: Kina Kind Cuisine appeared closed at the time of one October 2026 check, so it is left out here; several once-loved Pattaya vegan spots have closed, and HappyCow is the live register for this scene.
