Sao Paulo is huge, hungry and gloriously plant-powered: feijoada without the pork, churrasco without the beef, coxinha, moqueca and pão de queijo all rebuilt from plants and somehow still tasting like a celebration. Around Paulista, Consolacao and Jardins you can barely walk a block without finding another all-vegan kitchen showing off.

All eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Urba for a small, chef-led lunch worth the detour, Pop Vegan Food for the pizza buffet everyone loves, and Sushimar Vegano for sushi done properly, then keep grazing through bakeries, brunch spots and Brazil's first vegan churrascaria.
