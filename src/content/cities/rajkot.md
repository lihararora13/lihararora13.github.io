Rajkot is Gujarat's fourth city, and its food scene is proudly, stubbornly vegetarian. That is good news for vegans: the city is full of pure-veg dining rooms where thalis and South Indian plates are the everyday food, and this list picks the best of them, from the old city's Jawahar Road to the new Kalavad Road café belt.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Rajkot's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a Gujarati thali at The Grand Thakar, a coffee at Dough & Doppio, and a banana-leaf thali at Sarvasyam. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
