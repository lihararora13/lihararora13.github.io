Bhubaneswar is Odisha's temple city with a cafe scene that is only just finding its feet, and the vegan story here is written by pure-veg kitchens rather than dedicated plant-based restaurants. The best of them already think in vegan terms: Grains keeps a vegan meals board on the wall, Greens Cafe builds salads and international plates that skip the dairy without a fuss, and the temple kitchens of Govinda's and the old veg hotels serve food that was mostly plant-based before anyone called it that.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Bhubaneswar's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a vegan thali at Grains, a salad bowl at Greens Cafe, the sattvik thali at Govinda's, and a budget veg thali at Shree Annapurna. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
