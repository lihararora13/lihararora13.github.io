Essen spreads its vegan tables across the city centre, the Südviertel and Rüttenscheid, with one outpost in Schonnebeck, and the mix is generous: a fully vegan buffet in the Theaterpassage, an all-vegan kitchen in Frohnhausen doing birria tacos and momos, a fully vegan Vietnamese restaurant, and a vegetarian restaurant that has been going since 1987. Three of the eight picks are fully vegan, the rest carry clearly marked plant-based dishes, and the price level stays friendly throughout, with most plates landing well under twenty euros.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with a buffet plate at sattgrün, the birria tacos with jackfruit at Hummelbude, the seitan skewers at Peace Food, a vegan penne bolognese at Zodiac, a build-your-own bowl at finefine, a porridge with plant milk at Haferkater, a vegan margherita at 60 seconds to napoli, and a vegan ramen at Genki Ramen. A note on the traps: Zodiac and Haferkater are vegetarian and mixed menus respectively, so order the vegan-marked dishes and ask for plant milk explicitly at the porridge counter.
