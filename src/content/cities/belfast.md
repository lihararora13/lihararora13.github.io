Belfast's vegan scene has grown fast. Twenty years ago a dedicated vegan order meant a side of chips and an apology; today there is a fully vegan cafe and shop on the Ormeau Road, a fully vegan Asian fusion kitchen in the city centre, a Michelin-recognised tapas restaurant with its own vegan menu, and a converted Victorian stable yard serving one of the most ambitious vegan tasting menus on the island.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and the rest vegan-friendly. Start with a toastie at 387 Ormeau Road, the vegan laksa at Jumon, the falafel fritters at EDO, a vegan pizza at Pizza Punks, the vegan tasting menu at Molly's Yard, a falafel wrap at Umi Falafel, a vegan donut at Oh Donuts, and the vegan combo at Jebana. A note on the traps: The Honest Vegan on Lisburn Road closed in 2018, so take it off the shortlist before you walk down there expecting brunch.
