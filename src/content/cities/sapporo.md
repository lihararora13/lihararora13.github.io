Sapporo built its food fame on dairy, miso ramen and lamb, so eating vegan here takes a short, vetted list and a little planning. Maruyama holds most of it, a leafy neighbourhood of cafes and natural-food shops, with downtown and the station area covering ramen and soup curry, the two dishes Sapporo invented.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six with clearly marked vegan menus, so confirm the broth is fish-free where it matters. Start with miso ramen at Veggy Way, a set meal at Itadakizen, soba at LOHAS, vegan soup curry at Beyond Age, a lunch plate at Aoi Sora, bean onigiri at Mame Kitchen, a cafe set at SALLOGA, and the reservation ramen at Ichiryuan.
