Marrakech is a meat-heavy city on paper, but Moroccan cooking has always been generous to plants: bissara, tagines, couscous, harira and mint tea are vegan or nearly so, and the medina is full of vegetarian and vegan-friendly cafes that label their dishes clearly. The best of them cluster around Riad Zitoun and the spice square, walkable from Jemaa el-Fnaa, with rooftop views over the souks.

All eight picks are verified open as of October and November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with a stew at World Storytelling Cafe, a vegan plate at Earth Cafe, a rooftop lunch at Ayaso, and a special dinner at Bo and Zin. A note on the traps: fully vegan restaurants are still rare here, so most picks are vegetarian or vegan-friendly kitchens with labelled dishes, and hours shift during Ramadan.
