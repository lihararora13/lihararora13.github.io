Stavanger eats vegan in the old town and the east side, where an all-vegan Michelin Guide restaurant in an old canning factory anchors a scene of a fully vegan cafe in Stavanger Ost, a Syrian mezze house, an Ethiopian restaurant and a reservation-only seven-course restaurant. The picks are walkable from the harbour, with Bellies and Oransjeriet a short walk east.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the tasting menu at Bellies, the tofu scramble at Kafe Oransjeriet, the vegan mezze at Aladdin Cuisine, and the Ethiopian veg dishes at Gadja. A note on the traps: Bellies opens Wednesday to Saturday from four to ten only, and needs a reservation; Efendi opens Thursday to Saturday evenings only, and HappyCow notes to verify whether it is still fully vegetarian; and Restaurant K2 is reservation-only, Wednesday to Saturday, book by email.
