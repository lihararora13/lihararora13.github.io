Santiago might surprise you: behind the meat-heavy reputation of Chilean cooking sits one of South America's most likeable vegan scenes, clustered in the walkable streets of Barrio Italia and Providencia. The best tables here range from a sunny health food cafe with a perfect review score to Chile's very first vegan restaurant, and from a vegetarian institution that has been feeding the city since 1980 to a tiny sushi counter doing things with sweet potato that you will not find anywhere else.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with Sapiens for brunch, Vegan Bunker for the classic Chilean dishes made plant-based, and El Huerto for a long, generous vegetarian dinner, then book Kambo for sushi, Catapilcana for a late night out, Fuente Rica Rica for Chilean street food, El Vegan for burgers, and Veggie Lunch for pastries. A note on the traps: the much-loved Suculenta Sangucheria Vegana closed in May 2022, and TierraLibre is temporarily closed until further notice, so neither is on this list.
