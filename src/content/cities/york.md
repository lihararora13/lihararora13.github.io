York's vegan scene is compact and walkable, clustered around the Shambles, Goodramgate and Piccadilly, with one excellent outlier out by the river at Naburn. It mixes fully vegan cafes with old vegetarian institutions and market stalls, and the standard is high for a city this size: the top of the list would hold its own anywhere in the country.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with The Orchid on George Hudson Street, Junk Soul at SPARK:York and Tea by the Lock out at Naburn. The traps were mostly listings that had gone stale: a couple of long-closed cafes still appear in older guides, and one market stall that had moved pitches is listed here at its current home.
