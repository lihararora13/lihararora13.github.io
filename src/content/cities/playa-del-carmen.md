Playa del Carmen's vegan scene sits a few blocks off Quinta Avenida, the main tourist street, which is exactly where the best food is. Plantivoros is the open-air Mexican kitchen the guides agree on, Peace & Bowl is the healthy breakfast at the back of Mercado 30, Salvei is the proper night out on 5th Avenue, and Comet 984 is the 1950s diner where everything down to the milkshakes is plant-based. The cheap eats hold their own too: falafel wraps for 80 pesos, milanesa tacos for 25.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with the tacos and pozole at Plantivoros, an acai bowl at Peace & Bowl, the satay ramen at Salvei, a burger and a Snickers milkshake at Comet 984, and the bomblette at Fresco Hábito. A note on the traps: Plantivoros moved from its old corner of Calle 32 and Avenida 40 to Calle 14 Norte bis, so use the address on this page; The Pitted Date closed in February 2025, so do not follow older guides to it; and Tacos ALV across the highway is pickup-only by WhatsApp, so message first if you go.
