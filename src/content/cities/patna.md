Patna eats vegetarian the way it has for generations: thalis, dosas, litti and sweets, served in busy pure-veg dining rooms where the food is cooked fresh and priced for everyone. There is no dedicated vegan restaurant here, but the pure-veg base is wide and honest, from a bhojnalaya that has served thalis since 1931 to a litti shop that has fed the same crossroads for just as long.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Patna's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a Bansi thali at Bansi Vihar, a masala dosa at Sanwara, a Marwari thali at Abhiruchi, and a plate of litti chokha at Chourasiya Ji's. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
