Stellenbosch is a university town in the Cape Winelands, and its vegan eating is concentrated in a few walkable streets in the centre, Church Street, Plein Street and Andringa Street, plus the Helderberg road out of town. The scene is cafe-led rather than restaurant-led: breakfast bowls, toasties, burgers, pizza and a pay-by-weight harvest table, with the wine estates on the edge of town offering vegan menus if you book ahead.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly and none fully vegan. Start with a smoothie bowl at Edible Eden, avocado toast at Aunty Sophie, the quinoa bowl with falafel at Hygge Hygge, a lentil crunch wrap at Java Bistro, tofu scramble on toast at La Coco C, a vegan margherita at Col' Cacchio, a veggie burger at Basic Bistro, and the harvest table at Greengate. A note on the traps: there is no dedicated all-vegan restaurant in town, so vegans should stick to the labelled dishes on these menus, and the wine-estate restaurants need reservations.
