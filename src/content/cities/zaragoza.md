Zaragoza's vegan scene is small and serious, concentrated in the Casco Antiguo and around Calle San Miguel. There is one fully plant-based restaurant at the top, a vegetarian-eco restaurant that has been going since 1989, and a set of vegan-friendly kitchens that all cook proper vegan dishes, from a full vegan menu at Bokita to a vegan-leaning bar in La Magdalena.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at MATi on Calle San Miguel, La Retama on Calle Reconquista and Restaurante Baobab on Calle Arzobispo Apaolaza. The traps were real here: El Plato Reberde on Calle de San Lorenzo is permanently closed and does not appear on this list.
