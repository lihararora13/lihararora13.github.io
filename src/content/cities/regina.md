Regina's vegan scene is small and Indian-leaning: a pure-vegetarian Indian restaurant on Scarth Street, a vegetarian street-food spot on Victoria Avenue, a plant-based cafe on Broad Street, and an Ethiopian restaurant with a Friday vegan buffet. It is a prairie capital where the best vegan food is mostly South Asian.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 1 of them fully vegan. Start with the thali at Shree Akshar Veg, the pani puri at Royal Paan, the Friday vegan buffet at Selam Ethiopian Cuisine, and a plant-based lunch at Recharge Cafe. A note on the traps: Greenspot Cafe is 100% vegetarian and gluten-free, not all-vegan, so its cinnamon buns and quiches need a vegan check. The Gathering Table is a vegan bannock delivery business without a dining room, so it is not listed as a restaurant pick.
