Mainz eats vegan in the Neustadt and the old town, where HappyCow's top ten for the city reads like a vegan city guide on its own: a fully vegan Australian brunch bar, a vegan pizza joint, an organic bowl bistro, a vegan wine bar and a vegan bakery cafe, all within a few minutes of the Hauptbahnhof and the cathedral. It is one of the strongest small-city scenes in this batch.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with the scrambled tofu at Kiwi Corner, the vegan pizzas at NOAH, the kimchi bowls at Naturlik Lekkeria, and the regional dishes at WILLICHS. A note on the traps: Veganes Cigkofte was previously called My Cigkofte, so older guides list it under the old name; Loc Vegan was reported serving honey in drinks in July 2025, so verify the drinks before ordering; and Bobby's, Kelly's Fresh Kitchen and COCO Green Goodies are solid but sit outside HappyCow's top ten, so they are held as reserves.
