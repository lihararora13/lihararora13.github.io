Pretoria is South Africa's administrative capital, and its vegan eating is suburban rather than downtown: the best tables sit in Faerie Glen, Brooklyn, Hazelwood, Colbyn and Moreleta Park, in cafes, burger joints and Indian restaurants that label their vegan dishes clearly. There is one fully vegan restaurant, at Ashers Farm Sanctuary out in Boschkop, and the rest of the list is vegan-friendly places with proper vegan menus, from Egyptian and Indian cooking to pizza, burgers and health-cafe bowls.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with a toasted sandwich at Ashers Farm Sanctuary, the vegan French toast at Blos Cafe, a vegan margherita at Col' Cacchio, a Beyond burger at Hudsons, a vegetable thali at Geet, vegan starters at Namaskar, a falafel wrap at Cairo Kitchen, and an acai breakfast at La Coco C. A note on the traps: Pretoria's scene is thin on dedicated vegan restaurants, so most picks are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, and Ashers Farm Sanctuary keeps weekend hours, so check before driving out to Boschkop.
