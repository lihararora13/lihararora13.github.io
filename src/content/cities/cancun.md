Cancún's vegan scene lives downtown, not in the Hotel Zone: fully vegan antojito spots around Mercado 28 and Avenida Sunyaxchén, a Hare Krishna kitchen, and a set of Indian, Lebanese and Mexican restaurants with vegan dishes. It is small, cheap and very good value compared with the beach strip.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 4 of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan quesadilla at Bao Vegan, the birria tacos at Antojito Con Amor, the vegan tacos at Caribe Vegano, and a thali at Taste of India. A note on the traps: Gopal's is lacto-vegetarian, not fully vegan, so check each dish for dairy. Most of the best vegan food is downtown; Hotel Zone restaurants mostly offer vegan requests rather than dedicated menus.
