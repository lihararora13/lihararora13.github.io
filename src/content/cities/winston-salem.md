Winston-Salem's vegan scene is spread across the city's corridors rather than concentrated downtown: Third Street for the all-vegan grill, Acadia Avenue for the market, Fourth Street for the Mediterranean spot, and Stratford Road for the Indian and juice bars. The list leans on vegan-friendly kitchens with real vegan benches, and the one fully vegan anchor is the same grill that anchors Greensboro. It is a scene where the strongest meals are the ones with a specific cuisine behind them.

Start with a plate at Mike's Vegan Grill, a market plate at Acadia Foods, and a Mediterranean plate at Yamas, then work through Nawab, Zito, and Village Juice. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Acadia Foods is at 228 West Acadia Avenue, not 1203 as some listings say; Nawab is on South Stratford Road, not Reynolda Road; Village Juice is on South Stratford Road, not West Fourth Street; Dough Joe's is on Reynolda Village, not Brookstown Avenue; and To Your Health Bakery and Hot Dog City were held as backups and are not listed.
