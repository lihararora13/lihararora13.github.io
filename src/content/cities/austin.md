Austin runs on breakfast tacos, and it turns out the vegan ones
are some of the best. This is a city where food trucks became
institutions: a taco trailer from 2012 that converts carnivores,
a burger truck feeding the Red River 2am crowd, a pizza trailer
parked at a dive bar. Add in a five-hour smoked vegan brisket,
date-night dining by a fire pit, and a macrobiotic garden campus
by Zilker.

Eight picks across the city, all vegan save one beloved
vegetarian cafe that has fed South Austin since 2000. Bring an
appetite and a tolerance for trailer lines.
