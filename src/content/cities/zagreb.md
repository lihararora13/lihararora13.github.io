Zagreb does not shout about its vegan scene, it just quietly feeds you very well. The best tables cluster in the Upper Town and the streets around Ban Jelacic Square, with a couple of worth-the-walk stops south of the centre, and the cooking leans organic, seasonal and Croatian, with seitan, tempeh, falafel and very good bread doing the heavy lifting.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with strong vegan menus. Start with a falafel bowl at BioMania, a seitan cordon bleu at Zrno, a daily menu at Vegehop, falafel by the piece at Falafel etc, a Karma burger at Meatless, a burger at Vege Fino, brunch at Br00m44, and a stew at Oaza.
