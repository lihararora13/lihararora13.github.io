Darmstadt eats vegan around the Marktplatz and the Schustergasse, where a fully vegan kitchen in Bessungen anchors a scene of ramen, Tibetan dumplings, Thai curries, Moroccan tajines and market-square cafes. The picks cluster in the old town, walkable from the Luisenplatz, with one short walk south to the vegan anchor.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan bowls at Plantyful, the vegan ramen at Burari, the dumplings at Momo, and the curries at Green Thai. A note on the traps: Elisabeth, Cova Bowls, Mondo DELI and Zen Noodle are all listed as closed on HappyCow, so they stay off the list; Green Thai and L'Osteria share the same building at Wilhelminenstrasse 10, so check the door sign; and the vegan dishes at the mixed restaurants need an explicit vegan order.
