Iowa City punches above its size for a college town in the Heartland: one fully vegan restaurant on Prentiss Street, a vegetarian coffee shop, a falafel counter on the Northside, and a downtown of sandwich shops, pizzerias and co-ops with clearly marked vegan dishes. The ped mall and the university keep it lively year-round.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 1 of them fully vegan. Start with the tempeh Reuben at Trumpet Blossom, the falafel at Oasis, the tempeh BLT at Get Fresh Cafe, and the vegan Buddha slice at The Wedge. A note on the traps: The Wedge's old Highway 1 address is stale; the current pizzeria is on Iowa Avenue downtown. Trumpet Blossom is the city's only fully vegan restaurant, so most other picks are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan.
