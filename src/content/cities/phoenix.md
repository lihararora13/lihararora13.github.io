Phoenix might be the desert's best-kept vegan secret, and it barely has to try: 300 days of sunshine, a fierce local food scene, and a plant-based community that meets up nearly every week mean the best tables range from a sunny Midtown comfort-food classic to a pink-taco food-truck-turned-brick-and-mortar, with Grand Avenue packing two of the list's best into a single artsy block. Come hungry, start on 7th Street, and save room for soft serve at nami.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and every single one is fully vegan. Start with a Big Wac at Green, graze the Crazy Shrimp tacos at Earth, the Very Berry acai bowl at Goji Berry, fried guacamole at The Coronado, jackfruit birria at Tacos Veganos, a tSoynami soft serve at nami, buffalo cauliflower at Vegan & Vine, and the Soul XL platter at Nana's Kitchen, which moved from Chandler to Van Buren Street in October 2026. A note on the traps: Giving Tree Cafe, the 7th Street whole-foods cafe, is flagged permanently closed, so it did not make the cut, and the Loving Hut chain and Veggie Village were left off in favour of the stronger local consensus picks.
