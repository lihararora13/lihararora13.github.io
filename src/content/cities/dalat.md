Dalat's vegetarian scene is easy and central, with most kitchens on or near Phan Dinh Phung and Hoang Dieu, and it runs on buffets, noodle soups and hotpots suited to the cool highland evenings. The anchors are Vegan Heaven, a dedicated vegan restaurant, Au Lac, a vegan kitchen, and Bep OM Chay, a fully vegan cafe, with buffets and hotpot kitchens filling out the map. Egg, dairy, fish sauce and animal stock can turn a seemingly plant-based dish into something unsuitable for a strict vegan, so ask in the vegetarian kitchens.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the noodle soups at Vegan Heaven, the pho at Au Lac, the mushroom pho at Hoang Nhi, and the buffet at Nha Tam. A note on the traps: Vegan Heaven moved from 62 Phan Dinh Phung to 43 Nguyen Van Cu, so older guides send people to the wrong address; and since July 2025 the central wards have been combined into Xuan Huong ward, so a restaurant may appear under two ward names without having moved.
