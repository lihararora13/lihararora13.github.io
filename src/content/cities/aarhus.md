Aarhus does vegan food with Danish ease: a café here, a sushi bar there, a Mexican table and a porridge counter, almost all of it in the Midtbyen within a short walk of the cathedral and the harbour. HappyCow lists 69 vegan and vegetarian-friendly restaurants in the city, so the scene is deep even if the fully vegan flagships are few, and the food leans on pasta, sushi, bánh mì and tacos rather than Danish classics.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegan-friendly. Start with gyoza at Café Mellemfolk, pasta with pesto at Plant Food, vegan sushi at A+ Siam Sushi, and a bánh mì at Banhmi Bandits. One honest note on sourcing: individual venue websites could not be located for picks two through eight, so those rest on HappyCow's current listings, crawled a week before this guide was written, plus place data; the top two picks have their own sources. Café Gaya was considered as an eighth pick but its address could not be verified, so Frankies on Mejlgade is used instead.
