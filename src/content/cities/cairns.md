Cairns has a compact vegan scene around the Esplanade and Grafton Street, with a fully vegan bagel shop, a fully vegan cafe, and a plant-based cafe inside an organic store. The anchors are Bury Me Standing, a 100% vegan bagel and pastry shop, Snoogies, a 100% vegan cafe, and The Healthy Hub Cafe, a 100% plant-based cafe, with Asian, Vietnamese and gelato kitchens filling out the map. The scene is small enough to walk, and the best meals are the bagels and the vegan banquets.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the bagels at Bury Me Standing, the cafe dishes at Snoogies, the plant-based dishes at The Healthy Hub, and the Feed Me Banquet at Little Sister. A note on the traps: Bury Me Standing is open Friday to Sunday only, from 7am to 1pm, so do not plan a weekday visit; and a HappyCow trip page lists several Cairns venues as closed without naming them, so check hours before you go, especially for seasonal kitchens.
