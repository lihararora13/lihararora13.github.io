Heidelberg does not have a vegan quarter, it has a vegan habit: cafés and small kitchens spread between the Altstadt, the Weststadt, the Bahnstadt and Rohrbach, each doing one thing well. Breakfast belongs to the Weststadt and the old town, lunch to the soup bars and buffets around the Hauptstraße, and dinner to the fully vegan cat café in the Bahnstadt and the South Indian tables out in Rohrbach. The Neckar sets the pace here, and the best meals are the unhurried ones: a long brunch, a loaded buffet plate, a dosa eaten slowly.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the all-day breakfast among the rescue cats at Katzentempel, a smoothie bowl at Earth Bowls, a buffet plate at red, a soup and börek at Franz Soupmarine, the brunch plate at Café Nomad, a masala dosa at Madras Mania, a vegan arepa at Arepas.World, and cake among the plants at Café Botanik. A note on the traps: Earth Bowls is cash only and closes on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, Madras Mania is cash only and closed on Tuesdays, and the old Bahnstadt favourite Raavana is permanently closed, so it never made the list.
