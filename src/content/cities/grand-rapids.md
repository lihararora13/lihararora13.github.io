Grand Rapids' vegan scene is small and centered on two streets: Jefferson Avenue, where the worker-owned Bartertown Diner anchors a block of earth-conscious eating, and Fulton Street, where Little Africa serves the city's only fully vegan menu. The rest of the list runs through downtown bars, breweries, and neighborhood cafes, with Thai, Ethiopian, and American comfort food doing the heavy lifting. It is a scene that rewards asking, since several kitchens have deep vegan benches behind mixed menus.

Start with the group platter at Little Africa, a seasonal plate at Bartertown Diner, and the vegan sides at Stella's Lounge, then work through Lucy's, Anna's House, and the Mitten Brewing Company. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Brick Road Pizza and Gaia Cafe are both marked closed on HappyCow and were dropped despite still appearing in older guides; Marie Catrib's closed permanently in December 2019 and was dropped too.
