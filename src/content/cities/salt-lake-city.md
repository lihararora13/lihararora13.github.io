Salt Lake City punches well above its size for plant-based food. The scene is mostly downtown and walkable, with a diner that has been 100 percent plant-based for years, a sandwich shop that makes everything in house, and a Vietnamese spot and an arepa counter that keep things interesting.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegan-friendly with strong vegan menus. Start with biscuits and gravy at Vertical Diner, mushroom risotto at Zest, a pesto sub at Buds, orange chicken at Veggie House, a coconut curry bowl at Aubergine, an arepa at Arempas, pho at All Chay, and a scoop at Normal. A note on the traps: City Cakes and Cafe is closed and Passion Flour Patisserie is reported closed as of February 2026, so neither makes the list.
