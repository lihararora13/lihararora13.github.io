Mangaluru eats vegetarian the way the coast has always eaten: dosa and buns for breakfast, banana-leaf meals for lunch, filter coffee to finish. There is no all-vegan restaurant in the city yet, so this guide leans on the pure-veg institutions that locals actually swear by, from the much-loved Kadri Cafe near Vanas to the old Hampankatta tiffin rooms. Ask for dishes without ghee and curd and the kitchens here will happily oblige.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan. Start with a cone dosa at Kadri Cafe, a South Indian spread at Kaveshwara Cafe, the coastal-style veg meals at Hotel Janatha Deluxe, and a banana-leaf lunch at New Gokul. A note on the traps: ghee podi idli, curd-based snacks and ghee roasts are the house signatures here, so order the plain dosa and ask the kitchen to hold the ghee.
