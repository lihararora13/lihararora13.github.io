Atlanta doesn't just welcome vegans, it throws a full parade for them. With some 50 all-vegan spots across the city, the Southern capital earns its reputation one jackfruit barbecue sandwich at a time. Our six picks cover the whole spread: cheeky burgers, Cuban crunchwraps, raw kale wraps, old-school soul food, globe-trotting café classics, and a bakery worth crossing town for. Bring an appetite and leave a believer.
