Nantes does vegan food with a Loire shrug: no fuss, just good cooking, and most of it within a short walk of the centre. The fully vegan restaurants cluster in the Centre-Ville around Rue Armand Brossard and Rue Baron, with a bakery on the Quai de la Fosse and a tea house out in Dervallières, so a day of eating here is mostly a stroll. Expect French bistro plates rebuilt plant-based, raw and gluten-free bowls, burgers, and a bakery case that regulars plan their mornings around.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegan-friendly. Start with the Tofish and Chips at L'Ethiquête, a bowl at TerraVega, a Glasgow burger, an almond croissant from Délice Végétal, and a Snickers latte at Tumbleweed. Two notes: Oh K-fée d'MJ keeps a vegetarian label because the milk choices for drinks need separate attention, so check before ordering a milky coffee; and TerraVega has a second listing at 2 Rue Mathelin Rodier, but the restaurant on this page is the one at 22 Rue Baron.
