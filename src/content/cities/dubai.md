Dubai does vegan food the Dubai way: big, bold and a little bit extra, but with real substance underneath the shine. This is a city where a garden cafe in a Jumeirah villa can sit comfortably alongside a mall food-court burger and a Michelin-starred tasting menu, and where the plant-based cooking draws on Indian, Middle Eastern, Pan-Asian and Californian influences all at once.

All eight picks are verified open as of October 2026. Start with Seva Table for a slow garden breakfast, Dip Dash for Pan-Asian comfort, and Planet Terra for organic everyday eating, then graze from Alserkal Avenue to Expo City to Dubai Hills. A note on the traps: Plantoholic on the Palm is flagged closed, and the Wild & The Moon branch at DIFC's Gate Avenue has shut, so this list sticks to the trading Alserkal Avenue cafe and rooms that are actually serving right now.
