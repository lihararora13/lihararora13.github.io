Sendai is a strong vegan city for Tohoku: a fully vegan Ain Soph cafe on Jozenji-dori, a macrobiotic restaurant in Ichibancho, a vegetarian pizza oven in the suburbs, and a macrobiotic cafe by the station. Most of the best tables sit in Aoba Ward around Ichibancho and Kokubuncho.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Manoya and Nobatte are both flagged on HappyCow as having limited vegan options, so they are kept low in the ranking and the body says to check the vegan menu on arrival.
