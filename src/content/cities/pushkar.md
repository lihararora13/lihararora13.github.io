Pushkar is the most vegan-friendly town on this list, and it is not close: a holy town where vegetarian food is the default, the cafés are run by travellers and locals who know what vegan means, and the lake ghats are lined with places that serve Buddha bowls, falafel and smoothie bowls all day. Two fully vegan cafés anchor a guide that never leaves the old town.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and two of the eight are fully vegan. Start with a smoothie bowl at Honey & Spice, a falafel at Ganga Laffa, and a lake-view breakfast at Cafe Lake View. One note: Un-Earth Cafe is reported temporarily closed in 2026, so it stays off this list.
