Nottingham does vegan food with a community spirit. The best tables run from the volunteer-run Crocus Cafe in Lenton to the vintage tearoom of Bread and Butterflies in West Bridgford, with the Hockley quarter holding the city's liveliest strip: Stoney Street alone has Mowgli, Bunk and Annie's Burger Shack within a few doors of each other. Cafes, bakeries and burger joints outnumber fine dining here, and the scene is better for it.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and the rest vegan-friendly. Start with the shawarma seitan wrap at Crocus, the vegan high tea at Bread and Butterflies, a tiffin box at Mowgli, the vegan wings at Bunk, a vegan burger at Annie's, brunch at Cafe Roya, a grilled sandwich at the Corner Cafe, and a falafel wrap at Dash Vegan. A note on the traps: Prickly Pear, Nottingham's only 100% vegan cafe, is permanently closed, and the Number 12 cafe mentioned in older guides has closed too, so use the addresses on this page, not the ones in older guides.
