Lübeck does vegan food inside one of Germany's prettiest old towns, and the whole list fits inside a short walk of the Holstentor. The best tables cluster in the Innenstadt, around the Markt, the Mühlenstraße and the Obertrave, and they cook vegan sushi and noodle bowls, raw cakes and bircher muesli, baked potatoes piled high, vegan burgers and waffles, and a seasonal sharing menu with fermented flavours. This is a scene of small owner-run rooms plus two reliable chains, with two fully vegan anchors and a vegan-friendly bench behind them.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the vegan sushi at NI-Vegan, a raw cake at Marae, the vegan Vöner plate at Affenbrot, a Kumpir at Kartoffelspeicher, a baked potato at Erdapfel, a vegan burger at Peter Pane, a vegan waffle at Kleines Waffelhaus, and the seasonal sharing plates at Unterfreunden. A note on the traps: at both potato counters, say vegan clearly when you order, because cheese goes on by default, and at Erdapfel in particular, double-check what arrives, since reviews report dairy turning up after vegan orders.
