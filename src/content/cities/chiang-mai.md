Chiang Mai might be the most vegan-friendly city in Thailand, and it wears that lightly. The Old City alone holds a cluster of fully vegan kitchens within a short walk of Tha Phae Gate, from khao soi and massaman curry to pizza, burgers and pancakes, and the scene spills out to Nimman, the Night Bazaar and a temple garden west of the moat. It is the rare city where you never have to plan around being vegan, you just wander and eat.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and seven of the eight are fully vegan. Start with a khao soi at Goodsouls, a garden lunch at Reform Kafe, a slow lunch under the Bodhi tree at Pun Pun, a pizza at Rad Rabbit, a Burmese tea-leaf salad at Free Bird, a gyoza and pineapple fried rice night at Vegan Heaven, a drive out to the Vegano Bistro, and a breakfast bowl at Asa. A note on the traps: the original Taste From Heaven has no fresh 2026 confirmation, so its sister spot Vegan Heaven is the live one, and Chada Vegetarian still offers eggs on request, so it stays off a fully vegan list.
