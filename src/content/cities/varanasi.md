Banaras feeds pilgrims by the thousand, and most of that food is already vegetarian, which makes it one of the easiest old cities in India to eat plant-based. The vegan heart of it sits around Assi Ghat, where backpacker cafes, Tibetan kitchens and a women-run vegan cafe share the same lanes, and the ghats themselves supply the atmosphere for free.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. One is fully vegan, the wonderful Aum Cafe, and the rest are vegan-friendly, so ask for no ghee, no dahi and no cheese. Start with the chocolate banana pancakes at Aum, the wood-fired veg pizza at Pizzeria Vaatika, and the baati chokha at Baati Chokha, and let the evening aarti do the rest.
