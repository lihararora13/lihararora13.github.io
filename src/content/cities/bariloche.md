Bariloche is a seasonal mountain city, and its vegan scene follows the ski and summer seasons: a fully vegan food truck by the lake, a pay-by-weight vegetarian restaurant on San Martín, and a string of lakeside and Colonia Suiza tables where vegan dishes are marked on otherwise mixed menus. The cooking leans hearty, seitan asado, lentil stew and lake-view risotto, built for cold evenings.

The seitan asado at Rico Vegetariano and the fully vegan tacos at Limotruck are the two plates to plan around, with the lentil stew at Silvestre and the Indian street food at Baba's as backups. All eight pins on this list are neighbourhood-level approximate, as the venues are small, seasonal or lakeside, and the city body notes that. A second venue called Morfys without the Vegetariano suffix serves meat and is not this pick.
