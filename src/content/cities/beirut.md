Beirut might be the easiest city on this list to eat vegan without trying. Lebanese food is built on falafel, hummus, tabbouleh, mujadara, foul and mezze that are vegan by tradition, and the city has added a growing wave of dedicated vegan kitchens on top. The best eating clusters in Hamra, Gemmayzeh, Mar Mikhael and Bourj Hammoud, all walkable, all loud, all generous.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with a burger at Orenda, a vegan shawarma at Tabliyit Massaad, a falafel at Sahyoun, and a mezze spread anywhere that looks busy. A note on the traps: Luna's Kitchen, a 100 percent vegan spot, is listed as possibly temporarily closed, so it is not on this list.
