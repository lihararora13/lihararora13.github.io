Perth might be Australia's most underrated vegan city, and it barely has to try: year-round sunshine, a huge Asian community, and a fiercely loyal plant-based crowd mean the best tables range from a garden cafe in Fremantle to a laksa den in the southern suburbs, with Northbridge packing half the list into a few walkable blocks. Come hungry, book the weekend yum cha, and save room for dessert.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026: three are fully vegan and five are vegan-friendly, which is just how Perth rolls. Start with brunch at Flora & Fauna, graze the laksa and bao at Vegan World, hit the Sunday buffet at Loving Hut, and work your way through Govinda's ten-dollar thali, Kaze Bento's vegan katsu, Pearth's mushroom Benedict, Little Beans' Penang noodles, and a Snickers waffle at Crunchbox. A note on the traps: Mother, the former Raw Kitchen in Fremantle, is permanently closed, Juju Berry + Co in Mount Hawthorn has shut, and Veggie Mama in Mount Lawley is closed too, so none of them made the cut.
