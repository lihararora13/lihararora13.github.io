Casablanca is Morocco's biggest city, and its vegan eating clusters in Gauthier and Maarif, the modern quarters where contemporary restaurants and cafes sit, with a second thread of traditional Moroccan cooking that is often vegan by accident: vegetable couscous, tagine, zaalouk and bissara. There are only two fully vegan restaurants in the city, NIYA and Taste The Divine, and the rest of the list is vegan-friendly places with labelled vegan dishes, so confirm with staff where the menu is not labelled.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegan-friendly. Start with a burger at NIYA, a high-protein burger at Taste The Divine, a falafel bowl at Organic Kitchen, vegetable couscous at Al Mounia, a chia bowl at La Grande Brasserie, onigiri at Ukiyo, tofu satay at Chee Is Thai, and a salad at Bondi. A note on the traps: NIYA has two listings in Casablanca, the Rue Ain Asserdoun address on this page is the one to use, and at Organic Kitchen some labels have been reported incorrect, so state vegan when ordering.
