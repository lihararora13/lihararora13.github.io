Thessaloniki is Greece's second city and a booming food metropolis, and Greek cuisine is naturally friendly to vegan diets: meze, legumes, grilled vegetables, spanakópita and dolmades, with the rise of veganism spawning fully plant-based venues and encouraging traditional restaurants to offer vegan alternatives. The dedicated vegan spots are small, so the guide mixes them with traditional tavernas that adapt well: Roots for vegan gyros and moussaka, Avocado for a 100% vegan breakfast, and Gaea or Ouzeri Tou Likarou for an evening of shared mezzés on a terrace.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegan-friendly. Start with the vegan gyros at Roots, banana pancakes at Avocado Green Kitchen, a market bowl at Veggie Delights, a vegan lemon tart at Sweets & Roots, and a round of mezzés at Gaea. A note on the traps: Roots moved from its old Vassilissis Olgas address to Mpalanou 4 in the Athonos alley, so use the address on this page; OMG in SKG, the vegan street-food kiosk by the Arch of Galerius, is closed, so do not follow older guides to it; and in traditional tavernas always ask whether a dish contains milk, honey or whey, since vegetarian does not always mean vegan.
