Krabi's vegan scene is split between Ao Nang beach, where three fully vegan restaurants sit within walking distance, and Krabi Town inland, with a jay buffet and a night market. Ao Nang is the practical base: Plant-erian and The Planters are sister fully vegan restaurants, Govinda's is a 100% vegetarian and vegan Indian kitchen, and Cooper's Farm2Plate cooks vegan food from a permaculture farm. The scene is seasonal, with hours and menus shifting between high and low season, so check directly before a special trip.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the khao soi at The Planters, the curries at Plant-erian, the thali at Govinda's, and the farm plates at Cooper's. A note on the traps: Railay and Phra Nang have nothing verified, so order jay or tofu there rather than expecting a vegan restaurant; and several Ao Nang kitchens change hours between seasons, so confirm the day before you go.
