Coventry's vegan scene is not the prettiest in the country, but it is honest and well fed: a vegan cafe on Far Gosford Street, a meat-free chippy on Clay Lane, pure-vegetarian Indian kitchens on Foleshill Road and a couple of newer vegan-friendly rooms. The strength here is Indian vegetarian food, and the list leans into it.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at The Pod Cafe on Far Gosford Street, Veggie Corner on Clay Lane and Cravers on Gosford Street. The traps were minor: one much-listed cafe that closed in 2025 is not on this list, and a Foleshill Road kitchen that had changed hands is listed under its current name.
