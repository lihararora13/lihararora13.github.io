Monterrey is Mexico's meat-and-mountains city, and its vegan scene answers with pizzas, antojitos, curries and buffets that hold their own. Most of the best tables sit in Centro and Mitras, within a short ride of the Macroplaza, with one worth-the-drive stop in San Pedro Garza García.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with strong vegan menus. Start with a pizza at Vegarum, a wood-fired pizza at Pizzarnik, a pirata at Revolución Verde, a bowl at Vida Atemporal, a plate at Casa Macro, the buffet at Hare Krishna, a samosa at Shiva Station, and a calzone at Ixteriolo's.
