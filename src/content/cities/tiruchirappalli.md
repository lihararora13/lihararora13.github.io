Trichy is a temple-and-college town where vegetarian food is the default, not the exception, and the best of it is in Thillainagar's breakfast rooms and the Cantonment's newer veg kitchens. Veggies Express is the surprise: a modern vegetarian restaurant doing Thai red curry with pineapple tempeh and the city's first veg Mandi biryani. The rest is classic Tamil tiffin, done well and priced for students.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan. Start with dosa and uthappam at Shri Sangeetha's, the tempeh dishes at Veggies Express, a benne dosa at Benne Bhavan, and breakfast at Ezham Suvai near Thiruvanaikoil. A note on the traps: ghee roast, benne (butter) dosa and curd rice are the house signatures, so order plain and ask the kitchen to hold the dairy.
