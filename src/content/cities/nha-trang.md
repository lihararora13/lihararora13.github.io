Nha Trang's vegan scene is concentrated in the centre, around Nguyen Thien Thuat and Huynh Thuc Khang, with a fully vegan supershop, a vegan bistro, and a cluster of local chay kitchens within a few minutes' walk of each other. The best meals are the vegan bowls at iVegan, the hummus at Phyto, and the hotpots at But Viet, with cheap local kitchens filling the gaps.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the falafel bowl at iVegan, the hummus at Phyto, the hotpot at But Viet, and the pho chay at Bo De Sao. A note on the traps: Grounded Chay is temporarily closed, so it is left out; and Thong Dong can still be closed after its posted 9am start, so do not count on it opening on time.
