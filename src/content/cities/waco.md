Waco's vegan scene is a college-town one: a downtown coffee house with one of the city's most vegan-friendly menus, an upscale brunch spot on Washington Avenue, an organic milk-tea shop, and Indian, Japanese and Korean kitchens with vegan dishes. Baylor students keep the plant-based options coming, and the best tables are within a few minutes of campus.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. Start with the plant-based breakfast burrito at Dichotomy, the vegan brunch plates at Milo All Day, the vegan thali at Taz Indian Cuisine, and the vegan ramen at Teriyaki Park. A note on the traps: Waco's legendary barbecue scene has essentially no vegan options beyond bread and pickles, so the barbecue joints stay off this list. The Clean Plate 200 is a small soul-food pop-up with very limited hours, so it is not listed as a restaurant pick.
