Leicester's vegan scene is built on its Indian kitchens: Kerala cooking on Granby Street, pure-veg restaurants on Belgrave Road and a 100 percent vegan street-food stall on Welford Road. Around them are a vegetarian institution on Free Lane, a peace cafe in the Guildhall and a bakery, all within a short walk of the centre.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at Herb on Granby Street, Huns Vegan on Welford Road and The Good Earth on Free Lane. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegetarian restaurant that closed in 2024 is not on this list, and a Belgrave Road kitchen that had rebranded is listed under its current name.
