Ubud might be the easiest place on earth to eat vegan, and it does it with a grin. The town in Bali's jungle interior is packed with fully plant-based kitchens, from raw food cafes and permaculture gardens to wood-fired pizza and all-you-can-eat warung buffets, and the scene spills out from the palace crossroads into Penestanan, Sayan, Peliatan and the rice fields of Mas. It is the rare town where being vegan needs no planning at all, you just wander downhill and eat.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and seven of the eight are fully vegan. Start with a peanut miso ramen at Zest, a carrot-salmon bruschetta at Plant Bistro, a jack rendang at Moksa, a laksa bowl at Sayuri, a burrito at Sage, an afternoon buffet at Sawobali, a salad-bar lunch at Alchemy, and a rendang at Wild Vegan. A note on the traps: Moksa still lists a few vegetarian items, so ask for coconut syrup instead of honey there, and the popular Siboghana Waroeng and The Elephant serve meat or eggs alongside their vegan dishes, so they stay off a fully vegan list.
