Coimbra's vegan scene is small and student-shaped, concentrated in the old town around Rua Olivença, Rua João de Ruão and Rua Fernandes Tomás. One of the eight picks is fully vegan, a macrobiotic restaurant with no animal products at all, and the rest are vegan-friendly kitchens, from a vegetarian restaurant in a tower to a gelato shop with vegan flavours.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at Cozinha Consciente in the Galerias Topázio, Bruxa da Horta on Rua João de Ruão and Vhita Fit on Rua Dom João III. The traps were real here: Taberna de Portugal is in Leiria, not Coimbra, so it does not appear on this list.
