St. John's is one of the thinnest vegan scenes in this batch, centred on Duckworth Street downtown: a fully vegan Chinese restaurant, a vegetarian and vegan taco spot, and a handful of cafes and restaurants with vegan dishes. It is a small harbour-city scene where the best tables are within a few blocks of each other.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 1 of them fully vegan. Start with the Singapore noodles at Peaceful Loft, the Bev's taco at Poyo, the vegan dishes at Gingergrass, and a vegan plate at NJ's Kitchen. A note on the traps: The local vegan scene has shrunk in recent years, and several older guides list spots that have closed or changed menus; the picks below are the ones with current listings. A Cut Above Vegan Butchery is a market stall, not a restaurant, so it is not listed as a pick.
