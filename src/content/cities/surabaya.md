Surabaya's vegetarian scene is wide and scattered, with kitchens in Gubeng, Wonokromo and the centre, and it runs on Indonesian vegetarian cooking, with tempeh, tofu and vegetable dishes at its heart. The anchors are Loving Hut, an all-vegan restaurant, Sehat, a vegan depot, and SriWijaya, a vegan vegetarian restaurant, with vegan cafes and vegetarian kitchens filling out the map. Expect to travel between picks, and expect most kitchens to be vegetarian rather than fully vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan dishes at Loving Hut, the vegetarian buffet at Sehat, the vegan dishes at SriWijaya, and the vegetarian dishes at Verte Cafe. A note on the traps: Verte Cafe's address is listed two ways, Cisadane 26 on HappyCow and Jl. Jambi 47 in a guide, so check before you go; and the Indonesian vegetarian kitchens use egg and dairy, so vegans should ask for dishes without them.
