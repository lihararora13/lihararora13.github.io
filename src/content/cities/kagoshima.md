Kagoshima's vegan scene is thin and scattered, which is why this guide runs to seven picks rather than eight. The anchor is Vegetus Sara, a vegetarian restaurant whose vegan set menu reviewers call one of the best in Japan, with a vegetable cafe, a farm restaurant, a Sri Lankan kitchen with a vegan menu and a vegan bento cafe filling out the map around the Tenmonkan shopping district and the ferry port. Everything here is a short tram ride apart, but hours are short and several kitchens close midweek, so check the day before you go.

All seven picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the vegan set at Vegetus Sara, the vegetable plates at Vege Cafe Lo, the farm lunch at Vege Marche 19, and the vegan curry sets at Sri Lanka Kagoshima. A note on the traps: Vegetus Sara's menu is vegan except for one set that adds a side of fish, so order the vegan set by name; the Yakushima listings that HappyCow folds into Kagoshima results (A Heavenly Kitchen, Hiyoriya) are on a different island and are not counted here; and several older blog lists still send people to kitchens that have closed.
