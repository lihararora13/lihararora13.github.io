Tübingen might be the strongest small-city vegan scene in Germany, and it shows in the numbers: four of the eight picks are fully vegan, and the top of the list is genuinely distinctive, a 100 percent vegan Afro-Caribbean lunch restaurant with a daily-changing buffet, an all-vegan fast-food counter, a vegan specialty coffee café up by the AI centre, and a vegan bistro inside a zero-waste shop. The rest of the list fills the gaps the way a good university town should: a bistro café by the station, falafel and shawarma, build-your-own bowls in the old town, and a vegan Ayurvedic Indian imbiss.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with jollof rice at BongoRoots, köfte at Köstlich Vegan, lemon poppy pancakes at Apoteka, the Mittagstisch at Speicher, a tofu salad bowl at GenussArt, a vegan döner plate at Veggie Box, a signature bowl at Short Stories, and a masala dosa at Maharaja Imbiss. A note on the traps: Vegi's all-vegan claim is disputed in reviews, with non-vegan yoghurt sauce and parmesan reported, so it is not listed here. And mind the hours: BongoRoots is a weekday lunch spot closed at weekends, Apoteka opens Thursday to Sunday only, and Speicher is closed on Sundays and Mondays.
