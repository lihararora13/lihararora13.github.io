Portland, Maine punches well above its size for plant-based food: a walkable downtown where Congress Street alone holds a vegetarian bistro, an Eritrean restaurant, and a Thai tapas bar, with the Old Port adding pizza and donuts and East Bayside adding Tex-Mex smoke. The scene leans vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is the honest shape of it here, but the vegan dishes are marked, generous, and taken seriously.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all eight of them vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, which is worth knowing before you go: there is no all-vegan restaurant in the city right now. Start with the Siamese Dream Curry Noodles at Green Elephant, the vegan cookies at Bam Bam Bakery, the veggie tacos at Terlingua, a vegan pizza at Flatbread, a vegan donut at The Holy Donut, a tofu scramble at Hot Suppa, the vegetable combo at Asmara, and the tofu tapas at Boda.
