Cincinnati does vegan food the way it does its chili: with local pride and a counter-service heart. The scene clusters in three pockets, the historic Findlay Market and Over-the-Rhine downtown, the Northside business district on Hamilton Avenue, and the east-side neighborhoods of Evanston, Pleasant Ridge and Madisonville, and it leans comfort-first: deli sandwiches on sourdough, smoked jackfruit ribs, Cincinnati-style chili made vegan, and bakery cases full of cinnamon buns. The all-vegan spots carry the list, but the mixed cafes and the Nepali kitchen round it out with some of the best vegan breakfasts and dumplings in Ohio.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and Melt Revival and Bridges Nepali Cuisine vegan-friendly. Start with the Italian sandwich at Harmony Plant Fare, the jackfruit ribs at Ike's, the Sunny Burger at Loving Hut, a Philly cheesesteak hoagie at Vegan One Stop, a bourbon cherry cupcake at Happy Chicks, the vegan biscuits and gravy at Melt Revival, the vegan momos at Bridges, and a tasting board at Mad Cheese. A note on the traps: Harmony Plant Fare keeps Findlay Market hours and closes Mondays, Vegan One Stop's late-night window varies so check before a late run, and My Vegan's similarly named listings elsewhere are not this city's spots, so use the addresses on this page.
