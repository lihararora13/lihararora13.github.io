Manali is a cafe town first and a mountain town second. Old Manali is the cafe village, a lane that climbs from the bridge past the Manu Temple with more than seventy cafes, bakeries and dhabas on it, and Mall Road has the first-floor cafes over the market. The vegan eating is easy here: momos, thukpa and rajma-rice are on every menu, and the backpacker cafes keep a plant-based card alongside the regular one.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. None is all-vegan, so ask for no ghee, no dahi and no cheese, and skip the siddu, it uses ghee. Winter warning: several Old Manali cafes reduce hours or close in the cold months, so check before walking up the lane, and favour the year-round Mall Road tables in deep winter. Start with the plant-based pancakes at Little Sisters, the thukpa at Chopsticks, and the apple pie at Cafe 1947.
