Daegu's vegan scene is small, young and concentrated in Jung-gu, around Gukchaebosang-ro and Samdeok-dong. The anchor is The Common, a 100% vegan brunch cafe and zero-waste shop with resident rescue cats, and the city also holds a Loving Hut, a vegan bakery-bookshop, and Legume, Asia's first vegan Michelin-starred restaurant, which moved to the Dongdaegu Station area. Korean temple-food traditions sit underneath the scene, and the best meals mix Korean and Western cooking rather than copying either.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the tempeh plate at The Common, the vegan bulgogi at Loving Hut, a tasting menu at Legume, and the rice-salt bread at Chaekppang Gosranhi. A note on the traps: Legume moved from Jangchung-dong to the Dongdaegu Station neighbourhood, so older guides send people to the wrong address; several small vegan cafes in Daegu open only a few days a week, so check hours before you walk over.
