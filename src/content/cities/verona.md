Verona is a surprise: a small, dense historic centre with five all-vegan addresses, including the city's first vegan restaurant and a 100% vegan pastry shop from the same owners. The picks cluster within a few minutes of the Arena, from Piazzetta Portichetti to the San Zeno lanes, and they run from fine organic cooking to a vegan burger chain and an Asian street-food counter.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan carbonara at La Lanterna, the parmigiana at Flora, the cannoli at La Magnolia, and the pad thai at Nosh. A note on the traps: Kymi, a small 100% plant-based fast-food spot, could not be pinned to a verified street address, so it stays off the list; La Lanterna is not inexpensive, with dinner for two around 73 euros, and the radicchio dishes run bitter, so order the carbonara first; and Flower Burger is a chain, but it is the reliable informal fallback when the independents are full.
