Hoi An is one of the easiest places in Vietnam to eat vegan, with two layers: cheap local chay kitchens that cook Buddhist vegetarian food, and fully vegan cafes aimed at travellers. The best meals are the vegan versions of Hoi An's own specialities, white rose dumplings, cao lau and wontons, cooked without shrimp, and the town's vegetarian eating peaks on the first and fifteenth days of the lunar month, when local families eat chay.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan, and every one was checked on Google Maps in October 2026. Start with the set menus at Minh Hien, the fully vegan menu at V Vegan, the brunch at Kurumi, and the 20,000 dong banh mi at Le Hoi. A note on the traps: Am moved from Ly Thai To to Bau Oc Ha 1, An Hy moved from Tieu La to 59 Phan Chau Trinh, and Nhat Da also moved, so older guides send people to the wrong addresses; and small family places sometimes shut for a day after the full moon.
