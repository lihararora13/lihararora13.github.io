Prague may be famous for castles, cobblestones and beer halls, but its vegan kitchens have been quietly building something wonderful for years: Czech classics like goulash and svickova reimagined without a scrap of meat, Vietnamese pho and banh mi in a fairytale dining room, and brunch spots pouring oat lattes from early morning. This is a walkable, affordable capital where you can graze from a masala dosa to a tasting-style dinner without ever leaving the centre.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with Vegan's Prague for Czech classics under the castle, Palo Verde for a lazy brunch, and Strecha for lunch with a conscience, then book Linh for Vietnamese, Chutnej for a treat-yourself dinner, Dosa Dosa for South Indian comfort, Eaternia for diner fun, and Maitrea for a proper Old Town sit-down. A note on the traps: both Prague branches of Forky's are flagged permanently closed, so this list sticks to rooms that are actually serving right now.
