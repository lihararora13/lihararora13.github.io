Phnom Penh makes eating plant-based easy. The scene runs from the Russian Market's first 100% vegan cafe to a leafy garden restaurant in BKK1, with Khmer vegetarian cooking, Mediterranean vegan food and healthy brunch cafes in between. Most places sit in the Tuol Tompoung, BKK1 and riverside neighbourhoods, and prices run from a dollar a plate at the local vegetarian canteens to mid-range cafe prices.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and the rest vegan-friendly. Start with the Live Sandwich at Vibe, the king mushroom Aussie burger at Bong Bonlai, the falafel waffle benedict at ARTillery, an earth bowl at Backyard Cafe, the jackfruit tacos at Farm to Table, a vegan pho at Magnolia, a noodle soup at SURN YI and a hotpot at Quan Com Chay Orusay. A note on the traps: many Phnom Penh spots are vegetarian with vegan options rather than all-vegan, so confirm dishes are vegan when you order, and note that some local vegetarian canteens close by early afternoon.
