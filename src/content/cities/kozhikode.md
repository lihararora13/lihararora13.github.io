Calicut's food fame is built on biryani and halwa, but the vegetarian side of the city has quietly grown a proper vegan anchor. Vegan Valley bills itself as Kerala's first vegan cafe, serving burgers, Buddha bowls, pesto pasta and coconut-shell desserts, all plant-based. Around it, a wave of new pure-veg openings near the beach and the bus stand, plus the old lunch homes, give vegans a full day of eating.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and one of the eight is fully vegan. Start at Vegan Valley for the Buddha bowl and vegan burger, then Soak'd by Veg-O for masala dosa and soak'd idlis by the beach, Satara Veg Cafe for benne masala dosa, and Sri Venkatesh Lunch Home for a beachside vada and tea. A note on the traps: the beach cafes lean on curd, cheese and milkshakes, so check the menu before ordering the creamy stuff.
