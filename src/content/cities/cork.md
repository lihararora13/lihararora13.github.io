Cork's vegan scene is small and high quality, built around the English Market quarter: a vegetarian fine-dining room on Lancaster Quay, a vegetarian Italian on Castle Street, a cafe inside the Quay Co-op and a handful of excellent vegan-friendly kitchens, from Palestinian street food to Japanese noodles. None of the eight is fully vegan, but the standard of the vegan dishes is high across the board.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan. Start at Paradiso on Lancaster Quay, Sonflour on Castle Street and My Goodness Café on Sullivan's Quay. One note: My Goodness is inside the Quay Co-op at 24 Sullivan's Quay, so the Co-op is not listed separately. Book Paradiso ahead, as it is the city's most in-demand table.
