La Plata's vegan scene is small, young and concentrated in the casco urbano, the grid of numbered streets around the centre. Two fully vegan anchors lead it, a plant-based pizzeria and a monthly vegan pop-up, and the rest of the list is vegetarian takeaways, a natural-foods shop and a veggie cafe, all within walking distance of each other.

The plant-based pizzas at Crisana and the seitan sandwiches at the monthly Veganeando pop-up are the two experiences to plan around. Radio Pura Vida was checked and not used as it is a bar with weak vegan options, Sabba Vegan Bakery is a market stall with no fixed address, and Mama Quilla is frozen delivery only. The two all-vegan pins are approximate, as noted below.
