Ljubljana packs its vegan scene into a walkable old town, and it cooks with the seasons the way Slovenia always has: local vegetables, sourdough, mushrooms, buckwheat and berries, turned into bowls, pizzas, burgers and tasting menus that never feel like a compromise. Most of the best tables sit within a few minutes of Preseren Square, so a hungry afternoon can happily hop from a deli lunch to a juice-bar snack to a proper dinner.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian with a strong vegan menu. Start with the seasonal tasting plate at Veganika, a four-course lunch at Gaudi&Naan, a sourdough pizza at Grashka Deli, a whole-food bowl at Bistro Maha, an oyster mushroom burger at Crusty Kuhna, a smoothie bowl at Barbarella, a raw cake at Green Republic, and a vegan pizza at Top Pizza.
