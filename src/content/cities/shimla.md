Shimla is a walking city, and its food scene walks with it: Mall Road is a pedestrian strip of pure-veg restaurants, heritage cafes and sweet shops, with the Ridge at one end and the old bazaars at the other. The headline is The Baan Cafe, Shimla's first and only 100% vegan cafe, tucked away on a mountain lane in Longwood, and around it sits a bench of Mall Road institutions that have been feeding the hill station for generations.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. One is fully vegan, The Baan Cafe, and the rest are vegan-friendly, so ask for no ghee, no dahi and no cheese, and order the chole bhature, the Himachali dham plates and the momos. Start with the vegan pizza at The Baan Cafe, the chole bhature at Baljees, and the coffee at the Indian Coffee House, and let the Mall do the rest.
