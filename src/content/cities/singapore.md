Singapore packs a whole food world into one sunny island, and the vegan corner of it is thriving: Peranakan classics, Vietnamese pho, Japanese sushi, Indonesian rendang and Korean stews, all without a scrap of meat. Hawker spirit, fine-dining polish, and everything cheerfully in between.

All eight picks are verified open as of October 2026: five are fully vegan and three are beloved vegetarian institutions with deep vegan menus, labelled honestly below. Start with Whole Earth for the special meal, The Kind Bowl for the pho everyone talks about, and Cultivate for a calm, book-lined lunch, then MRT-hop to burgers, sushi and stews.
