Albany's vegan scene clusters on Lark Street and North Pearl Street downtown, with the suburbs holding the juice bar and the donut shop. The list has a strong all-vegan core, four of the eight, and the vegan-friendly rooms fill out the week with a bar, a burrito bar, an Italian restaurant, and a juice bar. It is a scene where the all-vegan rooms are the draw and the walkable downtown makes it easy to eat through the list in a day.

Start with a burger at Wizard Burger, a plate at Subculture, and a drink at Bar Vegan, then work through Bare Blends, The Hollow, and Bombers. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Organa Juice Bar was dropped after its geocode failed; and Bitchin Donuts' pin is address-level, so use the street address when navigating.
