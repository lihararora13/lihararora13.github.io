Sheffield eats vegan the way it does everything else: independently, generously and with a strong streak of DIY. The scene clusters around Kelham Island and Neepsend, Park Hill, Sharrow Vale Road and Crookes, and it runs from all-vegan junk food and Chinese takeaway to Middle Eastern mezze, slow brunch and a proper pub Sunday roast. Kelham Island alone could fill a weekend.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and the rest vegan-friendly. Start with the vegan beef wellington at Church - Temple of Fun, the hummus plate at South Street Kitchen, a Tokyo hot dog at Pom Kitchen, the crispy aromatic duck at Wawin, a vegan full English at Dana Cafe, the chocolate cheesecake at Fusion, the vegan Sunday roast at The Beer Engine, and a vegan ramen at Maki & Ramen. A note on the traps: Make No Bones closed in July 2023, Doner Summer has closed, the Blue Moon Cafe on St James Street has closed and its building is being converted, BurgerLolz closed in 2018 and Just Falafs closed in March 2022, so use the addresses on this page, not the ones in older guides.
