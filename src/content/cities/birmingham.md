Birmingham is a city that takes its food seriously, and its vegan scene is no exception: fine dining in a Victorian arcade, wholefood cafes in the Jewellery Quarter, and a Digbeth strip that has been flying the plant-based flag for years. The picks cluster in the city centre, Digbeth and Harborne, and they cover the full spread from multi-course tasting menus to falafel wraps eaten on the move.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with the tasting menu at Land, a breakfast burrito at Alterna, whatever Ba-Ha is inventing this week, jackfruit biryani at OMNOM, a thali at Barsana, a burger at Fressh, banana blossom fish and chips at the Warehouse Cafe, and a falafel wrap at Falafel Vegan Munch. A note on the traps: Birmingham has lost a few vegan favourites to closure in recent years, including Restaurant 1847, so these eight are the ones with fresh reviews and open doors right now.
