Kitchener's vegan scene is centred on downtown King Street and the Waterloo border: a plant-based cafe that doubles as the city's living room, a 100% vegan grocery and deli, a community cafe with a living green wall, and a set of sushi, Indian and bakery spots with vegan dishes. It is a twin-city scene shared with Waterloo.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 4 of them fully vegan. Start with the Big Bang sandwich at Cafe Pyrus, the deli plates at Coven, the mushroom burger at Fresh Ground, and the vegan sushi at The Humble Lotus. A note on the traps: Odd Burger's Waterloo Town Square location is in Waterloo, not Kitchener proper, so it is not listed as a Kitchener pick. Jane Bond is in uptown Waterloo, also outside Kitchener, and is left off for the same reason.
