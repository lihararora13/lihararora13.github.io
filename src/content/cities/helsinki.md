Helsinki has been building one of Northern Europe's best vegan scenes since the boom of 2015, and the first hub on Vaasankatu in Kallio has long since spread across the whole city. The picks here run from fine dining to fast food, Thai to Vietnamese to Finnish, and nearly every restaurant in town has at least one vegan option, which makes Helsinki one of the easiest capitals in Europe to eat plant-based.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with khao soi at Thai Vegan Kitchen, the tasting menu at Magu, a burger at Junky Vegan, vegan pho at Sen Chay, a Karelian pie at Rakastan, a sandwich at Plants By Us, a healthy lunch at Zucchini, and tapas at Yes Yes Yes. A note on the traps: the scene changes fast, with newcomers arriving and old favourites closing, so these eight are the ones with fresh 2026 reviews and open doors.
