Salzburg eats vegan in the old town and Mulln, where a crowd-funded vegan cafe, an Italian vegan bistro and a vegetarian Levantine restaurant anchor a scene that stretches from the Wolf-Dietrich-Strasse to the Europark mall. The picks cluster around the Salzach, walkable from the Hauptbahnhof, with one deliberate detour to the shopping centre.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan sandwich at GustaV, the creamy gnocchi at Vegitalian, the mezze at Furo, and the plant-based roast at The Keep. A note on the traps: The Green Garden is listed as permanently closed on Wanderlog despite a 2025 new-ownership post, so it stays off the list; Vegitalian's planned move to Nonntal never materialised in listings, and it is still at Mullner Hauptstrasse 26; and GustaV is closed most days of the week, so check its hours before going.
