Biarritz is a surf town first and a vegan town second, so the list is short on fully vegan rooms and long on good vegan-friendly kitchens: a 100 percent vegan, bio and gluten-free cafe at the top, then Mediterranean, market-hall, Cajun and cafe tables that all cook proper vegan dishes. Everything is within walking distance of the Grande Plage.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at Xuxu on Avenue de la Reine Nathalie, ZOU on Rue Gambetta and Les Enfants du Marché at the market hall. The traps were real here: Bali Bowls is temporarily closed, and Chéri Bibi and Palacito have both closed, so none of them appears on this list.
