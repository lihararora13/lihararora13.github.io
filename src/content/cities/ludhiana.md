Ludhiana eats like the rest of Punjab, only more so: wheat bread rather than rice, ghee-rich dal makhani, tandoori kulchas, stuffed parathas, and in winter makki di roti with sarson da saag. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian dhabas are usually separate businesses here, which makes the pure-veg circuit easy to follow and the vegan eating a matter of knowing the right requests.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. None is all-vegan, so the rule is simple: ask for food without ghee and curd, and choose dal tadka, chana masala, aloo gobi, or South Indian dosa and idli. Start with the plant-based plates at The Holy Tree in Jawaddi, the kulcha at Friends Dhaba, and the dosa at the South Indian counters, and Ludhiana will feed you well.
