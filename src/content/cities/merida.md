Mérida is an inland city of markets, plazas and neighbourhood restaurants, not a beach town, and its vegan scene works the same way: small kitchens in Centro, Santiago and García Ginerés, cooking Yucatecan food with Mayan ingredients. Muchas Vidas is the most useful name to know, a fully vegan spot in the Santa Ana area of Centro, and Crush on Parque Santa Lucía is the vegan fast-food counter for burgers and birria-style tacos. The scene changes quickly, with small operations moving between permanent kitchens and delivery, so check opening days before a special trip.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegan-friendly. Start with the burrito at Muchas Vidas, a vegan burger at Crush, a pizza at Distrito Vegano, avocado toast at JAJANA, and a kombucha at La Kombuchería. A note on the traps: Muchas Vidas has been listed at two nearby addresses in Centro, so use the Calle 70 address on this page; Distrito Vegano's pizza kitchen sits in Francisco de Montejo north of Centro, not downtown; and several Mérida vegan spots keep weekend-focused hours, so check the current opening days before you go.
