Kiel does vegan food the way a harbour city does everything: from everywhere at once, and without much fuss. The best tables cluster along Holstenstraße and the Dreiecksplatz, with a couple of gems out in Gaarden and Schreventeich, and they cook Vietnamese phở, Taiwanese dim sum and ramen, Mexican tacos, Moroccan tajine, organic café breakfasts and Asian desserts, with three fully vegan anchors and a deep vegan-friendly bench behind them. This is a scene of small, owner-run rooms rather than chains, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the phở at Veganam, the handmade dim sum at Home Taste Jia, a taco at Bäristo (get there at lunch, it only opens a couple of hours a day), a mochi at Yueye, a pizza bianca at Subrosa in Gaarden, a vegan burrito at El Sombrero Verde, an organic breakfast at Mmhio, and a vegan tajine at Moravid. A note on the traps: Kitty Rock Belly Full is gone from Gutenbergstraße, replaced by Moravid, and Yueye is not fully vegan despite some listings saying so, since dairy milk is still served alongside the vegan desserts.
