Spokane's vegan scene is compact and walkable, clustered around downtown and the South Hill, with a strong all-vegan core: a vegan comfort-food cafe, a vegan pizzeria, and a vegan bakery and lounge. Around them sit a farm-to-table restaurant, a juice cafe, a taco truck turned brick-and-mortar, and a public house, giving the list real range. It is a scene where the all-vegan rooms are the draw and the vegan-friendly rooms fill out the week.

Start with a plate at Rut, a slice at Allie's, and a pastry at Boots, then work through Mizuna, Cascadia, and Method Juice. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Patera was dropped after its geocode failed.
