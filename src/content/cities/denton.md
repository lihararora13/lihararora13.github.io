Denton's vegan scene is a university-town one centred on the Square and the UNT campus: an all-vegan meal-prep kitchen, a vegan grill on Mulberry Street, the university's own all-vegan dining hall, and a set of Indian, Mexican and deli spots with vegan dishes. It is small, student-priced and easy to walk.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 3 of them fully vegan. Start with the Frito pie at Nature's Plate, the So Good cheeseburger at The So Good Vegan Grill, the vegan Mexican food at Pepita's, and the vegan dawg at Render Texas Delicatessen. A note on the traps: Jackie Mays Burger, a plant-forward fast-food spot, is listed as temporarily closed, so it is not included. The So Good Vegan Grill's listed hours are limited, mostly Saturday mornings, so check before you go.
