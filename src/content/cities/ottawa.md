Ottawa does vegan food the Canadian capital way: unshowy, generous and very good. The scene runs on long-running vegetarian institutions, a couple of excellent vegan newcomers, and buffets that let you try a bit of everything, spread from Westboro to the ByWard Market.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with a falafel sandwich at Chickpeas, vegan poutine at Pure Kitchen, a thali at Peace-Garden, a pay-by-weight plate at The Green Door, the buffet at Govinda's, kimchi at Korean Mother's Meals, and a cupcake at Strawberry Blonde.
