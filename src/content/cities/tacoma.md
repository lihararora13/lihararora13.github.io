Tacoma sits in Seattle's shadow and its vegan scene is smaller for it, but what it has is distinctive: one all-vegan soul food kitchen, Quickie Too, in the Hilltop, and seven vegan-friendly restaurants where the vegan dishes are a real part of the menu, a dedicated vegan sushi menu, a separate vegan menu at a Mexican cantina, vegan hot dogs on Sixth Avenue, and a bakery with vegan breakfast sandwiches. The picks cluster in downtown, the Hilltop and Sixth Avenue, so the guide works as a walk between neighborhoods.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with the Crazy Jamaican Burger at Quickie Too, the Buena Dia Burrito at Happy Belly, a Vegan Dragon Roll at En Sushi, the Vegan Cubano at The Church Cantina, a North End Not Dog at The Red Hot, the Vegan Buffalo sandwich at Peterson Bros. 1111, a vegan breakfast sandwich at Corina Bakery, and the chana masala at Gateway to India. A note on the traps: Quickie Too is takeout only with limited days, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday core, and it is cashless; and Peterson Bros. 1111 is a 21+ bar, so it is an evening pick for adults.
