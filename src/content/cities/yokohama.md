Yokohama's vegan scene is bakery-led: a fully vegan bakery by the station, a fully vegan and gluten-free pastry shop in Hiyoshi, vegan scones in Isecho, and vegan gelato by the bay. Proper sit-down vegan restaurants are thin here, so this guide leans on bakeries, cafes and the hotel vegan meal service.

All 7 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 5 of them fully vegan. The Hotel Yokohama Camelot Japan entry sells frozen vegan meals at the front desk rather than running a restaurant; it is kept as the seventh pick because the city has no eighth verified sit-down vegan restaurant. Earth Bakery is run by the Chaya Macrobiotics chain.
