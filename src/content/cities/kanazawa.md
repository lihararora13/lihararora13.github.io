Kanazawa is a macrobiotic and organic cafe city: a 30-year-old natural-food restaurant, a seed-to-table cafe, a sushi bar with a labelled vegan set, and a mostly-vegetarian organic restaurant. The best tables are in the old town around Korinbo and Higashi Chaya.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 1 of them fully vegan. Love For All serves fish in one dish only and is 99 percent vegetarian; it is filed vegan-friendly, not all-vegan. Coil serves meat but has a labelled vegan sushi set, added in February 2026.
