Cardiff does vegan food with a Welsh shrug and an Indian accent: dosa, thali, samosa and vada pav, alongside fusion specials, Chinese plates and a Caribbean café, clustered in Roath, Cathays and the City Centre around Queen Street. The scene leans Indian because the city's best vegan cooking is Indian, and the prices are the kindest in this batch, with most picks at the cheapest tier.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with a daily fusion special at Luna's Vegan Corner, fried rice at Vegan Palace, a vegan mutton curry at Aubergine, a thali at Govinda's, and a masala dosa at Vegetarian Food Studio. Two notes: Aubergine relocated from 26 Clare Street to 36-38 Cathays Terrace, so use the address on this page, not the one in older guides; and Govinda's was previously listed as vegan but was reported in March 2026 to serve dairy, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly.
