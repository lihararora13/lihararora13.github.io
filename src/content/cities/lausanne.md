Lausanne eats vegan between the station, the old town and the lake, where a fully vegan, gluten-free cafe in the old town anchors a scene of a vegetarian buffet by the station, an Ethiopian restaurant by the lake and a Lebanese manakish counter near the Riponne. The picks are walkable in the centre, so plan by meal rather than by street.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan cheesecake and shepherd's pie at Racines, the buffet at Tibits, the Ethiopian platter at Le Nil Bleu, and the manakish at Man'Ouchy. A note on the traps: Zooburger at Rue Marterey 29 and Qasti at Rue Belle-Fontaine 2 both dropped, since their addresses now house different businesses, Sunday Zoo and Obeirut; the Green Up salad bar sits on the fourth floor of Globus on Rue Mercerie, so take the lift.
