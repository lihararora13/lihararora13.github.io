Okayama's vegan scene is small but genuine, clustered around JR Okayama Station and the Omotecho shopping streets. Two fully vegan kitchens anchor it: PEACH, a plant-based restaurant with a changing seasonal menu, and CBD Green, a vegan cafe that doubles as a CBD store. Around them sit reliable vegan-friendly counters serving soy-meat pasta, Vietnamese food and Indian curries, so a plant-based traveller can eat well for several days without repeating a meal.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with PEACH's seasonal set, the daily set at CBD Green, soy-meat Bolognese at Tasty+, a vegan pho at AOBABA, then the Indian vegan choices at Arati and Ali Baba. A note on the traps: the organic store Shizen Shokuhin Nazuna and the soy-ice-cream cafe Ray Classic are both closed, and the Kurashiki listings that HappyCow folds into Okayama results (Cafe Nature, Tsubame Kissashitsu) are a separate city half an hour away, so they are not counted here.
