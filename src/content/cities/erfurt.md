Erfurt is Thuringia's medieval capital, all half-timbered lanes and the Domplatz, and its vegan scene fits the old town like it was always there. Nearly everything sits within a short walk of the centre: two fully vegan spots, a big cafe with an extensive vegan menu, a Middle Eastern kitchen, a breakfast cafe, a cake cafe, a sushi bar with a vegan all-you-can-eat, and a porridge bar at the station for early starts. It is a compact scene, and that is its charm: you can eat your way across it on foot in a day.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the seitan gyros at Green Republic, a Sloppy Joe at babi's DELI, a slice of vegan rhubarb cake at Cognito, a vegan shawarma at ALMA's, a vegan breakfast at Cafe Musti, an avocado toast at Bennedito, a vegan sushi box at SUSHIdeluxe, and a Powerkater porridge at Haferkater. A note on the traps: Cognito is vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, it serves meat alongside its vegan menu; the second babi's DELI branch on Thomasstrasse is temporarily closed, so head to Kettenstrasse; and Bennedito opens Wednesday to Saturday only.
