Fort Collins is a Colorado State University town with a long vegetarian tradition, and its vegan eating is concentrated in Old Town and the university district, within a few blocks of College Avenue. There is one fully vegan restaurant, Tasty Harmony on Oak Street, and seven vegan-friendly restaurants where the vegan dishes are a real part of the menu: a family-owned vegetarian restaurant since 1976, a music venue with a tempeh sandwich, two pizza places with vegan cheese, a Thai restaurant, an Indian-American fusion spot with vegan momos, and a coffee roaster with vegan cookies. The food is hearty and affordable, four of the eight are price level 1.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with a tempeh Reuben at Tasty Harmony, a tofu scramble at Rainbow, a house-made tempeh sandwich at Avogadro's, a vegan specialty pizza at Slyce, a vegan cheese pizza at Beau Jo's, pad Thai with tofu at Bann Thai, vegan momos at Gaia, and a vegan cookie with an oat milk latte at The Bean Cycle. A note on the traps: only Tasty Harmony is fully vegan, so specify vegan elsewhere, and at Bann Thai say no egg and no fish or oyster sauce; and Rainbow was chosen over Blue Agave Grill, which has weaker vegan coverage.
