Rotterdam does vegan food with a modern port city's energy: bold, international and unpretentious. The best tables cluster in the centre around the Markthal and the Binnenweg, with a detour south to Maashaven for Happy Food & Health, and they cook everything from vegan sushi and Jordanian falafel to Dutch snacks, pay-by-weight buffets and all-you-can-eat tapas. This is a scene of big flavours and fair prices rather than white tablecloths.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with a pistachio tiramisu at Happy Food & Health, the buffet at Spirit, Dutch bitterballen at Backyard, a dragon roll at Nori, a rainbow burger at Copper Branch, tapas at Rozey, falafel at Nabati, and a dinner at Backstage Vegan Kitchen. A note on the traps: Spirit and Rozey are vegetarian with mostly vegan menus, and Nabati is vegetarian with every dish available vegan, so order the vegan dishes at those three.
