Ahmedabad is one of the easiest Indian cities to eat vegetarian in and one of the trickier ones to eat vegan in, because Gujarat's vegetarian cooking leans heavily on dairy: ghee, paneer, curd and butter are everywhere. The dedicated vegan scene is small, one fully vegan restaurant plus a fully vegan ice cream shop, but the city's cafes and fine-dining rooms have taken to marking vegan dishes clearly on their menus, so a vegan can eat very well across Bodakdev, Prahlad Nagar and Paldi if they know where to look.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian kitchens with marked vegan menus. Start with the tofu butter masala at The Vegan Kitchen, the vegan Buddha bowl at Sante, a Korean bowl at Lollo Rosso, the dim sum sushi at The Shaka Cafe, and a choco orange ice cream at That'so. A note on the traps: La Maison Vegan, the city's old all-vegan French cafe, closed in July 2024, and Terra at Shivalik Shilp is flagged permanently closed, so do not follow older guides to either; in the vegetarian spots, ask for the vegan-marked dishes and say no ghee and no dairy.
