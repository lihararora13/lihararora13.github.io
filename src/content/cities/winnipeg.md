Winnipeg does vegan food without any fuss: a Taiwanese-Chinese buffet that has been meat-free since 2001, a plant-based café inside a second-hand bookshop in Saint-Boniface, a Wolseley comfort-food room and a waterfront café on the Red River, plus bakeries that do nothing but vegan. The scene clusters downtown, in Saint-Boniface across the river, and in Wolseley, and the best meals are the unpretentious ones: a pay-by-weight buffet, a breakfast plate, a bowl of pho, a cinnamon roll.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly. Start with the Thyme burger at Thyme, the lunch buffet at Affinity, a cinnamon roll at Cinnaholic, the vegan pho at Spice Cove, a breakfast plate at Modern Electric Lunch, the gnocchi bowl at Bonnie Day, and a vegan pizza at Cibo. A note on the traps: Boon Burger, the city's old all-vegan burger café, has closed, so do not follow older guides to its Sherbrook or Bannatyne addresses; Plant Kitchen sells mostly by pre-order and at farmers' markets, so check its hours before driving to Autumnwood Drive.
