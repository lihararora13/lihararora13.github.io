Doha's vegan scene is young, polished and surprisingly deep, built around Qatar's first fully vegan restaurant, Evergreen Organics, founded in 2016, and a newer wave of cafes, fast-food joints and museum restaurants. The Pearl, West Bay and Msheireb hold most of the action, with smoothie bowls, burgers, pizza, brunch and bakes all fully plant-based. It is a scene that feels designed rather than accidental, and it shows.

All seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all seven are fully vegan. Start with brunch at MYLK, a smoothie bowl at Evergreen on The Pearl, a burger at Nosh Cravings, and a plant-based shawarma at Thalatheen. A note on the traps: Wild and the Moon, once on HappyCow's top list, is flagged as maybe closed since February 2025, so it is not on this list.
