Nanjing has been cooking Buddhist vegetarian food since the Liang dynasty, when Emperor Wu's court made the city the birthplace of Chinese temple cuisine, and the tradition still runs through the city's great temples. This is a city where the best vegan meal is often a temple meal: noodles at Jiming Temple, a buffet at the Confucius Temple, a feast at Niushou Mountain, and dumplings at Qixia.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegetarian with strong vegan menus. Start with the QīngXīn SùShí buffet, the black truffle fried rice at XiaoQiao DaMei, the Guanyin blessing noodles at Ji Ming Temple, and the sweet and sour lotus root at YI'DAN. A note on the traps: Nanchao Vegan and the Xinbai branch of Xin Cheng Vegan both closed in 2026, so do not follow older guides to either; the Jiangning branch of Xin Cheng Vegan is the one that is open. At XiaoQiao DaMei a few tofu dishes contain egg, so confirm ingredients when ordering, and at the temple restaurants ask for the vegan dishes by name, as Chinese vegetarian cooking often includes egg.
