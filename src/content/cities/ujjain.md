Ujjain is a temple city, and its food is temple food: pure-veg thalis, sattvik kitchens and the ISKCON Govindas restaurant, all within walking distance of Mahakaleshwar Temple. The city is meat-free in the temple core, but ghee and dahi are the default, so vegans still need to ask.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city. Bapu Ki Kutia, serving since 1964, and Govindas at ISKCON are the anchors.
