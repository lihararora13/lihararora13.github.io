Kelowna's vegan scene is one of the strongest in this batch: a fully vegan cafe downtown, a plant-based wine bar in the Landmark District, a 100% vegan and gluten-free bakery, and a set of Thai, Indian and Mexican kitchens with vegan dishes, all within a few minutes of Okanagan Lake.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 4 of them fully vegan. Start with the sweet chili seitan bowl at Naked Cafe, the chorizo ranchero bowl at Frankie We Salute You, a cake donut at Jaide and Joel's, and the vegan curry at Made In India. A note on the traps: MotherLove Kombucha's vegan menu has changed over time, so it is not listed as a full pick. Several older guides list King's Vegetarian Food, which keeps very limited hours, so it is left off in favour of stronger picks.
