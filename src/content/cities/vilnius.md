Vilnius is a vegan-friendly city, and its scene is small but genuine: a fully vegan bistro, a creative vegan kitchen, a dessert bakery, and a ring of vegetarian restaurants with serious vegan dishes, all clustered in the Old Town and the streets around Gedimino Avenue. The food leans towards bowls, burgers, beetroot soup and vegan cakes, with Lithuanian classics reimagined without the meat.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegetarian or vegan-friendly with clearly marked vegan dishes. Start with beetroot soup at RoseHip, whatever Vegafe is creating this week, a buddha bowl at Agni, a poppy seed cake at KIRAS, brunch at Sweet Love, falafel at Radharane, a beetroot pizza at Casa la Familia, and a smoothie bowl at The Urban Garden. A note on the traps: Vieta, once a favourite, closed in April 2026, and Chaika has been renamed KIRAS, so neither appears under its old name.
