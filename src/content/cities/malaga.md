Málaga's vegan scene is concentrated in the centre around Plaza de la Merced and the Soho, with a vegan restaurant doing a five-course menu under 30 euros, a vegetarian and vegan kitchen, a vegan tapas bar and a vegan cafe. Four of the eight picks are fully vegan, which is a strong ratio for a city this size.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start at Saladentro, Kinoa on Calle Dr. Miguel Díaz Recio and Byoko on Plaza de la Merced. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegan restaurant that closed in 2025 is not on this list, and a cafe that had moved is listed at its current address.
