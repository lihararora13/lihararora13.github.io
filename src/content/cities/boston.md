Boston may be famous for clam chowder and baked beans, but its vegan scene quietly rules the Northeast. Our 7 picks stretch from South Boston to Somerville: a three-time Best Vegan Restaurant winner, a retro diner that Guy Fieri had to visit, Boston's oldest all-vegan restaurant, and a mother-daughter Egyptian spot that will ruin all other bowls for you.

Bring an appetite and a sense of adventure. In Boston, vegan comfort food comes with history, personality, and more mock meat than you can shake a breadstick at.
