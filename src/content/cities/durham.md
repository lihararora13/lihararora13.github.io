Durham's plant-based scene is anchored by the Research Triangle's young crowd and Duke's campus, and it cooks Southern first: vegan soul food, barbecue sandwiches, and mac and cheese sit next to Ethiopian combos, Persian stews, and vegan wings with fourteen sauces. The fully vegan rooms lead, and the vegan-friendly rooms around them are strong enough that nobody at the table has to compromise.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegan-friendly. Start with the NC BBQ sandwich at Pure Soul, the OG Chikin sandwich at Banu, the Osh soup at Sage, the vegan combo at Goorsha, the vegan wings at Heavenly Buffaloes, a pub plate at Parts & Labor, the hummus at Nikos, and a vegan scoop at The Parlour. A note on the traps: Bklyn Bakery, often listed as a Durham vegan stop, has no storefront and trades only at the Durham Farmers Market and by pickup, so it is not listed here as a restaurant.
