Geneva is not a city that shouts about its vegan food, and that is exactly the charm: the scene is compact, quality over quantity, and built around long-running neighbourhood favourites rather than tourist traps. The Pâquis, just north of the lake, is the beating heart of it, home to three excellent Ethiopian kitchens where the vegan food is the point, not an afterthought, while the Old Town, Eaux-Vives and Jonction fill in the gaps with Indian tasting menus, Lebanese mezze and sunny café cooking.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with a mesob sharing plate at Little Ethiopia, a vegetarian degustation at Awash, injera at Café d'Éthiopie, the six-course table at à table chez Anou, a quinoa salad at Bio Fred, avocado toast at Green Gorilla, a seasonal plate at Tonic, and mezze at Le Figuier. A note on the traps: Mu-Food and Café Mutin, two of the city's best-known all-vegan names, have both permanently closed, so neither makes the list.
