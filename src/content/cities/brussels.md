Brussels has grown into one of Europe's strongest plant-based cities, with more than forty fully vegan restaurants and a vegan Michelin star to its name. The scene concentrates in Ixelles, Saint-Gilles and the old centre, and it cooks at every level, from dumplings and burgers to tasting menus.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with the tasting menu at humus x hortense, bao at Lil Bao, falafel at Pois Chiche, steamed dumplings at Mo Mo, house-made tofu at Terter, a burger at Kitsune, the buffet at Pure Veg India, and the bistro plate at Les 4 Jeudis.
