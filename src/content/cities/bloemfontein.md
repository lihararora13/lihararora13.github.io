Bloemfontein is a small, honest vegan scene built on cafes and home kitchens rather than dedicated vegan restaurants, and there is no fully vegan dining room in the city. The best eating clusters around Westdene and Dan Pienaar: MelG's Kitchen cooks a daily vegan menu from a home kitchen in Bayswater, CoffeeTalks and Koekela hold the cafe end, and the national chains KAUAI and Bootlegger fill the gaps in the shopping centres.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan. Order a day ahead at MelG's Kitchen for pakoras and dal fry, get the vegan falafel meal at CoffeeTalks, and the plant power salad at KAUAI. A note on the traps: Green Olive and Conscious 108 still appear in a 2023 round-up but could not be confirmed current, so they are not on this list, and Leafy Greens Cafe and Veggie Meals Bloem are delivery-only operations rather than sit-down picks.
