San Antonio does vegan food the way it does everything else: with Tex-Mex at the centre and a lot of heart. The scene runs from a downtown taqueria doing birria quesatacos to a Southside dry bar doing phish tacos, from a gluten-free Tex-Mex kitchen to a Venezuelan arepa shop, and it is one of the most fun vegan cities in Texas.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly with serious vegan menus. Start with a La Lucha taco at Plantaqueria, phish tacos at HASH, a daily special at Green Vegetarian, Tex-Mex plates at Viva Vegeria, vegan nachos at La Botanica, sweet potato waffles at Pharm Table, a Vegana arepa at Four Brothers, and a vegan club at The Cove. A note on the traps: Vegan Avenue is relocating after October 31, Kind Burgers is possibly temporarily closed, and Plantology is reported maybe closed, so none of them make the list.
