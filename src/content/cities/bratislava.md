Bratislava eats vegan in the old town, where seven fully vegan restaurants anchor one of the strongest vegan scenes in Central Europe, led by a venue ranked fifth in HappyCow's 2025 top ten vegan restaurants in the world. The picks are all in Stare Mesto, walkable from the main square, and the scene runs from raw food to Slovak halusky to vegan street food.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan. Start with the tasting menu at ViK, the Balans burger at Balans Bistro, the raw dishes at BEMBA, and the poke bowls at Green Leaf. A note on the traps: Balans Bistro relocated from Zivnostenska 2 to Jozefska 19, so use the current address; BEMBA relocated from Halkova 1 to Kollarovo namestie 17; Vegan Kiosk re-opened in 2020 in the same plaza, on Grosslingova 11; and Vegan Pub was originally called Fresh House, with all dishes starting vegan and guests choosing plant or dairy cheese.
