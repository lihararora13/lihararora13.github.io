Cuenca, the alternate that replaced Trujillo in this round, has a compact vegan-friendly scene in its UNESCO-listed historic centre and the El Ejido district to the south. Only one pick is fully vegan, the Fratello cafe on Av. 12 de Abril, and the rest are vegetarian delis and kitchens where vegan dishes are a real part of the menu, built on Ecuadorian staples like llapingachos, humita and mote.

The humita de choclo and llapingachos at Fratello are the plates to plan around, with the deli plates at Comer Sanar Vivir and the veggie fast food at Frijol and Mole as backups. Nandala Cocina Vegana was checked and dropped as temporarily closed. El Nuevo Paraíso has three conflicting listings under the same name and phone; the Pdte. Córdova 8-18 address is used as it is marked open.
