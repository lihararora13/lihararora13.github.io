Long Beach is the beach city that eats like a vegan town: Retro Row alone could carry a guide, and the scene stretches from Belmont Heights brunch to Westside sushi to Los Altos fast food. The food leans comfort, breakfast burritos, burgers, pizza, sushi, and brunch, with a beach-town looseness that makes every meal feel like a holiday.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan, no decoding required. Start with a waffle deluxe at The Grain Cafe, a Southern fried chick'n sammie at The Wild Chive, a Big Zac at Plant Power, a burger at Next Level, vegan sushi at Vegan Castle, a pizza at MangiaFoglie, a daily plate at PlanTita's Kitchen, and a breakfast bowl at Higher Taste.
