Quito's vegan scene clusters in La Mariscal and the Old Town, with a couple of stops in the north of the city, and it cooks with the Andes: quinoa, potatoes, corn, plantain and avocado, turned into set lunches, vegan versions of Ecuadorian classics, sushi, and very good cakes. Most of the best tables are also very cheap.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegetarian with strong vegan menus. Start with a daily special at Prana Veggie, a vegan Ecuadorian plate at Vegano De Altura, a veggie burger at El Maple, a set lunch at Quinua, the buffet at Govinda Gopal, an almuerzo at Kay Pacha, a plate at La Cuchara, and a soup at Donde Gopal.
