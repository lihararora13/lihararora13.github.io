Vizag is a port city that eats early and eats vegetarian more often than visitors expect. The tiffin rooms around Jagadamba and VIP Road serve ghee karam dosa and filter coffee from 7:30 in the morning, while the newer cafes in Madhurawada and around the beach do veg burgers, burrito bowls and hot chocolate for the evening crowd. There is no all-vegan restaurant in the city yet, so this guide mixes the two scenes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan. Start with a ghee podi masala dosa at Sripatham, a budget veg dinner at Suto Coffee, an Andhra thali at Vaisakhi, and a beach-road tiffin at Vasenapoli. A note on the traps: ghee dosa, paneer dishes and ras malai are the signatures here, so ask for the dosa without ghee and skip the dairy desserts.
