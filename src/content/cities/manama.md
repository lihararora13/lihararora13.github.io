Manama is the surprise of this region: Bahrain's capital has a real plant-based scene, with the fully plant-based Plant Cafe in Hamala, the zero-waste and 100% vegan Petiole Cafe in Janabiyah, and a plant-based dessert bar in the Harbour Gate towers. The fully vegan rooms sit outside the old centre, in Hamala, Janabiyah and Saar, while the old city contributes pure-vegetarian Indian restaurants and the dessert bars, so a vegan eats well across the whole metro area.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the mezze at Plant Cafe, the vegan breakfast plate at Petiole, and the vegan chocolates at Raw Candy. A note on the traps: several of the fully vegan rooms are outside central Manama, so a taxi is needed, and the old city's Indian restaurants are vegetarian rather than vegan, so always specify no dairy.
