Santa Cruz is a surf town with a health-food backbone, and its vegan eating is woven into the downtown rather than fenced off in a vegan quarter: the all-vegan anchor is Veg on the Edge in Abbott Square Market, and around it sit vegetarian cafes, a mushroom shop that cooks, a chocolate restaurant with a dedicated vegan menu, and noodle and pizza spots with labelled vegan dishes. Most of the eight cluster within a few blocks of Pacific Avenue, so the guide works as a walking day, and the food leans fresh and Californian, bowls, acai, noodles, falafel and pizza.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with a rice and quinoa bowl at Veg on the Edge, an acai bowl at Terra Superfoods, the tofu banh mi at Pretty Good Advice, the mushroom moco loco at Far West Fungi, the shiitake rice bowl at Chocolat, the Spicy Dan at Charlie Hong Kong, the vegan lahmacun at Arslans, and a vegan Detroit-style pie at Bookie's Pizza. A note on the traps: only Veg on the Edge is fully vegan, so ask for the vegan preparation elsewhere; and Dharma's, the much-loved Capitola spot, sits outside the city in Capitola and is not in this guide.
