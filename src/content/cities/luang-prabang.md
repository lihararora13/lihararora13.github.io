Luang Prabang's vegan scene is small and fragile, clustered around Kingkitsarath Road and the rivers, and it has lost several well-known kitchens in recent years. The anchor is Reunion Cafe, a fully vegan restaurant, with The Happy Cat, a sustainable cafe with a large vegan offering, and Two Little Birds, a vegetarian cafe, filling out the centre. Around them sit Thai and Lao restaurants with vegan dishes, a riverside restaurant, and a garden restaurant, so a plant-based traveller can eat well, but should check that kitchens are still open before crossing town.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the ramen at Reunion, the smoothie bowls at Two Little Birds, the vegan larb at Bamboo Garden, and the riverside meal at Dyen Sabai. A note on the traps: Bamboo Tree, Bouang, Indigo Cafe, Khaiphaen and Tamarind are all closed; The Happy Cat was previously called Luangprabang Cat Cafe; and Bamboo Garden relocated from Wisunalat Road, so older guides send people to the wrong street.
