Manaus is the thinnest Amazon capital on this list, but its seven picks are real: three fully vegan kitchens, including a snack specialist and an order-based vegan bakery, plus health-food shops and a raw-food store in the centre. The scene is spread between Centro, Flores and Aleixo, and it rewards a traveller who plans lunch around the vegan counters rather than expecting a vegan district.

The vegan snacks at Chikara Veg and Salgados Veganos are the practical core of the list, with Casa da Vovó Manu's kitchen as the sit-down pick. Two venues were checked and dropped: Restaurante Filosophicus is temporarily closed, and Restaurante Mesa Verde claims vegan food but lists grilled fish and sirloin steak, while the Korean restaurant Fugita now occupies its address and phone, so it is treated as closed and replaced.
