New York does not do half measures, and its vegan scene is no exception.
This is a city where you can eat vegan dim sum at lunch, Szechuan wontons
at dinner, and vegan Italian fine dining after that, all within a few
subway stops. Our eight picks cover the legends, the crowd-pleasers, and
the date-night rooms, and every one of them is fully vegan. Bring an
appetite and a MetroCard.
