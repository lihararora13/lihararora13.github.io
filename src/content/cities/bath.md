Bath does vegan food quietly and well, with the best tables tucked into the streets around the Abbey, the Circus and Walcot rather than shouted about on the main drag. The scene is small but serious: a pioneering fully vegan cafe, a plant-based bakery, a long-running Indian vegetarian kitchen and a fine-dining room that went largely plant-based, all within a ten-minute walk of each other.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the seasonal specials at The Green Rocket, a pastry at Morso Bakery, a thali at Sarthi and the tasting menu at Oak. No closures turned up among the shortlist: the vegan rooms that were checked are all still trading, and the only names dropped were out-of-town branches that never belonged on a Bath list.
