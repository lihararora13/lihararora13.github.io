Jodhpur, the blue city, feeds vegans through its old-city rooftops and its pure-veg institutions, and this list is built around both. The stepwell cafés of the walled city are the stars, with lake and fort views, while Sardarpura and Ratanada hold the classic vegetarian dining rooms that have fed the city for decades.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Jodhpur's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a sunset coffee at the Stepwell Cafe, a thali at Gypsy, and a dosa at Ratanada Vinayak. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
