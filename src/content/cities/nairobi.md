Nairobi does vegan food the practical way: through vegetarian Indian restaurants that have been feeding the city for decades, plus a newer wave of falafel joints, cafes and a fully vegan ice cream parlour. The Indian kitchens are the backbone, with masala dosa, chaat, thali and sweets that are vegan or vegan on request, while the falafel counters cover the quick lunch. It is a scene that rewards knowing where to look rather than wandering hopefully.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and most are vegetarian restaurants with strong vegan menus, so say the word vegan when you order. Start with mac-and-cheese at Soul Veg, a masala dosa at Chowpaty, falafel at Falafel House, the Sunday Gujarati thali at Vihar, and a scoop at King David's. A note on the traps: Level 24 Eatery, once a vegan-friendly rooftop, is listed as closing soon, so it is not on this list.
