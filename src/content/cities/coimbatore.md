Coimbatore is one of Tamil Nadu's great vegetarian cities, and it turns out it hides one of the world's most unusual vegan restaurants too. Padayal No Oil No Boil serves full South Indian meals, vada and payasam included, without a single dish ever meeting a flame, and its founder has built more than 2,500 raw vegan recipes around that idea. The rest of the scene is classic Kovai: banana-leaf meals at Annapoorna, family tiffin at Anandhaas, and a new wave of pretty RS Puram cafes like June that label their vegan options up front.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and one of the eight is fully vegan. Start with the raw vegan feast at Padayal, then do the RS Puram crawl: June for coffee and eggless bakes, Annapoorna for the iconic meals and filter coffee, and KOVE for a modern veg dinner. A note on the traps: the big tiffin rooms cook with ghee and curd, so ask for the vegan version of the dosa and meals, and save the sweets for Padayal or June.
