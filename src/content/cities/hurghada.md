Hurghada is a resort town, and its vegan food is Egyptian food first: koshary, ful, ta'ameya and lentil soup are plant-based by birth, and the two koshary houses on this list are the most reliable vegan meals in town. Around them sit a cafe with a dedicated vegan menu, two El Gouna resort restaurants, and a pair of small kitchens, so a vegan can eat well, but the scene is thin and seasonal, and El Gouna runs on resort hours.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan. Start with a koshary at Koshary El Tahrir, a vegan koshari at El Dar Darak, and the vegan grain bowl at Vibes. A note on the traps: Bliss, the town's one fully vegan address, was reported closed in 2020 and is not on this list, and El Gouna's venues are seasonal, so check hours outside the winter season.
