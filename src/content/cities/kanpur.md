Kanpur does not whisper about its vegetarian food. It is loud, proud, and seriously delicious: slow-cooked dals, smoky tandoori platters, crispy dosas, rich gravies, and thalis that have no right being this good without meat. The city's veg restaurant scene dominates, and the vegan eating is a matter of ordering the right dishes and asking for the dairy to stay out.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. None is all-vegan, so the rule is simple: order the dal, the chole, the dosa and the uttapam, and ask for no ghee, no dahi and no paneer. Start with the kadhai paneer-free thali at Shukla Bhojnalay, the arhar daal at Gyan Vaishnav, and the ragi idli at Swaagata South 19.
