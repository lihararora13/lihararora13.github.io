Taipei eats plant-based like it was always the plan: Buddhist vegetarian roots, night-market tofu, and a new wave of fully vegan kitchens doing everything from oat-milk ramen to crispy chicken burgers with total confidence. You are never more than an MRT ride away from something green and glorious.

All eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Sunami for the special dinner, BaganHood for the burger everyone raves about, and Plants for the slow, nourishing lunch, then graze your way through Vege Creek's vegetable wall, Mukuchi's ramen near Dihua Street, and CHAO's wok-fired plates after dark.
