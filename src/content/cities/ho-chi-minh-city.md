Saigon might be the easiest city in the world to eat vegan without even trying. The city's quan chay tradition means plant-based eating is woven into everyday life, from Buddhist vegetarian kitchens to sleek Thao Dien cafes, and the best tables here range from a serene garden restaurant to a tiny deli turning local cashews into camembert. Bring an appetite and a Grab app, and you can eat your way from District 1 to Thao Dien without ever repeating a dish.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with Hum for a special Vietnamese meal, Shamballa for a calm tea-house lunch, and Pi for a rooftop feast with friends, then book Kashew for cheese, Zeroism for brunch, Filthy Vegan for comfort food, La Moi for District 1 classics, and iVegan for a bright Ben Thanh breakfast. A note on the traps: the old Saigon Vegan branch on Lao Tu Street is permanently closed, the Organik House's restaurant status is unclear, and Kashew's Garden Villa branch has closed, so this list points you to the Thao Dien deli that is open now.
