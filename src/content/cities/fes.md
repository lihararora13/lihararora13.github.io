Fes is a medina city, and its vegan food is medina food: bissara soup with bread, vegetable tagines, vegetable couscous and falafel, eaten in riads and cafes off Talaa Kbira and around Batha. The scene is small but real, with one dedicated veggie bistro, Veggie Pause, and a handful of friendly rooms, and the best meals are the simplest ones, a bowl of bissara in the morning and a tagine at night.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with bissara and bread at the Bissara Shop, the Moroccan lasagna at Veggie Pause, and the vegetable tagine at El Forno. A note on the traps: several medina restaurants list vegan dishes that turn out to contain egg or dairy, so always confirm, and the old guides that call Fes a vegan desert are out of date.
