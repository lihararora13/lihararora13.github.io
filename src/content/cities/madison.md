Madison's vegan scene runs on two streets, State Street downtown and Atwood Avenue on the east side, and it leans hard into comfort food: vegan cheese curds, deep-dish pizza, Nashville hot chicken sandwiches, and diner meatloaf, all plant-based. The university keeps it young, the east side keeps it neighbourhood, and the kitchens take the vegan cooking seriously rather than treating it as a side project.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegan-friendly. Start with the Big Owl Burger at Green Owl, the vegan cheese curds at Justveggiez, the Veryberry acai bowl at Juicy Organics, the meatless loaf at Monty's, a vegan mac n cheese slice at Ian's, a vegan donut at Mother Fool's, the veggie combo at Buraka, and a vegan cupcake at Daisy Cafe. A note on the traps: Wicked Willow, often listed in Wisconsin vegan roundups, is in Stevens Point, not Madison, so it is not listed here.
