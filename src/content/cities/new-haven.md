New Haven's vegan scene clusters around Yale and the downtown squares, with Chapel Street, Broadway, Howe Street, and Whalley Avenue each holding a piece. The list runs from an all-vegan Caribbean kitchen in the Ninth Square Market to a fifty-year-old vegetarian institution, an Ethiopian restaurant, Indian street food, and a vegetarian market. It is a scene where the strongest meals are the ones with a specific cuisine behind them.

Start with a Caribbean plate at Caribbean Style Vegan, the Kindness Dinner at Claire's Corner Copia, and the combination platter at Lalibela, then work through Sherkaan, House of Naan, and Mamoun's falafel. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Caribbean Style Vegan's sources conflict about Tuesday service, so confirm the day before a special trip.
