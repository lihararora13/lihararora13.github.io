San Diego does not mess around with plants. This is the city where the all-vegan fine-comfort spot keeps winning Best Overall Restaurant against steakhouses, where the cocktail bar is named Kindred and plays death metal, and where a raw vegan cafe in Ocean Beach has been feeding beach bums for almost two decades. All eight picks are verified open as of October 2026.

The game plan writes itself: pizza and Nashville hot shrooms at Donna Jean, eggroll burgers at Kindred, a zero-waste tasting adventure up in Oceanside, flower cups at Plumeria, rooftop bowls above La Jolla, a raw feast in OB, Thai comfort in Hillcrest, and a box of cookies to take home. That is a full day. That is a full life, honestly.
