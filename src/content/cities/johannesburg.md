Johannesburg does vegan food the way it does everything else: big, bold, and with zero interest in being a side salad. The best tables are scattered across the northern suburbs, from Greenside's leafy streets and Sandton's shiny centre to a working organic farm out in Muldersdrift and a comic-book store in Bedfordview, and the top of the list is genuinely fun eating that wins over lifelong meat lovers.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan. Start with a Texan Chimichanga at The Fussy Vegan, the Zingy Burger at The Vegan Chef, the Shakshuka Stack at Leafy Greens, spicy ramen at Asian Herbivore, the signature vegan omelette at The Jozi Vegan, Thai green curry at Fruits and Roots, a Biscoff layer cake at With HÄRT, and a Beyond cheddar burger with a freakshake at Kaylee's. A note on the traps: Lexi's Healthy Eatery has shrunk to a single Sandton branch, its Rosebank shop is permanently closed, so it is off the list; Conscious 108 and The Greenside Café have no fresh 2025 or 2026 confirmation of trading, so they are off too; and Kaylee's Eatery now trades under the Comic Cafe theme inside the Comic Warehouse, same kitchen, same address.
