Albuquerque cooks vegan food with green chile, fry bread, sopapillas and blue corn, and that is exactly as good as it sounds. The scene clusters downtown, in Old Town, Nob Hill and the Sawmill district, with a Native-owned vegan kitchen, a vegan New Mexican diner and a couple of genuinely fancy rooms in the mix.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegetarian or vegan-friendly with strong vegan menus. Start with a chile relleno burrito at Vegos, an Ital-NDN taco at Itality, a Lucky Burger at Lucky Goose, a hot dish at Mata G, polenta at Seasons, a vegan pizza at Farina, the vegan special at Artichoke, and a masala dosa at Annapurna's. A note on the traps: the vegan burger spot that closed in July 2026 and the temporarily closed spots on HappyCow did not make the cut.
