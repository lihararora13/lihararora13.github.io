Porto Alegre has one of the deepest vegan scenes in Brazil, and it is almost entirely fully vegan: HappyCow lists 195 places in the city, and the top of the ranking is a run of 5.0-rated kitchens serving Brazilian comfort food, raw food, pizza, sushi and bakeries. The scene clusters in Cidade Baixa, Bom Fim, Rio Branco and Moinhos de Vento, all walkable from the centre, and the food is Brazilian first, with feijoada, xis burgers, pastel and rice and beans done entirely plant-based.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with the set lunch at Aurora, the feijoada at Cantina do Ian, a buffet plate at Picanha's Grill, a raw lasagna at La Rouge, a pizza at Fermento, a raw set menu at Raw Porto Alegre, a set meal at Baseado em Plantas, and a calzone at Donna Laura. A note on the traps: several of these kitchens have relocated in recent years, so use the addresses on this page, not the ones in older guides, and many are lunch-only or open limited evening hours.
