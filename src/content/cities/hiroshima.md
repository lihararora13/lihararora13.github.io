Hiroshima does not have a big vegan scene, and until recently it had no fully vegan restaurant at all, but what it has is serious: a brand-new 100% plant-based restaurant by Peace Park, the city's signature okonomiyaki rebuilt vegan, a temple-style vegetarian kitchen in the Koi hills, a handmade udon shop with its own plant-based broth, and a vegetarian Chinese restaurant rated 5.0. Most of the best tables sit in Naka Ward within walking distance of the Peace Memorial Park, with the station area covering the Indian option.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with the dinner course at JoGeSaYu, the vegan okonomiyaki at Nagataya, the vegan katsu sandwich at Kissa Saeki, the vegan ramen at Saishoku-Kenbi, the oroshi bukkake udon at Taiko Udon, the mapo tofu at Keiai, the vegan chili at Otis!, and the spinach and potato curry at Roopali. A note on the traps: the older guides that say Hiroshima has no fully vegan restaurant predate JoGeSaYu, which opened in May 2025; Kissa Saeki's rice is grown with the aigamo duck method, so strict vegans should favour its sandwiches and sweets; and Roopali has two branches, this guide uses the Wakakusacho one near the station.
