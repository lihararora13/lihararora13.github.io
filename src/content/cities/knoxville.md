Knoxville's vegan scene runs through downtown's Market Square and the Old City, with the university strip and Bearden adding more. The list is eclectic: an all-vegan cinnamon roll bakery out west, a Southwestern cafe near campus, tamales in the Old City, falafel, Mediterranean, Ethiopian, and a co-op market. It is a scene where the strongest meals are the ones that lean into a specific cuisine rather than a generic vegan menu.

Start with cinnamon rolls at Cinnaholic, the orange blossom special at Sunspot, and tamales at Good Golly Tamale, then work through Yassin's falafel, KoPita, and the Tomato Head's vegan pizza. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Yassin's Falafel House was reported as reopened downtown on Walnut Street in one guide, but its listing places it on North Peters Road, so the Peters Road address is used here and the possible relocation is flagged.
