Rosario is Argentina's third city, and its vegan scene runs on the student and lunch economy: restobars, rotiserias and takeaway counters that serve vegetarian and vegan food by weight, by the plate, or by the set lunch, mostly between 11 am and 3 pm. The best tables cluster in the centre, around the Mercado del Patio and the streets off the riverfront, and the food is hearty, cheap and unpretentious.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with the mushroom burger at Vrinda, a lunch plate at Sol Spring of Life, a takeaway plate at La Casa de Mei, a market lunch at VQV, a deli plate at Hevale, a rotiseria plate at Angel, a grocery-cafe lunch at Casa de Nicolas, and a takeaway plate at Vegetariano Natural. A note on the traps: most of these kitchens are lunch-only, several close on Sundays, and the rotiserias sell by weight, so say you want the vegan dishes when you point.
