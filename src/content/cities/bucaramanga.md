Bucaramanga has the strongest all-vegan share in Colombia on this list: four of its eight picks are fully vegan, clustered in Cabecera del Llano and Bolarquí, with vegetarian restaurants in the centre and Provenza filling out the rest. The cooking is Colombian comfort food rebuilt plant-based, tamales, ceviche, hamburguesas and empanadas, and the scene is compact enough to walk.

The burgers, ceviche and tamales at Azafrán and the tamalitos at Aldea Vegana are the plates to plan around. Siempreviva Cocina Vegana was checked and dropped as temporarily closed. A second Azafrán listing, Azafrán - Delicias Altas - IASD, was not used to avoid duplicating the brand, and La Jungla has no findable address.
