Srinagar is a hard city for vegans: Kashmiri cooking centres on meat, and the pure-veg tables are a small handful of old dhabas, a bakery and hotel restaurants around Residency Road, the Bund and Dal Lake. What exists is good, old and cheap, but it is almost entirely lacto-vegetarian, so every order needs the no-ghee, no-dahi, no-cheese ask.

All 7 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. Do not confuse this Srinagar with Srinagar in Garhwal, Uttarakhand: several travel sites mix the two, and the Garhwal sattvik listings do not belong here. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city.
