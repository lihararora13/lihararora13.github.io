Nelspruit, officially Mbombela, is the gateway to the Kruger and the Lowveld, and its vegan scene is exactly what a transit city has: mall restaurants, hotel cafes and garden restaurants that will feed a vegan, rather than any dedicated plant-based room. The eating clusters around Sonpark, Riverside Park and the i'langa Mall, and the national chains do a lot of the work.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan, and this is the weakest list in the region, so treat it as a survival guide rather than a food tour. Order the artichoke soft shell tacos at Doppio Zero, a plant power bowl at KAUAI, and the vegan options at The Garden. A note on the traps: there is no dedicated vegan restaurant in Mbombela despite what some aggregators suggest, and several listings that appear on vegan maps are ordinary steakhouses with a single salad.
