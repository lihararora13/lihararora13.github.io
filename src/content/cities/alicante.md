Alicante's vegan scene is spread across the old town and the centre, from a soul kitchen on Avenida Santander to a 100 percent vegan and gluten-free bakery on Calle Canalejas. One of the eight is fully vegan, and the rest are vegan-friendly kitchens, from Lebanese mezze to falafel and conscious food.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at Baobab Soul Kitchen & Bar on Avenida Santander, Míleljos Vegan and Falafelmania. The traps were real here: Bodhigreen has renamed as Beet Vegetariano and is listed under its current name, and Banaras has closed, so it does not appear on this list.
