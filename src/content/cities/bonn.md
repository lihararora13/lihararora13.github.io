Bonn does vegan food the way a Rhine university city should: compact, walkable, and surprisingly deep for its size. The best tables cluster in the old town around Münsterplatz and the Nordstadt, with a couple of gems out in Poppelsdorf, and they cook across the map: Japanese-Vietnamese fusion, Turkish street food, Middle Eastern mezze, a thirty-year-old vegetarian buffet, and a proper weekend brunch. Four of the eight picks are fully vegan, and the rest mark their plant-based dishes clearly, so the city rewards a wanderer with a different cuisine at nearly every stop.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the vegan salmon nigiri at Mandala, the Käsespätzle at Black Veg, a scrambled-tofu brunch at ESSKALATION, a çiğköfte wrap at Çiğköfte Vegan Land, a build-your-own buffet plate at CassiusGarten, a hummus classic at Mr and Mrs Humus, the pumpkin soup at Ananda, and a vegan poutine at Frittenwerk. A note on the traps: Ananda is vegetarian with many vegan dishes rather than fully vegan, so ask the staff about specific plates, and CassiusGarten is a mixed vegetarian-vegan buffet, so follow the vegan labels.
