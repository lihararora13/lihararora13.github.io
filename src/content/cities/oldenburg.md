Oldenburg does vegan food the way a university city does: small rooms, loyal regulars, and a surprising amount of range for a city its size. The best tables cluster in the Innenstadt around the Markt and the Lange Straße, with a second pocket up the Nadorster Straße in Donnerschwee, and they cook vegan Berliner and cheesecake, Vietnamese phở and sushi, vegetarian Indian thalis, vegan döner, Mediterranean falafel and organic seasonal German food, with two fully vegan anchors and a vegan-friendly bench behind them. This is a scene of owner-run cafés and one reliable chain, and it rewards a wander rather than a single destination.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with a vegan Berliner at Veggiemaid, the phở at Buddhachay, a thali at City Veg House, a vegan döner at BESCHTE! Nadorst, a homemade cake at Café Magdalena, a falafel plate at Gustav Grün, a seasonal dinner at Seidenspinner, and a vegan burger at Hans im Glück. A note on the traps: the Alhambra community kitchen is not a regular restaurant, so it never made the list, and Die jungen Wilden is a meat-and-vegan fusion spot with conflicting addresses, so it did not either.
