Plymouth's vegan scene is anchored by the Barbican and the city centre, with a fully vegan bistro-bar in a converted chapel, a market-hall stall, a warehouse cafe and a harbour-front chip shop doing banana-blossom fish and chips. It is a small scene with one outstanding table at the top and a solid set of vegan-friendly kitchens around it.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at Cosmic Kitchen on Palace Street, then Veggie Perrin's and the Almanac Cafe. The Harbour on the Barbican is worth a special mention: a seafood restaurant with a small vegan menu, including banana-blossom fish and chips and salt-and-pepper oyster-mushroom squid rings, verified on its current menu.
