Athens has been cooking plant-based food for centuries, thanks to the Greek Orthodox fasting calendar, and the modern vegan scene has caught up in style. The best tables cluster in three walkable pockets: Syntagma and Monastiraki in the centre, Koukaki just south of the Acropolis, and Exarcheia, the student quarter to the north. Between them you can eat vegan gyros, moussaka, pastitsio, souvlaki, truffle cheeseburgers, raw cakes and proper Greek pies, all without leaving the neighbourhoods you came to see.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with Rhino Vegan Beat for gyros near Syntagma, Veganaki for veganised Greek classics by the Acropolis, and Holy Llama for pastries and brunch, then try Cookoomela Grill for mushroom souvlaki in Exarcheia, Avit for a modern Mediterranean dinner, Mama Tierra for street food by the Acropolis Museum, Peas for a quiet Koukaki lunch, and Mother Vegan Bistro for homestyle Greek cooking with house-made desserts. A note on the traps: the original Mama Tierra dining room has closed, so only the Acropolis branch is listed, Avocado is vegetarian rather than fully vegan, and Athens Vegan Burgers went vegetarian in 2023, so none of those made the cut.
