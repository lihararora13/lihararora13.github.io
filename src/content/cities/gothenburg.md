Gothenburg is Sweden's greenest big city, and its vegan scene leans into that: punk cooperatives in Majorna, the oldest vegetarian restaurant in town in Haga, Indonesian and Polish kitchens, and a Michelin-starred restaurant that will happily cook you a full vegan tasting menu. The picks cluster in Majorna, Haga, Linné and the city centre, and they cover everything from seitan schnitzel to a five-course tasting menu.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with seitan at Blackbird, a burger at Crippas, gado gado at Sayur, pierogi at Bar Bez Mleczny, the daily special at Solrosen, dal at Govindas, the buffet at Andrum, and the vegetarian tasting menu at Koka. A note on the traps: several Gothenburg vegetarian favourites have closed in recent years, so these eight are the ones with fresh reviews and open doors right now.
