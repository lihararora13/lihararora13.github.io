Tokyo is huge, delicious, and quietly full of plant-based gems hiding in station basements, back streets, and even a digital art museum. Forget the idea that vegan Japan means plain rice and pickles: here it means creamy sesame ramen, vegan eel, fluffy pancakes, and falafel soft-serve, all done with obsessive Tokyo care.

All eight picks are verified open as of October 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. The game plan: book Saido for the special meal, queue for T's TanTan between trains, save AIN SOPH. Journey for pancakes, pair Uzu with teamLab Planets, and snack your way through Harajuku and Nakameguro in between. Tokyo, we are very full and very happy.
