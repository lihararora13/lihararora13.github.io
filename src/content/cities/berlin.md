Berlin is the city that turned plant-based eating from a lifestyle into
a full-blown competitive sport. This is where Michelin stars sit above
vegan doner shops, where fine dining tastes like a tasting-menu
daydream and street food tastes like a delicious fever dream. Eight
picks, zero compromises, and not a single boring salad in sight.

Bring an appetite and a stretchy waistband. Between Lucky Leek's tasting
menu and Brammibal's donuts, you will want to bookend every day with
something spectacular. This is vegan Berlin: bold, brilliant, and
unapologetically extra.
