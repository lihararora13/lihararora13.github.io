Pondicherry is where South India's vegan scene goes on holiday. Auroville, ten minutes up the coast, is a township built around conscious living, and its cafes cook the way they live: Terrassen is a fully vegan Indian restaurant, Conscious Cafe is the vegan-friendly spot locals recommend first, and Solitude Farm grows much of what it serves. Back in White Town, the French Quarter cafes do the rest, with cashew-cream pasta at Coromandel and scrambled tofu at Bread & Chocolate.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and two of the eight are fully vegan. Start with Terrassen's vegan Indian thali, a green pasta at Conscious Cafe, lunch under the trees at Solitude Farm, and a slow morning at Bread & Chocolate by the sea. A note on the traps: the White Town cafes also serve dairy and eggs, so ask for the vegan version, while the Auroville picks are the safe ground.
