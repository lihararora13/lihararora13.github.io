Toronto's plant-based scene is deep, delicious, and delightfully unpretentious. Our 8 all-vegan picks span Southern comfort, Chinese takeout classics, Vietnamese pho, Italian diner fare, and one very fancy townhouse tasting menu. Whether you are a lifer or a curious carnivore, Toronto will feed you well.
