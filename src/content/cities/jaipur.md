Jaipur is one of India's most naturally vegan-friendly cities, Rajasthani food was doing plant-based long before anyone called it that, and in 2026 the Pink City finally has its own fully vegan restaurant to anchor the scene. Most of the action is in C-Scheme, where farm-to-table cafes, matcha bars and a kombucha taproom sit within a few streets of each other, with an Ayurvedic rooftop near the old city for the view.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian kitchens with clearly labelled vegan options. Start with a Thai millet bowl at The Vegan Room, a farm salad at Anokhi, a kombucha at Quyu's, and a matcha at Midori. In the traditional spots, dal, sabzi and roti with no ghee will carry you happily through the day.
