Bochum is a university city with a vegan scene that punches well above its size: six of the eight picks are fully vegan, and most of them sit within a few minutes' walk of each other in the city centre, around Huestraße and Kortumstraße, with the rest out in Querenburg and Ehrenfeld. The range is the story here: a cat café, a Korean kimbap counter, a çiğköfte house, a vegan street-food truck turned shop, a Turkish vegetarian kitchen, and a burger bar famous for its vegan Taxiteller, a Ruhr plate of fries, currywurst and gyros gone fully plant-based.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the scrambled tofu among the cats at Katzentempel, the vegan döner at be.Well, the kimbap at Kimbap Spot, a çiğköfte wrap at ÇiğköfteM, a slice of vegan cake at Corba, the dicker Dürüm at Dooby's, the vegan döner at Hevalo, and the vegan Taxiteller at Blondies. A note on the traps: Blondies changed hands in early 2025 and meat is back on the menu, so order from the vegan-marked dishes, and Hevalo is a vegetarian kitchen, cash only, so bring euros and ask for the vegan plates.
