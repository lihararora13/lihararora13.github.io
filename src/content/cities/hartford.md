Hartford is a small vegan scene with a clear identity: Jamaican and Caribbean cooking leads, and the rest of the list runs through Indian kitchens, a pizza oven, and downtown bars. The capital city's vegan core is two all-vegan Jamaican spots, one a tiny cafe on Capitol Avenue and the other a North End kitchen, and the remaining picks are vegan-friendly kitchens with real vegan benches. It is a scene that rewards knowing exactly which doors to knock on.

Start with jerk tofu and ital stew at Fire & Spice, a plate at RastaRant, and the vegan pizza at Fire by Forge, then work through Ajamo, Dhaba Wala, and Spice Venue. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: ZoJu Foods went catering-only in April 2025 and its former Charter Oak Avenue location was reported closed in July 2026, so it is dropped; Vegan Bodega moved to New Britain and closed in May 2024, so it is dropped; and Blue Ginger Vegan Thai is in West Hartford, not Hartford proper, so it is not listed.
