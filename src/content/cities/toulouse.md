Toulouse does vegan food with the earnestness of a student city: small cafes with limited hours, a vegan fast-food counter, a canteen and a hot dog stand by the Jardin des Plantes. The best tables cluster in the centre around the Capitole and Place Saint-Georges, with a couple of gems across the river in Saint-Cyprien and out towards Les Minimes, and they cook French cafe food, Vietnamese dishes and vegan junk food, all of it plant-based or easily made so. This is a thinner scene than the Dutch and Belgian cities on this list, and the hours matter here: most of the vegan cafes open only for a few hours at lunchtime, a few days a week.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start with pastries at Brule, coffee and toast at Virgule, a burger at Furahaa, the daily plate at Croustille, a curry bowl at Aloe Vega, a Vietnamese dinner at Bep Chay, a vegan creme brulee at Sixta, and a hot dog at Velo Vege. A note on the traps: Le Canithe has closed and JUNGA now operates only as a food truck, so neither is on this list, and Bep Chay and Sixta are vegetarian with vegan options, so order the vegan dishes there.
