Athens is a college town with a proper vegan bench: a build-your-own bowl spot on Chase Street, a vegan barbecue sandwich at a white clapboard diner, a ramen bar with a vegan creamy ramen, and a set of downtown cafes, burger joints and Thai kitchens with vegan dishes. The University of Georgia keeps it young and cheap.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. Start with a build-your-own vegan bowl at Maepole, the tofu Q sandwich at White Tiger Gourmet, the spicy creamy vegan ramen at JINYA, and a vegan burger at Farm Burger. A note on the traps: Several Instagram guides for Athens, Georgia actually show Athens, Greece, with vegan spots such as Holy Llama and Veganaki that do not exist in Georgia; none of those are listed here. Grit, a vegetarian spot some guides list, is a bar with limited vegan food, so it is left off.
