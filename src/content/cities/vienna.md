Vienna may be famous for schnitzel, Sachertorte and coffee houses, but its vegan kitchens have been quietly building something wonderful for years: Austrian classics like goulash and Kaiserschmarrn reimagined without a scrap of animal product, Vietnamese pho in bright modern rooms, and fine dining tasting menus that treat vegetables like royalty. This is a walkable, tram-friendly capital where you can graze from a burger to a ten-course surprise menu without ever leaving the centre.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with JOLA for a special night, Velani for Austrian classics, and TIAN for Michelin-starred vegetables, then book Vevi for Vietnamese, Cafe Harvest for brunch, Swing Kitchen for burgers, Tisch for creative soul food, and Sattva for a mindful lunch. A note on the traps: the Swing Kitchen branch on Operngasse was reported closed in January 2026, so this list points you to the Schwedenplatz branch that is open now, and the beloved deli Pepper and Ginny is left off while its status is unclear.
