Alleppey is a town you arrive in by boat, and its vegetarian food is built for travellers: hot dosa and idli at dawn, banana-leaf sadya at noon, puttu and kadala curry when the boats come back. There is no all-vegan restaurant here yet, so this guide collects the pure-veg rooms that feed the backwaters crowds, from the Mullakkal temple-side Arya Bhavan to the Kuppapuram veg stop that boat passengers swear by.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan. Start with a ghee roast, hold the ghee, at Arya Bhavan, a sadya at Madras Veg Tables, podi masala dosa at Namaste Tiffins, and puttu with kadala curry at Avees. A note on the traps: payasam, ghee roast and curd-based dishes are the house style here, so ask the kitchen to keep it plant-based and the sadya will still be generous.
