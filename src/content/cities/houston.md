Houston does not do anything halfway, and that goes double for plants. In one day you can hit brunch at a Black-owned vegan cafe in the Museum District, grab raw tacos at a Third Ward classic that has been vegan since before it was cool, order a tower of vegan kolaches in Midtown, and close the night with quesabirria in Cypress Creek. All eight picks here are verified open as of October 2026, seven of them fully vegan.

The headline act is Ginger Mule, now the reigning all-vegan favorite in the Heights Waterworks space. But the city's soul is spread across its neighborhoods: Mo' Brunch + Brews for the vibes, Green Seed Vegan for the history, Sinfull Bakery for the sugar rush, and Govinda's temple-side buffet for a Sunday that resets everything. Bring stretchy pants, VegNews was not kidding.
