Freiburg is the city that cycles everywhere and eats like it, and the vegan scene follows the same logic: small, local, seasonal, and spread across the Wiehre, the Stühlinger and the Altstadt rather than gathered in one street. The strongest tables are the vegetarian institutions, the French bistro turning out galettes and crêpes, the breakfast café doing a proper vegan fry-up of a morning, and the potato restaurant that treats the humble tuber like a main event. It is a scene of regulars and neighbourhood rooms, not of destination dining, and that is exactly its charm.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with a champignon galette at Lieblingsplatz, the vegan breakfast at Café Alma, a cake and oat cappuccino at Café Hinterzimmer, a çiğ köfte wrap at Mr Fero, a sweet potato curry at Das Kartoffelhaus, a vegan döner at Amara, soup and cake at Huber, and a bowl to go from H&S Brothers Meal. A note on names: the HappyCow favourite Beb & Bene now trades as Lieblingsplatz at the same Konradstraße address, with the same owners and the same kitchen.
