Nagano City has few vegan spots, but the ones it has are good: oyaki, the stuffed Nagano bun, is vegan by default at Irohado, and Surya Dev does a fully vegan South Indian thali by reservation. Most of the list sits within a few minutes of Nagano Station and Zenkoji Temple.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 3 of them fully vegan. Surya Dev is reservation-only, closed Thursdays and Fridays, and needs booking by 9 pm the day before. Irohado has three shops in the city; the MIDORI station shop and the Zenkoji shop are listed separately.
