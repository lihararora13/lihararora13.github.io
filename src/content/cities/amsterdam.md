Amsterdam might be famous for canals and cycling, but it is quietly one of Europe's vegan capitals, with over 150 fully plant-based cafes and restaurants across the city. From cozy ramen joints to white-tablecloth tasting menus, the plant-based scene here is creative, confident, and deliciously unpretentious.

Our eight picks are all 100% vegan and cover every mood: fine dining at Bonboon, Mexican fiesta nights at Madre, hearty fusion at SOIL and TerraZen, and some of the best-reviewed plant-based ramen in Europe. Pack an appetite and a bike.
