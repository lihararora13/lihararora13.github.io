Copenhagen may be famous for smorrebrod, hot dogs and New Nordic tasting menus, but its vegan kitchens have gleefully claimed all three, and then kept going: fermented, foraged and flame-kissed vegetables, vegan carbonara, smorrebrod with plant-based salmon, and cakes so good nobody believes they are dairy free. This is a cycling, hygge-loving capital that takes sustainability seriously and dinner even more so.

All eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Ark for the big celebratory tasting menu, Bistro Lupa for its neighbourhood bistro magic, and Beyla for an all-day feast in Carlsberg Byen, then graze your way to Bistro Verde by Nyhavn, pizza at POW, and a very necessary cake stop at Kaf.
