Arequipa has one of the strongest vegan scenes in Peru outside Lima: five of its eight picks are fully vegan, and the historic centre, a UNESCO-listed grid of white volcanic stone, holds most of them within a few blocks. Two Lounge del Brujo branches anchor it, a historic-centre restaurant inside a colonial courtyard and a Cayma bar, alongside a therapy coffee shop, a fully vegan fast-food counter and a five-star vegan kitchen.

The tallarín saltado at Lounge del Brujo's historic-centre branch, the Buddha bowl at Satiba and the seitan plates at El Veggie are the three stops to build around. El Buda Profano was checked and dropped as temporarily closed. Two claims were downgraded: Prana Vegan Club is filed vegan-friendly because HappyCow reports from 2023 noted dairy and honey in some of its options, and Yurakuna's address is disputed, with HappyCow listing Jerusalén 201-D and place data listing Jerusalén 505, so the HappyCow address is used and the conflict is noted. Omphalos has moved from its old Bolívar 107 address to Bolognesi 116.
