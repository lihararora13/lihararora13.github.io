Mendoza is wine country, and its vegan scene is built for the lunch trade: rotiserias, buffets and plant-based kitchens that serve set lunches on weekdays, mostly between 11:30 am and 3 pm, with a few evening kitchens in Godoy Cruz and San Jose. The food leans Argentinian and oriental, with milanesas de seitan, empanadas, pastas and sushi sitting next to salads and set menus, and the best tables cluster in the city centre and its near suburbs.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are vegan-friendly rather than fully vegan, so ask which dishes are vegan when you order. Start with the seitan lomo at Tacuara, a buffet plate at Urkitza, a set lunch at Rincon de Sabores, a plate at Palmera, a lunch at Raiz, an evening plate at Fuego Verde, a pizza at Gopau, and a rotiseria lunch at Monte de Olivos. A note on the traps: most of these kitchens close after lunch, several are closed on weekends, and Tacuara has a second branch in Godoy Cruz, so use the Avenida Colon address on this page.
