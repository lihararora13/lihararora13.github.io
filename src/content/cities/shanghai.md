Shanghai has been cooking Buddhist vegetarian food since 1922, and the modern scene has only raised the stakes: two Michelin stars, Bib Gourmands, and a wave of fully vegan kitchens in Jing'an and Huangpu. This is a city where a tasting menu and a temple noodle bowl can both be the best meal of the trip, and where mock duck is a craft, not a compromise.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with the seasonal tasting menu at Fu He Hui, mock duck at Gong De Lin, sweet and sour pork that is not pork at Zaozi Shu, a mushroom hot pot at Yangege, mapo tofu hummus at Duli, soup dumplings at Qingchun Perma, pizza at Ru Pure Vegan, and dim sum at The Lakeside Veggie.
