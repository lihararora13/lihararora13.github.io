Norwich punches well above its weight for vegan food, with the best rooms clustered in the Lanes around St Benedicts Street and St Giles, plus a destination restaurant out at Trowse. The city has three fully vegan anchors, a plant-based cafe, a vegetarian Indian institution and a waffle house that has been going for decades.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start at River Green out at Trowse, Slice + Dice on St Benedicts Street and the Moorish Falafel Bar on Lower Goat Lane. The traps were minor: a couple of stalls that had moved within the Lanes are listed at their current addresses, and one much-shared guide still lists a cafe that closed two years ago.
