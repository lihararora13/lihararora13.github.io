Tunis is a small scene, and this list leans on it honestly: one fully vegan restaurant, a hotel with a serious vegan menu, and a set of Tunisian, Lebanese and international places where vegan dishes are the tradition rather than the exception. The geography splits three ways, the medina and Bab el Khadhra for lablabi and Tunisian cooking, Les Berges du Lac for Inward, and La Marsa and Gammarth on the coast for couscous, Lebanese food and hotel dining.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with small plates at Inward, the vegan menu at Azur, couscous at Dar Slah, spinach couscous at L'Arbre a Couscous, a falafel plate at Karam Lebnan, baked falafel at Symbiose, and lablabi at Badra or Lablebi. A note on the traps: Badra and Lablebi serve lablabi with egg as an optional extra, so order it without egg, and Inward keeps short hours, Tuesday to Friday noon to 4:00pm only.
