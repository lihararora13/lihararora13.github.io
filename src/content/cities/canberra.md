Canberra is not the first place you would expect a vegan scene, and that is exactly what makes it fun. The best tables are scattered across the city's village-like neighbourhoods, Dickson, Civic, Acton and Strathnairn, and they lean generous and unpretentious: big bowls of laksa, dosa with sambar, wood-fired pizza with vegan cheese, and cafe plates piled with colour. This is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than flashy openings, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food at all.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegetarian or omnivorous with serious vegan menus. Start with the laksa at Au Lac Dickson, a tempeh-avocado salad at My Rainbow Dreams, a vegan tiramisu at Smith's Alternative, a buckwheat waffle at Eighty Twenty, a daily lunch at the Food Co-op, a soup at Cafe Stepping Stone, a dosa at Rasam, and a Margherita with vegan cheese at SoLita. A note on the traps: Sweet Bones in Scullin is closed, Au Lac Woden looks temporarily closed, and the old Acai Brothers branch is gone, so none of them make the list.
