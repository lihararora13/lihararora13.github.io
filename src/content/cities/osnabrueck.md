Osnabruck eats vegan in the compact old town, where the scene runs on cafes and bars around the Osterberger Reihe and the Markt rather than on dedicated vegan restaurants: a vegan cigkofte shop, a sweet-potato waffle cafe, a museum cafe and a coffee shop in a converted petrol station. Everything sits within a short walk of the cathedral, and the single all-vegan anchor carries the list.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the cigkofte durm at Has Antep, the sweet potato waffles at Snackwunder, the breakfast board at Cafe Felka, and the veggie sandwich at MARE. A note on the traps: Dean and David and Polly Esthers are both listed as temporarily closed, so they stay off the list; Has Antep also has a Neumarkt branch that is temporarily closed, so go to the open Franz-Lenz-Strasse shop; and the xmeri listing still shows the closed Neumarkt address.
