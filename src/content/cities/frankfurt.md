Frankfurt is known for banks, but its vegan scene is quietly one of Germany's most interesting: a vegan kosher restaurant, a fast-food diner run by animal rights activists, and the world's first vegan Michelin-starred restaurant, all within a short walk of each other. The best tables cluster in the Westend and Nordend, with the Altstadt and Ostend filling in the fine-dining gaps.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian with a strong vegan menu. Start with the Israeli plates at Kuli Alma, hummus and shawarma at 269, a burger at Dominion, a sandwich at Savory, sharing plates at Leuchtendroter, the tasting menu at Seven Swans, pho at Grün Garten, and smashed avocado at Avocateur. Come hungry, and book Seven Swans well ahead.
