Kochi eats vegan the Kerala way: coconut, dosa, idli, sambar and banana-leaf meals that are already most of the way there, plus a small but real all-vegan scene in Fort Kochi that started with Kerala's first vegan cafe. The fully vegan kitchens cluster in Fort Kochi, while the great vegetarian restaurants of Ernakulam, Palarivattom and Marine Drive fill in the rest with dosa, sadhya and thali that turn vegan with a word to the kitchen.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian kitchens with serious vegan options. Start with the Veg Out Sushi at Loving Earth, the Notzerella Sticks at Halo, a millet biriyani at Prakruthi, a ghee-roast dosa at Gokul Oottupura, and the banana-leaf thali at Upahaara. A note on the traps: in the vegetarian spots, say no ghee and no curd, dosa and idli are vegan as served but the ghee roast is not, and Loving Earth has reopened bigger with a yoga studio after a closure, so use the Napier Street address on this page.
