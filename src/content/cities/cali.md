Cali does vegan food the way it does salsa: loudly, generously, and with the Pacific coast in every plate. HappyCow's June 2026 roundup of the city's eight most loved vegan restaurants is almost entirely fully vegan, and the food leans into Colombian classics rather than away from them, with vegan bandeja paisa, tamales and Pacific stews sitting next to burgers, tacos and breakfast plates. The scene clusters in San Antonio, San Fernando and the centre, all easy to walk between.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and Puro Cocina Honesta vegan-friendly. Start with the tofu scramble at Dulcinea, the tacos at Flor de Loto, a burger at Juki & Co, the vegan bandeja paisa at NADI, a family spread at Punto Verde, the Burger Tentacion at Sucursal Vegana, the tamal vegano at Vida y Salud, and a cazuela at Puro. A note on the traps: Verde Manantial Veg, which appeared in HappyCow's June 2026 roundup, is listed as temporarily closed and now works by order only, so it does not make this list, and Puro replaces it.
