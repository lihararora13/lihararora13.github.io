Funchal eats vegan in two pockets: the old town around Rua de Santa Maria and Rua Joao Tavira, where brunch cafes and small kitchens sit a few minutes from the Se cathedral, and the Lido hotel strip out west, where the island's only fully vegan restaurant and its best vegan sushi sit side by side. The scene is small and honest rather than sprawling, built on cafes, a rooftop gastrobar and a couple of serious kitchens, and the Atlantic light does a lot of the work that a big scene would do elsewhere.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the sea rainbow bowl at Mundo Vegan, the vegan freestyle sushi at SushiMari (ask for it, it is not on the printed menu), the vegan pancakes at Brunchology, and the mezze at Three House. A note on the traps: Bioforma is a health-food store with a counter, not a restaurant, so it stays off this list; Confort Food by Cintia is a small deli counter in a shopping plaza rather than a sit-down meal; and several Lido sushi bars look vegan-friendly from the street but run a fish-first menu, so only SushiMari, which regulars praise specifically for its vegan platters, makes the cut.
