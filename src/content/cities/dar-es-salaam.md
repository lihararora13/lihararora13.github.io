Dar es Salaam feeds vegans on two traditions: the Indian vegetarian restaurants of Kisutu, where dosa, idli, chaat and thali are the everyday food, and the cafes of Masaki and Upanga, where Buddha bowls, vegan burgers and Ethiopian stews fill the gap. There is no fully vegan restaurant in the city, but the Kisutu cluster is dense, cheap and genuinely good, and the Masaki rooms add the international end.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan. Start with the Jamaican-influenced plates at Kind Earth Eatery, the daily special at Mlima Sayuni, and the pani puri at Chowpatty Da. A note on the traps: Indian vegetarian does not mean vegan, so always specify no ghee or dairy, and Mlima Sayuni needs a day's notice for menu meals, otherwise you get the daily special.
