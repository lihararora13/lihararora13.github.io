San Juan has nearly twenty vegan and vegan-friendly restaurants, mostly in Santurce, Condado and Old San Juan: fully plant-based Puerto Rican comfort food, a vegetarian lunch spot, a vegan gelato shop, and a set of cafes with vegan dishes. Hours are the trap here, as many places close by late afternoon.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 3 of them fully vegan. Start with the plant-based mofongo at 100% HP, the lentil and plantain cake at Better Food Corps., the vegan sushi at Aliado, and a vegan gelato at Anita Gelato. A note on the traps: Many San Juan vegan spots are only open for breakfast or lunch, with few open past 4 pm, so check hours before going. Barrachina has stopped offering vegan options, so it is not listed. La Buena Mesa has undergone management changes and has no website, so its listing needs care.
