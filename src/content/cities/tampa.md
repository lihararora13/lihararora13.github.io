Tampa's vegan scene is spread across the city's corridors rather than concentrated downtown: Nebraska Avenue up north for the market and bakery, Kennedy Boulevard for the kitchen, Busch Boulevard for the comfort food, and South Tampa for the cafe and bakery. The list has a strong all-vegan core, five of the eight, and the vegan-friendly rooms fill out the week with Mediterranean, cafe, and Ybor City picks. It is a scene where the all-vegan rooms are the draw and the closures of the last year have thinned the field.

Start with a plate at Vegan International Co., a bowl at Pure Kitchen, and a comfort plate at The Twisted Vegan, then work through WeVegan, HaleLife, and Cafe Hey. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Florish closed in February 2026, Coco Lov is temporarily closed, and 3 Dot Dash and Black Radish are gone, all dropped; and the pins on this list are Census street-address interpolations rather than surveyed entrances, per the guide that verified them, so use the street addresses when navigating.
