Montpellier's vegan scene is one of the strongest in the south of France, concentrated in the Écusson, the old centre inside the former walls. Five of the eight picks are fully vegan, including a vegan fast-food spot, a vegan street-food counter and a fully vegan and gluten-free cafe, and the rest are serious vegan-friendly kitchens.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start at Peacefood Café, KVB on Rue de l'Aiguillerie and Vegorilla on Rue du Puits des Esquilles. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegan restaurant that closed in 2025 is not on this list, and a cafe that had moved within the Écusson is listed at its current address.
