Strasbourg does vegan food the way it does everything else: with Alsatian stubbornness and a lot of local pride. The best tables cluster on the Grande Île, the UNESCO-listed island at the city's heart, around the Grand'Rue, Rue de la Râpe and Place Saint-Étienne, with a detour to Petite France for kebabs and out to the Presqu'île Malraux for the canteen. Expect seitan kebabs, vegan pizza, bowls, a chocolate bistro and a proper coffee shop, all within a short walk of the cathedral.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with a seitan kebab at Végéman, a croissant at Origin, a seasonal plate at La Bouture, a chocolat chaud at Bistrot & Chocolat, and a vegan pizza at Pizz Arome. Two notes: the Harmonie pin is placed at neighbourhood level, the geocoder matched a listing a few doors from the guide address on the same block; and L'Essentiel chez Raphaël is a lunch-only weekday buffet, so plan it as a midday stop.
