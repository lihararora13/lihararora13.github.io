Greensboro's vegan scene is the most all-vegan-heavy list in the Carolinas block, with five of the eight picks fully vegan, and it runs from West Gate City Boulevard to Tate Street, Milner Drive, and West Market Street. The list leans on small, owner-driven rooms, from a grill and a burger joint to a street-food stall and a cinnamon-roll bakery, and the vegan-friendly rooms fill out the week with pizza, a cat cafe, burgers, and Indian food.

Start with a plate at Mike's Vegan Grill, a burger at Romeo's, and a street-food plate at WHATZ BEEF?, then work through Cinnaholic, Mellow Mushroom, and the Crooked Tail Cat Cafe. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Boba House closed and was dropped; Jal's Vegan Cuisine was dropped after its geocode returned the wrong venue; Fresh AF Vegan is a food truck and is not listed; and WHATZ BEEF? is open Thursday and Friday from 3 to 8pm only, so plan around its limited hours.
