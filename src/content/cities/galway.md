Galway's vegan scene is concentrated in the city centre, from Henry Street to the Spanish Arch, with one fully vegan anchor at the top and a set of excellent vegan-friendly kitchens around it: a Michelin Bib Gourmand restaurant on Sea Road, an Asian kitchen with a full vegan menu, a pizza spot in the old market buildings and a cafe on Prospect Hill.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at Greens & Co on Henry Street, Kai on Sea Road and Ré Nao by the Spanish Arch. The traps were real here: the Gourmet Offensive falafel bar has closed, as have the Lighthouse Cafe and Cupan Tae, and none of them appears on this list.
