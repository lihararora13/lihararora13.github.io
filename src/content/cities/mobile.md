Mobile is one of the thinnest vegan scenes in this batch: one organic vegan cafe inside a health-food store, a juice and acai spot downtown, and a set of Thai, Mexican and health-food kitchens with vegan options. It is a city where vegans plan around a few good tables rather than a district.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 1 of them fully vegan. Start with the Jamaican jerk wrap at The Sunflower Cafe, an acai bowl at Ginger and Spice, the vegan dishes at Thai Diner, and a vegan ice cream at Bop's. A note on the traps: Mobile has no large all-vegan restaurant district, and several older guides list spots that have since closed or moved; the picks below are the ones with current listings and hours. ATayloredExperience and Planticious Eats are pop-up and catering businesses without a fixed dining room, so they are not listed as restaurant picks.
