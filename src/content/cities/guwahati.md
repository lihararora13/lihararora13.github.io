Guwahati is the Northeast's biggest city, and its vegetarian food is the old-school kind: family-run pure-veg restaurants that have fed the same streets for decades. There is no dedicated vegan cafe here yet, but the pure-veg base is deep, from Gopal Maharaj's naan-and-dal dining room in Fancy Bazar to the sattvik thalis of Govinda's, and the newer Malhar shows the scene is starting to dress up.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Guwahati's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a thali at Gopal Maharaj, the Maharaja sattvik thali at Govinda's, an aloo tikki chaat at Kesar, and a fixed thali at Sagar Ratna. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
