Medellín does vegan food the way it does everything else: with warmth, colour and a stubborn refusal to do anything halfway. The scene splits neatly between El Poblado, where the polished cafés and fine-dining tables cluster around Manila and Parque Poblado, and Laureles, the leafy, lived-in neighbourhood where the Colombian kitchens do their best work. The city's best trick is taking the dishes Antioquia loves, bandeja paisa, arepas, empanadas, chicharrón, and rebuilding them entirely from plants, then serving them next to cauliflower tacos, vegan sushi-grade ramen and cashew-cheese pasta.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with the arepitas at Coraje, the cauliflower wings at Paloma, the tacos al pastor at Ginger Cocina, the empanadas at Shottas, a weekend cazuela at Vegan & Veggie, the bandeja paisa at Las Veganas, the black-garlic potatoes at Real Vegetal, and the almuerzo saludable at Alma Natura. A note on the traps: Helecho Vegan Sushi shows conflicting closure reports and its listing now reads permanently closed, so it stays off the list, and Cafe Zorba is a vegetarian spot with vegan options rather than a fully vegan kitchen.
