Changsha is a hard city for vegans and proud of its chilli: the vegan scene is a handful of Buddhist and Zen-tea vegetarian restaurants, mostly degustation menus built on mushrooms. Ask for less chilli as well as no egg and no dairy.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Lumen House is shown as closed on HappyCow and is left out. Jingxin and Jingxinlian share the same address at 238 Yingbin Road; they are separate listings, one a small Buddhist restaurant and one a Zen-tea vegetarian room, and both are kept.
