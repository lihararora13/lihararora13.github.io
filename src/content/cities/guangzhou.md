Guangzhou has been cooking Buddhist vegetarian food for centuries, and the modern scene has only raised the stakes: Michelin recognition, Bib Gourmands, and a wave of fully vegan kitchens across Yuexiu, Liwan, Tianhe and Haizhu. This is a city where a tasting menu and a ten-yuan temple buffet can both be the best meal of the trip, and where mock pork belly is a craft, not a compromise.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian Buddhist kitchen with a strong vegan menu. Start with a chicken pesto wrap at Roots, dim sum at Qing Chun by Perma, the plant-based Dongpo pork at Jiayuan, chrysanthemum siu mai at Hong Yi Su, and the buffet at Yuan Yuan next to Guangxiao Temple. A note on the traps: several restaurants share names and addresses, so use the exact addresses on this page. The Junyi Hui mall on Beijing Road lists both a Confidants & Vegans and a Fo You Yuan under the same phone number; this guide lists Confidants & Vegans there and the original Fo You Yuan at 1 Fuer Road in Liwan. The two Perma restaurants share one building at 222 Xingmin Road; this guide lists the L5 dim sum room, Qing Chun by Perma, not the Shop 501 venue.
