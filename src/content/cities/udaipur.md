Udaipur is the prettiest city on this list, and its vegan scene is quietly one of the strongest: a fully vegan café anchors a guide that runs from the lake-facing rooftops of the old city to the modern café belt of Panchwati and Sukhadia Circle. The pure-veg kitchens here are used to travellers asking questions, so the usual dairy check goes down easily.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, with one fully vegan anchor in Smokey Jo's Cafe. Start with a vegan breakfast at Smokey Jo's, a fine-dining dinner at FIRKI, and a Rajasthani thali at Khamma Ghani. One important note: Smokey Jo's moved from Chandpole Bridge to Palri Village in 2026, so do not go to the old address.
