Pasadena's vegan scene is concentrated and walkable: four fully vegan restaurants, two of them sharing an address on South Lake Avenue, a vegan French wine bar in Old Pasadena, and a set of vegan-friendly restaurants where the vegan dishes are a real part of the menu rather than a garnish. The South Lake Avenue corridor carries the fast-food end, Arroyo Parkway the Thai end, and Old Pasadena the brunch and wine end. It is the most all-vegan-dense of the California cities in this batch, and the only one with a vegan French restaurant.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly. Start with the pad Thai at My Vegan, the vegan cheese board at La Cave, a Next Level burger, a taco at Mas Veggies, the soyrizo benedict at Millie's, the vegetable dumplings at Dan Modern Chinese, a vegan arepa at Amara, and the hummus at True Food Kitchen. A note on the traps: HappyCow lists My Vegan twice under two near-identical names at the same address, it is one restaurant; and Next Level Veggie Grill and Mas Veggies Taqueria share the 200 South Lake Avenue building, so use the business name as well as the address.
