The city of Nawabs is famous for kebabs and biryani, but look past the old-city legends and Lucknow is quietly growing a plant-based side. Gomti Nagar has the health cafés, Indira Nagar has the city's only fully vegan kitchen, and Mahanagar has the artsy coffee spots where oat milk is never a strange request. It is a young scene, still finding its feet, and all the better for it: the owners are enthusiasts, the menus are personal, and nobody is phoning it in.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. One is fully vegan, O Vegan, a pre-order cloud kitchen in Indira Nagar, so order ahead and plan for delivery rather than a table. The rest are vegan-friendly, so ask for no ghee, no dahi and no cheese and the kitchens will happily oblige. Start with the chia pudding at O Vegan, the quinoa bowl at the Greenhouse Cafe, the sugar-free smoothie bowl at Cafe Hons, and finish with a plate of steamed momos in Gomti Nagar.
