Nashville may be famous for hot chicken, honky-tonks and meat-and-threes, but its vegan kitchens have happily claimed all of it: hot chick'n on white bread with a pickle, soul plates with candied yams and mac, brunch biscuits, and tacos straight out of Mexico City. This is comfort food country, and the plants are winning.

Seven picks are fully vegan and one is happily vegan-friendly, all verified open as of October 2026. Start with Vegelicious for the soul plate, Graze for brunch that runs all day, and AVO for nachos and a frozen avocado margarita, then graze onward to Sunflower, The BE-Hive and a market taco run.
