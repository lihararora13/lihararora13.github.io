Amritsar does not do vegan food the way a cafe city does. It does it the way a pilgrim city does: dhabas that have been open since before independence, kulchas stuffed and griddled in front of you, dal makhani simmered overnight, and the Golden Temple's langar feeding a hundred thousand people a day. The vegetarian base here is deeper than almost anywhere in India, and the vegan eating is a matter of knowing what to ask for.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. None is all-vegan, and the dhabas cook with ghee and white makhan as a point of pride, so say no ghee, no makhan, no dahi, and order the dal tadka, chana masala, aloo gobi and dosa. Start with the kulcha at Bharawan Da Dhaba, the dal makhani at Kesar Da Dhaba, and the poori-chhole at Kanha Sweets, and you will eat like Amritsar has eaten for a century.
