Eilat is Israel's Red Sea resort, and its vegan scene is the resort's scene: promenade cafes, hotel restaurants and mall branches that mark vegan dishes, rather than any dedicated vegan room. The eating clusters along the promenade and in the shopping centres, and the best vegan meals are hummus and falafel, Indian curries and Thai food, with the beach clubs doing the lighter end.

Eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan dishes at Mosh Beach, the vegan shawarma at Hummus Eliyahu, and the vegan hummus omelette at Cafe Optimi. A note on the traps: Ginger Asian Kitchen, Thaistory, Prego Pizza and Giraffe all show as closed on HappyCow, so they are not on this list, and Hummus Eliyahu was reported in December 2025 to serve meat after previously being listed vegetarian, so it is filed vegan-friendly. Seasonal: resort hours shift with the winter season, so check before you go.
