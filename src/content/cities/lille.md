Lille is a northern city that eats well and does not shout about it, and its vegan scene follows the same pattern: a couple of fully vegan flagships, then a ring of vegan-friendly bistros, cafés and one very good Algerian restaurant, spread between the Centre, Vieux-Lille, Wazemmes and Lille-Moulins. The food leans French and northern, moules-frites rebuilt vegan, hearty bowls, burgers and couscous, with a bakery-café for the sweet tooth.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegan-friendly. Start with the vegan moules-frites at Albedo Kitchen, a cookie at Itsy Bitsy, the burger végétal at La Clairière, and the couscous at DjurDjura. Two notes: Pickles moved from its original Rue Pierre Mauroy spot to 261 Rue Solférino, so use the address on this page; and La Clairière is listed as vegan-friendly here because the guides disagree on whether it is 100% vegan, so check the menu on the day.
