Jabalpur is the city of marble rocks and the Narmada, and its vegan scene is older than it looks: two of the seven picks have been feeding vegetarians since the 1980s, and the rest of the list runs from a lantern-lit village restaurant at Madan Mahal to a neon-lit café with a rooftop terrace. It is a list with real range, thali houses and continental cafés, old rooms and young ones, and every pick has earned its place with local love rather than novelty.

All seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, and an honest note: none of the seven is a strictly all-vegan kitchen. Jabalpur's scene is pure vegetarian with ghee, paneer and curd woven through it, so every pick here is vegan-friendly, and the ordering move is always the same, no ghee, no butter, no curd, no cheese, no paneer. Start at Nature's Tavern, pay respects at Navnita, and end the evening under the lanterns at Amoda.
