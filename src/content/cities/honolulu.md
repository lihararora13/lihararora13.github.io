Honolulu eats like an island that never needed meat to be delicious: the vegan scene here runs from a Japanese izakaya that converts meat-eaters to a wood-fired vegan pizzeria in Kakaako, from a cafe that has been feeding Oahu since 2010 to a downtown market that Surfrider certifies ocean-friendly. The food is tropical, generous, and very much its own thing.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan, no decoding required. Start with ramen at Tane, a Heart and Seoul bowl at Peace Cafe, a pie at Floralia, a burger at Simple Joy, a tofu plate at Umeke Market, tofu musubi at Juicy Brew, a bowl at ALO, and pho at Thuong An Lac. A note on the traps: Blondies Vegan Proteins closed its cafe in November 2025, so it does not make the list.
