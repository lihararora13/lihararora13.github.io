Brighton has one of the highest concentrations of vegan and vegetarian restaurants per capita in the UK, and it shows in the sheer range on this list. The best tables spread across the Lanes, North Laine and Kemp Town, from award-winning vegetarian fine dining that predates the current plant-based wave to fully vegan junk food, pizza, sushi and fish and chips, all of it within a short walk of the seafront.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one vegetarian with a serious vegan menu. Start with bao sliders at Bonsai, a pizza at Purezza, vegan cod and chips at The No Catch Co, a hoisin duck sushi burrito at Happy Maki, a tasting menu at Terre a Terre, mapo tofu at Tofu Vegan, a seitan doner at Beelzebab, and a smash burger at Oowee. A note on the traps: Iydea, once a HappyCow top-ten name, is closed, and a few vegetarian favourites are not fully vegan, so check the menu before assuming.
