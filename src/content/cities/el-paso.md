El Paso's vegan scene is small and spread between Sunset Heights, downtown and the east side: a plant-focused juice bar and cafe on Airway, a neighbourhood cafe with vegan breakfast burritos, a Mexican spot with vegan-labelled tacos, and a handful of cafes with vegan breakfast hashes and lattes. It is a scene to plan, not to stumble into.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan menudo at Juicery Plus, the vegan breakfast burrito at Savage Goods, the mushroom taco at Taconeta, and the breakfast hash at Piedmont Cafe. A note on the traps: Eloise, once the city's best-known vegan restaurant, has closed, and locals still mourn it; older guides listing it are stale. Healthy Bite's Regina Salad carries a vegan symbol but lists feta, so it is not listed here.
