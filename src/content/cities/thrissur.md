Thrissur's food scene revolves around the Round, and the pure-veg restaurants around it have been feeding temple crowds for decades. Hotel Bharath's dosa is the local benchmark, Pathans and Anupam are the old reliables, and newer spots like Tealogy and the pocket-sized Aghraharam Filter Coffee show the city catching up with cafe culture. There is no all-vegan restaurant in Thrissur yet, so this guide is the pure-veg circuit, eaten with the dairy held back.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan. Start with a masala dosa at Hotel Bharath, a banana-leaf meal at Pathans, filter coffee at Aghraharam, and a relaxed cafe lunch at Tealogy. A note on the traps: ghee roast, paneer dishes and payasam are the signatures here, so order plain and ask the kitchen to skip the dairy.
