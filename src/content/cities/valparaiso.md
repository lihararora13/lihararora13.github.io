Valparaiso does vegan food the way it does everything else: up a hill, in a painted house, with the Pacific in view. The scene clusters on Cerro Alegre and Cerro Concepcion, where cafes and small restaurants serve vegan pastries, completos and empanadas, and down in El Almendral, where long-running vegetarian kitchens serve thalis, set lunches and juices. It is a scene of cafes and lunch counters rather than fine dining, and it rewards walking.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with a medialuna at Medialuna, a completo at La Boca del Oso, a thali at Govindas, a torta at La Vitaminica, an empanada at Ruka Prana, a juice at Natur In, a pizza at Malizioso, and a set lunch at Bambu. A note on the traps: Bajon Vegan on General Cruz is listed as temporarily closed, so it does not make this list, and the two Govindas listings in town are separate kitchens, so use the General Cruz address on this page.
