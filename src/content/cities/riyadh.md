Riyadh is the thinnest Gulf scene in this batch, and the list is honest about it: one fully vegan fast-food chain, two vegetarian Indian restaurants where vegan means asking for no ghee or dairy, and a set of cafes and Lebanese and Armenian restaurants with labelled vegan dishes. The scene is spread across the north of the city, Al Malqa, Ar Rabi, Olaya and Al Murabba, so a car is the way to cover it.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with a vegan burger at Furahaa, North and South Indian dishes at Kailash Parbat, a dosa at Saravanaa Bhavan, pumpkin kebbah at Almayass, a yogi burger at Yogi Restaurant, foul at Cafe Bateel, a falafel sandwich at Operation Falafel, and the falafel sandwich at Tree House Cafe. A note on the traps: Moon Shell was dropped from this list because HappyCow reports it serving dairy as of July 2026 after previously listing it as vegan, and Cleanse and Glow was dropped for the same reason, reported to serve meat in July 2026. At the Indian restaurants, always specify no butter, ghee, cream or curd.
