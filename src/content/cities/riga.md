Riga is the thinnest vegan scene on this list, and it is honest about it: the capital has a handful of dedicated vegan spots and a larger circle of vegetarian cafes with serious vegan menus, and the best eating clusters in the Centrs district around Gertrudes, Terbatas and Skolas streets. The food leans cafe-style, falafel, bagels, cakes and raw desserts, with a couple of proper sit-down restaurants in the mix.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegetarian or vegan-friendly with clearly marked vegan dishes. Start with a burger at Kozy Eats, falafel at 2eat, cold beetroot soup at Maza Terapija, a raw dessert at Nirvana, weekend brunch at MIIT, a bagel at Big Bad Bagels, a thali at Rama, and the daily lunch at Garda Pupa. A note on the traps: Raw Garden, once a favourite, is permanently closed, and several Riga vegan names from older guides have gone, so these eight are the ones with fresh reviews and open doors right now.
