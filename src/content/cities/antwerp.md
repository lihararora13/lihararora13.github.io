Antwerp does vegan food with a port city's appetite for everything: Middle Eastern sharing plates, Italian trattorias, Mexican tacos, burgers and a home-restaurant that has been plant-based since before it was fashionable. The best tables cluster in the centre and in Zurenborg, with a couple of gems in Berchem and Zuid, and they cook with natural wines, organic ingredients and a lot of warmth. This is a scene of small rooms and long lunches rather than chains.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start with the four-course set menu at Simone's Kitchen, the hummus with harissa at HUMM, a kimchi croque at Camion, a vegan carbonara at Spritz, the three-course dinner at Planttrekkerij, vegan tacos at Pura Vida, Billy's Smoked BBQ Burger at Bar d'Henri, and a mezze platter at De Broers van Julienne. A note on the traps: Bar d'Henri is no longer fully vegan under its March 2025 ownership, Camion relocated from Paleisstraat to Hospitaalplein, and Spritz relocated from Oude Koornmarkt to Stoofstraat, so use the addresses on this page.
