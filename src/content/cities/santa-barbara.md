Santa Barbara is a coastal city with a small, hard-working vegan scene, and this guide is honest about its shape: one fully vegan restaurant, Rascal's on Haley Street, and seven vegan-friendly restaurants where the vegan dishes are labelled and the kitchens know what they are doing. The strength is Indian food, three of the eight are Indian restaurants with separate vegan menus, plus a fine-dining room in the Kimpton Canary hotel, a noodle bar, a ramen shop and a donut cafe. Most of the eight sit within a few blocks of State Street, so the guide works as a walk.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with the elote at Rascal's, the mushroom dandan noodles at Finch & Fork, a samosa at Apna Indian Kitchen, the chana masala at Indian Tandoori Kingdom, a vegetable curry at Flavor of India, a vegan noodle bowl at Empty Bowl, the vegan veggie ramen at Craft Ramen, and a vegan donut at Hook and Press. A note on the traps: three famous names are gone, Oliver's closed permanently in April 2024, Fala Bar closed in January 2025, and Satellite closed on September 13, 2026, so older guides that lead with them are out of date; and Backyard Bowls was reported in February 2026 to serve items containing collagen, so it is not in this guide.
