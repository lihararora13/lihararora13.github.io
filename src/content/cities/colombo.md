Colombo's vegan food is mostly Sri Lankan and South Indian food that was already plant-based: heirloom rice and curry, dosa, idli, hoppers and coconut-milk curries, with a growing layer of intentional plant-based cafes on top. The city's fully vegan anchor is Ranbath in Colombo 5, and the rest of the list is the Tamil vegetarian restaurants of Wellawatta, Bambalapitiya and Kollupitiya, where vegans have eaten well for decades.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian kitchens with clear vegan options. Start with heirloom rice and curry at Ranbath, a vegan English breakfast at Kumbuk, a masala dosa at Shanmugas, and a thali at Thalis. Ask for no ghee and no curd, coconut milk does the rest, and the food is some of the cheapest and tastiest on this whole site.
