Bilbao's vegan scene is one of the strongest in northern Spain, split between the Casco Viejo and Indautxu. Four of the eight picks are fully vegan, including a vegan bar on Calle Villarías, a vegan deli that opened in May 2026 and a vegan tapas bar, and the rest are vegan-friendly kitchens that take the vegan dishes seriously.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start at La Camelia Vegan Bar on Calle Villarías, Plant Bites on Calle Ercilla and La Madriguera, the vegan tapas bar. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegan restaurant that closed in 2025 is not on this list, and a cafe that had moved within the Casco Viejo is listed at its current address.
