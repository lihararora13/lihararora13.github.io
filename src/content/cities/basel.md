Basel eats vegan on both sides of the Rhine, where a fully vegan fine-dining room in Kleinbasel anchors a scene of vegan kebab, a vegan garden restaurant, a vegetarian buffet and Lebanese mezze in the old town. The picks spread from the Marktplatz to Klybeck and the Breite, so plan by meal rather than by street.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the tasting menu at Lauch, the vegan kebab at Vegitat, the garden dishes at Jardin, and the buffet at Tibits. A note on the traps: House of Vegan at Kohlenberg 4 is permanently closed and the company was deleted from the commercial register in August 2025, although vegan-map still lists it, so it stays off the list; Lauch's older guides give a Klingentalstrasse address, but the current restaurant is on Klybeckstrasse 241; and Tibits has two branches, so go to the old-town one on Stanzlergasse.
