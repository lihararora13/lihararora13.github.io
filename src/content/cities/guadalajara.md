Guadalajara's food identity is built on birria, carnitas and tortas ahogadas, and its vegan scene answers that challenge head-on: vegan birria with jackfruit, vegan tortas ahogadas, vegan pozole and vegan tacos, all served in the walkable Chapultepec and Colonia Americana neighbourhoods where most of the best tables sit within a few blocks of each other.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with a torta ahogada at La Flaca, chilaquiles at Choclo y Maíz, antojitos at La Vegana Garnacha, birria tacos at 100% Vegana Taqueria, a plate at El Vegano, a cafe lunch at Hipatia, a burger at Blooming Onion, and tacos at Estación Vegana.
