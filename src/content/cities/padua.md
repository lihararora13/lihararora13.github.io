Padua eats vegan around its great squares and its university streets, with the strongest cluster near Prato della Valle and the historic centre. The scene is anchored by a 100% vegan tasting restaurant and a 100% plant-based bistrot by the Prato, plus a vegetarian osteria from the organic movement, the city's historic vegetarian restaurant, and an Indian kitchen that local vegans rate 100 out of 10.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the saucer tasting menu at GingerGi, the plant-based Italian classics at Veganda, the beetroot borscht at Osteria di Fuori Porta, and the curries at Peace 'n' Spice. A note on the traps: the Gnam Gnam pin is square-level approximate, since the gelateria sits on Piazza dei Signori and the geocoder returned a neighbouring shop; and Corteverdechiara, a high-end vegetable tasting restaurant reviewed in November 2026, sits in Correzzola outside the city, so it stays off the Padua list.
