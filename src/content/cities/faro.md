Faro's vegan scene is small and concentrated in the old town, from a 100 percent vegan restaurant on Travessa da Madalena to a vegan buffet on Rua Filipe Alistao. Two of the eight picks are fully vegan, and the rest are vegan-friendly kitchens, from a vegetarian restaurant on Largo do Pé da Cruz to a Nepalese kitchen by the marina.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start at Outro Lado on Travessa da Madalena, Vegan Box on Rua Filipe Alistao and Alcachofra on Largo do Pé da Cruz. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegan restaurant that closed in 2025 is not on this list, and a cafe that had moved is listed at its current address.
