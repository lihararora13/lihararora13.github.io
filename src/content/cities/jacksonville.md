Jacksonville's vegan scene clusters in Murray Hill and Riverside, two historic neighborhoods west of downtown, with San Marco and the beach adding more. The list is comfort-food led: all-vegan Southern plates, a vegan bar with live music, an all-vegan bakery, and a vegan smoothie bar, plus ramen, pizza, and curry from friendly kitchens. It is one of the strongest all-vegan counts in this region, and the Murray Hill strip alone can carry a day of eating.

Start with the Buffalo Ranch Chickun Sandwich at Murray Hillbilly, a pressed sandwich at The Walrus, and a chocolate cupcake at Sweet Theory, then work through Foo Dog's curry, V Pizza, and the mushroom flatbread at Reggae Roots. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: 3 Natives was dropped after its listing resolved to the wrong address, and Naked Kitchen was researched and held as a reserve; Jax Beach Brunch Haus sits in Jacksonville Beach rather than Jacksonville proper, so plan the drive.
