Tartu eats vegan in the old town and the Aparaaditehas, where a restaurant in the Widget Factory cultural centre anchors a scene of a clubhouse restaurant, an Asian fusion house with a separate vegan menu, an Indian express and a pizza and pancake pub with a whole vegan menu page. The picks are walkable in the centre, and none of the eight is fully vegan, so order from the vegan sections.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan. Start with the carrot dogs and bao buns at Kolm Tilli, the vegan choices at Aparaat, the vegan section at Asian Chef, and the vegan pancakes at Eesti Pubi Ruuni. A note on the traps: Eesti Pubi Ruuni was previously called Ruunipizza, so use the current name; Poké Bowl Tartu Kvartal was reported in January 2025 to have limited vegan options, so it stays off the list; and Crepp has no vegan crepe options, so it stays off the list too.
