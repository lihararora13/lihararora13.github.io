Chennai might be the easiest Indian city to eat vegan in without trying, dosa, idli, sambar and rasam are already there, but its dedicated vegan scene is what makes it a joy. The fully vegan kitchens cluster in Thiruvanmiyur and Alwarpet, cooking jackfruit biryani, Chettinad pepper tofu and vegan sushi, while the city's beloved vegetarian restaurants fill in the rest with clearly marked vegan dishes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian kitchens with serious vegan menus. Start with jackfruit biryani at The Viridian Plate, vegan sushi at Eko Lyfe, a vegan pizza at Pumpkin Tales, and crispy lotus stem at Flower Drum. In the vegetarian spots, say no ghee and no curd and the kitchen will know exactly what you mean.
