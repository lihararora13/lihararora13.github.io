Santiago's vegan scene is small and walkable, concentrated in the old town around Rúa do Hospitaliño, Rúa da Conga and Rúa de San Pedro. Three of the eight picks are fully vegan, including a vegan cafe and a fully vegan restaurant, and the rest are vegan-friendly kitchens, from a Galician food-market stall to a vegetarian restaurant on Rúa do Vilar.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start at A Porta Verde on Rúa do Hospitaliño, The Green House on Rúa da Conga and The Veggie Carmen in the Galiciana food market. One caution: Black Cat, the vegan cafe on Rúa de San Pedro, is a different place from O Gato Negro on Rúa do Vilar, so do not confuse the two.
