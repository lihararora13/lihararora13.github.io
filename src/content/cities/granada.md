Granada's vegan scene is one of the strongest in Andalusia, concentrated in the centre around Plaza de los Girones, Calle Cruz and Calle Elvira. Six of the eight picks are fully vegan, including a vegan bakery, a vegan cafe and a 100 percent plant-based restaurant, and the rest are vegetarian kitchens with serious vegan menus.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start at Restaurante Vegano Hicuri on Plaza de los Girones, El Piano on Calle Santiago and Ítaca on Calle Cruz. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegan restaurant that closed in 2025 is not on this list, and a cafe that had moved is listed at its current address.
