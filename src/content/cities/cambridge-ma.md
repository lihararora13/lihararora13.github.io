Cambridge's vegan scene splits between Harvard Square's old vegetarian institutions and Kendall Square's newer, tech-adjacent counters, with Central Square and Inman Square in between. The strongest pick is a fully vegan ramen shop, and the rest of the list runs from a vegetarian diner and an organic cafe to vegetable-forward fine dining and Indian pizza. It is a scene where asking pays off, since several kitchens can make almost everything vegan on request.

Start with the vegan ramen at Ramen O' Bowl, a diner plate at Veggie Galaxy, and a bowl at Life Alive, then work through Fallow Kin, Nirvana, and the pizza at Veggie Crust and Area Four. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Clover Food Lab announced it would close in May 2026 and then reopened its Boston and Cambridge locations on June 9, 2026, so the verified-open location listed here is the Central Square shop at 496 Massachusetts Avenue, while the 1075 Cambridge Street site is permanently closed.
