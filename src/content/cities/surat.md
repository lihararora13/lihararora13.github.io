Surat eats big and eats veg, and the plant-based corner of the city is quietly excellent. Most of the action clusters around Piplod and Vesu, where cafés with proper vegan menus sit a short walk from each other, so a day of grazing is easy: a smoothie bowl for breakfast, a ragi pizza without cheese for lunch, and a jungle-themed dinner to finish.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, with one fully vegan anchor in Daily Bowl and the rest solid vegan-friendly kitchens where the plant-based dishes are the stars. Start with the vegan pasta at Thyme & Whisk, a bowl at Daily Bowl, and the multicuisine spread at Spice Villa. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and Surat will feed you very well indeed.
