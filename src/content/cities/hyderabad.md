Hyderabad's vegan scene has thinned out since its late-2010s cafe boom, and what survives is the good stuff: a handful of fully vegan kitchens, a few home kitchens and pop-ups, and the city's great vegetarian institutions, where dosa, idli and chana masala were vegan all along if you skip the ghee. The new wave is proudly Hyderabadi too, jackfruit biryani, vegan haleem and cashew curd rice.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian or vegan-friendly kitchens with clear vegan options. Start with a mock-meat pizza at e'woke, a dosa at Chutneys, and a soya chaap at Gabru Di Chaap. Ask about ghee and curd everywhere; the kitchens here are used to the question and happy to cook around it.
