East London is one of the thinnest scenes in this batch, and it is honest about it: a single fully plant-based cafe, the Khula Box in the Ashmel Spar Centre, carries the city, with a vegan caterer, a health eatery and a few friendly restaurants around it. The food is burgers and Buddha bowls and juices rather than fine dining, and most of the best eating sits within a few minutes of the beachfront and the city centre.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the Khula Burger on pita at the Khula Box, a vegan cake at Spoonful, and tofu ramen at Gyoza Home. A note on the traps: HappyCow flags the Khula Box as maybe closed, but its ordering page and recent local coverage show it trading, so it stays at rank 1 with that flag noted; the London-based E3 Vegan that dominates search results is the other East London, in England, and has nothing to do with this city.
