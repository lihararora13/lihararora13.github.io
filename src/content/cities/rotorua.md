Rotorua has no fully vegan restaurant, but it has a strong vegetarian core on Tutanekai Street and a scattering of cafes and restaurants with clearly marked vegan options. The anchors are BailwalG, a pure vegetarian Indian restaurant, Atticus Finch, a fusion restaurant with clearly marked vegan share plates, and Scope Cafe, the People's Choice Best Cafe, with Thai, Italian and market food filling out the map. Expect to ask for vegan dishes by name, and expect the Indian and Thai kitchens to use dairy and fish sauce unless you specify otherwise.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan. Start with the vegetarian thali at BailwalG, the vegan share plates at Atticus Finch, the vegan dishes at Scope Cafe, and the cauliflower wings at The Fainting Goat. A note on the traps: FTP Vegan Eats is a food truck, not a restaurant, so check the night market days before you go; and the name alone is not proof of vegan status, so it is filed vegan-friendly, not all-vegan.
