Portland has been out-veganing everyone for decades, and it shows. This is
a town where vegan dim sum, Sri Lankan rice-and-curry plates, and Detroit-style
pizza all compete for your weeknight attention, and where a taqueria's cashew
quesillo makes locals weep with pride.

We tasted around and ranked eight spots that actually deliver, every single one
fully vegan. Bring a rain jacket, leave room for dessert, and don't say we
didn't warn you about the hot chili pol roti.
