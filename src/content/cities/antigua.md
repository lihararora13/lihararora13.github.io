Antigua is a colonial tourist town, and its vegan scene is the densest in Central America outside the big capitals: two fully vegan restaurants, a health-food kitchen, an Indian-fusion dining room, and a farm restaurant, all within a few blocks of each other in the historic centre. The food mixes Guatemalan ingredients with international cooking, and the kitchens are used to vegan travellers, so the menus are clearly marked and the staff know what vegan means.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegan-friendly. Start with a burger at La Bruja, dumplings at Once:Once, a falafel plate at Samsara, a hot dish at Sat Nam, a vegetable pepian at 27 Adentro, a farm lunch at Caoba Farms, a falafel at Rainbow Cafe, and a curry at Indian Accent. A note on the traps: the scene is tourist-driven, so prices run higher than elsewhere in Guatemala, and several kitchens close one day a week, so check the hours on the day.
