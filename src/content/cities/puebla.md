Puebla's vegan scene is centred on the historic centre: a vegetarian pizza spot on Calle 3 Oriente, a long-running vegetarian buffet on Avenida 5 de Mayo, a vegan restaurant with a striking tiled facade, and a set of vegetarian cafes with vegan dishes. It is a colonial city where the best vegan food is mostly vegetarian kitchens with vegan options.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 3 of them fully vegan. Start with the beet carpaccio pizza at La Berenjena, the enmoladas at La Zanahoria, the vegan plates at Britches, and the vegan dishes at La Nectarina. A note on the traps: La Zanahoria and La Berenjena are vegetarian, not all-vegan, so confirm the vegan preparations. Several Puebla vegetarian spots use dairy in many dishes, so ask for vegan versions specifically.
