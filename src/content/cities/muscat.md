Muscat is a small scene, and this list is built on what the city actually has: a strong Indian vegetarian restaurant bench in Ruwi and Muttrah where dosa, dal and channa masala are vegan as served or with a word to the kitchen, plus hotel dining with separate vegan menus and a healthy cafe in Bawshar. There is one fully vegan pick, an ice cream parlour, and the rest are vegan-friendly, so the dairy question matters at every Indian table: specify no butter, ghee, cream, curd or yogurt.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with ice cream at Kind Kones Oman, dal fry at Dilkush, North Indian dishes at Kesar, a thali at Oman Express, the vegan menu at Aangan, vegetable curries at Bait Al Luban, avocado toast at Sprout, and the vegan mac and cheese at Tr Eat Cafe. A note on the traps: several Indian restaurants do not clearly label vegan dishes, so stick to well-known South Indian vegan dishes like dosa and dal, and confirm dairy is left out.
