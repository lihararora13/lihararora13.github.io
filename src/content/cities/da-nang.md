Da Nang runs on chay, Vietnam's centuries-old Buddhist vegetarian tradition, which means dedicated meat-free eateries are everywhere and most beloved Vietnamese dishes come in a plant-based version. The best tables split between the beachside An Thuong and My An streets near My Khe Beach, where the Western-style vegan cafes cluster, and the downtown Hai Chau neighbourhood, where the local chay restaurants serve banh mi, com tam and noodle soups for about the price of a coffee. This is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than tourist traps, and the top of the list includes a Michelin-recognised dining room.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with a tasting menu at Shamballa, a mi quang at Kurumi, a smoothie bowl at Roots, a Loving Burger at Loving Vegan, a vegan banh mi at Ngoc Chi, a set lunch at ANs, a salad bowl at Khong, and dumplings at Chay Corner.
