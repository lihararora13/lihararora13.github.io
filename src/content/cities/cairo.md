Cairo does not need a vegan scene, because Egyptian food was plant-based long before anyone called it that. Koshari, ful medames, taameya, baba ghanoush and mahshi are the everyday food of millions, and the Coptic fasting calendar means cooks across the city know how to make a satisfying meal with no meat, dairy or eggs. The dedicated vegan restaurants cluster in leafy Zamalek, Maadi and Heliopolis, and they are the cherry on top of a city where the street food is already vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with koshari at Abou Tarek, a vegan lasagne at Vegan In Our House, a bowl at Roots, and a tagged-menu lunch at Zooba. One watch-out: some koshari shops fry onions in ghee, so ask bidoun samna, without ghee, and you are golden.
