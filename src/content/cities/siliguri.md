Siliguri is the junction town where the plains meet the hills, and its vegetarian food is the practical, generous kind: thalis, dosas, momos and chaat served to travellers, traders and students. The pure-veg restaurants cluster around Sevoke Road and Station Feeder Road, and the newer cafes in the malls are starting to add the salads, pastas and noodle bowls that vegan travellers look for.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Siliguri's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a club kachori at Kesariya, a veg thali at Celebration Inn, jhol momos at Snackyee, and a dosa at Shri Maharaja. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
