Göttingen is a university town, and its vegan scene eats like one: compact, central, affordable, and built around the old town's half-timbered streets rather than spread across districts. Almost everything on this list sits within a ten-minute walk of the Markt, and the range is surprisingly wide for a city of a hundred and twenty thousand: fully vegan Vietnamese cooking, all-vegan kebab, Ethiopian sharing plates, smash burgers, Indian curries and pizza with vegan cheese. Two of the eight picks are fully vegan, and the rest are mixed kitchens with clear, well-labelled vegan dishes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with an udon bowl at Chay Vegan Kitchen, a crunchy kebab at Crunchy Kebab Vegan, vegan sushi at Gaia garden, a vegan platter at Abessina, a falafel burger at Helene, a vegetable curry at India-Haus, a vegan pizza at Pipapo, and a Veggie Crunchy at burgerme. A note on the traps: Gaia garden is nearly but not entirely vegan, a couple of its dishes contain egg, so order the vegan dishes by name, and the same goes for the mixed kitchens here, where the vegan preparation is the one to ask for.
