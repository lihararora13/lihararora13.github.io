Chester's vegan scene is compact and very walkable, strung along Eastgate Street, the Rows and Northgate Street, with a vegan cafe in Grosvenor Park and a market hall that hides a fully vegan stall. Four of the eight picks are fully vegan, which is a strong ratio for a city this size, and the rest are serious vegan-friendly kitchens rather than token options.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start at Shrub on Eastgate Street, Pink Lettuce on Bridge Street Row and the Vegan House stall in Chester Market. One important correction: Jaunty Goat used to be fully vegan but changed to fully vegetarian in late 2025, so it is listed here as vegan-friendly, not all-vegan.
