Shenzhen is China's youngest megacity, and its vegan scene matches: sleek malls, garden cafes and Buddhist restaurants that have been quietly feeding vegetarians for decades. The Luohu Buddhist strip and the Nanshan and Futian malls hold most of the action, and the food swings from temple-style mock meats to truffle fried rice and vegan pizza.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegetarian with strong vegan menus. Start with black truffle fried rice at Qingchun Perma, a Green Room lunch set, a pizza at Young Vegan, mock chicken drumsticks at The Summer Tea House, coconut curry at Da Zizai, the buffet at La Vegan, classic dishes at Jinhai Ge, and mock carp soup at Torango.
