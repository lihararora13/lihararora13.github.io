Reno's vegan scene splits between downtown's West Street Market, where two restaurants share one address, and Midtown's Virginia Street strip, with its health-food cafes and gastropubs. The list runs from an all-vegan falafel counter to a vegetarian Indian kitchen, a Vietnamese noodle shop, and a Mexican cantina, and the city's annual Vegan Chef Challenge keeps the scene active. It is a scene where the strongest meals are the ones that lean into a specific cuisine.

Start with falafel wraps at The Fix, the vegan hash at Great Full Gardens, and a plate at Sage Leaf, then work through Thali, La Mint, and Noble Pie. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: the Kitchen by Great Full Gardens is a separate South Reno location and is not listed, only the Midtown branch is; and the pins for The Fix, Thali, Great Full Gardens, and Sage Leaf are address-range approximations rather than surveyed entrances, per the guide that verified them.
