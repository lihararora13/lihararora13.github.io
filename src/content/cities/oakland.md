Oakland is, pound for pound, one of the best vegan cities in America, and it eats like it knows it. The Town's plant-based scene runs from Michelin-recognised fine dining in Rockridge to Louisiana soul food in Jack London Square, from Temescal taquerias to a macrobiotic institution that has been feeding people since before vegan was a word anyone used at dinner parties. This is a scene with range, and it is all vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan, no decoding required. Start with the Millennium burger, chik'n and waffles at Souley Vegan, mushroom pastor tacos at La Venganza, a croissant at Timeless Coffee, a full plate at Shangri-La, pho at Golden Lotus, itaya tacos at Rico Rico Vegano, and the buffet at Shangri-La on Linden.
