Santa Fe does plant-based food the way it does everything else: slowly, seasonally, and with chile on the side. The scene clusters around the Plaza and the Railyard, with a couple of essential stops out on St. Michael's Drive and Cerrillos Road, and it runs from Ayurvedic cafes and Jamaican food trucks to a nursery garden lunch and a white-tablecloth adobe dining room that has been feeding the city since 1980. Green chile, posole-style stews, and farm-market produce show up everywhere, and most kitchens here will happily veganise a dish if you ask.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with the roasted cauliflower tacos at Annapurna's, the combo plate at Ras Rody's, the smothered vegan breakfast burrito at Sweetwater, the daily prix fixe at the Plants of the Southwest kitchen, a veganised huevos rancheros at Cafe Pasqual's, a seasonal salad at Vinaigrette, the jerk tofu at Jambo, and a tofu scramble at the Tune Up. A note on the traps: Annapurna's Santa Fe cafe now sits at 1620 St. Michael's Drive, not the old Don Diego Avenue address in older guides, and Body Cafe, long listed as a Santa Fe vegan stop, is closed for a rebuild with its own site still saying coming soon, so it is not listed here.
