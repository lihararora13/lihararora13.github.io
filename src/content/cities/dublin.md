Dublin may be famous for literary pubs and pints of Guinness, but its vegan kitchens have been quietly building something wonderful for decades: wholefood dining rooms older than most of the diners, Temple Bar comfort food that takes fried chicken sandwiches very seriously, and bakeries turning out croissants nobody believes are dairy free. This is a compact, walkable capital where you can graze from a cinnamon roll to a tasting menu without ever leaving the city centre.

All eight picks are verified open as of October 2026. Start with The Saucy Cow for indulgent Temple Bar comfort, Cornucopia for a proper sit-down institution, and It's a Trap for a morning cinnamon roll, then book Glas for a special night, grab falafel at Umi, and finish with a giant slice from Bambino. A note on the traps: Sova Vegan Butcher, Blazing Salads and Kale+Coco have all closed, so this list sticks to rooms that are actually serving right now.
