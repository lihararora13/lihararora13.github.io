Santa Rosa's vegan scene is centred on Old Railroad Square and downtown: a plant-based eatery and mercantile, a farm-driven restaurant with vegan bowls, a Thai cafe, and a set of cafes and sushi spots with vegan dishes. It is a Sonoma County scene that rewards a walk between the Square and the downtown core.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 1 of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan miso mushroom pot pie at The Branch Line, the Thai tofu salad at East West Cafe, the carrot hummus at Spinster Sisters, and the vegan vegetable sushi at Zenshi. A note on the traps: Fern Bar and Cozy Plum are in Sebastopol, not Santa Rosa, so they are not listed as Santa Rosa picks. Vegan Mob is a pop-up food truck that visits Santa Rosa on occasional Saturdays, not a fixed restaurant, so it is not listed.
