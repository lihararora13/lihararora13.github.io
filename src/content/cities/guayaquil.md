Guayaquil, the alternate that replaced Vitória in this round, has a vegetarian lunch scene rather than a vegan one: none of its seven picks is fully vegan, and the list is built on vegetarian restaurants in the centre, Urdesa and Alborada that serve vegan dishes as part of a broader menu. The cooking is Ecuadorian almuerzo food, seco de carne vegetal, guatita, sancocho and lasaña, at budget prices.

The vegetarian almuerzo at Vive Mas y Mejor and the soy-meat lunch at Fenix are the two plates to plan around. Five venues were checked and dropped as temporarily closed: Veggie's Way, Falafel Jerusalem, Mr. Wang Ho, Buggie Burguer, and the Cafeteria and Restaurant Vegetariano on Av. Victor Emilio Estrada. Restaurante Vegetariano Segundo Paso, famous for its four-dollar vegan almuerzo, has no findable address and is not listed.
