Munich does vegan food the way it does everything else: properly, heartily, and with a beer garden's worth of confidence. The best tables cluster in the Glockenbachviertel, around Sendlinger Tor and the Westend, with a couple of gems out in Haidhausen and Maxvorstadt, and they cook Bavarian food first: schnitzel, Käsespätzle, Kaiserschmarrn and Leberkässemmeln, all of it fully plant-based, alongside Vietnamese pho, Turkish döner and some of the best vegan pizza in Germany. This is a scene of long-running local institutions rather than pop-ups, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who came for the pretzels and stayed for the plants.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, every one of them fully vegan. Start with the soya schnitzel at Max Pett, the Käsespätzle at Bodhi, a kimchi pizza at Dr Drooly, a tofu scramble at Emmi's Kitchen, a seitan döner at Erbils, a vegan Leberkässemmel at Die Vegane Fleischerei, the phở at Akimy, and a breakfast plate among the rescue cats at Katzentempel. A note on the traps: the Häberlstraße branch of Die Vegane Fleischerei is permanently closed, so head to the Frauenstraße shop instead, and the old vegan spots that quietly went vegetarian or closed never made the list.
