Rome might be the most delicious surprise in this whole directory: a city famous for carbonara and cacio e pepe turns out to have a fully vegan version of both, plus a cat cafe, a vegan cheese shop, a pay-by-weight buffet, and a ten-course tasting menu that regulars fly in from America to eat twice. The scene clusters in Ostiense and the Trieste quarter, with gems tucked into Trastevere and the Centro Storico, so the best tables range from a two-euro buffet plate to a white-tablecloth splurge. Come hungry, book the fancy one ahead, and save room for tiramisu.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and seven of the eight are fully vegan. Start with a carbonara at Rifugio Romano, graze the vegan omelet at Buddy, the seasonal tasting menu at Romeow, a loaded buffet plate at Ops!, the ten-course tasting at Vrindaa, glazed tempeh at Aromaticus, a stuffed focaccia at Fermaggio, and a crostata at 100% Bio. A note on the traps: Dall'Albero, the old vegan cheese lab on Via Salaria, closed for relocation years ago and never reopened, and Nativa, HappyCow's top-rated pick, sits out in Ostia Lido, a half-hour train ride from the centre, so it stays off this list for now.
