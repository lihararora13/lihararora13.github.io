Buenos Aires is famous for beef, which makes its vegan scene twice as fun: the city that invented the parrilla now grills seitan on it, stuffs empanadas without a scrap of cheese, and plates fine-dining botany inside a palace hotel. Palermo, San Nicolas and Recoleta are where the plant party is loudest.

All six picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Gioia for the fancy night, La Reverde for a proper vegan asado experience, and Let It V for sushi and fusion plates, then graze your way through milanesa joints and cosy vegan cantinas.
