Newcastle does vegan food with northern warmth and no fuss. The best tables cluster around the city centre, the Grainger Market, Bigg Market and Blandford Square, with the Ouseburn valley and Heaton holding the scene's two most characterful rooms, and they cook everything from Indian street food and vegan pub classics to Mexican small plates and slow brunch. This is a scene built on independents: a healing cafe on Buckingham Street, a bakery-cafe that took over the old Vegano spot, and a fully vegan pub pouring pints beside the Ouseburn.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and Super Natural and Tiny Tiny vegan-friendly. Start with the vegan Sunday roast at The Ship Inn, the samosa chaat at SnackWallah, the momo dumplings at Earthlings, the Sunrise Stack at The Tree Rooms, the tofu tikka masala at Karma Kitchen, the blue-tortilla tacos at Cantina NCL, a full veggie breakfast at Super Natural, and the vegan shakshuka at Tiny Tiny. A note on the traps: Vegano on Blandford Square served its last weekend in October 2025 and is now The Tree Rooms, and the vegan sushi spot Veganatomy closed its Chillingham Road shop at the end of March 2026, so use the addresses on this page, not the ones in older guides.
