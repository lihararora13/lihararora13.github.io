Belém's vegan scene is built around three fully vegan kitchens, a 100% plant-based burger joint that calls itself the first vegan burger bar in the city, a creative vegan kitchen in Marco, and a long-running vegetarian institution at Praça Batista Campos. Amazonian ingredients do the work here: jambu, açaí, tucupi and cupuaçu turn up on vegan menus in ways they do not anywhere else.

The feijoada at Purão Vegano, the burgers at Veg Burger and the moqueca de banana at Govinda are the three plates to plan around. Govinda's sister café Empório Natural was mentioned in its Instagram but no separate address could be found, so it is not listed separately. Every pick below was verified open in November 2026.
