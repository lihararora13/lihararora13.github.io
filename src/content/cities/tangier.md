Tangier is a port city with a literary past and a small, real vegan scene, and it is carried by one restaurant: NBTAHOUSE, a plant-based riad in the medina with a 5.0 HappyCow rating, is the reason a vegan comes to Tangier. Around it sit the medina's Moroccan rooms, Soli with its vegan kefta skewers, the clifftop Cafe Hafa, and a handful of Italian and Kasbah rooms that will feed a vegan, but the scene is thin and the medina addresses are approximate by nature.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the mezze platter at NBTAHOUSE, the vegan kefta skewers at Soli, and mint tea at Cafe Hafa. A note on the traps: one recent review found NBTAHOUSE's execution uneven and its menu descriptions inaccurate, so go for the mezze and the mushroomawarma rather than the priciest plates, and several medina listings are cafes with a single vegan dish rather than restaurants.
