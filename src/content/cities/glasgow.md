Glasgow's vegan scene is older than the Instagram version of plant-based food: Mono opened in 2002, Stereo followed, and the city has been building on that foundation ever since. The best tables are also gig venues, record shops and community spaces, which is exactly why they work: the food is comfort-first, the portions are large, and the rooms are full of people who are there for the music as much as the meal.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with the curry laksa at Suissi, the dan dan noodles at Stereo, the to-fish supper at Mono, tacos at Antojitos, small plates at The Glad Cafe, a natural wine at Sylvan, a falafel wrap at Falafel To Go, and a thali at Ranjit's. A note on the traps: The Green Goose, once on the top lists, is closed, and The 78's kitchen has had patchy hours, so the guide sticks to what is verified open.
