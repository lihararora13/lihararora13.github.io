Jeddah is the strongest vegan hub in Saudi Arabia, with three fully vegan restaurants, Nabati, Sinless and Moon Shell, and a set of vegan-friendly rooms around them. Nabati is the pioneer, Jeddah's first fully vegan restaurant with a zero-waste philosophy, and the scene clusters in Ar Rawdah and along the north Corniche, with Indian and Levantine rooms filling out the list.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start with the Nabati Benedict, a cashew cheese pizza at Sinless, and the molten midnight dessert at Moon Shell. A note on the traps: Saravanaa Bhavan in Jeddah was reported closed in 2026 and is not on this list, and the Vegan Street is a delivery-only option held as a reserve.
