Aachen sits where Germany meets Belgium and the Netherlands, and its vegan tables show it: a seasonal vegan restaurant with a cultural programme, a fully vegan kebab and pizza counter near Rothe Erde station, a Lebanese kitchen on the student strip, a Burtscheid café doing a named vegan breakfast, and a cat café in the old town. Three of the eight picks are fully vegan, the rest mark their plant-based dishes, and nearly everything sits within walking distance of the Dom and the Pontstraße, so the city is best done on foot.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the sticky orange tofu at Pfannenzauber, a vegan döner at Il Vegano, a vegan burger at Lolaparoli, a vegan mezze plate at AKL, the vegan breakfast at Ferbers, a vegan donut at TopTop Donuts, a tofu stir-fry at Asia Haus, and a slice of vegan cake among the cats at Katzencafé Milou. A note on the traps: Pfannenzauber and Lolaparoli keep evening hours on most days, so check before going, and Ferbers and TopTop are mixed menus, so order the vegan-marked dishes.
