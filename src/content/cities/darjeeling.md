Darjeeling feeds its visitors on momos, thukpa, parathas and hot chocolate, and the pure-veg restaurants around the Mall and Chowrasta have been doing it for years. The Udaan group's Grains restaurants are the backbone, big pure-veg dining rooms a short walk from the toy train station, while the smaller spots like Frank Ross Cafe and the Batasia Loop tiffin stall give the town its charm. Winter hours are shorter up here, so check before you climb the hill.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Darjeeling's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a thali at Grains Himalayan, fried momos and hot chocolate at Frank Ross Cafe, a paratha at the Batasia Loop tiffin stop, and first-flush tea at Happy Valley. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
