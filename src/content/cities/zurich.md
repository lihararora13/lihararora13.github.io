Zurich has been doing plant-based food longer than almost anywhere on earth: Haus Hiltl opened in 1898 and is the oldest vegetarian restaurant in the world. The modern scene builds on that history with vegan fast food, buffets, bakeries and a Michelin-starred vegetable tavern, all within a short tram ride.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the buffet at Haus Hiltl, a pay-by-weight plate at Tibits, a burger at Veganitas, the tavolata at Neue Taverne, a curry at Klara's Kitchen, a seitan doner at Vegitat, a croissant at Enzian, and dinner at Chimy's.
