London did not so much adopt vegan food as swallow it whole and make
it the point of the evening. This is the city where a chef can win a
Michelin star for a plant-based tasting menu on Old Street, where the
first fully vegan pub in town serves a Beet Wellington roast that
locals genuinely argue about, and where a Soho stalwart that opened in
1988 quietly went all-vegan and got better. Our eight picks run from
fine dining to fried "chicken", from Borough Market small plates to a
West Kensington buffet, all of them fully plant-based.

Rain is likely. A bad meal is not. Bring a brolly and an appetite.
