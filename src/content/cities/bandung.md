Bandung is not a big vegan city, and the guides are honest about that: there are only a handful of pure vegan restaurants, and after that you need vegetarian restaurants and places with vegan options. But what exists is good, from a fully vegan Indonesian comfort-food kitchen to the biggest and most economical vegetarian buffet in town, and the cafe culture around it is creative and growing.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and the rest vegan-friendly. Start with the nasi padang komplit at Hejo, the mushroom sate at Kehidupan, a buffet plate at Kuang Ming, the satay at Fortunate Coffee, a vegan chicken katsu curry at Soedirman, the vegetarian rendang at Moey, a falafel pita at Mom's and a grain bowl at Greens and Beans. A note on the traps: Burgreens Bandung is permanently closed, despite still appearing in older guides, and some Bandung venues that offer vegan options do not always have a full understanding of the requirements, so confirm dishes are vegan when you order.
