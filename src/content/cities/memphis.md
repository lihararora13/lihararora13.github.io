Memphis is a barbecue city, and its vegan scene answers in kind: vegan soul food is the story here, and it is a strong one, with Plant Based Heat and Imagine Vegan Cafe cooking the comfort food the city is famous for, entirely plant-based, and a raw kitchen, an African bistro, and a Caribbean counter filling out the rest. The picks are spread across the city, so a car helps, but every one of them is worth the drive.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with the oyster mushroom burger at Plant Based Heat, the chicken and waffles at Imagine Vegan Cafe, a raw bowl at Raw Girls, the Vegan Plate at Bala's Bistro, a vegan Triple B slider at Slider Inn, a bowl at City Silo, the vegan ramen at Good Fortune Co., and the jerk tofu at Curry n Jerk.
