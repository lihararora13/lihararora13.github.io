Kunming is the city of eternal spring, and its vegan food is rooted in Yunnan's Buddhist temple tradition as much as in its markets: crossing-bridge rice noodles, wild mushrooms, flower teas and all-you-can-eat buffets where everything is plant-based by principle. The best tables cluster in the old town around Wuyi Road and Minsheng Street, with the buffets spreading west to Xiyuan Road and south toward Dianchi Lake, and the food is Yunnan at heart: mushrooms in a dozen forms, tofu, rice noodles, bean jelly and steamed buns, at prices that rarely pass 25 yuan outside the fine-dining rooms.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegan-friendly. Start with the tomato-based crossing-bridge noodles at Flower Crossing, the stir-fried mushrooms and cold bean jelly at Qing Xiang Dian, the 22-yuan buffet at Worry-Free Tree, the ma po tofu at Ci Yun Zhai, a seasonal set menu at Ru An, the fried mushrooms at Juezhengjing, the 30-dish spread at Jingxin, and the yam soup at Zhenqingguan. A note on the traps: the Worry-Free Tree buffet is also listed on HappyCow under the name Wújíshù, same unit and same phone number, so it is one restaurant, not two. Ru An sits far south by the Dianchi convention centre and needs a reservation. Jingxin is 98% vegan, with eggs in a couple of dishes, and its serving windows are narrow, 11:30am to 1:30pm and 5:30pm to 7:30pm. Zhenqingguan is a temple restaurant open Monday to Saturday, 11am to 4pm only.
