Recife's vegan scene clusters in two pockets: the historic centre around Boa Vista and Santo Amaro, and the beachfront strip of Boa Viagem, where cafes and lunch counters sit a short walk from the sand. The cooking leans Brazilian and northeastern, feijoada and moqueca rebuilt without animal products, alongside Italian-leaning natural kitchens, and the strongest tables are the fully vegan ones that have been at it for a decade.

Start with the feijoada at VEGostices and the 100%-vegan burgers at Bigode Verde, then work through Frutteto's burrata frita and falafel salad and the lunch plates at Cozinha Celestial. No closures were found among the picks in this round, and every venue below was verified open in November 2026.
