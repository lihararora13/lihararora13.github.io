Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, the city travellers still know as Aurangabad, sits at the gateway to the Ellora and Ajanta caves, and its food scene is proudly vegetarian. This list leans on the city's best pure-veg dining rooms, where thalis, North Indian mains and Maharashtrian classics are the everyday food, and where a vegan who asks for the dairy held eats very well indeed.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what this scene genuinely supports right now. Start with a thali at Bhoj Thali, a Shevaga Handi at Sai Chaitraban, and a North Indian dinner at Kake di Hatti. A note on the headline act: The V-GAN Restaurant is vegetarian, not all-vegan, despite the name, so order with the usual dairy check.
