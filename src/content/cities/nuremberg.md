Nuremberg does vegan food with Franconian heartiness: schnitzel, gyros, döner and dumplings, all of it plant-based, clustered in the Altstadt around the Hauptmarkt and the castle, with a couple of gems out in Johannis and Gärten hinter der Veste. The scene runs from Bavarian bistro cooking to Lebanese hummus, Sichuan noodles and a cat café, and the Altstadt is compact enough to walk between all of them.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegan-friendly. Start with the schnitzel Wiener Art at Bodhi, hummus at The Hummus Connection, a vegan döner at Vegöner, Sichuan dumplings at Kaschemme, and breakfast at Katzentempel. One note: Vegöner has a second branch, Vegöner Süd at Augustenstraße 37, so use the Johannis address on this page; and My Veggy at the station is a takeaway counter, so eat on the move rather than expecting a sit-down dinner.
