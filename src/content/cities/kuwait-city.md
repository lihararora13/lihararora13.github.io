Kuwait City has no vegan district, but it has a real vegan anchor: Tashi, a 100% vegan sushi kitchen in Salmiya, and OVO, a vegan, vegetarian and flexitarian restaurant with two branches, Shuwaikh and Bneid Al Gar. Around them sit the pure-veg Indian restaurants of Salmiya, a health-food chain, and a handful of cafes with marked vegan dishes, so a vegan eats well if they know where to look.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the vegetables donut at Tashi, a broccoli salad at OVO, and a Gujarati thali at Sagar. A note on the traps: Ginger Vegetarian Restaurant is listed as temporarily closed and is not on this list, and VegMe, the pure-veggie restaurant announced for Mirqab, is opening soon rather than open, so it is not on this list either.
