Grenoble's vegan scene is one of the best in the Alps, concentrated in the hyper-centre around Rue Thiers, Rue d'Alembert and Place Championnet. Six of the eight picks are fully vegan, including a vegan fast-food spot, a vegan cafe and a vegan patisserie that opened in October 2026, and the scene is young, independent and easy to walk.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start at Locafé on Rue d'Alembert, La Bouillotte on Rue Boucher de Perthes and Gustavo on Rue Thiers. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegan restaurant that closed in 2025 is not on this list, and a cafe that had moved is listed at its current address.
