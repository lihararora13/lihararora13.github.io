Cartagena is not an easy city for vegans: the Caribbean coast cooks with seafood, beef and dairy as a matter of course, and coconut rice is often made with fish stock, so the dedicated places matter. The good news is that a real cluster has formed in the Walled City and Getsemani, with three fully vegan kitchens and a handful of flexible restaurants that treat plant-based dishes as proper cooking rather than side dishes. Most of it is walkable within the old town, and the tropical fruit and juice culture fills any gaps.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with the heart-of-palm ceviche at La Cevicheria Verde, the arepas de choclo at Alma, a grain bowl at Verdeo, the stuffed peppers at El Jardin Botanico, the vegan ceviche in a coconut at Cande, a lentil bowl at Gokela, a wrap at Sambal, and a pita en el bosque at Crepes & Waffles. A note on the traps: always ask whether the coconut rice was made without fish stock, and do not confuse La Cevicheria Verde with the famous fish cevicheria of a similar name a few streets away.
