Hanoi might be the easiest capital in Asia to eat vegan in, and it barely tries: centuries of Buddhist chay cooking mean plant-based pho, banh mi, banh goi and hotpot are just how the city eats, not a special diet. The best tables split between the walkable Old Quarter around Hoan Kiem Lake and the calmer, cafe-lined streets of Tay Ho by West Lake, and almost everything costs less than a coffee back home.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with Bao An for a street-food breakfast on a plastic stool, iVegan for a smoothie-bowl brunch, and Kiez for burgers and kebabs by the lake. Then duck down the alley to Hem for hotpot, graze Chayfood's pan-Asian menu, take a slow Western breakfast at La Studio, sit down to Dana's rice-warehouse feast, and finish at Abracado for bagels and cake. A note on the traps: Veggie Castle's Yen Ninh buffet carries conflicting closure reports from October 2026, so it stays off the list until that clears up, and The Veg and Jalus are both confirmed closed.
