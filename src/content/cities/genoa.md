Genoa eats vegan in the caruggi, the narrow alleys of the old town, where the city's naturally plant-based Ligurian cooking does half the work: farinata di ceci, focaccia without lard, panissa and cuculli are vegan by tradition, and the dedicated vegan kitchens cluster within a few minutes of Piazza De Ferrari. The scene is small, serious and local, built on a 100% vegan rosticceria, a vegan and raw restaurant, organic delis and vegetarian gastronomie rather than chains.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them all-vegan. Start with the focaccia and tempeh at Jaa Nu', the seitan cutlet at Soul Kitchen, the farinata at Pomdeter, and the handmade pasta at Pastificio Artigianale di Canneto. A note on the traps: InFusion, a 100% vegan raw kitchen near Brignole, could not be pinned to a verified street address, so it stays off the list; Green Bowl is filed vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan because its all-vegan status could not be confirmed against HappyCow or its own site; and the old-town focaccia bars vary, so ask for the oil-based focaccia without lard before ordering.
