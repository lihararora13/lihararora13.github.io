Jamshedpur is a steel-city food scene built on South Indian counters and pure-veg family rooms in Bistupur and Sakchi, with mall food courts doing the multi-cuisine work. Local guides list the same ten pure-veg names again and again, and dairy is the usual caveat.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. Two traps caught here: Hong Kong in Bistupur is questioned by locals as not actually vegetarian, so it is left out, and Little Metro Pure Veg is listed as temporarily closed. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city.
