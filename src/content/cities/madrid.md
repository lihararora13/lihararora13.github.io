Madrid may be famous for jamon and tortilla, but its vegan kitchens have gleefully veganised both, and then kept going: patatas bravas, calamari, croquetas, chorizo baguettes and cheesy nachos, all made from plants, all over Chueca, Malasana and Lavapies. This is a capital that likes to eat late, eat socially and eat plenty, and vegans are very much invited.

All eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Mad Mad Vegan for the big Chueca burger everyone recommends, Frutas Prohibidas for brunch worth queuing for, and Distrito Vegano for a celebratory feast, then tapas-hop from B13 to La Oveja Negra.
