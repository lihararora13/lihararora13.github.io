Seville does not shout about its vegan credentials, it just makes plant-based versions of beloved Andalusian dishes that taste authentically local. Tortilla without egg, spinach with chickpeas, seitan churrasco, vegan paella on Sundays, all of it served in market halls, Alameda bars and Triana bakeries that locals actually use.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly with serious plant-based menus. Start with a tortilla at Veganitessen, a seasonal plate at Veganí, a healthy bowl at Balino, a seitan churrasco at Arte y Sabor, tapas at La Bartola, an empanada at Obrador la Osa, tacos at Taquería la Lupe, and tapas at Bar Alfalfa. A note on the traps: Vegan Rock, the old Alameda tapas bar, is reported closed as of February 2026, so it does not make the list.
