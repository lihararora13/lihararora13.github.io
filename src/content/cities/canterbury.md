Canterbury's vegan scene is small and charming, clustered around Burgate, Mercery Lane and St Dunstans Street, all within the city walls. It has three fully vegan anchors, including a vegan cat cafe and a fully vegan poke bar, plus a plant-based pub and a handful of solid vegan-friendly kitchens.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start at The Veg Box Cafe on Burgate, Canterbury Tails on Mercery Lane and OmaOma for vegan poke. The traps were mostly stale listings: one guide still features a cafe that closed last year, and a bakery that moved across town is listed at its current address.
