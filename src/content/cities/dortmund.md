Dortmund is a Ruhr metropolis that eats like a neighbourhood: most of the good stuff sits within a few streets of the Kreuzviertel, around Saarlandstraße and Kreuzstraße, with the rest a short walk into the city centre and the harbour. The scene leans café and counter rather than white tablecloth: all-day breakfast, bowls, Ukrainian and Georgian plates, Neapolitan pizza and burgers, all of it plant-friendly and mostly priced for students and shift workers. Only one pick is fully vegan, but the vegan-friendly depth is real, and every kitchen here labels its plant-based dishes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the Rührtofu at VRÜH, the vegan beef wellington at Doppeltsolecker 2.0, the vegan gyros at Guttut, a marinated-tofu bowl at nosh, the farinata at Nansen, a vegan poutine at Frittenwerk, a vegan margherita at 60 seconds to napoli, and a plant-based burger at Hans im Glück. A note on the traps: nosh serves one salmon bowl alongside an otherwise vegan menu, so stick to the bowls marked vegan, and Doppeltsolecker and Guttut are vegetarian kitchens with vegan dishes, so order from the vegan side of the menu.
