Cebu is a meat-loving city, and its vegan community has answered with warmth and invention: jackfruit adobo, tofu sisig, vegan kare-kare, mushroom burgers and weekend buffets, mostly in Lahug, Escario and the Capitol area. Prices are gentle, portions are generous, and the cooks are delighted you showed up.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with plenty of vegan dishes. Start with jackfruit adobo at Lun-haw, the set meal at Wellnessland, tortang talong at Planet Vegis, mushroom sisig at Greens Vegan Hub, the buffet at Vegan Cebu City, a sattvic plate at Healthy U, a smoothie bowl at Mooshi Green Bar, and a Filipino classic at Kaon Nilumad.
