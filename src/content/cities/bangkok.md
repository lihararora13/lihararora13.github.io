Bangkok does not do half measures, and its vegan scene is gloriously extra: fiery Isaan plates, creamy khao soi near Khao San Road, vegan sushi in Ekkamai, and waffles with fried vegan chicken behind Benjasiri Park. This is a city where plant-based eating has deep roots and very good desserts.

All eight picks are verified open as of October 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with Tammang for the special-but-affordable Michelin meal, Kem-Kon for a Khao San feast, and Broccoli Revolution for the Sukhumvit classic, then eat your way down the BTS line one delicious stop at a time.
