Victoria is a small, walkable harbour city with a vegan scene that punches far above its size: five fully vegan restaurants, the most of any city in this batch, covering a buffet, a cafe and bakery, a Scandinavian art cafe, a raw juice bar and a plant-based bakery, plus three vegan-friendly restaurants where the vegan dishes are the point, a dosa house, a brunch cafe in an 1888 heritage home, and a downtown fusion spot. Most of the eight sit in downtown, Fernwood and Chinatown, so the guide works as a walk, with one pick across the harbour in Esquimalt.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegan-friendly. Start with the hot buffet plate at Green Cuisine, the Western Scrambler at Fern, an onigiri at Fika, a Conscious Creator smoothie at In Er G, a vegan breakfast sandwich at Bunny's Nook, the satay sandwich at be love, a masala dosa at Dosa Paragon, and the Golden Benny at Nourish. A note on the traps: Fika is open only 9am to 1pm daily, so go in the morning; be love's food menu is vegan but some drinks contain honey, so ask about the drinks; and Bunny's Nook is takeout only, Tuesday to Saturday.
