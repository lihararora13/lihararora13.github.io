Indianapolis does vegan food the way it does everything else: warmly, generously, and without fuss. The scene clusters around downtown, Mass Ave, Fletcher Place and Broad Ripple, and it leans hard into comfort food done right, seitan reubens, soul-food plates, burgers and brunch, with a few lighter, farm-fresh stops for balance.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegetarian or vegan-friendly with serious plant-based menus. Start with a reuben at 10th Street Diner, a soul-food plate at Black Leaf, a burger at Good Vegan Bad Vegan, a grain bowl at Flower Child, brunch at The Garden Table, a thali at Honest, a scoop at BRICS, and pancakes at Milktooth. A note on the traps: Three Carrots, the old downtown favourite, is closed, and The Alkaline Electric Goddess looks temporarily closed, so neither makes the list.
