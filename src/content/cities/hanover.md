Hannover does vegan food with a capital-city's confidence and a neighbourhood feel at the same time. The best tables spread from the List through the Mitte to Linden, with fine dining out in Linden-Nord, a weekend brunch spot in the Calenberger Neustadt, and a proper ice cream parlour to finish, and they cook Vietnamese phở, German fine dining, smash burgers, Asian bistro dishes and organic café food, nearly all of it fully vegan. This is a scene of long-running local institutions rather than pop-ups, and seven of the eight picks are fully vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the phở at Cham, the Saturday vegan buffet at Hiller, a smash burger at Liners, a lunch plate at Hippo Bio Vegan, the noodle bowls at Vegan Village, a tasting menu at Rüpel, a weekend brunch at vorveg, and a scoop of vegan ice cream at Nicezeit in Linden. A note on the traps: the Rüpel Food LAB kiosk is temporarily closed, so head to the main Rüpel restaurant on Kötnerholzweg instead, and The Seven Sundays is not fully vegan despite some listings saying so, so it never made the list.
