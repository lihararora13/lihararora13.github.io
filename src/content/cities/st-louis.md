St. Louis is a vegan city that rewards wandering South Grand, where half this list lives within a few blocks of each other. The scene is deep and local, with Indian street food, soul-food-adjacent comfort plates, a proper vegetarian bistro, and a bakery that has been at it since 2008.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian or vegan-friendly with serious vegan menus. Start with a taco flight at Terror Tacos, a street-food spread at Bombay Food Junkies, a seasonal plate at Tree House, brunch at Seedz, a cupcake at SweetArt Too, a deli sandwich at Vegan Deli & Butcher, takeout from Red Bird, and a loaded toast at Fiddlehead Fern. A note on the traps: Plant Craft Kitchen is reported closed as of October 2026 and Love 'n Light Cuisine looks temporarily closed, so neither makes the list.
