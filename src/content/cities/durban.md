Durban is South Africa's curry capital, and that is very good news for vegans. The city's huge Indian heritage means beans, lentils, rotis and bunny chow are everyday food here, not a special request, and the best tables lean into it: vegan bunny chow, dhal, seitan that mimics KFC, and curries that have been meat-free for generations. Add the beachfront, the warm Indian Ocean air, and a crop of fully vegan comfort-food spots in Umhlanga, and you have one of the friendliest vegan cities in Africa.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with a burger and milkshake at The Vegan Chef, a KFV rounder at Buttercup, a slow organic lunch at Earthmother, a bean bunny chow at Patel's, and a seaside veg thali at Kara Nichha's. A note on the traps: older guides still list Falafel Fundi, which has closed, so it is not on this list.
