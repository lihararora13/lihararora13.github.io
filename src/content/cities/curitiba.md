Curitiba has been quietly building one of Brazil's best vegan scenes for over a decade, and it shows in the depth of the list: a pioneering all-vegan diner from 2013, burger joints, themed buffets that change cuisine every day, and a gastro bar pouring cocktails next to plant-based tapas. The best tables cluster in Centro, Batel, Seminario and Bom Retiro, all walkable from the historic centre, and they cook Brazilian food first: coxinha, strogonoff, feijoada-style plates and burgers, all of it generous and made from scratch.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with a coxinha at Veg Veg, a burger at Paprica Vegan, a Big Veg burger at Rango de Planta, a buffet plate at Dom Veggie, a chef's special at Mahatma Gourmet, tapas at Miraflores, a brunch plate at Mornings, and a daily plate at Pierre Vegetariano.
