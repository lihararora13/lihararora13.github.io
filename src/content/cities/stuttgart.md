Stuttgart does vegan food with Swabian thoroughness: every pick on this page is fully vegan, from a döner counter to a fine-dining room, and most of them sit within a short walk of the Schlossplatz. The scene runs from Turkish kebab and Vietnamese pho to Swabian spätzle rebuilt plant-based, with a gluten-free café, a ramen bar and a bowl counter in the Europaviertel for good measure. It is the most complete vegan city in this batch, and the hardest to eat badly in.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all eight of them fully vegan. Start with a vegan döner at doen doen kebap, a croffle at Heaven's Kitchen, a bao bun at Soy Club, gnocchi at vhy!, a tasting plate at Veganes Gold, phở at Viegan, a falafel plate at Energetic Life, and a pumpkin falafel bowl at Yuícery Milaneo. No traps on this page: every kitchen is fully vegan, so the only decision is what to order first.
