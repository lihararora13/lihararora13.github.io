La Rochelle's vegan scene is small and spread around the old town and the port, from a co-op restaurant on Rue Auguste Rodin to a vegan bakery, a vegan taco truck and a tapas bar on Rue des Cloutiers. One of the eight is fully vegan, and the rest are vegan-friendly kitchens that take the vegan dishes seriously.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at Restokoop on Rue Auguste Rodin, Le 5inq on Avenue du Lazaret and Nana's Taco Truck. One caution: Un Piano dans la Théière was reported to have added dairy cheese in July 2023, so ask for the vegan cheese specifically when you order there.
