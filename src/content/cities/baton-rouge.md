Baton Rouge is a hard city to eat vegan in, and the scene knows it: the best tables are a plant-based fine-dining cafe on Bluebonnet, a plant-based wellness bar in Mid City, and a handful of ramen, Thai and Mediterranean kitchens that label vegan dishes clearly. It is small, scattered and worth planning around rather than wandering into.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Start with the barbecue jackfruit sandwich at The Plantry Cafe, the creamy vegan ramen at Daruma, the vegan boudin balls at Trap Haus, and the falafel at Albasha Express. A note on the traps: Several AI-written guides list invented restaurants such as The Green Kitchen, Lotus Plant-Based Bistro and Bayou Bites; none of those exist in Baton Rouge, so they are not listed here. Veganish Vibes is a pop-up with an uncertain fixed address and is left off for that reason.
