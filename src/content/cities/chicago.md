Chicago does not do anything halfway, and its vegan scene is no
exception. This is a city where a diner that has been meat-free since
1983 shares the spotlight with soul food that has been plant-based
for four decades, and where a taqueria decided all-vegan tacos were
the obvious choice. Our eight picks span diner classics, Asian fusion,
Korean Buddhist cooking, Mexican street food, and comfort food with
a beer garden.

Chicago is the city that proved vegan food can be the main event.
Bring a jacket and an appetite.
