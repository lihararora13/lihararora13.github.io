Bologna does vegan food the way it does everything else: with serious respect for pasta. The best tables cluster in the centre around Via Mascarella and the university streets, with a couple of gems out towards Saragozza, and they cook Bolognese food first: tortellini, tagliatelle, lasagne and ragu, all of it fully plant-based, alongside organic bistros, takeaway labs and a seasonal osteria. This is a scene with real depth, from fine dining to a takeaway counter, and the vegan tortellini here is not a compromise.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with gnocchetti with cashew cream at Botanica Lab, a lasagne at Estravagario, tortellini at ZEM, a vegan lasagne at Missfagiola, a burger at Basilico Food, a carbonara nomade at Pasto Nomade, a Pizza Terra at Il Rovescio, and tortelli at AmicEarth. A note on the traps: Il Rovescio serves meat, so order the clearly labelled vegan dishes, and Basilico Food and AmicEarth are vegetarian-leaning, so confirm the vegan dishes on the day.
