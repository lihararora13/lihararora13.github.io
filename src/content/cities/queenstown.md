Queenstown's vegan scene is compact and walkable around Church Street, Shotover Street and the waterfront, with a vegan hotspot, a bakery, a burger bar and a scattering of restaurants with vegan menus. The anchor is Yonder, a vegan hotspot on Church Street, with Bespoke Kitchen and Fergburger nearby, and Vietnamese, Indian, Mexican and fine dining restaurants with vegan dishes filling out the map. The scene is small enough to cover in a weekend, and the best meals are the vegan breakfasts and the burgers.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the vegan breakfast butties at Yonder, the muesli bowls at Bespoke Kitchen, the Holier Than Thou tofu burger at Fergburger, and the vegan menu at Saigon Kingdom. A note on the traps: Queenstown is seasonal, so hours can shorten in winter and some kitchens close early; and the fine dining and Indian restaurants use dairy, so vegans should specify no dairy.
