Raleigh's vegan food is at its best when it leans Southern: chicken and waffles, hush puppies, mac and cheese, biscuits and gravy, all plant-based and all taken seriously. Downtown and Glenwood South hold most of the list, with a couple of international stops that round it out beautifully.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegetarian or vegan-friendly with strong vegan menus. Start with chicken and waffles at The Fiction Kitchen, Nashville hot chicken at Element, trashcan nachos at The Dirty V, chik'n tikka at Pure Vegan, a garden salad at Irregardless, a diner plate at The Remedy, mezze at Sitti, and a Laotian spread at Bida Manda.
