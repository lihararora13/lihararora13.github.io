Richmond packs a lot of vegan food into a small, walkable city. Carytown alone could carry a guide by itself, with a vegan bakery, a tiny vegan takeout counter and a comfort-food spot, and then Manchester, Church Hill and the West End add cheesesteaks, tacos and fast food.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegan-friendly with serious vegan menus. Start with a supreme cheesesteak at Philly Vegan, a sandwich and a soft serve at Minglewood, al pastor tacos at Taco Vegana, a breakfast burrito at Tiny Vegan, a build-your-own burger at devil's lettuce, vegan fried chicken at 821 Cafe, Thai ramen at Goatocado, and BBQ tofu bites at Yummvees. A note on the traps: Elegant Cuizines looks temporarily closed, so it does not make the list.
