Seoul rewards the hungry vegan like few cities can: centuries of Buddhist temple cooking, street snacks rebuilt without the fish sauce, and a new wave of fully vegan kitchens from Itaewon to Haebangchon. Kimchi, tteokbokki and bulgogi all get the plant treatment here, and nobody is asking you to settle for a side salad.

All eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Plant Cafe for the burger-and-cake lunch everyone recommends, Balwoo Gongyang for the quiet special-occasion temple course, and Ohsegyehyang for generous Korean comfort plates, then snack your way through Insadong and climb Namsan to ByTOFU.
