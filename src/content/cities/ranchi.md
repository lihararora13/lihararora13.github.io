Ranchi's vegetarian food is pure-veg dining with a small-city warmth: family restaurants, mall food courts and station-side kitchens, all of them happy to cook without onion and garlic if you ask. The scene leans on institutions like Cream Centre, which has served pure-veg food since 1958, and Krsna, the delivery workhorse of Main Road, with newer spots like Pearl and The Good Food bringing a fresher, prettier energy.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Ranchi's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with the pani puri tower at Cream Centre, a dosa at Krsna, spring rolls at Pearl, and a plate of litti chokha at Dhamal Litti. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
