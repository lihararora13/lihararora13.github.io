Vadodara, or Baroda to old friends, is one of Gujarat's most quietly plant-friendly cities, and this list proves it: two genuinely all-vegan spots anchor a guide that stretches from Race Course Road to Sevasi. The scene splits neatly between the modern café belt of Diwalipura and Gorwa and the classic pure-veg dining rooms of Old Padra Road and Akota.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and two of the eight are fully vegan. Start with a bowl at Greenr, a vegan bake from Oasis, a ramen at Finca, and a masala dosa at Ganga South Indian. The pure-veg kitchens here cook with ghee and paneer as a matter of course, so the one-line ask, hold the dairy, does a lot of work.
