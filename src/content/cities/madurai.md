Madurai's vegetarian scene is older than most of the city's temples are tall, and it shows in the tiffin rooms around the Meenakshi temple: malligai idlis with four chutneys, ghee podi idli, banana-leaf meals and vellai appam, served fast and cheap. There is no all-vegan restaurant in Madurai yet, so this guide collects the pure-veg institutions that vegans can eat well at, with the dairy held back on request.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan. Start at Murugan Idli Shop on West Masi Street for the city's most famous idlis, then Sabarees for a banana-leaf meal, the Modern Restaurant for heritage tiffin near the temple, and Gopu Iyengar for old-school breakfast. A note on the traps: ghee roast dosa, jigarthanda and curd rice are the signatures here, so ask for plain dosa without ghee and skip the dairy sweets.
