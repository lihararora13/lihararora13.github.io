Haridwar is one of the easiest cities in India to eat vegetarian and one of the hardest to eat strictly vegan: the whole municipality is meat-free, egg-free and alcohol-free, and the lanes around Har Ki Pauri are lined with sattvik kitchens that cook without onion or garlic. The catch is ghee, butter and dahi, which are in almost everything, so the vegan ask still matters at every counter.

All 7 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city. Chotiwala has two outlets with the same mascot after a family split in the 1990s; both are pure veg and both are listed here under their current names.
