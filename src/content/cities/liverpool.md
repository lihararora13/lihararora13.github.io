Liverpool does vegan food the way it does everything else: loudly, generously and with a proper sense of humour. The best tables cluster around Bold Street and Duke Street in the Ropewalks, with detours to the Fabric District and Mossley Hill, and they cover the full spread from seitan burgers and loaded fries to dosa, thali and a full vegan Sunday roast. This is a scene built on long-running local favourites rather than chains, and the top of the list is genuinely fun food that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food at all.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with a burger at Down the Hatch, a Veggs Royale at The Vibe, a thali at Sanskruti, the combo for two at Bundobust, a beanburger at The Egg, sticky ribz at Guac 'n' Roll, a seitan steak at Glass Onion, and dan dan noodles at Woo Tan Scran. A note on the traps: several Bold Street favourites have come and gone over the years, so check opening days before you travel, Guac 'n' Roll only opens Thursday to Sunday.
