Fukuoka is famous for pork-bone ramen and cod roe, which makes its vegan cluster all the sweeter: a fully vegan take on Hakata ramen, macrobiotic cafes, a vegan bakery scene and breakfast spots that actually open early. Most of the action sits in Tenjin, Daimyo and Yakuin, with Hakata Station covering the train-day bento.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with tonkotsu-style ramen at Yadokari, a burger at Cafe NICE, keema curry at Sonu Sonu, the lunch plate at Rota Cafe, a bento at Evah Dining, ramen at Bugoro, a taiyaki at Hakata Hiiragi, and vegan Chinese at Takamiya Hanten.
