Brisbane might be Australia's most underrated vegan city, and it barely has to try: subtropical produce, a huge Asian community, and a fiercely loyal plant-based crowd mean the best tables range from a sunny New Farm brunch to a late-night arcade bar in the Valley, with South Brisbane and West End packing half the list into a few walkable blocks. Come hungry, book El Planta for dinner, and save room for Vege Rama's Oreo cake.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026: six are fully vegan and two are vegan-friendly, which is just how Brisbane rolls. Start with a sticky duck bao at Dicki's, graze the pescado tacos at El Planta, grab a counter-service curry at Vege Rama, try the vegan omelette at Vega Cafe, work through the Origin burger at The Green Edge, the Loving wrap at Loving Hut, the plant-based schwarma at NAÏM, and the karaage tofu at Netherworld. A note on the traps: You Came Again, Brisbane's first vegan tapas bar, closed in July 2025, Grassfed's plant-based burger bar shut in September 2023, and Paper Moon in Annerley is no longer fully vegan, so none of them made the cut.
