Rio does everything with its whole chest, and its vegan scene is no exception. The best tables cluster in the Zona Sul neighbourhoods visitors actually stay in, Ipanema, Copacabana and Botafogo, with a garden-side stop by the Jardim Botânico, and they cook Brazilian food first: feijoada without the pork, moqueca built on palm heart, strogonoff, tapioca and açaí, all of it loud, generous and made from scratch. This is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than tourist traps, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food at all.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with the Korean seitan at Teva Bistrô, a croissant at Teva Deli, a burger at Refúgio Veg, the palm heart lasagna at Vegan Vegan, a pay-by-weight plate at Bardana, onion rings at Só Verde, a soya meatball plate at Prana, and the baked pumpkin at Brota. A note on the traps: Refeitório Orgânico, the old Botafogo buffet, is closed, Inari has gone omnivorous, and Lasai, for all its two Michelin stars, is not a vegan restaurant at all, so none of them make the list.
