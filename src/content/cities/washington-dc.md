Washington DC eats its vegetables with conviction. Our nine picks run from
Michelin-starred tasting menus in Shaw to a legendary soul food cafe on Georgia
Avenue, an Israeli street food icon at Union Market, and two of the best vegan
bakeries on the East Coast up in Takoma.

Come hungry and bring an omnivore. This is a city where the plant-based table
is often the best table in the room.
