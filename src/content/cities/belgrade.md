Belgrade's vegan scene clusters in Stari Grad and Dorcol, the old neighbourhoods visitors actually walk, and it is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than tourist traps. Expect falafel bowls, pad thai, vegan sushi, raw cakes, dosas and a lot of very good bread, all of it cheaper than almost anywhere else in Europe.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegetarian with strong vegan menus. Start with whatever VegANGELov is cooking, a pad thai at Rai, a burger at Zdravo Ljubavi, a falafel bowl at Radost, a samosa at Mayka, a lasagna at Veganski Kiosk, a sandwich at jazzayoga, and a burrito at BioHarmony.
