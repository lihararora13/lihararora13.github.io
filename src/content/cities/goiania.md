Goiânia punches above its weight: five of its eight picks are fully vegan, the highest all-vegan share in this region, spread across Setor Oeste, Marista, Jardim América and Setor Bueno. The scene runs from a Loving Hut branch to a vegan atelier and a vegan cantina, with Brazilian comfort food, vegan pão de queijo and tapiocas doing the heavy lifting.

The vegan pão de queijo at Fruta e Raiz, the feijoada at Loving Hut and the burgers at Be Green Vegan are the three plates to plan around. Bendita Feijuca was checked and not used: it serves torresmo and costela, so it is a meat restaurant. Every pick below was verified open in November 2026.
