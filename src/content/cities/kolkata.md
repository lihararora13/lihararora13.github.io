Kolkata does not have a big dedicated vegan scene, and this list is honest about that: it leans on the city's excellent vegetarian and vegan-friendly kitchens, where the food was mostly plant-based already. Burmese cooking, Bengali vegetarian thalis, South Indian plates and modern cafe bowls do the heavy lifting, and every pick here has a real vegan menu or clearly veganisable dishes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Kolkata's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with tea leaf salad at Burma Burma, a mezze platter at The Daily, an Asian bowl at The Flamingo Bowl, and a veg thali at 6 Ballygunge Place. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
