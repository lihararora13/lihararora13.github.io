Montevideo is a beef town learning to love plants, and the vegans here are doing it the Uruguayan way: chivito de seitan, milanesa, empanadas and burgers, all without the meat. The best tables cluster in Centro, Cordon and Pocitos, with a detour to Punta Carretas and Ciudad Vieja, and they range from fast-food joints slinging Beyond Burgers to a fine, relaxed dining room where you eat at low tables on wooden floors. This is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than tourist traps, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food at all.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegetarian or mostly vegan with serious vegan menus. Start with a Beyond Burger at Guacamole, a vegan burger at Vrote, lentil empanadas at Namaste, a chivito de seitan at La Temeraria, a buffet plate at Sui Yuan, a pay-by-weight plate at Bosque Bambu, a raw bowl at Mercado Verde, and lentil tacos at Espacio Samsara.
