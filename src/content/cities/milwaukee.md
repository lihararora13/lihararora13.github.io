Milwaukee surprises everyone who expects only brats and cheese curds. The vegan scene runs from the Lower East Side to Bay View, the Third Ward and West Allis, and it covers the whole spread: fast-food comfort, crepes, brewery pizza, community-cafe cooking and one very good fancy night out.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegetarian or vegan-friendly with strong vegan menus. Start with a patty melt at Twisted Plants, a pizza and a pale ale at Dead Bird, a crepe at Beerline, whatever the day's menu is at Tricklebee, a flatbread at Temple Goddess, small plates at Odd Duck, momos at Dandan, and a diner breakfast at Comet Cafe. A note on the traps: the Twisted Plants location in Cudahy closed in 2025, so head to the West Allis or Brady Street spots instead.
