San Miguel de Allende has one of the most exciting plant-based scenes in central Mexico: a fully vegan taqueria, a vegetarian institution on Calle Aldama, a vegan bistro on Calle Correo, and a set of upscale restaurants with dedicated vegetarian tasting menus. It is a colonial city where vegan dining is taken seriously.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Start with the seitan al pastor tacos at Don Taco Tequila, the Thai green curry at Nirvana, the vegan pozole at KI'BOK, and the vegan chilaquiles at Néctar. A note on the traps: Nirvana is lacto-ovo vegetarian, not fully vegan, though many dishes can be adapted. Club Mexicana, which some guides list for San Miguel, is a London restaurant, not a San Miguel one, so it is not listed here.
