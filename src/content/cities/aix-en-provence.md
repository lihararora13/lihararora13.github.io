Aix has one of the strongest vegan scenes in Provence, concentrated in the Vieil Aix around Rue Aumône Vieille, Rue Lieutaud and Rue Lisse des Cordeliers. Five of the eight picks are fully vegan, including a 100 percent plant-based restaurant, a vegan burger bar and a 100 percent plant-based bakery, and the rest are serious vegan-friendly kitchens.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start at La Plume Végétale on Rue Aumône Vieille, La Cabane en Ville on Rue Lieutaud and La Vie Dirty Vegan Burger on Rue Lisse des Cordeliers. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegan restaurant that closed in 2025 is not on this list, and a cafe that had moved within the Vieil Aix is listed at its current address.
