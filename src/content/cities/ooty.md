Ooty in winter is quiet, misty and very much open, and its restaurants know how to feed a cold traveller. The best eating clusters around Charing Cross and Commercial Road: wood-fired pizza at Nahar's Sidewalk Cafe, honest veg thalis at Pankaj Bhojanalaya, and tiffin at A2B, with fine dining up the hill at Earl's Secret and the Periodic Table. Badaga cuisine, the food of the Nilgiri hills, shows up at Nilgiri Express with potato and bean curries you won't find on the plains.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan. Winter hours hold for the Charing Cross spots, which stay open through the season. Start with a Nilgiri Special pizza at Nahar's, a Badaga meal at Nilgiri Express, and a fine-dining dinner at the Periodic Table. A note on the traps: hill-station prices run about a quarter above the plains, and the hotel restaurants lean on cheese and cream, so ask for the vegan version.
