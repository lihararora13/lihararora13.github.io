Kuala Lumpur is a feast that never really stops, and its vegan scene has happily joined the party: nasi lemak without the anchovies, laksa with all the fire, satay made from lion's mane mushrooms, and not one but three fully vegan Japanese kitchens doing sushi, ramen and udon properly. From a pink heritage shophouse in Chinatown to the leafy streets of Sri Hartamas, this is a city where plant-based eating means eating more, not less.

All eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with The Hungry Tapir for the big Chinatown lunch everyone talks about, Sala for a burrito the size of your forearm, and Kusa for sushi night in Bukit Bintang, then noodle your way through Damansara Heights and TTDI.
