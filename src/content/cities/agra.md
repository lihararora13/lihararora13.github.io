Agra is a city people visit for one building, and then discover they need to eat for three days. The good news is that the Taj Ganj lanes outside the South Gate are thick with cafes and vegetarian restaurants, and the tourist economy means vegan requests are understood rather than stared at. The rooftop restaurants have the views, the lane cafes have the prices, and Fatehabad Road has the big vegetarian dining rooms.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, checked against Google Maps listings on 22 October 2026 by a local guide. None is all-vegan, so ask for no ghee, no dahi and no cheese. Start with the rooftop thali at Shankara Vegis, the breakfast at Joney's Place, and the chhole bhature at Bikanervala, and save the sunset for the Taj itself.
