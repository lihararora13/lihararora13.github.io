Tel Aviv takes vegan food more seriously than almost anywhere on earth, and it shows in every corner of this list. This is a city where one in ten residents eats plant-based, where the Carmel and Levinsky markets overflow with eggplants, peppers and tahini, and where a vegan tasting menu can be the hardest table in town.

All eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Opa for a special night out, Meshek Barzilay for a leafy Neve Tzedek brunch, and Anastasia for the all-day cafe classic, then graze your way from vegan sushi to a cashew-mozzarella pizza to a burger near Rabin Square. A note on the traps: old favourites Zakaim, Dosa Bar and 416 have all closed, so this list sticks to rooms that are actually serving right now.
