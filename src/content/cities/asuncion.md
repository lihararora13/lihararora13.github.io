Asunción's vegan scene is small and spread between the historic centre and the Herrera district, and this list is candid about its limits: three fully vegan picks lead it, an eco cafe in Herrera, a pink fast-food counter in the centre and a vegan kitchen on Cerro Corá, and the rest are vegetarian and ramen tables with vegan options. Six of the eight pins are neighbourhood-level approximate, as addresses in the city are often street-only.

The falafel sandwich at Eco Tienda Café and the pink sliders at CATSA are the two stops to build around. The big trap was Pink Cow on Alas Paraguayas, which still tops glowing guides but is closed: its HappyCow page is marked CLOSED with a last update of 5 October 2026, and place data agrees it is temporarily closed. Combitos HuiShin and Tembi'Uh have no findable addresses and are not listed, and Loving Hut Asunción has no address either. Oli's Food was not used as it sits in Fernando de la Mora, a neighbouring city, not Asunción.
