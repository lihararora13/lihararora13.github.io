Montreal takes its food seriously, and the plant-based kitchens here are no exception. This is a city where vegan sushi was pioneered before most people knew vegan sushi was a thing, where Indian curries and Greek gyros went fully plant-based without losing a drop of swagger, and where brunch in Verdun might just be the best meal of your trip.

All eight picks are verified open as of October 2026. The game plan: vegan butter chicken and thalis in Mile End, Greek comfort food in the West Island, torched jackfruit maki on Saint-Denis, ramen and dumplings on Duluth, a vegan pizza crawl on the Plateau, chick'n waffles in Verdun, nachos and hemp burgers in Milton-Parc, and a flower-filled sushi room downtown. Vive les plantes, as they say around here.
