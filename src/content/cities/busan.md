Busan gets overshadowed by Seoul, which is a shame, because the food here is some of Korea's best-value vegan eating: multi-course temple meals for the price of a sandwich, home cooking from a grandmother's kitchen, and a Michelin-listed plant-based dining room overlooking the sea. The scene is spread out, so pair each meal with the neighbourhood you are already visiting.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and one with a dedicated vegan menu. Start with the lotus-leaf rice set at Vegenarang, fried chicken at Soban Vegan, the tasting-style dinner at ARP, a bistro plate at HOME Bistro, soup and dumplings at Pyeonhan Jipbap, a pastry at April and May 45, a vegan plate at Tae Tae Tae, and a bansang set at Obuthan.
