Noida has quietly built one of the NCR's most practical vegan scenes: a genuine all-vegan kitchen, an organic bakery whose cakes are 100% plant-based, and a solid bench of South Indian and multi-cuisine restaurants where dosa and idli do the heavy lifting. It is less showy than Gurugram and more useful day to day.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. One is fully vegan, the desi plant-based kitchen Planttscape, and Poornatva's bakery range is 100% vegan even though the cafe also serves non-veg, so order from the vegan menu there. The rest are vegan-friendly: ask for no ghee and no dahi, and the South Indian kitchens will take care of you. Start with the broccoli kulcha at Planttscape, the BBQ tofu pizza at Poornatva, and a masala dosa at Dasaprakash.
