Sydney might be the prettiest city in the world to eat vegan in. This is a harbour city where the plant-based scene ranges from a two-hatted fine-dining degustation to a $7 chunky meat pie on Bondi Beach, and everything in between still feels like a sunny afternoon. No compromises, no sad side salads, just the full flavour dial turned up.

All six picks are verified open as of October 2026. The game plan: save up for the six-course tasting at Yellow, share a banquet at Yulli's in Surry Hills, slurp laksa on King Street in Newtown, book the pumpkin curry in bread bowls at Little Turtle, do a pizza night at Gigi's, and finish with a Funky Chunky pie by the ocean. Sydney, you absolute beauty.
