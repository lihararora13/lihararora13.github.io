Hsinchu's vegan scene splits between Hsinchu City and Zhubei, the county seat a short ride away, and the two halves are worth treating as one guide. Zhubei holds two MICHELIN Bib Gourmand vegetarian restaurants and two fully plant-based cafes, while Hsinchu City holds a plant-based bistro in a Japanese-era police dormitory, a vegan pasta and pizza kitchen, and a Michelin-selected vegan gua bao stall. Taiwan's vegetarian labels matter here: a place marked vegetarian can include egg and dairy, so the all-vegan picks below are the ones whose own descriptions say no animal products.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start with the small plates at Hsinchuku, the vegan pasta at Miracle Green House, the gua bao at Very Good Vegan Gua Bao, and the set menu at Monsoon. A note on the traps: Monsoon is booking only and opens irregularly, so confirm when you book; Tian Tu Shan, Pulau Redang and Douhe could not be confirmed open with 2025 or 2026 evidence and are left out; and Ya Su Zhai moved from Jinshan Street to Guangfu Road, so older guides send people to the wrong street.
