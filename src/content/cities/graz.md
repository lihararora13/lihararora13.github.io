Graz eats vegan in the old town and the university quarter, where Austria's longest plant-based tradition runs on a riverside vegan cafe, a 1976-founded buffet house that went fully vegan in 2021, a plant-based fine-dining room and a vegan burger chain. The picks cluster around the Hauptplatz and the Mur, walkable from the Hauptbahnhof, and six of the eight are fully vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start with the soy schnitzel at Cafe Erde, the buffet at Ginko, the bowls at Greenhouse, and the burgers at Swing Kitchen. A note on the traps: Gerüchteküche is a surprise tasting menu of at least six courses for around one hundred euros, Monday to Friday evenings only, so book ahead and bring a budget; Ginko closes on Sundays, and its sister cafe Greenhouse is the Sunday-open stop; and the KF erde canteen runs weekday lunch hours only.
