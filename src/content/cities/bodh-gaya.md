Bodh Gaya is one of the easiest towns in India for a vegetarian to eat in, because the whole town eats vegetarian by default. The cafes around the Mahabodhi Temple have fed monks and backpackers for decades on momos, thukpa, dosas and big breakfasts, and the GoVeg Project now serves a proper vegan thali for the pilgrims who want one. Note the rhythm of the town: several cafes, including Tibet Om and Be Happy, run on the October-to-March pilgrimage season, so winter is the best time to come.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and one of the eight, the GoVeg Project, is fully vegan. Start with steamed momos and thukpa at Tibet Om Cafe, a masala dosa at Nirvana, and the vegan thali at GoVeg Project. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
