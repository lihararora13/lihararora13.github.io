Buffalo does vegan food the way it does wings: bold, saucy and unpretentious. The scene is spread across the city's revival neighborhoods, the Old First Ward, Allentown, Elmwood Village and the West Side, and it leans on the dishes Buffalo already loves, reworked without animal products. There are two fully vegan anchors, Sunshine Vegan Eats on Jefferson Avenue and Sevens in the Old First Ward, and a strong bench of vegan-friendly restaurants where the vegan options are labelled and taken seriously, from Ethiopian stews to Detroit-style pizza and Indian curries.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegan-friendly. Start with the shrimp po'boy at Sunshine Vegan Eats, the vegan Reuben at Sevens, the vegan chik'n wings at Betty's, the vegan platter at Abyssinia, a vegan pizza at Hydraulic Hearth, a vegan cheese pizza at Extra Extra, a tofu curry at India Gate, and a tempeh sandwich at BreadHive. A note on the traps: Caffe at Amy's, listed in some guides as a vegetarian cafe, was reported in July 2025 to serve meat with limited vegan options, so it is not in this guide; and Sunshine Vegan Eats is opening a second location at 1412 Fillmore, so check which address you are heading to.
