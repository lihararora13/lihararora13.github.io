Cape Town may be braai country, but its plant-based crew is small, mighty and very tasty: a New York-style vegan deli slinging giant donuts, a tiny organic cafe growing its own sprouts by the sea, and a butcherie where every cut is made from plants. Most of the action hugs the City Bowl, Green Point and the Atlantic Seaboard, so you can graze from breakfast to beach sunset without crossing town.

All seven picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Grumpy & Runt for donuts and sandos, Sunshine for the famous charcoal burger, and Romeo and Vero for vegan biltong to take home, then work your way to Camps Bay for a seaside bobotie.
