San Sebastián is one of the great food cities of the world, and its vegan scene is small but real: one fully vegan restaurant at the top, a vegetarian kitchen in Antiguo, a taberna with vegan pintxos in the old town and a vegetarian tasting menu up on Ulía. The pintxos bars of the Parte Vieja are not vegan-friendly by default, so this list is the one to trust.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at Mapa Verde on Trueba Kalea, Orburu in Antiguo and Garraxi Taberna on Tejería. The traps were real here: Landare, the city's first vegan restaurant, has closed, as has Fresh Green, and Km.0 was reported closed in some directories, so none of them appears on this list. Maiatza has renamed as Vincent's Cafe, and it is listed under its current name.
