Fresno's vegan scene splits between the Galleria food hall downtown, where two all-vegan counters share one building, and the north side around Palm and Bullard, where the burger and wrap shops cluster. The Tower District adds the older vegan names. It is a comfort-food scene first: burgers, Philly cheesesteaks, animal-style fries, and loaded waffle fries, with raw and bowl options for the lighter days.

Start with the Campfire Burger and animal-style fries at Plant Slayer, the raw tacos at Raw Fresno, and a burger with loaded waffle fries at Planet Vegan, then work through Rappit Up, the bowls at Viva Be Bowld, and the tacos from La Jacka. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: La Jacka is a mobile taco truck, so its pin is the truck's listed base rather than a fixed dining room; the Fresno Loving Hut's currency is uncertain, since its freshest sources are old, so confirm hours before a special trip; Tower Blendz was researched and held as a reserve.
