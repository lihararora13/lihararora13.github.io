Kobe built its name on beef, but its vegan scene is small and genuine once you know where to look: a fully vegan and gluten-free café in Motomachi, a smoothie-bowl café on Kaigandori, a long-running vegetarian café in Sannomiya, a farmhouse café out in Nishi Ward, and a farm-to-table grocer on the slope up to Kitano. Most of the best tables sit within a fifteen-minute walk of Sannomiya and Motomachi stations, and the food leans Japanese first, quiche sets and brown-rice plates, curry, ramen-adjacent Thai dishes and seasonal vegetables, with the odd burrito and smoothie bowl.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with the vegan quiche set at Thallo, the Mermaid Lagoon bowl at Greenery, the curry with brown rice at Modernark, the Kiu Plate out in Nishi Ward, the vegan mixed plate at Yidaki, the salad bar at Days Kitchen, the pad thai at RISE, and the daily vegan meal at Farmstand. A note on the traps: Thallo opens weekends only and wants a lunch reservation, Kiu relocated from Kitanocho to Iwaokacho in Nishi Ward so use the new address, and in a city where dashi hides in miso soup and simmered vegetables, always confirm a dish is vegan rather than assuming it.
