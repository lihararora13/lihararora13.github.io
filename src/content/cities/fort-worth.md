Fort Worth's vegan scene is smaller than Dallas's and better for it: two fully vegan restaurants anchor it, Belenty's Love for Mexican and Spiral Diner for American comfort food, both in the Near Southside and Blue Bonnet Circle, and a vegan juice bar, a vegan taco trailer, and a Mediterranean buffet fill out the rest. It is a scene that cooks Texas food first and happens to be vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly. Start with the birria tacos at Belenty's Love, the Spiral Burger at Spiral Diner, the loaded nachos at Juice Junkies, the street tacos at The Green Tongue, the falafel at Dimassi's, a breakfast plate at Seven Mile Cafe, a vegan scoop at Melt, and a pastry at Black Coffee.
