Stockholm spreads itself across islands, bridges and impossibly pretty waterfront, and its vegan scene has spread right along with it: all-you-can-eat buffets on a clifftop in Sodermalm, smoothie bowls in Vasastan, vegan semla and meatballs inside the city cultural house, and burgers in the cobbled Old Town. Fika, Sweden's sacred coffee-and-cake pause, turns out to be even better when everything on the table is plant-based.

Seven picks are fully vegan and one is happily vegan-friendly, all verified open as of October 2026. Start with Hermans for the big buffet and the view, Mahalo for a colourful brunch, and Kafe Plattan for fika in the heart of the city, then wander on to Delivore, Fern & Fika and the Gamla Stan trio.
