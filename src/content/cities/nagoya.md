Nagoya is Japan's city of miso katsu and hitsumabushi, so its vegan restaurants are small, dedicated and easy to miss if you do not plan ahead. The payoff is food made by people who care deeply: organic vegetables from the restaurant's own farm, vegan katsu bowls, grain cuisine and izakaya plates that never lean on dashi.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with the tasting-style dinner at ORIBIO, a curry at Vegetiger, the set lunch at Colline de Tara, a rice bowl at Grains, a bento at Vegefirst, a macrobiotic plate at Pupu Kitchen, cafe dishes at Oteate, and ramen or a burger at Loving Hut.
