Chongqing is a hotpot city, and its vegan scene is built on Buddhist temple kitchens and all-you-can-eat vegan buffets in Yuzhong and Jiangbei. The word to use is chun su, purely plant-based, and the best value in the city is a 27-yuan vegan buffet by the Liberation Monument.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 6 of them fully vegan. Su Yi Xiang was previously called Su Man Xiang; it is listed here under its current name. All eight picks are vegan or vegetarian, with five fully vegan.
