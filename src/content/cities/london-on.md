London, Ontario has a compact vegan scene centred on Wortley Village and Dundas Street: a 100% plant-based kitchen, a vegetarian Chinese restaurant, a downtown salad bar, and a Mexican spot with a named vegan burrito. It is a university city where the best tables are within a short walk of each other.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 3 of them fully vegan. Start with the Full English brunch at Plant Matter Kitchen, the Lo Han Chai chow mein at Zen Gardens, the Green Bowl at The Green Window, and the vegan burrito at Under the Volcano. A note on the traps: The Tea Lounge and The Green Earth Cafe may no longer be fully vegan, according to local corrections, so they are not listed. Under the Volcano's vegan fajitas and enchiladas still mention dairy ingredients in their descriptions, so order the vegan burrito specifically.
