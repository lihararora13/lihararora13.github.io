Dharamshala's cafe scene is unlike any other hill town in India. Tibetan bakeries sit next to Italian kitchens, a 70-year-old family bakery shares the hillside with a vegan cafe, and the whole of McLeod Ganj runs on momos, thukpa, sourdough and hand-beaten cappuccino. It is the most cafe-dense town in this whole North push, and the vegan eating is the easiest of the lot.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. One is fully plant-based, Dharma Cafe on Temple Road, and the rest are vegan-friendly, with strong vegan and gluten-free benches. Start with the plant-based burger at Dharma Cafe, the vegan banana bread at Crepe Pancake Hut, and the thukpa at Tibet Kitchen, and wear good shoes: the main market, Bhagsu and Dharamkot are three different areas connected by steep uphill roads.
