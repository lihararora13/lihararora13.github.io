Fortaleza's plant-based scene is small but genuine, spread between the historic centre, the Iracema cultural quarter and the Aldeota and Meireles beachfront districts. The two fully vegan anchors are a kombi-turned-kitchen and a dedicated vegan restaurant in Benfica, and the rest of the list is vegetarian and health-food spots where vegan dishes are a real part of the menu rather than an afterthought.

The feijoada and lasanha at Malaguetta and the coxinha from the Rango Verde kombi are the two plates to plan around, with Annapurna's cultural-centre kitchen and Sooveggie's falafel as the backups. GoVegan Cozinha Vegana on R. Padre Valdevino was checked and dropped: it is temporarily closed. Seven picks are verified open in this round.
