Jeju is not a city scene, it is an island scene, and the vegan tables are spread across it: Aewol and Hallim on the west coast, Nohyeong-dong in Jeju City, Gujwa-eup and Jochon-eup on the east. That spread is the charm and the catch. You will not walk between these picks, you will drive, and the best days pair one or two of them with a beach or a trail. The cooking is split between Korean temple food and home-style bibimbap, Western cafe food, burgers, pasta and sandwiches, and a couple of destination rooms, the five-course set menu at Jag Eunbueok and the rooftop brunch at Jeju 901, that reward planning ahead.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegan-friendly. Start with the rice noodle bowls at Lan's Kitchen, an open sandwich at Avec, a burger at And Yu Cafe, the 901 Vegan Brunch at Jeju 901, the focaccia at Five Seventh, the five-course set at Jag Eunbueok, the bibimbap at Puleunsolmalg Eunhyang, and a curry rice at Tom Tom Curry. A note on the traps: several of these rooms keep short or shifting hours, Lan's Kitchen closes Wednesdays, Avec closes Wednesdays, Jeju 901 is Wednesday to Sunday only, Five Seventh's hours disagree across sources, and Jag Eunbueok needs a reservation at least a day ahead, so check Instagram or call before a special trip. Two pins are district-level rather than street-level, Jag Eunbueok in Seonheul-ri and Puleunsolmalg Eunhyang in Nohyeong-dong, because Korean rural addresses defeat the geocoder. And a warning on the closures: Santosha, the much-praised vegan restaurant in Andeok, closed in October 2025, LouLou Vegan closed in March 2025, and CHEESYLAZY is listed as temporarily closed with its shopfront reported turned into a zero-waste store, so do not trust older guides that still list them.
