Guatemala City's vegan scene is concentrated in Zona 14, Zona 4 and Zona 10, with four fully vegan anchors: an all-raw kitchen, a fully vegan restaurant inside a Zona 14 market, a vegan kitchen on the Zona 4 pedestrian street, and a vegetarian institution in Zona 15. The cooking leans Mexican and Guatemalan, vegan tacos al pastor, churrasco and burritos, with cashew-cream pasta and medovik on the side.

The falafel bowl at Plantiful, the vegan tacos al pastor at Moonrise and the cashew-cream fettuccini at Lavka are the three stops to build around. Two traps were caught: Artesano's storefront closed in September 2025 and it is delivery-only now, and Calendula Cafe was reported possibly closed in July 2024, so neither is listed. Lavka's address is disputed, with place data saying 4 Avenida 12-29 and HappyCow saying 22-29; the HappyCow address is used.
