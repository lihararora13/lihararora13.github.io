Boulder lost a couple of plant-based landmarks in late 2024, but the scene bounced back the way Boulder always does: with more colour, more farmers market produce, and more restaurants that happen to be vegan rather than making a fuss about it. The best tables cluster around Pearl Street and the east side, and they cook the way the city eats: fresh, outdoorsy, and generous with the vegetables.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and the rest with serious vegan menus. Start with a bowl of pho at Lotus Moon, a jackfruit enchilada at Leaf, a Glow Bowl at Flower Child, a grain bowl at Zeal, a burger at Just BE Kitchen, a sandwich at Naked Lunch, the Monday vegan buffet at Tsing Tao, and a bowl of spicy miso ramen at Jing Ramen.
