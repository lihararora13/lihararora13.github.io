Bristol has been one of Britain's great vegan cities for years, and it shows: the scene is independent, creative and proudly weird, clustered around Stokes Croft, Cheltenham Road and Gloucester Road with detours to Old Market and Clifton. Every single pick on this list is fully vegan, from Persian mezze bars and Portuguese small plates to Caribbean curries, Chinese takeaway and American junk food, and the city does all of it without a whiff of compromise.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the mezze at Koocha, a Francesinha at Plantuguese, a burger and shake at Oowee, a bean burger at Cafe Kino, curry and rice and beans at Fi Real, a thali at Vegan India, mock-meat noodles at Cheungs, and a slow brunch at East Village Cafe. A note on the traps: Bristol's vegan landscape changes fast, with old favourites closing and new ones popping up, so these eight are the ones with fresh reviews and open doors right now.
