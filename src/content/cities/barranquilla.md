Barranquilla has the smallest plant-based scene of Colombia's Caribbean cities, and this list is honest about that: two fully vegan kitchens, a Govinda's, an Indian vegetarian restaurant, a vegetarian garden restaurant and a veggie market, spread between El Prado, Alto Prado and the centre. The cooking leans Caribbean Colombian, sancocho, arepas and falafel in criollo stew, and the scene is best approached as a handful of good tables rather than a district.

The vegan garden plates at Vegan Garden and the sancocho vegetariano at El Huerto are the two stops to build around. Govinda's address was confirmed through its HappyCow review page after a city guide warned that Barranquilla addresses had changed. Fine-dining restaurants Manuel and Storia D'Amore were not used: they handle diet requests but are not vegan venues. Three pins are approximate, as noted below.
