Cambridge eats vegan with a student city's energy: a down-to-earth vegan bistro, a burger joint, a tasting-menu restaurant and a string of cafes, all within a short walk of each other between the station, the Grafton Centre and Regent Street. The scene is compact, so a weekend covers it, and the quality is high, with Stem & Glory having paved the way before its Station Road site closed.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and the rest vegan-friendly. Start with the Big Breakfast at Thrive, the Crunch burger at Doppleganger, a loaded burger at Vegan Vice, the ten-course tasting menu at Vanderlyle, the vegan pancakes at The Sage, patatas bravas at Pinchito, a three-mushroom pho at Pho, and brunch at the Urban Butterfly. A note on the traps: the Rainbow Cafe, the city's long-standing vegan institution, closed for good, Stem & Glory's Station Road site is closed, and The Tipsy Vegan on Quayside is permanently closed, so use the addresses on this page, not the ones in older guides.
