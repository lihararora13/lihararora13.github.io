Southampton's vegan scene runs from the High Street to Portswood, with two fully vegan anchors at the top: a 100 percent vegan cafe on Hanover Buildings and a fully vegan bistro-bar on the High Street. Around them are strong vegetarian Indian kitchens, a pancake house and a vegan Thai restaurant, all easy to reach on foot or by bus.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. Start at Cafe Thrive, Késarum on the High Street and Padharo on East Street. The traps were minor: one much-listed cafe that closed last year is not on this list, and a Thai restaurant that had moved is listed at its current address.
