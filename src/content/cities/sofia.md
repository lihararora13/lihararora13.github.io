Sofia does vegan food cheaply and without ceremony: a weekday set lunch of soup and a main costs six to seven euros, and a simple dinner runs fifteen to twenty euros a head before drinks. The fully vegan places cluster in the centre around Rakovski Street and the Women's Market, and the scene leans on Bulgarian traditions that were vegan all along: banitsa without egg or cheese, kyopolou and lyutenitsa, lenten sarmi and shopska without cheese. Loving Hut is the cheapest vegan lunch in the centre, Furna's vegan banitsa sells out by 10am, and Vita Rama is the fully vegan restaurant with a shop and a bakery.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly. Start with the vegan banitsa at Furna before 10am, a weekday lunch at Loving Hut, the daily menu at Vita Rama, and a raw cake at Raw & More. A note on the traps: Edgy Veggy, the vegan fast-food spot on William Gladstone, is closed, so do not follow older guides to it; Soul Kitchen in Lozenets has also closed and is looking for a new location; and Sun Moon's vegan banitsa and dips are the vegan orders there, since the bakery is vegetarian rather than fully vegan.
