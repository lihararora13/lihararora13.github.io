Bordeaux is famous for wine, and its vegan scene is quietly catching up with the same care: small, independent kitchens in Saint-Michel, Sainte-Croix and the Bastide, cooking Indonesian curries, French bistro plates, Italian focaccia and plant-based pastries with local, seasonal ingredients. It is not a big scene, but it is a thoughtful one, and the best tables feel like neighbourhood secrets.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with the Chick'n Nasi Goreng at Monkey Mood, the seasonal menu at Dis Leur, a croissant at Land & Monkeys, a beetroot gravlax plate at Souquo, focaccia at Matsa, a street-food bowl at Maison Bambou, a seasonal plate at Mamacam, and a banoffee tart at Michel MaBelle. Bring an appetite and a wine glass.
