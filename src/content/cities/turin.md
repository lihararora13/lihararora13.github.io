Turin does vegan food with the seriousness of a city that invented slow food. The best tables cluster in the Quadrilatero Romano and the centre, with a couple of gems further out in Cit Turin and San Salvario, and they cook Piedmontese food first: agnolotti del plin, bagna cauda, seitan roasts and gianduja desserts, all of it fully plant-based, alongside rainbow burgers and a pastry shop that could convert a sceptic. This is one of the strongest all-vegan line-ups in Italy, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all eight of them fully vegan. Start with a pistachio croissant at efrem, the chocolate cream dessert at Antonio Chiodi Latini, agnolotti del plin at Mezzaluna, a risotto at L'Orto Bistrot, seitan roast at L'Orto Gia Salsamentario, a carbonara at Idem Con Patate, a lasagne at La Gastronomia Veg, and a Cherry Bomb at Flower Burger. A note on the traps: Soul Kitchen, which appears in older Turin guides, closed on 31 December, and efrem relocated from Corso Vittorio Emanuele II to Via Almese, so use the addresses on this page, not the ones in older guides.
