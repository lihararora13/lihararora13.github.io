Malacca, officially Melaka since 2017, is a UNESCO-listed port town where the vegan scene runs on Chinese Buddhist vegetarian cooking, and it runs deep: this guide lists eight verified-open picks, and not one of them is fully vegan, which is the honest shape of the scene. What you get instead is a remarkable density of vegetarian rooms where vegans eat very well, provided you say vegan when you order and ask about egg and dairy. The cooking is Malaysian-Chinese at heart, mock meats, herbal soups, laksa, nasi lemak, buffets and economic rice, with one Western outlier, the vegan pizza at Kneading Peace, and one new Hainanese cafe by Jonker Street.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all eight vegan-friendly. Start with the monkey head mushroom satay at Botanist Cafe, the plum roast duck at Man Yuan Fang, the vegan laksa at Shui Xian Su Shi Yuan, a vegan pizza at Kneading Peace, the buffet at Anthony Vegetarian, the veggie bak kut teh at Lutan 21, the sweet sour mushroom rice at Mei Lin, and the nasi lemak at Greenbite Veggie Cafe. A note on the traps: Wednesday is the closing day for half this list, Anthony, Lutan 21, Mei Lin and Greenbite all close Wednesdays, and Botanist closes Tuesdays while Man Yuan Fang closes Monday and Tuesday, so plan the week, not just the day. Shui Xian's hours are unreliable, check before you go. And a note on the names: Botanist Cafe was Veggie Planet until 2018, Lutan 21 is a separate affiliated restaurant that older guides also describe as previously named Veggie Planet, and the city itself is Melaka on every sign, Malacca in most English guides.
