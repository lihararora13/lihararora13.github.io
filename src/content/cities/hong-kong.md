Hong Kong moves fast and eats faster, and its plant-based scene keeps right up: sourdough wraps in Central lanes, retro diner burgers up a Stanley Street lift, Thai curries in old Kowloon City, and dosas that have been flipping since 1981. Dim sum city has plenty for the meat-free crowd once you know the addresses.

All eight picks are verified open as of October 2026: five are fully vegan, one is almost entirely vegan with two optional add-ons, and two are long-running vegetarian kitchens with deep vegan menus, labelled honestly below. Start with TREEHOUSE for the wrap everyone queues for, Root Vegan for the special-but-cosy dinner, and Veggie4Love for pure diner joy, then MTR-hop out to Sham Shui Po, Wan Chai and Sai Kung.
