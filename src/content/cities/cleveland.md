Cleveland is easier to eat your way through than most people expect: the city has fully plant-based restaurants on the west side, the east side, and a few neighbourhoods in between, and its pierogi culture means even old-school bars keep a vegan dumpling on the menu. The food leans comfort, gyros, Philly cheesesteaks, mac and cheese, and bowls, with prices that still feel like the Midwest.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegan-friendly with serious vegan menus. Start with a seitan gyro at Cleveland Vegan, vegan krab fries at The Vegan Club, Bangkok tacos at Foodhisattva, a vegan Philly at Juicy Vegan, a Bravocado at Beet Jar, a veggie burger at Tommy's, a vegan special at Rowley Inn, and a vegan spicy chicken sandwich at Earth Bistro Cafe. A note on the traps: Squash the Beef's address could not be confirmed, so it does not make the list.
