Manchester does vegan food the way it does everything else: loud, proud, and mostly in the Northern Quarter. The best tables cluster within a few minutes' walk of Piccadilly Gardens, around High Street, Tib Street and Turner Street, with a second pocket down Wilmslow Road in Withington, and they cook everything from sourdough pizza and handmade pasta to rice-and-three curries, Pan-Asian mock meats and proper vegan ice cream, all of it plant-based. This is a scene of fiercely loved independents rather than chains, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who came for the music and stayed for the plants.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan. Start with a black truffle pizza at Purezza, truffle ravioli at Soots, a Korean tofu burger at Wholesome Junkies, a rice and three at Little Aladdin, popcorn tofu at Lotus, okra fries at Bundobust, the daily vegan lunch at Eighth Day, and a sundae at Ice Shack. A note on the traps: The Allotment, the city's most famous vegan restaurant, closed in summer 2026 and its Cathedral Gates site is becoming an Irish pub, despite still topping several guides; Vertigo's Cross Street and First Street sites are both listed permanently closed; Herbivorous, V-Rev, Vegan Shack and Desert Island Dumplings have all gone too. Greens in Didsbury is still open and verified, held as a reserve for this round.
