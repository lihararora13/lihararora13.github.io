Dunedin, or Otepoti, is a small student town on the south-east coast of the South Island, and its vegan scene is small but real: two fully vegan rooms and six vegan-friendly ones, almost all of them within a short walk of the Octagon in the city centre. The centre is the whole scene. Let Them Eat Vegan and Burger Plant are the two all-vegan anchors, Potpourri has been feeding vegetarians from Stuart Street since the 1970s, Yours runs on a pay-as-you-like cooperative model, Woof! is the evening bar, Dosa etc covers South Indian, Beam Me Up Bagels covers breakfast, and Careys Bay Historic Hotel is the one reason to drive out to the harbour. Everything except Careys Bay sits within a few blocks of the Octagon, so the guide works as a walking crawl.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with a loaded burrito at Let Them Eat Vegan, a That Fungi burger at Burger Plant, the sourdough and cabinet food at Yours, a Vegan Big Breakfast at Potpourri, a vegan dahi puri at Woof!, a masala dosa at Dosa etc, a vegan bagel at Beam Me Up Bagels, and the battered banana blossom fish and chips at Careys Bay. A note on the traps: most of the centre is daytime only, with Burger Plant the only fully vegan room open late and Woof! evenings only from Wednesday to Sunday; Yours is pay as you like and its hours disagree across sources, so check before you go; Dosa etc added meat to its menu in July 2024, so it is veg-options now rather than vegetarian; and Careys Bay has no dedicated vegan fryer, so strict vegans should favour the soup, jackfruit roti, curry and desserts over the fried dishes. One pin is neighbourhood-level rather than street-level, Woof! at the Octagon and Moray Place corner, as geocoding matched its street number to the wrong end of Stuart Street.
