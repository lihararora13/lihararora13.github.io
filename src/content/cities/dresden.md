Dresden does vegan food in one neighbourhood, and it does it thoroughly. Cross the Augustus Bridge into the Neustadt and nearly everything worth eating plant-based sits within a few streets of each other: old-school vegan German cooking, East Asian small plates, a vegan doner shop, a vegan bakery, and a fine dining room that labels its vegan dishes with pride. The scene is compact enough to walk in an afternoon and varied enough that no two meals repeat each other, and five of the eight picks are fully vegan, so ordering is blissfully simple at most tables.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the vegan schnitzel at Steffenhagen, the Green Tofu Stick at Vegan House, a seitan burger at Falscher Hase, a build-your-own seitan kebab at Der Dicke Schmidt, a slice of cake at V-Cake Cafe, a lunch bowl at SPROUT FOOD, the fried asparagus with vegan hollandaise at Glacis No.28, and a seasonal vegan main at Scheunecafe. A note on the traps: Scheunecafe has moved from Alaunstrasse to Gorlitzer Strasse 20, the old Der Dicke Schmidt on Rudolf-Leonhard-Strasse is permanently closed so head to the Rothenburger Strasse shop instead, SPROUT FOOD is a weekday lunch spot only, and Falscher Hase is closed on Tuesdays.
