Eugene is a university town with a hippie streak that never left, and its vegan eating is woven into the downtown and Whiteaker neighborhoods: three fully vegan spots, a non-profit cafe with a food-security mission, a raw and organic cafe, and a food cart, plus five vegan-friendly restaurants where the vegan dishes are labelled and the kitchens are used to them. The food is Pacific Northwest comfort, bowls, tempeh, breakfast and Indian curries, and the prices are kind, four of the eight are price level 1.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with a seasonal soup at Acorn, a raw taco at The Coco Nut, a tempeh reuben at Viva!, the vegan French toast at Morning Glory, a rice bowl at Darlene's, a vegetable coconut korma at Evergreen, mapo tofu at Bao Bao House, and a Yumm bowl with tofu at Cafe Yumm. A note on the traps: Viva! is a food cart and takeout only, so eat it on the go; and Darlene's is vegetarian with dishes that can be made vegan, so specify no dairy and no honey mustard when ordering.
