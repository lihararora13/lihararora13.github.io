Braga eats vegan in the compact historic centre, where the scene runs on a bus-station vegan kitchen, a long-running 100% vegetal buffet, a vegan Korean table upstairs on Rua do Raio, and a cluster of vegetarian restaurants with serious vegan plates. Everything sits within a ten-minute walk of the cathedral, and the five all-vegan anchors make it one of the strongest small-city scenes in this batch.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with the daily plate at Paladares Vegan, the lunch buffet at Hibiscus, the bibimbap at BapMokja, and the beetroot tartare at Anjo Verde. A note on the traps: Divino Grao, the vegan restaurant on Rua Nova de Santa Cruz, closed its doors on 8 August 2025 and now exists only as a meal-delivery service, so it stays off the list; Cor de Tangerina is in Guimaraes, not Braga, despite appearing in Braga searches; and the Semente pin sits at number 11 on the same small square as its listed number 13.
