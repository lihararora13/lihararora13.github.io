Adelaide punches above its weight for plant-based food, and the scene is wonderfully varied: a Lonely Planet-lauded vegan Greek truck turned restaurant, a fine-dining-adjacent plant-based room, a vegetarian Chinese institution, a fully vegan Thai restaurant, and a cafe with a secret garden. Most of it sits in the CBD or a short tram ride out.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with strong vegan menus. Start with a yiros at Staazi, a burger at Veggie Vie, vegan yum cha at Zenhouse, and pad Thai at Ping An. Note that Veggie Vie is closed on weekends, so plan a weekday visit, and Staazi is closed Mondays and Tuesdays.
