Ann Arbor has been cooking vegetarian food since 1973, when Seva opened, and the scene has only deepened since: a fully vegan filling station in a converted 1925 gas station in Kerrytown, a Korean-inspired vegan counter from the Zingerman's family, an all-vegan bakery by North Campus, and an Indian buffet where the vegan dishes outnumber the rest. The university keeps it young, and the kitchens keep it serious.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegan-friendly. Start with the buffalo cauliflower wings at Detroit Street Filling Station, the General Tso's cauliflower at Seva, the fried tofu sandwich at Little Kim, the Hangover Cure bagel at The Lunch Room, the chana masala at Earthen Jar, the beet burger at First Bite, the vegan dishes at Miss Kim, and the veggie combo at Blue Nile.
