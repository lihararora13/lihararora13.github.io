Prayagraj eats like a pilgrim city with a university town attached: Civil Lines has the cafes and South Indian counters, Katra and Loknath Gali have the heritage sweet shops and kachori counters going back to 1890, and pure-veg thalis are everywhere. Dairy is the thread through all of it, so order the vegan version explicitly.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. Celebrations on Cooper Road was listed as possibly temporarily closed in a recent local guide and is left out. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city.
