Córdoba is a seven-pick city, and the list leans on the old town: a 100 percent veggie and vegan tapas bar by the Miraflores Bridge, a Slow Food restaurant on the Ribera, a vegetarian tapas bar and a Bib Gourmand restaurant with a 14-course vegetarian tasting menu. It is a small scene, but the top of it is genuinely good.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at La Bicicleta on Calle Cardenal Gonzalez, LaMundi on Avenida del Gran Capitán and Amaltea on Ronda de Isasa. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegetarian restaurant that closed in 2025 is not on this list, and a tapas bar that had moved is listed at its current address.
