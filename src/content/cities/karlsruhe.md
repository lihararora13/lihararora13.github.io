Karlsruhe's vegan scene runs along the Kaiserstraße, the long spine of the fan-shaped city, and five of its eight picks are fully vegan, the strongest all-vegan count in this set. The anchors are a Vietnamese restaurant that was the city's first Asian vegan kitchen, a burger restaurant that has been fully plant-based since 2015, and a vegan restaurant that makes its own ice cream, backed by a vegan café, a small vegan kitchen, and a lunch-only vegetarian canteen. The vegan-friendly half adds Neapolitan pizza with a proper vegan range and an Asian restaurant that stays open late.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with phở at An, a Diablo burger at My Heart Beats Vegan, the roast bombe at Verde, cake and coffee at drei&zwanzig, the daily plate at Veg Veg, lunch at Kulturküche, a vegan pizza at 60 seconds to napoli, and the vegan dishes at Akoya. A note on the hours: Kulturküche serves lunch only, Tuesday to Friday from noon to two, drei&zwanzig is closed on Sundays and Mondays, and Verde's hours shift between lunch and dinner service, so check the day before you go.
