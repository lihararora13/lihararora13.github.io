Pune has quietly become one of India's most vegan-friendly cities, and the best of it clusters where you'd expect: the leafy lanes of Koregaon Park, with outposts in Sangamwadi, Kalyani Nagar and Baner for the rest of town. The scene leans healthy and wholefood, think ragi-base pizzas, Buddha bowls, cold-pressed juices and dairy-free desserts, but it never forgets that food should be fun first.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and the rest vegetarian kitchens with serious, clearly labelled vegan menus. Start with a ragi pizza at Sante, a milkshake at The Vegan Life, a Buddha bowl at Chafa, and a sugar-free cake at Artinci. A note on the traps: The Real Green Cafe, Pune's old vegan pioneer, is permanently closed, so it does not make the list.
