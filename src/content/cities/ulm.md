Ulm is the smallest city in this set and the most surprising, because its headline restaurant is a full vegan tavern cooking the Swabian food the region is famous for: Kässpätzle, Zwiebelrostbraten, Vleischküchle and Kaiserschmarrn, all of it plant-based and all of it house-made. The rest of the scene fans out from the Münsterplatz across the old town: a vegan café inside an Ashtanga yoga studio up on the Safranberg, a vegan and vegetarian breakfast café, an organic bistro, a bookish café, and a couple of quick counters, with a burger joint just across the Danube in Neu-Ulm.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the Zwiebelrostbraten at Virtshaus, a bowl at AYI Café, a bagel at Café Frida, the seasonal lunch at Erdapfel, hummus and cake at Aegis, a veggie burger at VeggyGO, a çiğ köfte dürüm at Çiğköftem, and a bowl at dean&david. A note on the practicalities: Virtshaus reopened under new ownership in April 2026 and asks for reservations, AYI Café is reached by the rear entrance at Leimgrubenweg 29 while building work continues, and VeggyGO sits across the river in Neu-Ulm, a short walk from the old town.
