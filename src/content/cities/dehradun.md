Dehradun is the valley city where the mountains start, and its cafe scene has grown up fast along Rajpur Road. The headline is Kind Greens, the city's first all-vegan cafe, opened in 2026, and around it sits a bench of pure-veg restaurants, organic cafes and healthy kitchens that make the Doon an easy place to eat plant-based.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. One is fully vegan, Kind Greens, and the rest are vegan-friendly, so ask for no ghee, no dahi and no cheese. Start with the matcha and smoothie bowls at Kind Greens, the avocado toast at House of Nuttmeeg, and the Mysore masala dosa at Udupi Gangotri, and let the mountain air do the rest.
