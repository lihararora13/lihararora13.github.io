Bangalore does vegan food the way it does everything else: early, earnestly and with an engineer's love of a good system. This is the city where India's first fully vegan restaurant opened, and the scene has only grown deeper since, splitting between the grand old whole-food cafés of Sadashivanagar and Richmond Town, the buzzing delivery kitchens of Koramangala and Indiranagar, and the farm-to-table Indian kitchens of Jayanagar. The best trick here is range: one day you're eating millet dosa and raw jackfruit biryani, the next you're on Rwandan agatogo, tempeh tikka makhani and a fully vegan chocolate truffle cake.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and five of the eight are fully vegan. Start with the beetroot hummus at JustBe, the Bombaat mockmeat wrap at Carrots, the broccoli almond soup at Green Theory, the beetroot and cashew croquettes at Vegan Vogue, the tempeh tikka makhani at Vegan Heat, the raw jackfruit biryani at Go Native, the orange chilli tofu at Enerjuvate, and finish with a chocolate truffle cake from Crave by Leena. A note on the traps: House of Seitan in Indiranagar is permanently closed, and Santé Spa Cuisine's Domlur bungalow is reported permanently closed in 2026, so both stay off the list; Luo Han's old HSR address is stale too, the same family kitchen now trades as Fortune in Koramangala.
