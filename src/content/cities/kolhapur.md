Kolhapur is famous for its misal, its chappals and its temple, and its food scene is proudly vegetarian. This list leans on the city's best pure-veg dining rooms, where thalis, sattvik meals and veg biryani are the everyday food, and where a vegan who asks for the dairy held eats very well indeed.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is exactly what Kolhapur's scene genuinely supports right now. Start with the Safar tasting menu at Basalt, a sattvik thali at Siddhagiri, and a veg biryani at Aroma. Ask the kitchen to hold the ghee, curd and paneer and you will eat very well here.
