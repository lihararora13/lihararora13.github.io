Halifax does vegan food the way a port city should: hearty, unfussy and a little bit theatrical, with the best of it clustered in the North End along Gottingen Street. Real Fake Meats is the city's first vegan butcher shop, Wild Leek has been the 100% vegan comfort-food café since 2013, and Springhouse is the licensed plant-based dining room a few doors down, so the three of them make a walkable vegan crawl in a single block. Downtown adds the farm-to-table Wooden Monkey and the quick bowls of Harvest Clean Eats, and the suburbs add falafel and diner breakfasts.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly. Start with the veggie donair at Real Fake Meats, the Wild Bowl at Wild Leek, a breakfast burrito at Springhouse, a falafel platter at Falafel Factory, the vegan seitan donair at The Wooden Monkey, and the vegan breakfast plate at Easy Street Diner. A note on the traps: The Wooden Monkey has a second location across the harbour in Dartmouth, so use the Grafton Street address on this page, and Chiko's Bake House on Inglis Street is flagged as possibly closed, so it is not listed here.
