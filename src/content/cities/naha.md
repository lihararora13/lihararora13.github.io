Naha is the easiest base for vegan food in Okinawa, and the scene is small enough to walk: most of the best tables sit within fifteen minutes of Kokusai-dori, between the Tsuboya pottery district and Kencho-mae station, with the airport covering arrivals and departures. The cooking is Okinawan first, island tofu, sea grapes, taco rice, chimaki and soba, rebuilt plant-based, alongside a Taiwanese buffet, a Thai-Indian ethnic kitchen and a department-store café, and the island's Blue Zone reputation shows in how much of it is built on local vegetables.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with the dal curry set at Mana, the Okinawan laksa at Tamatebako, the vegan taco rice at LaLa Zorba, the tantan noodles at Saborami, the chimaki at Kintsubo Shokudo, the taco rice at Cafe VG, the purple sweet potato pancakes at Rakuen Cafe, and the tempura rice bowl at Donburi Nantoya. A note on the traps: two names that appear in older guides are gone, RYUNESS is permanently closed and Ukishima Garden is flagged temporarily closed, so neither is included here; and several of these kitchens keep irregular hours, so check the current schedule before you go. For a splurge, Yotsutake in Kume serves a Ryukyu vegan kaiseki from ¥6,600 with a dance performance, but it needs a reservation the day before.
