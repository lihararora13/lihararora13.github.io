Kassel does vegan food the way it does documenta: quietly, confidently, and better than you expected from a city this size. The best tables cluster in the Vorderer Westen, the student-flavoured quarter west of the centre, with a couple of gems on Wilhelmshöher Allee and over in Unterneustadt by the Fulda, and they cover the full range: falafel and seitan kebab, bowls and chilli sin carne, big vegan breakfasts, proper coffee and cake, and organic ice cream made from plant milks. Five of the eight picks are fully vegan, which is a strong ratio for a city of two hundred thousand, and the scene leans on long-running local favourites rather than chains.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the falafel plate at Zum glücklichen Bergschweinchen, a crunchy kebab at Crunchy Kebab Vegan, a burger bowl at Herbstapfel, a vegan breakfast board at Satt & glücklich, overnight oats at Counter Café, a slice of vegan cake at Snäckday, a scoop of pumpkin seed ice cream at Eisdiele Frohnatur, and a vegan sharing platter at Abessina. A note on the traps: the Bergschweinchen has a second spot on Holländische Straße, so head to the Weserstraße original, and the vegan-friendly kitchens here all serve meat too, so order the vegan dishes by name.
