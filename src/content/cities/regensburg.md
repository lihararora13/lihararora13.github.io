Regensburg packs a surprising amount of vegan food into a very walkable old town, and almost all of it sits within a few lanes of the Danube. Four of the eight picks are fully vegan, including a seasonal restaurant, a cat café with all-day breakfast, a vegan sushi and tapas bar, and a small organic bio-café, and the vegan-friendly half is just as useful: a riverside vegetarian restaurant and bar across the Stone Bridge, a Middle Eastern kitchen, a health-focused café, and a brunch café for waffles and cake. It is a compact scene that rewards wandering rather than planning.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the seasonal main at Zur Goldenen Kaper, the all-day breakfast at Katzentempel, a vegan sushi set at Ānanda, cake and coffee at Valea, a plant-based cheeseburger at Green Heaven, a falafel plate at Mesopotamia, a falafel bowl at CLEAN FOOD, and a vegan waffle at Cafe Jolie. A note on the hours: Zur Goldenen Kaper closes on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and Green Heaven does not open until the afternoon on weekdays.
