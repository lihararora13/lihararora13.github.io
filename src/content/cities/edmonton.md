Edmonton does not shout about its vegan scene, which is exactly why it is such a good surprise. Downtown and Old Strathcona hold the best of it: an award-winning Indonesian restaurant, a vegan pizza and wine bar, an all-vegan punk pub, and cafes that have been feeding vegetarians for decades.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with ginger beef at Padmanadi, a Big Mack pizza at Die Pie, vegan wings at The Buckingham, a Pan-Asian bowl at Loma House, a veggie burger at The Clever Rabbit, falafel Friday at Cafe Mosaics, a vegan smoked tofu sandwich at Block 1912, and a vegan scoop at White Rabbit.
