Reykjavik is by far the easiest place in Iceland for plant-based travellers, and the capital's vegan scene has grown from almost nothing a decade ago into dedicated restaurants, cafes that lead with plant-based food, and most mainstream restaurants offering genuine vegan options. The picks cluster in the central 101 area, within easy walking distance of each other, and they cover pan-Asian comfort food, global bowls, vegan baking and a proper vegetarian institution that has been going since 1999.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan and three vegetarian or vegan-friendly with serious vegan menus. Start with pho at Vegan World Peace, dhal at Mama, noodles at Loving Hut, a pastry at Plantan, coffee and cake at Kaffi Vinyl, the lunch buffet at Gardurinn, a bowl at Glo, and a falafel wrap at Chickpea. A note on the traps: Reykjavik is small and the scene is thin, so book the popular tables ahead in summer, and check opening days in winter when hours shrink.
