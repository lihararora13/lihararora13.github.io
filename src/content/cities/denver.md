Denver's vegan scene grew up fast. The same crew behind the
city's original all-vegan comfort spot also runs a Capitol Hill
diner that never closes, a chef-driven team inside Edgewater
Public Market is turning celery root into a Reuben worthy of a
James Beard nod, and a Michelin-lauded pasta bar just packed up
its NYC fame and came home to RiNo. Our eight picks span old-guard
comfort food, a zero-waste Mediterranean tasting room, Italian
natural-wine nights, and all-vegan fast food.

At a mile high, everything tastes a little better. Bring an
appetite and a sweater.
