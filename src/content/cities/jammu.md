Jammu eats pure vegetarian by default: the pilgrim city runs on Vaishno dhabas, sattvik thalis and South Indian counters, with dairy everywhere and ghee in most kitchens. The best tables cluster around Trikuta Nagar near the railway station, Gandhi Nagar and the old city around Residency Road, and the food is hearty Dogra and Punjabi cooking rather than anything labelled vegan.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city, so this is a vegan-friendly-only guide. Ask for no ghee, no dahi and no cheese at every table.
