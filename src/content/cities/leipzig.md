Leipzig does vegan food with the energy of a city that has always been a little alternative: Vietnamese pho and summer rolls, vegan bratwurst and gyros, bao burgers and dumplings, piroggen and pistachio lattes, spread between Südvorstadt, Volkmarsdorf, Zentrum-Süd, Lindenau and Plagwitz. The scene leans Asian and it leans young, with a cat café and a Polish handmade café for the slower mornings, and the tram makes the whole spread easy.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegan-friendly. Start with a Vöner at Vleischerei, pho at Bödhi, a bao burger at Monchi Vegan, tofu in pyjamas at OUAI, breakfast at Katzentempel Leipzig Mitte, and piroggen at BABA Handmade Cafe. Two notes: Bödhi Leipzig is a different venue from Bodhi in Nuremberg, a different spelling and a different operator; and Katzentempel has a second Leipzig branch in the Ost, so use the Mitte address on Katharinenstraße on this page.
