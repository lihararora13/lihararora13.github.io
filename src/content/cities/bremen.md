Bremen does vegan food the way it does everything else: quietly, thoroughly, and with more range than you would expect from a city its size. The best tables cluster in the Ostertor and the Östliche Vorstadt, the old working-class quarters around the Steintor, with the station area covering the burger-and-shake end of things, and they cook from everywhere: Taiwanese noodles and soya mock meats, Vietnamese-inspired fast food, South Indian dosa, German collective-kitchen cooking, and proper vegan burgers. This is a scene of long-running local institutions rather than pop-ups, and seven of the eight picks are fully vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with the seasonal menu at the Veganes Küchen Kollektiv, the peanut noodles at Vegefarm, a late-night burger at Vengo, a shake at Vincent V by the Hauptbahnhof, a cold-pressed juice at pressgut, a vegan gyros at Apple & Eve, a breakfast plate among the rescue cats at Katzentempel, and a masala dosa at Vegano. A note on the traps: Green Bites has closed, so do not follow old listings to Lessingstraße, and the Veganbar that used to trade in the city is gone too.
