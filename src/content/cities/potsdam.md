Potsdam is Berlin's elegant neighbour, all palaces, parks and Dutch Quarter gables, and its vegan scene is as compact as the old town itself. Most of it sits within a few streets of the Brandenburger Strasse and the Lindenstrasse: two fully vegan spots, a handful of cafes, and two Vietnamese restaurants with serious vegan menus. Then Babelsberg, the old film-studio quarter across town, adds a Vietnamese restaurant and the city's most famous falafel. It is a small scene, but every pick earns its place.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and none of them duplicates a Berlin pick. Start with the tofu stir-fry at Udonge, a vegan cake at Cafe Ceci, a slice at Cafe Rosenberg, a tofu sushi bowl at Akari, an avocado toast at Miss Green Bean, the surprise vegan platter at Le's Cyclo, a cig kofte durum at CigkofteM, and a falafel pita at Damaskus Express. A note on the traps: Cafe Rosenberg used to be fully vegan but now serves eggs and dairy too, so order from the vegan dishes; Damaskus Express is cash only and weekdays only; and two names you may see in older guides are gone, Good dEATs closed permanently in 2016 and Madia is currently listed as temporarily closed, so neither made this list.
