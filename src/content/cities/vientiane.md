Vientiane has one of the strongest vegan scenes in Southeast Asia, with several fully vegan restaurants in the centre and a cluster of vegan cafes around the riverfront. The anchors are Vegan Center Cafe, a fully vegan buffet and restaurant, Reunion Cafe, a fully vegan kitchen, and Khouadin Vegetarian, a vegan buffet, with vegan cafes and a food truck filling out the map. The scene is concentrated enough to walk between picks, and the best meals are the buffets, which run from morning to early afternoon.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan. Start with the buffet at Vegan Center Cafe, the mock meats at Reunion, the buffet at Khouadin, and the cafe dishes at Veggie Lover. A note on the traps: Vegan Center Cafe relocated from Kualuang, so older guides send people to the wrong address, and it closes from 2pm to 3pm every day; and Oon Jai Marketplace is not fully vegan, as one drink on its menu contains honey, so order food, not that drink.
