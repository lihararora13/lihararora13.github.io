Lima might surprise you: behind the ceviche and pisco sours sits one of South America's most likeable vegan scenes, clustered in the walkable streets of Miraflores and Barranco with a detour to La Victoria for a perfect-rated hidden gem. The best tables here range from a pioneering raw food cafe and organic store to a food-court stall doing Latin American classics, and from an all-vegan bar remaking every Peruvian standard to a French-trained chef's bistro doing seitan ceviche and vegan pisco sours.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026. Start with Raw Cafe for breakfast, Noqa Vegan for a Latin American set menu, and SAMSARA for vegan ceviche and lomo saltado, then book Florencia for a homestyle Barranco dinner, El Jardín de Jazmín for gluten-free fusion, Seitan Urban Bistro for French-Peruvian cooking, OKEY Al Natural for a bargain meal of the day, and La Recontra for burgers in a shipping-container food court. A note on the traps: Oiga is flagged as possibly temporarily closed and La Verde is permanently closed, so neither is on this list.
