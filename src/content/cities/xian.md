Xi'an is one of the most unexpectedly vegan-friendly cities in China, and it shows in the range: a Buddhist temple canteen that has fed pilgrims for years, teahouses that treat a meal as a quiet ritual, a 21-yuan all-you-can-eat buffet, and a Black Pearl-recommended fine-dining room in the Gaoxin tech district. The best tables sit inside the old city wall and around Xiaozhai, with the Gaoxin picks a short metro ride west, and the food is Shaanxi at heart: noodles, mock meats, mushrooms and tofu, cooked without egg, dairy or honey at the fully vegan spots.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan and four vegan-friendly. Start with the vegan duck and fungi soup at Daxingshan, a set menu at Lotus, a spicy stew and tea at Pusu, the roast goose at An Duo, the buffet at Sumanxiang, the Daduhui spread, a set meal at Su Xin, and the set meals at Su Shi Xiang Shan. A note on the traps: Shangdao Vegetarian has closed, and the Tianlong Baoyan branch at Ci'en West Road is the same operation as the Daxingshan canteen, not a separate restaurant. Do not confuse the vegetarian Lotus at Hesheng Jingguang Center with a meat-serving western restaurant of the same name on Furong South Road. Su Shi Xiang Shan is the weakest pick, unrated with no reviews on HappyCow, so verify it on arrival.
