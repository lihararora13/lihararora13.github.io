Milan does vegan food the way it does everything else: with serious style and zero fuss. The best tables cluster around Porta Venezia and Stazione Centrale, with a couple of gems in Brera and Porta Nuova, and they cook Italian food first: cacio e pepe, lasagne, risotto and tiramisu, all of it fully plant-based, alongside vegan sushi counters, rainbow burgers and a Michelin-starred vegetarian tasting menu that will cook every course vegan if you ask when you book. This is a scene with real history, La Colubrina has been vegan since 1983 and Joia has held its star since 1996, and the newer places build on that head start instead of starting from scratch.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and Joia vegan-friendly. Start with the Cashew & Pepe at Linfa, the vegan cheese plate at La Colubrina, the Piatto Quadro lunch at Joia, an omakase-style spread at NORI WAY, a lasagne at Il Lughino, a Cherry Bomb at Flower Burger, the Birthday burger at Junk's, and the Uao Burger at GIARDÍ. A note on the traps: Linfa moved in August 2026 from Via Bergognone to Piazza Cavour 3, and NORI WAY relocated from Piazza Bruno Buozzi to Via Federico Confalonieri, so use the addresses on this page, not the ones in older guides.
