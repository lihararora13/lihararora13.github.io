Hamburg does vegan food the way it does everything else: thoroughly, generously and with a harbour city's appetite for the world. With 54 fully vegan restaurants in the city, the scene is deep, and the best tables are scattered across the neighbourhoods that make Hamburg Hamburg, St. Pauli and Sternschanze, St. Georg and Winterhude, the Altstadt and Fuhlsbüttel, each with its own flavour.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and all eight are fully vegan. Start with the tapas at TA Vegan House, the weekly menu at The Vegan Eagle, a burger at Apple & Eve, a pho at Greentable, tofu wings at Kkokki, a bowl at Chay, sushi at Bodhi St. Georg, and a bowl at Bodhi Winterhude. A note on the traps: several once-popular names, including Katers Köök and the Vietnamese spot on the old HappyCow lists, have closed in 2026, so none of them make the list.
