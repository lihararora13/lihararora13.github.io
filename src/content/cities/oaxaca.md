Oaxaca's vegan food tastes like Oaxaca: moles, tlayudas, memelas, mezcal, chocolate and corn in every form, made plant-based without losing the soul of the cuisine. The scene clusters in the Centro and Jalatlaco, all walkable, with a couple of stops in the Reforma and Ex-Marquezado neighbourhoods.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with strong vegan menus. Start with a vegan ceviche at Citronella, tacos at Los Muchitos, a tostada at Etnofood, falafel at Aguacate, a seasonal plate at Nanita, a bowl at Herbívora, a plate at Hierba Dulce, and a memela at Trigo Verde. A note on the traps: Verde el Alma sits in Puerto Escondido, not Oaxaca city, and Maya Papaya is in Zipolite and reported closed, so neither makes the list.
