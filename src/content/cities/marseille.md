Marseille does not do vegan food by the guidebook, and that is the whole point: the scene is a handful of passionate, independent kitchens clustered around Le Panier, Cours Julien and the 5th arrondissement, cooking everything from Armenian plates to vegan kebabs with the same sunny confidence the city brings to everything else. What it lacks in numbers it makes up for in quality, variety and sheer personality.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, six of them fully vegan and two vegetarian with serious vegan menus. Start with the tasting plates at Hododa, the three-course set at Mauvaise Herbe, a world-cuisine plate at Mariposas, a vegan kebab at John Silver, brunch at De Bon'heur, a falafel pita at Yafel, a bowl at Carry Aum, and a seitan shawarma at C'est Bon. The Vieux-Port is the postcard; Cours Julien is where you eat.
