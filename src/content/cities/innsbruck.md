Innsbruck eats vegan around the old town and the university quarter, where a fully vegan restaurant by the river, a 100% vegan pizzeria and a Turkish snack bar anchor a scene of cafes, bookshop bowls and evening-only vegetarian dining. The picks cluster south of the Inn, walkable from the Hauptbahnhof, and two of the eight are fully vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan. Start with the kimchi bowl at Olive, the vegan pizza at Meow, the cigkofte at Cigkoftem, and the rice pudding at Verenas. A note on the traps: Swing Kitchen at Herzog-Friedrich-Strasse 28 is reported closed since December 2025 on HappyCow, so it stays off the list despite appearing in older guides; Green Flamingo shares the Furstenweg 7 address with Meow Pizzeria Verde, so it is left out pending verification of whether it is a rebrand or a second unit; and Cigkoftem's older guides list Museumstrasse 34, while the current listing is Salurner Strasse 10.
