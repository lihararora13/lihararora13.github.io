Suzhou is a temple-and-noodle vegan city: Guisu does Suzhou-style vegan noodles by Hanshan Temple, Xiyuan Temple has its own vegetarian hall, and the old town lanes hide dumpling shops and lotus cafes. The Chinese word is su, plain, and chun su means purely plant-based.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Two traps caught here: Suwei Youshi is flagged maybe closed since January 2025 and is left out, and Wujie is shown as closed on HappyCow search results despite a September 2026 review, so it is dropped rather than risk a closed flagship. All eight picks are vegan or vegetarian.
