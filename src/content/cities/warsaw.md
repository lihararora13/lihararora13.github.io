Warsaw spoils vegans rotten. HappyCow counts 38 fully vegan restaurants in the Polish capital, and the food costs about half what it does in Western Europe, which means you can eat very well for very little. Polish classics get the vegan treatment everywhere: pierogi, pancakes, soups and cakes, alongside Mexican, Vietnamese and Indian kitchens.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan. Start with pierogi at Vege Miasto, a burrito at Momencik, a burger at Krowarzywa, the tasting dishes at Peaches, the buffet at Vega, a Vietnamese plate at Vegamama, pho at Moc Vegan, and falafel at Falafelove. A note on the traps: Mango Vegan, the old Bracka street-food spot, is permanently closed, so it does not make the list.
