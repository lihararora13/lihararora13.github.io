Mexico City was built on tacos, tortas and antojitos, and its vegan kitchens have gleefully claimed every single one: al pastor shaved off a trompo made of seitan, tortas taller than your fist, ceviche with no fish harmed, and pink tortillas dyed with beetroot just because they can. This is street food heaven, and the plants run the stalls.

All eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026. Start with Por Siempre Vegana for the tacos everyone talks about, Na Tlali for a slow, soulful mole lunch in San Angel, and Los Loosers for mushrooms done ten brilliant ways, then keep grazing through Roma Norte and Condesa.
