Haifa is a port city on the slopes of Mount Carmel, and its vegan eating is concentrated downtown around Natanzon Street and Derech Yafo, with a second cluster up in Central Carmel and the Krayot. The downtown strip is the story: three all-vegan falafel and hummus counters within a few minutes of each other, plus Abu Shaker's ten kinds of hummus, and the rest of the list spreads out to burgers, sushi and breakfast.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegan-friendly. Start with hummus and vegan shawarma at Hummus Bar, falafel at Falafel Kikar Paris, falafel at Michelle Falafel, hummus with fava at Abu Shaker, hummus and baba ganouj at Arabeska, the house-made vegan burger at Agadir Burger, vegan sushi at Atza Sushi Bar, and a Yo Egg shakshuka at Benedict. A note on the traps: Friday hours are short across the city and several places close for Shabbat, so check before a Friday or Saturday visit.
