Bikaner is a snack city first and a thali city second: bhujia and sweets in the old city, pure-veg cafes around Ambedkar Circle, and family dining rooms on KEM Road. Dairy is everywhere, from ghee in the dal baati to dahi on the chaat, so the vegan ask is essential.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city. Byte N Sips is the newest pure-veg cafe, Crossroad the newest pure-veg kitchen, opened August 2026.
