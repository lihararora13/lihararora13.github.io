Dhanbad is a coal-city pure-veg scene centred on Bank More and Govindpur Road: new pure-veg family restaurants, an 1984 frankie counter, and sweet shops that double as restaurants. Everything is lacto-vegetarian, so the vegan ask does the work.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city. Frankie, serving vegetarian food since 1984, is the oldest name on the list.
