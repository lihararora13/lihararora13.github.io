Florianopolis, or Floripa as everyone calls it, is an island of beaches, lagoons and surf, and its vegan scene matches the mood: relaxed, sunny and generous. The best tables split between the Lagoa da Conceicao village, where the lagoon-side cafes and tiki bars cluster, the southern beaches of Campeche, and the downtown Centro, and they cook Brazilian food first: pay-by-weight buffets, feijoada-style plates, tapioca and acai, alongside pizzas, burgers and falafel. This is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than tourist traps, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food at all.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan and five vegetarian or omnivorous with serious vegan menus. Start with a pay-by-weight buffet at Espaco Nutrir, a vegan pizza at Veganeria Stamm, flower wings at Taboo Tiki, a lasanha at Kulturas, a Taiwanese buffet plate at Tsan The, a chapati at Caapora, a burger at Ahimsa, and a garden lunch at Quintal de Casa.
