Bergen is a small, wet, beautiful city, and its vegan scene is small too: one fully vegan restaurant, then a ring of vegan-friendly cafés, bowl bars and restaurants clustered around the harbour, the Sentrum and Nøstet, all of it walkable from Bryggen. The food leans on burgers, bowls, Vietnamese and Chinese cooking, with a bakery and a gastropub for the slower evenings, and the quality is good even where the choice is narrow.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan and seven vegan-friendly. Start with a Garlicious burger at Dirty V, vegan miso ramen at Løvetann, a power bowl at Daily Pot, and vegan spring rolls at Enjoy Saigon. Two honest notes: Bergen is the thinnest scene in this batch, so expect one or two vegan dishes rather than a vegan menu at the lower-ranked picks; and at Pingvinen, the weakest pick, only one or two dishes are vegan, so check before ordering. At Daily Pot, one visitor found the soup cold in September 2026, so the bowls are the safer order.
