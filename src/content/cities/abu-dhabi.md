Abu Dhabi does not have a dedicated all-vegan restaurant that has survived verification, which is exactly why this list is honest about it. The capital's vegan eating happens in two places: plant-forward wellness cafes like ALKALIME on Saadiyat and The Raw Place, and the big vegetarian Indian kitchens downtown, where dosa, thali and curries are vegan on request if you ask about ghee and paneer. Add the hotel restaurants with dedicated vegan menus, Cafe 302 and Brick, and a vegan can eat very well here.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all of them vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan, which is the honest shape of this city's scene. Start with a bowl at ALKALIME, a vegan breakfast at Cafe 302, a dosa at Sangeetha, and a vegan pizza at Brick. A note on the traps: Naughty Vegan, a delivery-only name, is flagged as maybe closed, so it is not on this list.
