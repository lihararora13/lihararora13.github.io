Concepción's vegan scene is concentrated in the city centre, within a few blocks of Plaza Perú and the university quarter: a fully vegan comedor on Cochrane, a Syrian restaurant with falafel and hummus, a soup kitchen, a salad bar and a bubble-tea vegetarian restaurant. It is a compact, walkable scene, and the strongest table is the one that cooks seitan asado and tofu burgers.

The tofu burgers and seitan asado at Govindas Vegan, and the falafel and shawarma at Siriana, are the plates to plan around. Three venues were checked and dropped as temporarily closed: VeganStore in Galería Amanecer, Kinwa on Paicaví, and Fénix Café on Angol. Cheng Nan Shih, a Chinese restaurant on Freire, was a candidate but was cut in favour of the eight below.
