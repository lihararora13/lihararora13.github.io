Naples does vegan food the way it does everything else: loudly, generously, and with dough. The best tables cluster in the historic centre around Via Toledo and the university streets, with a detour up to Vomero for Arrecria and into Sanita for fried pizza, and they cook Neapolitan food first: pizza, pasta, parmigiana and fried pizza, all of it plant-based or easily made so, alongside burgers, bao and a proper organic deli. This is a scene built on pizza culture rather than health food, and the vegan versions are taken seriously here.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. Start with the Mama Bun at Green M'ama, the Maria Antonietta pizza at Vitto Pitagorico, a seasonal pasta at Arrecria, a cuzzetiello at Officina Vegana, the daily plate at O' Grin, a fried pizza at Isabella De Cham, a marinara at Pizzeria da Michele, and a deli plate at Bio C'e. A note on the traps: Isabella De Cham and Pizzeria da Michele are not fully vegan restaurants, so order the vegan dishes on their menus rather than assuming everything qualifies, and Vitto Pitagorico also has a second branch in Aversa, so use the Via Enrico Pessina address on this page.
