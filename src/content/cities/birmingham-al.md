Birmingham's vegan scene is compact and scattered across the city's neighborhoods rather than concentrated downtown. Five Points South holds the Indian and natural-foods core, Avondale and Woodlawn carry the brewery and burger energy, and the Summit brings the sushi. The list leans on vegan-friendly kitchens with real vegan benches rather than a deep bench of all-vegan rooms, and the one fully vegan anchor is a loud, joyful burger joint.

Start with a One Night Stand burger and loaded fries at Slutty Vegan, a plate at Golden Temple, and the vegan dishes at BayLeaf, then work through the brewery food at Back Forty and the vegan sushi at Blue Sushi. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Falafel Cafe could not be verified at a current address and was dropped, Eugene's Hot Chicken was dropped after its listing resolved to the wrong venue, and Epice's pin is address-level rather than name-confirmed.
