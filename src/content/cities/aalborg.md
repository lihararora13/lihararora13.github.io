Aalborg eats vegan in the centre, where a fully vegan cafe rated 5 on HappyCow anchors a scene of a Japanese all-you-can-eat with a vegan multi-course menu, a fine-dining vegan four-course menu, Vietnamese, Indian and ramen dinners and a patisserie cafe. The picks cluster around Boulevarden and Vesterbro, walkable from the station.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the soups and paninis at Morgen, Middag and Kaffe, the vegan multi-course menu at AI Japan, the four-course menu at Det Glade Vanvid, and the banh mi at AN DI. A note on the traps: Morgen, Middag and Kaffe lists limited hours on HappyCow, Monday to Thursday only, so check before you go; Chaacha was passed over, rated 1 on HappyCow; and Grillen Burgerbar was reported in May 2024 to have limited vegan options, so it stays off the list.
