Phuket is an island, not a city, and its vegan scene is spread across it: Phuket Town's old town in the east, Karon on the west coast, Rawai and Chalong in the south, and Cherngtalay in the north. That spread is the point. The old town is the easiest area for a vegan crawl, with Ubuntu Eat and The Vegan Table a short walk apart, while the south coast pairs Rawai's two vegan cafes with Chalong's two vegan rooms and the temples, and the north has the cheapest local eating on the island. Every pick in this guide is fully vegan, which makes Phuket the strongest all-vegan city in this batch.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, all eight fully vegan. Start with the biryani at Ubuntu Eat, a pizza at Soul Phuket, a burrito at All Vegan Cafe' Restaurant, an energy ball at Go Vegan Cafe, the pizza with vegan cheese at The Vegan Table, the mushroom larb at Pure Vegan Heaven, a Thai dish at Bodhi, and a 60 THB plate of rice and curries at 108 Vegetarian Cuisine. A note on the traps: Ubuntu Eat closes Thursdays, The Vegan Table changed ownership in June 2026 and its hours vary across sources, Pure Vegan Heaven's address and hours also vary across sources and it closes Sundays, and 108 Vegetarian Cuisine closes by 2pm and may be closed Sundays, so check hours before you drive across the island. Three pins are neighbourhood-level rather than street-level, Soul Phuket in Karon, Go Vegan Cafe in Rawai and Pure Vegan Heaven in Chalong, and Bodhi's pin is approximate for Chalong. And a warning: GoFresh was dropped from this guide after HappyCow flagged it temporarily closed, so do not trust older lists that still show it.
