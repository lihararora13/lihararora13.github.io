Yangon's vegan scene is thin and scattered, with no single vegan street, and it leans on vegetarian Indian kitchens, a plant-based cafe, and a vegetarian park restaurant. The anchor is Nourish Cafe, a healthy plant-based cafe in Bahan, with Veggie Park in People's Park and a pure-veg Indian delivery kitchen filling out the map. Expect to travel between picks, and expect most kitchens to be vegetarian rather than fully vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the falafel pita at Nourish, the mala xiang guo at Veggie Park, the veg biryani at Homemade.mmr, and the vegetarian set at Lanmadaw. A note on the traps: several Yangon listings on older guides are delivery-only or have moved, so check the address before you go; and the Indian vegetarian kitchens use dairy, so vegans should ask for dishes without it.
