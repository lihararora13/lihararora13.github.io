Vancouver has more vegan kitchens per capita than almost anywhere in North
America, and it wears the crown lightly. This is a city where a falafel shop
draws lines down Main Street, a white-tablecloth room plates seasonal
vegetables with tasting-menu reverence, and Commercial Drive's Vietnamese
kitchens keep the pho flowing late.

We ate across the city and ranked eight spots that earned it, from date-night
splurges to weeknight takeout heroes. Bring an appetite and a rain shell; the
food scene here is the real sunshine.
