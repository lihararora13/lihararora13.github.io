Ajmer is a pilgrim city with a new pure-veg fine-dine layer: lakeside rooftops above Ana Sagar, old-city bhojanalayas near the station and the dargah, and chaap counters in Vaishali Nagar. The food is Rajasthani and Punjabi, dairy-heavy, and best ordered with the no-ghee ask.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city. Baajro and Sabor are the new pure-veg fine-dine anchors, both verified active on Instagram in October and November 2026.
