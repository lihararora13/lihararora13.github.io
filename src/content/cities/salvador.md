Salvador cooks with dendê, coconut milk, okra and black-eyed peas, and its vegan restaurants know exactly what to do with them: moqueca built on palm heart, feijoada without the pork, acaraje, bobó and vatapa, all of it loud, generous and made from scratch. The best tables cluster in Barra, Santo Antonio, Graca and Itaigara, and they lean affordable and everyday, the kind of places locals eat at every week. This is a scene of long-running local favourites rather than tourist traps, and the top of the list is genuinely ambitious cooking that wins over people who never came looking for vegan food at all.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, two of them fully vegan and six vegetarian or natural with serious vegan menus. Start with a vegan burger at B Vegan, a moqueca vegana at Rango Vegan, a natural buffet plate at Puro Saudavel, a risotto at Saude na Panela, a feijoada vegetariana at Health Valley, a buffet plate at Manjericao, a natural buffet at Ramma, and a smoothie at Espaco Natural.
