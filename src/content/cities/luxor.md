Luxor feeds vegans on Egyptian food itself: koshary, ful, ta'ameya and lentil soup are plant-based by birth, and the city's best rooms are the ones that cook them well. The scene splits across the Nile, with Aroma's rooftop and dedicated vegan menu on the East Bank and the garden restaurants of the West Bank, Wannas Art Cafe and El Gezira, doing the quieter, greener cooking near the ferry and the tombs.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan, and the West Bank rooms are the heart of the list. Order the vegan tagine at Aroma, the falafel platter at El Gezira, and the vegan dishes at Wannas. A note on the traps: Luxor's vegan listings change with the tourist season, so check hours before crossing to the West Bank, and the Dome Village Home rooftop is a guesthouse restaurant rather than a walk-in dining room.
