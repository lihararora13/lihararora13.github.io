Jalandhar is Punjabi dhaba country: big pure-veg dining rooms on GT Road, South Indian counters in Model Town and old-market dhabas in Shastri Market. Dairy is the default in almost every dish, from butter on the kulcha to cream in the dal, so the vegan move is always the same ask, no ghee, no butter, no dahi.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 0 of them fully vegan. No all-vegan restaurant was found in the city. Haveli Heritage is the strongest table, a Punjabi-themed pure-veg restaurant open since 2001.
