Rennes is Brittany's capital and its vegan scene is small but real, spread across the centre from Rue du Vau Saint-Germain to Rue de Paris. There is one fully vegan anchor at the top, a vegetarian institution, a vegan-friendly banh mi spot and a set of neighbourhood kitchens that all cook proper vegan dishes.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start at Le Lapin Noir on Rue du Vau Saint-Germain, Pique-Prune and Angello on Place de Bretagne. The traps were minor: one much-listed vegan cafe that closed in 2024 is not on this list, and a restaurant that had moved across town is listed at its current address.
