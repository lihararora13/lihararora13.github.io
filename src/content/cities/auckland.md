Auckland is a harbour city that feeds vegans properly, from flaky mince and cheese pies with not a cow in sight to tempeh pad Thai, French croissants made entirely from plants, and burgers that started life in a food truck built from recycled materials. Ponsonby, Grey Lynn and Karangahape Road do most of the heavy lifting, with the city centre close behind.

Seven of these eight picks are fully vegan and verified open as of October 2026, and the eighth, East, is a vegetarian favourite where most of the menu is vegan and clearly labelled as such. Start with Khu Khu for the big Ponsonby dinner everyone recommends, Tart for the pie run, and Sunflower for a CBD Thai feast, then work your way to Grey Lynn for patisserie worth setting an alarm for.
