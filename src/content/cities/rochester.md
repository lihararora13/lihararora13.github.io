Rochester's vegan scene punches well above its size: six fully vegan restaurants for about 200,000 residents, the highest rate in New York State, and they cover the whole map, from the Park Avenue cafe strip to downtown Main Street, Monroe Avenue and the suburbs. The local signature is the Garbage Plate, Rochester's infamous pile of home fries, mac salad and meat, and two of the all-vegan spots answer it directly, The Red Fern with its Compost Plate and New Ethic with a Garbage Plate pizza. Ethiopian food, vegan pizza and a vegan butcher fill out a scene that feels built by locals for locals.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan and 1872 Cafe vegan-friendly. Start with the Compost Plate at The Red Fern, the Let's Go Buffalo pizza at Squatcho's, a butcher sandwich at GRASS fed, the Buffalo Chickpea Pizza at New Ethic, a Jamaican bowl at Allah's Kitchen, the daytime buffet at Natural Oasis, an Old Skool cinnamon roll at Cinnaholic, and the vegan burger at 1872 Cafe. A note on the traps: New Ethic trades inside 2 Ton Tony's Pizza on Titus Avenue with short evening hours, Wednesday to Saturday 4:30pm to 7:30pm, and Allah's Kitchen moved from Jefferson Avenue to Park Point Drive in Henrietta, so use the address on this page.
