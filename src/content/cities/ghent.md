Ghent does vegan food with the confidence of a city that started Thursday Veggie Day in 2009 and never looked back. The best tables cluster in the medieval centre around the Gravensteen and the canals, with a couple of gems out towards the Brugse Poort and the Bijloke, and they cook everything from vegan pizza and Flemish stews to tasting menus and sugar-free cakes, all of it plant-based. This is one of the easiest cities in Europe to eat vegan in, and the top of the list is genuinely inventive.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, seven of them fully vegan. Start with a margherita with cashew mozzarella at Plant A Pizza, brunch at Knol & Kool, a Tibetan Mama bowl at Le Botaniste, a pita kebab at Greenway, a tasting menu at Komkommertijd, cake and coffee at Madam Bakster, vegan pancakes at OhMA, and the daily special at KAROOT. A note on the traps: Komkommertijd's address is given as Bijlokekaai in some guides and Hoogstraat in others, so use the Bijlokekaai address on this page, and Le Botaniste also has branches in New York and Paris, so use the Hoornstraat original.
