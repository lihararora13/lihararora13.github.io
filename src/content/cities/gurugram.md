Gurugram eats like it works: fast, polished, and with a wellness streak a mile wide. The corporate belt has grown a proper vegan scene of its own, separate from Delhi, with a fully vegan restaurant, a fully plant-based cafe, and a vegan dessert parlour leading the charge. It is the most all-vegan-heavy guide in this whole North push, and the churn is real, so every pick here was checked open in November 2026.

Three of the eight are fully vegan, which is rare for an Indian city outside Bangalore. Start with Loving Hut in Sector 38, take the hummus platter at Greenr, and save the sweet course for White Cub. A note on what is not here: the old favourite Cafe Shunya is permanently closed and stays off the list, and Delhi's picks, Burma Burma and Naivedyam, are not repeated here even though they have Gurugram branches.
