Annecy is a seven-pick city, and honestly the thinnest in this region: the lakeside old town has plenty of restaurants, but very few that take vegan food seriously. The list leans on a veggie-friendly cafe on Rue Sainte-Claire, an organic cafe, a Thai vegetarian kitchen and a Michelin-starred room with a vegetable menu, plus a burger spot and a sushi chain with a vegan range.

Seven picks are verified open as of November 2026, none of them fully vegan. Start at La Cabane on Rue Sainte-Claire, Granny Smith on Rue Louis Revon and Café Barzoï on Rue de la Gare. The traps were real here: Greenpoint Veggie Burgers is temporarily closed, Le Freti is cheese-only raclette, La Cabane Iodée is seafood, and Le Cottage Bise is in Talloires, not Annecy, so none of them appears on this list. Sushi Shop is the thinnest verification on the list, a chain with a vegan sushi range rather than a vegan restaurant.
