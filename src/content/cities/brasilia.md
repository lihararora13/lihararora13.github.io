Brasília's vegan scene lives in the superquadras of Asa Norte and Asa Sul, where buffet restaurants, a fully vegan pizzeria and a vegan chocolate shop sit within a few blocks of each other. Three of the eight picks are fully vegan, including a gluten-free buffet and a pizzeria that opens only in the evenings, and the rest are long-running vegetarian and natural restaurants with deep vegan benches.

The gluten-free vegan buffet at Paladar Vegano, an evening pizza at Suprema, and the vegan chocolates at Sonho Vegan are the three stops to build a day around. One claim was checked and downgraded: The Plant is described as fully vegan on one guide, but its own offerings include a chicken wrap and another guide lists meat, chicken, pork and fish on its menu, so it is filed vegan-friendly. The Caldos e Sopas listing shares Boa Saúde's address and phone and is the same venue, not a second pick.
