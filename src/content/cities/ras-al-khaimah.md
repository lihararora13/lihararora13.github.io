Ras Al Khaimah is the UAE's quietest emirate for vegans, and its scene is split in two: the resort island of Marjan, where the Pullman runs the emirate's first dedicated vegan restaurant, and the old town, where pure-vegetarian Indian restaurants serve dosa and thali. There are very few fully vegetarian places in the emirate, so the Indian rooms do the heavy lifting, and a car or taxi is needed for most of them.

Eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, one of them fully vegan. Start with the plant-based menu at the Herbarium, a dosa at Dosa Kada, and the pure-veg thali at Najm Cafe. A note on the traps: several listings that appear on vegan maps are ordinary restaurants with a single salad, and the Al Hamra venues need a car, so confirm hours and reserve before travelling.
