Bern eats vegan in the old town and its edges, where a seven-course vegan haute cuisine restaurant in Liebefeld anchors a scene of station pitas, smoothie bowls, vegan pinsas and a vegetarian buffet. The picks cluster around the Bundesplatz and the station, walkable from the Hauptbahnhof, with one deliberate detour to Liebefeld, and four of the eight are fully vegan.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the seven-course menu at Le Caveau, the pitas at Veganitas, the smoothie bowls at Beans and Nuts, and the pinsas at Turbo Lama. A note on the traps: Le Caveau needs a reservation and opens Thursday to Saturday evenings only; Il Caffe Due is a fully vegan university cafeteria that closes during the semester break, so it is held as a reserve rather than a pick; and the vegan dishes at the mixed restaurants need an explicit vegan order.
