Columbia's vegan scene is the most all-vegan-heavy list in the Carolinas block, with five of the eight picks fully vegan, and it runs from Two Notch Road in the northeast to Main Street downtown, Rosewood Drive, and Millwood Avenue. The list leans on small, owner-driven rooms, from a vegan experience and a cafe to a meal-prep kitchen and a raw-food spot, and the vegan-friendly rooms fill out the week with a bistro, a farm-to-table restaurant, and a bourbon bar. It is a scene where the all-vegan rooms are the draw.

Start with a plate at The Reizod Vegan Experience, a cafe plate at The Hoot, and a bowl at Good Life Cafe, then work through Tasty As Fit, Rawtopian Bliss, and Motor Supply. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, five of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: A Peace of Soul closed permanently around June 2026 and was dropped; Rare Variety Cafe was dropped after its address could not be confirmed, with the guide placing it in the NOMA district near Columbia College rather than at 2300 Bull Street; and Good Life Cafe, Rawtopian Bliss, and Terra have address-level pins, so use the street addresses when navigating.
