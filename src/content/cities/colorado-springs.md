Colorado Springs' vegan scene clusters in two places: Old Colorado City on the west side, with its dive bars and Nepali and Salvadoran kitchens, and downtown Tejon Street, with its plant-forward counters and food hall. The list runs from an all-vegan dive bar and an all-vegan bakery to a vegetarian Indian burger joint and a natural-foods cafe, and the best meals are the ones that lean into comfort food rather than apologizing for it.

Start with the vegan crunchwrap and mac and cheese at Burrowing Owl, the jackfruit tacos at The Joint, and a pastry at Sweet Elizabeth's, then work through Gaia, Monse's pupusas, and Sherpa Garden. All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, three of them fully vegan. A note on the traps: Santana's Vegan Grill was reported closed in an August 2026 community thread despite a place card still showing it active, so it is dropped and not listed; Gaia Masala and Burger is filed vegan-friendly rather than all-vegan after a guide's menu notes mentioned meats, so its all-vegan claim is not taken at face value.
