Raipur is the quiet surprise of this push: a state capital with a genuinely enthusiastic pure-veg café scene, argued over by local food creators with real passion. The list runs from the sushi and dim sum of Godwit to the pan-Asian rooms of TAO and Asian Bistro, from the huge family menu at Mishhh to the grandmotherly thalis of Dadi's Paakshala, and finishes at Nectar, a concept kitchen whose menu changes by the day and whose Sunday dal baati thali needs a reservation.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, and an honest note: none of the eight is a strictly all-vegan kitchen. Raipur's scene is pure vegetarian with dairy close at hand, so every pick here is vegan-friendly, and the ordering move is always the same, no ghee, no butter, no curd, no cheese, no paneer. Nectar also serves non-veg dishes, so confirm your plates are the vegetarian ones. Start at Godwit, book Nectar ahead, and let the locals' arguments guide you to the rest.
