Des Moines has no fully vegan sit-down restaurant, and the scene is honest about it: the anchors are an all-vegan cinnamon-roll bakery, a Southeast Asian favourite on Ingersoll, a New York-style pizzeria in the Drake neighbourhood, and a set of Ethiopian, Mexican and Lebanese kitchens with clearly labelled vegan dishes. It is a vegan-friendly city rather than a vegan city, and it works best as a crawl.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 2 of them fully vegan. Start with a cinnamon roll at Cinnaholic, vegan pad Thai at Lucky Lotus, the vegan taco pizza at Dough Co., and the jackfruit quesabirria at El Michoacano Taqueria. A note on the traps: New World Kitchen's old brick-and-mortar vegan restaurant is gone; it now runs as a Monday-only vegan meal-prep pop-up, so it is listed as a pop-up rather than a restaurant. Dirt Burger, once a vegan burger spot, has closed.
