Melbourne doesn't do vegan food as an afterthought, it throws it a party and puts it on the poster. This is the city that invented the all-vegan fine-dining playbook and has been riffing on it ever since.

From Sichuan degustations and wood-fired pizzas to laksa that slaps and queso dip worth crossing town for, our five picks are the tables Melbourne locals brag about. Bring an appetite and an open mind, you'll leave with both firmly converted.
