Daejeon is famously called Korea's no-fun city, and its vegan scene is accordingly small, scattered between the old centre around Daeheung-dong and the university district of Yuseong-gu. The anchors are vegan bakeries rather than restaurants: Fall in the Pool, a vegan dessert cafe, and Sei Vegan, a vegan bakery, with a handful of fully vegan kitchens and vegan-friendly cafes around them. It is a scene that rewards a little planning, as several kitchens keep short hours.

All eight picks are verified open as of November 2026, four of them fully vegan. Start with the matcha cheesecake at Fall in the Pool, the vegan set at Yeobeop Bapsang, the plant-based plates at Orto, and the salads at Slow Cali. A note on the traps: Poke All Day Sejong is listed as maybe closed and sits in a different city, so it is not counted here; several Daejeon listings on older guides are bakeries with only one vegan cake at a time, so check the current menu before you go.
