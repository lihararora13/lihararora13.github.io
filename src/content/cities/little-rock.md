Little Rock's vegan scene is small, scrappy and mostly truck- and cafe-based: a 100% vegan comfort-food spot on Asher Avenue, a plant-based soul-food restaurant on MLK Drive, a vegan deli, and a set of cafes and food trucks that locals pass around by word of mouth. It rewards asking around and checking hours before you go.

All 8 picks are verified open as of November 2026, 6 of them fully vegan. Start with the cauliflower wings at Meme's Twisted Potato, the vegan shrimp at The HOME, the mushroom steak bites at Fye Vegan Chic, and the El-Goodo Bowl at City Silo. A note on the traps: Choca Flood, a vegan spot locals once recommended, was reported closed in September 2026. Several older guides list The Root Cafe as vegan; it is vegan-friendly with a mixed menu, not all-vegan.
